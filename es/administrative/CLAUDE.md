# Practice profile: Administrative Procedure & Public Services — Spain

Orchestrator cold-start for plugin `es-administrative`. Loaded after `es/CLAUDE.md`, before invoking a specific skill.

## Scope

Spanish administrative procedure — guiding through the common administrative procedure: deadlines, administrative silence, and administrative appeals.

## Forums & authorities

The acting administration and the **jurisdicción contencioso-administrativa**.

## Key sources of law

**Ley 39/2015 (LPAC)** — plazos (art. 21), silencio administrativo (arts. 24–25), recursos (arts. 112–126); Ley 40/2015 (LRJSP). For legal family, court hierarchy, and working language, see `es/CLAUDE.md`.

## Citation discipline

Follow the rules in `es/CLAUDE.md`. **Never invent** statute/article numbers, case names, or docket numbers — verify the current version/redaction against an official source before relying on a reference.

## When this plugin does NOT apply

Sector-specific regulatory compliance → `regulatory`; litigation before the courts → `litigation`.
