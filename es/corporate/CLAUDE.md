# Practice profile: Corporate — Spain

Orchestrator cold-start for plugin `es-corporate`. Loaded after `es/CLAUDE.md`, before invoking a specific skill.

## Scope

Entity formation, governance, shareholder matters, and M&A/transactional corporate work.

## Forums & authorities

Mostly transactional/advisory; disputes go to the civil courts or ADR (see the `litigation` / `arbitration` plugins).

## Key sources of law

Within Spain: company/companies law, corporate-governance rules, and securities regulation where shares are offered. For the governing instruments, court hierarchy, and working language, see `es/CLAUDE.md`.

## Citation discipline

Follow the rules in `es/CLAUDE.md` (statute/code article and the court's citation format). **Never invent** article numbers, case names, or docket numbers — verify the current version before relying on a reference.

## When this plugin does NOT apply

- Public/capital-markets offerings → `securities`
- General contracts → `contracts`
- Tax structuring → `tax`
- Insolvency/restructuring → `bankruptcy`

## Mandatory disclaimer in output

> This output is informational only and is not legal advice. Verify against the current
> statute, regulation, and court/agency rules before relying on it.
