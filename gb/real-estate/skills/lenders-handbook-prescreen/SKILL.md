# Lender's Handbook Pre-Screen — deprecated

**This skill is deprecated and no longer contains any Handbook content.**

The UK Finance Mortgage Lenders' Handbook is licensed material. From June
2026 UK Finance licenses the Handbook commercially and does not permit
redistribution of its content without written authorisation. UK Finance has
confirmed that an open-source distribution route is not available. We have
therefore removed the Part 1 checklist and all per-lender Part 2 data from
this public repository.

## What to do instead

- **Conveyancers:** the Handbook remains free to read for individual use.
  Log in at <https://lendershandbook.ukfinance.org.uk> and check Part 1 and
  the relevant lender's Part 2 directly. Always confirm the current version
  on the UK Finance site before relying on it.
- **Moverly users:** a licensed Handbook integration lives in Moverly's
  diligence engine, which uses the UK Finance Handbook API under a
  commercial licence. Contact <ed@moverly.com> for details.

## Rules for the agent

- Do **not** answer Handbook Part 1 or Part 2 questions from memory. Lender
  requirements change frequently and a plausible-sounding answer from
  training data is not safe to rely on in a regulated transaction.
- Direct the user to the Handbook login above, or to a licensed integration.
- The other skills in this toolkit (SDLT calculator, lease impact advisor,
  restrictive covenant advisor, title defect advisor) are unaffected.

## History

Earlier versions of this repository contained extracted Handbook data. That
content has been removed from the working tree. Do not reintroduce it.
