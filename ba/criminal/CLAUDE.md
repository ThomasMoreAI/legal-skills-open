# Practice profile: Criminal Law — Bosnia and Herzegovina

Orchestrator cold-start for plugin `ba-criminal`. Loaded after `ba/CLAUDE.md`, before invoking a specific skill.

## Scope

Criminal Law skills under the law of Bosnia and Herzegovina. Practice-area definition (`practices.json`, jurisdiction-agnostic): criminal law and defense: charges and pretrial detention (bail), motions (suppress/dismiss), plea bargains, sentencing, appeals/habeas, constitutional safeguards (4th/5th/6th Amendments), penal codes and ordinary offenses.

## Legal system

See `ba/CLAUDE.md` for the legal family, courts, core sources, and language.

## Citation discipline

Follow `ba/CLAUDE.md`. **Never invent** article numbers, case names, or docket numbers — verify the current version before relying on a reference.

## When this plugin does NOT apply

- Another area of law → the plugin for that practice area under `ba/`.
- Another country's law → that jurisdiction's plugin.
