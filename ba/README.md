# Bosnia and Herzegovina (`ba`)

Civil law tradition (Central European / former Yugoslav). Complex federal structure under the Dayton Constitution (Annex 4): the State, two Entities (Federation of BiH, Republika Srpska) and Brčko District each legislate within their competences. Cross-cutting context for every plugin: [`CLAUDE.md`](CLAUDE.md).
