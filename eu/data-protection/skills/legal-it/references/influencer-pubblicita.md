# Influencer marketing and advertising disclosure

Three layers, three enforcers. A collaboration can be lawful under one and unlawful under another.

| Layer | Instrument | Enforcer |
|---|---|---|
| Audiovisual media regulation | TUSMA + AGCOM guidelines and code of conduct | AGCOM |
| Consumer protection | artt. 20-23 Codice del Consumo (pratiche commerciali scorrette) | AGCM |
| Self-regulation | Codice di Autodisciplina della Comunicazione Commerciale + Digital Chart | IAP (Giurì) |

## AGCOM — the influencer regime

**Delibera 7/24/CONS of 10 January 2024**, *Linee-guida volte a garantire il rispetto delle disposizioni del Testo unico da parte degli influencer e istituzione di un apposito Tavolo tecnico*. It extends obligations from the **TUSMA** (Testo unico dei servizi di media audiovisivi, D.Lgs 8 novembre 2021 n. 208) to influencers. Verified on agcom.it.

**This has since been amended — do not cite the 2024 thresholds.** **Delibera 197/25/CONS**, adopted **23 July 2025**, published **5 August 2025**, *Modifiche alle linee guida di cui alla delibera n. 7/24/CONS e approvazione del codice di condotta rivolto agli influencer*. Verified on agcom.it. Status as at 2026-08-05.

Current position under the amended guidelines, as stated by AGCOM:

- an **influencer rilevante** is one who reaches, on at least one platform, **at least 500,000 followers** **or** **1,000,000 average monthly views**
- follower count is measured on the thirtieth day before the notification is submitted; monthly views are averaged over the preceding six months
- relevant influencers must file a **notification for AGCOM's public register**, with a deadline of **5 February 2026** (six months from publication) and an update whenever the thresholds are crossed in either direction
- registration is a transparency measure, not a professional qualification
- promotional content must be **immediately recognisable as advertising**, with a clear label such as **"Pubblicità"**, **"ADV"** or **"Sponsorizzato da …"**
- protection of minors and respect for fundamental rights apply to all content published by a relevant influencer
- non-compliance leads to warnings (*diffide*) and proportionate sanctions

Consequences for a brand, not just for the creator:

- below the thresholds the AGCOM regime does not bite, but the AGCM and IAP layers still do. "My influencer has 40,000 followers" is not a disclosure exemption.
- the brand cannot outsource the disclosure duty. An undisclosed paid post is the brand's misleading practice as much as the creator's.
- put the disclosure obligation, the exact wording, its placement and an audit right into the collaboration contract. Verbal briefings are not evidence.

## AGCM — undisclosed advertising as an unfair practice

Advertising that is not recognisable as such is a misleading omission. The B2C regime sits in **artt. 18 to 27 Codice del Consumo** (Parte II, Titolo III, Capo II); AGCM enforces it under art. 27, with a fine range of **EUR 5,000 to 10,000,000** (art. 27 comma 9, as replaced by D.Lgs 26/2023), a minimum of EUR 50,000 for art. 21 commi 3 and 4 practices, and up to 4% of turnover for widespread cross-border infringements (comma 9-bis).

**D.Lgs 2 aprile 2007 n. 145** is the parallel act, but its art. 1 states its purpose is *tutelare i professionisti* — it is the **B2B** misleading-advertising regime and does not govern consumer-facing influencer posts.

This layer has **no threshold at all**. It reaches a micro-creator, a brand's own employee posting about the product, and affiliate links.

Recent enforcement, verified on agcm.it:

- **PS12815, provvedimento n. 31573, adunanza 28 maggio 2025** — *Big Luca / Luca De Stefani*, **EUR 60,000** total (EUR 40,000 + EUR 20,000). Practice A expressly included promoting online *senza, peraltro utilizzare, nelle comunicazioni commerciali, alcuna dicitura di advertisement*; practice B concerned fake reviews and endorsements under art. 23 comma 1 lett. bb-ter) and bb-quater)
- **PS12818, provvedimento n. 31576, adunanza 28 maggio 2025** — *Michele Leka*, **EUR 5,000**, artt. 20, 21, 22
- four parallel 2025 cases closed with binding commitments including the insertion of advertising disclaimers

The 2024 Ferragni Easter-eggs case (PS12699) closed with **commitments**, not a fine. Do not cite it as a sanction.

## IAP — Codice di Autodisciplina and Digital Chart

The **Istituto dell'Autodisciplina Pubblicitaria** binds members of the self-regulatory system and anyone who has accepted the code by contract — in practice most Italian agencies and many advertisers, because the acceptance clause is standard in media contracts. The Giurì can order a post down quickly, long before an AGCM proceeding concludes.

**Codice di Autodisciplina della Comunicazione Commerciale, 71ª edizione, in force from 30 ottobre 2024, art. 7**, verbatim:

> **Art. 7 – Identificazione della comunicazione commerciale**
> La comunicazione commerciale deve sempre essere riconoscibile come tale. Nei mezzi e nelle forme di comunicazione commerciale in cui vengono diffusi contenuti e informazioni di altro genere, la comunicazione commerciale deve essere nettamente distinta per mezzo di idonei accorgimenti. Per quanto riguarda talune forme di comunicazione commerciale diffuse attraverso internet, i principali idonei accorgimenti sono indicati nel Regolamento Digital Chart.

**Regolamento Digital Chart, versione approvata il 30 ottobre 2024.** Verified on iap.it. **Trap: the PDF still linked from IAP's Digital Chart landing page is the superseded 2020 version. The binding text is the HTML page dated 30 ottobre 2024.**

Accepted wordings under the 2024 edition:

| Situation | Accepted wording |
|---|---|
| Paid relationship, art. 1.1 (money, goods, invitations, use of services or other consideration) | **pubblicità** or **advertising**; alternatively *promosso da … brand* / *promoted by … brand*, *sponsorizzato da … brand* / *sponsored by … brand*, or **adv/ad + brand** |
| Gifted product without a commissioning relationship, art. 3.1 | *prodotto inviato da … brand*, *product gifted by … brand*, *product sent by … brand* |
| Event invitation or free use of a service, art. 3.2 | *invitato/a da … brand* / *invited by … brand* |
| Discount codes and affiliate links, art. 4 | *link affiliato + brand* / *affiliate link + brand*, **together with** an art. 1 wording |
| In-feed advertising units, art. 6 | *Pubblicità/Advertising*, *Promosso da … brand*, *Sponsorizzato da … brand*, *Contenuto Sponsorizzato*, *Post Sponsorizzato*, *Presentato da … brand*, with their English equivalents |

Art. 5: no disclaimer is needed for self-promotion where the promoted work or brand coincides with the influencer's own name.

Two corrections to wordings that circulate widely and are **not** valid under the 2024 edition:

- **"in collaborazione con"** appeared only in the superseded 2020 version. It is not in the current endorsement list
- **"#suppliedby"** does not appear in the Italian Digital Chart at all, in either version. It is a UK convention

The 2024 text writes the wordings **without** the `#` prefix. The hashtag rule is positional, not a separate vocabulary.

## Placement, as the Digital Chart states it

The general rule: the disclosure must be *di immediata visibilità, senza la necessità di ulteriori azioni da parte dell'utente*, and it must be **preserved on reshares and reposts** across platforms.

- **Post**: as the first item of information. Where hashtags are used, one of the wordings must appear **within the first three hashtags**
- **Audio**: in the description and spoken at the start
- **Video**: as the first written information in the description, plus a written overlay in the opening scenes, repeated during livestreams
- **Stories and ephemeral content** (art. 1.2): overlaid visibly on **every** promotional item

Additional practical points: English-only tags on Italian-language content aimed at Italian consumers are weak — use an Italian wording. The platform's own "paid partnership" toggle is useful but not sufficient on its own. A bio-level disclosure never covers individual posts.

## Checkpoints

- [ ] Every paid or incentivised post carries a disclosure as the first item of information
- [ ] Where hashtags are used, the disclosure sits within the first three hashtags
- [ ] The wording is one of those in the 30.10.2024 Digital Chart — not "in collaborazione con", not "#suppliedby"
- [ ] The disclosure is in Italian for Italian-language content
- [ ] Gifted products disclosed as *prodotto inviato da …*
- [ ] Affiliate links and discount codes disclosed with the affiliate wording **plus** an art. 1 wording
- [ ] Stories: overlay on every promotional item, not one frame
- [ ] Disclosure preserved on reshares and reposts
- [ ] Employee and founder posts about own products disclosed
- [ ] Follower and view figures of each collaborator recorded, so the AGCOM threshold question is answered on evidence
- [ ] If a collaborator is a relevant influencer, registration status confirmed
- [ ] Disclosure duty and placement written into the contract, with an audit right
- [ ] Screenshots of published posts archived — the brand carries the burden of showing the disclosure existed
- [ ] No reliance on a bio disclosure or on the platform toggle alone
- [ ] All `[[…]]` placeholders resolved or reported as open
