# Practice profile: Immigration — European Union

Orchestrator cold-start for plugin `eu-immigration`. Loaded after `eu/CLAUDE.md`, before invoking a specific skill.

## Scope

Immigration skills under EU law. Practice-area definition (`practices.json`, jurisdiction-agnostic): immigration law: visas and petitions (USCIS), status and residency, asylum, deportation/removal, work and residence permits (pobyt/Aufenthalt), consular procedures for foreign nationals.

## Legal system

See `eu/CLAUDE.md` for the legal family, courts, core sources, and language.

## Citation discipline

Follow `eu/CLAUDE.md`. **Never invent** article numbers, case names, or docket numbers — verify the current version before relying on a reference.

## When this plugin does NOT apply

- Another area of law → the plugin for that practice area under `eu/`.
- Another country's law → that jurisdiction's plugin.
