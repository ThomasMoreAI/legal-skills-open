# Practice profile: Environmental Law — European Union

Orchestrator cold-start for plugin `eu-environmental`. Loaded after `eu/CLAUDE.md`, before invoking a specific skill.

## Scope

Environmental Law skills under EU law. Practice-area definition (`practices.json`, jurisdiction-agnostic): environmental law: EIA, EPA/NEPA, environmental permits, air/water/soil pollution, contaminated land, waste, biodiversity/endangered species, emissions trading/ETS and climate regulation when tied to environmental obligations.

## Legal system

See `eu/CLAUDE.md` for the legal family, courts, core sources, and language.

## Citation discipline

Follow `eu/CLAUDE.md`. **Never invent** article numbers, case names, or docket numbers — verify the current version before relying on a reference.

## When this plugin does NOT apply

- Another country's law → that jurisdiction's plugin.
