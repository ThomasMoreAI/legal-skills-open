# Application timeline

Status as at 2026-08-05. Basis: Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 Art 111 and 113, as amended by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 (Digital Omnibus on AI) of 8 July 2026, OJ L, 2026/1744, 24.7.2026, in force 27 July 2026.

The omnibus is the single most important thing to get right about this regulation today. Any timeline written before 24 July 2026 is wrong for high-risk systems.

## What Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 changed

The amending act is Art 1 of Reg (EU) 2026/1744; it also amends Regulation (EU) 2018/1139 (aviation, Art 2) and Regulation (EU) 2023/1230 (machinery, Art 3). It does **not** amend the GDPR.

| Art 1(40)(a) | Art 113, third para, point (a) | New Art 5(1)(ba), (bb) and Art 5(1a), (1b) apply from **2 December 2026**; the rest of Chapters I and II still from 2 February 2025. |

## Transitional rules, precisely

- **High-risk legacy systems (Art 111(2) as amended).** The Regulation applies to operators of high-risk systems placed on the market or put into service before the applicable Chapter III date only if, from that date, the systems are subject to significant changes in their designs. Recital 39 of Reg (EU) 2026/1744: the decisive factor is the date the *first unit of that type and model* was placed on the market; further units of the same type and model may continue without additional certification while the design is unchanged. Any significant design change after the date triggers full compliance including conformity assessment.
- **Public authority use.** No grandfathering past 2 August 2030 (Art 111(2), second sentence).
- **GPAI models (Art 111(3)).** Models placed on the market before 2 August 2025 must comply by 2 August 2027. Unchanged by the omnibus.
- **Generative systems and Art 50(2) (Art 111(4), new).** Four-month transitional period, expiring 2 December 2026, for systems placed on the market before 2 August 2026. Recital 38 of Reg (EU) 2026/1744. It covers the Art 50(2) marking obligation only. A system that is both interactive and generative had to comply with Art 50(1) on 2 August 2026 (Guidelines C(2026) 5054 final, point 153).
- **No retroactive labelling.** Content generated before 2 August 2026 need not be marked or labelled retroactively; but text generated before that date and *published* on or after it must be labelled (Guidelines C(2026) 5054 final, point 154).
- **Prohibitions bite regardless.** Art 111(1) and (2) both open "Without prejudice to the application of Article 5" — no grandfathering from a prohibition.

## Pending, not law

State these as pending. Do not present them as obligations.

- **Commission guidelines on high-risk classification (Art 6(5)).** Draft only; public consultation via EU Survey closed 23 July 2026. The statutory deadline was 2 February 2026.
- **Harmonised standards under Art 40.** CEN-CENELEC JTC 21 deliverables. As at 2026-08-05 no harmonised standard under the AI Act has been cited in the Official Journal, so no presumption of conformity is available under Art 40 or Art 53(4). - **Delegated acts under new Art 2(13)** limiting Art 9 to 15 and 17 to 25 obligations where Annex I Section A legislation gives equivalent protection: due by 2 August 2027, not yet adopted.
- **Commission guidelines on Art 8(2), 9(10) and 17(3)** for Annex I operators: due by 1 August 2027 (Art 96(1)(g) as inserted).
- **Post-market monitoring guidance and voluntary template:** due by 2 September 2027 (Art 72(3) as amended).

## Checkpoints

- [ ] Any date cited for high-risk obligations is 2 December 2027 or 2 August 2028, never 2 August 2026 or 2 August 2027
- [ ] Art 50 treated as already applicable, with the Art 111(4) transitional only for Art 50(2) and only for pre-2 August 2026 systems
- [ ] The 2 December 2026 date given for the new Art 5 prohibitions
- [ ] Grandfathering claim tested against "significant changes in design" and against the type-and-model rule in recital 39
- [ ] Public-authority deployment flagged with the 2 August 2030 backstop
- [ ] Nothing in the "pending" list stated as binding law
