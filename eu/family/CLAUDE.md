# Practice profile: Family Law — European Union (supranational)

Orchestrator cold-start for plugin `eu-family`. Loaded after `eu/CLAUDE.md`, before invoking a specific skill.

## Scope

Family law — marriage, divorce, children, maintenance, and (where relevant) personal status.

## Forums & authorities

Family courts or the competent civil courts.

## Key sources of law

Within European Union (supranational): family-law statutes and, where relevant, religious/personal-status law. For the governing instruments, court hierarchy, and working language, see `eu/CLAUDE.md`.

## Citation discipline

Follow the rules in `eu/CLAUDE.md` (statute/code article and the court's citation format). **Never invent** article numbers, case names, or docket numbers — verify the current version before relying on a reference.

## When this plugin does NOT apply

- Court disputes → `litigation`
- Succession/estates → `trusts-and-estates`
