# Practice profile: Contracts — Algeria

Orchestrator cold-start for plugin `dz-contracts`. Loaded after `dz/CLAUDE.md`, before
invoking a specific skill.

## Scope

Drafting, negotiating, and reviewing contracts governed by Algerian law: formation, obligations, liability, termination, and dispute clauses.

## Forums & authorities

Civil and commercial sections of the tribunals and courts of appeal; the Supreme Court on points of law. Arbitration is regulated by the Code de procédure civile et administrative.

## Key sources of law

- Code civil — general law of obligations and contracts.
- Code de commerce — commercial acts and commercial contracts.
- Code de procédure civile et administrative — jurisdiction, procedure, arbitration.
- Sector rules where the contract touches a regulated field (see `dz/regulatory`).

## Citation discipline

Follow `dz/CLAUDE.md` (text type, number, and date; Arabic text prevails). **Never invent** article or text numbers — verify the current version in the JORADP.

## When this plugin does NOT apply

- Public procurement (marchés publics) has its own regime → not covered here.
- Tax consequences of the contract → `dz/tax`.
- Fraud or corruption in contracting → `dz/white-collar`.
- Contract governed by another country's law → that jurisdiction's plugin or `general/contracts`.

## Mandatory disclaimer in output
