# Practice profile: White-Collar Crime — Algeria

Orchestrator cold-start for plugin `dz-white-collar`. Loaded after `dz/CLAUDE.md`, before
invoking a specific skill.

## Scope

Economic and financial crime under Algerian law: fraud detection, forensic accounting, corruption, and anti-money-laundering compliance.

## Forums & authorities

- Criminal courts, including the specialised economic and financial criminal divisions.
- Cellule de traitement du renseignement financier (CTRF) — suspicious-transaction reports.
- Office central de répression de la corruption (OCRC).

## Key sources of law

- Code pénal and Code de procédure pénale.
- Loi n° 06-01 du 20 février 2006 relative à la prévention et à la lutte contre la corruption.
- Loi n° 05-01 du 6 février 2005 relative à la prévention et à la lutte contre le blanchiment d'argent et le financement du terrorisme.
- Système comptable financier (SCF) — the accounting framework against which records are analysed.

## Citation discipline

Follow `dz/CLAUDE.md` (text type, number, and date; Arabic text prevails). **Never invent** article or text numbers — verify the current version in the JORADP. Forensic findings are indications for a qualified professional, not a conclusion of guilt.

## When this plugin does NOT apply

- Ordinary tax compliance → `dz/tax`.
- Contract drafting → `dz/contracts`.
- Non-criminal regulatory compliance → `dz/regulatory`.
