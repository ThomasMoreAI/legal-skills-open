# Practice profile: Regulatory — Algeria

Orchestrator cold-start for plugin `dz-regulatory`. Loaded after `dz/CLAUDE.md`, before
invoking a specific skill.

## Scope

Compliance with Algerian regulatory regimes: protection of personal data, industrial property filings, commercial registration, and sector rules that apply to a business activity.

## Forums & authorities

- Autorité nationale de protection des données à caractère personnel (ANPDP) — personal data.
- INAPI — trademarks, patents, and industrial designs.
- CNRC — commercial register.
- Sector regulators and ministries for the activity concerned.

## Key sources of law

- Loi n° 18-07 du 10 juin 2018 relative à la protection des personnes physiques dans le traitement des données à caractère personnel.
- Industrial-property texts (trademarks, patents) and their implementing decrees.
- Sector statutes and decrees for the activity concerned.

## Citation discipline

Follow `dz/CLAUDE.md` (text type, number, and date; Arabic text prevails). **Never invent** article or text numbers — verify the current version in the JORADP.

## When this plugin does NOT apply

- Tax filings and audits → `dz/tax`.
- Corruption, fraud, money laundering → `dz/white-collar`.
- Drafting the commercial contract itself → `dz/contracts`.
- EU GDPR questions for an EU controller → `eu/data-protection`.
