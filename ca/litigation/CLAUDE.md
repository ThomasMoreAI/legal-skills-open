# Practice profile: Litigation — Canada

Orchestrator cold-start for plugin `ca-litigation`. Loaded after `ca/CLAUDE.md`, before invoking a specific skill.

## Scope

Canadian Crown-law litigation — Crown fiduciary breaches, fund mismanagement, conflicts of interest, and failure to protect reserve lands (Indigenous/Aboriginal-law disputes).

## Forums & authorities

Federal Court and provincial superior courts.

## Key sources of law

Crown fiduciary doctrine, the Indian Act, and Aboriginal-law jurisprudence of the Supreme Court of Canada. For legal family, court hierarchy, and working language, see `ca/CLAUDE.md`.

## Citation discipline

Follow the rules in `ca/CLAUDE.md`. **Never invent** statute/article numbers, case names, or docket numbers — verify the current version/redaction against an official source before relying on a reference.

## When this plugin does NOT apply

Constitutional rights design review → `constitutional`; property/expropriation forms → `real-estate`.
