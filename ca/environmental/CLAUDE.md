# Practice profile: Environmental Law — Canada

Orchestrator cold-start for plugin `ca-environmental`. Loaded after `ca/CLAUDE.md`, before invoking a specific skill.

## Scope

Environmental law — permitting, compliance, liability, and remediation.

## Forums & authorities

The environmental authority and the administrative/civil courts.

## Key sources of law

Within Canada: environmental-protection statutes, permitting rules, and agency guidance. For the governing instruments, court hierarchy, and working language, see `ca/CLAUDE.md`.

## Citation discipline

Follow the rules in `ca/CLAUDE.md` (statute/code article and the court's citation format). **Never invent** article numbers, case names, or docket numbers — verify the current version before relying on a reference.

## When this plugin does NOT apply

- General regulation → `regulatory`
- Disputes → `litigation`
- Land/development → `real-estate`

## Mandatory disclaimer in output

> This output is informational only and is not legal advice. Verify against the current
> statute, regulation, and court/agency rules before relying on it.
