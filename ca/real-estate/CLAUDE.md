# Practice profile: Real Estate — Canada

Orchestrator cold-start for plugin `ca-real-estate`. Loaded after `ca/CLAUDE.md`, before invoking a specific skill.

## Scope

Ontario expropriation procedure — pre-flighting Forms 1–12 under O. Reg. 363/90 (statutory authority, compensation breakdown, notice timing) before service or filing.

## Forums & authorities

The expropriating authority and the **Ontario Land Tribunal**.

## Key sources of law

**Expropriations Act (Ontario)** and **O. Reg. 363/90** (prescribed forms). For legal family, court hierarchy, and working language, see `ca/CLAUDE.md`.

## Citation discipline

Follow the rules in `ca/CLAUDE.md`. **Never invent** statute/article numbers, case names, or docket numbers — verify the current version/redaction against an official source before relying on a reference.

## When this plugin does NOT apply

Crown fiduciary / Indigenous land claims → `litigation`; constitutional challenges → `constitutional`.
