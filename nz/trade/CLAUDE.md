# Practice profile: International Trade & Customs — New Zealand

Orchestrator cold-start for plugin `nz-trade`. Loaded after `nz/CLAUDE.md`, before invoking a specific skill.

## Scope

International Trade & Customs skills under the law of New Zealand. Practice-area definition (`practices.json`, jurisdiction-agnostic): international trade and customs law: customs valuation, tariffs, HTS/CN classification, origin of goods, certificates of origin, antidumping/countervailing duties, safeguards, CBAM, WTO/GATT and trade remedies.

## Legal system

See `nz/CLAUDE.md` for the legal family, courts, core sources, and language.

## Citation discipline

Follow `nz/CLAUDE.md`. **Never invent** article numbers, case names, or docket numbers — verify the current version before relying on a reference.

## When this plugin does NOT apply

- Another country's law → that jurisdiction's plugin.
