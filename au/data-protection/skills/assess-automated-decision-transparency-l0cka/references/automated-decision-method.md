## Decision inventory

For every system-mediated decision capture:

- the decision, date, accountable entity and affected group;
- whether a computer program makes the decision or performs a substantially
  and directly related step;
- the personal-information categories used;
- whether rights or interests could reasonably be significantly affected;
- the human role, override, explanation and contestability route; and
- current privacy-policy language, change owner and publication deadline.

Also route WA public-sector and covered-contractor uses to PRIS IPP 10 from 1
July 2026. Verify its specific data and significant-decision scope from current
official text. Refer discrimination, administrative, employment and consumer
law for separate review rather than claiming complete coverage.
