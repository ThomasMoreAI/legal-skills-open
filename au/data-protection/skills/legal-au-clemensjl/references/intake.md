# Intake

Answer before the first legal text is drafted. Unanswered items become `[[MISSING: …]]` in the draft and in the report. Never guessed, never plausibly filled in.

## Legal entity

1. Entity type — sole trader, partnership, trust, company (Pty Ltd), incorporated association, not-for-profit?
2. Full legal name exactly as registered, and every registered business name used publicly
3. ABN, and ACN if a company
4. Registered office and principal place of business (street address, suburb, state, postcode)
5. Contact email and at least one further channel that produces a response within a short time
6. Directors or responsible officers, if a company
7. Is the entity registered for GST?
8. Any occupational licence, AFSL, ACL credit licence, health practitioner registration, or other regulator authorisation, with the number

## Privacy scope

9. Annual turnover for the last financial year — above or at/below $3,000,000? This decides Privacy Act s 6D. Turnover means all income from all sources.
10. Does the business do any of: provide a health service and hold health information; disclose personal information about an individual for a benefit, service or advantage; provide a benefit, service or advantage to collect personal information from someone else; act as a Commonwealth contracted service provider; operate a residential tenancy database; act as a credit reporting body; report under the AML/CTF Act; hold accreditation under the Consumer Data Right; belong to a corporate group where a related body corporate is covered? Any one of these removes the exemption.
11. Has the entity opted in to Privacy Act coverage under s 6EA?
12. Are there employees, and are employee records involved? (The employee records exemption is separate and narrow.)

## Data handling

13. What personal information is collected — contact forms, accounts, orders, payments, newsletters, support tickets, server logs, device identifiers, IP addresses, location?
14. Is any of it sensitive information under the Privacy Act (health, racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, religious beliefs, sexual orientation, criminal record, biometric or genetic data, union membership)?
15. Purpose of each collection, and any secondary use planned
16. Retention period or the criterion that determines it, per category
17. Recipients — hosting, payment processor, fulfilment, email service, analytics, CRM, support desk, advertising networks
18. Which of those are located overseas, and in which countries? Which are "use" (retained effective control under contract) and which are "disclosure" (APP 8 engaged)?
19. Any profiling, scoring, automated decision-making, or AI-assisted decision that significantly affects a person's rights or interests? Needed for the APP 1.7–1.9 disclosures that commence 10 December 2026.
20. Who is the accountable person for privacy, and who handles access, correction and complaints?
21. Is there a data breach response plan, and who is on the response team?
22. Are children or people under 18 likely to use the service? Is the service likely to be accessed by children for the purposes of the Children's Online Privacy Code?

## Selling

23. Sells to consumers, to businesses, or both?
24. Goods, services, digital products, digital subscriptions, or a mix?
25. Where do customers live? Any sales into the EU, the UK, or California? That triggers foreign regimes on top of Australian law.
26. Payment methods and payment service provider
27. Delivery timeframes, shipping charges, delivery restrictions
28. Any voluntary change-of-mind returns policy, and its exact terms — this is optional and must not be presented as a legal right
29. Any manufacturer or supplier warranty against defects? If yes, its duration, what it covers, who bears claim expenses
30. Subscriptions or auto-renewals? Renewal terms, notice, cancellation route
31. Any conduct that could be an unsolicited consumer agreement — telemarketing, door-to-door, approaching people in public places?
32. Any advertised discounts, "was/now" pricing, or scarcity claims? Evidence for each?

## Marketing

33. Email, SMS, push, or instant messaging marketing planned?
34. How is consent obtained and recorded — wording, timestamp, source, IP?
35. Any purchased, rented, scraped or appended contact lists? (Assume unlawful until proven otherwise.)
36. Telemarketing to Australian phone numbers?
37. Any testimonials, reviews, influencer or affiliate content? Any incentives given for reviews?

## Platform and safety

38. Does the service host or transmit content posted by users — comments, reviews, profiles, forum, marketplace, messaging?
39. Does it have social-interaction features with feeds, likes, or stories that could make it an age-restricted social media platform under Online Safety Act Part 4A?
40. Any adult, gambling, or restricted-category content?
41. Is there a moderation policy, a reporting route, and a named contact for eSafety or law enforcement?

## Accessibility and delivery

42. Is this a government service, or delivered under a government contract? Then the Digital Service Standard applies on top of the Disability Discrimination Act 1992.
43. Target WCAG version and conformance level agreed with the business, and who tests it
44. Is there an accessibility statement and a channel for people who hit a barrier?

## Existing material

45. Is there an existing privacy policy, terms of service or returns policy? Where did it come from — a lawyer, a generator, a competitor's site, a Shopify or WordPress default, an AI draft?
46. Does any existing text mention GDPR, lawful basis, data controller, right to erasure, 72 hours, cooling-off, right of withdrawal, CAN-SPAM, or an Impressum? Each is evidence of a foreign template and the text is rewritten rather than patched.
47. Which platform generates the transactional emails and invoices, and can its templates be edited?
48. Who signs off legally, and by when is go-live planned?

## Checkpoints

- [ ] Every entity identifier collected exactly as registered, not as typed from memory
- [ ] Turnover answered with a figure or an explicit "unknown", not an assumption
- [ ] Every s 6D carve-out answered individually, not with a blanket "no"
- [ ] Overseas recipients listed with country, not as "may be transferred internationally"
- [ ] Sensitive information identified explicitly, including health data collected incidentally
- [ ] Every unanswered question carried into the draft as `[[MISSING: …]]`
