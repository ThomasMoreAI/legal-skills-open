# State and Territory breach map

Classify each pathway by legal status and verify its current text.

| Jurisdiction | Status at 14 August 2026 | Boundary or qualification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NSW | Statutory mandatory notification under PPIP Act Part 6A | Operative from 28 November 2023 for covered NSW public-sector agencies; includes personal and health information held by them. |
| Queensland | Statutory mandatory notification under the Information Privacy Act | State agencies from 1 July 2025; local government from 1 July 2026. |
| WA | Enacted, future statutory scheme | PRIS breach provisions commence 1 January 2027; before then distinguish voluntary, policy, contract and Commonwealth duties. |
| Victoria | Standards-based notification for bodies in scope of VPDSS | Do not call it a universal statutory regulator-and-individual notification scheme. |
| SA, Tasmania, ACT and NT | Administrative, internal, policy or voluntary pathways | Do not present them as equivalent to a statutory MNDB scheme. |

Always check Commonwealth NDB and sector-specific obligations separately.
