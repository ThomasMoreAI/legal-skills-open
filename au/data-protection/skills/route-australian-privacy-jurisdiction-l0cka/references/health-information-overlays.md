# Health-information overlays

Use this map only to select official sources for verification. It is not a
substitute for current legislation.

| Jurisdiction | Candidate local coverage | Commonwealth interaction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NSW | The HRIP Act covers NSW public agencies and specified private health handling. | A private health provider can have concurrent HRIP Act and Privacy Act obligations. PPIP Part 6A does not apply solely because HRIP applies. |
| Victoria | The Health Records Act applies broadly to public and private organisations handling health information. | Private organisations may also be APP entities. |
| ACT | The Health Records (Privacy and Access) Act covers public and private record keepers. | Private providers may also be APP entities. |
| Queensland, WA, SA, Tasmania and NT | Check public-sector privacy, health confidentiality and government-contract rules. No general local private-health privacy Act was identified at the registry date (14 August 2026). | Commonwealth privacy law is ordinarily the principal general privacy regime for private providers, subject to entity coverage and sector overlays. |

Also test My Health Record, healthcare identifiers, confidentiality, records,
professional and public-health legislation independently. Do not generalise the
Privacy Act section 26WD My Health Record NDB interaction to other health data.
