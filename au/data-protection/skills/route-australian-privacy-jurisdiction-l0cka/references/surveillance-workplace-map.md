# Surveillance and workplace map

Use the current official text and distinguish dedicated workplace legislation
from general surveillance-device law.

| Jurisdiction | Candidate material |
| --- | --- |
| NSW | Workplace Surveillance Act 2005 and Surveillance Devices Act 2007 |
| ACT | Workplace Privacy Act 2011 and Crimes (Surveillance Devices) Act 2010 |
| Victoria | Surveillance Devices Act 1999; a dedicated workplace-surveillance regime remained a proposal at 14 August 2026 |
| Queensland | Invasion of Privacy Act 1971 and any relevant criminal or police-powers provisions |
| WA | Surveillance Devices Act 1998 |
| SA | Surveillance Devices Act 2016 |
| Tasmania | Listening Devices Act 1991 and Police Powers (Surveillance Devices) Act 2006 |
| NT | Surveillance Devices Act 2007 |

For AI monitoring, separately test privacy, health, biometrics, employment,
industrial, discrimination and public-sector decision-making issues. The last
four topics are referrals, not complete analyses by this plugin.
