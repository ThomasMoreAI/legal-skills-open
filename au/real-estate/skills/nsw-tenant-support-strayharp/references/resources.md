## Stage 3a — Repairs → Fair Trading

1. **Tenancy complaint (conciliation, free):**
   - Online form: https://www.cas.fairtrading.nsw.gov.au/icmspublicweb/forms/Tenancy.html
   - Info page: https://www.nsw.gov.au/departments-and-agencies/fair-trading/complaints-and-enquiries/housing-and-property/tenancy-landlord-and-agent
   - Phone: 13 32 20 (8:30am–5pm weekdays)
2. **If unresolved → rectification order investigation (s 65C):**
   - https://www.nsw.gov.au/housing-and-construction/rules/rectification-orders-for-rental-properties
   - Preconditions: written request; request states what the repairs are; landlord failed without reasonable excuse. The pathway starts from the complaint service — complaint first, investigation second.
   - The order lists findings and a completion date: an official regulator finding of breach, strong evidence at NCAT later. It is a remedy for a current tenant — likely unavailable after vacating.

**Form-filling:** "Trader" = agency's legal/company name; the individual agent goes in the contact-person field. Amendments: 13 32 20 with reference number.

## Stage 3b — Agent conduct → Fair Trading

- Property professional complaint pathway: https://www.nsw.gov.au/departments-and-agencies/fair-trading/complaints-and-enquiries/housing-and-property
- Ground the complaint in the Rules of Conduct (see legislation.md § Agent conduct) with specific dated incidents and the written record attached.
- Threats of violence → police, not Fair Trading.

Operational notes: the form asks for RTA section numbers and orders sought (map via legislation.md). Respondent must be the correct legal entity with address for service — for an agency, attach an ASIC register search. Fees apply per the current schedule; concession (~25% of full fee) for pensioners and Legal Aid / community legal centre clients. Compensation jurisdiction limits apply (historically $15,000 general / $30,000 bond — verify current amounts via the NCAT fees/limits pages before quoting).
