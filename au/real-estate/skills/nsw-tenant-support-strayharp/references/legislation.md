## 1. Live verification protocol

Before quoting any section's words, any dollar amount, or any notice period — and always for anything marked (verify) — fetch the current text:

- **Whole current Act (authoritative):** https://legislation.nsw.gov.au/view/whole/html/inforce/current/act-2010-042
- **Per-section (AustLII, fast):** `https://www5.austlii.edu.au/au/legis/nsw/consol_act/rta2010207/s{N}.html` — e.g. s50, s65c (lowercase letter suffixes)
- **Agent rules:** `https://classic.austlii.edu.au/au/legis/nsw/consol_reg/pasar2022376/sch1.html`
- **Residential Tenancies Regulation:** search legislation.nsw.gov.au for the current version (prescribed periods, break-fee details, minor-alteration list live here)
- **Plain-language check:** the matching Tenants' Union factsheet (resources.md), noting its own law-change banner

If a fetch fails, say so and give the user the legislation.nsw.gov.au link rather than asserting from memory. A named-but-unverified deadline ("there is a time limit here — we need to confirm it") beats a confidently wrong one.

## 1A. Does the RTA apply? (check before citing anything)

The Act excludes several occupancy types (s 7–8). Applying RTA sections to them is confidently wrong advice — the skill's worst failure mode.

| Occupancy | Covered? | Route instead |
|---|---|---|
| Standard residential tenancy (sole or co-tenants on the agreement) | Yes | This skill |
| Sharehouse **head-tenant/sub-tenant** arrangements | Usually yes (written sub-tenancy) — verify | Share Housing Survival Guide: tenants.org.au/factsheet-share-housing |
| **Boarders and lodgers** (no exclusive possession — landlord/head-occupant controls the premises, house rules, room access) | **No** | Factsheet 14: tenants.org.au/factsheet-14-boarders-and-lodgers; Boarding Houses Act 2012 may apply; TAAS can still advise |
| Social housing (DCJ/community housing) | Yes, **plus Part 7** (s 136–156D: behaviour agreements, rebates, eligibility terminations, strikes) | tenants.org.au/resources/public-and-community-housing |
| Land lease communities (own the home, rent the site) | No — Residential (Land Lease) Communities Act 2013 | tenants.org.au/thenoticeboard |
| Retirement villages | No — Retirement Villages Act 1999 | Fair Trading retirement villages pathway |
| Short-term/holiday lets, hotels, Airbnb-style stays | No | Consumer law / Fair Trading general |
| Occupant not on the agreement | Sometimes (s 10, s 77) — verify | TAAS |

Distinguishing a lodger from a tenant turns on exclusive possession and control — if unclear from the user's description, ask about locks on their room, house rules, and whether the owner lives there, and flag the uncertainty to TAAS rather than assuming.

## 2. Core obligations

| Section | Content | Use |
|---|---|---|
| s 50 | Quiet enjoyment; s 50(2) landlord **or agent** must not interfere with reasonable peace, comfort or privacy in using the premises; s 50(4) term of every agreement | Harassment, bullying calls, excessive contact. Frame as intrusion into the home, not bad service |
| s 52 | Premises reasonably clean, fit for habitation, meeting the **minimum standards** (structural soundness, lighting, ventilation, electricity/gas outlets, plumbing, water, bathroom facilities) (verify list) | Habitability, mould, chronic disrepair |
| s 63 | Maintain in reasonable state of repair having regard to age, rent, prospective life; term of every agreement; carve-out only for tenant-caused disrepair | The repairs workhorse. Duration of documented inaction is usually the strongest fact |
| s 219 | Contracting out prohibited | No lease term can reduce these protections |

| Section | Situation | Key point |
|---|---|---|
| s 33–37 | Paying rent, receipts, records | A free and convenient payment method must be offered (s 35) |
| s 41 | **Rent increases** | Written notice 60 days; frequency capped at once per 12 months (verify scope post-reform). Wrong notice = ineffective increase |
| s 44 | Excessive rent | NCAT can review an increase or the rent itself |
| s 43, 45 | Rent reduction | Withdrawal of amenity; abatement where premises partly unusable/frustrated |

| s 88–89 | **Rent arrears** | Non-payment termination notice requires 14 days' arrears; **pay-and-stay**: paying the arrears (or keeping to an agreed repayment plan) generally neutralises the notice/termination (verify current mechanics). Never let a user assume arrears = automatic eviction |

## 8. Ending a tenancy

**By the tenant:**

| Section | Ground | Minimum notice |
|---|---|---|
| s 96 | End of fixed term | 14 days |
| s 97 | Periodic, no ground | 21 days |
| s 98 | Breach by landlord | 14 days; may end a fixed term early without compensation |
| s 98A | Information-disclosure contravention | 14 days (verify) |
| s 100 | Statutory early-exit grounds (e.g. offered social housing, aged-care admission) (verify list) | 14 days |
| s 104 | Hardship (fixed term) → NCAT | — |
| s 107 | **Break fees** — the section is titled "Landlord's remedies on abandonment"; the mandatory break-fee scale for ending a fixed term early without ground sits within it, tiered by proportion of term elapsed (verify current subsection and amounts before citing) | — |

s 98 mechanics: the agent cannot reject or convert a tenant's notice — only NCAT can (s 105). Landlord's revocation window under s 98(4): before the termination date and within 7 days of service (verify prescribed period). The real risk sits afterwards: if NCAT finds the breach insufficient to justify termination, a vacated tenant risks abandonment consequences (s 106–107). The sufficiency factors (s 103(2) by analogy): nature of breach, previous breaches, remedy steps by landlord, steps by tenant, tenancy history. Specialist advice before serving or acting on one — always.

**By the landlord (post-May-2025):** no-grounds termination abolished; a specified ground is required (s 84–87N): tenant breach (s 87C), sale (s 87D–E), significant renovation (s 87F), demolition (s 87G), landlord/family moving in (s 87M), others — each with its own notice period, several with mandatory supporting documents (s 85), re-letting exclusion periods, and offences for non-genuine grounds (s 86–87). Renovation ground unavailable while a rectification order is on foot. Every landlord notice → Eviction Notice Checker + TAAS.

**Protections around any termination:**

| Section | Protection |
|---|---|
| s 110–110B | Tenant may vacate early once a termination notice exists (rules differ periodic/fixed) |
| s 113 | Notice defects overlooked where no disadvantage — cuts both ways |
| s 115 | **Retaliatory eviction**: notice/application ineffective if wholly *or partly* motivated by the tenant enforcing rights (Fair Trading complaint, NCAT application, "any other action"). Tenant's application: before the termination date, within the prescribed period — deadline-critical |
| s 117 | Acceptance of rent after notice |
| s 119–121 | Lockouts and self-help eviction are offences; only NCAT/courts order possession |
| s 223 | Service rules — every deadline runs from service. Check before asserting any date |

## 10. Agent conduct — Property and Stock Agents Regulation 2022, Sch 1

| Rule | Content | Application |
|---|---|---|
| r 1 | Must know the Act incl. residential tenancy law | Misstating notice periods |
| r 3 | Honesty, fairness, professionalism; no misleading or deceptive conduct | Misstatements of rights |
| r 4 | Reasonable skill, care, diligence | Sustained inaction on repairs |
| r 5 | No high-pressure tactics, **harassment**, harsh or unconscionable conduct — not limited to "in a transaction" | Bullying calls |
| r 10 | Licensee-in-charge ensures staff compliance | Manager involvement puts the agency licence in play |

Plus Property and Stock Agents Act 2002 s 52 (misleading inducement). Fair Trading enforces — parallel to, not instead of, NCAT.

## 11. Deadlines table (compute on day one of any notice or event)

| Trigger | Clock |
|---|---|
| Any notice served | All periods run from service (s 223) |
| Rent increase notice | 60 days before it can take effect; frequency cap (verify) |
| Non-payment termination notice | 14 days' arrears precondition; pay-and-stay available (verify mechanics) |
| Tenant's s 98 notice served | Landlord revocation: before termination date AND within 7 days (verify) |
| Landlord's termination notice served | s 115 retaliatory application: before termination date, within prescribed period — diarise immediately |
| Pet request lodged | Landlord response within prescribed period or deemed consent (verify, ~21 days) |
| Urgent repair self-arranged | Reimbursement within 14 days of written demand |
| Rectification order issued | Amendment window 7 days |
| Database listing proposed | Dispute at NCAT; listings expire after 3 years |
| Vacating | Photos against ingoing condition report; initiate bond claim promptly |
