# AGLC4 citation templates and audit

Use this reference when producing a batch of citations, converting another style, or building a bibliography or References section.

## Fast source routing

| Source | Full-citation skeleton |
|---|---|
| Australian reported case | `Case Name (Year) Volume Report Series Start, Pinpoint (Judge).` |
| Australian neutral case | `Case Name [Year] Court Number, [Pinpoint] (Judge).` |
| Australian Act | `Title Year (Jurisdiction) Pinpoint.` |
| Journal article | `Author, ‘Title’ (Year) Volume(Issue) Journal Start, Pinpoint.` |
| Book | `Author, Title (Publisher, Edition, Year) Pinpoint.` |
| Chapter | `Author, ‘Chapter’ in Editor (ed), Book (Publisher, Edition, Year) Start, Pinpoint.` |
| Report | `Author, Title (Document Type Number, Full Date) Pinpoint.` |
| Parliamentary debate | `Jurisdiction, Parliamentary Debates, Chamber, Full Date, Pinpoint (Speaker).` |
| Webpage | `Author, ‘Document’, Website (Type, Full Date) Pinpoint <URL>.` |
| Treaty | `Title, conclusion wording Full Date, Series (entry into force wording Full Date) Pinpoint.` |
| UN document | `Author, Title, Resolution, UN Doc Number (Full Date) Pinpoint.` |
| Foreign source | use the jurisdiction chapter; otherwise adapt chapters 2–3 or rule 26 |

These are routing skeletons, not substitutes for the topic references.

## Metadata intake

For each source, record:

1. source type and jurisdiction;
2. authoritative title and author/parties;
3. publication, court, legislature, body, or series;
4. date, year, volume, issue, identifier, and starting page;
5. exact supporting pinpoint;
6. authoritative or preferred version status;
7. stable URL or archive only if useful; and
8. first footnote number and any short title.

Do not fill a blank from assumption. Use `unresolved: [field]` during drafting and remove or resolve every marker before issue.

## Conversion procedure

1. Strip the old citation system's styling without discarding source facts.
2. Resolve the source type and preferred authority version.
3. Rebuild the full citation from verified fields in AGLC4 order.
4. Add the exact pinpoint supporting the proposition or quotation.
5. Rebuild all subsequent references from the final footnote sequence.
6. Generate the bibliography/References entries from the verified full citations, not from shortened footnotes.
7. Compare the final citation back to the source to catch transposed names, dates, volumes, and pages.

## References section

When a document calls the AGLC4 bibliography a `References` section, keep the requested heading but apply rule 1.13 to its contents:

- include every source relied on;
- group into Articles/Books/Reports, Cases, Legislation, Treaties, and Other where useful;
- invert only the first author's first name and surname;
- alphabetise under rule 1.13; and
- omit the final full stop from each entry.

Do not copy shortened footnote forms, `Ibid`, `(n X)`, introductory signals, discursive footnote text, or pinpoint-only references into the References section unless the source-specific rule makes the pinpoint part of the source identity.

## Citation audit

For every citation, answer yes to each applicable check:

- **Identity:** does it identify the source actually relied on?
- **Preference:** is this the AGLC4-preferred report, official document, or published form?
- **Completeness:** are all required fields present and verified?
- **Order:** are elements in the source-specific order?
- **Typography:** are italics, single quotation marks, brackets, and roman elements correct?
- **Punctuation:** are commas, parentheses, semicolons, en dashes, and the final full stop correct?
- **Pinpoint:** does the pinpoint use the correct unit and support the proposition?
- **Sequence:** do `Ibid`, `(n X)`, short titles, and `at` still point to the intended source?
- **Retrieval:** is a URL useful, clean, stable, and placed correctly without an access date?
- **Bibliography:** is the entry complete, correctly transformed, categorised, sorted, and without a final full stop?

## Report findings

When reviewing citations, classify issues as:

- `source error`: wrong or unverifiable authority details;
- `AGLC4 format error`: correct source, wrong form;
- `pinpoint gap`: no precise support or incorrect pinpoint;
- `sequence error`: broken subsequent reference; or
- `unresolved metadata`: a required field cannot yet be verified.

Give the corrected form immediately after each finding.
