## Missing appendices

The contents list:

- appendix A, law report abbreviations, printed pages 297–326;
- appendix B, Australian medium-neutral unique court and tribunal identifiers, printed pages 327–331; and
- appendix C, pinpoint abbreviations, printed pages 332–333.

The source PDF jumps from printed page 296 to printed page 334. Do not claim that this package contains the complete appendix tables. For an abbreviation not expressly covered in these references:

1. check the source or court for its official identifier;
2. verify the abbreviation against an authorised complete AGLC4 copy;
3. write an unlisted report-series name in full and not in italics where rule 2.2.3 permits; and
4. disclose any unresolved abbreviation rather than guessing.
