# Practice profile: Regulatory & Compliance — Denmark

Orchestrator cold-start for plugin `dk-regulatory`. Loaded after `dk/CLAUDE.md`, before invoking a specific skill.

## Scope

Regulatory & Compliance skills under the law of Denmark. Practice-area definition (`practices.json`, jurisdiction-agnostic): broad sector regulatory compliance without a more specific practice: agency rules and guidance, licenses and registrations, compliance programs and audits, regulatory monitoring, AI regulation (EU AI Act) and sector-neutral compliance.

## Legal system

See `dk/CLAUDE.md` for the legal family, courts, core sources, and language.

## Citation discipline

Follow `dk/CLAUDE.md`. **Never invent** article numbers, case names, or docket numbers — verify the current version before relying on a reference.

## When this plugin does NOT apply

- Another area of law → the plugin for that practice area under `dk/`.
- Another country's law → that jurisdiction's plugin.
