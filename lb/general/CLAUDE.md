# Practice profile: General — Lebanon

Orchestrator cold-start for plugin `lb-general`. Loaded after `lb/CLAUDE.md`, before invoking a specific skill.

## Scope

General skills under the law of Lebanon. Practice-area definition (`practices.json`, jurisdiction-agnostic): outside any specific practice area: cross-cutting legal-operations tools — legal research, citation (Bluebook), intake/triage, document assembly, summarization, translation, billing, knowledge management, citation checking.

## Legal system

See `lb/CLAUDE.md` for the legal family, courts, core sources, and language.

## Citation discipline

Follow `lb/CLAUDE.md`. **Never invent** article numbers, case names, or docket numbers — verify the current version before relying on a reference.

## When this plugin does NOT apply

- Another area of law → the plugin for that practice area under `lb/`.
- Another country's law → that jurisdiction's plugin.
