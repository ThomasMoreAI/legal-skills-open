# Cross-cutting context: the legal system of Lebanon

Orchestrator cold-start for any plugin under `lb/`. Loaded before the plugin-specific `CLAUDE.md`.

## Legal family

Civil law tradition with strong French influence; personal-status matters are governed by the laws and courts of each recognised religious community.

## Courts

Court of Cassation; Council of State (Majlis Shura al-Dawla) for administrative matters; Constitutional Council.

## Core sources

Code of Obligations and Contracts, Code of Commerce, Code of Civil Procedure, Penal Code. Official publication: Official Gazette (Al-Jarida Al-Rasmiya).

## Language

Arabic (official); French widely used in legal practice.

## Citation discipline (mandatory for every plugin under `lb/`)

- Cite statutes by their official title, number, and date, and the article; cite cases by
  court, date, and docket or reference number.
- **Never invent** article numbers, case names, or references. If unknown, say so and ask
  the user to verify against the official publication.

## Working with current law

Statutes and regulations are amended frequently. A skill that depends on a specific rule
must state the assumed version or date and warn the user to confirm it is current.

## Mandatory disclaimer in output

> This output is informational only and is not legal advice. Verify against the current
> statute/regulation and the rules of the specific court or agency before relying on it.
