# Practice profile: Employment & Labor — Lebanon

Orchestrator cold-start for plugin `lb-employment`. Loaded after `lb/CLAUDE.md`, before invoking a specific skill.

## Scope

Employment & Labor skills under the law of Lebanon. Practice-area definition (`practices.json`, jurisdiction-agnostic): employment and labor law: employment contracts, termination/severance, worker classification, discrimination and harassment (Title VII/EEOC), wage/hour (FLSA), collective/union relations and labor arbitration, HR policies and internal HR investigations.

## Legal system

See `lb/CLAUDE.md` for the legal family, courts, core sources, and language.

## Citation discipline

Follow `lb/CLAUDE.md`. **Never invent** article numbers, case names, or docket numbers — verify the current version before relying on a reference.

## When this plugin does NOT apply

- Another area of law → the plugin for that practice area under `lb/`.
- Another country's law → that jurisdiction's plugin.
