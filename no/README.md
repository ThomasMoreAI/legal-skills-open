# Norway (`no`)

Nordic (Scandinavian) legal tradition; statute-based with strong weight on preparatory works (forarbeider) and Supreme Court practice. Not an EU member, but EEA law applies through the EEA Agreement. Cross-cutting context for every plugin: [`CLAUDE.md`](CLAUDE.md).
