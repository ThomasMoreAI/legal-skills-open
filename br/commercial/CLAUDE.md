# Practice profile: Commercial — Brazil

Orchestrator cold-start for plugin `br-commercial`. Loaded after `br/CLAUDE.md`, before invoking a specific skill.

## Scope

Commercial skills under the law of Brazil. Practice-area definition (`practices.json`, jurisdiction-agnostic): commercial/business contracts between businesses and trade models: franchising, distribution, supply, agency, services, ad funds, reselling, sales channels and B2B relationships.

## Legal system

See `br/CLAUDE.md` for the legal family, courts, core sources, and language.

## Citation discipline

Follow `br/CLAUDE.md`. **Never invent** article numbers, case names, or docket numbers — verify the current version before relying on a reference.

## When this plugin does NOT apply

- Another area of law → the plugin for that practice area under `br/`.
- Another country's law → that jurisdiction's plugin.
