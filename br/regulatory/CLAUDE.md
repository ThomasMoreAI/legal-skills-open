# Practice profile: Regulatory & Compliance — Brazil

Orchestrator cold-start for plugin `br-regulatory`. Loaded after `br/CLAUDE.md`, before invoking a specific skill.

## Scope

Brazilian regulatory compliance checks — identifying applicable regulations, required approvals, and risk areas for a proposed action, product feature, or business initiative.

## Forums & authorities

Sector regulators and the relevant federal/state agencies; ANPD for data matters.

## Key sources of law

Sector-specific Brazilian statutes and agency regulations; LGPD (Lei 13.709/2018) where personal data is involved. For legal family, court hierarchy, and working language, see `br/CLAUDE.md`.

## Citation discipline

Follow the rules in `br/CLAUDE.md`. **Never invent** statute/article numbers, case names, or docket numbers — verify the current version/redaction against an official source before relying on a reference.

## When this plugin does NOT apply

Personal-data compliance specifically → `data-protection`; litigation → `litigation`.

## Mandatory disclaimer in output

> This output is informational only and is not legal advice. Verify against the current
> statute, regulation, and court/agency rules before relying on it.
