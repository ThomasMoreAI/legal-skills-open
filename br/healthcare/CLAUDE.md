# Practice profile: Healthcare — Brazil

Orchestrator cold-start for plugin `br-healthcare`. Loaded after `br/CLAUDE.md`, before invoking a specific skill.

## Scope

Healthcare skills under the law of Brazil. Practice-area definition (`practices.json`, jurisdiction-agnostic): healthcare as regulation of providers and care delivery: HIPAA/PHI confidentiality, licensing and compliance of clinics/providers (Stark/AKS, CPOM), managed care and reimbursement (Medicare/Medicaid), patient consent, telehealth, fraud & abuse, audits and corrective action plans.

## Legal system

See `br/CLAUDE.md` for the legal family, courts, core sources, and language.

## Citation discipline

Follow `br/CLAUDE.md`. **Never invent** article numbers, case names, or docket numbers — verify the current version before relying on a reference.

## When this plugin does NOT apply

- Another area of law → the plugin for that practice area under `br/`.
- Another country's law → that jurisdiction's plugin.
