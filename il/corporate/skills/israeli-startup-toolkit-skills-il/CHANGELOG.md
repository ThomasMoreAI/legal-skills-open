# Changelog

## 1.3.2 - 2026-08-19

- Restored the deal-term parameters in the investment reference (SAFE conversion
  mechanics, note interest and maturity ranges, repayment at maturity, security)
  and presented them as commercial-terms tables. A previous pass had summarised
  them away; they are market information a founder negotiating a round needs.
- Renamed the reference to "Israeli Investment Deal Terms Reference" and its
  subheadings to "Key Commercial Terms", so the labelling matches what the file
  is: deal terms, not contract language.
- Updated the Bundled Resources line in both languages, which still described the
  file as providing agreement templates.

## 1.3.1 - 2026-08-19

- Reworked the investment-terms reference so it explains the terms a SAFE and a
  convertible note turn on, instead of carrying fill-in-the-blank agreement
  skeletons. Preparing the instrument is work for a licensed Israeli lawyer, and
  an executable-looking skeleton is the thing most likely to be signed without
  one. The commercial and Israeli-specific content is unchanged; what changed is
  that it is now a negotiation checklist rather than a document.
- Aligned the legal notice and the skill description with that: the skill
  explains and checklists, it does not draft legal documents.

## 1.3.0 - 2026-08-19

- Corrected the Section 102 capital-gains holding period: it runs 24 months from
  the date the shares are allotted and deposited with the trustee, not from the
  end of the tax year of grant.
- Recorded that a controlling shareholder is excluded from Section 102 entirely,
  and that the 30% rate for a material shareholder sits in Section 91(b)(2).
- Corrected the Section 102 filing timeline: notice at least 30 days before the
  grant, with the plan and trustee deemed approved if the assessing officer does
  not reply within 90 days.
- Added the Section 121B(a1) surtax limb: a further 2% on capital-source income,
  so a large capital-track gain reaches 30% rather than 28%.
- Added the Section 102(b)(3) listed-share bifurcation and the Section 102(z)
  company-wide track lock-in.
- Rewrote the angels benefit: it is a tax credit under the Encouragement of
  Knowledge-Intensive Industry Law (Temporary Order) 2023 capped at NIS 4
  million, with a separate NIS 5.5 million rollover deduction, and the order
  runs to 31 December 2026.
- Removed a Preferred Technological Enterprise qualifying test that does not
  exist in the statute, and replaced the Preferred Enterprise export condition
  with the Section 18A(c) competitive-enterprise test.
- Corrected the closely-held-company rule to the Section 81B 2% surcharge on
  undistributed excess profits, applying from tax year 2025.
- Corrected the Section 20A R&D deduction, which requires ministerial approval
  and otherwise spreads capital expenditure over three years.
- Published the company registration fees instead of routing to the form.
- Added securities-law coverage of the offeree count and the Section 15B(2)(a)
  employee compensation-plan exemption.
- Added a legal notice and description prefixes.
- Corrected the Innovation Authority incubator ceiling and the Tnufa and BIRD
  figures in the programs reference.
