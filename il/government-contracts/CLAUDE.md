# Practice profile: Government Contracts & Procurement — Israel

Orchestrator cold-start for plugin `il-government-contracts`. Loaded after `il/CLAUDE.md`, before invoking a specific skill.

## Scope

Israeli public-sector tenders (michrazim) — building a complete proposal package: parsing the tender, extracting threshold conditions, producing a compliance checklist, and drafting proposal components.

## Forums & authorities

Government and public-sector tender committees; the courts on tender challenges.

## Key sources of law

**Mandatory Tenders Law (חוק חובת המכרזים, 1992)** and its regulations; tender-specific terms. For legal family, court hierarchy, and working language, see `il/CLAUDE.md`.

## Citation discipline

Follow the rules in `il/CLAUDE.md`. **Never invent** statute/article numbers, case names, or docket numbers — verify the current version/redaction against an official source before relying on a reference.

## When this plugin does NOT apply

General regulatory compliance → `regulatory`; commercial contracts → `commercial`/`contracts`.

> This output is informational only and is not legal advice. Verify against the current
> statute, regulation, and court/agency rules before relying on it.
