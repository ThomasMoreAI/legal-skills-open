# Israeli car-accident claim: full guide

The one rule to keep straight: bodily injury is no-fault, property damage is fault-based. Everything below follows from that split.

## The three insurance types

| Policy | Hebrew | Mandatory | Covers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Compulsory | ביטוח חובה | Yes | Bodily injury only (driver, passengers, people outside the vehicle). Never property. |
| Comprehensive | ביטוח מקיף | No | Your own vehicle's damage (collision, theft, fire, flood, vandalism) plus liability for third-party property. |
| Third-party property | ביטוח צד ג' | No | Damage you cause to someone else's property. Not your own vehicle. |

## Bodily injury: no-fault (PLATD)

Under the Road Accident Victims Compensation Law, 1975 (חוק הפלת"ד), the vehicle user must compensate a person injured in a road accident involving the vehicle, regardless of fault. §2(ג) puts it beyond argument: `האחריות היא מוחלטת ומלאה, ואין נפקא מינה אם היה או לא היה אשם מצד הנוהג ואם היה או לא היה אשם או אשם תורם של אחרים`, so there is no contributory-negligence reduction on the injury side. In practice each injured person (driver, passengers, pedestrians) claims from the compulsory insurance (ביטוח חובה) of the vehicle they were in or were struck by. Fault is irrelevant to the injury claim.

A road accident for this law is an event in which a person suffers bodily injury due to use of a motor vehicle for transport purposes.

A serious or lasting-injury claim is assessed by a single medical expert appointed by the court under PLATD §6א, not by a ועדה רפואית (that is the Bituach Leumi mechanism), and it usually needs a lawyer. The law does not require a lawyer to start a claim, but for anything beyond minor injury you should consult one. This guide helps document and understand rights; it does not replace a lawyer.

### Exclusivity of the PLATD cause of action (§8)

`מי שתאונת דרכים מקנה לו עילת תביעה על פי חוק זה... לא תהיה לו עילת תביעה על פי פקודת הנזיקין בשל נזק גוף, זולת אם נפגע בתאונה שנגרמה על ידי אדם אחר במתכוון`. A road-accident victim therefore cannot sue the other driver in tort for bodily injury. §8(ב) extends the bar to a person who would have had a claim but for §22(ב), and §8(ג) preserves the tort claim of someone who never had a PLATD cause of action at all. Whether a §7-excluded person is such a someone, and so keeps a tort route, is a question the statute text does not settle and the case law must answer. Do not assert it either way; route to a lawyer.

### Electric bikes and scooters are outside PLATD

Kol Zchut, on the main bodily-injury claim page, states that under court rulings `אופניים וקורקינטים חשמליים לא נחשבים כרכב מנועי לפי חוק פיצויים לנפגעי תאונות דרכים. על כן, המידע בערך זה אינו רלוונטי למקרים כאלו`. The statutory hook is the §1 definition of רכב מנועי, which requires that `עיקר יעודו לשמש לתחבורה יבשתית`. The fallbacks for an injured rider are an ordinary negligence claim or דמי תאונה לנפגעי תאונות אישיות from Bituach Leumi. Compulsory-insurance bills for these vehicles were tabled and died in earlier Knessets; nothing found for 2024 to 2026 changes this.

## Property damage: fault-based

Property damage falls **outside PLATD entirely**. It is governed by the Torts Ordinance, which is why the §8 bar does not reach it and why a not-at-fault driver really can sue the other driver for the car.

Property liability follows fault: whoever caused the damage, or their insurer, pays.

- If you are at fault: the other party claims against your third-party (צד ג') cover. Your own car's damage is covered only if you hold comprehensive (מקיף).
- If you are not at fault: claim from your own comprehensive and pay the deductible (השתתפות עצמית); your insurer recovers from the at-fault party by subrogation (שיבוב) under the Insurance Contract Law, and refunds your deductible once it is paid in full. Or claim directly against the at-fault driver's third-party cover, or sue them (small claims for smaller sums).

## At the scene

- Safety first: hazard lights, warning triangle, move to safety only if drivable and nobody is hurt.
- Check for injuries; call emergency services if anyone is hurt.
- Photograph all vehicles, the damage, plates, the road position, signage, and skid marks.
- Exchange details: name, ID, phone, plate, and the other driver's insurer and policy number.
- Collect witnesses' names and phones.

The duty to exchange details on the spot applies to every accident (תקנה 145). Two limbs of the same regulation are routinely missed: where the vehicle hit is parked or its owner is absent, the driver must leave a written note with his details **and** report to the police within 24 hours (תקנה 145(ג)); and in a property-only accident that obstructs traffic, the driver must clear the road (תקנה 145(ב)). A driver merely passing an accident scene must stop and render aid (תקנה 146). An injury accident adds the duties to stop, render aid, and report to the police (תקנה 144). Failing to stop or to summon aid where a person was injured is a serious criminal offense under the Traffic Ordinance (Section 64א): up to 7 years imprisonment, rising to up to 14 years where the injury is serious or fatal.

## Who claims from which insurer (the allocation rule)

For bodily injury the carrier rule (PLATD Section 3) decides which compulsory insurer pays:

- A driver and the passengers in a vehicle claim against that vehicle's own compulsory insurance.
- In a multi-vehicle crash, each driver's own bodily injury goes to his own vehicle's insurer, regardless of who was at fault.
- A pedestrian or cyclist injured outside the vehicles claims against the vehicle that hit them (and where several vehicles were involved, against the involved drivers jointly).

So if two cars collide, each driver's injuries are covered by their own car's חובה. Do not chase the other driver's compulsory insurer for your own injuries.

## Who is NOT entitled (PLATD Section 7)

The no-fault rule has hard exceptions. A person is NOT entitled to bodily-injury compensation under PLATD if they: caused the accident deliberately; drove a vehicle without the owner's permission or a stolen vehicle (or rode in it knowing this); drove with no valid driving licence (a licence that merely lapsed for non-payment of the fee still counts as valid); used the vehicle to commit a felony; or drove with no valid compulsory insurance. §7(3)'s licence exception is wider than usually rendered: the full text excepts `רשיון שפקע מחמת אי תשלום אגרה או מחמת הגבלה שהוטלה לפי פרק ו'1 לחוק ההוצאה לפועל`, so a licence restricted through the Execution Law also still counts. §7(6) adds the owner or keeper who permitted another to drive uninsured and was himself injured in that drive.

Two safety valves exist off §7:

- §7א: a person who drove **by permission** of the owner or keeper without insurance, and who neither knew nor could reasonably have known, may claim from Karnit as a victim entitled under §12(ב).
- §7ב: dependants may claim `גם אם הנפגע עצמו לא היה זכאי לכך לפי סעיף 7`, and where the victim drove uninsured they claim from Karnit.

If any of §7 applies and no valve helps, the confident "you are covered regardless of fault" does not hold, whether any tort route survives under §8(ג) is a case-law question, and the user needs a lawyer to assess their position.

## Preserve the medical paper trail

For an injury, the medical documentation is what wins or loses the claim, more than the car photos. Anyone hurt should be examined the same day (ER or doctor), describe every affected body part to the doctor, and keep every medical record. An injury mentioned for the first time weeks later is easy for the insurer or Karnit to dispute.

## If it happened on the way to or from work

An accident on the way to or from work, or during work, may also be a work accident (תאונת עבודה). Report it to the National Insurance Institute (ביטוח לאומי) as well, because it can trigger separate rights and affects the calculation of the PLATD claim.

## The police confirmation

A police confirmation (אישור משטרתי) is a precondition for filing the insurance claim, in the police's own words on the gov.il service page: `אישור המשטרה מהווה תנאי הכרחי להגשת תביעה לפיצויים`. That page also warns that `אם הדיווח יישלח ללא מסמכים רפואיים, האירוע יסווג כתאונת נזק בלבד ולא כתאונת דרכים`, and that this classification can matter against insurers and in civil claims, so an injured user must attach the medical documents. Required attachments are an ID copy, medical documents where anyone was hurt, a damage estimate in a casualty-free accident, scene photos and videos, and, when the filer drove, a valid compulsory-insurance certificate for the day. A new driver may file online only if there were no casualties, and the confirmation usually arrives within about 10 working days. When there are no injuries (or injured people were treated and released within 24 hours), file the gov.il online light-accident report and receive the confirmation by email. In more serious cases report by phone or in person.

## Hit-and-run, untraced, or uninsured: Karnit

Karnit (קרנית), the Fund for Compensating Road Accident Victims, is established by §10 and §11, and its function is defined by §12: to compensate a victim entitled under the law `ואין בידו לתבוע פיצויים מאת מבטח` because the responsible driver is unknown, the driver has no insurance or none covering the liability, an authorised manager has been appointed to the insurer, or the insurer is in liquidation.

**The gating condition is "has no insurer of their own", and it is where most people go wrong.** Because §3(א) puts a driver and his passengers on their own vehicle's compulsory policy, an occupant of an insured vehicle struck by an untraced driver DOES have an insurer and therefore cannot go to Karnit. Karnit is the address for a pedestrian or a cyclist hit by an untraced vehicle, or for an occupant of an uninsured vehicle. Karnit also pays the hospital the treatment costs (§12(ב)). Karnit is for the injury side, not for repairing your car.

### Subrogation: two different regimes

Do not merge them.

- **PLATD §9 is a BAR with three exceptions.** `מי ששילם פיצויים המגיעים לפי חוק זה לא תהא לו זכות חזרה על אדם אחר החייב בפיצויים לפי חוק זה זולת` a right to go back against: a person not entitled under §7; a person with no insurance or none covering the liability, **except** someone whose annual policy expired within the 30 days before the accident; or the owner or keeper referred to in §7א. Liability on recourse is under the Torts Ordinance (§9(ב)), and §9(ג) preserves the inter-driver split under §3(ב).
- **Insurance Contract Law §62 is the ordinary property subrogation** that lets your מקיף insurer recover what it paid you from the at-fault party and refund your deductible. That is the mechanism at work in a property claim, and it is not the PLATD §9 rule.

### Motorcycles

§3(ג): where a road accident involves one or more motorcycles and one or more non-motorcycles, `ישלמו המבטחים של הרכב האחר למבטחים של האופנוע, 75% מהפיצויים` on bodily injury that the motorcycle's insurers must pay. This is an allocation between insurers, not something the victim claims; it does not change where the victim files.

### Accidents abroad

§2(א1) extends the law to an accident involving an Israeli vehicle where the victim is Israeli or a tourist, when it occurred `באזור או בשטחי האחריות האזרחית הפלסטינית או באזורים`. There is **no** general accident-abroad rule in PLATD. Anything beyond those territories is out of scope for this skill; route to a lawyer and to the traveller's own policy.

## The accident report (הודעה על תאונה)

The standard insurer report is one-sided and is filed with your own insurer. Every Israeli insurer's own form is built this way, so treat the one-sided structure as the practice to follow; whether Israeli law positively forbids a joint two-driver constat-amiable form is UNCERTAIN and no source is cited for it here. It captures: the insured and policy details, the driver, the accident details with a scene diagram, the impact zones, and witnesses, the third-party details, and the insured's signed declaration. Notify the insurer immediately after the accident, then submit the form with the photos, the police confirmation, and a damage assessment.

## Time limits

There are **two** periods and conflating them loses claims. Three years for a claim for insurance benefits against your own insurer (Insurance Contract Law §31), seven years for the tort claim against the at-fault driver and for the PLATD bodily-injury claim (Limitation Law §5), with the minors rule suspending the clock below 18 (Limitation Law §10). See `compensation-and-deadlines.md` section 6 for the §70 liability-insurance extension and the §31א warning duty. Report to the insurer immediately after the accident in any case (Insurance Contract Law §22).

## Disputes and escalation

If the insurer underpays or rejects unfairly, complain to the Public Inquiries Unit at the Capital Market, Insurance and Savings Authority. Filing the complaint does not bar going to court; for smaller property sums, file in small claims (route to israeli-small-claims-court).

## Advanced

- Subrogation (שיבוב / תחלוף): the mechanism by which a not-at-fault driver's insurer recovers from the at-fault party after paying the claim.
- Total loss: when the repair cost is high relative to the vehicle's value, the insurer declares a total loss (אובדן גמור) and pays the vehicle's worth instead of repairing. A car can also be declared a constructive total loss (אובדן להלכה), which gets a permanent note in the vehicle registry that lowers its resale value, so check how the insurer classified the car and what it does to future value.
- Diminished value (ירידת ערך): the drop in the car's value after a repaired accident is a recognized head of damage against the at-fault party, but it needs an assessor (שמאי) appraisal to prove. Rental and loss-of-use are also recoverable from the at-fault party, with proof.
