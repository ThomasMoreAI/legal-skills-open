# Practice profile: Real Estate — Israel

Orchestrator cold-start for plugin `il-real-estate`. Loaded after `il/CLAUDE.md`, before invoking a specific skill.

## Scope

Real Estate skills under the law of Israel. Practice-area definition (`practices.json`, jurisdiction-agnostic): real estate law: purchase and sale (PSA/LOI), leases (residential/commercial/NNN/ground lease), ownership and title, mortgage/deed of trust, easements, foreclosure/eviction, development and land use, conveyancing of oil & gas/mineral rights (oil & gas lease, royalty deed).

## Legal system

See `il/CLAUDE.md` for the legal family, courts, core sources, and language.

## Citation discipline

Follow `il/CLAUDE.md`. **Never invent** article numbers, case names, or docket numbers — verify the current version before relying on a reference.

## When this plugin does NOT apply

- Another area of law → the plugin for that practice area under `il/`.
- Another country's law → that jurisdiction's plugin.
