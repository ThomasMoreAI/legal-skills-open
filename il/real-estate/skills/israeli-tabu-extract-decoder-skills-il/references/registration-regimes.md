# Which register is this property in, and what document proves it

Start here whenever a user cannot find their property, or is holding a document that is not a land
registry extract. This is the highest-value section of the skill, because a very large share of
Israeli homes are not registered in the land register in the owner's name, and most guides never say
so.

## The core legal point

`סעיף 7` of חוק המקרקעין: `עסקה במקרקעין טעונה רישום; העסקה נגמרת ברישום`, and an unregistered
transaction is treated as an undertaking to transact.

So a purchase contract, a payment schedule, and a set of receipts are **not** a registered right.
They may be excellent evidence of an undertaking, and a caveat may protect that undertaking very
effectively, but they are a different thing from registered ownership. A user who does not understand
that distinction cannot understand anything else on this page.

## Telling the regimes apart

Ask what the user is actually holding and who issued it.

| Regime | What the user holds | What to say |
|---|---|---|
| Registered in the land register, freehold | A land registry extract naming them as owner | The ordinary case. Read it with `entry-types.md`, and establish settled versus unsettled first |
| Registered in the land register as a long lease from a public land body | An extract showing the State or a public land body as owner and the resident as `חוכר לדורות` | Still a registered right and still the right document. Do NOT tell this user their property "is not in the tabu" |
| Managed by the national land authority, not registered in the land register | A rights confirmation from that authority, not an extract | There is no extract to obtain. The authority's confirmation is the document. Route them to it |
| Also managed by a housing company (`חברה משכנת`) or occupancy body | Whatever that company issues | This is not a public register. Any binding confirmation has to come from the body managing the property. Route accordingly |

Where the user does not know which applies, the practical route is to ask who they pay, who they
approached at purchase, and what the last document they received actually says on its face.

## Why an apartment may not be registered yet

The common causes are structural rather than personal: the parcellation of the area may not be
complete, or the developer may not have completed the registration of the building as a condominium,
or may not have completed registering the individual buyer's rights in the unit.

Two things follow, and the skill should say both:

1. **This is common, not an anomaly, and not evidence that anything is wrong with the purchase.**
   Treating it as an emergency misleads the user.
2. **It is also not nothing.** Until the right is registered, what the user holds is an undertaking
   rather than a registered right, and the protection they have depends on what was registered in
   their favour, typically a caveat. That is precisely the question to take to an advocate.

The skill's job here is to name the regime, name the document, and route. It does not chase a
developer, assess whether a housing company is trustworthy, or advise on remedies.

## Jurisdictional note

The register covering Judea and Samaria is a separate system from the register for Israel proper. Its
extract categories and the rules about who may request one are not the same. If the property is
there, say that a different register and different access rules apply and route the user to the
correct service, rather than applying the Israel-proper procedure to it.

This skill deliberately does not restate that system's specific rules: the pages describing them
could not be read first-hand when the skill was written, and stating unverified procedure is worse
than routing.

## What to produce for a user in a non-register regime

1. The regime, named.
2. The document that stands in place of an extract, and who issues it.
3. Whether anything was registered in the user's favour in the meantime, and what that entry does.
4. The questions to take to an advocate, phrased as questions.

Do not produce reassurance and do not produce alarm. Produce the map.
