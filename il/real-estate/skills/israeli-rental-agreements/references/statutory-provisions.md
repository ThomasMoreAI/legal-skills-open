# Statutory provisions: residential tenancy (sections 25א-25טו) and guarantors

Section-by-section reference for the residential-tenancy chapter of חוק השכירות והשאילה, תשל"א-1971
(inserted by the 2017 amendment, chapter 1, siman vav), plus the guarantor rules in
חוק הערבות, התשכ"ז-1967. Quote section numbers from here rather than paraphrasing from memory.

Consolidated text: https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_השכירות_והשאילה

## 25ב - Form of the contract

The lease shall be made in writing and signed by both parties, and each receives a signed copy.
The section then says expressly: `אין באי־קיום הוראה זו כדי לגרוע מתוקפו של החוזה`. An oral
residential lease is therefore a VALID contract. It is harder to prove, and it triggers the 25ד
gap-filling route, but a tenant with an oral lease has a contract.

## 25ג + Second Schedule - Contents of the contract

25ג requires the lease to contain the particulars in the Second Schedule. The Minister may amend
the Schedule by order. The eight particulars:

1. The address of the apartment.
2. The parties' names, ID numbers and addresses.
3. A description of the apartment, its furniture and fittings, including any shared-property parts let with it.
4. The lease term, whether there is an option to extend and on what terms, and whether there is a right to cancel and on what terms.
5. The rent, when it is payable and how.
6. Any additional payments the tenant bears that are not rent, and their amount.
7. **Any defect or other non-minor shortcoming in the apartment known to the landlord at the time the contract is made, and anything else in the apartment, its surroundings, or the property serving it that causes or is likely to cause material disruption to the use of the apartment and is known to the landlord at that time.**
8. Further particulars prescribed by the Minister.

Item 7 is the landlord's pre-contract disclosure duty. It is a CONTENTS requirement under 25ג.
It is not in 25ב, which governs form only.

## 25ד - Gap-filling by the model lease

The Minister may prescribe a model residential lease (חוזה מצוי). Where one is prescribed:
if the lease was not made per 25ב and the parties did not agree otherwise, the model lease is
treated as the contract between them; if a provision required by 25ג is missing and the parties
did not agree otherwise, the model lease's provision on that matter is read in.

Before relying on this, confirm whether such regulations have in fact been prescribed and are
in force. If they have not, the route is unavailable and the answer changes.

## 25ה / 25ו - Suitable for living, and fit for habitation

Two distinct duties. 25ה is the general obligation to deliver an apartment suitable for living,
with אי-התאמה remedies. 25ו(א) requires the landlord to DELIVER (`ימסור`) the apartment fit for
habitation, judged solely against the First Schedule. 25ו(ב) makes delivery of an unfit apartment
a breach of contract. Under 25יד(1), 25ו is the only provision in the chapter that cannot be
varied at all, in either direction.

### First Schedule - when an apartment is NOT fit for habitation

A closed list. The apartment is unfit if ANY ONE of these is true:

1. It has no drainage system, including a system for the orderly disposal of sewage and waste water.
2. It has no electricity system or lighting system.
3. It has no ventilation and natural-light openings, and doors and windows to close those openings, INCLUDING a main entrance door with a locking means.
4. It has no drinking-water supply system.
5. **It has no partition between the toilet and the apartment.**
6. It poses an unreasonable risk to the tenant's safety or health.

Items 3, 5 and 6 are the ones most often engaged in sub-standard Israeli rentals and the ones most
often missing from summaries. Do not substitute a plausible-sounding list of amenities: a water
heater, for instance, is not in the Schedule, and the toilet partition is.

## 25ז - Delivery

The apartment is delivered on the agreed date, free of persons and objects. Under 25ז(ג) the
landlord provides maintenance and use instructions for the apartment, its installations and its
contents, no later than handover. Those instructions matter later in any argument about whether
damage was caused by unreasonable use.

## 25ח - Defects and repairs

- 25ח(א)(1): the tenant is responsible for a defect caused by UNREASONABLE use of the apartment.
- 25ח(א)(2): the tenant repairs such a defect at their own expense unless the parties agreed otherwise.
- 25ח(ב): the landlord repairs, at their own expense, every other non-minor defect, within a
  reasonable time and no later than 30 days from the tenant's demand; 3 days where the defect is
  urgent, meaning `פגם שאינו מאפשר מגורים בדירה באופן סביר`.
- 25ח(ג): if the landlord does not repair within that period, section 9(א) and (ב) apply mutatis mutandis.

### Section 9 - the remedies

- 9(א)(1): the tenant may repair the defect and demand REIMBURSEMENT OF REASONABLE EXPENSES.
- 9(א)(2): the tenant may REDUCE THE RENT, for as long as the defect is unrepaired, by the
  proportion in which the value of the tenancy fell because of it.
- 9(ב): a tenant intending to self-repair must give the landlord reasonable advance notice.
- 9(ג): no notice is required where the defect is urgent.

These are two different remedies and neither is "deduct the invoice from the rent". Remedy (2) is
measured by lost value, not by repair cost. A tenant who simply pays less rent is in arrears,
which gives the landlord a 25י(ג)(1) ground to realise the guarantee and a basis to sue for
possession.

## 25ט - Payments

25ט(א), closed list of what the tenant bears: the rent; taxes imposed on the occupier, including
arnona; payment for consumption of water, electricity, gas and heating; ONGOING maintenance
charges of the house committee; and classes of payment prescribed by the Minister.

25ט(ב): the tenant shall not directly bear any payment not listed in (א), and expressly not:

1. payment toward the PURCHASE OR UPGRADE of fixed systems or installations serving the apartment, except special adaptations or improvements the tenant requested;
2. premiums for insuring the apartment under BUILDING INSURANCE (ביטוח מבנה);
3. payments the landlord owes a third party, including a broker's fee where the broker acted for the landlord.

25ט(ג) preserves the tenant's liability for repair costs under 25ח(א).

A capital-works levy from the house committee is not "ongoing maintenance" and is therefore not
the tenant's. Contents insurance for the tenant's own belongings is not building insurance.

## 25י - The guarantee (ערובה)

**25י(א)** defines `ערובה` as a security for the tenant's obligations under the lease. The
definition carries NO monetary-outlay qualifier. That qualifier appears only in 25י(ב). So
subsections (ג), (ד) and (ה) govern EVERY guarantee, including a promissory note, a security
cheque and a guarantors' deed.

**25י(ב)** caps the CUMULATIVE amount of guarantees `הכרוכה בהוצאה כספית לשוכר`, expressly a bank
guarantee or cash, at the lower of: the rent for one third of the lease term, or three months'
rent. From 30.9.2026, per ס"ח 3510 of 31.3.2026, a guarantee from a `נותן ערבות אחר` (a licensed
credit provider, a deposit-and-credit services licensee, a stablecoin payment-services licensee,
or an insurer) is added alongside the bank guarantee and counts inside the same ceiling.

**A lease with an option.** 25י(ב)(1) measures the ceiling against the rent payable
`בעד שליש מתקופת השכירות`. Where the lease carries an option to extend, it is not settled whether
"the lease period" means the initial term or the term as extended, and the two give different
ceilings. 25יב uses a distinct expression for the extension (`תקופת ההארכה`), which points toward
the period actually contracted for, but that is an inference from the drafting rather than a
holding. State the uncertainty and, for a specific lease, take advice.

**25י(ג)** is a CLOSED list of grounds on which the landlord may realise a guarantee, each capped:

1. the tenant did not pay the rent on time, up to the unpaid rent plus contractual linkage and interest;
2. the tenant did not repair defects they were liable to repair under 25ח(א), up to the cost of repair;
3. the tenant did not pay charges owed under 25ט(א)(2) to (5), up to the unpaid amount;
4. the tenant did not vacate at the end of the term, in an amount per the contract.

"Damage beyond normal wear and tear" is NOT a ground. The ground is unreasonable use under 25ח(א).

**25י(ד)**: the landlord must notify the tenant a reasonable time in advance of an intention to
realise the guarantee, and allow a reasonable period to put right what needs putting right.

**25י(ה)**: at the end of the tenancy the landlord returns the guarantee or its balance,
`לרבות פירותיה` (including its fruits), within 60 days of the apartment being returned, or when
the tenant's debts under (ג) are settled, whichever is later.

## 25יא - Sale of the apartment during the tenancy

If the landlord transfers their rights under section 21(ב), they must notify the tenant IN WRITING
before the date they must hand the apartment to the buyer, stating the buyer's details and how to
make contact.

## 25יב - Extending the term

- 25יב(א): the landlord tells the tenant, a reasonable time before the end of the term, whether they wish to extend and on what terms.
- 25יב(ב): a LANDLORD holding an option must give notice no later than 90 days before the end of the term, and may exercise it only if the extension terms were fixed in the contract in advance, subject to those terms.
- 25יב(ג): a TENANT holding an option must give notice no later than 60 days before the end of the term.

A 90-day notice requirement on a landlord's option is the statutory minimum, not a red flag.

## 25יג - Cancellation without cause

- 25יג(א): a clause permitting the LANDLORD to cancel other than for breach is VOID unless the tenant also has such a right.
- 25יג(ב): where such a clause stands, the landlord gives at least 90 days' notice and the tenant at least 60 days'.

The 90/60 asymmetry is statutory. It is not "the standard model".

## 25יד - Contracting out

1. Section 25ו may not be varied at all.
2. Sections 6, 8 to 10, 15, 21(ב) and (ג), 23 to 25ה, 25ח(א)(1), (ב) and (ג), and 25ט to 25יג may be varied ONLY in the tenant's favour.

25ח(א)(2) is deliberately outside the list. A clause shifting a burden ONTO the tenant in any of
the listed provisions is void; a clause shifting one OFF the tenant is effective.

## 25טו - Application

25טו(א) excludes: an apartment in a hotel or let for vacation; in a מעון; in sheltered housing; in
student, pupil or worker dormitories; one subject to the Tenant Protection Law; a lease of three
months or less with no option to extend; a lease over ten years where the landlord has no right to
cancel earlier; a lease whose monthly rent EXCEEDS 20,000 NIS or another sum set by the Minister,
that sum being updated on 1 January each year by the change in the CPI, rounded to the nearest
10 NIS and published in רשומות; and other classes the Minister prescribes.

**25טו(ב) does NOT exclude family leases.** It provides only that section 25יד(2) does not apply
to a lease between a landlord and a tenant who is their `קרוב` (spouse, sibling, parent,
grandparent, descendant, the spouse's descendants, and the spouse of any of these). The chapter
continues to apply in full; the parties are merely free to agree otherwise. Absent such an
agreement, every protection stands.

**25טו(ג)**, sub-letting by a head tenant on a lease of ten years or less: the landlord's rights
under 16א(א) and 20א remain the landlord's, and the duties under 25ח(ב) and (ג) and the
prohibitions in 25ט(ב) fall on the LANDLORD, not on the head tenant.

## חוק הערבות, התשכ"ז-1967 - guarantors

Relevant whenever a lease is backed by personal guarantors, which the 25י(ב) ceiling does not
reach. Chapter 2 of the Guarantee Law applies to a guarantee given by an `ערב יחיד`.

- **s.19** `ערב יחיד`: a guarantor who is not a corporation, excluding the debtor's spouse and the debtor's partner. `ערב מוגן`: an ערב יחיד whose guarantee contract states a sum not exceeding a statutory ceiling (a higher ceiling applies where the guaranteed obligation is for buying a home for the debtor, their adult children or their parents).
- **s.20** those ceilings are updated on 1 January and 1 July each year by the CPI. Check the current published figure rather than quoting an old one. A residential-lease guarantee is normally well below the lower ceiling, so the guarantor is usually an ערב מוגן.
- **s.21(א)** an ערב יחיד guarantees only the sum stated in the guarantee contract, plus the additions in s.25. **s.21(ב): if no fixed sum is stated, the ערב יחיד is released from the guarantee entirely.**
- **s.22-23** the creditor must disclose specified particulars to an ערב יחיד before the guarantee is made, with consequences for non-disclosure.
- **s.27** an action may not be brought against an ערב מוגן until judgment has been given against the debtor AND the Execution Registrar has certified that all reasonable execution proceedings against the debtor were taken. Narrow exceptions apply where the debtor has died, been declared incapable, had a receivership order made, or left the country permanently.

So a guarantors' deed is not the open-ended exposure it looks like: an unstated sum releases the
guarantor outright, and a protected guarantor cannot be pursued before the tenant has been.
