# Walkthrough, defect log and demand letter

## How to run the walkthrough

Go room by room in a fixed order so nothing is skipped, and finish with the common areas and anything the buyer has exclusive use of (storage, parking, balcony). Bring a phone for photographs, a tape measure, a spirit level or a level app, a marble or a ball for checking slopes, a phone charger for testing sockets, and sticky notes to mark items in place so they show up in photographs.

For every finding record six things:

1. Room or location, specific enough to find again ("kitchen, under the sink", not "kitchen")
2. What is wrong, described factually, no diagnosis of cause
3. Whether it is visible today (this selects the notice rule later, so never leave it blank)
4. A photograph, with something in frame for scale, and a second wider shot for context
5. The date
6. The schedule row it plausibly falls in, marked as provisional

Describe, do not diagnose. "Brown stain 20 cm across on the ceiling below the bathroom" is usable. "Leak from the neighbour's plumbing" is a conclusion the buyer cannot prove and it hands the contractor an argument.

## Room checklist

Adapt this, do not recite it. Skip what does not apply.

**Every room**
- Walls and ceiling: cracks (note width and whether they run through anything), bulges, damp patches, stains, uneven paint, visible joints
- Floor: tiles that sound hollow when tapped, lipping between tiles, uneven grout, gaps at skirting, slope where there should not be one
- Doors: close and latch without binding, no rubbing on the frame, handles and locks work, no gaps at the top rail
- Windows: open, close and lock, seals intact, no draught, no water sitting in the track, insect screens present and fitting
- Electrics: every socket tested, every switch, light fittings present where specified, distribution board labelled
- Skirting, trims and silicone lines continuous and clean

**Kitchen**
- Every cupboard door and drawer aligned and closing, soft-close working where specified
- Worktop joins, sink seal, no movement of the sink
- Water on and off at every tap, hot arrives, no drip at the connections, no damp in the cupboard under the sink
- Waste runs away without pooling, no smell
- Appliance spaces match the specification, connections present

**Bathrooms and toilets**
- Floor falls towards the drain, water does not pool. Pour a bucket and watch
- Drain runs away fast, no gurgle from adjacent fittings
- Silicone continuous at the bath, shower tray and basin
- Toilet flushes and refills, cistern does not run on, pan does not rock
- Ventilation extracts, and check it actually vents rather than spinning against a blocked duct
- Water pressure at the shower with another tap running

**Balcony, exterior, common areas**
- Balcony floor falls away from the apartment, not towards it
- Railing solid, at the specified height, no movement when pushed
- Exterior cladding intact, nothing loose or detaching
- Sealing around every penetration through the facade
- Yard paving level, no subsidence, drainage running to a gully
- Note anything in the common property separately, since it goes on a different track

**Systems**
- Heating and cooling: run every unit, check drainage from the air conditioner, look for damp behind units
- Shelter or safe room: door closes and seals fully, ventilation fitted, blast valve present, escape hatch operable
- Water meter reading recorded on the day, electricity meter reading recorded on the day

## Handover protocol, what to sign

Do not advise refusing to sign. Advise controlling what is signed.

- Attach the written defect list and have the protocol reference it by name and date.
- Never sign wording like "התקבלה לשביעות רצוני המלאה" or "אין לי הערות" while items are open.
- Photograph every page of the signed protocol, including the attached list, before handing it back.
- Record the water and electricity meter readings on the protocol.
- If the contractor refuses to attach the list or record reservations, handwrite on the protocol, above the signature, that the signature confirms receipt of possession only and does not constitute acceptance of the apartment's condition or a waiver of any claim. Photograph it, then send the list separately the same day by a traceable method.
- A waiver demanded as the price of handing over the keys is void by statute, so the buyer is not choosing between the keys and their rights. Say this, because the pressure in the room is designed to make them believe otherwise.

## Demand letter template

Fill every bracket. Send by a method that proves delivery and keep the proof. Address it to the entity named as seller in the contract, not to the site foreman.

```
לכבוד
[שם החברה הקבלנית], [כתובת]
[תאריך]

הנדון: דרישה לתיקון ליקויים בדירה ברחוב [כתובת מלאה], [עיר]

1. אני הח"מ, [שם מלא], ת"ז [מספר], רכשתי מכם דירה ברחוב [כתובת] לפי
   הסכם מכר מיום [תאריך ההסכם].
2. הדירה הועמדה לרשותי ביום [תאריך המסירה].
3. בדירה התגלו הליקויים המפורטים להלן:

   | מס' | מיקום | תיאור הליקוי | תאריך גילוי |
   |-----|-------|--------------|--------------|
   | 1   |       |              |              |
   | 2   |       |              |              |

4. הליקויים התגלו במועדים המפורטים לעיל, ולטענתי הם באחריותכם לפי חוק
   המכר (דירות), התשל"ג-1973.
5. בהתאם לסעיף 4ב לחוק, הריני נותן לכם בזאת הזדמנות נאותה לתקן את
   הליקויים, ומבקש שתתקנו אותם בתוך זמן סביר.
6. אבקש את תשובתכם בכתב בתוך [14] יום ממועד מכתב זה, ובה מועד מוצע
   לביצוע התיקונים.
7. מכתב זה נשלח גם כהודעה על אי-התאמה לפי סעיף 4א לחוק, ואין בו כדי
   לוותר על זכות כלשהי העומדת לי לפי כל דין.

בכבוד רב,
[שם] [חתימה]
[טלפון] [דוא"ל]

העתק נשלח בדואר רשום / בדואר אלקטרוני ביום [תאריך].
```

Notes on drafting:

- Clause 5 is the operative one and must not be dropped. The law requires giving the contractor a proper opportunity to repair, and a letter that skips it, or that announces the buyer will repair and bill, weakens the position.
- Clause 4 is the buyer's own assertion, and it must stay that way. Do not rewrite it as a finding by this skill that a defect sits in a particular statutory period. The buyer states the dates and asserts the contractor is liable; the characterisation is theirs to make and a lawyer's to settle.
- Clause 7 makes the letter serve double duty as the statutory notice of non-conformity, which is what protects the one-year deadline for anything visible at handover.
- Never tell the user their deadline is safe, or that it is missed and the matter is over. Report the position and its limits, including the third clock (limitation) and the separate s.4a(b) rule for a hidden load-bearing defect, and route the conclusion to an advocate.
- Do not put a shekel figure in the letter. Quantifying damage is a licensed valuer's or engineer's work and an invented number will be used against the buyer.
- Do not assert that a defect is structural or fundamental. Describe what is visible and let the engineer classify.
- Keep the deadline realistic. Fourteen days for a written response is normal and reasonable, and an unreasonably short deadline invites the argument that no proper opportunity was given.

## Follow-up letter, when the contractor does not act

Reuse the structure, add: the date of the original letter, the fact that no response or no repair followed, a restatement that a proper opportunity to repair was given, and notice that the buyer will consider a complaint to the Registrar of Contractors and any other remedy available under law. Keep it factual and do not threaten anything the buyer is not prepared to do.
