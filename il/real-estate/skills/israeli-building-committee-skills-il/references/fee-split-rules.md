# Fee split rules for a bayit meshutaf

Statute: Land Law 1969 (חוק המקרקעין, התשכ"ט-1969), Chapter 6. All section numbers below refer to it unless marked "standard takanon" (the schedule, תקנון מצוי) or "Rental and Loan Law".

## 1. Order of precedence

1. A **registered takanon** that sets a cost rate (s.58(a), s.62(c)).
2. Where none is registered or it is silent: the **standard takanon** (s.64).
3. The statute's default: **floor area ratio** (s.58(a)).

A takanon can be registered by a single owner before any apartment is sold, which is the ordinary case for a new building, and once registered it binds everyone who buys later (s.63, s.62(c)). So a building may lawfully split equally even though no current owner ever agreed to it. Establish whether a takanon is registered before applying any default.

Two limits apply at every level:
- A payment of a kind or rate not set in the law binds an owner only with his consent (s.62(a); standard takanon s.12(b)).
- Attaching part of the common property to one apartment needs all owners (s.62(a)), and is never allowed for stairwells, the elevator or shelters (s.55(c)).

### The contested case: amending a registered takanon to an equal split

s.62(a) lets owners holding two thirds of the common property amend a takanon, but bars imposing
payments "מסוג או בשיעור שלא פורשו בחוק זה" without the consent of the owners they fall on. Whether
a move from floor area to an equal split crosses that line is unsettled: s.58(a) itself contemplates
"שיעור השתתפות אחר" set in the takanon, which supports the reading that the two thirds power alone
suffices. Do not state either reading as settled. Get the affected owners' consent, or put it to the
Mafkach under s.72(a).

Three situations, which lead to different answers:
1. A registered takanon sets the equal split: it binds, including a developer-registered one (s.62(c), s.63). Route is amendment or a challenge before the Mafkach.
2. No registered takanon, or it is silent: s.64 applies the standard takanon and the s.58(a) floor-area default governs. An equal split is practice with no legal basis.
3. An assembly resolution imposed the equal split: standard takanon s.12(b) means it has no effect against an owner who did not consent to it.

## 2. What the fees cover

s.58(a): expenses needed for **proper maintenance and management** of the common property and for **services required by law or customary**. "Proper maintenance" = keeping the common property as it was at completion of construction, including improvements later made with the owners' consent.

Common property (s.52) includes the land, roofs, external walls, foundation, stairwells, elevators, shelters, and heating or water installations serving all or most owners, even inside one apartment.

## 3. Counting floor area

| Item | Counted? | Section |
|---|---|---|
| Apartment floor area | Yes | s.57(a), s.58(a) |
| Balconies | No, unless the takanon says so | s.57(b) |
| External walls | No, unless the takanon says so | s.57(b) |
| Attached roof, yard, storage, parking | At the rate in the takanon; if none, at a rate the Mafkach sets | s.57(c) |

Do not invent a rate for an attached area. If the takanon is silent, show the split without it and flag that the Mafkach sets the rate.

## 4. There is no non-use exemption in s.58(a)

The statutory text contains no exemption for an owner who does not use a service. The only ways a share changes are a registered takanon clause or the owner's own agreement. Never tell a ground-floor owner he is exempt from an existing elevator's service contract on the basis of the statute.

## 5. Elevators: installation vs running costs (s.59F)

| Item | Who pays | Section |
|---|---|---|
| Decision to install where none exists | Owners of two thirds of the apartments, if a permit can be obtained and no material harm | s.59F(a) |
| Installation cost | Only the owners who decided (voted for) it; they may agree only some of them pay; non-deciders do not bear it | s.59F(b)(1) |
| Ground-floor owners | Do not bear installation, even if they voted for it | s.59F(b)(2) |
| Maintenance and operation | All owners | s.59F(b)(3) |
| Split | By floor area among the owners bearing that payment | s.59F(b)(4) |
| Opt-out | An agreed takanon may exclude s.59F | s.59F(c)(1) |
| Review | Mafkach may cancel, change or condition the decision, including with compensation | s.59F(d)(2) |

## 6. Worked example (illustrative inputs, not real figures)

Six apartments. Ground floor: apartments 1 and 2 at 90 sqm each. Apartments 3 and 4 at 100 sqm. Apartments 5 and 6 at 110 sqm. Areas exclude balconies (s.57(b)). No registered takanon.

**A. Roof waterproofing, total 36,000 (maintenance, all owners, s.58(a))**

Total area = 90 + 90 + 100 + 100 + 110 + 110 = 600.

| Apt | Area | Share | Payment |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 90 | 90/600 = 0.1500 | 5,400 |
| 2 | 90 | 0.1500 | 5,400 |
| 3 | 100 | 100/600 = 0.1667 | 6,000 |
| 4 | 100 | 0.1667 | 6,000 |
| 5 | 110 | 110/600 = 0.1833 | 6,600 |
| 6 | 110 | 0.1833 | 6,600 |

**B. Installing a new elevator, total 420,000, decided under s.59F**

Step 1: list who DECIDED (voted for) the installation. s.59F(b)(1): "בעלי הדירות המחליטים על התקנת המעלית ישאו בהוצאות התקנתה, ורשאים הם להסכים כי רק מקצתם ישאו בהוצאות כאמור". Owners who did not decide do not bear installation.

Step 2: remove ground-floor owners even if they voted for it (s.59F(b)(2)).

Illustration: apartments 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 voted for; apartment 6 did not (five of six apartments, above the two-thirds threshold). Payers = 3, 4, 5. Payers' area = 100 + 100 + 110 = 310.

| Apt | Share | Payment |
|---|---|---|
| 1, 2 (ground floor) | none | 0 |
| 6 (did not decide) | none | 0 |
| 3, 4 | 100/310 = 0.3226 | 135,483.87 each |
| 5 | 110/310 = 0.3548 | 149,032.26 |

If the deciders agree among themselves that only some of them pay, use that agreement instead.

**C. That elevator's monthly service contract**: all six apartments pay by the table in A, including apartment 6 and the ground floor (s.59F(b)(3)).

Sanity check against Kol Zchut's own example: 80 sqm out of 800 sqm is one tenth (10%).

Same numbers with the helper: `python3 scripts/fee_split.py --example` and `python3 scripts/fee_split.py --example --total 420000 --kind installation --exclude 6` (ground-floor apartments are marked `ground_floor` in the input and dropped automatically). The script floors each payment to the agora and gives leftover agorot to the largest-area payers first, so payments always sum to the total.

## 7. Tenants

- Rental and Loan Law s.25I(a)(4): the tenant bears current-maintenance payments to the vaad or management company.
- s.25I(b)(1): the tenant does not directly bear payments to buy or upgrade fixed systems, except adjustments the tenant asked for.
- s.25XV excludes some leases from this chapter; the lease itself sets the details.
- Toward the building the owner is liable; a tenant votes only with the owner's proxy (Kol Zchut).

## 8. Attached roof

Once attached, a part of the common property is treated like the apartment it is attached to and the common-property provisions no longer apply to it (s.55(c)); it counts in that apartment's area at the bylaws' or Mafkach's rate (s.57(c)). Who pays to waterproof an attached roof, and whether the slab under it stays shared, is not decided by statute text quoted here: it depends on the bylaws and is often disputed. Check the tabu extract and bylaws; unresolved disputes go to the Mafkach (s.72(a)).

- Unclear whether a cost is maintenance or an improvement: lay out both readings, route to the Mafkach.
