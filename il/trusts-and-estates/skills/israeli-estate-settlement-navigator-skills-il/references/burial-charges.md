# When a burial society may lawfully charge the family

Detail behind the short burial step in SKILL.md. Sources: National Insurance Law, section 266,
and the Bituach Leumi (Burial Grant) Regulations, 5736-1976, regulations 2 to 4.

## Step 1: does section 266 cover the deceased?

Section 266 of the National Insurance Law pays the burial grant for burial in Israel of:
- anyone who died in Israel, resident or not; and
- an Israeli resident who died abroad and is brought to Israel for burial.

A non-resident who died abroad and is brought to Israel for burial is outside section 266.
(Burial abroad is section 267, a different route: see `money-tax-and-employment-after-death.md`.)

Section 269: the whole burial-grant chapter does not apply to anyone who died as a direct
result of acts of war. A fallen soldier or a victim of hostile action is handled through a
separate track, and this analysis does not apply; survivor benefits route to
`israeli-survivor-benefits-navigator`.

## Step 2a: section 266 covers the deceased

Regulation 2 pays the grant only to a society that does not demand or accept any service fee
or other payment, directly or indirectly, in connection with the burial and the erection of
the headstone, on top of the grant. The exceptions are closed lists.

Regulation 3(a): a service fee or other payment, in connection with the burial and the
erection of the headstone, in these cases only:
1. burial in one of the closed cemeteries listed in the annex to the agreement;
2. burial in defined sections agreed between Bituach Leumi and the society and marked on a map
   attached to the agreement (capped at a percentage of the free plots);
3. burial in a grave plot purchased in the deceased's lifetime.

Regulation 4(a): a further payment that is NOT a service fee, in these cases only:
1. special costs of transferring the deceased from outside the society's area;
2. a detour or stop on the way to the cemetery at the explicit request of the family or a close
   friend, for a eulogy or last respects;
3. non-standard shrouds supplied at that request.

A demand outside these lists can be refused. A demand inside them is lawful.

## Step 2b: section 266 does not cover the deceased

Regulation 3(b): the society may charge a service fee or any other payment. The price is a
commercial matter to negotiate, and the family should not be told the charge is unlawful.

## The headstone

The headstone is not part of the burial services the grant funds, so it is a family cost,
except in specific cases where the state funds it (for example a solitary deceased with no
close relatives). Funeral, burial, and headstone costs rank first among the estate's debts
under section 104(a)(1) of the Succession Law, so whoever paid can normally be reimbursed from
the estate ahead of other creditors.
