# Institution-by-institution checklist

The Israeli-specific notification and document map. Work top to bottom, but skip
rows that do not apply. Misrad HaPnim forwards the death to many bodies
automatically, so for several rows your job is to confirm and to claim, not to
be the first to tell them.

| Institution (He) | Institution (En) | What to do | Documents | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| gov.il: ליווי לאחר פטירה | gov.il post-death accompaniment (one-stop) | Start here. Authenticated step-by-step service for a first-degree relative | Identification for the personal area | Downloads the death certificate, files and retrieves the order, shows matched benefits, runs the account and policy locators |
| רשות האוכלוסין וההגירה | Population and Immigration Authority (Misrad HaPnim) | Confirm the death is registered; order the death certificate + several certified copies | הודעת פטירה from the doctor/hospital | Certificate is free and downloadable immediately from the personal area; it is the master document everyone else asks for |
| חברה קדישא / מועצה דתית / עמותת קבורה אזרחית | Chevra Kadisha / religious council / civil burial society | Arrange burial | הודעת פטירה, deceased ID | Bituach Leumi pays the burial grant directly; do not pay for standard burial |
| ביטוח לאומי | Bituach Leumi | Confirm they know of the death; actively claim survivor benefits | תעודת פטירה, claimant ID | Benefits are NOT automatic; eligibility belongs to israeli-survivor-benefits-navigator |
| בנקים | Banks | Notify; expect the account to freeze; cancel standing orders; request a statement; ask explicitly about releasing funds for immediate death expenses | תעודת פטירה, sender ID; an indemnity letter where all owners died; later the order | Bulk release needs the order, but limited reasonable sums for burial, headstone, and related costs can usually be released BEFORE it, at the bank's discretion |
| קופות גמל / פנסיה / חברות ביטוח | Pension, provident funds, insurers | Notify; ask about beneficiary payouts and claim forms; use the clearing house (mislaka) to locate accounts | תעודת פטירה, claimant ID | Some payouts follow beneficiary designations rather than the estate |
| מעסיק | Employer | Notify payroll/HR; claim final salary, unused vacation, severance | תעודת פטירה, claimant ID and proof of the relationship | Severance on death goes to the statutory שאירים under section 5 of the Severance Pay Law, 5723-1963, who are not necessarily the יורשים, and it is not paid from the estate. For a death in the section 5(b1) determining period (from 7.10.2023 to 28 October 2026 on the reported expiry of the declaration, unless extended by ministerial order; confirm), heirs step in where there is no spouse or dependent child. Also ask about workplace-managed pensions and insurance |
| מטפל סיעודי / עוזרת בית שהנפטר העסיק | A caregiver or housekeeper the deceased employed | Settle the end-of-employment entitlements; the death counts as dismissal | Employment contract, payslips, pension-fund severance deposits | Last salary, notice, recuperation, vacation, severance are estate debts, except severance already in the pension fund; foreign caregiver payroll: `foreign-caregiver-payroll` |
| קופת חולים | HMO (kupat cholim) | Deregister the member | תעודת פטירה | Stops membership charges |
| חברות תשתית (חשמל, מים, גז) | Utilities (electricity, water, gas) | Close or transfer the account holder; issue final bill | תעודת פטירה, sender ID | Transfer is often easier than closing if the home stays occupied |
| עירייה / ארנונה | Municipality / arnona | Change the registered holder; ask about a survivor-linked discount | תעודת פטירה, occupancy proof | Discount tied to survivor benefits; confirm at the municipality |
| משרד הרישוי | Licensing Bureau (Misrad HaRishui) | Transfer the vehicle | תעודת פטירה + the order; spouse may have a simplified route | Do after the order is obtained. The license is renewed for relatives for ONE further year from its expiry and no second renewal is issued; the transfer adds no "hand" to the ownership count; confirm mandatory insurance with the insurer before anyone drives |
| משטרה / האגף לרישוי כלי ירייה | Israel Police / firearms licensing division | Deactivate a licensed weapon through the licensing bureau to keep it, or surrender it to the police within 30 days of death | כלי הירייה, תעודת פטירה, רישיון כלי הירייה של הנפטר | Section 5(b)(5) of the Firearms Law, 5709-1949 exempts an heir or estate administrator from the licence requirement for 30 days from the death; holding it after that without a licence is an offence. The police issue a deposit confirmation |
| לשכת רישום המקרקעין (טאבו) | Land Registry (Tabu) | Re-register real estate to the heirs | תעודת פטירה + the order + Land Registry extract | Do after the order is obtained |
| סלולר / אינטרנט / מנויים דיגיטליים | Cellular, internet, digital subscriptions | Cancel to stop recurring charges | תעודת פטירה, sender ID | Include streaming and app subscriptions on standing orders |
| חברות כרטיסי אשראי | Credit-card issuers | Notify each issuer; stop or move recurring charges on each card | תעודת פטירה, sender ID | Cards are often issued by a company separate from the bank, so cancelling the bank's standing orders does not stop them. Keep the phone line active until two-factor codes are moved |

## Sequencing reminders

- Get the death certificate and several certified copies before starting the cascade. Everything downstream needs it.
- Before notifying anyone, run the two free government asset-locators by the deceased's ID: Har HaKesef (הר הכסף, Ministry of Finance) for lost pension savings, life-insurance policies, and bank accounts, and Har HaBituach (הר הביטוח, Capital Market Authority) for all insurance policies. This surfaces accounts and policies the family did not know about.
- Bank freeze and asset release are two different moments: freeze happens on notice; bulk release happens against the succession or probate order. Limited sums for immediate death expenses are a separate, earlier route that has to be asked for by name.
- Before dividing anything among the heirs, invite the creditors and settle the known debts. An estate divided without doing so puts every heir under section 128(a) of the Succession Law, exposed up to the value of the WHOLE estate rather than their own share, with the burden of proving value on the heir.
- The order itself (צו ירושה / צו קיום צוואה) is obtained separately: route to `israeli-wills-inheritance`.
- Survivor benefits are a separate, claim-based track: route to `israeli-survivor-benefits-navigator`.
