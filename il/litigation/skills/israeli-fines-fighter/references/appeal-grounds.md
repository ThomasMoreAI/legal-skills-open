# Appeal Grounds Reference

## Parking Fine Appeal Grounds (עילות ערעור על דוח חניה)

### Procedural Grounds
- **Missing signage (שלט חסר):** No parking sign was present or was placed too far from the violation location. Municipal regulations require clear signage within reasonable distance.
- **Obscured signage (שלט מוסתר):** Parking sign was blocked by vegetation, construction, or another sign, making it unreadable to a reasonable driver.
- **Incorrect details (פרטים שגויים):** The ticket contains wrong vehicle plate number, color, make, or model. A material misidentification (a plate that is not the user's vehicle) defeats the ticket. Do NOT tell a user that any single-digit typo automatically voids it: immaterial clerical errors are generally curable, and relying on that will lose the case.
- **Outside enforcement hours (מחוץ לשעות אכיפה):** The ticket was issued outside the posted enforcement hours shown on the parking sign.
- **Defective meter (מדחן תקול):** The parking meter or pay station was not functioning, and no alternative payment method was reasonably available.

### Substantive Grounds
- **Medical emergency (מצב חירום רפואי):** The driver or a passenger experienced a medical emergency requiring immediate attention, making proper parking impossible.
- **Loading/unloading (פריקה וטעינה):** Commercial vehicles actively loading or unloading goods are generally permitted brief stops in no-parking zones (varies by municipality).
- **Valid disabled permit (תג נכה בתוקף):** The vehicle displayed a valid disabled parking permit (tav necheh) issued by the Ministry of Transport.
- **Vehicle breakdown (תקלה ברכב):** The vehicle experienced a mechanical failure that prevented moving it from the location.

## Traffic Fine Appeal Grounds (עילות ערעור על דוח תנועה)

### Owner Liability Defenses
- **Vehicle sold (הרכב נמכר):** The vehicle was sold before the violation date. Requires sale contract with date and buyer details.
- **Vehicle stolen (הרכב נגנב):** The vehicle was reported stolen before the violation. Requires police report with date.
- **Different driver (נהג אחר):** The registered owner was not driving. Owner must submit a statutory declaration (tatzir) identifying the actual driver within 90 days.

### Technical Defenses
- **Camera calibration (כיול מצלמה):** Speed cameras must be regularly calibrated. Request calibration records from the police. If calibration was overdue, the reading is unreliable.
- **Unclear photo (תמונה לא ברורה):** The camera photo does not clearly show the license plate or the violation.

### Circumstantial Defenses
- **Emergency (מצב חירום):** Driving to a hospital or fleeing danger. Requires documentation.
- **Police direction (הוראת שוטר):** A police officer directed the driver to perform the action that was later cited as a violation.

## Legal References

- **Parking fines:** Municipal bylaws (chukei ezer ironi'im) per municipality
- **Traffic fines:** Traffic Ordinance (pekudat hatnu'a) - New Version, 1961
- **Points system:** Traffic Regulations (takanot hatnu'a), 1961, Part 14A
- **Appeal process, criminal track:** Criminal Procedure Law [Consolidated Version], 5742-1982, ss.225A, 228-230.
- **Appeal process, administrative track:** Administrative Traffic Violations Law, 5784-2024 (hasagah s.8, arar s.19, surcharge s.10, municipal inspector s.28).
- (An earlier version of this file cited "Criminal Procedure Law (Alternative Enforcement - Administrative Offenses), 5746-1985". That title is חוק העבירות המינהליות, a different statute, and it does not govern traffic breirot mishpat.)

## Bus-lane (nat"z) grounds

Driving in a public-transport lane is failure to obey signs 501/503 under Traffic Regulation 22(a),
500 NIS (First Schedule item 24), usually enforced by a municipal camera under Traffic Ordinance
s.27A1. Since 8 February 2026 it sits on the administrative track, so the route is **hasagah then
arar, with no request to be tried**. Grounds, strongest first:

- **Notice served late.** Camera offences under s.27A/27A1 must be served within **4 months** of the
  offence (s.225A(a1)(1) CPL); the general fine-offence limit is 1 year (s.225A(a)).
- **The vehicle was permitted in the lane.** Sign 501 states who may use it and commonly permits
  taxis, and motorcycles in some cities.
- **The lane was not in force at that hour.** Many lanes operate only in posted hours.
- **Lawful use.** Sign 503 permits a vehicle turning at the nearest junction; also entry to reach
  abutting property, to pass a stopped obstruction, or on the direction of a police officer or inspector.
- **Signage or marking defect.** Missing, contradictory or unclear sign, faded lane marking, or a
  lane carrying a turn arrow that permits all vehicles to turn.
- **Defect in the notice or image**, or transfer of liability to the actual driver.

## Administrative-track deadlines (since 8 February 2026)

- **Hasagah:** 30 days from service; 90 days where the ground is that the user was not the driver
  (Traffic Ordinance s.27B). Decision due within 45 days. (Administrative Traffic Violations Law s.8.)
- **Arar** to בית הדין לתעבורה: 90 days from service, or 30 days from the hasagah decision (s.19(b)).
  Filing an arar bars a later hasagah on the same decision (s.8(d)).
- **No bakasha le-hishafet exists on this track.**
- Which track applies is fixed by the **offence date**, not today's date.

## Grounds this file previously omitted

- **Limitation on service** (above) - among the most frequently successful grounds for camera tickets.
- **Defective service (המצאה).** Every deadline runs from service, so a defect in it is a ground.
- **The sign was not lawfully erected**, or the road marking did not match the sign.
- **הגנה מן הצדק** where the authority's own conduct makes the prosecution unfair.
