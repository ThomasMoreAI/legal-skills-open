# The scope, the schedule, and the clause bank

Read this before drafting either document. Everything here is a structure the parties fill in. This
file supplies **no prices and no default periods**, because neither exists in law and inventing them
is the worst failure available in this domain.

## 0. The one thing to say out loud first

A renovation is a `חוזה קבלנות` under חוק חוזה קבלנות, התשל"ד-1974, and that statute is
**dispositive**:

> הוראות חוק זה יחולו כשאין בדין אחר הוראות מיוחדות לענין הנדון, ובאין כוונה אחרת משתמעת מן ההסכם בין
> הצדדים

It applies only where the contract is silent, and it contains **no warranty schedule whatsoever**.
Every בדק or אחריות period in the document is a number the parties choose. Tell the user this
explicitly, because they have almost certainly read the `חוק המכר (דירות)` periods (2, 3, 4, 5 and 7
years) and believe those apply. They govern a **new apartment bought from a developer**, a different
transaction under a different, mandatory statute.

## 1. The scope of work (כתב כמויות פשוט)

One table per trade. A line is not finished until all six columns are filled.

| Column | Why it exists |
|---|---|
| Room / location | Ties the line to a place, so "the bathroom" cannot later mean a different bathroom |
| Work | The verb. Demolish, level, waterproof, tile, install |
| Quantity / area | The unit of dispute. Square metres, linear metres, count of points |
| Specification | Brand, model, series, finish, thickness, colour, standard |
| Supply | Who buys it, and who carries a price rise between quote and purchase |
| Excluded | What this line does **not** include |

### Rules for filling it

- Write **supply and install** or **install only** on every line. This single distinction causes more
  renovation arguments than any other.
- An unspecified finish becomes the contractor's choice by default. Where the user has not decided,
  write a placeholder **and a decision deadline**, never a blank.
- The **Excluded** column is not padding. Unlisted work is the mechanism by which `תוספות` inflate a
  price, and the exclusion is what makes a later addition visibly an addition.
- Record the condition of what is being kept, not only what is being changed. Existing floors,
  neighbouring apartments, and the stairwell should be photographed and dated before work starts.

### Trade checklist, so a scope does not silently omit a trade

Demolition and waste removal; plumbing first fix; electrical first fix; waterproofing (איטום);
screed and levelling; tiling and stone; carpentry and kitchen; aluminium and glazing; plumbing second
fix and fixtures; electrical second fix and fittings; HVAC; plaster and paint; silicone and sealing;
final clean; and **gas**, which is licensed work under חוק הגז (בטיחות ורישוי) and is the trade most often left off a renovation scope entirely.

## 2. The payment schedule

### The principle

Tie every payment to **verified completion of a defined stage**. A date pays for elapsed time; a
stage pays for work. If the user insists on dates, say plainly what they are giving up.

Hold a **retention** released only after acceptance and after the first defects window closes. The
retention is what makes `סעיף 4` collectable:

> לא תיקן הקבלן את הפגם תוך זמן סביר... רשאי המזמין (1) לתקן את הפגם ולדרוש מהקבלן החזרת הוצאותיו
> הסבירות; (2) לנכות מן השכר, כל עוד לא תוקן הפגם, את הסכום שבו פחת שווי המלאכה או השירות עקב הפגם

Without money still unpaid, the right to deduct is theoretical.

### Structure to offer (the parties choose the percentages)

| Stage | Trigger |
|---|---|
| Mobilisation | On signature. Keep it modest; it buys materials and a start date, not progress |
| Per completed stage | Each defined stage in the scope, verified against its lines |
| Practical completion | All lines complete, snag list agreed |
| Retention release | After acceptance plus the agreed defect window, snags closed |

Sanity checks, whether you run the helper script or do it inline:

1. Stage percentages **plus retention** must sum to exactly one hundred.
2. No payment before work starts should exceed a modest mobilisation figure.
3. The retention must remain unpaid until after the acceptance date, otherwise it is not a retention.
4. Every stage trigger must be objectively verifiable by looking at the property.

### Who pays changes the legal position

- **Private homeowner paying.** `חוק מוסר תשלומים לספקים, התשע"ז-2017` does **not** apply. Its
  definition of `מזמין` lists state authorities, ministries, local authorities, budgeted bodies,
  bodies established by law, Mifal HaPayis, and an `עסק`. A private individual is not among them, and
  `עסק` is defined as a financial institution, `עוסק מורשה`, or `עוסק פטור`. A tradesperson therefore
  has **no statutory payment date** against a private homeowner; only the contract creates one.
- **A business paying** (company, עמותה, landlord operating as an `עוסק`). Section 3(ז) applies:
  payment not later than **45 days from the end of the month** in which the invoice was submitted,
  unless the parties expressly set another date in the contract, and that date may be set only where
  the special nature of the engagement requires it or it is not exceptionally unfair.
- **A public body paying.** Section 3(א): 45 days from submission or 30 days from month end. Section
  3(ב) extends this for `עבודות הנדסה בנאיות` to 85 days from submission or 70 days from month end.

### VAT

State the total as `כולל מע"מ` or `בתוספת מע"מ`, explicitly, on the scope and on every payment line.
Note whether the tradesperson is an `עוסק פטור`, who issues a `חשבונית עסקה` and a `קבלה` but may not issue a `חשבונית מס`, or
an `עוסק מורשה`, who charges VAT and may issue a `חשבונית מס`. A homeowner who is
himself a business gets no input VAT from a receipt, which changes the real price of the job.

## 3. Clause bank

Each of these is a choice the parties make. Say so; do not present any of them as supplied by law.

### Warranty, per trade

Set a separate period for each of: waterproofing, tiling and stone, carpentry, electrical, plumbing,
aluminium, paint. For each, state what counts as a defect and what counts as fair wear. A single
blanket period is what the parties default into and it serves neither of them.

### Defect notice and cure

`סעיף 3` sets the mechanism but no clock:

> על המזמין להודיע לקבלן על הפגם זמן סביר לאחר שגילה אותו או שהיה עליו לגלותו, ואם הפגם ניתן לתיקון,
> לתת לקבלן הזדמנות נאותה לתקנו

Convert both into concrete numbers of days. Then warn the homeowner, in the document, that
`סעיף 3(ב)` forfeits reliance on the defect if he does not do this, unless the contractor already knew
of it. Hiring a replacement before offering the cure opportunity is the classic way a good claim
dies. The narrow exception is `סעיף 4(ב)`: a defect whose repair is so urgent that the customer
cannot be required to wait.

### Delay

Agreed compensation (`פיצוי מוסכם`) with a daily rate, a cap, and a closed list of excusable delays
(the homeowner's own decision delays, access failures, force majeure, discovery of concealed
conditions). This is grounded in general contract law, preserved by `סעיף 7`, not in the Contract for
Services Law itself. Without a liquidated figure the homeowner must prove actual loss, which for a
home renovation is usually impractical.

### Working hours

Compliance with the hours is not a complete defence. Noise inside permitted hours can still be an
actionable nuisance if it is unusual, prolonged, or materially harms quality of life.

The national floor, from תקנות למניעת מפגעים (מניעת רעש), התשנ"ג-1992:

- **Regulation 4, renovation work in a residential building:** prohibited between **20:00 and 07:00**
  on weekdays, and from **17:00 on the eve of a rest day until 07:00 the day after** the rest day.
- **Regulation 5, machinery** for excavation, building, or demolition in a residential area:
  prohibited from **19:00**, an hour earlier, with the same rest-day rule.

Two points to put in the document. First, both prohibitions bind whoever **permits** the work
(`לא יבצע אדם ולא ירשה לאחר לבצע`), so the homeowner is exposed, not only the tradesperson. Second,
these national regulations impose **no midday break** on renovation work; where one exists it comes
from a municipal by-law. By-laws are frequently stricter than the floor, so draft to the stricter of
the two and cite the local `חוק עזר`.

### Common property, in a בית משותף

Anything touching `רכוש משותף` needs the owners' consent. Two hard rules:

- **Two different majorities, routinely conflated.** Taking an EXISTING piece of common property and
  attaching it to one apartment (`הצמדה`) requires the consent of **ALL** owners: `סעיף 62(א)` says
  `ואין לקבוע הצמדה של חלק מסויים מהרכוש המשותף לדירה פלונית אלא בהסכמת כל בעלי הדירות`. The
  three-quarters-of-apartments-and-two-thirds-of-common-property rule is `סעיף 71ב`, and it applies
  only to detaching common property in order to **build** an extension to an apartment, with 60
  percent where the extension is a `ממ"ד`. Quoting the majority for a corridor niche invites a
  proprietary trespass that the מפקח will unwind at the trespasser's expense.
- Read the building's **`תקנון`** first, registered or the `תקנון המצוי` in the Schedule to
  חוק המקרקעין. It binds successors (`סעיף 62(ג)`) and is a third layer over the national noise floor
  and the municipal by-law, commonly restricting hours, stairwell and lift use, and common-area works.
- Anything touching a **`ממ"ד`** needs `פיקוד העורף` clearance before design. Do not assume a
  protected-space wall or blast window may be altered.
- Stairwells, lifts, shelters, and installations intended to serve all owners can **never** be
  attached to one apartment (`סעיף 55(ג)`). A renovation that annexes stairwell space is a hard stop,
  not a consent negotiation.

### Materials

Say per line who buys. Where the contractor supplies, add a price-escalation mechanism and a
specification lock so a substitution needs written approval. Note that `סעיף 8(ב)` diverts a contract
to **produce or manufacture an asset** where the contractor supplies the main materials to חוק המכר
instead; that provision targets supplying a produced asset and does **not** convert an ordinary
renovation of an existing apartment into a sale.

### Insurance, and separately indemnity

Third-party cover naming the homeowner as an additional insured; **employers' liability** cover for
the crew working in the apartment; contract works cover once the job is structural or high value.
Certificates before the first working day. Present all of these as **contractual preconditions**.
None was verified as a statutory duty for a private home renovation, so do not describe them as legal
requirements.

Insurance is not indemnity. A policy has an excess, exclusions, and can lapse. Add a separate
indemnity from the tradesperson covering damage to neighbours and to the `רכוש משותף`, or the
homeowner is personally liable to the neighbour with no contractual recourse.

### Termination, and suspension

The clause bank is useless without an exit. Define material breach, the notice and cure period before
termination, and the accounting on termination: paid to date, less the reasonable cost to complete,
plus title to materials already paid for and on site, plus return of keys. Terminating without a
contractual right makes the HOMEOWNER the breaching party under חוק החוזים (תרופות). Give the
tradesperson the mirror: a right to suspend for non-payment after written notice, because suspending
without a clause is itself a breach.

### The tradesperson's side, in more than one line

Beyond mobilisation, stage payments, price escalation, access relief and the `סעיף 5` lien: a cap on
his own liability; a variation entitlement (not merely an excusable delay) when concealed site
conditions are discovered; protection against unpaid extras he was told verbally to start; interest
on late payment under חוק פסיקת ריבית והצמדה; and, where the payer is a business, the practical fact
that a `ניכוי מס במקור` will reduce what actually reaches him unless he holds an exemption
certificate. Note also that the `סעיף 5` lien over `נכס שמסר לו המזמין` gives him little on a
renovation, because the homeowner hands over no asset; his real leverage is the suspension clause,
which is exactly why it must be written.

### Subcontracting, change orders, access

Bar subcontracting or condition it on written approval, and keep the head contractor liable either
way. Require every extra to be scoped, priced, and signed **before** it is executed. Set access
hours, key handling, which facilities the crew may use, and who pays for water, electricity, and
waste containers.

### The contractor's own protections

Do not draft a one-sided document. The tradesperson's legitimate clauses are: a mobilisation payment,
stage payments that fall due on verified completion rather than on the homeowner's convenience, a
materials price-escalation mechanism, relief for delays the homeowner causes, and the statutory lien
in `סעיף 5`:

> לקבלן תהא זכות עכבון על נכס שמסר לו המזמין לביצוע מלאכתו או למתן שירותו, כדי תשלום הסכומים המגיעים
> לו מן המזמין עקב עיסקת הקבלנות

## 4. Handover

`סעיף 6` makes acceptance a legal event: the customer must accept at the agreed date, or absent
agreement within a reasonable time after the contractor says the work is finished, and until then the
contractor carries the risk as a `שומר שכר`. **Date the acceptance**, because it starts the
contractual warranty clock.

Handover checklist:

- Marked or as-built routing of **concealed plumbing and electrical lines**. The single most skipped
  item, and the one that protects the next renovation.
- Appliance warranties, manuals, and registration details.
- Surplus tile, stone, and paint, with batch numbers.
- Waterproofing test evidence for every wet room.
- The independent `חשמלאי בודק` certificate, where the contract required one.
- Confirmation that construction waste was removed lawfully.
- A signed acceptance protocol listing every outstanding snag with a date for each.

## 5. When it goes wrong

Written notice, then the cure opportunity, then a dated photographic record. Timing, from
חוק ההתיישנות, התשי"ח-1958:

- `סעיף 5`: seven years for a claim that is not in real property. A renovation contract claim is
  contractual, so seven years, even though the work is physically on land.
- `סעיף 8`: where the facts were unknown for reasons outside the plaintiff's control and unavoidable
  with reasonable care, the period starts on the day they became known. This is what covers a latent
  waterproofing failure.
- `סעיף 9`: an acknowledgment restarts the clock, and **an act constituting partial performance
  counts as an acknowledgment**. A contractor who comes back and does a partial repair may restart
  the period in the homeowner's favour.

**There is no 14 day right to cancel a renovation engagement.** תקנות הגנת הצרכן (ביטול עסקה) grant
the right only for the goods and services enumerated in their Schedule, and building, renovation, and
trade services are not among the twenty-three items. Regulation 6(א) separately excludes furniture
assembled in the consumer's home and goods manufactured to special measurements or requirements,
which covers custom joinery. A cancellation right may still arise under the distance-selling or
door-to-door provisions of חוק הגנת הצרכן itself if the contractor signed the homeowner up remotely
or at their door, but that is a different provision and must not be conflated with the regulations.

**Forum.** A dispute between apartment owners in a בית משותף about rights or duties under the
`תקנון` or the sections `סעיף 72(א)` enumerates goes to the **`מפקח על רישום המקרקעין`**, not to
the general courts; for `הסגת גבול` the plaintiff may choose court or the supervisor (`סעיף 72(ב)`,
`לפי בחירתו`). A neighbour's tort claim for damage caused by the works is NOT within `סעיף 72(א)`
and goes to court, where small claims may be open to him.
A claim against the contractor goes to small claims only if the plaintiff is an **individual** and
the sum is within the ceiling: the statute enacts 30,000 ש"ח index-linked, and the Justice
Minister's annual notice sets it at 39,900 ש"ח from 1 January 2026, which a full multi-trade
renovation claim usually exceeds; the court may also decline
where the plaintiff filed more than five claims that year.

Then stop. Drafting pleadings and assessing merits are out of scope.


## Cash, and the standard-form trap

**Cash.** חוק לצמצום השימוש במזומן is routinely mis-stated as a flat 6,000 ש׳ח cap. It is not.
`סעיף 2(א)` prohibits an `עוסק` giving or receiving a cash payment for a transaction in the course of
his business where the **price of the transaction** exceeds the amount in item (1) of the First
Schedule; `סעיף 2(ג)` puts the same prohibition on a non-`עוסק` PAYING an `עוסק`, which is the
homeowner. Between two non-`עוסק` parties the governing figure is item (2). Both amounts are as-of
2026 and amendable by order.

What is left lawful is not a budget equal to the Schedule figure. The definition of `תשלום במזומן` in
`סעיף 1` excludes `סכום הנמוך מבין אלה`: ten percent of the transaction price, or the Schedule amount.
The LOWER of the two. On any job priced below ten times the Schedule figure, and that is the ordinary
renovation, the ten percent limb binds and the lawful residue is well under it. Compute it from the
agreed price each time; never carry a number over from another job.

**Standard-form terms.** Where the tradesperson's terms are drafted in advance for use with many
clients they are a `חוזה אחיד`. `סעיף 4` presumes as `מקפח`, among others: `4(1)` a term exempting or
unreasonably limiting the supplier's liability; `4(2)` a term giving him an unreasonable right to
cancel, suspend or defer performance; `4(6)` a term limiting a remedy the client has by law; `4(6א)`
agreed compensation set unreasonably in the supplier's favour. `סעיף 5` voids outright a term limiting
the client's right to go to the courts (`5(א)`) and a term exempting the supplier from liability for
bodily injury or a malicious act (`5(ב)`).

The clause bank above deliberately offers a liability cap, a suspension right and agreed compensation.
Where the document is a standard form, say so to both sides: the tradesperson is building on clauses a
court may strike, and the homeowner has a defence to the cap he is being asked to sign. Terms
negotiated for one job are not a `חוזה אחיד` and this does not reach them.
