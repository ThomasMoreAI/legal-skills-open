# Permits, registration, and licensed trades

Read this before answering any question about whether a renovation needs a permit, who may lawfully
perform it, or what a certificate is worth. Every figure here carries its source and, where it
moves, its as-of date.

## 1. The permit gate

### The rule is broad, the exception is narrow

`סעיף 145(א)(2)` of חוק התכנון והבניה, תשכ"ה-1965 requires a permit for:

> הקמתו של בנין, הריסתו והקמתו שנית, כולו או מקצתו, הוספה לבנין קיים **וכל תיקון בו**, למעט שינוי פנימי בדירה

Read `וכל תיקון בו` carefully: **any repair to a building** is caught. A renovation is lawful without
a permit only if it lands inside the `שינוי פנימי` carve-out, or inside one of the separate exemption
regulations in section 2 below.

### The five conditions, all of which must hold

The statute defines `שינוי פנימי` inside the same paragraph. It is a change that:

> שאינו נוגע לצד החיצוני של הבנין, אינו פוגע בחזיתו או במראהו או בשלד של הבנין או ברכוש משותף או
> בצנרת או ציוד אחר המשרתים גם דירות אחרות, אינו פוגע בזולת ואינו משנה את שטחה של הדירה, למעט תוספת
> של שטח מרפסת שנסגרה כדין, או את מספרן של יחידות הדיור

Broken out, so each can be tested against a real job:

| # | Condition | Ordinary renovation that trips it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Does not concern the **exterior** of the building | Moving or enlarging a window, changing an external door |
| 2 | Does not harm the **חזית**, **מראה**, or **שלד** | Removing or opening a load-bearing wall, cutting a beam |
| 3 | Does not harm **רכוש משותף**, or piping or equipment **also serving other dwellings** | Relocating a bathroom or kitchen onto the shared riser |
| 4 | Does not harm others (`אינו פוגע בזולת`) | Work that damages or burdens a neighbouring apartment |
| 5 | Does not change the dwelling's **area** or the **number of dwelling units** | Splitting one apartment into two, enclosing a balcony not already lawfully enclosed |

Condition 5 carries its own carve-out: adding the area of a balcony that was **lawfully** enclosed
does not count as changing the area.

**How to use this.** Test the job against all five and name the one it trips. Do not predict what the
`ועדה מקומית` will decide; say the job is outside the carve-out and that the committee decides.
Whether a specific wall is load bearing is an engineering question, so route it to a licensed
engineer and come back with their answer rather than guessing from a floor plan.

## 2. Separately exempt works

תקנות התכנון והבנייה (עבודות ומבנים הפטורים מהיתר), התשע"ד-2014 exempt an enumerated list. The
regulations are heavily amended, so check the current text before relying on a condition.

**Preconditions attached to every exemption** (פרק א): the work is performed by someone holding a
right in the land; structural stability and occupant safety are assured; the work complies with the
applicable תכנית and with הנחיות מרחביות; and it does not harm a שימור site or the coastal strip.
Exempt work performed in breach of these is an unlicensed building offence, not permitted work. Say
this whenever you tell a user something is exempt.

### The three most-asked categories, verbatim conditions

| Work | Condition | Notice? |
|---|---|---|
| **סורגים** (תקנה 17) | Per ת"י 1635, and **one window in each dwelling** must carry a bar designed as an escape opening | **Yes, 45 days**, and uniquely to **both** the licensing authority **and the national fire authority** |
| **מצללה / פרגולה** (תקנה 12) | Area not exceeding **50 sq m or a quarter** of the ground area free of buildings, or of the roof, **whichever is larger** | Yes, 45 days, with a structural engineer's anchoring and stability certificate |
| **מזגן** (תקנה 19) | Output not exceeding **60,000 BTU**; hidden in the laundry recess set in the permit, or on the roof; only if neither is possible, on an external wall **not facing the street**; must meet the noise regulations | **No notice duty** |

**A fourth category that matters for this user's job: `החלפת רכיב ברכיב בעל מידות זהות`.** Replacing a
component with one of identical dimensions (windows, piping, cabling) is its own exemption, and
`מידות זהות` is the limit. A like-for-like window swap sits here; enlarging the opening does not, and
falls back to condition 1 of `שינוי פנימי` (it concerns the exterior of the building). Other
enumerated categories worth naming when they arise: `גדר וקיר תומך`, `גגון`/`סוכך`, `פרטי עזר` such as
cameras and lighting, `שלט`, and `ממ"ד` in low-rise housing, which is exempt only with the competent
authority's approval. Read the current regulation text before relying on any condition.

Two traps in that table:

1. The pergola cap is **the larger of** 50 sq m and a quarter of the free area. Guides state a flat
   50 sq m, which understates the exemption. Separately, the definition requires the gaps between the
   opaque parts to be evenly distributed and to be **at least 40 percent** of the shading surface, so
   a solid-roofed structure is not a `מצללה` at all and gets no exemption.
2. Air conditioning carries **no** notice duty. Do not apply the 45-day rule to it.

### The notice duty is per-regulation, not general

The 45-day notice attaches to specified regulations only, in the form:

> הודעה על ביצוע עבודה לפי תקנה זו ערוכה לפי הטופס שבתוספת, תימסר לרשות הרישוי בתוך 45 ימים ממועד ביצועה

Regulations carrying it include 8, 9, 11, 12, 14, 17, 21, 22 and its sub-regulations, 24, 24א, 28,
29, 30ד, 30לא to 30לד, 33, 34, 37, 38, 39, 39ו. Homeowner-relevant ones are 11 (גגון), 12 (מצללה),
17 (סורגים), 24 (photovoltaic), 29 (מחסן), and 39 (demolition). Regulations 18 (דוד שמש) and 19
(מזגן) have none. Never state the duty as a blanket rule of the scheme. The notice is filed through the מינהל התכנון online licensing system, so a user told a duty exists can actually discharge it.

## 3. Must the contractor be a קבלן רשום?

Two **independent** limbs, under תקנות רישום קבלנים לעבודות הנדסה בנאיות (היקף כספי ומהות מקצועית),
התשמ"ד-1984. Either one triggers the requirement.

### Limb 1, value

Work at a single site above the threshold, per branch. **As of 2026: 108,500 ש"ח per `ענף עבודה` and
56,750 ש"ח per `ענף משנה`.**

The last figures verified against a primary publication in רשומות were **103,250 and 54,000**,
effective 1 January 2025 (ק"ת 11787, 9 March 2025). The 2026 pair above is taken from Nevo's
consolidated text and is arithmetically consistent with it, but the underlying רשומות notice was not
located, so treat it as current-but-secondary.

**Always give the figure with its as-of date**, because regulation 3 re-indexes it:

> הסכומים האמורים ישתנו ב־1 בינואר של כל שנה לפי שיעור עליית מדד חודש אוקטובר שקדם למועד האמור לעומת
> מדד חודש אוקטובר שלפניו; הסכומים המתקבלים יעוגלו לסכום הקרוב שהוא מכפלה של 50 ש״ח

and the Registrar publishes the resulting amounts in רשומות. Tell the user to check the current
notice rather than rely on a number embedded in a skill.

### Limb 2, nature of the work

> עבודות הנוגעות לשלד של מבנה יבוצעו בידי קבלנים רשומים בלבד, אף אם אין היקפן הכספי חורג מן האמור

Any work touching the **שלד** requires a registered contractor **at any value**. Renovation guides
state only limb 1. A cheap job that opens a load-bearing wall is caught by limb 2 and is not lawful
without a registered contractor, regardless of price.

### Registration is per branch, and סיווג is a separate ceiling

Registration is recorded per `ענף`, so a contractor registered in one branch is not thereby
permitted at the threshold in another. Verify the registration, the branch, and the classification in
the `פנקס הקבלנים` kept by `רשם הקבלנים` at משרד הבינוי והשיכון.

`סיווג` is a separate concept: it caps the project value a registered contractor may take. In group
א, which contains sub-branch **131 שיפוצים**, the caps in **thousands** of shekels are:

| סוג | Cap (thousands ש"ח) | As of |
|---|---|---|
| ק | 825 | February 2026 |
| 1 | 1,927 | December 2025 |
| 2 | 3,854 | December 2025 |
| 3 | 7,708 | December 2025 |
| 4 | 15,416 | December 2025 |
| 5 | unlimited | |

So `סוג 1` is 1,927,000 ש"ח, comfortably above almost any apartment renovation. These re-index twice
a year, on 1 April and 1 October. Group א also covers sub-branches 160 (חשמלאות ותקשורת במבנים) and
190 (מיתקני תברואה); מיזוג אוויר (170) and איטום מבנים (134) sit in group ב with higher caps.

The instrument is `תקנות רישום קבלנים לעבודות הנדסה בנאיות (סיווג קבלנים רשומים), התשמ"ח-1988`. There
is no instrument called "תקנות סיווג קבלנים רשומים"; do not cite that name.

## 4. Electrical work

### Execution grades

Under תקנות החשמל (רשיונות), התשמ"ה-1985, a `חשמלאי מוסמך` may perform:

> כל עבודות החשמל, לרבות עריכת תכניות, במיתקן בעל עצמת זרם עד 80×3 אמפר, ובלבד שהמיתקן נמצא במבנה
> המשמש לדירות מגורים, משרדים או בתי מלאכה

An ordinary apartment installation sits well below the 3x80A ceiling above, so a `מוסמך` normally suffices, but confirm the actual rating rather than assuming it. Higher
grades (`ראשי`, `טכנאי`, `הנדסאי`, `מהנדס`) extend the ampacity ceiling. Ask to see the physical
licence and its grade, not a business card.

### Inspection is a different licence, and self-inspection is barred

Inspection is a separate licence class. Regulation 23:

> בעל רשיון חשמלאי־בודק סוג 1 רשאי לעסוק בביצוע בדיקות של מיתקן חשמלי בעל עוצמת זרם עד 80×3 אמפר
> במתח נמוך, **למעט עבודות חשמל אשר תוכננו וגם בוצעו על ידו**

The exclusion is cumulative (`תוכננו וגם בוצעו`). Practical consequence: **a certificate signed by
the same electrician who designed and did the work is worthless.** The inspection must come from an
independent `חשמלאי בודק` of the appropriate class (סוג 1 up to 3x80A, סוג 2 up to 3x250A, סוג 3 any
installation).

### The certificate is a contract term, not a statutory duty on the homeowner

**No statutory duty was located requiring a private homeowner to obtain a `תעודת בודק` after ordinary
apartment electrical work.** The Electricity Law binds the worker (a person may not perform
electrical work without a licence) and binds whoever installs or fundamentally alters an installation
to hold a written permit from the Director. The commonly cited "תקנות החשמל (בדיקת מיתקן חשמלי)" does
not appear to exist as an instrument, and the widely repeated periodic-inspection rule most likely
derives from תקנות הבטיחות בעבודה (חשמל), which are workplace-safety instruments and do not reach a
private dwelling.

**Therefore: require the certificate as a contractual handover artifact, and never tell the user the
law requires it.** Contractually requiring it is sound practice and costs nothing to state correctly.

## 4a. Waste, and the container on the street

Placing a `מכולה` for construction waste on a public road needs a permit from the local authority.
Who is exposed to the fine is a matter of the local `חוק עזר` and varies between authorities, so do
not tell the user it falls on the homeowner rather than on the contractor, or the reverse.
Municipal rules and fees vary, so make it a contract line naming who obtains the permit and who pays,
and have the user confirm the rule with their own municipality.

## 5. Safety, when the job is large

תקנות הבטיחות בעבודה (עבודות בניה), התשמ"ח-1988 put the duty on the `מבצע בניה`:

> מבצע בניה אחראי לכך כי כל עבודת בניה תתבצע בהנהלתו הישירה והמתמדת של מנהל עבודה שהוא מינהו

The appointment is notified to the regional labour inspector: the notice is `תיעשה על גבי טופס` obtainable from that office, is signed by **both** the `מבצע בניה` and the appointed `מנהל עבודה`, and is sent by registered post. Separately, work at height is defined by a concrete threshold: any
work, including access to the workplace, from which a worker could fall **more than 2 metres**. The threshold at
which a private apartment renovation becomes "עבודות בניה" for these regulations was **not** verified,
so do not assert one.

Ask the actionable question instead: **who is the `מבצע בניה` on this job?** If demolition,
scaffolding, or work at height is involved, the answer determines who must appoint a `מנהל עבודה`,
and the contract should name that party explicitly. Work at height is separately regulated.
