# Changelog

## 1.7.0 - 2026-08-27

Added a whole missing half of the workflow: what an oleh lives on AFTER the six-month basket. New Step 4b and `references/post-basket-entitlements.md` cover rent assistance from Misrad HaBinui VeHaShikun (automatic, month 7 to month 30 for anyone who made aliyah from 01.03.2024, capped at 95% of rent, not automatic past retirement age), havtachat kiyum from Misrad HaKlita in its six gated tracks as distinct from Bituach Leumi's havtachat hachnasa, the special old-age pension for an oleh who arrived past retirement age (1,838 / 1,941 NIS, 2026), the no-income-test daycare tier, the Student Authority tuition funding and its 36-month clock, and the state funding for licensing-exam prep and notarial translations.

Corrected eleven facts. Olim DO have the Acclimatization Year election (the skill previously said the opposite, which cost an oleh a year of deferral; gov.il's own service says otherwise, filed within 90 days and once only). Ulpan is 420 to 450 funded hours, not 500; 18 months is the entitlement period rather than a start-by deadline, 24 on havtachat hachnasa and extendable; the 80%-attendance rule belongs to the private voucher route. The driving tiers were wrong in three cells: there is no short "mivchan shlita", under 5 years' seniority means a full practical test, theory only follows two failures, the minimum-lessons exemption sits on the retest path, and the claimed 6-month-absence reset of the foreign-licence clock does not exist. The 2026 reform's 75-day rule has two cumulative limbs joined by "and", not "or". Arnona is 12 months out of 24 from registration, plus an 80% whole-apartment track for dependency cases. The Shinui Ma'amad background-check rule is "more than one year", not six months. Added the sal klita 24-month presence test, procedure 14.211, the clawback mechanism, and the five (not three) land crossings for cash declaration. Removed a mas rechisha oleh bracket above 20,183,565 that does not exist, and a 100,000 NIS oleh cash threshold with no source.

Reconciled the reference layer, which had drifted out of sync with SKILL.md on the facts the main file treats as headline corrections: the timeline guide promised a seventh basket payment and a 12-month conversion deadline, the checklist script rebuilt the country-of-origin licence system abolished in 2017, and the Hebrew document reference stated the six-month rule the English half warns against. Rewrote Troubleshooting around causes a branch actually resolves (separate accounts instead of a joint one, a changed account number, a trip abroad with no retroactive payment, the presence test, split aliyah) and stated the verified negative that income and savings never reduce the basket. Script fixes: the calculator now rejects a lone adult with children on the single track, rejects negative and non-integer ages instead of raising a traceback, and states what its total does not include.

Both files are back under the 5,000-word validator cap, which they had breached at HEAD, and section parity is restored at 29 each.
