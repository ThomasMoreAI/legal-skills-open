# What an oleh lives on AFTER the six-month sal klita

The sal klita tables cover six months. The entitlements below are what a household actually
runs on from month seven, and they are the most commonly missed part of the whole aliyah
workflow. Sources: Kol Zchut "סל קליטה", "סיוע בשכר דירה לעולים", "גמלת זיקנה מיוחדת לעולים
ולתושבים חוזרים", "סיוע להורים שהם עולים חדשים במימון מעונות יום ומשפחתונים", "מימון שכר לימוד
לסטודנטים עולים". The governing ministry procedure for the basket itself is
**נוהל משרד העלייה והקליטה מס' 14.211 - סל קליטה**; quote it when disputing a stopped payment
or a הסדרת חוב demand.

## 1. Rent assistance (קצבת שכר דירה), the largest post-basket payment

Rent help exists in two phases and the second one is paid by a DIFFERENT ministry.

- **During the basket:** rent assistance is part of the sal klita entitlement for olim with no
  apartment ("כל אדם שעולה לישראל זכאי לסל קליטה שכולל דמי מחיה למשך 6 חודשים וסיוע בשכר דירה").
  It is not one of the cash lines in the published tables.
- **After the basket:** a rent allowance from **משרד הבינוי והשיכון**, not Misrad HaKlita, paid
  **automatically** into the bank account.

| Aliyah date | Starts | Runs until |
|---|---|---|
| Up to 29.02.2024 | month **8** | end of year 5 (single parents: end of year 6) |
| From 01.03.2024 | month **7** | month **30** |

Extensions and special cases:

- Olim with no apartment living on Bituach Leumi benefits: **no time limit**.
- Pre-01.03.2024 cohort, recognized lone soldiers (by both the IDF and Misrad HaKlita): for the
  period they are lone soldiers, up to **36 months**.
- From-01.03.2024 cohort, anyone who enlists in the IDF or national service within 30 months of
  receiving oleh status: for the service period, up to **36 months**, whether or not recognized
  as a lone soldier.
- **נעל"ה graduates: from the FIRST month** of oleh status.

Conditions and exclusions:

- Requires a valid Teudat Oleh, "no apartment" status, and a rental in an eligible locality.
- **Not** automatic for someone who made aliyah after reaching retirement age. They qualify only
  if living on קצבת אזרח ותיק with השלמת הכנסה, or as נפגעי הנאצים with up to 49% disability, and
  they must apply. Those renting on the private market rather than from a first-degree relative
  get a supplement.
- **Excluded:** anyone still receiving sal klita; recipients of MoD rent assistance; subsidised
  public-housing tenants; residents of state-funded sheltered housing or care homes.
- Someone leaving a merkaz klita must file an **"אישור עזיבת מרכז קליטה"**.
- The payment is capped at **95% of the rent** and declines with time in the country.
- Hotline **\*2310**.

## 2. הבטחת קיום from Misrad HaKlita, and הבטחת הכנסה from Bituach Leumi

These are two different benefits at two different agencies, and the skill must not merge them.

**הבטחת קיום** is paid by **Misrad HaAliyah V'HaKlita** in the second half of the first aliyah
year, after the basket ends, in six separately-gated tracks:

1. olim who are ill or hospitalised
2. an oleh caring for a sick family member
3. an oleh who is pregnant
4. olim under 65
5. single-parent oleh families
6. olim with a disability, impairment, or blindness

**הבטחת הכנסה** is the Bituach Leumi income-support benefit, which the ministry's own sal klita
page names as the thing to check for at the end of month six. It normally carries an
employment-bureau registration condition, and studying at an ulpan can substitute for that
condition.

Route a pregnant oleh, or one nursing a sick spouse, to the MINISTRY track, not to the BL counter.

## 3. Special old-age pension (גמלת זיקנה מיוחדת)

An oleh who arrived past retirement age is **not insured** for old age and therefore has no
contribution record. That does NOT mean they get nothing: they are entitled to the special
old-age pension, paid at the basic single old-age rate.

- **1,838 NIS** to age 80; **1,941 NIS** from 80 (2026 values).
- Men who made aliyah above age 60 to 62 by birth-date table; women who made aliyah above
  retirement age (62 to 65 by birth date).
- Additions for a dependent spouse or children; השלמת הכנסה where income is low.
- Also covers a toshav chozer who never accumulated the qualifying period.

Never tell an elderly oleh that the old-age pension "depends on years of residency and
contributions" and stop there. That is the single most damaging wrong answer in this workflow.

## 4. Daycare subsidy (מעונות יום ומשפחתונים), with NO income test

An oleh parent who, at the start of the school year, has been in Israel **up to two years** since
aliyah gets **tier 3 of the tuition table for every child, with no income test**, provided BOTH
parents meet one of:

- both studying at an ulpan for at least **24 hours a week**, or
- both registered as job-seekers at שירות התעסוקה or at Misrad HaKlita, or
- one of each.

If one parent fails the condition, the tier is computed by the ordinary (non-oleh) rules. This is
worth more per month to a young family than the sal klita child supplement, and it is conditioned
on exactly the ulpan hours the skill discusses elsewhere.

## 5. Student Authority (המינהל לסטודנטים עולים)

Misrad HaKlita funds tuition for olim admitted to an institution recognized by the Student
Authority. **Check that the institution is recognized BEFORE enrolling.**

- Covers bachelor's and master's degrees, practical-engineer (הנדסאי) studies, certificate
  studies in the arts and complementary medicine, registered nurse, and certificate or conversion
  studies for existing degree-holders.
- Open to olim, children of olim, ezrach oleh, and katin chozer, present in Israel throughout.
- **Studies must begin within 36 months of receiving the status**, excluding IDF service (plus up
  to one year of permanent service), national service, and up to 4 years of hesder yeshiva.
  נעל"ה graduates who changed status: within 12 months of finishing the programme, and within 36
  months of the status change or programme end, whichever is later.
- Funding is conditional on community service (שח"ק) and is granted per study year.

## 6. Funded support for professional re-licensing

Misrad HaKlita funds, for olim, preparatory courses for the state licensing exams in general
medicine, accountancy, pharmacy, law, veterinary medicine and dentistry; a contribution toward
the **notarial translations** needed for licensing; accommodations in the CPA Council exam and in
the licensing authority's theory exam; vocational-training funding and vouchers; an employment
centre for olim; and assistance for immigrant scientists. Tell an oleh professional this BEFORE
they pay for translations and exam prep themselves.
