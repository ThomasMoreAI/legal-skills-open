# The filing package, document by document

Every figure here carries a year. Confirm the operative figure for the year you are filing.

## The four annual documents

Filed with the Registrar under the Associations Law, due 30 June of the year following the end of
the report period (s.36(ד)).

| # | Document | Who prepares it | Who signs or approves it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | דוח כספי | the board (ועד) | two board members sign; the general assembly approves |
| 2 | דוח מילולי | the board | the board |
| 3 | פרוטוקול ועדת ביקורת | ועדת ביקורת or the גוף מבקר | the committee or auditing body |
| 4 | דיווח אסיפה כללית | the board, recording the assembly | the assembly |

### 1. דוח כספי

s.36(א) requires the board to prepare, once a year for each tax year, a **מאזן** plus a statement of
income and expenditure, with the full detail listed in the Second Schedule. This binds **every**
amuta regardless of turnover.

s.36(ג) requires it to be drawn up per accepted accounting and reporting rules suited to the amuta's
situation and circumstances, and to give fair expression to the data in the books kept under s.35(א).

If the amuta is over the audit threshold (see the band table), s.37(א) requires the report to reach
the general assembly **audited by the appointed accountant**.

### The s.36(ב) attachment, most often missed

The board must attach a statement giving full and precise detail of every payment the amuta made or
undertook to make, in the reported year, to **each of the five highest-paid people** in the amuta,
including retirement terms. "Payments" is defined broadly: sums of money and anything worth money,
loans, securities or other rights, and any other benefit. It covers payments made to someone else
on their behalf or because of their employment.

Ask for this explicitly. It is a separate document from the financial report and its absence is a
common reason a package comes back incomplete.

### 2. דוח מילולי

A narrative account of the year's activity. Basis: s.37א.

### 3. פרוטוקול ועדת ביקורת

The recommendations of the ועדת ביקורת, or of the גוף מבקר where the general assembly elected one
under s.19(ב), on approving the financial report. Under s.36(א) the financial report goes to the
committee or auditing body **at least two weeks before** the general assembly, or earlier if the
takanon says so.

### 4. דיווח אסיפה כללית

Record that the general assembly approved the financial report.

## Band table, which duties apply

| Band | Threshold | Consequence | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Organs | any turnover | general assembly, board, audit committee | s.19(א) |
| Filing exemption | at or below 500,000 NIS | financial report not filed under s.36(ד), but still prepared, reviewed by the audit committee and approved by the assembly; verbal report and its Form 7א annexes still filed | Forms Regulations reg. 7(ה)(2) |
| Bookkeeping | above 750,000 NIS, not index-linked, measured on the previous year's receipts | double-entry books | Second Schedule item א |
| Audit | above 1,380,630 NIS for 2026, index-linked | appoint a רו"ח; report audited to the assembly | s.19(ג)(1), s.37(א) |
| Internal audit | above 10,000,000 NIS | appoint a מבקר פנימי, with the audit committee's agreement | s.30א |
| Foreign political donations | above 300,000 NIS | statement in the financial report | s.36א |

s.19(ג)(2) requires the audit amount to be published each February, but no notice appeared for 2015
to 2025. The 2014 notice gave 1,172,933 NIS; the 2026 amount, 1,380,630 NIS, was published in ילקוט
הפרסומים 14348 and states that it accounts for the 2014 to 2025 updates. For the 2024 and 2025 reports,
turnover between those two figures is an unsettled zone: see SKILL.md step 3.

s.37(ב): the Registrar may order an audit even below the threshold, on the audit committee's
request, on the request of a tenth of the members, or on the Registrar's own initiative.

## Certificate application

The certificate is issued for a named calendar year. A specimen reads
"אישור זה תקף מיום 01/01/2024 ועד ליום 31/12/2024".

Do not describe the certificate as being valid for two years by default. Two-year certificates do
exist: the Registrar's letter of 28.5.2024 addressed amutot holding a certificate for 2024-2025 and
required them to file their 2023 reports by 31.10.2024, failing which the 2025 year of the
certificate would be cancelled without further warning. So a two-year holder still files every
year. The conditions for granting a two-year certificate in later cycles were not found in a
primary source; confirm with the Registrar.

The certificate for year X is granted against the annual reports for year X minus 2.

## What this skill does not produce

- The audited financial statement or the auditor's opinion. Reserved to a רואה חשבון.
- Any תקנון drafting or amendment, and any legal opinion. Reserved to a lawyer.
- The Section 46 approval application itself. This skill covers reporting BY a holder of the
  approval, not acquiring the status.
