# Donor naming and the donation receipt book

Every figure here carries a year. Confirm the operative figure for the year you are filing.

**Naming donors generally.** Separately from foreign political entities, the Second Schedule lets
an amuta omit a donor's name from the financial report only where the receipt carries
"תרומה בעילום שם" in the donor-name field AND either the donation does not exceed the maximum the
Minister set, now **100,000 NIS a year** (Forms Regulations, reg. 7א(ב)), or the Registrar granted
a **special approval** not to name the donor (reg. 7ב). Two more figures sit beside it: the amuta
keeps a register of donors whose cumulative annual donation exceeds 20,000 NIS (reg. 7א(א)), and a
donor above 50,000 NIS who is at least 20% of the year's turnover must be named regardless
(reg. 7א(ג)). For all three figures an individual and the household members they support count
as one donor, as do a corporation, its controlling holder, and corporations under the same control
(reg. 7א(ד)). Do not confuse the 20,000 register figure with the foreign political-entity one;
they are different rules that happen to share a number.

**Receipt-book mechanics that bind the two workstreams together.** Donation receipts go in a
SEPARATE receipt book, the word "תרומה" must be printed conspicuously on every receipt in it, and
an amuta holding a Section 46 approval must additionally print on the receipt that it holds an
Income Tax approval for donations under Section 46. This is a concrete, checkable defect an
inspector will find, and it is where the Registrar workstream and the Section 46 workstream meet.
