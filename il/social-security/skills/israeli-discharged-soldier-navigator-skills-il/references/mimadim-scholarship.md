# Mimadim LiLimudim, full terms

Verified 2026-08-27 against the official page,
https://www.hachvana.mod.gov.il/MainEducation/HachvanaScholarship/Pages/UniformToStudies.aspx

The Department and Fund for Discharged Soldiers funds 100% of the university tuition set by the
Council for Higher Education (המל"ג), as recognition of service. Step 8 of SKILL.md carries the
summary; this file carries the money mechanics and the eligibility split.

## Who qualifies

Two tracks, both requiring full and proper IDF service:

1. **Combat soldiers** holding a "שחרור בכבוד-זהב" (gold-honour) discharge certificate.
2. **Special populations** holding a "שחרור בכבוד" certificate and meeting one of: entitlement to
   תשמ"ש הורים; parents who received family payments during service; discharged lone soldiers
   recognised as lone through the end of service; minorities; Arab, Druze, or Circassian
   dischargees; olim so defined during service.

Combat support (תומך לחימה) is not a listed track on its own. It is not an exclusion either,
because a tomech lechima may fall inside the special-populations basket. Send the user to verify
their own cohort rather than ruling either way.

## What it pays

| Aspect | Detail |
|---|---|
| Ceiling | The CHE university tuition, **12,017 NIS for תשפ"ו** |
| During the degree | Annual refund of up to **85%** of what was actually paid, capped at the ceiling. At the ceiling that is **10,214.45 NIS** |
| Final year | Top-up to **100%**, releasing the 15% accrued each earlier year. At the ceiling that residual is **1,802.5 NIS** |
| Tuition above the ceiling | Covered only to the CHE ceiling; the balance is the student's |
| Duration | 3 years of study, or 4 for a first degree in engineering, medicine (doctor), or architecture |
| Retroactivity | Granted retroactively for each earlier year in which the student was eligible and received a scholarship from the Department |
| Volunteer hours | None required |
| Not covered | Dorms, living costs, and anything that is not tuition |

The official page says "first degree and art certificate" in its summary and "first degree,
second degree, or art certificate" in its eligibility section. Do not promise master's coverage
on the strength of the second sentence.

## The application window

Five years from discharge from compulsory service, extended by:

- **+1 year (to 6)** for completing bagrut, a mechina, or the psychometric within those 5 years
- **+2 years (to 7)** for a continuous year or more of keva service after discharge
- **+3 years (to 8)** for both

A wrinkle worth stating to a user near the edge: Section 7A1(d) of the statute measures the
window as five years **from the end of the year of discharge** and gates on starting studies,
while the website frames it as five years from discharge and gates on filing the first
application. Where a user is close to either boundary, tell them the two formulations differ and
have them confirm with the Department rather than picking one.

## Interaction with the Charvot Barzel scholarship

A soldier eligible for both Mimadim and the separate "מלגת חרבות ברזל" cannot realise both in
the same academic year, and the order of realisation changes the total received. The official
page says so directly: "אם אתם זכאים גם למלגת 'חרבות ברזל', כדאי לבדוק קודם מהו סדר המימוש
המשתלם ביותר עבורכם."
