# Adjacent statutory duties triggered by hiring

This skill drafts the job advertisement, the screening framework and the offer letter. Hiring
triggers further duties it does NOT draft and does NOT advise on. Raise them with the user and
route them to the statute or to counsel.

**Do not state a deadline, a percentage, an employee-count threshold or a number of days for
any row below unless you have just read it at an authoritative source in this session.**

| Trigger | Duty | Statute to check |
|---|---|---|
| Every hire | A written notice of employment terms (הודעה לעובד על תנאי עבודה) is a **separate statutory document from the offer letter**, with its own prescribed particulars and its own deadline. An offer letter does not discharge it. | חוק הודעה לעובד ולמועמד לעבודה (תנאי עבודה והליכי מיון וקבלה לעבודה), התשס"ב-2002 |
| Every hire, larger employers | Publishing a sexual-harassment prevention תקנון, appointing a ממונה, and giving the new employee the תקנון | חוק למניעת הטרדה מינית, התשנ"ח-1998 and its employer-duty regulations |
| Screening | Demanding that a candidate produce a criminal-record extract, or consent to a register query, is itself prohibited | חוק המידע הפלילי ותקנת השבים |
| Candidate is not an Israeli citizen or resident | Employing a foreign national requires an employer permit and a valid work visa. This is the one place nationality is a lawful question, and it is a permit question, not a screening criterion. | חוק עובדים זרים, התשנ"א-1991 |
| Candidate is under 18 | Minimum working age, a pre-employment medical fitness certificate, restricted hours and night work, and a **reduced minimum wage set as a percentage of the adult rate**. Look the percentages up; do not assume. | חוק עבודת הנוער, התשי"ג-1953; חוק שכר מינימום, התשמ"ז-1987 |
| Candidate has a disability | Reasonable accommodation in the application route and in testing, not only at interview | חוק שוויון זכויות לאנשים עם מוגבלות, התשנ"ח-1998 |
| Scheduling work on the weekly rest day | Rest-day work is **prohibited without a permit**, and compensatory rest must be given. The 150 / 175 / 200 percent rates price work that is already permitted; they do not authorise it. | חוק שעות עבודה ומנוחה, התשי"א-1951 |
| Genetic or medical testing | Requiring a genetic test is prohibited; medical and personality testing turn on consent and proportionality | חוק מידע גנטי; חוק הגנת הפרטיות |
| Large employers | An annual gender pay-gap report, with an employee-count threshold. Check the current threshold before telling a user it does or does not apply. | חוק שכר שווה לעובדת ולעובד, התשנ"ו-1996 |
| Sector with a collective agreement or extension order | The agreement can raise every floor in the Step 5 table, and the notice of terms must name it | חוק הסכמים קיבוציים, התשי"ז-1957 |
