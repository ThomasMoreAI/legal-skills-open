# Succession in Estonia

Primary source: Notarite Koda and `parimine.notar.ee`.

Key points to verify:

- Succession opens upon death.
- Succession proceedings are carried out through a notary.
- The estate can include assets, rights, and obligations.
- A succession certificate is needed to prove heir rights for many transactions.
- Estate inventory may be relevant when debts are uncertain or material.
- A will may be notarised or made at home only if formal requirements are met; verify before relying on it.

Checklist:

1. Confirm death date and personal data.
2. Check whether a will or succession contract exists.
3. Identify legal heirs and spouse/registered partner.
4. List assets and debts.
5. Decide whether to ask the notary about estate inventory.
6. Prepare ID documents, civil-status documents, asset documents, and known debt documents.
