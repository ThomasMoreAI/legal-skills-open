# Real Estate Notarial Preparation

Prepare before contacting the notary:

- parties and identification data;
- property address and land register details;
- price, deposit, financing, loan and mortgage plans;
- marital property status of each party;
- possession transfer date;
- utilities and apartment association arrears status;
- encumbrances, servitudes, leases, use restrictions;
- energy performance and technical documentation where relevant.

Ask the notary:

- which documents are needed;
- who pays state fees and notary fees;
- how purchase price should be held or paid;
- whether any consent is needed;
- whether AML or sanctions checks require additional data.
