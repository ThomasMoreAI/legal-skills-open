# Knowledge Pack — UAE Golden Visa and Long-Term Residency

## Overview

The UAE has created a tiered long-term residency framework designed to attract investors, entrepreneurs, skilled professionals, and talent:

| Visa Type | Duration | Self-Sponsored | Key Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Golden Visa** | 10 years (renewable) | Yes | Investors, entrepreneurs, specialized talent, researchers |
| **Green Visa** | 5 years (renewable) | Yes | Skilled employees, freelancers, self-employed |
| **Standard Residency** | 2 years (employer-sponsored) | No | Standard employment |
| **Retirement Visa** | 5 years | Partially | Retirees 55+ |
| **Freelancer Permit** | 1–2 years | Yes | Freelance professionals |

---

## Golden Visa (10-Year Residency)

### Eligibility Categories

#### 1. Investors in Real Estate

- Property value: **AED 2,000,000** (minimum, standalone or with mortgage, in freehold areas).
- Property may be under mortgage if the paid-up portion meets AED 2M threshold.
- Property must remain owned throughout the visa validity.
- Can include properties in Dubai, Abu Dhabi, and other freehold-designated areas.

#### 2. Investors (Business Investment)

- **AED 2,000,000** in a UAE business (capital contribution).
- Establishment of a company with minimum capital AED 2M (MOEC/ADIO registered).
- Public investment funds: deposits in approved UAE investment fund of AED 2M+.

#### 3. Entrepreneurs

- Must establish or own a UAE startup or SME meeting one of:
  - Valued at **AED 500,000+** with endorsement from an accredited business incubator.
  - Previously established a company sold for **AED 7,000,000+**.
  - Received approval from UAE incubator or accelerator program.

#### 4. Specialized Talent

| Subcategory | Requirements |
|---|---|
| Doctors and scientists | At least PhD in a priority field; recommendation from relevant authority |
| Inventions and patents | Accepted patent from UAE or international authority with economic value |
| Creative professionals | Artists, athletes, cultural figures with DCCA/Ministry of Culture endorsement |
| Healthcare pioneers | Medical/scientific research recognition |
| Specialized engineers | Civil, electrical, AI, software, biomedical in strategic sectors |

#### 5. Outstanding Students and Graduates

- **UAE universities**: GPA of 3.75+ from accredited UAE university.
- **Top international universities** (world rankings): bachelor's degree or above from top 100 institution.
- Secondary school outstanding students with high academic achievements.

#### 6. Humanitarian and Other Categories

- Frontier scientists recognized by UAE.
- Individuals who made "distinguished contributions to UAE interests."

### Golden Visa — Key Features

- **No employer sponsor required** — self-sponsored residency.
- Holders may **sponsor family members** (spouse, children of any age — unlimited age for dependent children differs from standard sponsorship).
- Valid for **10 years**; renewable indefinitely if criteria maintained.
- Can **leave UAE for up to 6 months** without visa cancellation (standard residency cancels after 6 months outside UAE).
- Access to **bank accounts**, driving licenses, and other UAE services as resident.
- Real estate: non-GCC Golden Visa holders can own property in designated freehold areas.

## Green Visa (5-Year Residency)

The **Green Visa** introduced by Federal Decree-Law 29/2022 provides a 5-year self-sponsored residency option:

### Eligibility Categories

#### 1. Skilled Employees

- Minimum **AED 15,000/month** salary (employer letter required).
- Educational qualification: bachelor's degree or equivalent.
- Employed in a UAE company (not necessarily under Golden Visa criteria).
- Note: employment is not required — the Green Visa is self-sponsored; employee can change jobs without losing visa status.

#### 2. Self-Employed / Freelancers

- UAE Freelance Permit issued by relevant authority (DET, DIFC Authority, media free zones, etc.).
- Financial solvency: income of AED 360,000+/year from freelance activities.

#### 3. Investors (Small Business)

- Investing in a UAE business with approved structure.

### Green Visa — Key Features

- **5 years**, renewable.
- **Self-sponsored**: employer change does not affect visa status.
- **60-day grace period** after employment ends (without losing visa status).
- Sponsor **spouse and children** (children up to 25; unmarried daughters no age limit).
- **Easier renewal** compared to standard employment visa.

---

## Comparison: Golden vs Green vs Standard

| Feature | Golden Visa | Green Visa | Standard Employer-Visa |
|---|---|---|---|
| Duration | 10 years | 5 years | 2 years |
| Self-sponsored | Yes | Yes | No |
| Employer change | No impact | No impact | Requires new visa |
| Leave UAE duration | Up to 6 months | Up to 6 months | 6 months (cancellation) |
| Family inclusion | Spouse + all children | Spouse + children | Spouse + children (under 18) |
| Minimum salary req. | None (for investor/talent) | AED 15,000/month (employee) | Per employer |
| Real estate ownership | Yes (freehold designated) | Yes (freehold designated) | Yes (freehold designated) |
| Banking / accounts | Full access | Full access | Full access |

## Application Process

### Golden Visa

1. **Determine eligibility category** — investors, talent, student, etc.
2. **Gather supporting documents**:
   - Real estate: title deed + bank/mortgage statement showing AED 2M paid.
   - Investors: trade license + MOA + bank statements.
   - Talent: supporting body endorsement letter + qualifying credentials.
3. **Apply via ICA (Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs and Port Security)** — online portal or accredited service center.
4. **Entry permit issued** (if outside UAE) or status change (if inside UAE).
5. **Medical and Emirates ID** registration.
6. **Visa sticker** in passport + Emirates ID card issued.

Typical processing: **2–8 weeks** from complete application.

### Green Visa

1. Freelancer: obtain freelance permit from relevant authority.
2. Skilled employee: employer letter + qualification certificates.
3. Apply via ICA portal or GDRFA (Dubai) / ICA (Abu Dhabi + other).
4. Medical + Emirates ID.

## Family Sponsorship under Golden/Green Visa

- **Spouse and children**: included in the visa at no sponsorship dependency on an employer.
- **Adult children**: Golden Visa holders may sponsor children regardless of age (unique advantage).
- **Parents**: may be sponsored on visit visas; not directly on the Golden Visa in standard practice.
- **Domestic workers**: Golden Visa holders may sponsor domestic workers with standard domestic worker rules.

---

## Real Estate Golden Visa — Key Nuances

- **Multiple properties**: multiple properties can be aggregated to reach AED 2M threshold (within a single emirate — aggregate rules may vary).
- **Off-plan**: generally acceptable if the paid-up value is AED 2M+; some developers issue certificates for use in Golden Visa applications.
- **Leasehold vs freehold**: leasehold properties in designated areas may qualify; verify with DLD/ADJD.
- **Joint ownership**: each co-owner must independently meet the AED 2M threshold for individual eligibility.

---

## Tax and Financial Considerations

- UAE has **no personal income tax** — Golden/Green Visa holders retain full income.
- UAE-resident status has implications for **home-country tax residency** (183-day rule in most jurisdictions) — clients should seek tax advice in their home country.
- UAE-residency certificates: available from FTA for tax treaty purposes; Golden/Green Visa holders can obtain.

---

## Caveats & Currency

UAE Golden Visa criteria, salary thresholds, and real estate minimum values are subject to change by ministerial decision. The categories and their requirements have been updated multiple times since the Golden Visa was introduced in 2019. Verify current ICA portal requirements, as process improvements are introduced regularly. Some categories (e.g., specific talent fields) require endorsement from authorities that may have their own processing timelines.
