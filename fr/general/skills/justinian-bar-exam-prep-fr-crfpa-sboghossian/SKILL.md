# Justinian — French Bar Prep (CRFPA)

## Scope

The CRFPA exam is the gateway to the French bar (*avocat*). It is administered regionally and consists of three major components before entry into a 18-month EFB (École de Formation du Barreau) training program, which itself concludes with the CAPA (Certificat d'Aptitude à la Profession d'Avocat).

## Exam structure

### Written component 1: Note de synthèse (5 hours)

The note de synthèse is the most distinctive element of the CRFPA and the component that most candidates find hardest to master.

**What it is**: Given a dossier of 20–40 heterogeneous documents (case law, doctrine, legislation, press articles, official reports), the candidate must produce a structured, objective synthesis of ~4–6 pages that faithfully reports the content without personal commentary.

**Key skills**:
- Extract the common thread across disparate sources
- Identify the legal question(s) addressed by the dossier
- Organize the note with a clean plan (typically two parts, two sub-parts each — I.A., I.B., II.A., II.B.)
- Write in clear, neutral, impersonal prose
- No personal opinion; no outside knowledge beyond what is necessary to structure the material

**Common errors**:
- Importing outside knowledge or personal views
- Poorly balanced plan (one part much longer than the other)
- Missing key documents in the synthesis
- Exceeding or drastically under-using the allowed page count
- Poor neutrality of tone

**Justinian drill**: Timed note de synthèse practice (5-hour simulation), followed by evaluation against rubric: exhaustiveness, fidelity to sources, plan quality, prose clarity, neutrality.

### Written component 2: Specialty exam (3 hours)

The candidate chooses a specialty at registration. Options:

| Specialty | Key subjects |
|---|---|
| Droit civil | Obligations (contrats, responsabilité), famille, biens, successions |
| Droit des affaires | Sociétés, procédures collectives, droit bancaire, baux commerciaux |
| Droit pénal | Infractions, procédure pénale, droit pénal spécial |
| Droit social | Contrat de travail, licenciement, relations collectives, sécurité sociale |
| Droit administratif | Contentieux administratif, responsabilité de l'État, marchés publics |
| Droit européen et international | CJUE, CEDH, droit international privé, lex mercatoria |

**Format**: case study or legal consultation (consultation juridique) — applying the law to a fact pattern, structured as a legal advice memo.

**Justinian drill**: Timed specialty exam practice per selected subject; grading against professional-quality rubric.

### Oral component: Grand oral (15 minutes)

The candidate is asked to prepare and deliver an oral argument on a topic relating to the European Convention on Human Rights (ECHR / Convention EDH) or fundamental liberties.

**Typical format**:
- Preparation: 30–45 minutes
- Presentation: ~10 minutes
- Questions by the jury: ~5 minutes

**Key skills**:
- Structured oral argument (problem → rule → application → conclusion)
- Citation of ECHR articles and ECtHR jurisprudence
- Calm, professional delivery under pressure
- Anticipating and responding to jury questions

**Key ECHR provisions frequently tested**:
- Article 5 (liberty and security)
- Article 6 (fair trial)
- Article 8 (private and family life)
- Article 10 (freedom of expression)
- Article 1 Protocol 1 (property protection)

**Justinian roleplay**: Simulated grand oral with timer and jury-question simulation; feedback on structure, citations, and delivery.

## After CRFPA: EFB and CAPA

- 18-month training at the École de Formation du Barreau (EFB Paris or regional equivalent)
- Training includes: civil, criminal, commercial, administrative practice modules; work placement (stage en cabinet)
- CAPA: final bar exam; written + oral components assessing practical competence
- Justinian support for CAPA: legal drafting practice (consultations, conclusions, actes), mock orals, ethics preparation

## Study timeline

| Phase | Duration | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Foundation | Months 1–3 | Note de synthèse technique; specialty law review |
| Practice | Months 3–5 | Timed drills; jurisprudence flashcards; grand oral preparation |
| Final push | Month 6 | Full simulations; weak-area remediation |

## Justinian-specific support

- **Note de synthèse drills**: timed 5-hour simulations with dossier, followed by evaluator feedback
- **Specialty case studies**: practice consultations per specialty, graded to EFB standard
- **Grand oral roleplay**: structured oral argument + jury-question simulation
- **ECHR jurisprudence flashcards**: key ECtHR cases with ratio and ECHR articles
- **CAPA prep**: drafting of professional legal documents (conclusions, actes de procédure)

## Caveats

Bar exam format and subjects may vary by region (Paris CRFPA vs provincial CRFPAs differ in practice). Verify the specific requirements of your regional CFPA before relying on this guide. Law and jurisprudence evolve — always check for recent case law updates.
