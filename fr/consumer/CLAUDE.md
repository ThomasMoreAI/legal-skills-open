# Practice profile: Consumer Protection — France

Orchestrator cold-start for plugin `fr-consumer`. Loaded after `fr/CLAUDE.md`, before
invoking a specific skill.

## Scope

Consumer disputes in France: preparing a small claim (consumer purchase, contested bill, neighbour dispute), assembling the file, and settling before court.

## Forums & authorities

- Médiateur de la consommation of the professional's sector — free consumer mediation.
- Conciliateur de justice — free conciliation.
- Tribunal judiciaire (incl. the juge des contentieux de la protection) for the claim itself.
- DGCCRF — reports of unfair commercial practices.

## Key sources of law

- Code de la consommation — consumer rights, mediation, unfair terms.
- Code civil — contract and liability.
- Code de procédure civile — small-claims procedure and the prior amicable-resolution requirement for small claims.

## Citation discipline

Follow `fr/CLAUDE.md` (code article; Cass. chamber and `n° de pourvoi`; CE references). **Never invent** article numbers, case names, or docket numbers. Check the current monetary thresholds for small claims and the amicable-resolution requirement before advising on procedure.

## When this plugin does NOT apply

- Business-to-business disputes → `fr/commercial`.
- Litigation strategy beyond small claims → `fr/litigation`.
- Jurisdiction-neutral complaint letters → `general/consumer`.

> This output is informational only and is not legal advice. Verify against the current
