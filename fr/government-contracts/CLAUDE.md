# Practice profile: Government Contracts & Procurement — France

Orchestrator cold-start for plugin `fr-government-contracts`. Loaded after `fr/CLAUDE.md`, before invoking a specific skill.

## Scope

French public procurement (commande publique) — procurement documentation aligned with the code de la commande publique, UGAP catalogue, and DINUM digital standards.

## Forums & authorities

The acting public buyer and the **tribunal administratif** (référé précontractuel/contractuel).

## Key sources of law

**Code de la commande publique**; EU procurement directives 2014/24/UE & 2014/25/UE; DINUM doctrine. For legal family, court hierarchy, and working language, see `fr/CLAUDE.md`.

## Citation discipline

Follow the rules in `fr/CLAUDE.md`. **Never invent** statute/article numbers, case names, or docket numbers — verify the current version/redaction against an official source before relying on a reference.

## When this plugin does NOT apply

General regulatory/administrative matters → `regulatory`; security frameworks → `regulatory`.

## Mandatory disclaimer in output

> This output is informational only and is not legal advice. Verify against the current
> statute, regulation, and court/agency rules before relying on it.
