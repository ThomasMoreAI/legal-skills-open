# Droit de rétractation

The French term is **rétractation**. "Widerruf", "Widerrufsbelehrung" and "Rücktritt" are foreign terms and signal a template that was not written for France. Only the model form is called a *formulaire de rétractation*.

## Article L221-18 du Code de la consommation

Status as at 2026-08-05. Version en vigueur depuis le 1er juillet 2016.

« Le consommateur dispose d'un délai de quatorze jours pour exercer son droit de rétractation d'un contrat conclu à distance, à la suite d'un démarchage téléphonique ou hors établissement, sans avoir à motiver sa décision ni à supporter d'autres coûts que ceux prévus aux articles L. 221-23 à L. 221-25.

Le délai mentionné au premier alinéa court à compter du jour :
1° De la conclusion du contrat, pour les contrats de prestation de services et ceux mentionnés à l'article L. 221-4 ;
2° De la réception du bien par le consommateur ou un tiers, autre que le transporteur, désigné par lui, pour les contrats de vente de biens. Pour les contrats conclus hors établissement, le consommateur peut exercer son droit de rétractation à compter de la conclusion du contrat.

Dans le cas d'une commande portant sur plusieurs biens livrés séparément ou dans le cas d'une commande d'un bien composé de lots ou de pièces multiples dont la livraison est échelonnée sur une période définie, le délai court à compter de la réception du dernier bien ou lot ou de la dernière pièce.

Pour les contrats prévoyant la livraison régulière de biens pendant une période définie, le délai court à compter de la réception du premier bien. »

Where the trader has not provided the withdrawal information, the period is extended by twelve months from the expiry of the initial fourteen days (article L221-20).

## Article L221-21 — how the right is exercised

Article L221-21, **version en vigueur depuis le 19 juin 2026** (Légifrance, LEGIARTI000053310520, read 05.08.2026), now has four alinéas. The first two are the old rule; the last two are new and mandatory.

- **Alinéa 1** — the consumer withdraws by sending, before the period in article L221-18 expires, the model form mentioned at 7° de l'article L221-5 or **any other unambiguous statement** expressing the wish to withdraw.
- **Alinéa 2** — the trader **may** allow the consumer to fill in and submit that form or statement online, in which case an accusé de réception on a durable medium must follow **sans délai**. Still an option.
- **Alinéa 3** — « Pour les contrats conclus à distance via interface en ligne, le professionnel doit mettre à disposition une fonctionnalité gratuite permettant l'exercice du droit de rétractation avant l'expiration du délai de l'article L. 221-18. » **Obligation, not option.**
- **Alinéa 4** — a décret fixes how the function is presented and used, guaranteeing « un accès facile, direct et permanent ».

## The fonction de rétractation — resolved

This is France's transposition of **CRD article 11a**, inserted by **Directive (UE) 2023/2673**. It sits in the **Code de la consommation**, not the Code monétaire et financier, and it applies to **all distance contracts concluded through an online interface** — not only financial services — even though the transposing instrument is titled for distance marketing of financial services.

Instruments: **ordonnance n° 2026-2 du 5 janvier 2026** (amending L221-21) and **décret n° 2026-3 du 5 janvier 2026** (article D221-5). Both in force **19.06.2026** (art. 20 I of the décret), the same date as § 356a BGB in Germany and art. 54-bis Codice del Consumo in Italy. A shop selling into DE, FR and IT needs one function, not three — but the label strings differ per language and are prescribed, so they are not free translations.

What D221-5 prescribes, verbatim on the two points that get built wrong:

- The control is identified **lisiblement** by the words « **renoncer au contrat ici** » and must be « affichée sur l'interface en ligne de manière visible ». Not "Contact us", not buried in an FAQ, and permanently reachable per L221-21 al. 4.
- The declaration must let the consumer supply: (a) nom et prénom; (b) des indications détaillées permettant d'identifier le contrat; (c) des indications détaillées concernant le moyen électronique by which the confirmation is to be sent.
- A **separate confirmation step**, identified lisiblement by the words « **confirmer la rétractation** ».
- The trader sends, **dans un délai raisonnable**, an accusé de réception on paper or another durable medium, stating **the content of the declaration and the date and time it was sent**. Log both server-side; the timestamp is the evidence that the 14-day period was met.

## Refund and return

The trader refunds all sums paid, including the standard delivery charge, at the latest within fourteen days of being informed of the withdrawal, using the same means of payment unless the consumer expressly agrees otherwise and without additional cost. The trader may withhold the refund until the goods are recovered or the consumer provides proof of dispatch. The consumer bears the direct cost of return **only if the trader informed them of it beforehand** (articles L221-23 à L221-25).

## Article L221-28 — exceptions

Status as at 2026-08-05. Version en vigueur depuis le 28 mai 2022. Thirteen exceptions, in outline:

1. services fully performed before the end of the period and whose performance began with the consumer's prior express consent and acknowledgement of the loss of the right;
2. goods or services whose price depends on financial-market fluctuations outside the trader's control;
3. goods made to the consumer's specifications or clearly personalised;
4. goods liable to deteriorate or expire rapidly;
5. goods unsealed after delivery which cannot be returned for hygiene or health-protection reasons;
6. goods inseparably mixed with other items after delivery;
7. alcoholic beverages whose delivery is deferred and whose value depends on market fluctuations;
8. urgent maintenance or repair work at the consumer's home specifically requested by them;
9. audio or video recordings or computer software unsealed after delivery;
10. newspapers, periodicals or magazines, except subscriptions;
11. contracts concluded at a public auction;
12. accommodation other than residential, transport of goods, car rental, catering or leisure services provided on a specific date or period;
13. digital content not supplied on a tangible medium, where performance began with the consumer's prior express consent and acknowledgement of the loss of the right.

Points 1 and 13 only work if **both** declarations were obtained before performance began: express prior consent to immediate performance, and acknowledgement that the right of withdrawal is thereby lost. Two separate, non-pre-ticked checkboxes, stored with a timestamp. Inventing restrictions the law does not contain — "unopened only", "original packaging only", "no returns on sale items" — is an unfair term and a common defect.

## Formulaire type de rétractation

Mandated by the **annexe à l'article R221-1 du Code de la consommation**, in force since 28 May 2022 (décret n° 2022-424 du 25 mars 2022). It must be supplied with the precontractual information, in a form the consumer can save and print.

## Checkpoints

- [ ] The word used is « rétractation », never « Widerruf » or « rétraction »
- [ ] Fourteen-day period, with the correct starting point per contract type (art. L221-18)
- [ ] Trader's postal address, email and telephone appear in the notice
- [ ] Model form from the annexe à l'article R221-1 supplied, saveable and printable
- [ ] Return costs stated where the consumer is to bear them, otherwise the trader bears them
- [ ] Refund deadline of fourteen days stated, same means of payment
- [ ] Exceptions listed only where they genuinely apply, with no invented restrictions
- [ ] For immediate digital content: two separate, non-pre-ticked declarations, stored with a timestamp (art. L221-28 13°)
- [ ] Withdrawal function: the CRD article 11a question above is raised as open, not answered in either direction
- [ ] Twelve-month extension acknowledged where the information was not given (art. L221-20)
- [ ] All `[[…]]` placeholders resolved or explicitly reported as open
