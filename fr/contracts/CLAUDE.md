# Practice profile: Contracts — France

Orchestrator cold-start for plugin `fr-contracts`. Loaded after `fr/CLAUDE.md`, before
invoking a specific skill.

## Scope

Drafting French contracts, notably freelance and service agreements: essential clauses, general terms of sale, payment and liability terms, and protection of intellectual property.

## Forums & authorities

Tribunal judiciaire or tribunal de commerce depending on the parties; Cour de cassation on points of law.

## Key sources of law

- Code civil — law of contracts (as reformed in 2016).
- Code de commerce — general terms of sale and commercial negotiation between businesses.
- Code de la propriété intellectuelle — assignment and licence of rights.
- Code de la consommation where the client is a consumer.

## Citation discipline

Follow `fr/CLAUDE.md` (code article; Cass. chamber and `n° de pourvoi`; CE references). **Never invent** article numbers, case names, or docket numbers.

## When this plugin does NOT apply

- Invoicing and payment collection → `fr/commercial`.
- Consumer disputes → `fr/consumer`.
- Employment contracts → not covered (employment law).
