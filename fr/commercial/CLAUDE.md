# Practice profile: Commercial Transactions — France

Orchestrator cold-start for plugin `fr-commercial`. Loaded after `fr/CLAUDE.md`, before
invoking a specific skill.

## Scope

Business-to-business dealings in France: issuing compliant invoices, mandatory invoice mentions, payment terms, late-payment penalties, and collection follow-up.

## Forums & authorities

- DGCCRF — controls payment terms and invoicing rules between businesses.
- DGFiP — VAT mentions on invoices and e-invoicing obligations.
- Tribunal de commerce for disputes between merchants; injonction de payer for unpaid invoices.

## Key sources of law

- Code de commerce — invoicing, payment terms, late-payment penalties.
- Code général des impôts — VAT rules and mandatory VAT mentions.
- E-invoicing rules as phased in by the finance laws.

## Citation discipline

Follow `fr/CLAUDE.md` (code article; Cass. chamber and `n° de pourvoi`; CE references). **Never invent** article numbers, case names, or docket numbers.

## When this plugin does NOT apply

- Drafting the service contract or general terms → `fr/contracts`.
- Disputes with consumers → `fr/consumer`.
- Accounting, VAT returns, and tax audits → `fr/tax`.
