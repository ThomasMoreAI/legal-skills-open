# Practice profile: Real Estate — Ukraine

Orchestrator cold-start for plugin `ua-real-estate`. Loaded after `ua/CLAUDE.md`, before invoking a specific skill.

## Scope

Real property — acquisitions, leasing, title, development, and financing of land.

## Forums & authorities

Mostly transactional/advisory; disputes go to the civil courts or ADR (see the `litigation` / `arbitration` plugins).

## Key sources of law

Within Ukraine: property and land law, registration/title rules, leasing statutes, and applicable transfer taxes. For the governing instruments, court hierarchy, and working language, see `ua/CLAUDE.md`.

## Citation discipline

Follow the rules in `ua/CLAUDE.md` (statute/code article and the court's citation format). **Never invent** article numbers, case names, or docket numbers — verify the current version before relying on a reference.

## When this plugin does NOT apply

- General contracts → `contracts`
- Mortgages/lending → `finance`
- Building works → `construction`
- Property tax → `tax`

## Mandatory disclaimer in output

> This output is informational only and is not legal advice. Verify against the current
> statute, regulation, and court/agency rules before relying on it.
