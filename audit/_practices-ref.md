# Practice vocabulary (controlled) — from practices.json

Order = classifier priority (narrow before broad).

- **arbitration** — Arbitration & ADR: arbitration and ADR: mediation, arbitration clauses and institutional rules (AAA/ICC/LCIA/SIAC/HKIAC/SCC/MKAS), recognition and enforcement of awards (New York Convention), investment arbitration; labor arbitration (劳动仲裁) → employment; distinguish from litigation (proceedings in state/national courts) and sports (CAS as a forum — sports disputes go to sports)
- **antitrust** — Antitrust & Competition: antitrust/competition law: monopolization, mergers and merger control, cartels, vertical restraints, RPM/MAP; US (Sherman/Clayton, HSR), EU (Art. 101/102 TFEU, DMA, merger control); distinguish from general regulatory
- **environmental** — Environmental Law: environmental law: EIA, EPA/NEPA, environmental permits, air/water/soil pollution, contaminated land, waste, biodiversity/endangered species, emissions trading/ETS and climate regulation when tied to environmental obligations; distinguish from general regulatory compliance
- **construction** — Construction Law: construction law: construction contracts and works (EPC/AIA/subcontracts), defects and construction disputes, mechanic's liens and their foreclosure, building codes, claims and delays; distinguish from real-estate (transactions/leases/title), commercial (general B2B contracts) and litigation (general procedure)
- **trusts-and-estates** — Trusts & Estates: estate planning and instruments for death/incapacity: wills (will/pour-over), trusts (revocable/ILIT/CRT/SNT), probate and estate administration, fiduciary duties, powers of attorney (durable/healthcare POA), advance directives, guardian nomination for minors within a will/estate plan, elder law (Medicaid planning), forced heirship (Pflichtteil); distinguish from family (divorce/support/family guardianship proceedings) and tax (general tax; inheritance tax within estate planning belongs here)
- **employee-benefits** — Employee Benefits & Executive Compensation: employee benefits and executive compensation: ERISA, 401(k)/pension plans, 409A/280G, deferred comp, equity incentives, change-in-control/severance, bAV/IORP; a QDRO drafted to divide retirement benefits in a divorce → family; distinguish from employment (the employment relationship) and social-security (state benefits)
- **employment** — Employment & Labor: employment and labor law: employment contracts, termination/severance, worker classification, discrimination and harassment (Title VII/EEOC), wage/hour (FLSA), collective/union relations and labor arbitration, HR policies and internal HR investigations; distinguish from employee-benefits (pensions/401(k)/equity comp), white-collar (criminal investigations) and social-security (state benefits)
- **investment-funds** — Investment Funds & Asset Management: investment funds and asset management: PE/VC/hedge, LPA, capital calls, carried interest, GP/LP, side letters, fund subscription, Form ADV; distinguish from corporate (M&A/deals), securities/capital-markets and finance (lending)
- **capital-markets** — Capital Markets: capital markets: securities offering transactions and issuance disclosure — IPO/follow-on, prospectuses, S-1/S-3 shelf, 10-K/10-Q/8-K, underwriting, Reg D/Form D private placements, ATM programs; distinguish from securities (market participants, broker-dealers/investment advisers, market conduct, SEC/FINRA enforcement, ongoing public-company compliance) and corporate
- **securities** — Securities: securities regulation and market conduct: broker-dealers and investment advisers (FINRA/Reg BI), suitability, insider trading, proxy/DEF 14A, transfer agents, SEC/FINRA enforcement, SOX-ICFR and ongoing public-company compliance; a fund manager's Form ADV → investment-funds; distinguish from capital-markets (offering transactions, prospectuses and issuance disclosure S-1/10-K), investment-funds (PE/VC funds) and corporate (deals/governance)
- **bankruptcy** — Bankruptcy & Restructuring: insolvency and restructuring: Chapter 7/11/13, proofs of claim, automatic stay, DIP financing, plans of reorganization and disclosure statements, preference/avoidance actions, cross-border insolvency; European regimes (InsO, restructuring plans/TRLC); distinguish from corporate (solvent deals), litigation (general procedure) and finance (lending outside bankruptcy)
- **ip** — Intellectual Property: intellectual property: patents (applications, prior art, FTO, infringement, claim construction), trademarks (clearance, opposition, TTAB/EUIPO), copyright/DMCA, trade secrets (GeschGehG), IP licensing, open-source licenses; distinguish from tmt (telecom/media infrastructure), entertainment (content production/talent) and contracts (general agreements)
- **tax** — Tax: tax law: income/corporate/property tax, returns and forms (1099/SS-4/Schedules), tax audits and disputes with revenue authorities, tax planning, transfer pricing, R&D credits; national regimes (AADE/KAS/ACD, Russian Tax Code); inheritance tax within estate planning → trusts-and-estates; distinguish from social-security (contributions/state benefits) and nonprofit (501(c)(3) status)
- **real-estate** — Real Estate: real estate law: purchase and sale (PSA/LOI), leases (residential/commercial/NNN/ground lease), ownership and title, mortgage/deed of trust, easements, foreclosure/eviction, development and land use, conveyancing of oil & gas/mineral rights (oil & gas lease, royalty deed); operational oil & gas agreements (JOA/farmout/gathering) → energy; distinguish from construction (construction contracts/defects), finance (lending as such) and litigation (general procedure)
- **personal-injury** — Personal Injury & Torts: torts and compensation for harm: bodily injury, medical malpractice (med-mal), product liability, wrongful death, medical chronology/IME, demand packages; distinguish from litigation (general procedural work), healthcare (HIPAA/clinics/FDA, not case-specific medical-record review) and insurance (coverage and policies)
- **life-sciences** — Life Sciences & Pharma: pharma, medical devices, biotech, diagnostics: product lifecycle and FDA/EMA approval (IND, NDA, 510(k), MDR), clinical trials (GCP/IRB), GxP/GMP, labeling, drug and device promotion/advertising; distinguish from healthcare (providers/clinics, HIPAA, Medicare/Medicaid, reimbursement, Stark/AKS for provider relationships) and regulatory
- **government-contracts** — Government Contracts & Procurement: public procurement and government contracts: FAR/DFARS, GSA Schedule, bid protests, RFP/RFQ, subcontracting plans, OCI; public tenders (Vergaberecht, marché public, michrazim); distinguish from regulatory and corporate
- **sanctions** — Sanctions & Export Controls: sanctions and export controls: OFAC/SDN, EAR/ECCN, ITAR/USML, dual-use, deemed exports, embargoes, denied-party screening of counterparties in the export/trade context; sanctions/PEP screening as part of an AML program (KYC/SAR) → white-collar; distinguish from trade (customs/tariffs/trade remedies) and regulatory
- **trade** — International Trade & Customs: international trade and customs law: customs valuation, tariffs, HTS/CN classification, origin of goods, certificates of origin, antidumping/countervailing duties, safeguards, CBAM, WTO/GATT and trade remedies; if the core issue is OFAC/sanctions/EAR/ITAR/dual-use, choose sanctions; distinguish from regulatory (general compliance) and commercial (contracts)
- **tmt** — Technology, Media & Telecommunications: TMT as the infrastructure of technology, communications and platforms: telecom licensing, spectrum, interconnection, net neutrality, broadcasting, platform regulation (DSA/content liability), advertising claims and media regulation; distinguish from entertainment (production, talent, music/film/creator/content deals), ip (trademarks/patents/copyright), data-protection (personal data) and regulatory
- **maritime** — Maritime & Admiralty: maritime law: admiralty jurisdiction, carriage of goods by sea (COGSA/Hague-Visby), bills of lading, collisions, salvage, charter parties, maritime liens, Jones Act/seafarer injuries (here, not personal-injury), marine insurance; distinguish from transportation (land carriage) and litigation
- **aviation** — Aviation & Aerospace: aviation law: FAA/DOT/TSA, air carriage, air passenger rights (EU261/Reg 261), certification and airworthiness, aircraft leasing, airports; distinguish from transportation, maritime and regulatory
- **transportation** — Transportation & Logistics: land transport and logistics law: road/rail carriage, freight forwarding (CMR/HGB Speditionsrecht), road infrastructure and carrier licensing; distinguish from maritime, aviation and commercial
- **nonprofit** — Nonprofit & Charity: nonprofits/charities: 501(c)(3), Form 1023, charitable solicitation registration, fiscal sponsorship, endowments/UPMIFA, nonprofit governance; European analogues (Gemeinnützigkeit/Vereinsrecht, charity commission, fundacja/stowarzyszenie); distinguish from tax (general tax) and corporate (for-profit entities)
- **healthcare** — Healthcare: healthcare as regulation of providers and care delivery: HIPAA/PHI confidentiality, licensing and compliance of clinics/providers (Stark/AKS, CPOM), managed care and reimbursement (Medicare/Medicaid), patient consent, telehealth, fraud & abuse, audits and corrective action plans; distinguish from life-sciences (drug/device development, FDA/EMA approval, trials, GMP/GxP, labeling/promotion), personal-injury (case-specific medical-record review) and data-protection (general privacy)
- **cybersecurity** — Cybersecurity & Information Security: information security as a practice: NIS2/DORA/CRA, NYDFS 23 NYCRR 500, CMMC, SOC 2 / ISO 27001/27701, ANSSI/EBIOS/PSSI, incident response/operational resilience; distinguish from data-protection (privacy/personal data) and regulatory (general compliance)
- **data-protection** — Data Protection: personal data protection and privacy: GDPR/CCPA/CPRA and national regimes (LGPD/PIPA/APPI/DPDPA/POPIA/KVKK/Roskomnadzor), lawful bases, data subject rights (DSAR), ROPA/DPIA, cross-border transfers, consent/cookies, DPAs and processors, breach notifications; distinguish from cybersecurity (technical infosec NIS2/SOC2/27001), tmt (media/advertising) and regulatory (general compliance)
- **immigration** — Immigration: immigration law: visas and petitions (USCIS), status and residency, asylum, deportation/removal, work and residence permits (pobyt/Aufenthalt), consular procedures for foreign nationals; distinguish from administrative (general administrative procedures for citizens) and employment (the employment relationship)
- **family** — Family Law: family law: divorce/dissolution, custody and support (child/spousal), property division (incl. QDRO dividing retirement benefits in divorce), prenuptial agreements, parenting plans, domestic violence protection (DVRO), paternity, guardianship/conservatorship proceedings over incapacitated adults (Betreuungsverfahren); POA/advance directives and guardian nomination in a will → trusts-and-estates; distinguish from litigation (general procedure)
- **white-collar** — White-Collar & Investigations: business-crime compliance and investigations: FCPA/anti-bribery, AML/SAR/KYC (including sanctions and PEP screening within an AML program), qui tam/False Claims, grand jury, DOJ/DPA, monitorships, Stark/AKS investigations; distinguish from criminal (general criminal defense), sanctions (export controls/OFAC as such), regulatory (compliance programs) and employment (HR investigations)
- **criminal** — Criminal Law: criminal law and defense: charges and pretrial detention (bail), motions (suppress/dismiss), plea bargains, sentencing, appeals/habeas, constitutional safeguards (4th/5th/6th Amendments), penal codes and ordinary offenses; distinguish from white-collar (business/financial crime, FCPA/AML), litigation (civil/administrative procedure) and military (courts-martial/AWOL)
- **corporate** — Corporate: corporate law: entity formation and reorganization, M&A (SPA/APA/mergers), corporate governance (boards, shareholders, charters/bylaws), resolutions, dissolution, company-side VC financing (term sheets/SAFE/Series A); distinguish from commercial (B2B commercial contracts), securities/capital-markets (public offerings/disclosure), investment-funds (the fund itself, GP/LP, LPA, fund formation) and contracts (template agreements)
- **constitutional** — Constitutional & Public Law: constitutional law: the constitution and its application, fundamental rights/freedoms, constitutional review of norms, powers of state bodies, constitutional courts (BVerfG/TK/SCC), proportionality test; distinguish from regulatory, administrative and litigation
- **consumer** — Consumer Protection: consumer protection: consumer contracts and warranties, unfair/deceptive practices (UDAP), consumer-side credit disputes, distance selling, frankowicze/CHF; lender-side credit documents and disclosures (TILA/TRID/Reg Z) → finance; distinguish from contracts (general agreements) and commercial (B2B)
- **energy** — Energy & Utilities: energy law: electricity/gas/heat supply, power markets, energy trading, PPAs, grid and tariff regulation, licensing, renewables, oil & gas operational and service agreements (JOA/farmout/gathering/oilfield MSA), pipelines, LNG and infrastructure (EnWG/EEG/FERC); conveyancing of mineral rights (lease/royalty deed) → real-estate; distinguish from environmental (ecology, emissions, BImSchG permits), regulatory (general compliance) and commercial (contracts)
- **military** — Military & Defence Law: military and defence law: service members' status, pay and social protection, conscription/mobilization, military medical commissions (VLK), military/disciplinary regulations, AWOL/desertion, courts-martial; defense procurement and contracts (DFARS/DD-254) → government-contracts, CMMC → cybersecurity; distinguish from employment (civilian employment), criminal (ordinary criminal cases) and social-security
- **social-security** — Social Security & Welfare: social security and welfare: state pensions, benefits, social payments and disputes with social insurance authorities (ZUS, SGB/Bürgergeld, social insurance courts); distinguish from employment (the employment relationship, occupational pensions/bAV) and tax
- **administrative** — Administrative Procedure & Public Services: administrative procedure and public services: applications/requests to public authorities, passports/ID and consular services for citizens, choosing the request regime, challenging administrative acts (KPA/PPSA/CAS/TsNAP/UdSC); visas, residence permits, asylum/removal and foreign-national status → immigration; distinguish from regulatory (sector compliance) and litigation (court proceedings)
- **sports** — Sports Law: sports law: player contracts, anti-doping (WADA/NADA), CAS disputes, federation regulation, sponsorship; distinguish from litigation, arbitration (CAS as a forum) and commercial
- **gaming** — Gaming & Gambling: gaming law strictly as gambling/iGaming/betting: casino/bookmaker licensing, sports betting, lotteries, online gambling, AML in the gambling industry; do not place video games/esports without gambling stakes here — those usually belong to tmt, entertainment or sports; distinguish from regulatory
- **entertainment** — Media & Entertainment: media and entertainment as deals around content and talent: film/TV/music production, talent/artist contracts, influencer/creator agreements, content licensing, merchandising/endorsement, recording contracts, right of publicity; distinguish from tmt (telecom, platforms, broadcasting and advertising infrastructure), ip (copyright/trademarks as such) and sports
- **regulatory** — Regulatory & Compliance: broad sector regulatory compliance without a more specific practice: agency rules and guidance, licenses and registrations, compliance programs and audits, regulatory monitoring, AI regulation (EU AI Act) and sector-neutral compliance; this is a RESIDUAL bucket — if a narrower practice fits (cybersecurity, data-protection, sanctions, trade, life-sciences, healthcare, energy, government-contracts, white-collar, securities, antitrust, administrative, constitutional), choose it; use regulatory only when no specialized practice applies
- **insurance** — Insurance: insurance law: coverage and policy interpretation (duty to defend/indemnify), claims handling and denial, reservation of rights, subrogation, bad faith and coverage disputes, insurance compliance; distinguish from personal-injury (the injured party's tort claims, incl. policy-limits demands) and litigation (general procedure)
- **finance** — Finance & Banking: banking and lending (lender/bank side): loans and security (UCC Article 9), mortgages and TRID/TILA/Reg Z disclosures, deposit accounts, promissory notes, intercreditor/subordination, banking regulation (GLBA); consumer-side credit/UDAP disputes → consumer; distinguish from securities (capital markets/securities issuance), capital-markets and white-collar (AML/BSA/KYC, SAR — financial crime, not lending)
- **litigation** — Litigation: general court procedure in civil/commercial/administrative matters: pleadings, motions, discovery and depositions, expert reports, appeals, court forms and deadlines, judgments and enforcement, case-law research; if the dispute is subject-specific — choose the subject practice (personal-injury, real-estate, family); distinguish from arbitration (non-court forum) and administrative (administrative procedures outside court)
- **commercial** — Commercial: commercial/business contracts between businesses and trade models: franchising, distribution, supply, agency, services, ad funds, reselling, sales channels and B2B relationships; distinguish from contracts (general drafting/editing technique without a distinct business model) and from sector-specific contracts in ip, real-estate, employment, finance, corporate
- **contracts** — Contracts: general/template contractual instruments and contract drafting/review technique outside any specific sector or business model: NDAs, simple agreements, amendments, bills of sale, contract drafting/review methodology; if the subject matter indicates a B2B model — commercial, IP/licensing — ip, real property — real-estate, the employment relationship — employment, financing — finance, a company/deal — corporate
- **general** — General: outside any specific practice area: cross-cutting legal-operations tools — legal research, citation (Bluebook), intake/triage, document assembly, summarization, translation, billing, knowledge management, citation checking; the broadest RESIDUAL — only when no subject practice fits, nor regulatory/litigation
