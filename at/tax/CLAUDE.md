# Practice profile: Tax — Austria

Orchestrator cold-start for plugin `at-tax`. Loaded after `at/CLAUDE.md`, before invoking a specific skill.

## Scope

Tax skills under the law of Austria. Practice-area definition (`practices.json`, jurisdiction-agnostic): tax law: income/corporate/property tax, returns and forms (1099/SS-4/Schedules), tax audits and disputes with revenue authorities, tax planning, transfer pricing, R&D credits.

## Legal system

See `at/CLAUDE.md` for the legal family, courts, core sources, and language.

## Citation discipline

Follow `at/CLAUDE.md`. **Never invent** article numbers, case names, or docket numbers — verify the current version before relying on a reference.

## When this plugin does NOT apply

- Another area of law → the plugin for that practice area under `at/`.
- Another country's law → that jurisdiction's plugin.
