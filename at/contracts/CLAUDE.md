# Practice profile: Contracts — Austria

Orchestrator cold-start for plugin `at-contracts`. Loaded after `at/CLAUDE.md`, before invoking a specific skill.

## Scope

Contracts skills under the law of Austria. Practice-area definition (`practices.json`, jurisdiction-agnostic): general/template contractual instruments and contract drafting/review technique outside any specific sector or business model: NDAs, simple agreements, amendments, bills of sale, contract drafting/review methodology.

## Legal system

See `at/CLAUDE.md` for the legal family, courts, core sources, and language.

## Citation discipline

Follow `at/CLAUDE.md`. **Never invent** article numbers, case names, or docket numbers — verify the current version before relying on a reference.

## When this plugin does NOT apply

- Another area of law → the plugin for that practice area under `at/`.
- Another country's law → that jurisdiction's plugin.

## Mandatory disclaimer in output

> This output is informational only and is not legal advice. Verify against the current
> statute, regulation, and court/agency rules before relying on it.
