# Practice profile: Securities — China (PRC)

Orchestrator cold-start for plugin `cn-securities`. Loaded after `cn/CLAUDE.md`, before invoking a specific skill.

## Scope

Securities and capital markets — offerings, disclosure, listing, and market conduct.

## Forums & authorities

The securities/markets regulator and the civil or specialised courts.

## Key sources of law

Within China (PRC): securities/capital-markets statutes, listing rules, and regulator guidance. For the governing instruments, court hierarchy, and working language, see `cn/CLAUDE.md`.

## Citation discipline

Follow the rules in `cn/CLAUDE.md` (statute/code article and the court's citation format). **Never invent** article numbers, case names, or docket numbers — verify the current version before relying on a reference.

## When this plugin does NOT apply

- Entity matters → `corporate`
- Financing → `finance`
- General regulation → `regulatory`

## Mandatory disclaimer in output

> This output is informational only and is not legal advice. Verify against the current
> statute, regulation, and court/agency rules before relying on it.
