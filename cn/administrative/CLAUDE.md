# Practice profile: Administrative Procedure & Public Services — China

Orchestrator cold-start for plugin `cn-administrative`. Loaded after `cn/CLAUDE.md`, before invoking a specific skill.

## Scope

Chinese administrative-procedure review — checking administrative (public-security) case documents for procedural compliance across filing, summons, handling outcomes, evidence, and rights protection.

## Forums & authorities

Administrative authorities (public-security organs); administrative reconsideration and the administrative courts.

## Key sources of law

行政处罚法 (Administrative Penalty Law), 行政诉讼法 (Administrative Litigation Law), 行政强制法, and applicable public-security regulations. For legal family, court hierarchy, and working language, see `cn/CLAUDE.md`.

## Citation discipline

Follow the rules in `cn/CLAUDE.md`. **Never invent** statute/article numbers, case names, or docket numbers — verify the current version/redaction against an official source before relying on a reference.

## When this plugin does NOT apply

General regulatory/industry compliance → `regulatory`; criminal procedure → `criminal`.

## Mandatory disclaimer in output

> This output is informational only and is not legal advice. Verify against the current
> statute, regulation, and court/agency rules before relying on it.
