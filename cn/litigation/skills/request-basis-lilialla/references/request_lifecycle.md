# Request Lifecycle

Use this reference whenever a request basis is analyzed in detail. The lifecycle is the spine of request-basis analysis.

## Four Gates

| Gate | Core Question | Typical Contents |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Right arises | Did the right come into existence? | positive elements, legal transaction or statutory trigger, legal-act characterization, formation, effectiveness, right-not-arisen defenses |
| Right not extinguished | Has the right disappeared after arising? | performance, setoff, release, settlement, rescission consequences, merger |
| Right exercisable | Can the right be enforced now? | limitation, simultaneous performance, prior performance, insecurity defense, agreed conditions, procedural preconditions |
| Defense response | Can defenses be defeated? | counter-defenses, re-counter-defenses, waiver, interruption/suspension, defense no longer available |

## Output Rule

Every request basis selected for detailed analysis must show these gates, even if a gate is short.

Use concise labels:

- `产生`
- `未消灭`
- `可行使`
- `抗辩/反抗辩`

## Practice Rule

In real litigation or arbitration, each gate should connect to proof:

- element fact;
- burden holder;
- available evidence;
- dispute or denial;
- proof gap;
- verification task when law or case-law is unsettled.

## Legal-Act Gate

When a selected request basis depends on a legal act, the `Right arises` gate must not collapse into a conclusion. First identify the legal act model, then use that model to select the formation and effectiveness facts.

Typical triggers:

- contract-based payment, delivery, return, termination, or damages claim;
- reward advertisement or public unilateral promise;
- guaranty, debt joining, settlement, agency authority act, ratification, rescission, termination notice, waiver, or setoff notice;
- company resolution or other organizational act.

Minimum questions:

- What is the act type: unilateral, bilateral, multilateral, resolution, quasi-juristic act, or special statutory construct?
- What formation facts does that type require?
- What effectiveness facts or obstacles are pleaded or suggested by the facts?
- Which party bears the burden for the asserted formation fact, effectiveness obstacle, or defense?

## Failure Patterns

- Jumping from facts directly to conclusion.
- Treating a cause of action as the request basis.
- Listing positive elements but omitting extinguishment and exercisability.
- Listing defenses without locating their lifecycle gate.
- Treating client statements as proof that a gate is satisfied.
- Citing a special rule, such as reward advertisement, while skipping legal-act characterization, formation, and effectiveness.
