# Request Basis Concept

Working definition:

> A request basis is the normative or legal-transaction basis that supports a specific person requesting a specific performance, act, omission, return, or similar relief from a specific counterparty.

Core question:

```text
Who may demand what from whom on what normative/legal-transaction basis?
```

## Not The Same As

| Thing | Why not enough |
| --- | --- |
| Cause of action / 案由 | case classification, not the basis itself |
| Claim / 诉请 | requested result, still needs support |
| Article number | may be main norm, auxiliary norm, defense norm, referral norm, or definition |
| Legal relationship | background, not necessarily a specific demand |
| Element | part of a basis |
| Evidence | proves facts, not itself a basis |
| Case-law view | reference path, not the legal basis itself |

## Five Tests

1. Request-effect test: does the norm/legal act produce a request effect if elements are met?
2. Party-content test: can you state who claims what from whom?
3. Element test: can positive elements and defenses be decomposed?
4. Evidence-mapping test: can element facts and evidence targets be reverse-engineered?
5. Norm-function test: is the cited rule a main norm, auxiliary norm, defense norm, defense-to-defense, referral norm, or definition?

If most answers are missing, say:

```text
目前只能说这是候选案由/候选诉请/候选规范，尚未完整定位请求权基础。
```

## Cause Of Action Limit

In practical cases, do not let one cause of action replace request-basis analysis. Start the substantive analysis from claim goal, party roles, factual relationship, candidate request bases, and rule-obligation groups. For filing, pleading drafting, and case-law retrieval, candidate causes of action may appear early, but they remain procedural/retrieval labels and may be multiple, uncertain, or revised later.

## Contract Point

A valid contract or specific contractual obligation can itself be the request basis for a contract claim. Statutes may provide validity, interpretation, default, defense, or remedy rules; do not force every contract request into a single article number.

## Non-Payment/Non-Performance Example

Wrong:

```text
请求权基础是买卖合同纠纷。
```

Better:

```text
买卖合同纠纷是可能案由。若出卖人请求买受人支付价款，候选请求权基础是有效买卖合同项下的价款支付请求权；还需检视合同成立/生效、付款条件成就、未消灭、可行使及抗辩。
```
