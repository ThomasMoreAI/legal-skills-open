# Exam Mode

Use for 鉴定式案例学习 where facts are given. The goal is legal-application training, not evidence reconstruction or court prediction.

## Workflow

1. Identify claim target: who wants what from whom.
2. List candidate request bases.
3. If a rule-obligation group fits, use it to surface common elements, defenses, and excluded paths.
4. Explain order of analysis.
5. For each main request basis, visibly apply the request lifecycle:
   - right arises: positive elements and right-not-arisen defenses;
   - not extinguished: performance, setoff, release, settlement, etc.;
   - exercisable: limitation, simultaneous performance, prior performance, insecurity defense, etc.;
   - counter-defenses if needed.
6. If the right arises from a legal act, run the legal-act gate before concluding the right arises:
   - characterize the act: unilateral, bilateral, multilateral, resolution, quasi-juristic act, or special statutory construct;
   - explain why that characterization matters for formation;
   - check formation facts: expression, addressee or public statement, offer/acceptance if needed, completion of specified act if relevant, form or delivery if relevant;
   - check effectiveness facts and obstacles: capacity, true intent, illegality, public order, agency, approval, registration, condition or term.
7. State conclusion only after reasoning.
8. If reviewing a student answer, lead with missed bases, skipped elements, weak subsumption, and missed defenses.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent facts outside the case prompt.
- Do not convert a study case into litigation prediction unless asked.
- Do not claim current law has been verified unless MCP/authority search was done.
- Do not cite a special article, such as reward advertisement, and skip legal-act characterization, formation, and effectiveness analysis.
