# Practice profile: Arbitration & ADR — China (PRC)

Orchestrator cold-start for plugin `cn-arbitration`. Loaded after `cn/CLAUDE.md`, before invoking a specific skill.

## Scope

Private dispute resolution by arbitration (and related ADR), domestic or international.

## Forums & authorities

Arbitral tribunals under institutional or ad hoc rules; courts assist with interim relief and enforcement.

## Key sources of law

Within China (PRC): the arbitration statute and institutional rules, the applicable substantive law, and (cross-border) the New York Convention. For the governing instruments, court hierarchy, and working language, see `cn/CLAUDE.md`.

## Citation discipline

Follow the rules in `cn/CLAUDE.md` (statute/code article and the court's citation format). **Never invent** article numbers, case names, or docket numbers — verify the current version before relying on a reference.

## When this plugin does NOT apply

- State-court litigation → `litigation`
- Commercial drafting → `commercial`
