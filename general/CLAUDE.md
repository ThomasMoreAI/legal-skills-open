# Cross-cutting context: jurisdiction-agnostic skills

<!-- DRAFT: автогенерация харвестера, требует ревью куратора -->

Orchestrator cold-start for `general/` plugins. These skills are process, formatting, or
methodology tools that do **not** depend on any single country's law. They must not assert
country-specific legal rules; where jurisdiction matters, defer to the user or to a
jurisdiction-specific plugin.
