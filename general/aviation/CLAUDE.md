# Practice profile: Aviation & Aerospace — General (jurisdiction-agnostic)

Orchestrator cold-start for plugin `general-aviation`. (Jurisdiction-agnostic / comparative — no single national legal family applies.)

## Scope

Air-passenger-rights compensation under EU Regulation 261/2004 — categorising cancellation/long delay and computing Article 7 compensation by great-circle distance.

## Forums & authorities

Airlines; national enforcement bodies and ordinary courts of the passenger's forum.

## Key sources of law

**Regulation (EC) 261/2004** (Art. 5–7), CJEU air-passenger-rights case law; Montreal Convention 1999.

## Citation discipline

**Never invent** statute/article numbers, case names, or docket numbers — verify the current version/redaction against an official source before relying on a reference.

## When this plugin does NOT apply

Jurisdiction-specific aviation regulation → that country's `aviation`; litigation mechanics of a passenger claim → `litigation`.
