# Practice profile: Healthcare — General (jurisdiction-agnostic)

Orchestrator cold-start for plugin `general-healthcare`. Loaded after `general/CLAUDE.md`, before invoking a specific skill.

## Scope

Health-sector law — provider regulation, licensing, and patient-data compliance.

## Forums & authorities

Health-sector regulators and professional bodies; civil courts for malpractice.

## Key sources of law

Within General (jurisdiction-agnostic): health-sector regulation, professional-licensing rules, and patient-data/privacy law. For the governing instruments, court hierarchy, and working language, see `general/CLAUDE.md`.

## Citation discipline

Follow the rules in `general/CLAUDE.md` (statute/code article and the court's citation format). **Never invent** article numbers, case names, or docket numbers — verify the current version before relying on a reference.

## When this plugin does NOT apply

- General regulation → `regulatory`
- Health data specifics → `data-protection`
- Malpractice claims → `litigation`

## Mandatory disclaimer in output

> This output is informational only and is not legal advice. Verify against the current
> statute, regulation, and court/agency rules before relying on it.
