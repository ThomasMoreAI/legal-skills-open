# Connecticut — CTDPA

**Citation form**: Connecticut Data Privacy Act, Conn. Gen. Stat. §§ 42-515 to 42-525. Effective July 1, 2023. Subsequent amendments (notably "Senate Bill 3" in 2023 and further updates) have heightened minor protections. Enforced exclusively by the Connecticut Attorney General.

## Applicability

Conn. Gen. Stat. § 42-516. Applies to persons that conduct business in Connecticut or produce products or services targeted to CT residents and that, during the preceding calendar year:

1. Controlled or processed PD of **at least 100,000** consumers (excluding solely-payment-transaction data); OR
2. Controlled or processed PD of **at least 25,000** consumers AND derived **>25%** of gross revenue from sale of PD.

> The 25% threshold is lower than VA's 50% — a CT business meeting the 25k tier on a smaller share of sale revenue is in scope here while not in VA.

Exemptions: state agencies, non-profits, higher ed, financial institutions (GLBA, entity-level), HIPAA covered entities/BAs (entity-level), employment data, B2B.

## Consumer rights

Conn. Gen. Stat. § 42-518. Access, correct, delete, portability, opt-out of sale / targeted advertising / profiling with legal or similarly significant effects. **Right to appeal** within 60 days.

## Universal opt-out signal

Required effective Jan 1, 2025. Recognized signals include GPC. Must apply to known users, not only devices.

## Sensitive data

Defined at Conn. Gen. Stat. § 42-515(27). Standard categories. **Opt-in consent required.**

## Minor and teen-specific provisions

CT amended its statute in 2023 to add heightened protections for known minors. As to consumers known to be **at least 13 and less than 16 years of age**:
- **Default off** for processing for purposes of targeted advertising, sale, or profiling for legal/significant effects.
- Heightened data minimization for known minors.
- Specific design and notice obligations.

For consumers known to be under 13, COPPA-aligned obligations apply.

## Controller duties

Conn. Gen. Stat. § 42-520.
- Privacy notice with required content.
- Data minimization, purpose limitation.
- Reasonable security.
- Non-discrimination.
- Processor contracts.
- Data protection assessments for high-risk activities (sale, targeted ads, sensitive data, profiling with significant effects).

## Enforcement

AG only. **No private right of action.** Civil penalty up to **$5,000 per willful violation** under CUTPA framework. **Cure period sunset Jan 1, 2025.** AG may seek injunctive and equitable relief.

## CT-specific drafting considerations

1. The 25% revenue threshold catches mid-sized businesses that fall below the VA 50% threshold. Run the test separately.
2. UOOM recognition requirement effective Jan 2025 mirrors CO.
3. **Minor protections are stricter than CA's general framework** — a privacy program for an audience that includes 13–16 year-olds needs CT-specific defaults (off for sale, targeted advertising, profiling).
4. Cure period sunset Jan 2025; AG enforcement now without statutory right to cure.
