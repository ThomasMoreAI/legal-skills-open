# Texas — TDPSA

**Citation form**: Texas Data Privacy and Security Act, Tex. Bus. & Com. Code §§ 541.001 to 541.205. Effective July 1, 2024. Enforced exclusively by the Texas Attorney General.

## Applicability

Tex. Bus. & Com. Code § 541.002. Applies to persons that:

1. Conduct business in Texas or produce products or services consumed by Texas residents; AND
2. Process or engage in the sale of PD; AND
3. Are NOT a small business as defined by the United States Small Business Administration (SBA size standards) — **UNLESS** the entity sells sensitive PD without obtaining consent (in which case the law applies regardless of size).

> **No numerical consumer threshold.** Texas's threshold is structural: SBA size status. The SBA size standards vary by NAICS code (e.g., software publishers: $47M average annual receipts; many service industries: $9M–$22M). Confirm size status by NAICS code before declaring inapplicable.

Exemptions: government; non-profits; higher ed; financial institutions (GLBA, entity-level); HIPAA covered entities/BAs (entity-level); electric utilities; non-profit insurer associations; employment data; B2B.

## Consumer rights

Tex. Bus. & Com. Code § 541.051. Access, correct, delete, portability, opt-out of sale / targeted advertising / profiling with significant effects. **Right to appeal** within 45 days.

## Sensitive data

Defined at § 541.001(31). Standard categories. **Opt-in consent required** for processing.

**Special small-business exception**: a small business that does NOT sell sensitive data is exempt from TDPSA generally, but if it does sell sensitive data, the entity-level small-business exemption is forfeit and the entity is subject to TDPSA in full.

## Universal opt-out signal

Tex. Bus. & Com. Code § 541.055 — controllers must allow consumers to opt out via authenticated opt-out request methods. **TDPSA does not currently mandate UOOM (e.g., GPC) recognition by name** — unlike CO, CT, OR, etc. Confirm against current AG guidance, as Texas's enforcement posture has evolved post-effective-date.

## Controller duties

§ 541.052 et seq.
- Privacy notice (similar to other state baselines).
- **Specific TDPSA notice required** if the controller sells sensitive PD: must disclose at the point of collection or in the privacy policy "NOTICE: We may sell your sensitive personal data." Similar specific notice required if selling biometric or genetic data.
- Reasonable security; data minimization; purpose limitation.
- Processor contracts.
- Data protection assessments for high-risk activities.

## Enforcement

Tex. Bus. & Com. Code § 541.155. AG enforcement only. **30-day cure period** in effect. Civil penalty up to **$7,500 per violation**. **No private right of action.**

The AG has been notably more active under TDPSA than under prior state privacy regimes — see `references/enforcement.md` for current actions. Recent enforcement has emphasized children's privacy under SCOPE Act and biometric privacy under Tex. Bus. & Com. Code Chapter 503.

## TX-specific drafting considerations

1. **No numerical consumer threshold** — applicability depends on SBA size status. Many SMBs that are out of scope in CA/CO/CT may be in scope in TX once they exceed SBA NAICS size standards.
2. **Specific notice text required** for sensitive data sale — verbatim disclosures. Generic privacy policy language will not suffice.
3. UOOM recognition not statutorily mandated by name; confirm posture against AG guidance.
4. **Companion laws**: Texas has separate biometric (Tex. Bus. & Com. Code Ch. 503), data-broker registration, and SCOPE Act for minors. TDPSA does not displace these — they apply independently.
5. AG has been notably aggressive on enforcement; assume scrutiny rather than benign neglect.
