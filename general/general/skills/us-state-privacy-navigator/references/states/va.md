# Virginia — VCDPA

**Citation form**: Virginia Consumer Data Protection Act, Va. Code §§ 59.1-575 to 59.1-585. Effective Jan 1, 2023. Enforced exclusively by the Virginia Attorney General.

## Applicability

Va. Code § 59.1-576. Applies to persons that conduct business in Virginia or produce products or services targeted to Virginia residents and that, during a calendar year, control or process PD of:

1. **At least 100,000** consumers; OR
2. **At least 25,000** consumers AND derive over **50% of gross revenue** from the sale of PD.

Exempts: state agencies; financial institutions covered by GLBA (entity-level); covered entities and BAs under HIPAA (entity-level); non-profits; institutions of higher education. Excludes employees and B2B contacts from "consumer."

**Right to appeal** a denied request. Controller must respond to appeal within 60 days. If denied, must provide consumer with means to contact the AG.

**No universal opt-out signal** recognition required — VA is the outlier among most modern state laws. Controllers may still choose to recognize GPC voluntarily.

## Sensitive data

Va. Code § 59.1-575 (definitions). Includes racial/ethnic origin, religious beliefs, mental/physical health diagnosis, sexual orientation, citizenship/immigration status, genetic/biometric data processed for unique ID, PD of a known child, and precise geolocation.

**Opt-in (consent) required** before processing.

## Controller duties

Va. Code § 59.1-578. Privacy notice; data minimization; purpose limitation; reasonable security; non-discrimination; data protection assessments for high-risk activities (sale; targeted advertising; sensitive data processing; profiling with significant effects; activities that present heightened risk of harm).

## Sale, targeted advertising, profiling

- **Sale**: narrow — monetary consideration only.
- **Targeted advertising**: ads selected based on PD obtained from activities over time and across non-affiliated websites.
- **Profiling**: opt-out only when profiling produces a legal or similarly significant effect on the consumer.

## Enforcement

AG only. **No private right of action.** Civil penalty up to **$7,500 per violation**. **30-day cure period** still in effect. AG may seek injunctive relief and recovery of attorneys' fees.

## VA-specific drafting considerations

1. Narrow "sale" definition means many ad-tech disclosures that constitute "sale" in CA may not constitute "sale" in VA — but they may still constitute "targeted advertising." Disclose and offer opt-outs accordingly.
2. No UOOM duty. A controller may rely on a "Do Not Sell" link / form mechanism. A program built for CA's GPC mandate over-satisfies VA.
3. Cure period remains. AG enforcement to date has emphasized cooperative remediation.
