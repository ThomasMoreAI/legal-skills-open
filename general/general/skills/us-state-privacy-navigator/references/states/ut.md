# Utah — UCPA

**Citation form**: Utah Consumer Privacy Act, Utah Code §§ 13-61-101 to -404. Effective Dec 31, 2023. Enforced exclusively by the Utah Attorney General after referral from the Division of Consumer Protection.

## Applicability

Utah Code § 13-61-102. Applies to controllers/processors that:

1. Conduct business in Utah or produce products or services targeted to Utah consumers; AND
2. Have annual revenue of **$25 million or more**; AND
3. Satisfy ONE of:
   - Control or process PD of **at least 100,000** Utah consumers in a calendar year; OR
   - Derive **over 50% of gross revenue** from sale of PD AND control or process PD of **at least 25,000** Utah consumers.

> The combined revenue + activity test is stricter than most states. Many businesses meeting other states' thresholds will not meet Utah's because the $25M revenue threshold is mandatory in addition to the activity test.

Exemptions: GLBA, HIPAA, FCRA, DPPA, FERPA, COPPA carve-outs; air carriers; non-profits; higher ed; tribes; state agencies. Excludes employees and B2B.

## Consumer rights

Utah Code § 13-61-201. Access, delete, portability, opt-out of sale and targeted advertising. **No correction right.** **No profiling opt-out.** **No appeal right.**

> UCPA is the **weakest rights regime among comprehensive state laws.** Consumers have access, delete, portability, and opt-out only.

## Universal opt-out signal

**Not required.** Controller must offer opt-out via two methods (one of which a consumer can use without an account), but UOOM recognition is not statutorily mandated.

## Sensitive data

Defined at Utah Code § 13-61-101. Standard categories.

**Opt-out structure (notice + ability to opt out)** — *not* opt-in. Utah and Iowa are the only two states using opt-out for sensitive data. The controller must provide clear notice and an opportunity to opt out *before* processing.

## Controller duties

Utah Code § 13-61-302.
- Privacy notice (categories, purposes, sale/share categories of third parties, opt-out mechanism, contact).
- Reasonable security.
- Non-discrimination.
- Processor contracts.
- **No data protection assessment requirement.** UCPA is the only major state law without an assessment duty.

## Enforcement

AG, after a 30-day referral by the Division of Consumer Protection following an opportunity to cure. **30-day cure period in effect.** **No private right of action.** Civil penalty up to **$7,500 per violation**, plus actual damages.

## UT-specific drafting considerations

1. The combined revenue + activity threshold means many SMBs subject to other states are NOT subject to UCPA.
2. Sensitive data opt-out structure is unique. A program built to other states' opt-in standard exceeds UCPA — but a program built only to UCPA will fail in 17 other states.
3. The narrower rights regime means a multi-state program built to satisfy CO/CT/MD will far exceed UCPA. Don't size the program to UT.
4. Cure period remains; AG enforcement to date has been measured.
