# Document Type Library

> Full templates and structural patterns for 12 document types across languages. For quick reference, see the summary tables in the main SKILL.md.

---

## How to Use This File

1. Identify the document type from the source
2. Note the source and target jurisdiction
3. Read the structural pattern for that document type
4. Apply the correct formal opening/closing for the target language (see `legal-language-conventions.md`)
5. Preserve all structural elements during translation

---

## 1. Contract / Agreement (General)

**Structural Elements (must preserve):**
- Title / Document Type
- Parties and Recitals (WHEREAS clauses)
- Definitions Section
- Operative Clauses (numbered)
- Schedules / Annexures (if any)
- Signature Block
- Date and Place

**Common Variants:**
- Framework Agreement (sets up long-term relationship)
- Term Sheet / Letter of Intent (non-binding)
- Sales Agreement, Purchase Agreement, Service Agreement (specific obligations)

**Typical Recital Structure:**
```
WHEREAS Party A is engaged in [business activity];
WHEREAS Party B is engaged in [business activity];
WHEREAS the parties wish to [purpose];
NOW THEREFORE the parties agree as follows:
```

**Definitions Pattern:**
```
"In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:
'Business Day' means [definition];
'the Company' means [definition];
'Effective Date' means [date]."
```

**Operative Clause Pattern:**
```
"1. APPOINTMENT
1.1 The Company hereby appoints [Party] as [role], and [Party] accepts such appointment on the terms and conditions of this Agreement.
1.2 The appointment shall commence on [date] and continue until terminated in accordance with Clause [X]."

"2. OBLIGATIONS
2.1 [Party] shall [obligation].
2.2 [Party] may [permitted action]."
```

**Closing Pattern:**
```
"IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement as of the date first written above.

[PARTY A NAME]
By: ____________________
Name:
Title:
Date:

[PARTY B NAME]
By: ____________________
Name:
Title:
Date:"
```

## 2. Memorandum of Understanding (MoU)

**Structural Elements:**
- Title
- Parties and Date
- Background / Recitals
- Purpose and Scope
- Terms (less detailed than a contract)
- Duration and Termination
- Signatures

**Key Differences from Contract:**
- Generally non-binding (distinguish binding vs non-binding clauses)
- Subject to contract / "This MoU does not constitute a legally binding agreement"
- More general terms, framework for future contracts
- Less formal operative language

**Typical Non-Binding Language:**
```
"This MoU expresses the intentions of the parties and constitutes a statement of understanding. This MoU is not intended to create legally enforceable obligations between the parties."
```

## 3. Affidavit

**Structural Elements:**
- Court/Authority Header
- Title (e.g., "AFFIDAVIT OF [NAME]")
- Deponent Identity (full name, father's name, age, occupation, address)
- SOLEMN DECLARATION opening ("I, [full name], do solemnly affirm...")
- Numbered paragraphs of testimony
- Exhibits (if any) — "I exhibit marked [letter] to be the [document description]"
- Declaration of truth
- Jurat ("Solemnly affirmed/declared before me on [date] at [place]...")
- Signature and Stamp

**Jurat Formulas by Jurisdiction:**

🇬🇧 England & Wales:
```
"Swolemly Affirmed by the said [Name] at [Place] this [Date] day of [Month] 2024.

Before me,
____________________
[Commissioner for Oaths / Notary]"
```

🇮🇳 India:
```
"स्वयं को समक्ष पुष्टि की और हस्ताक्षर किए।
दिनांक: [Date]
स्थान: [Place]

____________________
[न्यायिक अधिकारी / राज्य के नोटरी]"
```

## 4. Power of Attorney (PoA)

**Structural Elements:**
- Title: "POWER OF ATTORNEY"
- Principal Identity (full name, father's name, nationality, address, PAN/passport)
- Recitals (WHEREAS clauses explaining purpose)
- Delegation Clause (defines scope of authority)
- Duration Clause
- Revocation Clause
- Acceptance by Attorney (optional)
- Principal Signature and Date
- Witness Signatures (2 witnesses)
- Notarization / Apostille / Stamp

**Key Language:**
- "hereby appoints" — for appointment
- "with full power to" — for scope grant
- "to act as my true and lawful attorney" — standard phrase
- "irrevocable" if applicable
- "this Power of Attorney shall remain in force until [date / event / revocation]"

## 5. Lease / Tenancy Agreement

**Structural Elements:**
- Title: "LEASE AGREEMENT" or "TENANCY AGREEMENT"
- Parties (Landlord and Tenant full details)
- Recitals / Background
- Definitions
- Property Description (address, boundaries, fixtures)
- Term (start date, end date, duration)
- Rent (amount, due date, payment method, penalty for late payment)
- Security Deposit
- Rights and Obligations of Landlord
- Rights and Obligations of Tenant
- Maintenance and Repairs
- Termination Clauses
- Renewal Terms
- Force Majeure
- Dispute Resolution (arbitration / court)
- Notices
- Schedules (inventory, floor plan)
- Signatures

**Key Clause — Rent Escalation:**
```
"The rent shall be increased by [X]% on each anniversary of the Commencement Date, by giving [30] days' prior written notice to the Tenant."
```

## 6. Court Order

**Structural Elements:**
- Court Header (name, seal, case number)
- Title: "ORDER" or "JUDGMENT"
- Parties
- Case Reference
- Background (summarized facts)
- Issues for Determination
- Findings (numbered)
- Operative Orders (numbered, the actual ruling)
- Compliance Timeline (if applicable)
- Judge's Signature and Date
- Court Seal

**Operative Order Pattern:**
```
"ORDER

1. The Defendant is directed to pay the Plaintiff the sum of [amount] within [30] days of this Order.

2. Costs of this action are awarded to the Plaintiff, to be assessed at [amount / to be agreed].

3. Liberty to apply.

Dated this [Date] day of [Month] 2024.

____________________
[Judge Name]
[Judge Designation]"
```

## 7. Legal Notice

**Structural Elements:**
- Sender's Address
- Date
- Recipient's Address
- Subject Line: "LEGAL NOTICE"
- Notice Number (if applicable)
- Statement of Facts
- Legal Basis
- Demand / Action Required
- Timeline for Response
- Consequences of Non-Compliance
- Signature

**Notice Patterns by Type:**

**Demand Notice (breach of contract):**
```
"TAKE NOTICE that by failing to deliver the goods by [date], you are in breach of the Agreement dated [date]. You are hereby called upon to remedy the breach within [14] days of this Notice, failing which [legal action / cancellation of contract / damages claim] will follow."
```

**Notice to Quit (tenancy):**
```
"TAKE NOTICE that your tenancy over [property address] shall stand terminated on [date] and you are required to vacate the premises and deliver vacant possession thereof on or before the said date. Failure to comply will result in legal proceedings for recovery of possession."
```

## 8. Will / Testament

**Structural Elements:**
- Title: "LAST WILL AND TESTAMENT"
- Testator Identity (full name, father's name, address)
- Recitals ("I, [name], being of sound mind...")
- Revocation of Prior Wills
- Declarations (family details, no undue influence)
- Specific Bequests (legacies to specific persons)
- Residuary Clause (who gets remaining estate)
- Executor Appointment
- Trustee Appointment (if applicable)
- Guardian Appointment (if minor children)
- Testator Signature
- Attestation Clause (witnesses confirm execution)
- Witness Signatures (at least 2)

**Attestation Clause:**
```
"Signed and declared by the above-named Testator as his Last Will and Testament in our presence, who in his presence and in the presence of each other have subscribed our names as witnesses. We declare that the Testator appeared to us to be of sound mind and not acting under duress or undue influence."
```

## 9. Corporate Resolution

**Structural Elements:**
- Company Header (name, CIN, registered office)
- Date and Venue
- Directors Present / Quorum
- Notice of Meeting Confirmation
- Item Number and Subject
- Preamble / Background
- Proposed Resolution (exact wording)
- Rationale
- Operative Part ("It was unanimously resolved that...")
- Authorization granted to specific persons
- Director Signatures
- Certificate of Incumbency (sometimes attached)

**Board Resolution Pattern:**
```
"CERTIFIED TRUE COPY OF THE RESOLUTION PASSED AT THE MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF [COMPANY NAME] HELD ON [DATE] AT [VENUE].

RESOLVED THAT:
1. [Subject] be and is hereby approved.
2. [Authorized Person] is authorized to execute all documents and do all acts necessary to give effect to this resolution.

CERTIFIED TRUE COPY
For [Company Name]

____________________
[Director Name]
Director"
```

## 10. Petition / Application (Civil)

**Structural Elements:**
- Court Header (name, address, case type)
- Title: "PETITION" or "APPLICATION"
- Petitioner Identity (full name, address, interest in matter)
- Respondent Identity
- Jurisdiction Statement
- Relevant Facts (numbered paragraphs)
- Grounds for Relief (numbered)
- Relief Sought (prayer clause)
- List of Documents (exhibits)
- Verification Clause ("I verify that...")
- Advocate's Vakalath (memo of appearance)
- Petitioner Signature
- Date and Place

**Prayer Clause Pattern:**
```
"YOUR LORDSHIP may graciously be pleased to:
(a) [First relief];
(b) [Second relief];
(c) Costs of this petition;
(d) Any other relief this Hon'ble Court deems fit and proper."
```

## 11. Immigration Document

**Structural Elements:**
- Official Header (Immigration Authority name and seal)
- Document Title
- Personal Details (name, DOB, nationality, passport number)
- Purpose of Document
- Grant Details (visa type, validity, conditions)
- Entry/Exit Conditions
- Conditions of Stay
- Employer/Sponsor Details (for work permits)
- Duration
- Official Stamps and Signatures
- QR Code / Hologram verification references

**Key Document Types:**
- Visa (tourist, business, work, student)
- Residence Permit
- Work Permit
- Entry/Exit Stamp
- Certificate of Registration (CRBT)
- No Objection Certificate (NOC)

## 12. IP Assignment Agreement

**Structural Elements:**
- Title: "INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT"
- Parties and Date
- Recitals (background of IP creation)
- Definitions ("Assigned IP", "Work Product", etc.)
- Assignment Clause (operative transfer of rights)
- Consideration (payment amount and terms)
- Representations and Warranties of Assignor
- Indemnity
- Confidentiality (if applicable)
- Further Assurances (obligation to execute further documents)
- Governing Law and Jurisdiction
- Signatures

**Assignment Clause Pattern:**
```
"1. ASSIGNMENT
1.1 For good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which is hereby acknowledged, Assignor hereby sells, assigns, transfers and conveys to Assignee all right, title and interest in and to the Assigned IP, including all intellectual property rights subsisting therein throughout the world.

1.2 Assignor agrees to execute all documents and do all acts necessary to perfect the assignment of the Assigned IP to Assignee."
```
