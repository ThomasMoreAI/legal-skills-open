# Lawyer Brief

Use this skill to organize a legal consultation packet.

## Workflow

1. Summarize facts chronologically.
2. Identify parties, documents, deadlines, money at stake, jurisdiction clues,
   and desired outcome.
3. Separate known facts from assumptions.
4. Draft exact questions for counsel and attach evidence index.

## Output

Return `One-page brief`, `Timeline`, `Documents index`, `Questions for lawyer`,
and `Decision needed`.
