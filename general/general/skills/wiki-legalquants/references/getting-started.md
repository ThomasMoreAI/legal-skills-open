# A first useful Wiki session

1. **Add:** “Add these articles and practice notes to my wiki.” Select trusted
   public or authorised non-matter sources. Review the receipt and source links.
2. **Return in a fresh task:** “What does my wiki say about this topic?” The
   registered wiki opens again; follow a note back to its source.
3. **Improve a draft:** “Compare this draft with my wiki. What am I missing,
   and what in my collection challenges it?” Review the linked omissions,
   counterarguments and conditional alternatives. The comparison saves nothing.
4. **Recover wording:** “Show the approved wording I stored for this issue.”
   Read the exact block, source and approval status. If none exists, the answer
   says so.
5. **Save a lesson deliberately:** “Save the reusable lessons from this
   conversation.” Review what qualifies as reusable knowledge.

Manual use is complete. If you want automation later, ask for “Wiki automation
settings,” choose Selected projects or All projects, and turn on automatic
retrieval. Saving new knowledge remains a deliberate request in a normal task.
