# Title VII: The Constitutional Council
**Articles: 56-63**

## Core Idea
The Constitutional Council is the guardian of the Constitution, ensuring that laws conform to constitutional principles and protecting fundamental rights. It exercises constitutional review, supervises elections and referendums, and rules on certain institutional disputes.

## Frameworks Introduced

### Composition (Article 56)
- **Nine Members**: The Constitutional Council shall consist of nine members. Their term of office shall be nine years and shall not be renewable. The Constitutional Council shall be renewed by one-third every three years. Three of its members shall be appointed by the President of the Republic, three by the President of the National Assembly, and three by the President of the Senate
- **Former Presidents**: Former Presidents of the Republic shall be members of right and for life of the Constitutional Council
- **Appointment Conditions**: The appointments made by each of these authorities may not be challenged. The provisions of this paragraph shall be applicable to the appointment which is to be made within the three months following the date of entry into force of this Constitution

### Powers and Jurisdiction

#### Constitutional Review (Article 61)
- **Abstract Review**: Organic acts, before their promulgation, and the rules of procedure of the Assemblies, before they are put into application, shall be submitted to the Constitutional Council which shall rule on their conformity with the Constitution
- **Concrete Review**: An act may be referred to the Council by the President of the Republic, the Prime Minister, or the President of one or the other Assembly. In the same manner, and under the same conditions, the regulations of the two Assemblies may be referred to the Council before they are put into application
- **60-Day Rule**: The Constitutional Council, in the conditions provided for by an organic act, shall examine the conformity with the Constitution of any law referred to it by the Prime Minister, a President of an Assembly, or sixty Deputies or sixty Senators

#### Electoral Supervision (Article 58-59)
- **Election Supervision**: The Constitutional Council shall supervise the regularity of the operations of the referendum provided for in Articles 11 and 89 below and of the elections to the Presidency of the Republic
- **Proclamations**: It shall examine claims and shall proclaim the results of the ballot
- **Election Disputes**: It alone may rule on the validity of the election of Deputies and Senators

#### Institutional Disputes (Article 57, 58)
- **Powers Disputes**: In the event of a dispute with respect to the elections of the President of the Republic, the Constitutional Council shall make a decision

### Decisions and Effects
- **Decision Authority**: The decisions of the Constitutional Council may not be appealed from. They shall be binding on all public authorities and on all administrative and jurisdictional authorities
- **Publication**: A provision declared inconstitutional may not be promulgated or put into application

### Special Procedure for Emergency Measures (Article 61.2)
- **Emergency Review**: Emergency measures taken by the President under Article 16 shall be referred for the opinion of the Constitutional Council by the President of the National Assembly, the President of the Senate, or sixty Deputies, or sixty Senators

## Mental Models
- Use **Constitutional Council as guardian** — final interpreter of Constitution
- Think of **abstract review as prevention** — catches unconstitutional laws before implementation
- Apply **electoral supervision** — ensures democratic legitimacy
- Use **binding decisions** — all authorities must respect Council rulings

## Anti-patterns
- **Appeal of decisions**: Constitutional Council decisions are final and not subject to appeal
- **Ignoring rulings**: Public authorities must respect Constitutional Council decisions
- **Political influence**: Council members must remain independent and impartial

## Key Takeaways
1. Constitutional Council has 9 members (3 each from President, National Assembly, Senate) + former Presidents
2. Council reviews conformity of laws with Constitution before promulgation
3. Council supervises presidential elections, parliamentary elections, and referendums
4. Council decisions are final, binding, and not subject to appeal
5. Council ensures that emergency measures respect constitutional limits

## Connects To
- **Title II (President)**: Council reviews presidential emergency measures
- **Title VI (Treaties)**: Council reviews treaty conformity with Constitution
- **All constitutional rights**: Council protects fundamental rights through constitutional review
