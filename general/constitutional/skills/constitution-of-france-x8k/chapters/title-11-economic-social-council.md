# Title XI: The Economic and Social Council
**Articles: 69-71**

## Core Idea
The Economic and Social Council is a consultative assembly that advises Parliament and Government on economic, social, and environmental matters. It represents organized civil society and provides expertise on proposed legislation.

## Frameworks Introduced

### Composition (Article 69)
- **Representation**: The Economic and Social Council, whose status is provided for by an organic act, shall consist of representatives of the economic and social categories so designated
- **Appointments**: Its composition and its rules of procedure shall be determined by an organic act

### Functions (Article 70)
- **Advisory Role**: The Economic and Social Council may be consulted by the Government and by the Chairmen of the Assemblies on any legislative, ordinance, or regulatory bills as well as on any problem of an economic, social, or environmental nature
- **Self-Initiation**: It shall give its opinion on such matters as may be referred to it by the Government

### Annual Report (Article 71)
- **Mandatory Consultation**: The Government shall consult the Economic and Social Council on any plan or program bill of an economic, social, or environmental nature
- **Public Report**: The Economic and Social Council shall submit an annual report on its activities to the President of the Republic, the Prime Minister, and the Chairmen of the Assemblies

## Mental Models
- Use **Council as expertise provider** — provides specialized knowledge on economic/social matters
- Think of **organized interests representation** — represents economic and social categories
- Apply **consultative role** — advises but does not decide

## Anti-patterns
- **Binding opinions**: Council opinions are advisory, not binding
- **Political representation**: Council represents organized interests, not political parties
- **Limited scope**: Council deals with economic, social, environmental matters

## Key Takeaways
1. Economic and Social Council represents economic and social categories
2. Council provides advisory opinions on legislative and regulatory matters
3. Government must consult Council on economic, social, or environmental plans/programs
4. Council submits annual report on its activities
5. Composition and procedures determined by organic act

## Connects To
- **Title III (Government)**: Government consults Council
- **Title IV (Parliament)**: Parliament chairs can consult Council
- **Title V (Relations)**: Council advises on matters within legislative domain
