# Title III: The Government
**Articles: 20-23**

## Core Idea
The Government determines and conducts the policy of the Nation, under the direction of the Prime Minister, who is responsible to Parliament. The Government is the executive branch that implements national policy, with the President presiding over the Council of Ministers but the Government remaining collectively responsible to the National Assembly.

## Frameworks Introduced

### Government Composition (Article 20)
- **Collective Body**: The Government shall consist of the Prime Minister and the other Ministers
- **Policy Direction**: The Government shall determine and conduct the policy of the Nation
- **Executive Power**: The Government shall have at its disposal the civil service and the Armed Forces
- **Responsibility**: The Government shall be responsible to Parliament under the conditions and procedures provided for in Articles 49 and 50

### Prime Minister (Article 21)
- **Appointment**: The Prime Minister shall direct the action of the Government
- **Implementation**: He shall be responsible for national defense. He shall ensure the execution of the laws
- **Regulatory Power**: Subject to Article 13, he shall exercise regulatory power and shall appoint to civil and military offices
- **Deputation**: He may delegate certain of his powers to Ministers
- **Substitution**: He shall supplant, in the event of necessity, the President of the Republic as chairman of the meetings of the Council of Ministers by virtue of an express delegation and for a determined agenda

### Council of Ministers (Article 9)
- **Presidency**: The President of the Republic shall preside over the Council of Ministers
- **Agenda**: The agenda shall be determined by the President of the Republic

### Cabinet Meetings
- **Regular Meetings**: The Council of Ministers shall meet regularly
- **Minutes**: Minutes of Council meetings shall be taken

## Key Concepts
- **Policy Direction**: Government sets and implements national policy
- **Collective Responsibility**: Government is collectively responsible to Parliament
- **Prime Minister Leadership**: Prime Minister directs Government action
- **Civil Service**: Government has authority over civil service
- **Armed Forces**: Government has authority over Armed Forces (operational command)
- **Regulatory Power**: Government can issue regulations (decrees and orders)

## Mental Models
- Use **Government as policy executor** — implements political direction
- Think of **Prime Minister as chief executive** — directs Government but under President's authority
- Apply **collective responsibility** — Government succeeds or fails together

## Anti-patterns
- **Individual minister autonomy**: Ministers cannot act against Government policy
- **Government vs Parliament**: Government must maintain Parliament's confidence
- **Ignoring Prime Minister**: President cannot bypass Prime Minister in Government direction

## Key Takeaways
1. Government determines and conducts national policy
2. Prime Minister directs Government action and is responsible to Parliament
3. President presides over Council of Ministers but Government is responsible to Parliament
4. Government has regulatory power and controls civil service and armed forces
5. Collective responsibility means Government acts as a unit
