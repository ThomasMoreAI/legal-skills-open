# Title XIV: Association Agreements
**Article: 88**

## Core Idea
Title XIV provides for association agreements between the French Republic and former colonies or territories, establishing a special relationship that falls short of full integration or independence.

## Frameworks Introduced

### Association Principle (Article 88)
- **Agreements**: The Republic may, by virtue of treaties or agreements, accept the existence of an organization consisting, with it or other States, of communities which will be endowed with the power of decision in regard to the common objectives and the coordination of their economic and social policies
- **Community Membership**: For the implementation of these aims, the competent institutions of the said communities may take decisions which shall be applicable in the internal law of each of the States which are members of the organization
- **Approving Authority**: Only the Republic shall have the power to authorize, by an act, the ratification of agreements providing for the creation of such an organization and the participation of France therein

## Mental Models
- Use **association as intermediate status** — between integration and independence
- Think of **economic coordination** — communities coordinate economic and social policies
- Apply **parliamentary approval** — agreements require parliamentary authorization

## Anti-patterns
- **Loss of sovereignty**: Association does not involve transfer of sovereignty
- **Automatic application**: Decisions require implementation through national law
- **Unilateral action**: France can only join with parliamentary approval

## Key Takeaways
1. Republic can enter association agreements creating decision-making communities
2. Communities coordinate economic and social policies
3. Community decisions apply in internal law of member States
4. French participation requires parliamentary authorization
5. Association maintains French sovereignty while allowing coordination

## Connects To
- **Title VI (Treaties)**: Association agreements are international treaties
- **Title I (Sovereignty)**: Sovereignty is maintained in association relationships
