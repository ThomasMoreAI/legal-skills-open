# Title XV: The European Communities and the European Union
**Articles: 88-1 to 88-4**

## Core Idea
Title XV establishes France's participation in the European integration process, recognizing the transfer of certain competencies to the European Communities and European Union. This title reflects the constitutional adaptation required by European integration.

## Frameworks Introduced

### EU Membership (Article 88-1)
- **Authorization**: The Republic shall participate in the European Communities and in the European Union, as provided for by the Treaty signed in Maastricht on 7 February 1992
- **Competency Transfer**: The law may, under the conditions provided for by the said Treaty, provide for the election by universal direct suffrage of representatives to the European Parliament

### Subsidiarity Principle (Article 88-3)
- **Subsidiarity**: Under the conditions provided for by the Treaty on European Union as it results from the Treaty signed in Lisbon on 13 December 2007, draft European legislative acts may be submitted to the French Parliament or to the Senate by the Government
- **Parliamentary Control**: Each Assembly may adopt a motivated resolution on any draft European legislative act

### Treaty Ratification (Article 88-5)
- **Ratification Procedure**: Any Government bill authorizing the ratification of a treaty relating to the accession of a State to the European Communities and to the European Union shall be submitted to a referendum by the President of the Republic
- **Exception**: By way of derogation from the first paragraph of Article 11, Articles 39 and 49 of the Constitution, the authorization to ratify a treaty which, without being contrary to the Constitution, would have provisions incompatible with it, may be given only after a constitutional revision

## Mental Models
- Use **EU integration as competency sharing** — France shares sovereignty with EU
- Think of **parliamentary participation** — French Parliament involved in EU legislation
- Apply **subsidiarity principle** — decisions at appropriate level (EU vs. national)

## Anti-patterns
- **Sovereignty transfer**: Competency transfer requires constitutional basis
- **Automatic ratification**: EU accession treaties require referendum
- **Constitutional violation**: Treaties incompatible with Constitution require amendment

## Key Takeaways
1. France participates in European Communities and European Union
2. French citizens can elect European Parliament representatives
3. French Parliament can adopt resolutions on draft European legislative acts
4. EU accession treaties require referendum
5. Treaties incompatible with Constitution require constitutional amendment

## Connects To
- **Title VI (Treaties)**: EU treaties are international agreements
- **Title I (Sovereignty)**: Sovereignty is shared with EU
