# NDA Generator (Public Tool)

## What it does

The NDA Generator is Louis's primary free lead-generation tool: a 60-second, no-login form that produces a jurisdiction-aware NDA (Non-Disclosure Agreement) in PDF and DOCX format. It demonstrates the platform's core value — fast, accessible legal document generation — while capturing emails and driving conversion to registered accounts.

Any user — whether a startup founder, a procurement officer, or an individual consultant — can generate a standard NDA without engaging a lawyer for a simple use case.

---

## Inputs (guided form)

### Required

| Field | Options / description |
|---|---|
| NDA type | One-way (unilateral) / Mutual (bilateral) |
| Disclosing party name | Full legal name |
| Receiving party name | Full legal name |
| Purpose of disclosure | Free text (e.g., "evaluation of a potential acquisition," "freelance software development project") — this populates the Purpose clause |
| Term | 1 year / 2 years / 3 years / 5 years / custom |
| Governing law | KSA / UAE (onshore) / DIFC / ADGM / Lebanon / English law / New York law |

### Optional add-ons

| Add-on | Description |
|---|---|
| Tech / software | Adds: software source code, algorithms, and technical specifications as specific categories of confidential information; adds residuals clause disclaimer |
| Financial / investment | Adds: financial projections, cap tables, and investment materials; MNPI (material non-public information) disclaimer |
| Healthcare | Adds: patient data, clinical trial information; references applicable health data laws (HIPAA for US nexus; MOH regulations for UAE; applicable KSA Health regulations) |
| Arabic bilingual | Generates Arabic + English parallel text version (required for UAE and KSA enforcement); Arabic governs in case of conflict unless user selects otherwise |

## Output

- **PDF** — formatted NDA with both parties' signature blocks; watermarked with "Generated by Louis — louis.haqq.ai"
- **DOCX** — editable Word version; watermarked in footer
- **Email capture** — to unlock the "clean" (un-watermarked) version and access variations (jurisdiction alternatives, industry add-ons not selected in the free form)
- **Referral code** — each generated NDA includes a unique referral link in the watermark footer, enabling viral sharing tracking

## Document structure (generated NDA)

The generated NDA follows this standard structure, populated with the user's inputs:

1. **Parties** — full legal names and addresses (users may add addresses or leave blank for the form-generated version)
2. **Recitals** — why the parties are entering into the agreement (populated from the Purpose input)
3. **Definitions** — "Confidential Information" (populated based on the selected add-ons); "Purpose"; "Receiving Party"; "Disclosing Party"
4. **Confidentiality obligations** — the receiving party's obligations: (a) hold in strict confidence; (b) use only for the Purpose; (c) disclose only to authorized personnel on need-to-know basis; (d) protect with the same standard of care as own confidential information (but no less than reasonable care)
5. **Exclusions** — information that is (a) publicly available; (b) independently developed; (c) known prior to disclosure; (d) received from a third party without restriction
6. **Permitted disclosures** — legal obligation / court order exception with notification obligation to disclosing party (where legally permissible)
7. **Term** — populated from the Term input; post-expiry obligations (typically: confidentiality obligations survive for [2 years] after the term)
8. **Return / destruction of information** — on request or at expiry: return or certified destruction of confidential materials
9. **No license** — disclosure does not grant any IP license; explicitly stated to prevent implied license arguments
10. **Governing law and dispute resolution** — populated from the Governing Law input; dispute resolution defaults for each jurisdiction:
    - UAE onshore: Dubai Courts or Abu Dhabi Courts (user selects)
    - DIFC: DIFC Courts
    - ADGM: ADGM Courts
    - KSA: Saudi courts / SADR (Saudi Center for Commercial Arbitration)
    - Lebanon: Lebanese Courts of Competent Jurisdiction
    - English law: English Courts (High Court, Chancery Division)
    - New York: New York courts
11. **General provisions** — entire agreement, amendment in writing, waiver, severability, no obligation to proceed with the principal transaction
12. **Signature blocks** — one per party; date field; witness field (optional; required in some MENA jurisdictions for enforcement)

## Jurisdictional calibration

The generated NDA includes jurisdiction-specific language adjustments:

| Jurisdiction | Adjustment |
|---|---|
| UAE onshore | Arabic bilingual version generated by default if add-on selected; arbitration clause optional; Dubai Law or Abu Dhabi Law selection |
| DIFC | Common-law drafted; references DIFC Contract Law; DIFC Courts jurisdiction |
| ADGM | Common-law drafted; ADGM Courts; references English law as supplementary |
| KSA | Arabic bilingual required for court enforcement; references Saudi Commercial Courts; Sharia law compliance clause ("subject to principles of Islamic Sharia") |
| Lebanon | References Code of Obligations and Contracts; Lebanese courts; LBP / USD fee provisions if applicable |
| English law | Standard UK/English NDA language; UKGDPR data note if personal data may be disclosed |
| New York | US market standard language; Delaware / NY governing law note |

## Behavior rules

- **Do not generate NDAs for illegal purposes.** If the stated Purpose suggests an illegal activity (e.g., "covering up a crime"), decline and explain why.
- **Flag limitations of the free tool.** Add a note at the bottom of every generated NDA: *"This NDA was generated by Louis (louis.haqq.ai) as a starting point. It is not a substitute for legal advice. For high-stakes or complex disclosures, have this agreement reviewed by qualified local counsel."*
- **Arabic bilingual version.** When the KSA or UAE onshore jurisdiction is selected, generate the Arabic version automatically (or prompt the user to select it); single-language English NDAs are not reliably enforceable in local courts in these jurisdictions.
- **No personal data stored for anonymous users.** The party names and NDA content entered by users who do not create an account are not stored after the session.

## Failure modes

| Failure mode | Response |
|---|---|
| User requests a jurisdiction not supported by the form | Generate a generic international NDA with English law as governing law; flag that local counsel review is recommended |
| User enters a purpose that is extremely vague ("general business discussions") | Generate the NDA with the vague purpose but flag in a banner: "A more specific purpose clause provides stronger legal protection — consider narrowing the description." |
| User requests a heavily customized NDA (e.g., trade secret add-on with 10-year term) | Generate the standard version; flag that customizations beyond the form options require the Pro tier or a drafting skill: [[prompt-pack-nda-unilateral]] or [[prompt-pack-nda-mutual]] |
