# Privacy and Terms

Use this skill to prepare inputs for legal pages or lawyer review.

## Workflow

1. Inventory product flows, data collected, accounts, payments, analytics,
   cookies, AI features, support, and integrations.
2. Map data to purpose, storage, sharing, deletion, and user controls.
3. Identify terms topics: acceptable use, billing, refunds, liability, IP,
   termination, support, and disputes.
4. Produce lawyer-ready inputs and open questions.

## Output

Return `Data inventory`, `Policy inputs`, `Terms inputs`, `Processor list`, and
`Lawyer questions`.
