# Practice profile: Transportation & Logistics — General (jurisdiction-agnostic)

Orchestrator cold-start for plugin `general-transportation`. (Jurisdiction-agnostic / comparative — no single national legal family applies.)

## Scope

International road-freight carrier liability — the carrier's liability under the CMR Convention for loss, damage, or delay in cross-border carriage of goods by road.

## Forums & authorities

Ordinary courts of the competent forum under the CMR.

## Key sources of law

**CMR Convention** (Art. 17, 23, 29); national transport law where applicable.

## Citation discipline

**Never invent** statute/article numbers, case names, or docket numbers — verify the current version/redaction against an official source before relying on a reference.

## When this plugin does NOT apply

Maritime carriage → `maritime`; aviation → `aviation`; jurisdiction-specific transport regulation → that country's `transportation`.
