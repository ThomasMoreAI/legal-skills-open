# Practice profile: Arbitration & ADR — Poland

Orchestrator cold-start for plugin `pl-arbitration`. Loaded after `pl/CLAUDE.md`, before invoking a specific skill.

## Scope

Private dispute resolution by arbitration (and related ADR), domestic or international:

- **`applying-new-york-convention`** — recognition and enforcement of foreign arbitral awards, setting-aside (skarga o uchylenie), and opposing such applications: mapping art. V of the 1958 New York Convention to the Polish KPC (recognition/enforcement art. 1214–1215; setting-aside art. 1205–1211), grounds for refusal, public-policy arguments.
- **`fetching-arbitration-rules`** — retrieving current institutional rules (SAKIG przy KIG, Sąd Arbitrażowy Lewiatan, ICC, LCIA, SCC, SIAC, HKIAC, VIAC, UNCITRAL) and the redaction applicable to the date of the arbitration agreement.

## Forums & authorities

Arbitral tribunals under institutional rules (SAKIG, Lewiatan) or ad hoc; the common courts assist with interim relief, setting-aside, and recognition/enforcement (KPC part five, arbitraż).

## Key sources of law

Within Poland: KPC część piąta (sąd polubowny), the institutional rules of the chosen forum, the applicable substantive law, and (cross-border) the 1958 New York Convention. Verify the current redaction of KPC and the institutional rules before relying on them. For the court hierarchy and working language, see `pl/CLAUDE.md`.

## Citation discipline

Follow the rules in `pl/CLAUDE.md`. **Never invent** article numbers, case signatures, or rule provisions — verify the current version before relying on a reference.

## When this plugin does NOT apply

- State-court litigation → `litigation`
- General commercial drafting → `commercial`

## Mandatory disclaimer in output

> This output is informational only and is not legal advice. Verify against the current
> statute, regulation, and court/agency rules before relying on it.
