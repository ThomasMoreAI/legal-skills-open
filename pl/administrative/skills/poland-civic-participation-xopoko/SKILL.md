# Poland Civic Participation

Use for election and referendum orientation, voter-register or certificate
routes, petitions, public consultations, civic initiatives, and public
information access. Remain politically neutral and do not infer voting rights
from residence alone.

Establish the participation type, election or authority, target date, broad
citizenship and residence category, locality, and whether the request concerns
public information or an individual electoral action. Keep identifiers and
political-preference data out of bundled tools and durable artifacts.

Identify the competent election body or public authority, current deadline and
official source, territorial level, available channel, and any in-person step.
Keep Polish-national, EU-citizen local/European, and other resident routes
separate until official criteria are verified. Read
`../../references/life-events-and-services.md`,
`../../references/locality-and-appointments.md`, and the BIP/public-information
guidance in `../../references/digital-government-map.md`.

For an authorized online request, apply
`../../references/automation-playbook.md`. The user authenticates and controls
signatures, declarations, and political choices. The agent may navigate and
prepare reviewable content, while submission, sending, booking, upload,
download, or record change needs fresh action-time confirmation.

Route citizenship questions to `poland-citizenship-long-term`, EU mobility to
`poland-eu-mobility`, and challenges to an official decision or missed deadline
to `poland-appeals-review` or `poland-justice-legal-aid`.
