# Poland Appeals and Administrative Review

Use after an adverse decision, request for supplementation, missed procedural
step, unexplained delay, or question about an official review or complaint
route. First-time procedure planning belongs to the substantive domain skill.

Record only the authority, procedure type, document type, delivery date if
known, stated deadline, requested effect, and locality in bundled plugin tools.
If the user explicitly authorizes review of a selected letter in a caller-owned
tool, inspect only the sections needed for the task and do not persist its
content. Ask the user to preserve the original, envelope or delivery receipt,
attachments, and proof of any prior response in their chosen secure storage.

Verify the issuing authority, current official review route, recipient,
delivery method, and deadline wording from primary official sources. Separate
what the notice states from any inference. If date arithmetic, legal grounds, or
the remedy is uncertain, preserve the deadline and refer promptly to qualified
legal help. Draft only a placeholder issue list or questions; do not provide
individualized pleading, representation, or outcome predictions.

Hands-on portal or correspondence assistance follows
`../../references/automation-playbook.md`. The user authenticates and signs or
attests; sending an appeal, complaint, supplementation, or attachment requires a
fresh visible summary and action-time confirmation. Do not let automation delay
deadline preservation or qualified review.

Route the underlying subject to its owner, such as `poland-stay-residence`,
`poland-employment-rights`, `poland-tax`, or `poland-benefits-support`. Read
`../../references/emergency-and-escalation.md` for urgent escalation.
