# UOKiK Abusive Clause Categories - Complete Reference

The 24 categories of abusive clauses under Art. 385(3) of the Polish Civil Code. Each category represents a specific type of consumer harm that courts have repeatedly sanctioned.

## How to Use This Reference

1. When auditing a contract, map each flagged clause to one or more categories below
2. Use the category number as a filter when searching the UOKiK registry actor (`category` parameter)
3. Compare your clause language against the example patterns - if the intent matches, the clause is at risk even if the wording differs

## Category Reference

### Category 1: General Clause - Art. 385(1) §1
**Polish:** Klauzula generalna art. 385(1) §1
**What makes it abusive:** Any clause that shapes consumer rights contrary to good practice and grossly violates consumer interests. This is the catch-all category for clauses that do not fit neatly into categories 2-24 but are still manifestly unfair.
**Example:** "The Company reserves the right to make changes to these Terms at any time without prior notice to the Customer."
**Top industries:** Telecom, e-commerce, financial services
**Enforcement level:** Very strict - this is the most frequently used category (~1,200+ entries)

### Category 2: Excluding Liability for Personal Injury - pkt 1
**Polish:** Brak odpowiedzialności za szkody osobiste
**What makes it abusive:** Clauses that exclude or limit the business's liability for death or personal injury caused to the consumer.
**Example:** "The Operator shall not be liable for any health consequences resulting from the use of the service."
**Top industries:** Fitness, tourism, medical services, construction
**Enforcement level:** Very strict - courts treat personal injury exclusions as per se abusive

### Category 3: Excluding Liability for Non-Performance - pkt 2
**Polish:** Brak odpowiedzialności za niewykonanie umowy
**What makes it abusive:** Clauses that exclude or limit liability when the business fails to perform or improperly performs the contract.
**Example:** "The Developer shall not be liable for delays in property delivery exceeding 12 months."
**Top industries:** Real estate, construction, IT services, delivery
**Enforcement level:** Strict - especially when combined with no right to withdraw

### Category 4: Preventing Set-Off of Claims - pkt 3
**Polish:** Brak możliwości potrącenia
**What makes it abusive:** Clauses that prevent the consumer from offsetting amounts the business owes them against amounts they owe the business.
**Example:** "The Customer may not set off any claims against the Company against amounts due under this agreement."
**Top industries:** Financial services, utilities, telecom
**Enforcement level:** Moderate

### Category 5: Failure to Allow Review of Terms - pkt 4
**Polish:** Brak zapoznania z postanowieniami
**What makes it abusive:** Clauses binding the consumer to terms they had no real opportunity to review before signing.
**Example:** "By signing this agreement, the Customer confirms having read and accepted all annexes, including those not attached hereto."
**Top industries:** Insurance, banking, real estate
**Enforcement level:** Strict - directly undermines informed consent

### Category 6: Transfer of Rights Without Consent - pkt 5
**Polish:** Przeniesienie praw bez zgody
**What makes it abusive:** Clauses allowing the business to assign its rights or obligations under the contract to a third party without the consumer's consent.
**Example:** "The Company may assign this agreement to any affiliated entity without the Customer's prior approval."
**Top industries:** Telecom, financial services, debt collection
**Enforcement level:** Moderate

### Category 7: Making Contract Conditional on a Promise - pkt 6
**Polish:** Uzależnienie od przyrzeczenia
**What makes it abusive:** Clauses that make the contract's conclusion contingent on the consumer's promise to enter into future contracts.
**Example:** "This promotional rate applies only if the Customer commits to subscribing to the premium plan upon renewal."
**Top industries:** Telecom, subscription services
**Enforcement level:** Moderate

### Category 8: Making Performance Dependent on Business's Will - pkt 7
**Polish:** Uzależnienie od woli przedsiębiorcy
**What makes it abusive:** Clauses that make performance of the business's obligations contingent on conditions that depend solely on the business's discretion.
**Example:** "Delivery dates are approximate and subject to the Seller's operational capacity."
**Top industries:** E-commerce, construction, furniture
**Enforcement level:** Strict - undermines the binding nature of the contract

### Category 9: Right to Binding Interpretation - pkt 8
**Polish:** Prawo do wiążącej interpretacji
**What makes it abusive:** Clauses granting the business the exclusive right to interpret the contract terms.
**Example:** "In case of any dispute regarding the scope of services, the Company's interpretation shall be final."
**Top industries:** Insurance, banking, IT services
**Enforcement level:** Strict

### Category 10: Unilateral Modification Without Cause - pkt 9
**Polish:** Jednostronna zmiana bez przyczyny
**What makes it abusive:** Clauses allowing the business to unilaterally change the contract terms (price, scope, conditions) without a valid reason specified in the contract.
**Example:** "The Provider reserves the right to modify the monthly subscription fee. Continued use of the service constitutes acceptance."
**Top industries:** Telecom, SaaS, utilities, fitness clubs
**Enforcement level:** Very strict - second most common category (~800+ entries)

### Category 11: Unilateral Declaration of Conformity - pkt 10
**Polish:** Stwierdzenie zgodności świadczenia
**What makes it abusive:** Clauses giving the business the sole right to determine whether its own performance conforms to the contract.
**Example:** "The Contractor shall determine whether the delivered work meets the agreed specifications."
**Top industries:** Construction, IT, renovation services
**Enforcement level:** Moderate

### Category 12: No Obligation to Refund - pkt 11
**Polish:** Brak obowiązku zwrotu
**What makes it abusive:** Clauses releasing the business from the obligation to return payments received from the consumer when the business has not performed.
**Example:** "Payments made are non-refundable regardless of whether the service was provided in full."
**Top industries:** Education, tourism, events, fitness
**Enforcement level:** Very strict (~400+ entries)

### Category 13: Loss of Right to Refund - pkt 12
**Polish:** Utrata prawa do zwrotu
**What makes it abusive:** Clauses that cause the consumer to lose the right to demand return of performance if they withdraw or terminate.
**Example:** "Upon cancellation, any advance payments shall be forfeited as liquidated damages."
**Top industries:** Real estate, construction, education
**Enforcement level:** Strict

### Category 14: Depriving the Right to Withdraw - pkt 13
**Polish:** Pozbawienie prawa do rezygnacji
**What makes it abusive:** Clauses that take away or limit the consumer's right to terminate or withdraw from the contract.
**Example:** "The Customer may not terminate this agreement during the initial 24-month commitment period."
**Top industries:** Telecom, fitness, insurance, subscription services
**Enforcement level:** Strict

### Category 15: Termination Without Valid Grounds - pkt 14
**Polish:** Wypowiedzenie bez przyczyn
**What makes it abusive:** Clauses granting the business the right to terminate the contract without a valid reason while the consumer lacks the same right.
**Example:** "The Company may terminate this agreement at any time with 14 days' notice without stating a reason."
**Top industries:** Telecom, SaaS, rental services
**Enforcement level:** Very strict (~600+ entries)

### Category 16: Payment Obligation Upon Withdrawal - pkt 15
**Polish:** Obowiązek zapłaty przy rezygnacji
**What makes it abusive:** Clauses requiring the consumer to pay a fee, penalty, or forfeit a deposit when they exercise their right to withdraw.
**Example:** "Early termination of the contract requires payment of a cancellation fee equal to 3 months' subscription."
**Top industries:** Telecom, fitness, education, real estate
**Enforcement level:** Strict

### Category 17: Disproportionate Penalty Clauses - pkt 16
**Polish:** Wygórowana kara umowna
**What makes it abusive:** Penalty clauses where the amount is grossly disproportionate to the damage caused by the consumer's breach.
**Example:** "Failure to return the equipment within 7 days shall result in a penalty of PLN 5,000 per day of delay."
**Top industries:** Telecom, car rental, equipment leasing
**Enforcement level:** Very strict (~500+ entries)

### Category 18: Auto-Renewal Without Explicit Consent - pkt 17
**Polish:** Przedłużenie przy braku oświadczenia
**What makes it abusive:** Clauses that automatically extend the contract for a definite period because the consumer failed to submit a declaration of non-renewal within an unreasonably short deadline.
**Example:** "The contract shall be automatically renewed for another 12 months unless the Customer submits written notice of non-renewal at least 60 days before expiry."
**Top industries:** Telecom, insurance, fitness, subscription services
**Enforcement level:** Very strict (~350+ entries)

### Category 19: Changing Characteristics of Performance - pkt 18
**Polish:** Zmiana cech świadczenia
**What makes it abusive:** Clauses allowing the business to change essential features of the product or service after the contract is concluded.
**Example:** "The Developer reserves the right to modify the floor plan and finishing materials used in the apartment."
**Top industries:** Real estate, construction, furniture, custom manufacturing
**Enforcement level:** Strict

### Category 20: Setting Price After Contract Conclusion - pkt 19
**Polish:** Ustalenie ceny po zawarciu
**What makes it abusive:** Clauses that allow the final price to be determined after the contract is signed, without giving the consumer the right to withdraw.
**Example:** "The final price shall be calculated based on the exchange rate on the date of delivery."
**Top industries:** Real estate, import/export, custom services
**Enforcement level:** Strict

### Category 21: Making Liability Conditional - pkt 20
**Polish:** Uzależnienie odpowiedzialności
**What makes it abusive:** Clauses that condition the business's liability on requirements not specified in the contract or that are unreasonably burdensome for the consumer.
**Example:** "Claims shall only be considered if reported in writing within 24 hours and accompanied by photographic evidence."
**Top industries:** Transport, logistics, tourism, e-commerce
**Enforcement level:** Moderate

### Category 22: Obligation to Perform Without Reciprocity - pkt 21
**Polish:** Obowiązek wykonania bez realizacji
**What makes it abusive:** Clauses requiring the consumer to fulfill all obligations even when the business does not fulfill its own.
**Example:** "Full payment is due regardless of whether the Contractor has completed all phases of the project."
**Top industries:** Construction, renovation, IT projects
**Enforcement level:** Strict

### Category 23: Preventing Access to Polish Courts - pkt 22
**Polish:** Uniemożliwienie sądów polskich
**What makes it abusive:** Clauses that force disputes to be resolved by arbitration or foreign courts, or otherwise restrict the consumer's access to Polish courts.
**Example:** "Any disputes shall be resolved exclusively by the arbitration court designated by the Company."
**Top industries:** International services, e-commerce, financial services
**Enforcement level:** Strict

### Category 24: Tying to Unrelated Contract - pkt 23
**Polish:** Uzależnienie od umowy bez związku
**What makes it abusive:** Clauses making the contract conditional on the consumer entering into another contract that has no direct connection to the original one.
**Example:** "The promotional internet rate requires the Customer to also purchase a home insurance policy from our partner."
**Top industries:** Telecom, banking, insurance
**Enforcement level:** Moderate
