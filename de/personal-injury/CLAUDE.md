# Practice profile: Personal Injury & Torts — Germany

Orchestrator cold-start for plugin `de-personal-injury`. Loaded after `de/CLAUDE.md`, before invoking a specific skill.

## Scope

Bodily-injury and accident claims — liability, causation, and damages.

## Forums & authorities

Civil courts; many claims resolve through insurers and settlement.

## Key sources of law

Within Germany: tort/delict law, limitation rules, and damages principles. For the governing instruments, court hierarchy, and working language, see `de/CLAUDE.md`.

## Citation discipline

Follow the rules in `de/CLAUDE.md` (statute/code article and the court's citation format). **Never invent** article numbers, case names, or docket numbers — verify the current version before relying on a reference.

## When this plugin does NOT apply

- Coverage questions → `insurance`
