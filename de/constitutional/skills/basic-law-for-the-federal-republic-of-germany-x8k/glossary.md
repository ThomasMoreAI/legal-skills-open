# Glossary of Terms - Basic Law for the Federal Republic of Germany

## A

**Abstimmungsmehrheit** - Decision majority. The majority required for Bundestag decisions (Article 42(2)).

**Aktive Sterbehilfe** - Active euthanasia. Not explicitly mentioned but implicitly prohibited by Article 2(2) protection of life.

**Allgemeines Gleichheitsprinzip** - General equality principle. Article 3(1) establishes that all persons are equal before the law.

**Amtshaftung** - Official liability. Article 34 establishes state liability for officials' actions.

**Antragsmehrheit** - Application majority. Special majorities required for certain parliamentary actions.

**Asylrecht** - Right to asylum. Article 16a guarantees asylum for politically persecuted persons with specified limitations.

**Aufenthaltsbestimmungsrecht** - Right to determine place of residence. Part of personal freedom under Article 2(2).

**Auslieferungsverbot** - Prohibition of extradition. Article 16(2) prohibits extradition of Germans.

**Ausschuss** - Committee. Parliamentary committees established under Article 45-45c.

---

## B

**Banknotenmonopol** - Banknote monopoly. Federal responsibility under Article 88.

**Beamtenrecht** - Civil service law. Regulated by federal framework legislation.

**Bundesadler** - Federal eagle. Symbol of the Federal Republic (Article 22).

**Bundesanteil** - Federal share. The Federation's portion of shared tax revenues (Article 106).

**Bundesaufsicht** - Federal supervision. Oversight of Länder execution of federal laws (Article 84).

**Bundesbahn** - Federal railway. Historically state-owned, now privatized (Article 87e).

**Bundesbank** - Federal bank. Central bank of Germany, now part of the European System of Central Banks (Article 88).

**Bundesbeteiligungen** - Federal participations. Federal ownership interests in enterprises.

**Bundesflagge** - Federal flag. Black-red-gold (Article 22).

**Bundesgebiet** - Federal territory. The territory of the Federal Republic of Germany.

**Bundesgesetzblatt (BGBI)** - Federal Law Gazette. Official publication for federal laws (Article 82).

**Bundeshaushalt** - Federal budget. Annual budget law (Articles 110-114).

**Bundeskanzler/-in** - Federal Chancellor. Head of the Federal Government (Article 62-69).

**Bundespräsident/-in** - Federal President. Head of state (Articles 54-61).

**Bundesrat** - Federal Council. Representation of Länder governments at the federal level (Articles 50-53).

**Bundesrecht** - Federal law. Laws enacted by the Federation.

**Bundesrepublik Deutschland** - Federal Republic of Germany. Official name of the state.

**Bundesschuld** - Federal debt. Regulated by borrowing limits (Article 115).

**Bundesstaaten** - Federal states. Alternative term for Länder.

**Bundesverfassungsgericht (BVerfG)** - Federal Constitutional Court. Highest court for constitutional matters (Articles 93-94).

**Bundesversammlung** - Federal Convention. Body that elects the Federal President (Article 54).

**Bundesverwaltung** - Federal administration. Direct administration by federal authorities (Article 86-89).

**Bundeswehr** - Armed forces. German military (Article 87a).

---

## C

**Causa-Methode** - Cause method. Method of tax revenue distribution.

---

## D

**Demokratieprinzip** - Democratic principle. One of the fundamental constitutional principles (Article 20(1)).

**Deutscher** - German. Definition under Article 116.

**Dienstleistungsfreiheit** - Freedom to provide services. Part of economic freedoms.

**Direktmandat** - Direct mandate. Directly elected Bundestag seat.

**Disziplinargesetzgebung** - Disciplinary legislation. Regulation of civil service discipline.

**Drittwirkung der Grundrechte** - Horizontal effect of fundamental rights. Application of basic rights between private parties.

---

## E

**Eigentliches Bundesland** - Proper federal state. A Land with full constitutional status.

**Eingriffsverwaltung** - Interfering administration. Administrative action restricting rights.

**Einschränkungsvorbehalt** - Reservation of restriction. Constitutional basis for limiting rights.

**Eisenbahn** - Railway. Federal railways regulated under Article 87e.

**Emergenz** - Emergency. Constitutional emergency provisions (Section Xa).

**Enteignung** - Expropriation. Regulated under Article 14(3).

**Entscheidungsfreiheit** - Decision-making freedom. Part of personal autonomy.

**Erforderlichkeitsklausel** - Necessity clause. Proportionality requirement for restrictions.

**Ergänzungs gesetzgebung** - Supplementary legislation. Framework legislation under concurrent powers.

---

## F

**Fachaufsicht** - Technical supervision. Oversight of proper execution.

**Föderalismus** - Federalism. The constitutional principle of division between Federation and Länder.

**Föderalismusreform** - Federalism reform. Constitutional amendments to federal-state relations.

**Freiheit der Berufsausübung** - Freedom of professional practice. Article 12(1) protects choice and practice of profession.

**Freiheit der Kunst** - Freedom of art. Protected under Article 5(3).

**Freiheit der Meinung** - Freedom of opinion. Article 5(1) first sentence.

**Freiheit der Person** - Personal freedom. Article 2(2) combined with Article 104.

**Freiheit der Presse** - Freedom of the press. Article 5(1) second sentence.

**Freiheit der Religion** - Freedom of religion. Article 4(1) and (2).

**Freiheit der Wissenschaft** - Freedom of science. Article 5(3).

**Freizügigkeit** - Freedom of movement. Article 11.

**Fristenlösung** - Time-limit solution. Approach to balancing rights (e.g., in abortion law).

**Fünfteilungsprinzip** - Five-part division. Structural principle of some constitutional areas.

---

## G

**Gebühren** - Fees. Public charges distinct from taxes.

**Geheimnis der Briefpost** - Secrecy of mail. Part of Article 10 protection.

**Gemeinden** - Municipalities. Local government units with constitutional guarantees (Article 28(2)).

**Gemeinschaftsaufgaben** - Joint tasks. Cooperative ventures between Federation and Länder (Articles 91a-91b).

**Generalermächtigung** - General authorization. Type of legislative delegation.

**Gleichberechtigung** - Equal rights. Gender equality under Article 3(2).

**Gleichheitsgrundsatz** - Equality principle. General principle of Article 3.

**Gliedstaat** - Member state. Constitutional term for Länder.

**Grundeigentum** - Real property. Protected under Article 14.

**Grundgesetz (GG)** - Basic Law. The German constitution.

**Grundrecht** - Fundamental right. Rights guaranteed in Articles 1-19.

**Grundrechtsschranken** - Limits on fundamental rights. Constitutional restrictions on rights.

---

## H

**Haushaltsausschuss** - Budget committee. Bundestag committee for budget matters.

**Haushaltsgesetz** - Budget act. Annual budget law.

**Haushaltsplan** - Budget plan. Government's financial plan.

**Hoheitsrecht** - Sovereign right. State authority.

---

## I

**Immunität** - Immunity. Parliamentary immunity (Article 46).

**Indienstnahme** - Requisition. Taking private property for public use.

**Inkompatibilität** - Incompatibility. Restrictions on holding multiple offices.

---

## J

**Justizgewährungsanspruch** - Right to judicial protection. Derived from Article 19(4).

---

## K

**Kabinett** - Cabinet. The Federal Government (Article 62).

**Kanzlermehrheit** - Chancellor majority. Majority required for Chancellor election (Article 63).

**KCulturelle Angelegenheiten** - Cultural matters. Primarily Land competence.

---

## L

**Länder** - States. The 16 federal states of Germany.

**Landesausschuss** - State committee. Body in some Länder constitutions.

**Landesbehörden** - State authorities. Land administrative organs.

**Landesgebiet** - State territory. Territory of a Land.

**Landesgesetz** - State law. Legislation enacted by a Land.

**Landesgrundgesetz** - State constitution. Constitution of a Land.

**Landeshoheit** - State sovereignty. Residual powers of Länder.

**Landesjustizverwaltung** - State justice administration. Land-level judicial administration.

**Landesparlament** - State parliament. Legislature of a Land.

**Landesrecht** - State law. Legal system of a Land.

**Landesregierung** - State government. Executive of a Land.

**Landtag** - State diet/parliament. Legislative assembly of most Länder.

**Legislativverfahren** - Legislative procedure. Process of law-making (Articles 76-78).

**Leistungsverwaltung** - Benefit administration. Administrative action providing benefits.

---

## M

**Mehrheitswahlrecht** - Majority voting system. Electoral system component.

**Minderheitenschutz** - Minority protection. Constitutional protection of minorities.

**Ministeranklage** - Ministerial accusation. Procedure against ministers (Article 61).

**Mißtrauensvotum** - Vote of no confidence. Constructive vote of no confidence (Article 67).

**Mittelbarer Zwang** - Indirect coercion. Means of enforcement.

---

## N

**Nachhaltigkeit** - Sustainability. Constitutional principle (Article 20a).

**Natürliche Person** - Natural person. Individual human being.

**Nichtigkeitsklage** - Action for nullity. Constitutional court proceeding.

**Notstand** - Emergency. Constitutional emergency state.

**Notstandsverfassung** - Emergency constitution. Section Xa provisions.

---

## O

**Öffentliche Gewalt** - Public authority. Any exercise of state power (Article 19(4)).

**Öffentliche Lasten** - Public burdens. Costs imposed by the state.

**Öffentliche Sicherheit** - Public security. Constitutional value.

**Öffentlicher Dienst** - Public service. Civil service.

**Öffentlichkeitsprinzip** - Principle of publicity. Open court proceedings (Article 169 GVG).

---

## P

**Parteien** - Parties. Political parties (Article 21).

**Parteienprivileg** - Party privilege. Special status of parties.

**Parteifähigkeit** - Party capacity. Legal standing to sue.

**Parlamentsarmee** - Parliament army. Concept of democratic control of armed forces.

**Parlamentsgesetze** - Parliamentary laws. Laws passed by legislatures.

**Passivwahlrecht** - Right to be elected. Part of electoral rights.

**Praktische Konkordanz** - Practical concordance. Method of constitutional interpretation balancing competing rights.

**Präsidialkabinett** - Presidential cabinet. Form of government under Weimar Constitution.

**Prozessfähigkeit** - Capacity to litigate. Legal standing in court.

---

## R

**Rechnungshöfe** - Courts of audit. Financial oversight bodies.

**Rechtsangleichung** - Legal harmonization. Making laws uniform.

**Rechtsauffassungsänderung** - Change in legal interpretation. Can affect precedent.

**Rechtsfähigkeit** - Legal personality. Capacity to hold rights and duties.

**Rechtsgarantie** - Guarantee of rights. Constitutional protection.

**Rechtskraft** - Legal force. Final and binding character of decisions.

**Rechtsordnung** - Legal order. The constitutional and statutory framework.

**Rechtspflege** - Administration of justice. Judicial system.

**Rechtsstaat** - Rule of law state. Constitutional principle (Article 20(3)).

**Rechtsverordnung** - Statutory order. Subsidiary legislation (Article 80).

**Reich** - Empire/Reich. Historical German state, predecessor to the Federal Republic.

**Religionsfreiheit** - Freedom of religion. Article 4.

**Richtlinienkompetenz** - Guideline competence. Chancellor's power to set government policy (Article 65).

---

## S

**Sachliche Zuständigkeit** - Subject-matter jurisdiction. Competence based on the matter.

**Schranken-Schranken** - Limits on limits. Constitutional constraints on restricting rights.

**Schutzbereich** - Scope of protection. The ambit of a constitutional right.

**Schwerbehindertenvertretung** - Representation of severely disabled persons. Constitutional protection.

**Selbstverwaltung** - Self-administration. Local government autonomy (Article 28(2)).

**Selbstverwaltungsgarantie** - Guarantee of self-administration. Constitutional protection of local autonomy.

**Sondervermögen** - Special fund. Public assets separate from general budget.

**Sozialstaat** - Social state. Constitutional principle (Article 20(1)).

**Sozialversicherung** - Social insurance. System of social protection.

**Sperrklausel** - Threshold clause. Five percent requirement for Bundestag representation.

**Staatsaufgaben** - State tasks. Public responsibilities.

**Staatsangehörigkeit** - Citizenship/nationality. Article 116.

**Staatsgebiet** - State territory. Territorial scope of state authority.

**Staatsgewalt** - State power. Sovereign authority.

**Staatshaftung** - State liability. Article 34.

**Staatskirchenrecht** - State church law. Regulation of church-state relations (Article 140).

**Staatsleitung** - State leadership. Government direction.

**Staatsorganisation** - State organization. Constitutional structure of the state.

**Staatsorganisationsrecht** - State organizational law. Constitutional law concerning state structure.

**Staatsrecht** - Constitutional law. Legal discipline concerning the state.

**Staatsvolk** - State people. The German people as constitutional subject.

**Stimmrecht** - Voting right. Electoral franchise.

**Störfall** - Disturbance/emergency. Situation requiring special measures.

**Subjektiv-öffentliches Recht** - Subjective public right. Individually enforceable claim.

**Subsidiaritätsprinzip** - Subsidiarity principle. Decision-making at the lowest appropriate level.

---

## T

**Tarifautonomie** - Tariff autonomy. Collective bargaining freedom.

**Tatsachenentscheidung** - Decision on facts. Constitutional court's factual determinations.

**Teilhaberechte** - Participatory rights. Rights to state benefits.

**Trennbankensystem** - Separation of banking system. Financial regulation principle.

---

## U

**Unantastbarkeit** - Inviolability. Characteristic of human dignity (Article 1(1)).

**Unionsrecht** - Union law. European Union law and its relationship to German law.

---

## V

**Verfassungsaufrag** - Constitutional mandate. Directives to the state.

**Verfassungsauslegung** - Constitutional interpretation. Methods and principles.

**Verfassungsbeschwerde** - Constitutional complaint. Individual complaint to Federal Constitutional Court.

**Verfassungsgesetz** - Constitutional law. Formal constitutional amendment.

**Verfassungsorgan** - Constitutional organ. Organs established by the Basic Law.

**Verfassungsrecht** - Constitutional law. The body of constitutional norms.

**Verfassungsstreit** - Constitutional dispute. Conflict over constitutional rights and duties.

**Verfassungstext** - Constitutional text. The written Basic Law document.

**Verfahrensgarantien** - Procedural guarantees. Constitutional process protections.

**Verhältnismäßigkeit** - Proportionality. Principle limiting state action.

**Verkehrsfähigkeit** - Capacity to contract. Legal ability to enter agreements.

**Vermögensschaden** - Property damage. Compensable harm.

**Verordnung** - Ordinance. Executive or administrative regulation.

**Verwaltung** - Administration. Executive function of the state.

**Verwaltungsakt** - Administrative act. Individual administrative decision.

**Verwaltungsgerichtsbarkeit** - Administrative jurisdiction. Court system for administrative disputes.

**Verwaltungshelfer** - Administrative assistant. Person performing administrative tasks.

**Verwaltungskompetenzen** - Administrative competences. Distribution of administrative authority.

**Verwaltungsrecht** - Administrative law. Law governing administration.

**Verwaltungsverfahrensgesetz (VwVfG)** - Administrative Procedure Act. Federal framework law.

**Volk** - People. The German people as constitutional sovereign.

**Vollziehende Gewalt** - Executive power. One of the three branches (Article 20(2)).

**Vorbehalt des Gesetzes** - Reservation of law. Principle requiring legal basis for state action.

**Vorfrage** - Preliminary question. Issue referred by one court to another.

**Vormundschaftsgericht** - Guardianship court. Court with family jurisdiction.

---

## W

**Wahlalter** - Voting age. Age requirement for electoral participation.

**Wahlbewerbung** - Candidacy. Right to stand for election.

**Wahlgleichheit** - Electoral equality. Principle of equal voting weight.

**Wahlkreiseinteilung** - Constituency division. Electoral district boundaries.

**Wahlperioden** - Electoral terms. Duration of legislative mandates.

**Wahlprüfung** - Election review. Verification of election results (Article 41).

**Wahlrecht** - Electoral law. Law concerning elections.

**Wahlrechtsgrundsätze** - Principles of electoral law. Articles 38, 28.

**Wahrnehmungsfreiheit** - Freedom of reception. Part of information freedom.

**Wehrverfassung** - Defense constitution. Section Xa emergency provisions.

**Weimarer Reichsverfassung (WRV)** - Weimar Constitution. 1919 German constitution, parts incorporated by Article 140.

**Wesensgehaltgarantie** - Guarantee of essential content. Core of a right that cannot be touched (Article 19(2)).

**Wettbewerbsneutralität** - Competition neutrality. Principle of state economic activity.

**Wiederaufnahmeverfahren** - Reopening of proceedings. Reconsideration of final decisions.

**Willkürverbot** - Prohibition of arbitrariness. Part of equality principle.

---

## Z

**Zentralbank** - Central bank. Bundesbank or European Central Bank.

**Zivilproduktion** - Civilian production. Non-military industrial output.

**Zustimmungsgesetz** - Consent law. Law requiring Bundesrat approval.

**Zwangsmaßnahme** - Coercive measure. State compulsion.

**Zweikammerprinzip** - Bicameral principle. Federal system with Bundestag and Bundesrat.

**Zweckbindung** - Earmarking. Restriction of funds to specific purposes.

**Zweckmäßigkeitserwägungen** - Considerations of expediency. Administrative discretion.

---

## Constitutional Principles

**Demokratie** - Democracy. Rule by the people (Article 20(1)).

**Föderalismus** - Federalism. Division between Federation and Länder (Article 20(1)).

**Rechtsstaat** - Rule of law. Government bound by law (Article 20(3)).

**Republikanismus** - Republicanism. Head of state is elected, not hereditary (Article 20(1)).

**Sozialstaat** - Social state. State responsibility for social justice (Article 20(1)).

**Würde des Menschen** - Human dignity. Highest constitutional value (Article 1(1)).
