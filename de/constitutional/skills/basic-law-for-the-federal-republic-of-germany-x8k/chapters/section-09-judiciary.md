# Section IX: The Judiciary (Articles 92-104)

## Overview
Section IX establishes Germany's judicial system, ensuring judicial independence and defining the structure and jurisdiction of federal and Land courts. This section is foundational to the Rechtsstaat (rule of law) principle embedded in the Basic Law.

---

## Article 92 - Judicial Power
**Organi del potere giurisdizionale**

Judicial power shall be vested in the judges; it shall be exercised by the Federal Constitutional Court, by the federal courts provided for in this Basic Law, and by the courts of the Länder.

---

## Article 93 - Federal Constitutional Court Jurisdiction
**Competenze della Corte costituzionale federale**

1. The Federal Constitutional Court shall decide:
   - On the interpretation of this Basic Law in case of disputes concerning the rights and duties of supreme federal organs
   - In case of disputes or doubts regarding the formal or substantive compatibility of federal or Land law with this Basic Law
   - In case of disputes between the Federation and Länder regarding rights and duties
   - In other public law disputes between the Federation and Länder, between different Länder, or within a Land
   - On constitutional complaints by any person claiming their basic rights have been violated
   - On constitutional complaints by municipalities regarding violations of their self-governance rights
   - In other cases provided for in this Basic Law

2. The Court may also decide whether federal legislation is no longer necessary under Article 72(4).

---

## Article 94 - Federal Constitutional Court Composition
**Composizione della Corte costituzionale federale**

1. The Federal Constitutional Court shall consist of federal judges and other members. Members shall be elected half by the Bundestag and half by the Bundesrat.

2. A federal law shall regulate its organization and procedure, and determine which decisions have the force of law.

---

## Article 95 - Supreme Federal Courts
**Corti supreme della Federazione**

1. The Federation shall establish as supreme courts:
   - Federal Court of Justice (civil and criminal)
   - Federal Administrative Court
   - Federal Finance Court
   - Federal Labor Court
   - Federal Social Court

2. The competent Federal Minister shall decide on the appointment of judges in these courts in agreement with a judicial selection committee.

3. A Joint Senate of these courts may be established to ensure uniform jurisprudence.

---

## Article 96 - Other Federal Courts
**Tribunali federali**

1. The Federation may establish a Federal Patent Court for matters concerning industrial property protection.

2. The Federation may establish military criminal courts as federal courts for the armed forces.

3. The Federal Court of Justice is the court of last resort for courts mentioned in paragraphs 1 and 2.

4. The Federation may establish federal courts for disciplinary proceedings and complaints concerning persons in federal service or public law relationships.

5. Federal law may provide that Land courts exercise federal jurisdiction for serious crimes (genocide, crimes against humanity, war crimes, threats to peaceful coexistence, state security).

---

## Article 97 - Independence of Judges
**Indipendenza dei giudici**

1. Judges shall be independent and subject only to the law.

2. Permanent judges may not be removed from office, suspended, transferred, or retired against their will, except by judicial decision and only for reasons and in the manner provided by law.

3. Special age limits may be set by legislation, upon whose attainment judges appointed for life shall be retired.

---

## Article 98 - Legal Status of Judges
**Stato giuridico dei giudici – Messa in stato d'accusa dei giudici**

1. The legal status of federal judges shall be regulated by a special federal law.

2. If a federal judge violates the principles of this Basic Law or a Land constitution, the Federal Constitutional Court may, upon Bundestag request with a two-thirds majority, transfer the judge to another position or retire them.

3. The legal status of Land judges shall be regulated by special Land laws.

4. In case of willful violations, judges may be removed from office.

---

## Article 99 - Land Constitutional Disputes
**Controversie costituzionali di un Land**

A Land law may assign jurisdiction over Land constitutional disputes to the Federal Constitutional Court and over other Land law matters to the supreme courts mentioned in Article 95.

---

## Article 100 - Concrete Norm Control
**Controllo normativo in concreto**

1. If a court considers a law unconstitutional and its decision depends on that law's validity, the court must refer the matter to the competent constitutional court (Land or Federal).

2. If there is doubt whether a rule of international law forms part of federal law and directly creates rights for individuals, the court must refer the matter to the Federal Constitutional Court.

3. If a Land constitutional court intends to deviate from a Federal Constitutional Court decision, it must refer the matter to the Federal Constitutional Court.

---

## Article 101 - Prohibition of Extraordinary Courts
**Inammissibilità dei tribunali straordinari**

1. No extraordinary courts shall be established. No one may be removed from the jurisdiction of their competent judge.

2. Specialized courts may be established only by law.

---

## Article 102 - Abolition of the Death Penalty
**Abolizione della pena di morte**

The death penalty is abolished.

---

## Article 103 - Fundamental Rights in Court Proceedings
**Diritti fondamentali nei giudizi**

1. Everyone shall be entitled to be heard in court proceedings.

2. An act may be punished only if it was punishable by law at the time it was committed.

3. No one may be punished more than once for the same act under general criminal laws.

---

## Article 104 - Restriction of Liberty
**Restrizione della libertà**

1. Liberty may be restricted only on the basis of a formal law and with observance of its prescribed forms.

2. Only a judge may decide on the permissibility and duration of deprivation of liberty.

3. Anyone provisionally arrested must be brought before a judge no later than the day following the arrest.

4. Relatives or trusted persons must be notified immediately of any judicial decision concerning deprivation of liberty.

---

## Key Principles

- **Judicial Independence**: Core protection for judges from executive or legislative influence
- **Rule of Law (Rechtsstaat)**: Judicial protection of all citizens against state power
- **Constitutional Review**: Federal Constitutional Court as guardian of the constitution
- **Due Process**: Fair trial rights and prohibition of double jeopardy
- **Abolition of Death Penalty**: Absolute protection of human dignity (Article 1)

## Mental Models

- **Judicial Pyramid**: Federal Constitutional Court at apex, federal supreme courts in the middle, Land courts at base, with each level having defined jurisdiction
- **Constitutional Guardianship**: Federal Constitutional Court as "guardian of the constitution" with power to review all state acts
- **Rights Protection Network**: Multiple pathways for citizens to assert constitutional rights - constitutional complaints, concrete norm control, Land constitutional courts
- **Separation of Powers Triangle**: Judiciary as equal branch with executive and legislature, each with distinct roles and mutual checks

## Framework Application

When analyzing German judicial matters:

1. **Identify the legal issue**: Is it constitutional, administrative, civil, criminal, or other?
2. **Determine court jurisdiction**: Which court has authority over this type of dispute?
3. **Check constitutional constraints**: Does the issue involve basic rights (Articles 1-19), federal-state relations, or other constitutional questions?
4. **Analyze remedies**: What procedural avenues exist for the affected party?
5. **Assess judge independence**: Are there any concerns about judicial impartiality or external influence?
