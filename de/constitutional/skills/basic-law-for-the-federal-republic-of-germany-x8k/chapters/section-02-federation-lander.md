# Section II: The Federation and the Länder
**Articles: 20-37**

## Core Idea
Section II establishes Germany as a federal State, defining the constitutional structure of the Federation and the Länder, their relationship, and the fundamental principles that govern the German state. These articles create the framework for German federalism and the rule of law.

## Frameworks Introduced

### Constitutional Principles of the Federal Republic (Article 20)

#### Democratic Principle (Article 20.1-2)
- **Federal Republic**: The Federal Republic of Germany is a democratic and federal State
- **All State Authority from the People**: All state authority is derived from the people
- **Exercise of Authority**: It shall be exercised by the people through elections and other votes and through specific legislative, executive, and judicial organs
- **Eternity Clause**: These principles are protected from amendment (Article 79.3)

#### Rule of Law (Rechtsstaat) (Article 20.3)
- **Binding of Legislature**: The legislature shall be bound by the constitutional order
- **Binding of Executive and Judiciary**: The executive and the judiciary shall be bound by law and justice
- **Separation of Powers**: Implied in the distinction between legislative, executive, and judicial functions

#### Social State Principle (Article 20.1)
- **Social State**: The Federal Republic of Germany is a social State
- **State Obligation**: The State must promote social justice and welfare
- **Implementation**: Through legislation, administration, and social security systems

### Right of Resistance (Article 20.4)
- **Right and Duty**: All Germans shall have the right to resist any person seeking to abolish the constitutional order if no other remedy is available
- **Limitations**: Only against attempts to abolish the constitutional order
- **Subsidiarity**: Only when no other remedy is available

### Protection of Natural Foundations (Article 20a)
- **Environmental Protection**: The State shall protect the natural foundations of life and animals by legislation and, in accordance with law and justice, by executive and judicial action, also in responsibility for future generations
- **Constitutional Value**: Environmental protection is a state objective with constitutional status

### Political Parties (Article 21)
- **Freedom to Form Parties**: Parties shall participate in the formation of the political will of the people
- **Constitutional Requirements**: Their formation and activity must be free and open; their internal organization must conform to democratic principles
- **Right to Equal Opportunities**: Parties must have equal opportunities to participate in the political process
- **Unconstitutional Parties**: Parties that seek to undermine or abolish the free democratic basic order or to endanger the Federal Republic are unconstitutional
- **Constitutional Court Review**: The Federal Constitutional Court decides on the question of unconstitutionality

### State Symbols (Article 22)
- **Capital**: The capital of the Federation is Berlin
- **Flag**: The federal flag shall be black-red-gold
- **Coat of Arms**: The federal coat of arms shall be the Federal Eagle

### European Union (Article 23)
- **EU Participation**: For the realization of a united Europe, the Federal Republic of Germany shall participate in the development of the European Union
- **Transfer of Sovereign Powers**: The Federation may transfer sovereign powers to the European Union by law with the consent of the Bundesrat
- **EU Citizens**: EU citizens have rights equivalent to German citizens regarding elections and access to public office
- **Subsidiarity**: The Federation shall respect the principle of subsidiarity in EU matters
- **Protection of Fundamental Rights**: The Federation shall ensure that EU law respects fundamental rights

### International Law (Article 24-26)
- **Transfer of Sovereignty** (Art. 24): The Federation may by law transfer sovereign powers to international organizations
- **International Peace** (Art. 26): Acts tending to and undertaken with intent to disturb the peaceful relations between peoples or to prepare for a war of aggression shall be unconstitutional
- **International Law Supremacy** (Art. 25): The general rules of international law shall be an integral part of federal law. They shall take precedence over the laws

### Federal Structure

#### Federation and Länder (Article 30)
- **Länder Competencies**: The exercise of state powers and the discharge of state functions shall be the responsibility of the Länder, except as otherwise provided or permitted by this Basic Law
- **Subsidiarity**: Länder have primary responsibility; Federation acts only when Basic Law assigns it competency

#### Länder Constitutions (Article 28)
- **Constitutional Autonomy**: The constitutional order in the Länder must conform to the principles of a republican, democratic, and social State governed by the rule of law
- **Local Self-Government**: Municipalities and associations of municipalities must have the right to regulate all local affairs on their own responsibility
- **Elections**: In the Länder, a representative body must be elected by the people

#### Territorial Organization (Article 29)
- **Territorial Changes**: The territory of the Länder may be redefined to ensure that the Länder are of sufficient size and capacity
- **Procedure**: Requires federal law with consent of Bundesrat and affected Länder; referendums may be required
- **Neugliederung**: Territorial reorganization to create viable Länder

#### Competency Distribution
- **Länder Sovereignty** (Art. 30): Länder have residual competencies
- **Federal Competencies** (Art. 70+): Federation has competencies only where Basic Law assigns them
- **Federal Precedence** (Art. 31): Federal law shall take precedence over Land law

### Administrative Cooperation (Articles 35-37)
- **Legal and Administrative Assistance** (Art. 35): All authorities of the Federation and the Länder shall render each other legal and administrative assistance
- **Federal Supervision** (Art. 37): If a Land fails to fulfill federal obligations, the Federal Government may request Bundesrat authorization to use federal coercion

## Mental Models
- Use **federalism as vertical separation of powers** — powers divided between Federation and Länder
- Think of **subsidiarity principle** — decisions at the lowest appropriate level
- Apply **Länder autonomy** — Länder have primary responsibility unless Federation assigned
- Use **cooperative federalism** — Federation and Länder cooperate in many areas

## Anti-patterns
- **Unitarism**: Federal structure requires respect for Länder autonomy
- **Centralization**: Federation cannot assume Länder competencies without constitutional basis
- **Non-cooperation**: Federation and Länder must cooperate in areas of shared responsibility
- **Discrimination against Länder**: All Länder must be treated equally

## Key Takeaways
1. Germany is a democratic, federal, social, rule of law State
2. All state authority derives from the people
3. Länder have constitutional autonomy and residual competencies
4. Federation has competencies only where Basic Law assigns them
5. Right of resistance protects constitutional order

## Connects To
- **Preamble**: Federal principles implement constitutional unity and diversity
- **Section I (Fundamental Rights)**: Fundamental rights apply to all state levels
- **Section VII (Legislation)**: Competency distribution determines legislative powers
