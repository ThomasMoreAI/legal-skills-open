# Section VII: Federal Legislation (Articles 70-82)

## Overview
Section VII of the Basic Law establishes the framework for legislative authority in the German federal system, defining the division of legislative powers between the Federation (Bund) and the Länder (states). This section is fundamental to understanding Germany's federal structure and the legislative process.

---

## Article 70 - Division of Competences
**Ripartizione delle competenze tra la Federazione e i Länder**

The Länder shall have the right to legislate insofar as this Basic Law does not confer legislative powers on the Federation.

The division of competences between the Federation and the Länder shall be determined by the provisions of this Basic Law concerning exclusive and concurrent legislative powers.

---

## Article 71 - Exclusive Legislative Power of the Federation
**Competenza legislativa esclusiva della Federazione**

In matters within the exclusive legislative power of the Federation, the Länder shall have power to legislate only when and to the extent that they are expressly authorized to do so by a federal law.

---

## Article 72 - Concurrent Legislative Power
**Competenza legislativa concorrente**

1. In the area of concurrent legislative power, the Länder shall have the power to legislate as long as and to the extent that the Federation has not exercised its legislative power.

2. The Federation shall have the right to legislate in the areas of:
   - Civil law, criminal law, court constitution, and judicial procedure
   - Status of persons (civil status)
   - Associations
   - Residence and establishment of foreigners
   - Refugees and expelled persons
   - Public welfare
   - War damage and reparations
   - Burial of war victims
   - Economic law (mining, industry, energy, crafts)
   - Labor law, social security, unemployment insurance
   - Promotion of scientific research
   - Expropriation
   - Transfer of land, natural resources, and means of production
   - Prevention of abuse of economic power
   - Agricultural and forestry production
   - Food security and agricultural markets
   - Land distribution and land reform
   - Water management
   - Admission to higher education institutions
   - Protection against infectious diseases
   - Food, feedstuffs, and consumer goods
   - Maritime and inland navigation
   - Air traffic
   - Railway traffic
   - Road traffic
   - Waste management
   - Air pollution control
   - Noise control
   - State liability
   - Medically assisted reproduction
   - Genetic engineering regulation
   - Transplant law

3. If the Federation exercises its legislative power, the Länder may enact deviating provisions in specific areas (hunting, nature protection, land distribution, water management, university admissions).

4. Federal laws may provide that federal regulations which are no longer necessary may be replaced by Land legislation.

---

## Article 73 - Areas of Exclusive Federal Legislation
**Ambiti della competenza legislativa esclusiva della Federazione**

The Federation shall have exclusive legislative power over:

1. Foreign affairs and defense (including civil defense)
2. Federal citizenship
3. Freedom of movement, passports, identity documents, immigration and emigration, extradition
4. Currency, monetary system, weights and measures, time determination
5. Customs union, commercial treaties, free movement of goods, foreign trade
6. Protection of German cultural property abroad
7. Air transport
8. Railways of federal ownership
9. Postal services and telecommunications
10. Legal status of federal civil servants and public employees
11. Industrial property rights, copyright, publishing rights
12. Combating international terrorism through federal criminal police
13. Statistics for federal purposes
14. Weapons and explosives
15. War victims' pensions and compensation
16. Peaceful use of nuclear energy and radiation protection

---

## Article 74 - Areas of Concurrent Legislation
**Ambiti della competenza legislativa concorrente**

Concurrent legislative power shall extend to:

1. Civil law, criminal law, court constitution, judicial procedure (excluding execution of remand), legal profession, notaries
2. Status of persons
3. Associations
4. Residence and establishment of foreigners
5. Refugees and expelled persons
6. Public welfare (excluding homes)
7. War damage and reparations
8. Burial of war victims and other war casualties
9. Economic law (mining, industry, energy, crafts, trade, banks, stock exchanges, private insurance)
10. Labor law, including workplace regulation and social security
11. Promotion of scientific research
12. Expropriation
13. Transfer of land, natural resources, means of production to common ownership
14. Prevention of abuse of economic power
15. Agricultural and forestry production, food security
16. Land distribution, water management
17. Admission to higher education institutions

---

## Article 76 - Legislative Initiatives
**Progetti di legge**

1. Bills may be introduced in the Bundestag by the Federal Government, by members of the Bundestag, or by the Bundesrat.

2. Bills introduced by the Federal Government must first be submitted to the Bundesrat. The Bundesrat shall have the right to state its opinion within six weeks. This period may be extended to nine weeks for important reasons.

3. Bills introduced by the Bundesrat must be submitted to the Bundestag by the Federal Government within three weeks, accompanied by its opinion.

---

## Article 77 - Legislative Procedure - Mediation Committee
**Procedura legislativa – Commissione di conciliazione**

1. Federal laws shall be adopted by the Bundestag.

2. The Bundesrat may, within three weeks of receiving an adopted bill, demand the convening of a committee composed of Bundestag and Bundesrat members to jointly review the bill.

3. If a bill requires the consent of the Bundesrat, the Bundestag must reconsider if the mediation committee proposes amendments.

4. If a bill does not require Bundesrat consent, the Bundesrat may raise objections within two weeks. The Bundestag may override these objections by a majority of its members (or two-thirds majority if the Bundesrat passed the objection with two-thirds).

---

## Article 78 - Final Adoption of Laws
**Approvazione definitiva delle leggi**

A law adopted by the Bundestag shall be finally adopted if the Bundesrat approves it, does not request mediation, does not raise objections within the time limit, or withdraws its objections, or if the Bundestag overrides the Bundesrat's objections.

---

## Article 79 - Amendment of the Basic Law
**Modifica della Legge fondamentale**

1. This Basic Law may be amended only by a law which expressly amends or supplements its text.

2. Such a law requires a two-thirds majority of the Bundestag members and a two-thirds majority of the Bundesrat votes.

3. Amendments to this Basic Law affecting the division of the Federation into Länder, their participation on principle in legislation, or the principles laid down in Articles 1 and 20, are inadmissible.

---

## Article 80 - Issuance of Statutory Orders
**Emanazione di ordinanze normative**

1. The Federal Government, a Federal Minister, or the Land governments may be authorized by a law to issue statutory orders (Rechtsverordnungen).

2. The content, purpose, and scope of such authorization must be determined in the law.

---

## Article 81 - Legislative State of Emergency
**Stato di emergenza legislativa**

1. If the Bundestag is dissolved under Article 68 and the Federal President declares a legislative state of emergency at the request of the Federal Government, bills rejected by the Bundestag may be enacted with Bundesrat consent.

2. Such laws shall lapse six months after the legislative state of emergency ends.

---

## Article 82 - Promulgation and Entry into Force
**Emanazione – Promulgazione – Entrata in vigore**

1. Laws adopted in accordance with this Basic Law shall be countersigned and promulgated by the Federal President and published in the Federal Law Gazette (Bundesgesetzblatt).

2. Each law and statutory order must specify the date of its entry into force. If no date is specified, they shall enter into force on the fourteenth day following the day of publication in the Federal Law Gazette.

---

## Key Principles

- **Federalism**: Clear division of legislative powers between Federation and Länder
- **Subsidiarity**: Länder legislate unless Federation has explicit power
- **Cooperation**: Bundesrat participation in legislation affecting Länder interests
- **Eternity Clause**: Core constitutional principles (Article 79.3) cannot be amended
- **Legal Certainty**: All laws must be properly promulgated and published

## Mental Models

- **Layered Authority**: Visualize as concentric circles with Federal exclusive powers at the core, concurrent powers in the middle, and Länder residual powers at the periphery
- **Checks and Balances**: Bundesrat as the institutional mechanism for Länder influence on federal legislation
- **Emergency Valve**: Articles 81 and 91a provide mechanisms for exceptional circumstances while maintaining constitutional order
