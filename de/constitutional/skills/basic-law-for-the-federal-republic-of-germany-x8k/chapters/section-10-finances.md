# Section X: Finance (Articles 104a-115)

## Overview
Section X establishes Germany's financial constitution, defining the revenue, expenditure, and financial relations between the Federation and Länder. This section creates a complex system of fiscal federalism that balances autonomy with solidarity.

---

## Article 104a - Expenditure Responsibility and Financial Responsibility
**Competenze di spesa – Finanze – Responsabilità**

1. The Federation and Länder shall each bear the expenses for their own tasks, unless this Basic Law provides otherwise.

2. If the Länder execute tasks on federal commission, the Federation shall bear the resulting expenses.

3. Federal laws that impose expenses on Länder and are executed by them may provide that the Federation bears all or part of the expenses. If the Federation bears half or more, the law shall be executed on federal commission.

4. Federal laws imposing financial obligations on Länder require Bundesrat consent if the resulting tasks must be financed by the Länder.

5. The Federation and Länder shall each bear their own administrative expenses and be reciprocally responsible for orderly administration.

6. The Federation and Länder shall bear the financial burdens arising from Germany's sovereign obligations under international or supranational law, with specific sharing arrangements for EU financial correction measures.

---

## Article 104b - Investment Grants
**Aiuti finanziari per gli investimenti**

1. The Federation may grant financial assistance to Länder and municipalities for particularly important investments to:
   - Prevent disturbances of overall economic equilibrium
   - Equalize different economic capacities within the federal territory
   - Promote economic growth

2. Details shall be regulated by federal law with Bundesrat consent or through administrative agreements based on the budget law.

3. The Bundestag, Federal Government, and Bundesrat must be informed about implementation and improvements achieved.

---

## Article 105 - Division of Tax Legislative Power
**Ripartizione delle competenze nella legislazione tributaria**

1. The Federation shall have exclusive legislative power over customs duties and fiscal monopolies.

2. The Federation shall have concurrent legislative power over other taxes if their revenue accrues to it in whole or in part, or if the conditions of Article 72(2) are met.

3. The Länder shall have legislative power over local excise taxes and luxury taxes, as long as they are not equivalent to federally regulated taxes.

---

## Article 106 - Distribution of Tax Revenues
**Ripartizione delle entrate tributarie e del gettito dei monopoli fiscali**

1. The Federation shall receive the revenue from:
   - Fiscal monopolies
   - Customs duties
   - Excise taxes (except as otherwise provided)
   - Transport taxes
   - Capital transfer taxes
   - Insurance taxes
   - Wealth taxes
   - Supplementary income and corporation taxes
   - EU-related taxes

2. The Länder shall receive the revenue from:
   - Wealth taxes
   - Inheritance taxes
   - Motor vehicle taxes
   - Real property transfer taxes (except as otherwise provided)
   - Beer taxes
   - Gambling taxes

3. Income tax, corporation tax, and value-added tax revenues shall be shared between the Federation and Länder, with distribution principles established by federal law with Bundesrat consent.

4. Value-added tax shares may be redistributed if the revenue-expenditure ratio changes significantly.

5. Municipalities shall receive a share of income tax revenue based on their inhabitants' tax capacity.

6. Municipalities shall receive revenue from property taxes and business taxes, with federal law regulating the sharing mechanisms.

---

## Article 106a - Transport Tax Allocation
**Quota fiscale per il trasporto pubblico di persone**

From January 1, 1996, the Länder shall receive a contribution from federal tax revenues for public passenger transport. Details shall be regulated by federal law with Bundesrat consent.

---

## Article 107 - Fiscal Equalization
**Ripartizione delle entrate fiscali – Perequazione finanziaria fra i Länder**

1. The Länder share of income tax and corporation tax shall accrue to individual Länder according to where the taxes are collected (local revenue principle).

2. Federal law with Bundesrat consent shall ensure appropriate equalization of the different financial capacities of the Länder, taking into account municipal financial needs.

3. Equalization payments between financially strong and weak Länder shall be regulated by law, with the Federation potentially providing supplementary assistance to weaker Länder.

---

## Article 108 - Financial Administration
**Amministrazione finanziaria della Federazione e dei Länder – Giurisdizione finanziaria**

1. Customs duties, fiscal monopolies, and federally regulated consumption taxes shall be administered by federal financial authorities.

2. Other taxes shall be administered by Land financial authorities, with federal law potentially regulating organization and procedures.

3. When Land authorities administer taxes accruing to the Federation, they act on federal commission, with Article 85(3) and (4) applying accordingly.

4. Federal law may provide for cooperation between federal and Land financial authorities.

---

## Article 109 - Budgetary Principles
**Bilancio economico della Federazione e dei Länder**

1. The Federation and Länder shall be autonomous and independent in their budgetary matters.

2. Both levels must consider overall economic equilibrium requirements in their budgets.

3. Federal law with Bundesrat consent may establish fundamental principles for budgets, cyclical fiscal adjustments, and multi-year financial planning.

4. To prevent economic equilibrium disturbances, federal law may prescribe maximum limits, conditions, and timing for borrowing by public entities.

---

## Article 110 - Budget Act
**Bilancio preventivo**

1. All Federation revenue and expenditure must be included in the budget; for federal enterprises and special funds, only expected surplus or deficit need be indicated.

2. The budget shall be established by law for one or more fiscal years, divided into annual periods, before the beginning of the first year of application.

3. The budget law and amendments must be submitted simultaneously to the Bundesrat and Bundestag.

4. The budget law may only contain provisions relating to Federation revenue and expenditure for the established period.

---

## Article 111 - Provisional Budget
**Anticipazione di bilancio**

1. If the budget law is not enacted by the end of a fiscal year, the Federal Government may make expenditures necessary for:
   - Maintaining legally established institutions
   - Fulfilling legal obligations
   - Continuing construction and supplies authorized in previous budgets

2. If tax and other revenues are insufficient, the Government may borrow up to one-quarter of the previous budget's total expenditure to continue financial administration.

---

## Article 112 - Extraordinary Expenditures
**Spese eccedenti e straordinarie**

Extraordinary expenditures beyond budget appropriations and not provided for in the budget law shall require Bundesrat consent.

---

## Article 113 - Expenditure Increases
**Aumenti di spesa**

Laws that increase expenditures proposed by the Federal Government shall require its consent.

---

## Article 114 - Audit and Budget Verification
**Consuntivo – Verifica di bilancio**

1. The Federal Court of Auditors shall examine the Federation's accounts and the legality and regularity of budget execution.

2. The annual audit report shall be submitted to the Bundestag, Bundesrat, and Federal Government.

---

## Article 115 - Credit Limitations
**Limiti del ricorso al credito**

1. The Federation may not assume credit obligations except for exceptional circumstances specified by federal law.

2. Investments that cannot be financed from current revenue must be limited to predictable amounts and interest burdens.

3. Credit for special trust funds and economic enterprises may be permitted by federal law.

---

## Key Principles

- **Fiscal Federalism**: Separation of revenue sources and expenditure responsibilities between Federation and Länder
- **Fiscal Equalization**: Mechanisms to reduce financial disparities between wealthy and poor Länder
- **Financial Autonomy**: Each level of government has independent budgetary authority
- **Solidarity Principle**: Stronger regions support weaker ones through equalization payments
- **Budget Discipline**: Constitutional limits on borrowing and deficit spending
- **Transparency**: Comprehensive audit and reporting requirements

## Mental Models

- **Financial Flow Diagram**: Visualize revenue collection → distribution → expenditure → equalization payments creating a circular system of fiscal federalism
- **Fiscal Equalization Pyramid**: Federation at top (redistributing), strong Länder in middle (contributing), weak Länder at bottom (receiving), with equalization flows between all levels
- **Budget Constitution Matrix**: Two dimensions - (1) Revenue sources (federal taxes, Land taxes, shared taxes), (2) Expenditure responsibilities (federal tasks, Land tasks, joint tasks)
- **Fiscal Discipline Triangle**: Three constraints on all government finance - legal limits (Article 115), economic equilibrium (Article 109), and political accountability (Articles 110-114)

## Framework Application

When analyzing German financial constitutional issues:

1. **Identify the financial flow**: Is it revenue collection, distribution, expenditure, or equalization?
2. **Determine constitutional classification**: Which level has authority and under which article?
3. **Check Bundesrat involvement**: Most financial legislation requires Bundesrat consent
4. **Analyze equalization mechanisms**: How do Articles 107 and 104b interact to address regional disparities?
5. **Assess borrowing limits**: Does the action comply with Article 115's debt restrictions?
6. **Evaluate audit requirements**: Are proper oversight mechanisms in place per the financial action?

## Constitutional Patterns

- **Vertical Fiscal Imbalance**: Federation has more revenue but Länder have more expenditure responsibilities
- **Horizontal Equalization**: Financial transfers from wealthy to poor Länder to ensure nationwide living standard equality
- **Cooperative Fiscal Administration**: Bundesrat consent required for most financial legislation affecting Länder
- **Emergency Financial Powers**: Federal authority to intervene in Länder financial crises (Article 109(4))
- **European Integration**: Adaptation of fiscal constitution to EU requirements and constraints
