# Section III: The Bundestag (Federal Diet)
**Articles: 38-49**

## Core Idea
The Bundestag is the elected representative body of the German people, exercising legislative power, electing the Federal Chancellor, and controlling the Government. It is the central democratic institution of the Federal Republic.

## Frameworks Introduced

### Composition and Election (Article 38)
- **Representation**: The members of the Bundestag shall be elected in general, direct, free, equal, and secret elections
- **Representatives**: They shall be representatives of the whole people, not bound by orders or instructions, and responsible only to their conscience
- **Term**: The Bundestag shall be elected for four years. Its term shall end when a new Bundestag convenes
- **Number of Members**: The Bundestag shall consist of at least 598 members (currently 735 with overhang and compensation seats)

### Organizational Principles (Articles 39-40)
- **Legislative Period** (Art. 39.1): The Bundestag shall convene at least once a year. The President of the Bundestag may convene it earlier
- **Self-Convening** (Art. 39.2): The President must convene the Bundestag if one-third of its members, the Federal President, or the Federal Chancellor so demand
- **Dissolution** (Art. 39.1): The Bundestag may dissolve itself only by a decision taken by a majority of two-thirds of its members
- **Quorum** (Art. 42.2): The Bundestag shall be able to take decisions only if a majority of its members is present
- **Presidium** (Art. 40.1): The Bundestag shall elect a President, Vice-Presidents, and recording clerks
- **Rules of Procedure** (Art. 40.1): The Bundestag shall adopt its own rules of procedure

### Publicity and Procedure (Articles 42-44)
- **Public Sessions** (Art. 42.1): The sessions of the Bundestag shall be public
- **Decision-Making** (Art. 42.2): Decisions require majority unless Basic Law provides otherwise
- **Committees** (Art. 43): The Bundestag and its committees may demand the presence of any member of the Federal Government
- **Committees of Inquiry** (Art. 44): The Bundestag has the right, and on the motion of one-quarter of its members the duty, to establish committees of inquiry

### Rights of Members (Articles 46-48)
- **Indemnity and Immunity** (Art. 46): Members may not be held liable or disciplined outside the Bundestag for their votes or for any utterance made in the Bundestag or in any of its committees
- **Freedom from Arrest** (Art. 46.2): Members may not be arrested or otherwise deprived of their personal liberty during a session without the permission of the Bundestag
- **Witness Refusal** (Art. 47): Members have the right to refuse to give evidence on persons who have confided in them in their capacity as members or to whom they have confided in that capacity
- **Remuneration** (Art. 48.3): Members shall be entitled to adequate remuneration ensuring their independence

### Elections and Scrutiny (Articles 41, 45)
- **Election Scrutiny** (Art. 41): The Bundestag shall verify the credentials of its members. It shall decide on any challenges to the election of a member
- **European Affairs Committee** (Art. 45): The Bundestag shall establish a committee for European Union affairs
- **Foreign Affairs Committee** (Art. 45a): The Bundestag may establish a committee for foreign affairs and defense
- **Parliamentary Commissioner for the Armed Forces** (Art. 45b): The Bundestag shall appoint a commissioner as an auxiliary of the Bundestag for the exercise of parliamentary control
- **Petitions Committee** (Art. 45c): The Bundestag shall establish a committee for petitions

## Mental Models
- Use **Bundestag as primary representative body** — direct election by the people
- Think of **free mandate** — members represent the whole people, not bound by instructions
- Apply **publicity principle** — sessions are public unless exceptional circumstances
- Use **parliamentary control** — Bundestag controls Government and investigates matters

## Anti-patterns
- **Imperative mandate**: Members cannot be bound by external instructions
- **Secret sessions**: Publicity is the rule; secrecy requires justification
- **Obstruction of investigation**: Bundestag must enable committees of inquiry
- **Discrimination in remuneration**: All members entitled to adequate remuneration

## Key Takeaways
1. Bundestag is directly elected by the people with free mandate
2. Bundestag exercises legislative power and controls the Government
3. Members have immunity and indemnity to ensure independent representation
4. Bundestag can establish committees for various purposes, including investigation
5. Bundestag elects the Federal Chancellor and can hold Government accountable
