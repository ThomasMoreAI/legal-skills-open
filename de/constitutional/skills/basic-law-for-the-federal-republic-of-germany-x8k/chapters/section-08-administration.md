# Section VIII: Execution of Federal Laws and Federal Administration (Articles 83-91)

## Overview
Section VIII establishes the framework for implementing federal laws and the structure of federal administration in Germany's federal system. It defines how the Federation and Länder cooperate in executing legislation, balancing federal standards with Land autonomy.

---

## Article 83 - Execution by Länder
**Attuazione nei Länder**

The Länder shall execute federal laws as their own matter unless this Basic Law provides or permits otherwise.

---

## Article 84 - Land Administration - Federal Oversight
**Amministrazione propria dei Länder – Controllo federale**

1. When the Länder execute federal laws as their own matter, they shall regulate the establishment of their authorities and the administrative procedures.

2. The Federal Government may, with the consent of the Bundesrat, issue general administrative provisions.

3. The Federal Government shall supervise the Länder's execution of federal laws. It may send commissioners to Land authorities with their consent.

4. If deficiencies are found in a Land's execution of federal laws, the Bundesrat shall decide on the legal violation. Appeals may be made to the Federal Constitutional Court.

5. For the execution of federal laws, the Federal Government may be authorized by federal law (requiring Bundesrat consent) to give special instructions for individual cases.

---

## Article 85 - Administration on Federal Commission
**Amministrazione in delega**

1. When the Länder execute federal laws on federal commission, the Federal Government may issue general administrative provisions with Bundesrat consent.

2. The Land authorities shall be subject to instructions from the competent supreme federal authorities.

3. The Federal Government shall supervise the legality and appropriateness of execution. It may request reports and documents.

4. Federal supervision extends to both the legality and the merits of administration.

---

## Article 86 - Direct Federal Administration
**Amministrazione propria della Federazione**

If the Federation executes laws through its own direct administration or through public law institutions under its direct authority, the Federal Government shall issue general administrative provisions unless a law provides otherwise.

---

## Article 87 - Matters of Direct Federal Administration
**Materie**

1. The following shall be administered by direct federal administration with subordinate administrative authorities:
   - Diplomatic and consular service
   - Federal finance administration
   - Federal postal service
   - Federal waterways and shipping administration

2. Federal laws may establish federal offices for:
   - Border protection
   - Central police information
   - Criminal police
   - Constitution protection
   - Data collection for constitution protection

3. For matters within exclusive federal competence, federal laws may establish supreme federal authorities and new public law institutions under direct federal authority.

---

## Article 87a - Armed Forces
**Forze armate**

1. The Federation shall establish armed forces for defense purposes. Their strength and basic organizational structure must be shown in the budget.

2. Except for defense, the armed forces may only be deployed to the extent expressly permitted by this Basic Law.

3. During a state of defense or tension, the armed forces may protect civilian objects and perform traffic regulation tasks necessary for their defense mission.

4. To defend against imminent threats to the Federation's existence or free democratic order, the Federal Government may deploy the armed forces to support police forces when police and federal border police are insufficient.

---

## Article 87b - Federal Armed Forces Administration
**Amministrazione delle forze armate federali e della difesa**

1. The federal administration of defense shall be carried out by a federal authority with its own administrative offices to fulfill personnel and material needs of the armed forces.

2. Other federal laws concerning defense may be executed by Land authorities on federal commission.

---

## Article 87c - Nuclear Energy
**Produzione e utilizzazione dell'energia nucleare**

Federal laws adopted under Article 73(1)(14) may provide that they shall be executed by the Länder on federal commission.

---

## Article 87d - Air Traffic Administration
**Amministrazione del traffico aereo**

1. Air traffic administration shall be carried out by federal administration. A federal law shall determine the public or private law organizational form.

2. Federal laws may transfer air traffic administration tasks to the Länder on federal commission.

---

## Article 87e - Railway Traffic Administration
**Amministrazione del traffico ferroviario**

1. Railway traffic administration for federal railways shall be carried out by direct federal administration. Federal laws may transfer railway traffic administration functions to Land authority.

2. The Federation shall ensure that railway infrastructure meets public interest needs, particularly transport requirements.

3. Federal railways shall be operated as private law enterprises owned by the Federation.

---

## Article 87f - Postal Services and Telecommunications
**Poste e telecomunicazioni**

1. The Federation shall ensure adequate and appropriate services in postal and telecommunications sectors.

2. Services shall be provided as private economic activities through Deutsche Post AG and other private companies.

3. The Federation may retain certain regulatory functions through a federal office.

---

## Article 88 - Federal Bank
**Banca federale – Banca centrale europea**

The Federation shall establish a currency and note-issuing bank as the Federal Bank. Its functions may be transferred within the European Union to the European Central Bank, which is independent and primarily committed to price stability.

---

## Article 89 - Federal Waterways
**Vie navigabili federali – Amministrazione della navigazione**

1. The Federation shall own the previously Reich-owned waterways.

2. The Federation shall administer federal waterways with its own offices. It may transfer administration to a Land if requested.

3. Waterway administration shall take into account agricultural and water management economic requirements in coordination with the Länder.

---

## Article 90 - Federal Highways
**Strade federali**

1. The Federation shall own the autobahns and previously Reich-owned roads.

2. The Länder or competent autonomous bodies shall administer federal highways on federal commission.

3. The Federation may assume direct administration of federal highways within a Land at its request.

---

## Article 91 - Internal State of Emergency
**Stato di emergenza interna**

1. To defend against threats to the Federation's or a Land's existence or free democratic order, a Land may request assistance from other Länder's police forces, federal institutions, or federal border police.

2. If the threatened Land cannot or will not address the threat, the Federal Government may place that Land's police under its directives and deploy federal border police units. Such orders must be revoked when the danger ends or upon Bundesrat request.

---

## Key Principles

- **Land Execution**: Principle that Länder execute federal laws as their own matter (Article 83)
- **Federal Supervision**: Limited to ensuring legality, not substituting Land judgment
- **Direct Administration**: Federal government administers only where explicitly provided
- **Cooperative Federalism**: Balance between federal standards and Land autonomy
- **Emergency Powers**: Federal intervention only in exceptional circumstances (Article 91)

## Mental Models

- **Administrative Federalism Matrix**: Two axes - (1) Who executes (Federation vs Länder), (2) Whose matter (Federal vs Land). Most federal laws fall in the "Länder execute federal laws as their own matter" quadrant
- **Subsidiarity Ladder**: Federal direct administration → Federal commission administration → Land execution with federal oversight → Pure Land administration
- **Emergency Escalation**: Normal cooperation → Federal directives → Federal assumption of command (Article 91a in Section VIIIa)
