# Section IV: The Bundesrat (Federal Council)
**Articles: 50-53**

## Core Idea
The Bundesrat is the federal representation of the Länder, through which the Länder participate in the federal legislative and administrative process. It ensures the federal character of the Federation and protects Länder interests at the federal level.

## Frameworks Introduced

### Functions (Article 50)
- **Länder Representation**: Through the Bundesrat, the Länder shall participate in the legislation and administration of the Federation
- **Federal Character**: The Bundesrat ensures the federal nature of the Federation
- **Länder Interests**: The Bundesrat protects the interests of the Länder

### Composition (Article 51)
- **Members**: The Bundesrat shall consist of members of the Land Governments which appoint them and recall them
- **Voting**: Each Land shall have at least three votes; Länder with more than two million inhabitants shall have four, with more than six million five, and with more than seven million six votes
- **Delegation**: Each Land may send as many members as it has votes
- **Uniform Voting**: The votes of a Land may only be cast uniformly and may only be cast by members present or their representatives

### Organization (Article 52)
- **Presidency**: The Bundesrat shall elect its President for one year
- **Rotation**: The office of President shall rotate among the Länder representatives
- **Rules of Procedure**: The Bundesrat shall adopt its own rules of procedure
- **Public Sessions**: The Bundesrat shall decide in public sessions unless otherwise provided
- **Quorum**: The Bundesrat shall be able to take decisions only when a majority of its votes is present
- **Majority**: The Bundesrat shall take its decisions with at least the majority of its votes

### Government Participation (Article 53)
- **Government Presence**: The members of the Federal Government shall have the right, and on demand the duty, to attend sessions of the Bundesrat and of its committees
- **Right to be Heard**: They must be heard at their request
- **Initiative Right**: The Federal Government must inform the Bundesrat of its intended legislative initiatives

## Mental Models
- Use **Bundesrat as Länder voice** — represents Länder interests at federal level
- Think of **weighted voting** — votes based on population but all Länder have minimum representation
- Apply **federal balance** — Bundesrat ensures Länder participation in federal matters
- Use **cooperation principle** — Federal Government and Bundesrat work together

## Anti-patterns
- **Unequal representation**: All Länder must have minimum representation regardless of size
- **Split votes**: Each Land must vote uniformly
- **Government dominance**: Bundesrat represents Länder, not Federal Government
- **Secrecy**: Sessions are public unless exceptional circumstances require otherwise

## Key Takeaways
1. Bundesrat represents the Länder in federal legislation and administration
2. Each Land has votes based on population (3-6 votes)
3. Länder Governments appoint and recall their representatives
4. Bundesrat ensures federal character and protects Länder interests
5. Federal Government participates in Bundesrat sessions with right to be heard
