# Section XI: Transitional and Final Provisions (Articles 116-146)

## Overview
Section XI contains the transitional and final provisions of the Basic Law, addressing historical legacies, implementation details, and the constitutional framework for Germany's reunification. These articles form the constitutional bridge between Germany's past, present, and future.

---

## Article 116 - Definition of "German" - Reintegration
**Definizione di "tedesco" – Reintegrazione della cittadinanza**

1. Unless otherwise provided by law, a German within the meaning of this Basic Law is any person possessing German citizenship or any person who has been admitted to the territory of the German Reich within the boundaries of December 31, 1937, as a refugee or expellee of German ethnic origin or as the spouse or descendant of such person.

2. Former German citizens who were deprived of their citizenship between January 30, 1933, and May 8, 1945, for political, racial, or religious reasons, and their descendants, shall, upon application, have their citizenship restored. They shall be considered as not having been deprived of citizenship if they took up residence in Germany after May 8, 1945, and have not expressed a contrary intention.

---

## Article 117 - Repeal of Conflicting Provisions
**Decadenza delle disposizioni in contrasto con i diritti fondamentali**

1. Any provision contrary to Article 3(2) (gender equality) shall remain in force until its adjustment to this constitutional principle, but not beyond March 31, 1953.

2. Laws that restrict freedom of movement on the basis of existing territorial limitations shall remain in force until their repeal by federal law.

---

## Article 118 - Reorganization of Baden and Württemberg
**Riorganizzazione del Baden e del Württemberg**

The reorganization of the territory comprising the Länder of Baden, Württemberg-Baden, and Württemberg-Hohenzollern may take place, deviating from Article 29, by agreement between the Länder concerned. If no agreement is reached, federal law shall regulate reorganization, which must provide for a popular referendum.

---

## Article 118a - Reorganization of Berlin and Brandenburg
**Riorganizzazione di Berlino e del Brandeburgo**

The reorganization of the territory comprising the Länder of Berlin and Brandenburg may take place by agreement between the two Länder with the participation of those entitled to vote, deviating from Article 29.

---

## Article 119 - Refugees and Expellees
**Rifugiati ed espulsi**

Pending federal legislation on refugees and expellees, particularly concerning their distribution among the Länder, the Federal Government may, with Bundesrat consent, issue statutory orders. In special cases, the Federal Government may also be authorized to give extraordinary instructions. These instructions must be addressed to supreme Land authorities unless urgency requires otherwise.

---

## Article 120 - Occupation Costs - War Burdens
**Spese di occupazione – Oneri di guerra**

1. The Federation shall bear the costs arising from occupation and other internal and external burdens resulting from the war. Federal law shall provide further details.

2. To the extent that war burden costs have been regulated by federal laws until October 1, 1969, the Federation and Länder shall bear them proportionally according to these laws. This shall not affect the legal regulation of compensation claims.

3. Revenues shall be transferred to the Federation at the same time as it assumes the costs.

---

## Article 120a - Burden Equalization
**Conguaglio degli oneri**

1. Laws serving burden equalization may provide, with Bundesrat consent, that they be executed within the compensation payments in part by the Federation itself and in part by the Länder on federal commission, and that the powers vested in the Federal Government and the competent supreme federal authorities under Article 85 be transferred in whole or in part to the Federal Equalization Office.

2. Article 87(3) sentence 2 shall remain unaffected.

---

## Article 121 - Definition of "Majority of Members"
**Definizione di "maggioranza dei membri"**

The majority of Bundestag members and the Federal Convention within the meaning of this Basic Law shall be the majority of the legal number of their members.

---

## Article 122 - Transfer of Legislative Competences
**Trasferimento delle competenze legislative**

1. From the date of the first Bundestag meeting, laws shall be enacted exclusively by the legislative organs established in this Basic Law.

2. From this date, the legislative organs dissolved under paragraph 1 and those bodies that advised on legislation through their consent shall cease to exist.

---

## Article 123 - Continuation of Previous Legislation
**Vigenza della legislazione precedente**

1. Laws in force before the first Bundestag meeting shall remain in force insofar as they do not conflict with this Basic Law.

2. Treaties concluded by the German Reich that pertain to matters for which Land legislators would be competent under this Basic Law shall remain in force if, according to general principles of international law, they remain valid and continue to have effect, subject to all rights and exceptions of the parties, until new treaties are concluded by the competent authorities under this Basic Law or until they are otherwise terminated by their provisions.

---

## Article 124 - Continuation as Federal Law
**Vigenza della legislazione esclusiva**

Laws concerning matters within the exclusive legislative power of the Federation shall become federal law within their area of validity.

---

## Article 125 - Concurrent Legislation Continuation
**Vigenza della legislazione concorrente**

Laws concerning matters within the concurrent legislative power of the Federation shall become federal law within their territorial area of validity if they apply uniformly in one or more occupation zones or if they were enacted to modify Reich legislation after May 8, 1945.

---

## Article 125a - Federal Law Continuation - Land Substitution
**Vigenza della legislazione federale – Sostituzione con la legislazione dei Länder**

1. Legislation that was enacted as federal law but can no longer be enacted as such due to amendments to Article 74(1), insertion of Article 84(1) sentence 7, Article 85(1) sentence 2, Article 98(3) sentence 2, or repeal of Articles 74a, 75, or 98(3) sentence 2, shall continue to apply as federal law but may be replaced by Land legislation.

2. Legislation enacted under Article 72(2) in the version valid until November 15, 1994, but which can no longer be enacted as federal law due to amendments to Article 72(2), shall continue to apply as federal law. Federal law may provide that it may be replaced by Land legislation.

3. Legislation that was enacted as federal law but can no longer be enacted as such due to amendments to Article 73 shall continue to apply as Land law but may be replaced by federal legislation.

---

## Article 125b - Framework Legislation Derogation
**Vigenza delle leggi quadro - Potere di deroga dei Länder**

1. Legislation enacted under Article 75 in the version valid until September 1, 2006, that could have been enacted as federal law thereafter, shall continue to apply as federal law. Land legislative powers and obligations remain unaffected. In matters under Article 72(3) sentence 1, Länder may enact deviating provisions, except that in matters under Article 72(3) sentence 1, nos. 2, 5, and 6, they may do so only before and to the extent that the Federation has exercised its legislative power after September 1, 2006.

2. Länder may enact deviating provisions from federal framework legislation enacted under Article 84(1) in the version valid until September 1, 2006, and from the rules on administrative procedures valid until December 31, 2008, only if corresponding federal administrative procedure rules have been modified after September 1, 2006.

---

## Article 125c - Exclusion from Joint Tasks
**Vigenza della legislazione esclusa dall'ambito dei compiti comuni**

Legislation enacted under Article 91a(2) in conjunction with paragraph 1, no. 1, in the version valid until September 1, 2006, shall remain in force until December 31, 2006.

---

## Article 126 - Judgment on Federal Law Status
**Giudizio sulla vigenza della legislazione come diritto federale**

The Federal Constitutional Court shall decide disputes concerning the continued validity of laws as federal law.

---

## Articles 127-129 - Administrative Transitions
**Adeguamento della legislazione della Bizona, Persistenza del potere dispositivo, Persistenza delle deleghe legislative**

These articles regulate the transition of administrative powers, the continuation of delegated legislative authority, and the persistence of existing administrative authorizations under the new constitutional order.

---

## Articles 130-135 - Property and Administrative Successions
**Assunzione delle istituzioni amministrative esistenti, Ex-dipendenti, Collocamento a riposo, Subentro, Successione nel patrimonio del Reich, Disciplina patrimoniale**

These articles address the succession of federal authority over existing administrative institutions, the status of former public employees, retirement provisions, federal succession to Reich property, and the transfer of assets between different levels of government.

---

## Articles 135a-136 - Financial Provisions
**Precedenti obbligazioni, Prima riunione del Bundesrat, Eleggibilità dei dipendenti pubblici, Notariato, Vigenza delle disposizioni per la denazificazione**

These transitional articles regulate the assumption of pre-existing financial obligations, the first Bundesrat meeting, public employee eligibility, notarial regulations in southern Germany, and the continued validity of denazification provisions.

---

## Article 140 - Incorporation of Weimar Constitution Provisions
**Diritto delle comunità religiose**

The provisions of Articles 136, 137, 138, 139, and 141 of the German Constitution of August 11, 1919 (Weimar Constitution) are an integral part of this Basic Law.

---

## Article 141 - Bremen Clause
**"Clausola di Brema"**

Article 7(3) sentence 1 shall not apply in a Land where on January 1, 1949, different Land law provisions were in force.

---

## Article 142 - Land Fundamental Rights
**Riserva a favore dei diritti fondamentali riconosciuti dalla legislazione dei Länder**

Unless Article 31 provides otherwise, Land constitutional provisions remain in force insofar as they guarantee fundamental rights in conformity with Articles 1-18 of this Basic Law.

---

## Article 143 - Validity of Derogations
**Termini di validità delle deroghe**

1. Law in the territory indicated in Article 3 of the Unification Treaty may deviate from this Basic Law provisions until December 31, 1992, insofar as adjustments to the Basic Law's legal system have not yet been completed due to the different situation there.

2. Derogations from Sections I, VIII, VIIIa, IX, X, and XI are permissible until December 31, 1995, but may not violate Article 19(2) and must be compatible with Article 79(3) principles.

3. Article 41 of the Unification Treaty and its implementation provisions remain in force insofar as they provide that property interventions in the territory under Article 3 of that Treaty have provisional character.

---

## Article 143a - Railway Privatization
**Competenza legislativa esclusiva in materia ferroviaria**

1. The Federation shall have exclusive legislative power for all issues arising from the transformation of federally administered railways into commercial enterprises.

2. Article 87e(5) shall apply accordingly.

---

## Article 143b - Postal Service Transformation
**Trasformazione delle Poste Federali Tedesche**

1. The Deutsche Bundespost, a special public asset, shall be transformed into private law enterprises under procedures established by federal law.

2. Pre-existing exclusive Federation rights may be assigned to resulting enterprises for a transitional period.

---

## Article 143c - Compensation for Task Termination
**Importi di compensazione per la soppressione dei compiti comuni**

From January 1, 2007, to December 31, 2019, the Länder shall receive annual compensation payments from the federal budget for the termination of joint task financing.

---

## Article 144 - Approval of the Basic Law
**Approvazione della Legge fondamentale**

1. This Basic Law must be approved by the representative assemblies of at least two-thirds of the German Länder in which it is to enter into force immediately.

2. If the application of this Basic Law is limited in one of the Länder specified in Article 23 or part thereof, that Land or part of a Land shall have the right to send representatives to the Bundestag under Article 38 and to the Bundesrat under Article 50.

---

## Article 145 - Entry into Force
**Entrata in vigore della Legge fondamentale**

1. The Parliamentary Council shall publicly proclaim the adoption of this Basic Law with the participation of the Greater Berlin deputies, enact it, and promulgate it.

2. This Basic Law shall enter into force at the end of the day of its promulgation.

3. It must be published in the Federal Law Gazette.

---

## Article 146 - Final Provision
**Termini di validità della Legge fondamentale**

This Basic Law, which after the achievement of the unity and freedom of Germany applies to the entire German people, shall cease to be in force on the day when a constitution adopted by the German people in free self-determination enters into force.

---

## Key Principles

- **Historical Continuity**: Bridge between Germany's constitutional past and future
- **Reunification Framework**: Constitutional mechanisms for German reunification
- **Gradual Transition**: Phased implementation of constitutional principles
- **Legal Certainty**: Clear rules for which pre-existing laws continue and which are repealed
- **Temporary Derogations**: Time-limited exceptions for former East German territories
- **Constitutional Supremacy**: Basic Law as the final, complete constitution until replaced by popular vote

## Mental Models

- **Constitutional Timeline**: Pre-1949 (Reich/Weimar) → 1949 (Basic Law adoption) → 1990 (Reunification) → Future (potential new constitution under Article 146)
- **Transitional Bridge**: Section XI as the constitutional architecture connecting Germany's fragmented past to its unified present
- **Legal Continuity Matrix**: Two axes - (1) Pre-1949 legal acts, (2) Post-1949 constitutional evaluation. Each cell determines whether the act continues, is modified, or is repealed
- **Reunification Layers**: West Germany (original Basic Law territory) → East Germany (Article 23/143 transitional provisions) → Unified Germany (Article 146 future constitution)

## Framework Application

When analyzing transitional or final provisions:

1. **Identify temporal context**: When was the provision enacted and when does it apply?
2. **Determine territorial scope**: Does it apply to all Germany, only former West, or only former East?
3. **Check expiration dates**: Many transitional provisions have built-in sunset clauses
4. **Assess historical purpose**: What problem or legacy does this provision address?
5. **Evaluate current relevance**: Has the transitional period ended or does the provision still have active effect?
6. **Trace constitutional evolution**: How has this provision been amended or supplemented since 1949?
7. **Connect to reunification**: How does this provision reflect or facilitate German unity?

## Constitutional Patterns

- **Historical Settlement**: Constitutional resolution of Germany's 20th century divisions
- **Legal Archeology**: Preservation of pre-1949 legal principles through incorporation (Article 140)
- **Asymmetric Federalism**: Different constitutional rules for different territories during transition periods
- **Constitutional Patience**: Recognition that full constitutional integration takes time (Articles 117, 118, 143)
- **Popular Sovereignty Reservation**: Article 146 as the ultimate expression of the people's constitutional power
