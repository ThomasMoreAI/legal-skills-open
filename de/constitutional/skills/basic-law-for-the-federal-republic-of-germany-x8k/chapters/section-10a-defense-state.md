# Section Xa: Defense State (Articles 115a-115l)

## Overview
Section Xa establishes emergency provisions for a state of defense (Verteidigungsfall), when Germany faces armed aggression. This section creates a special constitutional regime that temporarily alters normal constitutional arrangements to enable effective defense while maintaining constitutional safeguards.

**Note**: These provisions were inserted by the Seventeenth Amendment Act of June 24, 1968, as part of Germany's "Emergency Constitution" (Notstandsverfassung).

---

## Article 115a - Determination of Defense State
**Accertamento dello stato di difesa**

1. The Bundestag, with Bundesrat consent, shall determine that the federal territory is under armed attack or that such an attack is imminent (state of defense). This determination requires a two-thirds majority of votes cast, amounting to at least a majority of Bundestag members.

2. If circumstances absolutely require immediate action and insurmountable obstacles prevent timely Bundestag assembly, or if the Bundestag cannot meet, the Joint Committee may make this determination with a two-thirds majority of votes cast, amounting to at least a majority of its members.

3. The determination shall be promulgated by the Federal President in the Federal Law Gazette in accordance with Article 82. If this cannot be done in time, promulgation shall occur in another manner and be published in the Federal Law Gazette as soon as circumstances permit.

4. If the federal territory is under armed attack and competent federal organs cannot immediately make the determination under paragraph 1, the determination shall be deemed to have been made and promulgated at the moment the attack began. The Federal President shall announce this moment as soon as circumstances permit.

5. Once the defense state is promulgated and the federal territory is under armed attack, the Federal President may, with Bundestag approval, issue international law declarations on the defense state's existence. In cases under paragraph 2, the Joint Committee shall substitute for the Bundestag.

---

## Article 115b - Federal Chancellor's Command Authority
**Potere di comando del Cancelliere federale**

With the promulgation of the defense state, command authority over the armed forces shall be transferred to the Federal Chancellor.

---

## Article 115c - Expanded Federal Legislative Authority
**Ampliamento della competenza legislativa della Federazione**

1. For the defense state, the Federation shall assume concurrent legislative authority even over matters within Land legislative competence. Such laws require Bundesrat consent.

2. During the defense state, if required by circumstances, federal law may temporarily:
   - Regulate expropriation compensation differently from Article 14(3) sentence 2
   - Establish time limits for deprivation of liberty not exceeding four days when a judge cannot act within normal timeframes

3. To repel actual or imminent aggression, federal law may regulate federal and Land administration and finances in derogation of Titles VIII, VIIIa, and X, to safeguard Länder, municipalities, and municipal associations, especially financially.

4. Laws under paragraphs 1 and 2(1) may be applied in preparation for their execution even before the defense state enters into force.

---

## Article 115d - Urgent Legislative Procedures
**Proposte di legge urgenti**

1. For federal legislation, the provisions of Articles 76(2), 77(1) sentence 2, and 77(2-4), 78, and 82(1) shall not apply during the defense state; instead, paragraphs 2 and 3 below shall apply.

2. Federal Government bills designated as urgent must be submitted to the Bundesrat simultaneously with their introduction in the Bundestag. The Bundestag and Bundesrat shall deliberate these bills jointly without delay.

3. For laws requiring Bundesrat consent, their final adoption shall require a majority of Bundesrat votes. Further provisions may be established by rules of procedure approved by the Bundestag with Bundesrat consent.

4. For promulgation of laws, Article 115a(3) sentence 2 shall apply accordingly.

---

## Article 115e - Joint Committee
**Commissione comune**

1. If the Joint Committee determines with a two-thirds majority of votes cast (amounting to at least a majority of its members) that insurmountable obstacles prevent timely Bundestag assembly or that the Bundestag cannot decide, the Joint Committee shall take the place of the Bundestag and Bundesrat and assume their powers jointly.

2. The Basic Law may not be amended, abolished, wholly or partially suspended by a Joint Committee law. The Joint Committee may not enact laws under Articles 23(1) sentence 2, 24(1), or 29.

---

## Article 115f - Federal Border Police Deployment
**Impiego della polizia confinaria federale – Ulteriori poteri dispositivi**

1. During the defense state, the Federal Government may, to the extent required by circumstances:
   - Deploy the federal border police throughout the federal territory
   - Issue instructions to Land governments and, if urgent, to Land authorities, and transfer this power to designated Land government members

2. The Bundestag, Bundesrat, and Joint Committee must be promptly informed of measures taken under paragraph 1.

---

## Article 115g - Federal Constitutional Court
**Corte costituzionale federale**

The constitutional position and the performance of constitutional duties by the Federal Constitutional Court and its judges may not be prejudiced. The law on the Federal Constitutional Court may be amended by a Joint Committee law only to the extent necessary, with the Court's opinion, to ensure the Court's capacity to function. Until such a law is enacted, the Federal Constitutional Court may take necessary measures to ensure its functioning. Decisions under this paragraph require a majority of participating judges.

---

## Article 115h - Legislative Terms and Mandates
**Legislature e incarichi in scadenza**

1. Bundestag or Land assembly terms expiring during the defense state shall end six months after the defense state's termination.

2. The Federal President's term expiring during the defense state, and in case of early termination, the performance of their functions by the Bundesrat President, shall end nine months after the defense state's termination.

3. The term of a Federal Constitutional Court judge expiring during the defense state shall end six months after its termination.

4. If a new Federal Chancellor election is necessary by the Joint Committee, it shall elect a new Chancellor with a majority of its members; the Federal President shall submit a motion. The Joint Committee may express no confidence in the Chancellor except by electing a successor with a two-thirds majority of its members.

5. The Bundestag may not be dissolved during the defense state.

---

## Article 115i - Land Government Measures
**Assunzione di provvedimenti da parte dei Governi dei Länder**

1. If competent federal organs cannot take necessary measures to counter danger, and circumstances absolutely require immediate independent action in parts of the federal territory, Land governments or their designated authorities may take measures under Article 115f(1) within their competence.

2. Measures under paragraph 1 may be repealed at any time by the Federal Government and, concerning Land and subordinate federal authorities, also by Land minister-presidents.

---

## Article 115k - Rank and Validity of Emergency Provisions
**Rango e periodo di validità delle disposizioni di emergenza**

1. For the duration of their applicability, laws under Articles 115c, 115e, and 115g and statutory orders issued under these laws shall render incompatible provisions inapplicable. This shall not apply to provisions previously enacted under Articles 115c, 115e, and 115g.

2. Laws enacted by the Joint Committee and statutory orders issued under such laws shall expire no later than six months after the defense state's termination.

3. Laws containing provisions derogating from Articles 91a, 91b, 104a, 106, and 107 shall be valid at the latest until the end of the second fiscal year following the defense state's termination. They may be amended after the defense state ends by a Bundestag law with Bundesrat consent to be brought into conformity with Titles VIIIa and X.

---

## Article 115l - Repeal of Emergency Measures
**Abrogazione dei provvedimenti straordinari – Conclusione della pace**

1. The Bundestag may at any time, with Bundesrat consent, repeal Joint Committee laws. The Bundesrat may request the Bundestag to decide on this. If the Bundestag and Bundesrat decide, other Joint Committee or Federal Government measures taken to counter danger must be repealed.

2. The Bundestag may at any time, with Bundesrat consent, declare the defense state terminated by a decision that must be promulgated by the Federal President. The Bundesrat may request the Bundestag to decide on this. The defense state must be immediately declared terminated when its preconditions no longer exist.

3. Peace conclusion shall be decided by federal law.

---

## Key Principles

- **Emergency Constitutionalism**: Temporary constitutional adaptation for existential threats while preserving core constitutional values
- **Defense State as Exception**: Normal constitutional order continues unless specifically modified by Section Xa
- **Parliamentary Substitution**: Joint Committee as temporary legislature when Bundestag/Bundesrat cannot function
- **Proportionality**: Emergency measures limited to what is necessary for defense
- **Reversibility**: All emergency measures automatically expire or can be repealed
- **Constitutional Safeguards**: Federal Constitutional Court's independence and core functions preserved even during emergency

## Mental Models

- **Constitutional Emergency Layers**: Normal constitution (core) → Section Xa modifications (temporary layer) → Joint Committee actions (innermost emergency layer). Each layer can be peeled back as crisis abates
- **Defense State Timeline**: Attack/imminent attack → Determination (Article 115a) → Activation of emergency powers → Defense measures → Termination declaration → Return to normalcy
- **Institutional Substitution Chain**: Bundestag → Joint Committee (Article 115e) → Federal Government with expanded powers (Article 115c). Each substitution has defined limits
- **Emergency Powers Matrix**: Two dimensions - (1) Which institution gains power, (2) Which normal constitutional provision is modified. Section Xa explicitly maps these modifications
- **Safeguard Pyramid**: At base - Eternity Clause (Article 79.3) always applies. Next level - Federal Constitutional Court independence (Article 115g). Top - Specific time limits on all emergency measures

## Framework Application

When analyzing emergency constitutional issues:

1. **Determine applicability**: Is there an armed attack or imminent threat? (Article 115a trigger)
2. **Identify affected institutions**: Which organs cannot function normally? (Bundestag, Bundesrat, Federal Government)
3. **Trace substitution chain**: Who takes over which powers under Articles 115e, 115c, 115f?
4. **Check proportionality**: Are the emergency measures necessary and limited to defense purposes?
5. **Assess duration**: How long can these measures remain in force under Articles 115k-115l?
6. **Verify safeguards**: Are constitutional core principles (Articles 1, 20, 79.3) respected?
7. **Plan normalization**: What procedures exist for returning to normal constitutional order?

## Constitutional Patterns

- **Temporary Constitution**: Section Xa as a parallel constitutional order activated only during defense state
- **Institutional Redundancy**: Multiple backup mechanisms (Joint Committee, expanded federal powers) to ensure governance continuity
- **Automatic Sunset**: All emergency provisions expire automatically without renewal
- **Graduated Response**: Escalation of powers corresponds to severity of threat and institutional incapacity
- **Constitutional Memory**: The Basic Law remembers and preserves its normal order even during emergency, enabling automatic restoration
