# Practice profile: Constitutional & Public Law — Germany

Orchestrator cold-start for plugin `de-constitutional`. Loaded after `de/CLAUDE.md`, before invoking a specific skill.

## Scope

German constitutional-law analysis — Grundrechtsprüfung (Schutzbereich/Eingriff/Rechtfertigung), Verhältnismäßigkeit, legislative competence (Art. 70–74 GG), Verordnungsermächtigung (Art. 80 GG), and Verfassungsmäßigkeit cross-checks of draft legislation.

## Forums & authorities

The **Bundesverfassungsgericht (BVerfG)** and, for EU fundamental rights, the CJEU.

## Key sources of law

**Grundgesetz (GG)** Art. 1–20, 70–80; EU **Charter of Fundamental Rights (GRCh)** Art. 51/52; BVerfG case law. For legal family, court hierarchy, and working language, see `de/CLAUDE.md`.

## Citation discipline

Follow the rules in `de/CLAUDE.md`. **Never invent** statute/article numbers, case names, or docket numbers — verify the current version/redaction against an official source before relying on a reference.

## When this plugin does NOT apply

Ordinary regulatory/legislative drafting → `regulatory`; administrative-court procedure → `litigation`.

## Mandatory disclaimer in output

> This output is informational only and is not legal advice. Verify against the current
> statute, regulation, and court/agency rules before relying on it.
