# Product and packaging duties for small shops

These are the duties that never appear in a "legal texts" checklist and reliably hit small German shops. Registration precedes the first shipment; none of them is a text on the website, but a shop that misses them faces distribution bans and Abmahnungen.

Status as at 05.08.2026.

## Packaging — VerpackG until 11.08.2026, VerpackDG from 12.08.2026

**Anyone who ships goods to private end users places packaging on the market.** That includes the shipping box, filling material, tape and the sales packaging. There is no turnover or volume threshold: the first parcel triggers the duty.

The chain of duties is:

1. **Registration in the Verpackungsregister LUCID** at the Zentrale Stelle Verpackungsregister, **before** the first packaging is placed on the market
2. **Systembeteiligung** — a contract with a dual system for systembeteiligungspflichtige packaging
3. **Data reporting** to LUCID and to the system, in the intervals required
4. above the statutory volume thresholds, the **Vollständigkeitserklärung** audited by a registered expert

Unregistered distribution is prohibited — a de facto sales ban — and marketplaces are obliged to block non-registered sellers.

**Change with a near-term date:** the **VerpackDG** (Verpackungsrecht-Durchführungsgesetz), law of 13.07.2026, BGBl. 2026 I Nr. 207, published 17.07.2026, enters into force on **12.08.2026** together with Regulation (EU) 2025/40 (PPWR). The VerpackG ceases to apply at the end of **11.08.2026** and is largely replaced. Before drafting anything on packaging duties after that date, verify the current obligations against the VerpackDG and the PPWR rather than against VerpackG section numbers. `[[UNVERIFIED: the section numbering of the registration and system-participation duties under the VerpackDG. ## Electricals — ElektroG

Anyone who places electrical or electronic equipment on the German market for the first time is a Hersteller under the ElektroG and must be **registered with the stiftung ear before the first offer**. Selling without registration breaches §§ 6, 7 and 17 ElektroG, and marketplaces block unregistered offers. Distributors may not offer equipment from unregistered producers.

**§ 17 ElektroG — Rücknahmepflicht der Vertreiber:**

- Retailers with a sales area for electrical equipment of at least 400 m², and food retailers with a total sales area of at least 800 m² that offer electrical equipment at least occasionally, must take back old equipment free of charge on delivery of new equipment of the same type, and must take back small old equipment (external dimension up to 25 cm) on request without a purchase, limited per type.
- **Abs 2:** in distance selling, the thresholds are measured against **all storage and dispatch areas** for electrical and electronic equipment. Take-back must be ensured by suitable return options at a reasonable distance from the end user.

**§ 18 ElektroG — Informationspflichten gegenüber den privaten Haushalten:** information duties towards private households, including on the take-back obligation under § 17 Abs 1 and 2 ElektroG, separate collection, and the meaning of the crossed-out wheeled bin symbol.

**§ 17 Abs 1a — e-cigarettes and tobacco heaters.** In the consolidated text read on gesetze-im-internet.de 05.08.2026: distributors that carry, or carried within the last six months, **electronic cigarettes or electronic tobacco heaters** must take those back free of charge as old equipment at or immediately near the point of sale, and **may not tie the take-back to a purchase**. Abs 2 extends Abs 1 and Abs 1a to distance selling.

**§ 18a ElektroG — Kennzeichnung und Hinweispflichten. This is the provision that governs shop and shop-page notices, and it is the one usually missed.** Two different symbols, do not conflate them:

| Where | Symbol | Provision |
|---|---|---|
| Collection and take-back points set up by a party entitled under § 12 | **Anlage 3a** | § 18a Abs 1 |
| Entrance area of the retail premises — coloured, legible, **at least DIN A4**, in the direct line of sight of the customer flow, plus information on how take-back works there | **Anlage 3a** | § 18a Abs 2 |
| In the shop, immediately next to where the equipment is sold: notice that old equipment must be disposed of separately from unsorted municipal waste | **Anlage 3** (crossed-out wheeled bin) | § 18a Abs 3 |
| **Distance selling**: on the product pages, or before or during the order, legible and visible; plus information on how collection under § 17 Abs 2 S 2 and take-back under § 17 Abs 2 S 4 work | **Anlage 3a** | § 18a Abs 4 |

For a webshop, § 18a Abs 4 is the operative duty: the Anlage 3a symbol goes on the product pages or into the checkout — a link in the footer is not "auf den Seiten mit den entsprechenden Produkten oder vor oder bei der Bestellung".

## Batteries — BattDG

The **BattDG** (Batterierecht-Durchführungsgesetz) replaced the BattG and entered into force on **07.10.2025**, later than the originally planned 18.08.2025 because of the change of government. It implements Regulation (EU) 2023/1542.

Anyone placing batteries on the market in Germany — including batteries built into appliances and shipped with a device — must be registered and must ensure take-back through an EAR-approved producer responsibility organisation or an own take-back solution. Foreign producers need an authorised representative established in Germany. Registrations under the old BattG were revoked in the transition.

Section numbers, read on gesetze-im-internet.de 05.08.2026. The BattDG is a *Durchführungsgesetz*: most substantive duties sit in Regulation (EU) 2023/1542 and the BattDG supplies the German procedure, so **cite both** or the citation is incomplete.

| Duty | Provision |
|---|---|
| **Verkehrsverbote** — the sales ban | § 4 BattDG |
| **Registrierung der Hersteller** | § 5 BattDG iVm Art 55 Abs 2 UAbs 1 S 1 VO (EU) 2023/1542 |
| Rücknahmepflichten der Händler, Gerätebatterien | § 14 BattDG iVm Art 62 Abs 1 VO |
| Rücknahmepflichten der Händler, Starterbatterien; Pfandpflicht | §§ 18, 19 BattDG |
| **Informationspflichten der Händler** — the shop-notice duty | § 24 BattDG iVm Art 74 Abs 4 und 5 VO |
| Kennzeichnung of the take-back point | § 25 Abs 4 S 1 BattDG |
| Bevollmächtigter for a producer without a German establishment | § 40 Abs 2 S 1 BattDG |

Three points that decide what actually has to be built:

- **§ 5 Abs 1 S 2**: the registration duty falls on the producer (or its Bevollmächtigter) **even where a producer responsibility organisation has been designated** — expressly departing from Art 55 Abs 6 of the Regulation. Delegating to a PRO does not discharge it.
- **§ 5 Abs 1 S 4** — **Registrierungsfiktion**: the registration counts as granted twelve weeks after all the information required by Art 55 Abs 2 and 3 has been submitted, unless a refusal ground applies. On request the applicant gets that certified in writing with a registration number. Useful when a launch date is tight; do not treat it as automatic.
- **§ 4 Abs 3**: where the producer is not properly registered, **Händler may not make the batteries available and fulfilment service providers may not store, pack, address or ship them**. That reaches the logistics provider, not only the seller.

**For a distance seller, § 24 Abs 3 is the operative one.** Traders selling batteries to end users by distance contract must give the Art 74 Abs 4 and 5 information *and* the notices under § 24 Abs 1 and 2 — that end users may return spent batteries free of charge at the shop, and that they are **legally obliged** to return them — "gut sichtbar durch schriftliche und bildliche Hinweise in den von ihnen verwendeten Darstellungsmedien sowie leicht auffindbar auf der Internetseite", or enclosed with the shipment in writing. Note *bildliche*: text alone does not discharge it, and "leicht auffindbar" rules out burying it in the AGB.

## Textiles — TextilKennzG

Regulation (EU) No 1007/2011 governs textile fibre names and the labelling of fibre composition. The German **TextilKennzG** (Textilkennzeichnungsgesetz), in force since 24.02.2016, implements it and makes breaches an Ordnungswidrigkeit.

For a shop this means: fibre composition in percentages by weight, in German, both on a durable label on the product **and** in the online product description, visible before the purchase is concluded. Non-textile parts of animal origin must be identified.

## Other duties frequently missed

- **Elektro- und Elektronikgeräte, Verpackungen, Batterien: the register numbers belong in the shop.** The LUCID registration number and the WEEE-Reg.-Nr. DE are commonly required in the Impressum or the shop information by marketplaces, and stating them is good practice even where not statutory.
- **Energy labelling** for covered products under Regulation (EU) 2017/1369 — the label and the product information sheet must be shown in the distance-selling offer.
- **General product safety** under Regulation (EU) 2023/988 (GPSR), applicable since 13.12.2024: manufacturer and, where applicable, importer contact details and a responsible person in the Union must be stated in the offer, plus warnings and safety information. This is EU-level; see the `legal-eu` skill for the detail.

## Checkpoints

- [ ] LUCID registration completed before the first shipment, registration number recorded
- [ ] Contract with a dual system in place and reporting current
- [ ] Packaging duties re-checked against the VerpackDG and the PPWR for the period from 12.08.2026
- [ ] stiftung ear registration completed before the first offer where electrical equipment is sold, WEEE-Reg.-Nr. DE recorded
- [ ] Take-back thresholds calculated on storage and dispatch areas for distance selling (§ 17 Abs 2 ElektroG)
- [ ] § 18 ElektroG household information published and current against the 2026 amendment
- [ ] Battery registration and take-back arrangement under the BattDG in place, authorised representative where the producer is foreign
- [ ] Textile fibre composition in the product description and on the product label (TextilKennzG, VO (EU) 1007/2011)
- [ ] Energy label and product information sheet shown for covered products
- [ ] GPSR manufacturer, importer and responsible-person details in the offer
