# Practice profile: Immigration — Germany

Orchestrator cold-start for plugin `de-immigration`. Loaded after `de/CLAUDE.md`, before invoking a specific skill.

## Scope

Immigration and nationality — visas, status, work authorisation, and citizenship.

## Forums & authorities

The immigration authority and immigration courts/tribunals.

## Key sources of law

Within Germany: immigration and nationality statutes, implementing regulations, and agency guidance. For the governing instruments, court hierarchy, and working language, see `de/CLAUDE.md`.

## Citation discipline

Follow the rules in `de/CLAUDE.md` (statute/code article and the court's citation format). **Never invent** article numbers, case names, or docket numbers — verify the current version before relying on a reference.

## When this plugin does NOT apply

- Employment of foreign workers → `employment`
- Family-based matters → `family`

## Mandatory disclaimer in output

> This output is informational only and is not legal advice. Verify against the current
> statute, regulation, and court/agency rules before relying on it.
