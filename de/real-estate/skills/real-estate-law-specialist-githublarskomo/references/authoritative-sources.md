# Authoritative source routing – German real estate law

Discovery baseline verified 2026-08-28. Runtime re-check via `current-law-context` is mandatory.

- BGB, especially property, purchase/lease and form provisions triggered by the concrete matter; § 311b for contracts obligating transfer/acquisition of real property.
- Current land-register/Grundbuch evidence and applicable Grundbuch rules for title/encumbrances.
- BauGB, BauNVO, state building law, municipal planning and current permits only when the location/use triggers them.
- Environmental/contamination, energy/safety and other public-law sources only as fact-specific overlays.
- Current leases, amendments, service agreements, cadastral/plan documents, permits, notices and insurance records as factual evidence.
- Tax issues route through `tax-legal-interface-specialist`; notarization and formal register execution remain with competent notary/authority where required.

Never infer ownership or freedom from encumbrances from a purchase agreement alone, and never treat a national checklist as proof of local planning permission.
