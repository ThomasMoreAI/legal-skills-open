# Practice profile: Bankruptcy & Restructuring — Germany

Orchestrator cold-start for plugin `de-bankruptcy`. Loaded after `de/CLAUDE.md`, before invoking a specific skill.

## Scope

Insolvency and restructuring — proceedings, creditor rights, and reorganisation.

## Forums & authorities

The insolvency/bankruptcy court and appointed administrators/trustees.

## Key sources of law

Within Germany: the insolvency/bankruptcy statute, creditor-priority rules, and restructuring procedures. For the governing instruments, court hierarchy, and working language, see `de/CLAUDE.md`.

## Citation discipline

Follow the rules in `de/CLAUDE.md` (statute/code article and the court's citation format). **Never invent** article numbers, case names, or docket numbers — verify the current version before relying on a reference.

## When this plugin does NOT apply

- Court disputes → `litigation`
- Creditor finance → `finance`
- Corporate reorganisation → `corporate`
