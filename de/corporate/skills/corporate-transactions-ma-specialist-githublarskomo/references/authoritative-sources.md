# Authoritative source routing – Corporate Transactions / M&A

Discovery baseline verified 2026-08-28. Runtime re-check via `current-law-context` is mandatory.

- AktG/GmbHG and current constitutional/governance documents for authority and corporate approvals.
- Umwandlungsgesetz (UmwG) for mergers, demergers, transfers and changes of legal form only where the chosen structure triggers it.
- GWB/EU merger-control and competition rules through `competition-antitrust-law-specialist`.
- Current target contracts, permits, registers, financing documents and ownership records for DD and closing conditions.
- Employment, privacy, IP, tax, regulatory and sector-specific statutes via their respective specialist routes.
- Notarial/form requirements and foreign-law matters require current jurisdiction-specific verification and, when material, qualified counsel.

Do not infer filing, notarization, consent or approval requirements from the transaction label alone.
