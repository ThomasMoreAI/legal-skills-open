# Transitional and Final Provisions

## Core Idea
The Transitional and Final Provisions contain temporary norms that facilitated the transition from the previous constitutional order to the Republic, as well as final rules on the Constitution's entry into force and implementation.

## Key Provisions

### Transitional Norms
- **Institution Adaptation**: Existing institutions adapted to new constitutional framework
- **Legislation Continuity**: Pre-existing laws remain in force if compatible with Constitution
- **Judicial Transition**: Existing judges continue in office under new system
- **Temporary Organs**: Some organs continued temporarily until new ones established

### Final Provisions
- **Entry into Force**: Constitution entered into force on January 1, 1948
- **Publication**: Promulgated by Provisional Head of State
- **Interpretation**: Constitution must be interpreted as a coherent whole
- **Supremacy**: Constitution is supreme law of the Republic

## Mental Models
- Use **transitional provisions as bridge** — facilitated smooth transition to new order
- Think of **final provisions as foundation** — established Constitution's authoritative status

## Key Takeaways
1. Transitional provisions enabled practical shift to republican order
2. Constitution entered into force on January 1, 1948
3. Pre-existing compatible laws continued in effect
4. Constitution is supreme and directly applicable
5. Transitional period allowed gradual implementation of reforms
