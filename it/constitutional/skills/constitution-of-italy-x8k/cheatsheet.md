# Cheatsheet - Constitution of the Italian Republic (1948)

## 📋 Quick Reference: Constitutional Structure

### The Republican Foundation
```
Italy is a Democratic Republic founded on Labor (Article 1)
├── Sovereignty belongs to the people (exercised in constitutional forms)
├── Republican form CANNOT be amended (Article 139 - eternity clause)
└── Labor is the foundation, not just an economic activity
```

### Fundamental Principles at a Glance

| Principle | Article | Key Meaning |
|-----------|---------|-------------|
| **Human Dignity** | 2 | Foundation of all inviolable rights |
| **Equality** | 3 | Formal + substantive equality, remove obstacles |
| **Right to Work** | 4 | Republic promotes conditions for work |
| **Unity** | 5 | Republic is one and indivisible |
| **Minorities** | 6 | Protect linguistic minorities |
| **International Peace** | 11 | Repudiates war, promotes peace and justice |

## 🏛️ Institutional Framework

### Parliament: Perfect Bicameralism (Title I - Articles 55-82)

```
Perfect Bicameralism: Both chambers have IDENTICAL powers
├── Chamber of Deputies
│   ├── Members: 400 (since 2020 reform, was 630)
│   ├── Election: Direct universal suffrage, proportional
│   ├── Voters: Age 18+
│   ├── Candidates: Age 25+
│   └── Term: 5 years
└── Senate of the Republic
    ├── Members: 200 elected + life senators
    ├── Election: Regional basis
    ├── Voters: Age 25+
    ├── Life Senators: Former Presidents + up to 5 appointed
    └── Term: 5 years
```

**Key Principle**: Every law must be approved by BOTH chambers in IDENTICAL terms

**Parliamentary Functions:**
- Legislative power (exercised collectively)
- Political direction and control of Government
- Representative mandate (represent the Nation, not just constituency)
- Incompatibility: Cannot hold other conflicting offices
- Immunity: Cannot be held accountable for opinions/votes in Parliament

### Legislative Process
```
1. Initiative: Government, any Parliament member, Regional Councils, CNEL, or 50,000 voters
2. Committee Stage: Examined by relevant committee
3. Chamber Debate: Amendments can be proposed
4. Second Chamber: Must approve identical text
5. Back to First Chamber: If amendments made, returns for approval
6. Final Approval: Both chambers must approve identical text
7. Promulgation: President promulgates within 1 month
```

**Special Procedures:**
- **Decree-Laws**: Government issues provisional measures with force of law; Parliament must convert within 60 days
- **Legislative Delegation**: Parliament can delegate law-making power to Government with limits and time constraints

### Government Oversight Mechanisms

| Mechanism | Threshold | Effect |
|-----------|-----------|--------|
| **Motion of No Confidence** | 1/10 of members to sign, absolute majority to pass, must name new PM | Brings down Government |
| **Question Time** | Any member | Government must answer |
| **Interpellation** | Any member | Government must explain policy |
| **Investiture Vote** | Absolute majority in both chambers | Government obtains confidence |

## 👔 Executive Branch

### President of the Republic (Title II - Articles 83-91)

```
Election:
├── Electoral College: Parliament in joint session + 3 regional representatives each (1 for Aosta Valley)
├── Majority: 2/3 (ballots 1-3), absolute (ballot 4+), relative (ballot 7+)
└── Term: 7 years, cannot be immediately re-elected
```

**Powers and Duties:**

| Category | Specific Powers | Checks/Balances |
|----------|-----------------|-----------------|
| **Representative** | Sends messages to chambers, authorizes Government bills | Must have parliamentary support |
| **Executive** | Appoints PM, appoints/dismisses Ministers on PM proposal | PM must have confidence; counter-signature required |
| **Legislative** | Promulgates laws, can dissolve chambers, calls elections | Limited timing (not last 6 months, not during war) |
| **Judicial** | President of CSM, grants pardons, commutes sentences | Judicial independence maintained |
| **Military** | Commands armed forces, presides over Supreme Defense Council | Government directs military through PM |
| **Emergency** | Extraordinary measures in necessity/urgency | Subject to Government responsibility |

**Checks on Presidential Power:**
- Counter-signature: Most acts require proponent Minister's signature (ensures Ministerial responsibility)
- Impeachment: Parliament can impeach for high treason or attack on Constitution
- Trial: Constitutional Court (with special composition) tries President
- Responsibility: Not responsible for acts in exercise of functions (except treason/attack on Constitution)

### Government (Title III - Articles 92-100)

```
Composition:
├── Prime Minister (President of Council of Ministers)
│   ├── Appointed by: President of Republic
│   ├── Must obtain: Confidence of both chambers within 10 days
│   └── Role: Directs and is responsible for general Government policy
└── Ministers
    ├── Appointed by: President on PM proposal
    ├── Take oath: Before President and Parliament
    └── Responsibility: Individually for their departments, collectively as Government
```

**Government Functions:**
- Determines and conducts national policy (domestic and foreign)
- Directs public administration
- Commands armed forces (through PM as President of Council)
- Issues regulations for law execution

**Government-Parliament Relations:**
- Must have and maintain confidence of BOTH chambers
- Can be removed by constructive motion of no confidence
- Must answer questions and interpellations
- Must inform Parliament on its policy

## ⚖️ Judiciary (Title IV - Articles 101-113)

### Judicial Independence Framework
```
Principles:
├── Judges subject ONLY to the law (Article 101)
├── Judges are IRREMOVABLE (cannot be removed from office)
└── Judges are independent and impartial
```

**Judicial Organization:**

| Branch | Jurisdiction | Highest Body |
|--------|-------------|--------------|
| **Ordinary** | Civil and criminal matters | Supreme Court of Cassation |
| **Administrative** | Citizens vs. public administration | Council of State |
| **Accounting** | Public finance | Court of Accounts |
| **Military** | Military offenses (war time only) | Military Courts |
| **Constitutional** | Constitutional matters | Constitutional Court |

### Supreme Council of the Judiciary (CSM)
```
Composition:
├── Elected judges (majority)
├── Members from Parliament
└── Law professors

Functions:
├── Opinions on judicial appointments
├── Disciplinary actions
├── Career advancement
└── Judges' assignments
```

## 🏛️ Constitutional Court (Title VI - Articles 134-137)

```
Composition: 15 judges
├── 5 appointed by President of Republic
├── 5 elected by Parliament (3 by Chamber, 2 by Senate)
└── 5 elected by supreme ordinary and administrative courts

Term: 9 years, cannot be re-appointed
Incompatibilities: Cannot be member of Parliament or Regional Council
```

**Jurisdiction:**
- Judges constitutionality of laws and acts with force of law (State and Regions)
- Resolves conflicts of attribution between powers of State
- Resolves conflicts between State and Regions
- Judges accusations against President for high treason or attack on Constitution
- Can be accessed by State, Regions, or any person claiming constitutional rights violation

## 🗺️ Territorial Organization (Title V - Articles 114-133)

### Republic Composition
```
Italy = Municipalities + Provinces + Metropolitan Cities + Regions + State
All are autonomous entities with own statutes, powers, and functions
```

### Regions

| Type | Number | Special Features |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| **Ordinary Regions** | 15 | Standard competencies |
| **Special Statute Regions** | 5 | Enhanced autonomy |

**Special Statute Regions:**
1. Sicily
2. Sardinia
3. Trentino-Alto Adige
4. Friuli-Venezia Giulia
5. Valle d'Aosta

**Regional Institutions:**
- **Regional Council**: Legislative body (elected)
- **Giunta Regionale**: Executive body
- **President**: Head of regional government

**Regional Powers:**
- Legislative power in matters not reserved to State
- Concurrent legislation in some areas
- Administrative functions
- Statute as fundamental norm

### Local Governments

| Level | Role | Institutions |
|-------|------|--------------|
| **Municipalities (Comuni)** | Fundamental local entities | Mayor + Council |
| **Provinces** | Intermediate level | President + Council |
| **Metropolitan Cities** | Large urban areas | Special governance |

### Subsidiarity Principle
**Functions are attributed to:**
1. Municipalities (default)
2. Provinces (if Municipalities cannot exercise adequately)
3. Regions (if lower levels cannot exercise adequately)
4. State (subsidiary role, only if lower levels cannot exercise)

**Purpose:** Ensure exercise of functions as close to citizens as possible

### State-Regions Relations
- **Cooperation**: In matters of common interest
- **Coordination**: Special bodies for coordination
- **Conflict Resolution**: Constitutional Court resolves conflicts of attribution
- **Financial Autonomy**: Resources follow competencies

## 📝 Constitutional Amendment (Title VI - Articles 134-139)

### Amendment Procedure
```
1. Initiative: Members of Parliament, Government, Regional Councils, or 500,000 voters
2. First Reading: Each chamber approves text
3. Interval: At least 3 months between readings
4. Second Reading: Each chamber approves text AGAIN
5. Majority: Second approval requires absolute majority in each chamber
6. Referendum (optional): If requested by 1/5 of either chamber, 500,000 voters, or 5 Regional Councils
7. Promulgation: President promulgates constitutional law
```

**Eternity Clause (Article 139):**
- **REPUBLICAN FORM cannot be amended under ANY circumstances**
- This is the only explicit unamendable provision

## 🎯 Key Constitutional Principles

### 10 Most Important Principles
1. **Democratic Republic Founded on Labor** (Art. 1) - Defines Italian state identity
2. **Popular Sovereignty** (Art. 1) - Power belongs to the people
3. **Human Dignity** (Art. 2) - Foundation of all rights
4. **Substantive Equality** (Art. 3) - Republic must remove obstacles to equality
5. **Perfect Bicameralism** (Art. 55-82) - Both chambers have equal powers
6. **Constructive No Confidence** (Art. 94) - Motion must name new Prime Minister
7. **Judicial Independence** (Art. 101-113) - Judges subject only to law
8. **Republican Form Unamendable** (Art. 139) - Eternity clause
9. **Regional Autonomy** (Art. 114-133) - Decentralized territorial organization
10. **Constitutional Court Review** (Art. 134-137) - Guarantees constitutional supremacy

### Quick Decision Tree: Which Institution?
```
Is it about...
├── Rights interpretation? → Constitutional Court (if constitutional question) OR Ordinary Judiciary
├── Law-making? → Parliament (both chambers must approve)
├── Government formation? → President appoints PM, Parliament grants confidence
├── Government removal? → Parliament (both chambers) via constructive motion of no confidence
├── Constitutional dispute? → Constitutional Court
├── Regional autonomy? → Regions with their own statutes and powers
├── Judicial appointment? → Supreme Council of the Judiciary (CSM)
├── President accountability? → Constitutional Court (for treason/attack on Constitution)
└── Local government? → Municipalities, Provinces, Metropolitan Cities
```
