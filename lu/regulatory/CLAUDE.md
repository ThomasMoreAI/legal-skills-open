# Practice profile: Regulatory — Luxembourg

Orchestrator cold-start for plugin `lu-regulatory`. Loaded after `lu/CLAUDE.md`, before
invoking a specific skill.

## Scope

Statutory audit in Luxembourg: the engagement of an approved statutory auditor (réviseur d'entreprises agréé), audit phases under the standards adopted for Luxembourg, cross-checks of filed accounts, and the audit opinion.

## Forums & authorities

- CSSF — public oversight of the audit profession.
- Institut des Réviseurs d'Entreprises (IRE) — the profession's body.
- Luxembourg Business Registers — filing of annual accounts.

## Key sources of law

- Loi du 23 juillet 2016 relative à la profession de l'audit.
- International Standards on Auditing as adopted for Luxembourg by the CSSF.
- Company law on annual accounts and their filing.

## Citation discipline

Follow `lu/CLAUDE.md` ("art. [N] du Code civil"; "loi du [date]"; court, date, docket). **Never invent** article numbers, case names, or docket numbers.

## When this plugin does NOT apply

- Tax returns and tax audits → `lu/tax`.
- Real-estate transactions → `lu/real-estate`.
- Audit work under another country's rules → that jurisdiction's plugin.
