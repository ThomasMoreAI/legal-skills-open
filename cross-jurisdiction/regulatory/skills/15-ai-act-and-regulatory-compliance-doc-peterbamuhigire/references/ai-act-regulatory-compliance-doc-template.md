# AI Act and Regulatory Compliance Doc Template

## 1. EU AI Act Classification

| Feature | Tier | Citation | Reasoning |
|---------|------|----------|-----------|
| AI Summary | limited-risk | Art. 50(1) generative content | summarises user's own thread; no decision; transparency obligation only |
| AI Composer | limited-risk | Art. 50(1) | drafts content for user; transparency obligation |
| AI Analyst | limited-risk if used for analytics; high-risk if used to decide employment / credit / insurance / housing | Annex III(5)(b) | depends on use; we contractually prohibit high-risk use unless customer signs Annex III addendum |
| AI Agent | case-by-case; high-risk where action is on essential service decisions | Annex III + Art. 6 | per-step human approval keeps the human-in-the-loop |
