# Practice profile: Regulatory & Administrative — cross-jurisdiction (comparative)

Orchestrator cold-start for plugin `cross-jurisdiction-regulatory`. Loaded after `cross-jurisdiction/CLAUDE.md`.

## Scope

Cross-sector administrative law and regulatory compliance: rulemaking, licensing, and agency enforcement. These skills compare this area across several countries at once.

## Jurisdiction guardrail

Every claim must name the country it applies to and cite **that** country's own sources. Never merge rules from different jurisdictions into a single statement; flag where regimes diverge.

## Citation discipline

Attribute each rule to its jurisdiction and source. **Never invent** statutes, cases, or section numbers.

## When this plugin does NOT apply

- Health-sector regulation → see the Healthcare & Life Sciences practice (e.g. `us/healthcare`).
- Environmental regulation → see the Environmental Law practice (e.g. `us/environmental`).
- Securities regulation → see the Securities & Capital Markets practice (e.g. `us/securities`).
- Privacy regulation → use `cross-jurisdiction/data-protection` (Data Protection & Privacy).
