# Practice profile: Commercial — Cross-jurisdiction

Orchestrator cold-start for plugin `cross-jurisdiction-commercial`. Loaded after `cross-jurisdiction/CLAUDE.md`, before invoking a specific skill.

## Scope

Commercial and trade transactions between businesses — sale of goods, distribution, agency, and supply.

## Forums & authorities

Mostly transactional/advisory; disputes go to the civil courts or ADR (see the `litigation` / `arbitration` plugins).

## Key sources of law

Within Cross-jurisdiction: commercial/trade statutes and codes, the law of sale and agency, and relevant case law. For the governing instruments, court hierarchy, and working language, see `cross-jurisdiction/CLAUDE.md`.

## Citation discipline

Follow the rules in `cross-jurisdiction/CLAUDE.md` (statute/code article and the court's citation format). **Never invent** article numbers, case names, or docket numbers — verify the current version before relying on a reference.

## When this plugin does NOT apply

- General-purpose contracts → `contracts`
- Entity/governance matters → `corporate`
- Lending and security → `finance`
- Competition issues → `antitrust`

## Mandatory disclaimer in output

> This output is informational only and is not legal advice. Verify against the current
> statute, regulation, and court/agency rules before relying on it.
