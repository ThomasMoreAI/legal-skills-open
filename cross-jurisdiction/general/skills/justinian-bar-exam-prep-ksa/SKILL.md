# Justinian — Saudi Bar Exam Prep (KSA)

## Scope

The Saudi bar qualification, administered by the Saudi Authority for Lawyers (SAL), underwent significant reform starting in 2023, making it the central qualification gateway for licensed legal practice in the Kingdom. The exam reflects Saudi Arabia's dual legal system: a Sharia foundation overlaid with modern codified statutes enacted in conjunction with Vision 2030 reforms.

## Exam format (post-2023)

| Component | Format | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Multiple choice questions (MCQs) | Written (Arabic primary; some English sections post-2023) | 2–3 hours |
| Essays / legal problems | Written application of law to fact patterns | 2–3 hours |
| Oral examination | Interview with panel on legal knowledge and ethics | 15–30 minutes |

Arabic is the primary examination language. Some technical sections (particularly arbitration and commercial law) may allow English-language responses under post-2023 reforms — verify current SAL guidance.

## Subject coverage

### Sharia foundations — fiqh al-muamalat

Essential for all legal practice in KSA, where Sharia is the source of all law (Basic Law of Governance, Article 1):

- Principles of Islamic commercial transactions (fiqh al-muamalat): sale, lease, partnership, agency
- Prohibition of gharar (excessive uncertainty) and riba (interest): practical application in contracts and finance
- Islamic endowment (waqf) law
- Inheritance under Islamic law (mirath)

### Constitutional and administrative framework

- Basic Law of Governance (Nizam al-Asasi lil-Hukm)
- Council of Ministers Law
- Board of Grievances (the primary administrative court)
- Anti-Corruption Commission (Nazaha)

### Commercial law

- **Companies Law 2022** (major overhaul): LLC, JSC, partnership types, shareholder rights, dissolution
- **Commercial Court Law** and its Implementing Regulations (2021): dedicated commercial courts replacing mixed tribunals
- **Commercial Register Law**: incorporation, licensing
- **Agency and Distribution Law**: principles and limitations

### Labor and employment law

- **Labor Law** (Royal Decree M/51 and subsequent amendments, including significant 2024 amendments)
- Saudization (Nitaqat) requirements and employee-to-national ratios
- Termination, end-of-service gratuity, notice periods
- Occupational health and safety
- Collective labor and HRSD (Human Resources and Social Development Ministry) procedures

### Real estate

- **Real Estate Transaction Tax (RETT)**: rates, exemptions, registration
- Real Estate Registration Law and the Rosoom digital registry
- Construction permits and municipal law

### Intellectual property

- **Saudi Authority for Intellectual Property (SAIP)** — trademarks, patents, copyright
- GCC Trademark Law and Saudi implementing rules

### Data protection and privacy

- **Saudi PDPL** (Personal Data Protection Law): issued by Royal Decree 2021, effective 2023, amended 2024
- Cross-border data transfer requirements
- Sector-specific obligations (health data, financial data)

### AML and sanctions

- **Anti-Money Laundering Law** and Implementing Regulations
- FATF 40 Recommendations and Saudi FATF membership obligations
- Suspicious transaction reporting, KYC obligations
- UN and OFAC sanctions: practical KSA application

### Civil procedure

- **Code of Civil Procedure (post-2022 reforms)**: litigation before the general courts
- Summary proceedings, precautionary measures
- Enforcement of judgments, including foreign judgments

### Arbitration

- **Arbitration Law** (Royal Decree M/34, 2012) and its Implementing Regulations
- **Saudi Center for Commercial Arbitration (SCCA) Rules**
- Arbitrability, appointment, challenge, enforcement
- Interaction between Sharia and arbitration (governing-law issues)

## Exam technique

### MCQ strategy
- Saudi MCQs test precise statutory knowledge — the distinction between "may" and "shall" in the Arabic text matters
- Practice with Arabic-language questions to build terminology fluency
- For Sharia questions: use the principle to eliminate clearly wrong answers; then identify which established fiqh school's position (typically Hanbali in KSA) is reflected

### Essay strategy
Standard Saudi legal essay format:
1. State the legal issue precisely
2. Cite the applicable nizam (statute) and article
3. Apply to the facts
4. Conclude

### Oral exam
- Expect questions on ethics (conduct of lawyers under the SAL Code of Professional Conduct)
- Demonstrate calm command of Sharia + statutory interaction
- Be prepared to discuss recent reforms (Companies Law 2022, PDPL 2024 amendments)

## Caveats

The SAL exam format continues to evolve post-2023. Always verify the current exam structure, subject list, and registration requirements directly with the Saudi Authority for Lawyers before sitting. This guide reflects publicly known frameworks and should be used for preparation, not as an authoritative source on current exam rules.
