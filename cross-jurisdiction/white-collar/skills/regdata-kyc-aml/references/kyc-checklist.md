# EU 6AMLD KYC/AML Compliance Checklist

Reference checklist for entity verification under the EU 6th Anti-Money Laundering Directive (Directive 2018/1673) and related national legislation.

---

## Customer Due Diligence (CDD) - Standard Requirements

- [ ] **Identify the customer** - obtain official name, legal form, registration number, registered address
- [ ] **Verify identity** - confirm against an authoritative source (government registry, official document)
- [ ] **Identify the beneficial owner(s)** - determine all natural persons who ultimately own or control the entity
- [ ] **Verify beneficial owner identity** - confirm UBO information against a reliable source (CRBR, Societe.com, etc.)
- [ ] **Assess purpose of business relationship** - document why the relationship is being established
- [ ] **Conduct ongoing monitoring** - define review frequency based on risk level
- [ ] **Document everything** - retain all CDD records for minimum 5 years after end of relationship

## Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) - When Required

EDD is mandatory when any of these triggers are present:

- [ ] Customer or UBO is a Politically Exposed Person (PEP) or close associate/family member of PEP
- [ ] Customer is established in a high-risk third country (per EU list, updated periodically)
- [ ] Transaction is unusually complex or large with no apparent economic purpose
- [ ] Correspondent banking relationships with non-EEA institutions
- [ ] Any other scenario where risk assessment indicates elevated ML/TF risk

**Additional EDD measures:**

- [ ] Obtain senior management approval to establish or continue the relationship
- [ ] Establish source of wealth and source of funds
- [ ] Conduct enhanced ongoing monitoring with more frequent reviews
- [ ] Obtain additional documents, data, or information from reliable sources
- [ ] First payment through an account in the customer's name at an EEA credit institution

## UBO Identification Rules

**Primary test - ownership:**
- [ ] Identify any natural person holding >25% of shares or voting rights (directly or indirectly)
- [ ] For indirect holdings, trace through each level of the ownership chain
- [ ] Calculate effective ownership: multiply percentages through each tier (e.g., 60% of 50% = 30% effective)

**Secondary test - control through other means:**
- [ ] Shareholder agreements granting control beyond shareholding percentage
- [ ] Right to appoint or remove a majority of the board of directors
- [ ] Veto rights on key corporate decisions
- [ ] Contractual control (management agreements, franchise agreements, etc.)
- [ ] Control through trusts, foundations, or similar arrangements

**Fallback:**
- [ ] If no natural person is identified through ownership or control, record the senior managing official(s) as UBO
- [ ] Document the steps taken to identify the UBO and the reason for falling back

## Obliged Entity Categories

These entity types are required to perform CDD under 6AMLD:

- Credit institutions (banks)
- Financial institutions (payment services, e-money, investment firms)
- Auditors, external accountants, tax advisors
- Notaries and independent legal professionals (when involved in financial/real estate transactions)
- Trust or company service providers
- Estate agents (when involved in transactions exceeding EUR 10,000)
- Persons trading in goods (cash payments exceeding EUR 10,000)
- Providers of gambling services
- Crypto-asset service providers (added under expanded scope)

## Record-Keeping Requirements

- [ ] Copy of identity documents or reference to verification source - retain 5 years
- [ ] CDD and transaction records - retain 5 years after end of business relationship
- [ ] Risk assessment documentation - retain 5 years
- [ ] Suspicious activity reports (SARs) - retain per national FIU requirements
- [ ] Records must be sufficient to reconstruct individual transactions
- [ ] Records must be available to competent authorities and FIU on request

## Suspicious Transaction Reporting

Obliged entities must report to the national Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU) when:

- [ ] There is knowledge, suspicion, or reasonable grounds to suspect ML/TF
- [ ] Attempted transactions that raise suspicion, even if not completed
- [ ] Transactions inconsistent with knowledge of the customer's profile

**National FIUs:**
- Poland: Generalny Inspektor Informacji Finansowej (GIIF)
- France: Tracfin
- Austria: Geldwaeschemeldestelle (A-FIU)
- Spain: SEPBLAC

**Tipping-off prohibition:** Do not disclose to the customer or third parties that a SAR has been filed.

## PEP Screening Requirements

A Politically Exposed Person is a natural person who holds or has held a prominent public function:

- Heads of state, government, ministers, deputy ministers
- Members of parliament or similar legislative bodies
- Members of supreme courts, constitutional courts
- Members of courts of auditors or boards of central banks
- Ambassadors, charges d'affaires, senior military officers
- Members of administrative, management, or supervisory bodies of state-owned enterprises
- Directors and deputy directors of international organizations

**Extended scope:** Family members (spouse, children, parents) and close associates of PEPs are also subject to EDD.

**De-PEP period:** Most jurisdictions require continued EDD for 12-18 months after a PEP leaves their position.

## Polish AML Act - Specific Requirements

Poland's national transposition (Ustawa o przeciwdzialaniu praniu pieniedzy oraz finansowaniu terroryzmu, Dz.U. 2018 poz. 723) includes additional requirements:

- [ ] **CRBR registration** - all legal entities must register beneficial owners in the Central Register of Beneficial Owners (CRBR) within 7 days of incorporation or change
- [ ] **GIIF reporting** - suspicious transactions must be reported to the General Inspector of Financial Information
- [ ] **Transaction threshold** - mandatory CDD for occasional transactions >= EUR 15,000 (or equivalent)
- [ ] **Domestic PEP list** - Poland maintains its own PEP definitions aligned with EU standards
- [ ] **Group-wide policies** - parent companies of Polish subsidiaries must ensure group-wide AML/CFT procedures
- [ ] **Penalties** - up to PLN 5,000,000 or 10% of annual turnover for non-compliance

## High-Risk Third Country Considerations

When the customer, UBO, or transaction involves a high-risk third country (per EU Delegated Regulation):

- [ ] EDD is mandatory (not discretionary)
- [ ] Obtain additional information on the customer and UBO
- [ ] Obtain additional information on the intended nature of the business relationship
- [ ] Obtain information on the source of funds and source of wealth
- [ ] Obtain information on the reasons for the intended transactions
- [ ] Obtain senior management approval
- [ ] Conduct enhanced monitoring of the business relationship

**Current EU high-risk list** is updated periodically - always verify against the latest Commission Delegated Regulation.
