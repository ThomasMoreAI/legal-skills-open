Read the base act together with the operative amendment or current consolidated text.

## Application milestones as verified on 2026-08-09

Confirm these against current legislation before relying on them:

| Date | Operational milestone |
|---|---|
| 1 August 2024 | Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 entered into force. |
| 2 February 2025 | General provisions including AI literacy and the original prohibited-practice rules began applying, subject to later-amended exceptions. |
| 2 August 2025 | Governance provisions and GPAI-model obligations began applying. |
| 2 August 2026 | The general application date arrived; Article 50 transparency duties and enforcement for applicable duties began, subject to transitions and later amendments. |
| 2 December 2026 | New prohibited-practice provisions introduced by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 apply; the Article 50(2) transition for certain systems placed on the market before 2 August 2026 ends. |
| 2 August 2027 | Providers of GPAI models placed on the market before 2 August 2025 must comply, subject to any later amendment. |
| 2 December 2027 | Chapter III Sections 1–3 apply to high-risk systems classified under Article 6(2) and Annex III, except as specified in the amended text. |
| 2 August 2028 | Chapter III Sections 1–3 apply to high-risk systems classified under Article 6(1) and Annex I, except as specified in the amended text. |

Do not collapse “application,” “transition,” “enforcement power,” and an organization's own product deadline into one date. Legacy systems, public-authority use, substantial modifications, GPAI placement dates, and sector rules can change the result.

## Per-assessment legal update record

- Base regulation and amendment/consolidation identifier.
- Provisions relied on and whether later measures affect them.
- Relevant Member State authority and national measures.
- Guidance/standard status: final, draft, harmonised, common specification, voluntary code, or other.
- Qualified counsel reviewer, questions, and decision date.

If official sources cannot be reached or conflict, mark the relevant conclusion `pending current-law verification`.

## Staleness and reassessment

Do not treat a fixed age threshold as a legal safe harbor. The inventory checker uses 365 days by default only as an administrative backstop and allows a different documented threshold. Reassess immediately when the intended use, system/model version, vendor, geography, operator role, material modification, incident history, or governing law/guidance changes. A current date does not cure an event-triggered stale assessment.
