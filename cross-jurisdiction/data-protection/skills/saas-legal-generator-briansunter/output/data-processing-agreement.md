## 1. Background and Scope

### 1.1 Background

The Controller engages the Processor to provide [DESCRIPTION_OF_SERVICES] (the
"Services"), which involve the processing of personal data on behalf of the
Controller.

### 1.2 Scope

This DPA applies to the processing of Personal Data (as defined below) by the
Processor on behalf of the Controller in connection with the Services.

This DPA forms an integral part of the [MASTER_SERVICE_AGREEMENT] between the
Parties (the "Agreement").

### 1.3 GDPR Compliance

This DPA is intended to satisfy the requirements of Article 28(3) of the General
Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR") and any national implementing
laws, regulations, and secondary legislation.

## 2. Definitions

**"Affiliate"** means any entity that controls, is controlled by, or is under
common control with a Party.

**"Data Subject"** means an identified or identifiable natural person to whom
Personal Data relates.

**"GDPR"** means Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the
Council of 27 April 2016 on the protection of natural persons with regard to the
processing of personal data and on the free movement of such data, and any
national implementing laws, regulations, and secondary legislation.

**"Personal Data"** means any information relating to an identified or
identifiable natural person ("Data Subject").

**"Personal Data Breach"** means a breach of security leading to the accidental
or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure of, or access
to, Personal Data transmitted, stored, or otherwise processed.

**"Processing"** means any operation or set of operations which is performed on
Personal Data or on sets of Personal Data, whether or not by automated means.

## 3. Processor's Obligations

### 3.1 Acting on Instructions

The Processor shall process Personal Data only on documented instructions from
the Controller, unless required to do so by European Union or Member State law
to which the Processor is subject. In such case, the Processor shall inform the
Controller of that legal requirement before processing, unless that law
prohibits such information on important grounds of public interest.

### 3.2 Processing Details

The Processor shall:

- Process Personal Data only for the specific purpose(s) set out in the
  Agreement
- Process Personal Data in accordance with Appendix A (Processing Activities)
- Implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure a level
  of security appropriate to the risk
- Not process Personal Data for any purpose other than the performance of the
  Services

### 3.3 Confidentiality

The Processor shall ensure that persons authorized to process Personal Data are
committed to confidentiality or are under an appropriate statutory obligation of
confidentiality.

### 3.4 Security Measures

The Processor shall implement appropriate technical and organizational measures
to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk, including:

- **Pseudonymization and encryption** of Personal Data
- **Ability to ensure confidentiality, integrity, availability, and resilience**
  of processing systems and services
- **Ability to restore availability and access to Personal Data** in a timely
  manner in the event of a physical or technical incident
- **Process for regularly testing, assessing, and evaluating** the effectiveness
  of technical and organizational measures
- **Measures for user identification and authorization**
- **Measures for protection of data during transmission**
- **Measures for protection of data against unauthorized access**

The Controller may object to the appointment or replacement of a subprocessor if

### 3.6 Data Subject Rights

Taking into account the nature of the processing, the Processor shall assist the
Controller by implementing appropriate technical and organizational measures,
insofar as this is possible, for the fulfillment of the Controller's obligation
to respond to requests for exercising the Data Subject's rights laid down in
Chapter III of the GDPR.

These rights include:

- Right of access (Article 15)
- Right to rectification (Article 16)
- Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten") (Article 17)
- Right to restriction of processing (Article 18)
- Right to data portability (Article 20)
- Right to object (Article 21)

### 3.7 Data Deletion or Return

After the end of the provision of Services, the Processor shall, at the
Controller's choice:

- Delete all Personal Data processed on behalf of the Controller, or
- Return all Personal Data to the Controller

The Processor shall also delete existing copies unless European Union or Member
State law requires storage of Personal Data.

## 4. Data Breach Notification

### 4.1 Notification to Controller

The Processor shall notify the Controller without undue delay upon becoming
aware of a Personal Data Breach.

The notification shall at least:

- Describe the nature of the Personal Data Breach
- Provide the name and contact details of the contact point
- Describe the likely consequences of the Personal Data Breach
- Describe the measures taken or proposed to address the Personal Data Breach

### 4.2 Notification to Data Subjects

In the event of a Personal Data Breach that is likely to result in a high risk
to the rights and freedoms of Data Subjects, the Processor shall provide the
Controller with sufficient information to allow the Controller to meet its
obligation to notify the Data Subjects without undue delay.

### 4.3 Notification to Supervisory Authority

The Processor shall cooperate with the Controller, to the extent possible, to
enable the Controller to fulfill its obligation to notify the relevant
supervisory authority of a Personal Data Breach without undue delay and, where
feasible, not later than 72 hours after becoming aware of the breach.

## 5. Data Protection Impact Assessment and Prior Consultation

The Processor shall provide reasonable assistance to the Controller, to the
extent possible, with:

- Data protection impact assessments
- Prior consultations with the supervisory authority

as required by Articles 35 and 36 of the GDPR.

## 6. Controller's Responsibility

The Controller shall:

- Ensure that the processing instructions are lawful and comply with GDPR
- Obtain any required consents from Data Subjects
- Provide Data Subject privacy notices
- Maintain records of processing activities (Article 30 GDPR)
- Respond to Data Subject rights requests
- Conduct Data Protection Impact Assessments when required
- Notify supervisory authorities of Personal Data Breaches
- Implement appropriate technical and organizational measures

## 7. Audit and Inspection Rights

### 7.1 Audit Rights

The Processor shall make available to the Controller all information necessary
to demonstrate compliance with the obligations laid down in this DPA and allow
for and contribute to audits, including inspections, by the Controller or
another auditor mandated by the Controller.

### 7.2 Inspection Notice

The Controller shall provide reasonable notice (at least [NOTICE_PERIOD] days)
before conducting an audit or inspection.

### 7.3 Confidential Information

During any audit or inspection, the Processor may redact or withhold
confidential or proprietary information to the extent permitted by applicable
law.

## 8. International Data Transfers

### 8.1 Transfer to Third Countries

The Processor shall not transfer Personal Data to a country outside the European
Economic Area ("Third Country") unless:

- The Controller has authorized the transfer in writing, and
- Appropriate safeguards are in place in accordance with GDPR Chapter V

### 8.2 Standard Contractual Clauses

Where Personal Data is transferred to a Third Country, the Parties agree to rely
on Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission for the
transfer of Personal Data.

The Processor shall inform the Controller of any material change that affects
the Standard Contractual Clauses.

### 8.3 US Data Transfers

For transfers to the United States, the Parties agree to implement appropriate
safeguards, which may include:

- Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)
- Binding Corporate Rules (BCRs)
- GDPR Contractual Clauses
- Certification mechanisms (where applicable)

## 9. Term and Termination

### 9.1 Term

This DPA shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for the duration of
the Agreement.

### 9.2 Termination

Upon termination or expiration of the Agreement, the Processor shall, at the
Controller's option:

- Return all Personal Data to the Controller, or
- Securely delete all Personal Data

The Processor shall provide written certification of deletion within [TIMEFRAME]

### 9.3 Survival

The provisions of this DPA that are intended to survive termination shall
survive, including:

- Confidentiality obligations (Clause 3.3)
- Security measures (Clause 3.4)
- Data deletion obligations (Clause 3.7 and Clause 9.2)
- Audit rights (Clause 7) for [SURVIVAL_PERIOD] months
- Governing law and dispute resolution (Clause 12)

## 10. Limitation of Liability

### 10.1 Cap on Liability

The Processor's aggregate liability under this DPA shall be limited to
[LIABILITY_CAP] or the fees paid under the Agreement in the [TIMEFRAME] months

### 10.2 Exclusions

The liability cap shall not apply to:

- Death or personal injury caused by Processor's negligence
- Fraud or willful misconduct
- Breach of confidentiality obligations
- Data Protection Breaches caused by Processor's failure to implement
  appropriate security measures

## 11. Indemnification

The Processor shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the Controller from and
against any losses, damages, liabilities, and expenses arising from:

- The Processor's breach of this DPA
- The Processor's failure to implement appropriate security measures
- The Processor's unauthorized disclosure of Personal Data
- The Processor's violation of applicable data protection laws
