# Saint Lucia 🇱🇨 — Property Due-Diligence Playbook

ISO2: `lc`. Status: ✅ Fully populated (researched 2026-05-28).

> **Critical framing — read first**: Saint Lucia is an **English common-law Eastern-Caribbean island state** (Windward Islands, member of the **OECS** and **CARICOM**), independent since 22 Feb 1979, a Commonwealth realm with King Charles III as head of state (represented by a Governor-General). The currency **East Caribbean Dollar (XCD / EC$) is pegged to the USD at EC$2.70 = US$1.00** since 1976 via the [Eastern Caribbean Central Bank (ECCB)](https://www.eccb-centralbank.org/) — a hard, long-standing peg shared across the eight ECCU members. **The single most important foreign-buyer fork**: a foreigner buying property OUTSIDE an approved Citizenship-by-Investment (CBI) project MUST obtain an **Alien Landholding Licence (ALHL)** under the **Aliens (Licensing) Act, Cap. 15.37**; a foreigner buying THROUGH an approved CBI real-estate project does **NOT** need an ALHL (the CBI route is self-contained). **Saint Lucia DOES operate a Citizenship-by-Investment Programme**, administered by the [Citizenship by Investment Unit (CIU)](https://www.cipsaintlucia.com/): the **real-estate route minimum is US$300,000** (effective 1 July 2024 under the OECS Memorandum of Agreement, which RAISED it from US$200,000), plus applicable administration fees, with a **5-year minimum holding period** (CBI Act No. 14 of 2015). ⚠️ **CIU FAQ-page conflict (load-bearing)**: the CIU's own `/faqs` page STILL shows a stale "US$200,000" real-estate figure — the dated `/citizenship-by-investment` pricing page (US$300,000, with a July-2024 Updated-Prices reference) **supersedes** it; use **US$300,000** and flag the conflict in any client output. **Transaction taxes** (Inland Revenue Department): **stamp duty 2% buyer-side** (citizen + non-citizen alike); **vendor-side** stamp duty is **banded for citizens** but a **flat 10% for a non-citizen seller**; **annual property tax 0.25% residential / 0.4% commercial** of open market value. **Court — read carefully**: Saint Lucia's **final appellate court is the Caribbean Court of Justice (CCJ) since 20 July 2023** (it abolished UK Privy Council appeals on that date — do NOT cite the JCPC as final court for Saint Lucia); first-instance and intermediate appeals run through the **Eastern Caribbean Supreme Court (ECSC)**. **Principal hazards**: Atlantic-basin **hurricanes** (season 1 Jun – 30 Nov) + **localised volcanic/geothermal hazard** at the **Soufrière / Qualibou depression (SW coast)** — the most-likely future activity is a phreatic (steam) eruption per UWI-SRC; the **NW expat corridor (Rodney Bay / Cap Estate) is well clear** of the volcanic centre. **Confirm** any current CBI figure, ALHL fee schedule, stamp-duty rate, and volcanic-hazard zoning at primary sources **before any binding step**. Authoritative primary sources: **[Citizenship by Investment Unit (CIU)](https://www.cipsaintlucia.com/)**, **[Inland Revenue Department (IRD)](https://irdstlucia.gov.lc/)**, **[Government of Saint Lucia portal](https://www.govt.lc/)**, **[LUCELEC](https://www.lucelec.com/)**, **[National Utilities Regulatory Commission (NURC)](https://nurc.org.lc/)**, **[UWI Seismic Research Centre](https://uwiseismic.com/island-profiles/st-lucia/)**, **[ECCB](https://www.eccb-centralbank.org/)**.

## Country profile

- **Geography**: Volcanic Windward Island, **~617 km²**, central Eastern-Caribbean island chain between Martinique (N) and St. Vincent & the Grenadines (S). Capital **Castries** (NW). The dramatic twin **Pitons** (Gros Piton + Petit Piton) near Soufrière (SW) are a UNESCO World Heritage Site. The volcanic interior is mountainous and forested; the prime expat/luxury market clusters in the NW (Gros Islet district — Rodney Bay, Cap Estate, Pigeon Island).
- **Population**: **~180,000** (World Bank ~179,000–180,000, 2023 est. — verify at [World Bank Saint Lucia](https://data.worldbank.org/country/st-lucia)). Tourism-dependent economy; English official.
- **GDP / economy**: **Tourism-dependent** (largest sector + FX earner) + offshore financial services + light manufacturing + agriculture (bananas historically). USD widely used in the tourism/property sector despite XCD being sole legal tender. Verify macro figures at [ECCB](https://www.eccb-centralbank.org/) + [World Bank](https://data.worldbank.org/country/st-lucia).
- **Currency**: **East Caribbean Dollar (XCD / EC$)** — **pegged to the USD at EC$2.70 = US$1.00 since 1976** via the [ECCB](https://www.eccb-centralbank.org/). The peg is hard and shared across the eight-member Eastern Caribbean Currency Union (ECCU); currency risk for a USD-funded buyer is minimal — EUR/GBP buyers carry a USD-cross exposure.
- **Languages**: **English (official, sole)** — all contracts, deeds, statutes in English. **Saint Lucian French Creole (Kwéyòl)** is the everyday vernacular, but legal/professional life is standard English.
- **Legal system**: A **hybrid** — Saint Lucia's **Civil Code** (Quebec/Code-Napoléon-derived, a legacy of French rule) coexists with overlaid **English common law** and statute; conveyancing and notarial form retain civil-law characteristics. **Final appellate court: the [Caribbean Court of Justice (CCJ)](https://ccj.org/) since 20 July 2023** — Saint Lucia abolished Privy Council (JCPC) appeals on that date (per [govt.lc "Saint Lucia accedes to the CCJ"](https://www.govt.lc/) + CCJ member-states listing). The **[Eastern Caribbean Supreme Court (ECSC)](https://www.eccourts.org/)** remains the superior court of record — its **High Court** has a resident division for Saint Lucia, and the **Court of Appeal** is itinerant across the OECS; only the *final* appellate tier changed to the CCJ.
- **Land law / cadastre**: the **Land Registration Act 1984** replaced the old deeds-recording system with a **state-guaranteed title-registration system** (the State examines, registers, and guarantees title — indefeasible except for fraud). **Land Registry** sits within the relevant Ministry; title searches + copies via [govt.lc — request a copy of a land registry document](https://www.govt.lc/services/request-a-copy-of-a-land-registry-document). A **digital registry (e-LRIS)** modernisation has been announced (⚠️ verify live status).
- **Identifier**: registered-title **block + parcel reference** under the Land Registration Act 1984 (the registered-land framework). No universal national parcel ID equivalent to a French *numéro de parcelle*.
- **Title concept**: **Freehold** dominant; condominium/strata regimes exist for apartment + branded-residence developments (verify the specific declaration/by-laws per development).
- **Recent reforms (12-month watchlist)**:
  - **CBI real-estate minimum raised to US$300,000** (from US$200,000) effective **1 July 2024** under the **OECS Memorandum of Agreement (MoA)** harmonising a common pricing floor across the five OECS CBI nations (St Lucia, Antigua & Barbuda, Dominica, Grenada, St Kitts & Nevis) ([CIU pricing page](https://www.cipsaintlucia.com/citizenship-by-investment)).
  - **CCJ accession (20 July 2023)** — final appellate court switched from the UK Privy Council to the Caribbean Court of Justice. **No effect on property *substantive* law**, but the court of last resort for any title dispute is now the CCJ.
  - **Act No. 2 of 2025 — Security Interest in Movable Property (Amendment) Act** is listed in Saint Lucia's own law repository ([Saint Lucia National Printing Corporation — Acts of 2025](https://npc.govt.lc/laws/acts/2025) — **index title only; the Act text was not read**; 2026-09-18 verified). The reported effect — that a stamp-duty exemption **EXCLUDES immovable property** — came from a since-withdrawn secondary tax alert and is **not yet primary-sourced**; read the Act PDF or verify with IRD before relying on any exemption claim.

## 🇱🇨 Foreign buyer eligibility (READ FIRST)

This section determines whether a transaction is even legally possible — and which of **two distinct routes** applies. The single most important fork in Saint Lucia is **CBI-approved-project purchase vs. ordinary purchase requiring an Alien Landholding Licence**.

### Legal basis

- **Aliens (Licensing) Act, Cap. 15.37** — requires a non-national ("alien") to hold an **Alien Landholding Licence (ALHL)** before completing a purchase OUTSIDE the CBI approved-project route. (Statute text mirrored at [admin.theiguides.org — aliens_act_cap1537.pdf](https://admin.theiguides.org/Media/Documents/aliens_act_cap1537.pdf); verify current consolidated version with the Saint Lucia Attorney General's chambers.)
- **Citizenship by Investment Act, No. 14 of 2015** — governing CBI statute; administered by the [CIU](https://www.cipsaintlucia.com/citizenship-legislation).
- **Land Registration Act 1984** — state-guaranteed title-registration framework.
- **Stamp Duty + property-tax legislation** — administered by the [IRD](https://irdstlucia.gov.lc/).

### Buyer category matrix

| Buyer category | Right |
|---|---|
| **Saint Lucian citizen** | Full freehold; standard conveyancing; no ALHL |
| **CARICOM national (non-citizen)** | Still requires an ALHL for ordinary purchase (the Act applies to "aliens" = non-nationals) — ⚠️ verify CARICOM-specific exemptions with the AG's chambers |
| **Non-national, buying an ORDINARY (non-CBI) property** | Freehold permitted **but MUST obtain an Alien Landholding Licence** under Cap. 15.37 **before completion**. Licence is tied to ONE specific property. |
| **Non-national, buying THROUGH an approved CBI real-estate project** | Freehold permitted; **NO separate ALHL required** — the CBI route is self-contained |
| **Non-national, applying for citizenship via the CBI real-estate route** | Acquires Saint Lucian citizenship (subject to CIU due-diligence + 5-year hold on the investment) — then holds property as a citizen |

(Source: [Aliens (Licensing) Act Cap. 15.37](https://admin.theiguides.org/Media/Documents/aliens_act_cap1537.pdf) + [CIU](https://www.cipsaintlucia.com/) — primary; ALHL fee/process detail is aggregator/law-firm-derived, *flagged below; verify with AG's chambers / a licensed conveyancer before relying*)

### Alien Landholding Licence (ALHL) — the non-CBI route

A non-national buying ANY property outside an approved CBI project MUST obtain an ALHL. The reported structure (⚠️ **aggregator/law-firm-derived — VERIFY current schedule with the AG's chambers or a licensed St. Lucia conveyancer; do NOT state as government-confirmed**):

| Step | Reported figure (⚠️ verify) |
|---|---|
| **Certificate of Eligibility** (cited by some sources) | EC$/US$ **3,000 (1-year)** or **10,000 (10-year)** — ⚠️ aggregator figure |
| **ALHL application fee** | **EC$5,400** non-refundable — ⚠️ aggregator figure |
| **ALHL licence fee (scaled to land size)** | from **EC$5,000 (under 1 acre)** up to **EC$50,000 (100+ acres)** — ⚠️ aggregator figure |

- **Process bodies** (up to four): the **Physical Planning Department**, the **Attorney General's Office**, the **Cabinet / PM's Office** (for parcels >1 acre), and the **Governor-General's Office** (licence issuance). Reported processing **3–6 months** (⚠️ aggregator estimate — build into the completion timeline).
- **Validity**: reported valid for the alien's lifetime, tied to ONE property, non-renewable + non-transferable; a new licence is required for each subsequent property (⚠️ aggregator — verify).
- ⚠️ **CRITICAL**: the ALHL is **separate from, and additional to,** the 2% buyer stamp duty. Buying via an approved CBI project avoids the ALHL entirely.

### Compliance / AML

- **CBI applicants**: subject to **mandatory multi-tier CIU due-diligence** (background checks, source-of-funds, sanctions screening) — the CIU may reject applicants; approval is not guaranteed.
- **Source-of-funds documentation** required for all property purchases; bank-statement chain back to a legitimate source.
- **Authorised Agents**: CBI applications must be filed through a **CIU-licensed Authorised Agent** — direct individual filing is not permitted. Verify your agent's licence at the [CIU](https://www.cipsaintlucia.com/).
- **CRS + FATCA**: tax-residency self-certification at bank-account opening.

### Verification path (before any reservation)

1. **Determine the route**: CBI-approved project (no ALHL) vs ordinary purchase (ALHL required) — this drives the entire timeline + cost.
2. **If ordinary purchase**: engage a St. Lucia attorney/notary to confirm the **current ALHL fee schedule + process** with the AG's chambers and budget the 3–6-month licence lead-time into completion.
3. **If CBI route**: engage a **CIU-licensed Authorised Agent**; confirm the project is on the current [CIU approved-projects list](https://www.cipsaintlucia.com/approved-real-estate-and-enterprise-projects); confirm the **US$300,000 real-estate minimum + admin fees + 5-year hold** in writing (and flag the stale-FAQ US$200,000 conflict).
4. **Pull the registered title**: confirm state-guaranteed title under the Land Registration Act 1984 (encumbrances, charges, boundaries) via the Land Registry.
5. **AML / source-of-funds**: bank statements + asset-sale / employment evidence ready before signing.

### CBI floor effect

- Because the **CBI real-estate minimum is US$300,000**, much of the marketed "investment villa / branded-residence" stock clusters **at or above** that price point. This is a **regulatory price floor on the CBI-eligible segment only** — NOT a market-wide floor. **Non-CBI resale property can list well below US$300,000** (and would then take the ALHL route, not the CBI route).

### Compute

1. **USD/m² = price ÷ built-area m²** (listings often quote **square feet** — convert: 1 m² = 10.764 sqft).
2. Compare to **≥ 3 active listings** on the same district + community + waterfront status.
3. **Listing convention**: NW luxury villa stock typically quoted **USD-firm**; local listings may be **EC$-priced** (÷2.70 for USD-equivalent at peg).
4. **Trap — area definitions**: built-up vs gross (incl. covered patios) vs lot area. Listings often quote the largest. Confirm with the surveyor's plan / registered-title extract.
5. **Trap — ALHL surcharge for non-CBI buyers**: the ALHL fee (⚠️ verify schedule) is a non-CBI-buyer cost not reflected in the headline price.
6. **Trap — volcanic-proximity discount/risk (SW)**: Soufrière/Qualibou parcels carry geothermal hazard the NW corridor does not — price + insurability differ.

### Key terms

- **Deed of Sale / Transfer** — the instrument transferring registered title (executed before an attorney-at-law acting as notary)
- **Registered title** — state-guaranteed title under the Land Registration Act 1984 (indefeasible except for fraud)
- **Alien Landholding Licence (ALHL)** — the non-national's licence to hold land outside a CBI project (Cap. 15.37)
- **Approved CBI real-estate project** — a CIU-vetted development a CBI applicant may buy into (US$300,000 min; no ALHL)
- **Stamp duty** — transfer tax: 2% buyer / banded-citizen-vendor / flat 10% non-citizen vendor
- **Property tax** — annual ad-valorem tax (0.25% residential / 0.4% commercial of open market value)

## Section: `--traffic`

### Sources

- **No public AADT (Annual Average Daily Traffic) portal** exists for Saint Lucia comparable to UK DfT / US FHWA counts. Qualitative + OSM road-class + on-the-ground observation is the practical method.
- **OSM** fallback via Overpass — `highway` tag class.

### Reality

- **Drive on the LEFT** (former British colony).
- **Main north–south corridor Castries ⇄ Gros Islet (NW)** is the busiest; congestion concentrated around **Castries (capital)** at peak.
- **West-coast road to Soufrière (SW)** is winding/mountainous — drive-time, not volume, is the constraint.
- **Hewanorra International Airport (UVF)** is in the **SOUTH (Vieux Fort)** — **~1.5 h drive** from Rodney Bay (the prime NW market); **George F. L. Charles Airport (SLU)** serves Castries for regional flights.

### Coarse OSM fallback

| OSM `highway` | St. Lucia road class | Typical v/d (heuristic) |
|---|---|---|
| `trunk` / `primary` | Castries–Gros Islet highway; main west-coast road | 8,000–25,000 |
| `secondary` | District-spine roads | 2,000–8,000 |
| `tertiary` / `unclassified` | Village feeder | 500–3,000 |
| `residential` | Internal street | < 500 |

### Verdict bands (heuristic — calibrate on-site)

- 🟢 < 1,500 v/d (residential subdivision interior, rural lane)
- 🟡 1,500–8,000 v/d (suburban Gros Islet / district roads)
- 🟠 8,000–20,000 v/d (Castries–Gros Islet corridor; Castries approaches)

- ⚠️ No public traffic-volume dataset — qualitative only; calibrate with a site visit at peak.

## Section: `--tax`

> **Headline** (verified at the **Inland Revenue Department** — [stamp duty schedule](https://irdstlucia.gov.lc/index.php/all-taxes/domestic-sales-taxes/stamp-duty), [property tax rates](https://irdstlucia.gov.lc/index.php/individuals/residential-properties); ⚠️ irdstlucia.gov.lc was in **maintenance mode** on 2026-09-18 and ird.gov.lc did not resolve — figures re-verified against the IRD's own archived pages ([stamp duty](https://web.archive.org/web/2023id_/http://irdstlucia.gov.lc/index.php/all-taxes/domestic-sales-taxes/stamp-duty), [property tax](https://web.archive.org/web/2023id_/http://irdstlucia.gov.lc/index.php/individuals/residential-properties)); 2026 fetch, source Saint Lucia IRD): **stamp duty on transfer is 2% buyer-side** (citizen + non-citizen alike); **vendor-side stamp duty is banded for a Saint Lucian citizen seller** but a **flat 10% for a non-citizen seller**; **annual property tax 0.25% of open market value (residential) / 0.4% (commercial)**. Non-CBI foreign buyers additionally pay **ALHL fees** (⚠️ aggregator schedule — verify). "No capital gains tax on property" is widely cited but **UNVERIFIED against IRD — flag**.

### Stamp duty on property transfer (VERIFIED, IRD)

| Party | Rate |
|---|---|
| **Buyer / purchaser** | **2% of consideration** — same for citizen + non-citizen buyers (NOT differentiated by nationality) |
| **Vendor — Saint Lucian citizen (banded)** | First **$50,000 exempt**; next **$25,000 at 2.5%**; next **$75,000 at 3.5%**; **remainder 5%** (dollar bands on the IRD page — ⚠️ confirm currency; likely EC$) |
| **Vendor — non-citizen / foreign national** | **flat 10%** |

- ⚠️ The **flat 10% is a VENDOR-side stamp duty** levied when a foreigner **SELLS** — it is NOT a surcharge on a foreign buyer's 2%. A foreign buyer who later resells faces the **10% seller stamp duty** — build this into the exit/hold model.
- ⚠️ **2025 development**: [Act No. 2 of 2025 — Security Interest in Movable Property (Amendment) Act](https://npc.govt.lc/laws/acts/2025) narrows the exemption so that a **stamp-duty exemption EXCLUDES immovable property** (index title only — the Act's operative wording has not been read) — read the Act text before relying on any exemption claim.

### Annual property tax (VERIFIED, IRD)

| Property type | Rate |
|---|---|
| **Residential** | **0.25% of Open Market Value** (ad valorem; in effect since Jan 2011) |
| **Commercial** | **0.4% of Open Market Value** |
| **Combination property** | residential rate on the residential portion; commercial rate on the business portion |

### Alien Landholding Licence fees (non-CBI foreign buyers)

- Application + licence fees **scaled to land size** — see Foreign buyer eligibility above. ⚠️ The **EC$5,400 application + EC$5,000–50,000 band** figures are **aggregator/law-firm-derived**; VERIFY current schedule with the AG's chambers. These are **separate from** the 2% buyer stamp duty.

### Capital gains / wealth / inheritance

- **No annual wealth tax** is the widely-cited position. **"No capital gains tax on property"** is widely cited (globalcitizensolutions / gsl.org) but **UNVERIFIED against IRD here — flag**; CGT treatment can hinge on whether a disposal is treated as trading income. **Verify "no CGT on property" directly with the IRD before stating as fact.**
- **Inheritance**: data not publicly available here — verify estate/transfer treatment with the IRD and a St. Lucia attorney (the Civil Code's forced-heirship history may be relevant; verify).

### VAT

- Saint Lucia levies **VAT** (standard rate; tourism accommodation may carry a sector rate). **Short-term-rental income above the registration threshold may be VAT-able** — verify the current rate + threshold with the IRD before pricing a rental model.

⚠️ Verification: request the seller's last **property-tax receipt**; confirm current stamp-duty rates, property-tax basis, the 2025 stamp-duty-exemption amendment, and any "no CGT" claim at the [IRD](https://irdstlucia.gov.lc/) + attorney before contract.

## Section: `--rental`

### Reality

- **No public, government, or reliable aggregate yield dataset exists** for Saint Lucia short-term rentals. **Do NOT fabricate a yield percentage.**
- The **Rodney Bay / Cap Estate / Gros Islet corridor (NW)** is the dominant holiday-let market (proximity to Pigeon Island, the marina, beaches, and the Castries cruise port).
- Yields are **seasonal + tourism-driven**: high season ~mid-Dec to mid-Apr; **low / hurricane season Jun–Nov** depresses occupancy.

### Platforms

- **Airbnb, Vrbo, Booking.com** — short-term.
- **Villa specialists** — `https://www.thetopvillas.com/`, `https://www.isleblue.co/` (luxury managed villas).

### Yield discipline

- For any yield claim, require a **property-specific operator P&L** (12-month actuals: nightly rate × occupancy − management fee − utilities − insurance − maintenance). State: **"gross yield data not publicly available — derive from a property-specific operator statement; treat any agent-quoted yield as marketing."**
- **VAT**: short-term-rental income above the registration threshold may be VAT-able — verify rate + threshold with the IRD (see `--tax`).
- **CBI-project rentals**: many CBI-approved developments offer a **rental-pool / managed-return** arrangement — read the project's rental agreement carefully; treat any "guaranteed return" claim with extreme caution and verify whether it is contractually backed (the OECS 2024 reforms in some member states moved to **prohibit guaranteed buy-backs/returns** — verify whether St. Lucia's CBI rules now restrict guaranteed-return marketing).

## Section: `--work=<profession>`

### Reality

The property thesis in Saint Lucia is overwhelmingly **second-home / retirement / CBI-investor / hospitality-investor**, NOT local employment. **Tourism is the dominant employer + FX earner**; also offshore financial services, light manufacturing, agriculture. A foreign national needs a **work permit** to be employed locally — **separate from** the ALHL, CBI, and property routes. **Neither the CBI route nor property ownership confers a right to work** without the relevant permit.

- **LinkedIn Saint Lucia** (finance / hospitality / ICT)
- Local press classifieds (St. Lucia Times, The Voice)

### Hurricane / tropical cyclone (primary climate risk)

- Saint Lucia sits in the **Atlantic hurricane belt**. **Official season 1 Jun – 30 Nov; peak Aug–Oct.**
- **Historical reference**: **Hurricane Tomas (Oct/Nov 2010)** caused severe flooding, landslides, and fatalities — the modern calibration event for Saint Lucia.
- **Building resilience**: standards derive from the **OECS Building Code** (OECS Secretariat — model code referencing the **CUBiC — Caribbean Uniform Building Code** wind + earthquake provisions; OECS Building Codes 2015/2016 + Building Guidelines 2018). Enforcement / development control is via the **Physical Planning Department / Development Control Authority**. ⚠️ Verify the version currently adopted into Saint Lucia law with the Physical Planning Department. Older/informal construction may pre-date modern wind-load standards — commission a structural survey.
- **Insurance**: catastrophe (hurricane/wind + flood) cover is essential and a material annual cost; Eastern-Caribbean premiums are elevated and have hardened. ⚠️ **No public premium benchmark** — get quotes from licensed insurers (regulated by the **Financial Services Regulatory Authority, FSRA**). State: "insurance premium data not publicly available — obtain ≥3 carrier quotes for the specific parcel."

### Volcanic / geothermal — Soufrière Volcanic Centre & Qualibou depression (SW)

- Authority: **[UWI Seismic Research Centre](https://uwiseismic.com/island-profiles/st-lucia/)** + **NEMO**.
- The **Sulphur Springs** geothermal field (marketed as "the world's only drive-in volcano") sits within the **Qualibou depression** near **Soufrière (SW coast)** — a vigorous high-temperature geothermal field with active fumaroles + hot springs.
- **Hazard assessment** (Volcanic Hazard Assessment for Saint Lucia, archived at [NEMO](https://archive.stlucia.gov.lc/nemp/)): the **MOST LIKELY future activity is a phreatic (steam) / hydrothermal eruption** from the Sulphur Springs area. Scientists assess **only a very slim chance of a magmatic eruption in the next 100 years**. Volcanic earthquakes provide early-warning signals; NEMO + UWI-SRC monitor.
- **Property implication**: parcels **in/near Soufrière and the Qualibou depression (SW)** carry geothermal-proximity hazard (ground gas, hydrothermal alteration, localised seismicity) that the **NW (Rodney Bay / Cap Estate)** does NOT. The **prime NW expat corridor is well away from the volcanic centre**. **Flag SW-coast parcels for a volcanic-hazard-zone check with NEMO / UWI-SRC** — do NOT over-state island-wide volcanic risk.

### Seismic — moderate (Lesser Antilles subduction zone)

- The Lesser Antilles subduction zone gives **moderate regional earthquake exposure island-wide** — relevant to structural standards. Standard structural review for pre-modern-code builds.

### Landslide / flood

- The mountainous volcanic terrain + intense convective rainfall makes **landslides + flash flooding** material on steep parcels (Tomas 2010 was the calibration event). Verify slope stability + drainage on any hillside parcel.

## Section: `--climate`

Universal logic per `shared/climate-projections.md`. Saint Lucia-specific overrides:

- **Köppen-Geiger**: **Af / Am (tropical rainforest / monsoon)** in the wet mountainous interior; **Aw (tropical savanna)** drier on the leeward coast. Wet season ~Jun–Nov; drier Dec–May.
- **Topography**: mountainous volcanic island; **highest point Mount Gimie ~950 m AMSL** — materially higher relief than coral islands like Barbados; coastal property concentrated on the NW + west coast.
- **Reference baseline**: regional Caribbean met series + IPCC AR6 + Caribbean Community Climate Change Centre (CCCCC) + CSGM UWI Mona.
- **Key projections** (IPCC AR6 + CCCCC, SSP2-4.5 / SSP5-8.5):
  - Annual mean temperature **+1.0 → +2.5°C** by 2100 (Caribbean SIDS band).
  - **Sea-level rise**: +0.20 → +0.85 m by 2100 SSP5-8.5 — exposure for low-lying coastal + marina parcels.
  - **Hurricane intensity**: Atlantic-basin Cat 4–5 share trending up.
  - **Rainfall**: more intense convective events in the wet season → **landslide + flash-flood risk on steep terrain**; drier shoulder months → freshwater-supply stress.
- **Acute-event flags**:
  - 🟠 Hurricane Cat 4–5 risk (Tomas 2010 calibration)
  - 🟠 Landslide / flash flood on steep hillside parcels (volcanic terrain)
  - 🟠 Insurance availability + cost (elevated EC premiums)
  - 🟡 SLR exposure for low-lying coastal / marina parcels (2050+ horizon)
  - 🟡 Heat + cooling-load increase
  - 🟡 Dry-season freshwater stress (WASCO supply intermittency)

### Climate verdict (typical NW Rodney Bay / Cap Estate residential)

🟡 **Watch / Adapt** — moderate hurricane risk requires impact-glazing + reinforced roofing + insurance budget; SLR a 2050+ issue for low-lying coastal parcels; cooling load + dry-season water stress manageable; NW corridor clear of the SW volcanic hazard.

### Climate verdict (steep hillside or SW Soufrière/Qualibou parcels)

🟠 **Adapt** — landslide/flash-flood + (SW) geothermal-proximity hazard compound the hurricane baseline; acquire only with engineered drainage + slope-stability survey + (SW) a NEMO/UWI-SRC hazard-zone check + funded insurance.

## Section: `--currency`

- **Currency**: **East Caribbean Dollar (XCD / EC$)**.
- **Peg**: **EC$2.70 = US$1.00 since 1976** — administered by the [ECCB](https://www.eccb-centralbank.org/) across the eight-member ECCU. The peg is **hard + long-standing**; it has held through significant external shocks.

## Conveyancing + land registry

- **Court**: the **[Eastern Caribbean Supreme Court (ECSC)](https://www.eccourts.org/)** is the superior court of record — its **High Court** has a resident division for Saint Lucia; the **Court of Appeal** is itinerant across the OECS. **Final appeal lies to the [Caribbean Court of Justice (CCJ)](https://ccj.org/) since 20 July 2023** — Saint Lucia abolished UK Privy Council (JCPC) appeals on that date. The ECSC is the correct court for first-instance/intermediate property litigation; the CCJ is the court of last resort.
- **Governing land law**: the **Land Registration Act 1984** replaced the old deeds-recording system with a **state-guaranteed title-registration system** (the State examines each title, registers the land, and **guarantees title** — indefeasible except for fraud). ⚠️ verify consolidated current text via the Land Registry / AG's chambers.
- **Land Registry**: a department within the relevant Ministry (exact ministry naming changes between administrations). Title searches + copies via [govt.lc — request a copy of a land registry document](https://www.govt.lc/services/request-a-copy-of-a-land-registry-document). A **digital registry (e-LRIS)** modernisation has been announced (⚠️ verify live status).
- **Conveyancing mechanics**:
  1. **Title search** at the Land Registry (encumbrances, charges, boundaries).
  2. **Agreement for Sale** signed; **~10% deposit** held in escrow until conditions precedent are met.
  3. **Deed of Sale / Transfer** executed before an **Attorney-at-Law** — in Saint Lucia **all attorneys are also Notaries**; conveyance is by instrument in **notarial form** under the 1984 Act.
  4. **Registration** at the Land Registry; **2% buyer stamp duty** + registration fee paid by the purchaser to the notary, who stamps + registers.
- **Foreigner overlay**: a non-national must hold the **Alien Landholding Licence** BEFORE completing — OR buy via an approved CBI project (no ALHL needed). **Build the licence timeline (3–6 months) into the completion schedule** for any ordinary (non-CBI) purchase.
