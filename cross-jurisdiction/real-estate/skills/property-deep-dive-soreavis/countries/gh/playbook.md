# Ghana 🇬🇭 — Property Due-Diligence Playbook

ISO2: `gh`. Status: PARTIALLY OPEN — non-Ghanaians are restricted to **leasehold (max 50 years renewable)** under 1992 Constitution Art. 266; Ghanaian diaspora pathways via Akwaaba / Right of Abode + Pan-African Identity Card framework. Currency-volatile (GHS sharp depreciation 2022-23, IMF ECF May 2023 – concluded 27 Jul 2026, now a non-financing Policy Coordination Instrument). Customary tenure (stool / skin / family / clan land) overlays ~78% of total land area per Lands Commission classification — single largest due-diligence trap. Researched 2026-05.

## Country profile

- **Population**: ~33.5M (Ghana Statistical Service 2024 mid-year est.; 2021 Population & Housing Census recorded 30.8M; source `https://statsghana.gov.gh/`). Greater Accra ~5.4M; Ashanti (Kumasi metro) ~5.6M; Western (Sekondi-Takoradi) ~2.1M; Central (Cape Coast) ~2.9M; Eastern, Volta, Northern, Bono, Bono East, Ahafo, Savannah, North East, Upper East, Upper West, Oti, Western North = remaining 12 of the 16 regions (post-2018 region creation).

- **Admin levels**: 16 **Regions** (since 2018 creation of 6 new regions) → 261 **Metropolitan / Municipal / District Assemblies (MMDAs)**. Property rates + building permits = MMDA-level; cadastre + title registration = national (Lands Commission).
- **Currency**: **GHS — Ghanaian cedi** (₵, ISO 4217 GHS). **Régime**: managed-float; sharp depreciation 2022 (~GHS 6 → 14/USD peak Nov 2022 per Bank of Ghana official mid-rate); some recovery + renewed pressure 2023-25; **~GHS 11–12/USD as of May 2026 (BoG mid-rate); cedi appreciated ~20 % YoY from ~GHS 14 May 2025** — trajectory has reversed vs 2022-24 depreciation (2026-05-27 verified). Verify daily at `https://www.bog.gov.gh/`. **The IMF Extended Credit Facility is OVER.** The USD 3 billion 36-month ECF (approved 17 May 2023, extended three months to 16 Aug 2026) closed when the Executive Board completed the **sixth and final review on 27 July 2026**, clearing a final disbursement of about USD 371 million; Ghana has moved to a 36-month **non-financing Policy Coordination Instrument** (reform monitoring and technical assistance, no further Fund money). Do NOT describe GHS management as programme-conditioned. Source: IMF Country Report No. 2026/212 (4 Aug 2026) `https://www.elibrary.imf.org/view/journals/002/2026/212/002.2026.issue-212-en.xml` + Presidency of Ghana 15 May 2026 `https://presidency.gov.gh/ghana-successfully-completes-imf-bailout-programme-transitions-to-policy-coordination-instrument`. (2026-08-06 verified) USD informal pricing common in prime Accra (Cantonments, Airport, East Legon, Labone, Ridge, Roman Ridge) — flag in underwriting.
- **Languages**: English (official; courts, conveyancing); Twi (Akan), Ga, Ewe, Dagbani, Hausa major regional; English universally used in legal documents.
- **Legal system**: Common-law (English heritage; pre-1957 Gold Coast colonial legacy) + **customary law** (stool / skin / family / clan tenure) layered onto state framework. Property = common-law contract + Lands Commission registration + customary allocation.
- **Cadastre — Lands Commission (national)**:
  - **Lands Commission** (`https://lc.gov.gh/`) — Lands Act 2020 (Act 1036; consolidated 2020 reform of 6 prior land statutes). Four divisions: Public & Vested Lands Management; Survey & Mapping; Land Registration; Land Valuation.

  - **Two parallel registration systems coexist** during the transition: **Title** (definitive, indefeasible) vs **Deed** (record of transaction, not title) — Title supersedes Deed when district migrates.
- **Identifiers**: **Site Plan + Lithograph (cadastral plan)**, **Indenture / Lease number**, **Title Certificate Number** (in Title Registration districts).
- **Recent reforms** (verify date-stamped):
  - **Lands Act 2020 (Act 1036)** consolidating 6 prior land statutes — entered into force 2020 (`https://lc.gov.gh/`)
  - **IMF Extended Credit Facility** approved May 2023 — **CONCLUDED 27 July 2026** (sixth and final review); succeeded by a non-financing Policy Coordination Instrument
  - **E-Levy REPEALED April 2025** via Electronic Transfer Levy (Repeal) Act 2025 (President Mahama assented 2 Apr 2025; GRA directed all charging entities to cease collection immediately). Act 1075 (2022) + Act 1089 (2023) superseded (2026-05-27 verified, source: GRA + Presidency).
  - **Mahama 2025 fiscal reform package** (April 2025): E-Levy + Betting Tax + Emissions Levy repealed; **VAT Act 2025 (Act 1151) effective 1 Jan 2026** (overhauled scheme — see below); Income Tax Amendment 2025; Growth & Sustainability Levy Act; Gold Board Bill.   - **VAT Act 2025 (Act 1151) effective 1 Jan 2026** supersedes VAT Act 2013 Act 870 + Act 1082 (2022): 15 % headline VAT retained; **VAT Flat Rate Scheme (VFRS) abolished**; registration threshold raised GH₵200,000 → **GH₵750,000**; **NHIL (2.5 %) + GETFund (2.5 %) recoupled and now input-tax-credit eligible** (previously cascading); **COVID-19 Health Recovery Levy abolished 1 Jan 2026**. Effective combined VAT+levies ≈ 20 % (15 % + 2.5 % + 2.5 %) but with input credit recovery for NHIL+GETFund (2026-05-27 verified).
  - **Year of Return 2019** + **Beyond the Return 2020-onwards** (Ministry of Tourism, Culture & Creative Arts; Ghana Tourism Authority — `https://www.ghana.travel/`) — diaspora-engagement framework continuing

## Foreign buyer eligibility

Non-Ghanaian individuals and majority-foreign companies are constitutionally restricted to **leasehold** in Ghana. The 1992 Constitution Art. 266 is the foundational rule.

### 1992 Constitution Art. 266 — the ceiling

Source: Constitution of the Republic of Ghana 1992 (`https://www.parliament.gh/`).

| Clause | Effect |
|---|---|
| Art. 266(1) | "No interest in, or right over, any land in Ghana shall be created which vests in a person who is not a citizen of Ghana a freehold interest in any land in Ghana." |
| Art. 266(2)–(4) | Leasehold for non-citizens **shall not exceed 50 years at any one time**; renewable. |
| Art. 266(5) | Companies majority-Ghanaian-owned treated as Ghanaian for these purposes. |
| Art. 267 | Stool lands vested in the appropriate stool/skin in trust for subjects; managed via Office of the Administrator of Stool Lands (OASL — `https://oasl.gov.gh/`). |

### Practical foreign-buyer constraints

| Buyer type | Freehold | Leasehold |
|---|---|---|
| **Ghanaian citizen (incl. by descent / naturalised)** | ✅ Allowed (subject to underlying tenure type) | ✅ Allowed |
| **Non-Ghanaian individual** | ❌ **Constitutionally prohibited** | ✅ **Max 50 years at any one time, renewable** |
| **Company majority Ghanaian-owned** | ✅ Treated as citizen | ✅ Allowed |
| **Company majority foreign-owned** | ❌ Treated as non-citizen | ✅ Max 50 years renewable |

### Right of Abode + diaspora pathway

- **Right of Abode** (Immigration Act 2000 Act 573 ss. 17-18 as amended by Act 848 2012): renewable indefinite right to enter, live, work in Ghana; available to **persons of African descent**. Does NOT confer citizenship; does NOT remove the Art. 266 freehold bar by itself.
- **Citizenship by registration / naturalisation** (Citizenship Act 2000 Act 591) is the only route by which a non-Ghanaian gains the constitutional right to hold freehold; thresholds + procedure at Ministry of the Interior + GIS (`https://www.gis.gov.gh/`).
- **Akwaaba ("Welcome") / Year of Return / Beyond the Return**: diaspora-engagement programs — they coordinate visa, immigration, investment introductions; **they do NOT amend Art. 266 freehold restrictions**. Marketing language equating "Akwaaba" with "investor visa with property rights" oversimplifies — verify any specific package directly at GIS / Ministry of the Interior.

### Critical caveat — customary tenure overlays most parcels

Per Lands Commission classification, ~78% of land in Ghana is held under **customary tenure** (stool, skin, family, clan); ~20% public/state; ~2% private freehold/leasehold direct. "Buying" land outside major formally-allocated zones in Accra/Kumasi typically means: (a) chief/family allocation (with elders' witness + customary fee), (b) Indenture / Lease drafted by lawyer, (c) Concurrence from the Lands Commission, (d) registration. Skipping Lands Commission registration is the dominant fraud vector. Always insist on full chain (Allocation Note → Indenture → Concurrence → Registration → Title or Deed Certificate).

### Other restrictions

- **Agricultural land**: foreign acquisition framework subject to Lands Commission approval; large-scale farming via leasehold structures + investment registration with Ghana Investment Promotion Centre (`https://gipc.gov.gh/`).
- **Mineral-bearing land**: Minerals & Mining Act 2006 (Act 703) reserves mineral rights to the state regardless of surface title.
- **Coastal reserves + protected areas**: Forestry Commission + EPA approvals separately.
- **Diplomatic acquisition**: separate Ministry of Foreign Affairs framework.

### Compute

1. **Listing GHS/m² or USD/m²** = price ÷ m² built. Clarify "built-up area" vs "plot size"; **plot size convention**: 1 plot ≈ 70 ft × 100 ft ≈ 21.34 m × 30.48 m ≈ ~650 m² is the most common Accra/Kumasi residential plot — verify per listing as conventions vary.
2. Convert GHS ↔ USD at **BoG official mid-rate** (`https://www.bog.gov.gh/economic-data/exchange-rate/`) — managed-float regime; spread to commercial-bank rate typically 1-3%.
3. Cross-check 3+ listings + developer-direct quote + Knight Frank/Broll quarterly note before underwriting.
4. **Trap — dual-currency**: Cantonments / Airport / East Legon often quote in USD with GHS for show; USD price typically binding — confirm currency in writing in Indenture.
5. **Trap — off-plan vs cash**: headline GHS 1,200,000 off-plan may be 24-48 mo installment; cash-equivalent often 15-30% lower — demand cash-equivalent quote and delivery warranties.
6. **Trap — plot vs m² confusion**: "half-plot at GHS 600,000" in Pokuase ≈ 325 m² nominal but actual surveyed area can deviate ±10% — demand registered Site Plan with surveyed coordinates.
7. **Trap — declared vs actual**: stamp-duty declared often 20-40% below actual cash price (informal practice); verify per Lands Commission Land Valuation Division.

**Confidence**: MEDIUM — asking listings abundant in Accra/Kumasi; **transaction-verified price data not publicly available** (Lands Commission records paid + parcel-by-parcel). Foreign buyers should commission a local lawyer + valuer for 3-5 sold-comparables + cross-check Knight Frank/Broll quarterly.

## Section: `--tax`

### Annual property tax — MMDA-LEVEL (Property Rate)

| Item | Rate / source |
|---|---|
| **Property Rate** | **MMDA-set, typically 0.5%–3% of assessed rateable value annually** (Local Governance Act 2016 Act 936; verify per district) |
| **Authority** | Metropolitan / Municipal / District Assembly (e.g., Accra Metropolitan Assembly — AMA `https://ama.gov.gh/`; Kumasi Metropolitan Assembly — KMA) |
| **Assessed value** | Land Valuation Division (LVD), Lands Commission — typically below market; re-assessment cycles vary by MMDA |
| **Digital payment** | Ghana.gov property rate portal (`https://www.ghana.gov.gh/`) — selected MMDAs |

**Example**: East Legon house assessed GHS 800,000 rateable value × 1% AMA rate = GHS 8,000/yr (~USD 530-670 at 2025-26 GHS/USD). Annual property rate is generally low absolute USD; transaction taxes + registration + lawyer fees are the heavy drag on closing costs.

### Transaction taxes (one-time at purchase)

| Item | Rate | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| **Stamp Duty** | **0.25%–1% sliding** of consideration (Stamp Duty Act 2005 Act 689; verify GRA) | Ghana Revenue Authority (`https://gra.gov.gh/`) |
| **Lands Commission Concurrence Fee** | typically 1-2% of consideration (verify Lands Commission fee schedule) | Lands Commission (`https://lc.gov.gh/`) |
| **Land Title / Deed Registration Fee** | typically 0.5-1% of consideration (varies by district + Title vs Deed) | Lands Commission |
| **Capital Gains Tax (federal, seller)** | **15% on net realised gain** (Income Tax Act 2015 Act 896 as amended) | GRA |
| **VAT (commercial / certain new build)** | **15%** headline rate retained under **VAT Act 2025 (Act 1151) effective 1 Jan 2026** (supersedes Act 870 (2013) + Act 1082 amendments); residential sales generally NOT subject — verify scope at GRA (2026-05-27 verified) | GRA |
| **NHIL + GETFund Levy** | combined **5 %** (2.5 % NHIL + 2.5 % GETFund) on top of VAT for VAT-able transactions; **COVID-19 Health Recovery Levy abolished 1 Jan 2026** under VAT Act 2025; NHIL + GETFund now **input-tax-credit eligible** (previously cascading) — 2026-05-27 verified | GRA |
| **Buyer lawyer** | 1.5–5% (Ghana Bar Association scale) | GBA |
| **Agent commission** | typically 5–10% (often split, sometimes seller-borne) | Market practice |
| **Total foreign-buyer transaction cost (Accra)** | **~5–12% of price** | Sum of stamp + concurrence + registration + legal + agent |

### CGT (federal, seller, on sale)

Per Income Tax Act 2015 Act 896 + amendments:
- **Rate**: 15% on net realised gain (Schedule 1 of the Act; verify current rate at GRA)
- **Principal-residence exemption**: limited; verify scope at GRA
- **Non-resident sellers**: 15% same headline rate; withholding mechanism per GRA practice notes — verify per transaction

### Income tax on rental income

- **Resident individual**: graduated PAYE rates 0–35% (Income Tax Act 2015 Act 896 First Schedule, as amended; verify GRA brackets); rental income taxed via final-tax mechanism for some categories — verify GRA
- **Non-resident landlord**: rental income Ghana-sourced → final withholding; verify exact rate at GRA per transaction
- **Corporate landlord**: 25% standard CIT; specific real-estate concessions for first-purchase buyer through GREDA-registered developers

### VAT on real estate

- **Residential sale by individual**: generally NOT VAT-able
- **Commercial sale + new-build by VAT-registered developer**: 15% VAT + NHIL/GETFund/COVID levies may apply (verify GRA)
- **VAT (Amendment) Act 2022 (Act 1082)** raised standard rate from 12.5% to 15%; **superseded by VAT Act 2025 (Act 1151) effective 1 Jan 2026** — 15 % retained but VFRS abolished, threshold raised to GH₵750k, NHIL+GETFund recoupled as input-creditable, COVID levy abolished (2026-05-27 verified).

### Inheritance / estate

- **No federal inheritance tax**
- **Probate / Letters of Administration** at High Court Probate Registry; Administration of Estates Act 1961 (Act 63); fees vary
- **Customary inheritance**: matrilineal (Akan ethnic groups) vs patrilineal (Ewe, Ga, Northern groups) — Intestate Succession Act 1985 (PNDCL 111) provides statutory overlay; consult lawyer for testamentary planning under customary land

### Future risk

E-Levy + tax-revenue intensification under the 2023-2026 IMF ECF (programme concluded 27 Jul 2026; fiscal-consolidation direction now anchored by the successor Policy Coordination Instrument rather than by programme conditionality); MMDA property-rate digitization may surface previously unbilled parcels; potential VAT-base broadening to bring more residential developer sales into scope (verify Budget Statements at Ministry of Finance — `https://www.mofep.gov.gh/`).

**Confidence**: HIGH for federal framework (Stamp Duty Act 2005, Income Tax Act 2015 Act 896, VAT Act 2013 Act 870 as amended — all GRA-administered); MEDIUM for MMDA-level property rate + Lands Commission Concurrence schedules (district-variable); MEDIUM for specific 2026 fee values (verify per transaction at GRA + Lands Commission portal).

## Section: `--rental`

### Long-term residential

- **Governing law**: Rent Act 1963 (Act 220) + Rent Control regulations + customary practice — modernization debated for years; verify current status
- **Rent Control Department** under Ministry of Works & Housing — administers tenancy disputes (`https://mwh.gov.gh/`)
- **Annual rent-in-advance culture**: most Ghanaian residential leases require **1–2 years rent in advance** (legacy practice; informally enforced beyond Rent Act limits in many premium markets). Rent Act 1963 in principle limits advance rent for some tenancy classes — enforcement uneven; verify with Rent Control Department.
- **Notice**: 6 months for yearly tenancies (per Rent Act 1963); **eviction** through Rent Control + District Court (multi-month)
- **Deposit**: 1-2 months typical; no statutory escrow framework

### Tax on rental income

- **Resident individual landlord**: progressive 0-35% PAYE (Income Tax Act 2015 Act 896; verify GRA brackets); some categories taxed via final withholding — verify GRA
- **Non-resident landlord**: rental income Ghana-sourced → final withholding; tenant may be statutory withholding agent — verify GRA per transaction
- **Deductions**: maintenance, repairs (not capital improvements), management commission, mortgage interest

### Short-term rentals (STR / Airbnb)

**Status**: undergoverned at federal level; growing market in Accra (Cantonments, Airport, East Legon, Labone) + Cape Coast / Elmina heritage tourism corridor + Kumasi premium.

- **Ghana Tourism Authority (GTA)** (`https://gta.gov.gh/`) — Tourism Act 2011 (Act 817) registration framework for accommodation enterprises (license tier varies by establishment class); STR formal compliance rare in practice — verify scope.
- **VAT**: 15% applies if operator is VAT-registered (turnover threshold).
- **Estate / HOA covenants**: Trasacco / East Legon Hills / gated estates often **prohibit STR** via covenants — read estate by-laws BEFORE buying for STR strategy.
- **Year of Return / Beyond the Return** lifted Accra STR demand 2019-2024; sustained but moderating 2025-26.

### Strategic notes

- **Long-term gross yields** (USD-priced premium, Accra Cantonments/Airport/East Legon): ~5-8% USD nominal
- **Long-term gross yields** (mid-tier Accra suburbs in GHS): ~8-14% nominal GHS, materially eroded by inflation/depreciation
- **STR gross yields**: Cantonments/Airport/East Legon ~10-18% headline USD; **net after security + power backup + estate fees + management often half of gross**
- **Energy autonomy overlay** (see `--mains`): generators + LPG + solar — must price into underwriting
- **"Dumsor" (load-shedding)** legacy: less acute since 2017 but recurrent during fiscal/dollar squeezes (most recent material spell 2023-24); back-up infra is standard premium-property kit

**Confidence**: MEDIUM — federal/MMDA framework documented; STR is estate-rule-controlled. Verify by-laws + GTA registration scope before underwriting STR yield.

### Stool-land + customary tenure overlay — the dominant due-diligence trap

| Tenure type | Who allocates | DD path |
|---|---|---|
| **Stool land** (south, Akan / Ga / Ewe regions) | Stool (chief) in trust for the community; OASL collects ground rent | Verify Allocation Note from Stool + customary witnessing → Indenture/Lease drafted by lawyer → Lands Commission Concurrence → registration |
| **Skin land** (northern regions, parallel to stool) | Skin (Tindana / Earth Priest / Chief) | Same chain as stool, regional variants |
| **Family / clan land** | Family head + principal members | Allocation Note signed by family head + principal elders → Indenture → Concurrence → registration |
| **State / public land** | State, via Lands Commission / Public & Vested Lands Division | Direct lease from State; cleanest title chain |
| **Private freehold/leasehold** | Existing private owner | Indenture + chain of title check at Lands Commission |

**Critical fraud vector — double allocation**: a chief or family head sells the same parcel to multiple buyers; the buyer with first registered Indenture + Lands Commission concurrence prevails, but ahead of registration the buyer is exposed. Mitigation: parallel official Lands Commission search + Site Plan registered before payment + payment via lawyer escrow + community elders' on-site witnessing + physical site inspection.

### Flood + coastal risk

- **Accra urban flooding**: chronic during May-Jul main rains; severe events 2015 (Goil flood + fire deaths >150 — NADMO), 2018, 2022 — Odaw River + Korle Lagoon catchment, Kaneshie / Circle / Alajo / Achimota / Adenta corridors particularly exposed
- **Coastal erosion**: West African coastal zone losing 1-3 m/year in places (Atlantic Centre for Marine Sciences / EPA); Keta / Ada Foah / Aboadze / Cape Coast western segments most exposed; sea defence works at Keta + planned for Volta-region coast
- **Volta Lake levels** influence Akosombo Dam-area properties + flood plain communities downstream during peak releases

### Climate change (summary)

NDC: 64 GHG mitigation actions, ~64% conditional emissions reduction by 2030 vs BAU (Ghana NDC Update 2021 to UNFCCC; verify EPA + Ministry of Environment, Science, Technology & Innovation). Sea-level rise + coastal squeeze; northern Sahel-edge heat extremes; rainfall variability rising.

### Power grid — back-up infrastructure standard

National grid (ECG — Electricity Company of Ghana, NEDCo Northern Electricity, GRIDCo transmission) suffered chronic load-shedding ("dumsor") 2014-17, materially improved since but recurrent during fiscal/dollar squeezes (most recent material 2023-24). Premium Accra/Kumasi properties typically include:
- Standby diesel/petrol generator (5-15 kVA)
- Increasingly: solar + lithium battery (Bui Power Authority / private installers)

### Authority + legal basis

- **Ghana Immigration Service (GIS)** — `https://www.gis.gov.gh/`

### Major pathways

1. **Tourist + Business visa**: 30-90 days; not for working/residing; eVisa availability expanded 2023-24 (verify per nationality at GIS).
2. **Work Permit + Resident Permit**: tied to employer + role; 1-2 yr renewable; sponsor company application via GIS.
3. **Investor pathway via GIPC** (GIPC Act 2013 Act 865):
    - Foreign-owned enterprise registration with GIPC; minimum capital USD 200,000 (joint venture with Ghanaian partner) or USD 500,000 (wholly foreign-owned trading) — verify current thresholds at GIPC.
    - Automatic-quota expatriate visas under GIPC schedule (number tiered to FDI band).
    - GIS issues Resident Permit on the back of GIPC registration.
4. **Right of Abode (Akwaaba framework)**: under Immigration Act 2000 ss. 17-18 as amended by Act 848 (2012). Available to **persons of African descent** (verify exact eligibility wording with GIS / Ministry of the Interior). Confers indefinite right to enter, live, work; does NOT confer Ghanaian citizenship; does NOT amend Art. 266 freehold restriction. Application + supporting evidence of African descent + good character + means + clean record. **Specific procedural thresholds + fees + family-inclusion rules vary; verify directly at GIS — secondary aggregator language often oversimplifies.**
5. **Permanent Residence Permit**: for non-Ghanaian after typically 7+ years residence + supporting criteria (verify current at GIS).
6. **Citizenship by Naturalisation** (Citizenship Act 2000 Act 591): typically 7+ years residence + language + good character + means + integration evidence. Ministerial discretion. **Dual citizenship** allowed (1996 referendum amendment + Citizenship Act 2000) for Ghanaian-born; non-Ghanaian acquiring Ghana citizenship may need to renounce per their original country's rules.
7. **Citizenship by Registration / Descent**: through Ghanaian parentage (Constitution 1992 Art. 6-8); **Pan-African Identity Card / "Year of Return" framework** has been promoted as a diaspora-engagement program. Specific PAID issuance + benefits + connection to property rights vary by program iteration; verify at GIS / Ministry of the Interior + Ministry of Foreign Affairs (`https://www.mfa.gov.gh/`).

### NO golden-visa via passive real estate

Ghana does NOT offer a residency-by-property-purchase scheme (vs Portugal Golden Visa pre-Oct 2023, Greece Golden Visa, etc.). Real-estate purchase alone gives NO residence right. **Investor-route via GIPC USD 200k–500k+ in registered Ghanaian enterprise** (which can include a real-estate development company — but must be a going concern, not a passive-asset SPV) is the closest analog.

### Diaspora pathway

- **Year of Return 2019** + **Beyond the Return 2020-onwards**: Ministry of Tourism / Ghana Tourism Authority diaspora-engagement framework; coordinates visits, investment introductions, citizenship ceremonies for diaspora applicants meeting Citizenship Act criteria.
- **Pan-African Identity Card (PAID)**: ID card framework promoted as part of African-Union Free Movement / diaspora-connection initiative; **verify current PAID issuance status, benefits, and any property-rights interaction at Ministry of Foreign Affairs / GIS — has been subject to multiple program iterations + delays**.
- **Diaspora Affairs Bureau** (Office of the President / Ministry of Foreign Affairs Diaspora unit) — coordination point for diaspora investment + return.

**Confidence**: HIGH for Immigration Act 2000 + Citizenship Act 2000 + GIPC Act 2013 framework; MEDIUM for current eligibility thresholds (verify directly at GIS as recent program iterations evolve); LOW for specific Akwaaba / PAID procedural detail (secondary aggregators frequently oversimplify or conflate distinct programs — primary GIS / Ministry of Interior verification mandatory).

## Section: `--notary`

### Framework

Ghana operates **English common-law conveyancing**, not civil-law notariat:
- **Lawyer (GBA-registered, holding solicitor's licence)** drafts Indenture / Lease + due diligence on title chain
- **Commissioner for Oaths / Notary Public** administers oaths + authenticates (NOT the conveyancer)
- **Lands Commission** issues Concurrence + registers Indenture / Title
- **High Court Probate Registry** for probate (Administration of Estates Act 1961 Act 63)

### Process — Accra foreign-buyer typical flow

1. Letter of Offer / Sale Agreement + refundable deposit (5-15%); review by buyer's lawyer
2. Buyer lawyer due diligence: title search at Lands Commission Land Registration Division; chain of Indentures; Site Plan + boundary check; Stool / family allocation note review (if customary chain)
3. **Independent official Lands Commission search** (paid; verify current fee at Lands Commission portal) — surfaces double-allocation + encumbrances
4. Site Plan re-survey by licensed surveyor (verify boundaries on the ground)
5. Indenture / Lease drafted by buyer lawyer + reviewed by seller lawyer
6. **Stamp Duty assessment + payment** at GRA (~0.25-1% sliding per Stamp Duty Act 2005 Act 689; verify current schedule)
7. **Lands Commission Concurrence application** — confirms registrability of Indenture; adds 1-2% (verify current fee schedule)
8. **Land Title / Deed Registration** — definitive title in Title Registration districts (Accra, Kumasi pilots); evidentiary in Deed Registration districts; ~0.5-1% registration fee
9. Final payment + handover; Title Certificate or Registered Deed issued in buyer's name
10. Property Rate transfer at MMDA; utility transfers (ECG/NEDCo, GWCL)

### Timeline

Accra Title Registration districts: **3-9 months** typical foreign-buyer transaction (Concurrence + Title issuance the critical-path items); Deed Registration districts: 4-12+ months variable; rural / customary-tenure parcels: 6-18+ months not uncommon.

### Costs (Accra foreign-buyer, USD 250,000 example — verify per transaction)

- Stamp Duty ~0.5% mid: USD 1,250
- Lands Commission Concurrence ~1-2%: USD 2,500-5,000
- Title / Deed Registration ~0.5-1%: USD 1,250-2,500
- Lawyer 2-3% mid: USD 5,000-7,500
- Surveyor + Site Plan: USD 500-2,000
- Agent commission 5-10% (often seller-borne or split): USD 12,500-25,000 if buyer-borne
- Sundry (clearances, GhanaCard support docs, verifications): USD 200-1,000
- **Total Accra foreign-buyer**: ~5-12% of price (excl. agent commission if seller-borne, otherwise +5-10%)

### Trampas

- **Double allocation by stool / family head**: same parcel sold to multiple buyers; mitigate via Lands Commission search + first-registered Indenture wins + payment via lawyer escrow
- **Forged Site Plans / Indentures**: market-known fraud; insist on Lands Commission verified search + on-ground licensed-surveyor verification
- **Stool succession disputes**: chief who allocated may have been disputed / destooled; verify customary recognition + Houses of Chiefs (Regional / National)
- **Family dispute on family lands**: family-head may not have full elders' consent; require principal members' signatures + customary witnesses
- **State-acquisition overlay**: parcels notionally under state acquisition with compensation history disputes; verify Lands Commission Public & Vested Lands Division

## Section: `--exit`

### Sale process (Accra East Legon house, USD 350,000)

1. Engage GBA-registered lawyer + agent (~5-10% commission typical, often seller-borne)
2. Listing on meQasa.com + Property24 + private agent network; **6-18 months** typical Accra premium depending on price + currency stance
3. Pre-sale docs: original Indenture / Lease + Site Plan + Lands Commission Concurrence + Title Certificate (or Registered Deed) + chain-of-title docs + property rate clearance + estate dues + utility clearances + GhanaCard
4. Buyer offer + Letter of Acceptance + deposit (5-15% refundable)
5. Buyer due diligence — own lawyer + Lands Commission search + surveyor
6. Stamp Duty + Lands Commission Concurrence + Registration (Accra: 3-9 months on Concurrence + Registration)
7. CGT 15% on net gain settled at GRA (seller) — verify primary-residence exemption scope at GRA
8. Final payment + Indenture / Lease assignment + handover
9. Title / Deed updated at Lands Commission

### Seller tax obligations

- **CGT 15% on net realised gain** (GRA, Income Tax Act 2015 Act 896); verify primary-residence exemption scope
- **No federal property-tax exit penalty** — settle outstanding MMDA property rate
- Lawyer fees ~1-2% seller; agent commission ~5-10% (often shared)
- Stamp Duty + Concurrence Fee are buyer-side (no seller transfer tax beyond CGT)

### Repatriation

- **BoG-stamped inbound advice + authorized-dealer SWIFT confirmation at original purchase is critical** — without it, USD repatriation structurally restricted
- AML: FIC + bank reports >USD 10,000 equivalent; legal but documented
- Conversion at BoG-authorized dealer rate; spread typically 1-3%
- Outbound transfer via authorized dealer: documentation-heavy but legal for documented inflows

### Liquidity

- Accra premium (Cantonments, Airport Residential, East Legon, Trasacco, Roman Ridge): 6-18 months for fairly priced in stable currency stance
- **GHS depreciation freezes market** (USD-sellers wait, GHS-buyers flee) — 2022-23 crisis saw sharp slowdown
- Off-plan resale: thin secondary market
- Other regions: 12-36 months not uncommon for premium

### Seller cost (Accra, typical)

Agent 5-10% (often seller-borne) + CGT 15% on net realised gain + lawyer 1-2% + property rate / utility clearances minor → net to seller ~75-92% of gross depending on agent-cost split + CGT applicability.

**Confidence**: HIGH for federal CGT + Stamp Duty; MEDIUM for Accra closing-timeline (Concurrence variable per district); LOW for second-tier region liquidity.

## Caveats unique to GH (HEAVY — read all)

- **1992 Constitution Art. 266**: NO freehold for non-Ghanaians; **max 50 years leasehold renewable** — foundation of every foreign-buyer Ghana property transaction.
- **Customary tenure (~78% of land)**: stool / skin / family / clan land overlays dominate outside major formally-allocated zones; **Allocation Note → Indenture → Lands Commission Concurrence → Registration** is the chain — skipping any step is the dominant fraud vector.
- **Double-allocation fraud**: same parcel sold to multiple buyers by chief / family head; first-registered Indenture + Concurrence prevails — pre-payment Lands Commission search + lawyer escrow + on-site witnessing mandatory.
- **Parallel registration systems** (Title vs Deed) during transition — Accra + Kumasi advanced on Title; many districts still on Deed under Conveyancing Act 1973.
- **GHS depreciation 2022-23**: ~GHS 6 → 14/USD peak; recovery + renewed pressure 2023-25; **USD informal pricing dominates prime Accra suburbs**.
- **IMF Extended Credit Facility (May 2023 – concluded 27 Jul 2026)**: programme conditionalities shaped FX-availability + fiscal stance until the sixth and final review; the successor 36-month Policy Coordination Instrument is non-financing (reform monitoring only) — monitor IMF + BoG releases.
- **Akwaaba / Right of Abode + PAID**: African-descent diaspora pathway (Immigration Act 2000 ss. 17-18 as amended Act 848 2012); does NOT confer freehold rights despite secondary-aggregator language often equating it; verify directly at GIS.
- **No golden-visa via passive RE**: GIPC investor route requires USD 200-500k+ in registered going-concern enterprise.
- **BoG capital-flow documentation**: foreign capital inflow MUST be authorized-dealer-bank-documented for future USD repatriation — #1 procedural risk.
- **Energy / water autonomy** standard cost overlay (borehole + polytank + generator + LPG + increasingly solar): GHS 1,500-4,500/mo for mid-tier Accra premium home.
- **Accra urban floods** (Odaw catchment — Kaneshie, Circle, Alajo, Achimota, Adenta corridors): chronic during May-Jul main rains; verify drainage + insurance flood wording.
- **Coastal erosion**: West African shoreline retreat 1-3 m/yr in places (Keta, Ada Foah, Aboadze) — verify EPA Ghana coastal report + Climate Central for parcels < 200 m from shore.
- **GhanaCard + TIN integration**: required for property + bank transactions.
- **MMDA property rate** (0.5-3% assessed value range; AMA / KMA digitization underway via ghana.gov).
- **Stool succession + family-elders disputes**: surface customary chain disputes pre-purchase via Houses of Chiefs + family elders + lawyer.
- **State-acquisition overlay**: certain parcels under historical state acquisition with compensation history disputes; verify Lands Commission Public & Vested Lands.
- **Galamsey (illegal small-scale mining) overlay**: certain inland regions affected by water pollution + legal challenges to mineral-bearing surface lands.
