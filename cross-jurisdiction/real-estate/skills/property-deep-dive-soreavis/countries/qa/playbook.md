# Qatar 🇶🇦 — Property Due-Diligence Playbook

ISO2: `qa`. Status: ✅ Fully populated (researched 2026-04 / verified 2026-05-01).

> **Critical framing**: Qatar is a **foreign-buyer-restricted market** with TWO tiers: **freehold** in 9 designated zones (Law 16/2018 + Cabinet Decision 28/2020) and **99-year leasehold (renewable)** in 16 additional zones. Outside those zones non-Qataris cannot acquire any registered interest. Qatar has **NO recurring property tax**, **NO personal income tax**, **NO VAT yet** (deferred from GCC framework), and **NO citizenship-by-investment** — but offers a 5-year renewable Investor Residency at QAR 730k and Permanent Residency at QAR 1M. Currency risk vs USD is structurally zero — QAR is pegged at **3.64 to the USD since July 2001** (Qatar Central Bank fixed peg). Confirm zone status with the Real Estate Registration Department (REA) at the Ministry of Justice **before** signing any reservation form.

## Country profile

- **Postal code system**: **No nationwide postcode system**. Modern parcel-level identifiers:
  - **Zone No. + Street No. + Building No.** — Qatar's official addressing scheme issued by the Ministry of Municipality (formerly MMUP). Free national lookup at the Qatar Geographic Information System (`https://geoportal.gisqatar.org.qa/`)
  - **Plot No. (رقم القطعة) + Block No. (رقم البلوك) + Zone No. (رقم المنطقة)** is the cadastral identifier on Title Deeds
- **Admin levels**: Unitary state → **8 municipalities** (Doha, Al Rayyan, Al Wakrah, Umm Salal, Al Khor, Al Daayen, Al Shamal, Al Shahaniya) → zones (numbered)
- **Currency**: QAR (Qatari Riyal). Pegged USD 1 = QAR 3.64 since July 2001 (Qatar Central Bank — Emiri Decree 34/2001)
- **Languages**: Arabic (official, all formal contracts and Title Deeds in Arabic — bilingual EN/AR available in real-estate sector but Arabic prevails legally); English is the de-facto business language
- **Cadastre / land registry**:
  - **Real Estate Registration Department (REA)** — under Ministry of Justice — `https://www.moj.gov.qa/` — issues the Title Deed (Sanad Mulkiyya / سند ملكية); operates under **Law No. 5 of 2024 Regulating Real Estate Registration** (gazetted 5 Jun 2024; implementing regs Jun 2025) which repealed Law 14/1964, introduced electronic registration with full legal force, mandatory registrability of all real-rights dispositions, and penalties up to QAR 500,000 + 3 years' imprisonment for non-compliance (2026-05-27 verified, source K&L Gates 2024-09-06 + Sultan Al-Abdulla & Partners)
  - **Aqarat / RERA — General Authority for Regulating the Real Estate Sector (الهيئة العامة لتنظيم القطاع العقاري)** — `https://www.aqarat.gov.qa/` — established by **Law No. 28 of 2023**; central oversight body for developers, brokers, freehold zones, and the Qatar Real Estate Platform (QREP); foreign-buyer eligibility verification + broker-licence checks route through Aqarat alongside MCI (2026-05-27 verified, source aqarat.gov.qa primary)
  - **Ministry of Municipality** (MoM, formerly MME) — handles Affection Plans, building permits, Zone/Street/Building number assignment — `https://www.mm.gov.qa/`
  - **Ministry of Commerce and Industry (MCI)** — administers foreign-buyer designated-zone framework — `https://www.moci.gov.qa/`
- **Identifier(s)**:
  - **Title Deed (Sanad Mulkiyya — سند ملكية)** — issued by REA at registration; bears Plot No. + Block No. + Zone No. + Municipality
  - **Sale and Purchase Agreement (SPA)** — pre-registration binding; for off-plan sales the developer provides an interim allocation document until final Title Deed at handover
  - **Affection Plan (شهادة تخطيط)** — issued by Ministry of Municipality; cadastral plot info
- **Population**: ~3.05 M (2024 figure, published by the then **Planning and Statistics Authority**, whose statistics function passed to the **National Planning Council** — [npc.qa](https://www.npc.qa/); `psa.gov.qa` is egress-filtered); ~12% Qatari nationals + ~88% expatriates (2020 census + 2024 PSA bulletin)
- **Recent reforms** (12-month watchlist):
  - **Law 16/2018** + **Cabinet Resolution No. 28 of 2020** (a.k.a. Cabinet Decision 28/2020) + 2024 update — non-Qatari freehold expanded to 9 designated zones + 99-yr leasehold in 16 additional zones (was Pearl + West Bay Lagoon + Al Khor Resort only pre-2018)
  - **Law No. 28 of 2023** — establishes **Aqarat / RERA** (General Authority for Regulating the Real Estate Sector) as central regulator for developers, brokers, freehold zones, and the Qatar Real Estate Platform (QREP) (2026-05-27 verified, source aqarat.gov.qa)
  - **Law No. 5 of 2024 Regulating Real Estate Registration** — gazetted 5 Jun 2024; repeals Law 14/1964; introduces e-registration with full legal force; mandatory registrability of all real-rights dispositions; penalties up to QAR 500,000 + 3 years' imprisonment for non-compliance; implementing regulations issued Jun 2025 (2026-05-27 verified, source K&L Gates 2024-09-06)
  - **Ministry of Justice 2026 fee-cut + exemption-expansion** (Jan 2026) — certain transfer fees on farms / marine lands reduced from 1% to 0.25% (now uniformly 0.25%) (2026-05-27 verified, source Qatar Tribune 2026-01-15 + QNA 2026-01-15)
  - **Law 10/2018 — Permanent Residency** — first GCC-state permanent-residency programme; QAR 1M qualifying-asset threshold including freehold real estate
  - **Law 21/2018 — Investor Residency** — real-estate investment ≥ QAR 730,000 grants 5-year renewable Permanent Residency Card (per MCI 2020 implementing regulations)
  - **Qatar National Vision 2030 (QNV2030)** + **Third National Development Strategy (NDS-3, 2024–2030)** — guides infrastructure + climate adaptation policy
  - **VAT — NOT yet implemented** in Qatar as of 2026-05. GCC VAT Framework Agreement was signed by all 6 GCC states (2016); Saudi/UAE/Bahrain/Oman implemented 2018–2022; **Qatar + Kuwait have not enacted national VAT laws** — verify current status at `https://www.mof.gov.qa/`
  - **FIFA World Cup 2022 legacy** — Lusail Metro completed 2019; Lusail city, Msheireb Downtown Doha redevelopment delivered; oversupply absorption 2023–2026 affecting prices
  - **Qatar Tourism (QT)** STR licensing tightening post-2022 — informal Airbnb increasingly enforced

## 🇶🇦 Foreign buyer eligibility (READ FIRST)

This section determines whether a transaction is even legally possible. Confirm zone status BEFORE assessing price.

### Legal basis

- **Law 16/2018** "Regulating the Ownership and Use of Real Estate by Non-Qataris" — primary statute superseding earlier 2004 + 2010 framework
- **Cabinet Decision 28/2020** — implementing regulations; specifies the 9 freehold + 16 leasehold zones
- Administered jointly by **Ministry of Justice (REA)** + **Ministry of Commerce and Industry (MCI)** + Ministry of Municipality

### Tier 1 — 9 Freehold zones (non-Qataris, including non-GCC, can own outright)

Per Cabinet Decision 28/2020 (and 2024 restatement — verify current list at `https://www.moci.gov.qa/`):

1. **The Pearl-Qatar** (Doha — reclaimed islands; flagship freehold-for-foreigners development since 2004)
2. **West Bay Lagoon** (Doha — premium residential)
3. **Al Khor Resort** (Al Khor — coastal resort community)
4. **Lusail** (Al Daayen — flagship new city; FIFA World Cup 2022 venue)
5. **Al Kharaij** (Al Daayen)
6. **Al-Dafna** (Doha — financial district selected plots)
7. **Onaiza** (Doha — selected plots)
8. **Al-Wakra** (selected plots)
9. **Rawdat Al Jahhaniya** (Al Rayyan — selected plots)

(Zone names per MCI 2020 implementing regulations — exact list and any 2024–2026 additions: **verify at MCI freehold-zone lookup before reservation**.)

### Tier 2 — 16 Leasehold zones (non-Qataris: 99-year renewable usufruct only, NOT freehold)

Per Cabinet Decision 28/2020, leasehold-only zones include (illustrative, not exhaustive — confirm with MCI):

- **Msheireb Downtown Doha**, **Fereej Abdul Aziz**, **Al Mansoura**, **Al Sadd**, **Mirqab Al Jadeed**, **Al Doha Al Jadeeda**, **Al Ghanim Al Atiq**, **Al Rifa'a**, **Al Hitmi Al Atiq**, **Najma**, **Umm Ghuwailina**, **Al Khulaifat**, **Al Aziziya**, **Bin Mahmoud**, **Rawdat Al Khail**, **Old Al Hitmi**

(*Verify current list at MCI — `https://www.moci.gov.qa/` — leasehold-zone lookup.*)

### Outside designated zones

Non-Qataris **cannot acquire any registered interest** in real estate outside the 25 designated zones. Even renting commercial premises outside these zones is restricted to entities with appropriate commercial registration. **GCC nationals** historically have somewhat broader access via reciprocity arrangements — verify per nationality at REA.

### Visa via property — Investor Residency + Permanent Residency

| Item | Threshold | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| **Investor Residency Card (Law 21/2018)** | Real-estate investment **≥ QAR 730,000** (~USD 200k) in any of the 25 designated zones | MCI + Ministry of Interior (MOI) |
| Validity | **5 years renewable** while asset is maintained | MOI |
| Inclusion | Owner + immediate family (spouse + minor children) — visa-free entry | MOI |
| **Permanent Residency (Law 10/2018)** | Qualifying assets ≥ **QAR 1,000,000** (~USD 275k) — includes freehold real estate; plus other criteria (long Qatar residence + Arabic + clean record for non-investor PR routes) | MOI Permanent Residency Committee |
| Validity | Granted for life (subject to revocation conditions) | MOI |
| Annual cap | **~100 PR grants/year** under Law 10/2018 (per published guidance) | MOI |
| **Citizenship by Investment** | **NONE** — Qatari nationality is governed by Law 38/2005 with extremely narrow naturalisation pathway (typically 25 years residence + Arabic + Muslim) | MOI Naturalisation Department |

(Sources: Law 10/2018 PR; Law 21/2018 Investor Residency; MCI Investor Residency portal; MOI naturalisation framework Law 38/2005 — *verify current thresholds in QAR vs USD at the official Hukoomi government portal `https://hukoomi.gov.qa/`*)

### Compliance / AML

- **Qatar Central Bank (QCB)** AML rules (Law 20/2019 on Combating Money Laundering and Terrorism Financing) require **source-of-funds documentation** for property purchases
- **National Anti-Money Laundering and Terrorism Financing Committee (NAMLC)** under MOF — `http://www.namlc.gov.qa/en/index.html` — coordinates real-estate sector compliance under FATF MENAFATF assessments
- Cash transactions and high-value real-estate deals subject to enhanced due diligence; brokers + developers are designated reporting entities
- Source-of-funds: bank statements, employment letter, prior asset sale proof — required at REA registration

### Verification path

1. Confirm property is in a **designated freehold zone** (if buying freehold) OR designated leasehold zone (if 99-yr lease) — MCI lookup
2. Pull seller's current Title Deed extract from REA — verify ownership + encumbrances
3. If off-plan → verify developer registration with MCI + escrow account active (where applicable for the project)
4. Source-of-funds documentation ready before signing SPA
5. Investor Residency application can run in parallel post-registration

## Section: `--tax`

### Annual property tax

**There is NO annual real-estate property tax in Qatar** — neither at federal nor at municipality level. **This is unique among major GCC peers** (UAE has municipal housing fees; Saudi has White Land + Vacant Property Fees and Real Estate Transaction Tax; Bahrain has property tax for non-GCC owners).

What residents do pay annually:

| Item | Rate / amount | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| **Annual property tax** | **NONE** | — |
| **Municipal services** (waste, road maintenance) | Funded from general taxation; no per-property line | Ministry of Municipality |
| **Service charges** (compound / tower OA) | **QAR 18–80 / sqm / yr** depending on community/tier (Pearl-Qatar Owner Association is the most-developed regime) | Master developer / OA — UDC for Pearl-Qatar |
| **District cooling (where applicable)** | Capacity charge + consumption — typical residential **QAR 30–80 / sqm / yr** all-in | Qatar Cool (Pearl + West Bay), Qatar District Cooling Company |
| **Kahramaa** (electricity + water) | Tiered residential — first 4,000 kWh/month low; higher slabs progressive | Kahramaa `https://www.km.qa/` |

(Sources: UDC Pearl-Qatar OA fee schedule; Qatar Cool tariffs; Kahramaa residential tariff schedule — *verify current rates at `https://www.km.qa/`*)

### Service-charge tier reference (Doha 2026 — per listing platforms / OA bulletins)

| Tier | QAR/sqm/yr | Examples |
|---|---:|---|
| **Entry** | 18–35 | Older Al Sadd / Bin Mahmoud apartments (limited foreign-buyer freehold; mostly leasehold) |
| **Mid** | 35–55 | Lusail Fox Hills / standard towers |
| **Premium** | 55–80 | Pearl Porto Arabia / Viva Bahriya / Lusail Marina mid |
| **Luxury** | 80–130 | West Bay Lagoon villas / Pearl Tower 25 / branded |

(*Service-charge schedules are negotiated per OA — verify current per-building schedule with the master community / developer.*)

### Transaction taxes (one-time at purchase)

| Item | Amount | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **REA Property Registration Fee** | **0.25% of sale value** | Statutory; paid at REA registration (Ministry of Justice) |
| **Notarial / authentication fees** | ~QAR 100–500 fixed | Public Notary office (under MOJ) |
| **Real estate agent commission** | **2.5% (typical, negotiable)** of price — split or buyer-side per MOU | MCI-licensed brokers |
| **Conveyancer / lawyer** (optional but advisable for non-Qataris) | QAR 5,000–25,000 typical | Qatar Bar — local lawyer; Mansour Lawyers, Sultan Al-Abdulla, Al Tamimi etc. |
| **Mortgage registration** (if mortgaged) | ~0.25–0.5% of mortgage amount | REA + bank |
| **Bank mortgage processing fee** | 0.5–1% of loan | Per bank |
| **Property valuation** (mortgaged) | QAR 2,000–4,000 | Bank-appointed valuer |
| **Affection Plan + ID copies + translation** | QAR 200–800 admin | Ministry of Municipality |
| **VAT** | **NOT applicable** — Qatar has not enacted national VAT law as of 2026-05 (verify with MOF) | — |

(Sources: REA registration fee schedule; MCI broker regulations; bank schedules — *verify current fees at `https://www.moj.gov.qa/`*)

**Total transaction cost (buyer side, cash)**: **~3–4% of price** (0.25% REA + ~2.5% agent + lawyer + admin) — among the **lowest in the world** for foreign buyers.

### Personal income tax — 0% federally

- **NO personal income tax** in Qatar (Law 24/2018 income tax governs corporate/business activities only)
- **NO capital gains tax** on individual property sales (own-name natural person)
- **NO inheritance tax**

### Corporate income tax — 10% (when relevant)

- **Foreign-owned share** of profits earned in Qatar by corporate entities: **10% flat** (Law 24/2018 Income Tax + Executive Regulations 39/2019) — applies if you hold property in a Qatari company
- **Qatari + GCC-owned** corporate income: **0%** (in most cases)
- **QFC (Qatar Financial Centre)** entities: separate 10% regime within QFC zone
- **Withholding tax** on payments to non-residents from Qatar source: 5%

(Source: General Tax Authority (GTA) Qatar — `https://www.dhareeba.qa/` (online tax portal); Law 24/2018; Executive Regulations 39/2019)

### Inheritance

- **No inheritance tax** in Qatar
- **Sharia inheritance** applies as default for Muslim Qataris (and often by application to Muslim residents); for non-Muslim foreign residents, the **home-country law of the deceased** typically governs movable estate, but **immovable property in Qatar is governed by Qatari law** (lex situs) — practical outcome is Sharia application via Qatari courts unless a foreign-court order is recognised
- **Practical recommendation**: non-Muslim foreign owners should hold Qatar real estate via a structured ownership or a clear cross-border estate plan; consult a Qatar-licensed lawyer + home-jurisdiction counsel

### VAT — NOT YET implemented

- Qatar signed the **GCC VAT Framework Agreement (2016)** with all 6 GCC states
- **Saudi Arabia**, **UAE**, **Bahrain**, **Oman** have implemented VAT (5%, raised to 15% in Saudi 2020)
- **Qatar + Kuwait have NOT yet enacted national VAT laws** as of 2026-05; multiple deferrals since 2018
- **When introduced** (timing uncertain — verify at MOF), expected at **5%** standard rate per the GCC framework; first-sale residential expected to be 0%-rated (per Saudi/UAE precedent)

### Future risk

- **VAT introduction**: timing uncertain; once announced, expect ~12–18 month lead time per Saudi/UAE precedent
- **Excise tax** already exists (Law 25/2018) on tobacco / energy drinks / sugary drinks — separate from VAT
- **Property-tax introduction**: no announcements; politically improbable short-term but tracked

## Section: `--rental`

### Long-term residential

- **Tenancy law**: **Law 4/2008** "Regulating the Lease of Properties" + amendments — landlord/tenant rights, eviction grounds, rent-control framework
- **Mandatory tenancy registration**: Tenancy contracts must be registered at the **Rent Disputes Settlement Committee** under the Ministry of Justice (originally Lease Registration Office) — required for utility connections, residence-permit renewals, school enrolment
- **Rent increase cap**: Law 4/2008 historically limited increases to a fixed annual % (with subsequent suspensions and reforms — verify current rule); landlord must serve notice 90 days before contract end
- **Evictions**: Limited grounds — non-payment, unlicensed sub-letting, owner self-occupation. Disputes routed to the **Rent Disputes Committee** (separate from regular courts; faster resolution)

(Source: Law 4/2008; MOJ Rent Disputes Settlement Committee — *verify current rent-cap formula at MOJ*)

### Short-term rentals (holiday homes)

- **Authority**: **Qatar Tourism (QT)** — formerly Qatar National Tourism Council — `https://www.visitqatar.qa/` (consumer) + `https://www.qatartourism.com/` (regulator)
- **Hotel & Restaurant Operations Regulations** + Decree-Law on Tourism — govern any short-let operation
- **Residential STR < 30 days** generally **requires a hotel-class licence** issued by QT; operating Airbnb on a residential apartment without licence is non-compliant
- **Post-2022 enforcement**: World Cup 2022 saw temporary licensing flexibilities (FIFA fan-village permits); since 2023, **QT has tightened enforcement** of unlicensed short-lets
- **Building OA approval**: Pearl-Qatar OA + most West Bay towers have specific clauses on short-let; verify with master OA before STR strategy
- **Penalties**: fines + closure orders for unlicensed STR operation (per QT enforcement bulletins)

(Source: Qatar Tourism regulator pages; Decree-Law on Tourism — *verify current STR licensing pathway at QT*)

### Strategic notes

- **Service charge + cooling = 20–35% of gross yield** in many high-end towers — model NET yield, not gross
- **Building OA approval** for STR is the single biggest gate — confirm BEFORE buying if STR is the strategy
- **PIT = 0%** federally — but US/UK/EU tax residents will face their own home-country tax on Qatar rental income (Qatar's DTA network is mid-sized — 80+ DTAs, including UK, Italy, France, Germany, Singapore, India, China; verify your nationality)
- **Tenant pool**: heavily expatriate (88% of population); highly mobile (1–3 year contracts common, tied to employment visas); West Bay + Pearl + Lusail are core expat catchments

### Kafala reform notes

- **Kafala (sponsorship) system** materially reformed 2017–2020:
  - **Law 13/2018** + **Law 17/2020** — abolished No-Objection Certificate requirement for switching employers

  - **Exit-permit requirement** abolished for most workers (some senior roles still require employer authorisation)
- Reforms positioned Qatar as the **first GCC state** to materially overhaul Kafala — relevant for property buyers whose tenants are foreign workers (more job mobility = potentially shorter tenancies)

## Section: `--mains`

### Universal in serviced areas

Qatar urban property is essentially **always on mains** for water + electricity + sewerage + (often) cooling — single utility provider nationally for power/water.

| Service | Provider |
|---|---|
| **Electricity + Water** | **Kahramaa** (Qatar General Electricity & Water Corporation) — `https://www.km.qa/` — sole national provider |
| **Sewerage** | Public Works Authority (Ashghal) Drainage Affairs — `https://www.ashghal.gov.qa/` |
| **District cooling** | **Qatar Cool** (Pearl-Qatar + West Bay), **Qatar District Cooling Company (Qatar Cool)** brand alignment, Tabreed Qatar — separate bills; long-term concession contracts |
| **Internet / telecom** | **Ooredoo** (`https://www.ooredoo.qa/`) + **Vodafone Qatar** (`https://www.vodafone.qa/`) — duopoly |
| **Gas** | Bottled LPG (Qatargas Marketing) — most residential uses electric cooking; piped natural gas not common in residential blocks |

### Verification

1. Pull existing **Kahramaa account info** from seller — confirms active mains + no overdue
2. Off-plan: confirm developer has utility connection on the master plan (verify NOC at handover)
3. Pearl + West Bay + Lusail use **Qatar Cool** district cooling — separate bill on top of Kahramaa
4. Some compounds use **TSE (treated sewage effluent)** for landscape irrigation — separate cost line in OA budget
5. Rural Al Khor / Al Shahaniya plots: verify Kahramaa connection availability before plot-only purchase

### Tariffs (residential indicative)

- **Kahramaa electricity (Qatari nationals + GCC)**: subsidised — first 4,000 kWh/month at QAR 0.08/kWh; higher slabs progressive (verify current rate at `https://www.km.qa/`)
- **Kahramaa electricity (expatriates)**: tiered — first 2,000 kWh/month QAR 0.10/kWh; 2,000–4,000 QAR 0.13; 4,000+ QAR 0.16–0.20 (per Kahramaa schedule — *verify current rate*)
- **Kahramaa water**: tiered — first 25 m³/month low; 25–35 m³ mid; 35+ m³ progressive
- **Qatar Cool district cooling**: capacity charge + consumption — typical residential **QAR 30–80 / sqm / yr** all-in (per Qatar Cool tariff schedule — verify current at `https://www.qatarcool.com/`)

## Active fiscal incentives (2026)

- **Investor Residency (5-year renewable)**: real-estate investment ≥ QAR 730,000
- **Permanent Residency (Law 10/2018)**: qualifying assets ≥ QAR 1,000,000 (capped ~100/year)
- **0% personal income tax** on rental income (federal — but check home-country tax residency)
- **0% capital gains tax** on individual property sales (own-name natural person)
- **0% inheritance tax**
- **0% annual property tax**
- **0% VAT** (not yet implemented)
- **Free Zone — QFC (Qatar Financial Centre)**: separate 10% CT regime; 100% foreign ownership; profit repatriation allowed

## Mortgage market

- **QCB regulations** (Instruction 2011 + amendments) cap LTV:
  - **Qatari nationals**: up to **80%** for primary residence (verify QCB current ratio)

  - **Maximum tenor**: typically **20–25 years**, with age cap (loan to be repaid by age 65–70)

## Caveats unique to Qatar

- **Foreign-buyer zones strictly limited** — non-Qataris outside the 25 designated zones cannot acquire any registered interest; verify zone status before any reservation
- **Citizenship by investment is NOT available** — the "buy property → get citizenship" pitch is **not legal in Qatar**; only Investor Residency / Permanent Residency
- **NO VAT (yet)** — unique among major GCC peers; Qatar deferred while Saudi/UAE/Bahrain/Oman implemented; expect 5% introduction at some point — verify timing
- **NO recurring property tax** + **NO personal income tax** + **NO capital gains tax** — Qatar has the **lightest property-tax regime in the GCC** for individual foreign owners
- **Off-plan completion risk** — large developers (UDC, Qatari Diar, Barwa, Msheireb Properties) historically reliable; smaller developers higher variance; verify project status with MCI + master developer
- **Service charges + cooling can be 20–35% of gross rental yield** in high-end towers — model NET, never gross
- **Building OA short-term-rental policy is the single biggest STR gate** — Pearl-UDC OA + most West Bay towers have specific clauses; verify BEFORE buying if STR is the strategy
- **Sharia inheritance default for Muslim residents**; non-Muslim foreign owners should structure ownership / estate plan with Qatar-licensed counsel
- **AML source-of-funds documentation required** at REA registration under Law 20/2019
- **Currency**: QAR pegged USD at 3.64 since 2001 — **no FX risk vs USD**
- **No nationwide postcode** — addresses use Zone No. + Street No. + Building No. (Qatar GIS `https://geoportal.gisqatar.org.qa/`)
- **Construction-noise risk in Lusail** — large parts still under post-2022 build-out; verify radius before buying off-plan or near active sites
- **World Cup oversupply absorption** — Doha residential prices softened 2023–2025 as supply caught up with demand; may continue 2026
- **Limited DTA network for some nationalities** — Qatar has ~80+ DTAs; check your tax residency before structuring

## Quirks to know

- **Address scheme** = Zone No. + Street No. + Building No.; lookup at `https://geoportal.gisqatar.org.qa/` (Qatar GIS)
- **Title Deed (Sanad Mulkiyya)** issued in Arabic — bilingual EN/AR not standard; certified translation may be required for international use
- **Pearl-Qatar UDC OA** — Qatar's most-developed master OA regime; 5-year fee histories available, AGMs regular
- **Lusail Master Developer** is **Qatari Diar** (sovereign-wealth-backed) — generally reliable on completion + handover schedules
- **Msheireb Properties (MPC)** — Qatar Foundation subsidiary; Msheireb Downtown Doha is **leasehold-only for foreigners** despite premium positioning
- **Qatari Diar projects abroad** (London Olympic Village, Paris, etc.) — gives some indication of developer pedigree
- **GSAS (Global Sustainability Assessment System)** — Qatar's local equivalent of LEED/BREEAM; mandatory for new public buildings, optional for residential
- **Education City** (Qatar Foundation) — 8 international universities; major catchment driver for west Doha residential
- **Hamad International Airport (DOH)** — major Qatar Airways hub; flight-path noise considerations for north-Doha + Al Khor approaches
- **Courts**: civil disputes over property typically heard in **Real Estate Court** (under Court of First Instance, Ministry of Justice); tenancy disputes in **Rent Disputes Settlement Committee**; commercial disputes optionally in **Qatar International Court (QICDRC)** for QFC-related matters
