# 澳門 / Macau / Macao SAR 🇲🇴 — Property Due-Diligence Playbook

ISO2: `mo`. Status: ✅ Fully populated (researched 2026-05).

## Country profile

- **Postcode**: **none** — Macao has no postal-code system. Address: District (Freguesia / 堂區) → Street (Rua / 街) → Building (Edifício / 大廈) → Bloco → Floor / Flat
- **Population**: ~687,500 mid-2025 provisional ([DSEC](https://www.dsec.gov.mo/en-US/Statistic/Demography)); ~672,800 end-2024 ([DSEC Yearbook 2024](https://www.dsec.gov.mo/))
- **GDP per capita**: ≈ USD 70,000 (2024 nominal, IMF/DSEC) — high but volatile (gaming-revenue cycle)
- **Currency**: **MOP (Pataca / 澳門元)** — pegged to **HKD at MOP 1.03 = HKD 1.00** ([AMCM](https://www.amcm.gov.mo/en/financial-system/macao-currency)); HKD circulates freely; effectively USD-tracking via HKD-USD peg
- **Languages**: **Chinese (Cantonese 粵語; Traditional 繁體 written)** + **Portuguese** — both official under Basic Law Art. 9; Mandarin growing; English in tourism. Conveyancing instruments dual-language Chinese/Portuguese
- **Admin levels**: 1 SAR → **7 freguesias / 堂區** (5 on Macao Peninsula: Sé / São Lázaro / Santo António / São Lourenço / Nossa Senhora de Fátima; Nossa Senhora do Carmo on Taipa; São Francisco Xavier on Coloane); plus **Cotai reclamation** (administratively Taipa/Coloane). Municipal services consolidated under [IAM / 市政署](https://www.iam.gov.mo/) since 2019
- **Cadastre**: **Conservatória do Registo Predial (CRP) / 物業登記局** under [DSAJ — Direcção dos Serviços de Assuntos de Justiça / 法務局](https://www.dsaj.gov.mo/) — title-by-deeds register (Portuguese-derived civil-law); governed by [Código do Registo Predial — DL 46/99/M](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/)
  - Cadastral mapping: **DSCC — Direcção dos Serviços de Cartografia e Cadastro / 地圖繪製暨地籍局** — `https://www.dscc.gov.mo/`
- **Identifier**: artigo matricial + freguesia + edifício + fracção autónoma; condominium under [Código Civil — DL 39/99/M Art. 1313+](https://www.ihm.gov.mo/uploads/attachment/2022-05/1575862820491c59c2.pdf)
- **Title concept**: **All land in Macao is state-owned** under [Lei de Terras / Land Law (Law 10/2013)](https://www.bo.dsaj.gov.mo/pt/legis/rec/151010) effective **1 March 2014**. Private holdings are **leasehold** via:
  - **Concessão por aforamento / 永佃權批給** — Portuguese-era perpetual leasehold (near-freehold; annual ground rent / foro)
  - **Concessão por arrendamento / 租賃批給** — typical 25-year initial, renewable 10-year periods, max 50 years (Law 10/2013 Art. 47–49)
  - Many "expired without development" concessions reclaimed by Government 2015–2024 (Pearl Horizon case among others)
- **Foreign-buyer rule**: **Foreigners CAN purchase residential** with NO outright nationality bar, but face **Non-Resident Buyer Stamp Duty +10%** layered on standard stamp duty (see `--tax`). **NO golden-visa programme** in Macao
- **Visa**: ❌ **NO investment-residence visa** since the **Investment Residence Scheme (Regulamento Administrativo n.º 3/2005) was suspended by Regulamento Administrativo n.º 7/2007, in force 4 April 2007** (verified via [Boletim Oficial — RA 7/2007](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/isapi/go.asp?d=rega-7-2007pt) + DSI) and **NOT reactivated**. The **Talent Programme (人才引進制度)** — **Law 7/2023, "the Legal System for Talent Recruitment", in force 1 July 2023**, run by the **Talent Development Committee (人才發展委員會)** ([cdqq.gov.mo](https://www.cdqq.gov.mo/en/talent-recruitment-system)) — does NOT take property as a qualifying criterion (its three tracks are High-end Talents, Outstanding Talents and Advanced Professionals, scored on profession, employment, salary and habitual residence) (2026 data, source Talent Development Committee; 2026-09-18 verified). HKID-holders entitled 1-yr residence; tourist 30/90 days
- **Recent reforms (2024–2026)**:
  - **2018 cooling measures (Law 4/2018)**: Non-Resident Buyer Stamp Duty +10% + Resident second-property +5% — ⚠️ **still in force 2026**
  - **Jan 2023**: Casino concession renewal — 6 concessionaires (SJM / Galaxy / Wynn / MGM / Sands / Melco) for 10 yrs through 31 Dec 2032 ([DICJ](https://www.dicj.gov.mo/))
  - **2024**: Talent Programme operationally launches under [Lei 7/2023 — Regime jurídico de captação de quadros qualificados](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/2023/22/lei07.asp) (enacted 2023, in force 1 Jul 2023; Talent Development Committee, 人才發展委員會)
  - **Dec 2024**: LRT Barra extension opens (improves Peninsula-Taipa connectivity); Hengqin Line under construction
  - **Heritage Law enforcement (2013 – present)**: [Lei de Salvaguarda do Património Cultural — Law 11/2013 (BO 36/2013, 2 Sep 2013), as amended by Law 5/2026](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/2013/36/lei11.asp) — strict modification + buffer-zone restrictions around UNESCO **Macao Historic Centre** (22 buildings + 8 squares listed 2005)

⚠️ Macao listings typically quote **gross floor area / 建築面積** (`m² brutos`) — includes ~20–30% common area. **Utilizable area / 實用面積** (`área útil`) is the European-equivalent. Always confirm basis (per listing — verify with cadastre / on visit).

### Compute

1. **MOP/m² (gross)** = listing price ÷ gross area
2. **EUR/m²** ≈ MOP/m² ÷ ~8.8 (MOP/EUR Q1 2026 indicative; tracks USD/EUR via HKD-USD peg)
3. Cross-check DSEC HPI sub-index (Peninsula / Taipa / Coloane)
4. Last 6–12 months CRP-recorded transactions same building (certidão predial)
5. **Trap**: parking-space (`lugar de estacionamento`) typically a **separate fracção autónoma** with its own price (~MOP 800k–2.5M); confirm whether listing includes parking
6. **Trap**: management-fee + sinking-fund NOT in listing price — ~MOP 2,000–5,000/month for 100m² Cotai/NAPE flat

### Trend Q1 2022 → Q4 2025

- **DSEC HPI -25 to -35% peak-to-trough cumulative 2018 → 2024** est. (per quarterly DSEC archive — rates may have changed; verify latest release)
- **Stabilising late 2024 → 2025**: transaction volume rising on tourism rebound + Talent Programme inflows; price index ~flat YoY Q3-Q4 2025
- **Drivers**: gaming concession renewal Jan 2023 (10-yr stability) + China outbound-travel reopening Q1 2023 + Talent Programme 2024 + USD rate-cut cycle (rates track via HKD peg)

### Verdict bands (Macao-adjusted small-territory)

- 🟢 < 3,000 v/d (residential street, Coloane village)
- 🟡 3,000–15,000 v/d (district road, Taipa internal)
- 🟠 15,000–50,000 v/d (primary corridor — Avenida da Praia Grande, Avenida da Amizade, Avenida Almeida Ribeiro)

### Coarse fallback (OSM `highway`)

motorway = expressway / HZMB approach / Cotai elevated · trunk = principal distributor (e.g. Avenida da Amizade) · primary = district distributor · secondary = local distributor · residential = local access · service = back-of-house

⚠️ Macao Peninsula (~32 km² urban) hosts ~240,000 registered vehicles (2024 DSAT) → vehicle density among world's highest. Even "secondary" streets in Sé / São Lázaro can hit 20,000+ v/d at peak. Cross-check **bridge corridor noise** (Ponte de Sai Van / Ponte Governador Nobre de Carvalho / Ponte Flor de Lótus) for waterfront properties.

## Section: `--tax`

### Annual Property Tax — Contribuição Predial Urbana / 房屋稅

Statute: [Regulamento da Contribuição Predial Urbana — Law 19/78/M](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/), as amended.

| Charge | Rate | Base |
|---|---|---|
| **Property Tax — leased** | **8%** of gross actual rent (10% maintenance deduction → effective ~7.2%) | annual rent received. Rate reduced from 10% → 8% in 2023 under **Law 19/2022** (FY 2023 Budget); **re-enacted annually**, FY 2026 confirmed 8% (PwC Tax Summaries). (2026-05-27 verified.) |
| **Property Tax — owner-occupied / vacant** | **6%** of "rendimento colectável" (DSF-assessed notional rental value) | DSF `valor de avaliação` |
| **Owner-occupier rebate** | **Fixed MOP 3,500 reduction** on the property-tax liability (NOT a percentage) | Introduced under **Law 19/2022** for FY 2023; re-enacted each year (Law 25/2024 = FY 2025; Law 13/2025 = FY 2026). **Excludes corporates + non-Macao residents.** (2026-05-27 verified, source PwC + MdME + cdqq.gov.mo Talent Development Committee.) |

⚠️ **Effective owner-occupied Property Tax**: 6% × (DSF notional value × 0.9 maintenance deduction) **minus MOP 3,500 fixed reduction** (caps at actual liability). For mid-tier 80–120m² Taipa/Cotai apartment with notional value MOP 50,000/yr → 6% × 0.9 = MOP 2,700 base, minus MOP 3,500 = **MOP 0 payable**. Higher-value flats with bigger notional values keep effective rate close to 6% × 0.9 net of MOP 3,500. Verify the **boletim de cobrança da contribuição predial** issued annually. ⚠️ **Tax base = DSF notional value, NOT market price** — DSF reassessments lag; current values often anchored early-2010s.

### Stamp Duty (Imposto do Selo / 印花稅) — transaction-side

[Regulamento do Imposto do Selo — Law 17/88/M, updated 2018](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/):

| Consideration band | Rate |
|---|---:|
| ≤ MOP 2,000,000 | 1% |
| MOP 2,000,001 – MOP 4,000,000 | 2% (slice) |
| > MOP 4,000,000 | 3% (slice) |

Plus a **5% surcharge applied on the calculated stamp-duty amount** (NOT 0.5% of price) — effective rates therefore **1.05% / 2.10% / 3.15%**. (2026-05-27 verified, source PwC Tax Summaries + MdME + cdqq.gov.mo — supersedes prior "0.5% verba 1.1" wording.)

#### 2026 Policy Address resident exemption (Nov 2025, in force FY 2026)

- **Resident first-time homebuyer stamp-duty exemption raised** from MOP 3,000,000 → **MOP 6,000,000** (eligible resident, one residential unit), confirmed under FY 2026 Budget Law 13/2025 per Macao 2026 Policy Address. Supersedes the prior MOP 3M exemption introduced by Law 19/2022.
- **Resident self-use mortgage LTV cap raised 70% → 80%** (eligible residents, primary residence) — see `--finance` section.
- January 2026 residential mortgage approvals jumped **+76.7% MoM to MOP 1.59B** per AMCM. (2026-05-27 verified, source Macao News + gov.mo Policy Address [https://www.gov.mo/en/news/374463/](https://www.gov.mo/en/news/374463/) + MdME.)

### Special Stamp Duty (額外印花稅) — anti-flipping (Law 6/2011)

| Holding period | Rate |
|---|---:|
| Sale **< 1 year** from acquisition | **20%** of sale price |
| Sale **1–2 years** | **10%** of sale price |
| Sale **> 2 years** | nil |

### Non-Resident BSD (非首置買家額外印花稅) — Law 4/2018 cooling measures

| Buyer status | Additional rate |
|---|---:|
| Macao SAR resident — first-time buyer | nil |
| Macao SAR resident — second + property | **+5%** of price |
| **Non-resident buyer (foreigners + corporate)** | **+10%** of price |

⚠️ Effective for foreign buyer of MOP 5M apartment: standard stamp duty (1% × 2M + 2% × 2M + 3% × 1M = 90k) + 5% surcharge on the 90k stamp-duty amount (4.5k) + 10% non-resident BSD (500k) = **~MOP 595k (~11.9%)** total stamp-duty footprint. Verify exact computation with [DSF](https://www.dsf.gov.mo/).

### Other transaction-side costs

| Item | Cost (MOP) |
|---|---:|
| Notary fee (`escritura pública`) | ~8,000–25,000 typical residential |
| CRP registration fee | ~3,000–15,000 sliding by consideration |

### Capital gains

- **NO general CGT** for individuals on residential held > 2 years
- Sales **< 2 yrs** caught by **Special Stamp Duty** (20% / 10%)
- **Profissional Tax (7–12% progressive)** can recharacterise frequent flipping as professional trading
- **Complementary Tax (9–12%, effectively flat 12% above MOP 600k)** for corporate buyers

### Inheritance / Estate Duty

**Estate Duty / Imposto sobre as Sucessões e Doações abolished** c.2001 (transitional period under SAR establishment). **No estate / inheritance / gift tax** for transfers to lineal descendants/spouses. Civil-law forced-heirship rules per Código Civil apply. Probate via [Tribunais de Macau](https://www.court.gov.mo/).

### Future risk

- Periodic CGT discussion tied to gaming-revenue concentration; not currently signalled
- Annual Budget Law (`Lei do Orçamento`) — owner-occupier MOP 3,500 fixed reduction has been renewed every year since introduction but is technically annual; watch [Boletim Oficial](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/) Series I

## Section: `--rental`

### Long-term residential

- **Tenancy law**: [Código Civil](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/99/31/codcivpt/indice_art.asp?printer=1) Art. 969+ (Locação — Art. 969.º Noção; Art. 970.º Arrendamento e aluguer; Lei do Arrendamento Urbano absorbed into Civil Code 1999); minimal statutory rent control
- **Standard tenancy**: 2-year fixed term; 2-month security deposit + 1-month advance; 90-day renewal/termination notice
- **Property Tax 8%** on actual gross rent paid by landlord (~7.2% effective with 10% maintenance deduction) — Law 19/2022, FY 2026 confirmed; see `--tax`
- Utilities + management fee borne by tenant; rates by landlord typical

### Yields (Q4 2025 est., DSEC rental + price triangulation)

| Area | est. Gross yield |
|---|---:|
| Macao Peninsula (national avg) | ~3.0–3.5% |
| Northern Peninsula (Fátima) | ~3.5–4.5% |
| Sé / NAPE central | ~2.5–3.2% |
| Taipa | ~3.0–3.8% |
| Cotai luxury | ~2.0–3.0% |
| Coloane low-density | ~2.5–3.5% |

⚠️ Yields compressed vs HK (HK avg ~3.9% Q2 2025) — small rental pool + casino-worker demographic concentration in employer/government housing.

### Short-term rentals (民宿 / Airbnb / serviced apartments)

⚠️ **CRITICAL: short-term letting < 30 consecutive days requires hotel/guesthouse licensing** under [Decree-Law 16/96/M — Regime de Licenciamento dos Estabelecimentos Hoteleiros e Similares](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/), with enforcement and penalties under [Law 3/2010 — *Proibição de prestação ilegal de alojamento* (BO 31/2010)](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/2010/31/lei03.asp), as amended by Law 3/2022: providing accommodation in premises not intended for hotel use, by a person without an establishment licence, is the offence the 2010 law defines and sanctions (2026-09-18 verified).

| Stay duration | Regulation | Practical reality |
|---|---|---|
| ≥ 30 days | Outside hotel-law scope — ordinary tenancy | Serviced-apartment market viable |
| **< 30 days, unlicensed** | **ILLEGAL** — fines up to MOP 800,000 + closure orders | **Active DST enforcement 2024–2026** |
| < 30 days, licensed | Hotel / Pensão / Hospedaria licence from DST required | Most condominiums prohibit hotel use; licence requires fire-safety + modification works + condominium consent |

- **Authority**: [DST — Direcção dos Serviços de Turismo / 旅遊局](https://www.macaotourism.gov.mo/)
- **Practical conclusion**: credible Macao short-let strategy is **monthly+ serviced apartment** (≥ 30 days), NOT Airbnb-style nightly. Pursuing nightly without licence is enforcement exposure → fines + closure

### Specific risks (Macao)

- **Typhoons (颱風 / Tufões)**: 4–6 tropical cyclones/yr affect region; T8 issued ~3–6× per Jun–Oct season; **T10** rare but devastating — **Super Typhoon Hato (天鴿) 23 Aug 2017** caused 10 fatalities + ~MOP 12.5B damage; **Super Typhoon Mangkhut (山竹) 16 Sep 2018** issued T10 with major NAPE / inner-harbour flooding; Saola 2023; Ragasa 2025. SMG maintains [historical TC archive](https://www.smg.gov.mo/zh/page/c_57/c_subpage/c_57_19)
- **Storm surge / coastal flooding**: critical for **inner harbour / Porto Interior** (Avenida Almirante Lacerda, Rua das Lorchas, Rua do Almirante Sérgio) and **NAPE waterfront** — Hato 2017 inundated ground floors to 1–2m. Government built **Inner-Harbour Flood Prevention Barrier 2020–2024** but **NAPE remains exposed**
- **Pluvial flooding**: Black Rainstorm increasing frequency; low-lying Iao Hon / Areia Preta / Patane historically vulnerable
- **Earthquake**: passive margin of South China Block — **LOW** historical risk (no >M6 within 100km); design level MMI VI–VII / PGA ~0.07g (per SMG monitoring)
- **Subsidence (reclamation)**: **moderate-to-elevated** for reclaimed land — Macao expanded ~10km² (1840) → ~33km² (2025) via reclamation. **Cotai (entirely reclaimed 1990s–2000s)**, NAPE (1980s+), Areia Preta — verify pile-foundation + fill compaction history via DSOP records
- **Heritage-zone restriction**: any property within UNESCO **Macao Historic Centre buffer zone** (22 buildings + 8 squares + 8 protection zones, listed 2005) faces strict modification + maintenance limits under [Lei de Salvaguarda do Património Cultural — Law 11/2013 (as amended by Law 5/2026)](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/2013/36/lei11.asp); request [Instituto Cultural / 文化局](https://www.icm.gov.mo/) classification status before any renovation

### Build-era hazards

## Section: `--mains`

### National framework

- **SAAM — Sociedade de Abastecimento de Águas de Macau / 澳門自來水股份有限公司**: `https://www.macaowater.com/` — sole concessioned water utility (Suez group); imports >95% of fresh water from Zhuhai / Guangdong via Lapa Treatment Plant
- **DSPA**: `https://www.dspa.gov.mo/` — sewerage policy + water quality
- **IAM** (municipal services): drainage + sewer at municipal level
- **CEM — Companhia de Electricidade de Macau / 澳電**: `https://www.cem-macau.com/` — sole concessioned electricity utility
- **Gas**: NAM KWONG / 南光 (Macao Natural Gas) limited piped network; bottled LPG common in older buildings
- Coverage: **>99%** mains water + sewer; full reticulation outside one or two Coloane village pockets

### Verification

1. Request **SAAM water account confirmation** (`certificado de conta`) from seller
2. Confirm CEM electricity account transfer at completion
3. Older buildings (pre-1990): verify gas type (piped vs bottled); bottled LPG cylinders subject to CB annual safety inspection
4. Coloane rural / village: confirm septic tank vs mains sewer connection via IAM

### Costs (2026 typical)

| Scenario | Cost (MOP) |
|---|---:|
| SAAM water account opening (existing connection) | 0 admin (deposit ~800–2,500) |
| New water connection + meter | ~2,000–8,000 |
| CEM electricity account opening | ~1,000–3,000 deposit |
| Sewer connection (rural Coloane, mains available) | ~10,000–60,000 |
| Septic tank (rural, no mains) | ~40,000–150,000 |
| Quarterly water + sewer (typical 2-bed apt) | ~200–500/quarter |
| Monthly electricity (typical 2-bed AC-heavy) | ~800–2,500 (Apr–Oct heavy) |

## Section: `--finance`

### Mortgage market

- **Regulator**: [AMCM — Autoridade Monetária de Macau / 澳門金融管理局](https://www.amcm.gov.mo/)
- **LTV caps** (per [AMCM Notice 014/2018-AMCM + amendments](https://www.amcm.gov.mo/)):

| Property value | Max LTV (self-use, residential, resident) |
|---|---:|
| ≤ MOP 8,000,000 | **70%** |
| > MOP 8,000,000 | **60%** |
| Investment / second-home / non-self-use | typically **50%** |
| Non-resident borrower | typically **50%** (case-by-case, more conservative) |

- **DSR**: **50% of monthly income** (60% net-worth borrowers); +200bps stress-test framework
- **Rate structure**: HIBOR-linked dominant (HKD interbank, since MOP pegs HKD pegs USD); HIBOR 1M Q1 2026 ≈ 4.0–4.5% → effective mortgage ≈ 3.5–4.5%
- **30-year amortisation standard**; fixed-rate residential rare
- **Foreigner mortgages**: most banks offer to non-residents but typically demand:
  - Macao bank account (passport + proof of address; AMCM AML tightening 2020+)
  - 50% LTV maximum
  - Income evidence + tax returns from home country
  - Major non-resident-friendly: **BNU**, **Bank of China Macau**, **ICBC Macau**, **OCBC Wing Hang**, **BCM (Banco Comercial de Macau)**

### Banking access

Account opening requires Macao Resident ID Card (BIR / 居民身份證) for residents; for non-residents, in-person visit + W-8BEN/CRS forms; minimum-balance products common. AMCM enforces strict AML/KYC since 2020+ FATF reforms; non-resident accounts more selective.

⚠️ AMCM banking heavily integrated with HK (BOCHK / HSBC HK / SCB HK have Macao operations); cross-border MOP/HKD payments routed via interbank clearing.

## Section: `--currency`

### Pataca peg structure

- **MOP pegged HKD at MOP 1.03 = HKD 1.00** ([AMCM](https://www.amcm.gov.mo/en/financial-system/macao-currency))
- HKD pegged USD via Linked Exchange Rate System at HKD 7.75–7.85 = USD 1
- **Effective MOP/USD**: ~7.98–8.08 (Q1 2026 indicative)
- **HKD circulates freely as legal tender** in Macao retail; ~50% of consumer transactions in casinos + tourism settled in HKD
- AMCM Pataca Issue Institutions: BNU + Bank of China Macau

### What this means for buyers

- **USD-denominated buyers**: near-zero FX risk (MOP tracks HKD tracks USD)
- **HKD-denominated buyers** (most cross-border): near-zero FX risk (peg stable since 1995)
- **EUR / GBP / JPY buyers**: MOP effectively a USD-tracking currency; FX exposure = home-vs-USD exposure
- **CNY (RMB) buyers**: NOT pegged to RMB; cross-border capital flows from PRC subject to outbound controls
- **Hedging**: thin native MOP forward market — most hedging in HKD-equivalent leg

### Peg credibility

AMCM foreign reserves Q1 2026 ≈ USD 25B (~5× MOP monetary base). Peg stable through 1997–98 Asian crisis, 2008 GFC, 2020+ pandemic, USD-strength 2022–2024. **MOP-HKD peg at ~1.03 has been in effect since 1983** (predates SAR handover 1999). **Law 24/2024 — New General Tax Code** effective **1 January 2026** introduces source-based / territorial profits-tax treatment, OECD-aligned transfer-pricing rules, statutory 183-day tax-residency definition — material for corporate-vehicle buyers of Macao property; individual no-CGT outcome preserved. (2026-05-27 verified, source AMCM "The Pataca" + PwC + EY + MdME.)

⚠️ **Tail risk**: any sustained breakdown of HKD peg propagates to MOP. Position-size for non-USD/HKD buyers.

## Section: `--visa`

### ❌ NO golden-visa via property

**Property investment alone does NOT qualify for any Macao residence permit.** [Investment Residence Scheme — Despacho do Chefe do Executivo n.º 7/2007](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/) was **suspended on 2 April 2007** and **NOT reactivated** (verified via Boletim Oficial archive + DSI). This contrasts with HK New CIES (HK$30M+) which exists.

### Talent + residence schemes

| Scheme | Threshold | Visa | PR path |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Talent Programme (人才引進制度) — Talent Development Committee (人才發展委員會)** | High-skill / academic / specialised — invitation + assessment | 3–5 yr | 7 yrs continuous |
| **Non-Resident Worker (Blue Card)** | Employer-sponsored | 1–3 yr renewable | does NOT lead to PR |
| **HK SAR resident reciprocal** | HKID-holder | 1-yr residence | counts toward 7-yr |
| **Mainland China — case-by-case** | Mainland-Macao agreements | varies | varies |
| **Family reunification** | Spouse/parent of Macao PR | varies | 7 yrs |

### Talent Programme details ([Talent Development Committee / 人才發展委員會](https://www.cdqq.gov.mo/en/talent-recruitment-system))

- Launched 2024 under [Law 7/2023 — Regime de Captação de Talentos](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/)
- Three tracks: **High-end Talent** (senior executives / academic chairs), **Specialised Talent** (gaming, finance, tech), **Talented Persons** (high-achievers in priority sectors)
- Property-purchase NOT a qualifying criterion (unlike HK CIES which has HK$10M property bucket)

### Permanent Residence

7 years' continuous ordinary residence + intent test under [Law 8/1999](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/) → apply for **BIR-P / 永久性居民身份證** at [DSI](https://www.dsi.gov.mo/). Failure of intent test → application refused.

⚠️ Macao does NOT recognise dual-nationality for Chinese nationals (same as HK) — declare nationality change to DSI.

## Section: `--insurance`

### Mandatory

- **Fire Insurance (`Seguro de Incêndio`)**: required by every mortgage lender; covers building structure (NOT contents); typical premium **0.05–0.20% of sum insured** (per market quotes from Macao retail insurers)
  - Sum insured = **rebuild cost** (not market price) — typically MOP 25,000–MOP 60,000 per m² standard concrete frame
  - Multi-unit condominium often has **block-policy** purchased centrally by Owners' Assembly; pro-rata via management fee — confirm before duplicating

### Optional but standard

| Cover | Typical premium (2026) |
|---|---:|

| Flood / storm-surge | included in major bundles; **sub-limits common for inner-harbour ground-floor units** post-Hato 2017 |

## Section: `--notary`

### Conveyancing process (civil-law inherited)

Macao uses **civil-law notarial conveyancing** (Portuguese-derived) — opposite to HK's solicitor-based common-law model. The **Notário (公證員)** authenticates + executes the **escritura pública**.

- **Notarial structure**: [Cartórios Notariais Privados / 私人公證員事務所](https://www.dsaj.gov.mo/) regulated by DSAJ; plus **Cartório Notarial dos Registos e Notariado (1.º + 2.º)** state notaries

### Standard timeline

| Step | Day | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Contrato-promessa (preliminary contract / 預約買賣合同) | D0 | Buyer pays sinal/deposit ~10% to notary stakeholder |
| 2. Due-diligence + financing | D7–D45 | Buyer's solicitor (optional) reviews CRP certidão + cadastral plan + heritage status; bank mortgage approval |
| 3. Escritura Pública de Compra e Venda (formal deed / 不動產買賣公證書) | D45–D75 | Both parties + notary execute; full price paid (less mortgage proceeds + sinal) |
| 4. Registo na CRP | within 30 days | Buyer registered as new owner; mortgage charge registered |
| 5. Stamp Duty payment | within 30 days | DSF assesses + collects |

### Costs

| Item | Typical (MOP) |
|---|---|
| Notary fee (escritura) | ~8,000–25,000 + DSAJ fixed fees |
| Buyer's lawyer (advogado, optional) | ~8,000–30,000 |
| CRP registration fee | ~3,000–15,000 sliding |
| Cadastral plan certificate (DSCC) | ~500–2,000 |
| Stamp Duty | per Stamp Duty schedule (see `--tax`) |
| **Total (resident first-time)** | **~3–5% of price** |
| **Total (non-resident with +10% BSD)** | **~12–15% of price** |

### Notary selection + foreign buyer practical

Source: [DSAJ list of notários privados](https://www.dsaj.gov.mo/). State notaries (1.º + 2.º Cartório) and ~30 private notarial offices. ⚠️ Civil-law notary is **neutral public officer** — buyer should still consider an independent **advogado** for adversarial due-diligence, especially heritage-zone or cross-border purchases.

- **No need to be physically present**: notary accepts apostilled Power of Attorney for execution
- **AML / source-of-funds**: required under [Law 2/2006 — AML Law](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/) + AMCM AML Notices

## Section: `--retirement`

### Retirement to Macao scenario

Macao is **NOT a retirement-first destination** by international standards — high-CoL urban density, no specific retirement visa, **no golden-visa via property**. Most foreign retirees arrive via:

1. Family reunification (sponsored by Macao PR family)
2. Talent Programme retiree carve-out (rare; case-by-case)
3. HKID-holder reciprocal residence (1-yr renewable; HK retirees commonly maintain Macao secondary base)
4. Returning Macao PR
5. Mainland-China retirees with cross-border family

### Cost-of-living for retirees

- Median 2-bed apt rental Q1 2026: ~MOP 12,000–25,000/month outside prime
- Public healthcare via [SSM — Serviços de Saúde de Macau](https://www.ssm.gov.mo/): low-cost or free for residents at CHCSJ; **non-residents billed at full cost** (~MOP 1,500–5,000/day inpatient)
- Private health insurance for 65+: ~MOP 15,000–50,000/yr typical
- LRT + bus monthly: ~MOP 300–800
- Utilities (2-bed apt): ~MOP 1,200–3,000/month

### Tax position for retirees

- **No CGT general, no estate duty, no wealth tax** → low-tax retirement jurisdiction
- Foreign-source pension generally NOT taxed (territorial system)
- Profissional Tax (7–12%) only if working post-retirement
- **Property Tax 8%** if letting other Macao properties (Law 19/2022, FY 2026)

### Healthcare access

- **CHCSJ — Centro Hospitalar Conde de São Januário (山頂醫院)** — main public hospital, free or low-cost for residents
- **Hospital Kiang Wu (鏡湖醫院)** — large private/charity
- **University Hospital — Macau University of Science and Technology**
- HK private alternative ~1-hour ferry/HZMB drive

### Verdict for retirement-first buyers

🟡 **Watch** — viable for Cantonese-speaking retirees with existing Macao/HK family ties; questionable for moderate-net-worth retirees seeking lifestyle (Lisbon / Valencia / Chiang Mai dramatically cheaper). Best fit: returning Macao diaspora, HK retirees seeking cross-border base, Talent-Programme alumni transitioning.

## Section: `--digital-nomad`

### Visa pathway

Macao has **NO formal "digital nomad visa"** comparable to Portugal D8 / Spain DNV / Estonia DNV. Pathways:

1. Visitor (visa-free) entry — varies by passport; **30 days standard** (most Western); up to 90 days some — verify via [DSI](https://www.dsi.gov.mo/) — ⚠️ work technically prohibited
2. HKID-holder 1-yr residence (most common cross-border)
3. Talent Programme (high-skill specialists with employer/academic sponsorship)
4. Non-Resident Worker permit (employer-sponsored)

### Connectivity

- **Internet**: **CTM — Companhia de Telecomunicações de Macau / 澳門電訊** dominant; gigabit FTTB widespread; ~MOP 250–500/month for 1-Gbps
- **Mobile**: 3 Macau / SmarTone / China Telecom / CTM — ~MOP 100–350/month for 5G
- **Co-working**: limited but growing — The Workshop, MakerHive Macao, CPTTM (Macao Productivity Centre) — ~MOP 1,500–4,500/month hot desk
- **Time zone**: UTC+8 — same as HK / mainland China

### Cost-of-living for remote worker (Q1 2026)

- Studio short-let: ~MOP 8,000–18,000/month (Taipa, NAPE, Sé)
- Eating-out: ~MOP 50–150 cha chaan teng / 200–500 mid-range / 800+ Cotai integrated-resort
- Transport: ~MOP 300–800/month

### Tax position

- **Profissional Tax (7–12%)** applies on income for services rendered IN Macao (territorial source); remote worker employed by foreign employer working FROM Macao > 60 days/yr is technically liable for Profissional Tax on Macao-rendered portion
- No specific digital-nomad incentive

### Verdict for digital-nomad-first buyers

🟠 **Adapt** — workable as APAC base for established earners with HK / mainland family ties, but visa friction + DL 16/96/M < 30-day STR prohibition kills standard Airbnb-host model. If goal is short-let income while abroad, pivot to monthly+ corporate let. **Macao's digital-nomad value proposition materially weaker than HK + SG + JP**.

- **Energy efficiency labelling**: under **DSPA — Direcção dos Serviços de Protecção Ambiental / 環境保護局** ([energy hub](https://www.dspa.gov.mo/energytopics/)), which absorbed the functions of the former GDSE (能源業發展辦公室) on **1 January 2021 under Administrative Regulation 38/2020**; smaller stock + less mandatory disclosure than HK MEELS (2026 data, source DSPA; 2026-09-18 verified)
- **Macao 5-year Low-Carbon Plan** + **Sustainable Development Strategy** (DSPA)

### Property-level ESG

- **Green Building certification**: rare; mostly Cotai integrated-resort projects (Sands, Melco, Galaxy) with international LEED / BREEAM
- **Green-mortgage**: emerging (BNU + BOC Macau preliminary offerings); discount typically ~5–10bps for certified-green (selective)
- **Solar PV**: rare in residential due to dense vertical stock + typhoon-load envelope-design constraints

### Climate-physical risk (cross-section)

- Storm-surge + sea-level rise: progressive premium on inner-harbour + NAPE + Cotai low-elevation; insurance + retrofit cost rising
- Cooling-load growth + envelope-stress (curtain wall thermal cycling) + tropical-marine salt-corrosion → 30+ yr stock requires escalating façade-maintenance

- **Owners' Assembly (Assembleia de Condóminos)** governance under [DL 41/95/M (Condominium Regulations)](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/) — minority-protection via civil court
- **Heritage Law 11/2013** restricts modification within UNESCO buffer zones — verify any property within Macao Historic Centre buffer

## Caveats unique to Macao

- **All land is state-owned** ([Lei de Terras 10/2013](https://www.bo.dsaj.gov.mo/pt/legis/rec/151010)) — private holdings leasehold (concessão por arrendamento 25-yr renewable, max 50; or pre-2014 perpetual aforamento). Verify lease-type + remaining term + foro on cadastral plan
- **NO postal code system** — addresses use freguesia + street + edifício + bloco + floor/flat
- **NO golden-visa via property** — Investment Residence Scheme (Order 7/2007) suspended 2 April 2007, NOT reactivated; Talent Programme 2024 does NOT take property as criterion
- **Non-Resident BSD +10%** + Resident second-home BSD +5% — material to any foreign-buyer cost calculation
- **Special Stamp Duty 20% / 10%** for holds < 1 yr / 1–2 yrs respectively — hard anti-flip floor
- **Property Tax 8% on rent** vs **6% of DSF notional value (×0.9 maintenance deduction) minus a fixed MOP 3,500 reduction for owner-occupiers** — owner-occupier reset materially preferable; see `--tax`
- **Mandatory civil-law notary** (escritura pública) — neutral public officer, separate from buyer's optional advogado
- **Bilingual deeds** Chinese + Portuguese (Basic Law Art. 9) — both versions equally authoritative
- **UNESCO Macao Historic Centre buffer zone** — 22 buildings + 8 squares + 8 protection zones under [Lei de Salvaguarda do Património Cultural — Law 11/2013 (as amended by Law 5/2026)](https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/2013/36/lei11.asp); strict modification + maintenance limits enforced by Instituto Cultural
- **Storm-surge + typhoon catastrophic risk** — Hato 2017 + Mangkhut 2018 inundated inner harbour; insurance sub-limits tightening on ground/B1/B2 of low-elevation stock
- **Reclamation subsidence** — Cotai (entirely reclaimed 1990s+), NAPE (1980s+), Areia Preta — verify pile-foundation + fill compaction history via DSOP
- **DL 16/96/M < 30-day STR prohibition** — kills Airbnb-style short-let; pivot to monthly+ serviced apartment
- **Gaming-revenue dependency (~50% GDP)** — propagates to property cycle; concession renewal Jan 2023 (10-yr stability) reduces near-term political-risk discount
- **HKD circulates freely as legal tender** — many transactions quoted in HKD; MOP-HKD peg 1.03 stable since 1995
- **HKD-USD peg propagation** — any HK LERS stress propagates to MOP; position-size accordingly for non-USD/HKD-denominated buyers
- **Mainland Chinese capital controls** — outbound capital subject to PRC controls; cross-border mainland buyer typically uses HK/Macao banking infrastructure
- **AMCM tightened AML/KYC since 2020** — non-resident bank account opening more selective; pre-confirm with target bank
- **DSF reassessment lag** — Property Tax notional values often anchored early-2010s; reassessment cycle irregular
- **No equivalent of HK MBIS** (mandatory building inspection) — verification more discretionary; engage independent surveyor for any pre-2000 building
- **Limited public AADT traffic data** — DSAT does not publish granular counts comparable to HK Transport Department; rely on OSM heuristic + on-site assessment
