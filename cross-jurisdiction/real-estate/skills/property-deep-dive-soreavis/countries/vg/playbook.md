# British Virgin Islands 🇻🇬 — Property Due-Diligence Playbook

ISO2: `vg`. Status: ✅ Fully populated (researched 2026-05-28).

> **Critical framing — read first**: The British Virgin Islands (BVI) is a **British Overseas Territory** in the north-eastern Caribbean — self-governing under the Virgin Islands Constitution Order 2007, with the UK responsible for defence + external affairs and a Crown-appointed Governor. **NOT part of the UK or EU.** The currency is the **United States Dollar (USD)** — sole legal tender since 1959 (unusual for a UK territory), so **no exchange-rate risk for USD buyers**. The single most important foreign-buyer fact: a **non-Belonger** (foreign buyer) **cannot hold land without a Non-Belongers Land Holding Licence (NBLHL)** — a **Cabinet-granted, buyer-AND-property-specific, non-transferable licence** under the **Non-Belongers Land Holding Regulation Act (CAP.122)**. The licence must be obtained **before** the non-Belonger may hold land. Application fees are **USD 200 (individual) / USD 500 (company)** ([bvi.gov.vg](https://www.bvi.gov.vg/services/non-belongers-land-holding-licence), verified 2026-05-28); government quotes an **8–12 week** processing time while practitioners report **3–5 months** and sale agreements commonly allow up to **12 months** to secure the licence. **Stamp duty on transfer**: **4% for a Belonger transferee, 12% for a non-Belonger transferee** — both computed on **the higher of purchase price or market value**. The 4% Belonger rate sits in the **Stamp Act (Stamps (Amendment) Act 2022)**; the **12% non-Belonger rate's statutory home is the Non-Belongers Land Holding Regulation Act (CAP.122)** — not the Stamps Act. **Tax regime is near-zero direct tax**: no income tax, no capital gains tax, no inheritance/estate tax, no corporation tax, no VAT/GST. Real-estate holding taxes are a **modest annual land tax** (expat: USD 50 for ≤½ acre / USD 150 for ≤1 acre / +USD 50 per extra acre) and an **annual house tax of 1.5% of the notional annual rental value** (rates last primary-verified 2026-05-28 on the since-removed `bvi.gov.vg/content/property-tax`; **no live primary schedule** — verify with the [Inland Revenue Department](https://gov.vg/inland-revenue-department)). Title runs on a **registered-land (Torrens-style) system** under the **Registered Land Act / Registered Land Ordinance 1970 (CAP.229)**, administered by the **Department of Land Registry**. The dispute forum is the **High Court of Justice (Virgin Islands)**, a division of the **Eastern Caribbean Supreme Court (ECSC)** — do not imply a standalone non-ECSC national apex court. **Dominant physical risk**: hurricanes — **Hurricane Irma made a direct Category-5 hit on 6 September 2017** (government: ~**70% of buildings** lost or damaged); post-Irma the official building-code posture targets **Cat-5-and-above** resilience, and **insurance is expensive and scarce**. **Confirm** NBLHL fees/timeline, the stamp-duty schedule, the land/house-tax tables, and post-2017 building-code compliance at primary sources **before any binding step**. Authoritative primary sources: **[Government of the Virgin Islands (bvi.gov.vg)](https://www.bvi.gov.vg/)**, **[Department of Land Registry](https://gov.vg/land-registry)**, **[Virgin Islands Laws Online (laws.gov.vg)](https://laws.gov.vg/)**, **[Eastern Caribbean Supreme Court (eccourts.org)](https://www.eccourts.org/)**.

## Country profile

- **Status**: British Overseas Territory (BOT). Self-governing under the **Virgin Islands Constitution Order 2007**; UK responsible for defence + external affairs; **Governor appointed by the Crown**. NOT part of the UK or EU. English common law + equity apply.
- **Geography**: ~60 islands + cays; main inhabited islands — **Tortola** (largest, Road Town the capital), **Virgin Gorda**, **Anegada** (flat coral atoll), **Jost Van Dyke**. **The property market is concentrated on Tortola and Virgin Gorda.**
- **Population**: **~31,000** (last census baseline — verify current at [bvi.gov.vg / Central Statistics Office](https://www.bvi.gov.vg/)). Capital: **Road Town, Tortola**.
- **Currency**: **United States Dollar (USD)** — sole legal tender since 1959. **No exchange-rate risk for USD-base buyers**; minimal for EUR/GBP/CAD buyers (USD-correlated).
- **Economy**: Two pillars — (1) **tourism** (luxury + yachting; "sailing capital of the world"), (2) **offshore financial services** — the BVI is one of the world's largest incorporators of offshore companies (BVI Business Companies), regulated by the **[BVI Financial Services Commission (FSC)](https://www.bvifsc.vg/)**.
- **Languages**: **English (official, sole)** — all contracts, deeds, statutes in English.
- **Legal system**: **English common law** + Virgin Islands statutes (the Laws of the Virgin Islands). Final court of appeal: the **Judicial Committee of the Privy Council** (London).
- **Cadastre / land registry**:
  - **[Land Registry](https://gov.vg/land-registry)** — HR Penn Building (downstairs Alphonso Warner Insurance, adjacent Sunny Caribe), Road Town, Tortola; +1 284 468 2017; landregistration@gov.vg — the registry for **registered title** under CAP.229; chapter numbers verify at [Virgin Islands Laws Online](https://laws.gov.vg/) *(2026-09-18 verified; note the Lands Unit has been relocating since a 23 Jul 2025 remediation closure — confirm the counter address before travelling)*.
  - Note: in 2018 the Land Registry and the Lands & Survey Department were administratively **merged into the Land and Survey Department** (combined legal registry/repository for land registration + geographic data / Registry Maps). Both terms appear in current government materials.
- **Identifier**: **Registration Section + Block + Parcel number** under the registered-land system (Registry Map / parcel index). No French-style universal national parcel ID; the registered-land parcel number is the title key.
- **Title concept**: **Registered land (Torrens-style)** — title is guaranteed by registration, NOT by a chain of deeds. Governed by the **Registered Land Act / Registered Land Ordinance 1970 (CAP.229)** + Registered Land Rules 1972. **Condominium** regimes under the **Condominium Ordinance (CAP.219)**.
- **Recent reforms (12-month watchlist)**:
  - **Post-Irma building code upgrade** announced 27 Sept 2017 (targeting Cat-5+ resilience + climate-change + green-building considerations — per [bvi.gov.vg press release, 27 Sep 2017](https://www.bvi.gov.vg/media-centre/bvi-upgrade-building-codes-aftermath-hurricane-irma)); confirm current Building Code status with Town & Country Planning.
  - **Cabinet-approved merger of BVI Electricity Corporation (BVIEC) + Water & Sewerage Dept** into a new statutory body reported as the **Virgin Islands Energy and Water Company** (per Virgin Islands News Online — *secondary*; confirm current operating entity at bvi.gov.vg).
  - **Stamps (Amendment) Act 2022** — confirmed the 4% Belonger / 12% non-Belonger stamp-duty split (laws.gov.vg primary; GHP BVI legal update). Verify the current schedule before relying.
  - **NBLHL application forms revised 2023** (individual / company) — use the current form at [bvi.gov.vg](https://www.bvi.gov.vg/services/non-belongers-land-holding-licence).

## 🇻🇬 Foreign buyer eligibility (READ FIRST)

This section determines whether a transaction is even legally possible — and what process applies. In the BVI the gating mechanism is a **mandatory, Cabinet-granted, property-specific licence** (the NBLHL), NOT a mere administrative filing. **This is the #1 deal-timeline driver and the single most important foreign-buyer fact.**

### Legal basis

- **Non-Belongers Land Holding Regulation Act (CAP.122)** — the statutory basis for the NBLHL requirement; also the statutory home of the **12% non-Belonger stamp-duty rate**.
- **Registered Land Act / Registered Land Ordinance 1970 (CAP.229)** + Registered Land Rules 1972 — registered-title system.
- **Stamp Act (as amended — Stamps (Amendment) Act 2022)** — the 4% Belonger stamp-duty rate.
- **Conveyancing and Law of Property Ordinance (CAP.220)**, **Condominium Ordinance (CAP.219)**, **Land Acquisition Ordinance (CAP.222)**, **Land Adjudication Ordinance (CAP.223)**, **Intestate Estates Act (CAP.34)** — supporting framework (chapter numbers per [bvi.gov.vg — legislation list](https://bvi.gov.vg/department-faqs/80)) ❌ DEPRECATED — dead on 2026-09-18; verify with the authority named in this row. Statutes free at **[laws.gov.vg](https://laws.gov.vg/)**.

### Buyer category matrix

| Buyer category | Right |
|---|---|
| **Belonger** (broadly: BVI-status holders) | Full registered title; standard conveyancing; **4%** stamp duty |
| **Non-Belonger (individual or company)** | **CANNOT hold land without a Non-Belongers Land Holding Licence (NBLHL)** — Cabinet-granted, buyer-AND-property-specific, **non-transferable**, with conditions (advertising, development commitment, no-alteration-without-Cabinet-consent). **12%** stamp duty |
| **BVI company (Belonger-owned)** | Treated per Belonger status of ownership; verify with attorney |
| **BVI company (non-Belonger-owned)** | Treated as non-Belonger — NBLHL required; **USD 500** company application fee |

(Source: [bvi.gov.vg — NBLHL service page](https://www.bvi.gov.vg/services/non-belongers-land-holding-licence) + Non-Belongers Land Holding Regulation Act CAP.122 ([bvi.gov.vg — legislation list](https://bvi.gov.vg/content/besides-registered-land-act-1970-are-there-any-other-legislation-may-be-applied)) ❌ DEPRECATED — dead on 2026-09-18; verify with the authority named in this row + Harneys "Guide to Purchasing Property in the BVI" — *primary fees/timeline; practitioner detail flagged; verify with attorney before contract*)

### The NBLHL in detail [CRITICAL]

- **What it is**: Any person who is NOT a Belonger must obtain a Non-Belongers Land Holding Licence **before** they may hold/own land in the Territory. The licence is **granted by Cabinet** and administered by the **Ministry of Environment, Natural Resources and Climate Change (MENRCC)**. It is specific to the **named buyer AND the named property** — **not transferable**.
- **Application fees** (PRIMARY — [bvi.gov.vg](https://www.bvi.gov.vg/services/non-belongers-land-holding-licence), verified 2026-05-28):
  - Individual: **USD 200.00 per applicant** (non-refundable)
  - Company: **USD 500.00** (non-refundable)
  - Land Registry registration fee: **USD 2.50 per licence** (charged twice during the process)
- **Processing timeline** — diverging sources, use the longer for planning:
  - **bvi.gov.vg states "8–12 weeks"** (PRIMARY, verified 2026-05-28).
  - **Harneys (practitioner, SECONDARY)** states the process typically takes **3–5 months**, and that sale agreements typically allow **12 months** to secure the licence.
  - GAP NOTE: government quotes 8–12 weeks; practitioner experience runs longer. Budget the longer figure.
- **Supporting documents** (per gov + practitioner guides — verify on the current revised-Sept-2022 form): two **character references**; **financial references**; a **police certificate of good standing** (clean record); passport-size photographs; the application form.
- **Advertising requirement** [KEY CONDITION]: the property must be **advertised in a local BVI newspaper for FOUR (4) consecutive weeks** to give a Belonger or Resident the opportunity to purchase at the same price + terms as the non-Belonger's offer (per gov/practitioner guidance — verify exact current wording at bvi.gov.vg).
- **Development commitment** (undeveloped / partially developed land) [KEY CONDITION]: the applicant must **commit to spend a specified sum on development within a set period — usually 3 years**. Development requirements are **generally over USD 250,000**, scaling up with acreage; the commitment becomes a condition of the licence. Practitioner note: licences "typically include conditions prohibiting alterations without Cabinet consent" — put renovation plans in the initial application.

> ANTI-HALLUCINATION: exact development sums, advertising-weeks, and the current document checklist must be re-verified against the live application form before relying on them — `verify at bvi.gov.vg`.

### Compliance / AML

- The BVI is a major offshore-finance centre; **AML/KYC + source-of-funds documentation** is rigorous. Real-estate professionals + attorneys are subject to AML obligations under the BVI AML framework (FSC-supervised regulated sector).
- **Source-of-funds**: bank-statement chain back to legitimate source expected for any purchase.
- **CRS + FATCA**: tax-residency self-certification at BVI bank-account opening.
- **Beneficial-ownership**: BVI maintains a beneficial-ownership regime for BVI companies — UBO disclosed for corporate-held real estate.

### Verification path (before any reservation)

1. **Engage a BVI attorney** to file the **NBLHL application** for the specific buyer + property; budget the advertising (4 weeks) + development commitment (if undeveloped) into the timeline.
2. **Confirm Belonger/non-Belonger status** of the buyer — determines NBLHL requirement AND the 4% vs 12% stamp-duty rate.
3. **Official search of the land register** by the Registrar of Lands under s.35, Registered Land Act (CAP.229) — confirm registered proprietor, charges, cautions, restrictions.
4. **Survey / inspection** window (~60 days practitioner) — confirm boundaries against the Registry Map.
5. **Stamp-duty assessment** at Inland Revenue → 4% (Belonger) / 12% (non-Belonger) of higher of price/market value.
6. **Binding insurance quote** (windstorm/named-storm) BEFORE committing — the gating cost on older stock (see `--risks`).

### Compute

1. **USD/m² = price ÷ built-area m²** (listings usually quote **square feet** — convert: 1 m² = 10.764 sqft).
2. Compare to **≥ 3 active listings** on the same island + community + waterfront status (waterfront + post-Irma build status drive price more than island average).
3. **Listing convention**: all prices USD-firm (USD is legal tender).
4. **Trap — build era**: pre-Irma timber/older stock vs **post-2017-code** concrete/reinforced builds is the single biggest price-and-insurability discriminator (see `--risks`).
5. **Trap — NBLHL premium**: factor the **12% non-Belonger stamp duty + ~3yr development commitment** (on undeveloped land) into the all-in acquisition cost — these can dwarf the headline price differential between two parcels.
6. **Trap — exit liquidity**: the resale pool is thin (USD-millions, small buyer base). Price in a longer hold + slower exit.

### Key terms

- **Instrument of Transfer** — the registered-land transfer instrument (not a "deed of conveyance")
- **Registered proprietor** — the title holder shown on the Land Register
- **NBLHL (Non-Belongers Land Holding Licence)** — Cabinet licence required for non-Belonger ownership
- **Stamp duty** — 4% (Belonger) / 12% (non-Belonger) of higher of price/market value
- **Land tax / house tax** — annual holding taxes (see `--tax`)
- **Belonger** — broadly, a BVI-status holder (the local-status category)

## Section: `--tax`

> **Headline**: The BVI is a **near-zero direct-tax jurisdiction** — **no income tax, no capital gains tax, no inheritance/estate tax, no corporation tax, no VAT/GST, no annual net-wealth tax**. (Payroll tax exists on employment but is not relevant to passive property ownership.) Real-estate-specific charges: **one-time stamp duty** on transfer (**4% Belonger / 12% non-Belonger** of higher of price/market value), a **modest annual land tax**, and an **annual house tax of 1.5% of the notional annual rental value**. (Source: rates last primary-verified 2026-05-28 on the since-removed `bvi.gov.vg/content/property-tax`; **no live primary schedule** — verify with the [Inland Revenue Department](https://gov.vg/inland-revenue-department); near-zero-tax framing corroborated by Carey Olsen / Legal 500 private-client guides — *secondary*.)

### Stamp duty on transfer of land [KEY]

| Transferee | Rate | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| **Belonger** | **4%** | of the higher of purchase price or market value |
| **Non-Belonger** | **12%** | of the higher of purchase price or market value |

- **Statutory home**: the **4% Belonger rate** sits in the **Stamp Act (Stamps (Amendment) Act 2022)**; the **12% non-Belonger rate's statutory home is the Non-Belongers Land Holding Regulation Act (CAP.122)** — NOT the Stamps Act (audit-confirmed nuance 2026-05-28). Both rates verified via laws.gov.vg primary + GHP BVI legal update + Harneys guide.
- **Who pays**: the purchaser (per practitioner guidance).
- **IMPACT**: a **USD 2M villa** bought by a **non-Belonger = USD 240,000** stamp duty vs **USD 80,000** for a Belonger — a **3× differential** that is the single largest transaction-cost line for foreign buyers.

(Source: Harneys "Guide to Purchasing Property in the BVI" (PRACTITIONER/SECONDARY, verified 2026-05-28) + Stamps (Amendment) Act 2022 + Non-Belongers Land Holding Regulation Act CAP.122 ([laws.gov.vg](https://laws.gov.vg/)) — *verify current schedule at Inland Revenue / laws.gov.vg before relying*)

| Owner category | Rate |
|---|---|
| **Belongers / BVI companies** | **USD 10.00** on the first acre (or part); **USD 3.00 per acre (or part)** thereafter |
| **Expatriates (non-Belongers)** | **USD 50.00** for **½ acre or less**; **USD 150.00** for **>½ acre up to 1 acre**; **+USD 50.00 per additional acre (or part)** |

### Annual house tax (**rate schedule not currently published on a live primary source** — the legacy `bvi.gov.vg/content/property-tax` page was dropped in the gov.vg migration; verify with the **[Inland Revenue Department](https://gov.vg/inland-revenue-department)** and against the **Land and House Tax Act**; annual assessment lists at [Property Tax List](https://gov.vg/service-details/property-tax-list-2025). Figures below carried from the retired page, last primary-verified 2026-05-28)

- **1.5% per annum on the notional annual rental ("income") value of the house** — applies **regardless of owner Belonger status**.
- Notable: house tax is on **notional rental value, NOT capital value** — so the absolute amounts are **modest by international standards**.

### Payment window + penalty

- Pay between **1 September and 30 November** each year to avoid interest.
- **Late penalty: 20%** of amount payable; if still unpaid, a further 20% rolls into the next year (per Red Coral / Expanship summaries — **SECONDARY**; the gov page does not state the % verbatim — `verify exact penalty at bvi.gov.vg`).
- Annual assessment lists are published at bvi.gov.vg + Inland Revenue offices (Tortola + Virgin Gorda).

### Capital gains / inheritance / wealth / VAT

- **NO capital gains tax.**
- **NO inheritance / estate / gift tax.**
- **NO wealth tax.**
- **NO VAT / GST.**
- **NO corporation tax** (BVI Business Companies are a core export product).

⚠️ Verification: request the seller's last **land-tax + house-tax receipt**. Confirm the current stamp-duty schedule (laws.gov.vg / Inland Revenue), land/house-tax tables, and the late-penalty % with the [Inland Revenue Department](https://gov.vg/inland-revenue-department) + attorney before contract. **The land/house-tax tables are no longer backed by a live primary URL — the source page was removed in the gov.vg migration (2026-09-18 verified); treat the tables as last-primary-verified 2026-05-28 and re-confirm against the Land and House Tax Act or an IRD notice. ## Section: `--rental`

### Short-term / vacation rentals

- **Hotel Accommodation Tax**: **10%** payable by guests staying **six months or less** in hotels, apartments, houses, cottages, villas + similar accommodation; calculated on rental proceeds and **paid monthly** (per Best of BVI / industry guides — **SECONDARY**; `verify rate + filing at bvi.gov.vg / Inland Revenue`). This is the de-facto short-term-rental (STR) tax.
- **Trade Licence**: commercial operation generally requires a **Trade Licence**. Crucially, **some NBLHLs include a condition that the owner must obtain a Trade Licence BEFORE the property may be rented out** — i.e., the right to short-term-let can be a **licence condition, not an automatic right**. (Practitioner guidance — verify against your specific NBLHL conditions.)

### Long-term residential rentals

- Governed by ordinary contract + English common-law landlord-tenant principles. The market is small; long-term tenant demand is driven by expatriate finance-sector + hospitality workers + government staff.

### Yield benchmarks (per listing platforms — verify per property)

- **NO official yield series.** Agent/aggregator data cites avg villa **~$1,704/night**, apartment **~$1,371/night** (HomeToGo/Vrbo aggregation — **SECONDARY/listing, verify**; source publishes no "as of" date — treat as undated, re-pull current nightly rates before relying). High season **Dec–Apr**. Tourism is the primary demand driver ("sailing capital of the world").
- ⚠️ **Do NOT publish a headline yield %.** Compute net yield only from a **specific property's verified nightly rate × realistic occupancy** MINUS: the **10% accommodation tax** (verify), full-service villa **management (~20–30%)**, **house tax 1.5%**, **insurance** (high + sometimes unobtainable — see `--risks`), and a **hurricane-season void** (Aug–Oct).

### Strategic notes

- **Insurance is a meaningful — sometimes gating — operating cost** post-Irma (see `--risks`). Get a binding windstorm quote before modelling any yield.
- **STR right may be conditional** on the NBLHL + a Trade Licence — confirm BEFORE pricing a rental thesis.
- **Seasonality**: high season **mid-Dec to Apr** (yachting season); **hurricane season Jun–Nov** (peak Aug–Oct) materially depresses occupancy.

## Section: `--work=<profession>`

### Reality

The BVI economy is small (tourism + offshore finance). A **work permit is required** for non-Belonger employment, and the labour market is **tightly protected for Belongers**. Property buyers here are **overwhelmingly second-home / investment**, not relocating-for-work. The property thesis is **luxury second-home / yachting-base / offshore-finance-adjacent**, not local-employment.

### Job platforms

- **BVI Government — vacancies** ([bvi.gov.vg](https://www.bvi.gov.vg/)) for civil-service roles.
- **LinkedIn BVI** (active for offshore-finance: legal, fund admin, trust, corporate services).
- Local press classifieds (BVI Beacon, Virgin Islands News Online).

### Visa routes for working

| Route | Eligibility | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| **Work Permit (employer-sponsored)** | BVI employer + justification the role cannot be filled by a Belonger | Labour Department / Immigration |
| **Belonger / BVI status** | birth, descent, naturalisation, long residence | self-governing status route |
| **Property ownership (NBLHL)** | does **NOT** confer a right to work or reside long-term | MENRCC / Cabinet (land only) |

> NOTE: holding an NBLHL gives the **right to own the named property** — it does **NOT** confer residency or a work permit. Long-term residence + work are separate immigration matters.

### Catchment heuristics

- **Road Town, Tortola**: the offshore-finance + government + hospitality job cluster.
- **Outer islands**: thin formal employment outside resort hospitality + marine services.

## Section: `--climate`

Universal logic per `shared/climate-projections.md`. BVI-specific overrides:

- **Köppen-Geiger**: **Aw (tropical savanna)** — wetter Aug–Nov, drier Feb–Apr; warm year-round.
- **Coastal exposure**: most property is close to the coast; Tortola + Virgin Gorda are **mountainous** (Mount Sage, Tortola, ~523 m — the highest point in the BVI/USVI), so hillside parcels have elevation; **Anegada is flat coral** (highest point ~8.5 m) and is the **most SLR/surge-exposed** island.
- **Reference baseline**: IPCC AR6 + Caribbean Community Climate Change Centre (CCCCC) regional projections.
- **Key projections** (IPCC AR6 + CCCCC, SSP2-4.5 / SSP5-8.5):
  - Annual mean temperature **+1.0 → +2.5°C** by 2100 (Caribbean SIDS band).
  - **Hurricane intensity**: Atlantic-basin Cat 4–5 share trending up; **Irma 2017 (direct Cat-5)** is the calibration event — the official posture is that Cat-5 is "the new norm".

  - **Rainfall**: more intense convective events; dry-season stress on freshwater supply (a grid + cistern + RO issue).
  - **Coral bleaching**: increasing — affects beach + reef-tourism economics.

  - 🔴 Hurricane Cat 4–5 risk (Irma 2017 direct hit; insurance scarce/expensive)
  - 🔴 Low-lying coastal / Anegada parcels — surge + SLR exposure

  - 🟡 Freshwater-supply stress — cistern/RO dependence
  - 🟡 Heat stress increasing — cooling-cost escalation

## Section: `--currency`

- **Currency**: **United States Dollar (USD)** — sole legal tender since **1959** (unusual for a UK Overseas Territory).
- **No exchange-rate risk** for USD-base buyers; minimal for EUR/GBP/CAD buyers (USD-correlated).
- **No exchange controls** of the kind seen in pegged-currency states (e.g., no Central-Bank FX-registration gateway equivalent to Barbados/Bahamas) — the gating foreign-buyer step in the BVI is the **NBLHL** (see Foreign buyer eligibility), NOT a currency-registration filing.
- **Practical effect for the buyer**: pricing, financing, taxes, and fees are **all USD**; the only "currency" workstream is ordinary AML source-of-funds documentation (rigorous, given the offshore-finance context).

## Section: `--visa`

> **There is no property-linked residency or citizenship-by-investment route in the BVI.** Holding an **NBLHL** confers the **right to own the named property** — it does **NOT** grant residency, the right to work, or a path to BVI status. Long-term residence + work are separate immigration matters (Belonger status / residence permits / employer-sponsored work permits).

| Route | Threshold | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **NBLHL (land-holding licence)** | USD 200 individual / USD 500 company application fee | Cabinet-granted; right to **own the named property only** — NOT residency or work |
| **Work permit (employer-sponsored)** | BVI employer + Belonger-protection justification | Labour Department / Immigration |
| **Residence permit** | per BVI immigration rules | separate from land ownership |
| **Belonger / BVI status** | birth, descent, naturalisation, long residence | local-status route |
| **Citizenship by Investment (CBI)** | **NONE** | The BVI does NOT operate a CBI scheme (distinguish from St. Kitts, Dominica, Antigua & Barbuda, St. Lucia, Grenada). BVI status follows residence + the BOT/British-citizenship framework |

(Source: [bvi.gov.vg](https://www.bvi.gov.vg/) + NBLHL service page — *verify current immigration rules with the Immigration / Labour Department before relying; land ownership ≠ residency*)

## Caveats unique to VG

- **The NBLHL is mandatory, slow, and conditional** — the #1 deal-timeline driver. Budget **8–12 weeks (gov) to 3–5 months (practitioner)**, up to **12 months** in the sale contract. It is **buyer- AND property-specific, non-transferable**, and carries conditions: **4-week local advertising**, a **development commitment (usually ~3 years, generally >USD 250,000)** on undeveloped land, and **no alterations without Cabinet consent**. Put renovation plans in the initial application.
- **12% non-Belonger stamp duty** (3× the Belonger 4%) is the **dominant transaction cost** — on a USD 2M villa that is **USD 240,000**. Its statutory home is the **Non-Belongers Land Holding Regulation Act (CAP.122)**, NOT the Stamps Act (the 4% Belonger rate is the Stamps-Act rate). Confirm against the current schedule.
- **Hurricane is the dominant physical risk** — **Irma 2017 was a direct Cat-5 (~70% of buildings damaged per gov)**. **Insurance is expensive/scarce and may be unobtainable on older stock** — get a binding windstorm quote BEFORE committing; confirm **post-2017 building-code compliance** + structural survey.
- **Small, illiquid, USD-millions market** — exit may be slow; the resale buyer pool is thin.
- **No government price index / yield series** — all price + yield figures are listing/agent-derived (SECONDARY); verify per listing.
- **Near-zero direct tax** — no income/CGT/inheritance/corporation tax + no VAT; holding taxes are a modest annual land tax + a 1.5%-of-notional-rental house tax. Land/house-tax tables are PRIMARY-verified; the **20% late penalty + 10% accommodation tax are SECONDARY** — re-verify at bvi.gov.vg / Inland Revenue.
- **Registered-land (Torrens-style) title** under CAP.229 — title is guaranteed by registration, NOT by a chain of deeds. The registry is the **Department of Land Registry / Land and Survey Department** (administratively merged 2018).
- **Court / forum**: disputes go to the **High Court of Justice (Virgin Islands)**, a division of the **Eastern Caribbean Supreme Court (ECSC)** — the BVI also hosts a **Commercial Division** of the ECSC High Court (commercial claims ≥ USD 500,000). Final appeal: the **Privy Council** (London). It is NOT a standalone national apex court independent of the ECSC.
- **Land ownership ≠ residency**: the **NBLHL grants the right to own the named property only** — it does NOT confer residency, the right to work, or BVI status. **No CBI; no property-linked golden visa.**
- **STR right can be a licence condition**: some NBLHLs require a **Trade Licence before the property may be rented out** — short-term-letting is not an automatic right. Confirm against your specific licence conditions.
- **Utilities**: diesel grid (high cost); water often **cistern/RO**, not mains; fibre not universal — verify per parcel. The **BVIEC + Water & Sewerage merger entity** (reported as the Virgin Islands Energy and Water Company) is SECONDARY — confirm the current operating entity at bvi.gov.vg.
- **Outer-island reality**: Virgin Gorda / Jost Van Dyke / Anegada are **ferry-dependent**; serious medical care often means **off-island evacuation** (San Juan / St. Thomas).
- **Wire fraud against international buyers** is a rising regional pattern — verify wire instructions verbally with your attorney via a known phone number before sending funds.
