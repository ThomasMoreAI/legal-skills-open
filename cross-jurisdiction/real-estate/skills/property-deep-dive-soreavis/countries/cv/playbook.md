# Cape Verde 🇨🇻 — Property Due-Diligence Playbook

ISO2: `cv`. Status: ✅ Fully populated (researched 2026-05).

## Country profile

- **Population**: ~525,000 (World Bank 2024); domestic estimates ~600k including diaspora-linked residents. Last full census: **RGPH 2021** (INE — Instituto Nacional de Estatística, `https://ine.cv/`); next scheduled mid-decade.
- **Geography**: 10 islands, 9 inhabited, ~570 km off the West-African coast. Two groups: **Barlavento** (Santo Antão, São Vicente, São Nicolau, Sal, Boa Vista) and **Sotavento** (Maio, Santiago, Fogo, Brava). Land area ~4,033 km².
- **Capital**: **Praia** (Santiago) ~160k; second city **Mindelo** (São Vicente) ~70k; **Espargos / Santa Maria** (Sal) and **Sal Rei** (Boa Vista) are the tourism property hotspots.
- **Currency**: **CVE — Cape Verdean escudo**, fixed peg to the EUR at **1 EUR = 110.265 CVE since 1999** (Acordo de Cooperação Cambial with Portugal, supported by a Portuguese Treasury credit-facility line). Peg confirmed in force, and its credibility explicitly reaffirmed in the 2025 Article IV + 7th ECF / 3rd RSF review concluded 6 Feb 2026: 2025 average inflation **2.3 %** mirrored euro-area trends and was consistent with the peg, and gross international reserves reached an all-time high of **EUR 1,070 m — 7.6 months of prospective imports — at end-2025** (2026 data, source IMF PR26/036). Source: IMF Country Reports + BCV (`https://www.bcv.cv/`).
- **Languages**: **Portuguese** (official, administration, notary, tax, court documents); **Cabo-Verdean Crioulo / Kriolu** (lingua franca, two main variants: Sotavento Santiago-based and Barlavento São Vicente-based). English and Italian common in Sal/Boa Vista tourism zones.
- **Legal system**: **Lusophone civil-law tradition** (Portuguese-derived). Civil Code, Notarial Code, and Land Registry Code mirror Portuguese structure. Property transfer requires **escritura pública** (public deed) before a notary + inscription in the **Conservatória do Registo Predial**.
- **Cadastre / land registry**: **Cadastro Predial Nacional** under the **Direção Nacional do Cadastro** (modernisation programme co-financed by EU + Millennium Challenge Account ongoing since 2010s; coverage uneven by island — strongest in Sal, Boa Vista, Praia urban zone, weaker in interior Santiago and the Barlavento highlands). Title certification: **Certidão do Registo Predial**.
- **Postcode**: 4 digits (e.g., Praia `7600`, Sal Espargos `4111`, Mindelo `2110`)
- **Admin levels**: 22 **concelhos** (municipalities) → 31 **freguesias** (parishes). Each concelho has a **Câmara Municipal** with property-tax (IPI) and urbanism authority.
- **EU relationship**: Cape Verde is **not an EU member** but holds a **Special Partnership with the EU** (Cotonou + post-Cotonou Samoa Agreement framework, plus the 2007 EU–Cabo Verde Special Partnership). PT–CV bilateral preferences for Portuguese citizens (mobility, recognition of diplomas) under the **Acordo Quadro de Cooperação** Portugal–Cabo Verde.
- **Major reforms tracked (2024–2026)**:
  - **2026-01-01 — Major property-tax reform**: the **Imposto Único sobre o Património (IUP)** in force since 1999 was **repealed** and **replaced by two new codes**:
    - **IPI (Imposto sobre a Propriedade de Imóveis)** — annual ownership tax, base rate **0.1 %** of assessed value. Legal basis: **Lei n.º 55/X/2025 of 6 June 2025**, Boletim Oficial I Série n.º 46. (`https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/View?id=85811`)
    - **ITI (Imposto sobre a Transmissão de Imóveis)** — one-time transfer tax, **1 %** of transaction value (separate code: **Lei n.º 54/X/2025 of 6 June 2025**, Boletim Oficial I Série n.º 46, Art. 11.º; **3 %** with no exemption/reduction where the seller or buyer benefits from a privileged tax regime per the Código Geral Tributário) (`https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/View?id=85812`).
  - **2025 IMF Seventh Review under ECF + Third Review under RSF** (concluded 6 Feb 2026, PR26/036): real GDP growth 7.2 % in 2024; fiscal targets met; CVE-EUR peg credibility reaffirmed. (`https://www.imf.org/en/news/articles/2026/02/07/pr26036-cabo-verde-imf-concl-2025-aiv-consultation-7th-rev-under-ecf-and-3rd-rev-under-rsf`)
  - **2026-05-08 — 2025 growth revised UP**: the Feb-2026 **est. 5.2 % for 2025 is SUPERSEDED** — 2025 outturn **6.3 %**, with 2026 growth now projected **~4.7 % (IMF)** / **~4.8 % (World Bank)** (2026 data, source IMF PR26/146 of 8 May 2026 — staff-level agreement on the 8th ECF / 4th RSF review; World Bank Cabo Verde Economic Update of 14 Jul 2026 concurs). No Board-completion release for the 8th ECF review located as at 2026-08-07 — treat the 8th-review figures as staff-level, not Board-approved. (`https://www.imf.org/en/news/articles/2026/05/08/pr-26146-cabo-verde-imf-reaches-agreement-on-8th-rev-under-ecf-arrang-4th-rev-rsf-arrang`)
  - **2017 Permanent Residence for Foreign Investors** (the "Green Card" / RBI route): government-approved framework still operative; thresholds **8.8 M CVE (~€80k)** below-average-GDP municipalities / **13.2 M CVE (~€120k)** above-average — see `--visa` notes below and verify at `https://aai.gov.cv/`.
  - **2026 corporate-income-tax cut to 20 %** for IRPC standard, taxpayers under the **organised-accounting regime** — **Lei n.º 69/X/2025 of 31 December 2025 (Orçamento do Estado 2026), Art. 29.º**, amending **Art. 84.º of the CIRPC** (Lei n.º 82/VIII/2015); in force for economic year 2026 (from 2026-01-01). Published Boletim Oficial n.º 133, 1.º Suplemento, I Série. (`https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/DownloadAct?id=89137`) The immediately preceding rate was **21 %** (not 22 %) — rate path 25 % → 22 % (2019) → 21 % (2024) → 20 % (2026), per Governo de Cabo Verde "Política Fiscal" (governo.cv, Apr 2026). ⚠️ Tourism-zone preferential-regime treatment **not re-verified against a primary source** — confirm at DGCI / Ministério das Finanças before relying (`https://www.mf.gov.cv/`). (2026-08-07 verified)

## Foreign buyer eligibility (READ FIRST)

Cape Verde is **broadly permissive** for foreign buyers. There is **no nationality restriction** on buying urban residential or tourist property, and **no minimum residency requirement** for ownership. The constraints are sector-specific (agricultural land, ZDTI/POT plans, military/airport buffer zones) and fiscal (correct registration of foreign-currency inflow, NIF acquisition, notary deed in Portuguese).

### What foreigners can and cannot buy

| Asset | Foreigner-buyable? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Urban titled apartment / villa** | ✅ YES | Default safe — Sal, Boa Vista, Praia, Mindelo apartments + tourist developments. Verify Certidão do Registo Predial. |
| **Off-plan / VEFA in tourism zone** | ✅ YES (with care) | Most tourism developments are in **ZDTI** (Zona de Desenvolvimento Turístico Integral) or **ZRPT** (Zona de Reserva e Proteção Turística) under Plano de Ordenamento Turístico (POT). Check developer's title + construction licence + bank guarantee. (Default off-plan trap: developer insolvency. Demand sequential payments tied to milestones.) |
| **Restored historic property (Mindelo, Cidade Velha, Ribeira Grande)** | ✅ YES | Cidade Velha (Santiago) is **UNESCO World Heritage** — restoration permits required from IPC (Instituto do Património Cultural). |
| **Rural / agricultural land outside ZDTI** | ⚠️ CAUTION | No explicit foreign-ownership ban, but cadastre coverage is weakest in the rural interior; chain-of-title disputes common (see Cadastre limitations below). Many parcels are subject to customary tenure ("herança partilhada" — undivided inheritance shares across diaspora heirs) — **verify all heirs' consent**. |
| **Military / airport buffer / port zones** | ❌ NO | Restricted by national-security and aviation authorities. |
| **Maritime public domain (DPM — within 80 m of high-water line)** | ❌ NO | Inalienable public domain. Beachfront "concessions" exist (long-lease) but are not freehold. |
| **Protected natural areas** (e.g., Parque Natural Monte Gordo, Tartarugas marine reserves) | ❌/⚠️ | Severely restricted; some coastal Boa Vista / Maio parcels are inside protected zones — verify via Direção Nacional do Ambiente. |

### Mandatory pre-purchase verification

1. **NIF (Número de Identificação Fiscal)** — foreigner must obtain a Cape Verdean tax ID from DGI before signing the deed. Issued via Casa do Cidadão (`https://www.portondinosilhas.gov.cv/`).
2. **Certidão do Registo Predial** (≤ 6 months) — issued by the local **Conservatória dos Registos** confirming: owner, area, boundaries, mortgages, easements, pre-emption rights. Order before paying any deposit.
3. **Caderneta predial / matriz predial** — fiscal record from DGCI (now under Direção Nacional das Receitas de Estado, **DNRE**) confirming the property's tax matrix value and IPI status.
4. **Licença de utilização / habite-se** — Câmara Municipal-issued certificate that the building is legally constructed and habitable.
5. **Condomínio status** (apartments) — request **regulamento + actas de condomínio + certidão de quotas em dívida** to confirm no unpaid HOA fees.
6. **Tourism-zone POT compliance** — for parcels in ZDTI/ZRPT, confirm the project is included in the approved POT (Plano de Ordenamento Turístico) at Cabo Verde TradeInvest / Direção Geral do Ordenamento do Território.

### Cadastre limitations to know

- The **Cadastro Predial Nacional** modernisation programme (post-2010) prioritised Sal, Boa Vista, Praia urban, parts of São Vicente. Older parts of Santiago, Fogo, Santo Antão, São Nicolau, Maio, Brava have legacy registers with mixed quality.
- **Customary tenure ("posse" without title)** persists for rural land — *de facto* ownership recognised socially but not always formally registered. Foreigners must require **título registado** (registered title), not posse.
- **Diaspora-heir disputes**: Cape Verde has one of the world's largest diaspora-to-resident ratios. Many rural parcels have 5–20 heirs scattered across Portugal, US (New England), Netherlands, France. **All co-heirs must consent** to a sale or it can be voided. Demand a **certidão de habilitação de herdeiros** for any inherited parcel.

### Currency repatriation — FX framework

The **2017 FX liberalisation** package eliminated the prior central-bank-authorisation regime for routine foreign-investment inflows. As of 2025–2026:

- **Foreign currency inflow** for property purchase must enter through a **commercial bank licensed by Banco de Cabo Verde** (the major banks: BCA — Banco Comercial do Atlântico, BCN — Banco Caboverdiano de Negócios, Caixa Económica de Cabo Verde, Banco Interatlântico — BI, Ecobank).
- The bank issues a **declaração de câmbio** / **comprovativo de entrada de divisas** referencing the property purchase. **Keep this document forever** — it is the basis for repatriation rights on resale + rental income.
- **Repatriation of sale proceeds and rental income** is permitted in foreign currency for non-residents who properly registered the inflow at purchase. The escudo is *pegged* (1 EUR = 110.265 CVE) but **not freely floating** — repatriation processing typically takes days to weeks.
- **Skipping the bank channel** (cash, crypto, informal) = future repatriation right not guaranteed. Non-negotiable for serious investors.

### Confidence

**HIGH** for the foreigner-eligibility framework, NIF/notary process, and cadastre-modernisation status (multi-source: government + IMF + investment-promotion documentation). **MEDIUM** for the rural / diaspora-heir trap specifics (well-documented qualitatively but no published case-rate statistics). **MEDIUM** for ZDTI/ZRPT compliance procedures (POT plans are public but project-by-project verification still needed).

### Compute steps

1. Listing €/m² = listing € ÷ surface m². **Clarify which surface measure** — Cape Verde notarial deeds typically reference **área coberta** (built / covered) and **área de implantação** (footprint); listings often advertise **área útil** or **área bruta**.
2. Cross-check against the **matriz predial** value (DNRE record) — typically 30–60 % below market; use as a sanity floor.
3. **Trap — off-plan VEFA in ZDTI**: many tourist developments are sold "fully furnished" with management contracts. Confirm what's included (parking, beach-club access, rental-pool participation, deeds vs leasehold of common areas).
4. **Trap — rental-pool / managed-rental schemes**: check whether the unit is sold *with* a mandatory rental-pool contract (often 50/50 or 60/40 owner-operator split with limited owner-use weeks). The owner's share net of operator fees is often 30–50 % of gross.
5. **Trap — dual pricing**: foreign-buyer listings on UK portals can sit 15–30 % above local-market levels for the same unit type. Check **Bons Negócios** for the local benchmark.

### Confidence

**MEDIUM** — listing-derived ranges only; no national price index; foreign-buyer-portal bias acknowledged. **HIGH** for the existence and direction of the Sal-Boa-Vista price-premium phenomenon (multi-source agency reports + INE tourism dominance data agree).

## Section: `--traffic`

### Primary sources

- **Ministério das Infraestruturas, Ordenamento do Território e Habitação (MIOTH)** — `https://mioth.gov.cv/`. A "Direção Nacional dos Transportes Rodoviários" is not named on the ministry's site — data not publicly available; verify the current road-transport directorate at MIOTH (2026-09-18 verified)
- **Estradas de Cabo Verde, E.P.E. (ECV)** — under MIOTH; mandate "Desenvolver, manter e expandir a rede rodoviária nacional" (`https://home.estradas.cv`). Successor to the Instituto de Estradas de Cabo Verde (IECV). Traffic counts (**Recenseamento de Tráfego**) are not published on the current site — verify directly with ECV (2026 data, source home.estradas.cv; 2026-09-18 verified)
- **Câmaras Municipais** for urban-road counts (rare; few municipalities publish granular AADT).
- **OSM fallback** is the practical baseline for most of Cape Verde — government published traffic counts have very thin coverage outside Praia + Sal main routes.

### Cape Verde traffic terminology

- **TMD (Tráfego Médio Diário)** — same as PT TMD, equivalent to AADT
- Road classification: **EN (Estrada Nacional)** = primary inter-island national roads; **ER (Estrada Regional)** = secondary; **EM (Estrada Municipal)** = local; *strada urbana* in city core

### Verdict bands (TMD, generic — calibrated to small-island context)

- 🟢 < 500 v/d (rural EM, side street, most of interior Santo Antão / Fogo / São Nicolau)
- 🟡 500–3,000 v/d (urban quartier streets, secondary EN segments outside Praia)
- 🟠 3,000–10,000 v/d (Praia main corridors, Sal EN1 Santa Maria-Espargos, Mindelo Avenida Marginal)
- 🔴 > 10,000 v/d (Praia central — Av. Cidade de Lisboa, Av. da OUA; Sal airport access in peak season)

### Island-specific notes

- **Sal**: single main road **EN1-SL01** Espargos (airport / capital) → Santa Maria (tourism) carries virtually all tourism traffic. Peak season (Nov–Apr) congestion + rental-car volume notable. Dust + pothole maintenance variable. Tour-operator coach traffic concentrated 6 AM (airport pickup) and 10 PM (departures).
- **Boa Vista**: dominant axis **EN1-BV01** Rabil (airport) → Sal Rei (capital + tourism). Lower volume than Sal. Several large tourist resorts have private access roads off this corridor.
- **Santiago / Praia**: capital-city traffic — Avenida Cidade de Lisboa, Avenida Amílcar Cabral, Avenida da OUA carry heavy commuter + commercial loads. Praia–Cidade Velha (~15 km west) congested at weekends. Praia–Tarrafal (~75 km north) is the main inter-urban link.
- **São Vicente / Mindelo**: smaller scale; Avenida Marginal + port-area corridors are the only material congestion points.
- **Fogo, Santo Antão, São Nicolau, Maio, Brava**: rural traffic almost everywhere — sub-500 v/d except town centres on market days.

### Noise verdict — Cape Verde-specific add-ons

- **Funaná / live-music venues + Crioulo cultural events**: nighttime live-music spillover in Mindelo, Santa Maria, Praia tourist zones. Map venue density before signing.
- **Airport approach noise**: **Amílcar Cabral International (Sal, SID)** and **Aristides Pereira International (Boa Vista, BVC)** have flightpath noise affecting nearby parcels. Check distance + flightpath orientation. Praia (RAI) and São Vicente (VXE) airports also have urban impact.
- **Wind**: Cape Verde is in the trade-wind belt; **harmattan-related dust events** + sustained 30–40 km/h winds in Sal/Boa Vista create their own ambient noise — door rattle, sand-on-window, palm-frond impact.
- **Generators**: in some peri-urban / interior zones grid reliability remains uneven; diesel-generator operation 5–8 PM peak hours adds noise corridors.

## Section: `--tax`

> ⚠️ **Major reform — 2026-01-01**: the 25-year-old **IUP (Imposto Único sobre o Património)** was repealed and replaced by **IPI** (annual ownership) + **ITI** (one-time transfer). The figures below reflect the new regime. Verify the latest at DNRE / DGCI: `https://www.mf.gov.cv/web/dnre/direção-geral-das-contribuições-e-impostos`. (2025-2026 reform package; revisit cadence semi-annual.)

### One-time at purchase

| Item | Rate / amount | Source / notes |
|---|---|---|
| **ITI (Imposto sobre a Transmissão de Imóveis)** | **1 %** of transaction value (post-2026 reform) | Código do ITI, Lei n.º 54/X/2025 (Art. 11.º; 3 % where a party benefits from a privileged tax regime); payable to the Câmara Municipal *before* registration of the deed. (`https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/View?id=85812` for ITI Code; IPI Code is Lei n.º 55/X/2025, `https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/View?id=85811`) |
| **Imposto do Selo (stamp duty)** | **0.5 %** of transaction value (verify current) | Cabo Verde Stamp Duty Code — applies to the deed |
| **Notary fees** | ~**0.5–1.5 %** of transaction value (verify per notary; tariffs published by Conselho dos Notários) | Cartório Notarial; fees scale with deed value |
| **Registo Predial (land-registry inscription)** | ~**0.3–0.6 %** + fixed fees | Conservatória dos Registos |
| **Estate-agent fee** | typically **3–5 %** + **15 % IVA** (negotiable; sometimes split, sometimes seller-paid) | Market practice |
| **Total transaction cost (buyer side)** | **~3.5–6 %** of price (excl. agent commission) | Sum of above |

> 📊 Compared to PT (IMT 2–8 % + IS 0.8 %) and ES (ITP 6–11 %), CV transaction taxes are **markedly lower** — a deliberate signal of the tourism-investment strategy.

### Annual taxes

| Tax | Base | Rate | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **IPI (Imposto sobre a Propriedade de Imóveis)** | Assessed value (objective criteria: construction cost, location, area, quality, age, zoning) | **0.1 %** standard | Lei n.º 55/X/2025. Replaces the IUP residential rate (which had been 1.5 % of "valor patrimonial" since 1999, although effective collection on under-assessed values was much lower in practice). Revenue accrues 100 % to the Câmara Municipal. |
| **IPI agravamentos (penalty rates)** | Same base | **25 %** for *prédios devolutos / em ruínas* (vacant/derelict); **10 %** for incomplete façades; +**20 % per year** stacking for chronic dereliction | Anti-speculation / anti-blight provisions |
| **IPI exemption** | — | Rural buildings used exclusively for agriculture/forestry/livestock are exempt | |
| **Tourism-property IPI waiver** (legacy, verify continues post-2026 reform) | — | Properties used for tourism rental can qualify for **5-year IPI waiver** under tourism-investment incentive regimes | Verify at Cabo Verde TradeInvest + DNRE |

> 🔎 The IPI rate is **dramatically lower** than the prior IUP nominal 1.5 %, but the new code uses **objective valuation criteria** that may *raise* assessed values — the net cash impact varies. For a foreign owner of a Sal apartment with €200k market value and historic IUP base ~€80k → IUP at 1.5 % = ~€1,200/yr; new IPI at 0.1 % on a more accurate ~€180k base = ~€180/yr. Most residential foreign owners will see a **decrease**.

### Rental income — IRPS Categoria F-equivalent

- **IRPS rates 2025** (Lei do IRPS, declarative method) — *verify at DGCI*:

| Taxable income (CVE) | Rate |
|---|---:|
| 0 – 220,000 | exempt |
| 220,001 – 960,000 | **16.5 %** |
| 960,001 – 1,800,000 | **23.1 %** |
| > 1,800,000 | **27.5 %** |

(Source: PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries, Cabo Verde individual taxes, last reviewed 19 January 2026 — `https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cabo-verde/individual/taxes-on-personal-income`. Cross-check with DGCI for current LdF.)

- **Flat rental option**: rental income may be taxed at a **10 % flat rate** under specific regimes (verify current eligibility at DGCI).
- **Withholding** when paying companies: **10 %** WHT on rental payments by companies to landlords.
- **Non-residents**: subject to PIT only on Cabo-Verdean-source income at flat rates equivalent to the resident rates (with a 10 % flat option commonly applied to services).

### Capital gains on property

- **Resident individuals**: capital gain on property sale included in IRPS (Cat. G-equivalent — *mais-valias*); inflation indexation typically applies. Holding-period exemptions exist for primary residence reinvestment under conditions. **Verify at DGCI.**
- **Non-residents**: typically **flat rate** on net gain (10–20 % range depending on regime — confirm current at DGCI before relying).
- **No general wealth tax** in Cape Verde.

### IVA (VAT)

- **Standard IVA**: **15 %** (lower than PT 23 %, ES 21 %)
- **Tourism / accommodation services**: reduced rate has historically been considered (10 % proposed in past LdF cycles). **Verify current rate at DNRE before pricing rental rates.**

### Future risk

- IPI is **brand-new** (effective 2026-01-01). Implementation friction (assessment software, municipal capacity, foreign-owner portals) is expected through the first 18 months. Expect adjustments in 2027 LdF (Lei do Orçamento de Estado).
- Tourism-zone 5-year IPI waiver: confirm whether it survived intact through the IUP→IPI transition. (Status: pending DNRE clarification as of 2025–2026.)
- Non-resident rental flat rates and CGT rates revisit periodically through the LdF.
- Verify all rates at `https://www.mf.gov.cv/` and the current **Lei do Orçamento de Estado (LOE)** before relying.

⚠️ **Verification**: request the seller's most recent **caderneta predial** (matriz value) + the **certidão de não dívida** (proof no IPI/IUP arrears).

## Section: `--rental`

### Long-term residential leasing

- **Lei do Arrendamento Urbano (LAU)** governs residential leases. Standard 1-year contracts renewable; deposit (caução) typically 1–2 months.
- **Rent control**: no general control; revisions tied to inflation indexation.
- **Eviction**: judicial; for non-payment cases timelines 6–18 months realistic.
- **Long-let yields** (est., 2025): Praia residential ~5–7 %, Sal/Boa Vista off-tourist-zone ~4–6 %, Mindelo ~4–6 %.

### Short-let / tourism rental — the dominant foreign-buyer use case

> ⚠️ The regulatory framework for short-lets in Cape Verde is **less codified than EU equivalents** (no AL-style national register as in PT). Tourist-zone short-let is largely structured through:
>
> 1. **Tourism-development companies / rental-pool operators** (most ZDTI properties)
> 2. **Independent small-scale rental** via Booking.com / Airbnb / Vrbo (less regulated; tax-compliance pressure rising 2024–2026)

#### Track 1 — ZDTI rental-pool / managed-rental

- Most off-plan tourism developments (Sal, Boa Vista) sell with a **rental-pool contract** assigning the unit to a managing operator (e.g., Tortuga Beach Resort, Llana Beach, The Resort Group properties).
- **Owner share**: typically **40–60 % of net rental revenue** after operator fees + maintenance reserve.
- **Owner-occupancy weeks**: limited (often 2–6 weeks/year, peak/off-peak split).
- **Tax**: managing operator usually withholds + remits IRPC and IVA; owner declares net distribution as income (Cat. F or B).
- **Trap — over-promised yields**: developer brochures advertising "8–10 % guaranteed" are marketing. Verify by demanding *actual historical* operator distribution statements from existing owners (3+ years if available). Many "guaranteed" yield periods expire after 3–5 years and revert to actual occupancy share.

#### Track 2 — Independent short-let (Airbnb, Booking, Vrbo)

- Owner manages directly or via local manager.
- **Registration**: tourism authority (Direção Geral do Turismo, DGT-CV) operates a **Cadastro Turístico** for accommodation establishments. Booking platforms increasingly require evidence of registration. **Verify before listing.**
- **Tax**: rental income subject to IRPS (declarative) or 10 % flat option; IVA implications above thresholds.
- **Tourism tax (Taxa Turística)**: **TUR (Taxa de Turismo)** charged per overnight stay — historically ~€2/night/person (verify current; rates set by LOE annually). Operator collects and remits.

### Volcanic risk — Fogo (and Brava)

- **Fogo (Pico do Fogo)** is the **youngest and most active volcano in the Cape Verde archipelago**. Recent eruptions: **1951, 1995, 2014–2015** (last major). The 2014–2015 eruption destroyed much of **Chã das Caldeiras** village (inside the caldera); ~1,000 residents evacuated; lava flows damaged or destroyed homes, agricultural plots, and infrastructure.
- **Implication for property buyers**: any purchase **inside Chã das Caldeiras caldera** carries direct lava-flow + ashfall risk. **Insurance** for volcanic damage in this zone is severely limited or unavailable from commercial carriers — verify before buying. Outside the caldera (São Filipe town, peripheral coast), risk is ashfall + indirect (port/airport closure during eruption).
- **Brava** has a younger volcanic history; small-scale seismic swarms recorded periodically. No historical eruption in modern times but classified active by Smithsonian GVP.
- **Other islands**: not classified as actively volcanic in the modern era; Santo Antão has the most recent volcanic record outside Fogo (~10,000+ years ago).
- **Megatsunami research note**: the eastern flank of Fogo collapsed ~73,000 years ago, generating a paleo-tsunami that struck Santiago. Modern recurrence probability over a property-ownership horizon is very low but the academic literature exists (Ramalho et al. 2015) and is occasionally cited in catastrophe-modelling. Insurance carriers do NOT typically price this scenario.

### Cyclone / hurricane risk

- The **Atlantic hurricane season** (Jun–Nov) **forms tropical waves at low latitude near Cape Verde** ("Cape Verde-type hurricanes"); these typically intensify *as they leave* Cape Verde westward. **Direct hurricane landfall on Cape Verde is historically rare** but happens.
- **Hurricane Fred 2015 (Cat 1)**: rare direct hit; significant rain damage on São Vicente, São Nicolau.
- **Hurricane Erin 2025-08-10/11 (Cat 5 at peak elsewhere; tropical storm conditions affecting CV)**: torrential rains, flash flooding, landslides; **9 deaths, ~27,500 affected** per WHO PHSA report. Affected primarily São Vicente, São Nicolau, Santiago, Fogo.
- **Expected risk profile**: historically low landfall probability; **rainfall-flood + flash-flood + landslide** are the dominant cyclone-season hazards (vs. wind direct).

### Flood + flash-flood risk

- **Most vulnerable islands** (per WHO PHSA + Civil Protection): **São Vicente, São Nicolau, Santiago, Fogo** — all have steep topography + ribeiras (seasonal stream beds) prone to flash flooding in heavy rain.
- **Rainy season** mid-Aug to mid-Oct; long droughts between intense convective events make ribeira channels deceptively dry most of the year.
- **Verification before buying**: never buy a parcel **in or adjacent to a ribeira** (dry stream bed) without checking the local **Plano Director Municipal (PDM)** for flood-zone overlay. Several Praia + Mindelo neighbourhoods have known flash-flood corridors.

### Drought + water stress

- Cape Verde is **structurally water-stressed** (semi-arid climate, low rainfall). Multi-year droughts the norm. Most islands rely on **desalination** for potable water — coverage and reliability vary by island. Verify the property's water source (ELECTRA municipal vs. private well + cistern).
- Climate-change projections (World Bank Climate Knowledge Portal): rainfall declining further +1.5 to +3.0 °C under SSP2-4.5/SSP5-8.5 by 2050.

### Coastal erosion + sea-level rise

- Sandy beachfront on Sal / Boa Vista experiences seasonal + climate-driven erosion. Setback rules under the **Plano de Ordenamento Turístico (POT)** require minimum distance from high-water line — verify parcel compliance.
- SLR projections: ~30–50 cm by 2050 (IPCC AR6 mid-range); higher for high-emissions scenario. Low-lying coastal developments + saltwater intrusion into freshwater lenses are the second-order risk.

### Other natural risks

- **Sandstorms (calima)**: dust from Sahara, periodic, primarily affects Sal / Boa Vista / São Nicolau. Health risk for sensitive groups; cleaning + maintenance costs.
- **Wildfire**: limited (low vegetation density) but Santo Antão / Brava / Fogo have historic events.
- **Tsunami (distal)**: 1755 Lisbon tsunami affected Cape Verde coast historically; modern monitoring via INMG.

### Build-era hazards

| Era | Hazards |
|---|---|
| **Pre-1975 (colonial era)** | Mostly traditional masonry / pedra-e-cal; some Portuguese-era reinforced concrete; asbestos likely in roofs/insulation; lead pipes and lead paint typical |
| **1975–1990** | Post-independence construction; quality variable; pre-modern seismic codes; asbestos still common |
| **1990–2010** | Modernisation phase; reinforced concrete dominant; informal/clandestin construction widespread in peri-urban Praia / Mindelo |
| **2010–2020** | Tourism-boom development (Sal, Boa Vista off-plan); generally to modern standards but quality control varies by developer; **post-2014 Fogo eruption**: revised volcanic-zone construction guidance for Chã das Caldeiras |
| **Post-2020** | Modern code; ZDTI developments under POT regulation; energy/water efficiency emerging |

### Mandatory diagnostics at sale — Cape Verde specifics

> Cape Verde does not have a mandatory comprehensive diagnostic file at sale (no CV equivalent of FR DDT or PT certificado energético + ficha técnica). Buyer protections come from:

| Document / check | Required because | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Certidão do Registo Predial** (≤ 6 months) | Verifies title, owner, charges | Conservatória dos Registos |
| **Caderneta predial / matriz predial** | Confirms fiscal record, IPI/IUP base | DNRE / DGCI |
| **Licença de utilização / habite-se** | Verifies legal construction | Câmara Municipal urbanismo |
| **Certidão de não dívida (IPI/IUP, water, condomínio)** | Confirms no arrears | DNRE + ELECTRA + síndico de condomínio |
| **Plano Director Municipal (PDM) overlay** | Verifies zoning, flood/risk overlays, ZDTI/ZRPT inclusion | Câmara Municipal urbanismo |
| **Volcanic-hazard overlay (Fogo only)** | Confirms parcel relative to caldera + lava-flow zones | INMG + Câmara São Filipe |
| **Coastal-setback compliance (beachfront only)** | Verifies parcel respects DPM 80 m + POT setbacks | Direção Nacional do Ambiente + Câmara |
| **Independent structural survey** | Buyer-commissioned for any pre-1990 build, all volcanic-zone parcels, all beachfront | Independent engenheiro civil |

**Verdict**: due diligence is more **buyer-driven** in Cape Verde than in EU jurisdictions — there is no statutory mandatory diagnostic. Don't skip an independent structural engineer for any pre-1990 build, and never skip the title certificate.

### Climate projections to 2050 (World Bank CKP + IPCC AR6)

- +1.5 to +3.0 °C warming under SSP2-4.5 / SSP5-8.5
- Annual rainfall projected to decline further (already-arid baseline); intra-annual variability rising → more intense flash-flood events when rain occurs
- Sea-level rise ~30–50 cm by 2050 (IPCC AR6 mid-range), coastal-erosion + saltwater-intrusion accelerating
- Marine heatwaves rising; coral + reef-fishery impacts (relevant if buying near a marine protected area)

## Section: `--mains`

### Water + sewer + electricity operators

- **ELECTRA** — `https://www.electra.cv/` — national integrated utility for **electricity + water (potable, including desalination) + wastewater** across most islands. State-owned (under restructuring).
- **Águas e Energia da Boa Vista (AEB)** — Boa Vista-specific operator (concession arrangement)
- **Águas de Ponta Preta (APP)** — Sal-specific desalination + supply operator (concession)
- **Câmara Municipal sanitation departments** — operate local sewer + storm-water in many concelhos; coordinate with ELECTRA

### Verification path

1. Identify the operator from the island/concelho — ELECTRA is the default; AEB Boa Vista and APP Sal for those islands.
2. Listing language to check (in Portuguese):
   - **"ligado à rede pública / com canalização da rede"** = mains water + mains sewer ✅
   - **"furo / poço próprio"** = well / borehole — verify potability + concessão (water-extraction permit)
   - **"cisterna / depósito"** = cistern (water delivered by tanker truck — common on islands without desalination plant)
   - **"fossa séptica / fossa estanque"** = septic tank
   - **"saneamento autónomo"** = autonomous sewage system
3. Demand the latest **factura ELECTRA (water + electricity bill)** from seller — confirms connection and arrears status.
4. For new construction in peri-urban: check **certificado de ligação** (connection certificate).
5. Sal/Boa Vista tourist developments: most ZDTI projects have **on-site desalination + wastewater treatment** as part of the master plan — verify the developer's agreement with concessionaire.

### Power — reliability + generators

- **Grid reliability** is a known weakness in CV infrastructure. Sal + Praia have improved markedly (post-2015 generation upgrades + diesel-to-renewable transition). Outer islands and peri-urban zones still see scheduled + unscheduled outages.
- **Many tourism developments + business properties have on-site diesel/dual-fuel generators** with automatic transfer switches. Check whether your property has a generator and its maintenance contract.
- **Solar PV** rapidly expanding (Cape Verde has world-class solar resource); residential rooftop solar increasingly common. ELECTRA net-metering policy improving but variable.
- **Standard electrical**: 230 V / 50 Hz, **EU-style plug** (Type C / F).

### Gas

- **No piped natural-gas distribution network** for residential. **Bottled LPG (gás de garrafa)** is universal — ENACOL + GALP-style distributors. Subsidised retail price for cooking-size canisters.

- **Estatuto de Investidor Externo** (External Investor Status) — Cabo Verde TradeInvest (`https://cvtradeinvest.com/`): customs + IRPC incentives for investments above thresholds in tourism, ICT, ocean economy, light industry, renewables. Project-by-project negotiated.

## Caveats unique to Cape Verde

- **Cadastre coverage is uneven** — strongest in Sal, Boa Vista, urban Praia/Mindelo; weaker in rural Santiago, Fogo, Santo Antão, Brava, São Nicolau, Maio. **Always require título registado, never accept posse alone.**
- **Diaspora-heir consent** — Cape Verde has the world's largest diaspora-to-resident ratio (more Cape Verdeans abroad than at home). Many rural parcels have 5–20 dispersed heirs. Demand a **certidão de habilitação de herdeiros** for any inherited parcel; verify all heirs sign or have validly delegated POA.
- **Volcanic risk on Fogo (Pico do Fogo) + Brava** — direct lava-flow risk inside Chã das Caldeiras caldera; ashfall + secondary risk peripherally; insurance limited inside caldera.
- **Cyclone / flash-flood risk concentrated in São Vicente, São Nicolau, Santiago, Fogo** — verify property's elevation + ribeira proximity. **Hurricane Erin 2025-08 caused 9 deaths + ~27,500 affected** — recent baseline for risk pricing.
- **Sea-level rise + coastal erosion (Sal, Boa Vista beachfront)** — POT setback rules; verify parcel compliance + future climate exposure.
- **Off-plan / VEFA + rental-pool contracts (ZDTI)** — most Sal/Boa Vista tourist developments ship as packaged investment products with mandatory rental pools. Owner share of net often **40–60 %**; verify historical operator distributions before buying.
- **Currency repatriation only via licensed-bank channel** — keep the **declaração de câmbio** at purchase. Without it, future repatriation is not guaranteed.
- **Water-stress is structural** — most islands desalinate; verify supply reliability + cistern capacity.
- **Grid reliability variable** — generator + solar PV common; check on-site backup before assuming EU-style 24/7 power.
- **Lusophone civil-law process is similar to PT** — promessa de compra e venda (with sinal/deposit, typically 10–30 %) → escritura pública at the cartório notarial → registo predial. Total timeline 2–4 months typical.
- **Promessa de compra e venda is binding under specific clauses** — negotiate explicit **cláusula penal** (penalty for breach) and **sinal + princípio de pagamento** with notary séquestre to align incentives.
- **Estate-agent regulation is light** — verify agency registration with the relevant municipal authority + ask for prior sales references. UK-targeting agencies operate dual price lists; cross-check on Bons Negócios.
- **Restored historic property in Cidade Velha (Santiago, UNESCO World Heritage)** — restoration permits via IPC (Instituto do Património Cultural) + Câmara; façade + structural rules strict. Plan 12–24 months for restoration permits.
- **Mindelo cultural-heritage regulation** — colonial-era core has heritage protection; restoration permits required for Plateau and historic centre.
- **Time zone**: GMT-1 year-round (no DST) — convenient for EU-time-zone remote work; ~1 hour offset from Lisbon.
- **Languages of business**: Portuguese in formal/legal contexts; Crioulo socially. Foreign buyers often work via Portuguese-fluent lawyers/notaries — verify Portuguese-language deed comprehension or commission an authorised translation.
- **Inheritance for foreign owners** — Cape Verdean private-international-law typically applies the home-country law to non-resident owners' worldwide estate. **Make a Cape Verdean will** for the local property, drafted by a CV notary, naming the applicable foreign law explicitly.

## Quirks to know

- **Diaspora flight times + flight-cost arbitrage** — Sal (SID) is the international hub; TACV / Cabo Verde Airlines historically + LOT / TUI / TAP / Iberia / British Airways seasonal. Boa Vista (BVC) has direct UK + DE charters. Praia (RAI) is regional + TAP Lisbon. Internal interisland: TACV / Bestfly / TICV.
- **Festivals affect availability**: Carnaval (February — Mindelo São Vicente especially), Tabanca (May–Jun, Santiago), Mindelact theatre festival, Baía das Gatas music festival (August, São Vicente), Gamboa music festival (May, Praia). Plan visits + transactions outside peak weeks.
- **Pre-monsoon (Aug–Oct) flash-flood risk** — schedule property visits and inspections **outside** the rainy season for an honest baseline; revisit after a heavy-rain event to assess water-runoff behavior.
- **EU-style household appliances** — 230V/50Hz; UK appliances need plug adapters but voltage-compatible. US 110V appliances need transformers.
- **Internet** — fibre (FTTH) widespread in Praia / Mindelo / Sal Santa Maria + Espargos / Boa Vista Sal Rei; ADSL elsewhere; rural can be 4G-only. CVTelecom (former monopoly), Unitel-T+, Cabocom.
- **Banking for foreigners**: opening a CVE account requires NIF + passport + proof-of-address; takes days at BCA / BCN / Caixa Económica. Foreign-currency accounts (EUR / USD) available at major banks for non-residents.
- **Healthcare**: public + private mix; quality concentrated in Praia (Hospital Agostinho Neto) + Mindelo (Hospital Baptista de Sousa). Major medical referrals often to Lisbon (PT-CV cooperation arrangements).
- **Driving**: right-hand drive (RHD) — Cape Verde drives on the right. International + EU + UK licences accepted short-term; long-term residency requires CV licence conversion.
- **Inheritance for non-Lusophone foreigners** — make a Cape Verdean will for local property, drafted by a CV notary, naming the applicable home-country law via private-international-law referral.
- **Public holidays**: 1 Jan, Carnaval (Feb), 1 May, 5 Jul (Independence), 15 Aug, 12 Sep (National Heroes Day), 1 Nov, 25 Dec — administrative offices closed; plan deeds + permits accordingly.
- **Tax-residency trigger**: 183-day rule + permanent-home test; once tax-resident, worldwide income subject to IRPS — material decision for retirees considering relocation.
