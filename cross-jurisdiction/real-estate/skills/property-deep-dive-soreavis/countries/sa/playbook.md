# المملكة العربية السعودية / Saudi Arabia 🇸🇦 — Property Due-Diligence Playbook

ISO2: `sa`. Status: ✅ Fully populated (researched 2026-05).

> **Critical framing — read first**: Saudi Arabia is **historically one of the world's most foreign-buyer-restricted property markets**. The position is changing fast: the **Foreign Real Estate Ownership Law (نظام تملك غير السعوديين للعقار)** — **Royal Decree M/14 of 19/01/1447 AH (14 Jul 2025), gazetted in Umm Al-Qura 25 Jul 2025, effective 22 Jan 2026** — opens designated zones in Riyadh, Jeddah, and other major cities to non-GCC buyers (2026-05-27 verified, source Library of Congress Global Legal Monitor + White & Case + King & Spalding). **Mecca (Makkah) and Medina remain closed to non-Muslim foreign ownership** as a constitutional/religious matter. GCC nationals retain near-equal-to-Saudi rights. **The implementing regulations and the precise designated-zone list are live and evolving** — verify status with REGA (Real Estate General Authority) and the Royal Commission for Riyadh City **before** any reservation. Backdrop: the **Saudi Civil Transactions Law (نظام المعاملات المدنية) — Royal Decree M/191 of 29/11/1444 AH (18 Jun 2023), effective 16 Dec 2023** — is the first codified civil code in Saudi history (721 articles covering contract, sale, lease, property; retrospective effect on pre-2023 contracts). Currency risk vs USD is structurally zero — SAR is pegged at **3.75 to the USD since 1 Jun 1986** (Saudi Central Bank — SAMA).

Opens designated zones in Riyadh + Jeddah (priority) plus other major cities; scope includes residential, commercial, agricultural farms, NEOM/Qiddiya/Red Sea Global shares, and tokenised fractional ownership stakes.   - **2016 / 2025**: **White Land and Vacant Property Fees Law (نظام رسوم الأراضي البيضاء والعقارات الشاغرة)** — originally Royal Decree M/4 of 12/02/1437 AH at 2.5%/yr on undeveloped urban land >10,000 m²; **amended by Council of Ministers decision of 29 Apr 2025, published in Umm Al-Qura 12 May 2025**, which renamed the law, raised the annual ceiling to **up to 10% of land value**, lowered the threshold to **5,000 m²** (single parcel or aggregate holding of one owner within a city), and extended the charge to **vacant buildings** at a share of market rent capped at **5% of property value**. The [executive regulation (اللائحة التنفيذية لرسوم الأراضي البيضاء), Umm Al-Qura 22 Aug 2025](https://www.uqn.gov.sa/details?p=27369) sets a five-band tariff by urban-development priority — **10% / 7.5% / 5% / 2.5% / nil** — with the band fixed per geographic zone by ministerial decision (2026-09-18 verified, source Umm Al-Qura + [MOMAH](https://momah.gov.sa/ar/node/14963)). Administered by the **Ministry of Municipalities and Housing (MOMAH)**.
  - **2019**: **Mostadam (مستدام) green building rating** launched — Saudi-developed sustainability certification ([mostadam.sa](https://mostadam.sa/) under Ministry of Municipal Affairs)
  - **2020**: Tourism Law ([Royal Decree M/61 of 22/04/1441 AH](https://laws.moj.gov.sa/)) + 2021 STR Registration Decree — short-term rentals require Ministry of Tourism licensing — filed on **Tourism.sa** since 28 Aug 2026 (the earlier Sahel route is superseded)

## 🇸🇦 Foreign buyer eligibility (READ FIRST)

This section determines whether a transaction is even legally possible. Saudi Arabia's foreign-ownership regime is **the most restricted in the GCC** historically, and is undergoing a once-in-a-generation reform 2025–2026.

### Pre-2025 framework (still operative outside designated zones)

| Buyer category | Right |
|---|---|
| **Saudi national** | Full freehold anywhere in the Kingdom (excluding restricted military / strategic zones) |
| **GCC national** (UAE, Bahrain, Oman, Qatar, Kuwait) | Near-equivalent to Saudi nationals — freehold for residential/commercial use under **GCC Common Market 2008** ([gulfmigration.org GCC mobility](https://gulfmigration.org/)) — Mecca/Medina restrictions still apply per religion |
| **Non-GCC foreign national** (pre-2026) | **Severely restricted**. Permitted only via: (a) Foreign-Investment Real Estate Ownership Law 2000 (M/15 of 1421 AH) for licensed business activities (commercial property tied to a SAGIA-licensed enterprise); (b) Premium Residency holders (since 2019); (c) inheritance from a Saudi/GCC relative; (d) special exceptions per Royal Decree |
| **Non-Muslim of any nationality** | **Cannot own freehold property in Mecca (Makkah) or Medina (Madinah)** — constitutional/religious restriction; **cannot enter the haram boundaries of the two Holy Cities** in most circumstances |

### Foreign Real Estate Ownership Law — Royal Decree M/14 of 19/01/1447 AH (14 Jul 2025), effective 22 Jan 2026 — THE REFORM

**Source**: Royal Decree M/14 of 19/01/1447 AH (14 Jul 2025); gazetted Umm Al-Qura 25 Jul 2025; effective 22 Jan 2026 (2026-05-27 verified). [Library of Congress Global Legal Monitor 2026-02-11](https://www.loc.gov/item/global-legal-monitor/2026-02-11/saudi-arabia-new-law-opens-ownership-of-real-estate-properties-to-foreigners); [White & Case](https://www.whitecase.com/insight-alert/saudi-arabia-approves-landmark-real-estate-ownership-law-non-saudis); [Greenberg Traurig](https://www.gtlaw.com/en/insights/2025/7/saudi-arabia-enacts-new-real-estate-foreign-ownership-law-a-calibrated-opening-for-foreign-investors); REGA implementation portal `https://rega.gov.sa/`; [Etmam — MOMAH developer platform](https://etmam.momah.gov.sa/); for the foreign-investor route verify with **MISA (Ministry of Investment)**.

| Item | Status |
|---|---|
| **Effective date** | **22 January 2026** (180 days post-gazette publication 25 Jul 2025) |
| **Eligibility** | Non-GCC natural persons + non-GCC juridical persons (corporate buyers); includes tokenised fractional ownership stakes |
| **Permitted geographies** | **Designated zones** in Riyadh + Jeddah (priority) plus other major cities — final zone list maintained by REGA + Royal Commission for Riyadh City + Jeddah Development Authority |
| **Excluded geographies** | **Mecca + Medina remain closed to non-Muslim ownership** (constitutional); strategic / military zones; agricultural land outside designated zones |
| **Scope** | Residential, commercial, **agricultural farms**, NEOM/Qiddiya/Red Sea Global development shares, **tokenised fractional ownership** stakes — broader than prior law |
| **Investment thresholds** | Implementing regulations (2025–2026) will set per-zone minimum investment amounts — verify per parcel at REGA (pre-publication expectation: residential floor ≈ SAR 2–4M per dwelling, commercial higher — verify currency at REGA) |
| **Tax treatment** | Subject to standard regime: 5% RETT at acquisition, 15% VAT on commercial first sale, White Land + Vacant Property Fees (up to 10%/yr, banded) if undeveloped ≥5,000 m², no recurring property tax on developed land, no individual income tax on rental income — verify ZATCA rulings |
| **Authority** | REGA (Real Estate General Authority) + MOJ Notary — confirm zone status and eligibility BEFORE reservation; **Etmam is MOMAH's developer platform, not a foreign-investor channel** — verify the foreign-investor route with MISA (Ministry of Investment) |

⚠️ **Critical caveat**: The implementing regulations (اللائحة التنفيذية) are being finalized through 2025–2026. **Designated zones, investment thresholds, and excluded categories will evolve.** Treat any pre-2026 advice as provisional and re-verify at REGA before any binding step.

### Premium Residency (Iqama Khassa — إقامة مميزة) — property route

Source: [premiumresidency.sa](https://pr.gov.sa/) (Premium Residency Centre, under Council of Economic Affairs).

| Category | Threshold (2024 expansion — verify current) | Visa term |
|---|---|---|
| **Permanent (Limited)** | **One-time SAR 800,000** (~USD 213,000) payment to Premium Residency Centre | **Permanent**, renewable identity card every 10 yrs |
| **Renewable Annual** | **SAR 100,000/yr** payment | 1 yr renewable |
| **Real-Estate-Owner Premium Residency** (added 2024) | Property purchase ≥ **SAR 4,000,000** (~USD 1.07 M) in REGA-registered designated zone | Tied to property holding |
| **Investor Premium Residency** | Specified investment in licensed enterprise | Tied to investment |
| **Entrepreneur / Talent Premium Residency** | Specific qualifying tracks | Variable |

**Holder rights**:
- Right to own real estate (subject to Mecca/Medina restriction) including in designated zones
- Family sponsorship of spouse + children + parents
- Visit-visa issuance authority
- Multiple-entry travel
- Property + business ownership without local sponsor

⚠️ Specific real-estate threshold (SAR 4M) reportedly under the 2024 expansion **— verify against current PRC schedule before relying on this for a residency strategy**. Some sources cite SAR 1M / 4M / 5M tiers depending on category and date of update.

### GCC nationals — practical equivalence

Per [GCC Common Market 2008 + Resolution of GCC Supreme Council](https://www.gcc-sg.org/), GCC nationals enjoy near-equal rights including:
- Freehold ownership of residential + commercial property (excluding Mecca + Medina holy zones for non-Muslim GCC if any)
- Business ownership without local sponsor
- Free movement and employment
- Subject to the same stamp/RETT/VAT/White-Land-Tax framework as Saudis (no foreign-buyer surcharge)

### Verification path (before any reservation)

1. **Confirm property is in a designated zone for non-GCC** → REGA portal + Royal Commission for Riyadh City zone map + Jeddah Development Authority
2. **Pull the seller's current Title Deed (Sakk)** via MOJ Notary → verify ownership, encumbrances, mortgage status
3. **Check White Land + Vacant Property Fees compliance** if undeveloped parcel ≥ 5,000 m² → Ministry of Municipalities and Housing portal
4. **Confirm Premium Residency or non-GCC eligibility** at **REGA** (Etmam is the MOMAH developer platform, not a foreign-investor channel; verify the investor route with MISA) → obtain pre-approval letter for non-GCC purchase
5. **AML / source-of-funds**: SAMA + ZATCA require source-of-funds documentation per [AML Law 1439 AH (2017)](https://laws.moj.gov.sa/) for property transactions

## Section: `--tax`

### Annual property charges

**Saudi Arabia has NO recurring property tax on developed land** — neither owner-occupied nor let property pays an annual tax in the European sense. **BUT the White Land + Vacant Property Fees** apply to undeveloped urban parcels.

| Charge | Rate | Base | Statute |
|---|---|---|---|
| **White Land + Vacant Property Fees (رسوم الأراضي البيضاء والعقارات الشاغرة)** | **up to 10%/yr** on land (banded **10% / 7.5% / 5% / 2.5% / nil** by urban-development priority zone); **up to 5% of property value** on vacant buildings, set as a share of market rent | Land value (ministry-assessed) of **undeveloped urban land ≥ 5,000 m²** — single parcel or one owner's aggregate holding inside a city subject to the fee — plus long-unused buildings inside the approved urban boundary (vacant-property executive regulation still pending — verify at MOMAH) | [Executive regulation, Umm Al-Qura 22 Aug 2025](https://www.uqn.gov.sa/details?p=27369); [MOMAH](https://momah.gov.sa/ar/node/14963) (2026 regime, 2026-09-18 verified) |
| **Annual property tax on developed residential** | **NONE** | — | — |
| **Municipality services fees** (cleaning, lighting) | Bundled into utility bills (small SAR sums) | — | Per municipality |
| **Vacant Property / Vacancy Levy** | **PROPOSED 2024 — pending** ([SPA news 2024](https://www.spa.gov.sa/)) | TBD | Not yet enacted; monitor |
| **Zakat on land/property held for trading** | **2.5%** of the Zakat Base (2026 data, source ZATCA art. 15) | Applies to Muslim Saudi/GCC nationals on inventory + investment property held for trading | [ZATCA — Implementing Regulations for Zakat Collection, art. 15 + art. 48(8)-(9)](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/RulesRegulations/Documents/ZAKAT%20COLLECTION.pdf) |

### White Land + Vacant Property Fees — practical detail

- Applies to **undeveloped (white) land** of **≥ 5,000 m²** — single parcel or one owner's aggregate holding — inside a city subject to the fee (threshold cut from 10,000 m² by the 2025 amendment)
- Annual fee banded by urban-development priority zone: **10% / 7.5% / 5% / 2.5% / nil** of ministry-assessed land value, ceiling **10%** (was a flat 2.5%)
- Also reaches **vacant buildings** inside the approved urban boundary, at a share of market rent capped at **5% of property value** — the vacant-property executive regulation was still pending as at 2026-09-18; verify at MOMAH
- Designed as **anti-speculation incentive** to push hoarded land into development → significant policy lever; effective since 2017 (Phase 1 Riyadh; expanded phases 2020–2024)
- Penalty for non-payment: levy + statutory interest + can lead to forced public auction
- Source: [executive regulation, Umm Al-Qura 22 Aug 2025](https://www.uqn.gov.sa/details?p=27369) + [Ministry of Municipalities and Housing](https://momah.gov.sa/ar/node/14963) (2026-09-18 verified)

### Transaction taxes (one-time at purchase)

| Item | Rate | Statute / source |
|---|---|---|
| **Real Estate Disposition Tax (RETT — ضريبة التصرفات العقارية)** | **5% of disposal value** | [Royal Decree A/84 of 14/02/1442 AH (Oct 2020)](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Pages/Real-Estate-Disposal-Tax.aspx); paid by **seller** (statutory) but commercially negotiable |
| **Title Deed registration / MOJ Notary fees** | Nominal (SAR 200–2,000 typical) | MOJ schedule; recently digitalised via [najiz.sa](https://www.najiz.sa/) |
| **Real estate agent / broker commission** | **2.5% of price + 15% VAT** typical (negotiable; capped by Suhail/REGA practice direction) | REGA brokers Suhail platform |
| **Mortgage registration fee** | ~0.5% of loan amount typical | Per lender + SAMA |
| **Property valuation fee (mortgage)** | SAR 1,500–4,000 | Saudi Authority for Accredited Valuers (Taqeem) panel |
| **Lawyer / conveyancer (optional)** | SAR 5,000–25,000 typical | Saudi Bar Association registered |

### RETT — important detail

- Replaces VAT on most real-estate disposals (was 5% VAT pre-Oct-2020; raised to 15% Jul 2020 then carved out as 5% RETT Oct 2020)
- 5% applied to **disposal value** (sale price); statutory burden on seller but commercially passed to buyer per agreement
- **First-home exemption**: Saudi national first-time owner-occupiers exempt from RETT on properties up to SAR 1 million (some categories) — verify per ZATCA ruling
- **Inheritance + gift transfers between specific relatives** generally exempt from RETT
- Source: [ZATCA RETT page](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Pages/Real-Estate-Disposal-Tax.aspx)

### VAT (general — applies to commercial real estate + first-sale developments)

- Standard rate: **15%** (raised from 5% effective 1 Jul 2020) — [ZATCA VAT Law](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Pages/VAT.aspx)
- **Residential rental: VAT EXEMPT**
- **First sale of new residential by VAT-registered developer**: **VAT-exempt + RETT 5% applies** instead
- **Commercial property sale + lease**: standard 15% VAT
- **Mixed-use developments**: bifurcated treatment per ZATCA guidance

### Income tax / Zakat

| Status | Rate |
|---|---|
| **Saudi national / GCC individual** | **No personal income tax** on rental income; **Zakat 2.5%** on net wealth (incl. property held for trading) — Muslim citizens; collected by ZATCA |
| **Non-Saudi non-GCC individual** | **No personal income tax** on rental income (territorial system; no PIT on individuals) |
| **Saudi/GCC corporate** | **Zakat 2.5%** on Zakat base |
| **Foreign-owned corporate** | **20% Corporate Income Tax** on Saudi-source business profits — relevant for foreign-investor SPVs holding property |
| **Mixed Saudi/foreign corporate** | Zakat on Saudi share + CIT on foreign share |
| **Withholding Tax (WHT)** on rent paid to non-resident | **5%** WHT on rent paid to non-resident lessor by Saudi tenant (per [ZATCA WHT](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Pages/Withholding-Tax.aspx)) |

⚠️ **No personal income tax** in Saudi Arabia for individuals (Saudi or foreign) — among the world's most favourable individual tax regimes. **But foreign-owned corporates pay 20% CIT** on business profit including rental income from real estate held in the company.

### Capital gains

- **No CGT for individuals** — sale gains taxed only via 5% RETT on disposal value (not gain), no further CGT
- **Corporate gains** within scope of CIT (20%) for foreign-owned corporate; Zakat for Saudi-owned corporate
- **No estate / inheritance tax** in either case (Sharia inheritance applies)

### Inheritance — Sharia-default regime

- **Sharia inheritance applies as default** to all property in the Kingdom (Saudi + non-Saudi Muslims + non-Muslim foreigners)
- **Foreign non-Muslim property owners**: foreign Wills may be **partially recognized** under Saudi Private International Law for non-Muslim foreign nationals only — **not for Muslim heirs**
- **Documented intent + advance estate planning are essential** for foreign property holders — consult licensed Saudi lawyer; **Saudi courts have wide discretion** in recognizing foreign Wills
- **Mecca / Medina property**: cannot be transferred to non-Muslim heirs even via Will; tracks the haram-area constitutional restriction
- **DIFC Will / ADGM Will (UAE)**: **NOT enforceable** in Saudi Arabia; Saudi-resident or Saudi-property-holding foreign residents need separate Saudi-jurisdiction estate planning

### Long-term residential

- **Tenancy registration**: **Ejar (إيجار) platform** — `https://www.ejar.sa/` — mandatory unified electronic tenancy registration since 2018, run by Ministry of Housing in partnership with REGA

### Short-term rentals (Airbnb / vacation rentals)

### Jeddah floods — what changed (key recent events)

- **25 Nov 2009 Jeddah floods**: 124 confirmed deaths, ~10,000 homes affected; rainfall ~70mm in 4 hrs overwhelmed inadequate drainage; led to Royal Court investigation + 50+ municipal officials prosecuted
- **26 Jan 2011 Jeddah floods**: 11 deaths; second major event prompting accelerated drainage investment
- **2021–2022 events**: recurring flash flooding in Jeddah's southern districts (Quwaiza, Jameaa, Bany Malek)
- **23 Jan 2023 Mecca floods**: significant flooding around haram area
- **Reform**: SAR 23+ billion (USD 6.1B) Jeddah Stormwater Drainage Programme phased through 2030 ([Ministry of Municipalities and Housing — MOMAH](https://momah.gov.sa/)); Vision 2030 city-resilience pillar
- **Implication for buyers**: low-elevation Jeddah districts + wadi-channel-adjacent Riyadh/Jeddah parcels carry persistent flood risk — verify drainage history with REGA + Civil Defence; demand floor-elevation evidence

### Build-quality / fire risk

- **Saudi Building Code (SBC)** in force since 2007; major revisions 2018 (SBC 2018 series) tightened structural + fire + energy

- **Fire safety**: Civil Defence (الدفاع المدني) inspects + permits; high-rise ACP-cladding inventory unclear post-Grenfell era — request developer/owner Civil Defence Fire Compliance certificate

### Key context

Saudi Arabia is **one of the safest major countries globally** by per-capita crime metrics. **Homicide rate ~1.5 per 100,000** (2018 latest UNODC global data + Saudi sources; far below world median). Property crime rates low; petty theft + vehicle theft localized. **Hudud (criminal) penalties under Sharia + strong Public Security presence** create strong deterrence. **CCTV deployment expanding** in major cities under Vision 2030 Smart City framework (Riyadh smart-city sensor network 2022–2026).

- **Pickpocketing + petty theft**: low; mostly Hajj/Umrah season around Mecca/Medina haram districts (extreme crowd density)
- **Vehicle theft / break-in**: occasional in dense urban Riyadh/Jeddah (especially older neighbourhoods)
- **Cybercrime / fraud**: rising — phishing + Iqama-renewal scams targeting expatriates (cf. Anti-Cyber Crime Law 2007)
- **Domestic worker abuse / labour issues**: separate concern; Domestic Workers Welfare via MHRSD
- **Terrorism**: historically targeted (2003-2004 al-Qaeda + ISIS-affiliated 2014-2017); current threat **assessed low-medium** by Saudi National Counter-Terrorism Centre; Western embassies retain advisories

### Verdict bands (KSA-specific — vs national average)

- 🟢 < 50% national rate (most upmarket Riyadh/Jeddah districts; gated compounds; Diplomatic Quarter)
- 🟡 50–110% national rate (typical urban residential)

- 🔴 > 200% national in multiple categories (rare — selected hotspots near Hajj/Umrah peak times)

### Confidence

MEDIUM. Saudi Arabia does NOT publish granular district-level crime statistics in the way most Western countries do. Aggregated reports + Numbeo (limited sample) + diplomatic security advisories are the de facto reference. Methodology stable but transparency limited.

## Section: `--climate`

Universal logic per `shared/climate-projections.md`. KSA-specific overrides:

- **Köppen-Geiger**: BWh (hot desert) — most of country; BSh (semi-arid) coastal Asir + western highlands; Cwa (humid subtropical) limited southwest
- **Coastal exposure**: Red Sea (west) + Persian/Arabian Gulf (east) coasts; Jeddah, Yanbu, Red Sea Project, Khobar/Dammam Corniche all <5km from coast
- **Reference baseline**: NCM + GASTAT 1991–2020 normal series
- **Key projections** (IPCC AR6 + KAUST regional models, SSP2-4.5 / SSP5-8.5):
  - Annual mean temperature **+1.5 → +3.5°C** by 2100 (Arabian Peninsula warming faster than global)
  - Hot days (Tmax ≥ 45°C): currently ~30–60/yr in interior Riyadh → 80–120/yr by 2100 SSP5-8.5
  - Hot nights (Tmin ≥ 28°C): doubling/tripling
  - **Wet-bulb 35°C threshold**: projected to be approached in southern Red Sea coast + eastern Gulf coast by 2070+ under high-emissions
  - Sea-level rise: +0.30 → +0.85m by 2100 SSP2-4.5; +0.45 → +1.10m SSP5-8.5
  - Extreme rainfall hourly intensity: +20–40% in western Arabia

## Section: `--finance`

### Mortgage market

- **Regulator**: [SAMA — Saudi Central Bank (البنك المركزي السعودي)](https://www.sama.gov.sa/) — sets macroprudential LTV + DSR via [SAMA Real Estate Finance Rules](https://www.sama.gov.sa/en-US/Laws/Pages/Banking-Rules.aspx) (2014 + 2018 amendments)
- **Mortgage law basis**: [Real Estate Finance Law M/50 of 13/08/1433 AH (2012)](https://laws.moj.gov.sa/) + Registered Real Estate Mortgage Law M/49 of 13/08/1433 AH
- **Refinancer**: [Saudi Real Estate Refinance Co. (SRC — الشركة السعودية لإعادة التمويل العقاري)](https://www.srco.com.sa/) — PIF-owned; provides liquidity to retail mortgage market
- **Subsidy**: [Real Estate Development Fund (REDF — صندوق التنمية العقاري)](https://www.redf.gov.sa/) — Saudi-national-only down-payment + interest subsidy programs

### LTV caps

| Borrower | LTV cap |
|---|---:|
| **Saudi national, first home** | **90%** (SAMA + REDF subsidy combined) |
| **Saudi national, subsequent home** | 70% |
| **GCC national** | 70% (typical) |
| **Non-GCC expatriate (resident with valid Iqama)** | 60–70% (case-by-case; SAMA macroprudential) |
| **Non-resident foreign individual** | **rarely available** pre-2026; case-by-case for Premium Residency holders |
| **Investment / non-self-use** | typically 50–60% |

### Rate structure

- **Murabaha (مرابحة)** + **Ijara (إجارة)** Sharia-compliant structures dominate (cost-plus-profit + lease-to-own) — interest-bearing conventional mortgages also available but minority
- **Fixed-rate residential** common: 3–7 year fixed reset; 25–30 year amortization standard
- **Indicative rate Q1 2026**: ~4.5–6.5% Murabaha equivalent (tracking SAIBOR + Fed cycle via SAR-USD peg)
- **SAIBOR (Saudi Arabian Interbank Offered Rate)** Q1 2026: 1M ~ 4.5%, 12M ~ 5.0% — verify current at SAMA

### Foreigner mortgages

- Pre-2026: **highly restricted**; major Saudi banks (Al Rajhi, SNB, Riyad Bank, Alinma, Saudi Investment Bank) require valid Iqama + Saudi salary income + employer NOC
- Post-2026 (anticipated): designated-zone foreign buyers may access mortgages from Saudi banks + foreign-bank branches (HSBC Saudi, BNP Paribas, JPMorgan) — final framework rolling out 2025–2026
- **Currency restriction**: SAR-denominated mortgages standard; foreign-currency borrowing rare and tightly regulated by SAMA

### Banking access

Account opening requires **valid Iqama + Saudi mobile + National Address** for residents; non-residents face stricter compliance under SAMA AML rules + global KYC. Major non-resident-friendly: **HSBC Saudi (HBSA)**, **SNB (formerly NCB)**, **Al Rajhi Bank**, **Riyad Bank**, **Alinma**.

⚠️ Foreign-buyer mortgage access in 2026 will depend on REGA + SAMA implementing regulations. Until clarified, assume **cash purchase** is the default path for non-GCC foreign buyers; mortgage availability evolving.

## Section: `--currency`

### Saudi Riyal (SAR) — peg framework

- **Peg**: SAR 3.75 = USD 1, fixed since **1 Jun 1986** ([SAMA Monetary Policy](https://www.sama.gov.sa/en-US/Pages/default.aspx))
- **Peg type**: hard peg with active intervention by SAMA via FX reserves (~USD 437B as of Q4 2025 — IMF IFS); ~30+ months of import cover
- **Mechanism**: SAMA buys/sells SAR vs USD at fixed parity to maintain band; interbank SAR/USD trades within ±0.0005 typically
- **Inflation transmission**: Saudi monetary conditions de facto track US Fed via peg; SAIBOR moves in lockstep with USD LIBOR/SOFR

### What this means for buyers

- **USD-denominated buyers** (US): **near-zero FX risk** for purchase + ongoing payments
- **EUR / GBP / JPY buyers**: SAR effectively a USD-tracking currency; FX exposure = home-vs-USD exposure. Use forward contracts for transaction-cost-base lock-in if material
- **GCC buyers** (AED-pegged-USD, BHD-pegged-USD, OMR-pegged-USD, KWD-basket): near-zero FX risk
- **Hedging products**: deep FX-forward + options market via Saudi banks + Dubai/London for SAR pairs >USD 5M; est. 12-month USD/EUR forward bid-ask ~5–15 bps for SAR amounts >USD 1M (indicative bank-desk pricing, not a quoted rate — obtain a live quote)

### Peg credibility

SAMA's USD foreign reserves ≈ USD 437B (Q4 2025; IMF data) supported by **hydrocarbon current-account surplus** (~USD 60–120B/yr depending on oil price) + strong sovereign rating (Moody's Aa3, S&P A+, Fitch A+). Peg defended successfully through 1986–2026 — including 2014–2016 oil-price crash, 2020 COVID + oil-war shock, 2022 USD-strength cycle. **No serious near-term peg-break risk** assessed by IMF Article IV 2024.

⚠️ **Tail risk**: any sustained peg-breakdown would re-rate SAR-denominated property substantially in foreign-currency terms. Position-size with this in mind for foreign-currency-denominated buyers. Periodic academic + market debate on long-term sustainability — not a near-term break risk.

### Capital controls

- **No restrictions on outbound FX** for residents + non-residents (post-1986 liberalization)
- **AML / source-of-funds** requirements stringent: SAMA + ZATCA + Saudi Financial Intelligence Unit (SAFIU) require declaration for transfers >SAR 60,000 ($16,000) — under [AML Law 1439 AH (2017)](https://laws.moj.gov.sa/)
- **Real-estate transactions** require source-of-funds documentation (bank statements, employment, prior asset sale) — enforced at MOJ Notary

## Section: `--notary`

### Conveyancing process

Saudi Arabia uses a **civil-law notary system**: the [Notary Public (Kateb Adl — كاتب عدل)](https://www.moj.gov.sa/) under the Ministry of Justice is the sole authority for executing + registering property transfers. Notaries hold quasi-judicial authority and are bound by Saudi law + Sharia principles.

**Modern practice (2020+)**: most transfers now executed via **Najiz electronic notary platform** (`https://www.najiz.sa/`) — pre-booked appointment + digital execution + instant Sakk issuance for many cases.

### Standard timeline (typical residential Saudi/GCC buyer + seller)

| Step | Day | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Reservation / preliminary agreement | D0 | Initial deposit (typically 10% of price) to seller; non-binding pending due diligence |
| 2. Due diligence | D0–D14 | Title Deed verification (Sakk), encumbrance check, White Land Tax check, building permits, NOC from developer/Body Corporate |
| 3. Mortgage approval (if applicable) | D7–D45 | Bank Murabaha/Ijara approval + valuation |
| 4. **Notary appointment (Kateb Adl / Najiz)** | D30–D60 | All parties present (or PoA); seller + buyer + witnesses; new Sakk issued in buyer name; payment transferred via SAMA-monitored channel |
| 5. RETT 5% paid by seller within 30 days | D30–D90 | ZATCA filing |
| 6. Title transfer recorded in Real Estate Registry | D60–D90 | MOJ Real Estate Registry electronic ID issued (if applicable region) |

### Costs

| Item | Cost (SAR) |
|---|---|
| Notary registration fee | ~SAR 200–2,000 (modest) |
| Real Estate Registry fee | ~SAR 500–1,500 |
| RETT 5% (statutory seller, often passed) | 5% of price |
| Real estate broker commission | 2.5% + 15% VAT = ~2.875% effective per side |
| Mortgage registration | ~0.5% of loan |
| Property valuation (Taqeem) | SAR 1,500–4,000 |
| Lawyer (optional, foreign buyer recommended) | SAR 5,000–25,000 typical |
| Translation (foreign documents) | SAR 100–300/page certified |
| **Total transaction cost (cash buyer, no broker)** | **~5–8% of price** |
| **Total transaction cost (with mortgage + broker)** | **~8–12% of price** |

### Foreign buyer practical (post-2026 designated-zone)

- **Najiz electronic notary** typically requires Saudi National ID / Iqama; foreign non-resident buyer needs **Power of Attorney (Wakalah)** notarized at Saudi embassy in home country + apostille
- **Beneficial ownership disclosure**: corporate buyers must register with Saudi Companies Registry + ZATCA; UBO ID per AML
- **AML / source-of-funds**: bank statements + employment evidence + prior asset sale evidence — enforced at notary level

## Section: `--retirement`

### Retirement to Saudi Arabia scenario

Saudi Arabia is **not a traditional retirement destination** in the international sense, but is **emerging post-Vision 2030** as a viable option for:

1. **Returning Saudi diaspora** (Saudi nationals + heritage families)
2. **GCC nationals** seeking lower-cost-of-living than UAE/Qatar
3. **Premium Residency holders** (post-2024 expansion includes Real-Estate + Investor + Talent tracks)
4. **Expatriates with Iqama** retiring in place after long career

### No specific retirement visa

Unlike Portugal D7, Spain Non-Lucrative, or Malaysia MM2H, Saudi Arabia has **no dedicated retirement visa**. Retirees enter via:
- **Premium Residency** (most common for foreigners)
- **Family sponsorship** (Saudi child sponsors elderly parent)
- **Returning Saudi national** — automatic right

### Cost-of-living for retirees (2026)

- 2-bed apt rental Riyadh outside prime: SAR 35,000–80,000/yr (~USD 9,300–21,300)
- Public healthcare: free for Saudi nationals; expat retirees on Premium Residency need **private health insurance** SAR 8,000–25,000/yr
- Premium private healthcare: SAR 25,000–100,000/yr depending on cover
- Utilities: SAR 800–2,500/month (SEC + NWC)
- Eating out: mid-range SAR 50–150/meal; high-end SAR 250+
- **No alcohol** (sale prohibited; recent 2024 change: limited service in selected diplomatic settings; NEOM "tolerance zone" reportedly under planning — verify)
- **Climate**: Riyadh winter mild (10–22°C); summer extreme (40–50°C); Jeddah humid year-round; Asir highlands cool

### Tax position for retirees

- **No PIT, no CGT, no estate/inheritance tax** for individuals (excluding Sharia inheritance default mechanism)
- **No tax on foreign pension income** received in KSA
- **5% RETT** on real-estate disposal
- **15% VAT** on most consumption (inc. healthcare? — exempt; education exempt; food exempt 2018)
- **Zakat 2.5%** on Muslim Saudi/GCC nationals' net wealth (incl. property held for trading) — collected by ZATCA

### Healthcare access

- **Public** (Ministry of Health): free for Saudi nationals; Vision 2030 HSTP reform privatizing many tertiary hospitals
- **Private**: world-class in Riyadh + Jeddah (King Faisal Specialist Hospital, Dr. Al Habib Group, Saudi German Hospital, Dallah)
- **Premium Residency** holders have full healthcare access via private insurance

### Verdict for retirement-first buyers

🟡 **Watch / Adapt** — viable for high-net-worth retirees seeking GCC tax efficiency + Vision-2030-driven new-build communities (Diriyah, AlUla, Red Sea) AND Muslim retirees seeking proximity to Mecca/Medina. **Less viable** for non-Muslim Western retirees seeking cosmopolitan lifestyle (UAE Dubai/Abu Dhabi or Bahrain offer easier cultural fit, more permissive social environment).

## Section: `--exit`

### Resale market liquidity

- **Average days on market 2025 Q4**: ~60–120 days (Riyadh mid-tier mass private); 90–180 days (Jeddah mid-tier); 30–90 days (in-demand Riyadh Metro-served + KAFD); 180+ days (luxury > SAR 10M)
- **Cyclical sensitivity**: Saudi secondary market is **moderately liquid** in upcycle; **thin in downcycle** — 2014–2017 oil-price downturn saw transaction volumes drop 30–40% from 2013 peak per REGA data
- **REGA Real Estate Indicators**: monthly transaction-volume + price index → leading indicator of liquidity restoration
- **Foreign-buyer reform 2026**: anticipated to add liquidity in designated-zone primary market; secondary market resilience uncertain

- 20% CIT (foreign-owned company) or Zakat 2.5% (Saudi/GCC)
- Substantially worse than the no-individual-CGT default
- Document genuine investment intent at purchase

## Caveats unique to KSA

- **Foreign Real Estate Ownership Law — Royal Decree M/14 of 19/01/1447 AH (14 Jul 2025), effective 22 Jan 2026** — REGULATIONS LIVE-EVOLVING; designated zones, thresholds, and excluded categories rolling out 2025–2026; verify per-parcel at REGA before any reservation
- **Mecca + Medina non-Muslim ownership prohibition** — constitutional/religious; tracks haram boundaries; cannot be overridden by any commercial structure
- **Sharia-default inheritance** — DIFC/ADGM Wills NOT enforceable; foreign property holders need separate Saudi-jurisdiction estate planning
- **No personal income tax + no recurring property tax** — among world's most tax-favourable
- **5% RETT on disposal** (statutory seller) — primary transaction tax; replaces VAT on most real-estate disposals since 2020
- **15% VAT** on commercial real estate + STR + first-sale developments by VAT-registered developer
- **White Land + Vacant Property Fees up to 10%/yr** on undeveloped urban parcels ≥5,000 m² (banded by priority zone) — material anti-speculation lever
- **Saudization (Nitaqat)** — labour-market quota system; affects rental demand from expat workforce
- **Vision-2030 mega-projects** (NEOM, Diriyah, Red Sea, Qiddiya, Roshn) — first-generation execution + PIF-developer-concentration risk
- **Premium Residency Real-Estate route** SAR 4M threshold — verify current PRC schedule
- **Riyadh Metro Phase 1 (Dec 2024)** — material reset of property catchment values within 800 m of stations
- **Jeddah flood history** (2009 + 2011 + recurring) — drainage upgrade phased to 2030; verify property elevation + wadi-adjacency
- **SAR pegged USD at 3.75 since 1986** — zero FX risk for USD asset holders; meaningful FX risk vs EUR / GBP / floating currencies
- **Sharia-compliant Murabaha + Ijara** dominate mortgage market; conventional interest-bearing minority + foreign-bank-only
- **Cash + bank-transfer** dominate property settlement; SAMA AML + ZATCA source-of-funds enforced at MOJ Notary
- **Mostadam green building** mandatory for many new public-funded + PIF mega-projects
- **NEOM "tolerance zone"** — distinct cultural framework reportedly under planning (alcohol, mixed beaches) — verify final implementation
- **AML / source-of-funds**: SAMA + ZATCA require source-of-funds documentation per AML Law 2017; bank statements, employment, prior asset sale evidence needed at notary
- **No alcohol** countrywide outside specific Vision-2030 international zones — material lifestyle factor for some Western buyers
