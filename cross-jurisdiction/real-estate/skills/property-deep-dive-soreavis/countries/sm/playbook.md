# San Marino 🇸🇲 — Property Due-Diligence Playbook

ISO2: `sm`. Status: ✅ Fully populated (researched 2026-05).

## Country profile

- **Postcode**: 5 digits, range **47890–47899** — `47890` Città di San Marino (capital), `47891` Dogana / Serravalle, `47893` Borgo Maggiore, `47894` Chiesanuova, `47895` Domagnano, `47896` Faetano, `47897` Fiorentino, `47898` Montegiardino, `47899` Galazzano. Postcodes overlap with the surrounding Italian system but San Marino has its own ISO country code (SM) and dedicated codes
- **Admin levels**: republic → **9 Castelli (castles / municipalities)** — Acquaviva, Borgo Maggiore, Chiesanuova, Città di San Marino, Domagnano, Faetano, Fiorentino, Montegiardino, Serravalle. Each Castello has a **Giunta di Castello** (parish council) with limited local powers; the **Consiglio Grande e Generale (CGG)** is the unicameral national parliament (60 members, biannual *Capitani Reggenti* heads of state)
- **Population**: ~**33,987** (Ufficio Statistica, 30 Sept 2024 — `https://www.statistica.sm/`); +0.4% YoY; growth driven by foreign in-migration as Sammarinese births decline. By Castello (rounded, last available Castello-level breakdown):
  - Serravalle ~11,035 (largest, lowland N), Borgo Maggiore ~6,871, Città di San Marino ~4,054 (capital, mountain core), Domagnano ~3,568, Fiorentino ~2,563, Acquaviva ~2,144, Faetano ~1,192, Chiesanuova ~1,147, Montegiardino ~smallest
- **Surface**: ~61 km² total — Italy's third-smallest sovereign neighbour after Vatican City + Monaco; landlocked **enclave fully surrounded by Italy** (Emilia-Romagna prov. Rimini to N/W; Marche prov. Pesaro-Urbino to S/E)
- **Currency**: **EUR (€)** — adopted via [Monetary Agreement EU/San Marino 2001 (rev. 2012)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A22012A1217%2801%29); San Marino mints its own euro coins (since 2002, capped annual volume); **NOT a Eurozone member, NOT an EU member**. Same regime category as Andorra / Monaco / Vatican
- **Languages**: **Italian** (sole official); local **dialetto sammarinese** (Romagnol-derived) in informal use. Notarial acts and primary government legislation **in Italian** — translate verbatim when citing numbers
- **Cadastre**: **Ufficio Tecnico del Catasto e Cartografia (UTCC)** within Dipartimento Territorio e Ambiente — `https://www.gov.sm/pub1/GovSM/Dipartimenti/Dipartimento-Territorio-e-Ambiente/Ufficio-Tecnico-del-Catasto-e-Cartografia.html`. Public counter (Sportello Visure) at Via Piana 42, 47890 San Marino
- **Land registry**: **Ufficio del Registro e Conservatoria** within Segreteria di Stato per le Finanze e il Bilancio — `https://gov.sm/pub2/GovSM/Dipartimenti/Dipartimento-Economia-e-Finanze/Ufficio-del-Registro-e-Conservatoria.html`
- **Identifier**: Foglio + Mappale (sheet + parcel) per Castello — Italian-pattern but separate UTCC register
- **Key documents**: *atto pubblico di compravendita* (notarial deed), *visura catastale* (UTCC extract), *certificato del Registro* (Conservatoria extract), *certificato penale generale* (clean criminal record — required for foreign buyers under Legge 173/2018)
- **EU/Schengen status (CRITICAL)**: **EU customs union** since 1991 (extended to agricultural goods 2002 — Decisione 2/2002 Comitato di Cooperazione); **NOT in Schengen** — but de facto open border with Italy because the entire perimeter is Italian Schengen territory and there are no internal customs/immigration controls. Council of EU authorised Schengen-association negotiations 30 May 2024. **EU Association Agreement** (EU/Andorra/SM): negotiations concluded Dec 2023; Council of the EU approved the text 16 Jul 2026; signature scheduled 19 Oct 2026 in Brussels; not in force — entry into force pending ratification (verify at `https://www.esteri.sm/pub2/EsteriSM/en/UE/Accordo.html` + `https://andorraue.ad/ca/acord-d-associacio`)
- **Foreign-buyer regime (CRITICAL)**: residential acquisition by non-citizens governed by **Legge 24 dicembre 2018 n.173** + **Decreto Delegato 105/2019** + confirming **Legge 3 ottobre 2019 n.154**. Pre-2018 regime required prior authorisation of the *Consiglio dei XII* (parliamentary commission); the 2018 reform abolished the prior-authorisation requirement and replaced it with notary-verified eligibility checks + a **2-property cap per foreign individual**. See Foreign-buyer rules section below

## Section: `--tax`

### Annual property tax — Imposta Straordinaria sugli Immobili (ISI)

San Marino's recurring property tax is the **ISI (Imposta Straordinaria sugli Immobili)**, introduced by **Legge di Bilancio 22 dicembre 2011 n.200** (later amended; consolidated framework via Decreto Delegato 30 aprile 2018 n.44 — `https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/home/documento17100638.html`).

- **Primary residence (abitazione principale)**: **EXEMPT** — small/medium primary homes actually inhabited by the owner are not subject to ISI. ~85% of Sammarinese residents are owner-occupiers, so ISI primarily catches second homes + non-resident-held stock + larger/luxury primary residences exceeding the size threshold
- **Second home / non-resident-held residential**: ISI applies; rate depends on cadastral category + class with **a fixed component + a variable component** assessed on the *rendita catastale* (cadastral rental value) — published per-category bands in the Decreto Delegato schedule
- **150 EUR deduction** for owners holding a mortgage or lease contract on the property
- **Rate range (est.)**: effective combined ISI on a typical second-home apartment trends around **0.3–0.8% of cadastral value/yr** (rendita-based, NOT market-value based — cadastral values typically substantially below market) → in cash terms **a few hundred to low-four-digit € annually for typical apartments** — verify per parcel with Ufficio Tributario
- **Verification**: ask the seller for the most recent **avviso di pagamento ISI** (assessment notice) — single cleanest evidence of the actual annual ISI bill

> ⚠️ The exact ISI rates per category/class are set in the consolidated Decreto Delegato schedule + annual Legge di Bilancio adjustments; treat the 0.3–0.8% headline as `est.` indicative and verify the parcel-specific rendita + applicable rate at Ufficio Tributario (`https://www.gov.sm/pub2/GovSM/La-PA-Risponde/FAQ.html?tema=Tributario`) before relying on a pro-forma annual cost.

### Imposta Patrimoniale (annual wealth tax)

- **Imposta Patrimoniale Straordinaria** (Legge 21 dicembre 2017 n.147 art. 48) levies on **net wealth of residents** including (a) real estate, (b) financial assets globally, (c) net assets of Sammarinese companies — **applies to RESIDENTS** principally; non-resident foreign owners subject to ISI on the property only, not to Imposta Patrimoniale on global wealth
- Practical: relevant for buyers establishing San Marino tax residency; not a non-resident concern

### Transaction taxes — Imposta di Registro + Catastale + Ipotecaria

San Marino's transaction-tax stack on residential resale to a **private buyer (non-developer chain)** is materially lower than Italy's:

- **Imposta di Registro** (registration tax): **5% ordinary** proportional rate on onerous transfers of immovables; **€70 fixed nominal** for qualifying **prima casa** purchase (surface ≤200 m², buyer holds no other residential property); **3%** for **permute** (exchanges) between contiguous rural parcels. Verify deal-specific application at Ufficio del Registro (`https://www.finanze.sm/`) and via *notaio*. (2026-05-27 verified, source Emme Property notarial summary.)
- **Imposta Ipotecaria + Catastale** (mortgage + cadastre transfer tax): minor, typically **~0.5–1.5%** combined
- **Tassa di Concessione Governativa** (foreign-buyer fee): **€1,000 fixed administrative fee** under **Article 51 Legge 173/2018** — *NOT* an ad-valorem component. The non-resident buyer separately pays the ordinary Imposta di Registro (5%) without prima-casa benefits. (2026-05-27 verified, source Toccaceli Bronzetti studio on Article 51 Legge 173/2018.)
- **Notary fee**: regulated; typically **1–2% of price** all-in for standard residential — Sammarinese notarial profession is small (handful of practising *notai*); arancel similar to Italian notarial scale
- **Estate agent commission** (where engaged): typically **3% buyer + 3% seller** practice; verify per *mandato*

### Imposta Monofase (turnover-stage tax — VAT-equivalent)

- San Marino has **NO standard VAT (IVA)** as Italy does; instead operates an **Imposta Monofase** levied at single stage of the supply chain (typically import / first sale)
- **Residential resale between private parties = NOT subject to Monofase** (out of scope, only Registro/Ipotecaria/Catastale apply)
- **New-build first sale by a developer-builder reselling**: **17% Monofase on the sale value** (current rate; historically adjusted) — verify currency at Ufficio Tributario
- **No Monofase on subsequent private resale**

### Total transaction cost (buyer side, est.)

- **Resale, resident private buyer**: ~**5–9%** all-in (Registro + Ipotecaria + Catastale + notary + agent if applicable)
- **Resale, non-resident foreign buyer**: ~**6–11%** (above + tassa di concessione governativa)
- **New-build first sale (developer)**: **17% Monofase** + Ipotecaria/Catastale + notary → typically materially higher than resale; the Monofase is a meaningful uplift on first-hand sales

⚠️ The transaction-tax stack is **less standardised in publicly accessible English** than Italy's; the figures above synthesise multiple secondary Sammarinese-tax-advisory sources + consolidated legislation. **Treat the headline percentages as indicative ranges; obtain a specific notary-side cost projection (preventivo notarile) before signing the *compromesso***.

### Capital gains — Imposta sulle plusvalenze

- **IGR Section** treats real-estate gains in the personal-income framework: gains from sale of secondary properties are **taxable** under IGR (see income tax below); gains from sale of *abitazione principale* held for the qualifying period are typically **exempt**
- Holding-period rules + reinvestment-rollover exemption variants exist — verify with notary + Sammarinese commercialista at deal-stage

### Personal income tax — IGR (Imposta Generale sui Redditi)

Foundational law: **Legge 16 dicembre 2013 n.166** as amended (most recent material amendment: **Legge 12 novembre 2025 n.141**, gazetted in the *Bollettino Ufficiale*, in force 17 Nov 2025, provisions applying from the **2026 tax period**). The 141/2025 reform shifts personal-income relief from deductions to **tax credits (*detrazioni*)** — e.g. a new progressive *SMaC* detrazione — while **retaining (amending, not replacing)** the L.166/2013 progressive 9–35% bracket schedule below. (2026-07-02 verified, source Consiglio Grande e Generale archive + San Marino RTV + HLB / Toccaceli Bronzetti Legge 141/2025 digests.)

- **Progressive scale (residents, individuals)**, full 8-bracket schedule effective 2026 per Legge 141/2025: up to €10,000 → **9%**; €10,000.01–€18,000 → **13%**; €18,000.01–€28,000 → **17%**; €28,000.01–€38,000 → **21%**; €38,000.01–€50,000 → **25%**; €50,000.01–€65,000 → **28%**; €65,000.01–€80,000 → **31%**; **above €80,000 → 35%**. Brackets auto-adjust every two years by cost-of-living index (max 6 % adjustment), first adjustment 2028. (2026-05-27 verified, source HLB San Marino digest of Legge 141/2025.)
- **Corporate IGR**: **17% ordinary**; **temporarily 18% for tax periods 2026–2030** per Legge 141/2025 Art. 54 (*"In via straordinaria per i periodi d'imposta 2026 2027 2028 2029 e 2030 l'Imposta Generale sui Redditi è elevata al 18%"*); ordinary 17% resumes 2031 unless extended. (2026-05-27 verified, source HLB SM + Studio Bollini.)
- **Non-resident IGR (rental income from SM property)**: levied on the SM-source rental income; verify rate band with Ufficio Tributario — Italian-resident landlords also bear ITA tax via DTA
- **Frontalieri (cross-border workers)**: special regime under **Italy–San Marino DTA** (signed 2002, in force 2013, rev. 2012) — coordination via FAQ at `https://www.gov.sm/pub2/GovSM/La-PA-Risponde/FAQ.html?tema=Tributario`
- **No standalone wealth tax** beyond Imposta Patrimoniale Straordinaria above
- **No standalone inheritance tax in direct line** between qualifying heirs (similar Andorra/Monaco pattern; verify with notary for collateral-heir scenarios)

### Future risk

- **Legge 141/2025 IGR reform** entered force 2026 → top-rate / corporate-rate uplift; further calibration possible in 2026/2027 budget cycles
- **EU Association Agreement**: negotiations concluded **12 Dec 2023**; Commission signing/conclusion proposals adopted **26 Apr 2024**; Coreper approved the text unanimously and confirmed it is a **mixed agreement** **15 Jul 2026** (signature and ratification involve all 27 member states, Andorra, San Marino and the EU); Council of the EU approved the text **16 Jul 2026**; **signature ceremony scheduled 19 Oct 2026 in Brussels** ([Govern d'Andorra AA portal](https://andorraue.ad/ca/acord-d-associacio), news items 15 Jul + 16 Sep 2026); per Eunews (secondary, 16 Jul 2026) the Council decision covers signature **and provisional application** — scope and start of any provisional application not verified against the Council release; check consilium.europa.eu after signature. **Not in force** — entry into force pending EP consent and ratification by all parties. Verify status at [esteri.sm/UE/Accordo](https://www.esteri.sm/pub2/EsteriSM/en/UE/Accordo.html). (2026-05-27 verified, source esteri.sm + europarl A10-0003/2026; Coreper, Council and signature dates added 2026-09-24 per andorraue.ad.)

## Section: `--rental`

### Long-term residential

- Civil-law rental framework derived from Sammarinese codice civile + special tenancy provisions; broadly less regulated than Italy's *Legge 431/1998 cedolare secca* regime — typical lease structures: 4+4 (4 years renewable, residential) or *uso transitorio* (transitory) for short-term
- Tenant protections: standard notice periods (typically 6 months), no statutory rent-cap regime equivalent to Italy's *canone concordato*
- Deposit (*caparra* / *deposito cauzionale*): typically 2–3 months
- Rental income is **Erwerbseinkommen-equivalent** under IGR → progressive personal-income rate (residents) or non-resident rate

### Short-let (Bed & Breakfast / case vacanze / Airbnb)

- **Tourist-accommodation regulation** under Sammarinese *Legge sul turismo* + Decreto Delegato attuativi via **Segreteria di Stato per il Turismo** (`https://www.turismo.sm/`) — *strutture ricettive extralberghiere* (B&B, affittacamere, case e appartamenti vacanze) require communication / autorizzazione
- **Airbnb / short-term private letting**: small market; cross-border ITA rules (Rimini-province *codice CIR*) DO NOT apply but operators serving cross-border tourist flow follow ITA-side practice. Verify SM-specific licensing with Ufficio del Turismo before assuming legal short-let
- **Tassa di soggiorno** (tourist tax per overnight): small per-night levy where applicable — verify at `https://www.turismo.sm/`
- **Constraint reality**: the foreign-buyer 2-property cap (Legge 173/2018) + 2-property maximum makes large-portfolio short-let strategies effectively closed to non-residents

### Tax treatment

- Rental income → IGR (residents progressive 9–35%); non-residents on SM-source rental — verify rate band + DTA coordination with home country
- **No cedolare-secca-equivalent flat regime** comparable to Italy's 21%/26% — full progressive IGR applies
- Deductibles: maintenance, mortgage interest, ISI on the rented property, agency fees — per IGR rules
- **Tenant-side relief (Legge 141/2025)**: a fiscally-resident tenant using the dwelling as their *dimora effettiva* claims an IGR *detrazione* of **15% on 50% of the annual canone, up to a €6,000 canone ceiling** — renter relief, not a landlord deduction. (2026-07-02 verified, source HLB San Marino digest of Legge 141/2025.)

### Yields (est.)

- Long-term gross yield mid-tier apt 2026 ≈ **3–5%** (in line with Italian Adriatic-coast Rimini comparables; thin transaction sample → wide CI)
- Short-let gross yield (compliant, well-located): higher seasonal peaks (San Marino tourism: ~2M+ visitors/year; concentrated Apr–Oct + Christmas markets); but constrained by 2-property cap + small market

### Job platforms

- **Ufficio del Lavoro / Centro Servizi Lavoro**: state employment service — `https://www.lavoro.sm/`

### Work-permit gating

- **All non-Sammarinese employees** require a *permesso di soggiorno per lavoro* coordinated with employer; quotas set annually by Government decree
- **Frontalieri (cross-border, mainly Italian residents)**: large workforce — **~6,000–7,000 daily commuters from Italy**; cross-border employment governed by IT–SM bilateral framework (DTA + social-security agreement)
- **Self-employed (lavoro autonomo)**: registration with Camera di Commercio + ISS (social-security) + Ufficio Tributario; capital + business-plan requirements vary by activity

### Self-employment + entities

- **ISS (Istituto per la Sicurezza Sociale)**: `https://www.iss.sm/` — public health + social-security single regime
- **Forme societarie**: *S.r.l.* (Società a responsabilità limitata) min capital **~€25,500 est.** (verify at Camera di Commercio); *S.p.A.* min capital **~€77,000 est.** (verify at Camera di Commercio); corporate IGR **18% temporary for 2026–2030 (ordinary 17%)** — see Tax section
- **Lavoratore autonomo individuale**: simpler; ISS + IGR liability

### Salary benchmarks (2024–2025, est.)

- **Stipendio medio mensile lordo** (median monthly gross, all sectors): ~**€2,200–€2,800** est. (verify at `https://www.statistica.sm/` *Bollettino di Statistica*) — typically modestly below Italian Emilia-Romagna mean but with lower personal income tax up to mid-bracket
- **Banking / finance** (Banca di San Marino, Cassa di Risparmio della Repubblica di San Marino, Banca Sammarinese di Investimento, BSI): premium — €3,000–€6,000+/month senior
- **Pubblica Amministrazione**: by tariff schedule; competitive vs Italian comparables
- **Manifatturiero / industry**: well-developed niche industrial base (precision mechanics, ceramics, food processing); Italian-equivalent wages typical
- **Tourism / hospitality**: seasonal; €1,500–€2,200 typical
- **Italian-language competence is universal in the workforce**; strong Italian written + spoken is effectively a baseline requirement

### Cultural quirks

- Workforce structure: **~50% Sammarinese + ~50% Italian frontalieri** (with smaller other-EU and non-EU segments) — local labour-market dynamics are shaped by IT cross-border demand pressure on housing in Italian comuni adjacent to San Marino
- Industrial niches (precision mechanics, packaging machinery, consumer-goods manufacture) sustain higher-than-tourism-implied wages

### Specific risks

- **Earthquake (terremoto)** — **principal natural hazard**:
  - San Marino sits on the **Apennine seismotectonic margin** (foreland of the central Apennine chain) — moderate seismic hazard. Italian macro-zonation classes the surrounding ITA province of Rimini in **zona sismica 2** (medium-to-moderate); Pesaro-Urbino province ranges 2–3
  - Historic events: minor felt-tremors regular; significant nearby seismic events have occurred in central Italian Apennines (Norcia 2016, L'Aquila 2009 — felt but not damaging at SM)
  - INGV maintains the **RSM station** on SM territory — real-time monitoring streams via `http://terremoti.ingv.it/`
  - Modern construction (post-2003 ITA OPCM 3274 + post-2008 NTC 2008/2018 alignment via Sammarinese building regulations) is engineered to the hazard; **older masonry pre-1970** is the structural-risk concentration
- **Landslide / frana**: relevant for **mountain Castelli** (Città di San Marino, Faetano, Montegiardino) — Monte Titano + the Apennine ridge are prone to localised slope instability after intense rainfall + freeze-thaw cycles; verify parcel-level hazard zoning via UTCC + PRG layers
- **Flood (alluvione)**: limited but real — **Rio San Marino** + **Torrente Marano** + minor watercourses can flash-flood in extreme rainfall; lowland Castelli (Serravalle, Dogana) most exposed. Cross-border flood-risk context from Italian ISPRA + Regione Emilia-Romagna mapping
- **Wildfire**: low — limited wildland-urban interface; predominantly cultivated + residential terrain. Adjacent Apennine forest stands carry low-moderate summer fire risk during heatwaves
- **Snow loading + winter access**: occasional Apennine snow events; mountain Castelli (Città di San Marino at ~750m elevation) experience occasional persistent snow Dec–Mar — roof snow-load relevant for older heritage stock
- **Soil subsidence / freeze-thaw**: foundation + drainage detailing matters for pre-1980 stock with shallow footings

## Section: `--visa`

Universal logic per `shared/visa-programs.md`. San Marino-specific:

### Residenza Elettiva (passive residency, investor route)

- Foundational: **Legge 28 giugno 2010 n.118** + reforms via **Legge 6 dicembre 2017 n.94** + subsequent decreti delegati
- **Investment thresholds (Legge 94/2017)**:
  - **Real-estate route**: purchase of a property (or portion) for **≥ €500,000** to be used as own residence (with ancillary furnishing/renovation up to €150,000 includable in the calculation), OR
  - **Bond / fund route**: non-interest-bearing 10-year deposit of **≥ €600,000** in San Marino state bonds or in a fund established by the Camera
- Plus: clean criminal record (apostilled), proof of financial means, health-insurance coverage
- Permit: typically 1-year initial → renewals; **after 10 years from registration the residency is "consolidata"** (consolidated)
- **Residency obligation**: physical-presence threshold for tax-residency triggers separately (typically >183 days/year)
- **Annual quota**: maximum number of *residenze elettive* granted per year is set via decreto delegato — verify currency at `https://www.esteri.sm/pub1/EsteriSM/Servizi-per-utenza/Permessi-di-Soggiorno-e-Residenze.html`

### Residenza Atipica (atypical, retiree-pensioner)

- For EU / Switzerland / qualifying-country foreign citizens producing income from abroad; income parameters set by Government — verify currency at Esteri

### Residenza per Motivi Economici (entrepreneur / business)

- For investors subscribing **≥ 51% capital** in a Sammarinese-law company; **1–3 mandatory employees** depending on activity
- Deposit: **€75,000 first year, €150,000 from second year**, convertible to property purchase of equivalent value after third year (with State guarantee on the property)
- Verify currency at `https://www.esteri.sm/` + Camera di Commercio

### NOT a citizenship-by-investment jurisdiction

- ⚠️ **Sammarinese citizenship RESERVED**: naturalisation requires **30 years' continuous residence** (jus soli + jus sanguinis interplay; very long pathway). San Marino has never operated a CBI / golden-passport scheme
- **Dual nationality** is permitted (distinguishes San Marino from Andorra) but the very long naturalisation timeline limits practical value of citizenship as an investment-driven goal

### Confidence

HIGH for Legge 94/2017 + 173/2018 + 154/2019 framework (multi-year consolidated); MEDIUM-HIGH for current quantum (€500k property / €600k bond) and annual quota (decreto delegato adjusts) — *verify exact current quantum at* `https://www.esteri.sm/` *+ Decreto Delegato schedule before any application*.

## Section: `--notary`

### Process

- **Civil-law notary** (*notaio*) mandatory for all RE transfers — Sammarinese notarial profession is small (handful of practising notai); profession governed by *Legge sul notariato sammarinese*
- *Notaio* role: drafts the *atto pubblico di compravendita*, verifies identity (incl. apostilled passport for non-residents), confirms title via *certificato Conservatoria*, withholds and remits Imposta di Registro + Ipotecaria + Catastale + foreign-buyer concession tax, files the deed at Ufficio del Registro

### Standard timeline (typical residential resale)

| Step | Day | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1. *Proposta d'acquisto* / *compromesso* | D0 | Buyer pays **deposit ~10%**; preliminary contract drawn |
| 2. Foreign-buyer eligibility verification (Legge 173/2018) | D7–D30 | Notary collects + verifies clean criminal record + Legge 147/2005 list check |
| 3. Mortgage approval (if applicable) | D14–D45 | Bank valuation + offer letter |
| 4. UTCC visura + Conservatoria certificato | D30–D50 | Notary requests both pre-deed |
| 5. *Rogito* (deed signing) at notaio | D45–D75 | Buyer pays balance; notaio withholds Registro + Ipotecaria + Catastale + foreign-buyer concession tax |
| 6. Trascrizione at Ufficio del Registro | D75–D90 | Notaio files; title perfected |

⚠️ **Foreign-buyer eligibility gating**: under **Legge 173/2018** + DD 105/2019 + Legge 154/2019, the notary must verify:
1. **Clean criminal record (certificato penale generale)** from buyer's home jurisdiction — no convictions ≥2 years for non-cul­pable offences in last 15 years; no association/terrorism-financing convictions
2. **Property is not on Legge 147/2005 restricted list** (heritage / strategic constraints)
3. **2-property cap**: foreign individual can hold **max 2 residential units** SM-wide; non-commercial use only
4. **Clean Anti-Money-Laundering review** under BCSM AML rules

If any check fails, the deed is **null and void** — the notary's verification is gating.

### Costs

| Item | Typical |
|---|---|
| **Notaio fee** | ~1–2% of price (regulated arancel) |
| **Imposta di Registro** | ~3–6% (verify exact rate at Ufficio del Registro for the deal type) |
| **Imposta Ipotecaria + Catastale** | ~0.5–1.5% combined |
| **Tassa di Concessione Governativa** (foreign buyer) | €1,000 fixed admin fee (Art. 51 Legge 173/2018; 2026-05-27 verified) — NOT ad-valorem; verify current quantum at notary |
| **Imposta Monofase** (new build first sale by developer) | 17% in lieu of Registro |
| **Total transaction cost (buyer side, resident, resale)** | **~5–9%** of price |
| **Total transaction cost (buyer side, non-resident, resale)** | **~6–11%** of price |

### Foreign buyer practical

- **Power of attorney** acceptable: notaio accepts apostilled PoA for absent buyers
- **Source-of-funds**: notaio + bank both apply AML/CRS reviews
- **Beneficial-ownership disclosure**: corporate buyers register UBO with Sammarinese commercial registry (Camera di Commercio)
- **Italian-language closing documents**: *atto pubblico* drafted in Italian; non-Italian-speaking buyers should engage a sworn translator + Sammarinese *commercialista* / lawyer for review

Workable pathways: (1) **Residenza Elettiva** (€500k property / €600k bond) + foreign-employer remote work — gray area (verify with Esteri); (2) **Residenza per Motivi Economici** + own-SM-company route — clean but full IGR + ISS + Camera di Commercio exposure; (3) Visitor entry (de facto open via Italian Schengen perimeter) up to 90/180 days. ⚠️ Sustained "working from San Marino" without permit case-by-case risk

- **Co-working**: small + emerging market; cross-border Rimini coworking accessible (~25 km)

### Verdict for digital-nomad-first buyers

🟡 **Watch** — strong Italian-ecosystem connectivity + lower CoL than Monaco/Andorra, but tight visa framework (no DNV, gray area sub-residency). Best fit: HN remote-workers committing to Residenza Elettiva (€500k+) or company route.

## Foreign-buyer rules — Legge 173/2018 framework

### What changed in 2018

Pre-2018, all non-citizen acquisitions required **prior authorisation of the Consiglio dei XII** (parliamentary commission) — slow, discretionary, restrictive.

**Legge 24 dicembre 2018 n.173** abolished prior authorisation; replaced with **notary-verified eligibility + automatic right to acquire**. Implementing details in **Decreto Delegato 105/2019** + confirming **Legge 3 ottobre 2019 n.154**.

### Eligibility framework (current, 2026)

| Buyer | Status |
|---|---|
| **Sammarinese citizens** | Free to acquire — no special procedure |
| **Foreign individuals (any nationality)** | Eligible **IF**: (a) clean criminal record (no convictions ≥2y for non-cul­pable offences in last 15y; no organised-crime / terrorism-financing convictions); (b) property is not on Legge 147/2005 restricted list; (c) max 2 residential units; (d) non-commercial use only |
| **Sammarinese-law legal persons (companies)** | Eligible — productive / business use; per DD 105/2019 schedule |

### Practical implication

- **No quota, no preapproval** under the new regime — replaced by notary-verified checks
- **2-property cap per foreign individual** is the binding structural constraint for foreign portfolio strategies
- **Tassa di Concessione Governativa** is the explicit foreign-buyer fee replacing the old authorisation regime
- **Verification path**: notary collects + verifies all eligibility documents pre-rogito; failure = deed null

⚠️ **Pre-existing pre-2018 acquisitions** (made under the old Consiglio dei XII regime) were preserved by **Legge 154/2019** (saved-acquisitions clause) — retroactive validity confirmed.

- **Foreign-buyer 2-property cap** under Legge 173/2018 — binding structural constraint

- **Currency = EUR by Monetary Agreement (2001/2012)**, NOT Eurozone member
- **NOT in Schengen** (open ITA border de facto; Schengen-association negotiations authorised 2024)

- **No CBI / golden-passport**, **30-year naturalisation**, **dual nationality permitted**
- **Single integrated state utility (AASS)** — water, electricity, gas, sewer all from one operator
- **Italian-language closing documents** — non-Italian-speaking buyers need sworn translator + commercialista
- **No standalone HPI / OMI-equivalent index** — cross-reference adjacent ITA Rimini-province OMI for benchmarking
- **APE mandatory at sale** — aligned with EU EPBD
- **ISI primary residence exemption** structural; second-home and non-resident-held stock taxed
- **IGR top 35% (residents) — Legge 141/2025 reform effective 2026**; less competitive than MC/AD but materially below ITA IRPEF 43%
- **Small notarial profession** — handful of practising notai; pre-book at deal-stage
