# Oman 🇴🇲 — Property Due-Diligence Playbook

ISO2: `om`. Status: ✅ Fully populated (researched 2026-04 / verified 2026-05-01).

> **Critical framing**: Oman is a **strictly foreign-buyer-restricted market**. Non-GCC nationals can only acquire freehold real estate inside designated **Integrated Tourism Complexes (ITCs)** under Royal Decree 12/2006 (as amended). Outside an ITC, only **GCC nationals** can register a freehold title; non-GCC foreigners cannot acquire any registered residential interest. **Agricultural land is closed to foreigners full-stop.** Oman has **NO recurring property tax**, **NO personal income tax**, **NO capital gains tax for individuals**, and a 5% VAT regime (since Apr 2021). Currency risk vs USD is structurally zero — OMR is pegged at **USD 1 = OMR 0.3850 (i.e. 1 OMR ≈ USD 2.60) since 1986** by the Central Bank of Oman. Confirm ITC status with the Ministry of Housing & Urban Planning Land Registry **before** signing any reservation form.

## Country profile

- **Postal code system**: **3-digit numeric postal codes** assigned by Oman Post (`https://www.omanpost.om/`) — e.g., **Muscat 100**, **Seeb 111**, **Salalah 211**, **Sohar 311**, **Nizwa 611**. Postal codes identify post-office collection areas, not building-level addresses.
- **Parcel-level identifier**: **Plot Number + Block + Sector** on Title Deed (Sanad Mulkiyya — سند ملكية), issued by the Ministry of Housing & Urban Planning (MHUP) Land Registry Department.
- **Admin levels**: Sultanate → **11 Governorates** (Muscat, Dhofar, Musandam, Al Buraimi, Ad Dakhiliyah, Al Batinah North, Al Batinah South, Ash Sharqiyah North, Ash Sharqiyah South, Ad Dhahirah, Al Wusta) → **wilayats** (~60+) → villages.
- **Currency**: OMR (Omani Rial). **Pegged USD 1 = OMR 0.3850** since 1986 — Central Bank of Oman (CBO) maintains a fixed peg via direct intervention; one of the highest nominal-value currencies in the world (1 OMR ≈ USD 2.60).
- **Languages**: **Arabic** (official; Title Deeds, court documents, all formal contracts in Arabic). **English** is the de-facto language of business, real estate marketing, healthcare, and tourism — most ITC marketing material is bilingual EN/AR but Arabic prevails legally.
- **Cadastre / land registry**:
  - **Ministry of Housing & Urban Planning (MHUP)** — `https://gov.om/en/ministry-of-housing-and-urban-planning` — Land Registry Department issues Title Deeds (Sakk al-Mulkiyya / سند ملكية) and administers the Royal Decree 12/2006 ITC framework.
  - **National Survey Authority (NSA)** — under MHUP; maintains parcel maps, cadastral surveys, and the national geospatial register.
  - **Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MoCIIP)** — `https://gov.om/en/ministry-of-commerce-industry-and-investment-promotion` — broker licensing, foreign-investment approvals, ITC concession agreements.
- **Identifier(s)**:
  - **Title Deed (Sanad Mulkiyya — سند ملكية)** — paper + electronic; bears Plot No. + Block + Sector + Wilayat + Governorate.
  - **Sale and Purchase Agreement (SPA)** — pre-registration binding contract; off-plan sales typically use a developer Reservation Agreement → SPA → MHUP registration on handover.
  - **MHUP Property Registration Certificate** — issued at transfer; supersedes the prior Title Deed.
- **Population**: **~5.0 million** (2024 — National Centre for Statistics and Information, NCSI `https://ncsi.gov.om/`); ~62% Omani nationals + ~38% expatriates (largest expat communities: Indian, Bangladeshi, Pakistani, Egyptian, Filipino).
- **Recent reforms** (12-month watchlist):
  - **Royal Decree 89/2021 — Investor Residency Programme** — formalised 5-yr (≥OMR 250,000) and 10-yr (≥OMR 500,000) renewable residency tied to qualifying investment incl. ITC freehold property; major attractor for non-GCC foreign buyers since launch.
  - **Royal Decree 12/2006 (as amended)** — primary statute for non-GCC freehold ownership in ITCs; subsequent ministerial decisions expanded the ITC list (notably 2010, 2018, 2023–2024 designations).
  - **VAT introduced 16 April 2021** at **5%** standard rate (Royal Decree 121/2020 + Oman Tax Authority Executive Regulations). **First-sale residential by VAT-registered developer: 5% standard-rated** (NOT zero-rated, unlike UAE); resale residential + residential rental exempt; bare land exempt; commercial standard-rated 5% (2026-05-27 verified, source Oman Tax Authority VAT Guide for Real Estate + Deloitte/PwC/Crowe Oman).
  - **Royal Decree 56/2025 — Personal Income Tax Law** issued **22 Jun 2025**, **effective 1 January 2028**: **5% PIT on annual income > OMR 42,000** (first PIT in the GCC). Deductions/exemptions for education, healthcare, inheritance, zakat, donations, primary housing; OTA expects ~99% of population NOT subject (high threshold). Executive Regulations on real-estate-rental treatment pending (2026-05-27 verified, source Oman Tax Authority + EY + Deloitte + KPMG + DLA Piper June 2025 coverage).
  - **Oman Vision 2040** — long-term economic diversification framework; tourism + ITC investment is a named pillar.
  - **Excise Tax 2019** — 50–100% on tobacco/energy drinks/alcohol/sugary drinks; separate from VAT.
  - **Income Tax (corporate)** — Royal Decree 28/2009 (Income Tax Law); 15% standard CIT for most companies above OMR 30,000 threshold. **NO personal income tax** as of 2026-05 (a draft PIT framework has been discussed multiple times since 2022 — verify current status at Oman Tax Authority).
  - **ITC list expansion 2023–2024** — additional integrated tourism complexes designated by MHUP/MoCIIP (verify current MHUP designated-zone list before reservation).

## 🇴🇲 Foreign buyer eligibility (READ FIRST)

This section determines whether a transaction is even legally possible. Confirm ITC status BEFORE assessing price.

### Legal basis

- **Royal Decree 12/2006** "Permitting Non-Omanis to Own Real Estate in Integrated Tourism Complexes" — primary statute; subsequently amended by ministerial decisions and Royal Decrees expanding the ITC roster.
- **Royal Decree 29/2018** (Foreign Capital Investment Law) — broader foreign-investment framework administered by MoCIIP; allows 100% foreign ownership of business activity in many sectors (separate from real estate).
- **Royal Decree 89/2021 — Investor Residency Programme** — links qualifying real-estate investment to renewable residency.
- Administered jointly by **MHUP Land Registry** + **MoCIIP** + **Royal Oman Police Directorate of Civil Status & Passports** (residency permits).

### Tier 1 — Integrated Tourism Complexes (ITCs) — non-GCC freehold permitted

Per Royal Decree 12/2006 (as amended) — verify current designated list at **MHUP** before reservation. Established ITCs include:

| ITC | Governorate | Master developer | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Wave Muscat** (now **Al Mouj Muscat**) | Muscat | Omran + Al Mouj Muscat SAOG | Flagship ITC since 2006; freehold villas + apartments + marina |
| **Muscat Hills** | Muscat | Muscat Hills Resort | Golf-resort ITC; freehold villas + apartments |
| **Hawana Salalah** | Dhofar | Muriya / Orascom Development | Coastal ITC in Salalah; villas + apartments + hotel zone |
| **Jebel Sifah** | Muscat (south coast) | Muriya / Orascom | Marina + golf coastal ITC ~30 min from Muscat |
| **Saraya Bandar Jissah** | Muscat (east coast) | Saraya Oman | Premium coastal ITC; villas + apartments |

(Sources: MHUP designated-zone register, ITC master-developer corporate filings — *verify the current full list at `https://gov.om/en/ministry-of-housing-and-urban-planning` ITC lookup before reservation; additional ITCs designated 2023–2024 may not yet appear on every public list*.)

### Tier 2 — GCC nationals (broader access)

- **GCC nationals** (Bahrain, Kuwait, KSA, Qatar, UAE) can buy freehold residential property **across most of Oman** (not just ITCs) per GCC reciprocity protocols and Royal Decree provisions.
- Subject to limits per family/individual and certain sensitive zones (border areas, military buffers, Musandam exclave).

### Tier 3 — Outside ITC (non-GCC foreigners)

- **Non-GCC foreigners CANNOT acquire freehold or registered leasehold residential property outside designated ITCs.**
- **Long-term lease** of residential property (up to 50 years renewable for usufruct of land/buildings, per Civil Code) is theoretically available in some cases but rarely used for non-GCC residential investment — verify with MoCIIP.
- **Commercial / industrial property**: foreign-owned companies may acquire under specific licences via Foreign Capital Investment Law 29/2018 — case-by-case MoCIIP review.
- **Agricultural land**: **strictly prohibited** to non-GCC foreign ownership (no exceptions under current law).

### Visa via property — Investor Residency Programme (Royal Decree 89/2021)

| Item | Threshold | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| **5-year Investor Residency** | Investment ≥ **OMR 250,000** (~USD 650,000) — qualifying assets include ITC freehold real estate, business equity, or government bonds | MoCIIP + Royal Oman Police (ROP) |
| **10-year Investor Residency** | Investment ≥ **OMR 500,000** (~USD 1.3M) — same qualifying-asset categories | MoCIIP + ROP |
| Validity | Renewable while qualifying asset is maintained | ROP |
| Family inclusion | Spouse + children + parents (subject to current ROP guidance) | ROP |
| **Long-term Retiree Residency** | Stable foreign pension ≥ **OMR 4,000 / month** (~USD 10,400) + clean record + private health insurance | ROP |
| **Citizenship by investment** | **NONE** — Omani nationality is governed by Royal Decree (Nationality Law) with extremely narrow naturalisation pathway (typically 20 years residence + Arabic + service to Oman); **not available to foreign property buyers** | ROP Naturalisation |

(Sources: Royal Decree 89/2021 Investor Residency; MoCIIP investor-residency portal; ROP Civil Status & Passports — *verify current OMR thresholds and qualifying-asset definitions at `https://gov.om/en/ministry-of-commerce-industry-and-investment-promotion`*)

### Compliance / AML

- **Central Bank of Oman (CBO)** AML/CFT regime — Royal Decree 30/2016 + 2020 amendments — designates real-estate sector as a reporting entity.
- **Financial Intelligence Unit (NCFI — National Centre for Financial Information)** under ROP — receives suspicious-transaction reports; FATF MENAFATF assessments cover Omani real estate.
- **Source-of-funds documentation** required at MHUP registration: bank statements, employment contract or pension certificate, prior asset-sale evidence.
- Cash transactions are tightly scrutinised; large purchases typically settled by bank transfer through CBO-licensed banks.

### Verification path

1. Confirm property is inside a **designated Integrated Tourism Complex** (MHUP lookup) — non-negotiable for non-GCC buyers.
2. Pull seller's current **Title Deed (Sakk al-Mulkiyya)** from MHUP — verify ownership + encumbrances + service-charge clearance.
3. If off-plan → verify the master developer's ITC concession is current and the project is registered with MHUP/MoCIIP.
4. Confirm AML source-of-funds documentation is ready before signing SPA.
5. Investor Residency application can run in parallel post-registration via MoCIIP.

## Section: `--tax`

### Annual property tax

**There is NO annual real-estate property tax in Oman** — neither at central nor governorate level. **This is consistent with the GCC norm** (UAE has municipal housing fees; Saudi has White Land + Vacant Property Fees for undeveloped land ≥5,000 m²; Bahrain has property tax for non-GCC; Oman has none).

What residents do pay annually:

| Item | Rate / amount | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| **Annual property tax** | **NONE** | — |
| **Municipal services** (waste, road maintenance) | Funded from general taxation; no per-property line | Ministry of Local Affairs / Muscat Municipality |
| **Service charges** (ITC / compound / tower OA) | **OMR 4–18 / sqm / yr** depending on ITC tier | Master developer / OA — Al Mouj OMC, Muriya, Saraya |
| **District cooling / chiller** (where applicable) | Capacity charge + consumption — typical ~OMR 6–14 / sqm / yr in ITC apartments | District-cooling concessionaire (Tabreed Oman or developer subsidiary) |
| **Electricity + water** | See `--mains` | Nama Group (Nama Electricity, Nama Water) — successor to OPWP/Mazoon |

(Sources: Al Mouj OMC + Muriya OA fee schedules; Tabreed Oman tariffs — *verify current rates with each ITC management*.)

**Total transaction cost (buyer side, cash)**: **~4–5% of price** (3% MHUP + ~2% agent + lawyer + admin) — among the **lowest in the world** for foreign buyers.

### Personal income tax — 0% through 31 Dec 2027; 5% from 1 Jan 2028 (Royal Decree 56/2025)

- **0% PIT through 31 Dec 2027.**
- **Royal Decree 56/2025** issued **22 June 2025** enacts the **Personal Income Tax Law — first PIT in the GCC** — **5% rate on annual income > OMR 42,000, effective 1 January 2028**. Includes deductions/exemptions for education, healthcare, inheritance, zakat, donations, primary housing; OTA expects ~99% of population NOT subject (high threshold). Executive Regulations on real-estate-rental treatment pending as of May 2026 (2026-05-27 verified, source Oman Tax Authority `https://tms.taxoman.gov.om/portal/w/issuance-of-personal-income-tax-pit-law` + EY + Deloitte + KPMG + DLA Piper June 2025 alerts).
- **NO capital gains tax** on individual property sales (own-name natural person).
- **NO inheritance tax** (federal); Sharia inheritance applies as default for Muslim Omanis and is the practical outcome for Muslim foreign residents in Omani courts.

### Corporate income tax — 15% (when relevant)

- **Standard CIT**: 15% on taxable profit above OMR 30,000 (Royal Decree 28/2009 Income Tax Law + 2017 amendment).
- **SME concessional rate**: 3% for qualifying small enterprises under specific turnover/employment thresholds.
- **Withholding tax**: 10% on interest, royalties, technical/management fees paid to non-residents (subject to DTA relief).
- **Property held in an Omani company**: 15% CIT on rental + capital gain attributable to the company.

(Source: Oman Tax Authority — `https://tms.taxoman.gov.om/`; Royal Decree 28/2009 + Royal Decree 9/2017 amendment.)

### Inheritance

- **No inheritance tax** in Oman.
- **Sharia inheritance** applies as default for Muslim Omanis and is typically applied by Omani courts to Muslim foreign residents' Omani assets.
- For non-Muslim foreign owners, **immovable property in Oman is governed by Omani law** (lex situs); Omani courts may apply home-country succession to movable estate but not to land.
- **Practical recommendation**: non-Muslim foreign ITC owners should hold property via a clear cross-border estate plan; consult an Omani-licensed lawyer + home-jurisdiction counsel. There is **no Omani equivalent of DIFC/ADGM Wills** as of 2026-05.

### VAT — implemented 16 April 2021 at 5%

- **Royal Decree 121/2020** + Oman Tax Authority Executive Regulations introduced VAT at 5% standard rate.
- **First-sale residential** by VAT-registered developer: **5% standard-rated** (NOT zero-rated; differs from UAE which zero-rates first-sale residential within 3 years of completion) (2026-05-27 verified, source Oman Tax Authority VAT Guide for Real Estate).
- **Subsequent sale + residential rental**: **exempt** (input-VAT recovery limits).
- **Bare/undeveloped land**: exempt.
- **Commercial real estate**: 5% standard-rated.
- **STR (short-term rental)**: 5% applies to vacation rentals if total taxable supplies exceed the OMR 38,500/yr mandatory registration threshold.
- **Excise Tax** (separate; since 2019): 50–100% on tobacco, energy drinks, alcohol, pork, sugary drinks.

### Future risk

- **PIT introduction**: Oman has flagged a draft PIT framework multiple times (potentially affecting high-income individuals only); timing uncertain — *verify Oman Tax Authority bulletins*.
- **Property-tax introduction**: no specific announcements as of 2026-05; politically improbable short-term but tracked.

## Section: `--rental`

### Long-term residential

- **Tenancy law basis**: **Royal Decree 6/89** (Real Estate Lease Law) and amendments — landlord/tenant rights, eviction grounds.
- **Mandatory tenancy registration**: Tenancy contracts must be registered at the **Muscat Municipality** (or the relevant municipality) — required for utility connections, residence-permit renewals, and school enrolments.
- **Rent increase cap**: Rent increases are negotiable contract-by-contract; statutory rent control is limited; landlord must serve notice prior to renewal.
- **Eviction**: limited grounds — non-payment, owner self-occupation, unauthorised sub-letting. Disputes routed to **Muscat Municipality Rent Dispute Committee** (or the relevant governorate municipality) and ultimately to the courts.

(Source: Royal Decree 6/89 + amendments; Muscat Municipality Rent Disputes — *verify current rent-cap formula at MHUP / Muscat Municipality*.)

### Short-term rentals (holiday homes)

- **Authority**: **Ministry of Heritage and Tourism (MHT)** — `https://mht.gov.om/` — issues short-term rental licences for holiday homes; ITCs typically have streamlined approval pathways under their master-developer concession. **NOTE: MHT is `mht.gov.om` — `mohup.gov.om` is the separate Ministry of Housing & Urban Planning (MoHUP); do NOT conflate** (2026-05-27 verified).
- **MHT Holiday Home licence** required for STR < 30 days; building/community OA approval also required.
- **Al Mouj, Hawana Salalah, Jebel Sifah, Muscat Hills** generally permit licensed STR via the master OA — verify the specific tower/villa category before buying for STR.
- **Tourism levy + tourism dirham equivalent**: a tourism levy (typically 4% on tourist accommodation revenue) applies; verify at MHT.
- **VAT 5%** applies to STR if the operator's taxable supplies exceed the OMR 38,500/yr registration threshold.
- **Penalties**: fines for unlicensed STR operation per MHT enforcement bulletins.

(Source: MHT licensing pages — *verify current STR licensing pathway at `https://mht.gov.om/`*.)

### Strategic notes

- **Service charge + cooling = 20–35% of gross yield** in many high-end ITC apartments — model NET yield, not gross.
- **MHT Holiday Home licence + master-OA approval** are the two STR gates — confirm BEFORE buying if STR is the strategy.
- **Hawana Salalah seasonality**: khareef monsoon Jul–Sep drives most annual STR revenue; off-season occupancy is thin — model a 4-month-peak revenue concentration carefully.
- **PIT = 0%** federally — but US/UK/EU tax residents will face their own home-country tax on Omani rental income (Oman's DTA network covers ~35 countries including UK, India, Pakistan, Singapore — verify your nationality at OTA).
- **Tenant pool**: heavily expatriate (38% of population) for long-let in Muscat; mostly tourist for ITC short-let. Long-let tenancies often 1-year contracts tied to employment.

Oman urban property is essentially **always on mains** for water + electricity + sewerage; ITC apartment blocks usually include district cooling.

| Service | Provider |
|---|---|
| **Electricity** | **Nama Electricity Distribution** (formerly Mazoon Electricity, Majan Electricity, Muscat Electricity Distribution — consolidated under **Nama Group** since 2020) — `https://www.nama.om/` |

### Verification

1. Pull existing **Nama Electricity / Nama Water account info** from seller — confirms active mains + no overdue.
2. Off-plan: confirm developer has utility connection on the master plan (verify NOC at handover).
3. Al Mouj + Hawana use **district cooling** — separate bill on top of Nama.
4. Some ITC compounds use **TSE (treated sewage effluent)** for landscape irrigation — separate cost line in OA budget.

## Section: `--currency`

### Omani Rial (OMR) — peg framework

- **Peg**: USD 1 = OMR 0.3850 (i.e. 1 OMR ≈ USD 2.60), fixed since **1986** (Central Bank of Oman Monetary Policy).
- **Peg type**: hard peg with active intervention by CBO via FX reserves; ~6+ months of import cover.
- **Mechanism**: CBO buys/sells OMR vs USD at fixed parity; interbank OMR/USD trades within ±0.0005 typically.
- **Inflation transmission**: Omani monetary conditions de facto track US Fed via peg; OMR rates move in lockstep with USD rates (CBO repo rate tracks Fed Funds Rate).

### What this means for buyers

- **USD-denominated buyers**: **near-zero FX risk** for purchase + ongoing payments.
- **EUR / GBP / JPY buyers**: OMR effectively a USD-tracking currency; FX exposure = home-vs-USD exposure.
- **GCC buyers** (AED-pegged-USD, BHD-pegged-USD, SAR-pegged-USD, QAR-pegged-USD, KWD-basket): near-zero FX risk.

### Peg credibility

CBO's USD foreign reserves cover 6+ months of imports; supported by **hydrocarbon current-account surplus** (Oman 2023–2025 oil price-driven surplus) + sovereign rating (Moody's Ba1 stable, S&P BB+ positive, Fitch BB+ positive — all upgraded 2023–2024 on fiscal consolidation). Peg defended successfully through 1986–2026, including 2014–2016 oil-price crash and 2020 COVID + oil-war shock. **No serious near-term peg-break risk** assessed by IMF Article IV 2024.

⚠️ **Tail risk**: any sustained peg-breakdown would re-rate OMR-denominated property substantially in foreign-currency terms. Position-size with this in mind for foreign-currency-denominated buyers. Periodic academic + market debate on long-term sustainability — not a near-term break risk.

### Capital controls

- **No restrictions on outbound FX** for residents + non-residents.
- **AML / source-of-funds** requirements stringent: CBO + OTA + ROP-NCFI require declaration for transfers above thresholds — under AML/CFT Royal Decree 30/2016 + 2020 amendments.
- **Real-estate transactions** require source-of-funds documentation — enforced at MHUP registration.

## Section: `--notary`

### Conveyancing process

Oman uses a **civil-law notary system**: the **Public Notary** under the **Ministry of Justice and Legal Affairs (MoJLA)** — `https://www.mjla.gov.om/` — authenticates real-estate contracts; the **MHUP Land Registry** is the registering authority that issues the new Sakk al-Mulkiyya in the buyer's name.

**Modern practice (2020+)**: most transfers now use the **MHUP digital portal** for booking + part of the workflow; in-person notary attendance still standard for execution.

### Standard timeline (typical Omani/GCC buyer + seller)

| Step | Day | What happens |

| 1. Reservation / preliminary agreement | D0 | Initial deposit (typically 10% of price) to seller; non-binding pending due diligence |

| 5. MHUP 3% registration fee + utility transfers | D30–D60 | Land registry fee paid; Nama Electricity / Water account transferred |
| 6. Title transfer recorded | D60–D90 | MHUP electronic record updated |

## Section: `--retirement`

### Retirement to Oman scenario

Oman is **emerging as a GCC retirement destination** — quieter than Dubai, lower cost than Qatar, with strong cultural depth (UNESCO sites, Sultan Qaboos Grand Mosque, Nizwa Fort, Salalah khareef) and ITC infrastructure designed for residential lifestyle.

### Long-term Retiree Residency

- **Pension threshold**: OMR 4,000/month (~USD 10,400) — high relative to UAE (~AED 180k/yr or USD 49k/yr) and Portugal D7 (~EUR 760/month).
- **Other requirements**: clean criminal record + private health insurance + valid passport.
- **Typical retiree pathway**: combine Long-term Retiree Residency + ITC freehold + Investor Residency where applicable.

### Cost-of-living for retirees (2026)

- 2-bed apt rental Muscat outside ITC: OMR 350–700/month (~USD 910–1,820).
- 2-bed apt rental inside Al Mouj / Muscat Hills: OMR 600–1,200/month (~USD 1,560–3,120).
- Public healthcare: heavily subsidised for Omani nationals; expat retirees need **private health insurance** OMR 400–1,500/yr depending on cover.
- Premium private healthcare: OMR 1,500–4,000/yr depending on cover.
- Utilities: OMR 30–80/month (Nama + cooling).
- Eating out: mid-range OMR 5–15/meal; high-end OMR 25+.
- Alcohol: licensed in hotels + ITC restaurants + dedicated liquor stores (residents need a liquor permit) — more permissive than Saudi but more restrictive than UAE.
- **Climate**: Muscat winter pleasant (18–28°C); summer extreme (35–45°C); Salalah cool year-round + khareef monsoon Jul–Sep is unique GCC microclimate.

### Tax position for retirees

- **No PIT, no CGT, no estate/inheritance tax** for individuals.
- **No tax on foreign pension income** received in Oman.
- **3% MHUP fee** on real-estate disposal (statutory; transaction-side).
- **5% VAT** on most consumption (healthcare exempt; some food zero-rated).

### Healthcare access

- **Public** (Ministry of Health): heavily subsidised for Omani nationals; Royal Hospital + SQU Hospital are flagship tertiary centres.
- **Private**: world-class in Muscat (Burjeel, Aster Al Raffah, NMC, KIMS, Muscat Private Hospital).
- Long-term Retiree Residency + Dhamani-style private health insurance covers most retiree needs.

### Verdict for retirement-first buyers

🟢 **Go** for high-net-worth retirees seeking GCC tax efficiency + cyclone-aware ITC lifestyle + cultural depth (ideal for Salalah or Al Mouj). **Watch** for retirees on lower fixed-income pensions (OMR 4,000/month threshold is high). 🟠 **Adapt** for retirees prioritising cosmopolitan urban density (UAE Dubai + Bahrain Manama easier cultural fit; Oman is quieter, more conservative, and less English-language-dominant outside ITCs).

## Section: `--digital-nomad`

### Visa pathway

Oman has **NO formal "digital nomad visa"** comparable to Portugal D8 / UAE Green Visa freelancer track.

Workable pathways for remote-working visitors:

1. **Tourist eVisa** (30 days/visit, multi-entry options) — ⚠️ work technically prohibited; short remote-stay tolerated.
2. **Investor Residency** — for genuine high-income freelancers / entrepreneurs (OMR 250k+ threshold; high bar).
3. **Standard Employment Visa via local employer** — requires Omani entity sponsorship.
4. **Family Visa** via working family member.

### Connectivity

- **Internet**: **fiber FTTH widespread** in Muscat + Salalah + Sohar; gigabit available; 5G saturation good in Muscat (Omantel + Ooredoo + Vodafone Oman 5G operational since 2019).
- **Mobile**: OMR 10–25/month for 5G unlimited (Omantel, Ooredoo, Vodafone); eSIM widely supported.
- **Co-working**: Hubitat, The Studio Oman, Smart City Oman, Outwork (multiple Muscat locations); OMR 80–250/month hot desk.
- **Time zone**: UTC+4 — favours EU + Asia overlap; 8-hour gap to US East Coast.

### Cost-of-living for remote worker (Q1 2026)

- Studio apt rental Muscat: OMR 250–500/month.
- Shared compound (gated + amenities): OMR 350–800/month 1-bed.

- Single-meal eating-out: OMR 4–10 mid-range / OMR 20+ premium.
- Monthly transport: Otaxi/Careem dominant; OMR 50–150/month typical.

### Tax position

- **No PIT** for individuals — major pull factor for high-income freelancers.
- **5% VAT** on services + consumption.

- **No specific digital-nomad tax incentive** — but the absence of PIT IS the incentive.

### Verdict for digital-nomad-first buyers

🟡 **Watch** — Oman is favourable for established remote workers in tech / consulting / hospitality / Vision-2040 orbits, especially those valuing quieter pace + outdoor lifestyle (Wadi Shab, Jebel Akhdar, Salalah khareef). **Cultural friction lower than Saudi but higher than UAE** for the "freelance backpacker" archetype; Al Mouj + Hawana are international-standard tolerance zones. **No formal nomad visa** = practical pathway is short-stay tourist eVisa loops or ITC ownership with Investor Residency.

## Caveats unique to Oman

- **ITC-only foreign-buyer constraint** — non-GCC outside designated Integrated Tourism Complexes cannot acquire any registered residential interest; verify ITC status before any reservation. **This is more restrictive than UAE freehold zones** (which cover broad city districts).
- **Agricultural land BANNED to non-GCC** — no exceptions under current law.
- **Royal Decree 89/2021 Investor Residency** — major attractor for ITC buyers; combine ITC freehold + 5/10-yr residency.
- **Citizenship by investment is NOT available** — Omani nationality is governed by Royal Decree with extremely narrow naturalisation pathway; "buy property → citizenship" pitch is not legal in Oman.
- **NO recurring property tax + NO PIT + NO CGT** — among the world's most tax-favourable regimes.
- **VAT 5% from Apr 2021** — first-sale residential 5% standard-rated (NOT zero-rated, unlike UAE); resale residential + residential rental exempt; bare land exempt; commercial + STR standard-rated 5% (2026-05-27 verified).
- **Cyclone exposure materially higher than UAE/KSA/Qatar** — Cyclones Gonu 2007, Mekunu 2018, Shaheen 2021 baseline; verify drainage history + ITC seawall design + post-Shaheen upgrade coverage.
- **OMR pegged USD at 0.3850 since 1986** — zero FX risk for USD asset holders; meaningful FX risk vs EUR / GBP / floating currencies.
- **Smaller market depth than UAE/KSA** — secondary-market liquidity thinner; position ITC investments as lifestyle/retirement plays rather than pure rental yield.
- **Hawana Salalah is highly seasonal** (khareef monsoon Jul–Sep drives most annual STR demand — est.; verify occupancy with master OA) — annualised yield ≠ peak-season yield.
- **Sharia inheritance default for Muslim residents**; non-Muslim foreign owners should structure ownership/estate plan with Omani-licensed counsel; **no DIFC/ADGM-equivalent will framework** exists in Oman.
- **AML source-of-funds documentation required** at MHUP registration under Royal Decree 30/2016.
- **Postal codes are 3-digit area codes** (Muscat 100, Salalah 211); not building-level — parcel ID is Plot + Block + Sector on Title Deed.
- **No metro/rail in 2026** — car-dependent urbanism; Mwasalat bus + private + Otaxi/Careem dominate.
- **Alcohol licensed but restricted** — sold in licensed hotels/restaurants/ITC venues + dedicated liquor stores (residents need a liquor permit); more restrictive than UAE, more permissive than Saudi.
- **Tsunami background risk** from Makran Subduction Zone — National Tsunami Warning Centre operational since 2008; coastal-front parcels should verify evacuation plan.
- **Foreign-currency mortgages limited** — assume cash purchase is the default for non-GCC foreign buyers unless specifically arranged with a CBO-licensed bank.
