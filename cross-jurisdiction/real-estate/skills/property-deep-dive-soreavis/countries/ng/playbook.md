# Nigeria — Property Due-Diligence Playbook

ISO2: `ng`. Status: PARTIALLY OPEN with state-level fragmentation (Land Use Act 1978: leasehold-only via Certificate of Occupancy from State Governor) + currency-volatile (NGN floated Jun 2023, ~70%+ devaluation 2023-2024) + security-elevated (regional advisories vary by state). Researched 2026-04 / 2026-05.

## Country profile

- **Population**: ~227M (2024 NBS estimate / UN WPP projection from 2006 Census base; source `https://www.nigerianstat.gov.ng/`). Africa's most populous country. Lagos State ~22M (largest African city); Kano ~5M; Ibadan ~4M; FCT Abuja ~3M; Port Harcourt ~3M.
- **Postcode**: 6 digits via NIPOST (`https://www.nipost.gov.ng/`) — e.g., `101241` VI Lagos, `900288` Asokoro Abuja, `700240` Kano. Often omitted on listings.
- **Admin levels**: 36 **States** + 1 **FCT Abuja** → 774 **LGAs**. Property law + cadastre + key transaction taxes are STATE-LEVEL (LSLB, AGIS, etc.); federal layer = Stamp Duty, CGT, VAT, FX.
- **Currency**: **NGN — Nigerian Naira** (₦). **Reform Jun 2023**: CBN unified dual exchange-rate system at "willing buyer/willing seller"; managed-float; ~NGN 460/USD (early 2023) → ~750 (Jun 2023 merger) → ~1,650+/USD peak 2024 → **~NGN 1,370–1,400/USD May 2026** (CBN official; black-market spread ~₦20–25; naira **appreciated** materially from 2024 peak — 2026-05-27 verified). Verify daily at `https://www.cbn.gov.ng/`. **USD informal pricing dominates prime Lagos/Abuja RE** as hedge — flag in underwriting.
- **Languages**: English (official); Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa major regional; Pidgin universal. Conveyancing is English common-law.
- **Legal system**: Common-law (federal + state) + Sharia (personal-status only, 12 northern states) + customary in non-Sharia states. Property = common-law + **Land Use Act 1978** (overrides freehold).
- **Cadastre — STATE-LEVEL**:
  - **Lagos**: e-LandTitle Portal via **LSLB** (`https://landsbureau.lagosstate.gov.ng/`)
  - **FCT Abuja**: **AGIS** via FCDA (`https://agis.fcta.gov.ng/`)
  - Other 35 states: paper-based + partial digitization (Kaduna, Ogun, Rivers piloting)
  - **NO national cadastre** — single largest title-DD challenge for cross-state portfolios
- **Identifier**: **C of O Number** + **Survey Plan Number** + **Plot Number** + state Registration Number
- **Recent reforms** (verify date-stamped):
  - **Tinubu Administration since May 2023** — fuel subsidy removal, naira float, tax-reform agenda
  - **CBN Naira Float Jun 2023** + sequential FX-policy adjustments 2024-26
  - **Finance Acts 2019/2020/2021/2023** — VAT 7.5% (Finance Act 2019), Stamp Duty rationalization (NRS — Nigeria Revenue Service, formerly FIRS — `https://www.nrs.gov.ng/`)
  - **Lagos State Land Use Charge Law 2018** — major property-tax reform (`https://luc.lagosstate.gov.ng/`)
  - **Nigeria Tax Reform Acts 2025** — Nigeria Tax Act (NTA), Nigeria Tax Administration Act (NTAA), Nigeria Revenue Service Act (NRSA), Joint Revenue Board Act (JRBA); **signed by President Tinubu on 26 Jun 2025; commencement 1 Jan 2026** (2026-05-27 verified, source: State House Abuja + PwC NG + EY). Key outcomes: VAT **retained at 7.5 %** (proposed 12.5 % phasing DROPPED); residential rent **zero-rated**; CGT flat 10 % **REPLACED** — individuals taxed at progressive PIT bands (0–25 % top), corporates aligned with 30 % CIT; PIT bands restructured (₦800k tax-free threshold + 7 %/11 %/15 %/.../25 % top); CIT **retained at 30 %** (proposed 25 % NOT enacted; small companies ≤ ₦25M turnover at 0 % CIT; 15 % domestic minimum tax for large MNEs); FIRS reorganised and rebranded as **Nigeria Revenue Service (NRS)**.
  - **Land Use Act amendment debate 2023-26** — state-vs-federal discussion; no enacted change as of 2026-05
  - **Eko Atlantic City** ongoing reclamation Lagos (Bar Beach) — LASG + South Energyx

## Foreign buyer eligibility

**Lagos / FCT Abuja are the foreign-buyer-friendly framework**; outside these + a handful of other states, friction rises sharply. Every claim below traces to **Land Use Act 1978** + state-specific consent regimes.

### Land Use Act 1978 — the foundation

Source: Land Use Act, Cap L5, Laws of the Federation of Nigeria 2004 (consolidated; enacted 1978).

| Rule | Effect |
|---|---|
| All urban land vested in State Governor as trustee | No freehold; only leasehold via state grant |
| **Certificate of Occupancy (C of O)** | Primary statutory title — issued by State Governor; supersedes freehold |
| Typical residential leasehold | **99 years renewable** (shorter for commercial/agricultural in some states) |
| Right of Occupancy (rural) | Outside urban areas; assignable, governance varies |
| **Governor's Consent (LUA s. 22)** required for transfer/mortgage/sublease | Without it, transaction void/voidable |
| Foreigners CAN obtain C of O | No nationality bar at federal level under LUA 1978 |
| State discretion on consent | Niger Delta + Bauchi + restricted states case-by-case; security clearance possible |

### Lagos / FCT Abuja vs other states

- **Lagos State**: most foreign-buyer-friendly; LSLB + e-LandTitle Portal mature; LASRRA tracks residents; 80%+ of foreign-buyer activity (Ikoyi, VI, Lekki Phase 1, Banana Island, Eko Atlantic, Ajah/Ibeju-Lekki, Magodo, Ikeja GRA)
- **FCT Abuja**: AGIS + FCDA framework; diplomatic-tier (Maitama, Asokoro, Wuse II, Jabi, Gwarinpa)
- **Niger Delta states + Bauchi**: case-by-case consent + occasional security clearance; longer timelines
- **Northern Sharia states (Kano, Kaduna, Sokoto, etc.)**: foreigners CAN purchase under federal LUA, but customary-law overlay + security context affect practical access — consult State Lands Bureau

### Critical caveat — Governor's Consent Fee = top closing-cost item

Required for any subsequent assignment / mortgage / sublease (LUA s. 22). **Largest single closing-cost item** in Lagos — often higher than federal Stamp Duty:

- **Lagos**: **2026 Blue Book (Fair Market Value + Governor's Consent charges), effective 1 May 2026** — composite ~3 % headline rate (Consent Fee 1.5 % + CGT-assessment 0.5 % + Stamp Duty 0.5 % + Registration 0.5 %) BUT assessed FMV (Blue Book) revised sharply upward — **300 %+ uplift reported in Lekki / Ikoyi / Banana Island** per Aluko & Oyebode + Banwo & Ighodalo briefs (2026-05-27 verified). **This ~3 % headline composite on the uplifted Blue Book FMV SUPERSEDES the prior Lagos 8–15 % sliding Consent-Fee rate cited elsewhere in this playbook** — the FMV uplift, not the headline %, is now the driver. Total money cost still lands mid-single-digit % of true transaction price for prime Lagos. Verify per transaction at LSLB + current Lagos NBA conveyancer.
- **FCT Abuja**: ~8% (verify at AGIS)
- **Smaller states**: ~3-5%

**Total Lagos Governor's Consent + Stamp Duty + Registration: ~10-20% all-in** for foreign-tied purchases — among highest closing costs in Africa. Always price into TCO.

### Other restrictions

- **Agricultural land**: rural RoO use restrictions; foreign acquisition of large agricultural holdings often requires federal/state agri-ministry approval
- **Petroleum / mining-bearing land**: Nigerian Mining Act + Petroleum Industry Act 2021 reserve mineral rights to Federal Government regardless of surface title
- **Coastal / strategic zones**: case-by-case (Niger Delta active oil zones)
- **Diplomatic acquisition**: separate Ministry of Foreign Affairs + Federal framework

### Compute

1. **Listing NGN/m² or USD/m²** = price ÷ m² built. Clarify "built-up" vs "land area"; **plot conventions vary by state** (Lagos plot ≈ 648 m²; northern states may differ ≈ 464 m²)
2. Convert NGN ↔ USD at **CBN official rate** (`https://www.cbn.gov.ng/`) — post-Jun 2023 float, official ≈ parallel rate but stress events can re-create gaps
3. Cross-check 3+ listings + developer-direct quote + private research note (Estate Intel / Northcourt) before underwriting
4. **Trap — dual-currency**: prime Lagos/Abuja quote in USD with NGN for show; USD price binding — confirm currency in writing
5. **Trap — off-plan vs cash**: headline ₦80M off-plan may be 5-7 yr installment; cash-equivalent often 25-40% lower — demand cash-equivalent quote
6. **Trap — plot vs m²**: "half-plot at ₦50M" in Ajah ≈ 324 m² (Lagos) vs ≈ 232 m² (northern) — clarify in writing
7. **Trap — declared vs actual**: stamp-duty/CGT declared often 20-40% below actual cash price in NGN-domestic resale; foreign USD-cash via Form A/M is closer to actual

**Confidence**: MEDIUM — asking listings abundant in Lagos/Abuja; **transaction-verified price data not publicly available** (LSLB + AGIS records paid + non-queryable). Foreign buyers should commission local broker 3-5 sold-comparables + cross-check Estate Intel / Northcourt quarterly.

### Lagos-specific notes

- **Third Mainland Bridge** — among Africa's most heavily trafficked urban bridges; extreme peak congestion 7-10am / 4-8pm

- **Bus Rapid Transit (BRT)**: LAMATA-managed corridors (Mile 12 ↔ Oshodi ↔ Obalende, Ikorodu ↔ TBS) — meaningful infrastructure investment 2008-onwards; Lagos Blue Rail Line operational since 2023 (Marina ↔ Mile 2); Red Line Mile 2 ↔ Agbado phased opening 2024-26 (verify at LAMATA).

## Section: `--tax`

### Annual property tax — STATE-LEVEL

| State/FCT | Tax type | Rate / source |
|---|---|---|
| **Lagos** | **Land Use Charge (LUC)** | **0.076–0.394% sliding** annual on assessed value (Lagos State LUC Law 2018; `https://luc.lagosstate.gov.ng/`) |
| **FCT Abuja** | Tenement Rate | Set by FCT Area Councils (AMAC, Bwari, Gwagwalada, Kuje, Kwali, Abaji); verify per council |
| **Other states** | Tenement / Property Tax | LGA-administered; modest absolute NGN; varies widely |

### Lagos LUC detail (Lagos State LUC Law 2018; LIRS — `https://lirs.gov.ng/`)

- Owner-occupied residential: **0.076%**
- Owner + 3rd-party residential (mixed-use): **0.256%**
- Commercial / industrial: **0.394%**
- Pensioner / 70+ exemption: verify at LIRS
- Assessed value = LASG assessment, typically below market — re-assessment triggers bill jumps; annual due Q1

**Example**: Lekki Phase 1 apartment assessed NGN 100M → LUC = 100M × 0.076% = **NGN 76,000/yr (~USD 50/yr)**. Banana Island villa assessed NGN 1.5B → LUC = NGN 1,140,000/yr (~USD 760). Annual residential tax is low absolute USD; transaction cost is the heavy drag.

### Transaction taxes (one-time at purchase) — major closing-cost item

| Item | Rate | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| **Stamp Duty (federal)** | **1.5–3% sliding** | NRS (Nigeria Revenue Service, formerly FIRS) — Stamp Duty Act + Finance Act 2019/2020; `https://www.nrs.gov.ng/` |
| **CGT (federal, seller)** | **Regime change eff 1 Jan 2026 (Nigeria Tax Act 2025)** — flat 10 % REPLACED. Individuals: progressive PIT bands (0–25 %); Corporates: 30 % CIT-aligned. Primary-residence exemption now **once-in-a-lifetime** for dwelling-house + ~1 acre adjoining land (verify at NRS, 2026-05-27 verified) | NRS (formerly FIRS) — Nigeria Tax Act 2025; pre-2026: CGT Act Cap C1 LFN 2004 |
| **Governor's Consent Fee — STATE-LEVEL** | **Lagos 8–15% sliding (superseded by 2026 Blue Book — Consent-Fee headline now ~1.5% of uplifted FMV; see --foreign-buyer); FCT 8%; smaller states 3–5%** | State Lands Bureau / AGIS |
| **Registration Fee — STATE-LEVEL** | Lagos ~0.5–1%; varies | State Lands Registry |
| **Survey / Deed fees** | NGN 50k-500k+ | Registered Surveyor + State |
| **Buyer solicitor** | 3–10% (often 5%) | NBA-negotiated |
| **Agent commission** | 5–10% (often split) | Market practice |
| **Total foreign-buyer transaction cost (Lagos)** | **~10–20% of price** | Sum of consent + stamp duty + reg + legal + agent |

### VAT — federal (NRS, formerly FIRS)

Finance Act 2019 raised VAT from 5% to 7.5% (effective 1 Feb 2020):
- **Standard VAT**: **7.5%**
- **First sale of residential by developer**: **0% / exempt** (Finance Act 2019; verify scope at NRS)
- **Commercial property / leases**: **7.5% applies**
- **Nigeria Tax Act 2025 (effective 1 Jan 2026)**: VAT **retained at 7.5 %** (proposed 12.5 % phasing DROPPED in parliament); **residential rent is now zero-rated**; **input VAT claimable on services + fixed assets** (structural change vs pre-2026 input-VAT-on-goods-only regime); FG share of VAT distribution dropped 15 % → 10 % (states 55 %, LGAs 35 %). Source: NRS Portal Guide + PwC NG + The Africa Report (2026-05-27 verified).

### CGT (federal, seller, on sale)

Regime change eff 1 Jan 2026 under Nigeria Tax Act 2025 — flat 10 % REPLACED (verify at NRS):
- **Rate (post-2026)**: net gain (sale − adjusted cost base − allowable expenses) now taxed at **progressive PIT bands (individuals, 0–25 %)** / **30 % CIT-aligned (corporates)** — *not* a flat rate
- **Pre-2026 (superseded)**: flat 10% on net gain per CGT Act Cap C1 LFN 2004 + amendments
- **Primary-residence exemption**: now **once-in-a-lifetime** for dwelling-house + ~1 acre adjoining land (verify scope at NRS)
- **Non-resident sellers**: taxed on Nigeria-sourced gain under the post-2026 regime; withheld at completion in some state regimes

### PIT on rental income

- **Progressive bands restructured under Nigeria Tax Act 2025 (effective 1 Jan 2026)**: ₦0–800k **0 %** (new tax-free threshold); ₦800k–1.6M **7 %**; ₦1.6M–4.8M **11 %**; ₦4.8M–6.4M **15 %**; ₦6.4M–50M **17–23 %** sliding; above ₦50M **25 %** top marginal — administered by State IRS (Lagos: LIRS). Pre-2026 7-24 % scale superseded (2026-05-27 verified).
- **Non-resident landlords**: Nigeria-sourced → taxable in Nigeria; withholding rules apply
- **Deductions**: maintenance, repairs (not improvements), management commission, mortgage interest

### Inheritance / estate

- **No federal inheritance tax**
- **Probate / Letters of Administration** at State High Court Probate Registry; fees vary state (~3-10% of estate)
- **Sharia inheritance** for Muslim decedents in 12 northern states (mandatory shares)

### Future risk

Tax Reform Bills 2024-25 pending; state revenue intensification post-devaluation (Lagos LUC re-assessments + other states tenement digitization).

**Confidence**: HIGH for federal framework (Stamp Duty + CGT + VAT + Finance Acts — all NRS, formerly FIRS); MEDIUM for state-level Consent Fee + Registration sliding scales (Lagos LSLB best-documented, rates evolve — verify per transaction); MEDIUM for 2026 LUC assessed values (Lagos re-assessment cycle ongoing).

## Section: `--rental`

### Long-term residential

- **Governing law**: state-level (Lagos State Tenancy Law 2011, FCT Recovery of Premises Act) — common-law-derived
- **Annual rent-in-advance culture**: most Nigerian residential leases require **1–2 years rent in advance** (legacy of weak enforcement); Lagos Tenancy Law 2011 prohibits >1 year advance for new tenants in regulated areas (enforcement uneven)
- **Notice**: 6 months for yearly tenancies (Lagos); **eviction** via Magistrate/High Court 6-18 months
- **Deposit (caution fee)**: 1-2 months; no statutory escrow

### Tax on rental income

- **Resident landlord**: PIT progressive on net rental — restructured bands eff 1 Jan 2026 (₦800k tax-free threshold; 0/7/11/15/.../25% top; pre-2026 7-24% scale superseded — see --tax); state IRS administers, e.g., LIRS
- **Non-resident landlord**: rental income Nigeria-sourced → 10% withholding when tenant is a withholding agent; verify NRS + state IRS per case
- **Deductions**: maintenance, repairs (not capital improvements), management commission, mortgage interest

### Short-term rentals (STR / Airbnb)

**Status**: undergoverned federally; growing Lagos Lekki/Ikoyi/VI + Abuja Maitama.

- **Lagos State Tourism Promotion Authority** — STR licensing framework (rare formal compliance) — `https://lstpa.lagosstate.gov.ng/` ❌ DEPRECATED — dead on 2026-09-18; verify with the authority named in this row
- **Estate / HOA covenants**: most gated estates (Banana Island, Lekki Phase 1, Ikoyi) **prohibit STR** via covenants — read estate by-laws BEFORE buying for STR
- **VAT**: 7.5% applies if operator VAT-registered

### Strategic notes

- **Long-term gross yields**: Ikoyi/VI/Lekki ~4-6% USD; Lagos Mainland ~6-10% NGN; Abuja Maitama ~5-7%
- **STR gross yields**: Lekki/Ikoyi ~8-15% headline NGN; **net after security + diesel + estate fees often half of gross**
- **Energy autonomy overlay** (see `--mains`): generators + diesel + solar + LPG — NGN 200k-1.5M/month for mid-tier home — must be in any underwriting

**Confidence**: MEDIUM — federal/state IRS framework documented; STR is estate-rule-controlled. Verify by-laws before underwriting STR yield.

### Security situation — non-sanitized

Nigeria's security landscape is **highly state-dependent** and a critical input to any property purchase. UK FCDO + US State Dept advisories: **"exercise high caution" or "reconsider travel"** in many states.

| Region | Security context (2024-26) |
|---|---|
| **Lagos State** | Urban crime: armed robbery, "area boys" extortion, occasional kidnap-for-ransom expats; gated estates + private security partially mitigate; **most foreign-buyer activity here** |
| **FCT Abuja** | Diplomatic-belt baseline; periodic kidnap-for-ransom on outskirts; Maitama / Asokoro most secure |
| **Northeast (Borno, Yobe, Adamawa)** | **Boko Haram + ISWAP insurgency** ongoing; FCDO "advise against all travel"; NOT a foreign-buyer market |
| **Northwest (Kaduna, Zamfara, Sokoto, Katsina, Niger)** | **Banditry + mass kidnappings** persistent 2020-26 |
| **Southeast (Anambra, Imo, Abia, Ebonyi, Enugu)** | **IPOB / "unknown gunmen"** + Monday sit-at-home enforcement |
| **Niger Delta (Rivers, Bayelsa, Delta, Akwa Ibom)** | Oil-driven militancy waxes/wanes; Gulf of Guinea piracy; PH gated estates relatively secure |
| **Middle Belt (Plateau, Benue, Kaduna)** | Farmer-herder conflict + sectarian violence rural |

**Implication**: Lagos + FCT Abuja (+ select PH) are the practical foreign-buyer universe; gated estate + private security overlay is **mandatory cost item**.

### Flood + coastal risk

- **Niger + Benue rivers** annual flooding **Sep-Nov peak** (NIHSA AFO)
- **Major events**: 2012 (7M+ affected, 30+ states); 2022 (600+ deaths, 2.4M displaced); 2024 Maiduguri dam-break
- **Lagos urban flooding** chronic: VI, Lekki, Ajah corridors flood after heavy rains; 2017/2020/2024 recurrent
- **Coastal erosion**: Lagos Bar Beach historically eroded ~25-30 m/yr until **Eko Atlantic** seawall + reclamation (2008-onwards) stabilized — verify parcel proximity to coastline / reclamation boundary

### Climate change (summary)

NDC Paris: 47% conditional GHG reduction by 2030 (Federal Ministry of Environment, 2021 update). Lake Chad ~90% reduction since 1960s; Lagos/Niger Delta SLR exposure (see `--climate`); Northern heat extremes rising.

### Power grid — energy autonomy is mandatory cost overlay

National grid (NEPA → PHCN → DisCos post-2013 unbundling) notoriously unreliable; multiple 2024-25 national grid collapses (NERC + TCN). Most premium Lagos/Abuja properties run **diesel generator + inverter + battery + solar + LPG** stack:
- Mid-tier home (Lekki Phase 1): NGN 200k-800k/mo (~USD 130-530)
- Premium home (Banana Island, Maitama): NGN 800k-2.5M/mo (~USD 530-1,670)
- Solar + lithium increasingly economic 2023-26 vs diesel (REA + private installers)

## Section: `--visa`

### Authority + legal basis

- **Nigeria Immigration Service (NIS)** — `https://immigration.gov.ng/`
- **Federal Ministry of Interior** — `https://www.interior.gov.ng/` — quota + policy
- Legal basis: **Immigration Act 2015** + Immigration Regulations 2017 + amendments

### Major pathways

1. **Tourist + Business visa**: 30-90 days; not for working/residing; eVisa + visa-on-arrival programs expanded under 2020-2025 reforms (verify per nationality at NIS)
2. **STR-1 (Subject to Regular Application)**: long-term residence tied to employment OR investment; **investment threshold ≥ USD 100,000 in registered Nigerian business**; renewable; requires CAC company + expatriate quota + CERPAC
3. **CERPAC**: 2-year renewable; tied to specific employer + position; annual fee + biometrics
4. **Permanent Residence Permit (PRP)**: ~10-15 yrs CERPAC continuous + clean record (varies by case)
5. **Citizenship by Naturalization**: **15+ years continuous residence** + language + good character + means of livelihood; **dual citizenship** generally NOT allowed for native-born Nigerians (limited exceptions); ministerial discretion
6. **Citizenship by Descent** through Nigerian father (Constitution s. 25); maternal-only descent contested historically — verify current practice with NIS

### NO golden-visa via real estate

Nigeria does NOT offer a residency-by-property scheme. Property purchase alone gives NO residence right. **Only pathway**: Investor STR via ≥USD 100k in registered Nigerian business (which can include a RE development company) — must be a going concern, not passive RE.

### Diaspora

NIDCOM (`https://nidcom.gov.ng/`) — Nigerian Diaspora Investment Trust Fund + return-of-talent schemes for Nigerians abroad.

**Confidence**: HIGH for Immigration Act 2015 + NIS framework; MEDIUM for exact processing times (case-dependent).

## Section: `--notary`

### Framework

Nigeria operates **English common-law conveyancing**, not civil-law notariat:
- **Solicitor (NBA-registered)** drafts Deed of Assignment + due diligence on title chain
- **Notary Public** administers oaths + authenticates (NOT the conveyancer)
- **State Lands Bureau / Lands Registry** issues Governor's Consent + registers Deed
- **State High Court Probate Registry** for probate

### Process — Lagos foreign buyer

1. Letter of Offer + refundable deposit (10-20%)
2. Buyer solicitor due diligence: title search at LSLB; C of O chain verification; encumbrance check; Survey Plan + boundary
3. Independent legal search at LSLB Land Information Office (paid)
4. Deed of Assignment drafted by buyer solicitor + reviewed by seller solicitor
5. Stamp Duty assessment + payment at the NRS (Nigeria Revenue Service, formerly FIRS) (1.5-3% sliding)
6. **Governor's Consent application** to LSLB — LUA s. 22; **major delay point** — Lagos historically 3-12 months (digitizing 2024-26); FCT AGIS 1-3 months
7. Governor's Consent + Consent Fee paid (Lagos 8-15% — superseded by 2026 Blue Book, Consent-Fee headline now ~1.5% of uplifted FMV; FCT 8%)
8. Registration at State Lands Registry → Registration Fee
9. C of O updated/new in buyer's name
10. CGT settled at NRS by seller — net gain now taxed at progressive PIT bands (individuals, 0–25 %) / 30 % CIT-aligned (corporates) eff 1 Jan 2026; pre-2026 flat 10% superseded (see --tax)

### Timeline

Lagos: 4-12+ months (Consent is critical path); FCT Abuja: 2-6 months; other states: 3-12+ months variable.

### Costs (Lagos foreign-buyer, USD 200k example)

- Stamp Duty 2% = USD 4,000
- Governor's Consent ~10% mid-tier = USD 20,000
- Registration ~0.5-1% = USD 1,000-2,000
- Solicitor 5% = USD 10,000
- Agent commission 2.5-5% buyer-side = USD 5,000-10,000
- Survey + sundry = USD 500-2,000
- **Total Lagos**: ~15-20% of price for USD foreign-tied purchase
- FCT Abuja: ~12-15%; smaller states: ~8-12%

### Trampas

- **"Omo onile" community-claimant extortion**: Lagos peri-urban (Ajah, Ibeju-Lekki) — verify family-land releases + traditional-ruler endorsements separately
- **Forged C of O** — market-known fraud; insist on LSLB/AGIS verified search + Lands Bureau visit in person
- **"Government acquisition" overlay**: Lagos parcels notionally acquired decades ago without compensation; status uncertain — verify acquisition + excision/gazetting
- **Multiple sales**: mitigated by registered Deed + Consent (first-registered prevails); unregistered Deed exposes buyer
- **Survey boundary disputes**: registered surveyor + on-site verification pre-signing
- **Excision + Gazetting**: Lagos parcels under historical acquisition can be released via gazette — verify publication

**Confidence**: HIGH for federal Stamp Duty + CGT; MEDIUM-HIGH for Lagos LSLB (digitizing); MEDIUM for other states (paper-variable); LOW for Ibeju-Lekki/Ajah omo onile risk — on-site local broker + legal DD essential.

## Section: `--exit`

### Sale process (Lagos Ikoyi apartment, USD 500,000)

1. Engage NBA solicitor + agent (~5% commission typical)
2. Listing on PropertyPro.ng + Nigeria Property Centre + private agent network; **6-18 months** typical Lagos coastal depending on price + currency stance
3. Pre-sale docs: original C of O + Survey Plan + chain of Deeds of Assignment + Governor's Consent records + LUC tax clearance + estate dues + utility clearance
4. Buyer offer + Letter of Acceptance + deposit (10-20% refundable)
5. Buyer due diligence — own solicitor + LSLB/AGIS title search
6. Stamp Duty + Governor's Consent + Registration (Lagos: 4-12 months on Consent)
7. CGT on net gain settled at NRS (seller) — progressive PIT 0–25% / 30% CIT-aligned eff 1 Jan 2026; pre-2026 flat 10% superseded (see --tax)
8. Final payment + Deed of Assignment + handover
9. C of O updated at State Lands Registry

### Seller tax obligations

- **CGT on net gain** (NRS) — progressive PIT 0–25% / 30% CIT-aligned eff 1 Jan 2026 (pre-2026 flat 10% superseded; see --tax); once-in-a-lifetime primary-residence exemption may apply (verify scope)
- **No federal property-tax exit penalty** — settle outstanding LUC
- Solicitor fees ~1-2% seller; agent commission ~5% (often shared)
- Stamp Duty + Consent Fee are buyer-side (no seller transfer tax beyond CGT)

### Repatriation

- **CBN Form A/M/NCX at original purchase is critical** — without it, USD repatriation structurally restricted
- AML: NFIU + bank reports >USD 10,000; legal but documented
- Conversion at CBN-authorized dealer rate; spread typically 1-3%
- Outbound transfer via authorized dealer: documentation-heavy but legal for documented inflows

### Liquidity

- Lagos premium (Ikoyi, VI, Banana Island, Lekki Phase 1): 6-18 months for fairly priced in stable currency stance
- **NGN devaluation freezes market** (USD-sellers wait, NGN-buyers flee) — 2023-24 saw sharp slowdown
- Off-plan resale: thin secondary market
- Other states: 12-36 months not uncommon

### Seller cost (Lagos, typical)

Agent 5% + CGT on net gain (progressive PIT 0–25% / 30% CIT-aligned eff 1 Jan 2026; pre-2026 flat 10% superseded — see --tax) + solicitor 1-2% + LUC/utility clearances minor → net to seller ~85-92% of gross.

**Confidence**: HIGH for federal CGT + Stamp Duty; MEDIUM for Lagos closing-timeline (Consent variable); LOW for second-tier state liquidity.

## Caveats unique to NG (HEAVY — read all)

- **Land Use Act 1978**: NO freehold; only leasehold via C of O from State Governor — foundation of every Nigerian property transaction.
- **Governor's Consent (LUA s. 22)** required for any assignment/mortgage/sublease; **largest closing-cost item** in Lagos (pre-2026 ~8-15% sliding — superseded by 2026 Blue Book, headline now ~1.5% of sharply uplifted FMV; see --foreign-buyer), often higher than federal Stamp Duty. Total Lagos foreign-buyer closing ~10-20% of price — among highest in Africa.
- **State-level Consent Fee variance is the dominant closing-cost differentiator**: Lagos pre-2026 8-15% (superseded by 2026 Blue Book — see --foreign-buyer), FCT 8%, smaller states 3-5%.
- **Naira volatility 2023-26**: NGN floated Jun 2023; ~70%+ devaluation; ~NGN 460 → 1,500/USD. **USD informal pricing dominates prime Lagos/Abuja RE**.
- **CBN Form A/M/NCX paper-trail**: capital inflow MUST be CBN-documented for future USD repatriation. **#1 procedural risk** — get at fund transfer or accept NGN-only exit.
- **Security heterogeneous + serious**: kidnap-for-ransom expats Lagos/Abuja periodic; Boko Haram/ISWAP NE; NW banditry; IPOB SE; farmer-herder Middle Belt; Gulf of Guinea piracy. Foreign-buyer practical universe = Lagos + FCT + select PH; gated estate + private security MANDATORY overlay.
- **Energy autonomy mandatory cost overlay**: NGN 200k-2.5M/month for premium homes (NGN 2.4-30M/yr in TCO).
- **"Omo onile" (community-claimant) extortion**: Lagos peri-urban (Ajah, Ibeju-Lekki) — verify family-land releases + traditional-ruler endorsements separately.
- **Forged C of O** market-known fraud — insist on LSLB/AGIS verified search + Lands Bureau visit in person.
- **Government acquisition + excision/gazetting**: Lagos historical acquisitions released via gazette — verify status.
- **No national cadastre**: state-fragmented (Lagos LSLB / FCT AGIS digitized; others paper). Cross-state portfolio = parallel DD streams.
- **Lagos urban floods** (Lekki, VI corridor, Ajah): chronic; verify drainage + flood-cover wording on insurance.
- **Eko Atlantic**: reclaimed land with sea-wall; verify phase + completion certificate.
- **No golden-visa via RE**: STR investor pathway requires ≥USD 100k in registered Nigerian business as going concern, not passive RE.
- **BVN + NIN mandatory** for property + bank transactions.
- **Nigeria Tax Reform Acts 2025** — signed 26 Jun 2025, eff 1 Jan 2026; proposed VAT 12.5% phasing DROPPED (VAT retained 7.5%), proposed 25% CIT not enacted (retained 30%) — see --tax.
