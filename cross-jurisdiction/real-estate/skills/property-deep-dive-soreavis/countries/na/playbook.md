# Namibia 🇳🇦 — Property Due-Diligence Playbook

ISO2: `na`. Status: ✅ Fully populated (researched 2026-06-13).

> **Critical framing — read first**: Namibia is a **Roman-Dutch common-law + customary-law jurisdiction** (inherited from South Africa, under a 1990 Constitution), with the **Supreme Court of Namibia** (Windhoek) as both the final court of appeal AND the constitutional court ([Office of the Judiciary — About the Supreme Court](https://www.judiciary.na/pages/courts/)). The currency **NAD (Namibia Dollar) is pegged 1:1 to the South African Rand (ZAR)** as a member of the **Common Monetary Area (CMA)** — and **both NAD and ZAR are legal tender inside Namibia** ([Bank of Namibia Exchange Control FAQ](https://www.bon.com.na/Informations/FAQ/Exchange-Control.aspx)). **Foreign buyers are NOT banned from urban/residential freehold**: the constitutional default (Article 16(1)) opens property acquisition to "all persons", and **NO enacted statute prohibits foreign urban/residential freehold as of 2026-06-13**. The **one enacted foreign-ownership restriction is on agricultural (commercial) farmland** — under **section 58(1)(a) of the Agricultural (Commercial) Land Reform Act 6 of 1995** a foreign national may not acquire agricultural land without the prior written consent of the Minister, and the State holds a **preferent (right-of-first-refusal) right** ([NamibLII Act 6/1995](https://namiblii.org/akn/na/act/1995/6)). A **consolidating Land Bill** that WOULD prohibit foreigners from acquiring both communal and commercial land was **re-tabled in the National Assembly in October 2025 (Minister Inge Zaamwani) and is awaiting debate — it is a BILL, NOT enacted as of 2026-06-13** ([Mail & Guardian, 2025-10-08](https://mg.co.za/africa/2025-10-08-namibia-tables-sweeping-land-bill-to-expand-farm-expropriation-powers/)). **Tax stack headline**: one-time **transfer duty** (natural persons 0% up to N$1.1m, sliding to a top marginal **11%**; entities flat **12%**), one-time **stamp duty** (NAD 12 per NAD 1,000, natural-person exemption N$1.1m), both **effective 1 October 2024**; **VAT 15%**; **NO capital gains tax on ordinary assets** (exception: mining/petroleum licences), **NO inheritance/estate/gift tax, NO wealth tax** ([PwC Namibia, reviewed 18 Dec 2025](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/republic-of-namibia/corporate/other-taxes)). **Citizenship-by-investment: NONE** — only a 5-year-renewable Residence-by-Investment route exists. **Operative deeds law is the Deeds Registries Act 47 of 1937** (the 2015 Act was never validly commenced — set aside by the High Court in 2021). **Top hazards (national screening)**: drought/water-scarcity, river/urban/coastal flood, and wildfire all rated **HIGH** by [ThinkHazard! (GFDRR/World Bank)](https://thinkhazard.org/en/report/172-namibia) — but these are national flags; interior Windhoek (~1,700 m plateau) and the coast (Swakopmund/Walvis Bay) differ materially. **Confirm** every transfer-duty band, the Land Bill's enactment status, FX-repatriation procedure, and any investor/residence threshold at primary sources **before any binding step**. Authoritative primary sources: **[Namibia Revenue Agency (NamRA)](https://www.namra.org.na/)**, **[Bank of Namibia (BoN)](https://www.bon.com.na/)**, **[NamibLII (statutes/case law)](https://namiblii.org/)**, **[Office of the Judiciary](https://www.judiciary.na/)**, **[Namibia Investment Promotion & Development Board (NIPDB)](https://www.nipdb.com/)**, **[Namibia Statistics Agency (NSA)](https://nsa.org.na/)**.

## Country profile

- **Geography**: Large, arid south-western African state on the Atlantic coast, bordered by Angola (north), Zambia + Botswana (east), South Africa (south). Contains the **Namib Desert** (coastal) and **Kalahari** (east); the interior is an elevated plateau (Windhoek sits at ~1,700 m). Capital and largest city: **Windhoek** (Khomas region). Coastal towns **Swakopmund** + **Walvis Bay** (Erongo region) are the principal coastal property markets. **Exact land area — data not primary-verified this pass — verify at [Namibia Statistics Agency](https://nsa.org.na/)** (Namibia is one of the most sparsely populated countries on earth; precise figure not re-pulled).
- **Population**: **data not primary-verified this pass — verify at [Namibia Statistics Agency (NSA)](https://nsa.org.na/)**. Namibia ran a national Population & Housing Census in 2023; the headline count was not re-pulled to a primary source here. Do NOT rely on a memorised figure for any decision.
- **GDP per capita**: **data not primary-verified this pass — verify at [Bank of Namibia](https://www.bon.com.na/) / [Namibia Statistics Agency](https://nsa.org.na/) / [IMF DataMapper](https://www.imf.org/external/datamapper/profile/NAM)**. Upper-middle-income by World Bank classification; mining (diamonds, uranium), fishing, tourism, and a nascent green-hydrogen/oil-and-gas sector dominate — exact magnitudes not re-sourced this pass.
- **Currency**: **NAD (Namibia Dollar — N$)** — **pegged 1:1 to the South African Rand (ZAR)** as a member of the **Common Monetary Area (CMA)** (Namibia, South Africa, Lesotho, Eswatini). **Both NAD and ZAR are legal tender inside Namibia** and interchangeable at par; Namibia has **no independent monetary policy** (it tracks the South African Reserve Bank) (source: [Bank of Namibia Exchange Control FAQ](https://www.bon.com.na/Informations/FAQ/Exchange-Control.aspx) — primary/central-bank). The NAD was first issued **15 September 1993** (10, 50 and 100 dollar denominations) (source: [Bank of Namibia — History of Namibian Banknotes](https://www.bon.com.na/Currency/Namibian-notes/History-of-Namibian-Banknotes.aspx) — primary/central-bank; 2026-07-11 verified, source Bank of Namibia).
- **Languages**: **English is the sole official language** (widely reported; verify at [gov.na](https://www.gov.na/) / NSA). Afrikaans, German, Oshiwambo, Otjiherero, Khoekhoegowab (Nama/Damara), and others are widely spoken vernaculars. **Legal, deed, and conveyancing documentation is in English.**
- **Legal system**: **Roman-Dutch common law** (inherited from South Africa) + **customary law** (continued validity under Constitution Article 66) + English-derived court procedure, all under the **1990 Constitution**. Roman-Dutch common-law lineage shared with South Africa, Botswana, Lesotho, Eswatini, Zimbabwe (source: [Globalex/NYU — Namibia legal research guide](https://www.nyulawglobal.org/globalex/namibia1.html); [Legal Assistance Centre — "Law in Namibia: Common Law"](https://www.lac.org.na/projects/grap/Pdf/Law_4-Common_Law.pdf) — secondary-academic but unanimous).
- **Final court of appeal**: **Supreme Court of Namibia** (Windhoek), presided over by the **Chief Justice** — simultaneously the **highest appellate court AND the constitutional court**. A Supreme Court decision is final and "can only be reversed by an Act of Parliament that contradicts it, or by another ruling of the Supreme Court itself." Court hierarchy: **Magistrates'/Lower Courts → High Court → Supreme Court** (source: [Office of the Judiciary — About the Supreme Court](https://www.judiciary.na/pages/courts/) — primary).
- **Admin levels**: Namibia is divided into **regions** (e.g., Khomas — Windhoek; Erongo — Swakopmund/Walvis Bay), each subdivided into constituencies, plus local authorities (municipalities, town councils, village councils). **Exact region count — data not primary-verified this pass — verify at [Electoral Commission of Namibia](https://www.ecn.na/) / [gov.na](https://www.gov.na/)**.
- **Cadastre / deeds / land registry**:
  - **Directorate of Deeds Registry (DDR)** — registers title deeds, sectional titles, and leaseholds; operates under the **Deeds Registries Act 47 of 1937** (source: [NamibLII — Deeds Registries Act 47 of 1937](https://namiblii.org/akn/na/act/1937/47)).
  - Land + deeds administration falls under the **Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries, Water and Land Reform** — the ministry's own site prints the name in full and states it is "Mandated to Promote, Develop, Manage and utilize Agriculture, Fisheries, Water and Land Resources sustainably" ([mawlr.gov.na](https://mawlr.gov.na/), 2026 data; 2026-09-18 verified). **Re-verify after any cabinet reshuffle** — Namibian ministry naming has changed repeatedly.
  - **Surveyor-General** — official cadastral survey diagrams and erf/portion boundaries (under the same land ministry).
- **Identifier**: **Erf / portion number + Surveyor-General diagram + title deed number** (Roman-Dutch deeds-system identifiers, as in South Africa); sectional-title units carry a **scheme + unit number**. **No universal national parcel ID** equivalent to a French numéro de parcelle.
- **Title concepts**: **freehold title**, **sectional title** (apartments/units, under the **Sectional Titles Act 2 of 2009**), **leasehold**, plus a **starter title / land-hold title** for informal settlements under the **Flexible Land Tenure Act** (sources: [NamibLII — Sectional Titles Act 2 of 2009](https://namiblii.org/akn/na/act/2009/2); [NamibLII — Deeds Registries Act 47 of 1937](https://namiblii.org/akn/na/act/1937/47)).
- **Recent reforms (12-month watchlist)**:
  - **Transfer Duty Amendment Act No. 6 of 2024 + Stamp Duties Amendment Act No. 7 of 2024 — effective 1 October 2024**: revised the natural-person transfer-duty schedule, raised the stamp-duty natural-person exemption to N$1.1m, and expanded "property" to include shares/member's/trust interests in entities owning **residential** property (source: [PwC Namibia, 18 Dec 2025](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/republic-of-namibia/corporate/other-taxes); corroborated by multiple Namibian tax advisors — see `--tax`).
  - **Consolidating Land Bill — re-tabled October 2025, NOT enacted as of 2026-06-13; still listed among Bills under continuing debate at the 16 Sep 2026 National Assembly sitting (secondary — Namibia Updates session notice; on the agenda since 3 Jun 2026 per The Namibian via allAfrica)**: would replace the 1995 Agricultural (Commercial) Land Reform Act + the 2002 Communal Land Reform Act and ~12 laws/ordinances, and would **prohibit foreigners from acquiring both communal AND commercial land** (foreigners limited to leasing under job-/economy-benefit conditions); collusion to circumvent → fine up to N$50,000 or 10-year prison term (source: [Mail & Guardian, 2025-10-08](https://mg.co.za/africa/2025-10-08-namibia-tables-sweeping-land-bill-to-expand-farm-expropriation-powers/) — secondary news + the bill text it quotes). **STATUS = BILL.** See `--visa` / Foreign buyer eligibility / Caveats.
  - **Deeds Registries Act 14 of 2015 — never validly brought into operation**: its commencement and regulations were **set aside by the High Court in 2021** in *Bank Windhoek Limited v Minister of Agriculture, Water and Land Reform & others* (Case No. HC-MD-CIV-MOT-GEN-2021/00218) because the regulations breached the statutory ≥30-day-after-publication rule. **The 1937 Act therefore remains in force** (source: [NamibLII — Deeds Registries Act 47 of 1937](https://namiblii.org/akn/na/act/1937/47)). **Do NOT cite the 2015 Act as operative.**
  - **Residence-by-Investment programme** launched March 2023 (run with Henley & Partners) — a 5-year renewable residence permit, NOT instant PR or citizenship (advisory tier; see `--visa`).

## 🇳🇦 Foreign buyer eligibility (READ FIRST)

This section determines whether a transaction is even legally possible — and what process applies. **The headline is nuanced: Namibia does NOT ban foreign urban/residential freehold; the one enacted restriction is on agricultural (commercial) farmland; a broader prohibition exists only as a not-yet-enacted Bill.**

### Legal basis

- **Namibian Constitution, Article 16(1)**: "All persons shall have the right in any part of Namibia to acquire, own and dispose of all forms of immovable and movable property…" — the default is OPEN to **all persons**, not only citizens.
- **Namibian Constitution, Article 16(2)**: "Parliament may by legislation prohibit or regulate as it deems expedient the right to acquire property by persons who are not Namibian citizens" — a foreign-ownership ban is constitutionally **PERMITTED but must be ENACTED** (source: Namibian Constitution Art. 16, quoted in [Legal Assistance Centre brief, 20 April 2022](https://www.lac.org.na/projects/lead/Pdf/LAC_on_Regulation_of_Foreign_Land_Ownership_20_April_2022.pdf) — primary constitutional text via LAC; the Constitution is also on NamibLII for direct verification).
- **Agricultural (Commercial) Land Reform Act 6 of 1995, section 58(1)(a)** — the **one enacted foreign-ownership restriction**: a foreign national may **not, without the prior written consent of the Minister, acquire agricultural land**. Part VI of the Act is titled "Restriction on Acquisition of Agricultural Land by Foreign Nationals." Consent applications use a prescribed form (GN 257/1996). The State holds a **preferent (right-of-first-refusal) right** to purchase agricultural land before any private sale, and the Act permits expropriation (sources: [NamibLII — Act 6/1995](https://namiblii.org/akn/na/act/1995/6); [FAOLEX](https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/nam4463.pdf); [UNEP LEAP](https://leap.unep.org/en/countries/na/national-legislation/agricultural-commercial-land-reform-act-1995); GN 257/1996 [NamibLII](https://namiblii.org/akn/na/act/gn/1996/257)). **Verify the exact s.58 wording + the precise State-preferent-right section number at [NamibLII Act 6/1995](https://namiblii.org/akn/na/act/1995/6)** — the substance is confirmed but the verbatim sub-section text could not be machine-extracted this pass.
- **Deeds Registries Act 47 of 1937** — operative deeds + conveyancing law (NOT the 2015 Act).
- **Sectional Titles Act 2 of 2009** — apartments/units strata regime ([NamibLII](https://namiblii.org/akn/na/act/2009/2)).
- **Transfer Duty Act No. 14 of 1993** (amended by Act 6 of 2024) + **Stamp Duties Act No. 15 of 1993** (amended by Act 7 of 2024) — transaction taxes, administered by [NamRA](https://www.namra.org.na/).
- **Immigration Control Act, 1993** + **Namibian Citizenship Act, 1990** — residency + citizenship framework (no CBI provision).

### Buyer category matrix

| Buyer category | Right |
|---|---|
| **Namibian citizen** | Full freehold; standard Roman-Dutch deeds-system conveyancing |
| **Permanent resident** | Full freehold; standard conveyancing |
| **Non-resident foreign buyer — URBAN / RESIDENTIAL freehold** | **Permitted** under Art. 16(1); **no enacted statute prohibits it as of 2026-06-13.** Standard conveyancing via a conveyancer. (Watch the not-yet-enacted Land Bill — see below.) |
| **Non-resident foreign buyer — COMMERCIAL (urban) freehold** | **Permitted** under the same constitutional default; no enacted urban prohibition. |
| **Non-resident foreign buyer — AGRICULTURAL (commercial) land** | **Restricted**: requires **prior written Ministerial consent** (s.58(1)(a) ACLRA 6/1995) AND is subject to the **State preferent (first-refusal) right**. Treat as a gated, materially harder transaction. |
| **Company / close corporation / trust** | May hold freehold; **entity transfer duty is a flat 12%** (vs the natural-person sliding scale topping at 11%); buying the **SPV that owns a Namibian residential property** now itself triggers transfer duty (2024 reform — see `--tax`). |

(Source: Art. 16(1)/(2) via [LAC](https://www.lac.org.na/projects/lead/Pdf/LAC_on_Regulation_of_Foreign_Land_Ownership_20_April_2022.pdf) + [NamibLII Act 6/1995](https://namiblii.org/akn/na/act/1995/6) — primary constitutional + statutory scope. Urban-freehold openness is a structural inference from the constitutional default + the absence of any enacted urban prohibition; **verify with a Namibian conveyancer before contract.**)

### Visa / residency via property

**Namibia does NOT grant residence or citizenship automatically through property purchase.** There is **no property-linked residence threshold** equivalent to a Caribbean "buy-a-home-get-residency" route. Residence is obtained through the **Residence-by-Investment** programme or standard work/business/PR routes (see `--visa`), which are **investment- and company-based, not property-based**. **Citizenship-by-investment: NONE.**

### Compliance / AML

- **Financial Intelligence Act, 2012 (as amended)** — Namibia's AML/CFT framework; the **Financial Intelligence Centre (FIC)** sits within the Bank of Namibia. Conveyancers, estate agents, and banks are accountable/reporting institutions with KYC + suspicious-transaction-reporting duties. **Verify current obligations at [Financial Intelligence Centre Namibia](https://www.fic.na/)**.
- **Source-of-funds documentation** is required for property purchases; **cross-border fund flows OUTSIDE the CMA are exchange-controlled** (see `--currency`).
- **FATF context**: Namibia's AML regime has been under heightened international scrutiny — **verify current FATF/ESAAMLG status at [FIC Namibia](https://www.fic.na/)** before assuming standard timelines; KYC for non-residents can be lengthy.

### Verification path (before any reservation)

1. **Confirm the land class**: is the parcel **urban/residential** (open to foreigners) or **agricultural (commercial)** (Ministerial consent + State preferent right required)? Confirm zoning/classification with the local authority + the conveyancer.
2. **Engage a conveyancer** (admitted Namibian legal practitioner) to pull the **title deed** via the Directorate of Deeds Registry, confirm no encumbrances/bonds, and confirm municipal rates are paid.
3. **Surveyor-General diagram check**: confirm boundaries match the deed description.
4. **Watch the Land Bill**: confirm at signing whether the consolidating Land Bill has been enacted (it had NOT been as of 2026-06-13) — see Caveats + `--visa`.
5. **Exchange-control / FX-channel planning** (non-resident): route incoming funds through an Authorised Dealer; plan repatriation channel **before** purchase (see `--currency`).
6. **AML / source-of-funds**: assemble bank statements + source-of-funds evidence ahead of signing.

### N$/m² benchmarks

**Data not publicly available at a primary source** — the FNB HPI publishes average house values, not N$/m². For a per-m² figure, derive from active listings on the **same town + suburb** and tag each "(per listing — verify with cadastre / on visit)". Do NOT treat any listing-derived N$/m² as a fact.

### Compute

1. **N$/m² = listing price ÷ built-area m²** (clarify: built/floor area vs erf/land area — Namibian listings, like South African ones, often quote both; **erf = land plot, floor area = built**).
2. Cross-check against **≥ 3 active listings** on the same town + suburb (Windhoek suburb, Swakopmund vs Walvis Bay, etc.) — region matters enormously (Central ≈ 2× Southern by average).
3. **Currency convention**: all NAD; NAD = ZAR at par, so any ZAR-quoted comparable converts 1:1.
4. **Trap — region vs parcel**: the FNB regional averages are coarse (Central/Coastal/Northern/Southern); a Windhoek suburb and a Northern town are in different worlds. Never elevate a regional average to a parcel claim.
5. **Trap — sectional title**: as in ZA, sectional-title m² may exclude exclusive-use areas (parking, garden) — verify against the sectional-title plan.

### Key terms

- **Erf** — the surveyed land plot (Roman-Dutch deeds-system term, as in ZA)
- **Title deed** — sole legal evidence of ownership (deeds-registry system)
- **Sectional title** — apartment/unit strata regime (Sectional Titles Act 2 of 2009)
- **Conveyancer** — admitted legal practitioner who lodges transfer at the Deeds Registry
- **Transfer duty** — one-time acquisition tax (see `--tax`)

## Section: `--traffic`

### Sources

- **Roads Authority of Namibia (RA)** — `https://www.ra.org.na/` — national road network + (limited) traffic-count data; **no public live AADT portal comparable to UK DfT / US FHWA** as of 2026-06.
- **Namibian Police (NAMPOL)** — accident statistics (press-reported; no open AADT portal).
- **OSM** fallback via Overpass — `highway` tag class.

### Reality

Namibia does **NOT publish a public AADT (Annual Average Daily Traffic) portal**. **OSM road class + on-the-ground observation is the practical method.** Outside Windhoek + the coastal corridor, traffic volumes are very low by international standards (Namibia is one of the most sparsely populated countries on earth).

### Coarse OSM fallback

| OSM `highway` | Namibian road class | Typical v/d (heuristic — calibrate on site) |
|---|---|---|
| `trunk` / `primary` | National routes (e.g., B1 Windhoek–coast/north; B2 Swakopmund–Walvis Bay) | 5,000–30,000 (urban approaches) |
| `secondary` | Regional/arterial roads | 2,000–10,000 |
| `tertiary` / `unclassified` | Town feeder / gravel district roads | 200–3,000 |
| `residential` | Internal street | < 500 |

### Verdict bands (heuristic — calibrate with on-site visit)

- 🟢 < 1,000 v/d (residential subdivision interior, rural/gravel district road)
- 🟡 1,000–5,000 v/d (suburban Windhoek / coastal-town interior)
- 🟠 5,000–20,000 v/d (Windhoek arterials; B1/B2 urban approaches)
- 🔴 > 20,000 v/d (central Windhoek peak corridors; major Windhoek intersections)

### Caveats

- **Windhoek** carries the bulk of national congestion; peak commuter flows on the main arterials into the CBD. **Specific Windhoek AADT counts — data not publicly available — verify with the [Roads Authority](https://www.ra.org.na/) or City of Windhoek.**
- **B2 (Swakopmund ↔ Walvis Bay)** is the busy ~30 km coastal artery linking the two coastal towns + the port — the highest-volume non-Windhoek corridor.
- **Gravel-road prevalence**: a large share of the network is gravel; "tarred road frontage" vs gravel is a material listing distinction outside the main towns.
- **Long-distance B-roads**: low volume but high-speed; wildlife (kudu) collision risk is a real rural-driving hazard.

## Section: `--tax`

> **Headline**: One-time **transfer duty** (natural persons 0% up to N$1.1m, sliding to a top marginal **11%**; entities flat **12%**), one-time **stamp duty** (NAD 12 per NAD 1,000; natural-person exemption N$1.1m), both **effective 1 October 2024**. **VAT 15%**. **NO capital gains tax on ordinary assets** (exception: mining/petroleum licences), **NO inheritance/estate/gift tax, NO net-wealth tax**. Annual property tax = **municipal rates on municipal valuations** (city-specific) + a separate **land tax on commercial agricultural land**. Administered by **[NamRA](https://www.namra.org.na/)**.

### Governing statutes

- **Transfer Duty Act No. 14 of 1993**, amended by **Transfer Duty Amendment Act No. 6 of 2024** ([NamibLII GN ref](https://namiblii.org/akn/na/act/gn/2024/6)).
- **Stamp Duties Act No. 15 of 1993**, amended by **Stamp Duties Amendment Act No. 7 of 2024**.
- Both amendments **effective 1 October 2024**. Administered by **[Namibia Revenue Agency (NamRA)](https://www.namra.org.na/)**.
- The 2024 reform expanded "property" to include **shares / member's interests / trust interests** in entities owning **residential** property — so buying the SPV that owns a Namibian home now triggers transfer duty.

### Transfer duty — NATURAL PERSONS (effective 1 October 2024)

| Property value (NAD) | Transfer duty |
|---|---|
| up to **N$1,100,000** | **0% (nil)** |
| **N$1,100,001 – N$1,580,000** | **1%** of the excess over N$1,100,000 |
| **N$1,580,001 – N$3,150,000** | **N$4,800 + 5%** of the excess over N$1,580,000 |
| **N$3,150,001 – N$12,100,000** | **N$83,300 + 8%** of the excess over N$3,150,000 |
| over **N$12,100,000** | **N$799,300 + 11%** of the excess over N$12,100,000 |

- **Top marginal natural-person rate = 11%.**
- Arithmetic check: N$83,300 + 8% × (N$12,100,000 − N$3,150,000) = N$83,300 + N$716,000 = **N$799,300** ✓ (one secondary source mis-typed "799,000" — use **N$799,300**).

(Source: independently cross-confirmed by [Realtors Namibia, 14 Oct 2024](https://www.realtorsnamibia.com/news/general/navigating-the-new-transfer-duty-act-of-namibia-a-guide-for-buyers-and-sellers-14-10-24) + the IBN expat-buying guide + the Parliament Transfer Duty Bill schedule — regulated/advisory ×2 + the primary Bill. **Commencement 1 October 2024** is corroborated by [PwC Namibia (18 Dec 2025)](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/republic-of-namibia/corporate/other-taxes) + multiple Namibian law firms; to cite the exact Government Gazette notice number, verify at [NamRA](https://www.namra.org.na/).)

### Transfer duty — COMPANIES / CLOSE CORPORATIONS / TRUSTS (non-natural persons)

- **Flat 12%** of acquisition value (unchanged by the 2024 reform).
- **11% (top natural-person marginal) and 12% (entity flat) are DIFFERENT figures — do not conflate.**

### Stamp duty (immovable property)

- **NAD 12 for every NAD 1,000 (or part thereof)** of value/consideration on the transfer deed.
- **Natural-person exemption raised to N$1.1m** (Stamp Duties Amendment Act 7 of 2024, eff. 1 October 2024).

(Source: [PwC Namibia, 18 Dec 2025](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/republic-of-namibia/corporate/other-taxes) — regulated-tier; corroborated by WKH Inc.)

### VAT

- **Standard rate 15%** (with 0%-rated + exempt categories) (source: [PwC Namibia, 18 Dec 2025](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/republic-of-namibia/corporate/other-taxes)).
- **Residential-resale vs commercial/new-build VAT split at parcel level — data not detailed at primary source this pass — verify at [NamRA](https://www.namra.org.na/) / with a Namibian tax advisor.** (As a structural pattern across comparable jurisdictions, residential resale typically attracts transfer duty not VAT, while a registered vendor's commercial/new-build sale may attract VAT — but this is NOT primary-confirmed for Namibia here; do not rely on it.)

### Capital Gains Tax

- **NO CGT on ordinary assets** — PwC verbatim: "capital gains are **not taxed** in Namibia." This includes **residential property held as a capital asset**.

### Inheritance / estate / gift / wealth taxes

- **NONE.** PwC verbatim: "There are **no inheritance, estate, or gift taxes** in Namibia" and "There are **no net wealth/worth taxes** in Namibia" (source: [PwC Namibia — Other taxes (individual), 18 Dec 2025](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/republic-of-namibia/individual/other-taxes)).

## Section: `--currency`

- **Currency**: **Namibia Dollar (N$ / NAD)**.
- **Peg**: **1:1 with the South African Rand (ZAR)** as a member of the **Common Monetary Area (CMA)** (Namibia, South Africa, Lesotho, Eswatini). Namibia has **no independent monetary policy** and tracks the South African Reserve Bank (source: [Bank of Namibia Exchange Control FAQ](https://www.bon.com.na/Informations/FAQ/Exchange-Control.aspx) — primary/central-bank).
- **Legal tender**: **both NAD and ZAR are legal tender inside Namibia** and circulate interchangeably at par (source: [BoN Exchange Control FAQ](https://www.bon.com.na/Informations/FAQ/Exchange-Control.aspx)).
- **Exchange-control authority**: "Exchange Control is the responsibility of the **Ministry of Finance**, which has largely delegated this authority to the **Bank of Namibia**," which in turn delegates to **Authorised Dealers** (commercial banks) and ADLAs (source: [BoN Exchange Control FAQ](https://www.bon.com.na/Informations/FAQ/Exchange-Control.aspx)).
- **Cross-CMA vs outside-CMA flows**: transactions **within the CMA** (Namibia ↔ South Africa / Lesotho / Eswatini) are **not subject to exchange control**; flows **outside the CMA** are **exchange-controlled** and subject to AML cross-border disclosure. Non-resident investors' equity investments are broadly **not subject to exchange-control restrictions**, and local sale/redemption proceeds on non-resident-owned assets may be **regarded as freely remittable or used freely within the CMA** (source: regulated/legal-practitioner tier — [Namibweb Exchange Control summary](https://www.namibweb.com/exc.htm); [Cronjé Inc — Exchange Control](https://www.cronjelaw.com/exchange-control/) — quote but cross-check).
- **Non-resident PROPERTY-SALE repatriation OUTSIDE the CMA**: this is **exchange-controlled via Authorised Dealers** — but the **exact step-by-step procedure/forms for repatriating residential-property-sale proceeds outside the CMA are NOT publicly itemised**. **Verify at the [Bank of Namibia Exchange Control Division / Currency-and-Exchanges manual](https://www.bon.com.na/)** — do NOT assert specific forms. The structural rule (bring funds in through proper channels via an Authorised Dealer to preserve the outward-repatriation route) holds; the precise mechanics must be confirmed with the Authorised Dealer + conveyancer **before** purchase.
- **Practical effect for buyers**: a **South African (ZAR) buyer faces essentially zero FX friction** (intra-CMA, par peg, no exchange control). A **buyer from outside the CMA** (USD/EUR/GBP) must route funds through an Authorised Dealer and plan the repatriation channel up front. **Devaluation risk** = ZAR risk: the NAD inherits the rand's volatility against hard currencies, since it is pegged to and tracks the ZAR (the peg to the rand is firm; the rand's own value floats).

## Caveats unique to NA

- **Foreign ownership is NOT banned for urban/residential freehold.** The constitutional default (Art. 16(1)) is open to "all persons"; Art. 16(2) only **permits** Parliament to restrict non-citizens. The **one enacted restriction is on agricultural (commercial) land** (Act 6/1995 s.58(1)(a) — Ministerial consent + State preferent right). **Do NOT write a blanket foreign-ownership ban.**
- **The consolidating Land Bill is a BILL, NOT law (as of 2026-06-13).** Re-tabled in the National Assembly in **October 2025** (Minister Inge Zaamwani), awaiting debate; it would prohibit foreigners from acquiring **communal AND commercial land** (leasing-only exception). **Confirm enactment status at signing** — if enacted, the foreign-acquisition regime changes materially. (Tracked in `shared/regulatory-watch.md` — add an entry.)
- **NAD is pegged 1:1 to ZAR (Common Monetary Area); both NAD and ZAR are legal tender in Namibia.** No independent monetary policy. **Repatriation OUTSIDE the CMA is exchange-controlled by the Bank of Namibia** (exact procedure not publicly itemised — verify with the Authorised Dealer + BoN before purchase).
- **Transfer duty (eff. 1 Oct 2024): natural persons 0% to N$1.1m, sliding to top 11%; entities flat 12%.** 11% (top natural) and 12% (entity) are **different figures — do not conflate**. Buying the SPV that owns a residential property now triggers transfer duty.
- **NO CGT (ordinary assets), NO inheritance/estate/gift tax, NO wealth tax** — but property-trading profits can be taxed as **income**, and mining/petroleum-licence gains ARE taxed.
- **NO citizenship-by-investment.** Residence-by-investment only (advisory thresholds — verify at NIPDB).
- **Operative deeds law = Deeds Registries Act 47 of 1937**, NOT the 2015 Act (never validly commenced — set aside by the High Court in 2021). **Do NOT cite the 2015 Act as in force.**
- **Sectional Titles Act 2 of 2009** governs apartments/units (confirmed at NamibLII).
- **Final court of appeal = Supreme Court of Namibia** (also the constitutional court). Legal system = **Roman-Dutch common law + customary + constitutional.**
- **Drought / water-supply security is the dominant climate-cost factor** (national HIGH per ThinkHazard!) — Windhoek relies partly on direct potable water reclamation (Goreangab). Budget for supply interruptions + tariff escalation.
- **National flood "HIGH" is a screening flag, not a parcel claim** — interior Windhoek (~1,700 m plateau) and the low-lying coast (Walvis Bay / low Swakopmund) differ enormously. Cross-check municipal flood mapping per parcel.
- **Net electricity importer**: Namibian power security is partly tied to the regional grid (South African Power Pool). Consider standby power for continuous-occupancy parcels.
- **Investor/residence thresholds are advisory, NOT gov-confirmed** — every NAD 2m / 3.95m / 10m figure and the Digital Nomad Visa numbers must be verified at [NIPDB](https://www.nipdb.com/) + the Ministry of Home Affairs before relying.
- **Wire-fraud against international buyers**: verify conveyancer trust-account wire instructions verbally via an independently-sourced phone number before sending funds.
- **Country-profile basics (population, GDP/capita, area, exact region count)** were **not primary-verified this pass** — verify at [NSA](https://nsa.org.na/) / [gov.na](https://www.gov.na/).
