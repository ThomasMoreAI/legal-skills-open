# Pakistan 🇵🇰 — Property Due-Diligence Playbook

ISO2: `pk`. Status: ✅ Fully populated (researched 2026-06-13).

> **🇵🇰 Critical framing — read first.** Pakistan's property regime is **provincial at the land-record layer** — there is **no national cadastre**. Land records sit with each province's **Board of Revenue** (Punjab, Sindh, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Balochistan) + the Islamabad Capital Territory; **Punjab is the most digitised** ([Punjab Land Records Authority / PLRA, punjab-zameen.gov.pk](https://www.punjab-zameen.gov.pk/), under the PLRA Act 2017), the other provinces materially less so — **a per-province caveat is mandatory on every land-record claim**. Title is a **Record of Rights** (the mutation / *intiqal* entry in the *jamabandi* / *fard* + a registered deed under the **Registration Act 1908**), **presumptive, NOT a Torrens guaranteed title**. **The practical foreign-buyer market is overwhelmingly the DIASPORA** — non-resident Pakistanis (NRPs) + Pakistani Origin Card (POC) holders — channelled through the **State Bank of Pakistan's Roshan Digital Account (RDA) → Roshan Apna Ghar** scheme ([sbp.org.pk/RDAC/Ghar.html](https://www.sbp.org.pk/RDAC/Ghar.html), eligibility "Non Resident Pakistanis (NRPs) and Pakistani Origin Card (POC) holders"). **Non-origin foreign nationals** need **prior Federal-Government / Ministry-of-Interior approval to acquire immovable property**, under the **1980 Order made under Section 3 of the Foreigners Act, 1946** ([Foreigners Act 1946 — fia.gov.pk](https://fia.gov.pk/files/act/4.pdf)); **cantonment / border / diplomatic-enclave zones require security clearance**. Currency: **Pakistani Rupee (PKR / ₨)** is a **market-determined / managed float, NOT a peg** (under an active **IMF Extended Fund Facility** — verify SBP regime + IMF classification), with **exchange controls** via the SBP **Foreign Exchange Manual** governing repatriation. Tax stack headline: **FBR advance tax §236K on purchase (filer 1.5–2.5%) + §236C on sale (filer 4.5–5.5%) + CGT under §37(1A) Income Tax Ordinance 2001** — but **FBR property rates change with EVERY Finance Act; the figures here are Finance Act 2025 and you MUST "verify current Finance Act"**. **Final court of appeal for civil/property matters = Supreme Court of Pakistan** ([supremecourt.gov.pk](https://www.supremecourt.gov.pk/)); **constitutional jurisdiction was moved to the new Federal Constitutional Court by the 27th Amendment (signed 13 Nov 2025)** — verify current allocation at [na.gov.pk](https://na.gov.pk/). **Top hazards: earthquake + flood** (both have primary government mapping — PMD seismic, NDMA flood/atlas). **NO citizenship-by-investment** (a residency-only **Foreigner (Long Term Residency) Order, 2025** exists — residency, not nationality). Confirm any current FBR rate, provincial stamp duty, Interior NOC requirement, RDA eligibility, and repatriation mechanics at primary sources **before any binding step**. Authoritative primary sources: **[State Bank of Pakistan (SBP)](https://www.sbp.org.pk/)**, **[Federal Board of Revenue (FBR)](https://www.fbr.gov.pk/)**, **[Board of Investment (BoI) — invest.gov.pk](https://invest.gov.pk/)**, **[NDMA](https://www.ndma.gov.pk/)**, **[Pakistan Meteorological Department (PMD)](https://www.pmd.gov.pk/)**, **[NADRA](https://www.nadra.gov.pk/)**, the relevant **provincial Board of Revenue**, and the **Ministry of Interior**.

## Country profile

- **Geography / admin levels**: Federal parliamentary republic. **4 provinces** — **Punjab**, **Sindh**, **Khyber Pakhtunkhwa (KP)**, **Balochistan** — plus the **Islamabad Capital Territory (ICT)** and administered areas (**Azad Jammu & Kashmir**, **Gilgit-Baltistan**). **Land records are PROVINCIAL** (each province's Board of Revenue), analogous to Australian states — there is **NO single national cadastre**. Each province runs its own land-record body; ICT has its own arrangements. **Per-province caveat is mandatory on every parcel claim.**
- **Population**: **~235.95 million** (2024, World Bank — verify at [data.worldbank.org SP.POP.TOTL?locations=PK](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SP.POP.TOTL?locations=PK)). 5th most populous country. Various aggregators report a 2026 figure of ~241–259M; **do NOT cite a 2026 point figure as fact — use the 2024 WB anchor**.
- **GDP per capita**: **~US$1,485** (2024, World Bank current US$ — verify at [data.worldbank.org NY.GDP.PCAP.CD?locations=PK](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/NY.GDP.PCAP.CD?locations=PK)). Other aggregators report US$1,578–1,643; treat as the **lower-middle-income band**. Use the WB current-US$ figure as the anchor.
- **Currency**: **Pakistani Rupee (PKR / ₨)**. **Market-determined / managed float** under an active IMF programme — **NOT a peg**. 2024 average ~278.5 PKR/USD; 2024 high ~281.95 ([2024 data, exchange-rates.org aggregator — for the live rate verify sbp.org.pk](https://www.sbp.org.pk/)). IMF Country Report material notes the rupee has been **broadly stable vs USD since January 2024** — i.e. de-facto closer to a stabilised arrangement than a clean float during 2024–2026; **verify the current SBP regime + IMF AREAER classification**.
- **Legal system**: **Common-law-based** (English common-law legacy) overlaid with **Islamic law** — a mixed system. **Final court of appeal for civil/criminal (incl. property) matters = Supreme Court of Pakistan** ([supremecourt.gov.pk](https://www.supremecourt.gov.pk/)). **Constitutional interpretation, federal–provincial disputes, and fundamental-rights enforcement were transferred to a new Federal Constitutional Court (FCC) by the 27th Amendment** (passed by the National Assembly 12 Nov 2025; ratified & signed by the President 13 Nov 2025; new Article 175E) — **verify the exact Article 175E text + current allocation at [na.gov.pk](https://na.gov.pk/) / the Gazette** (the constitutional-court change is corroborated by multiple secondary sources but the operative Article text should be confirmed at primary). **No external / Privy Council appeal.**
- **Languages**: **Urdu (national)**, **English (official)** — English is used in the higher courts, statutes, and contracts, and is the practical language of land statutes and deeds (useful for foreign buyers). Plus regional languages (Punjabi, Sindhi, Pashto / *Pashtō*, Balochi, Saraiki).
- **Title concept**: the operative ownership record is the **mutation (*intiqal*) entry in the Record of Rights** (*jamabandi* / *fard*) at the provincial revenue authority — a **record of rights (presumptive), NOT a Torrens guaranteed-title register**. **Registration of the deed** (sale deed / registry) under the **Registration Act 1908** is a separate step from mutation. **Both matter.** Governing statutes also include the **Transfer of Property Act 1882** — verify primary text per province.
- **Cadastre / land registry — PROVINCIAL (no national registry)**:
  - **Punjab**: **Punjab Land Records Authority (PLRA)** under the **PLRA Act 2017**, with **Arazi Record Centres**; digitised *fard* / mutation records — [punjab-zameen.gov.pk](https://www.punjab-zameen.gov.pk/) (the portal is JS-heavy; verify on the ground).
  - **Sindh / KP / Balochistan**: respective **Boards of Revenue** — materially less digitised than Punjab; verify the parcel record in person.
  - **ICT (Islamabad)**: own land-record arrangements (verify with the ICT administration / CDA for scheme land).
- **Identifier**: there is **no universal national parcel ID**. Parcels are referenced by *khasra* / *khatauni* numbers in the Record of Rights at the provincial Board of Revenue, plus scheme-specific plot numbers in approved housing schemes (DHA, Bahria Town, etc.). **Verify per province + per scheme.**
- **Title concept (ownership types)**: **freehold** dominant in approved housing schemes + most urban resale; **allotment / lease from a development authority** (DHA, CDA, Bahria Town) common in planned schemes; **agricultural / rural land** is governed by provincial land-revenue law and is **subject to foreign-acquisition restrictions** (see Foreign buyer eligibility).
- **Recent reforms (12-month watchlist)**:
  - **27th Amendment to the Constitution (signed 13 Nov 2025)** — created the **Federal Constitutional Court** (Art. 175E); repositioned the Supreme Court primarily as the final appellate court for civil/criminal matters. Two senior SC justices resigned 13 Nov 2025 in protest (secondary — JURIST). **Verify operative text at [na.gov.pk](https://na.gov.pk/) / Gazette of Pakistan.**
  - **Foreigner (Long Term Residency) Order, 2025** — government-approved ~September 2025; residency-by-investment (residency only, NOT citizenship); BoI live page at [invest.gov.pk/long-term-residency](https://invest.gov.pk/long-term-residency). Digital portal "going live soon."
  - **Finance Act 2025** (effective 1 July 2025) — current FBR §236K / §236C advance-tax slabs (see `--tax`); **FBR property rates change with every Finance Act — always verify current.**
  - **IMF 37-month Extended Fund Facility (EFF) + Resilience & Sustainability Facility (RSF)** — Third EFF Review / Second RSF Review completed by the IMF Executive Board **8 May 2026** (IMF press release PR-26147 + IMF Country Report No. 26/101, May 2026 — verify at [imf.org](https://www.imf.org/en/Countries/PAK)). Exchange-rate flexibility is the IMF programme's "primary shock absorber."

## 🇵🇰 Foreign buyer eligibility (READ FIRST)

This section determines whether a transaction is even legally possible — and which process applies. **The realistic foreign-buyer market is the diaspora** (NRPs + POC holders) via the SBP Roshan Digital Account → Roshan Apna Ghar; **non-origin foreign nationals face a multi-gate approval regime** and fall outside the diaspora digital route.

### Legal basis

- **Foreigners Act, 1946 (Act XXXI of 1946), Section 3** — the statutory hook under which the Federal Government issued a **1980 Order** requiring foreign nationals to obtain **prior Federal-Government / Ministry-of-Interior approval** before acquiring or holding immovable property. ([Foreigners Act 1946 — fia.gov.pk](https://fia.gov.pk/files/act/4.pdf) PRIMARY; the **Foreigners Order 1951** is the companion control instrument — Refworld hosts both.) ⚠️ **The 1980 Order's own gazette text / SRO number is not publicly indexed** — verify the operative SRO at the **Ministry of Interior** ([interior.gov.pk](https://www.interior.gov.pk/)).
- **Registration Act 1908** + **Transfer of Property Act 1882** — conveyancing + registration (English common-law derivation), applied per province.
- **State Bank of Pakistan Foreign Exchange Regulation** — the SBP **Foreign Exchange Manual** governs the inward-investment FX channel + repatriation ([sbp.org.pk/fe_manual](https://www.sbp.org.pk/fe_manual/)).
- **Roshan Digital Account (RDA) / Roshan Apna Ghar** — SBP scheme for the diaspora property buyer ([sbp.org.pk/RDAC/Ghar.html](https://www.sbp.org.pk/RDAC/Ghar.html)).
- **Pakistan Cantonment Property Rules 1957 / Cantonments Act** — govern cantonment (military) land; foreign acquisition in cantonments/border/diplomatic-enclave zones requires security clearance. ⚠️ The **exact foreign-buyer restriction was not read from primary text** this pass — verify primary via the relevant **Cantonment Board / Military Lands & Cantonments**.

### Buyer category matrix

| Buyer category | Right |
|---|---|
| **Pakistani citizen (resident)** | Full ownership; standard provincial mutation + registration |
| **Pakistani citizen abroad — NICOP holder** (retains citizenship) | Full citizen property rights |
| **Pakistani-origin foreigner — POC holder** (former citizen / person of Pakistani origin) | **Buy / sell / own / manage property** — the cleanest diaspora ownership status (NADRA / MoFA — primary issuer; [nadra.gov.pk](https://www.nadra.gov.pk/), [mofa.gov.pk](https://www.mofa.gov.pk/)); **POC ≠ citizenship** |
| **Non-origin foreign national** | Can acquire **only with prior Federal-Government / Ministry-of-Interior approval** (1980 Order under s.3 Foreigners Act 1946); a valid **long-term visa** (tourist/short-term insufficient); **agricultural / rural land restricted** ⚠️ secondary-sourced — verify |
| **Cantonment / border / diplomatic-enclave acquisition (any foreigner)** | **Security clearance required** ⚠️ verify primary rule via the relevant Cantonment Board / Military Lands & Cantonments |

⚠️ **The non-origin-foreigner path is NOT the same as the diaspora path.** The SBP RDA / Roshan Apna Ghar scheme is open **only to NRPs + POC holders** — non-origin foreigners fall **outside** the digital diaspora route and must use the general foreign-acquisition + Interior-NOC path.

⚠️ **Single/secondary-aggregator claims to TREAT AS UNVERIFIED** (do NOT state as fact): a "300 sqm cap for foreigners in provincial capitals", a "bar on foreigners buying in Islamabad", a "$2,000 Property Purchase Permit renewable every 6 months", and "Provincial Home Department + FERA approval" all surfaced in single/secondary sources (ARAB MLS, law-firm blogs) and were **NOT corroborated at a primary instrument**. Write *"data not publicly available — verify at the Ministry of Interior + the relevant Provincial Home Department"* rather than relying on these.

### Visa-or-residency-via-property

- **NO citizenship-by-investment (CBI)** / "golden passport". CONFIRMED — the BoI Long Term Residency FAQ states it "**provides residency rights only. It does not grant nationality or citizenship**" ([invest.gov.pk/long-term-residency/FAQs](https://invest.gov.pk/long-term-residency/FAQs)).
- **Residency-by-investment EXISTS** under the **Foreigner (Long Term Residency) Order, 2025** (approved ~Sept 2025; residency only). BoI-stated figures (see `--visa` for the full, internally-unreconciled detail): a **minimum investment floor of USD 50,000** (materialized within one year via authorized banking channels; annual renewal USD 50,000) on the BoI landing page, while the BoI FAQ lists a **tier table of USD 300,000 / 400,000 / 700,000 for 5 / 7 / 10-year** durations. ⚠️ **These two BoI figures are not reconciled on BoI's own site — present both and verify which tier unlocks real-estate acquisition at [invest.gov.pk/long-term-residency](https://invest.gov.pk/long-term-residency).**
- **For the diaspora buyer**, the property pathway runs through **POC / NICOP + the RDA / Roshan Apna Ghar channel** — not a "golden visa." POC itself already confers visa-free multiple entry + indefinite stay + the right to buy/sell/own/manage property.
- **NO dedicated digital-nomad visa** surfaced at a primary source (see `--digital-nomad`).

### Compliance / AML

- **RDA channel**: KYC/AML-screened by the participating bank + SBP at account opening — this is the diaspora buyer's built-in AML gate.
- **Non-origin foreign-national transactions**: secondary sources cite a **police character certificate + proof of source of funds + Interior NOC** ⚠️ verify primary at the Ministry of Interior.
- **FATF context**: Pakistan was on the FATF "grey list" and was **removed in October 2022** (FATF plenary — secondary/well-documented); AML/CFT obligations on banks + DNFBPs remain. Verify current status at [fatf-gafi.org](https://www.fatf-gafi.org/).
- **Source-of-funds documentation** is the practical gate for repatriation — see `--currency`.

### Verification path (before any reservation)

1. **Confirm your buyer category** (citizen / NICOP / POC / non-origin foreigner) — this determines the entire process.
2. **Diaspora buyer**: open a **Roshan Digital Account** with a participating bank; confirm Roshan Apna Ghar eligibility + financing terms in writing ([sbp.org.pk/RDAC/Ghar.html](https://www.sbp.org.pk/RDAC/Ghar.html)).
3. **Non-origin foreigner**: confirm the **Ministry-of-Interior NOC** requirement + process (1980 Order under s.3 Foreigners Act 1946) **before** committing; confirm the parcel is **not in a cantonment / border / diplomatic-enclave restricted zone**.
4. **Pull the provincial Record of Rights** (*fard* / *jamabandi*) + confirm the **mutation (*intiqal*)** chain at the relevant Board of Revenue (Punjab via PLRA / Arazi Record Centre; other provinces in person).
5. **Confirm the deed is registrable** under the Registration Act 1908 + clear of encumbrances.
6. **Confirm the FX provenance plan** for repatriation (RDA channel = clean; outside it, documented inward-remittance provenance — see `--currency`).
7. **AML / source-of-funds** evidence ready before signing.

### Currency / units convention

- All prices in **PKR**; large amounts are quoted in **lakh** (100,000) and **crore** (10,000,000). **1 crore = PKR 10,000,000.**
- "Marla" + "kanal" are the traditional area units in Punjab/KP (1 kanal = 20 marla; 1 marla ≈ 25.29 m² in the standard reckoning — **verify the local marla definition, which varies**).

### STRUCTURAL CAVEAT — the DC-rate / FBR-valuation / market-rate gap

Pakistan's registered deed values are **structurally understated**. There are **three distinct numbers** for any parcel:
1. The provincial **DC rate** (administrative, historically well below market);
2. The **FBR valuation table** value (FBR has been pushing these toward market to widen the tax base);
3. The actual **market rate** (what the parcel trades at).

Buyers historically registered at the below-market "DC rate" to cut stamp duty + CGT. **Never treat a single listing or registered-deed figure as the true market price** — triangulate listing asking price, FBR valuation, and DC rate, and verify on visit.

### Compute

1. **PKR/m² = price ÷ built/plot area m²** (convert marla/kanal → m² using the verified local definition).
2. Compare to **≥3 active listings** on the same city + scheme (DHA / Bahria Town / Gulberg) + plot size — scheme + plot size drive price more than city average.
3. **Trap — DC-rate vs market**: ask which figure the seller's quoted "price" refers to; the registered value will likely be the DC/FBR figure, not the market price.
4. **Trap — approved vs unapproved scheme**: an unapproved / litigated housing scheme can trade at a steep discount for a reason — verify the scheme's approval status with the relevant development authority / Board of Revenue.
5. **Trap — file vs allotment vs possession**: "files" (allotment rights in not-yet-developed schemes) are a distinct, riskier asset from a possessed, mutated, registered plot — verify what is actually being sold.

### Key terms

- **Fard / jamabandi** — the Record of Rights extract (the operative ownership record)
- **Intiqal (mutation)** — the entry transferring ownership in the Record of Rights
- **DC rate** — provincial administrative valuation (tax floor, below market)
- **FBR valuation table** — federal tax valuation of immovable property by area
- **Marla / kanal** — traditional area units (verify local definition)
- **File** — an allotment right in an undeveloped scheme (riskier than a possessed plot)

## Section: `--traffic`

### Sources

- **There is NO public AADT (Annual Average Daily Traffic) portal** comparable to UK DfT counts or US FHWA HPMS. **OSM road class + on-the-ground observation is the practical method.**
- **[National Highway Authority (NHA)](https://nha.gov.pk/)** — national highway + motorway network (motorway/highway inventory; not a live AADT portal).
- **Provincial highway / transport departments** + city traffic police — per-province; not consistently digitised.
- **OSM `highway` tag** — fallback via Overpass.

### Coarse OSM fallback

| OSM `highway` | Pakistani road class | Typical v/d (heuristic) |
|---|---|---|
| `motorway` / `trunk` | Motorway (M-1/M-2/etc.) / National Highway | 15,000–50,000+ |
| `primary` | Major urban arterial / GT Road segments | 10,000–40,000 |
| `secondary` | City spine roads | 3,000–12,000 |
| `tertiary` / `unclassified` | Scheme feeder roads | 500–3,000 |
| `residential` | Internal scheme street | < 500 |

### Verdict bands (heuristic — calibrate with on-site visit)

- 🟢 < 1,500 v/d (gated-scheme interior street, rural lane)
- 🟡 1,500–8,000 v/d (suburban scheme spine, secondary city road)
- 🟠 8,000–25,000 v/d (major urban arterial, GT Road urban segments)
- 🔴 > 25,000 v/d (motorway segments, Karachi/Lahore core arterials at peak)

### Caveats

- **No primary AADT data** — the bands above are heuristic. Verify the specific road on visit (peak-hour observation) + check OSM class.
- **Urban congestion**: Karachi + Lahore have severe peak-hour congestion on core arterials; gated schemes (DHA, Bahria Town) self-manage internal traffic + access control.
- **Motorway network**: the M-series motorways (e.g. M-1 Islamabad–Peshawar, M-2 Islamabad–Lahore) are tolled, well-built, and a structural connectivity asset for scheme locations near interchanges.
- **Security-event road closures**: arterial closures during security operations / VIP movement are a known disruption in some cities — not predictable from traffic data.

## Section: `--tax`

> **⚠️ WARNING — FBR property tax rates change with EVERY Finance Act.** The figures below are **Finance Act 2025** (effective 1 July 2025) per the **FBR overseas FAQ** ([fbr.gov.pk/overseas-faqs/174240/174248](https://fbr.gov.pk/overseas-faqs/174240/174248) — PRIMARY gov tax authority). **ALWAYS say "verify current Finance Act"** — do NOT assume these persist into FY2026-27. **Provincial stamp duty / registration / CVT / annual property tax are PER-PROVINCE and NOT verified to a primary provincial source this pass** — see the open-gap notes below.

### Advance tax on PURCHASE — Income Tax Ordinance 2001 §236K

Collected at transfer by the registering authority. **Finance Act 2025** (FBR overseas FAQ — PRIMARY, eff. 1 July 2025):

| Fair Market Value | Filer (ATL) | Late Filer | Non-Filer |
|---|---|---|---|
| **≤ Rs. 50 million** | **1.5%** | 4.5% | 10.5% |
| **Rs. 50–100 million** | **2%** | 5.5% | 14.5% |
| **> Rs. 100 million** | **2.5%** | 6.5% | 18.5% |

### Advance tax on SALE — §236C

Collected from the seller at transfer. **Finance Act 2025** (same FBR FAQ — PRIMARY):

| Gross Consideration | Filer (ATL) | Late Filer | Non-Filer |
|---|---|---|---|
| **≤ Rs. 50 million** | **4.5%** | 7.5% | 11.5% |
| **Rs. 50–100 million** | **5%** | 8.5% | 11.5% |
| **> Rs. 100 million** | **5.5%** | 9.5% | 11.5% |

- **Overseas-Pakistani relief (PRIMARY, FBR FAQ)**: **non-resident POC / NICOP holders** (present in Pakistan <183 days/yr) **may access the FILER rate even without filing a return**, subject to FBR verification. This is a **material, citable benefit for the diaspora buyer** — confirm the current process at [fbr.gov.pk](https://www.fbr.gov.pk/).
- **§236C paid is adjustable** against final CGT liability.
- ⚠️ **ALWAYS verify the current Finance Act** — these slabs change annually.

### Capital Gains Tax (CGT) on immovable property — §37(1A) Income Tax Ordinance 2001

Statute: **Section 37(1A) of the Income Tax Ordinance, 2001** (sub-section (1A) inserted by the **Finance Act 2012**), charging gains on disposal of immovable property at the rates in **Division VIII, Part I, First Schedule**. A **March 2026 Islamabad High Court ruling** held property-sale gains taxable *only* under s.37(1A) (Business Recorder / Profit, 2026-03-25 — secondary). ⚠️ Still **"verify current Finance Act."**

- **Property acquired on/after 1 July 2024**: **flat 15% for ATL / filers, regardless of holding period** (the holding-period concept was **eliminated by Finance Act 2024**). Non-ATL → personal slab rates with a **15% minimum floor**. ([PwC Tax Summaries — Pakistan / Income determination](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/pakistan/individual/income-determination) — authoritative secondary, cross-checked to FBR-aligned figures.)
- **Property acquired on/before 30 June 2024** — holding-period slabs apply ([PwC Tax Summaries, verbatim](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/pakistan/individual/income-determination)):

| Holding period | Open plots | Constructed | Flats |
|---|---|---|---|
| **≤ 1 yr** | 15% | 15% | 15% |
| **>1–2 yr** | 12.5% | 10% | 7.5% |
| **>2–3 yr** | 10% | 7.5% | 0% |
| **>3–4 yr** | 7.5% | 5% | 0% |
| **>4–5 yr** | 5% | 0% | 0% |
| **>5–6 yr** | 2.5% | 0% | 0% |
| **>6 yr** | 0% | 0% | 0% |

### Provincial taxes (stamp duty / registration / CVT / town-transfer)

- **Stamp duty + registration fee + capital value tax (CVT, where applicable) + town/cantonment transfer fees are PROVINCIAL** and vary by province / city (Punjab, Sindh, KP, Balochistan, ICT). Commonly cited combined transfer cost is provincial stamp duty (often quoted in the low single-digit %) + registration. ⚠️ **Rates are province-specific and were NOT verified to a primary provincial source this pass** — *data not publicly available here; verify at the relevant **provincial Board of Revenue** / **Excise, Taxation & Narcotics Control** department.* **Do NOT state a provincial stamp-duty percentage as fact.**

### Annual property tax (provincial)

- A provincial **"property tax"** is levied by the provincial **Excise, Taxation & Narcotics Control** departments on the **annual rental value** of urban property; rates / valuation tables differ per province / city. ⚠️ **NOT verified to primary this pass** — *data not publicly available here; verify at the relevant provincial Excise & Taxation department.*

### Federal Excise Duty + other

- **Federal Excise Duty** has in some recent Finance Acts applied to allotment/transfer of commercial property + first allotment of plots — **status changes; verify the current Finance Act** at [fbr.gov.pk](https://www.fbr.gov.pk/).

### Seismic — HIGH exposure (load-bearing hazard)

- Pakistan sits on a highly active part of the **India–Eurasia collision zone** — **HIGH earthquake exposure**. Northern areas / **Azad Jammu & Kashmir** (2005 Kashmir quake, ~73,000+ deaths) and **Quetta / Balochistan** (1935 Quetta quake) are high-hazard zones.
- Earthquake intensity zones are defined as "**20% probability of exceedance in 50 years**" (PMD / seismic zonation — PRIMARY framing). The **Seismic Hazard Map of Pakistan** + PSHA report are the primary references ([seismic.pmd.gov.pk hazard-map](https://seismic.pmd.gov.pk/hazard-map.php) — the hazard-map page **403'd** to the fetcher; the body's PSHA report is the primary reference).
- ⚠️ **Mandatory: check the specific parcel against the PMD seismic zone + NDMA atlas — do NOT generalise a city to a parcel.**

### Flood — SEVERE recurrent (load-bearing hazard)

- Severe recurrent **riverine / monsoon flooding**. The **2022 floods** affected ~33 million people and submerged roughly a third of the country (widely documented; NDMA + UN — verify NDMA situation reports). The **2010 Indus floods** were also catastrophic.
- **Sindh + southern Punjab** (Indus basin) carry the **highest flood exposure**; flash flooding in KP / northern valleys.
- ⚠️ Verify the parcel against **NDMA flood mapping** + local drainage on visit.

### Cyclone

- **Arabian-Sea tropical cyclones** threaten the **Karachi / Sindh / Makran coast** (e.g. Cyclone Biparjoy 2023 prompted coastal evacuations — verify PMD). Coastal / low-lying Karachi also has **urban-flooding + sea-intrusion** risk.

### Other hazards

- **Drought** (Tharparkar / Balochistan); **landslides** (northern areas); **heatwaves** (Karachi 2015 heatwave ~1,200+ deaths — widely documented; verify PMD/NDMA); **GLOF** (glacial lake outburst floods) in Gilgit-Baltistan.

### Verdict driver

- **Seismic + flood are the two load-bearing hazards**; both have **PRIMARY government mapping** (PMD seismic, NDMA flood/atlas). **Always parcel-check** — do not generalise.

## Section: `--mains`

### Electricity

- **Chronic supply issues** — **load-shedding** (scheduled + unannounced) is a structural feature, worse in summer peak. Karachi's distributor is **K-Electric** (privatised); the rest of the country is served by **DISCOs** under the national grid. High tariffs + outages have been cited by the Karachi Chamber of Commerce as deterring investment ([Dawn / secondary, 2024](https://www.dawn.com/)). **Backup (UPS / inverter / solar / generator) is a normal household expectation — budget for it.**

### Water

- Urban water supply is **unreliable** and infrastructure is **aging**. Karachi: the **Karachi Water & Sewerage Corporation (KWSC)** reported a ~54 MGD shortfall after a power fault at the **Dhabeji pumping station** (the city's primary intake); main lines laid 1956–1971 are prone to ruptures ([Dawn / The Tribune, 2024 — secondary](https://www.dawn.com/)). **Tanker-water reliance + private bore / storage are common.** Lahore / Punjab are served by **WASA** bodies; **groundwater depletion** is a concern.

### Sewerage

- Municipal sewerage coverage is **incomplete**; older areas + informal settlements are underserved; **urban flooding from blocked drains** in the monsoon (especially Karachi).

### Foreign-buyer takeaway

- In any city, **verify the SPECIFIC scheme's water source** (piped vs tanker vs bore), **power backup**, and **sewerage connection ON VISIT**. Gated "approved housing schemes" (DHA, Bahria Town, etc.) typically **self-provide utilities at higher reliability** than legacy city areas — **but verify per-scheme; do NOT generalise.**

### Costs

- ⚠️ **Connection + backup costs not primary-verified this pass** — *data not publicly available here; verify with the local DISCO / K-Electric + WASA / KWSC + local contractors on visit.* Qualitatively, budget for a **UPS / inverter + battery** at minimum, and a **generator + solar** for continuous-occupancy reliability.

## Section: `--currency`

- **Currency**: **Pakistani Rupee (PKR / ₨)** — a **managed float** (market-determined since ~2023 IMF-programme reforms). **NOT a peg.** 2024 average ~278.5 PKR/USD ([2024, exchange-rates.org aggregator — live rate at sbp.org.pk](https://www.sbp.org.pk/)). IMF Country Report material notes the rupee has been **broadly stable vs USD since January 2024** — de-facto closer to a stabilised arrangement during 2024–2026; **verify the current SBP regime + IMF AREAER classification.**
- **IMF programme**: Pakistan is under an active **37-month Extended Fund Facility (EFF) + 28-month Resilience & Sustainability Facility (RSF)**; the **Third EFF Review / Second RSF Review was completed by the IMF Executive Board on 8 May 2026** (IMF PR-26147 + IMF Country Report No. 26/101 — verify at [imf.org PAK](https://www.imf.org/en/Countries/PAK)). The IMF programme treats **exchange-rate flexibility as the "primary shock absorber."**
- **Capital controls**: Pakistan operates **EXCHANGE CONTROLS** via the SBP **Foreign Exchange Manual** ([sbp.org.pk/fe_manual](https://www.sbp.org.pk/fe_manual/) — PRIMARY). Outward FX is regulated; the rupee is **not freely convertible** for residents.
- **Repatriation of property proceeds — the key rule**:
  - **RDA / Roshan Apna Ghar route**: "**Investments made in Pakistan through RDAs are fully repatriable and subject to full and final taxation**" ([SBP, sbp.org.pk/RDAC/Ghar.html — PRIMARY, verbatim](https://www.sbp.org.pk/RDAC/Ghar.html)). This is the **clean, guaranteed-repatriable channel.** ⚠️ The SBP page states this **general rule** for RDA investments; it is **understood to extend to RDA-financed property proceeds, but that is an INFERENCE, not a verbatim SBP itemization** — get bank/SBP confirmation in writing pre-purchase.
  - **General foreign-investment route (outside RDA)**: repatriation of disinvestment / capital-gains proceeds runs through SBP-regulated rupee accounts (notably the **Special Convertible Rupee Account / SCRA** for securities), and the Authorized Dealer (bank) allows the remittance only on submission of documentation **proving the original investment came in as foreign exchange**. ⚠️ **The exact SBP FE-Manual chapter+clause for IMMOVABLE-property sale-proceed repatriation by a non-resident (vs the securities/SCRA rule) was NOT pinned to one clause this pass** — *data not pinned to a single primary URL; confirm the operative FE-Manual clause for immovable property with the Authorized Dealer bank / SBP before buying.* Practical rule of thumb: **repatriability depends on documented inward-remittance provenance** of the purchase funds.
- **Takeaway**: clean repatriability exists **IF you buy through RDA / Roshan Apna Ghar**; **outside that channel, repatriation depends on documented inward-remittance provenance + SBP rules** — get bank/SBP confirmation in writing **BEFORE buying**. **FX / repatriation risk is the dominant financial risk for a foreign buyer.**

## Caveats unique to PK

- **Land records are PROVINCIAL, not federal** — there is **no national cadastre**. **Punjab (PLRA) is digitised; Sindh / KP / Balochistan are materially less so.** **Always give a per-province caveat** and verify the parcel record at the relevant Board of Revenue.
- **Title = mutation (*intiqal*) in the Record of Rights (*fard* / *jamabandi*) + a registered deed (Registration Act 1908)** — a **record of rights (presumptive), NOT a Torrens guaranteed title.** Verify **both** mutation and registration.
- **The realistic foreign-buyer market is the DIASPORA** via the SBP RDA → Roshan Apna Ghar (NRPs + POC holders **only**). **Non-origin foreigners fall outside this digital route** and need **prior Federal-Government / Ministry-of-Interior approval** to acquire property (1980 Order under s.3 Foreigners Act 1946).
- **Cantonment / border / diplomatic-enclave zones require security clearance** ⚠️ exact rule not read from primary text — verify via the relevant Cantonment Board / Military Lands & Cantonments.
- **FBR property tax rates change with EVERY Finance Act** — the §236K / §236C / CGT figures here are **Finance Act 2025**; **never state a future-year FBR rate without re-checking the current Finance Act at [fbr.gov.pk](https://www.fbr.gov.pk/).**
- **Provincial stamp duty / registration / CVT / annual property tax are PER-PROVINCE and unverified here** — verify at the relevant provincial Board of Revenue / Excise & Taxation department. **Do NOT state a provincial percentage as fact.**
- **The DC-rate / FBR-valuation / market-rate gap** is a structural feature of registered deed values — **never treat a single listing or deed figure as the true market price.**
- **PKR is a managed float, NOT a peg**, under an active IMF EFF (de-facto broadly stable vs USD since Jan 2024). **Capital controls** apply via the SBP Foreign Exchange Manual. **Repatriation is CLEAN only through the RDA / Roshan Apna Ghar channel**; outside it, it depends on documented inward-remittance provenance. **FX / repatriation risk is the dominant financial risk for a foreign buyer.**
- **Final court of appeal for civil/property matters = Supreme Court of Pakistan**; **constitutional jurisdiction moved to the new Federal Constitutional Court by the 27th Amendment (signed 13 Nov 2025)** — verify the current allocation at [na.gov.pk](https://na.gov.pk/).
- **NO citizenship-by-investment.** A residency-only **Foreigner (Long Term Residency) Order, 2025** exists; **its two published USD figures (USD 50,000 floor vs USD 300k/400k/700k tiers) are NOT reconciled on BoI's own site** — present both and verify.
- **Security geography matters**: **KP + Balochistan carry materially elevated terrorism / insurgency risk**; Karachi's load-bearing local risk is **STREET CRIME** (CPLC: 66,530 incidents Jan–Nov 2024). The diaspora market concentrates in **Lahore / Islamabad / urban Karachi**.
- **Seismic + flood are the two load-bearing hazards** — both have primary government mapping (PMD seismic, NDMA flood/atlas). **Always parcel-check.**
- **Chronic load-shedding + unreliable water** — backup power (UPS / inverter / solar / generator) + a verified water source are normal expectations; gated approved schemes self-provide at higher reliability (verify per-scheme).
- **"File" trading**: allotment "files" in undeveloped schemes are a distinct, riskier asset from a possessed, mutated, registered plot — verify what is being sold.
