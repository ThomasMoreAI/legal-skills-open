# Antigua and Barbuda 🇦🇬 — Property Due-Diligence Playbook

ISO2: `ag`. Status: ✅ Fully populated (researched 2026-05-28).

> **Critical framing — read first**: Antigua and Barbuda is an **English common-law twin-island state** in the Eastern Caribbean Leeward Islands (Commonwealth realm; King Charles III head of state via Governor-General; independent from the UK since 1981 — NOT a UK or EU territory). The currency **East Caribbean Dollar (XCD / EC$) is pegged at EC$2.70 = US$1.00** — a fixed peg held since 1976 and administered by the **[Eastern Caribbean Central Bank (ECCB)](https://www.eccb-centralbank.org/)** across the eight-member currency union. **Foreign (non-citizen) buyers CAN own freehold land**, but a purchase **outside an approved Citizenship-by-Investment (CBI) development** requires a **Non-Citizens Land Holding Licence (NCLHL)** under the **Non-Citizens Land Holding Regulation Act, CAP. 293** — commonly cited at **~5% of value** (*secondary — verify at [legalaffairs.gov.ag](https://legalaffairs.gov.ag/)*) with ~3–6 month processing. **Antigua and Barbuda DOES operate a Citizenship-by-Investment programme** (distinguish from Barbados, which does not): the real-estate route requires a **minimum US$300,000 investment in a government-approved project** with a **5-year hold** before resale, and the National Development Fund (NDF) donation route is **US$230,000** for a single applicant or family of up to 4 (**effective 1 August 2024** under the CBI (Amendment) Regulations 2024, No. 50 of 2024 — both VERIFIED at [cip.gov.ag](https://cip.gov.ag/)). **No personal income tax since April 2016** (widely reported — *verify at IRD / Ministry of Finance*). Transfer stamp duty is commonly cited at **seller 7.5% / buyer 2.5%** (*secondary / IRD — verify*); annual property tax **~0.1–0.5%** of assessed value (*secondary — verify at IRD*). **Hurricane risk is the dominant due-diligence axis**: Antigua and Barbuda sits in the Atlantic hurricane belt; **Hurricane Irma (Sept 2017, Cat 5) destroyed/damaged ~95% of buildings on Barbuda** and forced the evacuation of its entire population. **Barbuda is a fundamentally different and contested land regime** — historically held in COMMON (communal, no individual freehold), with post-Irma leasehold introduced to enable lending — **do NOT assume Barbuda land is freehold**. **Confirm** any current CBI threshold, NCLHL fee, IRD tax rate, and Barbuda tenure status at primary sources **before any binding step**. Authoritative primaries: **[Citizenship by Investment Unit (CIU)](https://cip.gov.ag/)**, **[Ministry of Justice & Legal Affairs](https://legalaffairs.gov.ag/)** (TLS-cert error observed 2026-05-28 — cite carefully), **[Land Registry Department (Landfolio — in-office search only)](https://legalaffairs.gov.ag/detail_page.php?page=landRegistry.php)**, **[Inland Revenue Department](https://ab.gov.ag/)**, **[Eastern Caribbean Supreme Court](https://www.eccourts.org/)**, **[ECCB](https://www.eccb-centralbank.org/)**.

## Country profile

- **Geography**: Twin-island sovereign state, Eastern Caribbean (Leeward Islands). **Antigua** (main island, ~280 km²), **Barbuda** (~160 km², sparsely populated, devastated by Hurricane Irma September 2017), plus the small uninhabited dependency of Redonda. Capital **St John's** (Antigua). V.C. Bird International Airport (ANU) is the regional hub.
- **Population**: **~94,000** (est. — *verify current at [statistics.gov.ag](https://statistics.gov.ag/) / World Bank*). Barbuda's resident population is small (~1,500–1,800 pre-Irma) and was evacuated entirely after Irma; partial return ongoing.
- **GDP profile**: tourism-dependent economy — **tourism ≈ ~60% of GDP** directly + indirectly (*secondary — verify*). CBI inflows are a material fiscal pillar. High-income-band Caribbean SIDS.
- **Currency**: **East Caribbean Dollar (XCD / EC$)** — **pegged at EC$2.70 = US$1.00 since 1976**, administered by the **[Eastern Caribbean Central Bank (ECCB)](https://www.eccb-centralbank.org/)** across the OECS eight-member union (*peg widely cited; primary = ECCB — verify*). **USD is widely accepted** in tourism + CBI property transactions; high-end deals are typically **USD-quoted**.
- **Languages**: **English (official, sole)** — all statutes, deeds, and contracts in English. Antiguan Creole is the everyday vernacular; legal/professional life is standard English.
- **Legal system**: **English common law** + Antiguan statutes. Member state of the **[Eastern Caribbean Supreme Court (ECSC)](https://www.eccourts.org/)** — superior court of record for the OECS, comprising the **High Court** (resident High Court division sits in Antigua) + the itinerant **Court of Appeal**. **Final appeal is to the Judicial Committee of the Privy Council (JCPC, London)** — Antigua and Barbuda has NOT adopted the Caribbean Court of Justice (CCJ) as its final appellate court (AG, with GD, rejected CCJ accession in a 2018 referendum) (VERIFIED — audit-cbi.md, 2026-05-28).
- **Conveyancing / land registry**: **Torrens-style registered title** under the **Registered Land Act, No. 17 of 1975** (brought all Antigua land — Crown + private — under registered ownership). System runs on **Landfolio** software (since 2004); **searches are conducted at the Land Registry Department — the Registry lists an online "Public Access Module" as a stated future aim, so no public parcel / instrument / owner / address / map search is available online** ([Ministry of Justice & Legal Affairs — Land Registry](https://legalaffairs.gov.ag/detail_page.php?page=landRegistry.php), 2026-09-18 verified). Title registration also governed by the **[Title by Registration Act, CAP. 429](https://laws.gov.ag/wp-content/uploads/2018/08/cap-429.pdf)** *(2026-09-18 verified, source Laws of Antigua and Barbuda)*. The Land Registry sits under the **[Ministry of Justice & Legal Affairs](https://legalaffairs.gov.ag/)** (2026-09-18: `legalaffairs.gov.ag` serves HTTP 200 with a valid certificate — earlier TLS error cleared; the `lands.gov.ag` Landfolio host has no DNS A record, checked 2026-09-18).
- **Identifier**: **registered parcel / instrument number** under the Registered Land Act 1975 (Landfolio). No French-style numéro-de-parcelle universal cadastral ID, but Landfolio provides parcel-level lookup.
- **Title concept**: **Freehold (fee simple)** dominant on Antigua (registered title). **Barbuda is a distinct regime** — historically **communal / common ownership** (no individual freehold), with post-Irma leasehold introduced; treat as a separate, contested system (see `--risks` + Caveats).
- **Recent reforms (12-month watchlist)**:
  - **CBI (Amendment) Regulations 2024 (No. 50 of 2024)** — raised the **NDF donation to US$230,000** (single applicant / family of up to 4), **effective 1 August 2024** (Antigua took a 30-day extension past the OECS 30 June 2024 deadline) (VERIFIED — cip.gov.ag NDF page + IMI Daily on the gazetted amendment, audit-cbi.md 2026-05-28). Real-estate minimum **US$300,000** with 5-year hold (VERIFIED — cip.gov.ag/real-estate/).
  - **OECS five-state CBI floor agreement (2024)** — AG/DM/GD/KN/LC Memorandum of Agreement to set minimum CBI pricing floors across the region; AG's increases align with this framework.
  - **No personal income tax since April 2016** — abolished; still in force per multiple 2024–2026 tax guides (*verify at IRD — this is a headline attraction so primary confirmation matters*).
  - **Barbuda Land Act / post-Irma tenure** — legal changes introduced leasehold post-Irma to enable banks/insurers to lend on Barbuda; status remains politically contested (*verify current Barbuda Land Act status*).

## 🇦🇬 Foreign buyer eligibility (READ FIRST)

This section determines whether a transaction is even legally possible — and which process applies. Antigua and Barbuda has **two distinct foreign-buyer routes**: (1) buy **inside an approved CBI development** (US$300,000+ → citizenship + streamlined ownership), or (2) buy **outside the CBI route** and obtain a **Non-Citizens Land Holding Licence (NCLHL)** under CAP. 293.

### Legal basis

- **Registered Land Act, No. 17 of 1975** — Torrens-style registered title (Landfolio).
- **Non-Citizens Land Holding Regulation Act, CAP. 293** — governs the licence a non-citizen must hold to own land outside the CBI route. (PDF hosted on the iGuides / UNCTAD portal `admin.theiguides.org` — *verify against [laws.gov.ag — Non-Citizens Land Holding (Amendment) Act 2020](https://laws.gov.ag/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/No.-32-Non-Citizens-Land-Holding-Regulations-Amendment-Act-2020.pdf) primary text*.)
- **Citizenship by Investment Act + CBI Regulations** (incl. the **[CBI (Amendment) Regulations 2024, No. 50 of 2024](https://cip.gov.ag/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Antigua-and-Barbuda-Citizenship-By-Investment-Amendment-Regulations-2024.pdf)**) — administered by the **[Citizenship by Investment Unit (CIU)](https://cip.gov.ag/)**.
- **Stamp duty / property-tax statutes** — administered by the **Inland Revenue Department (IRD)** under the Ministry of Finance.

### Buyer category matrix

| Buyer category | Right |
|---|---|
| **Antiguan & Barbudan citizen** | Full freehold; standard registered-title conveyancing |
| **Non-citizen buying INSIDE an approved CBI development** | Full freehold within the development; the development carries the foreign-ownership approval, and the purchase is handled within the CBI process (*confirm whether a separate NCLHL is waived for that specific purchase with the CIU + attorney — sources imply CBI-approved purchases are handled within the CBI process*) |
| **Non-citizen buying OUTSIDE the CBI route** | Freehold permitted **only after obtaining a Non-Citizens Land Holding Licence (NCLHL)** under CAP. 293 — property-specific, ~3–6 month processing, fee commonly cited **~5% of value** (*secondary — verify at legalaffairs.gov.ag*) |
| **Buyer of Barbuda land** | ⚠️ **Distinct/contested regime** — Barbuda land was historically held in COMMON (no individual freehold); post-Irma leasehold introduced. Do NOT assume freehold; obtain specialist Barbuda-specific legal advice and verify current Barbuda Land Act status before relying |

(Source: cip.gov.ag/real-estate/ [PRIMARY, CBI] + Non-Citizens Land Holding Regulation Act CAP. 293 [statute] + Henley & Partners / Global Citizen Solutions [secondary, NCLHL fee] — *NCLHL fee and CBI-exemption mechanics verify with attorney + CIU before contract*)

### Visa / residency via property — CBI route

| Route | Threshold (verify at CIU) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **CBI — Real Estate** | **US$300,000** in a government-approved project | Citizenship; **5-year hold** before resale (unless rolled into another approved A&B project). VERIFIED at cip.gov.ag/real-estate/ |
| **CBI — National Development Fund (NDF) donation** | **US$230,000** (single applicant / family of up to 4), **effective 1 Aug 2024** | Non-refundable donation; up from US$100,000. VERIFIED (CBI (Amendment) Regs 2024, No. 50 of 2024) |
| **CBI — University of the West Indies (UWI) Fund** | **US$260,000** (family of 6) | *Secondary — verify at CIU* |
| **CBI — Business investment** | Route exists | *Verify thresholds at CIU* |
| **Citizenship by other means** | Naturalisation by residence | Standard naturalisation outside CBI |

(Source: [CIU — cip.gov.ag/real-estate/](https://cip.gov.ag/real-estate/) + [cip.gov.ag/investment-options/ndf/](https://cip.gov.ag/) [PRIMARY for RE + NDF] + Henley / Global Citizen Solutions [secondary for UWI + business routes] — *verify current thresholds + fee schedule at CIU before relying*)

### CBI government fees (per cip.gov.ag, fetched 2026-05-28)

- **Processing fee**: **US$10,000** (single applicant); **US$20,000** (family of up to 4).
- **Due-diligence fees**: per applicant (*verify current schedule at CIU*).
- **Physical-presence requirement**: 5 days in aggregate within the first 5 years (*secondary — verify at CIU*).

### Compliance / AML

- **Source-of-funds documentation** is mandatory for both CBI and non-CBI purchases; the CIU runs **multi-stage due diligence** on all CBI applicants (one of the more scrutinised CBI programmes regionally).
- **CRS + FATCA**: tax-residency self-certification at bank account opening.
- **Beneficial-ownership disclosure** for corporate-held real estate.
- ⚠️ **US visa-relationship caveat**: Antigua and Barbuda CBI passport does **NOT** confer US E-2 treaty-investor eligibility (AG is not a US E-2 treaty country, unlike Grenada). Do not conflate the AG passport with a US-residency pathway.

### Verification path (before any reservation)

1. **Decide the route** — CBI-approved development (US$300k+) vs open-market purchase requiring NCLHL.
2. **Instruct the attorney to pull the registered title** via Landfolio at the [Land Registry Department](https://legalaffairs.gov.ag/detail_page.php?page=landRegistry.php) — confirm registered owner, no encumbrances, parcel/boundary match.
3. **NCLHL filing** (if non-CBI): property-specific application to Cabinet/Ministry; budget ~3–6 months + ~5% fee (*secondary — verify*).
4. **Engage a local attorney** (de-facto mandatory) — the NCLHL package + AML source-of-funds is document-heavy.
5. **IRD confirmation** — stamp duty (buyer 2.5% / seller 7.5% *secondary*) + annual property-tax account status.
6. **Barbuda only**: obtain Barbuda-specific specialist advice; do NOT assume freehold tenure.

## Section: `--traffic`

### Sources

- **No public AADT (Annual Average Daily Traffic) portal** for Antigua and Barbuda. OSM road class + on-the-ground observation is the practical method.
- **OSM** fallback via Overpass — `highway` tag class.

### Reality

- **Drive on the LEFT** (UK convention). Both right-hand-drive and left-hand-drive vehicles appear on roads.
- **Temporary local driving permit** required for visitors — purchasable on presentation of a home licence (~EC$50–100; *verify at police / Transport Board*).
- **Single main island**: St John's (capital) experiences peak congestion; rural roads are variable / potholed. V.C. Bird International Airport (ANU) is the regional hub on the north-east of Antigua.

### Non-Citizens Land Holding Licence (NCLHL) fee

### Capital gains / inheritance / wealth

- **Land Value Appreciation Tax**: non-citizen vendors reportedly pay **~5%** on the gain (current value minus purchase value incl. improvements) on disposal (*secondary — verify at IRD*).

## Section: `--rental`

> **Headline**: tourism ≈ ~60% of GDP (*secondary*) — short-let demand is the core rental thesis, heavily concentrated in the **Dec–Apr high season**. All yield figures are **SECONDARY** (brokerage / Airbtics). **Net yields are materially below gross** — hurricane insurance + salt-air maintenance + management are the drag. **Always compute net; never quote gross alone.**

### Long-term residential rentals

- **Gross yield**: ~**5%/yr** (early 2026 aggregator est.).
- Tenant pool is thinner than the tourist STR market — expatriate professionals, CBI owners renting between visits, and local tenants.

### Short-term / vacation rentals (Airbnb / villa)

- **Gross yield**: ~**6–10%** in tourist zones; Jolly Harbour villas ~**5–8%** (2026 brokerage est.).
- **Airbtics (Feb 2025 – Jan 2026)**: ~**1,597 active STR listings**, ~**55% occupancy**, **median monthly revenue ~US$15k** — ⚠️ this is a **peak-month** figure, NOT annualised; treat with care, and re-confirm the data is for Antigua & Barbuda (not Antigua, Guatemala).
- **Seasonality**: high season **Dec–Apr**; STR revenue heavily concentrated in winter months; July–October (hurricane season) materially depresses occupancy.

### Net-yield reality

- One aggregator models **~5% gross → ~3.2% net**, driven by **hurricane insurance + salt-air maintenance**. Caribbean net yields run materially below gross because of insurance + maintenance + management drag. **Compute net; never quote gross alone.**

### Strategic notes

- **CBI development resale lock**: a CBI-purchased property cannot be resold for 5 years — but it CAN be rented during the hold (rental income is a partial offset to the locked capital).
- **HOA / development rules**: branded CBI developments (Tamarind Hills, Hodges Bay, Nonsuch Bay) regulate short-term rental + management arrangements — read the development's rules before pricing a yield model.

(Source: TheLatinvestor + Airbtics 2025–2026 commentary — *secondary aggregator*; yields gross of vacancy + management + insurance + property tax; verify per-property)

## Section: `--risks`

> ⚠️ **TIER-1 RISK MARKET. Atlantic hurricane belt.** Hurricane + insurance is the single most consequential due-diligence axis for Antigua and Barbuda — and Barbuda carries a fundamentally different and contested land-tenure regime.

### Primary national hazard sources

| Source | URL | What it gives |
|---|---|---|
| **National Office of Disaster Services (NODS)** | `https://nods.gov.ag/` | Hurricane plans, shelters, alerts |
| **Antigua & Barbuda Meteorological Service** | via `https://ab.gov.ag/` | Hurricane + climate baseline |
| **US National Hurricane Center (NHC)** | `https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/` | Atlantic basin authoritative; primary regional reference |
| **Caribbean Disaster Emergency Management Agency (CDEMA)** | `https://www.cdema.org/` | Regional coordination |
| **UWI Seismic Research Centre (SRC)** | `https://www.uwiseismic.com/` | Caribbean seismic + volcanic monitoring |

### Hurricane (Atlantic basin) — high

- **Position**: Eastern Caribbean Leeward Islands — squarely in the Atlantic hurricane belt.
- **Season**: **1 June – 30 November**, peak **August–October**.
- **Hurricane Irma (Sept 2017, Cat 5)** devastated **Barbuda — ~95% of buildings destroyed/damaged**; the **entire population (~1,800) was evacuated** to Antigua and Barbuda was left effectively uninhabited for a period. **Antigua (main island) was less severely hit**, but Irma + Maria caused **~US$300M total property damage** nationally (Sources: UNEP; GFDRR / World Bank Post-Disaster Needs Assessment; Antigua Observer).
- **Implication**: A&B is a **high hurricane-risk jurisdiction**. Verify roof tie-downs, impact glazing, and reinforced construction on ANY property; older stock especially suspect.

### Building code + build quality

- A&B has a building code, but **enforcement has historically been weak** — the NODS director publicly stated Barbuda's pre-Irma code "was not adhered to" and that post-Irma reconstruction needs a more robust, enforced code.
- **Verify on ANY property**: roof tie-downs, impact-rated glazing, reinforced (concrete/block) construction, age of build. Modern CBI-development stock is generally better-engineered than older vernacular housing.

### Insurance — expensive, hard to obtain

- Hurricane / windstorm cover is **expensive and increasingly hard to obtain** ("practically out of reach" per UNEP reporting for many residents). Expect **high premiums + high deductibles** (often a % of insured value for named-storm losses).
- UNEP + the Department of Environment run a **revolving low-interest loan programme** for home hurricane-proofing.
- **Budget insurance as a major recurring cost line** — it is the primary driver of the net-yield gap (see `--rental`). **Get 3+ quotes for the specific property before contract.**

### ⚠️ Barbuda land-tenure note (DISTINCT, CONTESTED)

- Barbuda historically held **all land in COMMON** (communal ownership — **no individual freehold**). Post-Irma legal changes introduced **leasehold** to enable banks/insurers to lend.
- The title structure on Barbuda is **FUNDAMENTALLY different from Antigua** and is **politically contested + sensitive**.
- **Treat any Barbuda land claim with extreme caution; do NOT assume freehold.** Obtain Barbuda-specific specialist legal advice and **verify the current Barbuda Land Act status** before any commitment.

### Other hazards

- **Seismic**: Lesser Antilles arc — moderate background risk; monitored by [UWI SRC](https://www.uwiseismic.com/).
- **Drought / water scarcity**: low-rainfall island; freshwater stress is real (see `--mains` — heavy reliance on desalination).
- **Sea-level rise / coastal erosion**: exposure for low-lying coastal plots; model parcel-level scenarios.

## Section: `--mains`

> **APUA = Antigua Public Utilities Authority** — the **single statutory authority** providing **electricity, water, AND telecoms** (APUA inet / iMobile). This single-utility model is unusual. Primary: **[apua.ag](https://www.apua.ag/)**.

### Power — APUA

- APUA-generated, largely **diesel / fuel-oil thermal** (some solar). **Grid reliability is variable** — outages occur; many properties + developments run **backup generators** and increasingly **solar + battery**.
- Tariffs include a **fuel-variation charge** → bills track oil prices. *Verify current tariff at [apua.ag](https://www.apua.ag/).*
- **Standby generator + inverter advisable** for any continuous-occupancy property.

### Water — APUA

- APUA supplies; the island is **water-scarce (low rainfall)** and relies heavily on **desalination (reverse osmosis)**. Mains supply can be **intermittent**; many homes have **cisterns / storage tanks**.
- **Confirm reliable supply + storage** for any property — water-supply reliability is a meaningful due-diligence item here.

### Connectivity

- **APUA (inet / iMobile)** + **Flow (Cable & Wireless)** + **Digicel**.
- Fibre / broadband available in developed / expat areas (Jolly Harbour, St John's, English Harbour); patchier rural. Mobile 4G widespread; 5G limited. *Verify coverage at the specific property.*

## Section: `--currency`

- **Currency**: **East Caribbean Dollar (XCD / EC$)**.
- **Peg**: **EC$2.70 = US$1.00**, fixed since **1976**, administered by the **[Eastern Caribbean Central Bank (ECCB)](https://www.eccb-centralbank.org/)** across the eight-member OECS currency union (*widely cited; primary = ECCB — verify*).
- **USD circulates in parallel**: tourism + CBI property transactions are commonly **USD-quoted**; CBI thresholds are denominated in **USD**.
- **Practical effect for buyer**: **near-zero structural FX risk for USD-base buyers** given the long-standing fixed peg; minimal risk for EUR/GBP/CAD buyers (USD-correlated).
- **Devaluation risk**: the EC$2.70 peg has held since 1976 across multiple external shocks; the ECCB's currency-union structure + reserve discipline underpin credibility. As with any pegged regime, the peg is a **policy choice, not a structural certainty** — but the OECS peg has one of the longest unbroken records in the region.
- **No exchange-control repatriation gate equivalent to Barbados' CBB-registration step is cited for A&B** — but confirm capital-repatriation mechanics with the attorney + bank at purchase (*verify*).

## Section: `--visa` (CBI deep-dive)

Antigua and Barbuda operates one of the established Eastern Caribbean **Citizenship-by-Investment** programmes (run by the **[Citizenship by Investment Unit (CIU)](https://cip.gov.ag/)**). It is one of the more scrutinised CBI programmes regionally on due diligence.

### Investment options (verify current at CIU)

| Option | Minimum | Source / status |
|---|---|---|
| **Real Estate** | **US$300,000** in a government-approved project | **VERIFIED** — cip.gov.ag/real-estate/ ("purchase property valued at minimum US$300,000"), fetched 2026-05-28 |
| **National Development Fund (NDF) donation** | **US$230,000** (single applicant / family of up to 4) | **VERIFIED** — effective **1 Aug 2024** under the CBI (Amendment) Regulations 2024, No. 50 of 2024 (up from US$100,000); audit-cbi.md 2026-05-28 |
| **University of the West Indies (UWI) Fund** | **US$260,000** (family of 6) | *Secondary — verify at CIU* |
| **Business investment** | Route exists | *Verify thresholds at CIU* |

> ⚠️ **Do NOT use the old US$200k real-estate / US$400k figures** — superseded. Current real-estate minimum is **US$300,000** (VERIFIED); NDF is **US$230,000** (VERIFIED, eff. 1 Aug 2024).

### Real-estate route mechanics

- **Minimum**: **US$300,000** in a government-**approved** real-estate project (VERIFIED, cip.gov.ag/real-estate/).
- **Hold period**: the real estate **cannot be resold for 5 years** after purchase, unless rolled into another approved A&B real-estate project (VERIFIED, cip.gov.ag/real-estate/).
- **Joint application / co-investor**: secondary sources report two unrelated applicants may co-invest (each US$300,000, total US$600,000) to share one qualifying property — **NOT confirmed on the CIU page (re-fetched 2026-05-28)**. *Treat as `data not separately confirmed on CIU site — verify directly with the CIU`.*

### Government fees (per cip.gov.ag, fetched 2026-05-28)

- **Processing**: **US$10,000** (single applicant) / **US$20,000** (family of up to 4).
- **Due-diligence fees**: per applicant (*verify current schedule*).
- **Physical-presence requirement**: 5 days in aggregate within the first 5 years (*secondary — verify*).

### Caveats

- **5-year resale lock** constrains the secondary-market liquidity of CBI-purchased property — a CBI development's resale pool is largely other CBI buyers.
- **OECS pricing-floor alignment (2024)**: AG's increases follow the five-state (AG/DM/GD/KN/LC) Memorandum of Agreement on minimum CBI pricing.
- **No US E-2 treaty access** via the AG passport (unlike Grenada) — do not market the passport as a US-residency route.
- **Strong due diligence**: the CIU runs multi-stage vetting; applicants with adverse media / sanctions exposure are refused.
