# Commonwealth of The Bahamas 🇧🇸 — Property Due-Diligence Playbook

ISO2: `bs`. Status: ✅ Fully populated (researched 2026-05-07).

> **Critical framing — read first**: The Bahamas is a **no-income-tax common-law jurisdiction** with English-style conveyancing inherited from the British era. There is **NO personal income tax, NO capital gains tax, NO inheritance / estate tax, NO gift tax, NO wealth tax, and NO general corporate income tax for non-financial businesses**. What IS taxed: **annual Real Property Tax (RPT)**, **Stamp Duty + VAT on conveyances** (combined ~10% on most transactions, split by convention), **annual business licence + Business Licence Tax**, and **VAT (10%) on most goods and services since 2022**. **Foreign buyers** purchase on broadly equal footing with Bahamians under the **International Persons Landholding Act 1993 (IPLA)**, with a **registration vs permit** distinction depending on parcel size and use. The **currency (BSD)** is **pegged 1:1 to the USD** since 1973 — USD circulates interchangeably in property transactions. **Hurricane risk is the dominant physical risk** — the Atlantic hurricane belt traverses the archipelago annually (Jun–Nov, peak Aug–Oct); **Hurricane Dorian (Sep 2019)** destroyed an estimated 75%+ of homes on parts of Abaco and Grand Bahama and remains the calibration event for insurance + building-code conversations. **Confirm** any IPLA registration / permit requirement, the **Real Property Tax** band applicable, the **Stamp Duty + VAT split** at the contract stage, and the **insurance availability + cost** for the specific island/parcel **BEFORE any binding step**. Authoritative primary sources: **[Department of Inland Revenue (DIR)](https://inlandrevenue.finance.gov.bs/value-added-tax/about-vat/)**, **[Bahamas Investment Authority (BIA, Office of the Prime Minister)](https://www.bahamas.gov.bs/investment)**, **[Central Bank of The Bahamas (CBOB)](https://www.centralbankbahamas.com/)**, **[Bahamas National Statistical Institute (BNSI)](https://www.bahamas.gov.bs/statistics)**, **[Department of Lands and Surveys](https://www.bahamas.gov.bs/)**, **[Registrar General's Department](https://www.bahamas.gov.bs/)**.

## Country profile

- **Geography**: Archipelagic state, ~700 islands and ~2,400 cays across ~470,000 km² of ocean; ~30 inhabited islands; total land area ~13,880 km². Capital **Nassau** on **New Providence**; major secondary markets **Grand Bahama** (Freeport), **Abaco**, **Eleuthera**, **Exuma**, **Bimini**, **Andros**, **Long Island**, **Cat Island**, **San Salvador**, **Inagua**, **Berry Islands** (Family Islands collectively).
- **Population**: ~412,000 (2022 Census, BNSI — verify at [Bahamas National Statistical Institute](https://www.bahamas.gov.bs/statistics)). ~70% on New Providence (Nassau metro); ~14% on Grand Bahama; remaining ~16% across Family Islands.
- **GDP per capita**: ~USD 36,000 (2024, IMF WEO Oct 2024 nominal — verify at [IMF DataMapper](https://www.imf.org/external/datamapper/profile/BHS)). Tourism + financial services dominate; ~50% of GDP tourism-linked.
- **Currency**: **BSD (Bahamian Dollar — B$)** — pegged **1:1 with USD** since 1973 (CBOB Exchange Control). **USD universally accepted** in property transactions; high-end Lyford Cay, Albany, Old Fort Bay, Ocean Club Estates, Baker's Bay deals are USD-denominated by default. **Exchange Control Regulations 1956** govern non-resident foreign-currency flows; permission via [Central Bank of The Bahamas](https://www.centralbankbahamas.com/) Exchange Control Department for non-resident purchases is procedural rather than gatekeeping in most cases (post-IPLA 1993 liberalisation).
- **Languages**: **English (official, sole)** — all contracts, deeds, statutes in English. Bahamian Creole vernacular spoken locally.
- **Legal system**: **English common law** + Bahamian statutes; **Privy Council in London** retained as final court of appeal. Conveyancing = English-style abstract-of-title + Indenture deeds; **NO Torrens registry on most parcels** (see Title Chain section below). The **Land Registration Act 2002** introduced an opt-in title-registration system but adoption has been gradual; most older parcels still trade on the **abstract-of-title / chain-of-deeds** model.
- **Admin levels**: **31 districts** under the [Local Government Act 1996](http://laws.bahamas.gov.bs/) (excluding New Providence which is centrally administered); districts comprise **Family Islands** local administrators. New Providence is administered directly by central government departments.
- **Cadastre / land registry**:
  - **Registrar General's Department (RGD — Ministry of Legal Affairs)**: deed-recording office for conveyances, mortgages, IPLA registrations, and Land Registration Act applications. Public-search counters in Nassau.
  - **Department of Lands and Surveys (DLS — Ministry of Agriculture, Marine Resources & Family Island Affairs)**: maintains Crown Land records, official survey plans, and the cadastral / surveyor registry.
  - **Land Registration Act 2002**: opt-in Torrens-style registry; coverage incomplete; older parcels remain on the abstract-of-title model.
- **Identifier**: **Lot + Subdivision + Island/District** on the deed; modern parcels increasingly carry a **Real Property Tax Assessment Number** issued by DIR. **NO universal national parcel ID** equivalent to a French numéro de parcelle. Survey plans lodged with DLS are the authoritative boundary record.
- **Title concept**: **Freehold (fee simple)** dominant; **leasehold** (typically Crown Land 21–99 years) common on certain Family Islands and developmental sites; **commonage** (a Bahamian peculiarity — see Title Chain section) materially affects parts of Eleuthera, Cat Island, Long Island, Exuma.
- **Recent reforms (12-month watchlist)**:
  - **VAT rate 10%** since 1 Jan 2022 (7.5% on 1 Jan 2015 → 12% on 1 Jul 2018 → reduced to 10% on 1 Jan 2022). Verify at [DIR — About VAT](https://inlandrevenue.finance.gov.bs/value-added-tax/about-vat/).
  - **Stamp Duty / VAT-on-conveyance restructuring** — historically 10% Stamp Duty was the conveyance tax; subsequent reforms migrated most of this into VAT-on-conveyance to align with VAT framework. **Foreign buyers pay flat 10%** VAT-on-Conveyance on the full purchase price; Bahamian first-time / domicile (BDR) buyers access a graduated 2.5% → 10% scale. The 50/50 buyer/seller split is still customary, but the flat 10% applies first (2026-05-27 verified, source Higgs & Johnson 2025 + BREA + bahamas.gov.bs DIR procedures PDF).
  - **2025 VAT real-estate procedure reform** (eff. **1 Jul 2025**): VAT invoice from DIR **required before completing** the transaction; **30-day declaration** window when conveying beneficial interests; **3% joint-and-several penalty** on non-compliance; B$1,000 deposit for advance-ruling applications (2026-05-27 verified, source Higgs & Johnson tax legislative updates 2025).
  - **Real Property Tax (RPT) bands** indexed by Ministry of Finance / DIR via annual budget communication — verify current bands at [DIR — Real Property Tax](https://inlandrevenue.finance.gov.bs/real-property-tax/).
  - **Economic Permanent Residence (EPR) threshold**: minimum real-estate investment **B$1,000,000** effective **1 Jan 2025** (raised from B$750,000; **10-year minimum-hold clawback rule** — Immigration Board may revoke residence status if investment liquidated within 10 years) — verify current threshold + accelerated-processing investment band at [BIA](https://www.bahamas.gov.bs/investment) (2026-05-27 verified, source Fragomen + UNCTAD Investment Policy Monitor).
  - **International Persons Landholding Act 1993 (IPLA)** — primary statute governing foreign acquisition; periodic Department of Inland Revenue + BIA practice notes on registration vs permit thresholds.
  - **Hurricane Dorian (Sep 2019) post-disaster building-code review** — Ministry of Works updates to the Bahamas Building Code (BBC) for high-wind zones; insurance market re-rating ongoing.
  - **Beneficial-ownership register (Register of Beneficial Ownership Act 2018, amended 2020)** — closes anonymous corporate ownership; affects International Business Companies (IBCs) holding real estate.
  - **Common Reporting Standard (CRS) + FATCA** — Bahamas committed; tax-residency self-certification required for bank accounts financing property purchases.

## 🇧🇸 Foreign buyer eligibility (READ FIRST)

This section determines whether a transaction is even legally possible — and what process applies. The Bahamas operates one of **the most foreign-buyer-friendly regimes in the Caribbean** for residential real estate, calibrated through the IPLA permit/registration tiers.

### Legal basis

- **[International Persons Landholding Act 1993 (IPLA, Chapter 140)](http://laws.bahamas.gov.bs/)** — primary statute; replaced the more restrictive Immovable Property (Acquisition by Foreign Persons) Act and substantially liberalised foreign acquisition.
- **Exchange Control Regulations 1956** (under Central Bank of The Bahamas) — administrative permissions for non-resident currency flows.
- **Bahamas Investment Authority (BIA)** under the Office of the Prime Minister — administers permits + EPR investment-route filings.
- **Real Property Tax Act (Chapter 375)** — annual property-tax framework; foreign-owner band-differential historically embedded.
- **Stamp Act (Chapter 370)** + **Value Added Tax Act 2014** — transaction taxes.

### Buyer category matrix

| Buyer category | Right |
|---|---|
| **Bahamian citizen** | Full freehold; no registration / permit needed |
| **Bahamian permanent resident with right to work** | Full freehold; no IPLA registration required |
| **International (non-Bahamian) buyer — residential ≤ 5 acres for owner-occupation OR rental within a defined development** | **Registration only** (post-completion filing with the Registrar General + IPLA registration with DIR). No prior permit required. This is the standard pathway for most foreign buyers of single-family homes or condominiums in subdivisions. |
| **International buyer — undeveloped land OR property > 5 acres OR commercial OR for development/rental outside a defined development** | **Permit required** from the Bahamas Investment Authority (BIA) **before** completion. Application + government processing fee + due-diligence; processing typically 60–120 days (verify current SLA at BIA). |
| **Bahamian Domicile Resolution (BDR)** beneficiary | Specific reduced Stamp Duty/VAT rate available where the buyer formally resolves Bahamian domicile under the [Stamp Act / Domicile Act framework] — verify exact current eligibility + reduced-rate at DIR / Ministry of Finance |
| **Trust / IBC / corporate holding** | Permitted; subject to **Beneficial Ownership Register** disclosure (Register of Beneficial Ownership Act 2018, as amended). Bahamian holding companies often used for estate-planning + privacy + asset protection. |

(Source: [International Persons Landholding Act 1993](http://laws.bahamas.gov.bs/) + BIA practice notes — *verify current acreage/use thresholds at BIA before contract*)

### Visa / residency via property — Economic Permanent Residence (EPR)

The Bahamas operates an **Economic Permanent Residence (EPR)** route administered by the **Department of Immigration** under the **[Bahamas Nationality Act + Immigration Act, Chapter 191](http://laws.bahamas.gov.bs/)** + **Bahamas Investment Authority** for the investment verification.

| Item | Threshold (verify at BIA) |
|---|---|
| **Standard EPR** (any qualifying investment, including real estate) | **B$1,000,000 minimum investment** (raised from B$750,000 effective 1 Jan 2025; **10-year minimum-hold clawback rule** applies — verify current floor at [BIA](https://www.bahamas.gov.bs/investment)) (2026-05-27 verified) |
| **Accelerated processing** (~21-day SLA target) | **B$1,500,000+ investment** (verify current threshold; was previously B$1.5M; subject to budget revision) |
| **Annual residence card** (non-immigrant, separate from EPR) | Available to homeowners regardless of investment value; renewable annually; does NOT confer right to work |
| **Right to work** | NOT automatic with EPR — separate work-permit application via Department of Immigration |
| **Citizenship by investment (CBI)** | **NONE** — The Bahamas does NOT operate a CBI scheme (unlike St. Kitts & Nevis, Dominica, Antigua & Barbuda, Grenada, St. Lucia). Naturalisation requires 6–9 years' permanent residence + character/language vetting under the Nationality Act |
| **Tax residency** | Tax residency for non-tax purposes (CRS / treaty) requires 90+ days physical presence + tax-residency certificate (DIR) — relevant for international tax planning, not for any Bahamian tax (since there is no Bahamian PIT) |

### Compliance / AML

- **Financial Transactions Reporting Act 2018** + **Anti-Terrorism Act 2018** + **Proceeds of Crime Act 2018** govern AML.
- Real-estate brokers, attorneys, and IPLA filings are **designated non-financial businesses and professions (DNFBPs)** with KYC + suspicious-transaction-reporting obligations.
- **Source-of-funds documentation** required for all property purchases; bank-statement chain back to legitimate source.
- **Register of Beneficial Ownership Act 2018** (amended 2020) — corporate ownership transparency; ultimate beneficial owner disclosed to the Registrar General.
- **CRS + FATCA**: tax-residency self-certification required at bank account opening for purchase financing.
- **Bahamas removed from EU AML black-list 2022** + FATF grey-list 2020 — current standing materially improved but compliance regime now strict.

### Verification path (before any reservation)

1. **Confirm IPLA category**: registration-only or permit-required? Apply early at BIA if permit-required (60–120 day processing).
2. **Pull seller's title chain**: 30+ years' abstract-of-title via Bahamian attorney; OR Land Registration Act extract if registered title.
3. **DLS survey check**: confirm boundaries match deed description; commonage / Crown Land overlay verified.
4. **DIR Real Property Tax assessment**: confirm RPT account is current + no arrears (RPT lien follows title; from 1 Jul 2026 the Treasurer may sell property whose RPT has been in arrears for more than twelve months, a power that does not apply to owner-occupied property beneficially owned by a Bahamian — ss.25A–25Y, Act No. 21 of 2026).
5. **BIA + Exchange Control**: file post-completion IPLA registration + Exchange Control filings via attorney.
6. **AML / source-of-funds**: bank statements + employment / asset-sale evidence ready before signing reservation.

### Trend 2022 → 2026

- **Luxury New Providence + Paradise Island + branded estates**: appreciating ~5–10% YoY in USD-denominated transactions (Sotheby's + Knight Frank commentary; *secondary tier*) — driven by US/Canadian/UK foreign-buyer demand + tax-haven structural appeal.
- **Abaco recovery**: Hurricane Dorian (Sep 2019) caused ~75%+ residential damage on parts of Abaco + Grand Bahama; subsequent 2020–2024 price recovery uneven; **Baker's Bay recovered fastest**; Marsh Harbour / Treasure Cay / Hope Town slower.
- **Grand Bahama / Freeport**: structural decline pre-Dorian (Hutchison Whampoa exit + post-2008 financial crisis); Dorian compounded; recovery thesis depends on reinvestment.
- **Family Islands** (Eleuthera mainland, Long Island, Cat Island): thin market, episodic sales; pricing highly individual (waterfront vs interior; access vs remote).

### Compute

1. USD/m² = price ÷ built-area m² (usually quoted in **square feet** in The Bahamas — convert: 1 m² = 10.764 sqft).
2. Compare to ≥ 3 active MLS Bahamas + HG Christie + Damianos comparables in the same community / subdivision (community + waterfront-status matter more than island average).
3. **Listing convention**: most listings quote **price in USD** (or USD-equivalent BSD given the 1:1 peg) + **square footage built** + **lot square footage**. Clarify which.
4. **Trap — area definitions**: built-up area (BUA / heated/cooled), gross area (incl. covered porches), lot area (land only). Listings often quote the largest of these without flagging.
5. **Trap — IPLA permit overhead**: If parcel is > 5 acres OR undeveloped, BIA permit required → 60–120 day delay + government fee + attorney fee; price negotiation should account for this carrying cost.
6. **Trap — beachfront erosion**: Bahamian beachfront subject to natural shoreline migration; verify setback compliance with Crown Land / **Coast Protection Act** + DLS records.
7. **Trap — commonage**: pockets of Eleuthera, Cat Island, Long Island, Exuma have *commonage* (collective generational ownership) overlapping recorded title — see Title Chain section. Avoid commonage parcels unless your attorney is a specialist.

### Key terms

- **Indenture / Conveyance**: the deed transferring fee-simple title (English-style)
- **Abstract of Title**: 30+ years' chain of conveyances; the buyer's attorney prepares an opinion
- **Quieting of Titles Act 1959**: judicial title-quieting process for clouded chains; can take 1–7 years
- **Land Registration Act 2002**: opt-in Torrens-style alternative
- **Commonage**: collective unrecorded historical claim by extended family / community; common in some Family Islands
- **Crown Land**: state-owned land; lease terms 21 / 99 years typical; buyback/extension at Crown discretion
- **IPLA Registration / Permit**: post- or pre-completion filing for international buyers
- **Real Property Tax (RPT)**: annual tax, banded by use + value
- **Stamp Duty / VAT-on-Conveyance**: combined transaction tax (~10% above B$100k threshold)

### Caveats

- **Nassau congestion**: New Providence is geographically constrained (~207 km²); **peak congestion 7:30–9:30am, 4:00–6:30pm** weekdays especially around **Paradise Island bridge**, **Cable Beach corridor**, **Bay Street downtown**, **Sir Sidney Poitier Bridge** approaches.
- **Cruise-ship day surge**: Nassau Bay Street area floods with cruise tourists when 3+ ships are berthed at Prince George Wharf — affects walkability + property frontage on cruise-day calendar.
- **Family Islands**: traffic is rarely a noise issue — most Family Island roads are < 500 v/d; **scooter / golf-cart traffic** dominates Harbour Island, Hope Town, Green Turtle Cay (cars often restricted).
- **Hurricane evacuation**: traffic surge on evacuation routes (JFK Drive, Tonique Williams) during hurricane warnings.
- **Bay Street pedestrianisation**: Nassau central section closed to vehicles cruise-day periods.

## Section: `--tax`

> **Headline**: The Bahamas has **NO personal income tax**, **NO capital gains tax**, **NO inheritance / estate / gift tax**, **NO wealth tax**, **NO withholding tax on dividends/interest**, and **NO general corporate income tax for non-financial domestic businesses**. What IS taxed: **annual Real Property Tax (RPT)**, **Stamp Duty + VAT on conveyances**, **VAT (10%) on most goods/services since 2022**, **Business Licence Tax** (turnover-based), **import duties** on most goods, and **National Insurance contributions** (employment-related). This makes the Bahamas one of the world's lowest-tax jurisdictions for individual property owners.

### Annual property tax — Real Property Tax (RPT)

Governed by the **[Real Property Tax Act, Chapter 375](http://laws.bahamas.gov.bs/)** + annual budget amendments. Administered by the **[Department of Inland Revenue (DIR)](https://inlandrevenue.finance.gov.bs/real-property-tax/)**.

**Owner-occupied residential property — Bahamians and holders of a certificate of permanent residence with a right to work only** (Real Property Tax (Amendment) Act 2026, No. 21 of 2026, in force 1 Jul 2026; foreign permit-holders fall in the separate foreign owner-occupied class below) — graduated bands (verify current bands at DIR; figures reflect Real Property Tax Act consolidated framework as commonly published 2024–2025):

| Market value (B$) | Annual RPT rate |
|---|---|
| First **$300,000** (owner-occupied portion) | **0%** (exempt) |
| **$300,001 – $500,000** | **0.625%** |
| **Above $500,000** | **1.0%** (DIR RPT FAQ Q10) |
| **Annual cap** | total owner-occupied tax "shall not exceed one hundred and fifty thousand dollars" — **B$150,000** (2026 data, source Real Property Tax (Amendment) Act 2026 No. 21 of 2026 s.3(a)); a 0.5% band above $5M is not in DIR RPT FAQ Q10 — `data not publicly available — verify at DIR` |

(Sources: [DIR RPT FAQ](https://inlandrevenue.finance.gov.bs/real-property-tax/faqs-rpt/) Q10 for bands + [Real Property Tax (Amendment) Act 2026](https://laws.bahamas.gov.bs/cms/images/LEGISLATION/AMENDING/2026/2026-0021A/2026-0021A.pdf) for eligibility and cap — *the DIR FAQ has not been updated for the 2026 Act; confirm with DIR in writing*.) **Foreign owner-occupied property (new class, in force 1 Jul 2026)**: a non-Bahamian holding a residence permit / annual work permit (Immigration Act Ch. 191) or a Home Owner Resident Permit (IPLA Ch. 140) who occupies the property exclusively as a dwelling on a permanent or seasonal basis pays **0.625% on value up to B$1,000,000 and 1% above**, annual cap **B$200,000** — no exempt first slice in the Act text (2026 data, source Act No. 21 of 2026 s.3(b)). A foreign owner without such a permit is not owner-occupied — confirm the applicable schedule with DIR.

**Residential non-owner-occupied (≤4 units)** — per DIR FAQ:

| Market value (B$) | Annual RPT rate |
|---|---|
| Up to **$75,000** | **B$300 flat fee** |
| **Above $75,000** | **0.625%** of property value |

**Commercial (5+ units OR foreign-owned rentals)** — three-tier schedule per DIR FAQ:

| Market value (B$) | Annual RPT rate |
|---|---|
| First **$500,000** | **0.75%** |
| **$500,001 – $2,000,000** | **1.0%** |
| **Above $2,000,000** | **1.5%** |

(2026-05-27 verified, source [DIR Real Property Tax FAQs](https://inlandrevenue.finance.gov.bs/real-property-tax/faqs-rpt/). Foreign-owned rentals route into the commercial schedule.)

**Vacant land owned by non-Bahamians**: historically a higher rate than Bahamian-owned vacant land (foreign-owner band differential) — this differential has been part of policy debate; **verify current rate at DIR** before contract.

**Filing + payment**:
- **Annual valuation** by DIR Valuations Section; assessment notice issued.
- **Payment due 31 March** of each year for the calendar year (verify current due-date at DIR — has shifted historically).
- **Discount for early payment** (typically 10% if paid by the deadline) — verify.
- **Late-payment penalty**: surcharge + interest accrual.
- **Lien**: unpaid RPT becomes a charge on the property; transfers blocked at RGD until cleared.
- **Bahamian-owner exemption — primary residence portion**: First B$300,000 exempt for owner-occupied (DIR RPT FAQ Q10) — from 1 Jul 2026 the owner-occupied class covers only Bahamians and permanent residents with a right to work (Act No. 21 of 2026); foreign permit-holders pay the foreign owner-occupied schedule with no exempt slice.

### Transaction taxes (one-time at purchase)

The conveyance tax has been **restructured multiple times** since the introduction of VAT in 2015. The **current effective combined rate is ~10% on consideration above B$100,000**, structured as a combination of **Stamp Duty + VAT-on-Conveyance**. The exact split is set by the **Stamp Act + VAT Act** and amended by annual budget — **verify at DIR before contract**.

**Foreign buyers**: **flat 10% VAT-on-Conveyance** on full purchase price (no graduated bands; no first-time-buyer relief).

**Bahamian first-time / domicile (BDR) buyers**: graduated 2.5% → 10% scale (commonly published: 2.5% under $100k → 4% $100,001–$300k → 6% $300,001–$500k → 8% $500,001–$700k → 9% $700,001–$1M → 10% over $1M; qualifying first-time Bahamian buyers can apply for VAT relief):

| Transaction value | BDR-only graduated scale |
|---|---|

**Convention**: Stamp Duty + VAT-on-Conveyance is **conventionally split 50/50 between buyer and seller**, but is negotiable in the Sale Agreement. Always confirm split clause.

### Bahamian Domicile Resolution (BDR)

A reduced Stamp Duty / VAT-on-Conveyance rate is available where the buyer formally resolves **Bahamian domicile** (typically Bahamian citizens or permanent residents purchasing first or primary home; eligibility periodically extended). Verify current BDR-reduced-rate schedule + eligibility at DIR + Ministry of Finance.

### VAT (general, on goods/services)

- **Standard rate**: **10%** since **1 January 2022** (introduced at 7.5% on 1 Jan 2015; raised to 12% on 1 Jul 2018; **reduced to 10% effective 1 Jan 2022 via VAT (Amendment)(No. 2) Act 2021**, published Official Gazette 13 Dec 2021) — the 2022 move was a **cut, not an increase**, and the 10% standard rate was **still in force as at the FY2026/27 Budget Communication (27 May 2026)** (2026-08-07 verified, primary source [DIR — VAT Guidance on 2021 Amendments](https://inlandrevenue.finance.gov.bs/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/VAT-Guidance-2021-Amendments.pdf); secondary VATupdate + Orbitax + vatcalc).
- **VAT registration threshold**: B$100,000 turnover.
- **VAT on rental income**: long-term residential rental **exempt**; short-term rental + hotel-style accommodation **taxable** (see `--rental`).
- **VAT on other property-related supplies**: the 10% standard rate applies to property-related supplies including **commercial rent** and — explicitly listed — **HOA / management fees**; treat gated-estate HOA and managing-agent quotes as VAT-bearing unless the quote states otherwise (2026-08-07 verified, source DIR VAT FAQ — verify at [DIR — About VAT](https://inlandrevenue.finance.gov.bs/value-added-tax/about-vat/)).

### Personal income / capital gains / inheritance — 0%

- **NO personal income tax** in The Bahamas.
- **NO capital gains tax** on individual property sales.
- **NO inheritance / estate / gift tax**.
- **NO withholding tax on dividends, interest, royalties**.
- **NO wealth tax**.

This is the **structural appeal of Bahamian property** for international buyers — the *only* annual tax on the property itself is RPT.

### Corporate tax — 0% (general) but Business Licence Tax + 15% Corporate Income Tax for MNEs

- **General corporate income tax**: **NONE** for domestic non-financial businesses.
- **Business Licence Tax**: turnover-based annual fee; varies by turnover band; relevant for short-term-rental businesses operating commercially.
- **Corporate Income Tax (CIT) at 15%**: introduced for **multinational enterprises (MNEs) within scope of the OECD Pillar Two Global Minimum Tax** — effective for fiscal years starting **on or after 1 January 2025** (verify at DIR; legislation enacted 2024). Does NOT affect individual property owners or domestic Bahamian businesses below the OECD MNE threshold (€750M consolidated revenue).
- **Bank & Trust Companies Regulation Act + Insurance Act**: financial-services-sector specific levies.

### Future risk

- **OECD Pillar Two CIT 15%** for MNEs — already enacted; verify scope if structuring through a multinational.
- **VAT on residential conveyance** — has been adjusted twice since 2018; further changes plausible in budgets.
- **Foreign-owner RPT differential** — has been a recurring policy-debate item; current foreign-owner vacant-land rates may be revised.
- **Hurricane levy** debated periodically — not enacted.

⚠️ Verification: request seller's last **RPT receipt + RPT account statement** AND their **Real Property Tax Assessment Notice**. Confirm current rate bands + BDR eligibility + IPLA permit/registration category at DIR + attorney before contract.

## Section: `--rental`

### Long-term residential rentals

Governed by the **[Rent Control Act (Chapter 187)](http://laws.bahamas.gov.bs/)** (vestigial — applies only to certain pre-1975 properties under specific circumstances) + general **English common-law landlord-tenant principles** + **Conveyancing and Law of Property Act**.

- **Rent Control Act** has very limited modern application; most rentals are governed by ordinary contract.
- **Standard tenancy**: 1-year fixed term, monthly payment, 1 month security deposit + 1 month advance rent typical.
- **Eviction**: court-supervised under common-law principles; not as fast as the US "summary process" eviction, but materially faster than civil-law jurisdictions; 2–4 months typical for non-payment.
- **Long-term residential rental income**: **VAT-exempt**.
- **Tax**: **NO Bahamian personal income tax on rental income** — but **US/UK/EU tax-resident landlords pay home-country tax** on Bahamas-sourced rental income (subject to limited Bahamas DTA network — Bahamas has **TIEAs (Tax Information Exchange Agreements)** with major countries but **no comprehensive double-tax treaties**).

### Short-term rentals (Vacation Rental — Airbnb / VRBO / direct)

- **Regulatory status**: short-term rentals are **legal and widely operated**; the regime tightened materially **2018–2024**.
- **VAT registration**: required if turnover ≥ B$100,000; **VAT 10% on the rental** (vs long-term residential which is exempt).
- **Vacation Rental Business Licence**: required from local administrator / municipality + Ministry of Tourism, Investments & Aviation (MOTIA) — verify current procedure at [Ministry of Tourism](https://www.bahamas.com/) (industry portal) + Department of Inland Revenue.
- **Hotel Encouragement Act benefits**: do NOT apply to ordinary owner-operated short-term rentals; reserved for full hotel developments.
- **HOA / strata reality**: in Lyford Cay, Albany, Old Fort Bay, Ocean Club Estates, Baker's Bay, Emerald Bay (Exuma), Sandyport, Treasure Cay etc., **HOA / Owners Association rules typically restrict or prohibit short-term rentals** — read the Declaration / By-Laws **before** pricing a yield model.
- **Family Island reality**: vacation rentals dominant economic activity on Eleuthera (Harbour Island, Governor's Harbour), Exuma, Abaco, Long Island.

### Strategic notes

- **Hurricane insurance is the dominant operating cost** for short-term rentals — see `--risks` + Cost benchmarks.
- **Seasonality**: high season **mid-Dec to mid-Apr** (Northern-hemisphere winter); shoulder Apr-Jun + Nov-Dec; **low / hurricane season Jul-Oct** materially depresses occupancy.
- **Year-round demand only on**: Nassau (business + cruise + leisure); Paradise Island; Albany / Baker's Bay (high-end branded); Harbour Island (year-round honeymoon).
- **HOA / Owners Association rules** = the single biggest STR-feasibility check. Read before pricing.
- **Tax efficiency**: with no Bahamian PIT, the entire net rental yield (less RPT + VAT-on-STR + management) accrues to the owner; *home-country* tax-residency drives net.

### Hurricane (Atlantic basin) — THE dominant risk

- **Season**: **1 June – 30 November**, peak **15 August – 25 October**.
- **Historical reference**: **Andrew (1992)** — Cat 5 traversed northern Bahamas (Eleuthera, Andros); **Floyd (1999)**; **Frances + Jeanne (2004)** — both struck in 6 weeks; **Wilma (2005)**; **Irene (2011)**; **Sandy (2012)**; **Joaquin (2015)** — devastated southern Bahamas (Crooked Island, Long Island); **Matthew (2016)** — Grand Bahama + Andros; **DORIAN (Sep 2019)** — **Cat 5 stalled over Abaco + Grand Bahama for ~48 hr**, sustained winds 185 mph + 23-foot storm surge, **estimated 75%+ residential damage** on parts of Abaco; reset insurance market and code conversation; **Isaias (2020)**; **Nicole (2022)**.
- **Implication**: post-2019 (Dorian) builds + insurance practice materially tighter. Pre-2002 builds + non-engineered Family-Island stock often non-compliant with current code wind ratings.
- **Bahamas Building Code (BBC)**: wind-zone classification; high-wind zones require impact-rated glazing + reinforced roofing + uplift connectors. **Verify BBC compliance certificate** for any post-1992 build; for pre-1992, retrofit cost is meaningful.

### Insurance — thin and expensive

- **Hurricane peril** bundled with all-risk fire; **CAT deductibles 2–5%** of sum insured + per-storm aggregate caps; **rates ~0.4–1.5% of insured value/yr** for ordinary residential (varies materially by island / wind zone / build year).
- **Post-Dorian re-rating**: some carriers withdrew or tightened terms for Abaco / Grand Bahama; reinsurance capacity globally tightened 2020–2024; **Family Island remote properties may face declined coverage** or limited-line offers.
- **Insurance market**: regulated by **[Insurance Commission of The Bahamas (ICB)](https://www.icb.gov.bs/)**; major carriers **Bahamas First**, **Insurance Company of The Bahamas (ICB)**, **NUA Insurance Brokers**, **Royal Star Assurance**, **Family Guardian Insurance**.
- **Verification path**: get **3+ insurance quotes** for the specific parcel before contract; price the policy + deductible into the 5-year carrying-cost model.

### Flood / storm surge

- **Coastal storm surge** is the catastrophic mechanism — Dorian's 23-ft surge devastated Marsh Harbour, Treasure Cay, Hope Town. Verify **elevation above mean sea level** + **distance from shoreline**.
- **Flood maps**: **Disaster Risk Management (DRM) Authority (successor to NEMA) + Department of Lands and Surveys** maintain disaster-risk-management mapping; coverage uneven; **no FEMA-equivalent parcel-level flood-zone web viewer**. Verification path: request DLS / DRM Authority per-parcel review OR engage local engineering surveyor.
- **Sea-level rise (SLR)**: IPCC AR6 + **Climate Central Coastal Risk Screening Tool** (`https://coastal.climatecentral.org/`) — Bahamas is among the world's most SLR-exposed nations; **average elevation ~10 m AMSL**; large land areas < 5 m. By 2100 SSP5-8.5 (~+0.85 m SLR), material portions of Andros, Grand Bahama, Abaco, Inagua become flood-vulnerable.

### Seismic — LOW

- The Bahamas sits on a **stable platform** north of the Caribbean plate boundary; no major active fault crosses the archipelago.
- **Background seismicity**: occasional felt tremors from distant Caribbean events (Hispaniola, Puerto Rico).
- **Practical**: standard structural review only; not a primary risk factor.

### Sinkhole / karst geology

- The Bahamas is a **carbonate platform**; **karst geology** dominant; **blue holes** and **inland sinkholes** distinctive feature.
- **Risk**: localised sinkhole subsidence; engineering review for any structure on suspected karst feature.

### Tsunami

- Background risk from far-field Caribbean / Atlantic events; locally generated tsunami risk LOW.

### Climate change projections (2050 horizon — IPCC AR6 + Caribbean Community Climate Change Centre)

- **Hurricane intensity** trending up — increased Cat 4–5 share (Dorian 2019, Maria 2017, Irma 2017 cluster) — IPCC AR6 + NOAA Atlantic basin assessments.
- **Sea-level rise**: +0.20–0.50 m by 2100 SSP2-4.5; +0.45–1.10 m by 2100 SSP5-8.5; **acute exposure in low-lying Family Islands** (Andros, Inagua, parts of Abaco + Grand Bahama).
- **Drought**: northern Bahamas slightly wetter projections; southern Bahamas (Inagua, Crooked, Acklins, Mayaguana) drier — coral / freshwater-lens stress.
- **Coral bleaching + ocean acidification**: material to beach replenishment + diving-tourism revenue.
- **Extreme rainfall hourly intensity**: +20–40% likely by mid-century — affects drainage capacity for Nassau urban + Marsh Harbour.

### Water + sewer — fragmented + island-specific

| Operator | Coverage |
|---|---|

| **Grand Bahama Utility Company (GBUC, owned by Grand Bahama Port Authority)** | Grand Bahama / Freeport — water + power + sewer on private utility model |
| **Bahamas Power and Light (BPL)** | Electricity — national except Grand Bahama (GBPC) and certain Family-Island IPPs |

URLs: `https://www.wsc.com.bs/`, `https://www.bplco.com/`, `https://www.gbpa.com/` (Grand Bahama Port Authority).

### Sewer reality

- **Nassau urban core**: WSC public sewer in central downtown + selected subdivisions; **most New Providence residential** still on **individual septic tank** (not collective sewer).
- **Resort communities + master-planned developments** (Lyford Cay, Albany, Old Fort Bay, Sandyport, Ocean Club Estates, Baker's Bay, Emerald Bay): **project-internal water + sewer** (private package plants, often tertiary treatment), typically fully reticulated.
- **Family Islands**: **septic + cistern** standard. Cistern fed by rainwater catchment (almost universal — Bahamian roof catchment by code) + RO desalination (high-end) + truck-delivered water (some islands).
- **Listing clue**: "well + septic" or "cistern + septic" = no mains; "city water + city sewer" = WSC service (verify with operator).
- **Potability**: **rainwater cistern + RO drinking water** standard; tap-water potability variable by location.

### Power

- **National grid**: **Bahamas Power and Light (BPL)** — historically high tariffs + reliability issues; **frequent outages** especially during/post-storms.
- **Generator + inverter + solar standard** for any property requiring continuity (medical, freezer, work-from-home).
- **Grand Bahama**: **Grand Bahama Power Company (GBPC)** — separate utility under GBPA framework.
- **Family Islands**: **BPL or independent power producers (IPPs)**; some islands run on diesel-only with daily curtailment in low season.

## Section: `--crime`

### Primary source

- **[Royal Bahamas Police Force (RBPF) — Crime Statistics](https://www.royalbahamaspolice.org/)** — quarterly + annual reports; New Providence-dominant.
- **[Bahamas National Statistical Institute](https://www.bahamas.gov.bs/statistics)** — limited public time series; aggregated.
- **UNODC** + **UK FCDO Travel Advice** + **US State Department Crime & Safety Reports** — comparative reference.

### Key context

- The Bahamas has **elevated property + violent crime rates concentrated in specific Nassau districts** (south-central Nassau "Over-The-Hill" + western New Providence) — **homicide rate ~30+ per 100,000** historically (RBPF + UNODC; *figures fluctuate by year — verify current at RBPF*).
- **Crime is geographically concentrated**: Lyford Cay / Albany / Old Fort Bay / Paradise Island / Cable Beach / Sandyport are very low; Family Islands generally very low; **central/south Nassau materially higher**.
- **Property crime**: burglary + vehicle theft + opportunistic theft; **homes left unoccupied off-season** are a target — security service + alarm + caretaker arrangement standard for vacation homes.
- **Tourist-area crime**: pickpocketing + scams along cruise-ship corridor (Bay Street); **Atlantis + Baha Mar tourist zones** maintained at very high security.

### Specific risk vectors

- **Burglary of unoccupied vacation homes**: the operationally meaningful risk for international owners — signal occupancy via lighting, caretaker visits, neighbour relationships.
- **Vehicle theft**: more common Nassau urban; rare Family Islands.
- **Violent crime**: gang-related concentrated in specific Nassau districts; not a tourist-area risk but is reflected in RBPF homicide statistics.
- **Cyber-fraud / wire-fraud against international buyers**: **rising** — fraudulent wire-instruction emails impersonating attorneys are a documented Caribbean-wide pattern; ALWAYS verify wire instructions verbally with attorney via known phone before sending purchase funds.

### Verdict bands (Bahamas-specific)

- 🟢 < 50% national rate (Lyford Cay, Albany, Old Fort Bay, Ocean Club Estates, Baker's Bay, gated subdivisions; most Family Islands)
- 🟡 50–110% national rate (Cable Beach, Sandyport, Paradise Island residential, Eastern Road)
- 🟠 110–200% one+ category (East New Providence outer, parts of Pinewood Gardens, Carmichael Rd corridor)
- 🔴 > 200% national in multiple categories (Over-The-Hill — Bain Town, Grants Town, parts of Centreville; specific named gang hotspots)

### Confidence

MEDIUM. RBPF publishes aggregated district crime totals but not parcel-level detail comparable to most Western statistical offices. **Numbeo** + **diplomatic security advisories** (UK FCDO, US OSAC) supplement.

## Section: `--climate`

Universal logic per `shared/climate-projections.md`. Bahamas-specific overrides:

- **Köppen-Geiger**: **Aw (tropical savanna)** for most of the archipelago; **Am (tropical monsoon)** for some northern islands; **BSh (semi-arid)** in southern islands (Inagua).
- **Coastal exposure**: virtually every Bahamian property is < 5km from coast; **average elevation ~10m AMSL**; **highest natural point Mount Alvernia, Cat Island, ~63m AMSL**.
- **Reference baseline**: Bahamas Department of Meteorology 1991–2020 normal series + IPCC AR6 + Caribbean Community Climate Change Centre.
- **Key projections** (IPCC AR6 + Caribbean Community Climate Change Centre, SSP2-4.5 / SSP5-8.5):
  - Annual mean temperature **+1.0 → +2.5°C** by 2100 (lower-bound for Caribbean SIDS).
  - Hot days (Tmax ≥ 32°C): increasing materially; tropical nights (Tmin ≥ 24°C) becoming year-round in summer months.
  - **Sea-level rise**: +0.30 → +0.85 m by 2100 SSP5-8.5 — **acute exposure** for low-lying parcels (Inagua, parts of Andros + Grand Bahama + Abaco + Bimini).
  - **Hurricane intensity**: trending toward higher Cat 4–5 share (Dorian 2019 calibration event).
  - **Extreme rainfall hourly intensity**: +20–40% by mid-century — affects drainage + cistern overflow.
  - **Coral bleaching**: increasing frequency + severity — affects beach replenishment + reef-tourism economics.
  - **Drought**: southern islands drier projections; northern islands neutral-to-wetter.
- **Acute-event flags**:
  - 🔴 Coastal property < 5m AMSL on storm-surge-exposed coast (Marsh Harbour, Treasure Cay, Hope Town pre-Dorian footprints; Inagua south coast; Andros east coast)
  - 🔴 Hurricane Cat 4–5 risk applies to entire archipelago (no safe-zone exemption)
  - 🟠 Insurance market re-rating risk — premium escalation + coverage tightening
  - 🟠 Reef + beach erosion long-term affecting amenity value
  - 🟡 Heat stress increasing — operating cost (cooling) escalation

### Climate verdict (typical New Providence / Paradise Island residential, not directly storm-surge-exposed)

🟠 **Adapt** — material hurricane risk requires impact-glazing + reinforced roofing + insurance budget; SLR a 2050+ horizon issue for sub-5m elevation; cooling load increasing.

### Climate verdict (low-lying Family Island, Abaco/Grand Bahama post-Dorian footprint)

🔴 **Avoid or fortify** — Cat 4–5 hurricane history demonstrated; insurance availability + cost a binding constraint; SLR exposure compounds; only acquire with engineered post-2019-code construction + funded insurance + deep budget for hurricane window.

## Section: `--currency`

- **Currency**: **Bahamian Dollar (B$ / BSD)**.
- **Peg**: **1:1 with USD** since 1973 — a managed peg administered via **Exchange Control Regulations 1956** + CBOB monetary operations.
- **USD circulates in parallel**: virtually all property transactions are USD-denominated or USD-equivalent BSD; high-end luxury markets (Lyford Cay, Albany, Baker's Bay) quote and close USD-firm.
- **Exchange Control Department (CBOB)**: governs non-resident currency flows. Foreign buyers obtain Exchange Control approval (procedural, post-IPLA 1993 substantially streamlined) for incoming-currency property purchase + outgoing-currency on resale.
- **Practical effect for buyer**: zero structural FX risk for USD-base buyers; minimal risk for EUR/GBP/CAD buyers (USD-correlated). Devaluation risk: peg has held since 1973 through significant external shocks; **CBOB FX reserves** + IMF Article IV monitoring underpin credibility.
- **Verify**: any non-resident purchase requires Exchange Control approval routed via attorney + receiving Bahamian bank — this is typically a routine filing, not a gating risk.

## Active fiscal incentives + visa programs (2025–2026)

### Tax-incentive regimes (real-estate-relevant)

- **No personal income tax / capital gains / inheritance / wealth tax** — applies universally; no special application required.
- **Hotel Encouragement Act (Chapter 289)** — incentives for hotel + ancillary developments (NOT ordinary single-family vacation rentals): customs-duty exemption on construction materials + furnishings + 10–20-year tax-incentive period; targeted at developers building 10+ room hotels or commercial tourism developments.
- **Family Island Development Encouragement Act** — incentives for development on specified Family Islands.
- **Industries Encouragement Act** — non-real-estate but relevant for buyers establishing related Bahamian businesses.
- **City of Nassau Revitalization Act** — targeted incentives for downtown Nassau redevelopment.
- **Free Trade Zone — Grand Bahama (Hawksbill Creek Agreement, 1955)** — Grand Bahama Port Authority (GBPA) jurisdiction in Freeport: customs-duty + business-licence + property-tax concessions to **2054** under the original agreement (with periodic extensions); affects Freeport residential pricing dynamic.

### Residency / citizenship

- **Annual Residence Card (Homeowner)** — light-touch annual renewal for property owners; no right to work.
- **Economic Permanent Residence (EPR)**: **B$1,000,000** standard (raised from B$750,000 eff. 1 Jan 2025; 10-yr minimum-hold) / B$1,500,000+ accelerated.
- **Bahamian citizenship by descent / marriage / naturalisation** — 6–9 yr residence + Nationality Act vetting; **NO CBI**.

## Caveats unique to BS

- **Title chain is critical**: the **abstract-of-title** (English-style, not Torrens) means the buyer's attorney must trace 30+ years of conveyances. Older Family Island parcels may have **clouded chains** — Quieting of Titles Act process can run **1–7 years** to resolve. Avoid clouded-chain parcels unless your attorney is a specialist.
- **Commonage** (peculiar Bahamian doctrine): collective unrecorded historical claim by extended families on parts of Eleuthera, Cat Island, Long Island, Exuma — overlaps with recorded title. **Do not buy commonage land** without an attorney specifically experienced in commonage resolution.
- **Crown Land**: state-owned land leased on 21 / 99-year terms; lease renewal at Crown discretion; not the same as freehold — verify.
- **Hurricane Dorian (Sep 2019)** is the calibration event for any Abaco / Grand Bahama purchase. **Pre-2002 unrenovated builds in Dorian-affected areas should be priced + insured as high-risk**. Verify post-Dorian engineering certificate where applicable.
- **Insurance market is thin and expensive**: get **3+ quotes** for the parcel before contract; declined coverage on remote Family Islands is a meaningful walk-away signal.
- **HOA / Owners Association rules in branded gated estates** materially constrain short-term-rental income models — read Declaration / By-Laws before pricing yield.
- **VAT-on-conveyance + Stamp Duty stack**: ~10% combined above B$100k; split 50/50 by convention but negotiable; BDR reduces for Bahamian-domicile buyers.
- **No Torrens title on most parcels**: title insurance + thorough abstract is the buyer's protection.
- **IPLA permit (>5 acres or undeveloped)**: 60–120 day processing; plan timeline.
- **Wire fraud against international buyers** is rising — verify wire instructions verbally with attorney via known phone before sending purchase funds.
- **Cyclone-season closing**: avoid completion during peak hurricane months (Aug–Oct) where possible; insurance binders may have hurricane-eve cut-offs.
- **Family Island air access**: serious medical conditions require air-ambulance to Nassau or Miami — material for retirees + older buyers.
- **Beneficial-ownership disclosure** (Register of Beneficial Ownership Act 2018): IBC / trust holdings disclose UBO to Registrar General; affects privacy thesis.
- **No double-tax treaties** (Bahamas has TIEAs but not comprehensive DTAs) — home-country tax-residency drives tax outcome on Bahamas-sourced rent / capital gains realised in your home jurisdiction.
- **CRS + FATCA reporting**: Bahamian banks report account information to relevant tax authorities annually.
- **Power reliability**: BPL + GBPC outages are routine; budget for generator/inverter for any continuous-occupancy property.
- **Cistern + RO water culture**: even where mains exist, rainwater catchment + RO drinking is standard; budget cistern maintenance.
