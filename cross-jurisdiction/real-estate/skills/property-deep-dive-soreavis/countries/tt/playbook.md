# Trinidad and Tobago 🇹🇹 — Property Due-Diligence Playbook

ISO2: `tt`. Status: ✅ Fully populated (researched 2026-06-13).

> **Critical framing — read first**: Trinidad and Tobago is a twin-island **English common-law republic** in the southernmost Caribbean, off NE Venezuela. Final appeals go to the **UK Judicial Committee of the Privy Council (JCPC) in London — NOT the Caribbean Court of Justice**: T&T has acceded only to the CCJ's *Original Jurisdiction* (interpreting the Revised Treaty of Chaguaramas), retaining the JCPC for final civil and criminal appeals (source: [Globalex/NYU Law T&T guide](https://www.nyulawglobal.org/globalex/Trinidad_Tobago1.html)). Foreign buyers face **NO outright ban** but a **threshold + licence regime** under the **Foreign Investment Act, Chap. 70:07 (Act 16 of 1990)**: in **Trinidad**, a foreign investor may acquire up to **1 acre for residential** purposes and up to **5 acres for trade/business** *licence-free*; above those acreages a **licence of the President of Trinidad and Tobago** is required (source: Foreign Investment Act ss.6–7, [text via Ministry of Trade & Industry](https://tradeind.gov.tt/wp-content/uploads/2016/02/Foreign-Investment-Act-70.07.pdf); statute host [laws.gov.tt](https://laws.gov.tt/)). **Tobago is STRICTER**: under **The Foreign Investment (Tobago Land Acquisition) Order, 2007 (effective 16 February 2007)**, **ALL** foreign acquisition of **ANY** Tobago land requires a President's licence *regardless of size* — no 1-acre allowance. Even a licence-free buyer must **pay the price in an internationally traded currency through an authorised dealer (FIA s.10(1))** and **file a Third-Schedule notice with the Minister of Finance (s.8(2))**. **Currency**: the **TTD is a managed float with a de facto soft USD anchor**, trading ~**TTD 6.74–6.78 per USD** in 2025–2026 (source: [CBTT daily exchange rates](https://www.central-bank.org.tt/exchange-rates-daily/)); there is **no legal capital-repatriation bar**, but a genuine, multi-year **USD shortage means authorised dealers ration forex** — repatriating sale proceeds can be slow/uncertain, and this is the **single biggest practical exit risk** for a foreign owner (source: [EUROCHAMTT FX challenges](https://eurochamtt.org/); [IMF 2026 Article IV](https://www.imf.org/en/Countries/TTO)). **Tax stack headline**: annual **property tax reintroduced — residential 2% of Annual Taxable Value (ATV = Annual Rental Value less a 10% voids deduction) ≈1.8% effective on ARV**, cut from 3% by the **Property Tax (Amendment) Act 2024 (Act No. 3 of 2024, assented 27 Mar 2024)**, collection began 2024, first payment due 30 Sep 2024, then collection HALTED per the FY2026 Budget Statement of 13 Oct 2025 ([Budget Statement FY2026](https://www.finance.gov.tt/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Budget-Statement-FY-2026-1.pdf)) — confirm with IRD whether any assessment applies to the purchase year (source: [tax.mygovtt.com](https://tax.mygovtt.com/); [Ministry of Finance media release 18 Nov 2023](https://www.finance.gov.tt/tag/property-tax/)); **VAT 12.5%** (residential rent exempt); **CGT only on disposals within 12 months** (taxed as income); first-time-buyer **stamp duty TT$0 on residential house+land up to TT$2,000,000**. **NO Citizenship-by-Investment** programme exists, and buying property confers **NO residency, work, or citizenship right** (source: immigration guides; [Goldenvisas T&T](https://goldenvisas.com/)). **Top hazard**: **high seismic activity** — ~**260 earthquakes >M2.0/yr** with a historical M7.9 (1766) on record (source: [UWI Seismic Research Centre — Trinidad profile](https://uwiseismic.com/island-profiles/trinidad/)) — making seismic build-quality the #1 diligence item; hurricane direct-strike risk is comparatively **low** (south of the main Atlantic belt), but flooding is material in the wet season. **DUAL land-title system**: most land (~75–80%, *est. per Fitzwilliam Stone*) is **Old-Law deeds** with NO state title guarantee — an attorney must trace the chain of title; the minority is RPA/Torrens **Certificate of Title (indefeasible)**. **Confirm** every licence requirement, tax band, FX-availability position, and title status at primary sources **before any binding step**. Authoritative primary sources: **[Ministry of Finance](https://www.finance.gov.tt/)**, **[Inland Revenue Division / BIR](https://ird.gov.tt/)**, **[Central Bank of Trinidad and Tobago (CBTT)](https://www.central-bank.org.tt/)**, **[Ministry of Legal Affairs / laws.gov.tt](https://laws.gov.tt/)**, **[Immigration Division](https://nationalsecurity.gov.tt/)**, **[UWI Seismic Research Centre](https://uwiseismic.com/)**.

## Country profile

- **Geography**: Twin-island republic, southernmost Caribbean, off the NE coast of Venezuela. Two main islands — **Trinidad** (larger, ~4,768 km²) and **Tobago** (~300 km²) plus minor islets. Capital **Port of Spain** (Trinidad); Tobago's chief town is **Scarborough**. (Britannica / Wikipedia geographic reference.)
- **Population**: **~1.37 million** (2024, [World Bank](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SP.POP.TOTL?locations=TT) = 1,368,333). Projection-based aggregators (StatisticsTimes / Worldometer) cite ~1.5M; this playbook uses the World Bank figure throughout for internal consistency with the GDP/capita and crime-rate denominators below.
- **GDP per capita**: **~USD 19,315 nominal** (2024, [World Bank](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/NY.GDP.PCAP.CD?locations=TT)); **~USD 36,021 PPP** (2024, World Bank). One of the higher-income Caribbean economies — energy-driven (oil & gas / petrochemical). (Author note: World Bank renders the live figure via JS; confirm the exact 2024 number on the indicator page.)
- **Currency**: **Trinidad and Tobago dollar (TTD / TT$)**, CBTT-issued, managed float with a de facto soft USD anchor (see `--currency`).
- **Languages**: **English** (official, sole legal language — all deeds/contracts/statutes in English). Trinidadian English Creole is the everyday vernacular; Trinidadian Hindustani and French Creole heritage persist in some communities. (Britannica / Wikipedia.)
- **Legal system**: **English-derived common law** (former British colony; independent 1962, republic 1976). (Source: [Globalex/NYU Law](https://www.nyulawglobal.org/globalex/Trinidad_Tobago1.html).)
- **FINAL / APEX COURT — UK Judicial Committee of the Privy Council (JCPC), London — NOT the CCJ.** T&T retains the JCPC for final civil and criminal appeals; it has acceded ONLY to the CCJ's **Original Jurisdiction** (Revised Treaty of Chaguaramas), NOT its Appellate Jurisdiction. Domestic hierarchy: **Magistrates' Courts → High Court → Court of Appeal → JCPC**. (Sources: [Globalex/NYU Law](https://www.nyulawglobal.org/globalex/Trinidad_Tobago1.html); [FJC Judiciaries Worldwide](https://judiciariesworldwide.fjc.gov/country-profile/trinidad-and-tobago).) → For a high-value title dispute, the ultimate forum is the UK Privy Council.
- **Admin levels**: Trinidad has regional corporations and city/borough municipalities. **Tobago is governed by the Tobago House of Assembly (THA)**, established by the **Tobago House of Assembly Act, 1996**, with internal self-government (no separate primary law-making power; THA elections every 4 years). (Source: [Globalex/NYU Law](https://www.nyulawglobal.org/globalex/Trinidad_Tobago1.html).) → Tobago's distinct governance underpins its stricter land-acquisition rules (see Foreign buyer eligibility).
- **Cadastre / land registry**:
  - **Registrar General's Department** (Deeds Registry + Land Registry) under the Ministry of the Attorney General & Legal Affairs — records Deeds of Conveyance (Old Law) and Certificates of Title (RPA).
  - **Lands & Surveys Division** (Ministry of Agriculture, Land & Fisheries) — cadastral surveys / parcels. [VERIFY current departmental home + online search availability at the Registrar General.]
- **Identifier**: For RPA/Torrens land, a **Certificate of Title** (Volume/Folio reference). For Old-Law deeds land, ownership is proven by a **Deed of Conveyance** lodged at the Deeds Registry traced through a **Chain of Title** — **no universal national parcel ID** equivalent to a French *numéro de parcelle*.
- **Title concept**: **Freehold (fee simple)** dominant under a **dual registration system** (see below). Long leases exist (esp. state/THA land).

### TITLE CONCEPT / CADASTRE — DUAL SYSTEM (LOAD-BEARING for due diligence)

T&T runs **two parallel land-registration systems, both live** (Source: [Fitzwilliam Stone — Land Title Systems](https://fitzwilliamstone.com/legal-insight/land-title-systems-in-trinidad-and-tobago/); Cornerstone conveyancing pages):

1. **Old Law / Common Law system (deeds registration)** — the MAJORITY: **~75–80% of land** (*est., per Fitzwilliam Stone — cite as a law-firm estimate, not a hard statistic*). Ownership is proven by a **Deed (Deed of Conveyance)** lodged at the **Deeds Registry of the Registrar General's Department**. Title rests on a "Good Root of Title" (a deed ≥20 years old) traced through an unbroken **Chain of Title**. **NO state guarantee** — unregistered equitable interests can create title challenges; a rigorous attorney title search is essential. → higher diligence burden; chain gaps, squatting and adverse-possession are real risks.
2. **RPA / Real Property Act (Chap. 56:02), 1945** (in force 1 Jan 1946) — **Torrens title registration**, the minority of land. Ownership is proven by a **Certificate of Title** held at the **Land Registry**; the registered proprietor holds an **INDEFEASIBLE title** (challengeable only for fraud or adverse possession), built on the Torrens mirror / curtain / insurance principles. → much cleaner to verify; preferred. (Source: Fitzwilliam Stone; RPA text hosted at [finance.gov.tt — Real Property Act (PDF)](https://www.finance.gov.tt/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/The-Real-Property-Act.pdf).)

- **Modernisation — RTLA 2000 (status uncertain)**: The **Registration of Titles to Land Act 2000** (primary text hosted at [agriculture.gov.tt](https://agriculture.gov.tt/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/Registration-of-Titles-to-Land-Act.pdf)) was enacted to move toward a unified title-registration regime, but its **full operational/commencement status is not publicly confirmed** — sources still describe the dual Old-Law/RPA split as the LIVE regime. → State as: "enacted; full operational status not publicly confirmed — verify commencement at the Registrar General / [laws.gov.tt](https://laws.gov.tt/)." Do NOT assert a unified register is operational. [OPEN GAP]
- **DUE-DILIGENCE FLAG**: Because most land is Old-Law deeds, a buyer **cannot rely on a single authoritative "title register" lookup** the way one can in a pure-Torrens country — an attorney must trace the chain of title. NEVER treat a listing's ownership claim as confirmed without an attorney's title search. (per listing — verify with Registrar General / on attorney search.)

## 🇹🇹 Foreign buyer eligibility (READ FIRST)

This section determines whether a transaction is even legally possible — and what process applies. T&T operates a **threshold + licence regime under the Foreign Investment Act**, NOT an outright ban. The gating mechanism is **acreage + island (Tobago is stricter)** plus a **hard-currency-settlement requirement**, not equal-footing freehold.

### Legal basis

- **Foreign Investment Act, Chap. 70:07 (Act No. 16 of 1990)** — the controlling statute; came into force **17 August 1990** and **repealed the Aliens (Landholding) Act, Chap. 58:02** (so the old "Aliens Landholding Act" framing is OBSOLETE — do not cite it as current). Amended by 6/1991, 33/1995, 4/1997, 2/2005, **17/2007**. (Source: [text via Ministry of Trade & Industry](https://tradeind.gov.tt/wp-content/uploads/2016/02/Foreign-Investment-Act-70.07.pdf); statute host [laws.gov.tt](https://laws.gov.tt/); Cornerstone; Fitzwilliam Stone; UNCTAD.)
- **The Foreign Investment (Tobago Land Acquisition) Order, 2007** — prescribes ALL Tobago land as requiring a licence (effective 16 Feb 2007), operating as a s.6(2) prescribed-area Order. (Order name + date firm across multiple T&T law-firm sources; **primary Order text not retrieved — confirm at [laws.gov.tt — FIA Chap. 70:07 record (lists LN. 53/2007)](http://laws.gov.tt/ttdll-web/revision/list?offset=180&q=&currentid=768) / Official Gazette**.)
- **Real Property Act, Chap. 56:02 (1945)** + **Old-Law deeds registration** — the dual title system (above).
- **Property Tax Act 2009 (Chap. 76:04)** (reactivated by Finance Act 2023) + **Stamp Duty** regime (BIR) + **Income Tax Act** — transaction + holding taxes.
- **Immigration Act, Chap. 18:01** — residency routes (no investment-linked route; see `--visa`).

### Buyer category matrix

| Buyer category | Right |
|---|---|
| **T&T citizen** | Full freehold; standard conveyancing |
| **CARICOM national** | Treated as a NATIONAL, not a foreign investor (FIA s.2) — NOT subject to the foreign-investor licence regime |
| **Foreign investor — Trinidad, residential ≤ 1 acre** | **Licence-free** under FIA s.6(1); must still pay in hard currency (s.10(1)) + file a Third-Schedule notice with the Minister of Finance (s.8(2)) |
| **Foreign investor — Trinidad, trade/business ≤ 5 acres** | **Licence-free** under FIA s.7; same hard-currency + notice obligations |
| **Foreign investor — Trinidad, ABOVE the acreage limits** | **Licence of the President of Trinidad and Tobago** required (FIA; consistent with s.11(1) "licence of the President"); applications processed via the Ministry of Finance |
| **Foreign investor — ANY land in Tobago (any size)** | **President's licence required regardless of size**, per The Foreign Investment (Tobago Land Acquisition) Order, 2007 (effective 16 Feb 2007); examined with the THA |
| **Foreign investor jointly with a CARICOM-citizen spouse resident in T&T** | Licence-free even above limits (FIA s.8(1)(e)) |

(Source: Foreign Investment Act ss.2, 6, 7, 8, 10, 11 — [text via Ministry of Trade & Industry](https://tradeind.gov.tt/wp-content/uploads/2016/02/Foreign-Investment-Act-70.07.pdf); restated verbatim by Cornerstone conveyancing; corroborated by Fitzwilliam Stone, Veridian, Massy Realty. **Above-threshold/Tobago licence grantor = the President** — well-corroborated across law-firm sources; for statute-verbatim s.12 wording, pull from [laws.gov.tt — FIA Chap. 70:07 record](http://laws.gov.tt/ttdll-web/revision/list?offset=180&q=&currentid=768).)

> ⚠️ **"One residential parcel only" is NOT statute.** Some realtors state a foreign buyer is limited to a single residential parcel. This is **NOT in the Foreign Investment Act text reviewed** (Cornerstone explicitly does not state a single-parcel limit) — treat it as realtor guidance, not law. Verify in the Regulations / any Order before relying on it. [MUST-HEDGE]

### Visa / residency via property — NONE

- **Buying property confers NO residency, work, or citizenship right.** Property ownership and immigration status are entirely separate in T&T. (Consistent across immigration guides; no contradicting primary source found.)
- **NO Citizenship-by-Investment (CBI) programme.** T&T does NOT appear on any Caribbean CBI provider list (Henley & Partners, NTL Trust); [Goldenvisas](https://goldenvisas.com/) states T&T "does not currently offer a citizenship by investment route." Citizenship is by naturalisation/descent only. **Do NOT list T&T among Caribbean CBI countries.**
- Residency routes are general (5-year PR, work permit, spouse) and discretionary — see `--visa` and `--digital-nomad`.

### Compliance / AML

- **Settlement currency (FIA s.10(1))**: the consideration for land acquired by a foreign investor **must be paid in an internationally traded currency through a person authorised by law as a dealer in that foreign currency** (i.e. through a bank / authorised dealer), except where a T&T-incorporated company funds it from local capital/retained earnings. → Inbound money comes IN as USD/hard currency through a bank, creating a paper trail. (Source: FIA s.10(1), Cornerstone verbatim restatement.)
- **Third-Schedule notice (FIA s.8(2))**: on vesting of land in a licence-free foreign investor (under s.6/7/8(1)(e)), the investor and their attorney **must give notice of the vesting to the Minister in duplicate in the Third Schedule form**, with a true copy of the instrument; the Minister returns the duplicate within 7 days. → A licence-free foreign buyer STILL files a notice with the Minister of Finance.
- **Offence (FIA s.9)**: knowingly causing land to vest in a foreign investor without a required licence is an offence, fine **TT$100,000**.
- **Anti-nominee (FIA s.11)**: no person shall, without the **licence of the President**, hold land or local-public-company shares in trust for an unlicensed foreign investor — blocks nominee/trust workarounds.
- **AML / source-of-funds**: attorneys and real-estate agents are subject to T&T's AML/CFT framework (FIU-supervised); source-of-funds documentation expected. [Confirm current AML obligations with the attorney + FIU TT.]

### Verification path (before any reservation)

1. **Determine the licence trigger**: confirm island (Trinidad vs Tobago) + acreage + use. Tobago = licence always; Trinidad residential > 1 acre or commercial > 5 acres = licence; otherwise licence-free but notice-bound.
2. **Engage a T&T attorney for the title search**: Old-Law deeds parcels need a full chain-of-title trace at the Deeds Registry; RPA parcels need the Certificate of Title extract at the Land Registry.
3. **Arrange hard-currency settlement** through an authorised dealer (commercial bank) per FIA s.10(1).
4. **Plan repatriation early**: because USD is rationed (see `--currency`), discuss with the bank how/when sale proceeds could later be converted and remitted — there is no legal bar, but a real availability queue.
5. **File the Third-Schedule notice** with the Minister of Finance on a licence-free vesting (s.8(2)); or **lodge the President's licence application** via the Ministry of Finance for above-threshold / Tobago purchases.
6. **AML / source-of-funds**: bank statements + asset/employment evidence ready before signing.

### Compute

1. **TTD or USD/m² = price ÷ built-area m²** (some listings quote square feet — convert: 1 m² = 10.764 sqft).
2. Compare to **≥ 3 active listings** on the same area + segment + waterfront status (community + waterfront status drive price more than island averages).
3. **Listing convention**: confirm whether the asking price is TTD or USD; remember the foreign buyer pays in hard currency through an authorised dealer regardless (FIA s.10).
4. **Trap — title-system status**: an RPA/Torrens Certificate-of-Title parcel is faster and cleaner to convey than an Old-Law deeds parcel — confirm which system the parcel is on at the Registrar General (it materially affects diligence cost + timeline).
5. **Trap — area definitions**: built-up area vs gross area (incl. covered patios) vs lot area. Listings often quote the largest. Confirm against the survey plan / RPA Certificate.
6. **Trap — acreage vs the FIA threshold**: a Trinidad residential parcel above 1 acre triggers the President's-licence requirement for a foreign buyer — size is a *legal* discriminator, not just a price one.

### Key terms

- **Deed of Conveyance** — the Old-Law instrument transferring title (English-style); lodged at the Deeds Registry.
- **Chain of Title / Good Root of Title** — the unbroken deed history (root deed ≥20 years) an attorney must trace on Old-Law land.
- **Certificate of Title** — the RPA/Torrens (Real Property Act 1945) indefeasible-title alternative.
- **Annual Rental Value (ARV) / Annual Taxable Value (ATV)** — ARV is the assessed annual rent; **ATV = ARV less a 10% voids deduction** and is the base the reintroduced property tax is actually charged on.
- **Authorised dealer** — a bank licensed to deal in foreign currency; the mandatory settlement channel under FIA s.10.

## Section: `--tax`

> **Headline**: T&T **reintroduced annual property tax** (residential **2% of Annual Taxable Value (ARV less a 10% voids deduction) ≈ 1.8% effective on ARV**, cut from 3% by the Property Tax (Amendment) Act 2024 (Act No. 3 of 2024), collection began 2024 — then HALTED: the FY2026 Budget Statement (13 Oct 2025) states "We halted the Property Tax"; confirm with IRD whether any property tax is being assessed for the purchase year). Individual income tax is **25% / 30%** (split at TT$1,000,000 chargeable income) with a **TT$90,000 personal allowance**. **VAT 12.5%** (residential rent + financial services exempt). **CGT applies only to disposals within 12 months of acquisition** (taxed as ordinary income); longer holds are not subject to CGT. **First-time-buyer stamp duty is TT$0 on residential house+land up to TT$2,000,000**. A **Landlord Business Surcharge** (Finance Act 2025, effective 1 Jan 2026) is **creditable against income tax** — a cash-flow/timing cost, not a permanent yield haircut.

Administered by the **Inland Revenue Division (IRD) / Board of Inland Revenue (BIR)**, Ministry of Finance.

> ⚠️ **Data-quality warning**: the *official* portal **[tax.mygovtt.com/resources/tax-rates](https://tax.mygovtt.com/)** is **STALE** on income tax — it still shows the pre-2023 TT$84,000 allowance and an erroneous TT$120,000 30%-band threshold. The current figures below are from **[PwC Tax Summaries (last reviewed 02 Jun 2026)](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/trinidad-and-tobago/individual/taxes-on-personal-income)** + the 2023 budget record. Do NOT "upgrade" to the mygovtt portal income-tax numbers — this is the one place where a gov-domain page is less current than a dated Big-4 source.

### Annual Property Tax — REINTRODUCED 2024, HALTED 2025

- **Legal basis**: Property Tax Act 2009 (Chap. 76:04), collection machinery reactivated by the **Finance Act 2023**. Property tax was dormant ~14 years (effectively suspended after 2009/2010) and collection **commenced in 2024**. (Source: [tax.mygovtt.com](https://tax.mygovtt.com/) — its "3% On Annual Rental Value (ARV)" string is **(superseded)** by the Property Tax (Amendment) Act 2024, see the rate note below; [Office of the Prime Minister](https://www.opm.gov.tt/); [Ministry of Finance media release 18 Nov 2023](https://www.finance.gov.tt/tag/property-tax/); [IRD — Amended Property Tax Notices of Assessment](https://www.ird.gov.tt/news/amended-property-tax-notices-of-assessment); T&T Mortgage Bank explainer.) → **Collection was HALTED by the 2025 government**: the FY2026 Budget Statement (13 Oct 2025, p.22) states "We halted the Property Tax that created anxiety for thousands of people, including the elderly, retired and low-income" ([Budget Statement FY2026](https://www.finance.gov.tt/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Budget-Statement-FY-2026-1.pdf)). No Property Tax repeal Bill or Act was located on ttparliament.org this pass, so the Act and its 2% rate are read as still on the books with collection halted — the legal instrument of the halt is `data not publicly available — verify at IRD / Ministry of Finance`.
- **Residential rate: 2% of Annual Taxable Value (ATV)**, where **ATV = Annual Rental Value (ARV) LESS a 10% deduction** for voids/loss of rent — i.e. **≈1.8% effective on ARV**. The rate was cut **3% → 2%** by the **Property Tax (Amendment) Act 2024 (Act No. 3 of 2024, Gazette Legal Supplement Part A Vol. 63 No. 53, assented 27 Mar 2024)**. The Ministry of Finance media release **18 Nov 2023** ("Residential Homeowners Pay just 3% Property Tax") predates the Act and is **(superseded)** on the rate. (2024 data, source [IRD — Amended Property Tax Notices of Assessment](https://www.ird.gov.tt/news/amended-property-tax-notices-of-assessment).)
  - **Formula (COMPUTED, transparent)**: Annual Taxable Value = ARV × 0.90; Property Tax = ATV × 0.02 → **effectively 1.8% of ARV**.
  - **Worked example (IRD Notice-of-Assessment FAQ, live)**: ARV TT$24,000 → −10% = ATV TT$21,600 → ×2% = **TT$432/yr (~TT$36/mo)**.
- **First Notices of Valuation** issued from late 2023; **first-year payment was due 30 September 2024** (T&T Mortgage Bank explainer). **IRD declared every Notice of Assessment dated BEFORE 6 Mar 2024 null and void and reissued them at 2%** — a seller producing a pre-6-Mar-2024 notice is producing a voided one; ask for the reissued notice (source: [IRD](https://www.ird.gov.tt/news/amended-property-tax-notices-of-assessment)). A tax amnesty was extended into 2025 (~2 May 2025, Ministry of Finance).

### Capital Gains Tax (CGT)

- **Only gains on the disposal of a chargeable asset within 12 months of acquisition are taxable**, and those gains are **included with other income and taxed in the normal manner**. **Disposals after 12 months are NOT subject to CGT** (save a balancing allowance on depreciable assets). Excluded: gains on T&T securities; motor cars / household goods ≤ TT$5,000. (Source: [PwC Tax Summaries — Income determination, last reviewed 02 Jun 2026](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/trinidad-and-tobago/individual/income-determination).) → A foreign flipper selling within 12 months pays income tax on the gain; a longer hold has no CGT.

- **Standard VAT 12.5%.** Residential rental and financial services are **VAT-exempt**; basic foods / drugs / agriculture are **zero-rated**. (Source: [tax.mygovtt.com](https://tax.mygovtt.com/) — "12.5% Standard rate".)

### Seismic — HIGH (the dominant natural hazard)

A historical **M7.9 event (1766)** is on record. → **Seismic design / construction quality is the #1 build-quality due-diligence item.** Verify the structural standard of the build.
  - ⚠️ The "expected sooner rather than later" large-event prediction and any "UWI joined calls for a building code" attribution that circulate are **NOT on the cited UWI SRC page** — the high-seismic-hazard conclusion stands on the confirmed ~260 quakes/yr + historical M7.9 alone. - **Regime — MANAGED FLOAT with a de facto soft USD anchor.** T&T moved to a floating regime in **April 1993**; in practice the CBTT keeps the TTD in a tight band against the USD (a soft/unofficial anchor).
- **Rate (2025–2026 data)**: ~**TTD 6.74–6.78 per USD** — high in 2025 = 6.7804 (29 Dec 2025); low = 6.7075 (7 May 2026); spot mid ~6.74–6.75 in mid-2026. (Source: Wise USD→TTD history; TradingEconomics. **Quote the publish-day figure from the [CBTT daily exchange rates page](https://www.central-bank.org.tt/exchange-rates-daily/)**.)
- **Capital controls / repatriation (LOAD-BEARING)**:
  - **Legal position**: the **Exchange Control Act (Chap. 79:50) remains on the books** but was **substantively liberalised from April 1993** — **the repatriation of capital is NOT subject to exchange-control approval**, and there is no obligation to sell offshore-held foreign currency to authorised dealers. The remaining domestic rule is that residents must transact gold/foreign currency through **authorised dealers** only (absent CBTT approval). (Source: Fitzwilliam Stone restatement of the Exchange Control Act Chap. 79:50.) → **It is NOT correct to say there is "no formal exchange-control statute" — the statute exists; it is liberalised.**
  - **The real exit risk — USD AVAILABILITY (rationing)**: a genuine, multi-year **USD shortage** means authorised dealers **ration forex** (daily credit-card limits, transfer limits, allocation by priority). Result: importers cannot purchase sufficient forex promptly, suppliers go unpaid, and **foreign companies / owners cannot repatriate funds promptly — "even from USD accounts held in Trinidad and Tobago."** There is **no legal bar**, but a real availability queue. (Sources: [EUROCHAMTT — Foreign Exchange Challenges](https://eurochamtt.org/); T&T Chamber of Industry & Commerce Jan 2025 FX report; [IMF 2026 Article IV](https://www.imf.org/en/Countries/TTO) flagging FX strains; Finance Minister Imbert "shortage, not a crisis," Trinidad Express 2023.) → **A foreign seller is paid in TTD and must source USD through a rationing authorised dealer to repatriate — slow/uncertain. This is the single biggest practical exit risk for a foreign owner.**
- **Inbound is cleaner**: FIA s.10(1) REQUIRES the purchase consideration to be paid in an internationally traded currency through an authorised dealer — so the buyer's money comes IN as USD through a bank, satisfying the statute and creating a paper trail that can help (but does not guarantee) later repatriation.

## Caveats unique to TT

- **Foreign ownership is a THRESHOLD + LICENCE regime, NOT a ban**: licence-free up to **1 acre residential** (FIA s.6) and **5 acres trade/business** (s.7) in **Trinidad**; above that, a **President's licence** is required. The FIA repealed the old Aliens (Landholding) Act in 1990 — do not cite the latter as current.
- **TOBAGO IS STRICTER**: **ALL** foreign land acquisition in Tobago needs a **President's licence regardless of size**, per **The Foreign Investment (Tobago Land Acquisition) Order, 2007 (effective 16 Feb 2007)**. No 1-acre allowance in Tobago. (Order name + date firm across law-firm sources; primary Order text not retrieved — confirm at [laws.gov.tt](https://laws.gov.tt/).)
- **Even a licence-free foreign buyer must pay in hard currency through an authorised dealer (FIA s.10(1)) AND file a Third-Schedule notice with the Minister of Finance (s.8(2)).** Vesting without a required licence is an offence (TT$100,000 fine, s.9).
- **APEX COURT = UK Privy Council (JCPC), NOT the CCJ.** T&T acceded only to the CCJ Original Jurisdiction; final appeals go to London.
- **DUAL land-title system; ~75–80% of land is Old-Law DEEDS (no state title guarantee)** (*est., per Fitzwilliam Stone*), the rest RPA/Torrens **Certificate of Title (indefeasible)**. A buyer needs an attorney's chain-of-title search — there is **no single authoritative parcel register** to rely on for most land. **RTLA 2000** is enacted but its operational status is unconfirmed — do not assume a unified register.
- **Property tax was REINTRODUCED and collection began 2024** (Property Tax Act 2009 reactivated by Finance Act 2023). **Residential rate = 2% of ATV (ARV after a 10% voids deduction) ≈1.8% effective on ARV** — cut from 3% by the **Property Tax (Amendment) Act 2024 (Act No. 3 of 2024, assented 27 Mar 2024)**; Notices of Assessment dated before 6 Mar 2024 were voided and reissued at 2%; first-year payment was due 30 Sep 2024. Collection was then HALTED (FY2026 Budget Statement, 13 Oct 2025: "We halted the Property Tax"); no repeal Act located this pass, so the Act and 2% rate are read as still on the books — confirm with IRD whether any assessment applies to the purchase year. Do NOT quote 3% — that rate is superseded.
- **Currency = TTD, managed float, de facto soft USD anchor (~6.74–6.78/USD, 2025–2026; confirm at CBTT).** The **Exchange Control Act (Chap. 79:50) exists but is liberalised** — there is **no legal repatriation bar**, BUT a genuine, multi-year **USD shortage means repatriating sale proceeds can be slow/rationed** through authorised dealers — the **#1 practical exit risk** for a foreign owner.
- **NO Citizenship-by-Investment; buying property confers NO residency/work/citizenship right.** Don't list T&T among Caribbean CBI countries. **No digital-nomad visa found.**
- **NEW from 1 Jan 2026: Landlord Business Surcharge 2.5%/3.5% (Finance Act 2025 = Act No. 14 of 2025)** — **creditable against income tax** (a cash-flow/timing cost + a one-time TT$2,500 BIR registration, deadline 30 May 2026), NOT a flat yield haircut for a compliant landlord. CGT: no tax on >12-month holds; <12-month disposals taxed as income. VAT 12.5% (residential rent exempt).
- **Water (WASA) supply is intermittent — only ~19–27% of customers get 24/7 supply (19% in 2024; ~27% Jan–Apr 2025); on-site storage tanks are effectively essential.** Electricity (T&TEC) is cheap/subsidised but verify a dated reliability metric at T&TEC/RIC.
- **Seismic hazard is HIGH** (~260 quakes >M2/yr; historical M7.9 in 1766; UWI SRC) — seismic build-quality is the top hazard item. **No mandatory national building code is confirmed in force** (only the voluntary TTBS TTS 599) — verify current status at TTBS; do not assume code compliance. Hurricane direct-strike risk is comparatively LOW (south of the belt) but flooding is material in the wet season.
- **Crime: 2024 was a record (≈625 murders, ≈45.7/100k computed on the World Bank 1.37M denominator); 2025 fell ~42% to 369 (lowest since 2014).** Highly localised — never generalise the national rate to a neighbourhood; Tobago is materially safer than crime-hotspot Trinidad areas. **Verify the current State-of-Emergency status.**
- **Wire-fraud against international buyers**: verify wire instructions verbally with the attorney via a known phone before sending funds.
