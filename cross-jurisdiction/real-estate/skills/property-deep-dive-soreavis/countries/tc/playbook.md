# Turks and Caicos Islands 🇹🇨 — Property Due-Diligence Playbook

ISO2: `tc`. Status: ✅ Fully populated (researched 2026-05-28).

> **Critical framing — read first**: The Turks and Caicos Islands (TCI) is a **British Overseas Territory** in the northern Caribbean (a self-governing BOT under an English common-law / Torrens-registered-title system). The currency is the **US Dollar (USD)** — official tender, so there is **zero exchange-rate risk for USD-base buyers**. TCI is **OPEN to foreign freehold buyers with NO general restriction and NO prior government permission**: a foreigner may take freehold title in their own name, jointly, or via a **TCI-registered company** (a foreign individual/entity may own 100% of that company's shares; foreign *companies* cannot directly hold TCI land — they incorporate locally first). The fiscal headline is the inverse of most jurisdictions: **TCI has NO income tax, NO capital gains tax, NO inheritance tax, and NO annual property/real-estate tax**. The **entire** transaction tax is a one-time **stamp duty (transfer duty)** on the transfer, computed on a **slab basis on the FULL value** — Providenciales bands **6.5% / 8% / 10%** at the **$25k / $250k / $500k** thresholds; outer-island bands **5% / 6.5%** (Stamp Duty Ordinance, **Chapter 19.05** — *bands below are from editor-reviewed secondary sources; pull exact current bands from the statute before relying*). Stamp-duty **waivers/concessions** exist only for **TCI Status Card holders / British Overseas Territories Citizens (BOTC)** under a Citizen Homeowner Policy — **NOT for general foreign buyers**. The land register operates under the **Registered Land Ordinance, Chapter 9.01** (Ordinance 11 of 1967) — a **Torrens-style registered-title system** (use **"Ordinance"**, never "Act"). The dispute forum is the **Supreme Court of the Turks and Caicos Islands** (TCI's own superior court — *not* part of the Eastern Caribbean Supreme Court). The dominant physical risk is **hurricane** — **Hurricane Irma (Sept 2017) passed as a Category 5** and is the modern calibration event (followed ~2 weeks later by Maria). Power is supplied by **Pelican Energy TCI** (rebranded from FortisTCI, Sept 2025) at **~$0.45/kWh (Jan 2026)** — high by Caribbean standards; potable water is **RO-desalinated seawater**. **Property ownership confers NO residence, work, or status rights** — those are separate immigration tracks. **Confirm** any stamp-duty band, registry status, residence pathway, and insurance availability at primary sources **before any binding step**. Authoritative primary sources: **[gov.tc — Land Registry](https://gov.tc/landregistry/)**, **[gov.tc — Stamp Duty Ordinance Ch. 19.05](https://www.gov.tc/agc/component/edocman/19-05-stamp-duty-ordinance-2/viewdocument/1988?Itemid=)**, **[gov.tc — Hotel & Tourism Accommodation Tax](https://www.gov.tc/revenue/htt)**, **[Energy & Utilities Department (EUD)](https://eud.gov.tc/)**, **[Supreme Court of the Turks and Caicos Islands](https://judicial.tc/)**, **[Turks & Caicos Tourist Board — Accommodation Licensing](https://turksandcaicostourism.com/accommodation-licensing/)**.

## Country profile

- **Status**: **British Overseas Territory (BOT)** — self-governing internally with a Governor appointed by the UK Crown; the UK retains responsibility for defence, external affairs, and internal security. Land title is held under the registered-title system of the Registered Land Ordinance (real-estate marketing often frames title as "guaranteed by the British Crown" — *aggregator phrasing*; the legal protection derives from the state-backed register, not a separate Crown guarantee).
- **Geography**: An archipelago of two island groups — the **Turks Islands** (Grand Turk, Salt Cay) and the **Caicos Islands** (Providenciales, North/Middle/South/East/West Caicos + cays) — south-east of The Bahamas. **Capital: Cockburn Town, Grand Turk**. **Economic + property-market centre: Providenciales ("Provo")** — the **Grace Bay** corridor is the premium beachfront submarket. Outer islands (Grand Turk, North/Middle/South Caicos, Salt Cay) are far cheaper and thinner-volume.
- **Population**: **~46,000** (commonly cited estimate, 2020s — `est.`; the 2012 census recorded **31,458** and the resident population has grown substantially since via tourism-sector in-migration). **`Exact current population — data not publicly verified here; verify at gov.tc Department of Statistics.`**
- **Currency**: **US Dollar (USD)** — official currency and sole legal tender. **No exchange-rate risk** for USD-denominated buyers; minimal currency risk for EUR/GBP/CAD buyers (USD-correlated). No central bank of its own; monetary policy is effectively imported via the USD.
- **Languages**: **English (official, sole)** — all contracts, instruments of transfer, and ordinances are in English. Haitian Creole and Spanish are spoken by parts of the immigrant workforce, but legal/professional life is standard English.
- **Legal system**: **English common law** + TCI Ordinances. **Conveyancing**: a **Torrens-style registered-title system** under the **Registered Land Ordinance, Chapter 9.01** — title by registration with a state-backed register of proprietors, charges, leases, cautions, and restrictions. This is materially cleaner / lower-title-risk than a chain-of-deeds system. **Dispute / enforcement forum: Supreme Court of the Turks and Caicos Islands** (TCI's own superior court of record — original civil + criminal jurisdiction, appellate over the Magistrate's Court; handles land disputes, charge/mortgage enforcement, probate). **TCI is NOT part of the Eastern Caribbean Supreme Court.**
- **Cadastre / land registry**:
  - **[Land Registry Department (gov.tc/landregistry)](https://gov.tc/landregistry/)** — Registrar of Lands based in **Grand Turk** (Tel +1 649-338-2359, tcilandregistry@gov.tc); maintains the register of titles under Ch. 9.01.
  - **Department of Planning** — building / development control + the TCI building code (hurricane standards).
- **Identifier**: **Parcel number** under the registered-land system (block + parcel / registration section). No French-style universal numéro de parcelle but the Torrens register assigns a conclusive parcel identifier.
- **Title concept**: **Freehold (fee simple)** dominant and available to foreigners. **Leasehold** common for resort/condo arrangements (leases > 2 years must be registered). **Strata (condominium)** ownership available under a **Strata Titles** statute (*confirm exact chapter at gov.tc/agc*).
- **Recent reforms (12-month watchlist)**:
  - **FortisTCI rebranded to "Pelican Energy TCI" (Sept 2025)** following acquisition by Vision Ridge Partners — both names appear in current sources; the entity, monopoly position, and regulator (EUD) are unchanged.
  - **Hotel & Tourism Accommodation Tax (HTT) 12%** — the operative tourism-accommodation tax on short-term rentals + hotels (gov.tc/revenue/htt — ✅ PRIMARY); hosts collect 12% and retain records 7 years.
  - **Stamp-duty bands** — confirm against the Stamp Duty Ordinance Ch. 19.05 each budget cycle; the slab structure and thresholds below are from editor-reviewed secondary sources, not the statute text.

## 🇹🇨 Foreign buyer eligibility (READ FIRST)

This section determines whether a transaction is even legally possible — and what process applies. **TCI is among the most foreign-buyer-friendly jurisdictions in the Caribbean**: there is **no foreign-ownership restriction, no alien-landholding licence, and no prior government consent** for the purchase itself. The only foreign-buyer-specific friction is that **stamp-duty waivers are NOT available to foreigners** (they exist only for Status-Card holders / BOTC), and that **corporate buyers must use a TCI-registered entity**.

### Legal basis

- **Registered Land Ordinance, Chapter 9.01** (Ordinance 11 of 1967; in force 1 Nov 1968; revised ed. as at 31 Aug 2009, amended 1971/1995/1997/2006) — the Torrens-style registered-title system. **Official title is "Ordinance" — some secondary sources (e.g. legal500) paraphrase it "Registered Land Act (CAP 9.01)"; "Ordinance" is correct.**
- **Stamp Duty Ordinance, Chapter 19.05** — transfer-duty regime + anti-avoidance on indirect (share-transfer) transfers.
- **Strata Titles** statute — condominium / common-area regime (*confirm exact chapter at gov.tc/agc*).
- **Hotel, Restaurant & Tourism Taxation Ordinance** — the **12% Hotel & Tourism Accommodation Tax (HTT)** on accommodation services.

(Source: [legal500 TCI Real Estate Comparative Guide](https://www.legal500.com/guides/chapter/turks-caicos-real-estate/) — lawyer-authored ⚠️ secondary; [visittci buying guide](https://www.visittci.com/real-estate/buying-guide) — secondary; cross-checked vs. statute text at [faolex/FAO mirror of Ch. 9.01](https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/tci107736.pdf). *Confirm framing at [gov.tc Land Registry](https://gov.tc/landregistry/).*)

### Buyer category matrix

| Buyer category | Right |
|---|---|
| **TCI Belonger / Status Card holder / BOTC** | Full freehold; **eligible for stamp-duty concessions/waivers** under the Citizen Homeowner / Stamp Duty Reduction Policy |
| **Foreign individual (any nationality)** | **Full freehold on equal terms** — own name or jointly; **no prior permission, no licence**. Stamp duty payable at full rate (no concession). |
| **Foreign-owned TCI company** | **Full freehold** — must be a **TCI-registered entity**; a foreign individual/entity may own **100% of the shares**. **A foreign company cannot directly hold TCI land** — it must incorporate locally first. |
| **Lessee (lease > 2 years)** | Allowed; **lease must be registered** at the Land Registry to be effective against third parties |
| **Strata / condominium owner** | Allowed under the Strata Titles statute (resort condos commonly held this way) |

(Source: [legal500 TCI](https://www.legal500.com/guides/chapter/turks-caicos-real-estate/) + [visittci buying guide](https://www.visittci.com/real-estate/buying-guide) — secondary, lawyer/industry-authored; *verify corporate-holding + concession eligibility with a TCI attorney before contract*)

### Property ≠ residence / work / status

- ⚠️ **Buying property in TCI confers NO residence, work, or immigration status by itself.** There is no "buy a property → get residency" pathway analogous to a golden visa.
- **Permanent Residence Certificate (PRC)**, **Belonger / Status** grants, and **work permits** are **separate immigration tracks** administered by TCI Immigration. A PRC *can* be linked to a qualifying investment in some categories, but it is not automatic on a real-estate purchase.
- **`Verify current PRC / residence-by-investment / work-permit rules at gov.tc Immigration`** — out of core property scope, but a material expectation-setter for buyers.

### Compliance / AML

- **TCI is a regulated offshore + tourism jurisdiction** with AML/CFT obligations on attorneys, real-estate agents, and company-service providers (DNFBPs) — KYC + source-of-funds + suspicious-transaction reporting.
- **Source-of-funds documentation** required for property purchases; bank-statement chain back to a legitimate source.
- **Beneficial-ownership** disclosure for TCI-company-held real estate (the BOT beneficial-ownership framework applies).
- **CRS / FATCA**: tax-residency self-certification at bank account opening.

### Verification path (before any reservation)

1. **Engage a TCI attorney** (buyer-side) to run the title search and prepare/review the instrument of transfer.
2. **Title search at the Land Registry** — under Torrens, the register is the conclusive record (low title-risk vs deeds systems); confirm proprietor, charges, cautions, restrictions.
3. **Confirm stamp-duty exposure** — slab rate on full value (Provo 6.5/8/10%; outer islands 5/6.5%); **payable within 30 days of execution**; no concession for foreign buyers.
4. **Corporate structuring (if used)** — incorporate a TCI company (100% foreign-owned permitted) BEFORE contract if buying via entity.
5. **Insurance** — obtain hurricane/windstorm insurance quotes BEFORE contract (a binding constraint, not a formality — see `--risks`).
6. **AML / source-of-funds** — bank statements + asset-sale / employment evidence ready before signing.

## Section: `--tax`

> **Headline — the inverse of most jurisdictions**: TCI has **NO income tax, NO capital gains tax, NO inheritance/estate tax, NO gift tax, NO annual property/real-estate tax, NO wealth tax, and no VAT/GST on property**. The **entire** fiscal burden on a property transaction is a **one-time stamp duty (transfer duty)** paid on the transfer. On rentals, a **12% Hotel & Tourism Accommodation Tax (HTT)** applies to short-term accommodation. There is **no recurring holding tax** — the carrying cost of TCI property is utilities + insurance + HOA/condo fees, not government tax.

### Stamp duty (transfer duty) — the only transaction tax

Governed by the **Stamp Duty Ordinance, Chapter 19.05** (gov.tc/agc). Computed on a **SLAB basis — the rate applies to the WHOLE consideration / value, not the marginal amount** (creating threshold cliffs).

**Providenciales** (the premium-market island):

| Consideration value (USD) | Stamp duty rate |
|---|---|
| Under **$25,000** | **0%** |
| **$25,000 – $250,000** | **6.5%** of full value |
| **$250,000 – $500,000** | **8%** of full value |
| **Over $500,000** | **10%** of full value |

**Outer islands** (Grand Turk, North/Middle/South Caicos, Salt Cay):

| Consideration value (USD) | Stamp duty rate |
|---|---|
| Under **$25,000** | **0%** |
| **$25,000 – $100,000** | **5%** of full value |
| **Over $100,000** | **6.5%** of full value |

- **Slab cliff illustration** (visittci): a $245,000 sale is taxed 6.5% on the full value (≈ $15,925); a $255,000 sale is taxed 8% on the full value (≈ $20,400). A $10,000 price difference around the $250k threshold swings the duty by ~$4,500. The same cliff exists at $500k (Provo) and $100k (outer).
- **Payment deadline**: **within 30 days** of execution of the transfer.
- **Who pays**: contractually the **purchaser**, typically.
- **Share transfers** in a landholding company: anti-avoidance applies to indirect transfers — secondary sources cite an apportionment formula (A/B × C × 8%); **`verify the exact share-transfer formula + rate at gov.tc / Stamp Duty Ordinance Ch. 19.05`**.
- ⚠️ **Bands above are from editor-reviewed secondary sources (visittci, cross-checked vs legal500), NOT the statute text. Pull exact current bands from the Stamp Duty Ordinance Ch. 19.05 (gov.tc/agc) before relying.**

(Source: [visittci buying guide — stamp duty](https://www.visittci.com/real-estate/buying-guide) — editor-reviewed secondary; cross-checked vs [legal500 TCI](https://www.legal500.com/guides/chapter/turks-caicos-real-estate/); statute at [gov.tc AGC — Stamp Duty Ordinance Ch. 19.05](https://www.gov.tc/agc/). *Verify exact bands at the statute.*)

### Stamp-duty concessions / waivers — NOT for foreign buyers

- **gov.tc operates a Stamp Duty Reduction / Waiver Policy + a Citizen Homeowner Policy** (via the exemptions portal) — these reduce or waive stamp duty for **TCI Status Card holders / BOTC / qualifying citizen homeowners ONLY**.
- ⚠️ **General foreign buyers do NOT qualify** — budget full slab-rate stamp duty.

(Source: [gov.tc — Stamp Duty exemptions/waiver portal](https://exemptions.gov.tc/) — primary.)

### Annual / recurring property tax — NONE

- **There is NO annual property tax, no recurring real-property tax, no land tax** in TCI. This is a defining feature: the holding cost is utilities + insurance + HOA/condo fees only.

### Hotel & Tourism Accommodation Tax (HTT) — on rentals

- **12%** on accommodation services, under the **Hotel, Restaurant & Tourism Taxation Ordinance** (gov.tc/revenue/htt — ✅ **PRIMARY**).
- Hosts **collect 12% on every short-term stay** and must **retain records 7 years**.
- Applies to vacation rentals + hotels. See `--rental` for licensing + business-licence detail.

### Income / CGT / inheritance / wealth — NONE

- **NO personal or corporate income tax.**
- **NO capital gains tax** — gains on resale are untaxed in TCI (home-country tax may still apply; **`consult home-country tax advisor`** — e.g. US citizens taxed on worldwide gains regardless).
- **NO inheritance / estate tax; no gift tax.**
- **NO wealth tax; no VAT/GST on property.**

## Section: `--rental`

### Short-term rentals (vacation rental — the dominant model)

TCI has an **active short-term / vacation-rental market** — it is common for US/UK/Canada buyers to buy a villa or Grace Bay condo and rent short-term. Grace Bay resort condos are frequently sold **inside managed rental programmes** (hotel-operated, with the operator taking a substantial cut).

- **Accommodation licensing**: all rental accommodation must be **licensed** — licensing + renewals are now administered under the **Department of Tourism Regulations** (Turks & Caicos Tourist Board). (Source: [turksandcaicostourism.com — Accommodation Licensing](https://turksandcaicostourism.com/accommodation-licensing/) — official tourism dept.)
- **HTT 12%**: hosts collect the **12% Hotel & Tourism Accommodation Tax** on every stay and must retain records 7 years (gov.tc/revenue/htt — ✅ PRIMARY).
- **Business licence**: operating a rental business requires an applicable **business licence** from the Revenue Department — a fee schedule exists (visittci hosts a Business Licence Fee Schedule PDF). **`Pull exact STR / accommodation + business-licence fees at gov.tc.`**

### Long-term rentals

- Ordinary contract + common-law landlord-tenant principles. Thinner market than the tourism STR segment; demand from the resident workforce. **`Specific long-let yield data not publicly available from a primary source.`**

### Yield benchmarks

- ⚠️ **`Specific net rental-yield % — data not publicly available; no government index. Verify via a licensed TCI property manager / globalpropertyguide / multiple live comparables.`**
- **Model NET, not gross.** STR economics in TCI are dominated by:
  - **12% HTT** on accommodation;
  - **Management / rental-programme fees** (often **~20–35% of gross** for managed Grace Bay condos; sometimes higher for full hotel-operated programmes);
  - **Insurance** (high — hurricane peril, see `--risks`);
  - **Power** (~$0.45/kWh — high by Caribbean standards, see `--mains`);
  - **HOA / condo fees** on resort units (can be substantial for branded residences).
- **Seasonality**: peak winter season (Northern-hemisphere Dec–Apr) commands high ADR; summer + hurricane season (Jun–Nov) materially depresses occupancy.

### Strategic notes

- **The headline ADR is misleading** — after HTT + management + insurance + power + HOA, the net owner yield can be a fraction of gross. **Always build a net model with quoted (not assumed) management + insurance + HOA figures.**
- **Managed-rental condo programmes** trade owner-control for hands-off operation — read the rental-programme agreement (revenue split, owner-use blackout dates, mandatory refurbishment contributions) before relying on a yield projection.
- **All rentals must be licensed** (Tourism Regulations) + business-licensed (Revenue) — unlicensed operation is non-compliant.

### Hurricane — THE dominant risk

- **TCI sits squarely in the Atlantic hurricane belt** (season **1 June – 30 November**); hurricane exposure is the **#1 physical risk** and a binding diligence + insurance constraint.
- **Baseline event — Hurricane Irma (Sept 2017)**: passed **over TCI as a Category 5**, with the north side of the eye over the islands for **2+ hours**; sustained winds cited **in excess of ~156 mph** (locally higher). Irma **demolished roofs/structures, downed ~1,300 utility poles, and destroyed communications infrastructure**. Worst-hit: **South Caicos, Grand Turk, Salt Cay**. **Hurricane Maria** followed ~2 weeks later (Sept 2017). (Source: [NOAA NHC TCR AL112017](https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/data/tcr/AL112017_Irma.pdf); ReliefWeb damage maps; visittci hurricanes page — primary + secondary.)
- **Build-quality split**: TCI enforces a **hurricane building code**; **newer code-compliant buildings fared far better than older stock** in Irma — a documented code-built utility structure on Grand Turk took only minor damage at Cat-5 winds (visittci / bdcnetwork — secondary). **`Pull the current TCI Building Code / Physical Planning standards at gov.tc (Dept of Planning).`**

### Insurance — essential and a major recurring cost

- **Hurricane / windstorm insurance is essential** and one of the largest recurring carrying costs of TCI property. **Post-Irma reporting noted many owners were uninsured**, which severely complicated rebuild.
- **Caribbean hurricane-zone premiums are high and have risen.** **`Verify current premium ranges + CAT deductibles with a TCI broker — specific figures not publicly available.`**
- **Verification path**: obtain **insurance quotes for the specific parcel BEFORE contract** and price the premium + deductible into the carrying-cost model. Insurance availability + cost is a binding constraint, not a formality.

### Coastal flood / storm surge / erosion

- **Low-lying islands** → coastal + beachfront parcels carry **storm-surge + erosion exposure**. **`Check parcel elevation + surge zone on visit / with a surveyor.`**
- Beachfront Grace Bay frontage commands the premium but concentrates surge + erosion + insurance exposure.

### Sea-level rise (long-term)

- TCI is a **low-lying limestone archipelago** — long-term SLR exposure for beachfront / sub-few-metre parcels. IPCC AR6 Caribbean SIDS band applies regionally. **`Model parcel-level SLR with Climate Central Coastal Risk Screening Tool (https://coastal.climatecentral.org/).`**

### Seismic / volcanic

- TCI is on the **carbonate Bahama/Caicos platform** — **no active volcano** and lower seismic exposure than the Lesser Antilles arc. Not a primary risk factor for modern builds. (Background Caribbean far-field seismic risk only.)

### Freshwater / logistics

- **Limited natural freshwater** → reliance on **RO desalination + cisterns** (see `--mains`); reliance on **imports**; **single-airport-island logistics dependency** for Provo.

## Section: `--currency`

- **Currency**: **US Dollar (USD)** — official currency and sole legal tender.
- **No exchange-rate risk for USD-base buyers.** Prices, contracts, taxes, and mortgages are all USD-denominated.
- **No local currency, no peg to manage, no exchange-control on the currency itself** — TCI uses the USD directly (it has no national currency of its own). This removes the FX-registration / repatriation gating step that exists in pegged-currency jurisdictions (e.g. Barbados, Bahamas).
- **Practical effect**: **zero structural FX risk** for USD buyers; minimal correlated risk for EUR/GBP/CAD buyers. Capital + gains repatriation is not gated by a currency-control registration (unlike pegged-BBD Barbados) — but **AML / source-of-funds** documentation still applies on the way in.

## Caveats unique to TC

- **NO recurring property tax at all** — no annual land/property tax, no CGT, no income/inheritance/wealth tax. The carrying cost is **utilities + insurance + HOA/condo fees**, not government tax. This is a defining, near-unique feature.
- **The ONLY transaction tax is one-time stamp duty (transfer duty)** — **slab on the FULL value**: Provo **6.5% / 8% / 10%** ($25k / $250k / $500k); outer islands **5% / 6.5%** ($25k / $100k). **Slab cliffs** at $250k / $500k (Provo) and $100k (outer) — a few thousand dollars over a threshold can swing duty by thousands. **Payable by the purchaser within 30 days.** **Pull exact bands from the Stamp Duty Ordinance Ch. 19.05 (gov.tc/agc) — bands here are editor-reviewed secondary, not statute text.**
- **Stamp-duty waivers are for Status-Card / BOTC holders ONLY** — general foreign buyers pay full slab rate (no concession).
- **OPEN to foreign freehold buyers — no permission, no licence** — but **corporate buyers must use a TCI-registered entity** (100% foreign-owned permitted; a foreign company cannot directly hold TCI land).
- **Property ≠ residence / work / status.** No golden-visa pathway; PRC / Belonger / work permit are separate immigration tracks. No CBI programme.
- **Registry statute is the Registered Land Ordinance, Chapter 9.01** — a **Torrens-style registered-title system** (low title-risk vs deeds systems). Use **"Ordinance"**, never "Act" (some secondary sources mis-paraphrase it as "Registered Land Act").
- **Dispute / enforcement forum is the Supreme Court of the Turks and Caicos Islands** — TCI's own superior court of record; **NOT part of the Eastern Caribbean Supreme Court**.
- **Currency is USD** — no FX risk for USD buyers; no currency-control repatriation gate (unlike pegged-currency Caribbean jurisdictions). AML / source-of-funds still applies.
- **Hurricane is the dominant risk** — **Irma 2017 was a Category 5** over TCI (Maria followed ~2 weeks later). Insurability + code-compliance (post-2002) + elevation are core diligence items. **Many owners were uninsured post-Irma** — obtain 3+ insurance quotes pre-contract; insurance is a major recurring cost.
- **Power is high by Caribbean standards** — **~$0.45/kWh (Jan 2026, Pelican Energy TCI / ex-FortisTCI)** on a diesel-generation island grid; strong solar/PV economics. **FortisTCI rebranded to Pelican Energy TCI (Sept 2025)** — both names appear in current sources.
- **Water is RO-desalinated** — no natural freshwater; TCWC produces, Provo Water Company distributes (~90% of Provo) from its Grace Bay RO plant; many villas have private cisterns.
- **No official price index** — all $/sqft and median figures are **brokerage / secondary-aggregator** sourced (no government sold-price register). Treat as indicative; cross-check ≥ 3 live comparables.
- **STR economics are net-driven** — model **HTT 12% + management 20–35% + insurance + ~$0.45/kWh power + HOA**, not headline ADR. All rentals must be **licensed** (Tourism Regulations) + business-licensed (Revenue).
- **Medical access is limited** — serious cases are air-evacuated off-island; factor medical-evacuation insurance into any retirement / older-buyer thesis.
- **Wire fraud against international buyers** — verify wire instructions verbally with the attorney via a known phone before sending funds.
- ⚠️ **Population ~46k is an estimate** — verify current census/estimate at the TCI Department of Statistics.
