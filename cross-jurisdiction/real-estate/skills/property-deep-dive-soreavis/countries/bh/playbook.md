# Bahrain 🇧🇭 — Property Due-Diligence Playbook

ISO2: `bh`. Status: ✅ Fully populated (researched 2026-04 / verified 2026-05-01).

> **Critical framing**: Bahrain is the **most open foreign-buyer market in the GCC**. Under **Legislative Decree No. 2 of 2001 + Legislative Decree No. 43 of 2003 (as amended by Edicts 67/2006 + 38/2023)** — NOT Law 27/2017 (which regulates the real-estate sector / RERA / escrow, separate regime) — non-GCC nationals can acquire **freehold** in an extensive list of **designated areas** (Amwaj Islands, Reef Island, Durrat Al Bahrain, Diyar Al Muharraq, Bahrain Bay, Al Areen, Diplomatic Area, Seef, Juffair, Riffa Views, parts of Saar, etc.) (2026-05-27 verified, source Baker McKenzie + Manal Dhahi + Trowers & Hamlins). GCC parity is codified separately in **Legislative Decree No. 40/1999** (Ownership of GCC Nationals of Constructed Property and Land — grants GCC nationals near-Bahraini-national treatment). Outside designated zones, foreigners are limited to leasehold or commercial-zone usufruct. Bahrain has **NO personal income tax**, **NO capital gains tax** (individuals), **NO inheritance tax**, **light Municipal Tax** on rented property (~7–10%) but typically minimal on owner-occupied, and **VAT 10%** (raised from 5% in Jan 2022). **DMTT 15% on in-scope MNEs (consolidated revenues ≥ EUR 750M) LIVE since 1 Jan 2025 under Decree-Law 11/2024** (OECD Pillar Two GloBE alignment; NBR-administered). **Tourist Accommodation Levy BHD 3/room/night** on hotels + hotel apartments since 1 May 2024 (Ministry of Tourism / BTEA). Currency risk vs USD is structurally zero — BHD is pegged at **0.376 to the USD since December 2001** (Central Bank of Bahrain fixed peg) — by face value the BHD is among the highest-value circulating currency units (1 BHD ≈ USD 2.659 from the CBB peg). Confirm current designated-area list with the **Survey & Land Registration Bureau (SLRB)** + **RERA Bahrain** **before** signing any reservation form.

## Country profile

- **Postal code system**: **3-4 digit Bahraini Postal Codes** issued by Bahrain Post (under the Ministry of Transportation and Telecommunications) — `https://www.bahrainpost.bh/`. Modern parcel-level identifiers:
  - **Block No. + Road No. + Building No.** — the official addressing scheme (e.g., "Building 123, Road 456, Block 789, Manama")
  - **Parcel No. (رقم العقار)** is the cadastral identifier on Title Deed (Wathiqat Mulkiyya / وثيقة ملكية)
- **Admin levels**: Unitary state → **4 governorates** (Capital / Muharraq / Northern / Southern; Central was merged into Capital + Northern in 2014) → blocks (numbered)
- **Currency**: BHD (Bahraini Dinar). Pegged USD 1 = BHD 0.376 since December 2001 (Central Bank of Bahrain — formal peg). 1 BHD = ~USD 2.659. Among the highest-value circulating currency units by face value.
- **Languages**: Arabic (official, all formal contracts and Title Deeds in Arabic — bilingual EN/AR is common in real-estate and banking sectors); English is the de-facto business + everyday language (probably the most English-fluent GCC market alongside UAE)
- **Cadastre / land registry**:
  - **Survey & Land Registration Bureau (SLRB)** — `https://www.slrb.gov.bh/` — issues the Title Deed (Wathiqat Mulkiyya / وثيقة ملكية); maintains the national cadastre and digital land register
  - **Real Estate Regulatory Authority (RERA)** — `https://www.rera.gov.bh/` — established by Law 27/2017; regulates developers, brokers, escrow accounts, off-plan sales, and the foreign-buyer designated-area framework
  - **Ministry of Housing and Urban Planning (MOHUP)** — `https://www.housing.gov.bh/` — national housing policy, low-income housing programmes (Mazaya, Tas'heel — Bahraini-only)
- **Identifier(s)**:
  - **Title Deed (Wathiqat Mulkiyya — وثيقة ملكية)** — issued by SLRB at registration; bears Parcel No. + Block No. + Governorate
  - **Sale and Purchase Agreement (SPA)** — pre-registration binding; for off-plan sales the developer provides a RERA-registered interim allocation document until final Title Deed at handover
  - **CPR (Central Population Register)** — Bahrain national/resident ID — required for any registration; expats need a valid CPR
- **Population**: ~1.49 M (2024 — Information & eGovernment Authority `https://www.iga.gov.bh/`); ~52% Bahraini nationals + ~48% expatriates (Indian + Pakistani + Bangladeshi + Filipino + Egyptian + GCC largest expat communities, per IGA Census 2020 + 2024 bulletins)
- **Recent reforms** (12-month watchlist):
  - **Legislative Decree No. 2 of 2001 + Legislative Decree No. 43 of 2003** (amended by Edicts 67/2006 + 38/2023) — primary foreign-freehold statutes (NOT Law 27/2017, which is the real-estate-sector regulator) (2026-05-27 verified, source Baker McKenzie + Manal Dhahi Legal Consultants)
  - **Legislative Decree No. 40/1999** — Ownership of GCC Nationals of Constructed Property and Land — grants GCC nationals near-Bahraini-national treatment
  - **Law 27/2017** ("Regulating the Real Estate Sector") + amendments — established RERA; mandates escrow accounts; regulates developers/brokers (NOT a foreign-freehold statute; restrict references to RERA/escrow/broker regulation)
  - **Decree-Law No. 11 of 2024** — Domestic Minimum Top-Up Tax (DMTT) 15% on MNE groups with ≥ EUR 750M consolidated annual revenues; effective FY beginning on/after **1 January 2025**; aligns with OECD Pillar Two GloBE Model Rules; NBR-administered; advance instalments + filing in 2025 (2026-05-27 verified, source NBR + EY + KPMG Fakhro + BDO Bahrain)
  - **Tourist Accommodation Levy** — BHD 3/room/night on hotels + hotel apartments effective **1 May 2024** (Ministry of Tourism + BTEA — quarterly declaration); hotels + tourism restaurants also subject to 5% tourism levy (separate from the BHD 3 nightly fee) (2026-05-27 verified, source KPMG Bahrain & GCC Tax News 05 May 2024 + AGBI + BTEA E-services portal)
  - **Law No. 19 of 2017 — Family Law (نظام الأسرة)** — first unified family law in Bahrain covering BOTH Sunni and Jaafari (Shia) doctrines in separate chapters; superseded the partial 2009 codification (which had applied only to Sunnis) (2026-05-27 verified, source bahrain.bh official PDF + Musawah Bahrain)
  - **Decree 6/2017** + amendments — Self-Sponsored Residence Permit (Real Estate Investment) — 10-yr renewable for property owners ≥ BHD 200,000
  - **Decree 16/2022 — Golden Residency Visa** — 10-yr renewable; property route ≥ BHD 200,000 (~USD 530k); also routes for talented professionals, retirees with BHD 4,000+/month income, and high-skill workers
  - **VAT raised from 5% to 10%** effective 1 January 2022 (Royal Decree-Law 33/2021); first-sale residential by registered developer 0%-rated; resale residential VAT-exempt; commercial standard-rated
  - **National Spatial Strategy 2050** (MOHUP, 2021) — integrated land-use, housing supply, transport
  - **Bahrain Economic Vision 2030** + **Bahrain Renewable Energy Action Plan** + **NDC update 2021** — 30% emissions reduction by 2035 vs BAU
  - **Diyar Al Muharraq, Dilmunia, Marsa Al Seef, Bahrain Marina, Durrat Al Bahrain Phase 2** — ongoing master-planned freehold developments
  - **Saudi Causeway 2 (King Hamad Causeway)** — under planning/construction (target completion late-2020s) — second causeway link to Saudi Arabia for road + rail; will materially affect commute economics for Saudi-employed property buyers

## 🇧🇭 Foreign buyer eligibility (READ FIRST)

This section determines whether a transaction is even legally possible. Confirm zone status BEFORE assessing price.

### Legal basis

- **Legislative Decree No. 2 of 2001 + Legislative Decree No. 43 of 2003** (as amended by Edicts 67/2006 + 38/2023) — primary foreign-freehold statutes designating zones where non-GCC nationals can acquire freehold (2026-05-27 verified, source Baker McKenzie)
- **Legislative Decree No. 40/1999** — GCC parity statute (Ownership of GCC Nationals of Constructed Property and Land)
- **Law 27/2017** "Regulating the Real Estate Sector" — established RERA; mandates escrow accounts; regulates developers/brokers — this is the sector-regulator law, NOT the freehold-eligibility law (2026-05-27 verified)
- **Decree 6/2017** (and amendments) — Self-Sponsored Residence Permit (Real Estate Investment)
- **Decree 16/2022** — Golden Residency Visa (multi-route including property)
- Administered jointly by **RERA** + **Survey & Land Registration Bureau (SLRB / السجل العقاري)** + **Ministry of Justice**

### Tier 1 — Designated freehold zones (non-Bahrainis, including non-GCC, can own outright)

Per Law 27/2017 + RERA-maintained list. The Bahrain regime is **the most expansive in the GCC** — designated areas cover most modern flagship developments and large parts of central Manama / Muharraq / Riffa. Indicative zones (verify current RERA list before reservation):

- **Amwaj Islands** (Muharraq — reclaimed islands; flagship freehold-for-foreigners since 2003)
- **Reef Island** (Capital — reclaimed island, Manama bay)
- **Durrat Al Bahrain** (Southern — coastal master-planned community + marina)
- **Diyar Al Muharraq** (Muharraq — reclaimed islands; large new-city development)
- **Bahrain Bay** (Capital — central Manama waterfront; Four Seasons / Wyndham branded towers)
- **Al Areen** (Southern — desert-side resort + residential)
- **Saar** (Northern — selected freehold plots in this established expat-villa community)
- **Diplomatic Area** (Capital — central Manama; selected towers freehold)
- **Seef District** (Capital — commercial + residential west Manama; selected plots)
- **Juffair** (Capital — high-rise residential + US-naval-base catchment)
- **Riffa Views** (Southern — gated villa community)
- **Marsa Al Seef**, **Dilmunia Island** (Muharraq — health/wellness reclaimed island), **Bahrain Marina** (Capital), **Water Garden City** (Capital), **Tala Island**, **Durrat Marina**, **Al Bareh**, others

(Zone names per RERA list — exact current list and any 2024–2026 additions: **verify at RERA designated-area lookup before reservation**: `https://www.rera.gov.bh/`.)

### Tier 2 — Outside designated zones

Non-Bahrainis (and often non-GCC specifically) **cannot acquire registered freehold** outside designated zones. Available structures:

- **Long-term lease (musataha / usufruct)** — up to 50 years renewable in some commercial zones
- **Commercial / business-zone usufruct** — for properties tied to a registered Bahraini company (CR — Commercial Registration); subject to MOICT (Ministry of Industry and Commerce) approval
- **GCC nationals** have broader rights via reciprocity arrangements (e.g., Saudi/UAE/Qatari/Kuwaiti/Omani citizens) — verify per nationality at SLRB

### Visa via property — Self-Sponsored Residence Permit + Golden Residency Visa

| Item | Threshold | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| **Self-Sponsored Residence Permit (Real Estate) — Decree 6/2017** | Property **≥ BHD 200,000** (~USD 530k) in designated zone | Nationality, Passports & Residence Affairs (NPRA) — Ministry of Interior |
| Validity | **10 years renewable** while property maintained | NPRA |
| Inclusion | Owner + spouse + children (no work-permit required) | NPRA |
| **Golden Residency Visa — Decree 16/2022** | Property **≥ BHD 200,000** OR retiree with BHD 4,000+/month pension OR talented/high-skill worker OR investor in regulated business | NPRA + LMRA (Labour Market Regulatory Authority) |
| Validity | **10 years renewable** | NPRA |
| Inclusion | Owner + immediate family | NPRA |
| **Citizenship by Investment** | **NONE** — Bahraini nationality is governed by Citizenship Act 1963 + amendments; naturalisation is discretionary, typically requires 25 years residence + Arabic + clean record + service to Bahrain | Ministry of Interior — Naturalisation Department |

(Sources: Decree 6/2017 amendments; Decree 16/2022 Golden Residency Visa; NPRA visa portal `https://www.evisa.gov.bh/` — *verify current thresholds in BHD vs USD at the official Bahrain.bh portal `https://www.bahrain.bh/`*)

### Compliance / AML

- **Central Bank of Bahrain (CBB)** AML rules (CBB Rulebook Volume 5 — Specialised Licensees) require **source-of-funds documentation** for property-related transactions through banks
- **Ministry of Industry and Commerce (MOICT)** regulates real-estate sector AML compliance — `https://www.moic.gov.bh/`
- **Bahrain Financial Intelligence Unit (BFIU)** — under Ministry of Interior — coordinates SARs (Suspicious Activity Reports) under FATF MENAFATF assessments
- Real-estate brokers + developers are designated reporting entities under Law 4/2001 (AML) and subsequent amendments
- Source-of-funds: bank statements, employment letter, prior asset sale proof — required at SLRB registration for non-resident foreign buyers

### Verification path

1. Confirm property is in a **designated freehold zone** for non-Bahrainis — RERA lookup + SLRB confirmation
2. Pull seller's current Title Deed extract from SLRB — verify ownership + encumbrances
3. If off-plan → verify developer registration with RERA + project registered + escrow account active (Law 27/2017 mandatory)
4. Source-of-funds documentation ready before signing SPA
5. Self-Sponsored Residence Permit / Golden Residency application can run in parallel post-registration (NPRA)

### Compute

1. Listing BHD/sqm = listing BHD ÷ BUA sqm (BUA = Built-Up Area; Bahrain listings sometimes quote total floor area or land-plot for villas — clarify which)
2. Cross-check with **RERA Real Estate Index** for the same quarter (citywide trend, not zone-level)
3. Cross-check with **SLRB transaction registration** for similar buildings/zones (registered prices may be available via RERA / consultancy aggregators)
4. **Trap**: Off-plan list price ≠ ready market — apply 5–15% off-plan-to-handover discount when comparing
5. **Trap**: Bahrain Bay branded buildings (Four Seasons / Wyndham) carry 40–80% premiums vs adjacent non-branded — don't average them in
6. **Trap**: Service charges + EWA (Electricity & Water Authority) + cooling are SEPARATE — gross yield headlines often ignore them
7. **Trap**: Some listings quote total price for "fully-furnished + parking + maid's room" — strip out chattel and parking allocations to compare like-for-like

## Section: `--tax`

### Annual property tax

**Bahrain has a light Municipal Tax regime** — distinct from most other GCC peers:

| Item | Rate / amount | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| **Municipal Tax — rented residential property** | **~7% of annual rental value** (paid by tenant or landlord per contract; commonly tenant) | Ministry of Municipalities Affairs and Agriculture / NBR |
| **Municipal Tax — rented commercial / furnished** | **~10% of annual rental value** | Ministry of Municipalities Affairs and Agriculture / NBR |
| **Owner-occupied residential** | Typically **minimal/waived** for Bahraini owners; varies for expat owner-occupiers (verify with municipality) | Local municipality |
| **Service charges** (compound / tower OA) | **BHD 0.300–1.500 / sqft / yr** depending on community/tier (Amwaj, Bahrain Bay, Reef Island OAs are most-developed) | Master developer / OA |
| **District cooling (where applicable)** | Capacity charge + consumption — typical residential **BHD 0.500–1.500 / sqft / yr** all-in | Tabreed Bahrain (Reef Island, parts of Bahrain Bay), private chillers per development |
| **EWA** (electricity + water) | Tiered residential — first slabs subsidised for nationals; expats pay full tariff | Electricity & Water Authority `https://www.ewa.bh/` |

Worst case (filed >60 days, full 2.0% SLRB) tops out near 5%; filing within 60 days saves 0.3pp.

### Personal income tax — 0%

- **NO personal income tax** in Bahrain (unique alongside Qatar/UAE/Kuwait/Oman/Saudi within GCC; only Saudi has Zakat for nationals)
- **NO capital gains tax** on individual property sales (own-name natural person)
- **NO inheritance tax**
- **NO withholding tax** on dividends/royalties paid to non-residents (one of the most tax-friendly regimes globally for individuals)

### Corporate income tax — 0% generally; DMTT 15% on MNEs LIVE from 1 Jan 2025

- **No general corporate income tax** in Bahrain for non-MNE companies (unique among major GCC peers)
- **EXCEPTION 1**: Oil & gas companies pay **46% corporate tax** under Law 22/1979 (specific to upstream oil sector)
- **EXCEPTION 2 (LIVE since 1 Jan 2025)**: **Domestic Minimum Top-up Tax (DMTT) 15%** under **Decree-Law 11/2024** — applies to MNE groups with consolidated annual revenues **≥ EUR 750 million** in at least 2 of the prior 4 fiscal years; covers constituent entities (companies, branches, permanent establishments) in Bahrain; aligns with OECD Pillar Two GloBE Model Rules; filing registration + tax return + advance instalment payments in 2025; NBR-administered (2026-05-27 verified, source NBR + EY Global tax alert + KPMG Fakhro + BDO Bahrain + FTI Consulting + Middle East Briefing).
- **Implication**: holding property through a non-MNE Bahraini company doesn't attract income tax on rental income; MNE-vehicle property holding may now trigger 15% DMTT — assess group-level revenue.

(Source: National Bureau for Revenue (NBR) Bahrain — `https://www.nbr.gov.bh/`; Law 22/1979; Royal Decree-Law 33/2021 (VAT))

### Inheritance

- **No inheritance tax** in Bahrain
- **Sharia inheritance** applies as default for Muslim Bahrainis (and often by application to Muslim residents); for non-Muslim foreign residents, the **home-country law of the deceased** typically governs movable estate, but **immovable property in Bahrain is governed by Bahraini law** (lex situs) — practical outcome for non-Muslim foreign owners is Sharia application via Bahraini courts unless a foreign-court order is recognised
- **Practical recommendation**: non-Muslim foreign owners should hold Bahrain real estate via a structured ownership or a clear cross-border estate plan; consult a Bahrain-licensed lawyer + home-jurisdiction counsel; some non-Muslim residents use offshore holding company + foreign will

### VAT — 10% standard rate (raised from 5% in Jan 2022)

- **Bahrain implemented VAT at 5%** on 1 January 2019 under the GCC VAT Framework Agreement (2016)
- **Raised to 10%** effective **1 January 2022** under Royal Decree-Law 33/2021 — second GCC state after Saudi to raise VAT (Saudi went to 15% in July 2020)
- **First sale of new residential** by registered developer: **0%-rated** (per NBR Real Estate VAT Guide)
- **Resale residential**: **exempt** (no input VAT recovery for exempt suppliers)
- **Commercial property** (sale + lease): **10% standard-rated**
- **Short-term residential (STR)** if turnover > **BHD 37,500/yr** (mandatory registration threshold): **10% standard-rated**

### Future risk

- **VAT increase**: monitored; Bahrain followed Saudi precedent in 2022 from 5% → 10%; further increases possible but no announcement as of 2026-05
- **Excise tax** already exists on tobacco / energy drinks / sugary drinks (Law 40/2017) — separate from VAT
- **Property-tax introduction at federal level**: no announcements; Municipal Tax framework is the structural answer

## Section: `--rental`

### Long-term residential

- **Tenancy law**: **Law 27/2014** "On the Lease of Real Estate Properties" — landlord/tenant rights, eviction grounds, rent-cap framework
- **Mandatory tenancy registration**: Tenancy contracts should be registered (commonly required for utility connections, residence-permit renewals, school enrolment); enforcement varies by municipality
- **Rent increase cap**: Law 27/2014 historically limited increases to a fixed annual % (verify current rule — generally landlord must serve notice 90+ days before contract end; first 3-year period under tenant-protective rules)
- **Evictions**: Limited grounds — non-payment, unlicensed sub-letting, owner self-occupation. Disputes routed to the **Rent Disputes Committee** (under Ministry of Justice) — separate from regular courts; faster resolution

(Source: Law 27/2014; MOJ Rent Disputes Committee — *verify current rent-cap formula at MOJ + RERA*)

### Short-term rentals (holiday homes)

- **Authority**: **Bahrain Tourism and Exhibitions Authority (BTEA)** — `https://www.btea.bh/` — operates within the Ministry of Tourism framework; consumer brand "Visit Bahrain" `https://www.bahrain.com/`
- **Tourism Establishments Law** + Decree-Law on Tourism — govern any short-let operation
- **Residential STR < 30 days** generally **requires a tourism-licensed accommodation classification** issued by BTEA; operating Airbnb on a residential apartment without licence is non-compliant (enforcement variable but tightening)
- **Building OA approval**: Amwaj OA + most Bahrain Bay / Reef Island towers have specific clauses on short-let; verify with master OA before STR strategy
- **Penalties**: fines + closure orders for unlicensed STR operation (per BTEA enforcement bulletins)
- **VAT**: 10% applies to STR revenue if total taxable supplies > BHD 37,500/yr (NBR mandatory registration threshold)

## Section: `--mains`

### Universal in serviced areas

Bahrain urban property is essentially **always on mains** for water + electricity + sewerage + (often) cooling — single utility provider nationally for power/water.

| Service | Provider |
|---|---|
| **Electricity + Water** | **EWA** (Electricity & Water Authority) — `https://www.ewa.bh/` — sole national provider |
| **Sewerage** | Ministry of Works — Sanitary Engineering Affairs — `https://www.works.gov.bh/` |
| **District cooling** | **Tabreed Bahrain** (Reef Island + parts of Bahrain Bay), private chillers per development (Amwaj, Diyar) — separate bills; long-term concession contracts |
| **Internet / telecom** | **Batelco** (`https://www.batelco.com/`) — incumbent + **stc Bahrain** (`https://www.stc.com.bh/`) + **Zain Bahrain** (`https://www.bh.zain.com/`) — three-way competition |
| **Gas** | Bottled LPG (Bahrain Petroleum Company / BNGC); piped natural gas not common in residential blocks |

### Verification

1. Pull existing **EWA account info** from seller — confirms active mains + no overdue
2. Off-plan: confirm developer has utility connection on the master plan (verify NOC at handover)
3. Reef Island + Bahrain Bay parts use **Tabreed** district cooling — separate bill on top of EWA
4. Amwaj + Diyar Al Muharraq use private chillers per tower — verify cost structure
5. Some compounds use **TSE (treated sewage effluent)** for landscape irrigation — separate cost line in OA budget

### Tariffs (residential indicative)

- **EWA electricity (Bahraini nationals)**: subsidised — first slabs at BHD 0.003/kWh; higher slabs progressive (verify current rate at `https://www.ewa.bh/`)
- **EWA electricity (expatriates)**: tiered — first 3,000 kWh/month BHD 0.029/kWh; 3,000–5,000 BHD 0.032; 5,000+ BHD 0.060+ (per EWA schedule — *verify current rate*)
- **EWA water**: tiered — first 60 m³/month low; 60–100 m³ mid; 100+ m³ progressive
- **Tabreed district cooling** (where applicable): capacity charge + consumption — typical residential **BHD 0.500–1.500 / sqft / yr** all-in (per Tabreed tariff schedule — verify current)
