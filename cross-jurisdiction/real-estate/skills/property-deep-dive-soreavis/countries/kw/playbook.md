# دولة الكويت / Kuwait 🇰🇼 — Property Due-Diligence Playbook

ISO2: `kw`. Status: ✅ Fully populated (researched 2026-05).

> **Critical framing — read first**: Kuwait is **the most foreign-buyer-restricted property market in the GCC** for residential private housing. Under [Decree-Law 74/1979 on Real Estate Ownership by Non-Kuwaitis (قانون رقم 74 لسنة 1979 في شأن تنظيم تملك غير الكويتيين للعقار)](https://www.csb.gov.kw/) and subsequent amendments, **non-GCC foreign individuals cannot own residential private housing** (with extremely narrow case-by-case exceptions for diplomats, royal families, or specific Council of Ministers approvals). **Decree No. 195 of 2025** materially amended the 74/1979 framework: it permits **foreign-partnered companies listed on licensed Kuwaiti stock exchanges, plus real-estate funds and investment portfolios licensed by Kuwaiti authorities, to own real estate for operational purposes** — explicitly excluding plots/land designated for private housing (the residential-citizen exclusivity is preserved) (2026-05-27 verified, source legal500.com Decree 195/2025 brief + lexis.ae Jan 2025 + agbi.com Feb 2025 + UNCTAD Investment Policy Monitor measure 4972). **GCC nationals (Saudi / UAE / Bahrain / Qatar / Oman)** are treated equivalently to Kuwaitis under GCC reciprocity for residential ownership. Long-term lease (≤30 years renewable) is the practical pathway for non-GCC expatriates wishing to occupy a residence. Kuwait has **NO recurring property tax**, **NO personal income tax**, **NO capital gains tax for individuals**, and **VAT has NOT been implemented** (along with Qatar — both have deferred from the GCC framework). **DMTT 15% on in-scope MNEs (consolidated revenues ≥ EUR 750M) effective FY beginning 1 Jan 2025** under Kuwait's DMTT Law (Executive Regulations issued 29 Jun 2025) — Pillar Two alignment (2026-05-27 verified, source DLA Piper Gulf Tax Insights Jan 2025 + EY + KPMG Kuwait + PwC). Kuwait offers **NO golden-visa via real estate** — uniquely absent in the GCC. Currency: **Kuwaiti Dinar (KWD)** is the world's highest face-value currency (~USD 3.25 / KWD); managed-float against an undisclosed currency basket since 20 May 2007 (previously USD-pegged). **CBK Discount Rate cut to 3.50% effective 10 Dec 2025** (from 3.75% on 17 Sep 2025) — held into 2026 (2026-05-27 verified, source CBK press release 10 Dec 2025). **National Assembly was dissolved + some constitutional articles suspended 10 May 2024 (Emir Meshaal decree, up to 4-yr suspension)** — executive decrees now dominate legislation, which explains the rapid issuance of Decree 195/2025 + DMTT. Confirm any non-GCC ownership pathway with the **Ministry of Justice Real Estate Registration Department** and the **Council of Ministers** **before** any binding step.

- **Currency**: **KWD (د.ك — Kuwaiti Dinar)** — managed float against undisclosed basket since 20 May 2007 ([CBK press release, 20 May 2007](https://www.cbk.gov.kw/en/cbk-news/announcements-and-press-releases/press-releases/2007/05/200705200000-the-kuwaiti-dinar-kd-re-pegged-to-a-basket-of-currencies)); pre-2007 USD-pegged; KWD ≈ USD 3.25 (Q1 2026 indicative); **highest face-value currency globally**

  - Governorates: **Al Asimah (Capital — Kuwait City)**, **Hawalli**, **Farwaniya**, **Mubarak Al-Kabeer**, **Ahmadi**, **Jahra**
  - Major property markets: Kuwait City (Sharq, Mirqab, Dasman), Salmiya + Salwa (Hawalli), Mishref + Bayan + Salam (Mubarak Al-Kabeer), Jabriya (Hawalli), Bneid Al Qar, Surra, Mishref

  - **Ministry of Justice — Real Estate Registration Department (إدارة تسجيل العقارات بوزارة العدل)**: `https://www.moj.gov.kw/` — sole authority for property registration and Title Deed (سند ملكية / Sanad Mulkiyya) issuance under [Decree-Law 5/1959 on Real Estate Registration](https://www.moj.gov.kw/) (as amended; statute text not published on the MoJ portal — verify at MoJ Real Estate Registration Department)
  - **Public Authority for Civil Information (PACI — الهيئة العامة للمعلومات المدنية)**: `https://www.paci.gov.kw/` — operates the **Kuwait Finder** addressing system + civil/population registry + the official cadastral GIS layer
  - **Kuwait Municipality (بلدية الكويت)**: `https://www.baladia.gov.kw/` — building permits, zoning, planning permissions
  - **Ministry of Commerce and Industry (MOCI — وزارة التجارة والصناعة)**: `https://www.moci.gov.kw/` — commercial-property licensing + foreign-investment under the Foreign Direct Investment Law 116/2013 (KDIPA)

- **Title concept**: Freehold (مُلك تام) for Kuwaiti and GCC nationals; **non-GCC foreign individuals cannot own residential property** under Decree-Law 74/1979 (with very narrow exceptions); long-term lease (إجارة طويلة الأجل) ≤30 years renewable is the alternative
- **Foreign-buyer rule**: **VERY RESTRICTIVE** — see dedicated Foreign-buyer eligibility section below
- **Recent reforms (12-month watchlist)**:
  - **Kuwait Vision 2035 (New Kuwait — كويت جديدة)** — diversification programme; **Mubarak Al-Kabeer Port + Silk City (Madinat Al-Hareer)** on Boubyan Island. ⚠️ Programme status is **not verifiable from outside Kuwait** — the National Development Plan secretariat's site [`https://www.scpd.gov.kw/`](https://www.scpd.gov.kw/) resolves but refuses non-Kuwaiti traffic (checked 2026-09-18); verify delivery status at SCPD from a Kuwaiti connection, or against Ministry of Finance budget documents [`https://www.mof.gov.kw/`](https://www.mof.gov.kw/).
  - **Foreign Direct Investment Law 116/2013 (qudwa) + KDIPA (Kuwait Direct Investment Promotion Authority)** — 100% foreign ownership permitted in licensed business activities, but **does NOT extend to residential real estate** ([KDIPA](https://www.kdipa.gov.kw/))
  - **Real Estate Brokers Law (قانون الوسيط العقاري)** — periodic revisions through MOCI to license + regulate brokerage; latest amendments ongoing
  - **Bedoon (stateless residents) issue** — ~100,000 residents lack documentation; **cannot own property** under any framework; long-standing political concern
  - **VAT — NOT yet implemented** in Kuwait as of 2026-05 (Kuwait + Qatar are the only two GCC states yet to enact national VAT laws despite the 2016 GCC Framework Agreement) — verify at [Ministry of Finance](https://www.mof.gov.kw/)
  - **STR / Airbnb informal tolerance** — no dedicated short-term rental licensing regime; Ministry of Tourism (under MOI) + Ministry of Awqaf and Islamic Affairs (MOAA) tolerance variable
  - **Council of Ministers periodic amendments** to FDI / real-estate ownership regimes — slow and conservative

## 🇰🇼 Foreign buyer eligibility (READ FIRST)

This section determines whether a transaction is even legally possible. Kuwait's foreign-ownership regime is **the most restrictive in the GCC** and has not undergone the kind of reform Saudi Arabia announced in Jan 2025.

### Legal basis

- **[Decree-Law 74/1979 on Real Estate Ownership by Non-Kuwaitis (قانون رقم 74 لسنة 1979)](https://www.moj.gov.kw/)** (verify at Ministry of Justice Real Estate Registration Department) — primary statute regulating foreign ownership; **broadly prohibits non-Kuwaiti non-GCC ownership of residential real estate**
- **Subsequent amendments** (Law 20/2002 + Council of Ministers resolutions) — narrow case-by-case exceptions; have not opened broad designated-zone schemes comparable to UAE Dubai / Qatar / Saudi Arabia 2026
- **GCC Common Market 2008** — extends Kuwaiti-equivalent ownership rights to nationals of UAE, Bahrain, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Oman (subject to reciprocity)
- **Decree-Law 17/1959 on Aliens Residence (نظام إقامة الأجانب)** — governs expatriate residency (Iqama); does NOT confer property-purchase rights
- Administered jointly by **Ministry of Justice Real Estate Registration Department**, **Council of Ministers**, and (for commercial / FDI activities) **MOCI / KDIPA**

### Buyer category matrix

| Buyer category | Right |
|---|---|
| **Kuwaiti national** | Full freehold anywhere (excluding restricted military / strategic / Boubyan-area zones); subject to Kuwait Municipality zoning |
| **GCC national** (Saudi, UAE, Bahrain, Qatar, Oman) | Near-equivalent to Kuwaitis under GCC Common Market 2008 — freehold for residential/commercial, subject to **reciprocity** (i.e., the home GCC state must offer Kuwaitis comparable rights — verify per nationality at MOJ / Council of Ministers) |
| **Non-GCC foreign national (individual)** | **Generally CANNOT own residential property** under Decree-Law 74/1979. Narrow exceptions: diplomats accredited to Kuwait; specific Council of Ministers approvals (rare; royal-family or strategic-investor case-by-case); inheritance from a Kuwaiti/GCC relative may be permitted with subsequent disposal obligation |
| **Non-GCC foreign company** | Limited to commercial premises tied to a KDIPA-licensed enterprise under FDI Law 116/2013; NOT residential |
| **Long-term lease (≤30 years renewable)** | Available to non-GCC expatriates; not freehold; tenancy registered with Ministry of Justice; common pathway for senior expats wishing residential stability |
| **Bedoon (stateless residents)** | Cannot own under any framework — long-standing political/legal issue |

### What this means in practice

- The "buy a residence in Kuwait → own it freehold" pitch is **NOT legally available** for non-GCC foreign individuals
- The "buy property → get a residency / golden visa" pitch is **NOT available at all** — Kuwait has **no real-estate-linked residency programme** (uniquely absent in the GCC)
- For non-GCC expatriates, the practical residential pathway is **rental** (Decree-Law 35/1978 tenancy law) or **long-term lease** (≤30 yrs renewable) — both subject to employer-Iqama maintenance
- Commercial / industrial property can be held via a **KDIPA-licensed entity** with 100% foreign ownership for permitted business activities — but **not residential**

### Visa via property — NONE (uniquely absent in GCC)

| Item | Kuwait |
|---|---|
| **Investor / Golden Residency via real estate** | **NONE** — no real-estate-linked residency or investor-visa pathway |
| **Citizenship by investment** | **NONE** — Kuwaiti citizenship governed by [Decree-Law 15/1959 on Kuwaiti Nationality](https://www.moi.gov.kw/) (verify at Ministry of Interior) — strictly hereditary; naturalisation extremely rare (typically requires 20+ yrs residence + Arabic + Muslim + valuable contribution; political process; Council of Ministers + Emir's approval) |
| **Permanent residency for foreigners** | **NONE in formal sense** — Iqama under Decree-Law 17/1959 is renewable but tied to employer / family / property; some long-residing expats report informal recognition after 20+ yrs but not codified |
| **Golden Visa equivalent** | **NONE** |

### Compliance / AML

- **Central Bank of Kuwait (CBK)** AML regime under [Law 106/2013 on Combating Money Laundering and Terrorism Financing](https://www.cbk.gov.kw/) — banks + designated non-financial businesses (real-estate brokers, lawyers, notaries) are reporting entities
- **Kuwait Financial Intelligence Unit (KFIU)** — receives Suspicious Transaction Reports
- **Source-of-funds documentation** required for property purchases above thresholds; bank statements, employment, prior asset sale evidence enforced at MOJ Notary
- FATF MENAFATF assessment cycle covers Kuwait

### Verification path (before any reservation)

1. **Confirm buyer eligibility** — for non-GCC individuals, the default answer is "cannot own"; verify any claimed exception (diplomatic, Council of Ministers approval) at MOJ Real Estate Registration Department
2. **For GCC nationals**, confirm reciprocity status at MOJ / Council of Ministers
3. **Pull seller's current Title Deed (Sanad Mulkiyya)** from MOJ Real Estate Registration → verify ownership + encumbrances + mortgage status
4. **Long-term lease alternative**: if pursuing ≤30-yr lease, register with MOJ; verify zoning / Kuwait Municipality permitted use
5. **AML / source-of-funds**: bank statements + employment + prior asset-sale evidence ready before signing

## Section: `--tax`

### Annual property charges

**Kuwait has NO recurring property tax — neither owner-occupied nor let property pays an annual real-estate tax.** This is unique among GCC peers (UAE has municipal housing fee; Saudi has White Land + Vacant Property Fees; Bahrain has property tax for non-GCC; Oman has municipal property tax).

| Charge | Rate | Base | Statute |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Annual property tax** | **NONE** | — | — |
| **Municipality services fees** | Bundled into utility bills (small KWD sums) | — | Kuwait Municipality |
| **Vacant land levy** | **NONE** as of 2026-05 (no White-Land-Tax equivalent) | — | — |
| **Zakat** | Voluntary religious obligation; not state-collected from individuals | — | — |
| **National Labour Support Tax (NLST)** | **2.5%** of net profit | Listed Kuwaiti companies (excluding government + GCC) | Law 19/2000 |
| **Zakat tax** (corporate) | **1%** of net profit | Listed Kuwaiti shareholding companies | Law 46/2006 |
| **Kuwait Foundation for the Advancement of Sciences (KFAS) contribution** | **1%** of net profit | Listed Kuwaiti shareholding companies | Voluntary historical, now codified |

(Source: [Ministry of Finance — Department of Income Tax (DIT)](https://www.mof.gov.kw/) + Kuwait tax law framework — *verify current rates at MOF*)

### Transaction taxes (one-time at purchase)

| Item | Rate | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Real Estate Registration Fee (MOJ)** | ~**0.5–2% of sale value** (banded by transaction value, per MOJ schedule) | Paid at MOJ Real Estate Registration; verify current schedule |
| **Notarial / authentication fees** | ~0.5% additional, fixed minor amounts | Public Notary office (under MOJ) |
| **Real estate agent commission** | **2.5% (typical, negotiable)** of price | MOCI-licensed brokers |
| **Mortgage registration** | ~0.25–0.5% of mortgage amount | MOJ + bank |
| **Bank mortgage processing fee** | 0.5–1% of loan | Per bank |
| **Property valuation** (mortgaged) | KWD 100–300 | Bank-appointed valuer |
| **Lawyer / conveyancer (optional, advisable for non-Kuwaitis)** | KWD 500–3,000 typical | Kuwait Bar — local lawyer |
| **VAT** | **NOT applicable** — Kuwait has not enacted national VAT law as of 2026-05 (verify with MOF) | — |

(Sources: MOJ Real Estate Registration fee schedule; MOCI broker regulations — *verify current fees at `https://www.moj.gov.kw/`*)

**Total transaction cost (buyer side, cash, where eligible)**: **~3–4% of price** (~1% MOJ + ~2.5% agent + lawyer + admin) — among the lowest in the world.

### Personal income tax — 0%

- **NO personal income tax** in Kuwait (Kuwait has no PIT regime for individuals — Kuwaiti or foreign)
- **NO capital gains tax** on individual property sales (own-name natural person)
- **NO inheritance tax**

### Corporate income tax — 15% (foreign-corporate only)

- **Foreign-owned corporate share** of profits earned in Kuwait: **15% flat** (Decree-Law 3/1955 + Law 2/2008 amendment) — applies if you hold property in a foreign-owned Kuwaiti company
- **Kuwaiti + GCC-owned corporate income**: **0%** (subject to NLST 2.5% + Zakat 1% + KFAS 1% for listed companies = effective ~4.5%)
- **Withholding tax**: Kuwait does NOT have a formal WHT regime; certain payments to non-residents may be retained pending tax-clearance certificate

(Source: [Ministry of Finance — Department of Income Tax](https://www.mof.gov.kw/) + Decree-Law 3/1955 + Law 2/2008)

### Inheritance — Sharia default

- **No inheritance tax** in Kuwait
- **Sharia inheritance** applies as default for Muslim Kuwaitis (and often by application to Muslim residents); for non-Muslim foreign residents, the **home-country law of the deceased** typically governs movable estate, but **immovable property in Kuwait is governed by Kuwaiti law (lex situs)** — practical outcome is Sharia application via Kuwaiti courts unless a foreign-court order is recognised
- **Practical recommendation**: non-Muslim foreign owners (in the rare case they are eligible to own) should hold Kuwait real estate via a structured ownership or a clear cross-border estate plan; consult a Kuwait-licensed lawyer + home-jurisdiction counsel

### VAT — NOT YET implemented

- Kuwait signed the **GCC VAT Framework Agreement (2016)** with all 6 GCC states
- **Saudi Arabia, UAE, Bahrain, Oman** have implemented VAT (5%, raised to 15% in Saudi 2020)
- **Kuwait + Qatar have NOT yet enacted national VAT laws** as of 2026-05; multiple deferrals since 2018
- **When introduced** (timing uncertain — verify at MOF), expected at **5%** standard rate per the GCC framework; first-sale residential expected to be 0%-rated (per Saudi/UAE precedent)

### Future risk

- **VAT introduction**: timing uncertain; once announced, expect ~12–18 month lead time per Saudi/UAE precedent
- **Excise tax** (selective tax on tobacco / energy drinks / sugary drinks) — debated but not enacted as of 2026-05 — verify
- **Property tax introduction**: no announcements; politically improbable short-term but tracked

## Section: `--rental`

### Long-term residential

- **Tenancy law**: **[Decree-Law 35/1978 on Lease of Properties (قانون رقم 35 لسنة 1978 في شأن إيجار العقارات)](https://www.moj.gov.kw/)** + amendments (statute text not published on the MoJ portal — verify at MoJ) — landlord/tenant rights, eviction grounds, rent regulation framework
- **Tenancy registration**: Long-term tenancy contracts can be registered at the Ministry of Justice for enforceability; some apartments require tenancy registration for Iqama renewal, school enrolment, utility connections
- **Rent increase**: historically governed by Decree-Law 35/1978; mid-term increases for existing tenancies generally restricted; 5-year fixed-term contracts common with renewal review
- **Standard tenancy structure**: 1-year fixed term renewable; **monthly rent (post-dated cheques common)**; 1-2 month security deposit; landlord pays municipality fees, tenant pays utilities (MEW)
- **Evictions**: Limited grounds — non-payment, unauthorised sub-letting, owner self-occupation. Disputes routed to **Civil Court — Rent Chamber** under MOJ; not a separate dedicated tribunal as in Qatar/UAE

(Source: Decree-Law 35/1978; MOJ — *verify current rent regulation framework at MOJ*)

### Short-term rentals (Airbnb / vacation rentals)

⚠️ **AMBIGUOUS REGULATORY STATUS** as of 2026-05.

- **No dedicated STR licensing regime** comparable to UAE Dubai DTCM or Saudi Sahel platform
- **Hotel & Furnished Apartment Licensing**: under Ministry of Interior + Ministry of Awqaf and Islamic Affairs (MOAA) for some categories — applies to hotels + serviced apartments, not casual residential STR
- **Practical position**: informal Airbnb / short-let activity exists in Salmiya + Kuwait City but is **not formally licensed**; building Owner Association rules vary; municipality + utility-account holders may face penalty if discovered
- **Tax**: no VAT, no STR-specific levy
- **Building OA approval** is the practical gate where applicable

### Strategic notes

- **Non-GCC foreign buyers cannot generally own** — rental-investment thesis is **not legally available** to non-GCC individuals; long-term lease + sub-let scenarios subject to lessor consent
- **Tenant pool**: heavily expatriate (70% of population); highly mobile (1–3 year contracts common, tied to employment Iqama); Salmiya + Hawalli + Farwaniya are core expat catchments
- **PIT = 0%** federally — but US/UK/EU tax residents pay home-country tax on Kuwait rental income; Kuwait's DTA network is mid-sized (~80 DTAs incl. UK, France, Germany, Italy, Singapore, Russia, China, India)
- **Currency**: KWD income; EUR / USD-base owners face managed-float FX exposure (small but non-zero)

## Section: `--currency`

### Kuwaiti Dinar (KWD) — managed-float framework

- **Framework**: managed float against an **undisclosed currency basket** since **20 May 2007** ([CBK press release, 20 May 2007](https://www.cbk.gov.kw/en/cbk-news/announcements-and-press-releases/press-releases/2007/05/200705200000-the-kuwaiti-dinar-kd-re-pegged-to-a-basket-of-currencies))
- **Pre-2007**: USD-pegged at KWD 0.29963 = USD 1
- **Post-2007**: basket-managed; CBK actively intervenes; KWD has appreciated modestly vs USD since 2007
- **Q1 2026 indicative**: KWD 1 ≈ USD 3.25 (verify current at CBK)
- **Mechanism**: CBK targets stability against trade-weighted basket; intervenes in interbank market; daily fixing published
- **KWD distinction**: **highest face-value currency globally** (1 KWD ≈ 3.25 USD ≈ 3.0 EUR)

### What this means for buyers

- **USD-denominated buyers** (US): **modest FX risk** — KWD has appreciated 5–15% vs USD over 2007–2026; not a hard peg
- **EUR / GBP / JPY buyers**: KWD effectively a basket-tracking currency with USD weight; FX exposure modest
- **GCC buyers** (AED-pegged-USD, BHD-pegged-USD, OMR-pegged-USD, QAR-pegged-USD, SAR-pegged-USD): small FX risk via KWD basket vs their USD pegs
- **Hedging products**: FX-forward + options market via Kuwaiti banks + London for KWD pairs >USD 1M

### Peg credibility

- CBK foreign reserves ~USD 50B+ (Q4 2025 — verify CBK quarterly bulletin)
- Sovereign Wealth Fund (Kuwait Investment Authority — KIA) ~USD 1.0T (Jul 2025 Global SWF ranking — up from ~USD 846B Jul 2024; KIA is the 5th-largest SWF globally, 3rd in Gulf behind PIF and ADIA; 2026-05-27 verified) (one of the world's largest)
- Sovereign rating: Moody's A1, S&P A+, Fitch AA- — among GCC's strongest
- Basket framework defended successfully through 2008 GFC, 2014–2016 oil-price crash, 2020 COVID, 2022 USD-strength cycle
- **No serious near-term FX-regime-break risk** assessed by IMF Article IV

⚠️ **Tail risk**: any sustained KWD weakening would raise foreign-currency-denominated property values; unlikely near-term given KIA + CBK reserves.

### Capital controls

- **No restrictions on outbound FX** for residents + non-residents
- **AML / source-of-funds** requirements stringent under [Law 106/2013](https://www.cbk.gov.kw/) — declarations for transfers >KWD 3,000 (~USD 9,750)
- **Real-estate transactions** require source-of-funds documentation enforced at MOJ Notary

## Section: `--retirement`

### Retirement to Kuwait scenario

Kuwait is **not a traditional retirement destination** in the international sense, and the **absence of a real-estate-linked residency pathway** materially constrains retirement-via-property strategies for non-GCC foreigners.

Practical pathways (limited):

1. **Returning Kuwaiti diaspora** (Kuwaiti nationals + heritage families)
2. **GCC nationals** retiring in Kuwait — allowed via reciprocity
3. **Long-residing expatriates** maintaining Iqama via family or employer (older-age Iqama feasible for senior expats with family residency)
4. **No formal "retirement visa"** — Kuwait does NOT offer a Portugal D7 / Spain Non-Lucrative / Malaysia MM2H equivalent

### Cost-of-living for retirees (2026)

- 2-bed apt rental Salmiya / Hawalli: KWD 350–700/month (~USD 1,140–2,275)
- Public healthcare: free for Kuwaiti nationals; expat retirees rely on private health insurance KWD 200–800/yr (basic) or premium private cover KWD 800–3,000/yr
- Premium private healthcare (Dar Al-Shifa, Royale Hayat): higher tiers
- Utilities: KWD 30–80/month (MEW post-2017 expat-tariff reform)
- Eating out: mid-range KWD 5–15/meal; high-end KWD 30+
- **Alcohol prohibited** — no sale; consumption / possession illegal
- **Climate**: winter mild (10–22°C); summer extreme (45–50°C+)

### Tax position for retirees

- **No PIT, no CGT, no estate/inheritance tax** for individuals
- **No tax on foreign pension income** received in Kuwait
- **No real-estate-linked residency** — long-term residence depends on family / employer Iqama maintenance

### Healthcare access

- **Public** (Ministry of Health): free for Kuwaiti nationals; expat access reduced post-2017 tariff reforms (full-cost or co-pay)
- **Private**: Dar Al-Shifa, Al-Salam International, New Mowasat, Royale Hayat — high-quality but expat-self-pay or private-insurance-funded

### Verdict for retirement-first buyers

🔴 **Avoid** — for non-GCC foreigners seeking to retire in Kuwait via property purchase, **the legal pathway is not available**; Kuwait does not offer real-estate-linked residency. For GCC nationals retiring, viable. Other GCC destinations (UAE Golden Visa, Saudi Premium Residency, Qatar Investor Residency, Bahrain Golden Residency, Oman Investor Residency) offer property-linked retirement pathways that Kuwait does not.

## Active fiscal incentives (2026)

- **0% personal income tax** on rental income (universal — Kuwaiti + foreign individual)

- **CBK regulations** cap LTV (CBK Instructions):
