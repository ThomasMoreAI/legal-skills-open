# St. Kitts and Nevis 🇰🇳 — Property Due-Diligence Playbook

ISO2: `kn`. Status: ✅ Fully populated (researched 2026-05-28).

> **Critical framing — read first**: St. Kitts and Nevis (the **Federation of Saint Christopher and Nevis**) is an **English common-law twin-island state** in the Lesser Antilles, independent since **19 September 1983** — a Commonwealth realm with King Charles III as head of state via a Governor-General. It is **NOT a UK or EU territory**; no EU rules apply. The currency **XCD (East Caribbean Dollar) is pegged at EC$2.70 = US$1.00** by the [Eastern Caribbean Central Bank (ECCB)](https://www.eccb-centralbank.org/) — a long-standing peg in force since 1976 (verify still current at ECCB). The defining feature of the property market is the **world's oldest active Citizenship-by-Investment (CBI) programme, established 1984**, administered by the [Citizenship by Investment Unit (CIU)](https://ciu.gov.kn/). **Foreign buyers can own freehold property outright** via TWO routes: (a) buy any open-market property but first obtain an **Alien Land Holding Licence (ALHL)** — fee ~10% of price (~3 months) under the Aliens Land Holding Regulation Act; or (b) buy a **CIU-approved development under the CBI programme** — **EXEMPT from the ALHL** and conferring citizenship. **Post-October-2024 CBI real-estate minimums (VERIFIED at [ciu.gov.kn](https://ciu.gov.kn/real-estate-investment/)): US$325,000** for a developer/approved-development unit ("resaleable after seven years") and **US$600,000** for a private single-family home — both with a **mandatory 7-year hold**. The cheapest route is the **Sustainable Island State Contribution (SISC) donation, floor US$250,000**. CBI government fees are **separate and on top** (due-diligence US$10,000 main applicant; processing US$25,000 main applicant). Tax (IRD-verified): **no income tax, no capital gains tax, no inheritance/estate tax**; annual property tax is modest (St. Kitts residential ~0.2% building + ~0.2% land; Nevis ~0.156% building + ~0.75% land, due 30 June). **Stamp duty shifted to the BUYER in a 2023 reform** (~6% / 5% condo / 14% Special Development Areas / 18.5% South East Peninsula — `MEDIUM confidence, verify at sknird.com`). **Hurricane exposure is the primary physical risk** (Atlantic belt, 1 Jun – 30 Nov; CUBiC building code ~125 mph design wind). Power on St. Kitts = **SKELEC**; Nevis = **NEVLEC**. **Confirm** the current CBI minimums, hold period, ALHL fee, stamp-duty schedule, and Nevis-specific land rules at primary sources **before any binding step**. Authoritative primary sources: **[CIU](https://ciu.gov.kn/)**, **[Inland Revenue Department (IRD)](https://www.sknird.com/)**, **[Ministry of Justice & Legal Affairs — Land Registry](https://www.legal.gov.kn/)**, **[SKELEC](https://www.skelec.kn/)**, **[ECCB](https://www.eccb-centralbank.org/)**, **[Government of St. Kitts and Nevis portal](https://www.gov.kn/)**.

## Country profile

- **Geography**: Twin-island federal state in the Leeward Islands of the Lesser Antilles. **Saint Kitts (St. Christopher) ~168 km²** + **Nevis ~93 km²** (~261 km² total), separated by a ~3 km channel ("The Narrows"). Volcanic origin — **Mount Liamuiga (St. Kitts, ~1,156 m)** and **Nevis Peak (~985 m)** are both dormant/extinct stratovolcanoes. Capital **Basseterre** (St. Kitts); main town on Nevis is **Charlestown**.
- **Population**: **~47,000–48,000** (smallest sovereign state in the Americas by population — verify current at [World Bank](https://data.worldbank.org/country/st-kitts-and-nevis) / SKN Statistics Department). **Small-state caveat**: parcel-level data is thin; expect island/town-level granularity only.
- **GDP per capita**: **~USD 20,000–22,000 nominal** (recent World Bank — *verify at* [World Bank St. Kitts and Nevis](https://data.worldbank.org/country/st-kitts-and-nevis)). Economy driven by **tourism + CBI revenue**; the sugar industry closed in **2005**.
- **Currency**: **East Caribbean Dollar (XCD / EC$)**, issued by the [ECCB](https://www.eccb-centralbank.org/). **Pegged at EC$2.70 = US$1.00** (in force since 1976 — verify still current at ECCB). Real-estate prices in the CBI/expat market are commonly quoted in **USD**.
- **Languages**: **English (official, sole)** — all contracts, deeds, statutes in English. Saint Kitts Creole (a vernacular) is spoken everyday but legal/professional life is standard English.
- **Legal system**: **English common law** + Federation statutes. Superior court = the **Eastern Caribbean Supreme Court (ECSC)** — a **High Court of Justice** division sits with a resident judge. **Final appeal: the Judicial Committee of the Privy Council (JCPC) in London** — St. Kitts and Nevis has **NOT** adopted the Caribbean Court of Justice (CCJ) as its final appellate court (VERIFIED — audit-confirmed JCPC retained; verify current status at [JCPC](https://www.jcpc.uk/)).
- **Constitutional structure**: A **federation** — **Nevis has substantial constitutional autonomy** under the **Nevis Island Administration (NIA)**, running aspects of its own land administration and concessions separately from the Federal (Basseterre) government. The Land Registry has a **separate Nevis branch**. **Treat Nevis figures separately where they diverge** (notably the annual land-tax rate — see `--tax`).
- **Cadastre / land registry**:
  - **[Land Registry of Saint Christopher (St. Kitts) and Nevis](https://www.legal.gov.kn/land-registry/)** — under the Ministry of Justice & Legal Affairs; established by the **Land Registry Act No. 10 of 2017**; headquartered in St. Kitts with a **branch in Nevis**; led by a **Registrar of Lands** + Assistant Registrar.
  - **Inland Revenue Department (IRD)** — assessed-value roll for property-tax purposes.
- **Identifier**: Registration-section / block / parcel references under the registered-title system, OR a deed reference in the Register of Deeds under the unregistered system (see Foreign buyer eligibility). **No universal national parcel ID** equivalent to a French numéro de parcelle.
- **Title concept**: **TWO title systems run side-by-side** — the **unregistered system** (a *deed*, recorded in the Register of Deeds, governed by the **Conveyancing and Law of Property Act**) and the **registered system** (a **Certificate of Title / COT**, governed by the **Title by Registration Act**). The registered (COT) system gives the cleaner title guarantee. **Confirm WHICH system the target parcel sits under** before relying on the title position.
- **Recent reforms (12-month watchlist)**:
  - **CBI October-2024 reform**: real-estate minimums **reduced** to **US$325,000** (developer) and **US$600,000** (private), and the SISC donation floor set at **US$250,000** — a deliberate competitiveness move after the 2023–24 OECS due-diligence tightening (VERIFIED at [ciu.gov.kn](https://ciu.gov.kn/real-estate-investment/); do NOT cite the old US$200k/US$400k/US$800k figures as current).
  - **2024 OECS Memorandum of Agreement**: the five OECS CBI states (St. Kitts & Nevis, Antigua & Barbuda, Dominica, Grenada, St. Lucia) harmonised a **US$200,000 floor for the cheapest donation route** — ⚠️ this OECS US$200k floor is NOT the SKN real-estate minimum and NOT SKN's own published SISC figure (US$250k).
  - **2023 stamp-duty reform**: shifted the transfer-tax/stamp-duty burden from **SELLER to BUYER** per the amended Stamp Act (`MEDIUM confidence — confirm current schedule at` [sknird.com](https://www.sknird.com/)).
  - **Land Registry Act No. 10 of 2017** modernised the registry and created the Nevis branch.

## 🇰🇳 Foreign buyer eligibility (READ FIRST)

This section determines whether a transaction is even legally possible — and which of the two routes applies. The choice between **CBI-approved purchase** (ALHL-exempt + citizenship) and **open-market purchase** (ALHL ~10% fee, no citizenship) is the single most consequential decision in any SKN property transaction.

### Legal basis

- **Aliens Land Holding Regulation Act** — requires a non-citizen to obtain an **Alien Land Holding Licence (ALHL)** before holding open-market land.
- **Land Registry Act No. 10 of 2017** — the registry framework + Nevis branch.
- **Title by Registration Act** — registered (Certificate-of-Title) system.
- **Conveyancing and Law of Property Act** — unregistered (deed) system.
- **Saint Christopher and Nevis Citizenship Act** + **CBI regulations** — administered by the [Citizenship by Investment Unit (CIU)](https://ciu.gov.kn/); the statutory basis for the CBI routes.
- **Stamp Act** (as amended 2023) + property-tax provisions — administered by the [IRD](https://www.sknird.com/).

### Buyer category matrix

| Buyer category | Right |
|---|---|
| **SKN citizen** | Full freehold; standard conveyancing; no ALHL |
| **Non-citizen — OPEN-MARKET purchase** | **Full freehold** but must first obtain an **Alien Land Holding Licence (ALHL)** under the Aliens Land Holding Regulation Act. Fee **~10% of purchase price** (`~10% per market & legal sources — confirm exact at` [gov.kn](https://www.gov.kn/) / Ministry of Justice & Legal Affairs); **~3 months** to obtain. No citizenship conferred. |
| **Non-citizen — CBI-APPROVED development** | **Full freehold + citizenship**; **EXEMPT from the ALHL and the ~10% fee** — the principal cost driver behind the CBI structure. Minimums US$325,000 (developer) / US$600,000 (private). 7-year hold. |
| **CBI naturalised citizen** | Full freehold + work rights + visa-free travel per the SKN passport; treated as a citizen for ALHL purposes thereafter |

(Source: [CIU — Real Estate Investment](https://ciu.gov.kn/real-estate-investment/) + [CIU — Private Real Estate Investment](https://ciu.gov.kn/private-real-estate-investment/) + Aliens Land Holding Regulation Act — *the ALHL exemption for CBI-approved purchases is VERIFIED in concept; the ~10% fee is market/legal-sourced, confirm exact at gov.kn before relying*)

### Citizenship-by-Investment — the central hook (deep-dive in `--visa`)

- **World's oldest active CBI programme — established 1984**, administered by the [CIU](https://ciu.gov.kn/).
- **Real-estate routes (post-Oct-2024, VERIFIED verbatim at ciu.gov.kn)**:

| Route | Current minimum (main applicant) | Prior figure | Source (fetched 2026-05-28) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Developer's real estate** (condo / share in an Approved Development) | **US$325,000** — *"resaleable after seven years"* | was US$400,000 | [ciu.gov.kn/real-estate-investment/](https://ciu.gov.kn/real-estate-investment/) — VERIFIED |
| **Private real estate** (single-family Approved Private dwelling) | **US$600,000** — *"shall not be resold for a period of at least seven (7) years"* | was US$800,000 | [ciu.gov.kn/private-real-estate-investment/](https://ciu.gov.kn/private-real-estate-investment/) — VERIFIED |
| **SISC** (Sustainable Island State Contribution = donation; cheapest route, for comparison) | **US$250,000** single applicant or family of up to 4 | raised Oct 2024 | [ciu.gov.kn/sustainable-island-state-contribution/](https://ciu.gov.kn/sustainable-island-state-contribution/) — VERIFIED |

- **7-year hold** (VERIFIED on both CIU pages). **Resale-to-the-next-CBI-applicant** is the well-trodden exit: after 7 years a developer unit can be resold and the next buyer can themselves claim citizenship on it — this recyclability underpins the resale market. **CIU caveat (VERIFIED)**: property sold **before** the 7-year hold expires does NOT qualify for a later citizenship application *"unless the Federal Cabinet is satisfied that substantial further investment was injected … by way of further construction, renovation or otherwise."* → early resale breaks CBI-recyclability unless materially improved.

### CBI government fees (SEPARATE from, and ON TOP OF, the property price — VERIFIED ciu.gov.kn)

| Fee | Amount |
|---|---|
| **Due-diligence fee** | Main applicant **US$10,000**; dependant 16+ **US$7,500** |
| **Post-approval / processing fee** | Main applicant **US$25,000**; spouse **US$15,000**; dependant under 18 **US$10,000**; dependant 18+ **US$15,000** |

(Source: [ciu.gov.kn/real-estate-investment/](https://ciu.gov.kn/real-estate-investment/), fetched 2026-05-28 — these are additional to the US$325k / US$600k property cost.)

### Compliance / AML

- **Due-diligence regime tightened 2023–2024** under OECS-wide pressure (US Treasury + EU scrutiny of Caribbean CBI). Expect rigorous source-of-funds, multi-tier background checks, mandatory interview, and biometrics.
- **Source-of-funds documentation** required for any purchase (CBI or open-market); bank-statement chain back to legitimate source.
- **CRS + FATCA**: tax-residency self-certification mandatory at local bank account opening.
- **Beneficial-ownership disclosure** for corporate-held real estate.

### Verification path (before any reservation)

1. **Decide route**: CBI-approved development (ALHL-exempt + citizenship) vs open-market (ALHL ~10% + no citizenship). This drives the entire cost stack.
2. **If CBI**: confirm the development is on the **current CIU Approved Developments list** (only listed projects qualify); confirm the unit price meets the US$325k / US$600k floor; budget the separate CBI fees.
3. **If open-market**: file the **ALHL** under the Aliens Land Holding Regulation Act; budget ~10% + ~3-month timeline.
4. **Confirm title system**: registered (Certificate of Title) vs unregistered (deed in Register of Deeds) — pull the title search at the Land Registry.
5. **Nevis-specific**: if buying on Nevis, verify land rules + concessions with the **NIA** (separate from Basseterre) and note the higher Nevis residential land-tax rate (~0.75%).
6. **AML / source-of-funds**: bank statements + employment / asset-sale evidence ready before signing reservation.

### Compute

1. **USD/m² = price ÷ built-area m²** (listings often quote **square feet** — convert: 1 m² = 10.764 sqft).
2. Compare to **≥ 3 active listings** on the same area + waterfront-status (community + waterfront drive price far more than island average).
3. **Listing convention**: CBI + expat market quotes **USD-firm**; local-market listings may be EC$-priced (peg EC$2.70 = US$1.00 → divide by 2.70 for USD).
4. **Trap — CBI-floor pricing distortion**: a unit priced at *exactly* US$325,000 is priced to the CBI floor, not to intrinsic market value — the citizenship benefit is bundled into the price. The same unit on the open market (non-CBI) may be worth materially less.
5. **Trap — developer projections**: occupancy + rental-return figures from CBI developers are seller-controlled — tag `est., developer projection — request actuals`.
6. **Trap — title-system status**: registered (Certificate-of-Title) parcels convey cleaner than unregistered (deed) parcels. Verify at the Land Registry.
7. **Trap — SE Peninsula stamp duty**: the Christophe Harbour zone carries the highest stamp-duty band (~18.5% — see `--tax`); materially affects total cost there.

### Key terms

- **Deed of Conveyance** — instrument transferring title under the unregistered system
- **Certificate of Title (COT)** — registered-system title document (Title by Registration Act)
- **Register of Deeds** — where unregistered-system deeds are recorded
- **Alien Land Holding Licence (ALHL)** — the ~10% licence a non-citizen needs for an open-market purchase
- **Approved Development** — a CIU-listed project whose units qualify for the CBI real-estate route
- **SISC** — Sustainable Island State Contribution (the CBI donation route)

## Section: `--tax`

> **Headline**: St. Kitts and Nevis has **NO personal income tax, NO capital gains tax, and NO inheritance/estate tax** — a core CBI pitch and headline draw (verify current at [IRD](https://www.sknird.com/)). Real-estate-specific taxes: a **modest annual property tax** (St. Kitts residential ~0.2% building + ~0.2% land; Nevis ~0.156% building + ~0.75% land, due 30 June) and a **one-time stamp duty / transfer tax** on sale — which the **2023 reform shifted to the BUYER** (`MEDIUM confidence schedule — verify at sknird.com`). **A foreign open-market buyer also pays the ALHL ~10%** (see Foreign buyer eligibility); **CBI-approved purchases avoid the ALHL** — a large part of why the CBI structure dominates the foreign market.

### Annual property tax — VERIFIED at IRD

Governed by Federation property-tax provisions; administered by the [IRD](https://www.sknird.com/property-tax/). Tax base = **IRD-assessed value**. **Payable on or before 30 June each year** (1%/month interest on arrears).

| Island / class | Building rate | Land rate |
|---|---|---|
| **St. Kitts — Residential** | **0.2%** | **0.2%** |
| **St. Kitts — Commercial** | 0.3% | 0.3% |
| **St. Kitts — Accommodation** | 0.2% | 0.2% |
| **Nevis — Residential** | **0.156%** | **0.75%** ⚠️ notably higher than St. Kitts |
| **Nevis — Commercial** | 0.3% | 0.2% |

(Source: [sknird.com/property-tax/](https://www.sknird.com/property-tax/), fetched 2026-05-28 — VERIFIED. **Note the Nevis residential LAND rate (0.75%) is ~3.75× the St. Kitts rate (0.2%)** — a material recurring-cost difference for land-heavy Nevis holdings.)

## Section: `--rental`

### Reality

Tourism-driven **short-term / villa-rental market** (Frigate Bay, Christophe Harbour, Nevis). The **long-term residential rental market is thin** (small population). CBI condo units are frequently placed in **developer rental-management / hotel pools**.

### Yield benchmarks (aggregator-level — verify per property)

- **Gross rental yields**: reported **~2–5%** (≈4% average) gross; net often **~3–6%** depending on type / occupancy (globalpropertyguide-derived — tag `est., aggregator-level`). Treat as indicative only — **no official index**.
- **CBI condo units in hotel/rental pools**: developer occupancy + return projections are **seller-controlled** → tag `est., developer projection — request actuals`. The bundled-citizenship premium in the purchase price also depresses the effective yield versus the same unit bought purely as an investment.

### Strategic notes

- **Hurricane insurance is a meaningful operating cost** in the Caribbean — see `--risks`; budget it explicitly into any yield model.
- **Seasonality**: high season **mid-Dec to mid-Apr** (Northern-hemisphere winter); **low / hurricane season Jun–Nov** materially depresses occupancy.
- **HOA / resort-management rules** at branded estates (Christophe Harbour, branded residences) regulate short-term rentals — read the declaration / by-laws before pricing a yield model.
- **7-year CBI hold** locks the unit: a CBI-route buyer cannot freely flip for resale yield within the hold window (and early resale breaks CBI-recyclability unless materially improved — see Foreign buyer eligibility).

## Section: `--work=<profession>`

### Reality

The property thesis is overwhelmingly **CBI / second-home / retirement / hospitality-investor**, NOT local employment. Local employment for non-citizens requires a **work permit**; **CBI confers citizenship and therefore full work rights**. With **no personal income tax**, earned income is untaxed at the personal level (verify current at IRD).

### Employment landscape

- **Dominant sectors**: tourism & hospitality (resorts), public sector, financial / offshore services, and **CBI-funded construction**.
- **Foreign-worker permits** required for employment unless the worker is a citizen (incl. CBI-naturalised) or CARICOM Skills-certificate holder.

### Visa routes for working

| Route | Eligibility | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **CBI citizenship** | US$325k / US$600k real estate (7-yr hold) or US$250k SISC donation + fees | Confers citizenship → full work rights; visa-free travel on the SKN passport |
| **Work permit (employer-sponsored)** | SKN employer + justification | Standard route for a salaried non-citizen |
| **CARICOM Skills Certificate** | CARICOM citizen + recognised skill | Free movement + work within CARICOM |

### Catchment heuristics

- **Basseterre / Frigate Bay (St. Kitts)**: the bulk of formal employment — government, finance, hospitality, retail.
- **Christophe Harbour (SE Peninsula)**: luxury-hospitality + construction cluster.
- **Nevis (Charlestown)**: smaller; tourism + NIA public sector + offshore-finance niche.

### Hurricane (Atlantic basin) — PRIMARY RISK

- **Position**: Leeward Islands, squarely in the **Atlantic hurricane belt**. Season **1 June – 30 November**, peak **August–October**. ~80 recorded hurricane events since Columbus (OAS vulnerability assessment).
- **Historical reference**: **Hugo (1989)**, **Luis & Marilyn (1995)**, **Wilma (2005)** — Wilma's surge + wind caused severe damage despite no direct eye hit. **Irma (2017)** passed with comparatively minor SKN damage (~2 homes severely damaged). **Storm surge + wind** are the property-level hazards.
- **Building code**: the **Caribbean Uniform Building Code (CUBiC)** is incorporated in the draft **St. Kitts–Nevis Building Code**; design wind = reference pressure ~0.83 kPa ≈ 3-second gust **~56 m/s (125 mph)**, 50-year return (OAS / CUBiC). Post-Wilma codes were strengthened. **VERIFY a given property's build standard + elevation.**
- **Implication**: this is a **higher-hurricane-risk jurisdiction than Barbados** (which sits south of the main track); engineered post-strengthening construction + funded insurance are non-negotiable.

### Insurance — material annual TCO line

- **Windstorm / hurricane cover is effectively mandatory** (and required by any mortgagee) and is a **material annual TCO line** in the Caribbean — budget for it explicitly. Get **3+ quotes** for the specific parcel before contract.
- The Federation participates in the **CCRIF** (Caribbean Catastrophe Risk Insurance Facility — sovereign parametric pool) — but **that protects the GOVERNMENT, not the homeowner**; homeowners need private cover.

### Seismic / volcanic — background, non-zero

- **Mount Liamuiga (St. Kitts) and Nevis Peak (Nevis)** are dormant/extinct stratovolcanoes; the Lesser Antilles arc is seismically active. Monitored by [UWI SRC](https://www.uwiseismic.com/).
- **Practical**: standard structural review for older builds; background Caribbean seismic + ashfall risk (regional volcanoes), not a primary discriminator for modern construction.

## Section: `--currency`

- **Currency**: **East Caribbean Dollar (XCD / EC$)**, issued by the [ECCB](https://www.eccb-centralbank.org/) for the OECS monetary union.
- **Peg**: **EC$2.70 = US$1.00** — a long-standing peg in force since **1976** (verify still current at ECCB).
- **USD circulates in parallel**: tourism + CBI + expat property transactions are commonly **USD-quoted or USD-firm**; local-market listings may be EC$-priced.
- **Practical effect for buyer**: **zero structural FX risk for USD-base buyers**; minimal risk for EUR/GBP/CAD buyers (USD-correlated). The peg has held for decades through external shocks; the ECCB monetary union (8 member territories sharing the XCD) underpins credibility — **but the peg is a policy choice, not a structural certainty**.
- **No exchange-control gating** of foreign-buyer purchase funds equivalent to the Barbados CBB regime — but **source-of-funds documentation is rigorous** under the CBI / AML framework (see Foreign buyer eligibility).

## Section: `--visa` (Citizenship-by-Investment deep-dive)

St. Kitts and Nevis runs the **world's oldest active Citizenship-by-Investment programme — established 1984** — administered by the [CIU](https://ciu.gov.kn/sustainable-island-state-contribution/). The 2024 OECS Memorandum of Agreement harmonised a US$200,000 donation floor across the five OECS CBI states; SKN's own published figures sit above that for real estate and at US$250k for its SISC donation.

### Real-estate routes (post-October-2024 reform — VERIFIED ciu.gov.kn)

| Route | Minimum (main applicant) | Hold | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Developer's real estate** | **US$325,000** | **7 years** | Condo / share in a CIU-Approved Development; *"resaleable after seven years"* (VERIFIED verbatim). The dominant CBI product. |
| **Private real estate** | **US$600,000** | **7 years** | Single-family Approved Private dwelling; *"shall not be resold for a period of at least seven (7) years"* (VERIFIED verbatim). |
| **SISC donation** (cheapest, non-property) | **US$250,000** (single or family of up to 4) | n/a | Sustainable Island State Contribution — the donation route, for comparison. Additional dependants: under 18 ~US$25,000 each; 18+ ~US$50,000 each (*verify exact at CIU SISC page*). |

(Source: [ciu.gov.kn/real-estate-investment/](https://ciu.gov.kn/real-estate-investment/) + [ciu.gov.kn/private-real-estate-investment/](https://ciu.gov.kn/private-real-estate-investment/) + [ciu.gov.kn/sustainable-island-state-contribution/](https://ciu.gov.kn/sustainable-island-state-contribution/) — VERIFIED 2026-05-28. **Do NOT cite the old US$200k/US$400k/US$800k figures as current.**)

### Government fees (SEPARATE — on top of property price; VERIFIED)

| Fee | Amount |
|---|---|
| **Due-diligence** | Main applicant **US$10,000**; dependant 16+ **US$7,500** |
| **Post-approval / processing** | Main applicant **US$25,000**; spouse **US$15,000**; dependant < 18 **US$10,000**; dependant 18+ **US$15,000** |

### Hold period + resale-as-exit

- **7-year mandatory hold** (VERIFIED on both CIU pages).
- **Resale-to-the-next-CBI-applicant** is the well-trodden exit: after 7 years a developer unit can be resold and the **next buyer can themselves claim citizenship** on it — this recyclability underpins the resale market.
- **CIU caveat (VERIFIED)**: a property **sold before** the 7-year hold expires does **NOT** qualify for a later citizenship application *"unless the Federal Cabinet is satisfied that substantial further investment was injected … by way of further construction, renovation or otherwise."* → early resale **breaks CBI-recyclability** unless materially improved.

### Process

- Applications must go through an **Authorised Agent**; rigorous **source-of-funds + multi-tier due diligence** (tightened 2023–2024 under OECS / US Treasury / EU scrutiny). Mandatory interview + biometrics.
- The development must be on the **current CIU Approved Developments list** for a real-estate-route purchase to qualify.
- **ALHL exemption**: CBI-approved purchases are **exempt** from the Alien Land Holding Licence (~10%) that open-market foreign buyers pay — a major structural cost advantage.

### Property linkage + caveats

- **Bundled-citizenship premium**: a unit priced at exactly the CBI floor (US$325k) is priced *to the floor*, not to intrinsic open-market value — the citizenship benefit is embedded in the price; the same unit bought purely as an investment may be worth less.
- **Liquidity is hold-gated**: you cannot freely exit within the 7-year window without breaking CBI-recyclability (unless materially improved).
- **Programme scrutiny risk**: Caribbean CBI programmes face periodic EU visa-free-travel + US Treasury scrutiny — a structural medium-term policy risk to the citizenship benefit (track the [regulatory-watch](../../shared/regulatory-watch.md) entry).

(Source: [CIU](https://ciu.gov.kn/private-real-estate-investment/) primary, fetched 2026-05-28 — *verify current minimums, fees, Approved-Developments list, and due-diligence requirements at the CIU before relying*)
