# Sint Maarten (sx) — Property Due-Diligence Playbook

ISO2: `sx`. Status: ✅ Populated (researched 2026-05-27). Constituent country within the Kingdom of the Netherlands since the constitutional reform of **10 October 2010** (so-called "10-10-10"). Southern ~34 km² half of the island of Saint-Martin / Sint Maarten; the northern French half (FR Saint-Martin, ISO `MAF` / FR-overlay) is **out of scope** for this playbook — see `countries/fr/playbook.md` for the metropolitan-FR overlay applicable to that side.

## Country profile

- **Status**: Autonomous constituent country within the Kingdom of the Netherlands (alongside Aruba, Curaçao, the NL). Own constitution (*Staatsregeling van Sint Maarten*, 2010), own Parliament (*Staten van Sint Maarten*, 15 seats), own Council of Ministers (*Ministerraad*), own judiciary (Common Court of Justice of Aruba, Curaçao, Sint Maarten and BES; final cassation to Hoge Raad NL).
- **Surface**: ~**34 km²** (Dutch half of the 87 km² island; northern French half ~53 km²). World's smallest land mass shared between two sovereign jurisdictions.
- **Capital**: Philipsburg (Great Bay; cruise + government centre).
- **Population**: **42,449** at 1 January 2025 (Department of Statistics STAT rebased estimate, benchmarked to the 2022 Population and Housing Census count of **41,902** — [STAT release, 29 Jun 2026](https://www.sintmaartengov.org/news/Pages/Rebased-Population-Estimates-and-Vital-Statistics-for-January-1-2025.aspx); 2026-09-18 verified); cross-border movement with FR Saint-Martin is unrestricted (no land border; *Treaty of Concordia* 1648).
- **Currency**: **Netherlands Antillean guilder (ANG / NAƒ)** — pegged to **USD at 1.79 ANG = 1 USD** since 1971; **USD is broadly accepted in parallel** for retail, rents, real-estate transactions. The Central Bank of Curaçao and Sint Maarten (CBCS — `https://www.centralbank.cw/`) issues the ANG. A long-pending replacement currency, the **Caribbean guilder (XCG)**, was launched in Curaçao + Sint Maarten on **31 March 2025** at the same 1.79 = 1 USD peg (CBCS announcement) — ANG ↔ XCG transition is the live FX reality in 2026.
- **Languages**: **Dutch + English** are both official (English is the primary language of daily commerce, education, real-estate documents); **Papiamentu** has cultural standing but is the heritage language of Curaçao / Bonaire / Aruba rather than SX-native — Sint Maarten's de facto vernacular is **English-Creole + Dutch for government**. Notarial deeds (*notariële akte*) can be drafted in Dutch or English at the notary's discretion.
- **Legal system**: **Civil law** (Dutch tradition); property law derived from Dutch Caribbean civil code; deed-based registration (*notariële akte van levering*) recorded at the Cadastre & Land Registry Foundation.
- **Cadastre + Land Registry**: **Cadastre and Land Registry Foundation Sint Maarten (Kadaster Sint Maarten)** — `https://kadaster.sx/` — autonomous foundation; sole national registry for title, mortgage deeds, boundary stake-out, map services, mortgage-deed lodgment.
- **Identifier**: parcel ID via *meetbrief* (measurement brief) + Kadaster registration; addresses use street + number (no postal-code system equivalent to NL).
- **Key documents**: *notariële akte van levering* (notarial deed of delivery), *meetbrief* (cadastral survey extract), *eigendomsbewijs* (proof of ownership extract), *hypotheekakte* (mortgage deed), *verklaring van geen bezwaar* (no-objection certificate where applicable).
- **EU status**: **NOT in the EU**. Sint Maarten is one of the **EU Overseas Countries and Territories (OCT)** associated with the EU under Part Four TFEU + Council Decision (EU) 2021/1764. **Not in Schengen**, not in EU customs union. Visa-free entry for EU/EEA/UK/US/CA/most Western nationals up to 90 days.
- **Foreign-buyer regime (CRITICAL — buyer-friendly headline)**: **no restrictions on foreign ownership of real estate**. Non-residents may purchase *eigendom* (freehold) or *erfpacht* (long lease) without prior permission. The two foreign-buyer hooks are the **headline 4% transfer tax (overdrachtsbelasting)** and **NO recurring annual property tax in practice** (the statutory *grondbelasting* exists on paper but has not been enforced for decades and is now subject to a February 2026 abolition bill — see `--tax` below).
- **Hurricane reality (CRITICAL)**: **Hurricane Irma (6 September 2017, Category 5, sustained 295 km/h winds)** caused damage equivalent to **~255% of GDP** (US$2.73 bn total damages + losses per World Bank NRRP) and damaged ~19,400 of ~40,000 documented homes. Risk profile + building-code regime + insurance market are all defined post-Irma. See `--risks`.

## Major reforms / regulatory landscape

- **2010-10-10 Constitutional reform** — Netherlands Antilles dissolved; Sint Maarten became autonomous constituent country within the Kingdom (with Aruba and Curaçao); BES islands (Bonaire, Sint Eustatius, Saba) became special municipalities of the NL.
- **Hurricane Irma 6 Sept 2017** — Category 5 landfall; National Recovery and Resilience Plan (NRRP) adopted 2018; Sint Maarten Trust Fund (NL-financed, World-Bank-managed) established for "Build Back Better" reconstruction (NRRP — `https://nrpbsxm.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/08/NRRP.pdf`).
- **2025-03-31 Caribbean guilder (XCG) launch** — CBCS introduced the XCG replacing the ANG in SX + CW at 1:1 conversion; same 1.79 = 1 USD peg. Real-estate contracts spanning the transition reference both currencies (verify draft language on every deed).
- **2026-02-13 Land Tax + Inheritance Tax abolition bill** — Minister of Finance Marinka J. Gumbs presented to Parliament a bill to **formally abolish *grondbelasting* (land tax) + *successiebelasting* (inheritance tax)** on grounds that both have been "on the books for decades but never enforced" *(SXM Talks, 16 Feb 2026 — verify Parliament passage at `https://www.sintmaartengov.org/`)*. Status at time of writing: **proposed, pending Parliament approval** — but the de-facto reality (no enforcement) has not changed for decades.
- **2026-05-22 GEBE oversight decree** — Bureau Telecommunication and Post (BTP) formally designated as supervisor over NV GEBE's electricity concession (first structured oversight in 15+ years); decree published in National Gazette 22 May 2026 *(721news / SXM Talks)*.

For active regulatory items see `shared/regulatory-watch.md`.

### Compute

1. USD/m² = price / built area per Kadaster *meetbrief*
2. SX-specific premium drivers: **beach proximity** (Cupecoy / Mullet Bay / Dawn Beach), **gated community + on-site security** (Porto Cupecoy, The Cliff), **post-Irma rebuild compliance** (concrete + impact-glazed shutters lowers insurance premium materially)

## Section: `--tax`

### Annual property tax — *Grondbelasting* (LAND TAX)

**The foreign-buyer-friendly headline: in practice, Sint Maarten currently has NO enforced annual recurring property tax.**

- **Statutory position**: *Landsverordening grondbelasting* (Land Tax Ordinance, pre-2010 Netherlands Antilles legacy) provides for a land tax of nominally **~0.3% of assessed value** on improved + unimproved land *(secondary sources — `https://www.doingbusinessdutchcaribbean.com/st-maarten/tax-sxm/`)*. The statute remains on the books.
- **Enforcement reality**: per Minister of Finance Marinka J. Gumbs (16 Feb 2026, SXM Talks press release): the land tax + inheritance tax "have been on the books for decades; however, they were never enforced." **Sint Maarten Tax Administration (`https://www.tax.sx/`)** lists income tax, profit tax, monthly returns + vehicle tax as its administered tax base — **NO property / land tax appears in the tax.sx administered-taxes list** (verified 2026-05-27).
- **Pending abolition (2026-02-13)**: Council of Ministers approved + presented to Parliament a bill to **formally abolish *grondbelasting* + *successiebelasting***. Status at writing: **pending Parliament passage** *(verify at `https://www.sintmaartengov.org/`)*.

> ⚠️ **Practical guidance**: do **not** rely on second-hand "0.3% property tax" figures circulated in foreign-investor marketing literature without confirming **current enforcement status** with the Sint Maarten Tax Administration directly. Treat any quoted *grondbelasting* figure as a **non-active statutory ceiling**, not a real annual cost. **Annual recurring outlay is effectively dominated by HOA/strata fees + insurance** — see `--insurance` and `--retirement`. Verify directly at `https://www.tax.sx/` or call +1 721-542-0349 / -2236.

### Transaction tax — *Overdrachtsbelasting* (TRANSFER TAX)

- **Rate**: **4% of purchase price (or assessed value, whichever is higher)** — flat. Paid by buyer at closing. Withheld + remitted by the *notaris* (notary). Single-tier, no first-time-buyer relief, no progressive surcharge.
- **Source**: administered by Sint Maarten Tax Administration (`https://www.tax.sx/`); confirmed across multiple agent + tax-advisory sources for 2026 (idr.sx · doingbusinessdutchcaribbean.com · easysxm.com).
- This is the **headline foreign-buyer cost**. Contrast with Italy (~9–15% all-in, est. — see `countries/it/playbook.md`), France (~7–8% all-in, est. — see `countries/fr/playbook.md`), Spain (~10–13% all-in, est. — see `countries/es/playbook.md`) — SX's 4% transfer-tax floor is materially lower.

### Other transaction items

- **Notary fee**: regulated; typically **1–1.5% of price** (verify per deal with the chosen *notaris*).
- **Kadaster registration**: deed-lodgment fee; typically **modest fixed + small ad-valorem** (verify current schedule at `https://kadaster.sx/`).
- **Estate-agent commission**: typically **5% (seller-side) + sometimes split with buyer's agent** — verify per *bemiddelingsovereenkomst* (brokerage agreement).
- **Mortgage stamp / *hypotheekrecht***: where applicable, small ad-valorem on the mortgage deed (paid at *hypotheekakte* lodgment).

### Total transaction cost (buyer side, est., 2026)

- **Cash resale**: **~5–6%** all-in (4% transfer + 1–1.5% notary + small registration); agent commission typically borne by seller.
- **Mortgage-financed**: **~6–8%** (above + mortgage stamp + bank fees).

### Rental income tax — *Inkomstenbelasting* on rental income

- **Individual ownership**: rental income forms part of taxable income under the **Sint Maarten income tax (*inkomstenbelasting*)** progressive scale — verify current bracket schedule at `https://www.tax.sx/`. Non-residents are taxed on Sint-Maarten-source income (including rental income from SX property).
- **BV ownership**: many foreign investors hold SX rental property via a **Sint Maarten BV (*besloten vennootschap*)** — the BV is then liable for *winstbelasting* (profit tax) on rental net income. **Profit tax rate ~34.5%** (statutory rate; verify current at tax.sx). Setup cost typically US$3,500–6,000 + annual maintenance US$1,500–3,000 *(per idr.sx — verify per professional)*.
- **No flat-rate "imputed rental income" regime** equivalent to NL Box 3 — actual income / actual costs basis.

### Capital gains tax on real estate

- **Individual sellers**: Sint Maarten does **not levy a standalone capital gains tax on private real estate sales** *(verified across multiple 2024–2026 secondary sources; primary confirmation via tax.sx — no CGT category listed)*. This is the **second foreign-buyer-friendly headline** alongside no enforced annual property tax.
- **Caveat**: if real-estate trading rises to the level of *business activity* (frequent flipping, developer-style operations), proceeds may be re-characterised as business income subject to *inkomstenbelasting* / *winstbelasting*. Verify with SX tax advisor for high-frequency / developer-style holding patterns.
- **Caveat 2**: BV-held property — gains realised inside the BV form part of *winstbelasting* base.
- **US-resident sellers**: regardless of SX position, US federal CGT applies on worldwide gains for US persons — coordinate via DTA / FTC with US CPA.

### Inheritance tax — *Successiebelasting*

- Statutory framework exists but, per Minister of Finance Feb 2026, "never enforced." Abolition bill before Parliament (2026-02-13). **Treat as effectively zero in practice**, but coordinate with foreign jurisdictions' inheritance/estate taxes (US estate tax for US persons; UK IHT for UK domiciliaries).

### Source-of-truth for every tax above

**Sint Maarten Tax Administration**: `https://www.tax.sx/` — administered-tax list: income tax, profit tax, monthly returns (wage + turnover + room), vehicle tax. **No property / land / capital-gains category listed on tax.sx** (verified 2026-05-27).

## Section: `--rental`

### Long-term residential

- Civil-law tenancy framework derived from Dutch Caribbean civil code; **less prescriptive than NL Box 3 / huurbescherming regime** — fewer tenant-protection escalators.
- Typical lease structure: 1-year renewable; deposit (*borg*) typically **1–2 months**.
- No statutory rent cap; market-rate setting.
- **Currency reality**: long-term leases routinely quoted + paid in **USD** despite legal-tender ANG/XCG — buyer-investor should verify the lease's currency clause (USD vs ANG vs XCG vs "at peg rate").

### Short-let (vacation rental / Airbnb)

- **Room tax (BBO / *kamerbelasting* / occupancy tax)**: **5% room tax** levied on hotel + short-let guest accommodation *(per doingbusinessdutchcaribbean.com)* — operator-collected, remitted to Tax Administration. Verify SX-specific licensing for short-let operations at `https://www.sintmaartengov.org/`.
- **Insurance constraint**: post-Irma, short-let occupancy can affect insurance underwriting — disclose to the insurer.
- **HOA / strata constraints**: many Cupecoy / Maho / Dawn Beach HOAs impose **minimum-stay rules** (e.g., 7-day minimum) to manage cruise-passenger turnover. Verify HOA bylaws before assuming sub-week Airbnb-style turnover is permitted.
- **Cross-border note**: French Saint-Martin operators follow FR *meublé de tourisme* rules (Article D324-1 CT) — these do **not** apply to SX-side property; distinct regimes.

### Yields (est., 2026)

- Long-term gross yield, mid-tier 2-bed Cupecoy / Simpson Bay: **5–8%** (post-tax, post-HOA, post-insurance — net materially lower)
- Short-let gross yield (compliant, well-managed Cupecoy / Mullet Bay): **8–14%** seasonal peaks (Dec–Apr high season, ~2M cruise-passenger annual flow, est. 2026 — verify current figure at the SX port authority / airport authority; may have changed) — but high operational + turnover-cost drag; vacancy in shoulder-season Jun–Nov.

⚠️ **Net-after-all-costs reality check**: per idr.sx 2026 scenarios — US$350k condo all-in carrying cost **US$10,500/yr** (HOA ~US$400/mo + utilities + insurance + maintenance) → break-even gross rent ~6% before void allowance. **HOA + insurance + cooling/AC dominate** the operating cost line in SX, materially above EU comparables.

### Specific risks

- **Hurricane (PRIMARY HAZARD — defines the risk profile)**:
  - **Hurricane Irma, 6 September 2017, Category 5** (sustained 295 km/h winds) — landfall on Saint-Martin / Sint Maarten; **Hurricane Maria followed less than two weeks later**. Combined damages + losses across the two hurricanes: **US$2.7 bn** — damages **US$1.4 bn (73% of nominal GDP)** and losses **US$1.3 bn (68% of nominal GDP)**; housing-sector damages **US$442 m**, losses **US$22.1 m**, recovery needs **US$533.75 m** (22.8% of total national needs). The island's housing stock at the time was **~19,400 units accommodating 40,000+ documented residents**; the plan states that reliable data on the extent of damage and the number of units affected **is not readily available**, so no dwelling-level damage count is citable. *(World Bank / Government of Sint Maarten, National Recovery and Resilience Plan — [NRRP PDF](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/793011623753566547/pdf/Sint-Maarten-National-Recovery-and-Resilience-Plan-A-Roadmap-to-Building-Back-Better.pdf); 2026-09-18 verified).*
  - **Hurricane season**: 1 June – 30 November; peak Aug–Oct.
  - **Post-Irma rebuild benchmark**: NRRP "Build Back Better" requires **hurricane-resistant standards** (impact-rated glazing, hurricane shutters / accordion-shutters / Bahama shutters, concrete-block or reinforced-concrete construction, reinforced roof-to-wall connections, underground utility ducting for new build). The **pre-Irma wood-frame + tin-roof stock** that did not survive 2017 is the single largest extant structural-risk concentration.
  - **Pre-Irma stock**: any pre-2017 wood-frame structure that survived Irma was either (a) sheltered by topography, (b) retrofitted post-Irma, or (c) likely still vulnerable — verify per parcel structural audit before purchase.
- **Earthquake**: Caribbean tectonic plate margin — **low-to-moderate** seismicity at SX (Leeward Islands seismic zone). Modern construction is designed to a hurricane-loading envelope that incidentally covers most seismic loading.
- **Flood**: limited topographic catchments; flash flooding possible in low-lying coastal + valley areas during tropical-cyclone rainbands. Cupecoy / Maho / Simpson Bay lowlands + the Salt Pond rim are exposed.
- **Tsunami**: low residual risk per CDEMA Caribbean tsunami hazard mapping — verify per coastal-parcel elevation.
- **Sea-level rise + coastal erosion**: material on multi-decade horizon for beachfront parcels (Cupecoy, Mullet Bay, Dawn Beach); coastal-setback regulation evolving — verify with VROMI building permit office.
- **Volcanic ash deposition**: low residual risk from Soufrière Hills (Montserrat, ~250 km SE) — sporadic ash advisories.

### Build-era hazards

| Era | Hazards |
|---|---|
| **Pre-1995 wood-frame + tin-roof** | Hurricane-resistance: LOW — most pre-Irma stock either destroyed 2017 or not yet retrofitted; lead-paint residual, asbestos in older 1960s–70s additions |
| **1995–2017 concrete-block, basic** | Hurricane-resistance: MODERATE — varies widely; many had partial roof failure in Irma |
| **Post-Irma rebuild / new build 2018+** | Hurricane-resistance: HIGH (where compliant with NRRP Build Back Better — impact-glazed, shuttered, reinforced-concrete) |
| **Luxury post-Irma (Cupecoy / Dawn Beach 2019–2026)** | Hurricane-resistance: HIGH; insurability + premium materially better |

- **Sea-level rise**: +0.2–0.5 m by 2050; +0.5–1.2 m by 2100 — material for beachfront parcels

- **NV GEBE — N.V. GEBE (Gemeenschappelijk Elektriciteitsbedrijf Bovenwindse Eilanden)**: `https://nvgebe.com/` — sole national utility for **electricity + drinking water + (limited) cable TV** in Sint Maarten + Saba + Sint Eustatius. Combined-utility model (no separate water board).
- **Telecom / fibre**: incumbent **TelEm** (`https://www.telem.sx/` — state-owned), with **UTS / Flow / Digicel** competing on mobile + some fixed.
- **Sewer**: limited centralised network (Philipsburg core + some new-build sub-divisions); **septic tank dominant** for most residential parcels — verify per parcel.
- **2026 oversight shift**: NV GEBE concession + tariffs newly under **BTP (Bureau Telecommunication and Post)** structured oversight per **National Gazette decree 22 May 2026** (first structured oversight in 15+ years) — tariff-setting + fuel-clause auditing now under regulator review.

### Verification

- **Most populated urban + suburban areas**: GEBE mains (electricity + water) reach virtually all populated zones — individual verification typically needed only for remote hilltop or new-subdivision parcels
- **Septic vs mains sewer**: assume septic unless verified otherwise — request septic-tank capacity + last-pump-out record
- **Connection certificate**: request from GEBE + VROMI at sale

### Costs (est., USD, 2026)

| Scenario | Cost |
|---|---:|
| GEBE electricity + water new connection (standard residential) | 500–1,500 |
| GEBE deposit (residential, new account) | 200–500 (returnable) |
| FTTH / cable internet activation (TelEm / Flow) | 0–150 (often promo) |
| Septic tank installation (new parcel) | 4,000–10,000 |
| LPG cistern (for cooking; piped gas not universal) | 1,500–4,000 |

⚠️ **Electricity tariff sensitivity**: GEBE tariffs include a **fuel clause** indexed to international fuel prices — typical monthly electricity bill for a 2-bed AC-cooled condo runs **US$200–US$450/mo** depending on AC usage. Tariff regulation under BTP oversight is the active reform (2026-05); fuel-clause surplus of ~12.6M ANG (2022–2024) identified by BTP audit *(per smn-news.com / 721news 2026-05)*. ## Section: `--currency`

### Currency status

- **Caribbean guilder (XCG)** — launched **31 March 2025** by CBCS, replacing the Netherlands Antillean guilder (ANG) at **1:1** conversion in Sint Maarten + Curaçao
- **Peg**: **1.79 XCG = 1 USD** (same as legacy ANG peg, in place since 1971) — administrative peg held by CBCS
- **USD broadly accepted in parallel** for retail, rent, real-estate listings + most professional services; legal-tender remains XCG for government + statutory items
- Cross-border: SX has no exchange controls on USD movement; AML reporting thresholds under CBCS rules

### What this means for buyers

- **USD-denominated buyers**: **near-zero FX risk** for purchase + ongoing payments (peg holds; redenomination via legal-tender ANG → XCG operationally complete 2025–2026)
- **EUR / GBP / CAD / non-USD buyers**: full home-currency / USD exposure — depth available via SX banks routing through US correspondents; use forward contracts for transaction lock-in on material deals
- **Peg credibility**: 55+ years of stability under ANG; XCG inherits the same peg + CBCS administrative regime — high credibility but ultimately a managed peg, not a market rate

### Operational note

- All SX banks + the *notaris* will use the **CBCS reference rate** (1.79 XCG = 1 USD) for closing-day currency conversion if the deed is denominated in XCG while funds arrive in USD
- Many residential closings are negotiated in USD throughout; the deed may reference USD with XCG-equivalent or USD outright — verify the *koopovereenkomst* (purchase agreement) currency clause

## Section: `--visa`

Universal logic per `shared/visa-programs.md`. Sint Maarten-specific:

### First-Time Investor Permit (residency-by-investment-equivalent)

- **Authority**: Ministry of Justice — Immigration and Border Protection Service (A.Th. Illidge Road #8, Philipsburg)
- **Official page**: `https://www.sintmaartengov.org/services/pages/First-Time-Investor-Permit.aspx`
- **Headline contribution**: **Naf 50,000 (~US$27,900 at 1.79 peg)** payment to the **Crime Combating Fund Sint Maarten** account (this is a one-off contribution, not the investment threshold itself)
- **Investment threshold (per secondary sources)**: intention to invest **~ANG 900,000 (~US$500,000)** in a business or real estate, fulfilled within 18 months *(per BrightPath Caribbean + walshww.com 2026 summaries — verify currency at the official Immigration office)*. A separate path frequently cited by real-estate-marketing literature uses a **US$255,000 real-estate floor** as a more accessible entry point — treat as **secondary-source claim; verify currency with Immigration directly**.
- **Required documents** *(per `sintmaartengov.org/services/pages/First-Time-Investor-Permit.aspx`, verbatim)*: passport copy (all pages, picture page in color); original police record ≤6 months old, translated to Dutch/English; marriage certificate (legalised/apostille, ≤3 years); birth certificate (legalised/apostille, ≤3 years); proof of current address; proof of income; proof of payment of Naf 50,000 Crime Combating Fund contribution
- **Process gating**: **"All first-time applicants must await the decision of their residency application abroad and not on the island"** — verbatim from the official page. Permit fee is non-refundable.

### Dutch-American Friendship Treaty (DAFT, 1956) — preferential US-citizen route

- US citizens benefit from preferential entrepreneur / investor treatment under the **1956 Dutch-American Friendship Treaty** — materially lower capital threshold (commonly cited **~US$20,000**) vs the non-treaty Naf 900,000 floor *(per BrightPath / walshww — verify with Immigration)*
- This is the **single largest foreign-buyer category quirk** for SX — US persons have a structurally easier residency-by-business route than non-US foreigners

### NOT a citizenship-by-investment jurisdiction

- ⚠️ Sint Maarten has **NEVER operated a CBI / golden-passport scheme**. Naturalisation requires Dutch nationality acquisition under Kingdom-wide rules — typically **5+ years lawful residence + Dutch civic-integration test + language requirement**
- Confusingly some marketing literature conflates SX with St Kitts (`kn` — has CBI) or Antigua (`ag` — has CBI) — these are **DIFFERENT JURISDICTIONS**. SX is NL Kingdom + no CBI

### Confidence

MEDIUM-HIGH for the Naf 50,000 Crime Combating Fund contribution + document list (primary-verified at sintmaartengov.org); MEDIUM for the Naf 900,000 / US$500,000 investment threshold + US$255,000 real-estate floor (secondary-source level; verify current quantum at Immigration directly).

## Section: `--notary`

### Process

- **Civil-law notary** (*notaris*) mandatory for all RE transfers — Sint Maarten notarial profession is small (handful of practising *notarissen*); profession governed by Dutch-tradition notarial law transposed for SX
- *Notaris* role: drafts the *koopovereenkomst* (purchase agreement) + *notariële akte van levering* (notarial deed of delivery); verifies identity (including apostilled passport for non-residents); confirms title via Kadaster *eigendomsbewijs* search; withholds + remits **4% overdrachtsbelasting**; files the deed at Kadaster Sint Maarten
- Either Dutch or English deed-drafting is acceptable; English is operational reality for foreign-buyer transactions

### Standard timeline (typical residential resale)

| Step | Day | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1. *Koopovereenkomst* (purchase agreement) | D0 | Buyer pays **deposit ~10%** held in *notaris* escrow |
| 2. *Bedenktijd* (cooling-off) | D0–D3 | 3-day buyer cooling-off (Dutch-tradition) — verify SX-specific application |
| 3. Mortgage approval (if applicable) | D14–D45 | Bank valuation + offer letter |
| 4. Kadaster title search + *meetbrief* | D14–D40 | Notaris requests pre-deed |
| 5. *Levering* (deed signing) at *notaris* | D45–D75 | Buyer pays balance; notaris withholds overdrachtsbelasting |
| 6. Kadaster lodgment | D75–D90 | Notaris files; title perfected |

### Costs

| Item | Typical |
|---|---|
| **Notaris fee** | ~1–1.5% of price (verify per *notaris*) |
| ***Overdrachtsbelasting*** | **4%** flat |
| **Kadaster registration** | Small fixed + small ad-valorem (verify current schedule) |
| **Total transaction cost (buyer side, cash)** | **~5–6%** of price |
| **Total transaction cost (buyer side, mortgaged)** | **~6–8%** (above + mortgage stamp + bank fees) |

### Foreign buyer practical

- **Power of attorney** acceptable: notaris accepts apostilled PoA for absent buyers
- **Source-of-funds**: notaris + bank both apply AML/CRS reviews under CBCS rules
- **Beneficial-ownership disclosure**: corporate buyers (BV) register UBO with Sint Maarten Chamber of Commerce
- **Closing documents in English** typically for foreign-buyer transactions; Dutch version may be required for some statutory filings

## Section: `--integrity`

### Sint Maarten-specific integrity flags

- 🔴 **Real-estate-developer fund-opacity (Reddit thread r/SXM `1koayw9`, "Warning about Real Estate Developer Tarnishing SXM's Reputation")** — owner-rep / developer-fund-opacity pattern documented in the ROADMAP (Batch C deferred-pending-demand prior to this playbook). Pattern: developer takes deposits + progress payments without clear escrow segregation; project completion + handover delays; investor recourse limited. **Mitigation**: require **notaris-held escrow** for all pre-completion payments; demand **stage-payment milestones tied to independent surveyor sign-off**; verify developer track record across at least 2 prior completed projects; check Sint Maarten Chamber of Commerce + Court of First Instance (Common Court of Justice) for prior litigation.
- 🟠 **Pre-Irma stock disclosure asymmetry** — sellers + agents have economic incentive to under-disclose pre-2017 structural compromise (partial roof failure, post-Irma patch repairs without full retrofit). **Mitigation**: require an **independent structural engineer post-Irma resilience assessment** for any pre-2017 build; verify against insurer claims history.
- 🟠 **HOA reserve-fund opacity** — multi-unit condo HOAs (Cupecoy / Maho / Dawn Beach belt) can carry undisclosed special-assessment exposure for post-Irma rebuild + common-element repair. **Mitigation**: require **3 years HOA financials + minutes + special-assessment history** before signing.
- 🟡 **Statutory-vs-enforced tax confusion** — second-hand foreign-investor marketing literature routinely quotes a "0.3% property tax" figure that is **statutorily on the books but not actually enforced** for decades (per Minister of Finance, 16 Feb 2026). **Mitigation**: confirm current enforcement directly with Sint Maarten Tax Administration (`https://www.tax.sx/`) before relying on any quoted property-tax cost in pro-forma cashflow.
- 🟡 **Currency-transition (ANG → XCG) deed-language drift** — deeds spanning the 31 March 2025 transition may reference ANG, XCG, or USD inconsistently. **Mitigation**: insist the *koopovereenkomst* + *notariële akte* explicitly state currency + the CBCS peg (1.79 = 1 USD) used for closing-day conversion.
- 🟡 **SX vs MAF conflation in listings** — cross-border platforms list both halves of the island; foreign buyers can confuse the legal/tax regime applying to a specific parcel. **Mitigation**: verify the **Kadaster Sint Maarten registration** confirms the parcel is on the Dutch side; if French-side, this playbook does NOT apply (see FR overlay).
- 🟡 **CBI conflation** — marketing literature occasionally conflates Sint Maarten with St Kitts (`kn`) or Antigua (`ag`) CBI programmes. **SX has NO CBI**; naturalisation is via Dutch Kingdom rules + 5+ years residence. Treat any "Sint Maarten passport-by-investment" claim as a red flag.

### Confidence

HIGH for the integrity flag set (Reddit-documented developer pattern + tax-enforcement reality confirmed by Minister of Finance + currency-transition is CBCS-primary).

## Section: `--language`

Universal logic per `shared/language.md` + `shared/foreign-language-preservation.md`. SX-specific:

- **Official languages**: **Dutch + English**
- **Daily commerce + real-estate operations**: predominantly **English**
- **Notarial deeds**: drafted in either Dutch or English (notaris discretion); English is operational reality for foreign-buyer deals

## Section: `--relocation`

Universal logic per `shared/relocation.md`. SX-specific:
