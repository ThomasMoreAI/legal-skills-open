# Barbados 🇧🇧 — Property Due-Diligence Playbook

ISO2: `bb`. Status: ✅ Fully populated (researched 2026-05-07).

> **Critical framing — read first**: Barbados is an **English common-law island state** in the south-eastern Caribbean (outside the main hurricane track but still inside the Atlantic basin). The currency **BBD is pegged 2:1 to USD since 5 July 1975** — confirmed by [Central Bank of Barbados (CBB)](https://www.centralbank.org.bb/) and unbroken through 2026. **Foreign buyers face NO ownership restriction**: non-residents may buy freehold residential property on the same terms as Barbadians, **subject to** mandatory **registration of incoming foreign currency with the Central Bank of Barbados** (this is the gating step — without registered FX, capital and gains cannot be repatriated on resale). Property tax stack: **annual Land Tax** (banded, owner-occupied 0–1.0% on improved value, capped at BBD 100,000), **one-time Property Transfer Tax (PTT)** of **2.5%** on consideration above **BBD 150,000** (where a building exists; vacant land has no exemption), **one-time Stamp Duty** of **1%** on the gross consideration paid by the **seller**, and **17.5% VAT** on professional services (legal, agency) — but **VAT does NOT apply** to the property sale itself (real estate is VAT-exempt). **Citizenship-by-investment: NONE** (distinguish from St. Kitts, Dominica, Antigua & Barbuda, St. Lucia, Grenada). Residency routes are the **Welcome Stamp** (12-month digital-nomad visa, **USD 50,000+ income threshold**, renewable, tax-exempt on Barbados-sourced income from foreign employment — **renewed by Cabinet to 31 Dec 2026**) and the **Special Entry & Reside Permit (SERP)** (5-year renewable; Category 2 property-owner threshold **USD 300,000**). **Hurricane risk**: Barbados sits at **13.1°N, 59.5°W** — the easternmost Caribbean island, **south of the main Atlantic hurricane track** — but **Hurricane Beryl (1 July 2024)** passed ~150 km south as a Cat 3, generating heavy swell, storm surge and damaging the south + south-west coasts (~BBD 193M ECLAC-assessed effects; per ECLAC 2025 (LC/TS.2025/45) **50 homes** damaged (21 severely damaged / collapsed / unsafe) + **~240 of 312 active fishing vessels** totally or partially damaged + Bridgetown Port + Fisheries Marina + breakwater + airport damage) (2026-09-24 verified, source: [ECLAC 2025 (LC/TS.2025/45) Beryl Assessment](https://www.cepal.org/en/publications/82157-assessment-effects-and-impacts-hurricane-beryl-barbados-2024)). The prior comparable pass was Hurricane Ivan (7 Sep 2004) ~165 km south of Barbados at 100-105 kt / Cat 3 (est., computed from NHC best track 11.6N 59.4W 100 kt at 1200 UTC and 11.8N 61.1W 105 kt at 1800 UTC vs Bridgetown 13.10N 59.62W), with >176 Barbados homes completely destroyed (NHC TCR AL092004); post-Beryl insurance re-pricing: data not publicly available — verify with carriers and FSC Barbados. **Confirm** any current PTT band, Land Tax assessment, FX-registration status, and Welcome Stamp / SERP eligibility at primary sources **before any binding step**. Authoritative primary sources: **[Barbados Revenue Authority (BRA)](https://bra.gov.bb/)**, **[Central Bank of Barbados (CBB)](https://www.centralbank.org.bb/)**, **[Barbados Statistical Service (BSS)](https://stats.gov.bb)**, **[Immigration Department](https://immigration.gov.bb/)**, **[Lands and Surveys Department](https://www.landsandsurveys.gov.bb/)**, **[Invest Barbados](https://www.investbarbados.org/)**.

## Country profile

- **Geography**: Single coral-limestone island, **~432 km²**, easternmost of the Caribbean island chain (separate from the volcanic Lesser Antilles arc). Capital **Bridgetown** (St. Michael parish). 11 parishes: **St. Michael** (capital, Bridgetown), **Christ Church** (south coast — DNV / retirement / Grantley Adams International Airport), **St. James** (west coast — luxury "Platinum Coast"), **St. Peter** (north-west coast — Speightstown, luxury), **St. Lucy** (north), **St. Andrew** (north-east — Scotland District), **St. Joseph** (east), **St. John** (east), **St. Philip** (south-east), **St. George** (interior), **St. Thomas** (interior).
- **Population**: **~282,500** (2024, World Bank — verify at [Barbados Statistical Service](https://stats.gov.bb)). Population near-flat / mildly declining; 2010 Census ~277,800. Fertility below replacement; net migration slightly negative; ageing demographic.
- **GDP per capita**: **~USD 26,500 nominal** (2024, World Bank) / **~USD 22,700 PPP** (2024, IMF — verify at [IMF DataMapper Barbados](https://www.imf.org/external/datamapper/profile/BRB)). Tourism + offshore financial services + ICT dominate; tourism is the single largest economic driver (direct + indirect — share figure not independently sourced here; verify magnitude at [CBB](https://www.centralbank.org.bb/) or WTTC).
- **Currency**: **BBD (Barbados Dollar — Bds$ / B$)** — **pegged 2:1 to USD since 5 July 1975** ([CBB — Fixed for Forty-One Years](https://www.centralbank.org.bb/news/general-press-release/fixed-for-forty-one-years), 2016 anniversary post). **USD widely accepted** in tourism + west-coast luxury property; high-end deals (Sandy Lane, Royal Westmoreland, Apes Hill, Port Ferdinand, Port St. Charles) typically **quoted USD-firm**; mid-market local listings **BBD-priced**. **Exchange Control Regulations** under the **Exchange Control Act, Cap. 71** require **registration of incoming foreign currency** with the [CBB Foreign Exchange Department](https://www.centralbank.org.bb/foreign-exchange) for non-resident property purchase — the registration is **the legal gateway for future repatriation of capital + gains**.
- **Languages**: **English (official, sole)** — all contracts, deeds, statutes in English. Bajan Creole (Bajan dialect) is the everyday vernacular but legal/professional life is standard English.
- **Legal system**: **English common law** + Barbadian statutes, with the **Caribbean Court of Justice (CCJ)** as the final court of appeal since 2005 (Barbados was a founding member; replaced the Privy Council). **Conveyancing**: a **dual system** — most parcels still trade on the older **deeds-recording / abstract-of-title** model (chain-of-deeds at the Land Registry under the Property Act, Cap. 236); the **Title Registration Act, Cap. 229 (1979)** introduced an opt-in **Torrens-style "Certificate of Title" system** but adoption has been slow — most older parcels remain on the deeds-recording system. The **Land (Title Proceedings) Act 2011-7** modernised the judicial path to register clouded titles.
- **Admin levels**: **11 parishes** (no parish councils since 1969 — central government administers; parish boundaries used mainly for ecclesiastical, electoral, and address-identification purposes). Bridgetown is the only municipality.
- **Cadastre / land registry**:
  - **[Lands and Surveys Department (LSD)](https://www.landsandsurveys.gov.bb/)** — Crown-land records, official survey plans, cadastral maps; under the Ministry of Housing, Lands & Maintenance.
  - **Land Registry / Registration Office** — deed-recording office for conveyances, mortgages, and Title-Registration-Act applications. Public-search counter in Bridgetown.
  - **[Barbados Revenue Authority — Land Tax Division](https://bra.gov.bb/About/Tax-Types/Land-Tax/)** — annual valuation roll for Land Tax purposes; valuation references inform but do not equal market value.
- **Identifier**: **Parish + plan reference + lot number** + (where applicable) **Certificate of Title number** under the Title Registration Act 1979. **No universal national parcel ID** equivalent to a French numéro de parcelle.
- **Title concept**: **Freehold (fee simple)** dominant; **leasehold** less common (some Crown leases + commercial leasehold); **strata title / condominium** under the **Condominium Act, Cap. 224A** for apartment / villa-development common-area regimes.
- **Recent reforms (12-month watchlist)**:
  - **Welcome Stamp renewed by Cabinet to 31 December 2026** (the 12-month digital-nomad visa launched June 2020 — among the earliest DNV programs globally). Verify current status at [Welcome Stamp official portal — Visit Barbados](https://www.visitbarbados.org/barbados-welcome-stamp).
  - **Personal Income Tax reduction for income year 2026 — IN FORCE**: 12.5% → **11.5%** on BBD 25,001–75,000; 28.5% → **27.5%** above BBD 75,000 (BBD 25,000 personal allowance unchanged); combined relief ~BBD 26.1M/yr. 2025 schedule remained 12.5%/28.5% with BBD 25,000 allowance (2026-05-27 verified, source: [BRA Policy Note 20 Apr 2026](https://bra.gov.bb/News/Policy-Notes/Reduction-of-Personal-Income-Tax-R); KPMG TaxNewsFlash Mar 2026).
  - **Resilience and Regeneration Fund** employee contribution **raised to 0.25% (from 0.10%)** of gross earnings effective **1 April 2025** ([2025 Budget](https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmg/bb/pdf/Barbados%20budget%20review%202025.pdf)).
  - **2025 Budget**: **PTT removed for deeds of gift** to children/dependants for land < 1 acre (gifts only — does not affect arm's-length sales) ([KPMG TaxNewsFlash 2025-03](https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmg/bb/pdf/Barbados%20budget%20review%202025.pdf)).
  - **Hurricane Beryl (1 July 2024)** passed ~150 km south of Barbados as Cat 3 — the prior comparable pass was Hurricane Ivan (7 Sep 2004) ~165 km south of Barbados at 100-105 kt / Cat 3 (est., computed from NHC best track 11.6N 59.4W 100 kt at 1200 UTC and 11.8N 61.1W 105 kt at 1800 UTC vs Bridgetown 13.10N 59.62W); post-Beryl insurance re-rating: data not publicly available — verify at [FSC Barbados](https://www.fsc.gov.bb/); ECLAC-assessed effects ~BBD 193M (~0.15% of GDP); damage concentrated south + south-west coasts + fishing infrastructure ([ECLAC, 2025 — LC/TS.2025/45](https://www.cepal.org/en/publications/82157-assessment-effects-and-impacts-hurricane-beryl-barbados-2024)).
  - **Land Tax (Rate of Tax) Order, 2024** consolidated current bands ([S.I. 2024 No. 57 — BRA PDF](https://bra.gov.bb/attachment?file=Attachments%2FLand+Tax+%28Rate+of+Tax%29+Order%2C+2024.pdf&name=Land+Tax+%28Rate+of+Tax%29+Order%2C+2024)).
  - **Republic transition** (30 November 2021): Barbados became a parliamentary republic; King replaced by President as ceremonial head of state. **No effect on property law** (Crown references in older statutes read as references to the State).

## 🇧🇧 Foreign buyer eligibility (READ FIRST)

This section determines whether a transaction is even legally possible — and what process applies. Barbados operates **one of the most foreign-buyer-friendly regimes in the Caribbean** for residential real estate; the gating mechanism is **FX registration with the Central Bank**, not an ownership restriction.

### Legal basis

- **Property Act, Cap. 236** — general property and conveyancing law (English common-law derivation).
- **Title Registration Act, Cap. 229 (1979)** — opt-in Torrens-style registered-title system.
- **Land (Title Proceedings) Act, 2011-7** — judicial title-quieting modernisation.
- **Condominium Act, Cap. 224A** — strata-title regime.
- **Exchange Control Act, Cap. 71** — administered by [Central Bank of Barbados (CBB)](https://www.centralbank.org.bb/foreign-exchange/exchange-control-guide); governs registration of incoming foreign currency for non-resident property purchase.
- **Property Transfer Tax Act, Cap. 84A** + **Stamp Duty Act, Cap. 91** + **Land Tax Act, Cap. 78A** — transaction + holding taxes (Land Tax Act, Cap.78A on the [BRA legislation index](https://bra.gov.bb/About/Legislation-Regulations); Cap. 84A text at the [Office of the Attorney General](https://oag.gov.bb/attachments/Property%20Transfer%20Tax%20(Amendment)%20Act,%202018-45.pdf), Cap. 91 + Cap. 84A also listed at [CAIPO](https://caipo.gov.bb/legislation)).
- **Immigration Act, Cap. 190** + **Welcome Stamp regulations** + **Special Entry & Reside Permit (SERP) framework** — residency routes.

### Buyer category matrix

| Buyer category | Right |
|---|---|
| **Barbadian citizen** | Full freehold; standard conveyancing |
| **Permanent resident with citizenship-equivalent rights** | Full freehold; standard conveyancing |
| **Non-resident foreign buyer (individual or company)** | **Full freehold on equal footing**, subject to **CBB Exchange Control registration** of the incoming purchase funds. Registration is **administrative**, not gatekeeping in normal cases — but it is the legal precondition for repatriating capital + gains on resale. |
| **Welcome Stamp holder** | Treated as non-resident for tax + Exchange Control purposes; full freehold rights as above |
| **SERP holder (Category 2 property-owner)** | Full freehold; Cat. 2 threshold USD 300,000+ property value |

(Source: [CBB Exchange Control Guide](https://www.centralbank.org.bb/foreign-exchange/exchange-control-guide) + [Invest Barbados — Tax Rates](https://www.investbarbados.org/barbados-tax-rates/) + [Withers — Owning Property in Barbados](https://www.withersworldwide.com/en-gb/insight/read/owning-property-in-barbados-taxation-and-expenses) — secondary tier; *verify with attorney before contract*)

### Visa / residency via property — Welcome Stamp + SERP

| Route | Threshold (verify at Immigration Department) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Welcome Stamp** (12-month DNV) | **USD 50,000 minimum annual income**, **employed/contracted outside Barbados**, mandatory health insurance | Individual fee **USD 2,000**; family fee **USD 3,000**; 12-month renewable; renewals receive 25% discount; **tax-exempt on Barbados-sourced presence for foreign-employment income**; processing ~7 business days; programme renewed by Cabinet to **31 Dec 2026** (verify at [Welcome Stamp portal](https://www.visitbarbados.org/barbados-welcome-stamp)) |
| **SERP — Category 1 (high-net-worth)** | **USD 2M Barbados investment + USD 5M+ net worth** | Renewable 5-year permit; auto work-permit fee USD 15,000 (60+) or USD 20,000 (under 60) |
| **SERP — Category 2 (property owner)** | **USD 300,000+ property value** | Renewable 5-year residence permit; no automatic right to work |
| **SERP — Category 3 (special skills)** | Skills critical to Barbadian growth | Case-by-case |
| **SERP — Category 4 (parent/grandparent of Barbadian)** | 60+ age + Barbadian descendant | Family-reunification route |
| **Standard Permanent Residence** | 5+ years lawful residence + good character | Non-investment route; multi-year |
| **Citizenship by Investment (CBI)** | **NONE** | Barbados does NOT operate a CBI scheme. Naturalisation requires 5+ years' residence + character + Bajan-history test under the Barbados Citizenship Act, Cap. 186 |
| **Tax residency** | 182+ days physical presence OR domicile | [BRA](https://bra.gov.bb/) determines; relevant for treaty + worldwide-income scope |

(Source: [Immigration Department — SEP](https://immigration.gov.bb/pages/SpecialEntryPermit.aspx) + [Welcome Stamp portal](https://www.visitbarbados.org/barbados-welcome-stamp) + [Invest Barbados](https://www.investbarbados.org/) — *verify current thresholds + processing SLA before relying*)

### Compliance / AML

- **Money Laundering and Financing of Terrorism (Prevention and Control) Act 2011** + amendments: AML framework.
- **Real-estate brokers + attorneys**: regulated as **Designated Non-Financial Businesses and Professions (DNFBPs)** with KYC + suspicious-transaction reporting obligations.
- **Source-of-funds documentation** required for all property purchases; bank-statement chain back to legitimate source.
- **CRS + FATCA**: tax-residency self-certification mandatory at Barbadian bank account opening.
- **Beneficial-ownership disclosure**: under the Companies Act + AML framework; ultimate UBO disclosed for corporate-held real estate.

### Verification path (before any reservation)

1. **Confirm CBB Exchange Control registration plan**: attorney + authorised dealer (commercial bank) prepare the credit advice + supporting docs for CBB registration of incoming USD/EUR/GBP funds.
2. **Pull the title chain**: 30+ years' chain of conveyances if deeds-recording, OR Certificate of Title extract if registered under the Title Registration Act 1979.
3. **LSD survey check**: confirm boundaries match deed description; coastal-setback compliance under the Coastal Zone Management Act.
4. **BRA Land Tax confirmation**: confirm Land Tax account current + no arrears (Land Tax can become a charge on title).
5. **Welcome Stamp / SERP eligibility filings** (if residency-linked): Immigration Department application before or alongside completion.
6. **AML / source-of-funds**: bank statements + employment / asset-sale evidence ready before signing reservation.

- **Inland / east-coast rural**: thin volume; pricing highly individual.

### Compute

1. **USD/m² = price ÷ built-area m²** (listings often quote **square feet** — convert: 1 m² = 10.764 sqft). Caribbean condition.
2. Compare to **≥ 3 active listings** on the same parish + community + waterfront-status (community + waterfront-status drive price more than parish average).
3. **Listing convention**: most west-coast luxury listings quote **USD-firm**; mid-market local listings often **BBD-priced** (peg 2:1 means USD-equivalent is the ÷2 conversion).
4. **Trap — area definitions**: built-up area vs gross area (incl. covered patios) vs lot area. Listings often quote the largest. Confirm with strata plan or surveyor's plan.
5. **Trap — coastal setback**: Coastal Zone Management Act setback restrictions (typically 30–50 m from high-water mark depending on coastal class) limit buildable area; verify with [Coastal Zone Management Unit](https://www.coastal.gov.bb/).
6. **Trap — title-system status**: Title Registration Act 1979 (Certificate of Title) parcels are easier + faster to convey than deeds-recording parcels. Verify status at the Land Registry.
7. **Trap — Beryl 2024 impact**: insurance availability + premium are now meaningful price-discriminators; 3+ insurance quotes pre-contract is now mandatory practice.

### Key terms

- **Conveyance / Deed of Conveyance** — the instrument transferring title (English-style)
- **Abstract of Title** — chain of conveyances at the Land Registry (deeds-recording system)
- **Certificate of Title** — Title Registration Act 1979 Torrens-style alternative
- **Land Tax** — annual property tax on improved/site value (banded)
- **Property Transfer Tax (PTT)** — one-time 2.5% on consideration > BBD 150,000 (where building exists)
- **Stamp Duty** — one-time 1% on consideration; paid by seller statutorily
- **Strata title** — condominium common-area regime under Cap. 224A
- **Coastal Zone Management** — setback + buildable-area regime under Cap. 394 (Coastal Zone Management Act)

- **ABC Highway** (Adams-Barrow-Cummins, the orbital ring road) carries the bulk of cross-island commuter traffic; peak congestion **7:00–9:00am, 4:00–6:30pm** weekdays; bottlenecks at Garrison roundabout + Warrens roundabout + Sheraton.
- **Highway 1** (west-coast tourist spine — Bridgetown ⇄ Speightstown via Holetown): **tourist-season peak Dec–Apr** materially above shoulder; Holetown-village stretch frequently slow.
- **Highway 7** (south-coast tourist spine — Bridgetown ⇄ Oistins): heavy peak; St. Lawrence Gap nightly entertainment-traffic spike.
- **Cruise-day surge**: Bridgetown port can disembark 8,000+ passengers per peak day; affects walkability + waterfront frontages on cruise calendar.
- **Crop Over (Jul–Aug)**: Kadooment Day + grand kadooment route closures — major traffic disruption to St. Michael + Bridgetown.

## Section: `--tax`

> **Headline**: Barbados has **moderate personal income tax** (**11.5% / 27.5%** bands with BBD 25,000 personal allowance, income year 2026 — reduced from 12.5% / 28.5%), **NO capital gains tax**, **NO inheritance tax**, **NO general wealth tax**. Real-estate-specific taxes: **annual Land Tax** (banded, owner-occupied 0–1.0% on improved value, capped at BBD 100,000 total annual tax), **one-time Property Transfer Tax** (2.5% on consideration > BBD 150,000 where a building exists), **one-time Stamp Duty** (1% on consideration, paid by seller), and **17.5% VAT** on professional services associated with the transaction (legal, agency) — but **VAT does NOT apply to the sale of real estate itself** (Cap. 87 Schedule 1 exemption).

### Annual property tax — Land Tax

Governed by the **Land Tax Act, Cap. 78A** + the **Land Tax (Rate of Tax) Order, 2024** ([S.I. 2024 No. 57 — BRA PDF](https://bra.gov.bb/attachment?file=Attachments%2FLand+Tax+%28Rate+of+Tax%29+Order%2C+2024.pdf&name=Land+Tax+%28Rate+of+Tax%29+Order%2C+2024)). Administered by the [BRA](https://bra.gov.bb/About/Tax-Types/Land-Tax/).

**Residential improved property** — graduated bands on **improved value**:

| Improved value (BBD) | Annual Land Tax rate |
|---|---|
| First **$150,000** | **0%** (exempt) |
| **$150,001 – $450,000** | **0.10%** |
| **$450,001 – $850,000** | **0.70%** |
| **Above $850,000** | **1.0%** (capped at **BBD 100,000 total annual tax** above this band) |

**Residential unimproved (vacant) land** — site-value rates:

| Site value / lot size (BBD) | Annual Land Tax rate |
|---|---|
| **< 4,000 sqft (≈371 m²)** | **0.80%** of site value |
| **> 4,000 sqft, value up to $450,000** | **0.90%** of site value |
| **> 4,000 sqft, value > $450,000** | **1.0%** of site value |

**Non-residential (commercial/other)**: **0.95%** of improved value (no exemption).

(Source: [BRA Land Tax — How is Land Tax calculated](https://bra.gov.bb/attachment?file=Attachments%2FLand+Tax+%28Rate+of+Tax%29+Order%2C+2024.pdf&name=Land+Tax+%28Rate+of+Tax%29+Order%2C+2024) + Land Tax (Rate of Tax) Order 2024 — *VERIFY current bands at BRA before relying; bands apply 2024–2026 fiscal year per S.I. 2024 No. 57 — rates may change at next budget*)

**Filing + payment**:
- **Annual valuation** by BRA Valuation Department; **2025–2026 Land Tax bills issued 12 May 2025** ([BRA press release](https://bra.gov.bb/News/Press-Releases/BRA-RELEASES-2025-2026-LAND-TAX-BI)).
- **Discounts for early payment**:
  - In-person by **11 June 2025**: **10% discount**
  - In-person by **11 July 2025**: **5% discount**
  - Online via Land Tax portal through **10 August 2025**: **10% discount**
- **Late-payment**: surcharge + interest accrual.
- **Lien**: unpaid Land Tax can become a charge on the property; transfers blocked until cleared.
- **Pensioner concession**: Barbadian residents 60+ may qualify for a Land Tax rebate on owner-occupied residence (verify current concession at BRA).

### Property Transfer Tax (PTT) — one-time at sale

Governed by the **Property Transfer Tax Act, Cap. 84A** ([statute id + amending Act at the Office of the Attorney General](https://oag.gov.bb/attachments/Property%20Transfer%20Tax%20(Amendment)%20Act,%202018-45.pdf); also listed at [CAIPO](https://caipo.gov.bb/legislation)). Administered by [BRA](https://bra.gov.bb/).

| Transaction | PTT rate |
|---|---|
| **Sale of land WITH a building** | **2.5%** of consideration **above BBD 150,000** (first BBD 150,000 exempt) |
| **Sale of vacant land (no building)** | **2.5%** of full consideration (NO exemption) |
| **Sale of long-term lease** | 2.5% (per Cap. 84A) |
| **Sale of private-company shares** | 2.5% above BBD 50,000 |
| **Stock-Exchange-listed shares** | Exempt |
| **2025 Budget — gift to children/dependants of land < 1 acre** | **PTT removed** ([2025 Budget](https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmg/bb/pdf/Barbados%20budget%20review%202025.pdf)) |

**Statutory liability**: **vendor pays PTT** (per Cap. 84A); commonly borne by vendor in practice.

(Source: [PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries — Barbados Other Taxes](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/barbados/corporate/other-taxes) + [BRA](https://bra.gov.bb/) + [Withers — Owning Property in Barbados](https://www.withersworldwide.com/en-gb/insight/read/owning-property-in-barbados-taxation-and-expenses) — *verify current threshold at BRA; threshold last revised by Stamp Duty/PTT amendment — confirm at signing*)

### Stamp Duty — one-time at sale

Governed by the **Stamp Duty Act, Cap. 91** (statute id cross-referenced on BRA's [Land Development Duty Act, Cap. 78](https://bra.gov.bb/About/Legislation-Regulations/Land-Development-Duty-Act-Cap-78) legislation page — "*Instruments of transfer are not deemed duly stamped under the Stamp Duty Act, Cap. 91…*"). Administered by [BRA](https://bra.gov.bb/).

- **Conveyance / sale of real estate**: **BBD 10 per BBD 1,000 (or part thereof)** = **1.0%** of gross consideration / fair market value (whichever higher).
- **Statutory liability + custom**: **vendor pays** (per Cap. 91 — buyer does NOT pay Stamp Duty on a conveyance under Barbadian practice).
- **Mortgage**: BBD 3 per BBD 500 = 0.6% of mortgage amount (paid by mortgagor — typically the buyer if financing).
- **Stamping deadline**: **30 days from execution** to avoid penalty.

(Source: [PwC — Other Taxes](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/barbados/corporate/other-taxes) + [Barbados Sotheby's — Buying & Selling Property](https://www.barbadossothebysrealty.com/sites/default/files/editoruploads/BuyingAndSellingPropertyInBarbados.pdf) — secondary; *verify with attorney's engagement letter*)

### VAT (general, on goods/services)

- **Standard rate**: **17.5%** (Barbados **Value Added Tax Act, Cap. 87** — verify text at Barbados Supreme Court / BRA) (2026-05-27 verified — corrected from prior erroneous "Cap. 349 Bahamas portal" reference).
- **Reduced rate**: **10%** for hotel + guest-house + tourism accommodation supplies.
- **Mobile services**: **22%**.
- **Real estate sale**: **VAT-EXEMPT** (Schedule 1 exemption — financial services, real estate, medical, education).
- **Property-related professional services** (legal, agency, valuation, surveyor): **17.5% VAT** added to the fee.
- **VAT on rental income**: long-term residential rental **VAT-exempt**; short-term tourism accommodation **10% reduced rate**.

### Personal income tax

| Income band (BBD gross, **income year 2026**) | Rate |
|---|---|
| Personal allowance | First **BBD 25,000** (exempt) |
| **BBD 25,001 – 75,000** | **11.5%** (was 12.5%) |
| **Above BBD 75,000** | **27.5%** (was 28.5%) |

(Source: [BRA Policy Note OGC No. 001/2026](https://bra.gov.bb/News/Policy-Notes/Reduction-of-Personal-Income-Tax-R) — the administering authority's own statement of the operative rates, announced in the 2026/2027 Budget on 16 March 2026. (2026-08-06 verified))

> **Read the bands on the right basis.** BRA states them on **gross** income (above BBD 25,000 → 11.5%; above BBD 75,000 → 27.5%). Statutory and practitioner sources often state the same schedule on **taxable** income after the BBD 25,000 allowance — 11.5% on the first BBD 50,000 of taxable income, 27.5% thereafter. These are the same schedule, not two different ones. The **2025** schedule was 12.5% / 28.5% on the same band structure.

- **Tax residency**: 182+ days physical presence OR domicile.
- **Welcome Stamp holders**: NOT tax-resident; **exempt from Barbadian income tax on foreign-employment income** earned during the Stamp.
- **Resilience and Regeneration Fund** employee contribution: **0.25%** of gross earnings (raised from 0.10% effective 1 April 2025).
- **National Insurance Scheme (NIS)**: est. ~10.1% employee + ~11.25% employer (rate year not established here — these are indicative, undated; **verify current rates + effective date at NIS** before relying on them).

### Capital gains / inheritance / wealth

- **NO capital gains tax** in Barbados (no separate CGT regime).
- **NO inheritance / estate tax**; **no gift tax** generally (PTT applies to transfers of real estate above thresholds — including by gift, except 2025-Budget exemption for gifts to children/dependants of land < 1 acre).
- **NO wealth tax**.

### Future risk

- **Personal Income Tax reduction** for income year 2026: **11.5%** (BBD 25,001–75,000) / **27.5%** (above BBD 75,000); BBD 25,000 allowance unchanged (2026-05-27 verified, source: BRA Policy Note 20 Apr 2026).
- **Land Tax bands** historically restated by S.I.; next revision plausible at 2026 / 2027 budget.
- **Hurricane levy / disaster-resilience charge** debated periodically post-Beryl 2024 — not enacted as of May 2026.
- **OECD Pillar Two Global Minimum Tax (15% CIT for in-scope MNEs)**: Barbados has implemented in line with OECD framework — affects MNEs with €750M+ consolidated revenue, NOT individual property owners.

## Section: `--rental`

### Long-term residential rentals

Governed by ordinary contract + the **Residential Tenancies Act, Cap. 230** + general English common-law landlord-tenant principles.

- **Standard tenancy**: 1-year fixed term, monthly payment, 1 month security deposit + 1 month advance rent typical.
- **Eviction**: court-supervised; 2–4 months typical for non-payment under the Residential Tenancies Act.
- **Long-term residential rental income**: **VAT-exempt**.
- **Income tax on rental income**: **resident landlord** taxed at standard PIT rates (11.5%/27.5% for income year 2026, on net rental income after allowable expenses); **non-resident landlord** subject to **withholding tax 25%** on gross rent (subject to applicable double-tax treaty — Barbados has DTAs with UK, USA, Canada, CARICOM, China and others).
- **Allowable deductions**: maintenance, mortgage interest, Land Tax, insurance, agent fees, depreciation per BRA practice.

### Short-term rentals (Vacation Rental — Airbnb / VRBO / direct)

- **Regulatory status**: **Vacation Rental Regulations** under the **Tourism Development Act**; rentals must be **registered with the Barbados Tourism Marketing Inc. (BTMI) / Barbados Tourism Investment Inc. (BTI)** + the **Hotels & Resorts Commission**.
- **VAT**: **10% reduced rate** on tourism accommodation supplies (vs 17.5% standard).
- **Room Rate Levy / Product Development Levy**: tourism levies under the Tourism Development Act apply to short-term accommodation; verify current rate at BRA.
- **HOA / strata reality**: in Sandy Lane, Royal Westmoreland, Apes Hill, Port St. Charles, Port Ferdinand, **HOA / Owners Association rules typically allow but tightly regulate short-term rentals** — read the Declaration / By-Laws **before** pricing a yield model.
- **Welcome Stamp + retirement demand** on the south coast (Christ Church) drives a documented **shift from short-term to long-term rentals 2024–2025** (Hannah Properties, Spot Blue, *secondary*) — long-term executive rentals to corporate + DNV + returnee tenants now competitive with STR yields.

### Strategic notes

- **Hurricane insurance is now a meaningful operating cost** post-Beryl 2024 — see `--risks`. Pre-Beryl Barbados enjoyed lower insurance premiums than BS / JM; post-Beryl re-rating: data not publicly available — verify with 3+ carrier quotes and FSC Barbados.

### Hurricane (Atlantic basin) — moderate, not absent

- **Position**: Barbados sits at **13.1°N, 59.5°W** — the easternmost Caribbean island, **south of the main Atlantic hurricane track** which typically curves north of Barbados toward Hispaniola/Bahamas/US east coast.
- **Season**: **1 June – 30 November**, peak **August–October**.
- **Historical reference**: **Hurricane Janet (Sep 1955)** — centre passed not far off the South Coast; 57 killed, 8,100 homes destroyed, 20,000 homeless (per ECLAC 2025 historical review). **Hurricane Ivan (7 Sep 2004)** — Cat 3 (100–105 kt) ~165 km south (est., computed from NHC best track vs Bridgetown 13.10N 59.62W); >176 homes completely destroyed (NHC TCR AL092004). **Tropical Storm Tomas (Oct 2010)** caused tree + roof damage. **Hurricane Beryl (1 July 2024)** — Cat 3, passed ~150 km south of Barbados; per ECLAC 2025 **50 homes** damaged (21 severely damaged / collapsed / unsafe); **~240 of 312 active fishing vessels** totally or partially damaged; Bridgetown Port + Fisheries Marina + breakwater + airport damage; ECLAC-assessed effects ~BBD 193M (~0.15% GDP) (2026-09-24 verified) ([ECLAC, 2025 — LC/TS.2025/45](https://www.cepal.org/en/publications/82157-assessment-effects-and-impacts-hurricane-beryl-barbados-2024)).
- **Implication**: Barbados is **lower hurricane risk than BS / JM / Cayman** but **NOT a no-risk jurisdiction**. Post-Beryl insurance re-pricing: data not publicly available — verify with Barbadian carriers and FSC Barbados.
- **Building code**: **Barbados National Building Code (BNBC)** — wind-zone standards apply; impact-rated glazing + reinforced roofing increasingly standard for post-2010 builds; pre-2002 stock often non-compliant with current wind ratings.

### Insurance — re-rating post-Beryl

- **Hurricane peril** bundled with all-risk fire; **CAT deductibles 2–5%** of sum insured + per-storm aggregate caps; **rates ~0.30–1.0% of insured value/yr** for ordinary residential pre-Beryl; **0.5–1.5%+ post-Beryl** (carrier-specific; *verify with 3+ quotes*).
- **Insurance market**: regulated by the **[Financial Services Commission (FSC)](https://www.fsc.gov.bb/)**; major carriers **Sagicor General Insurance**, **ICBL (Insurance Corporation of Barbados Ltd.)**, **BICO General Insurance**, **CGM Gallagher (broker)**, **NUA / Trident**.
- **Verification path**: get **3+ insurance quotes** for the specific parcel before contract; price the policy + deductible into the 5-year carrying-cost model.

### Coastal flood / storm surge

- **Coastal storm surge** is the principal acute coastal mechanism — Beryl's swell + surge concentrated damage on south + south-west coasts (St. Philip, Christ Church south, St. Michael south fishing villages).
- **Coastal Zone Management Act, Cap. 394** + **CZMU**: setback regulations (typically 30–50 m from high-water mark, varies by coastal class); buildable-area + drainage requirements.
- **Sea-level rise**: IPCC AR6 projects **+0.30 → +0.85 m by 2100 SSP5-8.5** for the eastern Caribbean; Barbados is a **coral platform**, average elevation modest but not as flat as BS; **highest natural point Mount Hillaby, St. Andrew, ~340 m AMSL**.
- **Climate Central Coastal Risk Screening Tool**: `https://coastal.climatecentral.org/` — useful for parcel-level SLR exposure scenario.

### Seismic — moderate (Caribbean plate margin)

- The Caribbean tectonic plate's eastern margin (subduction zone) lies east of the Lesser Antilles; **historical M6+ events** include the 1843 Antigua/Guadeloupe earthquake (felt in Barbados) and various 20th-century events.
- **Background seismicity**: monitored by [UWI SRC](https://www.uwiseismic.com/); Barbados is on the back-arc side of the Lesser Antilles — risk lower than St. Lucia / Martinique / Guadeloupe but not zero.
- **Practical**: standard structural review for pre-1980 builds; not a primary risk factor for modern builds.

### Volcanic — none direct

- Barbados has **no active volcano** (it is a coral-limestone platform, not an arc volcano like St. Lucia, St. Vincent, Martinique).
- **Ashfall risk**: La Soufrière (St. Vincent) 2021 eruption sent ash to Barbados; a recurring possible nuisance for property maintenance + air-quality.

### Tsunami

- Background Caribbean tsunami risk from far-field events (Hispaniola, Puerto Rico); locally generated tsunami risk LOW but non-zero given the eastern subduction margin.
- **Caribbean Tsunami Warning System** via NOAA + UWI SRC.

### Climate change projections (2050 horizon — IPCC AR6 + Caribbean Community Climate Change Centre + CSGM UWI)

- **Hurricane intensity** trending up — increased Cat 4–5 share in the Atlantic basin; **Beryl 2024** as recent calibration event for an earlier-season + further-south major hurricane.
- **Sea-level rise**: +0.20–0.50 m by 2100 SSP2-4.5; +0.30–0.85 m by 2100 SSP5-8.5 (IPCC AR6 — reconciled with the figure cited under § Coastal flood / storm surge); **acute exposure for low-lying south + south-east coast parcels** (some Christ Church + St. Philip fishing villages).
- **Drought**: eastern Caribbean trending drier in shoulder months; freshwater-supply stress on a country dependent on groundwater + desalination.
- **Coral bleaching + ocean acidification**: material to beach replenishment + reef-tourism economics.
- **Extreme rainfall hourly intensity**: +20–40% likely by mid-century — affects drainage + flash-flood risk in interior catchments (Codrington, St. Joseph, St. Andrew).

### Water — public utility, single national operator

| Operator | Coverage |

### Sewer reality

- **Bridgetown Sewerage Project** + **South Coast Sewerage Project**: collective-sewer footprint covers central Bridgetown + parts of the south-coast tourism corridor (Hastings, Worthing, St. Lawrence Gap, Maxwell — capacity-constrained historically with periodic complaints).
- **West coast (St. James, St. Peter)**: largely **NOT** on collective sewer — most properties on **individual septic + soakaway** (resort estates often have private package treatment plants).
- **Rural / interior parishes**: septic + soakaway standard.
- **Listing clue**: "BWA water + BWA sewer" = collective service in covered zones; "BWA water + septic" = no collective sewer (most of west coast + interior).
- **Potability**: BWA tap water is **potable** across the national network; Barbados has long been considered to have one of the most reliable + drinkable public water supplies in the Caribbean — though periodic supply interruptions occur in dry-season high-demand periods.

### Power

- **[Barbados Light & Power Company (BLPC, Emera-owned)](https://www.blpc.com.bb/)** — sole national grid; tariffs historically high by global standard.
- **Solar + battery** uptake increasing under [Fair Trading Commission](https://www.ftc.gov.bb/) net-metering framework.
- **Outages**: occasional, especially during/post-storms; less frequent than BS / JM but **post-Beryl 2024 disruptions** affected south + south-west coasts for days.
- **Standby generator + inverter** advisable for any continuous-occupancy property.

## Section: `--currency`

- **Currency**: **Barbados Dollar (Bds$ / BBD)**.
- **Peg**: **2:1 with USD since 5 July 1975** — administered via Exchange Control + CBB monetary operations ([CBB — Fixed for Forty-One Years](https://www.centralbank.org.bb/news/general-press-release/fixed-for-forty-one-years), 2016 anniversary post; peg unbroken through 2026).
- **USD circulates in parallel**: tourism + west-coast luxury property transactions are USD-denominated or USD-equivalent BBD; high-end branded estates (Sandy Lane, Royal Westmoreland, Apes Hill, Port St. Charles, Port Ferdinand) quote and close USD-firm.
- **Exchange Control Department (CBB)**: governs non-resident currency flows under the **Exchange Control Act, Cap. 71**. **Foreign buyers MUST register their incoming purchase funds with CBB via an authorised dealer (commercial bank) + attorney** — this is the legal precondition for repatriating capital + gains on resale. The registration is procedural, not gatekeeping in normal cases.
- **Practical effect for buyer**: zero structural FX risk for USD-base buyers; minimal risk for EUR/GBP/CAD buyers (USD-correlated). **Devaluation risk**: peg has held since 1975 through significant external shocks (2008 crisis, 2018 IMF EFF programme, Covid, Beryl 2024); CBB FX reserves + IMF Article IV monitoring underpin credibility — but Barbadian commentators (e.g., Rasheed J. Griffith) periodically debate sustainability; **the peg is a policy choice, not a structural certainty**.
- **Critical foreign-buyer step**: any non-resident purchase requires **CBB Exchange Control registration** routed via attorney + authorised dealer — typically a routine filing, but **failure to register at purchase blocks repatriation on resale**. See `--finance` and Foreign buyer eligibility above.

## Section: `--digital-nomad` (Welcome Stamp deep-dive)

Barbados pioneered the modern Caribbean DNV with the Welcome Stamp launched **30 June 2020** — among the earliest digital-nomad visa programmes globally. The Stamp made Barbados a reference point for similar programmes that followed (Antigua's NDR, Cayman's GCCP, Curaçao's @HOME, Bermuda's Work From Bermuda).

### Eligibility

- **Income**: **USD 50,000+ per year** from sources **outside Barbados** (employment contract, freelance contracts, business ownership, investment income).
- **Employment**: applicant must be employed by or contracted to companies/clients **outside Barbados**.
- **Documentation**: 3–6 months bank statements, employment contracts / freelance agreements, prior tax returns acceptable.
- **Health insurance**: mandatory — valid for the visa period; from home country or Barbadian carrier.
- **Family**: spouse + dependent children under 26 may be included on the family application.
- **No Barbadian work permitted** under the Stamp.

### Fees

- **Individual**: **USD 2,000** application fee.
- **Family**: **USD 3,000** application fee.
- **Renewal discount**: **25% off** subsequent renewals (so individual renewal ~USD 1,500).

### Duration + renewal

- **12-month visa**, renewable indefinitely subject to approval + payment.
- **No statutory cap** on renewals.

### Tax position

- Welcome Stamp holders are **NOT Barbadian tax residents** during the Stamp period.
- **No Barbadian income tax** on foreign-employment income earned during the Stamp.
- Holder remains tax-resident in **home jurisdiction** (subject to home-country rules — e.g., US citizens taxed on worldwide income regardless of physical presence; UK/EU residents typically lose home tax-residency after 183+ days abroad — *consult home-country tax advisor*).

### Processing

- ~**7 business days** typical processing time.
- Apply online via the [Welcome Stamp portal](https://www.visitbarbados.org/barbados-welcome-stamp).

### Programme status

- **Renewed by Cabinet to 31 December 2026** (verify current status at portal — Cabinet has renewed multiple times since 2020 launch).
- Pre-2026 Cabinet-level review cycles have consistently extended; programme has become a structural part of Barbados' tourism + ICT strategy.

### Property linkage

- Welcome Stamp **does NOT require property ownership** or rental commitment.
- Many holders rent long-term (~6–12 month leases) on the south coast (Christ Church) — driving the **2024–2025 long-term-rental shift** documented by industry secondary sources (Hannah Properties, Spot Blue).
- For property-buyers, the Welcome Stamp can serve as a **"try before you buy"** route — a year on-island assessing fit before committing to SERP Cat. 2 (USD 300,000+ property route).

(Source: [Welcome Stamp portal — Visit Barbados](https://www.visitbarbados.org/barbados-welcome-stamp) + [Citizen Remote — Barbados DNV](https://citizenremote.com/visas/barbados-digital-nomad-visa/) + [TaxHackers — Barbados DNV 2025](https://taxhackers.io/blog/en-barbados-digital-nomad-visa) — primary portal + secondary aggregators; *verify current threshold + fee + Cabinet renewal status at portal before relying*)

## Caveats unique to BB

- **CBB Exchange Control registration is the foreign-buyer gating step**: not an ownership restriction, but a **mandatory administrative filing** (via attorney + authorised dealer) for the incoming purchase funds. **Failure to register at purchase blocks future repatriation of capital + gains on resale**. Always confirm in writing with attorney before completion.
- **Vendor-heavy closing-cost structure**: **PTT 2.5% + Stamp Duty 1.0% + agent 5% + VAT** all fall on the vendor — total ~**9–10.5%** vendor side. Built into asking-price. **Buyer-side closing costs are unusually low** (~2–3%) by comparison.
- **PTT exemption only above a building**: the BBD 150,000 PTT exemption applies **only where a building exists** on the land. **Vacant-land sales attract PTT on the full consideration** with no exemption.
- **Land Tax cap BBD 100,000/year** above the BBD 850,000 band — a ceiling that makes Barbadian Land Tax the **lowest annual property tax burden in the Caribbean** for ultra-luxury holdings.
- **Title-system duality**: most parcels still on **deeds-recording / abstract-of-title**, not the Title Registration Act 1979 Torrens-style system. Specialist attorney essential. Land (Title Proceedings) Act 2011-7 modernised the judicial path to register clouded titles.
- **Hurricane Beryl (1 July 2024)** is the **modern calibration event** — the prior comparable pass was Hurricane Ivan (7 Sep 2004, Cat 3 ~165 km south — est. from NHC best track; >176 homes destroyed per NHC TCR AL092004); post-Beryl premium re-pricing: data not publicly available — verify with carriers and FSC Barbados. **3+ insurance quotes** for any oceanfront / south-coast / south-east-coast parcel pre-contract is now mandatory practice.
- **South + south-east coasts are the storm-surge-exposed coasts**: not the west coast (which faces away from prevailing storm tracks). St. Philip + south Christ Church + south St. Michael fishing villages were Beryl 2024 footprint.
- **HOA / Owners Association rules in branded gated estates** (Sandy Lane, Royal Westmoreland, Apes Hill, Port St. Charles, Port Ferdinand) materially constrain short-term-rental income models — read Declaration / By-Laws before pricing yield.
- **VAT 17.5% on professional services** (legal, agent, valuation) but **VAT-EXEMPT on the property sale itself** (Cap. 87 Schedule 1).
- **Tourism accommodation reduced VAT 10%** (vs 17.5% standard) for short-term rentals + hotels.
- **Welcome Stamp** is a structural new demand driver (since 2020) for the long-term rental market on the south coast.
- **No CBI**: Barbados does NOT operate a citizenship-by-investment programme (distinguish from St. Kitts & Nevis, Dominica, Antigua & Barbuda, St. Lucia, Grenada). Naturalisation requires 5+ years residence.
- **Wire fraud against international buyers** is rising — verify wire instructions verbally with attorney via known phone before sending purchase funds.
- **Crime (St. Michael 2024 spike)**: 2024 saw a sharp homicide-rate rise concentrated in specific Bridgetown urban districts — west-coast / south-coast residential and tourist corridors remained low; track 2025 annual update.
- **CBB peg policy**: the 2:1 USD peg has held since 1975 but is a policy choice, not a structural certainty — Barbadian commentators periodically debate sustainability under reserve-pressure scenarios.
- **Compact-island reality**: ~432 km² total — anywhere is within ~60 min drive of Bridgetown + QEH; medical access materially better than Family-Island BS / remote JM.
