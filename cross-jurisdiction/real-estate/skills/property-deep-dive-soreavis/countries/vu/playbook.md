# Vanuatu 🇻🇺 — Property Due-Diligence Playbook

ISO2: `vu`. Status: ✅ Fully populated (researched 2026-06-13).

> **Critical framing — read first**: Vanuatu is a **Melanesian archipelago republic** in the South Pacific (Ring of Fire / New Hebrides subduction zone). **There is NO freehold — full stop.** The 1980 independence Constitution **abolished all freehold land and vested ALL land permanently in the indigenous custom owners** (Constitution **Chapter 12, Articles 73–75**: "All land in the Republic of Vanuatu belongs to the indigenous custom owners and their descendants"; only indigenous citizens hold *perpetual* ownership — [consolidated Constitution, gov.vu](https://www.gov.vu/images/legislation/constitution-en.pdf)). Foreigners — **and** non-custom-owner ni-Vanuatu — hold land ONLY via a **registered lease** under the **Land Leases Act [Cap 163]**; **75 years is the maximum/ceiling**, not a default (historically rural ~30 yrs, urban ~50 yrs, up to 75 yrs for major projects — [PacLII National Land Legislation Review 2007](http://www.paclii.org/vu/other/vanuatulandreview2007.html)). A "purchase" is therefore an **assignment of an existing lease with N years remaining** + an annual ground rent payable to the custom-owner lessor — **years-remaining is the #1 diligence item**, and the gate is **registration + Lessor's Consent to Transfer** at the **Department of Lands, Survey & Registry** ([mol.gov.vu](https://mol.gov.vu/index.php/lands-dept)). Currency: **Vatu (VUV)**, no subunit; the RBV runs a **basket peg** (vatu pegged to a trade/transactions-weighted basket of USD/AUD/NZD/EUR, actively managed, **realigned under a new IMF-advised framework in 2025** — IMF 2025 Article IV [PR-25314](https://www.imf.org/en/news/articles/2025/09/25/pr-25314-vanuatu-imf-executive-board-concludes-2025-article-iv-consultation) describes a "pegged exchange rate regime"; [RBV/PMO press release](https://pmo.gov.vu/en/public-information/press-release/1131-the-vanuatu-currency-and-exchange-rate-framework.html)). Tax-stack headline: **ZERO direct tax** — no personal income tax, no corporate income tax, no capital gains tax, no inheritance/wealth tax, no withholding tax (their absence is the evidence; there is no Income Tax Act — [VFIPA "Low tax jurisdiction"](https://investvanuatu.vu/untapped-potential/low-tax-jurisdiction/)). What you DO pay: **15% VAT** (residential rent is VAT-EXEMPT — [Dept of Customs & Inland Revenue, Cap 247](https://vanuatucustoms.gov.vu/taxes-and-licensing.htmlvalue-added-tax-vat/introduction.html)) and **12.5% Rent Tax on gross rental income** ([DCIR, Rent Tax Act Cap 196](https://vanuatucustoms.gov.vu/taxes-and-licensing.htmlrent-tax.html)). **Citizenship-by-investment is a DONATION, NOT property-linked**: the **Development Support Programme (DSP)** is a non-refundable contribution to the National Development Fund (~USD 130,000 floor, single applicant — secondary, verify) — **you cannot buy citizenship by buying real estate**. The CBI is **regulatorily and reputationally contested**: the EU **permanently revoked** Vanuatu's visa-free Schengen access (suspended from 8 Nov 2022; visa required from 4 Feb 2023; **permanent visa requirement from 3 Feb 2025**, via **Regulation (EU) 2025/11** of 19 Dec 2024, Council adoption 12 Dec 2024 — [EU Council](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2024/12/12/vanuatu-council-ends-visa-exemption/)) — the first time the EU fully revoked a visa-free agreement over an investor-citizenship scheme. **Top hazard: extreme natural-hazard exposure** — Vanuatu is routinely ranked **#1 in the World Risk Index** (verify edition); Cyclone Pam (Cat 5, Mar 2015); twin Cyclones Judy & Kevin (Cat 4, Mar 2023); **M7.3 earthquake 17 Dec 2024 ~30 km W of Port Vila** (14 dead, ~US$231.7M damage — NDMO via [ReliefWeb/OCHA](https://reliefweb.int/disaster/eq-2024-000227-vut)); active volcanoes (Yasur/Ambae). Final court of appeal: the **Court of Appeal of Vanuatu** — a republic since 1980, **no UK Privy Council link** ([Judiciary of Vanuatu](https://courts.gov.vu/about-us/court-of-appeal)). **Confirm** lease years-remaining, ground rent, Lessor's/Minister's consent, the duty stack, and current CBI/residency status at primary sources **before any binding step**.

## Country profile

- **Geography**: Y-shaped archipelago of **~80 islands** (~65 inhabited) in Melanesia, South Pacific, ~1,750 km east of northern Australia. Land area **~12,189 km²**. Capital **Port Vila** on **Efate** (Shefa Province); second town **Luganville** on Espiritu Santo (Sanma Province). (Cross-checked: Vanuatu Tourism Office / Wikipedia "Geography of Vanuatu" — secondary; verify at VNSO.)
- **Population**: **~300,000–340,000**. Figures vary by source/year — 2020 census **~300,019** ([Vanuatu National Statistics Office](https://vnso.gov.vu/) via [Pacific Community SDD](https://sdd.spc.int/vu)); aggregator estimates 331k–342k (2024). *Latest official headcount + census year — data not publicly re-verified here; verify at VNSO / SPC SDD ([sdd.spc.int/vu](https://sdd.spc.int/vu)).*
- **GDP per capita**: **~USD 2,700–3,300** (2024) — lower-middle-income ([World Bank, Vanuatu GDP per capita](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/NY.GDP.PCAP.CD?locations=VU); TradingEconomics USD 2,694.70 (2024)). Real GDP growth forecasts for 2025 **diverge by source**: a Vanuatu-government (DoFT/RBV) forecast of **~3.7%** vs. the **IMF ~1.7%** ([IMF Country Report 25/277](https://www.imf.org/-/media/files/publications/cr/2025/english/1vutea2025001-source-pdf.pdf)) and **ADB ~1.5%** ([ADB Vanuatu economy](https://www.adb.org/where-we-work/vanuatu/economy)) — the domestic forecast is materially more optimistic; treat the growth number as range-bound, not a single point (RBV site SSL-blocks automated fetch — confirm the RBV figure in browser).
- **Currency**: **Vatu (VUV)** — ISO 4217 **VUV**, **no subunit** (no cents). Introduced 1981. Regime = **basket peg** (NOT a hard peg to one currency, NOT a free float): the RBV pegs the vatu to a transactions/trade-weighted basket of major trading-partner currencies (USD, AUD, NZD, EUR), actively managed by the RBV Financial Markets Department, **realigned under a new IMF-advised basket framework in 2025** (RBV characterises the 2025 move as a "realignment", not a devaluation). Sources: IMF 2025 Article IV ([PR-25314](https://www.imf.org/en/news/articles/2025/09/25/pr-25314-vanuatu-imf-executive-board-concludes-2025-article-iv-consultation), "pegged exchange rate regime"); IMF Technical Assistance Report "Currency Basket Framework and FX Market Operations" 2026 ([IMF eLibrary 2026/026](https://www.elibrary.imf.org/view/journals/019/2026/026/article-A001-en.xml) — uses "basket peg"; bot-blocked to automated fetch, verify in browser); [RBV/PMO press release](https://pmo.gov.vu/en/public-information/press-release/1131-the-vanuatu-currency-and-exchange-rate-framework.html).
- **Languages**: **Three official** — **Bislama** (English-based creole, lingua franca), **English**, **French** (Constitution Art. 3 — verify exact wording at gov.vu). Trilingual legacy of the pre-1980 Anglo-French Condominium ("New Hebrides"). Legal/conveyancing documents are typically in English and/or French.
- **Legal system**: **Mixed** — English common law + French civil-law influences + customary land law. **Final court of appeal: the [Court of Appeal of Vanuatu](https://courts.gov.vu/about-us/court-of-appeal)** (the domestic apex court; ≥2 Supreme Court judges sitting together per Constitution Art. 50). Hierarchy: Island Courts / Magistrates' Courts → [Supreme Court](https://courts.gov.vu/about-us/supreme-court) → Court of Appeal. **NO UK Privy Council appeal** (republic since 1980).
- **Admin levels**: **6 provinces** — Torba, Sanma, Penama, Malampa, **Shefa** (Efate + Port Vila), Tafea — plus **2 municipal councils** (Port Vila, Luganville). (Secondary — verify at VNSO.)
- **Cadastre / land registry**:
  - **[Department of Lands, Survey & Registry](https://mol.gov.vu/index.php/lands-dept)** (within the Ministry of Lands & Natural Resources) — Registry Section registers leases that have completed the Lease Registration Process; Lease Execution Section; Planning & Enforcement Section processes **negotiator certificate** applications + lease-class changes; also valuation, survey/mapping, land acquisition, dispute resolution. Registry digitised 2007 (secondary).
  - **No universal national parcel ID** equivalent to a French *numéro de parcelle* documented here — leases are identified by lease title number + survey plan. Verify the lease title reference at the Lands Dept.
- **Title concept**: **Leasehold ONLY for foreigners** (and non-custom-owner ni-Vanuatu). **Perpetual custom ownership** is reserved to indigenous citizens (Constitution Art. 75). Apartment / multi-unit developments use **strata title** under the **Strata Titles Act 2000** (the strata lots themselves sit over a registered lease — verify exact Cap at PacLII).
- **Recent reforms (12-month watchlist)**:
  - **2025 RBV currency-basket realignment** — RBV adopted a new IMF-advised currency-basket framework in 2025; characterised by RBV as a realignment, not a devaluation ([RBV/PMO press release](https://pmo.gov.vu/en/public-information/press-release/1131-the-vanuatu-currency-and-exchange-rate-framework.html)). *Exact basket weights + realignment magnitude (%) — data not publicly re-verified here; verify in IMF CR 25/277 + the 2026 TA report.*
  - **EU visa-free revocation permanent — visa requirement applies from 3 Feb 2025** — Regulation (EU) 2025/11 of 19 December 2024 (Council final adoption 12 Dec 2024) moved Vanuatu from Annex II Part 1 to Annex I Part 1 of Reg (EU) 2018/1806 ([EU Council press release](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2024/12/12/vanuatu-council-ends-visa-exemption/); [EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2025/11/oj/eng)). Material to CBI demand + reputational standing.
  - **M7.3 Port Vila earthquake 17 Dec 2024** — damaged buildings + two major Port Vila water reservoirs; reset perceived seismic-build risk for any Port Vila/Efate purchase ([ReliefWeb/OCHA/UNDP citing NDMO](https://reliefweb.int/disaster/eq-2024-000227-vut)).

## 🇻🇺 Foreign buyer eligibility (READ FIRST)

This section determines whether a transaction is even legally possible — and what process applies. Vanuatu does **NOT** bar foreigners from holding land, but it bars **everyone** (including foreigners) from holding **freehold**, because freehold does not exist. The only instrument available to a foreigner is a **registered lease**.

### Legal basis

- **Constitution of the Republic of Vanuatu, Chapter 12 (Arts 73–81)** — vests all land in indigenous custom owners; abolished freehold at 1980 independence; perpetual ownership reserved to indigenous citizens (Art. 75); government may acquire/redistribute land in the public interest (Arts 79–80). Primary: [consolidated Constitution, gov.vu](https://www.gov.vu/images/legislation/constitution-en.pdf); cross-confirmed [Constitute Project](https://www.constituteproject.org/constitution/Vanuatu_2013), [FAOLEX](https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/van132926.pdf).
- **Land Leases Act [Cap 163]** — registration + dealings in leases (the instrument every foreign buyer uses). Primary statute text: [FAOLEX / Ecolex](https://www.fao.org/faolex/results/details/en/c/LEX-FAOC088905/).
- **Strata Titles Act 2000** — apartment / multi-unit strata regime (formal short title "Strata Titles Act"; verify exact Cap at PacLII).
- **Stamp Duties Act [Cap 68]** — transaction stamp duty. *Cap number not independently confirmed here — verify at PacLII vu/legis.*
- **AML/CFT framework** — administered via the **Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU)** under the State Law Office; real-estate dealings + the CBI fall in scope. *Verify current obligations at the FIU.*

### Buyer category matrix

| Buyer category | Right |
|---|---|
| **Indigenous ni-Vanuatu custom owner** | **Perpetual custom ownership** (Constitution Art. 75) — the only perpetual title in Vanuatu |
| **Non-custom-owner ni-Vanuatu citizen** | **Leasehold only** (no perpetual title unless acquired through a recognised system of custom tenure) |
| **Foreign individual** | **Leasehold only** — take or assign a registered lease (≤75-yr ceiling) under Land Leases Act [Cap 163]; subject to Lessor's Consent to Transfer (+ Minister of Lands consent on certain leases) |
| **Foreign company** | **Leasehold only**, on the same lease basis; if the lease sits in a company, the lease may be acquired indirectly via **share transfer** of the lessee company (different duty treatment — see `--tax`) |
| **Strata-lot buyer (apartment)** | Strata lot under the Strata Titles Act 2000 — the underlying tenure is still a **lease**; confirm the head-lease years-remaining |
| **Citizenship by Investment (CBI / DSP)** | **NOT property-linked** — a donation, not a purchase; buying real estate does NOT confer citizenship (see `--visa`) |

(Source: Constitution Ch. 12 [gov.vu](https://www.gov.vu/images/legislation/constitution-en.pdf) + Land Leases Act [Cap 163] [FAOLEX](https://www.fao.org/faolex/results/details/en/c/LEX-FAOC088905/) + Lands Dept [mol.gov.vu](https://mol.gov.vu/index.php/lands-dept) — primary for tenure; lease-assignment mechanics partly agency-secondary, *verify with a Vanuatu conveyancing lawyer before contract*)

### Visa / residency via property

- **No CBI via property.** Citizenship is obtained only via the **DSP donation** (see `--visa`), never by buying real estate.
- **Residency via property — NOT primary-confirmed.** Whether a registered lease can underpin an investor/residence permit, and the qualifying thresholds, are **data not publicly available — verify at the [Vanuatu Department of Immigration & Passport Services](https://immigration.gov.vu/)** (do NOT assume a property lease grants residency).

### Compliance / AML

- **Real-estate dealings + the CBI fall under Vanuatu's AML/CFT regime** (Financial Intelligence Unit, under the State Law Office). CBI-driven AML scrutiny is high after the EU revocation + sustained OECD/EU attention (see `--visa`). *Verify current source-of-funds / KYC obligations at the FIU.*
- **Source-of-funds documentation** should be expected for any significant lease purchase and is mandatory for the DSP.

### Verification path (before any reservation)

1. **Pull the lease at the Lands Dept**: confirm the **lease title number**, **years remaining**, **annual ground rent**, registered lessor (custom owner), and any caveats/charges at the [Department of Lands, Survey & Registry](https://mol.gov.vu/index.php/lands-dept). *Years-remaining is the single most important number.*
2. **Confirm the gate that applies**: a **new lease over custom land** needs a **negotiator's certificate** (Planning & Enforcement Section); a **resale (assignment of an existing registered lease)** needs **registration + Lessor's Consent to Transfer** and, on certain leases, **Minister of Lands consent** — confirm which path applies to your specific lease (agency sources; *verify at Lands Dept*).
3. **Survey check**: confirm the surveyed boundaries match the lease plan (Survey/Mapping Section).
4. **Ground-rent + consent status**: confirm ground rent is current and obtain the lessor's written consent terms in advance.
5. **AML / source-of-funds**: assemble bank statements + asset-sale evidence before signing.
6. **Engage a Vanuatu conveyancing lawyer** — lease assignment, consents, and duty calculation are not DIY for a foreign buyer.

### Compute

1. **A purchase = lease assignment.** The asking price buys the **remaining lease term** + the building, NOT perpetual title. Always confirm **years remaining on the lease** + the **annual ground rent** to the custom owner + whether any **renewal/extension** is pre-agreed in the lease.
2. **USD/m² = price ÷ built-area m²** (listings may quote AUD or USD — convert at a **dated** RBV rate; do not invent one). Cross-check ≥ 3 active listings on the same locality.
3. **Trap — declining-term value.** A 50-yr lease from c.1980 has **~4 years remaining in 2030** unless renewed — value falls as the term shortens. A short residual term materially impairs both price and mortgageability.
4. **Trap — currency of quote.** Trophy stock is often AUD- or USD-quoted; local stock VUV-quoted. The vatu **moved on the 2025 basket realignment** — re-pull the rate at point of use.
5. **Trap — thin comparables.** Nationwide active supply is small; a single outlier listing skews any "average". Treat any per-m² figure as indicative only.

### Key terms

- **Lease (registered)** — the only instrument a foreigner holds (Land Leases Act [Cap 163])
- **Assignment / transfer of lease** — what a "sale" actually is (existing lease + N years remaining)
- **Ground rent** — annual rent payable to the custom-owner lessor
- **Lessor's Consent to Transfer** — the lessor's written consent required to assign certain leases
- **Negotiator's Certificate** — the gate to negotiate a *new* lease over custom land
- **Strata lot** — apartment unit under the Strata Titles Act 2000 (sits over a lease)
- **Custom owner** — the indigenous owner in whom perpetual title is vested (Constitution Art. 75)

## Section: `--tax`

> **Headline**: Vanuatu is a **ZERO-direct-tax jurisdiction** — **NO personal income tax, NO corporate income tax, NO capital gains tax, NO inheritance/estate tax, NO wealth tax, NO withholding tax**. There is no Income Tax Act; the absence is the evidence ([VFIPA "Low tax jurisdiction"](https://investvanuatu.vu/untapped-potential/low-tax-jurisdiction/), primary `.vu` gov agency; the [Dept of Customs & Inland Revenue tax menu](https://vanuatucustoms.gov.vu/taxes-and-licensing.html) lists only Rent Tax, VAT, Turnover Tax + licensing — no income/CGT category). What you DO pay: **15% VAT** (residential rent VAT-EXEMPT), **12.5% Rent Tax** on gross rental income, and on a lease purchase a **stamp duty + government registration fee** (rates are agency-secondary — see below). No annual national land tax; instead **annual ground rent** to the custom-owner lessor + possible **municipal rates**.

### Zero direct tax — the core fact

- **No personal income tax, no corporate income tax, no CGT, no inheritance/estate/gift tax, no wealth tax, no withholding tax.** Cross-confirmed at a primary `.vu` gov agency: [VFIPA "Low tax jurisdiction"](https://investvanuatu.vu/untapped-potential/low-tax-jurisdiction/). The Dept of Customs & Inland Revenue administers ONLY Rent Tax, VAT, and Turnover Tax (+ licensing) — the **absence** of an income/CGT category on the official tax list is the primary evidence. **CITABLE AS FACT.**

### VAT — primary

- **VAT = 15%** on most goods/services supplied by registered persons. Introduced **1998 at 12.5%** (VAT Act No. 12 of 1998); **raised to 15% in 2018**. **Registration threshold: taxable supplies > VUV 4,000,000 in any 12-month period.** **Residential rental accommodation is VAT-EXEMPT.** Legal basis: **Value Added Tax Act [Cap 247]** + **Tax Administration Act No. 37 of 2018** (in force 1 Jan 2020). Primary: [Dept of Customs & Inland Revenue, VAT introduction](https://vanuatucustoms.gov.vu/taxes-and-licensing.htmlvalue-added-tax-vat/introduction.html) (identical content also served at `customsinlandrevenue.gov.vu`). **CITABLE AS FACT.**

### Rent Tax — primary

- **Rent Tax = 12.5% on GROSS rental income** (no expense deductions), filed as **2 × 6-monthly returns per year**. **For individual landlords, the first VUV 200,000 of rental income is exempt.** Applies to long-term residential rental; commercial/short-term rental falls under **VAT** instead (if over the VUV 4M threshold). Legal basis: **Rent Tax Act [Cap 196]**. Primary: [Dept of Customs & Inland Revenue, Rent Tax](https://vanuatucustoms.gov.vu/taxes-and-licensing.htmlrent-tax.html). **CITABLE AS FACT.**

### Property-transaction duties — one-time at lease assignment (agency-secondary — `est.`, verify)

> ⚠️ The split below is **agency / conveyancing-secondary**, not the primary fee schedule — label `est.` and verify at the **Stamp Duties Act + Land Leases Act [Cap 163] fee schedule / Lands Dept**. Do NOT state as fact, and do NOT assert the base is "unimproved value" (agency sources apply it to **purchase price**).

| Item | Rate (est. — secondary) | Base |
|---|---|---|
| **Stamp Duty** (on a lease-title purchase) | **~2%** of purchase price (est.) | Purchase price (per agency sources) |
| **Government Registration / Title-Transfer fee** | **~5%** of purchase price (est.) | Purchase price (per agency sources) |
| **Legal fees** | **~0.5–1%** (est.) | Purchase price |
| **Stamp Duty if acquired via company-share transfer** | **~4%** of share-transfer value (est.); the 2% title stamp duty does NOT apply (the title itself isn't transferred) | Share-transfer value |

(Source: [vanuatuisland.org — buying land in Vanuatu](https://www.vanuatuisland.org/buying-land-in-vanuatu) + [buying on Aore Island](https://www.vanuatuisland.org/buying-on-aore-island) ❌ DEPRECATED — dead on 2026-09-18; verify with the authority named in this row — *secondary / conveyancing tier*; statute frame Land Leases Act [Cap 163] [FAOLEX](https://www.fao.org/faolex/results/details/en/c/LEX-FAOC088905/) + Stamp Duties Act [Cap 68] *(Cap unconfirmed — verify at PacLII)*. **Exact rates + base — data not publicly confirmed at primary; verify at the Stamp Duties Act + Land Leases Act fee schedule / Lands Dept.**)

### Holding costs — annual

- **No annual national land/property tax.** Instead you pay **annual ground/lease rent** to the custom-owner lessor (set in the lease — confirm the amount + escalation in the specific lease).
- **Municipal rates** may apply in **Port Vila / Luganville**. *Exact municipal-rate basis — data not publicly available; verify at the Port Vila / Luganville municipal councils.*

### Capital gains / inheritance / wealth

- **NO capital gains tax**, **NO inheritance/estate tax**, **NO gift tax**, **NO wealth tax** — all absent (VFIPA primary, above). This is a core part of Vanuatu's positioning as a low-tax jurisdiction.

### Future risk

- **OECD/EU scrutiny** of Vanuatu's offshore + CBI positioning is sustained (see `--visa`) — the zero-tax framework itself is a periodic target of international-tax pressure. *Monitor any introduction of new direct taxes at DCIR / Ministry of Finance.*
- **Rent Tax / VAT rate changes** — re-verify rates at DCIR before relying (last primary check: 2026-06-13).

⚠️ Verification: request the seller's lease (ground-rent amount + escalation) + any municipal-rate receipts. Confirm the **stamp duty + registration fee** at the Stamp Duties Act + Land Leases Act [Cap 163] fee schedule / Lands Dept **before contract** — the 2%/5%/4% figures are `est.` secondary.

## Section: `--rental`

### Long-term residential rentals

- **Thin formal market**; expat rentals concentrated in **Port Vila** (Mele, Pango, Tassiriki, town).
- **Residential rent is VAT-EXEMPT** ([DCIR primary](https://vanuatucustoms.gov.vu/taxes-and-licensing.htmlvalue-added-tax-vat/introduction.html)).
- **Rent Tax = 12.5% on gross rental income**, first **VUV 200,000 exempt** for individual landlords, **2 × 6-monthly returns/yr**, no expense deductions ([DCIR primary, Rent Tax Act Cap 196](https://vanuatucustoms.gov.vu/taxes-and-licensing.htmlrent-tax.html)).
- **Tenancy terms** (notice periods, deposit norms, eviction process) — *data not publicly available here; verify at the State Law Office / a Vanuatu lawyer.*

### Short-term rentals (STR — tourism)

- **Tourism-driven** (Port Vila + outer-island resorts). A property let as STR is a **taxable activity for VAT** once turnover **> VUV 4,000,000** ([DCIR threshold, primary](https://vanuatucustoms.gov.vu/taxes-and-licensing.htmlvalue-added-tax-vat/introduction.html)).

- **Active volcanoes** include **Yasur (Tanna)** and **Ambae/Manaro** (the 2017–2018 Ambae eruption forced full-island evacuation). PRIMARY: [VMGD](https://www.vmgd.gov.vu/). Ashfall + evacuation risk concentrated near active centres — verify the specific island's volcanic alert level on visit.

## Section: `--currency`

- **Currency**: **Vatu (VUV)** — ISO 4217 **VUV**, **no subunit** (no cents). Introduced 1981.
- **Regime: BASKET PEG** — the vatu is pegged to a **transactions/trade-weighted basket** of major trading-partner currencies (**USD, AUD, NZD, EUR**), **actively managed** by the RBV Financial Markets Department; **realigned under a new IMF-advised basket framework in 2025** (RBV characterises it as a **"realignment", not a devaluation**). This is **NOT a hard single-currency peg** (like BBD/USD) and **NOT a free float**. Sources: IMF 2025 Article IV ([PR-25314](https://www.imf.org/en/news/articles/2025/09/25/pr-25314-vanuatu-imf-executive-board-concludes-2025-article-iv-consultation), "pegged exchange rate regime"); IMF TA Report "Currency Basket Framework and FX Market Operations" 2026 ([IMF eLibrary 2026/026](https://www.elibrary.imf.org/view/journals/019/2026/026/article-A001-en.xml), "basket peg" — bot-blocked, verify in browser); [RBV/PMO press release](https://pmo.gov.vu/en/public-information/press-release/1131-the-vanuatu-currency-and-exchange-rate-framework.html). *Exact basket weights + the de-facto IMF AREAER label + the 2025 realignment magnitude (%) — data not publicly re-verified here; verify in IMF CR 25/277 + the 2026 TA report.*
- **Indicative rate (dated — re-pull at point of use)**: RBV indicative **USD 1 = 117.05 VUV (12 Jun 2026)** ([RBV](https://www.rbv.gov.vu/); RBV site SSL-blocks automated fetch — confirm in browser). Independent mid-market June-2026 quotes sit **~118.4–120.3 VUV/USD** (Wise/XE — secondary; buy/sell vs mid-market explains the small gap). **The vatu moved on the 2025 realignment — always use a DATED rate and re-pull it.**
- **Practical effect for buyer**: a basket peg means **moderate FX risk** for a single-currency (e.g. USD-only) buyer — the vatu can be realigned against any one basket member (as it was in 2025). Hold a dated rate for any conversion; never invent one.

## Caveats unique to VU

- **NO FREEHOLD EXISTS.** The 1980 Constitution (Ch. 12, Arts 73–75) abolished freehold and vests **all** land permanently in indigenous custom owners. A foreigner can **only** hold a **registered lease** (Land Leases Act [Cap 163]). **Never describe a Vanuatu purchase as freehold.**
- **A "purchase" = an assignment of an existing lease with N years remaining** + an annual ground rent to the custom-owner lessor. **Years-remaining is the #1 diligence item** — a 50-yr lease from c.1980 is deep into its term. **75 years is the maximum/ceiling, NOT a default** (historically rural ~30, urban ~50, up to 75 for major projects).
- **The gate differs by transaction**: a **new** lease over custom land needs a **negotiator's certificate**; a **resale (assignment)** needs **registration + Lessor's Consent to Transfer** (+ Minister of Lands consent on certain leases). Don't conflate the two.
- **Zero direct tax is real and primary-sourced** (no income/corporate/CGT/inheritance/wealth/withholding tax — VFIPA primary), BUT you still pay **15% VAT**, **12.5% Rent Tax** on gross rental income, and a one-time **stamp duty + registration fee** on the lease purchase.
- **Property-transaction duty rates are `est.`/secondary** (~2% stamp + ~5% registration on the purchase price; ~4% stamp on share-transfer value if buying the lessee company). **Verify at the Stamp Duties Act + Land Leases Act [Cap 163] fee schedule / Lands Dept** — do NOT treat as fact, and do NOT assert the base is "unimproved value".
- **Currency = basket peg, not a hard peg or a float.** The vatu is pegged to a trade-weighted basket (USD/AUD/NZD/EUR), actively managed, and was **realigned in 2025** under an IMF-advised framework — single-currency buyers carry moderate FX risk. Use a dated rate (RBV USD 1 = 117.05 VUV, 12 Jun 2026).
- **CBI (DSP) is a DONATION, not property-linked** — buying real estate gives you **no** citizenship. And the CBI is **contested**: the **EU permanently revoked Vanuatu's visa-free Schengen access — the permanent visa requirement applies from 3 Feb 2025** (Reg (EU) 2025/11 of 19 Dec 2024, Council adoption 12 Dec 2024), after suspending the waiver from 8 Nov 2022 (visa required from 4 Feb 2023). NEVER present the CBI as "open and problem-free".
- **Extreme, co-located natural-hazard exposure** — cyclone (Pam Cat-5 2015; Judy/Kevin Cat-4 2023) **+** earthquake/tsunami (M7.3 Port Vila, 17 Dec 2024, 14 dead, ~US$231.7M) **+** active volcanoes (Yasur/Ambae) **+** SLR. Vanuatu is routinely ranked **#1 in the World Risk Index** (verify edition). Engineered construction + funded insurance + a medevac plan are baseline requirements.
- **Healthcare is limited** — Vila Central Hospital is the main facility; **medevac to Australia/NZ is common** for serious cases. Outer-island purchases require medevac-capable insurance.
- **FX repatriation rules are NOT primary-confirmed** — do not assume free repatriation of sale proceeds/profits; verify at the RBV + IMF AREAER.
- **No dedicated digital-nomad visa**; the headline foreign-resident route is the DSP citizenship donation, not a remote-worker visa.
- **Many primary statute texts are bot-blocked** (PacLII 403, RBV SSL, IMF/EUR-Lex bot-block) — exact constitutional/statute wording + Cap numbers should be confirmed in a browser, not copied from secondary article-number attributions.
