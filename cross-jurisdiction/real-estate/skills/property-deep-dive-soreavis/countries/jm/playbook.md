# Jamaica 🇯🇲 — Property Due-Diligence Playbook

ISO2: `jm`. Status: ✅ Fully populated (researched 2026-05-07).

## Country profile

- **Postcode**: Jamaica has **no nationwide postcode system in active civilian use**. A 1990s "Postal Code" pilot (e.g. `Kingston 5`, `Kingston 8`) is still printed on some addresses, but the **post-2007 alphanumeric system never rolled out**. Addresses rely on `street, district/community, parish` (e.g., "10 Hope Road, Kingston 6, St. Andrew") — verify on visit.
- **Admin levels**: **3 counties** (Cornwall, Middlesex, Surrey — administrative legacy, rarely used in practice) → **14 parishes** → **electoral divisions / districts / communities**. Tourist-relevant parishes: **Kingston / St. Andrew** (capital metropolitan area — KSA), **St. James** (Montego Bay), **Westmoreland** (Negril), **St. Ann** (Ocho Rios, Runaway Bay), **Portland** (Port Antonio), **Hanover** (Tryall, Lucea), **Trelawny** (Falmouth), **Manchester** (Mandeville — diaspora-favoured cool highlands).
- **Currency**: **JMD** (Jamaican dollar). 1 USD ≈ **155–161 JMD** (BOJ daily rate range Sep 2025 – May 2026 — verify daily at `https://boj.org.jm/market/foreign-exchange/`). **Tourist-corridor + high-end transactions widely USD-denominated** (Montego Bay villas, Tryall, Round Hill, Negril cliff condos, Ocho Rios beachfronts, Kingston upper-tier New Kingston / Cherry Gardens / Norbrook). **Local-market and middle-tier housing typically priced in JMD**. Jamaica's exchange-control regime is liberal post-1991 reforms (BOJ Foreign Exchange Act): no FX permit needed for property purchase, but wires of significant size trigger AML / KYC review under **POCA** (Proceeds of Crime Act).
- **Languages**: English (official). Jamaican Patois (Patwa) widely spoken — most legal/conveyancing communication is in standard English.
- **Foreign-ownership freedoms**: 🟢 **Jamaica is one of the few jurisdictions globally with full foreign-purchase parity**. Foreigners face **no restrictions** on residential, commercial, or agricultural land ownership — no border zone, no maritime setback purchase ban, no nationality quotas. Foreign individuals + companies can hold freehold title on equal footing with Jamaican citizens. The only mandatory administrative step is obtaining a **TRN (Taxpayer Registration Number)** from TAJ to be named on title and pay property tax. (Sources: Base Legal Jamaica overview; Mat anda Ra Clarke conveyancing notes — secondary; statutory basis is the **Registration of Titles Act** which makes no nationality distinction.)
- **Cadastre / land registry**:
  - **National Land Agency (NLA)** — Office of Titles / Land Titles Division: `https://www.nla.gov.jm/` — Torrens-style **Registered Title** (under the **Registration of Titles Act**, originally 1889 model from NSW Australia). Online search: **eLandJamaica** `https://elandjamaica.nla.gov.jm/`.
  - **Registrar General's Department (RGD)**: `https://rgd.gov.jm/` — births/deaths/marriages, Common-Law deeds for **unregistered land** (~40 % of Jamaica's land remains unregistered per NLA's Systematic Land Registration Programme).
  - **Survey Department** (within NLA) — pre-checked plans, surveyors registered under **Land Surveyors Act**.
- **Identifier**: **Volume + Folio** (e.g., "Vol. 1234 Folio 567") on a **Certificate of Title** = primary title evidence for registered land. **Common-Law deeds** (a chain of recorded conveyances at RGD) for unregistered land — far weaker, requires possession-based statutory declarations to bring to title.
- **Ownership types**: **freehold** (dominant); **leasehold** (urban commercial common, residential rare; Crown leases inland); **strata title / scheme** under the **Registration (Strata Titles) Act** for condos and gated developments; **joint tenancy / tenancy in common** standard.
- **Recent reforms**:
  - **Stamp Duty Reform 2019** — flat **JMD 5,000 per conveyance** replacing previous ad-valorem stamp duty (TAJ "Stamp Duty Reform" notice, 2019). **Transfer Tax cut 5 % → 2 %** (2019 budget).
  - **Property Tax reform 2017** — nine-band structure on **unimproved land value**; rates from **JMD 1,000 flat to 0.90 %** of UV.
  - **Income Tax Threshold raised** to **JMD 1,902,360 effective 1 April 2026** (was **JMD 1,799,376** from 1 Apr 2025 and **JMD 1,700,088** from 1 Apr 2024) — second of three phased increases announced in the 2025 Budget; **JMD 2,003,496 from 1 April 2027** toward a JMD 2,000,000 target (2026 data, JIS/TAJ 14 Apr 2026 `https://jis.gov.jm/increase-in-income-tax-threshold-now-in-effect`; Revenue Measures 2025-2026, MoFPS).
  - **GCT registration threshold raised** from JMD 10M to **JMD 15M effective 1 April 2025** (KPMG Budget Review 2025).
  - **Hurricane Beryl (Cat 4 landfall 3 July 2024)** + **Hurricane Melissa (Cat 5 landfall 28 Oct 2025, Westmoreland — strongest landfall in Jamaican recorded history)** drove insurance market hardening + post-storm building-code reinforcement debate (NHC TCR AL132025; ODPEM situation reports). **Insurance and HOA reserve-fund implications still evolving 2026** — verify carrier appetite per parish.

### Compute

1. USD/m² = price ÷ **net floor area** (`square feet` is the local convention — convert: 1 m² = 10.76 ft²; JMD-listings often quote `J$/sq ft`).
2. Compare to ≥ 3 active RealEstate.com.jm + RAJ MLS comparables in the **same community + strata/development** (HOA / strata mgmt fees and reserve-fund posture matter more than parish average).
3. **Trap — currency**: confirm whether price is **`USD firm`**, **`USD or JMD equivalent at closing`**, or **JMD-only**. Closing FX shifts can move the deal 1–3 % over a 60–90-day escrow.
4. **Trap — registered vs unregistered land**: an "unregistered" lot held under Common-Law deed at RGD is materially harder to mortgage, insure, and resell than a Registered Title. Verify via NLA eLandJamaica before pricing the deal — see `--risks` for title-due-diligence checklist.
5. **Trap — square-foot definition**: listings quote variously gross floor area, net floor area, "under air," or strata aggregate (incl. balcony / parking). Confirm with strata plan or surveyor's plan.

### Key terms

- **Certificate of Title** — primary registered-title evidence (Registration of Titles Act).
- **Volume + Folio** — title identifier (e.g., "Vol. 1234 Folio 567").
- **Common-Law title / unregistered land** — chain-of-deeds at RGD; weaker than registered title; ~40 % of Jamaica's land remains unregistered (NLA Systematic Land Registration Programme).
- **Surveyor's identification report (ID)** + **pre-checked plan** — boundary verification by a Land Surveyors Board–licensed surveyor.
- **Stamp Duty / Transfer Tax** — see `--tax`.
- **Strata** — condo / scheme common-area regime under **Registration (Strata Titles) Act**.
- **Returning Resident** — diaspora category (lived abroad ≥ 3 yrs, returning permanently); customs concessions on personal effects/vehicle (PICA + Jamaica Customs).

## Section: `--tax`

### Annual property tax

Governed by **Property Tax Act** + **Land Valuation Act**; administered by **TAJ** (collection) and **NLA** (valuation roll).

- **Tax base**: **Unimproved Value (UV)** of the land — the price the land alone would fetch disregarding buildings, crops, improvements (Land Valuation Act). **NOT market price**, **NOT building value**. UV is set by NLA's most recent valuation roll (last general revaluation **2013** — post-2013 valuations apply to subsequent transfers; periodic revaluation pending) — **verify current UV via NLA / TAJ Property Tax Query**.
- **Rate structure (9 bands, 2025-2026, per TAJ Property Tax page)**:

| UV band (JMD) | Annual property tax |
|---|---|
| Up to 400,000 | **JMD 1,000 flat** |
| 400,001 – 800,000 | **0.50 %** of UV |
| 800,001 – 1,500,000 | **0.55 %** |
| 1,500,001 – 3,000,000 | **0.60 %** |
| 3,000,001 – 4,500,000 | **0.65 %** |
| 4,500,001 – 7,000,000 | **0.70 %** |
| 7,000,001 – 12,000,000 | **0.75 %** |
| 12,000,001 – 30,000,000 | **0.80 %** |
| > 30,000,001 | **0.90 %** |

Source: **TAJ Property Tax** `https://www.jamaicatax.gov.jm/property-tax2` + **Local Authorities of Jamaica FAQ** (2025 page) — verify current-year rates each tax year.

- **Filing**: due **1 April annually**; can be paid in full or in two/four instalments. Late-payment penalty + interest. Property tax is shared between **central government (TAJ)** and the **parish council** for parochial services (street lights, garbage, parochial roads).
- **Note**: Jamaica has **no exemption tier for primary residence** as such — UV bands apply uniformly.
- **Equalisation**: because UV (often 2013-base) lags market price materially in tourist / KSA prime zones, **effective property tax as % of market price is very low** — frequently < 0.10 % of market value (computed: UV ÷ market est. ~20-50 % in prime zones [no published UV-to-market ratio — inferred from 2013-base valuation-roll lag] × 0.6-0.9 % UV rate).

### Transfer Tax (one-time at sale) — Transfer Tax Act

- **Rate**: **2 %** of the **gross consideration** (sale price) **or market value, whichever is higher** (TAJ; PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries).
- **Statutory liability**: **vendor pays** (per Transfer Tax Act); commonly **borne by vendor in practice** — distinguish from purchaser-side stamp duty.
- **Refund mechanism**: where transfer tax > **37.5 % of the actual capital gain**, vendor can claim refund of excess (since Jamaica has no separate CGT, this caps the effective burden on low-gain sales — PwC).

### Stamp Duty — Stamp Duty Act

- **Post-2019 reform**: **flat JMD 5,000** per conveyance instrument (TAJ "Stamp Duty Reform" notice 2019), replacing the prior ad-valorem regime.
- **Custom**: **buyer + seller split 50:50** (JMD 2,500 each) — confirm in agreement.
- **Stamping deadline**: **30 days from execution** to avoid penalty (TAJ).

### Closing-cost stack (typical resale, 2025-2026 figures)

| Item | Rate | Who pays (custom) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Transfer Tax** | **2 %** of higher of price/MV | **Vendor** | Statutory + custom both vendor |
| **Stamp Duty** (conveyance) | **JMD 5,000 flat** | Split 50/50 | Reform 2019 |
| **Attorney conveyancing fee** (purchaser-side) | **1.5–3.0 % of price + 15 % GCT on fee** | **Buyer** | Range per Jamaicans.com fees survey + DunnCox FAQ + Sagicor Property; 2-3 % is most-cited middle |
| **Attorney conveyancing fee** (vendor-side) | **1.5–3.0 % + 15 % GCT** | Vendor | Mirror of above |
| **Registration fee (NLA)** | **0.5 %** of consideration | Buyer | Per Sagicor Property; verify current schedule with NLA |
| **Surveyor's identification report** | **JMD 35,000–150,000** | Buyer | Mandatory if title boundaries unverified |
| **Title insurance (optional, growing)** | **0.4–1.0 %** one-time | Buyer | Newer in JM market; protects against title defects |
| **GCT on attorney + agent fees** | **15 %** | Whichever side commissioned | Standard rate (PwC) |
| **Estate agent commission** | **5 % + 15 % GCT** typical | Vendor | RAJ-member typical scale |

> **Buyer-side total mandatory** (excl. agent commission): **~3–5 %** of price (attorney 2-3 % + GCT 0.3-0.45 % + registration 0.5 % + half stamp duty + survey).
> **Vendor-side total mandatory**: **~5-9 %** (transfer tax 2 % + agent 5 % + GCT on agent 0.75 % + attorney 1.5-3 % + GCT on attorney + half stamp duty).

### GCT (General Consumption Tax / VAT)

- **Standard rate**: **15 %** (TAJ; KPMG Budget Review 2025; PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries).
- **Tourism sector**: effective **~10 %** on hotel + accommodation services.
- **Telephone services + handsets**: **25 %**.
- **Real-estate transfers between individuals**: **GCT does not apply on the sale price**; GCT **does apply on legal + agent + valuer + survey fees** at 15 %.
- **GCT registration threshold**: **JMD 15M annual turnover** effective 1 April 2025 (was JMD 10M; KPMG Budget Review 2025).

### Income tax — landlord side (long-term rental income)

- **Resident individual**: **25 % on chargeable income up to JMD 6M**, **30 % above**. (PwC; QuickBooks Global Tax Tables.)
- **Tax-free threshold**: **JMD 1,902,360 from 1 April 2026** (was **JMD 1,799,376** from 1 Apr 2025 and **JMD 1,700,088** from 1 Apr 2024) — second of three phased increases announced in the 2025 Budget, with **JMD 2,003,496 from 1 April 2027** toward a JMD 2,000,000 target (2026 data, JIS/TAJ 14 Apr 2026 `https://jis.gov.jm/increase-in-income-tax-threshold-now-in-effect`; Revenue Measures 2025-2026, MoFPS; JIS notice).
- ⚠️ **Threshold steps on 1 April, but income tax is assessed on the calendar year** — the blended tax-free amount for **calendar 2026 is JMD 1,876,614**, not the JMD 1,902,360 headline. Use the blended figure in any full-year landlord computation (JIS/TAJ 14 Apr 2026).
- **Pensioner + Golden Ager (65+) exemptions**: **JMD 250,040 each**, on top of the threshold — a 65+ pensioner landlord is **JMD 2,376,654 tax-free in 2026** (combined figure as published by JIS/TAJ 14 Apr 2026; re-derive against TAJ before relying on it to the dollar).
- **Resident company**: **25 % CIT** (regulated companies — large unregulated may be 33⅓ % under different schedules; verify with PwC for the entity type).
- **Non-resident landlord**: **withholding tax 25 %** on gross rent (subject to applicable double-tax treaty — UK, Canada, USA, Germany, etc., may reduce; PwC).

### Capital gains

- **Jamaica has no capital gains tax** (PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries; KDH Chartered Accountants).
- **Transfer Tax 2 % is the de-facto gain tax** on real-estate transfers.
- **Refund** available where Transfer Tax exceeds **37.5 % of actual gain** — preventing punitive impact on low-gain transfers.

### Estate / inheritance

- **No estate or inheritance tax** as such; **Transfer Tax 1.5 %** applies on death-transfer of real estate (TAJ Estate Tax page; KDH; PwC). Distinct from inter-vivos 2 %.
- **Stamp duty** applies on probate/letters of administration (sliding scale on estate value).

## Section: `--rental`

### Long-term residential rentals

Governed by the **Rent Restriction Act** (1944, since amended) — applies primarily to lower-rent dwellings (controlled-rent thresholds under the **Rent Assessment Board**). Most market-rate rentals fall outside the rent-controlled tier and follow common-law contracts. Standard 1-year tenancies, security deposit 1-3 months. Tenant protection moderate; possession proceedings via **Resident Magistrate's Court (Parish Court)** — slow (typically 6-18 months for a contested eviction).

### Long-term rental tax (resident landlord)

- **Individual**: **25 % up to JMD 6M chargeable, 30 % above** on net rental income (gross less allowable expenses: management, agent, repairs, property tax, insurance, mortgage interest). **Tax-free threshold JMD 1,902,360 from 1 Apr 2026** (blended **JMD 1,876,614** for the calendar-2026 assessment — see `--tax`; JIS/TAJ 14 Apr 2026).
- **Company**: **25 %** CIT (regulated company); verify rate per entity type via PwC Jamaica.
- **Non-resident landlord**: **25 % withholding** on gross rent (subject to double-tax treaty relief).
- **GCT 15 %**: long-term residential rentals **exempt**; commercial + short-stay tourism rentals **subject** at 15 % standard or **10 %** tourism-effective.

### Short-term rentals (Airbnb / villa rental)

Regulatory status (verified 2026-05):

- **Tourism Product Development Company (TPDCo)**: `https://www.tpdco.org/` — licenses **villas + apartments + small hotels** under the **Tourist Board Act** + **Tourist Establishment Regulations**. **Villa rentals require TPDCo licence + Jamaica Tourist Board (JTB) inspection** (fire safety, sanitation).
- **Strata / HOA reality**: in Tryall, Round Hill, Half Moon, Coral Gardens, Drax Hall, Sandcastles strata communities, the **strata corporation by-laws are the binding constraint** — many forbid <30-day rentals, require central rental-pool participation, or charge a transient-rental levy. **Read the strata by-laws + 3 yrs of strata financials before pricing a short-let yield model**.
- **Tax**: **GCT at 10 % effective** on accommodation (tourism); income tax on net at landlord schedule; non-resident WHT 25 % gross.
- **2024-2026 trend**: TPDCo enforcement of villa-licensing tightening post-Beryl + post-Melissa, with insurance carriers demanding TPDCo registration as condition of cover.

### Strategic notes

- **Highest STR yield zones**: **Montego Bay tourism corridor (Rose Hall / Ironshore / Coral Gardens)**, **Negril**, **Ocho Rios resort condos** — branded rental pools (Half Moon, Round Hill, Tryall) historically deliver 4-7 % net yields per published manager statements (secondary; verify per project).
- **Negril West End cliffs**: high seasonality (Dec-Apr peak), exposure to Atlantic-side hurricane risk recently demonstrated (Beryl 2024 + Melissa 2025).
- **Port Antonio / Frenchman's Cove**: boutique market, lower absolute volume; premium rates in season.
- **Kingston STR**: nascent; mostly business-traveller Cherry Gardens / New Kingston flats; 2-3 % gross yield typical (secondary).
- **Mandeville / inland highland**: weak STR demand — diaspora long-stay rather than tourist short-let.
- **TPDCo licence + strata by-law clearance + HOA approval + insurance binder** = the four boxes to tick before pricing a yield model.

## Section: `--work=<profession>`

### Job platforms

- **CaribbeanJobs.com** + **JamaicaJobs.com** + **Splash Jamaica Jobs** — generic; **LinkedIn Jamaica** active for finance / tech / tourism / BPO; **Ministry of Labour and Social Security**: `https://mlss.gov.jm/`.

### Self-employment

- **TRN (Taxpayer Registration Number)** required for invoicing.
- **Sole trader** common; **Limited company** (Jamaica Companies Act 2004) easy to incorporate via **COJ (Companies Office of Jamaica)**: `https://www.orcjamaica.com/`.
- **GCT registration** mandatory above **JMD 15M turnover** (from 1 April 2025).
- **Profession-specific licensing** via colleges / boards (Medical Council, Bar Council via General Legal Council, ICAJ for accountants, BoEJ for engineers, RAJ for real estate, etc.). **Foreign-degree recognition** via University Council of Jamaica (UCJ) for certain professions.

### Foreign worker

- **Work Permit** via **Ministry of Labour and Social Security** (MLSS): `https://mlss.gov.jm/`. **CARICOM Skilled-Nationals** (10 categorised professions) get free movement under CSME — no work permit needed. **Otherwise**: employer-sponsored permit; renewable annually.
- **Returning Resident** category (PICA) — diaspora / 2nd-gen Jamaicans with documented prior residence get customs concessions on personal goods + a smoother route to permanent residence.
- **Permanent Residence** (PICA: `https://www.pica.gov.jm/immigration/permanent-residence`): typical pathway is **3 years on temporary residence permit → permanent residence application** (often 3-6 month processing if documents complete; in practice can stretch to 12-24 months — anecdotal forum reports). Retirees demonstrate financial self-sufficiency (pension + bank statements).
- **Citizenship**: Jamaica has **no formal Citizenship-by-Investment programme**. Naturalisation typically requires 5 years + good character (Citizenship Act).
- **Visa-free for tourism**: 90-180 days for many nationalities (US, UK, Canada, EU all visa-free entry — verify per nationality at PICA).

### Salary benchmarks (2024-2025)

> Source: STATIN Labour Force Survey + MLSS minimum-wage notices; verify current at `https://statinja.gov.jm/` + `https://mlss.gov.jm/`.

- **National Minimum Wage**: **JMD 16,000 / week** (general, effective 1 June 2024; verify current with MLSS).
- **Median monthly gross (urban formal sector, 2024)**: ~**JMD 90,000–180,000** (~USD 580–1,150 at 2026 FX) — STATIN aggregates; secondary.
- **Kingston upper-tier corporate / banking / tech**: **USD 1,500–5,000/mo** professional roles (LinkedIn Jamaica + employer disclosures — secondary).
- **BPO / contact-centre** (Montego Bay Free Zone, Portmore, Kingston): rapidly growing sector; **USD 400–1,000/mo** typical (industry reports — secondary).
- **Tourism / hospitality (Montego Bay / Negril / Ocho Rios)**: tipped roles + service charge multiply base materially.

### Catchment heuristics

- Within 30 min of **Kingston Metropolitan Area (KMA = Kingston + St. Andrew + parts of St. Catherine)** = full job market (finance, government, legal, BPO, tech, education).
- Within 30 min of **Montego Bay**, **Spanish Town**, **Portmore**: BPO + tourism + retail.
- Within 30 min of **Ocho Rios / Mandeville / May Pen**: mid-tier formal employment.
- **Negril / Port Antonio / inland rural**: thin formal salaried market; expat / returnee self-employment + remote work dominant.
- **Inland mountain (Cockpit Country, Blue Mountains, Trelawny interior, St. Mary deep rural)**: subsistence + agriculture; very thin formal employment.

### Hurricane (Atlantic basin)

- **Season**: **June 1 – November 30**, **peak Aug-Oct**. Jamaica sits in the central-Caribbean cyclone path; long historical record of major impacts.
- **Recent landfalls**:
  - **Hurricane Beryl** — Cat 4 landfall **3 July 2024**, southern coast; worst in **St. Elizabeth, Clarendon, Manchester, Westmoreland, Hanover** (UNOCHA + ReliefWeb situation reports; ACAPS country profile July 2024).
  - **Hurricane Melissa** — **Cat 5 landfall 28 Oct 2025 between Belmont and New Hope, Westmoreland**, sustained winds 185 mph, central pressure 897 mbar (NHC TCR AL132025). **Strongest landfall in Jamaican recorded history**. **~150,000 structures damaged**, **~120,000 roofs lost**, **~24,000 buildings totalled**, **40-50 % of hotels damaged**, **40 % of public health system damaged** (CNN; World Vision; International Medical Corps 6-month update). Worst-hit parishes: **St. Elizabeth, Westmoreland, St. James, Trelawny, Hanover**.
- **Historical reference**: **Gilbert 1988** (catastrophic Cat 3 cross-island traverse, baseline for code revisions), **Ivan 2004** (passed S, drove damage), **Dean 2007** (passed S), **Dennis + Emily 2005** (parallel passes), **Sandy 2012** (E coast).
- **Building-code implication**: post-Gilbert 1988 + **JNBC 1995** + **JNBC 2018 / 2021** revisions tightened wind provisions (ASCE 7-derived). **Resort-grade post-2010 builds materially safer** than 1970s-1980s middle-class housing. **Hurricane Melissa 2025 will drive further code review** — track Bureau of Standards Jamaica + ODPEM publications through 2026-2028.
- **Insurance**: hurricane peril typically **bundled with all-risk fire** in homeowner policies; **CAT deductibles 2-5 % of sum insured**; **rates ~0.4-1.0 % of insured value/yr** est. (Jamaica's **General Insurance Association of Jamaica (IAJ)** + **Financial Services Commission (FSC)** market reports — secondary). **Post-Melissa carrier appetite hardening across west-coast parishes; premium increases + sublimit tightening expected through 2026-2027** — verify current carrier panel + windstorm sublimits on the specific parish before closing.

### Earthquake / seismic

- **Tectonic context**: Jamaica sits on the **Gonâve microplate** between the North America + Caribbean plates; **Enriquillo–Plantain Garden Fault Zone (EPGFZ)** runs onshore through Jamaica's south side (segments include the **Plantain Garden Fault** in the east) and offshore north of Hispaniola; **same fault zone produced the Haiti 2010 M7.0**.
- **Major historical**: **Port Royal 1692 M7.5+** (catastrophic, sank the Port Royal peninsula); **Kingston 1907 M6.5** (massive damage to colonial Kingston, ~800-1,000 deaths); **Montego Bay 1957 M6.4**; minor ongoing seismicity monitored by **Earthquake Unit UWI Mona**.
- **Hazard zoning**: MGD + UWI Earthquake Unit publish probabilistic seismic-hazard maps; **Kingston metro + south coast + Montego Bay** are highest-hazard. **JNBC seismic provisions** (post-2018) require ductile detailing for new RC structures.
- **Pre-1907 colonial-era buildings** (where any survive): masonry-bearing-wall vulnerable; **pre-1990 unreinforced-masonry residences**: high seismic risk.

### Flood

- **High-risk basins**: **Hope River + Mona basin** (Kingston metro), **Rio Cobre** (St. Catherine — Spanish Town, Bog Walk gorge), **Black River** (St. Elizabeth — Lower Black River wetland), **Rio Grande** (Portland), **Great River** (St. James / Hanover), **Wag Water + Yallahs** (St. Andrew east).
- **Urban Kingston flash-flooding**: chronic in **Half-Way-Tree gully system, Sandy Gully, Trafalgar gully**, low-lying **Greater Portmore** following heavy rain.
- **Coastal storm surge**: **Kingston Harbour rim, Port Royal, Hellshire, Negril 7-mile beach back-shore, Treasure Beach (St. Elizabeth), Black River town, Old Harbour Bay, Falmouth historic district, Port Antonio**.
- **WRA** publishes basin-level flood maps + **ODPEM** publishes parish hazard profiles — **no consolidated public parcel-level flood-zone web viewer** equivalent to FEMA NFHL or UK EA flood map. **Verification path**: request from WRA/ODPEM per-parcel review OR consult parish council Development Order + St. Andrew / Kingston Metropolitan Region Master Plan.

### Landslide / mass movement

- **Blue Mountains, John Crow Mountains, Cockpit Country, Don Figuerero Mountains, Santa Cruz Mountains, Mocho Mountains** — chronic landslide risk during hurricane / tropical-storm rains. **MGD susceptibility maps** + USGS post-Melissa landslide-hazard mapping `https://www.usgs.gov/programs/landslide-hazards/science/2025-hurricane-melissa-landslide-hazards`.
- **Hillside Kingston** (Jacks Hill, Beverly Hills, Stony Hill, Red Hills, Skyline Drive): localised slope-instability following extreme rainfall — verify retaining-wall + drainage history for each parcel.

### Climate change projections (2050 horizon)

- **Hurricane intensity** trending up (more Cat 4-5 share) — IPCC AR6 + NOAA Atlantic basin assessments. Beryl 2024 + Melissa 2025 align with this trajectory.
- **Sea-level rise**: **+20-50 cm by 2100** (RCP4.5–8.5 range); **acute exposure** in Kingston Harbour rim, Port Royal, Hellshire, Negril 7-mile beach back-shore, Treasure Beach, Black River — IPCC AR6 + Climate Central Coastal Risk Screening Tool.
- **Drought**: south coast (St. Elizabeth, Manchester south, Clarendon south) + Hellshire increasingly arid; **NWC water-supply curtailments** during 2014-2015, 2019-2020 droughts demonstrate sensitivity.
- **Coral / marine ecosystem stress** affecting beach sand replenishment economics — north-coast tourism corridor.

## Section: `--mains`

### Water + sewer operator

- **National Water Commission (NWC)** — `https://www.nwcjamaica.com/` — single national operator for urban water + sewer (publicly owned). **Office of Utilities Regulation (OUR)** regulates: `https://our.org.jm/`.
- **Rural / un-served areas**: **parish councils** + community-managed systems share rural-water provision (Commonwealth Governance reference).
- **Sewer reality**:
  - **Urban Kingston (Half-Way-Tree, New Kingston, Liguanea, Mona, Cherry Gardens, Beverly Hills core)** + **Spanish Town centre** + **Portmore** + **central Montego Bay** + **central Mandeville** + **Ocho Rios resort core** + **Negril resort strip**: **NWC mains sewer + water typically reticulated** (verify per address — rapid expansion gaps exist on the periphery).
  - **Resort communities** (Tryall, Round Hill, Half Moon, Sandals/Beaches resorts, GoldenEye, Frenchman's Cove): often **project-internal package treatment plants** with bulk water supply; standard for high-end gated developments.
  - **Rural Westmoreland / St. Elizabeth / Trelawny / St. Mary / Portland / hillside St. Andrew**: **septic tank (`soak-away` / `absorption pit`) + water tank (`black tank` rooftop or ground cistern)** is standard, not mains. Tank fed by **NWC truck delivery**, **shallow well**, or **rainwater-harvesting roof catchment**.
  - **Listing language**: "tank, pump and soak-away" or "rainwater catchment" = **not on NWC mains**; "NWC mains" or "fully serviced" = mains (verify with NWC).
- **Potability**: NWC water is **treated and meets WHO drinking-water standards** at point of leaving the treatment plant — but **distribution-network age + rooftop tank stagnation** mean most middle-class households **filter or boil drinking water**. **Bottled water** widespread.

### Power

- **Single distributor**: **Jamaica Public Service Company Limited (JPS)** — `https://www.jpsco.com/` — owns + operates transmission + distribution; serves **~701,670 customers (2024 figure per JPS / OUR)**. Regulator: **OUR**.
- **Reliability**: **historically variable** — outages from grid faults + tropical storms common. **Post-Hurricane Melissa Oct 2025**: JPS spent ~6-10 weeks restoring service across worst-hit western parishes (JPS + JIS situation reports). **Inverter + battery backup** ("UPS" or "solar + battery") increasingly standard for any middle-class urban household and most resort properties.
- **Tariff**: **published by OUR** — residential block-tariff structure; **~USD 0.30-0.45 / kWh all-in** (2025 est., per OUR + JPS rate cards — verify current; among the higher Caribbean rates per OUR/JPS).

## Active fiscal incentives + visa programs (2025-2026)

### Tax-incentive regimes

- **No CBI (Citizenship-by-Investment)** — Jamaica unlike St. Kitts / Antigua / Dominica / Grenada / St. Lucia does **not** offer a CBI programme.
- **No bespoke "Pensionado" tax regime** comparable to Costa Rica or Panama. Returning Residents get **customs concessions** (PICA + Jamaica Customs) on personal effects + one motor vehicle, but no bespoke property-tax exemption.
- **Charities Act + Approved Tourism Project** designations (Tourist Board Act / TPDCo) provide construction-side incentives (customs-duty + GCT relief on imported materials) for **registered hotel + attraction projects** — niche for residential-buyer perspective.
- **Income tax threshold raised** to **JMD 1,902,360** from 1 Apr 2026 (JMD 2,003,496 scheduled from 1 Apr 2027) + **Reverse Income Tax Credit JMD 20,000** for low-paid taxpayers (JIS/TAJ 14 Apr 2026; JIS Get-the-Facts; KPMG Budget Review 2025).

### Residency / citizenship pathways

- **Visa-free entry**: 90-180 days for US, UK, Canada, EU nationals (verify per nationality at PICA `https://www.pica.gov.jm/`).
- **Returning Resident** (PICA): Jamaican-national diaspora resident abroad ≥ 3 years; customs concessions + simplified residence registration. **Documented at consulate of last residence + PICA on arrival.**
- **Permanent Residence** (PICA): 3 years on temporary residence permit → permanent residence application; financial-self-sufficiency review; processing **3-6 months published, often 12-24 months in practice** (anecdotal — verify with PICA).
- **Naturalisation** (Citizenship Act): **5 years residence + good character** — standard pathway (no investment fast-track).
- **CARICOM Skilled Nationals**: 10 categorised professions get free movement under **CARICOM Single Market & Economy (CSME)** — no work permit needed.
- **Marriage to Jamaican citizen**: spousal residence registration with PICA.

## Caveats unique to JM

- **🟢 No foreign-ownership restrictions** — Jamaica is one of the few jurisdictions globally with full foreign-purchase parity. Foreign individuals + companies buy on equal footing with citizens; no border zone, no maritime setback purchase ban, no nationality quotas. Only mandatory step is obtaining a **TRN**. (Statutory basis: Registration of Titles Act, no nationality distinction.)
- **Title chain — Registered vs Unregistered land** is the **single biggest JM-specific risk**. ~40 % of Jamaican land remains unregistered (NLA Systematic Land Registration Programme). **Common-Law title** = chain of recorded deeds at RGD, materially weaker than Registered Title; bringing-to-title via NLA can take **12-36 months** + additional cost (statutory declarations from 2 long-term knowers of the land per Registration of Titles Act). **Always verify Vol/Folio via eLandJamaica + insist on Registered Title at closing**, or price the conversion cost into the deal.
- **Surveyor's identification + pre-checked plan** essential — historic boundaries can disagree with modern GPS; informal access tracks across neighbours' parcels are widespread.
- **Hurricane Melissa Oct 2025 aftermath**: **west-coast parishes (Westmoreland, St. Elizabeth, St. James, Trelawny, Hanover) materially affected** — distressed re-listings + insurance hardening + roof-retrofit market. **Verify post-storm structural integrity report + insurance binder + carrier appetite for the specific parish** before pricing any deal in west-coast tourism corridor through 2026-2027.
- **Insurance market hardening 2026**: post-Melissa carriers tightening windstorm sublimits + raising CAT deductibles + pulling out of some west-coast parishes. **Get insurance binder confirmed BEFORE waiving the financing/insurance contingency**.
- **JMD vs USD pricing** — confirm `USD firm` vs `JMD or USD equivalent at closing` in the Sale Agreement; FX shifts during 60-90 day escrow can move the wire 1-3 %.
- **Strata reserve-fund discipline is uneven** in older condos. Insist on **3 years strata financials + reserve-fund statement + by-laws + post-Melissa special-assessment status**. Special assessments for roof retrofits + insurance shortfalls are common 2026.
- **Power reliability + backup cost**: budget USD 8-25k for solar + inverter + battery on any property unless a resort with project-grade backup. JPS rates are among the higher in the Caribbean (~USD 0.30-0.45/kWh).
- **Crime by parish varies dramatically** (see `--crime`): national homicide rate is high (~40/100k 2024), but distribution is **highly concentrated in specific Kingston / St. James / St. Catherine / Clarendon postal areas**; resort-corridor + most upper-tier residential communities are materially insulated. **STATIN + JCF Statistics + UNODC** are the only reliable primary sources — avoid forum / aggregator extrapolations.
- **AML — POCA (Proceeds of Crime Act)**: source-of-funds declaration required for property purchases > thresholds; **closing attorneys are gatekeepers** under POCA + DNFBP guidance.
- **Returning Resident** customs benefits (PICA + Jamaica Customs) — diaspora buyers should claim concessions on personal effects + 1 vehicle on first repatriation.
- **No Citizenship-by-Investment** (unlike Caribbean neighbours St. Kitts, Antigua, Dominica, Grenada, St. Lucia). Permanent residence requires 3 years temp + financial review.
- **Squatter / adverse possession**: **12 years adverse possession** can convert occupants to legal owners under the **Limitation of Actions Act**. Vacant rural land buyers must verify possession history; walk the boundary; talk to neighbours; check fence-lines.
- **Restrictive covenants** common on subdivision titles — single-family-only clauses, height limits, race-restrictive covenants (legacy, unenforceable but on title) — review carefully. Modification via Restrictive Covenants (Discharge & Modification) Act.
- **Heritage + protected coastline**: **Jamaica National Heritage Trust (JNHT)** lists Port Royal, parts of Falmouth historic district, Spanish Town colonial core; **NEPA** controls beach licences + coastal-zone permits. Beach access in Jamaica is contested (**Beach Control Act**) — public access not as automatic as in some jurisdictions.

## Quirks to know

- **Volume + Folio** is the unique title identifier; physical Certificate of Title document under the Registration of Titles Act = the title evidence; **eLandJamaica online search** is the modern verification path.
- **Common-Law title is NOT a Registered Title**; if buying unregistered land, price + plan for the **bringing-to-title** process (statutory declarations + NLA registrar review, 12-36 months).
- **Returning Resident status (PICA)** is a structured concession category — diaspora 2nd-gen buyers should explore the documentation.
- **Strata / scheme by-laws bind the buyer** — read carefully for STR restrictions, pet rules, exterior-modification rules, reserve-fund posture.
- **Notary / Justice of the Peace** can witness statutory declarations + many routine documents — distinct from attorney conveyancing.
- **Tax Compliance Certificate (TCC)** from TAJ — required by vendor for Transfer Tax clearance; verify status.
- **Beach Control Act** — coastal property does NOT automatically include beach licence; NEPA permits required for any beach-side construction.
- **Mortgage market**: NCB, JN Bank, Scotiabank Jamaica, Sagicor Bank, JMMB Bank offer residential mortgages (typically 15-30 year, JMD or USD variants); **diaspora-friendly mortgage products** marketed by NCB + JN Bank + Scotiabank (Jamaica-Homes editorial). Loan-to-value typically 80-90 % for owner-occupiers; mortgage interest rates JMD ~10-13 % / USD ~6-9 % range 2026 (verify current with bank).
- **TRN (Taxpayer Registration Number)** — mandatory for all named on title (including foreign buyers); apply at TAJ office or online.
