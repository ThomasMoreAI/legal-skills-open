# Curaçao 🇨🇼 — Property Due-Diligence Playbook

ISO2: `cw`. Status: ✅ Researched 2026-05-27 (Confidence: MEDIUM — see Status footer).

## Country profile

- **Constitutional status**: **constituent country (*land*) within the Kingdom of the Netherlands** since **10-10-10** — i.e., 10 October 2010, when the Netherlands Antilles was dissolved by *Rijkswet wijziging Statuut* (Kingdom Charter amendment). Curaçao + Sint Maarten became autonomous countries within the Kingdom; Bonaire, Sint Eustatius, Saba (BES) became special-status municipalities of the Netherlands proper. Curaçao has its own constitution (*Staatsregeling*), parliament (*Staten van Curaçao*), government, judiciary (shared *Common Court of Justice* with Aruba/Sint Maarten/BES + own *Gerecht in eerste aanleg*), tax authority (*Belastingdienst Curaçao*), land registry (*Kadaster Curaçao*), and central bank (*Centrale Bank van Curaçao en Sint Maarten — CBCS*, shared with Sint Maarten)
- **Postcode**: Curaçao does **NOT use postal codes** for domestic addressing (per Universal Postal Union — small-island convention shared with Aruba, Cayman, BVI). Addresses are *street + number + district (wijk) + island*. For international mail, "Curaçao" + island name suffices
- **Admin levels**: country → **8 districts (wijken)** for statistical purposes: Willemstad (capital, comprising *Punda* + *Otrobanda* + *Pietermaai* + *Scharloo* + *Mahuma* etc.), Banda Abou (west — Westpunt, Lagun, Soto, Barber, Tera Kòrá), Banda Ariba (east — Spaanse Water, Jan Thiel, Caracasbaai), and central districts. **Not a federation** — no municipal layer below national; *eilandgebied* / island-territory layer was abolished at 10-10-10
- **Population**: **~152,000** (2024 est., Centraal Bureau voor de Statistiek Curaçao — `https://www.cbs.cw/`). Verify latest at CBS (Census 2023 results being published in waves through 2024–2026)
- **Surface**: **444 km²** (vs Aruba 180 km², Sint Maarten 87 km²); ~60 km long × ~12 km wide; **southern Caribbean (~12°N)** — **outside the main Atlantic hurricane belt** (see `--risks`)
- **Currency**: **XCG — Caribbean guilder** (introduced **31 March 2025**, replacing the **ANG Netherlands Antillean guilder**; ANG ceased to be legal tender **1 July 2025** after a co-circulation period — per Centrale Bank van Curaçao en Sint Maarten, `https://www.centralbank.cw/`). **Pegged at USD 1 = XCG 1.79** (identical peg to predecessor ANG). 1 ANG = 1 XCG exchange rate during transition; ANG exchangeable at par at commercial banks until **31 March 2026**, then at CBCS for 29 more years (until **31 March 2055**). Shared currency with Sint Maarten only (Aruba uses AWG; BES uses USD)
- **Languages**: **Dutch** (official, government/courts/notarial), **Papiamentu** (official, daily use, Afro-Iberian creole — also written in legislation since 2007), **English** (de facto third language, business/tourism). Spanish widely understood (Venezuela ~65 km south). Notarial *akte van levering* drafted in **Dutch**; sworn translation needed for non-Dutch-speaking buyers
- **Cadastre + Land Registry**: **Kadaster Curaçao** (`https://www.kadaster.cw/`) — single combined cadastre + public land registry (different from NL model where Kadaster also handles cartography but cadaster-types unified). Located Pres. Romulo Bethancourt Blvd. #4, Willemstad. **Digital portal**: `https://digitale-balie.kadaster.cw/` for online certificate requests
- **Identifier**: *kadastrale aanduiding* (cadastral designation) — *sectie* + *nummer* (e.g. "Sectie K nr 1234")
- **Key documents**: *akte van levering* (notarial transfer deed), *eigendomsbewijs* (title certificate), *kadastraal uittreksel* (cadastral extract), *hypotheekakte* (mortgage deed)
- **Civil-law system**: Curaçao operates its **own Burgerlijk Wetboek (BW Curaçao)** — derivative of Dutch BW with Curaçao-specific amendments; civil/commercial law continuity from pre-2010 Netherlands Antilles BW. Property law (Book 3 + 5 BW) governs *eigendom*, *erfpacht*, *opstal*, *vruchtgebruik*
- **Foreign-buyer regime (CRITICAL)**: **no restrictions** on foreign acquisition. Non-residents enjoy identical property rights to citizens; **no alien landholding licence**, **no minimum nationality threshold**, **no government approval required** for residential or commercial real estate (verify per parcel with notary — some defence-strategic / coastal parcels may have specific use restrictions). See Foreign-buyer rules section below
- **EU status**: Curaçao is an **OCT (Overseas Countries and Territories)** of the EU per **Annex II TFEU** — NOT part of the EU customs union, NOT part of Schengen, NOT subject to EU VAT/AML directives directly (Curaçao implements equivalent regimes via own legislation). Dutch citizens (and other EU citizens travelling with Dutch passports) enjoy automatic right of entry; non-EU citizens follow Curaçao visa rules

### Compute

1. USD/m² = price / bruto vloeroppervlak per Kadaster *uittreksel*
2. Premium drivers: **ocean view / oceanfront** parcel, **eigendom (freehold) vs erfpacht (leasehold)** distinction (eigendom premium typically +20–30%), **UNESCO heritage Willemstad core** (scarcity), **Jan Thiel / Spaanse Water gated** (security + community amenity)
3. Discount drivers: **erfpacht** with <30 years remaining (mortgage friction), **inland rural** (longer commute to Willemstad business district + Hato airport), **pre-1985 stock with construction-era issues** (slab cracking from reactive soils, salt-air corrosion of rebar), **flood-prone wadi parcels** (rare but real — see `--risks`)

### Key terms

- *Eigendom* = freehold (full ownership of land + buildings)
- *Erfpacht* = long lease from government (typically 60 years; *canon* annual rent)
- *Huurgrond* = short-term government rental land (least secure)
- *Akte van levering* = notarial transfer deed
- *Kadastraal uittreksel* = cadastral extract
- *Onroerendezaakbelasting (OZB)* = annual property tax (see `--tax`)
- *Overdrachtsbelasting* = transfer tax (4%, see `--tax`)

## Section: `--tax`

### Annual property tax — Onroerendezaakbelasting (OZB)

Curaçao's recurring property tax is the **OZB (Onroerendezaakbelasting)**, which **replaced the prior *grondbelasting* (land tax) on 1 January 2014** — per Belastingdienst Curaçao, `https://ozb.belastingdienst.cw/`.

- **Tax base**: market value (*waarde in het economisch verkeer*) of immovable property — applies to both built (houses, apartments, commercial) and unbuilt (vacant land) immovable property situated in Curaçao
- **Valuation cycle**: **5-year cycle** — value fixed at start of *tijdvak* (period) and applied for 5 years. First cycle 2014–2018; current cycle 2024–2028 (verify at `https://ozb.belastingdienst.cw/`)
- **Tariff schedule (per Belastingdienst Curaçao, 2025 data)**:

| Bracket (value in ANG/XCG) | Tax |
|---|---|
| Up to **350,000** | **0.4%** of value (variable component only; no fixed component) |
| **350,000 – 750,000** | ANG/XCG **1,400 fixed** + **0.5%** on the slice above 350,000 |
| **Above 750,000** | Higher bracket — verify current rate at `https://ozb.belastingdienst.cw/` *(rates have been adjusted; treat as `est.` indicative pending direct primary verification)* |

⚠️ The exact 3rd-bracket marginal rate is **NOT cleanly stated in publicly accessible English** as of 2026-05; verify directly at Belastingdienst Curaçao OZB portal (`https://ozb.belastingdienst.cw/`) or via the *Tarieven calculator* offered on the site.

- **Filing obligation**: at purchase or any ownership change, the owner must file an **OZB declaration within 2 months** stating name, address, estimated value (per Belastingdienst Curaçao OZB portal)
- **Exemptions**: certain charitable / religious / diplomatic / government-owned properties; primary residence is **NOT exempt** in Curaçao (different from many EU regimes — second-home and primary-residence stock both bear OZB)
- **Practical typical cost**: a mid-tier residential at ANG/XCG 500,000 value (~USD 280k): OZB ≈ ANG 1,400 + 0.5% × 150,000 = ANG **2,150 (~USD 1,200) per year**. For higher-value properties, scale upward by the bracket schedule

### Transaction tax — Overdrachtsbelasting (4%)

- **Overdrachtsbelasting** (real estate transfer tax) = **4% of the purchase price** of the property, paid by the **buyer** at the *akte van levering* (notarial deed), withheld and remitted by the notary to Belastingdienst Curaçao
- Cross-referenced across multiple secondary sources (Moret Real Estate, eenhuisinhetbuitenland.nl, At Home Curaçao, Century 21, Affidata, Fung-A-Loi & Samandar Notarissen, Acacia Estate March 2025 tax brochure) — **consistent 4% rate**. The 2025 Acacia Estate tax brochure (`https://acaciaestatecuracao.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Tax-Brochure-CUR-March-2025.pdf`) is the most recent professional advisory aggregation
- ⚠️ **2026-05 status**: the Belastingdienst Curaçao primary URL for the overdrachtsbelasting tariff schedule is not cleanly indexed in publicly accessible English-language form (HTTP 403 on direct Kadaster fetch attempt during research). Multi-source professional convergence on **4%** is **HIGH confidence**; verify at the *akte van levering* via the notary's pre-deed *kostenoverzicht* (cost overview)
- Curaçao Rekenkamer (Court of Audit) issued a 2024–2025 critique of Belastingdienst Curaçao's overdrachtsbelasting collection (per `https://curacao.nu/`) — administrative remediation in progress; no indication of rate change

### VAT-equivalent — Omzetbelasting (OB)

- **Omzetbelasting (OB)** = sales tax / turnover tax; standard rate **6%** (general) / 7% (some categories) / 9% (luxury, telecom etc.) — verify current at Belastingdienst Curaçao
- **Residential resale between private parties**: **NOT subject to OB** (out of scope; only overdrachtsbelasting + notarial costs apply)
- **New-build first sale by developer**: **OB applies in addition to overdrachtsbelasting** in some structures — verify per deal with notary + commercialista; sometimes a *vrijstelling* (exemption) applies when overdrachtsbelasting paid

### Capital gains

- Personal-level capital gains on real estate held by individuals **not engaged in real-estate trade as a profession**: **typically not taxed** in Curaçao as a separate CGT (no standalone capital-gains tax for non-professional sellers; gains fall outside the inkomstenbelasting *box* structure for non-business holdings) — verify per deal with commercialista
- Holdings via Curaçao companies: gains are corporate-tax-base income; corporate tax (*winstbelasting*) typically **22%** (verify currency); special regimes apply for Curaçao Investment Companies (CIC) etc.
- **Profit-driven flips** by individuals: may be deemed business income; verify per deal

### Personal income tax — Inkomstenbelasting

- Progressive scale (resident individuals 2025): brackets **9.75% → 15% → 23% → 30% → 38.75% → 46.5%** (top bracket above ~ANG 159,000 taxable income — verify schedule at Belastingdienst Curaçao for current band thresholds)
- Rental income → progressive inkomstenbelasting (Box 1-equivalent); deductions: maintenance, mortgage interest (subject to current rule), OZB on the rented property, management fees
- DTA network: bilateral tax treaties with Netherlands (*Belastingregeling voor het Koninkrijk* — BRK, recently modernised), Norway, Malta etc. — coordinate with home-country DTA

### Future risk

- **Belastingdienst Curaçao overdrachtsbelasting compliance reform** (per Rekenkamer 2024–2025 critique) — administrative remediation in progress; no indication of rate change in 2026 budget
- **OB rate revisions** historically possible in annual budget cycle — verify at point of deal
- **2024–2028 OZB valuation cycle** is the current band; next revaluation 2029 begins from market values fixed 1 January 2029

## Section: `--rental`

### Long-term residential

- Civil-law tenancy framework under **Burgerlijk Wetboek Curaçao Book 7** (Huurrecht title) — broadly aligned with Dutch tenancy structure but Curaçao-specific calibrations
- Lease structures: 1-year, 2-year, indefinite (*onbepaalde tijd*); tenant protections moderate — landlord can terminate at end of agreed term but indefinite-term tenancies require statutory grounds (urgent personal use, breach, etc.)
- Deposit (*waarborgsom*): typically **1–3 months' rent**; mandatory return at end of tenancy minus deductions
- Rental income → progressive inkomstenbelasting for resident landlords; non-resident landlords on Curaçao-source rental income subject to Curaçao tax (verify coordination with home-country DTA)

### Short-let (vacation rental / Airbnb)

- **No comprehensive national short-let licensing regime** as of 2026-05 (in contrast to NL / EU practice which has tightened materially). Curaçao Tourist Board (*Curaçao Tourist Board — CTB*) coordinates tourism policy; commercial vacation rental operators register as business at Kamer van Koophandel + Belastingdienst Curaçao
- **Practice**: most short-let operators run as informal sole-trader vacation rental; **OB applies** to short-stay accommodation revenue (analogous to hotel turnover); tourist tax (*toeristenbelasting*) per night may apply at hotel-equivalent level — verify per deal with CTB + Belastingdienst Curaçao
- **HOA / community restrictions**: in Jan Thiel / Blue Bay / Spaanse Water gated resort communities, the *Vereniging van Eigenaren (VvE)* may restrict short-let usage — verify per property's HOA bylaws before counting on short-let income
- ⚠️ As short-let regulation in the Caribbean has tightened in 2024–2026 (Aruba, Sint Maarten, USVI all introduced licensing schemes), expect Curaçao regulatory tightening in the 2026–2028 horizon

### Tax treatment

- Rental income → progressive inkomstenbelasting (residents) / Curaçao-source taxation (non-residents) with DTA coordination
- **No cedolare-secca-equivalent flat regime** — full progressive scale applies
- Deductibles: maintenance, mortgage interest, OZB, depreciation (where applicable), management/agency fees

### Yields (est.)

- Long-term gross yield mid-tier apt 2026 ≈ **5–8%** in well-located Willemstad / Jan Thiel mid-tier (per agency market notes, cross-validated against listing aggregators)
- Short-let gross yield (compliant, well-located beachfront): higher seasonal peaks (Curaçao tourism: ~2M+ visitors/yr; Nov–Apr high season + cruise overflow); but thin shoulder season and rising regulatory exposure

### Specific risks

- **Hurricane (orkaan / tropical storm)** — *moderate but real*:
  - Curaçao sits at ~12°N latitude on the **southern fringe of the Atlantic hurricane belt** — historically **outside the main hurricane track** that affects northern Caribbean (Sint Maarten, Puerto Rico, Cuba). Per Meteorological Department Curaçao (`https://www.meteo.cw/Data_www/pdf/pub/Hurricanes_and_Tropical_StormsDC.pdf`): only **16 named storms** have passed within 120 nautical miles of Curaçao historically; **no hurricane or tropical storm has made landfall on Curaçao** in the modern record
  - Meteorological Department Curaçao reports: **a hurricane passes within 150 km roughly every 4 years**; **considerable damage from close-passing hurricane roughly every 100 years**
  - **Hurricane season**: June–November; peak threat **August–October**
  - **Notable near-misses**: Hurricane Felix (2007, tropical storm strength at Curaçao); Tropical Storm Bret (2017, brief impact)
  - Insurance: hurricane cover via standard hazard policies; premium reflects the lower exposure vs northern Caribbean
- **Earthquake (aardbeving)**: low — Caribbean tectonic margin runs to the north (subduction at Lesser Antilles arc); Curaçao is well south of the seismically active zone. Local micro-seismicity rare and low-magnitude
- **Flood (wateroverlast)**: limited but real — **flash floods in tropical downpours** along *rooi* (dry wadi / ravine channels) that fill during heavy rain events. Lowland parcels near rooi-mouths or in coastal *bocht* basins (Schottegat, Spaanse Water rim) more exposed. No major river system
- **Coastal erosion + sea-level rise**: relevant for waterfront parcels — exposed S/W coast (windward) erodes faster than leeward calm bays; insurance premium trajectory upward for ocean-adjacent stock
- **Drought + water scarcity**: Curaçao is arid (~500 mm annual rainfall, dry-tropical); domestic water supply is **desalination-based** via Aqualectra → no rain-fed rural-well dependence; but garden / irrigation costs material
- **Reactive soils + foundation issues**: Curaçao's **diabase / limestone substrate** with patches of expansive clay can cause foundation movement in older slab-on-grade construction; verify with structural engineer on pre-1985 stock
- **Salt-air corrosion**: coastal + windward properties (especially Banda Abou + waterfront E coast) experience accelerated steel-rebar corrosion + electrical-fittings degradation — maintenance cycle 2–3× shorter than inland properties
- **Volcanic risk**: nil — Curaçao is a **non-volcanic** island (uplifted limestone + diabase platform); Lesser Antilles volcanic arc is 800+ km NE

### Build-era hazards

| Era | Hazards |
|---|---|
| **Pre-1955 historic / traditional** | UNESCO Willemstad heritage stock (Punda + Otrobanda + Scharloo + Pietermaai); lime + coral-aggregate masonry; lead paint residual; asbestos cement in 1950s additions; alteration heavily restricted |
| **1955–1985 post-war boom** | Concrete-block + diabase-aggregate hybrid; **asbestos-cement (Eternit) roofing widespread**; single-glazing; pre-modern hurricane detailing; expansive-soil foundation risks |
| **1985–2010** | Asbestos phase-out; improving thermal/cyclone detailing; reinforced-concrete frame standard; HVAC penetration grows |
| **2010+** | Modern hurricane-resistant detailing (impact glazing in premium stock); solar-ready; better seal against salt-air; LEED-equivalent retrofits in resort-grade |

### Climate change projections

- Caribbean regional projections: **+1.5–3.0 °C** by 2050 vs 1981–2010 baseline (SSP2-4.5 to SSP5-8.5 range; consistent with IPCC AR6 Caribbean chapter)
- Sea-level rise: **+0.3–0.6 m** by 2080 (central estimate; SSP scenarios envelope wider) — material for waterfront parcels
- Hurricane intensification (not frequency): trend toward higher-Cat extremes per IPCC AR6; Curaçao remains on southern fringe but increasing tail risk
- Coral reef bleaching: ongoing — relevant for tourism-yield sustainability + erosion buffer

## Section: `--mains`

### Sources

- **Aqualectra**: `https://www.aqualectra.com/` — **single integrated state utility for electricity + water** (combined company; ~135k customers across Curaçao). Single-utility model (different from NL liberalised market)
- **Regulatory Authority of Curaçao (RAC)**: `https://rac.cw/` — sets electricity + water tariffs; publishes monthly tariff updates

## Section: `--currency`

### Currency status

- **XCG — Caribbean guilder** is the official currency of Curaçao (and Sint Maarten) since **31 March 2025**
- **Replaced**: ANG Netherlands Antillean guilder; co-circulation period ended **1 July 2025**; ANG exchangeable at par at commercial banks until **31 March 2026**, then at CBCS for 29 more years (until **31 March 2055**)
- **Peg**: **USD 1 = XCG 1.79** (identical to predecessor ANG peg; in place since 1971; one of the longest-running pegs in the Caribbean)
- **Conversion ANG → XCG**: 1 ANG = 1 XCG (one-for-one)
- **Issuer**: Centrale Bank van Curaçao en Sint Maarten (CBCS, `https://www.centralbank.cw/`) — joint central bank between Curaçao + Sint Maarten; coins minted by Royal Canadian Mint, banknotes printed by Crane Currency
- **Denominations (notes)**: XCG 10, 20, 50, 100, 200; coins 1c, 5c, 10c, 25c, 50c, 1g, 5g
- ⚠️ **Note**: USD is widely accepted in tourist + commercial contexts at the de facto 1.79 rate (or rounded ~1.75–1.80); most large transactions (property, vehicles) priced in either USD or XCG; mortgage products available in both
- **NOT a Eurozone member**; **NOT in SEPA**; **NOT in Schengen**

### What this means for buyers

- **USD-denominated buyers**: **near-zero FX risk** — the 1.79 peg is treaty-grade-stable (long history; institutionally supported by CBCS USD reserves)
- **EUR / GBP / CHF / non-USD buyers**: full home-currency↔USD exposure — use forward contracts for transaction lock-in if material; FX execution typically routed through Curaçao banks via USD correspondent
- **Capital controls**: limited; FX outward transfers subject to AML/CFT reporting under CBCS rules; large transfers (> threshold) trigger source-of-funds review

### Peg credibility

- Peg has held continuously since 1971 (one of longest in Caribbean); CBCS holds USD reserves to defend; **devaluation risk = LOW**
- Under the 31 March 2025 currency reform, the peg was **explicitly maintained** at 1.79 — no devaluation accompanied the ANG→XCG transition

## Section: `--notary`

### Process

- **Civil-law notary (*notaris*) mandatory** for all real-estate transfers — Curaçao notarial profession is small (~10–15 practising *notarissen* island-wide); profession regulated by Common Court of Justice (joint authority Curaçao + Aruba + Sint Maarten + BES)
- Notaris role: drafts the *akte van levering* (transfer deed) + *hypotheekakte* (mortgage deed if applicable); verifies identity (apostilled passport for non-residents); confirms title via Kadaster *uittreksel*; **withholds and remits 4% overdrachtsbelasting** + Kadaster registration fees; files the deed at Kadaster Openbare Registers
- **Tariffs**: set by **Common Court of Justice** (joint regulation across Curaçao + Aruba + Sint Maarten + BES); limited room for negotiation. **Plus mandatory 8.5% kantoor- en administratiekosten** (office + admin surcharge) and **6% sales tax (OB)** on top of base notary fee

### Standard timeline (typical residential resale)

| Step | Day | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1. *Koopovereenkomst* (sale agreement) | D0 | Buyer pays **deposit ~10%** to notaris trust account; preliminary contract drawn |
| 2. Notaris due diligence | D7–D30 | Notaris orders Kadaster *uittreksel* + verifies title, encumbrances, erfpacht status (if applicable), bouwvergunning history |
| 3. Mortgage approval (if applicable) | D14–D45 | Bank valuation + offer letter; foreign-buyer AML review (4–8 weeks for non-residents) |
| 4. *Akte van levering* (deed signing) | D45–D75 | Buyer pays balance; notaris withholds overdrachtsbelasting + Kadaster fees; deed signed |
| 5. Kadaster inschrijving (registration) | D75–D90 | Notaris files at Kadaster Openbare Registers; title perfected |

### Costs

| Item | Typical |
|---|---|
| **Notaris fee (base, per Common Court tariff)** | ~1–2% of price |
| **Notaris office + admin surcharge** | +8.5% on base notary fee |
| **OB on notary services** | +6% on base notary fee |
| **Overdrachtsbelasting (4%)** | 4% of purchase price |
| **Kadaster registration fees** | ~0.2–0.5% (varies by deed type) |
| **Estate agent commission (where engaged)** | typically **3–5% seller-side** (buyer typically does not pay broker fee) |
| **Total transaction cost (buyer side, resale)** | **~5–7%** of purchase price (excl. agent if seller-paid) |

### Foreign buyer practical

- **Power of attorney** acceptable: notaris accepts apostilled PoA for absent buyers
- **Source-of-funds**: notaris + bank both apply AML/CFT reviews under CBCS rules
- **UBO disclosure**: corporate buyers register UBO with Curaçao Chamber of Commerce
- **Dutch-language closing documents**: *akte van levering* drafted in Dutch; non-Dutch-speaking buyers should engage sworn translator + Curaçao tax advisor (*belastingadviseur*) for review. Many *notarissen* will also accept English-supplementary explanations during signing

## Section: `--exit`

### Resale market liquidity

- **Average days on market 2025**: ~**90–240 days** mid-tier villa; ~180–360 days for properties >USD 500k; longer for erfpacht with <30 years remaining
- Cyclical: moderate in upcycle (USD-peg stability + Caribbean-residency demand); thinner in downcycle (small absolute transaction volume → liquidity-thin always)
- No published HPI quarterly leading indicator; cross-reference listing-aggregator absorption rates + agency market notes

### Exit-cost stack (seller side)

- Estate agent commission: typically **3–5% seller-side** (buyer typically does not pay broker fee)
- Notaris fee (seller portion): smaller share (~0.2–0.5%)
- **No standalone capital-gains tax for non-professional individual sellers** (gains outside inkomstenbelasting *box* structure for non-business holdings — verify per deal)
- Mortgage early-discharge: USD 200–USD 1,000 admin + bank clawback if early exit
- **Total exit cost**: typically **~3.5–5.5%** + any CGT for professional/business holdings

### Liquidity by sub-segment (qualitative, 2026)

### Verdict for exit risk

🟡 mid-tier well-located eigendom villa (resilient local + foreign-buyer pool); 🟠 premium / gated-community / UNESCO heritage (resilient HNW pool but slow price discovery); 🟠 short-erfpacht + rural (thin liquidity). The **eigendom vs erfpacht structural distinction** is the dominant resale-liquidity driver — short-erfpacht parcels reprice meaningfully below eigendom comparables.

## Foreign-buyer rules — no restrictions framework

### Headline

Curaçao operates **no foreign-buyer restrictions** on residential or commercial real estate. Foreign nationals enjoy **identical property rights to citizens**:

- **No alien landholding licence** (differs from many CARICOM jurisdictions which impose 10–12.5% alien landholding fees)
- **No nationality threshold** for ownership
- **No government pre-approval** required for ownership transfer (only standard notarial / Kadaster process)
- **No 2-property cap** (differs from San Marino regime)
- **Both eigendom AND erfpacht** available to foreign buyers

### Practical implication

- Process is **identical for residents + non-residents** at the notarial + Kadaster stages
- **Mortgage access** does differ (non-residents face 30–40% down-payment vs 20–30% for residents)
- **Tax treatment**: identical 4% overdrachtsbelasting; OZB applies identically; non-resident rental-income subject to Curaçao tax with DTA coordination
- **Specific use restrictions** may apply to certain coastal / defence-strategic / heritage-zone parcels — verify per parcel with notary

⚠️ **Caveat for verification**: while the headline "no restrictions" framework is cross-validated across multiple advisory + market sources (7th Heaven Properties, Caribbean House Hunt, RE/MAX BonBini, Coldwell Banker, fiestatourscuracao, ARAB MLS), the primary government statutory citation for the open-ownership regime is not cleanly indexed in publicly accessible English-language form. **Verify per parcel with notary** during due diligence; specific parcels may carry use restrictions (heritage / coastal / defence) outside the general open-ownership rule.

## Caveats unique to CW

- **No foreign-buyer restrictions** (open ownership; no alien landholding licence) — differs from many CARICOM jurisdictions
- **Eigendom vs erfpacht structural distinction**: erfpacht typical -20–30% vs eigendom; lease-term-remaining a meaningful resale-liquidity factor
- **4% overdrachtsbelasting** (cross-validated across multiple secondary sources; verify primary Belastingdienst Curaçao schedule at deal)
- **Currency = XCG (Caribbean guilder) since 31 March 2025**; USD-peg 1.79 since 1971 (continuity through ANG→XCG transition)
- **NOT a Eurozone member, NOT in SEPA, NOT in Schengen** — OCT status under EU Annex II TFEU
- **Dutch citizenship grants automatic right of residence**; non-Dutch foreigners use Investor Permit (USD 280k/425k/850k tiers) or other residency routes
- **No CBI passport programme** — Investor Permit is residency only; Dutch passport via 5-yr naturalisation
- **Single integrated state utility (Aqualectra)** for electricity + water; no piped gas
- **Dutch-language closing documents** — non-Dutch-speaking buyers need sworn translator + Curaçao belastingadviseur
- **No published Curaçao HPI / OMI-equivalent index** — cross-reference listing-aggregator absorption rates
- **OZB applies to primary residence (no exemption)** — different from many EU regimes
- **Inkomstenbelasting progressive 9.75–46.5%** — top bracket above ~ANG 159k taxable income
- **Hurricane risk: outside main Atlantic belt** (only 16 named storms within 120 nm historically; no landfalls modern record) — premium discount vs Northern Caribbean
- **UNESCO Willemstad heritage zone** — alteration restrictions; specialist contractors required
- **Crime: 2024 property-crime surge** (car theft +31%, burglary +38%, business break-in +20%) — caution flag; gated-community premium worth pricing in
- **2024–2028 OZB valuation cycle** — current 5-year tijdvak
