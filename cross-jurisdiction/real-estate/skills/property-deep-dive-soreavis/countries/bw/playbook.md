# Botswana 🇧🇼 — Property Due-Diligence Playbook

ISO2: `bw`. Status: ✅ Fully populated (researched 2026-06-13).

> **Critical framing — read first**: Botswana is a **landlocked, semi-arid, upper-middle-income, diamond-led** Southern African state with **three tenure types** — **tribal land** (administered by **Land Boards** under the **Tribal Land Act**, the largest share of the country), **state land** (vested in the President, delegated to the Minister of Lands), and **freehold** (the smallest share, concentrated in **Gaborone, Lobatse, Francistown** and the **Tuli Block** farm belt). **Non-citizens cannot buy tribal land**; state land is accessed by **leasehold** (reported **50-year** non-citizen / **99-year** citizen — per land-governance sources, see `--visa`); **freehold is the principal foreign-buyer route**, and there is **no blanket licence board** for urban residential freehold — BUT acquiring **agricultural land** (including Tuli Block farms) requires **Ministry of Agriculture consent under the Land Control Act, 1975** ([Land Portal narrative](https://landportal.org/book/narratives/2021/botswana)). The currency **BWP (Pula)** is a **crawling-band peg to a 50% South African rand / 50% SDR basket** (introduced May 2005; basket revised to 50/50 from the previous 45/55, decided Dec 2024) — **NOT a hard peg and NOT a free float**; the **downward crawl was 1.51% p.a. through 2024 / Jan 2025, raised to 2.76% effective 11 July 2025**, and trading margins widened from ±0.5% to ±7.5% the same day ([Bank of Botswana Monetary Policy Report Oct 2025](https://www.bankofbotswana.bw/)). **Exchange controls were abolished 9 February 1999** — profits, rents, dividends and **full disinvestment proceeds are freely repatriable** ([BoB Milestones](https://www.bankofbotswana.bw/milestones)) — the load-bearing positive for foreign rental income + exit. Transaction-tax headline: **transfer duty** under the **Transfer Duty (Amendment) Act, 2023 (No. 5 of 2023, eff. 3 May 2023)** is **5% for citizens** (first BWP 1.5m exempt) and **10% on the first BWP 2m + 15% on the excess for non-citizens** (the old flat **30%** is STALE) ([Orbitax](https://www.orbitax.com/news/archive.php/Botswana-Introduces-Transfer-D-52703)). Final court: **Court of Appeal of Botswana** (no onward UK Privy Council appeal since the **Court of Appeal Act 1973**). Top hazard: **water scarcity / drought (HIGH, ThinkHazard!)** in a Kalahari semi-arid country, compounded by a **live, oscillating BPC electricity load-shedding risk**. **No operational citizenship-by-investment programme exists as of 2026-08-07** — the enabling **Citizenship (Amendment) Act 2025 (Act No. 30 of 2025)** (multiple citizenship + an investor "economic citizenship" route) **passed Parliament 17 Dec 2025 and HAS BEEN ASSENTED TO**, and a commencement order — **Statutory Instrument No. 38 of 2026 — Citizenship (Amendment) Act (Date of Commencement) Order, 2026** — is listed among papers received for tabling on Parliament's Business Update ([Parliament of Botswana Business Update, 3rd Meeting of the 2nd Session of the 13th Parliament](https://botswanaspeaks.gov.bw/media/winter%20session%20order%20paper%20-%202026/Parliament%20Business%20Update_Legislative%20Meeting%202026%20.pdf)); its commencement date and whether it commences the whole Act are data not publicly available — verify at the Government Gazette / MLHA. The Ministry of Labour & Home Affairs had told Parliament on **24 Feb 2026** that the Regulations to operationalise the Act were still being finalised; **no CBI launch notice or gazetted investment threshold was located**. **Confirm** every rate, lease term, and the CBI status at primary sources before any binding step. Authoritative primary sources: **[Botswana Unified Revenue Service (BURS)](https://www.burs.org.bw/)**, **[Bank of Botswana (BoB)](https://www.bankofbotswana.bw/)**, **[Government of Botswana (gov.bw)](https://www.gov.bw/)**, **[Statistics Botswana](https://www.statsbots.org.bw/)**, **[Botswana Investment & Trade Centre (BITC)](https://www.bitc.co.bw/)**.

## Country profile

- **Geography**: **Landlocked** Southern African state, **~581,730 km²** (mostly the Kalahari Desert / semi-desert). Borders South Africa (S/SE), Namibia (W/N), Zimbabwe (NE), and a short Zambia frontier (N, at Kazungula). **No coast** → no cyclone/coastal-flood/tsunami exposure (see `--risks`).
- **Capital**: **Gaborone** (SE corner, near the South African border). Other principal urban centres: **Francistown** (NE), **Molepolole**, **Maun** (gateway to the Okavango Delta), **Lobatse**, **Selibe-Phikwe**, **Kasane** (NE, Chobe). **Local authorities** (city/town/district councils) levy property **rates** (see `--tax`).
- **Population**: **~2.5 million** (2024, World Bank — verify at [Statistics Botswana](https://www.statsbots.org.bw/)). One of the most sparsely populated countries on earth; population concentrated in the eastern corridor (Gaborone–Francistown).
- **GDP per capita**: **~USD 6,940 GNI/capita** (2024, World Bank Atlas method); **GDP ~USD 19.4 bn** (2024, World Bank); **~USD 18,000 PPP/capita** (2024, World Bank/regulated aggregators) — **upper-middle-income**. (World Bank country brief [worldbank.org/.../country/botswana](https://www.worldbank.org/ext/en/country/botswana); [data.worldbank.org BW](https://data.worldbank.org/?locations=BW-XT) — World-Bank-tier, citable; for census-grade figures pull [Statistics Botswana](https://www.statsbots.org.bw/).)
- **Macro flag**: World Bank Macro Poverty Outlook reports an **estimated ~0.9% GDP CONTRACTION in 2025**, driven by the **diamond-market downturn** (Botswana is diamond-led via **Debswana**, the Government-of-Botswana / De Beers JV). ([World Bank MPO Botswana PDF](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/bae48ff2fefc5a869546775b3f010735-0500062021/related/mpo-bwa.pdf).) This is the same revenue-stress story behind the July 2025 FX crawl increase + widened margins (see `--currency`).
- **Currency**: **Botswana Pula (BWP, symbol P)** — **crawling-band peg** to a **50% ZAR / 50% SDR** basket (since May 2005; basket revised to 50/50 from 45/55, decision Dec 2024). NOT a hard peg, NOT a free float. ([BoB Current Exchange Rate Framework](https://www.bankofbotswana.bw/content/current-exchange-rate-framework).) See `--currency` for crawl rate + margins.
- **Languages**: **English** (official) + **Setswana** (national) — verify at the Constitution / gov.bw before stating as fact. All conveyancing, deeds, and statutes are in English.
- **Legal system**: **Dual** — **Roman-Dutch common law** (received via the Cape Colony) operating alongside **customary law**. ([gov.bw Hierarchy of Courts](https://www.gov.bw/legal/hierarchy-courts).)
- **Final court of appeal**: **Court of Appeal of Botswana** — highest court and final arbiter; based in Gaborone; hears appeals from the High Court and Industrial Court; established by the **1966 Constitution**. Appeals to the **UK Privy Council** continued until 1973 and were **abolished by the Court of Appeal Act 1973** — since then **NO onward Privy Council appeal**. ([gov.bw Hierarchy of Courts](https://www.gov.bw/legal/hierarchy-courts); [Administration of Justice](https://www.justice.gov.bw/).)
- **Cadastre / land registry**:
  - **[Ministry of Lands & Agriculture](https://www.gov.bw/)** — land policy + state land.
  - **Land Boards** (& sub-Land Boards) under the **Tribal Land Act** (Boards created 1970) — administer **tribal land** allocations.
  - **Registrar of Deeds / Deeds Registry** — registers **freehold + state-land title deeds**; collects transfer duty and remits to **BURS**.
- **Identifier**: **plot/lot number + deed reference** at the Deeds Registry (freehold + state-land lease). **No universal national parcel ID** equivalent to a French numéro de parcelle. Tribal-land rights are recorded by the relevant **Land Board**, not the Deeds Registry.
- **Title concept**: **freehold (full ownership)** — the principal foreign-buyer route, scarce; **state-land leasehold** (Fixed Period State Grant); **tribal-land allocation** (Land Board, not available to non-citizens as freehold).
- **Recent reforms (12-month watchlist)**:
  - **Citizenship (Amendment) Act 2025 (Act No. 30 of 2025)** (multiple citizenship + an investor "economic citizenship" route) **passed Parliament 17 Dec 2025** (tabled 16 Dec 2025 by Minister of Labour & Home Affairs Maj. Gen. Pius Mokgware) **and HAS BEEN ASSENTED TO** — explicitly to attract high-end investors; economic citizens are barred from voting, public office, free government education, government funding, disciplined-forces employment and **the allocation of state or tribal land** ([gov.bw Daily News](https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/89462); [MLHA Committee of Supply speech, 24 Feb 2026](https://www.botswanaspeaks.gov.bw/media/COMMITTEE%20OF%20SUPPPLY%20SPEECHES/MLHA%20-%20Committee%20of%20Supply%20Speech%20FINAL%20%20-%2024-02-2026.pdf)).   - **Pula basket revised to 50% ZAR / 50% SDR** (from 45/55; decided Dec 2024, applies to 2025 implementation); **downward crawl 1.51% → 2.76%** and **trading margins ±0.5% → ±7.5%** effective **11 July 2025** ([BoB MPR Oct 2025](https://www.bankofbotswana.bw/)).

## 🇧🇼 Foreign buyer eligibility (READ FIRST)

This section determines whether a transaction is even legally possible — and what process applies. The **single most important fact**: Botswana has three tenure types, and a non-citizen can realistically hold only **freehold** (scarce) or **state-land leasehold (~50-yr)**; the largest land category (**tribal land**) is closed to foreign freehold ownership.

### Legal basis

- **Tribal Land Act** — Land Boards administer tribal-land allocations (Boards created 1970). Tribal land **cannot be sold to non-citizens** as freehold ([Land Portal](https://landportal.org/book/narratives/2021/botswana); [Tenure Security](https://tenuresecurity.org/country-profile/botswana/)).
- **State Land Act** — state land vested in the President, delegated to the Minister of Lands; granted by **Fixed Period State Grant** / **Deed of Fixed Period State Grant** (leasehold).
- **Land Control Act, 1975** — **non-citizens must obtain Ministry of Agriculture permission to acquire rights to AGRICULTURAL land** (the consent gate for the Tuli Block farm belt; NOT a blanket licence on all freehold) ([Land Portal](https://landportal.org/book/narratives/2021/botswana)).
- **Transfer Duty Act (Cap. 53:01)** as amended by the **Transfer Duty (Amendment) Act, 2023 (No. 5 of 2023)** — transaction tax (see `--tax`).
- **Deeds Registry Act** — freehold + state-land title registration.

### Buyer category matrix

| Buyer category | Right |
|---|---|
| **Citizen of Botswana** | Full freehold; full state-land/tribal-land access (99-yr state lease for citizen residential, per land-governance sources); 5% transfer duty above BWP 1.5m |
| **Non-citizen (individual)** | **Freehold permitted** (urban residential — no blanket licence board found); **state land by leasehold only (~50-yr)**; **tribal land NOT available** as freehold; non-citizen transfer duty 10%/15% (see `--tax`) |
| **Non-citizen buying AGRICULTURAL land** (incl. Tuli Block farms) | Requires **Ministry of Agriculture consent under the Land Control Act, 1975** before acquiring rights (Land Portal — *verify with the Ministry / your conveyancer*) |
| **Company / non-citizen-held entity** | State-land residential allocated to companies/non-citizens reported on the **50-year** Deed of Fixed Period State Grant (per land-governance sources / State Dept ICS — confirm against the State Land Act) |

(Sources: [Land Portal narrative](https://landportal.org/book/narratives/2021/botswana); [Tenure Security country profile](https://tenuresecurity.org/country-profile/botswana/); US State Dept Investment Climate Statement Botswana — secondary/regulated tier where noted; *the freehold-only foreign-buyer route + tribal-land closure are multi-source corroborated; the exact lease term carries a soft attribution — see `--visa`*)

### Visa / residency via property

- **No "buy property → get residency" scheme** was found. Property purchase alone does **not** confer residency.
- Residency runs through **employment / investment / permanent-residence permits** administered by gov.bw ([Residence Permit](https://www.gov.bw/residency-and-work/residence-permit-application); [Permanent Residence](https://www.gov.bw/residency-and-work/permanent-residence-application)). Reported **investor-permit thresholds** are **agency-sourced** — see `--visa`.
- **No operational citizenship-by-investment programme** as of 2026-08-07 (Citizenship (Amendment) Act 2025 assented; commencement order SI No. 38 of 2026 listed on Parliament's Business Update — commencement date and scope data not publicly available, verify at the Government Gazette / MLHA; no gazetted CBI threshold — see `--visa`).

### Compliance / AML

- Conveyancing attorneys and banks run **KYC / source-of-funds** checks. The relevant instrument is commonly the **Financial Intelligence Act** — **specific AML/source-of-funds statute: verify (Financial Intelligence Act)** before citing it as the controlling provision.
- **Source-of-funds documentation** should be expected for any property purchase; foreign buyers should prepare bank-statement chain + apostilled identity docs.

### Verification path (before any reservation)

1. **Confirm tenure type of the parcel**: freehold (Deeds Registry) vs state-land lease (Deeds Registry) vs tribal-land allocation (Land Board). A non-citizen can only take freehold or a state-land lease.
2. **If the parcel is agricultural** (e.g. Tuli Block): confirm the **Land Control Act, 1975 Ministry of Agriculture consent** path with your conveyancer before paying a deposit.
3. **Pull the title deed** at the **Deeds Registry**; confirm no encumbrances and that boundaries match.
4. **Confirm transfer-duty band** (non-citizen 10%/15%) and whether VAT applies (VAT waives transfer duty — see `--tax`).
5. **Source-of-funds / AML**: prepare documentation for the conveyancing attorney + bank (Financial Intelligence Act regime — verify the specific citation).
6. **Plan FX inflow + repatriation**: exchange controls are abolished (Feb 1999) → rents + sale proceeds freely repatriable; route funds through a commercial bank (see `--currency`).

## Section: `--tax`

> **Headline**: Botswana has **no separate flat capital-gains-tax rate** (gains on immovable property are taxed as income with an **inflation-indexed cost base**), **VAT at 14%**, **corporate income tax at a flat 22%**, **progressive personal income tax (0–25%)** with a **BWP 48,000 tax-free threshold**, and a one-time **transfer duty** (5% citizen / 10%+15% non-citizen). Annual property **rates** are levied by city/town/district councils, not nationally. **Tax authority: [BURS](https://www.burs.org.bw/).**

### Transfer duty — one-time at transfer (purchaser pays)

Governed by the **Transfer Duty Act (Cap. 53:01)** as amended by the **Transfer Duty (Amendment) Act, 2023 (No. 5 of 2023)**, effective **3 May 2023**. Paid by the **purchaser** to the **Registrar of Deeds**, who remits to BURS.

| Buyer | Rate | Threshold |
|---|---|---|
| **Citizen** | **5%** | on value **exceeding BWP 1.5 million** (first BWP 1.5m exempt; threshold raised from BWP 1m by the 2023 Act) |
| **Non-citizen (foreigner)** | **10% on the first BWP 2 million + 15% on the excess above BWP 2 million** | no exemption threshold (replaced the old flat **30%**) |

- **History (3 data points)**: 5% (pre-2019) → flat **30%** non-citizen (2019, a deliberate brake on foreign land acquisition) → **10%/15% progressive** (2023 Amendment, eff. 3 May 2023). The **30% figure is STALE — do not cite as current.**
- **Tribal & state-land exclusion**: the 2023 Amendment **excluded new allocations of tribal and state land** from the scope of the Transfer Duty Act.
- **VAT interaction**: where **VAT is payable** on a property sale (the seller is a VAT vendor), **transfer duty is WAIVED** — mutually exclusive, no double charge.

(Sources: [Orbitax — Botswana Introduces Transfer Duty changes](https://www.orbitax.com/news/archive.php/Botswana-Introduces-Transfer-D-52703); [Bookbinder Business Law](https://bookbinderlaw.co.bw/transfer-duty-amendment-act/); KPMG TaxNewsFlash 3 May 2023; Law Society of Botswana guidelines — regulated-aggregator/professional tier. **The Government Gazette / BURS primary guideline is the fact-tier source** — verify at [BURS](https://www.burs.org.bw/) before a binding step.)

### VAT — 14%

- **Standard rate: 14%**, reverted **1 April 2023** after a temporary **12%** cut that ran **3 Aug 2022 – 31 March 2023** (confirmed in the 2023 Budget Speech, 6 Feb 2023; no further change indicated through 2025).
- **Registration threshold: BWP 1,000,000** turnover (PwC, reviewed 09 Jan 2026).
- **Property interaction**: where VAT is payable on a sale, **transfer duty is waived** (see above).

(Source: [PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries — Botswana, Other Taxes](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/botswana/corporate/other-taxes), reviewed 09 Jan 2026 — regulated-aggregator tier; *verify current rate at* [BURS](https://www.burs.org.bw/).)

### Capital gains on immovable property

- **No separate flat CGT rate.** Gains on **Botswana-source immovable property** are **included in taxable income** and taxed at the ordinary rates (corporate **22%**; individuals on the PIT brackets below).
- **Cost base is inflation-indexed**: 10% compound annual addition from acquisition to 30 June 1982, then a CPI-based uplift to the date of sale.

(Source: [PwC — Botswana, Income determination](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/botswana/corporate/income-determination), reviewed 09 Jan 2026 — regulated-aggregator tier; *verify computation with a Botswana tax adviser / BURS before relying*.)

### Corporate income tax — flat 22%

- **CIT: single flat rate 22%** (PwC, reviewed 09 Jan 2026, [taxsummaries.pwc.com/botswana/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/botswana/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)).

### Personal income tax (RESIDENT) — progressive

| Taxable income (BWP) | Tax on lower limit (BWP) | Rate on excess |
|---|---|---|
| 0 – 48,000 | 0 | **0%** |
| 48,000 – 84,000 | 0 | **5%** |
| 84,000 – 120,000 | 1,800 | **12.5%** |
| 120,000 – 156,000 | 6,300 | **18.75%** |
| over 156,000 | 13,050 | **25%** |

- **Tax-free threshold = first BWP 48,000.**
- **Tax residency test**: an individual is resident if they have **a permanent place of abode in Botswana AND are physically present ≥ 183 days** in the current or previous tax year (both elements).

(Source: [PwC — Botswana, Taxes on personal income](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/botswana/individual/taxes-on-personal-income) + [residence page](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/botswana/individual/residence), both reviewed 09 Jan 2026 — regulated-aggregator tier; *verify current brackets at* [BURS](https://www.burs.org.bw/).)

## Section: `--mains`

### Power — Botswana Power Corporation (BPC)

- **[Botswana Power Corporation (BPC)](https://www.bpc.bw/)** is the national electricity utility. Domestic generation is **Morupule-coal-centric**; Botswana historically **imports power from South Africa (Eskom) and the region (SAPP)**.
- **Load-shedding is a LIVE, oscillating reliability risk.** Through 2025: cascaded failures — **Morupule B Unit 2 (14 Feb 2025)**, **Unit 4 (22 Mar 2025)**, with **Unit 3 in long-term planned outage** — forced BPC load management. **Unit 2 returned to service 5 Nov 2025** and SAPP imports allowed load-shedding to cease — **but load-shedding RETURNED in December 2025** (BPC published a load-shedding schedule for **24 Dec 2025**). National demand **~640 MW peak / ~440 MW off-peak**.
- (Sources: [gov.bw Daily News](https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/85627); BPC official channel load-shed schedule 24 Dec 2025; Sunday Standard / Mmegi corroborating — *cite the dated BPC notice at the time of purchase, because the status oscillates.*)
- **Practical**: a **standby generator and/or inverter + battery** is advisable for any continuous-occupancy property given the recurring load-shedding.

## Section: `--currency`

- **Currency**: **Botswana Pula (BWP, symbol P)**.
- **Regime**: **crawling band / crawling peg**, introduced **May 2005**; targets real-effective-exchange-rate (REER) stability via a continuous gradual "crawl" of the trade-weighted rate. **NOT a hard peg, NOT a free float.** Parameters are reviewed periodically and changed via BoB announcements. ([BoB Current Exchange Rate Framework](https://www.bankofbotswana.bw/content/current-exchange-rate-framework); [History of Exchange Rate Policy](https://www.bankofbotswana.bw/content/history-exchange-rate-policy-botswana).)
- **Basket**: **50% South African rand (ZAR) / 50% SDR** — revised to equal 50/50 weights from the previous **45% rand / 55% SDR** (which had held since **January 2017**); the 50/50 weighting applies from the 2025 policy implementation, **decided December 2024**. (BoB Monetary Policy Reports April & October 2025; "Note to Editors — Pula Exchange Rate Framework Explained" — *sourced from BoB monetary-policy reporting rather than a single standalone gazette line, but multi-source corroborated.*)
- **Rate of crawl (dated)**: **downward crawl 1.51% p.a. through 2024 and adopted again January 2025**, **increased to 2.76% effective 11 July 2025** ([BoB Pula Exchange Rate in 2024](https://bankofbotswana.bw/index.php/news/pula-exchange-rate-2024); BoB Monetary Policy Report October 2025). **Downward / negative crawl = deliberate nominal depreciation** to offset the inflation differential vs the basket.
- **Trading margins**: effective **11 July 2025**, Pula trading margins widened from **±0.5% to ±7.5%** (buy/sell spread) to encourage an interbank FX market (BoB MPR October 2025).
- **Why the 2025 changes**: the increased crawl + widened margins are part of the **diamond-revenue-stress** story (estimated ~0.9% GDP contraction in 2025 — see Country profile).
- **Exchange controls — ABOLISHED**: **all remaining exchange controls were abolished on 9 February 1999** ([BoB Milestones](https://www.bankofbotswana.bw/milestones)). → **profits, dividends, rents, fees, and capital — including full disinvestment proceeds — are freely repatriable.** This is the load-bearing positive for a foreign buyer's rental income and exit. (US State Dept ICS Remittance Policies corroborates.)
- **IMF Article VIII**: Botswana maintains IMF Article VIII current-account convertibility. The **exact Article VIII acceptance date is `not cleanly confirmed at a primary source this pass — verify at IMF / BoB`** (the operative repatriation fact is the 9 Feb 1999 exchange-control abolition, which is solid).
- **Practical effect for a buyer**: there are **no FX controls blocking repatriation**, but the **crawling-band peg is a managed depreciation** — a BWP-denominated asset held over years will tend to **lose value in ZAR/SDR/USD terms** at the prevailing crawl rate. Model the crawl (2.76% p.a. as of 11 July 2025) into any multi-year holding-period return.

## Active fiscal incentives + visa programs (2025–2026)

### Tax-relevant facts

- **No separate flat CGT** (gains on immovable property taxed as income, inflation-indexed base).
- **VAT 14%** standard (reverted 1 Apr 2023); VAT waives transfer duty where payable.
- **Transfer duty** 5% citizen / 10%+15% non-citizen (Transfer Duty (Amendment) Act 2023).
- **Free repatriation** of rents, profits, and capital (exchange controls abolished 9 Feb 1999) — a structural positive for foreign investors.
- **Specific real-estate investment-incentive regimes**: `data not publicly available in this pass — check` [BITC](https://www.bitc.co.bw/) `for current investor incentives.` Do not assert a named incentive without a primary source.

## Caveats unique to BW

- **Three tenure types — foreign buyers route via FREEHOLD or state-land leasehold only.** Tribal land (Land Board–administered, Tribal Land Act, the largest share of the country) **cannot be sold to non-citizens** as freehold. Freehold is scarce (Gaborone/Lobatse/Francistown/Tuli Block). **Confirm the tenure type of the actual parcel before paying anything** — a "for sale" sign does not establish freehold title.
- **Agricultural land needs Ministry of Agriculture consent (Land Control Act, 1975)** for non-citizens — directly relevant to **Tuli Block farm purchases.** No blanket licence board governs urban residential freehold, but the agricultural-land consent gate is real.
- **State-land lease split (50-yr non-citizen / 99-yr citizen)** is **multi-source corroborated but not a verbatim State Land Act gazette quote** — keep the soft attribution and confirm against the State Land Act / the specific Deed of Fixed Period State Grant.
- **Transfer duty for non-citizens is 10% on the first BWP 2m + 15% on the excess** (Transfer Duty (Amendment) Act 2023, eff. 3 May 2023) — **NOT the old flat 30%.** Citizens: 5% above a BWP 1.5m exemption. **VAT, where payable, waives transfer duty.**
- **BWP is a CRAWLING-BAND peg to a 50% ZAR / 50% SDR basket (since May 2005) — NOT a hard peg, NOT a free float.** The crawl is **downward (managed depreciation): 1.51% through 2024/Jan-2025, raised to 2.76% from 11 July 2025**; trading margins widened to ±7.5% the same day. **Model the crawl into any multi-year return** — a BWP asset tends to lose value in ZAR/SDR/USD terms.
- **Exchange controls were abolished 9 Feb 1999 → rents, profits, and full disinvestment proceeds are FREELY REPATRIABLE.** This is the single biggest structural positive for a foreign buyer's income + exit.
- **Final court = Court of Appeal of Botswana (no Privy Council onward appeal since the Court of Appeal Act 1973). Legal system = Roman-Dutch common law + customary law.**
- **No operational citizenship-by-investment programme as of 2026-08-07** — the enabling **Citizenship (Amendment) Act 2025 (Act No. 30 of 2025)** (multiple citizenship + an investor "economic citizenship" route) **passed Parliament 17 Dec 2025 and was assented to**; a commencement order (SI No. 38 of 2026) is listed on Parliament's Business Update, but its date and scope are unverified and the investor route is not open (MLHA told Parliament on 24 Feb 2026 the operationalising Regulations were still being finalised). Any "Botswana passport by investment at USD 75k–90k" pitch traces to **promoter marketing (Arton Capital's pre-registration portal), not gov.bw** — verify launch + threshold at gov.bw / the Citizenship Act. Note also that **economic citizens would be barred from state/tribal-land allocation** — a direct property consequence.
- **No "buy property → residency" scheme; no dedicated digital-nomad visa.** Residency is permit-based.
- **Electricity load-shedding (BPC) is a live, oscillating risk** — Morupule B unit failures through 2025; load-shedding ceased Nov 2025 then **returned in December 2025** (BPC schedule 24 Dec 2025). Cite the dated BPC notice at purchase; budget backup power.
- **Water scarcity / drought is HIGH (ThinkHazard)** and is the **dominant habitability hazard** in this Kalahari semi-arid country. **River flood + urban flood + wildfire are also HIGH; earthquake is MEDIUM** (M6.5 Moiyabana 2017); **cyclone is Low and there is no coastal/tsunami exposure (landlocked).**
- **Reserved-for-citizens economic activities** (Trade Act 2008, Liquor Act 2003, Industrial Development Act) — not a property-purchase restriction, but relevant if you plan to **run a business** (guesthouse retail element, car wash, takeaway, etc.) from the property.
- **Macro context**: diamond-led economy (Debswana = GoB/De Beers JV); **estimated ~0.9% GDP contraction in 2025** on the diamond-market downturn — the same stress behind the July 2025 FX changes.
- **Non-resident mortgage availability is undocumented** — do not assume a Botswana home loan is available to a foreigner; **default to a cash-purchase model** unless FNBB/Absa Botswana confirm terms.
- **Several figures are genuinely not published** (primary HPI, dated rental yield, foreign-mortgage LTV, STR licensing, WUC outage metrics, exact Article VIII date, CBI threshold, conveyancing tariff, dated climate normals, salary bands) — each is flagged "data not publicly available — verify at <authority>" rather than guessed.
