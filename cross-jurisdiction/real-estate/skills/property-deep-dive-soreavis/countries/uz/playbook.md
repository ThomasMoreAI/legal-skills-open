# Uzbekistan (UZ) — Property Due-Diligence Playbook

ISO2: `uz`. Status: ✅ Fully populated (researched 2026-05).

## Country profile

- **Population**: ~37.5 million (2025 State Statistics Committee — `stat.uz` / Davlat statistika qoʻmitasi); largest in Central Asia. Tashkent (capital, separate "city of republican significance") ~3.0M; Samarkand ~580k; Namangan ~650k; Andijan ~470k; Bukhara ~290k; Nukus (capital of Karakalpakstan) ~330k; Fergana ~320k. Median age ~28 — youngest population in the post-Soviet space; ~50 % of population under 30.
- **GDP per capita**: ~US$3,000 nominal (2024 IMF / World Bank); real GDP **+6.5 % 2024** + **+7.7 % 2025** (CBU 2025 outcomes statement, [INVEXI summary](https://invexi.org/press/central-bank-of-uzbekistan-2025-outcomes-7-7-economic-growth-with-inflation-easing-to-7-3/)). Largest Central Asian economy by population, second by output (after Kazakhstan).
- **Currency**: **UZS** (Uzbekistan soʻm / so'm / sum / сум). 1 USD ≈ **12,300–12,950 UZS** (Q1–Q2 2026; CBU reference rate via [cbu.uz/en/](https://cbu.uz/en/)); 1 EUR ≈ 13,200–14,100 UZS. **Dual-rate "black market" gap closed September 2017** under Mirziyoyev liberalisation (Decree UP-5177); pre-2017 official rate ~3,000 UZS/USD vs unofficial ~8,000/USD (≈ 2.5× spread). Since Sep 2017 the soʻm has been **managed-float** with active CBU intervention; cumulative depreciation 2017 → 2026: ~3,200 UZS → ~12,500 UZS = **~290 % nominal devaluation** over ~9 years. CPI inflation **7.3 % end-2025** (CBU; expected 6.5 % end-2026); CBU policy rate **14.0 % unchanged** since April 2025 ([CBU press release 11 Dec 2025](https://cbu.uz/en/monetary-policy/publications/press-releases/3143821/)).
- **Languages**: **Uzbek** (state language, oʻzbek tili — Latin script per **2023 Constitution Art. 4** + phased Latin transition originally 1995 deadline, repeatedly deferred; Cyrillic still widely used in legacy documents and older signage); **Russian** (de facto language of business, finance, real-estate listings, government technical documents — no longer constitutionally protected after 1995 but ubiquitous in practice); **Karakalpak** (state language within Republic of Karakalpakstan); English limited to Tashkent CBD + IT Park residents + tourism + diaspora-return circles. **Real-estate listings + cadastre + tax filings predominantly in Russian**, with Uzbek-Latin parallel where state portal compels.
- **EU/regional status**: **No EU candidate status**. EU **Enhanced Partnership and Cooperation Agreement (EPCA)** signed 2018 (in force since 1 July 2025 per Council of the EU), expanding the 1996 PCA. **GSP+ beneficiary** since 10 April 2021 (preferential trade access). **Not in EAEU** (declined membership; observer status since December 2020 — Russia-led customs union). **SCO observer 2001 → full member 2001 → continues**; **Organization of Turkic States** member; **CICA** (Conference on Interaction and Confidence-Building Measures in Asia) member. Multi-vector foreign policy with active reorientation toward "Middle Corridor" Trans-Caspian trade route post-2022.
- **Postcode**: 6 digits (e.g., Tashkent central districts `100000`–`100214`; Samarkand `140100`–`140199`; Bukhara `200100`–`200199`).
- **Admin levels**: **12 regions (viloyatlar — singular viloyat / область in Russian)** + **1 autonomous republic (Republic of Karakalpakstan / Qoraqalpogʻiston Respublikasi)** + **1 independent city of republican significance (Tashkent / Toshkent shahri)** = **14 first-level units**. Subdivided into **175 districts (tumanlar — singular tuman / район)** + city districts (туманлар) + 119 cities + ~1,000 urban-type settlements. The 12 viloyats: Andijan, Bukhara, Fergana, Jizzakh, Namangan, Navoi, Qashqadaryo, Samarkand, Sirdaryo, Surxondaryo, Tashkent (region — separate from Tashkent city), Khorezm. Source: [Subdivisions of Uzbekistan, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subdivisions_of_Uzbekistan) cross-checked against [stat.uz administrative classification](https://stat.uz/en/).
- **Cadastre**: **Cadastre Agency under the Committee for Urbanization and the Housing Market (Oʻzbekiston Respublikasi Urbanizatsiya va uy-joy bozori qoʻmitasi huzuridagi Kadastr agentligi)** at [kadastr.uz](https://kadastr.uz/en) — established by **Presidential Decree UP-60 of 28 January 2022** consolidating the prior State Committee on Land Resources, Geodesy, Cartography and Cadastre (GKZGDK), and **since re-subordinated from the Ministry of Economy and Finance to the Committee for Urbanization and the Housing Market — the re-subordination instrument is not published on the agency site; verify the current parent body at [kadastr.uz](https://kadastr.uz/en)** (2026 data, source Kadastr agentligi site masthead; 2026-09-18 verified). Operates the **unified e-Cadastre / e-Kadastr** national digital register of immovable property, land parcels, addresses + cadastral plans. **Modernisation backbone**: World Bank "Modernization of Real Property Registration and Cadastre Project" (P151746, US$20M IDA credit approved 24 May 2016, extended; full nationwide digitisation of cadastral + registration documentation completed 2.5× ahead of original scope per Bank ISR — see [WB project page](https://documents.worldbank.org/en/publication/documents-reports/documentdetail/690831467989526521/uzbekistan-modernization-of-real-property-registration-and-cadastre-project)). Rosreestr (Russia) cooperated on cadastral valuation methodology starting 2021.
- **Identifier**: **Cadastral number (kadastr raqami / кадастровый номер)** identifies each immovable object; format used by Cadastre Agency follows region.district.block.parcel hierarchy (verify exact format per parcel at kadastr.uz). Each apartment, house, commercial unit, land plot has a unique cadastral record cross-referenced to a **technical passport (tekhpasport)** maintained by State Unitary Enterprise "Yer Kadastri" / regional cadastre offices.
- **Primary legal information system**: **Lex.uz (Milliy huquqiy axborot bazasi)** — official national legal database under Ministry of Justice: [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/) — Constitution, Codes (Civil, Land, Tax, Criminal, Family, Labour), Presidential decrees (PF/UP), Cabinet resolutions (PQ/QM), all subordinate acts. Russian + Uzbek (Latin + Cyrillic) versions; partial English. **NormaTiv (Norma)** at [norma.uz](https://www.norma.uz/) is a widely-used commercial legal aggregator for practitioners.
- **Foreign-buyer rule**:
  - **Buildings + structures (apartments, houses, commercial premises)**: foreigners + foreign legal entities + stateless persons may **own freehold** per Civil Code + Law on Privatisation of Housing Stock + Cabinet of Ministers **Resolution No. 384 of 13 July 2022** (`PQ-384`; publicly announced 16 July 2022) which expanded eligibility to **citizens of 108 countries** to acquire built real estate **without a residence permit** ([Kun.uz coverage of PQ-384](https://www.kun.uz/en/news/2022/07/16/government-citizens-of-108-countries-can-buy-real-estate-without-a-residence-permit-in-uzbekistan)). PQ-384 also imposes per-region **minimum purchase price thresholds**: **≥ US$150,000 (under-construction) / US$180,000 (completed)** in Tashkent city + Tashkent region + Samarkand; **≥ US$70,000 / US$85,000** in other regions — verify current schedule at [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/) before listing. Pre-2022 regime required foreigners to first obtain Uzbekistan residence permit — that gate is now removed for the 108-country list (which includes EU/UK/US/CA/AU/NZ/JP/KR + GCC + most Asia). (2026-05-27 verified, source Mondaq + Azizov & Partners.)
  - **Land**: per **Land Code Articles 17–18**, foreign individuals + foreign legal entities + stateless persons + enterprises with foreign investment **CANNOT own land** under any pathway. They may hold land plots only on **leasehold (ijara)** for terms typically up to **49 years** (renewable). **Land in Uzbekistan is fundamentally state property** under the Constitution Art. 55 (private land ownership reformed 2018+ — only Uzbek citizens + Uzbek legal entities qualify; Decree UP-5495 of 2018 enabled limited private land for citizens; foreigners excluded). Diplomatic missions are the only foreign-entity exception per Land Code Art. 18.
  - **Practical implication**: a foreigner buying an apartment **owns the building unit** but the **underlying plot remains state-leased** to the building (typically held by the apartment-owners' association or developer). For a standalone house ("hovli") purchase, the foreigner buys the structure; the plot must be re-titled to a leasehold or transferred via a citizen co-owner (LLC vehicle is a common workaround — see Caveats). Source: [Azizov & Partners legal memo](https://azizovpartners.uz/en/memos/acquisition-of-real-estate-by-foreigners-in-uzbekistan/) + [Gratanet land rights brief](https://gratanet.com/publications/land-rights-for-foreign-investors-in-uzbekistan).
  - **Agricultural land**: BANNED for foreigners (citizens-only); also restricted to specific agricultural-purpose entities even for citizens. Permanent prohibition under Land Code.
  - **Border / strategic / military zones**: additional restrictions per Cabinet zoning lists (verify per parcel via cadastre extract).
- **Visa**:
  - **Visa-free 30-day entry** for ~**90 nationalities** (EU/EEA, UK, CA, AU, NZ, JP, KR, IL, Türkiye, GCC, most Latin America + Asia per [Visa policy of Uzbekistan, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visa_policy_of_Uzbekistan)). 2019 + 2020 + 2024 expansions; verify per nationality at **MFA Consular Department**: [mfa.uz](https://www.mfa.uz/) or [e-visa.gov.uz](https://e-visa.gov.uz/).
  - **e-Visa** (introduced 15 July 2018): up to 30 days for ~58 additional nationalities; **US$20** fee; online application via [e-visa.gov.uz](https://e-visa.gov.uz/).
  - **5-year "Golden Visa" (Investor Residence Permit)** — entered into force **1 June 2025** per **Presidential Decree PF-67 (also cited as UP-67) of 18 April 2025** ([Astana Times Apr 2025](https://astanatimes.com/2025/04/uzbekistan-to-offer-five-year-residence-permits-to-foreign-investors-starting-june/) + [Library of Congress Global Legal Monitor 27 Jun 2025](https://www.loc.gov/item/global-legal-monitor/2025-06-27/uzbekistan-new-program-aims-to-enhance-foreign-investments-in-exchange-for-residence-permits/)). This is a **non-refundable one-off donation** — **US$250,000** for the main applicant + **US$150,000 per accompanying family member** (spouse/child/parent), paid to a state-designated account — **with NO real-estate, business, or job-creation condition** (INVEXI, quoting the decree text: "The Decree does not stipulate additional conditions, such as investments in the economy, real estate purchases, or job creation"; [IMI Daily Apr 2025](https://www.imidaily.com/central-asia/uzbekistan-introduces-new-5-year-donation-based-golden-visa) + [Fragomen 22 Apr 2025](https://www.fragomen.com/insights/uzbekistan-new-golden-visa-pathway-forthcoming.html)). **Not yet operational**: the implementing Cabinet/MIA resolution was **not adopted as of October 2025** ([Azizov & Partners memo 13 Oct 2025](https://azizovpartners.uz/en/memos/investment-visa-and-residence-permit-in-uzbekistan)) — banks cannot yet legally accept the donation; verify operational status before relying. (2026-07-02 verified — corrects earlier brief that mislabelled this as a real-estate route and cited "UP-89".)
  - **Separate pre-existing real-estate-for-residence route** (distinct from the PF-67 donation Golden Visa; Presidential Decree UP-5611 appendix 6, amended by UP-101, per IMI Daily reporting) — qualifying property-purchase thresholds:
    - **US$300,000** in **Tashkent city + Tashkent region**
    - **US$200,000** in **Samarkand, Bukhara, Namangan, Andijan, Fergana, or Khorezm regions**
    - **US$100,000** in **Karakalpakstan + all other regions not named above**
  - **3-year residence permit** (separate, pre-existing): ~US$250,000 in authorised capital of Uzbekistan-registered company. **10-year residence**: US$3M into manufacturing/services enterprises.
  - **IT Visa** — 3-year multi-entry visa for IT specialists, founders, investors of **IT Park residents**. Income threshold US$30,000/year for IT specialists; US$10,000 minimum for investor route. **Allows real-estate purchase at any value without minimum threshold** + reside any region without re-registration ([IT Park Uzbekistan IT Visa page](https://www.it-park.uz/en/) + [outsource.gov.uz/en/explore-opportunities/it-visa](https://outsource.gov.uz/en/explore-opportunities/it-visa)). **IT Park residency framework** offers 0 % CIT, 0 % PIT for resident IT employees, 0 % VAT for export services through 2040 (Presidential Decree on IT Park) — major draw for tech founders considering relocation.

### Price trend (2020 → 2025)

- **Tashkent secondary market**: nominal prices roughly **doubled in USD terms 2020 → 2025** (~US$550/m² → ~US$1,082/m²), driven by post-COVID recovery + Russian-relocation wave 2022–2024 + IT Park-driven Tashkent gentrification + persistent inflation. Cumulative ~+96 % nominal USD over 5 years.
- **2025 dynamics**: prices began **cooling** in Tashkent secondary (Stat.uz Q3 2025 print showed mild MoM declines after the spring peak per Gazeta.uz Jul 2025), while regional cities **continued accelerating** — the strongest 2025 H1 growth was **Bukhara +25.6 %** + Sirdaryo **+23.1 %** + Samarkand **+19.2 %** (UzDaily Q4 2025 release). This reflects post-tourism-boom in heritage cities + Tashkent's mid-segment saturation.
- **2025 H1 housing volume +10.6 %** vs H1 2024 (Gazeta.uz); 2025 full-year Tashkent housing sales **+15 %** YoY ([Kun.uz Q3 2025](https://kun.uz/en/51877141)).
- **Russian-relocation impact**: ~100,000–150,000 Russian nationals relocated to Uzbekistan 2022–2024 (estimates vary; Tashkent + Samarkand absorbed the bulk); structural lift to mid-segment Tashkent rents + sale prices, partial unwinding 2024–2025 as cohort either naturalised, moved on, or returned.

### Verdict bands (residential US$/m², Tashkent reference)

- 🟢 within ±10 % of district segment median (per Uybor.uz + Stat.uz, ≥ 5 comparables same district + condition class)
- 🟡 ±20 %
- 🟠 ±30 % — investigate: pre-1991 panel building seismic retrofit status? Cadastral value vs market gap (under-declared transaction)? Land-lease term remaining for the underlying parcel? IT-Visa or Investor-residence-tied minimum threshold ($100k/$200k/$300k)?
- 🔴 > ±30 % — significant red flag

### Compute

1. US$/m² = USD listing ÷ m² advertised. Convert to UZS at CBU rate on contract day if needed.
2. Compare to Stat.uz quarterly print + Uybor.uz district median.
3. **Trap — "общая площадь" (total area, общая) vs "жилая площадь" (living area)**: Soviet/post-Soviet convention typically advertises "общая" including walls + balconies + corridors. 100 m² total ≈ 70–75 m² living. Confirm which figure before per-m² comparison.
4. **Trap — "черновая отделка" (rough finish / shell+core) for new-builds**: Tashkent + regional new-builds frequently sold without internal finishing (no flooring, internal walls in some cases, no plumbing fixtures). Finishing budget: typically **US$200–US$400/m²** (est., Tashkent contractor quotes 2024–2025) to reach habitable; verify per developer schedule.
5. **Trap — under-declared transaction value**: historically common practice to declare a notarial purchase price below true contract value to reduce notary fee + future CGT base; tightening 2024–2026 with cadastral value floors (Tashkent 3.3M UZS/m² / ~US$265 minimum cadastral floor). For foreign buyers, **always declare full true value** — under-declaration creates AML + future-sale CGT exposure.
6. **Trap — parking**: rarely included in Tashkent new-build prices; expect US$8,000–US$25,000 for an underground space in central Tashkent (est., developer schedules 2024–2025 — verify per development).
7. **Trap — real-estate-for-residence threshold (NOT the PF-67 donation Golden Visa)**: the **PF-67 5-year "Golden Visa" is a pure donation route** (US$250k + US$150k/family member, **no property condition**) — buying property does **not** earn it. The property-purchase residence pathway is a **separate pre-existing real-estate-for-residence route** (Tashkent US$300k, Samarkand/Bukhara US$200k, other regions US$100k): the contract value must be **at or above** the relevant threshold AND the property must be of a qualifying type — per Azizov & Partners, **secondary-market residential and non-residential property do not qualify** (verify with the Migration Service before signing). Listings that under-quote the cadastral floor or include unfinished status may not qualify. The PF-67 donation route's implementing Cabinet/MIA resolution was **not adopted as of October 2025** (Azizov & Partners) — banks cannot yet accept donations, so that pathway may not be operational; verify before relying.
8. **Trap — "investment under construction" purchase (preliminary contract / dolevoye uchastie)**: pre-completion purchases were liberalised for foreigners by 2022 reforms ([Tashkent Times Apr 2022](https://tashkenttimes.uz/national/8728-foreigners-to-be-able-to-invest-in-uzbekistan-real-estate-under-construction-to-pay-less-income-tax)); verify developer's escrow account compliance + state registration of the preliminary contract before any pre-payment.

## Section: `--traffic`

### Primary sources

- **Ministry of Transport of the Republic of Uzbekistan (Transport vazirligi)** at [mintrans.uz](https://www.mintrans.uz/en) — sectoral plans, road master plans
- **Republican Joint-Stock Company "Oʻzavtoyoʻl"** ("Uzavtoyul" — formerly "Uzavtoyul" State Committee for Highways) — national highway operator under Ministry of Transport; manages M-prefix (republican) + A-prefix (regional) roads; publishes traffic surveys for international + state highways. **Note**: comprehensive AADT-equivalent open data is **not consistently published online** — primary surveys often available only on request from regional Uzavtoyul offices.
- **Tashkent City Khokimiyat (Toshkent shahar hokimligi)** at [tashkent.uz](https://tashkent.uz/) — Tashkent transport, metro, bus + BRT (Bus Rapid Transit) data
- **Toshkent Metropolitan (Tashkent Metro)** — operated under Ministry of Transport; 4 operating lines (Chilanzar, Uzbekistan, Yunusabad, Circle Line — opened 2020 phased) + ground extensions ongoing
- **Yandex Maps** + **2GIS** — only commercial-grade real-time traffic in UZ (Yandex dominant; 2GIS strong street-level for Tashkent/Samarkand). Google Traffic patchy outside Tashkent CBD.

### Key term

**AADT** equivalent in Uzbek: `oʻrtacha kunlik harakat zichligi` (Latin) / `ўртача кунлик ҳаракат зичлиги` (Cyrillic); in Russian: **`среднесуточная интенсивность движения` (ССИД)**.

### Verdict bands (residential noise impact)

- 🟢 < 1,000 v/d (rural side road, mahalla side street, Tashkent residential cul-de-sac in "массив" inner blocks)
- 🟡 1,000–5,000 v/d (small municipal road, secondary urban street)
- 🟠 5,000–20,000 v/d (urban arterial, A-prefix regional road, Tashkent inner ring like Buyuk Ipak Yoʻli, Furqat, Mukimi, Sharaf Rashidov)
- 🔴 > 20,000 v/d (M-prefix republic highways: M39 Tashkent–Termez–Afghanistan border via Samarkand, M37 Tashkent–Bukhara–Turkmen border, M34 Tashkent–Kazakh border; Tashkent main avenues: Amir Temur, Mustaqillik, Bobur, Shota Rustaveli, Olmazor, Mukimi, Halqlar Doʻstligi)

### Coarse fallback (OSM `highway` class)

- `motorway` = M-prefix republic highway
- `trunk`/`primary` = A-prefix regional / inter-viloyat road
- `secondary` = viloyat / tuman road
- `tertiary` = local connector
- `residential` = mahalla / massive street

### Notes

- **Tashkent Metro**: 4 lines + ~50 stations (2024–2025 expansion); properties within ~600 m of station carry ~10–15 % premium (est., Uybor.uz district analysis 2024 — verify per listing). Circle Line (Halqa yoʻli, opened phased 2020–2023) above-ground, runs around Tashkent city.
- **Tashkent BRT** (Bus Rapid Transit): launched 2017 on selected corridors; ongoing expansion.
- **Tashkent Ring Road / Big Tashkent Ring Road**: bypass corridor; properties along corridor see freight noise + improved access.
- **Soviet-era arterial layout**: Tashkent (rebuilt entirely after the **26 April 1966 Mw 5.2 earthquake** — see `--risks`) retains wide Soviet boulevards (Amir Temur, Mustaqillik, Olmazor) → fast traffic + significant noise on properties within 200 m of arterials.
- **Trap — "5 minut metrodan" / "5 минут от метро"** (5 minutes from metro): Tashkent metro coverage thicker than Almaty's but still patchy in outer districts (Sergeli, Bektemir, Yangihayot) — confirm walking distance not "marshrutka" / shared-taxi distance.
- **Trap — Fergana Valley (Andijan, Namangan, Fergana)**: dense rural population; main inter-city roads carry significant freight + agricultural traffic during harvest seasons (cotton + fruit logistics).

## Section: `--tax`

### Currency note

UZS, USD/UZS ≈ 12,300–12,950 (Q1–Q2 2026 CBU reference rate, managed-float). All annual property taxes payable in UZS to local Tax Committee account; conversions in playbook use approximate **12,500 UZS/USD** as a rounding mid-point — verify against current CBU rate at [cbu.uz/en/](https://cbu.uz/en/).

### Tax-code regime context

- **Tax Code of the Republic of Uzbekistan** (Code No. ZRU-599 of 30 December 2019, in force 1 January 2020 as amended through 2025–2026) — comprehensive consolidated code replacing the 2007 Tax Code. Amended annually via the **State Budget Law** + interim **Presidential Decrees** (PF/UP) + **Cabinet Resolutions** (PQ/QM).
- **2023 PIT reform**: replaced the prior progressive 3-tier scale (7.5 %/16.5 %/22.5 %) with a **flat 12 % PIT rate** for residents (employment, self-employment, rental, and CGT — Tax Code Art. 381, [PwC Uzbekistan Individual taxes](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/republic-of-uzbekistan/individual/taxes-on-personal-income)).
- **2025 changes**: **property tax indexed +10 %**; minimum cadastral floors raised (Tashkent 3.3M UZS/m², regional centres 2.2M UZS/m², other 1.3M UZS/m²) — see [Kun.uz tax-rise summary](https://kun.uz/en/84676729) + [EY Uzbekistan tax updates 2025](https://www.ey.com/en_uz/technical/tax-alerts/2025/01/changes-in-taxation-in-uzbekistan).
- **2026 changes** (effective 1 January 2026; [EY Uzbekistan tax updates 2026](https://www.ey.com/en_uz/technical/tax-alerts/2026/01/uzbekistan-tax-updates-2026) + [Kun.uz Deputy PM Kuchkarov 22 Dec 2025](https://kun.uz/en/news/2025/12/22/deputy-pm-kuchkarov-details-which-taxes-will-rise-and-which-will-stay-unchanged-in-2026)):
  - **VAT**: rate **unchanged at 12 %** (no rise in 2026; AI-driven real-time invoice analysis system launched 1 Jan 2026 — high-risk invoice input VAT recoverable only after supplier remits to budget)
  - **Corporate income tax**: base rate **unchanged at 15 %**
  - **Corporate property tax**: base rate **unchanged at 1.5 %**
  - **Property tax (legal entities) + land tax (legal entities + individuals) + water-use tax**: **+7 % indexation** on average
  - **PIT**: flat **12 %** unchanged

### Annual property tax — Property Tax of Individuals (`Jismoniy shaxslarning mol-mulk solig'i` / Налог на имущество физических лиц)

Governed by Tax Code Section XV, Articles 419–433 (verify in [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/) Tax Code).

**Residential rate schedule (effective 2025; 2026 rates indexed +7 % on cadastral floor base, rate percentages unchanged per Kuchkarov briefing)**:

| Property type & area | Rate (% of cadastral value) |
|---|---:|
| Houses + apartments **up to 200 m²** (urban + rural) | **0.34 %** |
| Urban properties **200–500 m²**; rural **>200 m²** | **0.45 %** |
| Urban properties **>500 m²** | **0.6 %** |
| Premises used for entrepreneurial activity / rented commercially (individuals) | **2.0 %** |

Source: Tax Code Art. 425; [Kun.uz tax brief Dec 2024](https://kun.uz/en/84676729); [tax-legal.uz VAT page](https://tax-legal.uz/en/value-added-tax/).

**Cadastral value floor (per m², 2025 — used when individual cadastral assessment is below floor or absent)**:

| Locality | Floor (UZS/m²) | Approx USD/m² @ 12,500 |
|---|---:|---:|
| **Tashkent city** | **3,300,000** | ~$264 |
| Regional centres + Tashkent oblast | **2,200,000** | ~$176 |
| Other localities | **1,300,000** | ~$104 |

Source: [Kun.uz](https://kun.uz/en/84676729) + Tax Committee guidance; 2026 indexation of individuals' property-tax figures is NOT stated in the EY 2026 alert (EY covers legal-entity property-tax base rates + land tax only) — data not publicly available in the sources read; verify in Law ZRU-1108 at lex.uz (verify exact 2026 floors at [gov.uz/en/soliq](https://gov.uz/en/soliq) before relying).

### Example calculation (Tashkent mid-segment apartment)

A Uybor.uz listing: Tashkent Yashnabad 70 m² 2-bedroom apartment, 2018 build, asking price US$95,000 (~UZS 1,187,500,000):
- Cadastral value floor calculation: 70 m² × 3,300,000 UZS/m² (Tashkent floor 2025) = **UZS 231,000,000 (~US$18,500)** assessed cadastral
- Property tax band: ≤200 m² → 0.34 % rate → **UZS 785,400/year (~US$63/year)** at floor; if individual cadastral is higher, rate applies on actual cadastral value
- est. only if the +7 % 2026 indexation (per the Kuchkarov briefing cited in the rate-schedule heading above — not stated in the EY 2026 alert; verify in ZRU-1108) applies to individuals: ~UZS 840,000/year (~US$67/year)
- **Annual property tax in UZ is materially low** (typically US$50–US$200/year for an Tashkent mid-segment flat) — among the lowest in the broader Central Asia / CIS region.

> Effective property-tax rate on **market value** (not cadastral) is typically **0.05–0.15 %** for Tashkent residential — even with the 2025 floor uplift (and any 2026 +7 % indexation, unconfirmed for individuals), the tax burden remains negligible by global standards. Verify per parcel with the **Tax Committee** at [gov.uz/en/soliq](https://gov.uz/en/soliq) or via your taxpayer cabinet (`my.soliq.uz`).

### Land tax (Yer solig'i / Земельный налог)

- For **individual landowners** (Uzbek citizens only — foreigners cannot own land): per Tax Code Art. 434–442; rates differentiated by region + zone + use class; expressed in UZS/m²/year × zoning coefficient
- For **leased land** (foreign-owned buildings on state-leased plot): land lease payment goes to Cabinet of Ministers / regional khokimiyat per the lease contract; **no separate land tax** for the foreign apartment owner (the lease payment is built into the building-association common-area dues / kommunalka)
- Tashkent zone-1 (premium centre) land-use base rate in apartment-buildings absorbed pro-rata into building common area; usually negligible per apartment

### Transaction taxes (one-time at purchase)

- **Property transfer tax / state duty (Davlat boji / госпошлина)**: **0 %** transfer tax (Uzbekistan has **no separate property transfer tax** for individuals on residential transactions — confirmed PwC Uzbekistan tax summary + Azizov & Partners memo)
- **VAT (QQS / НДС)**:
  - **Resale between individuals (secondary market)**: **EXEMPT** (out of VAT scope — individual non-entrepreneur sales)
  - **First sale by VAT-registered developer (primary market)**: **residential generally exempt** under Tax Code Art. 244; commercial first sale subject to VAT 12 %
  - Verify exact treatment at [tax-legal.uz/en/value-added-tax](https://tax-legal.uz/en/value-added-tax/) + 2026 Tax Code consolidated text on [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/)
- **Notary fee (Notarial yig'im / нотариальный сбор)**: **mandatory notarisation** for any immovable property purchase contract per Civil Code; sliding scale per Cabinet Decree on Notarial Tariff — typically **0.1 %–1.0 %** of contract value; verify current schedule at [adliya.uz](https://www.adliya.uz/) (Ministry of Justice)
- **State registration fee at Cadastre Agency**: **data not publicly available — verify at the [Cadastre Agency](https://kadastr.uz/en)**; the English portal surfaces the registration services but publishes no tariff table (2026-09-18 verified). Expedited processing is offered at a higher fee.
- **Real-estate agent (rieltor)**: typically **2–4 %** paid by seller; foreign buyers occasionally asked to pay buyer-side commission — negotiate
- **Total transaction cost (buyer side, individual–individual resale)**: **~0.3 %–2.0 %** before agent commission — among the lowest in the broader CIS/Asia
- **AML / cash-payment limit**: per Currency Regulation Law No. ZRU-573 of 2019 + Cabinet decrees, real-estate purchases over thresholds (verify current cap at CBU) require **bank-transfer settlement**; cash payments above limits trigger AML reporting via Department for Combating Economic Crimes under the Prosecutor General's Office. **For foreign buyers, bank transfer is the standard** + recommended for AML defensibility.

### Capital gains tax (CGT) on sale

- **Resident individual** sale of residential immovable: **12 % flat PIT** on net gain (Tax Code Art. 381 + 387). Holding-period exemption rules: verify in [PwC individual income](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/republic-of-uzbekistan/individual/income-determination) — generally no full exemption regardless of holding period, but documented basis (purchase contract, improvements) reduces taxable gain
- **Non-resident individual** sale of UZ-located residential: **20 % flat** on gross or net (verify per PwC Uzbekistan + Tax Code Art. 388 — non-resident rate higher than resident)
- **Resident landlord** rental income: **12 % flat** PIT (subject to filing per below)
- **Non-resident landlord**: **12 % WHT** on UZ-source rental income (reduced from 20 % by Presidential Resolution; verify current at PwC) — paid by the tenant or property manager as tax agent

### Personal income tax — general (Tax Code Art. 381)

| Category | Rate |
|---|---|
| **Resident individual (worldwide income)** | **12 % flat** |
| Non-resident individual (UZ-source income) | **12 % flat** (reduced from prior 20 %) |
| Capital gains — resident | **12 %** |
| Capital gains — non-resident on UZ real-estate sale | **20 %** |
| Self-employed individual entrepreneur (IE) — simplified regime | **4 %** (turnover up to ~UZS 500M; verify current threshold) |

- **Tax residency**: 183+ days in any 12-month period rolling
- **Tax filing**: Form for individuals with non-employment income (rental, self-employment, foreign income) by **1 April of year + 1**; payment by 1 June of year + 1; via [my.soliq.uz](https://my.soliq.uz/) taxpayer cabinet
- **TIN (STIR — Soliq toʻlovchining identifikatsiya raqami)**: 9-digit individual taxpayer identification number; **prerequisite for property purchase, banking, IE registration**; foreigners obtain at any Tax Committee branch with passport

### AML / cash limits + currency-payment rules

- **Currency Regulation Law No. ZRU-573 of 22 October 2019** (in force 23 Oct 2019, amendments 2021–2025): Currency Liberalisation Law — replaces the pre-2019 strict capital-control regime. Residents must transact in **UZS** for UZ-located goods + services + immovable property (Art. 8); foreign-currency payments between residents prohibited except in specifically licensed cases. Non-residents may price contracts in foreign currency but settlement of UZ-real-estate transactions occurs in UZS at CBU reference rate on contract day.
- **Cash-payment limit**: per Cash Discipline rules, large cash payments are restricted; for individuals buying real estate, **bank transfer is the standard**, especially for high-value transactions due to AML scrutiny under the **Department for Combating Economic Crimes (DBEK) under the Prosecutor General's Office**.
- **AML supervisor**: **Department for Combating Economic Crimes (PGO)** + **CBU AML/CFT division** + **Financial Monitoring Department (Ministry of Economy and Finance)**. Notaries are obligated reporting entities under the Law on AML/CFT (LRU-660 of 2009 as amended).
- **Capital movement**: investor-protection guarantee under Investment Law (LRU-598 of 25 Dec 2019) enables repatriation of foreign-currency proceeds from sale of UZ real estate — subject to tax clearance + AML review at CBU.

### Future risk

- **Property-tax modernisation**: ongoing transition from area-based + cadastral-floor system toward genuine market-aligned cadastral valuation (Rosreestr-supported pilots since 2021, [WB ISR](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/963031624984183169/txt/Disclosable-Version-of-the-ISR-Modernization-of-Real-Property-Registration-and-Cadastre-P151746-Sequence-No-13.txt)) — when complete, effective property-tax burden may rise materially; monitor Tax Committee announcements
- **VAT real-time invoice AI system**: launched 1 Jan 2026; high-risk invoice flagging may delay input VAT recovery — affects developers + commercial RE more than individual residential buyers
- **Currency-control evolution**: 2019 liberalisation continues to be refined; 2024 + 2025 amendments tightened reporting on outward transfers >US$10k; monitor [cbu.uz](https://cbu.uz/) bulletins
- **Real-estate-for-residence threshold** (separate pre-existing route, distinct from the PF-67 donation Golden Visa): $100k/$200k/$300k by region; thresholds may be revised in 2026–2028 budget cycles
- **Cadastral floor**: Tashkent floor 3.3M UZS/m² (2025) → ~3.53M UZS/m² est. if the Kuchkarov-briefing +7 % 2026 indexation applies (not stated in the EY 2026 alert — verify in ZRU-1108); long-run trajectory aligns toward ~50–70 % of market price as Rosreestr methodology matures

### Long-term residential

**Tax options for individual landlord** (foreigner with valid 5-year residence permit / IT Visa / company-tied work permit + STIR):

1. **PIT 12 % flat without IE registration** — simplest path:
   - **12 % flat** on gross rental income for residents; **12 % WHT** for non-residents (paid by tenant as tax agent)
   - Annual filing on the individual income declaration (Form via my.soliq.uz) by **1 April year + 1**; payment by 1 June year + 1
   - Example: Tashkent 2-bed flat at UZS 5,500,000/month (~US$440) → UZS 66M annual income → **UZS 7.92M PIT (~US$634/year)**

2. **Individual Entrepreneur (IE / Yakka tartibdagi tadbirkor / ИП) — simplified turnover regime**:
   - **4 % of gross turnover** (turnover up to ~UZS 500M ~ US$40k; verify current threshold)
   - Register at any Tax Committee branch in 1–3 days; ~free
   - Filing via [my.soliq.uz](https://my.soliq.uz/) monthly/quarterly
   - Better path for landlords with multi-property portfolios

3. **LLC ("MChJ" — Mas'uliyati cheklangan jamiyat / ООО)** — for portfolio investors:
   - 15 % CIT on profit + 5 %/10 % dividend WHT
   - Foreign founders unrestricted; useful for multi-unit + STR-management portfolio
   - Property held in LLC name avoids personal-name foreign-buyer thresholds + simplifies AML

### Short-term rentals (STR / Airbnb / Booking)

**Status (2025–2026)**: largely treated as ordinary rental for tax purposes; **emerging municipal-level regulation**; no national STR-specific licence regime as of write date. Some discussion of restrictions in Tashkent / Samarkand / Bukhara — verify current city-level rules before listing.

#### Tax classification

- **Individual operating informally**: 12 % PIT on net rental + risk of being reclassified as "entrepreneurial activity" → triggers IE registration + retroactive penalties
- **IE under 4 % turnover regime**: works for STR if cap respected
- **VAT registration**: required if turnover crosses VAT threshold (verify at [gov.uz/en/soliq](https://gov.uz/en/soliq) — currently UZS 1B / ~US$80k turnover)
- **Tourist tax / accommodation fee**: NONE national; some Tashkent + Samarkand + Bukhara districts experimenting with municipal-level levies — verify per khokimiyat before listing

#### Practical (2025–2026)

- **Building/HOA approval**: newer Tashkent + Samarkand developments increasingly include STR restrictions in bylaws — verify before buying

### Seismicity — material concern, especially Tashkent + Fergana Valley

- Uzbekistan sits on the **southern Tian Shan + Pamir collision zone** with active deformation. Seismicity is **moderate-to-high in the south + east** (Tashkent, Fergana Valley, Surxondaryo, Samarkand) and lower in the western desert + Karakalpakstan.
- **1966 Tashkent earthquake (26 April 1966, Mw 5.2, depth 3–8 km, epicentre central Tashkent)**: foundational event in Uzbek seismic awareness. Despite moderate magnitude, the **shallow focal depth + dense unreinforced adobe + masonry housing stock** caused **massive destruction** — most pre-1966 buildings were damaged or destroyed, 200,000–300,000 left homeless, 15–200 deaths (low casualty count attributed to early-morning timing + low collapse-fatality of single-storey adobe). Reconstruction was a Soviet all-Union effort 1966–1976; modern Tashkent's wide boulevards + concrete-panel housing dominate as legacy. Source: [1966 Tashkent earthquake, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1966_Tashkent_earthquake) + [ReliefWeb 50-year retrospective](https://reliefweb.int/report/uzbekistan/1966-2016-50-years-tashkent-earthquake) + [seismic-risk assessment Nature Sci Rep 2026](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-026-44585-3).
- **Current seismic risk** ([Nature Sci Rep 2026 paper on Tashkent's residential buildings, 1966 scenario]): approximately **4.0 % of Tashkent housing stock** — primarily brick + adobe buildings constructed before 1966 — may experience severe damage under a **Mw 5.5 earthquake scenario**. Post-1966 Soviet panel + post-2000 frame construction performs significantly better; **independent structural survey strongly advised** for any pre-1966 structure (rare in central Tashkent post-reconstruction but present in old mahalla quarters of Samarkand + Bukhara).
- **Fergana Valley** (Andijan, Fergana, Namangan): high regional seismicity; **1902 Andijan Mw 6.4** earthquake (~4,500 deaths); densely populated with mixed-age housing stock. Buyers should request seismic-zoning certification from regional khokimiyat / Institute of Seismology before deposit.
- **Building code**: **KMK 2.01.03-19 "Construction in seismic regions"** + earlier KMK seismic codes — partial Eurocode 8 alignment ongoing. Seismic-resistance class is required disclosure on building registration certificates per Ministry of Construction order — verify on technical passport.
- **Soviet panel-block stock** (5-storey + 9-storey + 16-storey panels constructed 1970s–1980s): retrofit status uneven; documented vulnerability of poorly-detailed joints in some series. Independent structural survey advised for pre-1991 panel buildings.

### Flood + drought + water-scarcity (climate-driven)

- **Major rivers**: Syr Darya (Fergana Valley → Aral basin), Amu Darya (south + west → Aral basin), Zarafshan (Samarkand–Bukhara), Surxondaryo, Chirchiq (Tashkent region) — all transboundary.
- **Drought + water-stress** is the dominant hydrological hazard, not flood — Uzbekistan has been dramatically affected by the **Aral Sea crisis** (sea volume reduced ~90 % since 1960; eastern Aral basin is now the **Aralqum desert**, ~60,000 km²), and central Asia faces accelerating **water-deficit risk by 2050** ([UNEP Atlas + ADB Climate Adaptive Water Resources Management report](https://www.adb.org/sites/default/files/linked-documents/53120-001-ssa.pdf)): water supply forecast to drop **57 → 52–54 billion m³** while demand rises **59 → 62–63 billion m³** — water deficit growing from ~2 billion m³ to **11–13 billion m³** by 2050.
- **Glacier loss in Pamir + Tian Shan headwaters**: severe by 2050 — projected **1.75–2.25 °C** mean temperature increase; reduced reservoir inflows; **GLOF (glacial-lake outburst flood) risk** elevated in transition phase.
- **Spring snowmelt + flash-flood events**: increasing intensity per UNEP + Uzhydromet projections; precipitation more extreme but less frequent. Tashkent + Samarkand + Fergana cities historically buffered by reservoir + canal systems but suburban gully-floods + mahalla drainage failures during cloudburst events documented 2020–2025.
- **2025–2026 drought watch**: ongoing reduced precipitation in Surxondaryo + Karakalpakstan; agricultural-water stress affecting rural property utility supply.

### Aral Sea region (Karakalpakstan) — chronic environmental hazard

- **Karakalpakstan + parts of Khorezm**: among the world's most degraded environmental zones — desiccated Aral Sea bed releases **toxic dust** (salts, residual pesticides incl. DDT/HCH from Soviet cotton agriculture, heavy metals); chronic respiratory + cancer + reproductive health impact documented over decades by WHO + UNDP.
- **Property-investment relevance**: Nukus + nearby cities have low absolute property prices (~US$200–US$400/m²) but **acute environmental + health risk** for long-term residence; tourism focus on Aral coast + Mizdakhan necropolis is niche.
- Source: [UNEP press release on Climate Change Atlas Uzbekistan](https://www.unep.org/news-and-stories/press-release/climate-change-drives-rising-temperatures-and-severe-drought) + [Aral Sea Resilience Portal, UNESCAP](https://rrp.unescap.org/aral-sea).

### Air quality — Tashkent winter pollution (chronic concern)

- Tashkent ranks among Central Asia's **most-polluted capitals** in winter (Nov–Mar): coal + gas-fired CHPP emissions, vehicle fleet (~700k cars), residential coal heating in suburban "private sector" (chastny sektor / xususiy sektor), seasonal inversion in the Tashkent basin.
- Winter PM2.5 spikes routinely **>100–250 μg/m³** (vs WHO 24-hr guideline 15 μg/m³); 2024 + 2025 episodes triggered school-closure advisories.
- Property buyers in Tashkent should factor air filtration (HEPA + activated carbon) + double-glazing into renovation budget; suburban + foothill (Bostanlik) cleaner than valley floor.

### Climate change projections

- **Mean annual temperature**: **+1.75–2.25 °C** by 2050 (UNEP Atlas + ADB); summer warming faster (+2 to +2.75 °C) than winter (+1.5 to +2 °C)

1. **Listing language**:

## Section: `--currency` (UZ-specific extension)

Uzbekistan has a **managed-float UZS** since the **2 September 2017 currency liberalisation** (Presidential Decree UP-5177 of 2 Sep 2017) which collapsed the prior dual-rate system (~3,000 UZS/USD official vs ~8,000 unofficial — ~2.5× spread) into a unified rate. Key dynamics:

- **CBU policy rate**: **14.0 % April 2025–April 2026 (unchanged)** ([CBU 11 Dec 2025 release](https://cbu.uz/en/monetary-policy/publications/press-releases/3143821/)) — among the highest in the broader region; persistent high-rate stance to anchor inflation expectations
- **Inflation**: **7.3 % end-2025 actual** vs target 5 %; CBU forecast **6.5 % end-2026**; **disinflation trajectory underway** (CBU statement)
- **USDUZS trajectory 2017–2026**: ~3,200 (Sep 2017 post-liberalisation) → ~12,500 (Q1 2026) — **~290 % cumulative depreciation over 9 years**; depreciation pace moderating since 2023
- **Exchange-rate exposure during purchase**: contracts denominated in USD between non-resident parties are common, but **settlement of UZ-real-estate transactions must occur in UZS** at CBU reference rate on transaction day per Currency Regulation Law ZRU-573 of 2019
- **Capital controls**: relatively liberal post-2019 vs pre-2017 strict regime; banks must report large transfers; AML/CFT scrutiny applies; outward transfers >US$10k flagged; investor-protection guarantee under Investment Law LRU-598 enables repatriation of sale proceeds (subject to tax clearance)
- **Soʻm outlook**: CBU statement Dec 2025 cites **stronger-than-expected appreciation** in H2 2025 dampening import inflation; 2026 trajectory expected modest depreciation (~3–5 %) absent external shocks
- **Practical buyer hedge**: keep purchase reserve in USD until 5–10 days before notarial signing; convert at contract-day CBU rate; consider holding USD via Click/Payme/Uzum multi-currency accounts (CBU-licensed) for interim parking
- **Sanctions context**: Uzbekistan is **not under Western sanctions**; UZ banks have correspondent banking with EU + US + UK + GCC; some UZ banks limit USD/EUR services for nationals of sanctioned jurisdictions (Russia, Belarus) due to secondary-sanction risk — generally not an issue for EU/UK/US/CA/AU buyers

## Quirks to know

- **STIR (TIN) `XXXXXXXXX`** for individuals (9-digit); **prerequisite** for property purchase, IE registration, banking; obtain at any Tax Committee branch in ~1 day with passport
- **Tashkent district names**: Mirabad / Mirobod, Yakkasaray / Yakkasaroy, Yashnabad / Yashnobod, Mirzo Ulugbek / Mirzo Ulug'bek, Chilanzar / Chilonzor, Yunusabad / Yunusobod, Shaikhantakhur / Shayxontohur, Sergeli / Sergeli, Bektemir / Bektemir, Yangihayot / Yangihayot, Almazar / Olmazor, Uchtepa / Uchtepa — **dual transliteration** (Russian-derived vs Uzbek-Latin); confirm cadastral number not just street address
- **Latin vs Cyrillic Uzbek**: official scripts coexist; Latin is constitutional standard since 1995 but Cyrillic is widely retained in legacy documents, signage, older generation; documents may exist in either or both
- **Mahalla committee (mahalla qoʻmitasi)**: traditional Uzbek neighbourhood self-government unit; ~10,000+ mahallas nationally; the mahalla raisi (chair) is a useful first contact for parcel-level due-diligence + community context
- **"Korzinka" / "Havas" / "Makro"**: dominant supermarket chains — proxy for "is this neighbourhood adequately served"
- **Click + Payme + Uzum**: dominant fintech super-apps — used for banking + RE classifieds + payments + e-gov interactions; foreign buyers should obtain a local-bank card + Click/Payme account for friction-free transactions (requires STIR + UZ-issued bank card)
- **Notarial mandatory**: any property contract must be notarised — not optional (Civil Code)
- **Cadastre same-day registration**: express 24h service available at major Public Service Centres in Tashkent
- **"Общая площадь" trap**: advertised m² typically includes balconies + walls (общая) — confirm cadastral m² not advertised m²
- **Dual-language documents**: contracts often in Uzbek (Latin) + Russian; legal effect with Uzbek-language version per Constitution but Russian text governs in practice for foreigner contracts
- **Karimov-era → Mirziyoyev-era**: pre-2017 documents may reference defunct ministries (e.g., "GKZGDK" State Committee for Land Resources — replaced 2022 by Cadastre Agency); verify current authority for any pre-2017 record
- **Cyrillic legacy**: pre-2000 cadastral records may be Cyrillic-only; digitisation rollout has bridged most but rural archive verification may require translation
