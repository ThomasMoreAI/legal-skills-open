# Cayman Islands 🇰🇾 — Property Due-Diligence Playbook

ISO2: `ky`. Status: ✅ Fully populated (researched 2026-05-28).

> **Critical framing — read first**: The Cayman Islands is a **British Overseas Territory (BOT)** — a self-governing, English-common-law jurisdiction in the western Caribbean (three islands: Grand Cayman, Cayman Brac, Little Cayman) with the UK retaining defence + foreign affairs and a Crown-appointed Governor (Cayman Islands Constitution Order 2009, as amended 2020 — verify at [gov.ky](https://www.gov.ky/)). The currency **KYD / CI$ is pegged to USD at CI$1 = US$1.20** (fixed since 1974 under the Currency Law; US$1 ≈ CI$0.83–0.84) — verify at [Cayman Islands Monetary Authority (CIMA)](https://www.cima.ky/). **Foreign buyers face NO general ownership restriction**: non-Caymanians may buy and hold **freehold** real estate with **no restriction on size, value, or number**, and **no alien-landholding licence or government consent** is required for an ordinary residential/land purchase (verified as a *negative* against multiple strong-secondary legal sources — Appleby 2025, Chambers, Carey Olsen, Provenance; **no primary statute imposing a foreign-ownership consent regime found**; confirm the absence at [gov.ky](https://www.gov.ky/) / [Lands & Survey](https://www.caymanlandinfo.ky/)). **Tax stack is the defining feature**: ❌ NO income tax · ❌ NO capital gains tax · ❌ NO inheritance/estate tax · ❌ NO annual property/land tax · ❌ NO corporation tax. The **one transaction lever = STAMP DUTY ON TRANSFER OF LAND**: **7.5% standard**, with a **NEW 10% band on consideration of CI$2,000,000 or above effective 1 January 2026** (Stamp Duty (Rates of Duty)(No.2) Regulations 2025 — verified primary; slab on whole value, not marginal). Title is **Torrens-style, state-guaranteed registered title** under the **Registered Land Act (2018 Revision)** (the published 2018 Revision PDF itself reads "Registered Land **Law**" — Cayman renamed all "Laws" → "Acts" in the 2020 constitutional reform, so current practitioner guides cite the "Act" form; both refer to the same statute — verify latest revision at [legislation.gov.ky](https://legislation.gov.ky/)). The civil court is the **Grand Court of the Cayman Islands** — a superior court of record with unlimited civil + criminal jurisdiction (mirrors England's High Court); **NOT** the Eastern Caribbean Supreme Court (Cayman is outside the ECSC system); appeals → **Cayman Islands Court of Appeal (CICA)** → **Judicial Committee of the Privy Council (JCPC, London)** (verify at [judicial.ky](https://judicial.ky/)). **Hurricane risk is the primary physical hazard**: **Hurricane Ivan (Sept 2004)** is the baseline catastrophe — passed ~30 mi (50 km) of Grand Cayman as a Cat 4/5 system; **over 80% of buildings damaged**; the current legislated building code is **IBC-2009-based**. Short-term rentals require a **Hotel Licensing Board tourism-accommodation licence** + a **13% Tourist Accommodation Tax**. **Confirm** the 1 Jan 2026 stamp-duty band, registry revision, residency-investment thresholds, insurance + water tariffs, and STR licence status at primary sources **before any binding step**. Authoritative primary sources: **[Government of the Cayman Islands (gov.ky)](https://www.gov.ky/)**, **[Lands & Survey Department / caymanlandinfo.ky](https://www.caymanlandinfo.ky/)**, **[Cayman Islands legislation (legislation.gov.ky)](https://legislation.gov.ky/)**, **[Judicial Administration (judicial.ky)](https://judicial.ky/)**, **[Cayman Islands Monetary Authority (CIMA)](https://www.cima.ky/)**, **[WORC — Workforce Opportunities & Residency Cayman](https://www.worc.ky/)**, **[Economics & Statistics Office (ESO)](https://www.eso.ky/)**.

## Country profile

- **Geography**: Three low-lying limestone islands in the western Caribbean — **Grand Cayman** (largest; capital **George Town**; ~76% of the property market by value), **Cayman Brac**, and **Little Cayman**. Grand Cayman's property market concentrates on the **Seven Mile Beach (SMB) corridor**, George Town, South Sound, West Bay, and Rum Point / Cayman Kai. **Highest natural point is The Bluff on Cayman Brac at ~43 m (141 ft)** ([The Bluff — Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Bluff_(Cayman_Islands)) — secondary); **Grand Cayman is very low-lying** (much of it a few metres above mean sea level), which is the core flood / storm-surge / sea-level-rise driver (see `--risks` + `--climate`).
- **Population**: **~83,000–87,000** (2024 est., Economics & Statistics Office — verify exact current figure at [ESO](https://www.eso.ky/); do not over-precise). High net in-migration of expatriate workers; among the highest GDP-per-capita economies in the Caribbean.
- **GDP per capita**: among the highest in the Caribbean (financial-services + tourism economy) — verify current figure at [ESO](https://www.eso.ky/). The jurisdiction finances government **without income tax** (revenue is duty-, fee-, and tourism-tax-based).
- **Currency**: **Cayman Islands dollar (KYD / CI$)** — **pegged to USD at CI$1 = US$1.20 since 1974** (fixed peg under the Currency Law, administered by [CIMA](https://www.cima.ky/)). **USD is accepted in everyday commerce** and the property market is effectively **USD-denominated** (peg makes USD figures stable). See `--currency`.
- **Languages**: **English (official, sole)** — all contracts, deeds, and statutes are in English.
- **Legal system**: **English common law** + Cayman statutes. The **Grand Court of the Cayman Islands** is the superior court of first instance (modern form by the Grand Court Law 1975; constitutional court since 1984; specialist **Financial Services Division** since 2009). **NOT part of the Eastern Caribbean Supreme Court.** Appeals → **Cayman Islands Court of Appeal (CICA)** → **Judicial Committee of the Privy Council (JCPC)** (verify at [judicial.ky](https://judicial.ky/)). **Conveyancing** runs on a **Torrens-style, state-guaranteed registered-title system** under the **Registered Land Act (2018 Revision)** — see `--price` Key terms + Foreign buyer eligibility.
- **Admin levels**: Grand Cayman is divided into districts (George Town, West Bay, Bodden Town, East End, North Side); Cayman Brac + Little Cayman are the Sister Islands. No municipal property-tax tier exists (no annual property tax — see `--tax`).
- **Cadastre / land registry**:
  - **[Lands & Survey Department (caymanlandinfo.ky)](https://www.caymanlandinfo.ky/)** — the **Land Registry Section** (maintained by the **Registrar of Lands**) holds the conclusive register; the **Valuation & Estates Office (VEO)** administers stamp duty on transfer of land.
  - **System**: title by registration under the **Registered Land Act (2018 Revision)**; the **State guarantees the register** (Part X government indemnity). Title-chain investigation common in deeds-recording systems is unnecessary — the current register is conclusive.
- **Identifier**: **Block + Parcel** reference under the Land Register (single source of truth — shows proprietor, charges, easements, restrictive agreements, cautions).
- **Title concept**: **Freehold (absolute) registered title** dominant; **strata title** for apartments/condominiums (common on the SMB corridor); leasehold less common.
- **Recent reforms (12-month watchlist)**:
  - **Stamp duty 10% luxury band — effective 1 January 2026**: a new 10% rate (up from the 7.5% standard) applies where the consideration (higher of price or market value) is **CI$2,000,000 or above**, on residential property OR land — **Stamp Duty (Rates of Duty)(No.2) Regulations 2025** (verified primary at [gov.ky](https://www.gov.ky/) / [radiocayman.gov.ky](https://www.radiocayman.gov.ky/); slab on whole value, not marginal; cross-checked Cayman Compass 18 Dec 2025). A companion **Land Holding Companies Share Transfer Tax (Amendment) Act 2025** mirrors the 10% tier for share-transfer acquisitions of land-holding companies. **Verify exact statutory text + threshold at [legislation.gov.ky](https://legislation.gov.ky/) before any client memo.**
  - **Registry-statute citation note**: cite **"Registered Land Act (2018 Revision)"** (current practitioner-citation style post-2020 "Law"→"Act" rename); the published 2018 Revision PDF itself reads "Registered Land **Law**". Do **not** use the deprecated "Registration (Land) Law (1996 Revision)". Confirm latest revision at [legislation.gov.ky](https://legislation.gov.ky/).
  - **Fuel-cost-charge cap on electricity** announced for summer 2026 (CI$0.18/kWh cap for ~90% of households, 101–2,000 kWh/mo) — verify cap status at [cuc-cayman.com](https://www.cuc-cayman.com/) (see `--mains`).

## 🇰🇾 Foreign buyer eligibility (READ FIRST)

This section determines whether a transaction is even legally possible — and what process applies. The Cayman Islands operates **one of the most foreign-buyer-friendly residential real-estate regimes in the Caribbean**: there is **no ownership restriction and no licence requirement** for non-Caymanians; the only buyer-side differential is **stamp duty**.

### Legal basis

- **Registered Land Act (2018 Revision)** — Torrens-style, state-guaranteed registered-title system; title passes by registration, not by deed. (Published 2018 Revision PDF reads "Registered Land Law" — pre-2020 "Law"→"Act" rename; verify at [legislation.gov.ky](https://legislation.gov.ky/).)
- **Stamp Duty Act (2019 Revision)** + subordinate **Stamp Duty (Rates of Duty) Regulations** (incl. the **(No.2) Regulations 2025** introducing the 1 Jan 2026 10% band) — the transaction lever. (Verify at [legislation.gov.ky](https://legislation.gov.ky/).)
- **Land Holding Companies Share Transfer Tax Act** (+ 2025 amendment) — captures stamp duty on acquisitions via share transfer of a land-holding company (mirrors the 10% tier).
- **Currency Law** + **CIMA** framework — the CI$/USD peg.
- **Immigration (Transition) Act + Customs and Border Control framework + WORC** — residency / work-permit routes (separate from ownership).

> **No primary statute imposing a foreign-ownership consent regime was found.** The "no restriction / no licence" position is verified as a *negative* against multiple strong-secondary legal sources (Appleby 2025 guide, Chambers Real Estate, Carey Olsen, Provenance). **Confirm the absence at [gov.ky](https://www.gov.ky/) / [Lands & Survey](https://www.caymanlandinfo.ky/) before relying.**

### Buyer category matrix

| Buyer category | Right |
|---|---|
| **Caymanian citizen / status-holder** | Full freehold; eligible for first-time-buyer stamp-duty concessions (foreigners cannot use) |
| **Permanent resident** | Full freehold; standard process |
| **Non-resident foreign buyer (individual or company)** | **Full freehold on equal footing** — no licence, no consent, no restriction on size/value/number. The only differential is **stamp duty** (7.5% standard; **10% on consideration ≥ CI$2M from 1 Jan 2026**), which in practice mostly affects non-Caymanian luxury buyers. |
| **Foreign company / trust** | Permitted; standard AML/KYC through attorney; share-transfer acquisitions of land-holding companies caught by the Land Holding Companies Share Transfer Tax (mirrors the 10% tier). |

(Source: Appleby Cayman property guide 2025 + Chambers Real Estate Cayman + Carey Olsen "Buying residential property in the Cayman Islands" — secondary/practitioner tier; *verify with attorney before contract*. Stamp-duty rates: Stamp Duty (Rates of Duty)(No.2) Regulations 2025 — primary.)

### Residency ≠ ownership

- **Buying property does NOT confer residency or a right to work.** Residency is a separate immigration matter handled by **[WORC (Workforce Opportunities & Residency Cayman)](https://www.worc.ky/)** + Customs & Border Control.
- **Residency by Investment** — the **commonly cited CI$2,000,000 figure is the threshold for the _lifetime_ Certificate of Permanent Residence (Persons of Independent Means)** (CI$2,000,000 in developed real estate; 250-person annual quota, s.30 Immigration (Transition) Act (2022 Revision)), **not for the 25-year Residency Certificate** — whose independent-means route carries a different, lower threshold and whose substantial-business-presence route is ownership/role-based (no fixed sum). ⚠️ The widely-repeated "CI$2,000,000 for the 25-year certificate" **conflates the two** — VERIFY the exact current threshold + category at [WORC](https://www.worc.ky/) before relying (budget-cycle volatile). See `--visa`.

### Compliance / AML

- **Cayman AML / KYC framework** applies to all real-estate purchases; **attorneys are obliged ("designated") entities** with source-of-funds + suspicious-transaction reporting duties. Regulator: **[Cayman Islands Monetary Authority (CIMA)](https://www.cima.ky/)**.
- **Source-of-funds documentation** required for all purchases; bank-statement chain back to legitimate source.
- **Beneficial-ownership disclosure** under the Cayman beneficial-ownership regime for corporate-held real estate.

### Verification path (before any reservation)

1. **Pull the Land Register entry** for the Block + Parcel via attorney (single source of truth — proprietor, charges, easements, restrictive agreements, cautions, overriding interests).
2. **Confirm the stamp-duty rate** that applies: 7.5% standard, or **10% if consideration ≥ CI$2M** (1 Jan 2026 onward) — confirm at [legislation.gov.ky](https://legislation.gov.ky/) / via attorney; Valuation & Estates Office may assess market value if under-declaration suspected.
3. **Strata bylaws check** (if a condo): confirm STR permission/restriction + strata-fee level (substantial on beachfront condos — see `--rental`).
4. **AML / source-of-funds** ready before signing.
5. **Residency filing (if residency-linked)**: WORC application — note residency is a *separate* process from purchase.

### Compute

1. **USD/sqft = price ÷ built-area sqft** (listings quote sqft; 1 m² = 10.764 sqft).
2. Compare to **≥ 3 active listings** on the same district + waterfront-status (waterfront drives price more than district average).
3. **Trap — strata fees**: beachfront condo strata/HOA fees are substantial and the dominant reason gross yield (~6–7%) collapses to net (~2%) — see `--rental`. Always pull the strata budget.
4. **Trap — area definitions**: built-up vs gross (incl. covered patios) vs lot area. Confirm with the strata plan / surveyor.
5. **Trap — stamp-duty band**: from 1 Jan 2026, consideration ≥ CI$2M is taxed at **10% on the whole value** (slab, not marginal) — a CI$2,000,001 purchase pays meaningfully more stamp duty than a CI$1,999,999 one. Model the cliff.
6. **Trap — surge/elevation**: Grand Cayman is very low-lying; verify the parcel's elevation + storm-surge-zone exposure (see `--risks`).

### Key terms

- **Registered Land Act (2018 Revision)** — the Torrens-style registered-title statute (published 2018 PDF reads "Law")
- **Block + Parcel** — the Land Register parcel identifier (single source of truth)
- **Transfer of Land** — the instrument; **registration, not the deed, passes legal title**
- **Mirror / Curtain / Insurance** — the three Torrens principles: register reflects all material facts bar overriding interests; purchaser need not look behind the register; the State guarantees correctness (Part X indemnity)
- **Strata** — condominium common-area regime (common on SMB)
- **Stamp duty on transfer of land** — 7.5% standard; 10% on consideration ≥ CI$2M (1 Jan 2026)
- **Overriding interests** — interests binding even if not on the register (e.g. certain short leases, rights of way); check before completion

## Section: `--traffic`

### Sources

- **[National Roads Authority (NRA)](https://www.caymanroads.com/)** — road network + capital works (verify portal at gov.ky / NRA).
- No public AADT (Annual Average Daily Traffic) portal comparable to UK DfT or US FHWA — **on-the-ground observation is the practical method**.

### Reality

- **Drive on the LEFT** (UK convention).
- **Main congestion** is the **George Town ⇄ Seven Mile Beach corridor** at weekday peaks. Grand Cayman is **car-dependent** — there is **no rail**; public transport is a mini-bus / shared-taxi network plus private vehicles.
- **Ongoing road works** — the **East-West Arterial** extension and corridor-widening projects target the GT/SMB commuter bottleneck; verify current infrastructure status at [NRA](https://www.caymanroads.com/) / gov.ky.

|---|---|---|
| `trunk` / `primary` | Esterley Tibbetts / Linford Pierson / main GT arteries | 15,000–35,000 |
| `secondary` | District-spine roads | 3,000–12,000 |

### Verdict bands (heuristic — calibrate on site)

- 🟢 < 1,500 v/d (residential subdivision interior; Sister Islands; East End / North Side)
- 🟡 1,500–8,000 v/d (suburban West Bay / Bodden Town / South Sound)
- 🟠 8,000–25,000 v/d (GT/SMB corridor arterials — Esterley Tibbetts, West Bay Road)
- 🔴 > 25,000 v/d (George Town approaches + SMB corridor at weekday peak)

### Caveats

- **Cruise-ship days**: George Town can disembark large cruise-passenger volumes on peak days — affects walkability + waterfront-frontage traffic on the cruise calendar.
- **Hurricane-season evacuation**: in a major-storm warning, the GT/SMB corridor is the principal congestion + sheltering route; factor into any continuous-occupancy property.

## Section: `--tax`

> **Headline**: The Cayman Islands has **NO direct taxation** — ❌ no income tax · ❌ no capital gains tax · ❌ no inheritance/estate tax · ❌ no annual property/land tax · ❌ no corporation tax. This is the defining feature of the jurisdiction. The **one transaction lever is STAMP DUTY ON TRANSFER OF LAND**: **7.5% standard**, **10% on consideration ≥ CI$2,000,000 effective 1 January 2026** (slab on whole value, not marginal). (Source: [PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries — Cayman Islands](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cayman-islands) + Stamp Duty (Rates of Duty)(No.2) Regulations 2025 — primary.)

### No direct taxes

- **No income tax · No capital gains tax · No inheritance / estate tax · No annual property / land tax · No corporation tax · No wealth tax.** (Source: [PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries — Cayman Islands](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cayman-islands); cross-check [gov.ky](https://www.gov.ky/).)
- **Home-country tax still applies**: US persons file US returns (FATCA / worldwide income); other nationals per home-country rules. Cayman levies nothing, but the home country may. *Consult a home-country tax advisor.*

### Stamp duty on transfer of land — the one lever

Governed by the **Stamp Duty Act (2019 Revision)** + subordinate **Stamp Duty (Rates of Duty) Regulations**. Administered via the **[Lands & Survey Department — Valuation & Estates Office](https://www.caymanlandinfo.ky/)**.

| Consideration (higher of price or market value) | Stamp duty rate |
|---|---|
| **Below CI$2,000,000** | **7.5%** |
| **CI$2,000,000 or above** (from **1 Jan 2026**) | **10%** (slab on whole value — NOT marginal) |

(Source: **Stamp Duty (Rates of Duty)(No.2) Regulations 2025** — verified primary at [gov.ky](https://www.gov.ky/) / [radiocayman.gov.ky](https://www.radiocayman.gov.ky/); in force 1 Jan 2026; cross-checked Cayman Compass 18 Dec 2025 + Bedell Cristin briefing + Orbitax. **VERIFY exact statutory text + threshold + effective date at [legislation.gov.ky](https://legislation.gov.ky/) before any client memo.**)

- **Who pays / when**: payable by the **buyer** (unless contractually shifted), due **within 45 days** of the dutiable instrument / contract. (Source: CIREBA stamp-duty page + Cayman Resident — secondary.)
- **Valuation**: the **Valuation & Estates Office** may assess market value if it suspects under-declaration; duty is on the **higher** of price or assessed market value. (Source: PwC + caymanlandinfo.ky.)
- **Share transfer of land-holding companies**: the **Land Holding Companies Share Transfer Tax** (+ 2025 amendment) captures stamp duty on acquisitions structured via share transfer, mirroring the 10% tier — closes the corporate-wrapper avoidance route.

### Mortgage stamp duty (separate)

- **~1%** on mortgages **under CI$300,000**; **~1.5%** on **CI$300,000+**. (Source: CIREBA stamp-duty page — **secondary**; verify current at [legislation.gov.ky](https://legislation.gov.ky/cms/images/LEGISLATION/PRINCIPAL/1973/1973-0005/1973-0005.pdf).)

### Caymanian-only concessions (foreigners cannot use)

> These are **budget-cycle volatile** — figures are CIREBA-current as researched 2026-05-28; re-verify each at [legislation.gov.ky](https://legislation.gov.ky/) / [gov.ky](https://www.gov.ky/).

| Concession (Caymanian first-time buyer) | Rate |
|---|---|
| First developed residential property | **0% up to CI$550,000**; 3.75% on CI$550,001–650,000 portion |
| First raw-land parcel | **0% up to CI$250,000**; 3.75% on CI$250,001–350,000 portion |

(Source: CIREBA — **secondary**; *verify current thresholds at legislation.gov.ky; these change in budget cycles*. Foreign buyers cannot use these.)

### Tourist Accommodation Tax (on short-term rentals)

- **13%** Tourist Accommodation Tax (TAT) on the gross room rate of short-term/tourism accommodation — see `--rental`. (Source: [ourcayman.ky](https://www.ourcayman.ky/) — primary tourism portal.)

### Capital gains / inheritance / wealth

- **NO capital gains tax · NO inheritance / estate tax · NO gift tax · NO wealth tax** in the Cayman Islands.

### Future risk

- The **1 Jan 2026 10% stamp-duty band** is recent — verify exact statutory text/threshold/effective date at [legislation.gov.ky](https://legislation.gov.ky/) before quoting in any client memo.
- Caymanian-only concession thresholds restated in budget cycles — track annually.
- No income/CGT/property tax has been proposed; the duty-and-fee revenue model is structurally entrenched, but **monitor** budget statements.

⚠️ Verification: confirm the applicable stamp-duty rate (7.5% vs 10%) + valuation basis + the 45-day payment window with attorney + the Valuation & Estates Office before contract.

## Section: `--rental`

### Long-term residential rentals

- Governed by ordinary contract + general English common-law landlord-tenant principles (no rent control on the foreign-buyer-relevant segment). Verify current tenancy-law detail at [gov.ky](https://www.gov.ky/).
- **No income tax on rental income** (Cayman levies no income tax) — but the **home country may tax** worldwide rental income (e.g. US persons).
- **Long-term asking rents (June 2025, secondary listings survey)**: 1-bed ~**US$3,171/mo** · 2-bed ~**US$4,024** · 3-bed ~**US$6,463** · 4+ bed ~**US$7,317**. (Source: secondary listings survey via market commentary — *verify per property*.)

### Yields (2025, secondary — ERA Cayman / market commentary)

| Metric | Value | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| **Gross yield — SMB condos** | **~6–7%** | 1-bed often >7% (high demand, low vacancy) |
| **Net yield** | **~2%/yr** | ⚠️ Much lower after high insurance + substantial strata/condo (HOA) fees |

(Source: ERA Cayman rental-yield note 2025 — **secondary**; treat as ballpark, not guaranteed. The gross-to-net collapse is driven by **catastrophe insurance + beachfront strata fees** — see `--risks` + `--price`.)

### Short-term / tourism rental (STR) regime — PRIMARY

- A **Tourism Accommodation Licence is MANDATORY** to let short-term to visitors (incl. Airbnb / villas / condos), issued by the **Hotel Licensing Board (HLB)** via the **Department of Tourism (DoT)**. (Source: [ourcayman.ky](https://www.ourcayman.ky/) — official tourism business portal.)
- **Three inspections required**: Department of Tourism, Department of Environmental Health, Cayman Islands Fire Service.
- **Licence fee**: **CI$250** for a 1–10-bedroom property; **+CI$25/bedroom above 10**. Licence year for apartments/condos/villas/guesthouses **expires 31 August**; renewal due by **1 May**. (Source: [ourcayman.ky](https://www.ourcayman.ky/).)
- **Tourist Accommodation Tax (TAT): 13%** on the gross room rate, charged to the guest and remitted to DoT **within 28 days of month-end**. **Monthly occupancy report mandatory** (even if zero). (Source: [ourcayman.ky](https://www.ourcayman.ky/) + visitcaymanislands.com TAT FAQ — primary.)
- **Strata bylaws may separately restrict or permit STR** — read the specific development's bylaws **before** pricing any yield model.

### Strategic notes

- **Net yield is the real number**: ~6–7% gross collapses to ~2% net after insurance + strata fees. Model net, not gross.
- **Catastrophe (windstorm) insurance** is a major carrying cost (see `--risks`) — get a live quote before pricing a yield model.
- **STR licensing + 13% TAT + monthly reporting** is a compliance obligation, not optional — budget the admin overhead.

### Hurricane (Atlantic basin) — the primary physical risk

- **Hurricane Ivan (Sept 2004) — the baseline catastrophe**: Ivan passed within ~30 mi (50 km) of Grand Cayman on **11–12 September 2004** as a **Category 4/5** system (~165 mph peak winds nearby). **Storm surge 6–9 ft**, wave heights ~20 ft; the island was nearly completely over-swept except the extreme north-east. **Over 80% of buildings damaged.** (Sources: [gov.ky — "The Impact of Hurricane Ivan on Grand Cayman" PDF](https://gov.ky/documents/35692/0/The+Impact+of+Hurricane+Ivan+on+Grand+Cayman.pdf) — **primary gov**; NOAA NHC Tropical Cyclone Report AL092004.)
- **Season**: Atlantic basin **1 June – 30 November**, greatest risk **August–October** (peak Sept–Nov locally).
- **Implication**: Cayman is a **genuine major-hurricane-exposure jurisdiction** — Ivan is the calibration event, not a freak. Underwrite every parcel against a repeat Ivan.

### Building code (post-Ivan resilience)

- **Pre-1994: no legislated building code.** The current legislated code is based on the **IBC 2009 (International Building Code)**, with high wind-load design standards. (Source: gov.ky Ivan review; Planning Department — **verify current code edition at [gov.ky](https://www.gov.ky/) / Planning Department**.)
- Post-Ivan: stricter codes + strengthened Planning compliance/oversight; newer code-compliant structures fared materially better. ⚠️ **Enforcement historically criticised** (insurer commentary 2010) — verify the specific building's permit + age + that it meets current code on inspection.
- **Practical buyer check**: confirm **year built, roof tie-down, impact glazing / storm shutters, elevation vs storm-surge zone**.

### Flood / storm surge

- **Grand Cayman is a low-lying limestone island** — **storm surge + rainfall flooding are the dominant flood risks**. Check the parcel's elevation + surge-zone mapping ([Lands & Survey](https://www.caymanlandinfo.ky/) / [HMCI](https://www.gov.ky/web/hmci)).
- **Sea-level rise / coastal erosion** on beachfront parcels (long-term — see `--climate`; up to a third of Grand Cayman's current land mass projected below the 2050 high-tide mark per a Cayman Compass-cited projection — secondary).

### Seismic — background Caribbean risk

- Cayman lies near the **Cayman Trough / Mid-Cayman Spreading Centre** plate-boundary system; **background seismicity exists** but is not the primary risk relative to hurricanes. ⚠️ **Parcel-level seismic hazard: data not publicly available in a free primary feed for this pass — standard structural review for older builds; verify at gov.ky / regional seismic services.** Do NOT fabricate a seismic-hazard rating.

### Insurance

- **Catastrophe (windstorm) insurance is expensive and a major carrying cost** — premiums scale with rebuild value and **drive the gap between ~6–7% gross and ~2% net yield** (see `--rental`). 2004 Ivan payouts notably did **not** cover business interruption / lost income for many. (Sources: Indigo home-insurance guide; Cayman News Service insurer commentary — **secondary**.)
- ⚠️ **No primary premium tariff is publicly available — get a live quote. Mandatory windstorm cover with a %-of-sum-insured deductible is the norm; verify deductible + per-storm caps with 3+ carriers.** Do NOT publish a fabricated premium rate.

### Risk dashboard — typical Grand Cayman residential

| Class | Severity | Action priority |
|---|---|---|
| **Hurricane (Ivan-class)** | 🟠–🔴 | Confirm post-IBC-2009 build + impact glazing + roof tie-down + insurance availability + reasonable premium; underwrite against a repeat Ivan |
| **Storm surge / coastal flood** | 🟠 (low-lying / beachfront) / 🟡 (interior, higher ground) | Verify elevation + surge-zone mapping (HMCI / Lands & Survey) |
| **Sea-level rise (long-term)** | 🟠 | 2050+ exposure for low-lying / beachfront parcels — Grand Cayman is very low; model scenarios |

| **Seismic** | 🟡 | Background Caribbean risk; standard structural review for older builds; parcel-level data not public |

## Caveats unique to KY

- **No annual property tax / income tax / CGT / inheritance tax** — the jurisdiction's defining feature; the **only transaction lever is stamp duty on transfer of land**.
- **1 Jan 2026 stamp-duty 10% band on consideration ≥ CI$2M** is a **slab on the whole value, not marginal** — a CI$2,000,001 purchase pays meaningfully more than a CI$1,999,999 one; **model the cliff**. Verify exact statutory text/threshold/effective date at [legislation.gov.ky](https://legislation.gov.ky/) (Stamp Duty (Rates of Duty)(No.2) Regulations 2025) before any client memo. Companion **Land Holding Companies Share Transfer Tax (Amendment) Act 2025** closes the share-transfer-wrapper route.
- **Registry statute name**: cite **"Registered Land Act (2018 Revision)"** (current post-2020 "Law"→"Act" citation style); the published 2018 Revision PDF itself reads **"Registered Land Law"**. Do **not** use the deprecated "Registration (Land) Law (1996 Revision)". Confirm latest revision at [legislation.gov.ky](https://legislation.gov.ky/).
- **Court is the Grand Court of the Cayman Islands — NOT the Eastern Caribbean Supreme Court** (Cayman is outside the ECSC). Appeals → Cayman Islands Court of Appeal → Judicial Committee of the Privy Council.
- **Torrens-style, state-guaranteed registered title** (Mirror / Curtain / Insurance; Part X government indemnity) — title risk is low; the current Land Register entry is conclusive; full historic title-chain investigation is unnecessary. Still verify the Block + Parcel register entry + overriding interests + cautions before completion.
- **"No foreign-ownership restriction / no licence" is verified as a *negative*** against multiple strong-secondary legal sources (Appleby, Chambers, Carey Olsen, Provenance), not a single primary statute imposing none — confirm the absence at [gov.ky](https://www.gov.ky/) / [Lands & Survey](https://www.caymanlandinfo.ky/).
- **Residency ≠ ownership**: buying does not confer residency or a work right; residency is a separate WORC process. Residency-by-investment thresholds (commonly cited CI$2M for the 25-year certificate) are **budget-volatile — verify at WORC**.
- **Hurricane Ivan (2004) is the baseline catastrophe** — Cat 4/5, ~80% of buildings damaged on a near-completely-over-swept low island. Current building code is **IBC-2009-based**. Underwrite every parcel against a repeat Ivan; verify build year + impact glazing + roof tie-down + elevation/surge zone.
- **Catastrophe (windstorm) insurance is a major carrying cost** — drives the gap between ~6–7% gross and ~2% net yield; no public tariff (get 3+ live quotes); mandatory windstorm cover with a %-of-sum-insured deductible is the norm.
- **Net rental yield (~2%) is the real number**, not gross (~6–7%) — insurance + substantial beachfront strata fees collapse it. Model net.
- **Short-term rentals require a Hotel Licensing Board tourism-accommodation licence + three inspections + the 13% Tourist Accommodation Tax + mandatory monthly occupancy reporting** — plus strata bylaws may separately restrict/permit STR.
- **Electricity is expensive** — the CUC base rate roughly doubles once the monthly fuel-cost charge is added (~CI$0.32/kWh all-in, secondary); a summer-2026 fuel-charge cap was announced (verify at cuc-cayman.com).
- **Mains water is desalinated (RO)** and relatively costly; tariff not publicly captured this pass — verify at waterauthority.ky / cwco.com.
- **Crime is low by international standard but rose in 2024** — total crime +7%, serious violence +62% (incl. the Ed Bush Stadium mass shooting); firearm crime fell 21%; track the RCIPS 2025 report.
- **Wire / conveyancing fraud against international buyers** is a documented Caribbean-wide pattern — verify wire instructions verbally with the attorney via a known phone number before sending funds.
- **AML / source-of-funds (not FX registration) is the foreign-buyer compliance gate** — attorneys are obliged entities under the CIMA-regulated AML framework.
- **Per-area median CI$/sqft is not in a free primary feed** — never fabricate; point to CIREBA MLS analytics or a Lands & Survey valuation.
- **Compact-island medical reality**: Grand Cayman has strong hospital access (HSA George Town Hospital + Health City); the **Sister Islands are materially more limited** (air-evacuation pathway for serious cases).
