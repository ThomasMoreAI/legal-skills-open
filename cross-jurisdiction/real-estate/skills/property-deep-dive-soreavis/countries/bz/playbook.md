# Belize 🇧🇿 — Property Due-Diligence Playbook

ISO2: `bz`. Status: ✅ Fully populated (researched 2026-05-07).

## Country profile

- **Postcode**: Belize **does not operate a nationwide postcode system**. Addresses use `street/lot, town/village, district` (e.g., "Lot 27, Coconut Drive, San Pedro Town, Ambergris Caye, Belize District"). The post system relies on PO Boxes for delivery; street-level postal delivery is limited outside town centres. Verify on visit.
- **Admin levels**: **6 districts** — **Belize, Cayo, Corozal, Orange Walk, Stann Creek, Toledo** — plus the special-status municipality of **San Pedro Town** (Ambergris Caye, in Belize District but treated separately for many planning purposes). Belmopan is the capital (Cayo District); Belize City is the largest urban centre and former capital (Belize District).
- **Currency**: **BZD** (Belize dollar). **Pegged to USD at BZ$2.00 = US$1.00 since May 1976** (Central Bank of Belize). The peg is a cornerstone of Belize's monetary regime — no FX risk for USD holders. **Tourist-corridor + foreign-buyer transactions are widely USD-denominated** (Ambergris Caye, Caye Caulker, Placencia luxury, Cayo expat parcels). **Local-market and middle-tier housing typically priced in BZD**.
- **Languages**: **English (sole official language)** — a legacy of British Honduras (independence 1981). Spanish widely spoken (~50 % L1 in Cayo, Corozal, Orange Walk districts). Kriol (Creole) is the lingua franca; Maya languages (Kekchi, Mopan, Yucatec) in Toledo + parts of Stann Creek; Garifuna along southern coast. **Legal/conveyancing is conducted in English** under English Common Law foundations.
- **Foreign-ownership freedoms**: 🟢 **Belize is one of the most foreigner-friendly property jurisdictions in the Americas**. The **Aliens Landholding Act was repealed in 2001** — foreigners no longer require a permit / licence / nationality quota to acquire freehold ("fee simple") residential, commercial, or agricultural land. Foreign individuals + companies own on equal legal footing with Belizean citizens. Statutory basis: post-2001 absence of nationality-based restriction in the **Registered Land Act, Chapter 194 (Revised Edition 2020)**. (Sources: RE/MAX Belize — secondary; primary statutory basis is the repeal — verify the Statutes of Belize 2001 amendment with attorney.)
- **Cadastre / land registry**:
  - **Lands and Surveys Department + Land Registry / Land Titles Unit** — within the **Ministry of Natural Resources, Petroleum & Mining**: `https://naturalresources.gov.bz/`
  - **Registered Land Act, Chapter 194** — modern Torrens-style title for **declared lands** (compulsory registration areas; transactions registered at the Land Registry).
  - **Titles Registry** — older system handling **undeclared lands** (much of rural Belize, many islands and southern districts) via **Deed of Conveyance** + indenture chain. **Undeclared land = materially weaker than registered title**; conveyance must still be lodged at the Titles Registry to be enforceable against third parties.
  - **Valuation Unit** (Ministry of Natural Resources): `https://naturalresources.gov.bz/index.php/valuation-unit/` — sets the **unimproved-value (UV)** roll on which annual property tax is assessed.
- **Identifier**: Land titles identified by **parcel / block / register section** under the Registered Land Act (e.g., `Block X, Parcel Y, Registration Section Z`) for declared lands; older **Deeds of Conveyance** are referenced by **lodgement number + indenture** at the Titles Registry for undeclared lands.
- **Ownership types**:
  - **Fee simple (freehold)** — strongest form; dominant for foreign + local residential transactions; available island-wide.
  - **Leasehold** — National Lands leases (Crown lease) common on islands + coastal strips; commercial leases standard.
  - **National Lands** — government-owned; can be applied for via Lands Department but not freely sold.
  - **Maya customary tenure** — communal land use under traditional system in **Toledo District** (~41 Maya villages); recognised by **Caribbean Court of Justice (CCJ) 2015 ruling** (Maya Leaders Alliance v Attorney General of Belize) as legally equal to Western property ownership. **Government draft Maya Customary Land Tenure Policy 2024** in consultation; intended to formalise village-level communal title (Belmopan-published policy framework — verify current statutory progression before any Toledo-District deal).
- **Recent reforms / context**:
  - **Stamp Duties (Amendment) Act, 2024** (Act No. 33 of 2024 — assented 9 Dec 2024, gazetted 14 Dec 2024, **in force 1 January 2025**; National Assembly of Belize) — new **s.64(12)** charges duty on **any** conveyance or transfer of stock or shares by sale, exchange **or gift** at **5 %** (companies incorporated in Belize) / **7 %** (companies incorporated outside Belize), on value or consideration whichever is greater, so the share-transfer route into a property-holding company is **taxed, not exempt**. **The Act does not mention IBCs** — the split turns on place of incorporation, not offshore status. It also re-bands mortgage duty (**s.59**), replaces the exemption list (**s.69**, with **s.69(g)** further amended by **Act No. 7 of 2025**, gazetted 11 Feb 2025, also in force 1 January 2025) and extends land-transfer duty to joint-tenancy conveyances (**s.72**) — see `--tax`. Verify the consolidated current text with a Belize attorney before transacting.
  - **Income Tax exemption increased** from BZD 26,000 to **BZD 29,000 effective 1 January 2025** (Belize Tax Service / Government of Belize budget measures; Love FM Belize News).
  - **QRP age threshold lowered from 45 to 40** (effective ~2023 per current Belize Tourism Board QRP page) — verify for current applications at `https://www.belizetourismboard.org/programs-events/retirement-program/`.
  - **Hurricane Lisa (Cat 1, 2 November 2022)** — landfall near Belize City; ~5,000 homes damaged, 500 destroyed, ~USD 100M losses (NHC TCR AL152022; IFRC DREF). Most recent direct major-impact event; relevant to insurance + structural-condition diligence on Belize-District 2020-or-older builds.

### Compute

1. **USD/sq ft** = price ÷ **net floor area** (`square feet` is the universal local convention; 1 m² = 10.76 ft²). Conversions for European buyers: multiply US$/sq ft by **10.76** to get **US$/m²**, then apply the **current USD→EUR rate** to reach €/m² (two distinct steps — a unit conversion then a currency conversion; compute with the live FX rate).
2. Compare to **≥ 3 active comparables on Belize Real Estate Search + RE/MAX Belize** in the **same community + island/peninsula segment** (HOA/strata governance + reserve-fund discipline matter materially in condo-heavy markets like San Pedro / Caye Caulker / Placencia).
3. **Trap — declared vs undeclared land**: a parcel held under Deed of Conveyance at the Titles Registry (undeclared) is materially harder to mortgage, insure, and resell than a Registered Land Act parcel. Verify with the Lands Department + your attorney **before pricing**. See `--risks`.
4. **Trap — Maritime (Queen's Chain)**: the **66-foot strip** along the high-water mark is **public Crown reserve** — purported "beachfront fee simple" parcels often abut, not include, the foreshore. Verify the title boundary against the Lands & Surveys Department survey diagram.
5. **Trap — Maya customary tenure (Toledo District)**: **CCJ 2015 ruling** + 2024 draft policy mean any Toledo-District deal outside an existing fee-simple title needs Maya village-level due-diligence (Maya Leaders Alliance / Toledo Alcaldes Association consultation). **Treat any Toledo-District land transaction without explicit attorney clearance on customary-rights overlay as red-flag** until the 2024 draft policy is enacted and recorded.
6. **Trap — currency**: confirm `USD firm` vs `BZD or USD equivalent at closing`. Because of the BZD/USD peg, FX risk is structurally low — but verify the peg holds at closing (it has held since 1976; CBB primary).

### Key terms

- **Fee simple** — full freehold ownership (strongest title form).
- **Land Certificate** — modern title document under the Registered Land Act for declared lands.
- **Deed of Conveyance** — older title document for undeclared lands (Titles Registry).
- **Declared / undeclared land** — see `--risks` and Country profile.
- **Queen's Chain** — 66-foot Crown reserve along high-water mark (public foreshore).
- **National Lands** — government-owned land (rural unallocated, much of southern Toledo, many cayes).
- **QRP** — Qualified Retired Persons programme (Belize Tourism Board administered).
- **IBC** — International Business Company (offshore-finance vehicle; can hold Belize property under preferential 7 % stamp duty per RE/MAX Belize secondary; verify with BTS + attorney). ⚠️ The **Stamp Duties (Amendment) Act 2024 does not mention IBCs** — its new **s.64(12)** share-transfer duty splits **5 % / 7 %** on **place of incorporation** (in / outside Belize), not on offshore status (in force 1 January 2025 — see `--tax`).

## Section: `--traffic`

### Sources

- **Ministry of Infrastructure Development and Housing (MIDH)** + **Belize Roads Authority** (formerly the National Road Safety Council) — main-road inventory; traffic counts published in feasibility / project reports rather than a public AADT viewer.
- **OSM** fallback via Overpass — `highway` tag class. **No public, parcel-level AADT portal exists for Belize.**

### Key term

**AADT (Annual Average Daily Traffic)** — used in MIDH project reports for major highways: **George Price Highway** (Belize City – Belmopan – San Ignacio – Guatemala border, the spine), **Philip Goldson Highway** (Belize City – Corozal – Mexico border), **Hummingbird Highway** (Belmopan – Dangriga), **Southern Highway** (Dangriga – Punta Gorda). Coverage outside these corridors is sparse — **rely on OSM road class + on-site observation** for community-level traffic.

### Verdict bands (heuristic; calibrate with on-site visit)

- 🟢 < 1,500 v/d (rural lane, San Pedro Town side streets, Caye Caulker car-free interior, gated subdivision interior)
- 🟡 1,500–10,000 v/d (community collector, Belize City suburb side roads, Belmopan ring roads)
- 🟠 10,000–35,000 v/d (urban arterial — Belize City Northern + Western Highway feeders; San Ignacio Burns Avenue; Belmopan main spine)
- 🔴 > 35,000 v/d (rare — peak segments of George Price Highway near Belize City; Northern Highway near Phillip Goldson International Airport access)

| OSM `highway` | BZ road class | Typical v/d band |
|---|---|---|
| `trunk` | George Price / Philip Goldson / Hummingbird / Southern Highway | 5,000–25,000 |
| `primary` | named feeder + town-bypass | 2,000–10,000 |
| `secondary` | district main road | 800–4,000 |
| `tertiary` / `residential` | community / village street | < 1,500 |

### Caveats

- **Golf-cart corridor noise** dominates Ambergris Caye (Coconut Drive in San Pedro is iconic): vehicle counts under-state the acoustic + vibration load of golf-cart + small-truck mix.
- **Caye Caulker is car-free** (golf carts + bicycles + walking); traffic v/d figures don't apply.
- **Belize City urban core** (Albert Street, Queen Street, Princess Margaret Drive): mixed pedestrian-vehicle congestion in tourist hours.
- **Hurricane road-closures**: Belize Roads Authority + NEMO publish post-storm road-status updates; segments of the southern Hummingbird + Southern Highway have closed for days post-major-storm historically.

## Section: `--tax`

### Annual property tax

Governed by the **Land Tax Act** + **Real Property Tax Act**; administered by the **Lands Department / Valuation Unit** (assessment) and **municipal authorities + Treasury** (collection).

- **Tax base**: **Unimproved Value (UV) of the land** — the price the bare land alone would fetch, disregarding buildings, crops, fences, landscaping. **Buildings are NOT taxed** (this is the key Belize-specific feature). UV is set by the **Valuation Unit** roll; revaluation cycles vary by district.
- **Rate structure** (typical, per Ceiba Realty + Vida Realty + Will Mitchell guides — secondary; verify with district authority for the specific parcel):

| Area | Annual rate on UV |
|---|---|
| **Rural districts** (most of country) | **~1 %** of UV |
| **Belize City + major urban areas** | **~1.5 %** of UV |
| **Belmopan municipality** | up to **~2 %** of UV (per secondary aggregator data) |

- **Effective tax burden**: **typically < USD 200 per year** on a residential parcel — frequently quoted as the **"lowest property tax in the Americas"** by Belize-real-estate marketing (International Living + Vida Realty + Ceiba — secondary, but consistent across sources). Computed: a UV of BZD 20,000 in a rural district at 1 % = BZD 200/yr (USD 100/yr). For a USD 500k beachfront condo on Ambergris Caye where the unimproved-land share is small (most of the value is the structure), the annual tax bill remains modest because the building is excluded.
- **Filing**: due **annually, typically by 1 April**; collected by the relevant municipal/town authority or central Treasury.
- **No primary-residence exemption tier** as such; UV-band rates apply uniformly within district.

### Stamp Duty (transfer tax)

Governed by the **Stamp Duties Act** (as amended by the **Stamp Duties (Amendment) Act, 2024** — Act No. 33 of 2024, assented 9 Dec 2024, gazetted 14 Dec 2024, **in force 1 January 2025**; **s.69(g)** further amended by **Act No. 7 of 2025**, gazetted 11 Feb 2025, also in force 1 January 2025).

**Headline rates** (per RE/MAX Belize + Ceiba Realty 2025 guides — secondary; verify via Belize Tax Service + attorney):

| Buyer category | Rate |
|---|---|
| **Belizean citizens + CARICOM nationals** | **5 %** of consideration (over BZD 20,000 / USD 10,000 threshold) |
| **Foreign buyers — direct (personal-name)** | **8 %** of consideration (over BZD 20,000 / USD 10,000 threshold) |
| **Foreign buyer via IBC structure** | **7 %** (per RE/MAX Belize — secondary; verify with BTS) |

- **Threshold**: first **BZD 20,000 (USD 10,000)** of consideration is **stamp-duty-exempt**.
- **Tax base**: **higher of purchase price or market value** (Lands Department valuation may override).
- **Liability**: **buyer pays** (custom + statute).
- **Share-transfer duty — new s.64(12)** (Act No. 33 of 2024, in force **1 January 2025**): duty is charged on **any conveyance or transfer of stock or shares by sale, exchange or gift** — **5 %** where the company is **incorporated in Belize**, **7 %** where it is **incorporated outside Belize** — on **value or consideration, whichever is greater**. The share-transfer route into a property-holding company is therefore **taxed, not exempt**. **The Act does not mention IBCs**: the split turns on place of incorporation, not offshore status (National Assembly of Belize, Act No. 33 of 2024 — primary).
- **Mortgage duty — s.59 re-banded** (same Act): **0 %** on the first **BZD 100,000** secured; **1.5 %** on the excess above BZD 100,000 for a **mortgage**; **0.6 %** on the excess for an **assignment or transfer of mortgage** and for a **reconveyance, release, discharge or surrender**.
- **Exemptions — s.69 replaced** (same Act; **s.69(g)** further amended by **Act No. 7 of 2025**, gazetted 11 Feb 2025, also in force 1 January 2025): conveyances between **spouses, common-law spouses, siblings, children and parents**; **testamentary disposition and intestacy**; **charitable transfers up to BZD 25,000 in any 12 months**; **agricultural or residential leases under 2 years at rent under BZD 2,000 per month**. ⚠️ There is **no grandparent or grandchild exemption**, despite agent marketing claiming one.
- **Joint tenancy + intra-group — s.72** (same Act): land-transfer duty **extended to joint-tenancy conveyances**; **company-to-company transfers exempt** where the **beneficial owner** as defined in **s.2(1) Money Laundering and Terrorism (Prevention) Act** is the same on both sides.
- Verify the **consolidated text** with a Belize attorney before transacting.

### GST (General Sales Tax / VAT-equivalent)

- **Standard rate**: **12.5 %** (Belize Tax Service `https://bts.gov.bz/gst-faq/`).
- **GST registration threshold**: **BZD 75,000 / year** turnover (per Belize Tax Service — primary).
- **Real-estate transactions**:
  - **Resale of existing property between individuals** — **GST does not apply** (out of scope for non-trader transfers).
  - **New construction sold by a developer / contractor** — **GST applies at 12.5 %** if the developer is GST-registered (turnover > BZD 75k); the buyer effectively bears it via the price.
  - **Real estate agent commissions + legal + survey fees** — GST 12.5 % on the fee (where the provider is GST-registered).
- Verify GST treatment for each transaction with the buyer's attorney + the seller's GST registration status.

### Closing-cost stack (typical resale, foreigner buyer, 2025-2026)

| Item | Rate / amount | Who pays | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Stamp Duty (foreign buyer)** | **8 %** of consideration over BZD 20,000 | **Buyer** | 7 % via IBC; 5 % Belizean/CARICOM |
| **Mortgage stamp duty** (only if financed) | **0 %** on the first BZD 100,000 secured; **1.5 %** on the excess (Stamp Duties Act s.59 as re-banded by Act No. 33 of 2024, in force 1 Jan 2025) | Buyer (as mortgagor) | **0.6 %** on the excess for assignment / transfer of mortgage and for reconveyance, release, discharge or surrender |
| **Land Registry registration fee** | nominal — flat fee schedule (~BZD 150 / USD 75 typical per RE/MAX Belize secondary) | Buyer | Verify current schedule at Land Registry fee page |
| **Attorney conveyancing fee** (buyer-side) | **1.5–3 % of consideration + 12.5 % GST on fee** | Buyer | Typical range per Ceiba + RE/MAX guides; verify on quote |
| **Title search + due-diligence** | USD 300–800 typical | Buyer | Lands Department search + encumbrance check |
| **Title insurance** (optional, growing in foreign-buyer corridors) | **0.4–1.0 % of consideration**, one-time | Buyer | First American + Stewart Title operate via local brokers; protects against title defects. **Not universal — many transactions close without it.** |

> **Total buyer-side mandatory cost** (excluding agent commission): **~10–13 % of consideration** for a foreign buyer (8 % stamp + 1.5–3 % attorney + 12.5 % GST on attorney fee + small registration + survey + optional title insurance). **Cash-purchase basis** — add **mortgage stamp duty** (s.59: 0 % to BZD 100,000 secured, then 1.5 % on the excess) if the purchase is financed.

### Capital gains

- **Belize has no capital gains tax** (Belize Tax Service; PwC-equivalent secondary; International Living). **Confirmed widely; verify status before reliance** since this is one of the country's marketed advantages.
- **No estate / inheritance tax**.
- **No wealth tax**.

### Income tax (relevant for landlord side)

Governed by the **Income and Business Tax Act**; administered by **Belize Tax Service (BTS)**.

- **Headline rate**: **flat 25 %** on chargeable employment + business income (BTS).
- **Tax-free threshold**: **BZD 29,000** effective **1 January 2025** (raised from BZD 26,000; BTS + Government of Belize budget measure 2024-2025; Love FM Belize News Dec 2024).
- **Personal relief**: **BZD 19,600** for incomes > BZD 29,000 (after the threshold step); intermediate reliefs in BZD 26,001–29,000 band.
- **Resident vs non-resident**: residents taxed on worldwide income; non-residents taxed on **Belize-sourced** income only (territorial features for non-residents).
- **QRP holders**: **fully exempt from Belize tax on foreign-source income, capital gains, and inheritance** (Belize Tourism Board QRP page — primary).
- **Rental income (long-term residential)**: taxed at 25 % on net (after allowable expenses) for residents/landlords above threshold; non-resident landlord may face withholding — verify with BTS for the specific income source.
- **GST 12.5 %**: **long-term residential rental between individuals — typically out of GST scope** (verify); **commercial + tourism short-stay rental — within GST scope** at 12.5 % above the BZD 75k threshold.

## Section: `--rental`

### Long-term residential rentals

Common-law landlord/tenant principles apply (English Common Law foundation post-British-Honduras independence 1981); no comprehensive rent-control statute equivalent to Jamaica's Rent Restriction Act. Standard 6-month to 1-year tenancies; security deposit 1-2 months. Possession proceedings via **Magistrate's Court**; eviction can take 6-18 months for a contested case. Tenant protection moderate; market-rate landlord/tenant relationships dominate.

### Long-term rental tax (resident landlord)

- **Income tax 25 %** on net rental income above the BZD 29,000 threshold; allowable expenses include management, repairs, property tax, insurance, mortgage interest.
- **GST 12.5 %**: long-term residential rental between individuals **typically out of GST scope** (verify per BTS GST-FAQ).
- **Non-resident landlord**: 25 % flat on Belize-sourced rental income; no double-tax treaty network beyond CARICOM + a few legacy treaties (verify per nationality with BTS + your home-country tax adviser).
- **QRP holders**: **rental from Belize-source property remains within Belize tax** (the QRP exemption covers **foreign-source income**, not Belize-sourced rents) — verify with QRP-specialist attorney.

### Short-term rentals (Airbnb / vacation-rental / villa)

Regulatory status (verified 2026-05):

- **Belize Tourism Board (BTB)**: `https://www.belizetourismboard.org/` — licenses **hotels + bed-and-breakfasts + vacation-rental properties** under the **Hotels and Tourist Accommodation Act**. **Vacation-rental hosts are technically required to register + license** with BTB; enforcement on Ambergris Caye + Placencia has been tightening through 2024-2025 (BTB notices + AmbergrisCaye.com community discussion — secondary).
- **HOA / strata reality**: in Coco Beach, Grand Caribe, The Phoenix, Las Terrazas, La Beliza, Coral Bay Villas, and similar gated condo / strata developments, **HOA / strata by-laws are the binding constraint** — many require central rental-pool participation, minimum stay limits, or transient-rental levies. **Read by-laws + 2-3 years of HOA financials before pricing a yield model**.
- **Tax**: BTB **9 % hotel accommodation tax** on nightly rates; income tax on net at landlord schedule; GST 12.5 % may apply on commercial + tourism short-stay rental above threshold.
- **2024-2025 trend**: BTB tightening enforcement; insurance carriers increasingly demanding BTB registration as condition of cover; pricing competition softening as Ambergris Caye + Placencia inventory grows (Brevitas + RE/MAX Belize 2025 reports — secondary).

### Strategic notes

- **Highest STR yield zones**: **Ambergris Caye (San Pedro Town + North-of-the-bridge resort condos)**, **Placencia Peninsula**, **Caye Caulker**. RE/MAX Belize 2025 secondary data: average Ambergris Caye nightly rate **USD 284 (2025)**, **+15 % YoY**; **net rental yields 8-15 %** quoted by tourism-corridor brokers (verify per project + by examining strata occupancy data).
- **Caye Caulker**: smaller scale; lower price floor; tourism-dependent; minimal infrastructure for high-end villa segment.

### Hurricane (Atlantic basin)

- **Season**: **June 1 – November 30**, peak **August-October**. Belize sits along the western Caribbean cyclone path; long historical record of major impacts.
- **Recent + historic landfalls** (Wikipedia "List of Belize hurricanes"; explorebelizeplaces.com timeline; NHC TCRs — secondary aggregator + primary NHC reports):
  - **Hurricane Lisa (2 November 2022)** — Cat 1 landfall ~11.5 mi SW of Belize City, sustained 90 mph (NHC TCR AL152022). **~5,000 homes damaged, 500 destroyed, ~6,500 families affected, ~5,000 displaced**, **USD 100M losses** (IFRC DREF; PAHO situation reports). Belize City inundated; power + water disrupted for days. **Most recent direct major-impact event** — reference for current risk-pricing on Belize-District 2020-or-older builds.
  - **Hurricane Earl (4 August 2016)** — Cat 1 (75 mph) landfall near Belize City; moderate coastal damage.
  - **Hurricane Iris (9 October 2001)** — small but powerful **Cat 4 (145 mph)** landfall at **Monkey River Town (Stann Creek / Toledo coast)**. **24 deaths, USD 250M damage**, 95 % of buildings destroyed in 35 villages; **72 % of Toledo District + 50 % of Stann Creek District buildings destroyed**; ~15,000 homeless. **Reference event for Placencia + southern coast risk-pricing** — modern Placencia building stock dates largely to post-Iris reconstruction.
  - **Hurricane Mitch (October 1998)** — threatened Belize for 5 days as Cat 5; turned aside but drove the **founding of NEMO (1999)**.
  - **Hurricane Hattie (October 1961)** — Cat 5 catastrophic landfall on Belize City; **drove the relocation of the capital to Belmopan (founded 1970, capital from 1970)**. Reference for why Belmopan exists as inland capital.
  - **Hurricane Keith (2000)**, **Dean (2007)**, **Richard (2010)** — additional notable impacts.
- **Sub-region exposure**:
  - **🔴 Most exposed**: **Ambergris Caye, Caye Caulker, Belize City, Northern Cayes, Corozal coast, Orange Walk lowlands, Placencia Peninsula, southern Stann Creek + Toledo coast** (Iris reference).
  - **🟠 Medium**: **Hopkins / Dangriga (Stann Creek)**, **interior Belize District inland**.
  - **🟡 Lower**: **Cayo District inland (Belmopan, San Ignacio, Benque Viejo)** — Belmopan was sited inland in 1970 specifically to reduce hurricane exposure; it remains Belize's lowest-hurricane-exposure populated area.
- **Building-code implication**: building codes + practice tightened post-Iris (2001) + post-Lisa (2022). Resort-grade post-2010 builds materially safer than 1970s-1980s middle-class housing. **Verify current insurance carrier appetite per district before closing** — Lisa 2022 + the broader Caribbean post-Beryl/Melissa hardening trend means west-coast / coastal premiums are rising.
- **Insurance**: home insurance bundling hurricane peril with all-risk fire is standard via Belize-licensed carriers (RF&G Insurance Company, Belize Insurance Centre, Insurance Corporation of Belize, etc., reinsured through international markets); **CAT deductibles 2-5 %** of sum insured typical; **rates ~0.4-1.0 % of insured value/yr** est. (secondary aggregator data — verify with carrier).

### Earthquake / seismic

- **Tectonic context**: Belize sits on the relatively stable interior of the **Caribbean Plate**. **Low seismicity** compared with Hispaniola, Jamaica (Gonâve microplate), or the Pacific subduction zones of Costa Rica + Guatemala.
- **Historical**: minor felt events occasionally; **1976 earthquake offshore Honduras-Guatemala** felt in southern Belize; **2009 Honduras M7.3 (Roatán offshore)** felt in Belize — minimal damage. **No Belize-internal historic seismic catastrophe comparable to Jamaica 1907 or Costa Rica Cinchona 2009**.
- **Building code**: not a seismic-design priority compared with hurricane wind. Verify the structure was engineered for the wind load it actually faces.

### Flood

- **High-risk basins**: **Belize River (Belize City, Burrell Boom, Belmopan corridor)**, **New River (Orange Walk Town)**, **Rio Hondo (Corozal corridor)**, **Mopan + Macal (Cayo / San Ignacio / Benque)**, **Sittee + South Stann Creek**, **Monkey River + Moho (Toledo)**.
- **Belize City**: low elevation (mostly < 2 m AMSL), causeway-and-mangrove geography; **chronic urban flash flooding** during heavy rain + storm surge. Hurricane Lisa 2022 inundated much of the urban area.
- **Coastal storm-surge zones**: **Belize City, Ambergris Caye low-lying interior, Caye Caulker, Placencia village (peninsula tip), Hopkins, Dangriga, Punta Gorda waterfront**.
- **NEMO + the Hydrology Unit** publish basin-level + district-level flood assessments — **no consolidated public parcel-level flood-zone web viewer** equivalent to FEMA NFHL. Verification path: NEMO district committee + parish council Development Order + on-site inspection.

### Title risk — the single biggest BZ-specific gotcha

- **Declared vs undeclared land**: see Country profile + `--price` § Compute. **Undeclared land (Deed of Conveyance via Titles Registry)** is materially weaker than registered title under the Registered Land Act. Conversion to declared / registered title is possible but requires **survey + statutory declarations + Lands Department lodgement + ~6-18 month process**. Price the conversion cost into the deal.
- **Title insurance scarce**: not universal in Belize (much of the foreign-buyer market closes without it); some attorneys arrange via **First American Title** or **Stewart Title** local representatives. Strongly recommended for any non-trivial transaction.
- **Quiet-title proceedings**: where an undeclared chain has gaps, a **quiet-title application** to Supreme Court of Belize may be required — costly + slow.
- **Boundary disputes**: surveyor's pre-checked plan + on-site monumentation walk-through with neighbour acknowledgement is essential; informal fences + access tracks across neighbours' land are common rural-Belize realities.

### Maya customary-tenure overlay (Toledo District) — unique to Belize

- **CCJ 2015 ruling** (Maya Leaders Alliance v Attorney General of Belize): Caribbean Court of Justice recognised **Maya communal land rights in Toledo District** as **legally equivalent to Western property ownership**.
- **2024 draft Maya Customary Land Tenure Policy** in consultation across **41 Maya villages in Toledo District** (Government of Belize / Ministry of Indigenous Peoples' Affairs; San Pedro Sun reporting). Policy would formalise village-level communal tenure on a circle-radius basis (2-6 km depending on village population). **Status (2026 data)**: still draft; consultation ongoing; not enacted as legislation in the sources read. The CCJ closed its monitoring of the 2015 Consent Order on 30 Oct 2025 (CCJ President's closing remarks — primary) and on 30 Jul 2026 dismissed a Toledo Alcaldes Association / Maya Leaders Alliance application for clarification, saying the scope of Maya customary tenure will remain uncertain until codified by the laws of Belize (7News Belize transcript — secondary).
- **Implication**: any **Toledo District land transaction** outside an existing fee-simple title needs Maya village-level + alcaldes due-diligence. Treat the customary-tenure overlay as a **🔴 deal-breaker** for parcels not clearly outside Maya communal claim until the 2024 policy is enacted + recorded.
- **Cultural Survival** (Indigenous-rights NGO) has criticised the 2024 draft as not meeting **Free, Prior, and Informed Consent** standards; political contention through 2025-2026 likely. Verify status before closing any Toledo deal.

### Climate change projections (2050 horizon)

- **Hurricane intensity**: trending up (more Cat 4-5 share) — IPCC AR6 + NOAA Atlantic basin assessments. Iris 2001 + Lisa 2022 align with this trajectory.
- **Sea-level rise**: **+20-50 cm by 2100** (RCP4.5–8.5 range); **acute exposure** along the entire reef-fronted coast — Ambergris Caye, Caye Caulker, all northern + central cayes, Belize City causeway/mainland edge, Placencia, Hopkins. **Most of Belize City sits < 2 m AMSL**. (Sources: IPCC AR6 + Climate Central Coastal Risk Screening Tool; CZMAI national context.)
- **Reef bleaching**: Belize Barrier Reef System (UNESCO World Heritage) is under thermal stress — implications for tourism economics + beach replenishment + storm protection.
- **Rainfall variability**: inland droughts (Cayo / Toledo) + extreme rainfall events both intensifying.

## Section: `--mains`

### Water + sewer operator

- **Belize Water Services Limited (BWS)**: `https://www.bws.bz/` — single national piped-water utility for urban + most peri-urban areas. Public Utilities Commission (PUC): `https://puc.bz/` — regulates tariffs.
- **Sewer reality**:
  - **Urban Belize City + Belmopan core + parts of San Ignacio**: BWS reticulated water + some piped sewer (verify per address — 2022 Census reports **76.5 % flush toilets connected to either septic or public sewer; 87.4 % public-grid lighting**; pure mains-sewer share is materially lower than that headline because most "flush" households are on septic).
  - **Ambergris Caye (San Pedro Town core)**: BWS reticulated water; **sewer is partial** — some areas on town sewer, many on individual septic + soak-away or building-cluster package treatment plants. Verify per development.
  - **Caye Caulker, Placencia village, Hopkins, Punta Gorda, Dangriga, Corozal Town, Orange Walk Town**: BWS water typically reticulated in town centre; **sewer rarely reticulated** — septic + soak-away is standard; rainwater harvesting common as supplementary supply.
  - **Rural villages, remote cayes, Toledo interior, Cayo interior**: **off-grid water** (rainwater harvesting + cistern + well) + septic + soak-away is standard. Many properties operate **fully off-grid** (solar PV + rainwater + septic).
- **Listing language**: **"on BWS mains"** = piped water; **"cistern + rainwater catchment"** or **"well + cistern"** = off-grid water; **"on town sewer"** = piped sewer; **"septic + soak-away"** = self-contained sewer.
- **Potability**: BWS treated water meets WHO drinking-water standards at point of leaving the treatment plant — but **older distribution + rooftop tank stagnation** mean most middle-class + foreign-buyer households **filter or boil drinking water**. **Bottled water + reverse-osmosis filters** widespread.

### Power

- **Belize Electricity Limited (BEL)**: `https://www.bel.com.bz/` — single distributor for most of the country. Some industrial + isolated supply via independent producers + Hummingbird grid via Mexico's CFE. Regulator: PUC.
- **2022 Census**: **87.4 % of households on public-grid lighting** — significant rural off-grid balance.
- **Reliability**: variable; outages from grid faults + tropical storms + cross-border supply disruptions are common. **Inverter + battery backup** ("solar + battery" or generator) is standard for any middle-class Belize City household and most resort properties.
- **Tariff**: published by PUC; residential block-tariff structure; **~USD 0.20–0.40 / kWh all-in** (est., Caribbean band — vintage not pinned) — verify current tariff at PUC + BEL rate cards.
- **Off-grid**: many Cayo + Toledo + caye properties run **full off-grid solar + battery + diesel generator** as the only practical option; cost premium baked into price.

## Active fiscal incentives + visa programs (2025-2026)

### Qualified Retired Persons (QRP) Programme — flagship

Administered by **Belize Tourism Board (BTB)**: `https://www.belizetourismboard.org/programs-events/retirement-program/`.

- **Eligibility**: **age 40+** (lowered from 45+ effective ~2023; BTB current page).
- **Income requirement**: **USD 2,000 / month** (or USD 24,000 / year) from a **source outside Belize**, in **USD, GBP, EUR, or CAD**. Pension, retirement income, or annuity — must be deposited into a Belizean bank, credit union, or licensed financial institution.
- **Residency requirement**: must spend **at least 30 consecutive days** in Belize per year.
- **Key benefits**:
  - **Exemption from Belize tax** on **all foreign-source income**, **capital gains**, and **inheritance**.
  - **One-time duty-free import** of household goods, vehicles (under 5 years old), boats, and light aircraft within the first programme year.
  - **Permanent QRP status** (renewable; ID card USD 200 + USD 25 annual renewal).
- **Fees**: USD 150 application; USD 1,000 applicant fee; USD 750 per dependent; USD 200 ID card.
- **Working in Belize**: **prohibited** for income from local sources (programme is retirement-intent).
- **Comparison to Costa Rica Pensionado (~USD 1,000/mo, est. — verify in `countries/cr/playbook.md`) + Panama Pensionado (~USD 1,000/mo, est. — verify in `countries/pa/playbook.md`)**: against those est. comparators, Belize QRP's **USD 2,000/mo** (sourced above) is **~2× the income threshold** but offers a **fuller tax-exemption package** on foreign-source income.

### Tax-incentive regimes

- **No CBI (Citizenship-by-Investment) programme**. Belize ended its earlier Economic Citizenship Programme in 2002.
- **No worldwide tax for non-residents** — non-residents taxed only on Belize-source income (territorial features).
- **Capital gains tax 0 %** — Belize has no CGT.
- **Estate / inheritance tax 0 %** — Belize has neither.
- **GST registration threshold BZD 75k** + Income tax exempt threshold **BZD 29,000** (effective 1 Jan 2025).

### Residency / citizenship pathways

- **Visa-free entry**: 30-90 days for US, UK, Canada, EU, Caribbean nationals (verify per nationality at `https://immigration.gov.bz/`).
- **Tourist visa extensions**: monthly extensions available at Immigration offices; many expats live on rolling tourist extensions for years (anecdotal — but not a route to PR).
- **Temporary residence permit**: 1 year, renewable; pathway to PR.
- **Permanent Residence (PR)**: ~1 year continuous on temporary residence + clean record + financial self-sufficiency → PR application; processing 6-18 months.
- **Citizenship**: **5 years' residence + good character** under Belize Nationality Act. **No CBI fast-track**.
- **CARICOM Skilled Nationals**: free movement under CSME for 10 categorised professions.

## Caveats unique to BZ

- **🟢 Foreign-ownership parity** — Aliens Landholding Act repealed in 2001; foreigners own freehold on equal footing with citizens. No border zone, no maritime setback purchase ban (but Queen's Chain is public Crown reserve), no nationality quota. Only mandatory administrative steps are TIN registration + closing attorney + Lands Department lodgement.
- **English Common Law foundation** (former British Honduras until independence 21 September 1981) — distinct from Spanish-civil-law neighbours Mexico, Guatemala, Honduras. Conveyancing closer to Jamaica + Anglo-Caribbean than to Costa Rica + Panama. Court of last resort: **Caribbean Court of Justice (CCJ)** — Belize is full member of CCJ Appellate Jurisdiction since 2010.
- **English is the sole official language** — uniquely accessible legal + admin process for English-speaking foreign buyers compared with Hispanic neighbours.
- **BZD pegged to USD at 2:1 since May 1976** — structural FX stability; cornerstone of CBB monetary regime; confirmed primary at `https://www.centralbank.org.bz/`.
- **Property tax on UV (unimproved land value) only** — buildings exempt, making **annual property tax often < USD 200/yr** even on high-value beachfront — the country's headline tax-friendliness claim, well-documented secondary.
- **No capital gains tax + no estate tax + no inheritance tax + no wealth tax** — among the most tax-friendly Caribbean / LatAm jurisdictions for property holders. Verify current at BTS before reliance.
- **Stamp Duty 8 % (foreigner) / 5 % (Belizean / CARICOM) / 7 % (foreigner via IBC pre-2024)** on consideration > BZD 20k — modestly higher transaction tax than Costa Rica (~3-4 %) or Panama (~3-4 %), offset by zero CGT.
- **Stamp Duties (Amendment) Act 2024** (Act No. 33 of 2024 — assented 9 Dec 2024, gazetted 14 Dec 2024, **in force 1 January 2025**) — new **s.64(12)** taxes **any** conveyance or transfer of stock or shares by sale, exchange or gift at **5 %** (company incorporated in Belize) / **7 %** (incorporated outside Belize) on value or consideration whichever is greater, so the share-transfer route into a property-holding company is **taxed, not exempt**; the Act **does not mention IBCs**. It also re-bands mortgage duty (**s.59** — 0 % to BZD 100,000 secured, 1.5 % on the excess; 0.6 % on assignment / transfer / reconveyance / release / discharge / surrender), replaces the **s.69** exemption list (spouses, common-law spouses, siblings, children, parents; testamentary disposition + intestacy; charitable ≤ BZD 25,000 in any 12 months; agricultural / residential leases under 2 years at rent under BZD 2,000 per month — **no grandparent or grandchild exemption**, despite agent marketing claiming one), and extends land-transfer duty to **joint-tenancy conveyances** while exempting company-to-company transfers where the **beneficial owner** (s.2(1) Money Laundering and Terrorism (Prevention) Act) is the same on both sides (**s.72**). **s.69(g)** further amended by **Act No. 7 of 2025** (gazetted 11 Feb 2025, also in force 1 January 2025). Verify the consolidated text with a Belize attorney before transacting.
- **Declared vs undeclared land** is the **single biggest BZ-specific title risk**. Undeclared land via Deed of Conveyance is materially weaker than registered title under the Registered Land Act; conversion is possible but slow + costly (6-18 months). Always verify with the Lands Department before pricing.
- **Title insurance is scarce + optional** — many transactions close without it. Strongly recommended for any foreign-buyer transaction given undeclared-land prevalence.
- **Maya customary tenure (Toledo District)** — CCJ 2015 ruling + 2024 draft Maya Customary Land Tenure Policy in consultation. **Treat any Toledo District land transaction outside an existing fee-simple title as red-flag** until the 2024 policy is enacted + recorded. Outside Toledo, irrelevant.
- **Queen's Chain** — 66-foot Crown reserve along high-water mark; "beachfront fee simple" parcels typically abut, not include, the foreshore. Verify boundary against Lands & Surveys Department survey diagram.
- **QRP (Qualified Retired Persons)** — flagship retirement programme; **age 40+ + USD 2,000/month foreign income + 30 days/year minimum stay**; full tax exemption on foreign-source income, CGT, inheritance; one-time duty-free import. Administered by Belize Tourism Board.
- **No CBI (Citizenship-by-Investment)** — earlier programme ended 2002. Permanent residence requires 1 year temp + financial review; citizenship 5 years.
- **Hurricane risk highest** along the entire coastline + cayes (June-November season). Lisa 2022 (Belize City) + Iris 2001 (Placencia / Toledo) are the modern reference events. **Belmopan inland exists specifically because Hattie 1961 destroyed Belize City** — Cayo District is structurally lowest-hurricane-exposure populated area.
- **Seismic risk LOW** — Belize sits on the Caribbean Plate interior; no major historic seismic catastrophe internal to Belize.
- **Reef + mangrove ecosystem** — Belize Barrier Reef System (UNESCO) is under thermal-stress + bleaching pressure; tourism economics + storm protection depend on reef health.
- **Off-grid is normal outside town centres** — many Cayo + Toledo + caye properties run full off-grid solar + battery + cistern + septic. Budget USD 25-60k for full system on undeveloped rural / island parcels.
- **Crime by district varies dramatically** — see `--crime`. South-side Belize City is materially elevated; resort-corridor + Cayo + most upper-tier residential are materially insulated.
- **Belize City ≠ Belmopan ≠ tourist corridor** — three distinct property markets + risk profiles. Don't conflate.
- **"Spanish Lookout" + Mennonite communities (Cayo District)** — established farming + manufacturing communities; cash-economy traditions + community-internal land tenure can complicate adjacent transactions; not a buy-into-the-community option (verify with attorney for any adjacent deal).
