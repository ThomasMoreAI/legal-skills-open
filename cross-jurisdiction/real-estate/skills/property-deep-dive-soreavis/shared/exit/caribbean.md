## 🇩🇴 Dominican Republic

- **DOM**: Punta Cana/Bávaro 120-250 / Cap Cana 150-300 / Santo Domingo (Piantini/Naco) 100-180 / Las Terrenas/Samaná 150-300+
- **Commission**: seller-pays 3-6% (5% common; luxury negotiable to 3-4%) + 18% ITBIS on commission only
- **Contract**: contrato de exclusividad 3-6 mo
- **MLS**: no formal MLS; AEI (Asociación de Empresas Inmobiliarias) members share informally; Encuentra24 + Punta Cana-specific portals
- **Discount**: 10-20% off asking; CONFOTUR developments may transact closer to ask
- **Sell-side**: residents capital gains 27% on net gain; **non-residents 27% withholding on gain** (or 1% of sale price advance, lower of two); **CONFOTUR exempt zones for first sale within program**; commission 3-6%
- **Liquidity**: 🟠 MEDIUM Punta Cana/Cap Cana tourist core (USD-priced); 🟠 LOW-MEDIUM Santo Domingo prime; 🔴 LOW interior / Samaná off-peak
- **Quirks**: **CONFOTUR (Law 158-01) tax incentives** for tourism-zone properties — verify eligibility transfers; USD-priced coastal markets, DOP-priced inland (FX exposure on inland exit); title irregularities common — Jurisdicción Inmobiliaria certificates required

## 🇯🇲 Jamaica

- **DOM**: Kingston (New Kingston / Cherry Gardens / Norbrook / Stony Hill) ~120-240 / Montego Bay (Tryall / Round Hill / Iron Shore / Coral Gardens) 150-365+ (highly seasonal — winter Nov-Apr peak) / Negril cliff villas 180-365+ / Ocho Rios beachfronts 150-300 / Falmouth historic 200-365+ / interior 250-500+ [verify with NLA / agency advisory]
- **Commission**: **5-7% seller-pays typical** (Realtors Association of Jamaica — RAJ regulated; Real Estate Board licensure) + 15% GCT on commission for commercial / commission on sale of residences GCT-exempt — verify per transaction
- **Contract**: bilateral Sale Agreement with deposit (10% standard) → conveyance via Jamaican attorney → registration at NLA (National Land Agency); **TRN (Taxpayer Registration Number) mandatory for foreign sellers** to receive proceeds + register clearance; foreign-ownership PARITY (no nationality bar — Registration of Titles Act)
- **MLS**: NO formal MLS; **JamaicaProperties.com + RealEstateJamaica.com + Coldwell Banker Jamaica + Sotheby's International Realty Jamaica + Century 21 Jamaica** dominate; ~40% of land remains UNREGISTERED (common-law possessory title — verify Torrens vs unregistered chain)
- **Discount**: 8-15% off asking typical; **post-Hurricane Melissa (Cat 5, 28 Oct 2025) Westmoreland properties trading at significant discounts 2026-2027** as insurance-market hardens + reconstruction supply absorption; tourist-corridor (Montego Bay / Negril / Ocho Rios) stronger; Kingston upper-tier sticky
- **Sell-side**: **2% Transfer Tax + JMD 5,000 flat Stamp Duty** post-2019 reform (was 5% transfer + 4% stamp pre-2019 — material reduction); **CGT 0% individuals (UNIQUE)**; commission 5-7% + GCT-on-commercial; legal/conveyance ~2% (1% buyer + 1% seller convention); **non-residents face POCA AML scrutiny on large wires**
- **Liquidity**: 🟢 HIGH Montego Bay tourist-corridor villas (Tryall / Round Hill / Negril / Ocho Rios) — UK/US/CA/diaspora demand strong; 🟠 MEDIUM Kingston upper-tier (New Kingston / Cherry Gardens / Norbrook); 🟠 MEDIUM Falmouth historic; 🔴 LOW Kingston inner-city (security premium); VERY LOW rural unregistered; **Hurricane Melissa Cat 5 28 Oct 2025** (Westmoreland) — strongest Atlantic hurricane in Jamaican history; insurance market hardening expected through 2026-2027
- **Quirks**: **Foreign-ownership PARITY** 🟢 — UNIQUE in Caribbean (no nationality restrictions, no border zone, no maritime setback, no quotas); only mandatory step: TRN from TAJ; tourist-corridor villa transactions widely USD-denominated (Montego Bay / Tryall / Round Hill / Negril / Ocho Rios / Kingston upper-tier); local-market + middle-tier housing typically priced in JMD; **CGT 0% individuals (UNIQUE)**; ~2.83M pop (Kingston Metro 1M+, Montego Bay 110k); 5-character postcodes (Universal Postal District 2007 incomplete rollout); English official + Jamaican Patois (Patwa); **NO formal RBI / CBI via real estate** — standard residency via PICA Work Permit / Marriage / Investor (case-by-case discretion); Hurricane Melissa Cat 5 28 Oct 2025 + 1907 Kingston M6.5 + 1692 Port Royal M7.0 baseline; Cane River + Hope River + Rio Cobre flooding; BSJ JNBC building-code review + insurance market hardening expected through 2026-2027

## 🇧🇸 The Bahamas

- **DOM**: New Providence (Lyford Cay / Albany / Old Fort Bay / Ocean Club Estates / Cable Beach prime) ~90-180 / New Providence secondary (Sandyport / Westridge / Eastern Road) 120-240 / Grand Bahama (Lucaya / Freeport) 180-365+ / Family Islands (Abaco / Eleuthera / Exuma / Harbour Island) 120-300+ (highly seasonal — winter Nov-Apr peak) / Baker's Bay private-island 150-365+ [verify with BREA — Bahamas Real Estate Association]
- **Commission**: **6% seller-pays** (BREA standard, regulated since 1995) + 10% VAT on commission; luxury private-island negotiable to 4-5%; off-MLS + private-treaty 3-4% common at HNW tier
- **Contract**: bilateral Sale Agreement → IPLA REGISTRATION at BIA (single-family ≤2 acres) OR IPLA PERMIT (>2 acres / commercial / undeveloped land >5 acres for commercial use) → conveyance via Bahamian attorney → Stamp Duty + VAT-on-conveyance at DIR → registration at Registrar General; **CBOB Exchange Control Permission letters customary at significant non-resident-purchase size**
- **MLS**: **BREA MLS exists** (regulated multi-listing service — UNIQUE in Caribbean for private-broker-network MLS); **Damianos Sotheby's International / HG Christie / Christie's International / Coldwell Banker Lightbourn / Mackey-Daly + ERA Dupuch / Bahamas Realty** dominate prime; foreign-buyer-friendly broker network well-developed
- **Discount**: 5-15% off asking typical; **post-Hurricane Dorian (Sep 2019) Abaco + Grand Bahama recovery still incomplete** — insurance-driven price discovery distortions persist 6+ years; private-island enclaves (Lyford Cay / Albany / Old Fort Bay / Ocean Club Estates / Baker's Bay) trade closer to ask; HNW tier sticky
- **Sell-side**: **Stamp Duty + VAT-on-conveyance combined ~10%** above B$100k (split by convention 50/50 buyer/seller — verify exact split at contract); **NO PIT, NO CGT, NO inheritance/estate, NO gift, NO wealth tax** (UNIQUE — no exit tax on sale); commission 6% + 10% VAT; legal ~2.5% (1.25% each side); **CBOB Exchange Control endorsement at non-resident sale enables reverse-repatriation of original USD-import basis** — verify documentation chain at sale
- **Liquidity**: 🟢 HIGH New Providence Lyford Cay / Albany / Old Fort Bay / Ocean Club Estates / Baker's Bay (US/CA/UK/global HNW demand); 🟠 MEDIUM Cable Beach / Sandyport / Westridge prime; 🟠 MEDIUM Family Islands premium (Abaco / Eleuthera / Exuma / Harbour Island); 🔴 LOW Grand Bahama Freeport; VERY LOW Family Islands rural; **Hurricane risk DOMINANT physical risk** — Atlantic belt traverses archipelago annually; Dorian 2019 calibration event (~75% home destruction Abaco / Grand Bahama)
- **Quirks**: **IPLA 1993 (International Persons Landholding Act, Cap 140)** liberal regime — single-family ≤2 acres by REGISTRATION (not permit); >2 acres OR commercial = PERMIT from BIA; **NO PIT, NO CGT, NO inheritance/estate, NO gift, NO wealth tax** (UNIQUE — uniquely light tax stack); **annual Real Property Tax (RPT) bands budget-revisable** — verify current bands at DIR before any transaction-cost computation; **VAT 10% standard since 2022** (raised 7.5%→10%); **BSD pegged 1:1 USD since 1973** (CBOB) — USD universally accepted; high-end Lyford Cay / Albany / Old Fort Bay / Ocean Club Estates / Baker's Bay USD-denominated by default; **Property-Acquired Visa Program (BIA): B$750k → PR consideration / B$1.5M → expedited ~21-day review**; ~412k pop (~70% New Providence, ~14% Grand Bahama, ~16% Family Islands); 4-digit postcodes; English official; **Hurricane Dorian (Sep 2019)** destroyed ~75%+ of homes on parts of Abaco + Grand Bahama — calibration event for insurance + building-code conversations; **NO universal national parcel ID** (deed-based common-law conveyancing) — Survey plans lodged with DLS authoritative

## 🇧🇧 Barbados

- **DOM**: West Coast "Platinum Coast" prime (Sandy Lane / Royal Westmoreland / Apes Hill / Port Ferdinand / Port St. Charles / Holetown / Mullins) ~120-300 / South Coast (Christ Church — Hastings / Worthing / Dover / St. Lawrence Gap) 120-240 / North-West (Speightstown St. Peter prime) 150-300 / Bridgetown + St. Michael CBD 150-300 / Interior parishes (St. Thomas / St. George / St. Joseph / St. John / St. Andrew) 200-365+ [verify with Lands and Surveys Department + Land Registry]
- **Commission**: **5% seller-pays typical** (Barbados Real Estate Association — BREA-affiliated agencies); 17.5% VAT on commission services (RE sale itself VAT-EXEMPT); luxury private-treaty negotiable to 3-4%
- **Contract**: bilateral Sale Agreement with deposit (10% standard) → conveyance via Barbadian attorney → registration at Land Registry under Property Act Cap. 236 OR Title Registration Act Cap. 229 (1979) for opt-in Torrens; **CBB Exchange Control registration of incoming foreign currency mandatory** at purchase under Exchange Control Act Cap. 71 — administrative not gatekeeping but is the legal precondition for capital + gains repatriation on resale
- **MLS**: NO formal MLS; **Realtors Limited / Christie's International / Sotheby's International / RE/MAX Caribbean / Coldwell Banker / Sandy Lane Real Estate** dominate prime; **Bajan Realtor Association + BREA**-affiliated agencies; deeds-recording vs Title Registration parallel systems
- **Discount**: 5-15% off asking typical; **post-Hurricane Beryl (1 July 2024 Cat 3)** insurance cost is material to the DCF — post-Beryl re-rating not documented by a primary source; verify with 3+ carrier quotes and FSC Barbados; ECLAC-assessed effects ~BBD 193M; tourist-corridor (West Coast Platinum + South Coast) sticky on the upside; interior parishes deeper discount tolerance
- **Sell-side**: **Stamp Duty 1% on seller** + **PTT 2.5%** above BBD 150,000 (where building exists; vacant land no exemption — buyer pays PTT) + commission 5% + 17.5% VAT on commission = combined seller-side ~7-9% of sale price; **NO individual CGT** (UNIQUE selling-point); **CBB Exchange Control endorsement at non-resident sale enables reverse-repatriation** of original FX-import basis — verify documentation chain at sale; legal/conveyance ~1-2%
- **Liquidity**: 🟢 HIGH West Coast Platinum (Sandy Lane / Royal Westmoreland / Apes Hill / Port Ferdinand / Port St. Charles) — UK/US/CA/global HNW demand strong; 🟢 HIGH Bridgetown CBD high-rise + South Coast Christ Church (Hastings / Worthing / Dover / St. Lawrence Gap) DNV-friendly; 🟠 MEDIUM Speightstown + North-West St. Peter; 🔴 LOW interior parishes (St. Thomas / St. George / St. Joseph / St. John / St. Andrew); **Hurricane risk Atlantic basin** — Beryl 1 Jul 2024 Cat 3 passed ~150 km south; prior comparable pass Hurricane Ivan 7 Sep 2004 (Cat 3 ~165 km south, est. from NHC best track); post-Beryl insurance re-rating not documented by a primary source — verify with carriers and FSC Barbados
- **Quirks**: **Foreign-ownership PARITY** 🟢 — no nationality bar on residential / commercial / agricultural freehold; **CBB Exchange Control registration is the gating step** (administrative, not gatekeeping in normal cases — but is the legal precondition for capital + gains repatriation on resale); **NO CITIZENSHIP-BY-INVESTMENT** (distinguishes from KN/DM/AG/LC/GD which DO offer CBI); residency routes: **Welcome Stamp 12-month DNV USD 50,000+ income** (renewed by Cabinet to 31 Dec 2026; tax-exempt on Barbados-sourced foreign-employer income) + **SERP 5-yr renewable** (Cat. 2 property-owner threshold USD 300k+); **2025 Budget**: PTT removed for deeds of gift to children/dependants for land <1 acre; Resilience and Regeneration Fund employee contribution raised 0.10% → 0.25% effective 1 Apr 2025; **PIT band reduction announced** for income year 2026 (BRA Policy Note 20 Apr 2026 — verify exact bands when gazetted); **BBD pegged 2:1 USD since 5 July 1975** (CBB) — unbroken peg through 2026; **Hurricane Beryl 1 Jul 2024 Cat 3** ECLAC effects ~BBD 193M — post-Beryl insurance re-rating not documented by a primary source — verify with carriers and FSC Barbados; ~282,500 pop / English official + Bajan Creole; CCJ final court of appeal since 2005; republic 30 Nov 2021 — no property-law effect; deeds-recording vs Torrens-style Title Registration parallel systems

## 🇧🇿 Belize

- **DOM**: San Pedro Town (Ambergris Caye) ~90-180 (highly seasonal — winter Nov-Apr peak) / Caye Caulker 120-240 / Placencia (Stann Creek) 90-180 / Cayo District (San Ignacio / Spanish Lookout / Bullet Tree expat) 150-300 / Belize City (Marine Parade / Buttonwood Bay) 200-365+ / Belmopan 200-365+ / Toledo District (Punta Gorda) 250-500+ / interior 365+ [verify with Lands and Surveys Department / Land Registry]
- **Commission**: **5-7% seller-pays typical** (informal market — Belize Real Estate Brokers Association nascent regulatory framework); some buyer-pays in tourist-corridor; no formal VAT on commission (Belize's GST 12.5% applies to commercial commission but residential commission generally exempt)
- **Contract**: bilateral Sale Agreement → conveyance via Belize-licensed attorney → registration at **Land Titles Unit** (Registered Land Act Cap. 194 — declared lands modern Torrens-style title) OR **Titles Registry** (older system handling undeclared lands via Deed of Conveyance + indenture chain); **Stamp Duty Act + Land Tax Act** transactional charges
- **MLS**: NO formal MLS; **RE/MAX Belize / Coldwell Banker / Sotheby's International Realty / Century 21 / Caye Realtors / 7th Heaven Properties / Belize Property Center / IMS** dominate; **Ambergris Caye-focused brokers** (San Pedro Real Estate / Tropical Properties / Pristine Bay); declared-vs-undeclared land split critical
- **Discount**: 10-20% off asking typical; **tourist-corridor (Ambergris Caye / Caye Caulker / Placencia / Cayo)** stronger; interior + Toledo deeper discount tolerance; **post-Hurricane Lisa Cat 1 (2 Nov 2022)** Belize District 2020-or-older builds priced for structural-condition diligence; **Maya communal-land disputes Toledo District** complete deal-breaker for affected parcels
- **Sell-side**: **General Sales Tax / Stamp Duty 8% on >USD 10k value** (consider primary-residence + first-time-buyer exemptions — verify with attorney); **CGT 0% individuals** on residential disposals (UNIQUE); commission 5-7%; legal/conveyance ~2-3%; **non-residents face standard scrutiny** but no specific withholding (Belize foreign-buyer-friendly post-2001 ALA repeal)
- **Liquidity**: 🟢 MEDIUM-HIGH San Pedro Ambergris Caye + Placencia tourist-corridor (US/CA/UK/expat retirement demand); 🟠 MEDIUM Caye Caulker + Cayo expat-corridor; 🟠 LOW-MEDIUM Belize City prime (Marine Parade / Buttonwood Bay) + Belmopan; 🔴 LOW Belize City secondary; VERY LOW Toledo District (Maya customary-land-overlay barred for foreigners de facto pending policy formalization); VERY LOW interior + rural; **Hurricane Lisa Cat 1 (2 Nov 2022)** + Atlantic belt seasonal exposure
- **Quirks**: **Foreign-ownership PARITY since 2001 Aliens Landholding Act repeal** 🟢 — no permit / quota / nationality bar for residential / commercial / agricultural freehold (one of the most foreigner-friendly jurisdictions in the Americas); **DEAL-BREAKER**: Maya communal customary tenure in Toledo District (CCJ 2015 Maya Leaders Alliance v AG ruling) — **Government 2024 draft Maya Customary Land Tenure Policy still in consultation** (verify before any Toledo deal); declared-vs-undeclared land split: declared = Torrens-style modern title; **undeclared = materially weaker Deed of Conveyance**; **Stamp Duties (Amendment) Act 2024 (Act No. 33 of 2024)** taxes share transfers under new s.64(12) at 5 % (company incorporated in Belize) / 7 % (incorporated outside Belize) — the Act does not mention IBCs; **Income Tax exemption raised BZD 26k → BZD 29k effective 1 Jan 2025**; **QRP age threshold lowered 45 → 40** (Belize Tourism Board); **BZD pegged 2:1 USD since May 1976** (Central Bank of Belize) — peg cornerstone of monetary regime; tourist-corridor + foreign-buyer transactions widely USD-denominated (Ambergris Caye / Caye Caulker / Placencia / Cayo expat parcels); local-market BZD-priced; ~410k pop / NO nationwide postcode (street/lot, town/village, district addressing); English sole official + Spanish ~50% L1 in Cayo/Corozal/Orange Walk + Kriol lingua franca + Maya (Kekchi/Mopan/Yucatec) Toledo + Garifuna southern coast; conveyancing in English; **Hurricane Lisa Cat 1 (2 Nov 2022)** ~5,000 homes damaged + 500 destroyed + ~USD 100M losses (most recent direct major-impact event)

## 🇰🇾 Cayman Islands

- **DOM**: Seven Mile Beach (SMB) corridor beachfront condos ~120-365+ (highest-value, thinnest pool) / George Town + South Sound mid-condo ~120-240 / single-family Grand Cayman ~120-300 / residential land 180-365+ / Sister Islands (Cayman Brac / Little Cayman) 300-700+ [CIREBA 2025: condos ~186 days avg time-to-sell, secondary; per local practice — verify with 2-3 CIREBA brokers per micro-market]
- **Commission**: **seller-pays; CIREBA fixed sliding scale 4% (consideration ≥ US$9.95M) → 10% (lower-value property)** — typically **5-7%** mid-market (CIREBA Code of Ethics mandates the scale; members cannot discount — "price-fixing" scrutiny raised by Cayman Compass 2023-2024; a competing non-CIREBA association formed Jan 2024) — verify current scale + which side at broker

- **MLS**: **CIREBA MLS exists** (broker-controlled multi-listing — most listings syndicate); Sotheby's / ERA Cayman / RE/MAX Cayman / Provenance (Christie's) dominate prime; no public parcel-level price index (Lands & Survey Valuation & Estates Office does valuations, not a HPI)
- **Discount**: ⚠️ no published asking-to-sale gap series — `data not publicly available`; high-end SMB beachfront is sticky on the upside; per local practice — verify against ≥3 active CIREBA listings same district + waterfront-status
- **Sell-side**: **NO CGT, NO income tax, NO inheritance tax, NO annual property tax — no exit tax on sale** (the defining feature); the only transaction lever is **stamp duty on transfer (7.5% standard; 10% on consideration ≥ CI$2M from 1 Jan 2026, slab on whole value)** — payable by the BUYER (so not a seller-side cost, but materially narrows the high-value buyer pool from 1 Jan 2026); seller-side cost = **commission 5-7% (CIREBA scale) + legal ~1.5-2%**; **no foreign-seller withholding**
- **Liquidity**: 🟢 HIGH SMB corridor + George Town prime condos (deep US/CA/UK/global buyer pool, USD-denominated via CI$1=US$1.20 peg); 🟠 MEDIUM West Bay / South Sound / Rum Point-Cayman Kai waterfront; 🔴 LOW residential land + interior; VERY LOW Sister Islands (Cayman Brac / Little Cayman — thin formal market, air-evac medical); **Hurricane Ivan (Sept 2004, Cat 4/5, >80% buildings damaged) is the calibration catastrophe** — catastrophe-insurance cost + availability is the binding carrying-cost constraint (collapses ~6-7% gross yield to ~2% net)
- **Quirks**: **Foreign-buyer PARITY** 🟢 — no licence / consent / quota / size limit for non-Caymanians (one of the most foreign-buyer-friendly regimes in the Caribbean; gate is AML/KYC + source-of-funds, NOT FX or licence); **USD-denominated market** (CI$/US$ peg since 1974); **1 Jan 2026 stamp-duty 10% band ≥ CI$2M** is a new buyer-side cliff at the luxury end — model the cost to the resale buyer when pricing exit; **STR requires Tourism Accommodation Licence + 13% Tourist Accommodation Tax** (affects buy-to-let exit thesis); Caymanian-only first-time-buyer stamp-duty concessions cannot be used by foreign buyers

## 🇧🇲 Bermuda

> ⚠️ **Geography note**: Bermuda is a **North Atlantic** British Overseas Territory (~1,035 km ESE of Cape Hatteras, NC) — **NOT in the Caribbean**. Grouped in this region file per the backfill request; do not describe Bermuda as Caribbean in client output.

- **DOM**: ⚠️ **no primary sale-price index or DOM series exists** (`data not publicly available`); the licence-eligible non-Bermudian segment (top-ARV houses + a slice of condos) is structurally **very thin and slow** — multi-million-USD entry, narrow buyer pool — `per local practice — verify`; island-wide average home ~US$1m (Royal Gazette press 28 May 2025, secondary) but a foreign buyer shops only the top ARV tier (Paget ~$3-10m; Tucker's Town frequently $10m+)
- **Commission**: **~5% seller-pays** (guideline set by the **Bermuda Chamber of Commerce Real Estate Division** Real Estate Handbook; listing office ~2.5%, listing agent ~half of that ~1.25%) — verify current at Chamber of Commerce
- **Contract**: bilateral sale agreement; conveyance by Bermudian attorney; **non-Bermudian sale requires the buyer to clear the ARV floor + obtain an Alien Landholding Licence (ALL)** — this gates who the seller can sell TO (a non-Bermudian reselling can only sell to another licence-eligible buyer or a Bermudian); registered electronic title via Land Title Registry Office (LTRO) under the Land Title Registration Act 2011
- **MLS**: NO formal public MLS; **Coldwell Banker Bermuda Realty / Rego Sotheby's International Realty / Sinclair Realty** dominate; **no primary government sale-price index** (Bermuda publishes ARV — Annual Rental Value — not a sale-price HPI)
- **Discount**: ⚠️ `data not publicly available` — no published asking-to-sale gap; per local practice — verify against current listings + Land Valuation ARV
- **Sell-side**: **NO CGT, NO income tax, NO inheritance/estate tax — no exit tax on the gain** (government revenue is payroll tax, customs duty, annual Land Tax, and conveyance stamp duty); seller-side cost = **commission ~5% + legal ~1.5-2%**; **conveyance stamp duty (marginal bands 2.1 → 7.35%, ~7.35% on the slice > $1.5m) is buyer-side** but the **Alien Landholding Licence fee (~12.5% house / ~8% condo / ~6.5% resort / ~6% PRC-holder of price, SECONDARY — verify at Dept of Immigration)** is the headline buyer-side cost that **structurally suppresses the foreign-resale buyer pool** — a ~15-22% buyer transaction load is THE liquidity constraint on exit
- **Liquidity**: 🔴 **LOW-to-VERY-LOW for the non-Bermudian-eligible segment** — one of the world's most restrictive foreign-buyer regimes (statutory ARV floor + discretionary Ministerial licence + ~12.5% licence fee) shrinks the resale buyer pool to other licence-eligible HNW non-Bermudians + Bermudians; 🟠 MEDIUM for the Bermudian-eligible mainstream market (deeper local pool); prime enclaves (Tucker's Town, Paget, Fairmont/resort-linked) trade among global ultra-wealthy but **slowly**; **North Atlantic hurricane HAZARD is high but VULNERABILITY is low** (reef-limestone/concrete + Bermuda-roof construction + strict code + top-3 global reinsurance hub = resilient, well-insured stock — insurance is NOT the liquidity drag that it is on low-lying Caribbean islands)
- **Quirks**: **One of the most RESTRICTIVE foreign-buyer regimes globally** 🔴 — non-Bermudian resale is gated by the ARV floor + ALL licence + ~12.5% licence fee; **anti-fronting provisions** (Immigration and Protection Act 1956 Part VI) block trust/nominee circumvention; **BMD pegged 1:1 USD** (no FX risk, USD circulates at par); **no island-wide piped mains water** — each home collects potable supply via the white stepped Bermuda roof into an underground tank (a genuine condition/cost line on any sale — buyers diligence tank condition); first-time-buyer stamp-duty relief generally targets Bermudians, not foreign buyers
