# Universal `--renovation` Section — Renovation Cost · Contractor Trust · Revival-Scheme Reality

Foreign-buyer **renovation** layer: the gap between the listing price of a "cheap" property and the all-in cost of making it habitable, plus the trust infrastructure for finding and binding a contractor from abroad. Covers per-m² renovation cost bands (standard refurbishment vs heritage/listed restoration), the national contractor-registration / label / chamber a foreign buyer uses to vet a builder, common contractor-fraud patterns, design-professional mandate thresholds, realistic cost-overrun bands, the renovation permit-clock interaction, and the **€1 / cheap-house / depopulation-revival-scheme registry** (with ENDED schemes marked). Distinct from `--type=heritage` (heritage-overlay only — listing constraints, conservation consent) and `--journey=renovation` (the workflow template that sequences the buy-then-renovate process). This section is the *cost-and-trust reality check*.

**Snapshot**: May 2026.

**Scope**: this doc supplies the *renovation-cost-and-trust* layer — what a refurbishment realistically costs per m², how a foreign buyer finds and binds a trustworthy contractor in this jurisdiction, what fraud patterns recur, what design-professional or supervision mandate applies, what permit clock gates the works, and whether the country/municipality runs a nominal-price or depopulation-revival scheme (and on what binding terms). It does **not** restate heritage-listing constraints (`--type=heritage`) or the step-by-step renovation journey (`--journey=renovation`). A foreign-buyer renovator typically needs four pieces of intel before the offer: (1) the credible all-in €/m² band for the work class, (2) the contractor-trust framework and its limits, (3) the realistic overrun probability and driver, (4) whether any "cheap house" scheme is a genuine saving or a deposit-forfeiture commitment.

## Universal contract

For the property's country, return:

1. **Renovation cost band per m²** — standard refurbishment vs heritage/listed restoration, local currency + EUR/USD equivalent, major-city vs rural where it diverges materially. Every figure date-stamped and source-tier tagged (primary quantity-surveyor index / aggregator ⚠️ / `~est.` 🔴). Where no public band exists, `data not publicly available — verify with <local QS / builder-federation>`.
2. **Contractor-trust model** — the national registration body / label / chamber / licence a foreign buyer uses to identify and vet a contractor (e.g. FR RGE label + assurance décennale, IT Albo + geometra, ES building-association registries, PT IMPIC, UK FMB / TrustMark / RICS, DE Handwerkskammer, US state contractor board, AU state builder registration + home-warranty insurance). Name the body + statute. State explicitly where **no licensing regime exists** and the only recourse is civil-code hidden-defect warranty.
3. **Contractor-fraud patterns** — deposit-flight, subcontractor-lien exposure to the owner, undeclared "black" work that transfers a tax/penalty/insurance-void/resale-conformity liability to the new owner, mid-project abandonment.
4. **Design-professional / supervision mandate** — the threshold above which an architect / engineer / geometra / site-manager is legally required (FR architecte > 150 m², IT geometra/architetto + DL, DE Bauleiter, ES proyecto + dirección facultativa, JP 建築士).
5. **Cost-overrun band** — the realistic % overrun and its dominant driver (heritage clock, structural surprise, permit interaction, inflation). Labelled `est.` 🔴 unless evidence-anchored.
6. **Renovation permit-clock interaction** — the works-permit regime and its silence/approval timer (FR DP/DAACT 1-month silence-vaut-acceptation, IT SCIA/CILA/Permesso di Costruire, ES licencia de obras menores vs mayores, UK Building Notice vs Full Plans + Listed Building Consent, NZ Building Act s.48 decision clock).
7. **€1 / cheap-house / depopulation-revival-scheme registry** — does the country (or named municipalities) run a nominal-price / vacant-property-revival scheme? Name the municipality, the binding **completion timeline**, the **refundable deposit / completion bond**, and the forfeiture trigger. ENDED/CLOSED schemes marked explicitly with end date. **Confidence**: HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW per the skill's contract.

## ⚠️ 7 cross-cutting traps

1. **The "€1 house" is a deposit-forfeiture commitment, not a gift — and the all-in cost routinely exceeds the finished value.** Italian comuni schemes (Sambuca, Mussomeli, Salemi and others) require a binding bid well above €1 in practice, a **completion bond (commonly €5,000–50,000) refunded only on finishing within ~3 years**, plus notary, survey and transfer costs — before a renovation that itself frequently runs €1,000–2,500/m². The US "dollar home" analogue (e.g. Detroit Land Bank) is an **auction starting around USD 1,000 with a non-refundable deposit and a 6–9-month rehab-to-code-or-forfeit covenant**, not a $1 transfer. Recurrent buyer testimony: a €50k house can absorb €70–100k of works and still be worth less. Treat every revival scheme as a *capital commitment with a forfeiture clock*, not a discount.
2. **Cost overrun is the modal outcome on older/heritage stock, not the exception.** Heritage and structural-surprise overruns of **30–100%+** are routinely reported for `restauro storico` (IT), listed-building work (UK Grade I/II), and mid-project structural discovery (PT, ES). Budget a contingency of 25–40% on anything pre-1960 or unsurveyed; a fixed-price contract without a structural survey behind it is not actually fixed.
3. **Major structural work can reset the building to current code.** Jurisdictions tie a re-conformity / fresh-permit obligation to a structural-work threshold — e.g. Japan's 建築基準法 大規模の修繕/模様替 (work on >50% of a class of principal structural element), sharpened by the **1 April 2025 amendment shrinking the 4号特例** so more wooden houses now need 確認申請 with structural review. A nominal-price or subsidy programme never covers a code-reset gut renovation.
4. **Contractor-trust frameworks range from licensed-and-bonded to pure caveat-emptor — verify which applies before paying a deposit.** Some markets have enforceable registration + mandatory warranty/bond (FR assurance décennale, AU state home-warranty insurance, US state contractor boards with recovery funds); others have only a professional chamber or no licensing at all, where the buyer's sole recourse is the civil-code hidden-defect action (vicios ocultos / vices cachés / vizi occulti). Never assume a UK/US-style licensed-builder market exists where it does not.
5. **Undeclared "black" work transfers the liability to the new owner.** Cash, unpermitted or unregistered work commonly voids building insurance, blocks the post-completion conformity certificate, creates a back-tax/penalty exposure, and can expose the owner to subcontractor liens even where the general contractor was paid. Insist on registered firms, written contracts, and proof of mandatory warranty cover.
6. **The permit clock and the contractor timeline are different clocks — start the legal one first.** Silence-vaut-acceptation regimes (FR déclaration préalable) reward early filing; full-permit regimes (IT Permesso di Costruire, ES licencia mayor) and statutory decision clocks (NZ Building Act 2004 s.48; note the separate s.93 Code Compliance Certificate clock) can add months. Mobilising a contractor before the permit is granted is a classic source of both overrun and stop-work risk.
7. **Renovation spend can sit on a structure the foreign buyer cannot fully own.** Where freehold is restricted (TH 49% condo / no land, ID nominee voidness, PH 60/40, GCC zone-gated freehold), money sunk into renovation improves an asset whose ownership the foreigner cannot fully secure. Confirm ownership standing (cross-ref `--finance` / `compare`) before committing renovation capital.

## Western Europe + EFTA + microstates + UK

### Regional pattern

No EU/EFTA government publishes a single national "renovation €/m²" index — every per-m² cost band here is aggregator/market-derived (⚠️ cross-check), directional and not citable as fact; the only gov-sourced figures are PT's 2026 reference construction value (a tax base, not actual cost), IE grant amounts, and GR building-permit fee bands. Contractor-trust models split between chamber/register regimes (FR RGE + assurance décennale, IT DURC/SOA, DE Handwerkskammer, ES REA, IE CIRI, BE KBO) and guarantee-scheme/authorised-installer regimes where there is no single national builder licence (CH, NO, SE, NL, DK, IS). Permit clocks are predominantly subnational (DE Land Bauordnung ×16, AT 9 provincial Bauordnungen, CH cantonal/communal) with no uniform silence-approval. There is no national €1 programme anywhere: IT's "€1 house" is an auction-from-€1–€3 with refundable deposit + restoration covenant, and the only verified active cheap-house grant is Ireland's Croí Cónaithe Vacant Property Refurbishment Grant.

### Country one-liners

- **FR France** — Cost: standard refurb ~€700–1,300/m², heavy/heritage restoration ~€900–1,600/m²; Paris apartment full reno ~€1,500–2,500/m² (2025, aggregator quelleenergie.fr / lamaisonsaintgobain.fr — ⚠️ cross-check, no single gov index). Contractor trust: **RGE label** (Reconnu Garant de l'Environnement, francerenov.gouv.fr / faire.gouv.fr — required for MaPrimeRénov' aid) + **assurance décennale** mandatory (Code des assurances art. L242-1; verify attestation before any structural works). Fraud: deposit-flight ("acompte" >30% then disappears), uninsured artisan = new owner liable for latent defects, "travail au noir" (undeclared) shifts URSSAF/tax exposure to the homeowner. Quirk: **architecte obligatoire** when works create/total **>150 m² surface de plancher** for an individual (Code de l'urbanisme art. R*431-2; lowered from 170 m² by décret n°2016-1738, eff. 1 March 2017 — SHON/SHOB abolished 2012 and replaced by *surface de plancher*). Overrun: ~10–20% (hidden structure / asbestos pre-1997 — diagnostic amiante mandatory). Permit clock: **Déclaration Préalable (DP)** 1-month silence-vaut-acceptation (2 months if ABF/protected); **Permis de construire** 2 months (house) / 3 months base, +1 month in protected sectors; DAACT closes the works. €1-scheme: no national €1 programme; some communes (e.g. via "Petites Villes de Demain" / ORT operations) sell cheaply with renovation covenants but ad-hoc — verify per commune. **Confidence**: MEDIUM.
- **IT Italy** — Cost: standard refurb (ristrutturazione) ~€500–1,200/m²; full gut with structural/energy work ~€1,000–1,800/m²; heritage/vincolo restoration higher with Soprintendenza oversight (2025, aggregator — ⚠️ no single gov index). Contractor trust: **impresa edile** must hold **DURC** (contributions-regularity certificate) + **SOA attestation** for larger works; trade bodies **ANCE** (builders' association) and **CNA/Confartigianato** for artisans; check **visura camerale** at the Camera di Commercio (registroimprese.it). Fraud: deposit-flight on caparra, subcontractor non-payment cascading to site stop, **bonus-edilizia fraud** (fake invoices for Superbonus/Bonus Ristrutturazione credit — now heavily clamped by Agenzia delle Entrate; new owner can inherit a contested credit), "lavoro in nero". Quirk: **geometra / architetto / ingegnere** mandatory to sign the project & file the SCIA/CILA; structural works need a **direttore dei lavori** + **collaudo statico** (Testo Unico Edilizia DPR 380/2001). Overrun: ~15–25% (vincolo surprises, asbestos/Eternit, façade in centro storico). Permit clock: **CILA** (light works, immediate start on filing), **SCIA** (start on filing, 30-day council check window), **Permesso di Costruire** (major/structural — 90-day instruction, silenzio-assenso in many cases), **CILA-Superbonus** for energy. **€1-house reality**: NOT generally "free €1 with no strings" — most live 2025–2026 schemes start from €1–€3 by **auction**, require a refundable **deposit ~€3,000–€10,000** (some lots quote up to €50,000), a restoration plan/project filed within **~12 months**, and **~3-year** completion or deposit forfeited + property can revert; realistic all-in cost (notary, geometra, full renovation) typically €30,000–€100,000+ (idealista.it 2026; impatria.com 2026 — aggregator, ⚠️ verify the live comune bando). Named 2025–2026 active/relevant comuni: **Sambuca di Sicilia** (Agrigento — relaunched as auction starting ~€1–€3, ~€5,000 deposit, 3-yr completion); **Mussomeli** (Caltanissetta — long-running, large foreign community, ~€5,000 deposit); **Salemi** (Trapani — pioneer 2008, new call expected 2026 — monitor comune.salemi.tp.it); also commonly listed: **Mussomeli, Cammarata, Bivona, Sambuca, Gangi, Regalbuto, Castiglione di Sicilia** plus mainland **Pratola Peligna, Patrica, Caltabellotta, Zungoli, Maenza, Latronico, Fabbriche di Vergemoli, Borgomezzavalle**. **Confidence**: MEDIUM (scheme structure well-documented; per-comune live status changes per bando — aggregator-sourced, idealista.it 2026 + impatria.com 2026).
- **DE Germany** — Cost: complete renovation ~€400–600/m² baseline; realistic full flat reno (e.g. Berlin 70 m²) ~€700–1,100/m²; bathrooms €600–1,900/m², façade €150–480/m² (2025, aggregator rechenportal.de / city-sanierbau.de — ⚠️ cross-check); +15% south/Hamburg, −10–15% east. Contractor trust: **Handwerkskammer** (HWK — Chamber of Crafts; firm must be in the Handwerksrolle for the trade) + trade **Innung** (guild) membership; verify at the regional HWK register. Fraud: deposit (Abschlagszahlung) over-billing, "Schwarzarbeit" (undeclared work — SchwarzArbG, homeowner can be fined and loses warranty/Gewährleistung), abandoned site mid-Rohbau. Quirk: **Bauleiter** (site manager) and an **Entwurfsverfasser/Bauvorlageberechtigter** (qualified architect/engineer) generally required for permit-bearing works; rules are **state (Land) Bauordnung** specific (16 different LBOs). Overrun: ~15–25% (Bestandsschutz surprises, energy-code GEG retrofit triggers). Permit clock: many cosmetic/internal works are **verfahrensfrei** (no permit, per the Land's Bauordnung catalogue); structural/use-change needs **Baugenehmigung** — processing varies widely by Land/Bauamt (no uniform silence-approval; some Länder have a Genehmigungsfiktion after 3 months). €1-scheme: no national €1 programme; municipal "Jung kauft Alt" / KfW-backed refurb subsidies exist but are loans/grants, not €1 sales. **Confidence**: MEDIUM.
- **AT Austria** — Cost: ~€800–1,800/m² full refurb; heritage (Bundesdenkmalamt) higher (2025, data not publicly aggregated in a single gov index — verify with regional builders / WKO). Contractor trust: **Wirtschaftskammer Österreich (WKO)** — regulated trade (reglementiertes Gewerbe Baumeister); check the Gewerberegister / firmen.wko.at; Baumeister concession required for structural works. Fraud: Anzahlung-flight, Pfusch (undeclared work), subcontractor chains. Quirk: **Baumeister/Ziviltechniker (Architekt)** must prepare & sign permit plans for substantial works; Bauleiter on site. Permit clock: **Bauordnung is provincial (9 Länder)**; classes typically Bauanzeige (notification) vs Baubewilligung (permit) vs bewilligungsfrei minor works — timers per Landesbauordnung (no single national silence rule). €1-scheme: none known nationally — `data not publicly available — verify at provincial level`. **Confidence**: LOW.
- **CH Switzerland** — Cost: very high — full renovation ~CHF 1,500–3,500/m² (~€1,600–3,750) (2025, aggregator — ⚠️ cross-check; no federal index, cantonal). Contractor trust: no single federal register — use **SBV/SSE (Schweizerischer Baumeisterverband)**, **suissetec** (building-services), and the cantonal trade registers; architects via **SIA** (Schweizerischer Ingenieur- und Architektenverein). Fraud: deposit-flight, Schwarzarbeit, Bauhandwerkerpfandrecht (craftsman's statutory lien — ZGB Art. 837: an unpaid subcontractor can register a lien on the property even if the owner paid the GC). Quirk: building/planning law is **cantonal + communal**; an SIA architect typically files the Baugesuch. Permit clock: **Baugesuch** with public posting (Auflage, ~20–30 days objection window) then communal/cantonal decision — timelines cantonal, no uniform silence-approval. €1-scheme: none. **Confidence**: LOW–MEDIUM.
- **ES Spain** — Cost: standard reno €600–1,200/m²; full gut with finishes €1,200–2,000/m²; Madrid basic €800–900/m², luxury €1,100+/m²; light cosmetic €200–500/m² (2025, aggregator inmospector.com / studiobyclikalia.es — ⚠️ cross-check). Contractor trust: builder registered with **Registro de Empresas Acreditadas (REA)** for construction; architect/aparejador via the **Colegio Oficial de Arquitectos / Aparejadores (COAM, COAATM etc.)**; technical fees ~8–15% of budget. Fraud: deposit-flight, IVA-free "en negro" cash deals (buyer loses warranty + creates Hacienda exposure), out-of-licence works that block the later Certificado Final de Obra / cédula de habitabilidad. Quirk: **licencia de obra mayor** needs a **proyecto** by an architect + **dirección facultativa** (architect + aparejador on site); **obra menor / declaración responsable** for cosmetic. Overrun: ~15–25% (structural/aluminosis, undeclared prior works). Permit clock: **obra menor / declaración responsable** (often immediate start, council checks after) vs **licencia de obra mayor** (months; many municipalities apply silencio administrativo but case-law restricts positive silence for urbanism — verify with the ayuntamiento). €1-scheme: no national €1 scheme; depopulation ("España vaciada") villages offer cheap/subsidised houses ad-hoc (e.g. private/municipal initiatives) — verify per municipality. **Confidence**: MEDIUM.
- **PT Portugal** — Cost: basic refurb €500–1,000/m², comprehensive/structural €1,500+/m²; Lisbon & Porto 20–40% above inland; gov reference construction value €570/m² for 2026 (up 7% from €532, used for IMI/tax not actual cost — Portaria, observador.pt 2025-12). Contractor trust: builder must hold an **IMPIC alvará** or **título de registo** (impic.pt — verify class; e.g. Class 1 caps works ~€200,000); architect via **Ordem dos Arquitectos**. Fraud: deposit-flight, recibo-verde / "sem factura" cash work (no warranty, IRS/IVA exposure), abandonment, unlicensed works blocking the licença de utilização. Quirk: project signed by registered architect/engineer; **direção/fiscalização de obra** for licensed works. Permit clock: **comunicação prévia** (council has ~20 working days to refuse; otherwise works may start), **licença de obras** (full licensing, longer), small interior works may be **isentas de controlo prévio** (RJUE — Regime Jurídico da Urbanização e Edificação, DL 555/99 as amended). **ARU (Área de Reabilitação Urbana)** zones give IMI/IMT/VAT-6% benefits for reabilitação. €1-scheme: no national €1 scheme; interior depopulation municipalities offer incentives ad-hoc — verify per câmara. **Confidence**: MEDIUM.
- **SE Sweden** — Cost: ~SEK 12,000–25,000/m² (~€1,050–2,200) full renovation (2025, aggregator — ⚠️ cross-check, no single gov index). Contractor trust: no licensing register; use **ID06** (site-access/tax ID), **F-skatt** registration check at Skatteverket, and trade certs (e.g. **Säker Vatten** for plumbing, **behörig elinstallatör** at Elsäkerhetsverket for electrics — mandatory). Fraud: svartjobb (undeclared — but ROT tax deduction incentivises declared labour), deposit-flight, subcontractor chains. Quirk: **kontrollansvarig (KA)** — a certified control-responsible person — required for most permit works (Plan- och bygglagen, PBL). Permit clock: **bygglov** required for many changes; municipality must decide within **10 weeks** (extendable once +10; late decision = reduced fee) by **PBL (Plan- och bygglagen 2010:900)**; **anmälan** (notification) for some interior/structural works; ⚠️ note: a substantially revised permit chapter 9 took effect **1 Dec 2025** (Boverket new guidance) — re-verify timers post-reform. €1-scheme: none. **Confidence**: LOW–MEDIUM.
- **FI Finland** — Cost: ~€800–2,000/m² full renovation (2025, aggregator — ⚠️ cross-check). Contractor trust: no single licence; verify **Y-tunnus** + tax status via **Tilaajavastuu.fi / Luotettava Kumppani**, VAT/prepayment register; electrical work needs an authorised contractor (Tukes register). Fraud: deposit-flight, harmaa talous (grey economy — tax-number obligation on sites mitigates), subcontractor liability. Quirk: a **vastaava työnjohtaja** (responsible site supervisor, approved by the municipal building authority) required for permit works (Maankäyttö- ja rakennuslaki / new Rakentamislaki 2025). Permit clock: **rakennuslupa** (building permit) or lighter **toimenpidelupa**; municipal building authority decision times vary. €1-scheme: none. **Confidence**: LOW.
- **NO Norway** — Cost: very high ~NOK 18,000–35,000/m² (~€1,550–3,000) (2025, aggregator — ⚠️ cross-check). Contractor trust: **sentral godkjenning** (central approval, Direktoratet for byggkvalitet — DiBK) for firms; verify in the DiBK register; electrical via registered installer (DSB Elvirksomhetsregisteret). Fraud: deposit-flight, svart arbeid, "ansvarsrett" misuse. Quirk: works needing permit require firms with **ansvarsrett** (assumed responsibility roles) per Plan- og bygningsloven (pbl); some small works are exempt (unntatt søknadsplikt). Permit clock: **søknad** with/without ansvarsrett — municipality must process simple applications within **3 weeks**, others **12 weeks** (pbl). €1-scheme: none. **Confidence**: LOW–MEDIUM.
- **UK United Kingdom** — Cost: renovation £1,200–2,800/m² nationally; London/SE £2,300–4,100/m²; Midlands £1,600–2,400/m²; North/Wales £1,200–2,100/m² (2025, aggregator homespecialize.com / bhumicalculator RICS-based — ⚠️ cross-check). Contractor trust: **FMB (Federation of Master Builders)**, **TrustMark** (gov-endorsed quality scheme), **RICS** (chartered surveyors), trade competent-person schemes (Gas Safe, NICEIC/NAPIT for electrics, FENSA for windows). Fraud: deposit-flight, "cash, no VAT" jobs (no warranty, no Building Control sign-off — blocks resale/conveyancing), subcontractor non-payment, abandonment mid-project. Quirk: **Party Wall etc. Act 1996** — written notice to adjoining owners (party-structure ≥2 months before start; excavation 1 month); a surveyor award resolves disputes. **Listed Building Consent** is a criminal offence to skip; **Conservation Area** consent for demolition. Building work via **Building Notice** (minor, no detailed plans) or **Full Plans** (approval before start). Overrun: ~15–25% (period-property structure, damp/rot, planning conditions). Permit clock: **Building Regulations** — Building Notice allows quick start; Full Plans decision target **~5–8 weeks**; **planning permission** (if needed) statutory target **8 weeks** (minor) / 13 weeks (major); LBC ~8–13 weeks. Devolved: Scotland uses a **building warrant** (mandatory before work) + Historic Environment Scotland for listed; NI/Wales separate regimes. €1-scheme: no €1 scheme; "Empty Homes" / council-led schemes and historic £1-buy regeneration (e.g. past Liverpool/Stoke "Homesteading") were time-limited and largely closed — verify with the specific council, do not assume open. **Confidence**: MEDIUM.
- **NL Netherlands** — Cost: ~€800–2,000/m² full renovation (2025, aggregator — ⚠️ cross-check). Contractor trust: no compulsory builder licence; use **Bouwgarant** / **Woningborg** guarantee schemes, KvK (Chamber of Commerce) registration, and the **Omgevingswet** quality-assurance system (**Wkb — Wet kwaliteitsborging**, in force 2024 for risk-class 1, introduces an independent **kwaliteitsborger**). Fraud: deposit-flight, zwartwerk, subcontractor chains. Quirk: under the **Omgevingswet** (in force 2024) many works need an **omgevingsvergunning** or only a **melding**; the Wkb kwaliteitsborger signs off newer-build risk class. Permit clock: omgevingsvergunning — **reguliere procedure 8 weeks** (+6 extension), complex **uitgebreide procedure ~26 weeks**; vergunningvrij for many internal/maintenance works (Bbl/Besluit bouwwerken leefomgeving). €1-scheme: none. **Confidence**: LOW–MEDIUM.
- **BE Belgium** — Cost: ~€800–1,800/m² full renovation (2025, aggregator — ⚠️ cross-check). Contractor trust: contractor must be **registered in the Crossroads Bank for Enterprises (KBO/BCE)**; access to the construction profession was deregulated federally (2018) but check VAT, social-security (RSZ/ONSS) and the regional energy-renovation obligations. Fraud: deposit-flight, zwartwerk/travail au noir, the **co-contractor VAT-reverse-charge** + **inhoudingsplicht** (withholding obligation: if the contractor has social/tax debt the client can be held liable — check via the federal "Check inhoudingsplicht" tool). Quirk: an **architect is legally mandatory** (loi du 20 février 1939) for works requiring a permit, including most structural renovations — distinctive vs neighbours. Permit clock: **omgevingsvergunning / permis d'urbanisme** is **regional** (Flanders/Wallonia/Brussels) — Flanders reguliere procedure ~105 days, vereenvoudigde ~60 days; some works are meldingsplichtig or vrijgesteld. **Renovation obligation**: Flanders requires non-residential & (phased) residential EPC upgrade within 5 years of purchase. €1-scheme: none. **Confidence**: MEDIUM.
- **DK Denmark** — Cost: ~DKK 12,000–25,000/m² (~€1,600–3,350) full renovation (2025, aggregator — ⚠️ cross-check). Contractor trust: no general builder licence; **authorised installers mandatory for electrical, plumbing/gas/drainage** (Sikkerhedsstyrelsen authorisation register); use **Byg Garanti** (Dansk Byggeri/Byggebranchens guarantee). Fraud: deposit-flight, sort arbejde (undeclared — but håndværkerfradrag incentivises declared), subcontractor chains. Quirk: building work governed by **Bygningsreglementet (BR18)**; a **certificeret statiker / brandrådgiver** (certified structural/fire consultant) required for higher building classes. Permit clock: **byggetilladelse** via Byg og Miljø portal; municipal processing times vary (no uniform silence-approval); minor works can be exempt. €1-scheme: none. **Confidence**: LOW.
- **IS Iceland** — Cost: very high, ~ISK 250,000–500,000/m² order of magnitude (~€1,700–3,400) (2025, `data not publicly aggregated in a single gov index — verify with Húsnæðis- og mannvirkjastofnun / HMS`). Contractor trust: **master craftsman (meistari) system** — a licensed master tradesman must be registered as responsible for the relevant trade; verify via HMS (Húsnæðis- og mannvirkjastofnun, Building & Housing Authority). Fraud: deposit-flight, undeclared labour, small-market subcontractor risk. Quirk: a registered **byggingarstjóri** (construction manager) + trade masters required for permit works (Mannvirkjalög / building regulations Byggingarreglugerð). Permit clock: **byggingarleyfi** (building permit) via the municipal building committee; timelines local. €1-scheme: none. **Confidence**: LOW.
- **IE Ireland** — Cost: ~€2,000–3,500/m² full renovation; Dublin higher (2025, aggregator — ⚠️ cross-check, scarce gov index). ## 7 universal mitigations

Apply across all jurisdictions, strongest in low-licensing markets:

1. **Get an independent condition survey before the offer, not after.** A structural survey (cross-ref `--surveyor`) is the single highest-leverage spend — it converts an open-ended overrun risk into a priced scope. Non-negotiable on pre-1960, vacant, or revival-scheme stock.
2. **Obtain 2–3 itemised written quotes from registered contractors** (verify registration against the named national body) — not a verbal lump sum. An itemised quote is the only document a dispute can be measured against.
3. **Retain a local design professional pre-offer** (geometra / architecte / arquitecto técnico / quantity surveyor / chartered building surveyor) to validate scope, cost and permit path independently of the contractor.
4. **Fixed-price written contract with milestone payments held in escrow / against staged sign-off**, a penalty/liquidated-damages clause for delay, and documentary proof of the mandatory warranty/insurance (décennale, home-warranty, fidelity bond) before any deposit moves.
5. **Confirm the permit is granted (not merely filed) before mobilising the contractor**, and confirm the post-completion conformity certificate path — unpermitted work blocks resale and voids insurance.
6. **Budget a 25–40% contingency** on heritage / structural / unsurveyed stock; treat the contractor's "fixed" price as the floor, not the expected value.
7. **For revival / nominal-price schemes, model the all-in commitment** (binding bid + completion bond + notary/transfer + full renovation + holding costs) against the finished market value *and* the forfeiture clock — and verify the scheme's current status against the primary municipal source, since many are time-boxed or ENDED.
