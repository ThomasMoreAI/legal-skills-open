# Universal `--connectivity` Section

Foreign-buyer broadband / fixed-internet profile: official address-level fibre availability checker, dominant ISPs with FTTH product line, tariff bands (entry / mid / gigabit) with currency + EUR/USD equivalent, FTTH urban coverage, rural fallback (5G FWA / Starlink / fixed-wireless), Starlink licence status, build-era / strata trap, country-specific quirk. Covers all 126 supported countries.

**Snapshot**: May 2026. Tariffs from carrier price pages May 2026 unless noted; FX-volatile markets (AR, TR, EG, NG, LB) use ~May 2026 rates — bands age in months not years. Starlink licence status moves — verify the [Starlink coverage map](https://www.starlink.com/map) + the national regulator before relying on the registry below for time-critical purchases.

**Anti-hallucination**: every checker URL must resolve to a regulator or government broadband portal; tariff figures must be from a carrier price page (linked) or sourced to a regulator's price-comparator (e.g. CRC CO, OCECPR CY, NMHH HU AMCI, OSIPTEL PE, BTC SE PTS). Where a checker doesn't exist nationally, the entry says `no national checker — query carrier direct`. Where a precise FTTH percentage isn't published as a national statistic, the entry says `~Xx% urban (regulator estimate)` rather than asserting a national figure. Forbidden phrasings — see [`shared/anti-hallucination.md` § Forbidden phrasings](./anti-hallucination.md) for the full replacement table; in this doc tariffs and FTTH figures are sourced or hedged with `~` / `est.` per the contract.

**Disambiguation — `--connectivity` vs `--digital-nomad` vs `--mains`**: `--connectivity` = address-level broadband verification (fibre availability checker, ISP list, tariff bands, rural fallback, Starlink licence, strata trap) for property buyers. `--digital-nomad` = nomad-specific filter (national Speedtest tier, coworking density, time-zone overlap, DNV availability) — uses national medians, not address-level data. `--mains` = utility verification (sewer / water / gas / electricity), occasionally references the dominant ISP. When both run, this section is the authoritative broadband layer.

## Universal contract

For the property's country, return:

1. **Broadband-checker URL** — official primary-source address-level fibre availability map (regulator or state portal). If none exists, say `no national checker — query carrier direct` and link the dominant carrier's coverage tool.
2. **Dominant ISPs** — 3-5 named carriers with FTTH product line + market position (incumbent / cable / altnet / muni / satellite-fallback). Where a wholesale-retail split exists (UK Openreach, IT Open Fiber, NL KPN/Glaspoort, JP NTT FLET'S, AU nbn co, NZ Chorus + LFCs, IS Míla + Ljósleiðarinn), call it out.
3. **Tariff bands (date-stamped 2025-2026)**: entry (~50-100 Mbps) / mid (~300-500 Mbps) / gigabit. Local currency + EUR or USD equivalent. State whether **internet-only or bundle (TV+landline included)**. Add install fee + standard contract length (12mo / 24mo / no-contract).
4. **FTTH urban coverage + rural fallback** — what's available outside the fibre footprint (DSL/VDSL on legacy copper, 5G FWA from which carrier, Starlink, fixed-wireless / WISP, satellite). Rural-vs-urban gap matters: national FTTH percentages frequently mask 30-70 point urban-vs-rural splits.
5. **Starlink licence status (CALENDAR-ACTIONABLE REGISTRY)** — `licensed` (with date + regulator press release) / `pending` (no commercial licence yet) / `banned` / `grey-market` / `not-yet-on-coverage-map`. Status moves; cross-check against the national regulator + Starlink coverage map.
6. **Build-era / strata trap** — country-specific (e.g. older condominium needs HOA / SVJ / borettslag / talohyhtiö / consorcio / asamblea / kat malikleri kurulu / 小区 / mahalla approval for new fibre riser; gated estate has anchor-ISP exclusivity).
7. **Quirk** — one-line country-specific note (e.g. ZA load-shedding → ISP UPS premium; LB Ogero rebuild post-2019 banking collapse + EDL outages mandate generator; CN GFW + IPLC dedicated-international-line $1k-3k+/mo for foreigners; AR FX volatility means USD-equivalents fluctuate monthly; BR fibre cheap relative to GDP per capita; MV Starlink primary-tier on outer atolls).
8. **Confidence**: HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW

## Cross-cutting traps to surface every time

These appear across multiple countries and warrant a buyer-facing flag whenever the user is shopping abroad:

1. **"Fibre available" on the listing ≠ active line at this unit** — the listing/agent may report neighbourhood FTTH availability; the actual flat may need 4-12 weeks for an Openreach / CETIN / Open Fiber / Chorus / nbn co / NetLink / LFC truck-roll. Always (a) check the official regulator address-checker, (b) ask the seller for the **active line ID** (e.g. UK Openreach connection ref, FR référence prise PTO, ES código ICT) before signing.

2. **National FTTH percentages mask rural/urban gaps** — Spain 92% national vs 79% rural; UK 74% national but 90%+ urban with isolated rural gaps; France 91% homes-passed but rural commune averages drop to 50%; Brazil ~70% national but highly skewed to capital + tier-1 cities; Australia mixes FTTP + FTTC + FTTN + HFC + Fixed Wireless + Sky Muster — the same nbn statistic can mean very different real-world experience. Always query at-address, not at-country.

3. **Starlink legality varies — and changes** — currently **banned**: CN, IR, SY, RU, BY, AF, CU, KP. **Pending / no-commercial-licence**: SA, MA, EG, BH, KW, AM, AZ, GE, UZ, TH (frequency-stage), TR, SS, SD, IL (security review), some Caribbean. **Licensed in flight / disputed**: ZA (ICASA 30%-BEE-equity blocker — grey via roaming; expected legal launch 2026-2027), IN (DOT GMPCS pending), JM (explicitly blocked), BZ (PUC retail-blocked, rural/emergency only). **Recently licensed**: AE (TDRA licence 28 Aug 2026), JO (Feb 2026), CO (Apr 2024), NG (May 2023), KE (Jul 2023), UA-D2C (Jun 2025 via Kyivstar), KZ (2024), MY (2024), KH (2024), BS (2023), TW (2024), ID (2024). Status moves fast — re-verify before relying.

4. **5G FWA does not replace fibre for upload-heavy work** — 5G Home / fixed-wireless typically offers strong download (200-500 Mbps consistent) but asymmetric upload (10-50 Mbps). Symmetric-gigabit FTTH or true PTP-ethernet (only common in IS Reykjavík Fibre, KR/JP/SG/HK/TW/TH urban metros, US muni-fibre Chattanooga/Longmont, FR symmetric-gigabit add-on tiers) is required for video conferencing at scale, large-file syncs, screen-share. Listings that say "1 Gbps" almost always mean 1 Gbps DL / 50-200 Mbps UL.

5. **Strata / HOA / riser approval can stall fibre install for months** — older apartment blocks frequently need general-assembly approval before a new fibre riser is run. **Strong individual right-to-fibre laws** (single owner can install over assembly objection): FR (Loi Pintat / CPCE L.34-8-3), IT (D.Lgs. 259/2003 Art. 91 + Law 12/2019), PT (DL 123/2009), ES (Ley 9/2014). **Assembly approval routinely required**: DE Mietshaus pre-1995, AT WEG Eigentümerversammlung, NL VvE majority, BE syndic AG, CH Stockwerkeigentum Verwaltung, SE BRF / DK ejerforening / FI taloyhtiö (often pre-locked by bulk contract), CZ panelák SVJ, HU társasház közgyűlés, RU/socialist-era ОСББ across CEE/Balkans, IL Israeli condominium board, IN society/RWA AGM, ZA body corporate, AU Owners Corporation, JP マンション 管理組合, KR 아파트 단지. Allow 4-12 weeks if assembly approval is required.

6. **Municipal / state monopolies in microstates and small markets** — single licensed operator, no negotiation, often ties to expat residency:
   - **MC** Monaco Telecom (Iliad-controlled) — sole licensed operator
   - **AD** Andorra Telecom (state STA) — sole licensed operator
   - **LI** FL1 (Telecom Liechtenstein) — effective monopoly
   - **SM** TIM San Marino — sole licensed fibre operator
   - **MV** Dhiraagu / Ooredoo Maldives — duopoly, Starlink as 4th player
   - **UY** Antel (state) — historic monopoly, partially liberalised post-2023
   - **EC** CNT (state) — universal-service obligation, often sole rural option
   - **CV** CVTelecom (former state monopoly) — limited competition
   - **MO** CTM — near-monopoly, regulated price reductions
   - **LB** Ogero (state) — wholesale + retail incumbent post-banking-collapse
   - **CN** Telecom / Unicom / Mobile (state trio) — no private alternative
   - **EG** WE / Telecom Egypt (~80% subs) — dominant state ISP
   - **BZ** BTL Digi (state) — effective fibre monopoly
   
   Microstate buyers should price the lack of competition into TCO; expect 10-30% premium over comparable regional tariffs and longer install-windows.

7. **Contract length traps + bundle pricing** — 24-month locks are EU-norm (DE Telekom MagentaZuhause 24mo standard, AT A1 24mo, IT TIM 24mo, PT MEO/NOS 24mo with steep early-exit fees, RO/HU/SK 24mo). UK now **anti-CPI-rise** post-Ofcom 2024 reform but step-rises (BT +£4 every 31 March) replaced the old CPI+3.9%. ES Digi / PT Digi / UK altnets often **no-permanencia / no-contract**. Promo "first-12-months at €X" tariffs nearly double at month 13 — read the steady-state price. Bundle-with-TV is the published price in many markets — strip out the TV cost when comparing internet-only (where the carrier publishes it). 24-month contract subsidies are often clawed back on early exit + landlord-departure clauses are not standard.

## Regional patterns

### Western Europe + Microstates (FR, DE, UK, IT, ES, NL, BE, CH, AT, IE, PT, LU, MC, AD, LI, SM, MT)

Western Europe splits into (a) high-FTTH + strong consumer-protection markets (FR, ES, PT, IE, NL, LU, MT) where gigabit is mainstream and switching is friction-light; (b) cable-vs-fibre transition markets (DE, AT, BE, CH, IT) where take-up lags coverage and 24mo locks dominate; (c) UK as wholesale-retail-Openreach-style market with strongest individual right-to-fibre laws and many altnets; and (d) microstate monopolies (MC, AD, LI, SM) where one licensed operator covers >95% FTTH at 10-30% premium. ### North America + Oceania + Latin America + Caribbean (US, CA, MX, AU, NZ, VU, BR, AR, CL, CO, PE, EC, UY, PA, CR, DO, PY, BB, TT, BZ, JM, BS, KY, BM, TC, VG, KN, AG, LC, GD)
