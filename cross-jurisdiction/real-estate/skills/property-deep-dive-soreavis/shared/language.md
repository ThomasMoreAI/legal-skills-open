# Universal `--language` Section

Foreign-buyer language requirements: deed-language rule, sworn-translator-at-signing mandate, POA / apostille chain, mandatory diagnostic-document language, sworn-translation cost band. Covers all 126 supported countries.

**Snapshot**: May 2026 (VN / TH / AE re-verified 2026-09-17). Apostille statuses move — verify HCCH status table for any time-critical POA execution. Two 2025-2026 status changes already in flight: **VN in force since 11 Sep 2026 (acceded 31 Dec 2025) — but Germany, Austria and Czechia objected (Art. 12(2)), so NO apostille between VN and DE/AT/CZ (consular legalisation persists there)**; **TH deposited accession 30 Jun 2026, in force 28 Feb 2027 (Art. 12(2) objection window closes ~30 Dec 2026 — six months from HCCH notification, entry into force 60 days later)**. Recent past changes still relevant: **CN in force 7 Nov 2023**, **AE not a member** (still requires full MOFAIC legalisation chain), **SA in force 7 Dec 2022**, **ID in force 4 Jun 2022**, **CA acceded 11 Jan 2024**, **RW in force 5 Jun 2024**, **SG in force 16 Jan 2021**, **MA in force 14 Aug 2016**.

**Anti-hallucination**: every statutory citation should resolve to the named act/article (FR Loi 25 ventôse XI; IT L. 89/1913 Art. 54-55; DE § 5 + § 16 BeurkG; AT § 62-63 NO + SDG 1975; NL § 42 Wna + Wbtv 2009; BE Loi 10 avril 2014; PL Prawo o notariacie 1991 Art. 92; CZ Zákon 358/1992 Sb.; HU Notary Position Law 30/2004; ID UU 30/2004 + UU 24/2009 + 2018 CC ruling; IL Notaries Law 5736-1976; CA QC Charter of the French Language amended by Bill 96 eff. 1 Sept 2022; US CA Civ. Code § 1632, FL Stat. § 695.26; CR DE 975/2017; CO Resolución 10547/2018 + 1959/2020). Cost bands are mid-2026 industry-reported / association-published — label `(industry-reported)` or `(statutory tariff)` when known.

**Disambiguation — `--language` vs `--notary` vs `--visa`**: `--language` = deed-language rule + sworn-translator regime + POA-translation chain for foreign buyers. `--notary` = transaction-completion process (days from offer to deed, closing costs, notary fee). `--visa` = residency/RBI/CBI programs. Some POA-chain content overlaps `--notary`; this section focuses on the **language layer** (deed-language, sworn translator, document-translation chain).

## Universal contract

For the property's country, return:

1. **Closing-deed language** — what language must the deed / Sale & Purchase Agreement / escritura / Tapu / sertifikat / matricula be in? Bilingual permitted? Which version controls?
2. **Sworn interpreter at signing** — mandatory if buyer doesn't speak local language? On demand? Not used (common-law)?
3. **POA / apostille chain** — Hague Apostille member status; chain from foreign-language POA → apostille / consular legalisation → sworn translation → local notary acceptance
4. **Mandatory diagnostic / disclosure document language** — DPE (FR), APE (IT), CEE (PT), Energieausweis (DE/AT), EPC (UK/IE/MT/CY), CPE (LU), BER (IE), GEAK (CH), Energielabel (NL), PEB / EPC region (BE), PEA (GR/CY) — local-language-only or bilingual standard?
5. **Sworn-translation cost band** — typical hourly / per-page cost (industry-reported or statutory tariff)
6. **Confidence**: HIGH (statutory + clear advisory + register URL) / MEDIUM (statutory but practice varies) / LOW (no clear regulation or thin documentation)

## Cross-cutting traps to surface every time

These appear across multiple countries and warrant a buyer-facing flag whenever the user is shopping abroad:

1. **Hague Apostille membership confusion** — the chain differs dramatically between member and non-member states. **Non-members in scope** (still require full consular legalisation): **AE, EG, JO, KW, LB, QA** (MENA); **NG, KE, GH** (SSA); **CN-Hong Kong (apostille via PRC since 7 Nov 2023; HK retains own apostille stream)**, **TW** (political-status non-party — TECO/TECRO authentication), **KH, LK, MV, MY, TH (until 28 Feb 2027)** (Asia); VN is a member since 11 Sep 2026 — except vs DE / AT / CZ, whose Art. 12(2) objections keep the consular chain pairwise. For non-member-origin POAs going to a member country, or vice-versa, the chain is: notarisation → origin MFA → destination Embassy abroad → destination MFA at home → sworn translation. Plan **4-8 weeks lead time**. Always verify current status at [HCCH status table](https://www.hcch.net/en/instruments/conventions/status-table/?cid=41).

2. **"Sworn translator" means different things in different jurisdictions** — three distinct regimes:
   - **Statutory sworn-translator profession** (FR interprète assermenté, DE vereidigter Dolmetscher, AT allgemein beeideter Dolmetscher, IT interprete giurato, ES intérprete jurado, NL beëdigde tolk, BE traducteur juré, GR via 2021 register, CY post-2019, PL tłumacz przysięgły, EE vandetõlk, RO traducător autorizat, ES intérprete jurado, ID penerjemah tersumpah, BR tradutor público juramentado, AR traductor público matriculado, CO via Resolución 1959/2020 chain) — a regulated profession with state register
   - **Court-admitted translator** (ZA High Court rules, JP shihō shoshi vouching, KR via 법원행정처, TH Land-Office-recognised, IN State High Court roster) — admission by the court, not a separate licence
   - **No sworn profession; notary-public-certified translation** (IL Notaries Law 5736-1976, US, UK, IE, AU, NZ, NG, KE, GH, BS, BB, BZ, JM) — the notary public attests to translation accuracy, not a separately-sworn translator. Common-law jurisdictions almost universally fall into this third category.

3. **Bilingual deeds — the local-language version always controls** — confirmed in: IT (Notarial Law); DE (BeurkG); ID (UU 24/2009 + 2018 CC ruling — Indonesian text controls despite bilingual permission); MX (escritura protocolised version); HU (Hungarian text governs in lawyer-bilingual practice). The buyer's English-friendly side is courtesy, not legally controlling. Read the local-language version (or have it read by an independent translator NOT supplied by the seller).

4. **Diagnostic documents are universally local-language-only** — DPE, APE, CEE, Energieausweis, EPC, BER, GEAK, Energielabel, PEB regions, PEA, ΠΕΑ, Cédula de Habitabilidad, Caderneta Predial, Visura Catastale, Nota Simple, Folio extract, Tax Declaration, Title Deed search — all issued in the local language. Buyer pays for own translation. No country in scope requires a bilingual diagnostic document at issuance.

5. **State / regional variation breaks single-country assumptions** — **CA Quebec Bill 96** (effective 1 Sept 2022) requires French for Land Register filings, with English-only escape clause requiring **all parties' written unanimous consent** for residential <5 dwellings; rest of Canada is English. **US California Civ. Code § 1632** requires translation of negotiated mortgage terms in Spanish/Chinese/Tagalog/Vietnamese/Korean if trade conducted in those languages. **CH cantonal**: deed language follows canton (DE / FR / IT / Romansh per location). **BE language regions**: Dutch in Vlaanderen, French in Wallonie, German in 9 Ostbelgien communes, FR/NL choice in Bruxelles. **IN state language**: Tamil Nadu Tamil, Karnataka Kannada, Maharashtra Marathi (English universal at sub-registrar). **ES regional**: Catalan, Euskera, Galego, Valenciano permitted in their CCAA. Verify the specific state/canton/region's rule before assuming.

6. **The "consular POA bypass"** — most civil-law countries' consulates abroad can directly draft the property POA in the local language (poder notarial / procuração / Vollmacht / procura / pełnomocnictwo / πληρεξούσιο / vekâlet / wakala). This bypasses BOTH the apostille step AND the sworn-translation step. Confirmed available at: FR, IT, ES, PT, DE, AT, NL, BE, LU, GR, CY, PL, CZ, MX, BR, AR, CO, CL, PE, TR, IL. Check availability at the destination country's consulate in your country of residence — often the cheapest and fastest path for foreign buyers.

7. **Sworn-translator-cost band varies 10× across the region** — statutory-tariff jurisdictions (DE JVEG €1.95-4.00/line; PL regulated 1,125-char page; AT GebAG; NO most expensive at NOK 1,200-2,500/page ≈ €100-220) vs market-rate (most others). Common-law jurisdictions lowest (US/UK/AU/NZ ~$50-150/page market); high-friction non-Hague chains (UAE, Egypt, Lebanon) most expensive end-to-end due to multi-step legalisation (~$1,500-3,500 total per POA chain).

## Regional patterns

### Western Europe (FR, IT, ES, PT, DE, AT, CH, NL, BE, LU, IE, UK, GR, MT, CY)

Western Europe splits cleanly into civil-law sworn-translator-statutory jurisdictions (FR, IT, ES, DE, AT, CH, NL, BE, LU, GR via post-2021 register, CY post-2019) and common-law solicitor-handled (UK, IE) plus English-bilingual-pragmatic (MT, CY). PT is a hybrid — no central sworn-translator register but DL 237/2001 requires notarial certification of translation accuracy. All 15 countries are Hague Apostille members. Consular-POA bypass available throughout.

- **FR**: Acte authentique in French only per **Article 73 Loi du 25 ventôse an XI** + **Article L. 111-2 Code de l'organisation judiciaire**. Mandatory **interprète assermenté** from Cour d'appel list ([experts-judiciaires.org](https://www.experts-judiciaires.org/), [courdecassation.fr](https://www.courdecassation.fr/)) if buyer doesn't understand French — notary refuses to proceed otherwise. POA → apostille → sworn translation → notaire (FR-consulate procuration authentique bypasses translation). DPE/ERP/amiante/plomb/électricité/gaz/assainissement French-only (Art. L. 271-4 CCH). **Cost**: ~€60-150/hour at acte; ~€40-80/page written ([SFT](https://www.sft.fr/)) (industry-reported). **Confidence**: HIGH.
- **IT**: Rogito in Italian only per **Articles 54-55 Notarial Law (L. 89/1913)** + Codice Civile. Bilingual permitted; Italian text controls. **Interprete giurato** from Tribunale Albo CTU + **two instrumental witnesses** mandatory if buyer doesn't understand Italian. POA → apostille → sworn translation (asseverata in Tribunale) → notaio (IT-consulate POA bypasses). APE / conformità impianti / visura catastale / ipotecaria all Italian-only. **Cost**: ~€50-120/hour interpretation; ~€30-50/page asseverazione + €16 marca da bollo every 4 pages ([AITI](https://aiti.org/)). **Confidence**: HIGH.
- **ES**: Escritura pública in Spanish per **Ley del Notariado 1862** + **Reglamento Notarial Art. 148-150**. Catalonia / País Vasco / Galicia / Valencia / Balearic Islands allow regional co-official languages. Mandatory **intérprete jurado** from MAEC register ([exteriores.gob.es](https://www.exteriores.gob.es/es/ServiciosAlCiudadano/Paginas/Traductores-Interpretes-Jurados.aspx)). POA → apostille → sworn translation → notario (ES-consulate poder notarial bypasses). CEE / nota simple / IBI / Cédula de Habitabilidad / ITE Spanish + regional. **Cost**: ~€30-80/hour; ~€30-60/page ([APTIJ](https://www.aptij.es/)). **Confidence**: HIGH.
- **PT**: Escritura in Portuguese per **Código do Notariado (DL 207/95)** via **Casa Pronta** one-stop or notário. **No central sworn-translator register** — translations certified by notário/advogado/solicitador/Câmara/consulado per **DL 237/2001 Art. 38**. POA → apostille → notarial certification of translation in PT → notário/Casa Pronta (PT-consulate procuração bypasses). Certificado Energético (CE) by ADENE / caderneta predial urbana / certidão de teor predial / Ficha Técnica da Habitação Portuguese-only. **Cost**: ~€25-60/page certified; ~€40-90/hour interpretation ([APT](https://www.apt.pt/)). **Confidence**: MEDIUM (no central sworn register; certification model differs).
- **DE**: Beurkundung in German per **§ 5 BeurkG**. Bilingual permitted; German controls. **Mandatory** under **§ 16 BeurkG** if party doesn't sufficiently understand German — **allgemein beeidigter Dolmetscher** sworn by Land OLG; notary records oath. Federal database: [justiz-dolmetscher.de](https://www.justiz-dolmetscher.de/). Simultaneous oral translation of every clause; written Übersetzung commonly attached. POA → apostille → certified translation by öffentlich bestellter Übersetzer → notarisation (DE-consulate Vollmacht bypasses). Energieausweis (GEG 2024) / Grundbuchauszug / Flurkarte / Teilungserklärung / Wohngeldabrechnung German-only. **Cost**: **JVEG statutory tariff** €1.95/line (55 chars) for court work, up to €4.00 difficult ([JVEG § 11](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/jveg/__11.html)); notary-level interpretation ~€70-120/hour ([BDÜ](https://bdue.de/)). **Confidence**: HIGH.
- **AT**: Notarial deed in German per **§ 62 Notariatsordnung**. Bilingual permitted; German controls. **Allgemein beeideter und gerichtlich zertifizierter Dolmetscher** under **SDG 1975** mandatory if buyer doesn't understand German. Central register [justizdolmetscher.at](https://justizonline.gv.at/jop/web/exl-suche/do) / ÖVGD. POA → apostille → certified translation → notarial acceptance (AT-consulate Vollmacht bypasses). Energieausweis (EAVG 2012) / Grundbuchauszug / Flächenwidmungsplan / Wohnungseigentumsvertrag German-only. **Cost**: **GebAG** statutory tariff for court work; private ~€1.60-2.50/line written, ~€80-130/hour interpreting ([ÖVGD](https://www.gerichtsdolmetscher.at/)). **Confidence**: HIGH.
- **CH**: Cantonal deed language — DE in 19 cantons, FR in Genève/Vaud/Neuchâtel/Jura, IT in Ticino, Romansh in parts of Graubünden. Each canton maintains its own list of **traducteurs-jurés / vereidigte Übersetzer** (e.g., [ge.ch](https://ge.ch/), [zh.ch](https://www.zh.ch/)). No federal register. POA → apostille → cantonal-language sworn translation → notary (CH-consulate POA bypasses). GEAK/CECB / extrait du registre foncier / règlement de copropriété cantonal-language. **Cost**: ~CHF 100-220/hour interpretation; ~CHF 3-6/line written ([ASTTI](https://www.astti.ch/)). **Confidence**: HIGH (cantonal variation flagged).
- **NL**: Notarial deed default Dutch; **§ 42 Wet op het notarisambt** allows **Dutch / English / French / German** at parties' request if notaris speaks the language. **Beëdigde tolk** from **Wbtv register** ([bureauwbtv.nl](https://www.bureauwbtv.nl/), Wet beëdigde tolken en vertalers 2009) mandatory if no shared language. POA → apostille → sworn translation → notaris (NL-consulate volmacht bypasses). Energielabel (RVO) / Kadaster uittreksel / splitsingsakte / VvE-stukken Dutch-only. **Cost**: ~€80-130/hour interpreting; ~€0.18-0.30/word written ([NGTV](https://ngtv.nl/)). **Confidence**: HIGH.
- **BE**: Region-dependent per **Loi du 18 juillet 1966** — Dutch in Vlaanderen, French in Wallonie, German in 9 Ostbelgien communes, FR/NL choice in Bruxelles. **Traducteur-interprète juré / beëdigd vertaler-tolk** from federal register under **Loi du 10 avril 2014** (operational 2019), maintained by SPF Justice / FOD Justitie ([justice.belgium.be](https://justice.belgium.be/)). POA → apostille → sworn translation → notaire/notaris (BE-consulate POA bypasses). PEB Wallonie / EPC Vlaanderen / Certificat PEB Bruxelles / attestation citerne mazout / état du sol / bodemattest region-language. **Cost**: ~€60-110/hour interpretation; ~€35-55/page written ([CBTI-BKVT](https://www.cbti-bkvt.org/)). **Confidence**: HIGH.
- **LU**: French / German / Luxembourgish per **Loi du 9 décembre 1976 Art. 7** (FR dominant in property practice). **Traducteur-interprète assermenté** from MoJ list ([mj.gouvernement.lu](https://mj.gouvernement.lu/fr/service-citoyens/expert-judiciaire/liste-experts-traducteurs.html)). POA → apostille → sworn translation into FR/DE → notaire (LU-consulate POA bypasses). CPE per loi 1993 / RGD 2007 / extrait cadastral / règlement de copropriété FR/DE. **Cost**: ~€80-130/hour; ~€40-65/page (industry-reported). **Confidence**: HIGH.
- **IE**: English; common-law solicitor handles transactions (no civil-law notary system). Irish (Gaeilge) is first official language but English universal. **No statutory sworn-translator regime** — solicitor + ITIA-Certified Legal Translator panel ([translatorsassociation.ie](https://www.translatorsassociation.ie/)); court interpretation via Courts Service procurement; NRPSI ([nrpsi.org.uk](https://www.nrpsi.org.uk/)) for court use. POA → apostille → English certified translation → solicitor (IE-embassy POA accepted directly). BER (SEAI) / PRA Folio English/Irish. **Cost**: ~€50-100/hour interpreting; ~€0.12-0.20/word certified (ITIA). **Confidence**: MEDIUM (no statutory sworn at conveyancing).
- **UK**: English (Wales: Welsh permitted under Welsh Language Measure 2011 but English universal). Solicitor / licensed conveyancer handles. **No statutory sworn-translator at conveyancing** — recommended via **CIOL** ([ciol.org.uk](https://www.ciol.org.uk/)) or **ITI** ([iti.org.uk](https://www.iti.org.uk/)); NRPSI for court only. POA → apostille → certified English translation (statement of truth) → solicitor (UK-notary-public POA universally accepted). EPC (EPB Regs 2012) / HM Land Registry title plan / TA6 PIF / Local Search English-only. **Cost**: ~£60-120/hour interpreting; ~£0.10-0.18/word certified (ITI). **Confidence**: MEDIUM (no statutory sworn at conveyancing).
- **GR**: Greek only per **Notarial Code (L. 2830/2000) Art. 9**. **Ορκωτός μεταφραστής/διερμηνέας** — **MFA Translation Service abolished 1 Sept 2021 (L. 4781/2021)**; now translations done by **certified lawyers** under Code of Lawyers Art. 36 OR by **registered sworn translators** in new MFA Register. Info at [mfa.gr](https://www.mfa.gr/) / [pio.gov.gr](https://metafraseis.services.gov.gr/). POA → apostille → translation by lawyer or sworn translator → notarial acceptance (GR-consulate πληρεξούσιο bypasses). ΠΕΑ / τοπογραφικό διάγραμμα / οικοδομική άδεια / ΕΝΦΙΑ / dilosi N.4495/2017 Greek-only. **Cost**: ~€40-100/hour interpretation; ~€25-60/page translation ([PEM](https://www.pem.gr/)). **Confidence**: MEDIUM (post-2021 transitional regime).
- **MT**: Maltese / English bilingual per **Constitution Art. 5** — most konvenju + kuntratt finali in **English** in expat practice. Sworn interpreter on demand via Court Services Agency translator panel — not statutorily mandatory given English option. POA → apostille → English translation if not in EN/MT → notary (international POAs accepted in English directly). EPC under LN 47/2018 / Land Registry / Public Registry / PA compliance EN/MT. **Cost**: ~€50-90/hour; ~€30-50/page (industry-reported). **Confidence**: MEDIUM (English access reduces formalism).
- **CY**: Greek primary per **Constitution Art. 3** + **Sale of Land (Specific Performance) Law Cap. 232**; **English bilingual common** in expat market — SPAs routinely English with Greek summary lodged at Land Registry. **Registered sworn translator** under **Law 45(I)/2019** (replaced old PIO ad-hoc system) — Council for Registration of Sworn Translators at [pio.gov.cy](https://www.pio.gov.cy/en/). POA → apostille → sworn translation into Greek → lawyer/Land Registry (CY-consulate POA bypasses). EPC (Law 142(I)/2006) / Title Deed / Έρευνα / Άδεια Οικοδομής Greek/English. **Cost**: ~€40-80/hour; ~€25-50/page (post-Law-45(I)/2019 register). **Confidence**: HIGH (post-2019 statutory regime + English bilingual practice).

### CEE / Baltics / Balkans (CZ, SK, PL, HU, RO, BG, SI, EE, LV, LT, HR, RS, ME, BA, MK, AL, MD)

Densest sworn-translator-register region after Western Europe. CZ/SK/PL/SI/HR/RS/AL all run statutory sworn-translator regimes; HU has unique post-2024 OFFI→MKIFK transition; BG/LV/MD operate notary-attestation-of-translator-signature framework rather than a true sworn profession. EE has post-2020 vandetõlk regime ([justdigi.ee](https://www.justdigi.ee/en/legal-services/sworn-translators)). All 17 countries are Hague Apostille members.

- **CZ**: Notářský zápis in Czech per **§ 73 Notarial Code (Zákon č. 358/1992 Sb.)**. **Soudní tlumočník** mandatory if buyer doesn't understand Czech ([kstcr.cz](https://www.kstcr.cz/), Komora soudních tlumočníků a soudních překladatelů; reformed by Zákon č. 354/2019 Sb.). POA → apostille → Czech sworn translation → notář. **Cost**: ~CZK 450-650 per 1,800-char page; legal docs CZK 900-1,000. **Confidence**: HIGH.
- **SK**: Notárska zápisnica in Slovak per **Notarial Code 323/1992**. Sworn interpreter from Ministry of Justice register (~1,341 translators, ~1,240 interpreters). POA → apostille → sworn Slovak translation. **Cost**: ~€20-30 per 1,800-char page; interpreter EUR 40-80/hour. **Confidence**: HIGH.
- **PL**: Akt notarialny in Polish per **Art. 2 § 3 Prawo o notariacie** (Ustawa z 14 lutego 1991, Dz.U. 1991 nr 22 poz. 91) — a foreign-language version may be added only on a party's request, via the notary's own certified language competence or a **tłumacz przysięgły** ([ISAP consolidated text](https://isap.sejm.gov.pl/isap.nsf/download.xsp/WDU19910220091/U/D19910091Lj.pdf), 2026 consolidation). Where a party does not know Polish and no translation is attached, **Art. 87 § 1 pkt 1** obliges the notary to translate the deed personally or through a translator; **Art. 92** governs deed contents, not language. **Tłumacz przysięgły** from Ministry of Justice register ([arch-bip.ms.gov.pl](https://arch-bip.ms.gov.pl/pl/rejestry-i-ewidencje/tlumacze-przysiegli/)). POA → apostille → sworn Polish translation. **Cost**: regulated **1,125-char page**; market PLN 40-70/page; statutory tariff for court work. **Confidence**: HIGH.
- **HU**: Közokirat or **lawyer-countersigned ügyvéd által ellenjegyzett okirat** in Hungarian. **OFFI merged into MKIFK Hungarian Gazette Publishing and Legal Translation Centre Ltd 16 Oct 2024** — formal monopoly on certified state-facing translations persists; szakfordító for business. Bilingual EN/HU or DE/HU lawyer-drafted contracts standard practice; Hungarian text governs. POA → apostille → Hungarian translation. **Cost**: ~HUF 4-8 per character (legacy OFFI tariff); lawyer-bilingual often bundled. **Confidence**: MEDIUM (post-merger transition + bilingual lawyer drafting substitutes).
- **RO**: Act notarial in Romanian. **Traducător autorizat** from Ministry of Justice list ([just.ro](https://www.just.ro/)) mandatory — signature-presence at notary. POA → apostille → sworn Romanian translation, signature legalised by Romanian notary. **Cost**: ~€6-12/page (~RON 30-60). **Confidence**: HIGH.
- **BG**: Notarialen akt in Bulgarian (Cyrillic). **No fully integrated EU-style sworn-translator register** — translators listed by **Ministry of Foreign Affairs Consular Relations Directorate**, signature certified by Bulgarian notary public on each translation. Interpreter for buyer comprehension. POA → apostille → MFA-listed translator + notary signature certification. **Cost**: ~€10-20/page; interpreter ~€50-100/deed. **Confidence**: MEDIUM (regulator framework via MFA + notary chain).
- **SI**: Notarski zapis in Slovenian. **Sodni tolmač** from Ministry of Justice register (twice-yearly public call) mandatory. POA → apostille → sodno overjeni prevod. **Cost**: ~€25-45 per 1,500-char page; interpretation ~€80-150/deed. **Confidence**: HIGH.
- **EE**: Notarial deed primarily Estonian (notary may use other language with sufficient command). Since **1 Jan 2020**, only **vandetõlk** (Vandetõlgi seadus / Sworn Translators Act) produces official translations. Register at [justdigi.ee](https://www.justdigi.ee/en/legal-services/sworn-translators). Vandetõlk uniquely permitted to share office with notary. POA → apostille. **Cost**: ~€30-60/page. **Confidence**: HIGH.
- **LV**: Notarial in Latvian. **No statutory sworn-translator status** — `zvērināts tulks` is colloquial. Translator declares accuracy + signs; notary certifies translator's signature. POA → apostille. **Cost**: ~€20-40/page + notary signature-cert fee. **Confidence**: MEDIUM (framework real but "sworn" terminology misleading).
- **LT**: Notarial in Lithuanian — notaries cannot legally execute property transactions in any other language. **Prisiekusysis vertėjas** sworn by regional court mandatory. POA → apostille → sworn Lithuanian translation. **Cost**: per-process EUR 200-600; per-page sworn EUR 20-40. **Confidence**: HIGH.
- **HR**: Javnobilježnička isprava in Croatian (Latin script). **Sudski tumač** from Ministry of Justice register ([hjk.hr](https://www.hjk.hr/) Croatian Notaries Chamber for notarial side). POA → apostille → sudski tumač translation; third-country buyers also need MoJ reciprocity consent before recording. **Cost**: ~€25-45 per 1,500-char page; interpretation ~€100-200/deed. **Confidence**: HIGH.
- **RS**: Javnobeležnička isprava in Serbian (Cyrillic primary; Latin co-official). **Stalni sudski tumač / prevodilac** from official register **mandatory** — absence is direct ground for invalidating transaction. Acquaintance/employee/relative translators expressly not accepted. POA → apostille → sworn Serbian translation. **Cost**: ~€15-35/page; interpretation ~€80-150/deed. **Confidence**: HIGH.
- **ME**: Notarial deed in Montenegrin. **Sudski tumač** mandatory; translator must also sign all sales contracts. POA → apostille → sworn translation; most non-resident purchases via apostilled POA. **Cost**: ~€50/hour interpretation; per-deed translator fees ~€150-200; per-page ~€15-30. **Confidence**: HIGH.
- **BA**: Notarial deed in local language. Script practice differs by entity — **FBiH/Brčko Latin predominant; RS Cyrillic predominant** (both official). Notarial regime since 2007; lawyers may also draft contracts. **Sudski tumač** from entity register (FBiH Federal MoJ / RS MoJ) required. POA → apostille → sworn translation for relevant entity. **Cost**: ~€15-30/page; interpretation ~€80-150/deed. **Confidence**: MEDIUM (split entity framework).

### Sub-Saharan Africa (ZA, NG, KE, MU, CV, SC, GH, RW, NA, BW)

Common-law dominant (ZA, NG, KE, GH) with minimal sworn-translator infrastructure; Lusophone (CV) follows PT-tradition; Francophone-influenced (MU, RW) bilingual; SC English-French-Creole mix. **Apostille members**: ZA, MU, CV, SC, RW (newest, in force **5 Jun 2024**); **non-members**: NG, KE, GH.

- **ZA**: English / Afrikaans deed at Deeds Office (11 official languages constitutional but deed in EN/AF). **High Court-admitted sworn translator** under Rules of Court — SATI accreditation alone NOT statutory. [hcsti.org.za](https://www.hcsti.org.za/). POA → apostille (Hague since 1995). **Cost**: ~ZAR 200-500/page. **Confidence**: HIGH.
- **NG**: English federal lingua franca; Lagos / FCT Abuja / Rivers Land Registries English. **No statutory sworn-translator** — translations validated by **affidavit before Commissioner for Oaths or notary public**. **NOT Hague Apostille member**. Consular chain via Nigeria MFA Abuja. **Cost**: certified translation + affidavit ~NGN 30-80k for multi-page POA. **Confidence**: MEDIUM (state-level variation).
- **KE**: English + Swahili co-official; Lands Office English. **Certified translator + advocate/notary affidavit** (no formal sworn profession). **NOT Hague Apostille member**. Consular chain via Kenya MFA. **Cost**: ~KES 5-15k/document. **Confidence**: MEDIUM.
- **MU**: English statutory + French drafting practice (most acte authentique in French) bilingual at notaire. Supreme Court / Mauritius Bar sworn translator list for non-EN/FR. POA → apostille (Hague since 20 Dec 1968 by UK extension). **Cost**: ~MUR 500-1,500/page. **Confidence**: HIGH.
- **CV**: Portuguese at Conservatória do Registo Predial. **Tradutor ajuramentado** from Tribunal de Comarca lists; Ministério da Justiça oversight. POA → apostille (Hague since 13 Feb 2010). **Cost**: ~CVE 1,500-3,500/page (~€14-32). **Confidence**: MEDIUM.
- **SC**: English at Land Registry; French + Seychellois Creole also official. Supreme Court interpreter/translator list (limited domestic supply — many use MU or RSA translators). POA → apostille (Hague since 30 Mar 1979). **Cost**: ~SCR 300-1,000/page. **Confidence**: MEDIUM.
- **GH**: English at Lands Commission. **No statutory sworn-translator** — certified translation + Commissioner of Oaths affidavit. **NOT Hague Apostille member**. Consular chain via Ghana MFA Accra. **Cost**: ~GHS 200-800/document. **Confidence**: MEDIUM.
- **RW**: Three official: Kinyarwanda, English, French. RLMUA deeds in English/French foreign-buyer practice. Sworn translator from High Court list. POA → apostille (Hague **in force 5 Jun 2024** — newest among SSA). **Cost**: ~RWF 8-20k/page. **Confidence**: HIGH.
- **NA**: **English** deed/conveyancing — English is the sole official language and **"legal, deed, and conveyancing documentation is in English"** per the playbook; **Roman-Dutch common-law** lineage shared with ZA (so an English-speaking buyer needs no sworn translation). **No statutory sworn-translator regime documented** (no SATI/High-Court-style register surfaced this pass — unlike ZA); conveyancing handled by an **admitted conveyancer**, not a civil-law notary. Operative deeds law is the **Deeds Registries Act 47 of 1937** (NOT the 2015 Act — set aside by the High Court in 2021, never validly commenced). POA → **Hague Apostille status not confirmed this pass — `verify at` [HCCH status table](https://www.hcch.net/en/instruments/conventions/status-table/?cid=41)** → conveyancer-certified translation if a non-English document arises. Title deed + Surveyor-General diagram English-only. **Cost**: conveyancing/attorney + any translation fee **`data not publicly itemised — verify per conveyancer`** (no national statutory tariff pulled this pass; do NOT assume a band). **Confidence**: MEDIUM (deed-language HIGH/statutory; sworn-translator framework + cost undocumented). **Sources**: [NamibLII — Deeds Registries Act 47 of 1937](https://namiblii.org/akn/na/act/1937/47), [LAC common-law note](https://www.lac.org.na/projects/grap/Pdf/Law_4-Common_Law.pdf).
- **BW**: **English** deed/conveyancing — **"all conveyancing, deeds, and statutes are in English"** per the playbook (English official, Setswana national); **Roman-Dutch common-law** (received via the Cape). **No statutory sworn-translator regime documented**; conveyancing handled by a **conveyancing attorney**, not a civil-law notary. Freehold + state-land title registered at the **Registrar of Deeds / Deeds Registry**. Foreign buyers should prepare **apostilled identity docs + source-of-funds chain** for the attorney/bank (Financial Intelligence Act AML regime — specific citation flagged for verification in the playbook). POA → **Hague Apostille status not confirmed this pass — `verify at` [HCCH status table](https://www.hcch.net/en/instruments/conventions/status-table/?cid=41)** (the playbook references "apostilled documents" in the AML/banking context but does NOT confirm Botswana's Convention membership) → attorney-certified translation if a non-English document arises. **Cost**: conveyancing-attorney + Deeds Registry fee **`data not publicly available — confirm a Law Society of Botswana tariff / quote`**; no sworn-translation band documented. **Confidence**: MEDIUM (deed-language HIGH/statutory; sworn-translator framework + cost undocumented). **Sources**: [gov.bw Hierarchy of Courts](https://www.gov.bw/legal/hierarchy-courts), [BURS](https://www.burs.org.bw/).

### Asia + Central Asia + Caucasus (JP, KR, TW, HK, SG, MO, TH, MY, ID, VN, PH, VU, IN, PK, CN, KH, LK, MV, KZ, AM, AZ, GE, UZ)

Largest region with widest Apostille variation. **Members**: JP, KR, SG (since 16 Jan 2021), CN (since **7 Nov 2023**), MO (via PRC), HK (separate stream), ID (since **4 Jun 2022**), PH (since 14 May 2019), IN, KZ, AM, AZ, GE, UZ. **VN (since 11 Sep 2026 — not vs DE / AT / CZ, Art. 12(2) objections)**. **Non-members in scope**: TW (political non-party), TH (deposited 30 Jun 2026, **in force 28 Feb 2027**), MY, KH, LK, MV. JP / IL / HK / KR / SG operate **no statutory sworn-translator profession** — notary-public-certified or court-interpreter routes. ID has the only fully statutory sworn register (penerjemah tersumpah at [ahu.go.id](https://ahu.go.id/)).

- **JP**: Japanese at Legal Affairs Bureau (Hōmukyoku); touki shinsei in Japanese. **No statutory sworn-translator** — private tsuyaku interpreter at signing + certified translator for foreign POA, with shihō shoshi vouching. POA → apostille (Hague since 1970). **Cost**: per-page JPY 5,000-12,000; tsuyaku at signing JPY 30-60k. **Confidence**: HIGH.
- **KR**: Korean at Registration Office (등기소). Notarisation (공증, kongjeung) of foreign POAs; certified translation + notary office attestation. No single statutory sworn register — court interpreters (법원행정처) + notary public. POA → apostille (Hague since 14 Jul 2007) + Korean certified translation + 공증. **Cost**: per-page KRW 30-80k; notary fee KRW 25-50k/document. **Confidence**: MEDIUM.
- **TW**: Mandarin Chinese (Traditional) at Land Administration Office. Notarisation by 公證人 / 民間公證人; sworn translator from MoJ-published roster. **NOT Hague Apostille (political non-party)** — TECO/TECRO authentication via origin TECO + MOFA Taipei. **Cost**: ~TWD 800-2,500/page. **Confidence**: HIGH.
- **HK**: English + Chinese (Traditional) co-official at Land Registry. **No statutory sworn translator** — translator's certificate of accuracy + solicitor witnessing or notary public attestation. Apostille via HK High Court / Registrar (continued post-1997). **Cost**: per-page HKD 300-800; solicitor witness ~HKD 500-1,500/document. **Confidence**: HIGH.
- **SG**: English at SLA; e-CT under Torrens. **No statutory sworn-translator** at completion — certified translation + notary public or Singapore Academy of Law-registered notary attestation. POA → apostille (Hague since **16 Jan 2021**). **Cost**: ~SGD 50-150/page; notarisation SGD 75-150/document. **Confidence**: HIGH.
- **MO**: Portuguese + Chinese (Traditional) co-official at Conservatória do Registo Predial. **Tradutores ajuramentados** from Direcção dos Serviços de Assuntos de Justiça list. Apostille via PRC extension (and previously Portuguese pre-1999); DSAJ Macao issues. **Cost**: ~MOP 300-700/page. **Confidence**: MEDIUM.
- **TH**: **Thai only** at Land Office (Krom Thi Din). Land-Office-recognised translator at the desk for foreign-buyer condo transfers (only freehold pathway). **NOT yet a Hague Apostille party** — instrument of accession deposited 30 Jun 2026, **in force 28 Feb 2027** (HCCH status table, verified 2026-09-17; Art. 12(2) objection window closes ~30 Dec 2026 — six months from HCCH notification, entry into force 60 days later). Until 28 Feb 2027: full consular chain via Thai MFA Department of Consular Affairs. **Cost**: per-page THB 800-2,000; full chain THB 4,000-8,000 + translator at Land Office THB 3,000-6,000. **Confidence**: HIGH (status flagged for re-stamp).
- **MY**: Bahasa Malaysia statutory; English de facto at JKPTG; bilingual standard. Certified translator from **Institut Terjemahan & Buku Malaysia (ITBM)** ([itbm.com.my](https://www.itbm.com.my/)). **NOT Hague Apostille member as of 2026-05**. Consular chain via Malaysia MFA Putrajaya. **Cost**: ~MYR 50-150/page. **Confidence**: HIGH.
- **ID**: **Bahasa Indonesia statutory** at BPN/ATR. Per **UU 30/2004** (Notary Position Law) akta notaris in Bahasa Indonesia; per **UU 24/2009** + **2018 Constitutional Court ruling** bilingual permitted but **Indonesian text controls**. **Penerjemah tersumpah** from **Kemenkumham AHU register** ([ahu.go.id](https://ahu.go.id/)) **mandatory** at PPAT signing — verify SK number on translator's stamp. POA → apostille (Hague **in force 4 Jun 2022**). **Cost**: ~IDR 200-500k/page. **Confidence**: HIGH.
- **VN**: Vietnamese at Sở Xây dựng + provincial LURC offices. Foreign-language contracts → notarised + MoJ-approved translator (phiên dịch viên). **Hague Apostille in force since 11 Sep 2026 (acceded 31 Dec 2025) — except vs Germany / Austria / Czechia, which objected under Art. 12(2) (no apostille between VN and those 3; consular chain persists there — Vietnam codified the pairwise rule in Decree 293/2026/NĐ-CP Art. 5(4))**. Vietnamese apostilles are issued by the MFA Consular Department (Hanoi) and the HCMC Department for External Relations (HCCH authority record, updated 23 Jul 2026); Decree 293 Art. 9(1) charges the existing consular-legalisation fee, no separate apostille fee is published — verify the rate at dichvucong.mofa.gov.vn. DE / AT / CZ documents: full consular chain via Vietnam MFA. **Cost**: ~VND 150-400k/page MoJ-approved translation. **Confidence**: HIGH (re-verified 2026-09-17 against HCCH status table + Decree 293/2026/NĐ-CP).
- **PH**: English + Filipino co-official; **English standard** for Sale Deeds at Registry of Deeds. **No statutory sworn-translator** — Philippine consular notarisation (origin) or local notary + LRA acknowledgment. POA → apostille (Hague **in force 14 May 2019**). **Cost**: certified translation + notarisation PHP 1,500-5,000/document. **Confidence**: HIGH.
- **VU**: **Leasehold instrument** (NOT a freehold deed) in **English and/or French** — three official languages (Bislama / English / French, the Anglo-French Condominium legacy); legal/conveyancing documents are typically English and/or French per the playbook. **Foreigners (and non-custom-owner ni-Vanuatu) take leasehold ONLY** — perpetual custom ownership is reserved to indigenous citizens (Constitution Art. 75); apartments use strata title over a registered lease (Strata Titles Act 2000). **Mixed common-law + French-civil-law** system; lease assignment, consents and the negotiator certificate are handled by a **Vanuatu conveyancing lawyer**, not DIY. **No statutory sworn-translator regime documented**; the deed-language layer is light given English is official. POA → **Hague Apostille status not confirmed this pass — `verify at` [HCCH status table](https://www.hcch.net/en/instruments/conventions/status-table/?cid=41)**. **Cost**: sworn-translation band **`data not publicly available — verify with a Vanuatu conveyancing lawyer`** (even the statutory stamp-duty base is agency-secondary / unconfirmed at primary in the playbook — do NOT assume a band). **Confidence**: LOW (thin deed-language documentation; leasehold-only tenure; apostille + cost undocumented). **Sources**: [Department of Lands, Survey & Registry](https://mol.gov.vu/index.php/lands-dept), [Constitution](https://www.gov.vu/images/legislation/constitution-en.pdf).
- **IN**: **State-language-dependent**; English universally accepted at every Sub-Registrar's Office; state language sometimes required for stamp paper / affidavits (TN Tamil; KA Kannada; MH Marathi; HI north-belt). Sworn translator from **State High Court interpreter list** (Bombay HC, Madras HC, Delhi HC). POA → apostille (Hague since 14 Jul 2005) + English/state-language certified translation. MEA e-Sanad handles apostille. **Cost**: per-page INR 500-2,000; full chain INR 5,000-15,000. **Confidence**: HIGH.
- **CN**: **Mandarin Chinese (Simplified) only** at Real Estate Registration Centre (不动产登记中心). Certified translator on local Notary Office (公证处) approved list. **POA → apostille (Hague in force 7 Nov 2023)** + Chinese certified translation by Notary-Office-recognised translator. Pre-Nov-2023 consular-chain POAs remain valid. HK/MO apostille streams separate. **Cost**: per-page CNY 100-300; Notary Office cert CNY 200-500/document. **Confidence**: HIGH.
- **KH**: Khmer at General Department of Cadastre. **NOT Hague Apostille member**. Full consular chain via Cambodia MFA Phnom Penh. Plan **4-8 weeks**. Note: foreigners cannot own land (only strata above ground floor or via local nominee — verify ownership pathway). **Cost**: ~$25-60/page (heavily dollarised). **Confidence**: MEDIUM.
- **LK**: Sinhala / Tamil / English official; Registrar General routinely English for foreign buyers. Sworn translator from District Court list. **NOT Hague Apostille member**. Consular chain via Sri Lanka MFA Colombo. **Cost**: ~LKR 1,500-4,000/page. **Confidence**: MEDIUM.
- **MV**: Dhivehi official; **English** practical for foreign-leasehold (foreigners cannot freehold — long-leasehold via SEZ Act / tourism leases). Bilingual Dhivehi-English contracts; Dhivehi summary attached for registry. **NOT Hague Apostille member**. Consular chain (often via Sri Lanka or India consulates). **Cost**: ~MVR 500-1,500/page. **Confidence**: LOW.
- **PK**: **Urdu (national) + English (official)** — English is **"the practical language of land statutes and deeds"** per the playbook (used in the higher courts, statutes and contracts); plus regional languages (Punjabi, Sindhi, Pashto, Balochi, Saraiki) as spoken vernaculars. **English is the practical deed/registry language**; the playbook does NOT document regional languages appearing in deeds (unlike IN, whose playbook records state-language use on stamp paper/affidavits) — provincial document-language practice not established this pass, verify per province. **Common-law-based + Islamic-law** mixed system. Title is **two-step**: the **mutation (*intiqal*) in the Record of Rights (*jamabandi*/*fard*)** at the provincial Board of Revenue (presumptive, **NOT Torrens**) **plus** registration of the sale deed under the **Registration Act 1908** — both matter; **land records are PROVINCIAL (no national cadastre)**. **No statutory sworn-translator regime documented**. POA → **Hague Apostille status not confirmed this pass — `verify at` [HCCH status table](https://www.hcch.net/en/instruments/conventions/status-table/?cid=41)** → certified translation of any non-English document. **Cost**: sworn-translation band **`data not publicly available — verify per province / with a Pakistani conveyancing lawyer`**. **Confidence**: MEDIUM (deed-language HIGH — English statutory/practical; provincial variation + sworn-translator framework + cost + apostille undocumented). **Sources**: [Registration Act 1908 / Transfer of Property Act 1882 — verify primary per province], [Punjab Land Records Authority](https://www.punjab-zameen.gov.pk/).
- **KZ**: Kazakh + Russian co-official at MoJ Property Rights Registration. Sworn translator from local court / notary-approved list for non-KZ/non-RU. POA → apostille (Hague since 30 Jan 2001). **Cost**: ~KZT 2,500-6,000/page. **Confidence**: HIGH.

- **AZ**: Azerbaijani (Latin script) at State Service for Property Issues. **Mütərcim** from MoJ / Notary Chamber list. POA → apostille (Hague since 2 Mar 2005). **Cost**: ~AZN 15-40/page. **Confidence**: HIGH.

## Universal mitigations (apply regardless of country)

These five mitigations work across all 126 countries and prevent the most common foreign-buyer language pitfalls:

1. **Always verify Hague Apostille status for both the POA-issuing country and the property country at [hcch.net status table](https://www.hcch.net/en/instruments/conventions/status-table/?cid=41) the day you start the chain** — non-membership adds 4-8 weeks. Watch for in-flight changes (TH in force 28 Feb 2027; VN in force since 11 Sep 2026, not vs DE / AT / CZ).

2. **Use the destination country's consulate POA when available** — civil-law consulates abroad can draft the property POA in the local language directly, bypassing both apostille and sworn-translation steps. Confirmed: FR, IT, ES, PT, DE, AT, NL, BE, LU, GR, CY, PL, CZ, MX, BR, AR, CO, CL, PE, TR, IL.

3. **Engage an independent sworn translator NOT recommended by the seller / agent / developer** — same conflict-of-interest principle as the buyer's lawyer. Use the country's official sworn-translator register (justiz-dolmetscher.de, exteriores.gob.es, justizdolmetscher.at, bureauwbtv.nl, justice.belgium.be, kstcr.cz, peritostraductoresautorizados.com.mx, ahu.go.id, etc.).

4. **For bilingual deeds, read the local-language version (or have it read by your independent translator)** — confirmed in IT, DE, ID, MX, HU: the local-language text controls regardless of what the English-friendly side says. Buyer's English version is courtesy, not legally controlling.

5. **Budget for sworn-translation cost upfront** — ranges from $25/page (UY/PA/EC market) to $200/page (NO statutory) per-page; full POA chain $300-3,500 depending on Hague vs consular legalisation. Non-Hague jurisdictions (UAE, Egypt, Lebanon) have the highest end-to-end cost due to multi-step legalisation.
