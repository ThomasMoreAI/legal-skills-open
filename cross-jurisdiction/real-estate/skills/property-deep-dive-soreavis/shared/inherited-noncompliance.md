# Universal `--inherited-noncompliance` Section — Inherited Unpermitted Construction · Cadastral Mismatch · Regularise-or-Demolish

Foreign-buyer **inherited-noncompliance** layer: the risk that a prior owner's extension / renovation / ADU was never permitted or signed off, OR that the building does not match the cadastre — and that this **runs with the property, not the seller**, so the new owner inherits the resale block, the mortgage block, and the demolition exposure. Covers the local concept and term, how liability transfers to the buyer, the resale/mortgageability knock-on, the regularisation path and amnesty windows (with ENDED ones marked), demolition/enforcement exposure, indemnity-insurance availability, and the cadastral-conformity rule. Distinct from `--permits` (FORWARD consenting of the buyer's own future works), `--surveyor` (physical condition inspection), `--notary` (conveyance mechanics), and `--latent-defect` (recourse AGAINST the seller — this section is the *regularise-or-demolish* regime). This is the *what did I just inherit, and can I sell it* check.

**Snapshot**: May 2026.

**Scope**: this doc supplies the *pre-existing illegality* layer — the country-specific regularisation path (cost, feasibility, amnesty windows), demolition exposure, and how unpermitted/non-conforming construction gates resale and mortgageability for the new owner. It does **not** restate forward building consenting (`--permits`), physical condition (`--surveyor`), or recourse against the seller for a concealed defect (`--latent-defect`). A foreign buyer typically needs four pieces of intel before the offer: (1) does the building match the cadastre and the permit record, (2) if not, is regularisation feasible and at what cost / within what window, (3) is there demolition or enforcement exposure, (4) does any of this block resale or a mortgage.

## Universal contract

For the property's country, return:

1. **Local concept + term** — the jurisdiction's name for unpermitted/non-conforming construction (IT abuso edilizio / difformità + conformità catastale · ES obra sin licencia / fuera de ordenación + cédula + antigüedad/AFO · PT desconformidade / legalização · FR défaut de conformité / récolement · UK lack of Building Regs completion certificate + planning enforcement · DE Schwarzbau + Bestandsschutz · Western Balkans legalizacija/ozakonjenje · TR kaçak yapı + Yapı Kayıt Belgesi · JP 違反建築 vs 既存不適格 + 検査済証 · HK Unauthorized Building Works).
2. **Liability transfer** — confirm it runs with the property and the new owner inherits it; the notary/conveyancer's duty to flag, and what is NOT flagged.
3. **Resale + mortgageability knock-on** — lenders declining, cadastral mismatch freezing the conveyance.
4. **Regularisation path + amnesty windows** — feasibility, cost basis, current status of any sanatoria/condono/legalizacija window, date-stamped; ENDED windows marked.
5. **Demolition / enforcement exposure** — enforcement prescription / time-limits and real precedent.
6. **Indemnity-insurance availability** — where a legal-indemnity policy exists (and its "void if you ask the council" mechanic), or explicitly that no market exists.
7. **Cadastral / title-conformity rule** + **Confidence** HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW.

## ⚠️ 7 cross-cutting traps

1. **Public-law building illegality runs with the PROPERTY, not the seller — you inherit it.** Recourse against the seller for concealment is a separate, exception route (the `--latent-defect` domain) and does not stop the municipality acting against you as the current owner. Across every jurisdiction researched, the building's illegality follows the land.
2. **A clean title extract will NOT surface a prior owner's unpermitted works.** The land/title register records ownership, not permit or as-built conformity. This is the single most load-bearing buyer-education point — the document a foreign buyer trusts most is silent on the risk that matters here.
3. **Cadastral / as-built mismatch can freeze the conveyance entirely.** Italy's *conformità catastale* gates the rogito; ES Catastro, PT Caderneta Predial, FR cadastre carry analogous blocks. The deal does not merely get cheaper — it can stop.
4. **It blocks resale and mortgageability, and indemnity cover evaporates the moment the council is asked.** Lenders routinely decline homes with unpermitted improvements (HK UBW is the canonical mortgage-killer); UK legal-indemnity policies are void once the buyer or their solicitor contacts the council to enquire.
5. **Regularisation feasibility and amnesty windows are jurisdiction-specific and time-boxed.** Italy's 1985/1994/2003 condoni are CLOSED (only the *sanatoria* accertamento route remains); the Western Balkans mass-legalisation reforms (RS new law Oct 2025, HR Bačić criminalisation 1 Jan 2026, SI Art. 146 GZ-1 annulled) have hard deadlines; many windows have ENDED. A scheme that existed last year may not exist now.
6. **Demolition and enforcement are real, not theoretical, and the clock varies.** IT Ischia demolition precedent; UK LURA 2023 s.115 uniform 10-year enforcement immunity (England, in force 25 Apr 2024); ES enforcement is *imprescriptible* on rústico/protected land; HK removal orders run with the land and are enforced.
7. **Reforms can shift the burden ONTO the buyer, and "amnesty certificates" confer no permanent legality.** Portugal's Simplex DL 10/2024 removed the obligation to present the *licença de utilização* at the deed — moving the inherited-noncompliance risk onto the buyer. Türkiye's *Yapı Kayıt Belgesi* (İmar Barışı 2018) is not a building permit and can be overridden by kentsel dönüşüm / quake-safety demolition.

## Regional patterns + country one-liners

The six regional blocks below give the local concept, liability transfer, resale/mortgage knock-on, regularisation path and demolition exposure per country, with anti-hallucination flags surfaced honestly. Amnesty-window dates are date-stamped and ENDED windows marked; no regularisation scheme is asserted without a primary source; the "runs with the property" principle is the constant.

## Western Europe + EFTA + microstates + UK

### Regional pattern

In all 24 jurisdictions public-law building illegality runs with the property, not the person — the buyer inherits the regularise-or-demolish exposure on completion. Recourse against the seller (the `--latent-defect` domain) is the exception route and generally requires proving fraudulent concealment (DE *arglistig verschwiegen*, FR *vice caché*, NL non-disclosure of *verborgen gebrek*); it is NOT an automatic remedy and does not stop the municipality acting against the current owner in the meantime. Cadastral/title registers record ownership and extent, not works-consent; the conformity check is always a separate building-authority matter. A mature retail indemnity-insurance market exists effectively only in the UK/IE (lack-of-Building-Regs and planning-default policies, void if the authority is approached).

### Country one-liners

- **IT Italy** — Concept: *abuso edilizio* (works without/against title) and *difformità* (deviation), plus **conformità catastale** + **conformità urbanistico-edilizia** — the deed (*rogito*) must declare the title references and that the building matches the planimetria catastale; mismatch makes the act voidable (L.52/1985 art.29-bis; art.46 DPR 380/2001). Liability passes wholly to buyer once the deed completes; the *notaio* checks declared titles and the cadastral conformity statement (often via a *relazione tecnica integrata*) but does NOT physically inspect — undocumented *difformità interne* routinely pass. Cadastral or urbanistic non-conformity freezes the rogito; Italian lenders will not mortgage an *immobile non conforme*. Regularisation: *accertamento di conformità* — **art.36** (full *doppia conformità*: lawful both at build time AND at application) for serious abusi; **art.36-bis** (reduced/single-track double conformity for partial difformità / variazioni essenziali) introduced by the **Salva Casa decree DL 69/2024 → L.105/2024** ([source: lavoripubblici.it / agefis.it summarising L.105/2024](https://www.lavoripubblici.it/news/sanatoria-abusi-edilizi-differenze-artt-36-36-bis-termini-presentazione-35579)). Historic *condono* windows (1985 L.47, 1994 L.724, 2003 L.326) are CLOSED — no general condono open as of 2026; Salva Casa is a *sanatoria* reform, NOT a fourth general condono — do not describe it as an "amnesty/condono". Demolition orders are enforceable and survive ownership change — the Ischia / Casamicciola precedent (post-2017 quake demolition campaign on condono-pending properties) shows demolition is real, not theoretical. Title-defect insurance exists in IT but is thin and will not cure an outstanding demolition order. Cadastral rule: must match Catasto Fabbricati planimetria to convey. **Confidence**: HIGH.
- **ES Spain** — Concept: *obra sin licencia* (unlicensed), *fuera de ordenación* (non-conforming-but-tolerated), and *asimilado a fuera de ordenación (AFO)* in Andalucía and similar autonomous-community regimes. Key buyer documents: **cédula de habitabilidad / licencia de primera ocupación** (occupancy) and **certificado de antigüedad** (age certificate) used to register via *declaración de obra nueva por antigüedad* once the disciplinary-action period has prescribed ([source: legalizatuvivienda.com; certicalia.com](https://legalizatuvivienda.com/preguntas-frecuentes/)). Liability passes to buyer; the *notario* records the *declaración de obra nueva* and checks Registro/Catastro but the AFO/antigüedad route REGISTERS the building once enforcement has prescribed — it does NOT legalise the urbanistic illegality; the illegality persists, only enforcement is time-barred. Surface/footprint mismatch between *escritura*, Registro de la Propiedad and **Catastro** stalls conveyance; lenders typically decline *fuera de ordenación* or AFO-only properties or lend at reduced LTV. Regularisation: *expediente de legalización* if works are compatible with current planning; otherwise AFO/antigüedad — prescription window varies by autonomous community (~4–8 yrs typical from completion, NOT a single national figure; rústico/protected land can be **imprescriptible**, so antigüedad does not run and demolition risk persists — Andalucía coastal demolitions are the standing precedent). No mature equivalent of the UK indemnity-policy market; risk priced into the discount. Cadastral rule: Catastro must reconcile with Registro to convey cleanly. **Confidence**: HIGH.
- **PT Portugal** — Concept: *obra ilegal / sem licença* and *desconformidade* with the approved project under the **RJUE** (Regime Jurídico da Urbanização e Edificação). The **Simplex Urbanístico, Decreto-Lei n.º 10/2024 (in force March 2024)** REMOVED the obligation to exhibit the *licença de utilização* at the purchase deed ([source: Sérvulo & Associados; idealista.pt](https://www.servulo.com/pt/investigacao-e-conhecimento/As-transaces-imobiliarias-sem-titulos-urbanisticos-Alteraces-operadas-pelo-Decreto-Lei-n-102024-de/8666/)) — this *increases* inherited-noncompliance risk for buyers (it shifts the burden ONTO the buyer; the notary/conveyance no longer forces the licence check — the opposite of a protective reform). Liability passes to buyer; *notário* no longer gatekeeps the utilisation licence post-DL10/2024. Lenders still require *licença de utilização* + reconciled **caderneta predial** (Finanças) vs *registo predial* (Conservatória) vs built reality; mismatch stalls financing. Regularisation: *legalização* via the municipal *Câmara* with architecture/specialty projects, *memória descritiva*, caderneta predial, where compatible with the PDM. Municipality can order *demolição* / *reposição*; no general amnesty open 2026. Indemnity: not a developed market. Cadastral rule: caderneta predial (matriz) ≠ legal title but must reconcile with Conservatória and built area. **Confidence**: HIGH.
- **FR France** — Concept: *construction sans autorisation* / *non conforme*, and absence of **DAACT** (Déclaration Attestant l'Achèvement et la Conformité des Travaux) — the post-works conformity declaration; the mayor may carry out *récolement* (conformity inspection) within 3 months of DAACT (or longer for protected zones). Prescription: **criminal prosecution prescribes 6 years from completion**; the **administrative/civil action by the commune is ~10 years**, and demolition demands tied to certain illegalities can be effectively imprescriptible in protected contexts ([source: service-public.gouv.fr fiche F642](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F642)). Liability passes to buyer; absence of DAACT must be disclosed to the buyer — failure to disclose exposes the seller to *vice caché* / damages, which is the buyer's main recourse for *inherited* non-conformity. The *notaire* flags missing DAACT/permits in the *acte* but does not inspect the works. French lenders increasingly require DAACT + permit pack; missing conformity can block or discount the sale. Regularisation: *permis de régularisation* / *déclaration préalable* a posteriori where works comply with current PLU; no general amnesty. Court-ordered *démolition* possible within prescription windows, more readily in *loi Littoral* / *loi Montagne* / protected zones. Indemnity: not a standard product. Cadastral: French *cadastre* is fiscal, not a legal title register; mismatch is a flag but conveyance turns on the *titre de propriété* + servitudes, not cadastre per se. **Confidence**: HIGH.
- **UK United Kingdom** — Concept: works lacking **planning permission** and/or a **Building Regulations completion certificate**; "unauthorised development" subject to local-authority enforcement. The **Levelling-Up and Regeneration Act 2023** abolished the old split (4-yr rule for operational development & change-of-use-to-single-dwelling vs 10-yr for everything else) — **s.115 LURA amended s.171B TCPA 1990 to a uniform 10-year enforcement immunity period for ALL breaches in England, in force 25 April 2024** (not 26 Oct 2023, the Royal Assent date; the operative date is via the April 2024 commencement/secondary legislation), with transitional protection: breaches *substantially complete before 25 April 2024* keep the old 4-year rule ([source: GOV.UK; JLL; Charles Russell Speechlys](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/enforcement-appeals-key-changes-under-the-levelling-up-and-regeneration-act)). This is England; Wales/Scotland/NI retain their own regimes (Scotland generally 10-yr; verify per nation). Liability passes to buyer with the land — enforcement runs with the property, not the person. Building Regs breaches: local-authority enforcement of *Building Act 1984* is generally time-limited but the structure remains non-compliant indefinitely (safety/insurance/resale risk persists past any limitation). Conveyancing solicitor flags missing planning/Building-Regs certs; lenders/CML Handbook commonly require regularisation or **indemnity insurance**. Regularisation: retrospective planning application; **Regularisation Certificate** (Building Regs reg.18) for post-Nov-1985 unauthorised building work; Lawful Development Certificate once immune. Enforcement notice → potential requirement to undo works; rare for minor domestic but legally available. Indemnity: mature market — *lack-of-Building-Regs* and *planning indemnity* policies are standard, but **void if the council/building control is contacted** about the issue (must not "approach the authority"). Cadastral: HM Land Registry records title/extent, not works-consent. **Confidence**: HIGH.
- **DE Germany** — Concept: *Schwarzbau* (built without/against *Baugenehmigung*) and *Bestandsschutz* (legacy protection — Schwarzbauten generally have NONE unless either old enough that no permit would be needed under current law OR a *nachträgliche Baugenehmigung* / retroactive permit is granted) ([source: wohneigentum.nrw; juraforum.de](https://www.wohneigentum.nrw/beitrag/schwarzbau)). Liability passes to buyer — sales almost always contract out warranty (*gekauft wie gesehen* / Haftungsausschluss); the buyer carries post-completion defects EXCEPT where the seller *arglistig verschwiegen* (fraudulently concealed) a known illegality, which the buyer must prove — that fraud exception is the principal inherited-illegality recourse. Lenders require *Baugenehmigung* + matching *Bauakte*; an unpermitted extension can block financing. Regularisation: *nachträgliche Baugenehmigung* only if the building now meets current building law and no overriding *Denkmalschutz*/nature objection; otherwise *Rückbauverfügung* (demolition order) — the harshest *Bauaufsicht* measure, issued when not approvable. No federal amnesty; *Landesbauordnung* varies by Bundesland. Indemnity: not a standard German product — risk handled via price, escrow or *Rückbau* costing. Cadastral: *Liegenschaftskataster* records parcels/footprint; encumbrances sit in the *Baulastenverzeichnis* (separate from the Grundbuch) — buyer must check both. **Confidence**: HIGH.
- **AT Austria** — Concept: *Schwarzbau* / *konsenslose bzw. konsenswidrige Bauführung* under the nine provincial *Bauordnungen* (e.g. Wr. BauO, Bgld/NÖ/Tirol BauO) — there is no single federal building code. *Baukonsens* (building consent) attaches to the structure; deviation = *vorschriftswidriger Bau*. Liability passes to buyer; the *Notar*/Treuhänder handles Grundbuch transfer but does not certify building-law conformity — the *Baubehörde* (municipality) enforces. Banks require *Baubewilligung* + *Benützungsbewilligung*; mismatch with the *Bauakt* impedes financing. Regularisation: *nachträgliche Baubewilligung* where compatible with the *Flächenwidmungs-/Bebauungsplan*; otherwise *Beseitigungsauftrag* (removal order). No general amnesty 2026 — *data not publicly available for a current amnesty window; verify with the relevant Landesbaubehörde*. Indemnity: not a standard product. Cadastral: *Grundbuch* (legal) + *Kataster* (BEV, technical) must reconcile. **Confidence**: MEDIUM.
- **CH Switzerland** — Concept: *Bau ohne Baubewilligung* / *construction sans permis* / *Schwarzbau* under cantonal building law (26 cantonal regimes) + the federal **RPG/LAT** distinction between building zone and **non-building zone (ausserhalb der Bauzone / hors zone à bâtir)**. Decisive precedent: **BGE 147 II 309 (Federal Court, 28 April 2021)** — the 30-year restoration-limitation period (established 1981 *inside* building zones) **does NOT apply outside the building zone**; the authority's claim to restore the lawful state hors zone does **not lapse, ever** ([source: Schweizerisches Bundesgericht / BGE 147 II 309](https://www.bger.ch/ext/eurospider/live/de/php/aza/http/index.php?highlight_docid=atf://147-II-309:de&lang=de&type=show_document&zoom=YES)). Liability passes to buyer; the *Notar* handles the Grundbuch deed but cantonal *Baubehörde* enforces. Swiss lenders require *Baubewilligung*; non-conforming hors-zone property is hard to finance and a non-time-barrable restoration claim is a severe latent encumbrance. Regularisation: *nachträgliches Baubewilligungsverfahren* if zone-compatible; outside the building zone restoration/demolition is the likely outcome and inherited illegality there is effectively permanent exposure. Demolition (*Wiederherstellung des rechtmässigen Zustands*) is actively enforced, especially hors zone. Indemnity: not a standard product. Cadastral: *Grundbuch* + cantonal *amtliche Vermessung* must reconcile. **Confidence**: HIGH (hors-zone non-forfeiture); MEDIUM (in-zone 30-yr rule applied per-canton).
- **NL Netherlands** — Concept: *bouwen zonder/in strijd met omgevingsvergunning* (illegal building) and *strijdig gebruik*, under the **Omgevingswet** (in force 1 Jan 2024 — bestemmingsplannen folded into the municipal *Omgevingsplan*). Limitation: classically NO statutory limitation for illegal buildings; under the Omgevingswet a **5-year enforcement-immunity period** applies to certain bouw/gebruik prohibitions ([source: lawspot.nl; Hekkelman](https://www.lawspot.nl/verjaringstermijn-voor-illegale-bouwwerken-onder-de-nieuwe-omgevingswet-wat-je-moet-weten/)). Liability passes to buyer — the municipality can order the *current* owner to remove an inherited illegal structure; buyer's recourse against seller is limited (verborgen gebrek / conformiteit art.7:17 BW only if non-disclosure provable). Notaris transfers title; lenders/valuers flag unpermitted works; *vergunningvrij* (permit-free) categories exist and reduce the issue for minor works. Regularisation: current owner applies for a retroactive *omgevingsvergunning*; courts apply a lenient "concrete prospect of legalisation" test. Demolition: *last onder bestuursdwang/dwangsom* can compel removal. Indemnity: not a developed product. Cadastral: *Kadaster* records parcels/ownership, not works-consent. **Confidence**: HIGH.
- **BE Belgium** — Concept: *bouwmisdrijf / stedenbouwkundige inbreuk* (Flanders) / *infraction urbanistique* (Wallonia/Brussels) — regionalised regimes (VCRO Flanders; CoDT Wallonia; CoBAT Brussels). Flanders uses a **vermoeden van vergunning** (presumption of permit for pre-existing buildings) and an *as-built attest* concept; a *stedenbouwkundig uittreksel/inlichtingen* is required at sale and the seller/notary must disclose known infractions. Liability passes to buyer; in Flanders the *herstelvordering* (restoration claim) can run against successive owners. Notary obtains the urban-planning extract; an unresolved *bouwmisdrijf* can block financing and triggers disclosure duty. Regularisation: *regularisatievergunning* where zone-compatible; otherwise *herstelmaatregel* (restoration/demolition or *meerwaarde* payment). Some "non-prosecution"/minor-infraction categories exist post-2018 VCRO reform — *exact current thresholds: data not publicly available here — verify with Departement Omgeving / regional code*. Indemnity: not standard. Cadastral: *Kadaster/AAPD* (fiscal) separate from notarial title. **Confidence**: MEDIUM.
- **SE Sweden** — Concept: *olovligt byggande* ("svartbygge") — building without *bygglov* under **Plan- och bygglagen (PBL)**. Enforcement limitation: the municipal *byggnadsnämnd* generally cannot order rectification of an *olovlig åtgärd* more than **10 years after it was carried out** (PBL 11 kap. 20 § — the "tioårspreskription"), but this **does NOT apply to a change of use to a dwelling** (no time bar there). Liability passes to buyer; the *byggnadsnämnd* enforces; the *byggsanktionsavgift* (sanction fee) can attach. Lenders/valuers flag missing *slutbesked* (final approval) / bygglov; conveyance is private (no Latin notary) so buyer due diligence is heavier. Regularisation: retroactive *bygglov i efterhand* if PBL-compatible. Demolition: *rättelseföreläggande* possible within the 10-yr window. Indemnity: not standard. Cadastral: *Lantmäteriet* fastighetsregister. **Confidence**: MEDIUM (10-yr/use-change carve-out: verify exact PBL cite at boverket.se).
- **FI Finland** — Concept: building without *rakennuslupa* (building permit) under the **Rakennuslaki / new Building Act 751/2023** (replaced the Land Use and Building Act, in force largely **1 Jan 2025**). Liability passes to buyer; municipal *rakennusvalvonta* enforces; the building must have a *loppukatselmus* (final inspection) to be lawfully used. Lenders flag missing permit / final inspection. Regularisation: retroactive permit if plan-compatible; otherwise *uhkasakko* (conditional fine) / removal. Demolition: enforcement available; *data not publicly available for a current general amnesty — verify at ym.fi / kuntaliitto.fi*. Indemnity: not standard. Cadastral: *Maanmittauslaitos* property register. **Confidence**: MEDIUM (new Building Act 2025 transition — verify provisions at ym.fi).
- **NO Norway** — Concept: *ulovlig tiltak* (illegal measure — built without/against *byggetillatelse*) under **plan- og bygningsloven (pbl)**. The municipality has a **duty to pursue** illegal works (pbl §32-1); for minor matters the municipality *may* refrain. Norway's earlier time-limit on pursuing illegal building was **repealed (the foreldelse/5-yr foreldelsesfrist for pålegg was removed from 1 Jan 2024)** — illegality is now effectively non-time-barred ([general — verify exact date/section at lovdata.no / dibk.no]). Liability passes to buyer; the *current* owner receives the *pålegg om retting/riving*. Lenders flag missing *ferdigattest* / *brukstillatelse*; common older-home issue. Regularisation: retroactive application if plan-compatible; otherwise *pålegg om riving* (demolition order) + *tvangsmulkt* (coercive fine). Indemnity: *boligkjøperforsikring/eierskifteforsikring* covers some seller-disclosure disputes but NOT the public-law illegality itself. Cadastral: *Matrikkelen* (Kartverket). **Confidence**: MEDIUM (2024 foreldelse repeal — verify at lovdata.no).
- **DK Denmark** — Concept: *ulovligt byggeri* — building contrary to the *byggetilladelse* / Bygningsreglementet (BR18) or local plan; remedy is *lovliggørelse* (legalisation) — either *retlig* (retroactive permit) or *fysisk* (physical removal). The municipality generally must require *lovliggørelse* of *ulovlige forhold* with **no general limitation period** for public-law illegality. Liability passes to buyer; municipality issues the *påbud* to the current owner. The **tilstandsrapport / huseftersyn** + *ejerskifteforsikring* regime addresses physical condition and seller liability, but unpermitted works are flagged in the *BBR (Bygnings- og Boligregister)* — a BBR mismatch (built area ≠ registered) is the classic red flag and must be reconciled. Regularisation: *retlig lovliggørelse* if plan/BR-compatible; else *fysisk lovliggørelse*. Indemnity: *ejerskifteforsikring* covers seller-disclosure-type defects, not the municipal illegality. Cadastral: *matriklen* (GST) + *BBR* (must match built reality). **Confidence**: MEDIUM (no-limitation general rule — verify at bygst.dk / sik.dk).
- **IS Iceland** — Concept: building without *byggingarleyfi* (building permit) under the *Mannvirkjalög* (Building Act 160/2010) / Byggingarreglugerð, enforced by *Húsnæðis- og mannvirkjastofnun (HMS)* / municipal *byggingarfulltrúi*. Liability passes to buyer; the *Þjóðskrá* property/Fasteignaskrá registration and built reality must reconcile. Regularisation: retroactive permit / lokaúttekt (final inspection) if compliant; otherwise removal order + coercive measures. Demolition/enforcement available. *Indemnity / current amnesty: data not publicly available — verify at hms.is*. Cadastral: *Fasteignaskrá / Þjóðskrá Íslands*. **Confidence**: LOW (sparse public-source detail on inherited-illegality transfer mechanics).
- **IE Ireland** — Concept: *unauthorised development* (no/insufficient planning permission) under the **Planning and Development Act 2000**. The **seven-year rule (s.157(4) PDA 2000)**: a planning authority may not serve an enforcement notice for unauthorised development more than 7 years after commencement — BUT this bars *enforcement only*, it does NOT grant permission; the development remains *unauthorised but immune*, and the bar does not save breaches of a *condition relating to use of land* ([source: lawsociety.ie; tpdsolutions.ie; cgsolicitors.ie](https://www.lawsociety.ie/gazette/in-depth/a-brief-history-of-time)). Liability passes to buyer with the land. Conveyancing requires a **Certificate / Opinion of Compliance** with planning permission & Building Regulations (Building Control Act 1990) from an architect/engineer — absence stalls or collapses the sale; the Law Society conveyancing requisitions probe this. Regularisation: *retention permission* (retrospective planning) — note Ireland is tightening retention loopholes; or rely on the 7-yr immunity (registers risk, not cure). Demolition: enforcement notice/court order available within the 7-yr window. Indemnity: planning-default indemnity policies are used in IE conveyancing similarly to UK. Cadastral: *Tailte Éireann* (Land Registry/Registry of Deeds) records title, not consent. **Confidence**: HIGH.
- **GR Greece** — Concept: *afthereta* (αυθαίρετα — arbitrary/illegal constructions) and *poleodomikes paravaseis*. Greece runs successive **legalisation/settlement laws** — the consolidating regime is **N.4495/2017** (preceded by N.4178/2013, N.4014/2011). The category 1–4 *taktopoiisi* (settlement) window was extended to **31 March 2026, with a further extension to 31 March 2028** ([⚠️ aggregator/law-firm sources — roumpa.com, dimisign.gr — primary text at ypen.gov.gr / TEE should be confirmed before publishing the 2028 date](https://roumpa.com/taktopoiisi_authaireton_lixi_/)). A property cannot be transferred unless the engineer's certificate (*veveosi michanikou*) declares no unauthorised works OR they are settled/exempt. Liability passes to buyer; the engineer's certificate + the **Electronic Building Identity (Ηλεκτρονική Ταυτότητα Κτηρίου)** are the gating documents at the notarial deed — the e-ID is **mandatory for essentially all property transfers, parental gifts, bank loans and licences** (clarified by N.4759/2020; mandatory for permits issued after 1 Feb 2021, with a transition for existing buildings) ([source: greeklawfirm.gr; malliarilawoffice.gr summarising N.4495/2017 + N.4759/2020](https://www.greeklawfirm.gr/news-and-events-el/legal-update-electronic-building-identity-within-the-modernization-of-spatial-and-urban-planning-legislation-in-greece/)). False/incomplete e-ID declarations: fines €2,000–€20,000 by undeclared surface. A transfer is legally void without the engineer's certificate; lenders require it. Regularisation: *taktopoiisi/nomimopoiisi* under N.4495/2017 with fees + the electronic dossier. A national demolition body exists for forest/coastal/arbitrary structures; enforced selectively. Indemnity: not standard. Cadastral: **Ktimatologio (Hellenic Cadastre)** rollout — declarations mandatory; mismatch blocks transfer. **Confidence**: MEDIUM (settlement-window end-date depends on aggregator sources; e-ID mandate well-sourced).

Demolition/enforcement available. *Current amnesty / indemnity market: data not publicly available — verify with the relevant commune / Ministère du Logement*. ## CEE + Western Balkans + Baltics + Caucasus

### Regional pattern

In almost every jurisdiction the liability runs with the building, not the builder — the registered owner of the day inherits the enforcement order, the demolition exposure and the legalisation cost; the buyer's only structural defence is a pre-contract cadastre/land-registry conformity check. The operative risk vector is the land-registry vs cadastre vs reality triangle plus mass *legalizacija* statutes whose amnesty windows open and close, so the window state as of the buyer's completion date is load-bearing. Title-indemnity insurance for unpermitted works is, across the entire region, effectively unavailable as a retail product (a handful of UK/DE underwriters quote bespoke for high-value cross-border deals only) — treat "indemnity: none" as the default. 2025–2026 brought several regime resets: CZ tightened to default-removal (1 Jul 2024), Serbia replaced its *ozakonjenje* statute (Oct 2025 law, applications from 8 Dec 2025), and Croatia criminalised illegal construction (1 Jan 2026).

### Country one-liners

- **PL Poland** — Concept: *samowola budowlana* (building without permit/notification or against an objection); regularisation = *legalizacja*. Liability transfer: the buyer steps into the full procedural and legal position of the prior owner at every stage, including after a first-instance administrative decision — a pending or latent *nakaz rozbiórki* (demolition order) follows the property to the new owner (Sourced — biznes.gov.pl, poradnikprzedsiebiorcy.pl). Notary does NOT independently verify physical-vs-permit conformity; *księga wieczysta* check is standard but registers title, not build legality. A self-declared *samowola* with no occupancy permit (*pozwolenie na użytkowanie*) impairs mortgageability and registration; banks routinely refuse. Two tracks: (a) standard *legalizacja* (building detected within ~10 yrs) requiring a *opłata legalizacyjna* that can run to tens/hundreds of thousands PLN; (b) simplified *uproszczona legalizacja* — a Dec 2025 amendment shortened the qualifying age from 20 → 10 years and eliminated the legalisation fee (in force ~late Nov/early Dec 2025; costs reduce to *inwentaryzacja powykonawcza* ~1,000–3,000 PLN + technical opinion ~1,500–4,000 PLN + 82 PLN court fee) (Sourced — wolthome.pl, kancelaria-bonaartis.pl summarising the Dec 2025 nowelizacja; *verify exact in-force date in Dz.U. before relying*). ~2,000 *samowola* cases legalised in 2024 per GUNB. No general prescription extinguishing a *nakaz rozbiórki* — the standard route's 10-yr cut-off is to *qualify for the cheaper track*, not a limitation bar. Indemnity: none. Conformity rule: confirm the building appears with an occupancy permit / closed legalisation in the building-authority file AND that *ewidencja gruntów i budynków* (EGiB cadastre) footprint matches reality before exchange. **Confidence**: MEDIUM-HIGH (Dec 2025 amendment via secondary legal commentary; statute text not directly fetched).
- **CZ Czech Republic** — Concept: *černá stavba* (black building); ex-post regularisation = *dodatečné povolení stavby*. Liability transfer: enforcement attaches to the current owner; *katastr nemovitostí* records title, the building's legality is a separate matter the buyer inherits. An un-collauded / unpermitted structure impairs mortgage and clean transfer. The new Building Act (zákon č. 283/2021 Sb., účinnost 1 July 2024) *tightened* this sharply — the authority will in most cases order removal straight away (typical 60-day deadline) rather than retro-approve; *dodatečné povolení* now survives mainly for compliant garden/minor structures with neighbour consent; large residential/office černé stavby built without a permit are no longer routinely legalisable (Sourced — dostupnyadvokat.cz; corroborated barthelemy.cz). This is a regime reversal from the pre-2024 "build underground, get retro-approved later" practice — do not carry forward old "it can be legalised later" framing. No amnesty window; removal is the default outcome. Indemnity: none. Conformity rule: require the *kolaudační rozhodnutí / kolaudační souhlas* (occupancy decision) and check the *katastr* building footprint and *číslo popisné* against reality — post-2024 a missing kolaudace is materially harder to cure than pre-2024 deals assumed. **Confidence**: MEDIUM-HIGH.
- **SK Slovakia** — Concept: *čierna stavba*; regularisation historically via *dodatočné stavebné povolenie*. Liability transfer: runs with the building / current owner; *kataster nehnuteľností* = title, not legality. No *kolaudácia* / unregistered building impairs mortgage and transfer. A new building regime took effect 1 April 2025 (zákon č. 201/2022 Z.z. on construction + zákon č. 25/2025 Z.z.). Black buildings erected *before* 1 Apr 2025 can still be examined for *spôsobilosť stavby na užívanie* / retro-permit, with a hard filing deadline of 31 March 2029; structures built *after* 1 Apr 2025 cannot be legalised retroactively at all (Sourced — geo-group.sk, uupv.sk official portal, slov-lex 25/2025; *verify the 31 Mar 2029 cut-off against slov-lex transitional provisions before relying*). Minor-structure reporting threshold raised 25 → 50 m². Post-deadline or post-2025 builds → demolition at owner's cost. Indemnity: none. Conformity rule: for any building lacking *kolaudácia*, confirm a legalisation application is on foot before the 31 Mar 2029 window and that the *kataster* footprint matches; a post-1-Apr-2025 unpermitted addition is incurable — price as demolition risk. **Confidence**: MEDIUM-HIGH.
- **SI Slovenia** — Concept: *nedovoljen objekt* / *nelegalna gradnja*; legalisation = *legalizacija* (objekt + *uporabno dovoljenje za stare objekte*). Liability transfer: runs with the current owner; *zemljiška knjiga* (land register) ≠ build legality; the *kataster nepremičnin* footprint is the conformity reference. A *nedovoljen objekt* cannot be cleanly transferred/mortgaged. Critical window state: the long-standing-structure legalisation route under Art. 146 of the Gradbeni zakon (GZ-1) is NOT currently usable — the Constitutional Court annulled Art. 146 GZ-1 (decision July 2025), and its application had already been suspended nationwide since December 2023 pending that review (Sourced — kwslovenia.com legal commentary; *verify the exact Ustavno sodišče decision number and any replacement provision at gov.si / Uradni list*). A reportedly proposed successor rule (automatic permit for houses built before 1995) is draft / not enacted as of May 2026 — do NOT rely on it; any brief pitching Slovenian legalisation under Art. 146 is stale. The general route — legalisation per the rules in force *at the time of construction*, within 5 yrs of GZ-1 — remains, but the headline "old building amnesty" is in limbo. Inspectorate (*gradbena inšpekcija*) can order removal; no safe amnesty currently. Indemnity: none. Conformity rule: do not price in Art. 146 legalisation; require *uporabno dovoljenje* (occupancy permit) or proof of "deemed legal" pre-1967/1998 status, and a *kataster* match. **Confidence**: MEDIUM (annulment via secondary source; statute-level confirmation pending).
- **HR Croatia** — Concept: *nezakonito izgrađena zgrada* ("divlja gradnja"); legalisation = *ozakonjenje*. Liability transfer: runs with the owner; an illegal building cannot be sold, nor registered in the *katastar* or *zemljišna knjiga*, and the owner is fineable — so the buyer of a not-yet-legalised structure inherits a non-transferable, non-mortgageable asset (Sourced — multiple Croatian legal/agency sources; cross-checked). Eligibility is tied to existence by 21 June 2011 and visibility on the State Geodetic Administration (DGU) digital orthophoto of that date, at least at "rough construction" stage; protected zones (nature parks, maritime domain, state land/forest/water) are excluded. The original 30 June 2018 application deadline was repealed (no time bar to apply under the existing *Zakon o postupanju s nezakonito izgrađenim zgradama*). NEW — 2025 "Bačić" reform package: Parliament passed three reform laws (construction, spatial planning, energy efficiency) reopening *ozakonjenje* for pre-21-June-2011 buildings not previously applied for, and making illegal construction a CRIMINAL offence, with fines €20,000–€30,000; in force 1 January 2026 (Sourced — croatiaweek.com, mpgi.gov.hr; *verify final statute text in Narodne novine*) — pre-2026 framing of *ozakonjenje* as a purely administrative fine matter understates post-2026 exposure. Inspectorate removal for non-legalisable / post-cut-off builds; the 2011 orthophoto is the hard evidentiary line. Indemnity: none. Conformity rule: require the *rješenje o izvedenom stanju* (legalisation decision) AND confirm the building is on the DGU 2011 DOF and the *katastar*/land-register footprint matches before any deposit — post-1-Jan-2026 the criminal exposure makes an open *ozakonjenje* a hard stop, not a price chip. **Confidence**: HIGH (well-documented, multi-source).
- **HU Hungary** — Concept: building without/against permit; legalisation permit = *fennmaradási engedély*. Liability transfer: runs with current owner; *földhivatal* (land office) cadastral map vs reality is the conformity reference; *fennmaradási engedély* is typically obtained by the seller *before* sale precisely because it follows the property. An unregistered/unpermitted structure not on the cadastral map impairs mortgage and clean transfer. Only buildings built without permit within a 10-year window can get a *fennmaradási engedély*; from 1 Oct 2024 legalisation fines rose sharply (e.g. an unauthorised 25 m² extension → ~HUF 3 million *építésügyi bírság*); OTÉK replaced by TÉKA from 1 Jan 2025 (new national zoning/technical baseline against which legality is judged). Buildings >10 years old whose unauthorised part complies with current rules can be legalised without a fine (Sourced — vg.hu, e-epites.hu official portal). Removal where legalisation refused; the 10-yr age threshold is the practical pivot. Indemnity: none. Conformity rule: require the *fennmaradási engedély* (or proof the >10-yr no-fine route applies) and a *földhivatal* map match; post-1-Oct-2024 the fine exposure on a fresh unauthorised extension is materially higher. **Confidence**: MEDIUM-HIGH.
- **RO Romania** — Concept: *construcție fără autorizație de construire* (or built in breach of it); regularisation = *intrare în legalitate*. Liability transfer: without documentation the construction is not deemed finalised and cannot be normally registered in *cartea funciară* — sale/mortgage blocked; demolition cost is the owner's and is enforced by *executare silită* (Sourced — juridice.ro, sfat-avocat.ro). Blocked until registered with the *Oficiul de Cadastru și Publicitate Imobiliară* (OCPI/ANCPI) on the basis of a *certificat de atestare a edificării*. The 3-year contravention prescription (running from actual completion) is pivotal — built within 3 yrs → contravention penalty + legalisation on an attestation considering urbanism compliance; older than 3 yrs → register on a building-attestation certificate (the de-facto common route for old rural builds). Fines RON 1,000–100,000 for unauthorised works. Demolition ordered where *intrare în legalitate* is impossible (non-compliant with PUG/PUZ urbanism) — the 3-yr bar limits the *fine*, not the demolition power for non-conforming builds. Indemnity: none. Conformity rule: require the *certificat de atestare a edificării* and OCPI registration with matching footprint; never accept "old enough so it's fine" — confirm urbanism conformity, the demolition power survives prescription. **Confidence**: MEDIUM-HIGH.
- **BG Bulgaria** — Concept: *незаконен строеж* (illegal construction) vs *търпим строеж* ("tolerable" — a statutory status that legalises pre-cut-off non-permitted builds for transfer purposes without full legalisation). Liability transfer: an *незаконен строеж* must be removed at the owner's cost; runs with the property; *кадастър* (cadastre) + *имотен регистър* (property register) are the conformity references. *търпим строеж* status (a *удостоверение за търпимост*) is what enables sale/mortgage of an old non-permitted building; without it, transfer is impaired. Post-completion permit validation was removed in 2003; a Oct 2012 ЗУТ (Spatial Development Act) amendment let owners validate buildings built up to 2003; the *търпимост* regime fixes successive cut-off dates by amendment (most recently buildings built before specified dates — *exact current cut-off: data not publicly available in this pass — verify in the current ЗУТ § преходни разпоредби*). March 2024 bills to digitalise ЗУТ + Cadastre Act were tabled (Sourced — schoenherr roadmap, mrrb.bg; cut-off date flagged for verification). Removal for genuinely illegal builds; *търпимост* is the practical shield for old ones. Indemnity: none. Conformity rule: require either *разрешение за ползване* / Акт 16 (occupancy) or a *удостоверение за търпимост* matching the cadastral footprint; "tolerable" ≠ "legal for further works" — extensions still need fresh consent. **Confidence**: MEDIUM (current ЗУТ cut-off date not pinned).
- **RS Serbia** — Concept: *bespravna gradnja* / *bespravno izgrađen objekat*; ex-YU mass legalisation. Liability transfer: an un-legalised building cannot be entered in the *katastar nepokretnosti* → not transferable/mortgageable; the buyer inherits the un-upis state and demolition exposure (*rešenje o rušenju*). MAJOR 2025 RESET: the prior *Zakon o ozakonjenju objekata* (legalisation) regime was replaced by a new law adopted 24 Oct 2025, the *Zakon o posebnim uslovima za evidentiranje i upis prava svojine* ("Law on Special Conditions for Recording and Registration of Property Rights"), aimed at ~4.8 million illegal objects: fully digital platform, no construction permit / urban approval required, no expensive technical docs, with applications opening 8 Dec 2025 (45 days post-publication) and a target cost of ~€100 vs the old €1,500–€7,500 (Sourced — attorney.rs, lexetdomus.co.rs, cityexpert.rs, d2plaw.com summarising the Oct 2025 law; *verify final text + window in Sl. glasnik RS*) — briefs naming the old *ozakonjenje* statute as the live route are out of date. Objects must exist on the ground and be visible on satellite imagery; objects temporarily connected to utilities under the 2023 Ordinance get permanent connections post-registration. *rešenje o rušenju* exposure persists for objects that cannot be recorded; no general prescription. Indemnity: none. Conformity rule: do NOT transact on an un-recorded *bespravni objekat* assuming the old ozakonjenje path — confirm status under the new Oct 2025 regime (application filed/recorded on the digital platform) and a *katastar* upis with matching footprint. **Confidence**: MEDIUM-HIGH (very recent law, secondary sources; window dates flagged).
- **ME Montenegro** — Concept: *bespravni objekat* / *divlja gradnja*; legalisation = *legalizacija bespravnih objekata*. Liability transfer: runs with owner; un-registered objects face removal; *katastar* upis blocked until legalised. NEW 2025 LAW: a new *Zakon o legalizaciji bespravnih objekata* was published 6 Aug 2025, in force 14 Aug 2025; local governments have 1 year to register illegal objects, citizens 6 months to file legalisation requests; objects not in the cadastre must initiate registration by 14 Feb 2026 or face a removal decision; a dedicated Administration for Legalisation with a 5-year mandate; payment in up to 360 monthly instalments (basic housing) / 120 (other) / 60 (luxury tourist, higher rates); social-category reductions up to 50–90%; legal-person fines €1,000–€40,000 for non-compliance (Sourced — standard.co.me, monitor.co.me, podgorica.me PDF of the law; *verify final text in Sl. list CG*). Removal decision after the 14 Feb 2026 cadastre-registration deadline. Indemnity: none. Conformity rule: for coastal (*primorje*) objects expect a special *naknada* and stricter terms — confirm the object is registered/within the legalisation pipeline and the *katastar* footprint matches before completion; the 14 Feb 2026 trigger is calendar-actionable. **Confidence**: MEDIUM-HIGH (recent law; final gazette text flagged).
- **BA Bosnia & Herzegovina** — Concept: *bespravno izgrađen objekat*; legalisation = *legalizacija* — entity-split regime (FBiH vs Republika Srpska vs Brčko, plus cantonal laws in FBiH). Liability transfer: runs with owner; un-legalised → blocked *zemljišna knjiga* / *katastar*. FBiH — legalisation conducted per cantonal *Zakon o legalizaciji bespravno izgrađenih građevina*; objects built before 5 April 1992 treated under a separate legality-determination track. Republika Srpska — objects built by end-1991 deemed legally built (decision issued on aerial photos / contracts of the period); RS amendments (notably Oct 2022) allowed free legalisation for houses built up to 31 Dec 2013 (Sourced — bih-pravo.org, paragraf.ba, vladahbz.com cantonal law PDF; *cantonal variation is wide — verify the specific canton/entity statute and any live window for the subject parcel*). Inspectorate removal for non-legalisable builds; deadlines are entity/canton-specific and have repeatedly slipped. Indemnity: none. Conformity rule: identify the entity AND canton first — there is no single national rule; require the *rješenje o legalizaciji* (or pre-1992/1991 legality decision) with a *katastar*/ZK footprint match. **Confidence**: MEDIUM (fragmented entity/cantonal law; no single source pinned).
- **MK North Macedonia** — Concept: *бесправно изградени објекти*; legalisation under the *Закон за постапување со бесправно изградени објекти* (Law on Treatment of Unlawful Constructions, 2011). Liability transfer: runs with owner; un-legalised objects not in the *катастар* → impaired transfer/mortgage. The 2011 law triggered ~353,800 self-declarations within 6 months; legalisation fee set very low (~61 MKD/m², ≈ €1/m²) — designed as a fiscal/registration sweep, not a quality gate; municipal backlogs are large (e.g. >15,000 cases in Gostivar) and processing remains slow as of mid-2020s (Sourced — researchgate study on MK cadastre legalisation; *current open/closed status of the declaration window: data not publicly available in this pass — verify with Agencija za katastar na nedvižnosti (AKN) / the line ministry*). Removal for objects outside the declared/legalisable set; enforcement historically weak. Indemnity: none. Conformity rule: require the legalisation *решение* and AKN cadastral entry with matching footprint; a 2011-era self-declaration that never completed is NOT a clean title — verify the *решение* issued, not just that a declaration exists. **Confidence**: LOW-MEDIUM (window status not confirmed this pass).
- **AL Albania** — Concept: *ndërtime pa leje* (constructions without permit) / informal areas; legalisation historically under Law 9482/2006 "On the legalisation, urbanisation and integration of illegal constructions", administered first by ALUIZNI, now folded into the State Cadastre Agency (ASHK / Agjencia Shtetërore e Kadastrës). Liability transfer: runs with owner; without an ASHK legalisation permit + ownership certificate the object cannot be mortgaged or cleanly transferred. A reported "31.12.2025" filing deadline circulates in Albanian geodetic-service material — NOT confirmed against an official ASHK source in this pass; data not publicly verified — verify at ashk.gov.al / e-albania before relying (Sourced for the agency/law framework — en.wikipedia ALUIZNI, venice.coe.int Law 9482; deadline flagged unverified). Conformity rule: require the ASHK legalisation decision AND the issued *certifikatë pronësie* with an ASHK cadastral footprint match — an "in process with ALUIZNI/ASHK" status is not transferable-clean. **Confidence**: LOW-MEDIUM (framework solid; the 2025 deadline explicitly unverified).
- **EE Estonia** — Concept: unauthorised construction / building without a building permit (*ehitusluba*) or notice; cured via retrospective *kasutusluba* (use permit). Removal/alteration orders enforceable; no amnesty cushion. Indemnity: none. Conformity rule: require the *kasutusluba* and confirm the building matches the **ehitisregister** entry; a missing use permit is a financing blocker, not a formality. **Confidence**: MEDIUM-HIGH.

Liability transfer: runs with the current owner; the Real Property Cadastre and Register (Registrų centras) records title and the registered building — an unregistered/unpermitted building or footprint mismatch blocks clean transfer/mortgage; on registration the property is mapped against neighbours for boundary conflict (Sourced — baltic-legal.com on the LT cadastre/register; *country-specific buyer-liability/legalisation procedure detail: data not publicly available in this pass — verify at registrucentras.lt and the State Territorial Planning & Construction Inspectorate (VTPSI)*). ## MENA + GCC + Israel + Türkiye

### Regional pattern

In every jurisdiction here the title/ownership register (TR tapu / IL Tabu / AE DLD title / SA sakk-Najiz / JO DLS / OM mulkiyya / others) records ownership, not permit/as-built conformity — the operative non-compliance check is always a separate municipal / permit-authority check, and a clean title extract will NOT surface a prior owner's unpermitted works (the single most load-bearing buyer-education point). No UK-style legal-indemnity-insurance market for inherited planning non-compliance was identified in any of TR/IL/AE/SA/JO/OM/BH/KW/QA/LB — treat as unavailable across the region. The analogous local trap is conflating a YKB / amnesty certificate with a building permit (TR) and conflating a clean title extract with planning compliance (all 10). Lebanon must carry a prominent active-crisis disclaimer; the OEA/GDUP verification chain was structurally weakened by 2014 Ministry of Interior circulars *before* the 2019 crisis.

### Country one-liners

- **JP Japan** — Concept: two legally distinct categories that MUST NOT be conflated. **既存不適格 (kizon-futekikaku, "existing non-conforming")** = building lawful when built, later fell out of step with tightened codes — NOT illegal, no penalty while used in current form (Building Standards Act / 建築基準法 framework, [japaneselawtranslation.go.jp](https://www.japaneselawtranslation.go.jp/en/laws/view/4024/en); [hachise.com](https://www.hachise.com/how/nonconforming.html)). **違反建築 / 違法建築 (ihan/ihō kenchiku)** = built in breach of the code as it stood — exposed to administrative correction orders, penalties, criminal punishment. ## LATAM + Caribbean

### Regional pattern

The throughline across all LATAM civil-law jurisdictions: the building must be reflected in the land registry (averbação / declaratoria de fábrica / recepción definitiva / final de obra) — a house physically present but absent from the registry is, legally, "a lot with no building," and that gap transfers to the buyer with the title. No title-indemnity product exists for unpermitted-build risk in any LATAM civil-law jurisdiction researched — the universal cure is statutory *regularisation*; budget it as a closing-price adjustment, not an insurable risk (the one nuance: U.S.-style title insurance IS sold in MX (fideicomiso deals) and DO (tourist-zone deals) but standard policies exclude *known* permitting/zoning non-conformity and will not retroactively cure). DO ≠ DM: this section covers the Dominican Republic (DO) only; Dominica (DM) is out of scope for property-deep-dive. All peso (AR) fee/fine figures are nominal and inflation-volatile — treat any quoted ARS amount as `verify at point of purchase`; structural/process facts are stable.

### Country one-liners

- **BR Brazil** — Concept: *obra não averbada / construção irregular*. Two distinct gates: (1) municipal **Habite-se** (*certificado de conclusão / carta de habitação*) — prefeitura attests the build matches the approved *projeto*; (2) **averbação da construção na matrícula** at the Cartório de Registro de Imóveis (RI) — the build is recorded against the property's *matrícula*. A property can have Habite-se but no averbação (registry still shows bare *terreno*), or neither. Liability transfer: the *matrícula* is the legal description of what is sold — if it says "terreno" the buyer legally bought land, not a house; the non-conformity passes with the title and the buyer inherits the regularisation cost and any pre-existing municipal *auto de infração*. The *cartório* will flag a mismatch on a deed-registration attempt; a diligent escritura review (certidão de matrícula atualizada vs physical reality) is the buyer-side check — request a recent certidão de matrícula and compare built m² to what's averbado before signing. Resale / mortgage knock-on: SEVERE — without averbação the bank will not finance (the *garantia hipotecária / alienação fiduciária* attaches to what's on the matrícula), the property cannot be used as loan collateral, and IPTU/ITBI valuations and any future sale are blocked or delayed; the single most common LATAM "I can't sell what I bought" trap. Regularisation / amnesty: (a) ordinary route — obtain Habite-se (or *regularização* + structural laudo where Habite-se is unobtainable) then averbar; (b) **REURB — Regularização Fundiária Urbana, Lei nº 13.465/2017** (federal, in force; promulgated 11 Jul 2017; statute number verified directly at planalto.gov.br), the dedicated mass land/building regularisation regime (REURB-S social / REURB-E specific), run by the *município* with registry integration; municipalities also run local *anistia* programmes case-by-case (verify the specific município's current decree — these lapse and renew). Demolition / enforcement: municipal *embargo* (work-stop) and *demolição* are statutory remedies for insanável violations (built on setback, public easement, environmental APP); fines + IPTU retroactivo are the routine exposure. Indemnity insurance: no Brazilian title-indemnity product covers averbação gaps — the only cure is to regularise; budget it as a closing-price adjustment. Registry/cadastre-conformity rule: a construction must be averbada na matrícula to be legally part of the property; absence is the defining defect of this section in BR. **Confidence**: HIGH (statute number + federal source verified; REURB is real — Lei 13.465/2017 confirmed at planalto.gov.br).
- **MX Mexico** — Concept: *construcción sin licencia / sin manifestación de construcción*; the buyer's specific trap is a mismatch between the **escritura** (and the *uso de suelo*) and the physical reality — extra storeys, an *ampliación*, or a use the *uso de suelo* doesn't permit. State/municipal competence (no single national rule): CDMX uses *manifestación de construcción* + *aviso de terminación de obra*; most states use a municipal *licencia de construcción* + *constancia de alineamiento y número oficial*. Liability transfer: the buyer inherits the unpermitted work, any open municipal *multa*, and the registry/cadastre discrepancy; the *notario público* verifies title and the *certificado de libertad de gravamen* but does NOT as a rule physically verify built m² against the licence — so a clean escritura can still sit on an unpermitted build. Buyer-side duty: cross-check the *boleta predial* / cadastral built area and *uso de suelo* against what is standing. Resale / mortgage knock-on: INFONAVIT/FOVISSSTE/bank mortgage *avalúo* and the *régimen* (esp. *propiedad en condominio* / *régimen de propiedad en condominio*) can be blocked where the build is undeclared; resale stalls on the *avalúo* and on a buyer's own lender diligence. Regularisation / amnesty: municipal *regularización de obra* — submit a *solicitud de regularización*, as-built plans by a certified DRO (*Director Responsable de Obra*), structural-safety *dictamen*, retroactive licence fees + *recargos*, Civil-Protection sign-off; some municipalities run periodic *programas de regularización* (verify the specific municipality's current programme — they are local and time-boxed). Demolition / enforcement: court-/municipality-ordered *demolición* is reserved for insanable cases (encroachment, unsafe structure, *uso de suelo* incompatible); fines from ~MX$10,000 + *clausura* (work-seal) are the routine exposure (2025 reporting; nominal — verify locally). Indemnity insurance: U.S.-style title insurance is available in MX (First American/Stewart via *fideicomiso* deals) but standard policies exclude *known* permitting/zoning non-conformity — it will NOT retroactively cure an unpermitted build; treat as no cover for this risk. Registry/cadastre-conformity rule: escritura + *uso de suelo* + cadastral built area must reconcile with the physical structure; the *uso de suelo* mismatch is the MX-specific kill-switch. **Confidence**: MEDIUM-HIGH (process verified across municipal + secondary sources; figures are state-variable and nominal — flagged).
- **CL Chile** — Concept: *obra sin permiso de edificación / sin recepción definitiva*. The two municipal gates are the **permiso de edificación** and the **recepción definitiva** from the *Dirección de Obras Municipales (DOM)* — a build without *recepción definitiva* is informally regular at best. Liability transfer: passes with title; the buyer inherits the missing *recepción*, the discrepancy vs the *Conservador de Bienes Raíces* inscription, and any DOM/Juzgado de Policía Local proceeding. Resale / mortgage knock-on: SEVERE — banks require *recepción definitiva* for a mortgage; a self-built or *ampliación* without it is effectively unmortgageable and resale-impaired until regularised. Regularisation / amnesty: **"Ley del Mono" — Ley N° 20.898** (in force; the most recent extension entered into force 25 Apr 2023 and runs the simplified regime to **31 Dec 2027** — date-stamped, calendar-actionable, verify at bcn.cl / minvu.gob.cl before each output; if a buyer is relying on Ley del Mono near the sunset, flag the deadline explicitly), a simplified DOM procedure granting *permiso* + *recepción* simultaneously with reduced municipal *derechos*. Eligibility caps (from secondary/architect sources — verify current statutory text): typically dwelling <90 m² with *avalúo fiscal* <1,000 UF, or <140 m² with <2,000 UF, no pending DOM/JPL claims. Demolition / enforcement: DOM may order *demolición* for insanable breaches and levy fines; the practical exposure is unmortgageability rather than demolition for routine ampliaciones. Indemnity insurance: no Chilean title-indemnity product for missing *recepción* — Ley del Mono is the cure; budget the regularisation. Registry/cadastre-conformity rule: *recepción definitiva* from the DOM is the conformity anchor; absence is the defining CL defect. **Confidence**: HIGH (statute number + 2027 sunset cross-checked across MINVU/BCN + secondary; date-stamped and flagged for re-verify).
- **AR Argentina** — Concept: *obra sin permiso de construcción / en contravención* and *metros no declarados* (sin *final de obra* / *conforme a obra*). Municipal competence (each *municipio* has its own *Código de Edificación*); province-level cadastral declaration (e.g. **ARBA** in Buenos Aires Province) is a *separate* obligation from municipal regularisation and does NOT cure a missing municipal *final de obra*. ## Africa + Anglo non-EU

### Regional pattern

The code-compliance / planning-conformity obligation runs with the property — the current owner inherits the duty to legalise, abate fines, or remove a prior owner's work; the post-completion conformity instrument (certificate of occupancy / Code Compliance Certificate / occupation certificate / approved building plans / permis d'habiter) matching the as-built is the operative buyer check, not the title register, which records ownership only. US / CA / AU enforcement, retroactive-permitting and title-insurance scope are set at state/province/municipality level — NSW, Ontario and California/Florida are illustrative exemplars only, never national rules. Title-insurance treatment splits sharply: standard US ALTA policies generally do NOT cover unpermitted work (only narrow carrier-specific enhanced endorsements do); Canadian/AU/NZ residential title insurance commonly DOES cover pre-existing work-order / permit / by-law non-compliance where the insured was unaware (policy-specific, not government-sourced); SA/EG/NG/KE/MU/MA/TN/GH/RW/CV/SC/NA/BW have no standard product (NA/BW: Roman-Dutch deeds-system jurisdictions where title insurance is not standard, as in ZA — assume not available; verify). Several conformity instruments are NOT amnesties: NZ Certificate of Acceptance and AU NSW Building Information Certificate are explicitly weaker than full compliance certificates.

### Country one-liners

- **US United States** — Concept: "unpermitted work" / "work without a permit"; legalisation route is a *retroactive* / *after-the-fact* / *"as-built"* permit, defined jurisdiction-by-jurisdiction by the Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ — city/county building dept). A separate failure mode is a **certificate of occupancy (CO) mismatch** (actual use/structure differs from the CO; some jurisdictions also issue CofO/CofC). No federal building code — model codes (IBC/IRC) are adopted and amended locally. Liability transfer: code-compliance obligation runs with the property — the current owner inherits the duty to legalise, abate fines, or remove work even though a prior owner built it (general US conveyancing principle; cross-check the specific AHJ — ⚠️ aggregator-sourced, not a single statute) [[Salisbury Township PA](https://salisburylehighpa.gov/the-risks-of-buying-a-home-with-unpermitted-renovation-work/)] [[Nolo](https://www.nolo.com/legal-encyclopedia/discovering-unpermitted-construction-when-selling-your-home.html)]. - **NZ New Zealand** — Concept: "unconsented work" / building work done without a building consent. The remedial instrument is a **Certificate of Acceptance (CoA)** under **s 96–99A, Building Act 2004**: a territorial authority *may* issue a CoA only if satisfied, to the best of its knowledge and belief and on reasonable grounds, that **insofar as it could ascertain** the work complies with the Building Code — i.e. a CoA is explicitly weaker than a Code Compliance Certificate (CCC) [[Building Act 2004 s 96 (NZ Legislation)](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2004/0072/latest/DLM306854.html)] [[Auckland Council — CoA](https://www.aucklandcouncil.govt.nz/en/building-and-consents/certificate-acceptance-unconsented-building-work.html)]. ## 7 universal mitigations

1. **Commission a cadastral-vs-reality conformity check before the offer** (cross-ref `--surveyor`) — compare the as-built footprint and internal layout against the cadastre and the permit history, not just the title.
2. **Demand the full permit chain from the seller in writing** — original building permit + every subsequent works permit + the completion/conformity certificate (IT certificato di agibilità, JP 検査済証, UK Building Regs completion certificate, HK Occupation Permit). Absence is the red flag.
3. **Price the regularisation, not the hope of it** — obtain a written feasibility + cost estimate from a local design professional for the specific defect, and confirm the amnesty window is open against the primary municipal source on decision-day.
4. **Make the offer conditional on regularisation or a priced abatement** — a conformity contingency, not a verbal assurance, so an un-regularisable defect is an exit.
5. **Confirm mortgageability with the actual lender before committing** — many decline unpermitted works outright; do not assume.
6. **Do not rely on a legal-indemnity policy you have triggered** — understand that enquiring with the council voids it; treat indemnity as a fallback for genuinely dormant risk only, where the market exists.
7. **Re-verify the regularisation/amnesty status within 30 days of decision-day** — these windows open and close on political timelines (Western Balkans 2025-26, IT Salva Casa, PT Simplex); a stale assumption here is a six-figure error.
