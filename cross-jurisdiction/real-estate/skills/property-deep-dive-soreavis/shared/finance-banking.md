# Universal `--finance` Section — Banking Sub-section Extension

Foreign-buyer **bank-account access** layer — tax-ID prerequisite chains, FATCA acceptance for US persons, named expat-banking arms with minimums, hidden traps (Catch-22 cycles, residency-coupled online banking, FX-account restrictions), and the 2024-2026 reform calendar that re-shapes onboarding. Loaded when `--finance` is invoked alongside the existing per-country mortgage logic in each playbook.

**Snapshot**: May 2026.

**Scope**: this doc supplies the *banking-access* layer — how a non-resident buyer actually opens an account in country X to receive notary funds, pay property tax, and hold rental income. The *credit* layer (mortgage availability, LTV cap, 10-yr fixed rate, FX-loan rules) lives in `shared/finance.md` and each country playbook's `--finance` section. A buyer often needs both: a mortgage approval is meaningless if the funds can't be wired to a local IBAN, and a domiciliation account is mandatory in many lender contracts (FR/IT/CH).

## Universal contract

For the property's country, return:

1. **Tax-ID name + acquisition channel + lead time** (e.g., DE Steueridentifikationsnummer mailed 2-6 wk after Anmeldung; ES NIE via consulate 4-12 wk or police in-country; IT codice fiscale at consulate often same-day; BR CPF at consulate ABROAD 1-2 wk; KZ IIN at PSC inside KZ 2-3 days). State whether tax-ID can be obtained BEFORE bank account or whether it's a Catch-22.
2. **Non-resident account-opening pattern** — one of: (a) fully remote from abroad; (b) on a visiting trip with notarised POA; (c) in-person mandatory at branch; (d) referral-only / private-bank-only. Name 1-2 specific banks per country with documented non-resident channels.
3. **FATCA acceptance for US persons** — bucketed as **Standard** (most major retail banks open US-tax-person accounts routinely), **Restricted** (only specific desks / Premier-tier / private banking accept), or **Refused** (nearly all banks refuse; only named expat-arms work). Name 1-3 expat-banking arms or international desks that DO accept US persons (HSBC Expat Jersey is the universal fallback; Charles Schwab International for US-side investing; Citi IPB Singapore USD 200k AUM; UBS Wealth Management; LGT Bank).
4. **Expat-banking-arm minimum** where applicable (HSBC Premier ~USD 75-100k AUM; LGT Bank CHF 1M; Lombard Odier CHF 1M; UBS Wealth USD 2M+).
5. **Hidden trap** — country-specific friction the published process does not mention (DE Anmeldung Catch-22 + SCHUFA score-zero; ES Modelo 720 reporting; PL PESEL-or-bust; IL te'udat zehut zara distinction; AR Corralito-2001 memory; SARB SDA R1M / FIA R10M; CN SAFE USD 50k/yr face-scan; AU foreign-buyer ban on established dwellings; CA non-Canadian purchase ban).
6. **2024-2026 reforms** (date-stamped, regulator-source-cited) — AMLA Frankfurt 1 Jul 2025; EU Instant Payments Reg 2024/886 receive-9 Jan 2025 / send + VoP 9 Oct 2025; UK FCA Consumer Duty closed-products 31 Jul 2024; CN PBoC + CBIRC + CSRC tightening 1 Jan 2026; AU FRCGW 12.5%→15% 1 Jan 2025 + foreign-buyer ban 1 Apr 2025; NZ partial reopen 19 Dec 2025; HK CARF + CRS 2.0 from 2028/2029.
7. **Confidence**: HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW per the skill's contract — HIGH where named-bank policy is published; MEDIUM where pattern is well-documented but bank-by-bank execution varies; LOW where bank lists/minimums shift quarterly without public source.

## ⚠️ 7 cross-cutting traps

1. **Tax-ID Catch-22 is the universal trap.** The order is `tax-ID → bank account → utilities → property tax`, but in many jurisdictions one prerequisite needs another that needs the first (DE: Anmeldung needs address → bank wants Anmeldung → landlord wants bank-confirmation; PL: PESEL needs registered address → online banking needs PESEL → registered-address needs bank for utilities; CR: Firma Digital needs DIMEX → DIMEX needs in-country presence → presence is hard without account). The workarounds are bank- and country-specific (BR CPF via consulate, AT Erste/Sparkasse pre-open without Anmeldung, DE Commerzbank international desk pre-arrival, HSBC Expat Jersey universal). Without a workaround, the timeline doubles.
2. **FATCA is signed in 100+ jurisdictions but executed bank-by-bank.** US persons routinely face de-facto refusal at branch-level even where the IGA is in force (CY, KW, SA, CN, FI mainstream retail, LV/CY post-Russian de-risking, EU mainstream Sparkasse / Volksbank / Crédit Agricole). The reliable channels are HSBC Expat Jersey, Premier-tier carve-outs (HSBC / DBS Treasures / UBS Wealth Management / Citi IPB / Charles Schwab International), and named private-bank desks (Lombard Odier, LGT, Pictet, Julius Baer at HNW). Caucasus (GE, AM) is the standout regional exception where US-person retail onboarding is genuinely Standard.
3. **Online banking is residency-coupled even where the account isn't.** Switzerland (PostFinance, ZKB, Raiffeisen) restricts online banking to residents even when an account exists; CN UnionPay-only access for some non-res accounts; KR ARC + visa-status check; Norway D-number holders need BankID issued bank-by-bank; Sweden samordningsnummer accounts often lack BankID/Swish (major operational restriction in cashless SE); JP "non-resident yen account" excludes overseas remittance + cash card + bill payments. A "I have an account" claim is not a "I can transact" claim.
4. **Mortgage-domiciliation requirements survive even where the tied-sale law was annulled.** France's Loi Sapin II 2017 / Decree 2017-1099 was annulled by Conseil d'État (5 Dec 2018) and abrogated by Loi PACTE 22 May 2019 — yet Crédit Agricole / BNP Paribas / Société Générale still require salary/pension domiciliation in practice as a "rate-condition" rather than a tied sale. CH lenders demand domiciliation for the loan term. IT lenders typically bundle account + mortgage. Buyers should treat this as a standing requirement.
5. **Currency-control and exchange-control regimes constrain repatriation even where account opening is easy.** ZA SARB (SDA R1M + FIA R10M, TCS PIN required); IN LRS USD 250k/yr + TCS 20% > INR 10 lakh; CN SAFE USD 50k/yr individual cap with face-scan; KR USD 50k/yr + USD 20k cash-purchase reporting; AR cepo cambiario (lifted Apr 2025 but USD account access for non-res still constrained); MA + TN Office des Changes / BCT non-resident-dirham/dinar regime — repatriation only against documented original FX inflow; NG Certificate of Capital Importation (CCI) at inflow is the gating doc for property-sale-proceed exit. Bank account is necessary but not sufficient for capital movement.
6. **Caribbean + Mediterranean small-banking-sector de-risking compounds property-buyer friction.** CY post-2018 Russian-shell cleanup + 2013 bail-in scar tissue; LV post-ABLV-2018 de-risking; MT MFSA enforcement post-Pilatus + HSBC Malta sale to CrediaBank Dec 2025 (transition uncertainty through 2027); BS / BB / BZ post-CFATF cleanup tightened; MC FATF grey-list Jun 2024 + EU AMLD high-risk addition proposed 2025. KYC documentation is heavier than pre-2020 reputation suggests.
7. **Foreign-buyer bans + property-purchase restrictions interact with banking-access policy in non-obvious ways.** AU foreign-individual ban on established dwellings 1 Apr 2025 – 31 Mar 2027 (only new builds); CA non-Canadian-purchase ban extended to 1 Jan 2027; NZ Overseas Investment Act partial-reopen 19 Dec 2025 for AIP/Investor visa holders at NZD 5M+; MV constitutional freehold ban (only 99-yr SEZ leasehold > USD 1B); NG / GH / KE 50-99-yr leasehold caps for foreigners. A "yes you can open an account" is not a "yes you can buy property" — verify the property regime separately at `--permits` / `countries/<iso2>/playbook.md`.

## Western Europe + EFTA + microstates + UK

### Regional pattern

Across the 23 jurisdictions covered, four shared structural patterns govern non-resident bank-account access. (1) **Tax-ID-before-bank rule is universal in EU/EEA**: every Eurozone member except IT (codice fiscale at consulate, often same-day) imposes a domestic identifier (NIE/NIF/AFM/BSN/CPR/HETU/personnummer/PPSN/Steuer-ID) that must precede or coincide with KYC. The handful of exceptions are non-residents using EU "international" desks (BNP Paribas, HSBC, Société Générale) which can pre-open with passport + foreign tax-ID and demand the local TIN within 30-90 days. (2) **SEPA + AMLD6 + IPR convergence is real but bank-by-bank execution diverges**: Verification of Payee mandatory 9 Oct 2025 (Eurozone); receipt of instant payments mandatory 9 Jan 2025; sending mandatory 9 Oct 2025 ([Reg. EU 2024/886](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/886/oj/eng)). The single AML rulebook (Regulation 2024/1624) becomes directly applicable 10 Jul 2027 with a EUR 10,000 cash-payment cap ([eucrim](https://eucrim.eu/news/the-eu-new-aml-single-rulebook-regulation/)); AMLA in Frankfurt began initial operations 1 Jul 2025, full direct supervision 1 Jan 2028 ([EUR-Lex summary](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/EN/legal-content/summary/authority-for-anti-money-laundering-and-countering-the-financing-of-terrorism.html)).

(3) **FATCA-restrictive culture in EU mainstream retail**: most major retail banks decline US persons by default at the branch level. The reliable channels are HSBC Expat (Jersey), Premier-tier carve-outs, and named expat/private-banking arms with explicit US-person desks (UBS, Lombard Odier, LGT, Pictet at HNW levels). The CJEU is examining FATCA-IGA GDPR compliance in 2025-26 ([Mayer Brown 2025](https://www.mayerbrown.com/en/insights/publications/2025/12/eu-court-of-justice-to-examine-gdpr-compliance-of-fatca-related-bank-data-transfers)) but no operational change yet. (4) **Open Banking divergence post-Brexit**: UK FCA Consumer Duty (full effect 31 Jul 2024 incl. closed products) and ongoing Variable Recurring Payments rollout sit alongside but separate from EU PSD3/PSR drafts; UK challengers (Starling/Monzo/Revolut) are residency-flexible but Barclays/Lloyds/HSBC have closed most non-resident retail accounts since 2023 ([Experts for Expats](https://www.expertsforexpats.com/advice/expat-money-and-finance/barclays-and-other-uk-financial-institutions-are-closing-british-expats-accounts)).

(5) **Microstate compression**: MC, AD, LI, SM, MT, IS — small-market banking constrained by EU passporting + correspondent-bank de-risking. MC was added to FATF grey list Jun 2024; EU AMLD high-risk list addition was proposed 2025 ([Fincrime Central](https://fincrimecentral.com/monaco-grey-list-eu-aml-compliance-impact-2025/)); MC, SM, AD all face progressively tighter CDD windows from EU monetary-agreement obligations (AD must transpose 2024 AML package by 31 Dec 2028).

### Country one-liners

**AD (Andorra)** — NRT (Número de Registre Tributari) issued by Departament de Tributs i Fronteres on application with passport, ~1-2 weeks (verify at [tributs.ad](https://www.tributs.ad/)); only three banks (Andbank, Creand ex-Crèdit Andorrà, MoraBanc) — non-resident accounts case-by-case, in-person only, source-of-funds documentation enhanced; private-banking entry est. EUR 350-500k ([andorrainsiders.com](https://andorrainsiders.com/en/banks-accounts-payments-sepa-andorra/)); FATCA: **Restricted** — handled at private-banking tier only, named US-person desk not publicly disclosed, verify directly; trap: no SEPA Instant participation symmetry with EU mainland for retail-level operations; **confidence MEDIUM** — bank policy is not public, "contact us" is the published process.

**AT (Austria)** — Steuerliche Identifikationsnummer assigned by Finanzamt after Anmeldung at Meldeamt (within 3 days of move-in), Steuernummer separate; non-residents can open without Anmeldung at Erste/Sparkasse and Raiffeisen Bank International, in-person mandatory, est. EUR 15-20/year fee ([Erste/Sparkasse non-resident](https://www.sparkasse.at/erstebank-en/privatkunden/konto-karten); [housinganywhere.com](https://housinganywhere.com/Austria/opening-bank-account-in-austria)); FATCA: **Restricted** — Erste Private Banking and RBI accept US persons selectively, retail mostly refuses; trap: many landlords require an Austrian account before they sign — bank-first sequence is enforceable; **confidence MEDIUM** — fees and policy verified, US-person specifics not public per bank.

**BE (Belgium)** — National Register Number (Rijksregisternummer / NN) issued by commune within ~2-4 weeks after registration; non-residents get a "bis-nummer" via Crossroads Bank — banks (KBC, BNP Paribas Fortis, ING, Belfius) accept non-resident applications, expect 2-6 weeks KYC, NN required within ~90 days of opening to keep account fully active ([expatica.com](https://www.expatica.com/be/finance/banking/opening-a-bank-account-in-belgium-741553/)); FATCA: **Restricted** — Belgian court has held FATCA-IGA breaches GDPR (2023), banks remain cautious with US persons; trap: itsme app and full digital onboarding generally unavailable without Belgian eID; **confidence MEDIUM** — bank-level execution variable, NN-deadline window verified.

**CH (Switzerland)** — No single tax-ID for individuals at federal level — cantonal Einwohnerkontrolle registration within 8 days of move-in, AHV (social security) number issued separately; non-residents: UBS retail open requires CH tax residency ([UBS Switzerland](https://www.ubs.com/ch/en/services/accounts-and-cards/daily-banking/private-account-adults/personal-account.html)) — PostFinance and ZKB accept with "Swiss connection," CHF 360/yr non-resident fee at UBS and ZKB, EUR 25/mo at PostFinance ([thepoorswiss.com](https://thepoorswiss.com/best-swiss-bank-non-resident/); [moneyland.ch](https://www.moneyland.ch/en/swiss-bank-fees-abroad)); FATCA: **Restricted** — UBS Wealth Management and Pictet/Julius Baer/Lombard Odier maintain US-person desks; UBS minimum est. USD 2M for private-wealth ([top10privatebanks.com](https://top10privatebanks.com/bank/ubs-private-banking.html)); trap: e-banking access often residency-restricted (PostFinance, ZKB) — non-resident accounts often access-limited; FINMA Circ. 2016/7 (video ID) under partial revision through 27 Feb 2026 ([Loyens & Loeff](https://www.loyensloeff.com/insights/news--events/news/digital-onboarding-revisited--finma-proposes-amendments-to-its-finma-circular-on-video--and-online-identification/)); **confidence HIGH** — fees published, US-person policy clear at top tier.

**DE (Germany)** — Steueridentifikationsnummer (IdNr) automatically mailed by BZSt 2-4 weeks after Anmeldung at Bürgeramt ([BZSt](https://www.bzst.de/EN/Private_individuals/Tax_identification_number/tax_identification_number_node.html)); non-residents: Commerzbank, Deutsche Bank Premium accept; Postbank generally won't open non-resident retail; mass challengers (N26, Bunq) work for EU residents only; FATCA: **Restricted** — Deutsche Bank Wealth Management accepts US persons; many retail banks (Sparkassen, Volksbanken) decline at branch level; trap: classic "Anmeldung Catch-22" — landlord wants bank → bank wants registered address → registration wants address — workaround is Commerzbank/Deutsche Bank international desk pre-arrival; SCHUFA score-zero penalty for new arrivals; reform: BaFin AML interpretation guidance updated 1 Feb 2025 ([HSF Kramer](https://www.hsfkramer.com/notes/fsrandcorpcrime/2025-posts/key-changes-bafin-aml)), Finanzmarktdigitalisierungsgesetz (FinmadiG) transposes EU AMLR provisions; **confidence HIGH** — IdNr lead-time and Catch-22 documented at BZSt.

**DK (Denmark)** — CPR-nummer issued by ICS (International Citizen Service) or commune after residence permit + address; bank account requires CPR + MitID; MitID needs CPR + valid ID document ([nemkonto.dk](https://www.nemkonto.dk/en/citizen/nemkonto-for-citizens/assigning-a-nemkonto/); [lifeindenmark.borger.dk](https://lifeindenmark.borger.dk/apps-and-digital-services/nemkonto-your-public-bank-account)); non-residents: very limited — usually requires CPR; FATCA: **Standard** for Danske Bank/Nordea/Jyske once CPR is in place — declared US persons routinely onboarded but enhanced KYC; trap: NemID retired 31 Oct 2023 — MitID-only; non-resident NemKonto can be a foreign IBAN once you have CPR + MitID ([nemkonto.dk foreign account](https://www.nemkonto.dk/en/citizen/nemkonto-for-citizens/assigning-a-nemkonto/)); **confidence HIGH** — CPR + MitID circular dependency well-documented.

**ES (Spain)** — NIE via Spanish consulate abroad (~2-6 weeks, sometimes 4-8) or in-person at Comisaría de Policía in Spain ([costaluzlawyers.com](https://www.costaluzlawyers.com/nie-spain/); [hilivsolution.com](https://www.hilivsolution.com/blog/articles/get-nie-spain)); non-residents: Banco Santander, BBVA, CaixaBank accept with NIE — non-resident certificate (renewed every 2 years, ~EUR 15-30) is a recurring cost; FATCA: **Restricted** — Santander Private Wealth and BBVA Premium accept US persons; mass-market retail varies; trap: NIE Catch-22 (need NIE for account, but consular route can be slow); Modelo 720 (foreign-asset reporting) penalty regime softened by Law 5/2022 post-CJEU C-788/19 (27 Jan 2022) — fines now EUR 20-20,000 per data point with 4-year limitation ([lawants.com](https://www.lawants.com/en/modelo-720/); [tejadasolicitors.com](https://tejadasolicitors.com/blog/form-720-spain/)) but obligation persists for tax residents; **confidence HIGH** — NIE flow verified, Modelo 720 reform sourced.

**FI (Finland)** — Henkilötunnus (HETU) issued by DVV (Digital and Population Data Services Agency) — non-resident "international account" possible at Nordea/OP but limited (often no online banking, no Finnish e-ID for suomi.fi); HETU + valid ID + right to reside required for full Nordea onboarding ([Nordea FI](https://www.nordea.fi/en/personal/our-services/accounts-payments/how-to-open-banking-services-when-you-are-new-to-finland.html)); FATCA: **Restricted** — Nordea Private Banking accepts US persons, OP retail typically declines; trap: even with HETU, non-residents often can't get Nordea ID app / OP-mobile required for SCA/Strong Customer Authentication; **confidence MEDIUM** — official Nordea page documents flow but US-person stance not on bank's public site.

**FR (France)** — Numéro fiscal SPI issued by DGFiP after first tax declaration OR via espace particulier creation (request via [impots.gouv.fr](https://www.impots.gouv.fr/) — code by post in ~3 weeks); non-residents: BNP Paribas International Clients Desk, Société Générale Sogexia/International, HSBC Continental Europe accept remote pre-onboarding with English-language support; in-person typical for full activation ([connexionfrance.com](https://www.connexionfrance.com/practical/can-you-open-a-bank-account-in-france-without-a-local-address/636684)); FATCA: **Restricted** — SocGen Private Banking and BNP Paribas Wealth accept US persons; LCL/Crédit Agricole/Banque Populaire frequently refuse; trap: mortgage-domiciliation clause history — Loi Sapin II 2017 framework and Decree 2017-1099 (allowing 10-yr salary domiciliation tied to rate benefit) was annulled by Conseil d'État (5 Dec 2018) and the regime was abrogated by Loi PACTE 22 May 2019 — clause is no longer enforceable as a tied sale ([dalloz-actualite.fr](https://www.dalloz-actualite.fr/flash/domiciliation-bancaire-conseil-d-etat-annule-decret-n-2017-1099-du-14-juin-2017); [legifiscal.fr](https://www.legifiscal.fr/actualites-fiscales/2132-credit-immobilier-clause-domiciliation-salaire-nouveau-changement.html)); **confidence HIGH** — Sapin II / PACTE chronology cleanly verified.

**GR (Greece)** — AFM issued same-day at any DOY tax office on passport + proof of address; non-residents apply at DOY Katoikon Exoterikou, Mesogeion 207, Athens, must nominate a Greek tax representative ([Lexidy guide](https://www.lexidy.com/services/greece/immigration/tax-id-number/); [gov.gr](https://www.gov.gr/en/arxes/anexartete-arkhe-demosion-esodon-aade/anexartete-arkhe-demosion-esodon-aade/apodose-arithmou-phorologikou-metroou-aphm-kai-kleidarithmou-se-phusiko-prosopo)); banks: Eurobank offers remote opening; Piraeus, Alpha, NBG generally require in-branch; FATCA: **Restricted** — Eurobank Private Banking accepts US persons; retail declines; trap: post-2024 HFSF privatisation completion (Eurobank/Alpha consolidation) — KYC standards harmonised but slow; **confidence MEDIUM** — DOY/AFM flow well-documented, bank-by-bank US-person policy not public.

**IE (Ireland)** — PPSN (Personal Public Service Number) issued by Department of Social Protection — appointment system, can take 4-12 weeks; non-residents: AIB and BoI accept Non-Irish TRN if applicant resides outside Ireland ([AIB non-resident requirements](https://aib.ie/our-products/savings-and-deposits/non-resident-personal-identification-requirements); [aib.ie PPSN](https://aib.ie/our-products/savings-and-deposits/ppsn-details)); AIB requires in-branch presentation within 30 days of account open for non-residents; FATCA: **Standard** — Irish banks routinely onboard US persons (post-IGA); trap: PPSN Catch-22 if returning Irish citizen with no recent address; Central Credit Register check standard; **confidence HIGH** — AIB pages are explicit on non-resident steps.

**IS (Iceland)** — Kennitala issued by Þjóðskrá (Registers Iceland) — must apply in person, requires registered address ([movingtoiceland.com](https://movingtoiceland.com/kennitala-iceland/); [landsbankinn.is](https://www.landsbankinn.is/en/news/2020/09/14/moving-to-iceland-and-need-a-bank-account)); non-residents: Landsbankinn, Íslandsbanki, Arion — kennitala prerequisite, no remote opening, in-person mandatory; Íslandsbanki government stake fully sold May 2025 ([islandsbanki.is](https://www.islandsbanki.is/en/milestone/banking-in-iceland)); FATCA: **Restricted** — banks comply with US IGA but US-person retail accounts limited; trap: kennitala requires physical presence + registered address — non-resident remote onboarding not viable; **confidence HIGH** — Þjóðskrá and Landsbankinn pages confirm.

**IT (Italy)** — Codice fiscale at any Italian consulate abroad or Agenzia delle Entrate office in Italy — often same-day (consulate may take ~5 working days) ([consdetroit.esteri.it](https://consdetroit.esteri.it/en/servizi-consolari-e-visti/servizi-per-il-cittadino-italiano/codice-fiscale-italian-tax-code/); [valenteit.com](https://valenteit.com/how-to-get-your-italian-fiscal-code-and-open-a-bank-account-foreign-buyers-complete-guide/)); non-residents: Intesa Sanpaolo "non-resident" account and UniCredit accept during purchase trips, partial digital onboarding + video verification; PostePay, Hype, Isybank as digital alternatives (residency-flexible); FATCA: **Restricted** — Intesa Private Banking and UniCredit Private Banking accept US persons; retail varies; trap: non-resident accounts have higher fees and operational limits ([tuttosemplice.com](https://blog.tuttosemplice.com/en/bank-account-in-italy-for-foreigners-a-complete-guide-to-opening-one-in-2025/)); SEPA Instant via "Bonifico Istantaneo" mandatory across SEPA from Oct 2025; **confidence HIGH** — codice fiscale flow and bank-by-bank pattern documented.

**LI (Liechtenstein)** — Tax-ID (Steuernummer) issued by Steuerverwaltung after Anmeldung; non-resident banking is private-banking-tilted: LGT Bank CHF 1,000,000 minimum, VP Bank CHF 1,000,000, Kaiser Partner Privatbank CHF 500,000 ([thebanks.eu/Kaiser](https://thebanks.eu/banks/9873#products); [easyglobalbanking.com](https://www.easyglobalbanking.com/alternative-banking-hubs/liechtenstein-private-banking-for-non-residents/)); FATCA: **Restricted** — LGT Bank handles complex US-person profiles regularly; trap: virtually no retail-tier non-resident option — entry minimums are private-banking grade; FMA + EFTA Surveillance Authority working on DAC8 transposition (EU deadline 31 Dec 2025); **confidence HIGH** — bank minimums published, US-person handling sourced.

**LU (Luxembourg)** — Matricule (13-digit national ID) issued at registration; non-residents accepted at BIL, BCEE/Spuerkeess, BGL BNP Paribas with enhanced KYC; private banking minimums est. EUR 250k-1M depending on tier (verify at [cssf.lu](https://www.cssf.lu/) and bank-direct); FATCA: **Restricted** — BIL Private Banking and BGL Wealth accept US persons selectively; trap: CSSF Circular 24/856 codifies AML/CFT due-diligence and tightened beneficial-ownership checks ([fund-xp.lu](https://fund-xp.lu/uncategorized/cssf-faq-on-circular-cssf-24-856/)); DAC8 transposition extending automatic exchange to crypto + insurance + tax rulings ([EY 2025](https://www.ey.com/en_gl/technical/tax-alerts/luxembourg-to-transpose-dac8-extending-automatic-information-exchange-on-crypto-assets-life-insurance-income-tax-rulings-for-individuals-and-digital-currencies)); **confidence MEDIUM** — bank minimums vary, exact thresholds not published.

**MC (Monaco)** — No personal tax-ID for individuals (no income tax for residents except French nationals); residency requires bank-letter of EUR ~500k deposit + clean record; banks: SBM, CFM Indosuez (Crédit Agricole), CMB (Mediobanca) — minimums for residency relationship EUR 500k; non-resident relationships at est. EUR 2M+, full private banking EUR 1M-3M+ ([monacorg.com](https://www.monacorg.com/private-banks-in-monaco/); [easyglobalbanking.com](https://www.easyglobalbanking.com/alternative-banking-hubs/open-a-bank-account-in-monaco/)); FATCA: **Restricted** — CMB and CFM accept US persons at private-banking tier; trap: FATF grey-listed Jun 2024 → enhanced cross-border due-diligence costs and timelines through 2025-26; Sovereign Order 11.242 of 30 May 2025 amended Monaco's AML framework (publ. Journal de Monaco 6 Jun 2025) ([Phoenix Consulting](https://phoenix.mc/en/aml-cft-10-sovereign-order-11-242-of-may-30-2025/); [Fincrime Central](https://fincrimecentral.com/monaco-grey-list-eu-aml-compliance-impact-2025/)); **confidence HIGH** — minimums and grey-list status well-sourced.

**MT (Malta)** — Tax Number issued by Commissioner for Revenue post-residency registration; non-residents: BOV (Bank of Valletta) and HSBC Malta accept with enhanced KYC, often slow ([imin-malta.com](https://imin-malta.com/blog/banks-in-malta/)); FATCA: **Restricted** — HSBC Malta accepts US persons (HSBC group policy); BOV variable; trap: HSBC Malta sale to CrediaBank (Greece) signed Dec 2025, completion expected early 2027 — service uncertainty in transition; MFSA enforcement remains heightened post-Pilatus aftermath; banking due-diligence widely reported as lengthy; **confidence MEDIUM** — HSBC Malta sale verified, US-person desk policy bank-direct only.

## CEE + Western Balkans + Baltics + Caucasus

### Regional pattern

Three regulatory tiers operate in parallel here. **EU-AMLD jurisdictions** (PL, CZ, SK, HU, SI, HR, RO, BG, EE, LV, LT, CY) inherit Directive 2024/1640 (AMLD6, in force 9 Jul 2024, transposition deadline 10 Jul 2027) and the Frankfurt-headquartered EU Anti-Money Laundering Authority (AMLA), which began operations 1 Jul 2025 under Regulation 2024/1620 ([EUR-Lex 2024/1620](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32024R1620), [DLA Piper](https://www.dlapiper.com/en-us/insights/publications/global-anti-corruption-perspective/new-eu-anti-money-laundering-rules-what-to-know)). **EU-aspirant Western Balkans + Moldova** (AL, BA, ME, MK, RS, MD) run AMLD-equivalent national regimes audited via MONEYVAL, with FATF status as the most actionable signal. **Caucasus** (AM, AZ, GE) sits outside EU AMLD entirely; their central banks (NBG, CBA, CBAR) operate FATCA-IGA but with quite different US-person posture.

**Euro-adoption shake-out.** HR joined the eurozone 1 Jan 2023 (conversion rate 7.53450 HRK/EUR, IBANs unchanged) ([ECB](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/euro/changeover/croatia/html/index.en.html)). BG follows 1 Jan 2026 at 1.95583 BGN/EUR ([ECB pr250708](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/pr/date/2025/html/ecb.pr250708~b9676a9fa8.en.html), [Council](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2025/07/08/bulgaria-ready-to-use-the-euro-from-1-january-2026-council-takes-final-steps/)). RO has slipped: ERM II entry now targeted 2026/2027, eurozone 2029/2030 per government statements Feb 2026 ([Wikipedia: Romania and the euro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romania_and_the_euro)). ME uses EUR unilaterally (no IGA with ECB). SK adopted EUR 2009; SI 2007. The non-euro CEE block (PL, CZ, HU) and non-euro Balkans/Baltics-EU set (none — all Baltics are eurozone) work in domestic currency with EUR sub-accounts.

**Post-Russia/Ukraine de-risking.** LV and CY took the heaviest hit. Latvia's ABLV liquidation (board decision 26 Feb 2018, FinCEN s.311 finding) restructured the entire Baltic non-resident sector; depositor compensation passed from Citadele to Latvijas Banka 1 Apr 2025 ([Latvijas Banka](https://www.bank.lv/en/operational-areas/customer-protection/information-for-former-bank-clients)). Cyprus has carried bail-in scar tissue since 2013 plus EU/UK sanctions cleanups 2022-2024. Russian/Belarussian-passport de-banking is near-universal across CEE-EU. CY, LV, EE additionally screen non-EU non-residents harder than the regional baseline.

**SEPA expansion.** RS joined SEPA geographic scope 22 May 2025 (operational from Nov 2025, earliest ORD May 2026); MD, ME, MK, AL preceded ([EC Enlargement](https://enlargement.ec.europa.eu/news/european-commission-welcomes-inclusion-serbia-single-euro-payments-area-2025-05-22_en)). EU Instant Payments Regulation 2024/886 imposed SEPA-Instant receive 9 Jan 2025, send + Verification of Payee (VoP) free-of-charge 9 Oct 2025 across all eurozone PSPs ([EUR-Lex 2024/886](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/886/oj/eng), [PwC Legal](https://legal.pwc.de/en/news/articles/verification-of-payee-requirements-vop-under-the-eus-instant-payments-regulation-ipr)). Non-euro CEE PSPs follow on extended timelines.

**Caucasus as US-person fallback.** GE (TBC, Bank of Georgia) and AM (Ameriabank, Evocabank, ID Bank) are the two regional jurisdictions where FATCA W-9 onboarding is *standard rather than disqualifying* ([TBC for non-residents](https://tbcbank.ge/en/for-non-residents)). EE's e-Residency (digital ID) does **not** unlock LHV personal banking — LHV still requires demonstrable economic ties to Estonia and one in-person visit unless the applicant holds an Estonian passport, ID card, or residence permit ([LHV non-residents](https://www.lhv.ee/en/non-residents)). Universal fallback for any country on this list: HSBC Expat (Jersey).

### Country one-liners

**AL (Albania)** — NIPT for legal entities / NID personal number for residents (Civil Status Office, requires residence permit); account-opening: BKT, Credins (#2 by assets 2024), Raiffeisen Albania (#3, 14.48% market share) accept non-res with passport + proof-of-address, in-person preferred, Raiffeisen offers video-onboarding for residents ([Raiffeisen Albania](https://www.raiffeisen.al/en/individuals/ways-to-bank/banking-accross-borders.html)); FATCA: Standard (Albania-US IGA Model 1 in force); trap: removed from FATF grey list 27 Oct 2023 ([FATF](https://www.fatf-gafi.org/en/publications/High-risk-and-other-monitored-jurisdictions/Increased-monitoring-october-2023.html)) so correspondent-banking spreads narrowed, but cash-economy + EUR-deposit-rate floor still high; SEPA member; **Confidence: MEDIUM**.

**CZ (Czechia)** — rodné číslo for permanent residents / **VČP (vlastní číslo plátce) starting "710"/"720" assigned by Finanční úřad** for non-residents ([Czech TIN guide](https://lookuptax.com/docs/tax-identification-number/czechia-tax-id-guide)); account-opening: ČSOB, Komerční banka (KB, Société Générale subsidiary until 2024 sell-down to a domestic consortium), Raiffeisenbank CZ, Česká spořitelna (Erste); non-res accepted with passport + proof-of-address + sometimes residence permit; premier tiers (ČSOB Premier / KB Magnifica / Raiffeisen Premium) typical CZK 100k-500k minimum balance; FATCA: Standard; trap: non-EU non-residents face heavier KYC than EU-passport holders; **Confidence: HIGH**.

**EE (Estonia)** — isikukood (personal code) issued by PPA on residence permit / e-Residency digital ID separately; account-opening: LHV, Swedbank EE, SEB EE, Coop Pank — **e-Residency alone does NOT qualify for LHV personal account**; LHV requires "clear connection to Estonia" + one in-person branch visit unless holder has Estonian passport/ID card/residence permit ([LHV non-residents](https://www.lhv.ee/en/non-residents)); LHV business accounts available for e-Resident companies with Estonian operational nexus; FATCA: Standard but selective; trap: 1 May 2024 LHV updated AML fee schedule for enhanced controls; PSD2 SCA tightening across all EE PSPs; **Confidence: HIGH**.

**GE (Georgia)** — Personal ID Number (issued to residents) / passport sufficient for non-res; account-opening: TBC Bank, Bank of Georgia — historically opened on passport-only same-day, but **2026 tightening** requires bank statements 6-12 months + proof-of-income + local-address evidence + written purpose statement ([Easy Global Banking 2026 guide](https://www.easyglobalbanking.com/alternative-banking-hubs/open-bank-account-georgia-non-resident-2026/)); FATCA: **Standard — US persons explicitly welcome with W-9** ([TBC for non-residents](https://tbcbank.ge/en/for-non-residents)); GE is the regional FATCA-haven of choice for US-tech expats; trap: NBG Regulation 33/04 tightened customer onboarding; "1 day to open" claim now stale post-2024; **Confidence: MEDIUM-HIGH**.

**HR (Croatia)** — **OIB (Osobni identifikacijski broj)** assigned by Porezna uprava, issued same-day at counter for residents / 2-4 weeks for foreign legal entities requiring apostilled register extract + Croatian-translated POA ([porezna-uprava](https://porezna-uprava.gov.hr/en/person-al-identification-number-pin-oib-information-on-the-general-rules-and-assigning-of-oib/7320)); OIB obtainable **before** bank account; account-opening: Zagrebačka banka (UniCredit), PBZ (Intesa), Erste Croatia — non-res accepted in-person with passport + OIB; FATCA: Standard; trap: **euro since 1 Jan 2023 at 7.53450 HRK/EUR — IBANs unchanged ([ECB Croatia changeover](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/euro/changeover/croatia/html/index.en.html)); HR removed from FATF grey list 13 Jun 2025** ([AML UAE](https://amluae.com/croatia-mali-and-tanzania-removed-bolivia-and-virgin-islands-uk-added-fatf-grey-list-june-2025-update/)) — correspondent-banking pricing now normalised; **Confidence: HIGH**.

## MENA + GCC + Israel + Türkiye

### Regional pattern

The dominant access pattern across the GCC (AE/SA/QA/KW/BH/OM) is **residence-permit-as-prerequisite**: full current accounts require a state-issued resident ID (Emirates ID / Iqama / QID / Civil ID / CPR / Resident Card). Tourists and non-residents are channelled into deposit-only or "investor" accounts, classified as enhanced-due-diligence (EDD) cases with source-of-funds and source-of-wealth verification per central-bank CDD/KYC rules. Two structural relaxations landed in 2025: SAMA's "Visitor ID" amendment to the Account Opening Rules (circular 472021863, dated 25 Sep 2025) and CBUAE's "Tourist Identity" digital onboarding pilot with ICP/ADCB (Apr 2026) — both still time-bound and bounded in product scope.

Across the **wider MENA arc** (MA/EG/TN/JO), exchange-control regimes (Office des Changes / BCT / CBE / CBJ) sort accounts into a "convertible" or "non-resident" bucket where inward transfers can be repatriated only if the original FX import was documented at deposit. Morocco's Office des Changes IGOC (updated Jan 2024) and Tunisia's BCT non-resident-dinar/devise framework remain the gating mechanism; Egypt's 7 Aug 2024 CBE circular explicitly removed bank-level obstacles to non-resident-foreigner account opening but left AML/ATF due-diligence intact.

**FATCA-IGA coverage** is uniform on paper — AE, SA, QA, KW, BH, OM, IL, TR, JO, EG, MA, TN are all Model 1 IGA jurisdictions per the IRS FATCA registration listing — but practical US-person acceptance varies. Saudi, Qatari, and Kuwaiti retail banks are documented as quietly reluctant onboarders for US persons due to FATCA admin overhead; UAE (HSBC UAE, Standard Chartered UAE), Israel (Hapoalim, Leumi, Discount, Mizrahi-Tefahot all collect W-9), and Türkiye (post-2014 IGA) remain the most US-person-friendly locally. HSBC Expat Jersey is the universal fallback when a local bank refuses.

**Two anomalies sit outside the regional pattern.** Lebanon has been effectively closed to new non-resident retail account opening since the Oct 2019 collapse — BdL Circulars 158 and 166 (renewed annually, latest Dec 2025: caps raised to USD 1,000 and USD 500 monthly for legacy depositors) regulate withdrawals from pre-Oct-2019 accounts only; new account opening is constrained by the unresolved USD 80–83 bn financial gap and Law No. 23/2025 bank-resolution framework. Türkiye is the inverse — easy onboarding (vergi kimlik numarası obtainable same-day at any vergi dairesi or via the Digital Tax Office) but post-2021 lira-crisis FX-outflow controls that dominated through 2024–2025; the FX-protected deposit (KKM) scheme was wound down on a phased schedule and the CBRT terminated all KKM openings/renewals effective 23 Aug 2025, with the KKM balance falling from >USD 140 bn (mid-2023) to USD 11 bn (Aug 2025).

**Israel's distinction**: te'udat zehut zara (foreign-resident ID number) supports a non-resident account only at a narrow set of banks (Bank of Jerusalem, Discount, some Hapoalim/Leumi branches), with proof that neither the holder nor family is permanently resident and no Israel-based business — full investment accounts effectively require Aliyah/Olim status. Bank of Israel post-7-Oct-2023 supervisory guidance tightened source-of-funds documentation for foreign-resident clients.

### Country one-liners

**AE (United Arab Emirates)** — Emirates ID issued by ICP after residence visa (typically 7–14 days post-medical); current accounts at Emirates NBD / ADCB / FAB / Mashreq / HSBC UAE / Standard Chartered UAE require Emirates ID with no exceptions, while non-residents are limited to savings/fixed-deposit accounts classified as EDD per CBUAE CDD/KYC Guidance; Apr 2026 CBUAE+ICP+ADCB "Tourist Identity" initiative now allows in-app non-resident onboarding for digital debit-card accounts; FATCA Standard for US persons (HSBC UAE / Standard Chartered UAE accept; HSBC Expat Jersey fallback); trap — "investor account" vs "current account" distinction critical for non-residents and FATF grey-list exit (23 Feb 2024) raised AML scrutiny on source-of-funds for cross-border inflows; **Confidence: HIGH**.

**BH (Bahrain)** — CPR (Central Population Register card) required for full current accounts at Ahli United Bank / NBB / BBK; non-residents can open accounts (e.g. NBB Al Mumayaz, minimum BHD 2,000 ≈ USD 5,300) with passport + reference letter + address proof; no SAMA/CBUAE-style Visitor-ID short-cut documented; FATCA Standard (Model 1 IGA); trap — non-resident accounts subject to bank-by-bank EDD and CBB Sijilat foreign-ownership disclosure (KPMG alert, 7 Dec 2025) for property-holding entities; **Confidence: MEDIUM** (CBB rulebook less English-public than SAMA/CBUAE).

**EG (Egypt)** — National ID for residents, passport-based for non-residents; CBE circular dated 7 Aug 2024 explicitly mandated banks to open non-resident accounts for foreign clients (NBE / CIB / QNB Egypt / Banque Misr) — both EGP and foreign-currency (USD/EUR) accounts available with passport + address proof + AML/ATF due diligence; FATCA Standard; trap — Mar 2024 EGP devaluation and shift to flexible exchange rate (CBE policy rate raised 600 bps to 27.75%) means historic FX-account valuation is unstable; outbound foreign-currency transfers from EGP local accounts still gated by CBE FX availability; **Confidence: HIGH** (CBE 7 Aug 2024 circular widely reported).

**IL (Israel)** — te'udat zehut for citizens / te'udat zehut zara for foreign residents; Bank Hapoalim / Bank Leumi / Discount / Mizrahi-Tefahot all FATCA-compliant and collect W-9 from US persons under the 2014 Model 1 IGA; non-resident accounts available at Bank of Jerusalem (proof of no Israel residence/business required) and a subset of branches at the major four; trap — Bank of Israel post-7-Oct-2023 supervisory tightening on source-of-funds declarations for foreign-resident clients; full investment-account access in practice requires Aliyah/Olim status; **Confidence: HIGH**.

**JO (Jordan)** — National ID for residents; CBJ Foreign Currency Instructions allow licensed banks to open non-resident accounts in JOD or foreign currency on proof of foreign residence (Arab Bank / Cairo Amman Bank / Jordan Kuwait Bank); Jordanian non-residents must re-prove foreign-residence status every 3 years or account reverts to resident; FATCA Standard (Model 1 IGA); trap — Arab Bank Switzerland is a separate legal entity for international clients; CBJ has not published a 2024-specific reform but maintains AML/CFT updates aligned to FATF MENAFATF cycle; **Confidence: MEDIUM**.

**KW (Kuwait)** — Civil ID required for residents to open accounts at NBK / KFH / Boubyan / CBK; non-Kuwaitis without residency cannot open standard personal accounts per CBK instructions (passport-only insufficient for retail current account); CBK mandated "My Identity" mobile-app authentication for online remittance via exchange companies (2024); FATCA Standard on paper (Model 1 IGA) but US-person retail onboarding documented as restrictive; trap — departing-resident accounts may be force-closed; NBK Capital Smart Wealth (NBK Invest app) is the documented carve-out allowing non-residents to maintain investment accounts opened while resident; **Confidence: MEDIUM**.

**LB (Lebanon)** — National ID / residence permit irrelevant — banking sector effectively closed to new non-resident retail accounts since Oct 2019; BdL Circulars 158 and 166 (latest amendments via intermediary circulars 746/747, effective 1 Dec 2025: caps raised from USD 800→1,000 and USD 400→500 monthly) regulate withdrawals from legacy pre-Oct-2019 USD accounts only; "Lollar" (haircut bank-USD at ~LBP 15,000) vs fresh-USD distinction persists; Law No. 23/2025 (Jul 2025) on Reforming and Reorganizing the State of the Banks passed but draft Financial Gap Law (Dec 2025) yet to resolve USD 80–83 bn deposit gap; FATCA status moot — new accounts not opening; trap — any "fresh account" advice from a broker should be cross-checked against current BdL circulars and the Salam-government IMF track; **Confidence: LOW** (policies shift weekly, IMF mission concluded 5 Jun 2025 without final program).

**MA (Morocco)** — CIN (Carte d'Identité Nationale) for residents; Office des Changes "compte en dirhams convertibles" (NRF) available to non-resident foreigners at Attijariwafa / BMCE Bank of Africa / Banque Populaire / Crédit du Maroc / SGMA on passport + foreign-residence proof, with FX-import declaration required for cash deposits (≤6-month validity); IGOC updated 2 Jan 2024; FATCA Standard (Model 1 IGA); trap — MAD repatriation only against documented original FX inflow at the same bank; Morocco exited FATF grey list Feb 2023 after Law 12-18 (2021) AML reforms tightened beneficial-ownership reporting; **Confidence: MEDIUM** (Office des Changes English documentation reasonable; bank-level practice variable).

**OM (Oman)** — Resident Card issued by ROP after employment visa; full accounts at Bank Muscat / National Bank of Oman / Bank Dhofar / Sohar International require Resident Card + salary certificate + sometimes employer NOC; non-residents largely refused at most banks but Sohar International is documented as opening accounts for non-resident ITC (Integrated Tourism Complex) property buyers using purchase docs as supporting evidence; FATCA Standard (Model 1 IGA); trap — non-resident account access is bank-by-bank discretion under CBO KYC, not a published rulebook entitlement; **Confidence: MEDIUM**.

**QA (Qatar)** — QID required for full current account at QNB / CB / Doha Bank / QIIB / Masraf Al Rayan / Al Ahli Bank; non-residents/tourists offered restricted accounts (passport + home-country bank reference + address proof) with no chequebook and limited transactional capability; FATCA Standard on paper (Model 1 IGA) but US-person retail onboarding documented as cautious; trap — QID is tightly coupled to residence visa and employer; account access lapses on visa cancellation; QFC (Qatar Financial Centre) offshore route exists for entity-level structures but not for personal property-buyer use cases; **Confidence: MEDIUM**.

**SA (Saudi Arabia)** — Iqama for residents, with full accounts at SNB / Al Rajhi / Riyad Bank gated by Iqama copy + KSA address + home-country address per SAMA Account Opening Rules § 200.1.3; **2025 reform — SAMA circular 472021863, dated 25 Sep 2025, added "Visitor ID" (issued by Ministry of Interior) as a valid account-opening identification, time-bound to ID validity and visit purpose**; non-resident-not-QFI personal accounts remain rarely approved outside QFI/Tadawul or government-contract channels; FATCA Restricted in practice (Model 1 IGA on paper, but US-person retail onboarding documented as reluctant); trap — accounts auto-frozen on Iqama/Visitor-ID expiry per SAMA rulebook; **Confidence: HIGH**.

**TN (Tunisia)** — CIN for residents; BCT non-resident-dinar (convertible) and foreign-currency accounts available to foreign individuals at BIAT / Attijari Bank / STB / UIB / ABC Tunisia under the 1976 Foreign Exchange and External Trade Code; non-residents are exempt from most exchange controls; **2026 Finance Law (adopted by parliament 2 Dec 2025, entry-into-force 1 Jan 2026 pending implementing decrees) lifts the prior-BCT-authorisation requirement for residents holding FX or convertible-dinar accounts**; FATCA Standard (Model 1 IGA); trap — currency declaration required at customs for re-export above 5,000 TND (~USD 1,600); **Confidence: MEDIUM** (BCT documentation French-only for many circulars).

**TR (Türkiye)** — Vergi kimlik numarası obtainable same-day at any vergi dairesi (10–15 minutes) or via the Digital Tax Office's foreigner portal; passport + tax ID sufficient to open accounts at Garanti BBVA / İş Bankası / Akbank / Yapı Kredi / Ziraat for non-residents; FATCA Standard since the 2014 IGA; **2024–2025 KKM (FX-protected deposit) wind-down — CBRT halted KKM for legal entities effective 15 Feb 2025, then terminated all openings/renewals effective 23 Aug 2025; KKM balance fell from >USD 140 bn (mid-2023) to USD 11 bn (21 Aug 2025)**; trap — banks tightened non-resident onboarding from 2024 amid lira-stabilisation push (CBRT policy rate normalised under post-Jun-2023 reset); foreign-currency cash-withdrawal limits at branches; **Confidence: HIGH**.

## APAC + Central Asia

### Regional pattern

APAC + Central Asia retail banking for non-resident property buyers splits into four practical buckets. The **two foreigner-account hubs** — Singapore and Hong Kong — accept non-resident applicants by branch visit with passport + proof-of-address, but enforce wealth-tier minimums (DBS Treasures S$350k for non-EP holders; HSBC HK Premier HKD 1M Total Relationship Balance or HKD 380/month penalty after the 6-month waiver). Both hubs are the practical workaround for buyers from CN/JP/KR/TW where domestic retail banking is residency-coupled and slow. Macau follows HK's pattern but harder — BIR (Bilhete de Identidade de Residente) or Non-Resident Worker blue card is effectively required at most counters.

**Residency-coupled retail banking** dominates JP, KR, TW, CN. Japan classifies anyone resident <6 months as `非居住者` (non-resident) and offers only a "non-resident yen account" with no remittance, no cash card, no bill payments — practically useless for property closing. The standard route requires Zairyu Card (在留カード) + 6 months of address registration + often My Number (マイナンバー). Korea requires the ARC (외국인등록증) plus a visa-status check; a 2024 FX overhaul (Improvement Measures for FX Market Structure, July 2024) extended trading hours and admitted 40 Registered Foreign Institutions, but retail outflow ceilings (USD 50,000/year per individual without prior BoK notification, USD 20,000 per cash purchase reporting threshold) remain. Taiwan needs ARC + work permit / spouse / Gold Card. China retains the SAFE USD 50,000/year individual conversion cap with face-scan enforcement from 2024, and from 1 Jan 2026 banks must keep transaction records for 10 years (was 5) and verify ID on transfers >RMB 5,000 / USD 1,000.

**Foreigner-friendly SE Asia + Central Asia** — KH, KZ, LK, MV, UZ, VN — accept passport + visa + tax ID with relatively low friction at large banks (ABA / Acleda in Cambodia, Kaspi / Halyk in Kazakhstan, Vietcombank / BIDV in Vietnam, Bank of Ceylon / HNB in Sri Lanka). The 2024-2025 wave reshaped this zone: Kazakhstan moved IIN issuance from embassies to in-country PSC visits (effective 1 Jan 2025), Cambodia mandated dual-currency (KHR + USD) accounts (NBC, 19 Mar 2024), Maldives forced tourism FX into bank deposits (Foreign Currency Act 32/2024 + MMA Reg 2024/R-91), and Sri Lanka closed out import restrictions under the IMF EFF (relaxations Dec 2024–Jan 2025).

**India sits alone** with the NRE/NRO/FCNR triplet under FEMA: NRE is INR-denominated, fully repatriable, tax-free interest (for NRI-source funds); NRO is INR-denominated for India-source income with USD 1M/financial-year repatriation cap; FCNR(B) is foreign-currency term deposit, fully repatriable. PAN is mandatory for NRO (or Form 60), advisable for NRE/FCNR per RBI Master Direction No. 14 (updated Apr 2025). The Liberalised Remittance Scheme keeps the USD 250,000/financial-year ceiling with TCS at 20% on >INR 10 lakh annual remittance (Finance Act 2023, in force 1 Oct 2023, amended thresholds 2024).

**FATCA acceptance is bimodal**. SG/HK/JP/KR/TW/PH/MY/TH/ID/IN/KH/MV/KZ all sign IGAs and the standard banks accept US-person KYC with extra documentation; **CN is technically Standard but de facto restrictive** — most US-person buyers route through HSBC HK, DBS/OCBC/UOB SG, Citi IPB Singapore (USD 200k AUM), or HSBC Expat Jersey instead of a mainland account. Charles Schwab International remains the universal US-side fallback for liquid investing.

### Country one-liners

**CN (China)** — `个人所得税号` (Personal Tax ID) issued by the Tax Bureau (税务局) on residency; non-residents typically rely on passport-based pre-purchase wires from HK or SG. Account opening at BoC / ICBC / CCB / ABC requires in-person visit with passport + visa + Chinese phone number; many branches have de facto declined non-resident retail accounts since 2017. FATCA: technically Standard (CN signed Model 2 IGA-in-substance) but in-practice restrictive — US persons typically use HSBC HK Premier or DBS Treasures SG. Hidden trap: SAFE USD 50,000/year individual conversion cap with face-scan enforcement; new PBoC + CBIRC + CSRC rule from 1 Jan 2026 raises record-keeping to 10 years and ID-verification threshold to RMB 5,000 / USD 1,000 per transfer ([sinoblawg.com](https://www.sinoblawg.com/china-is-tightening-foreign-exchange-control/)). Confidence: HIGH on regulator framework, MEDIUM on bank-by-bank application.

**HK (Hong Kong)** — HKID for residents, passport-only for non-residents. Major banks HSBC HK / Hang Seng / BOC HK accept non-resident applicants in branch via "Digital Banker" self-service kiosks for eligible passports ([hsbc.com.hk](https://www.hsbc.com.hk/international/account-opening/)). FATCA: Standard at all major banks; HSBC Premier requires HKD 1,000,000 Total Relationship Balance or HKD 380/month penalty after the 6-month waiver ([hsbc.com.hk Premier](https://www.hsbc.com.hk/accounts/products/premier/)). Hidden trap: bank-by-bank de-risking on Russian / Iranian persons since 2022; CARF + CRS 2.0 transposition consultation through 6 Feb 2026 with legislative amendments targeted 2026, AEoI under CARF from 2028 and CRS 2.0 from 2029 ([kpmg.com/cn](https://kpmg.com/cn/en/insights/2026/01/tax-alert-02-hk-the-proposed-implementation-of-carf-and-crs2-in-hk.html)). Confidence: HIGH.

**ID (Indonesia)** — NPWP (Nomor Pokok Wajib Pajak) for tax residents; passport-only opening now possible at select banks for tourist-route accounts post-2023 (still narrow). KITAS / KITAP standard for residents at BCA / Mandiri / BNI / BRI. FATCA: Standard, BCA Premier preferred for US-tax persons. Hidden trap: OJK Regulation 26/2024 expanded permitted banking activities but did not relax the non-resident NPWP precondition for an FX account ([arma-law.com](https://www.arma-law.com/news-event/newsflash/indonesias-banking-business-activities-expansion-a-closer-look-of-ojk-reg-26-2024)); non-KITAS retail accounts remain bank-discretionary, not statutory. Confidence: MEDIUM — written policy is clear; operational variance per branch is wide.

**IN (India)** — PAN (Permanent Account Number) is mandatory for NRO accounts and effectively required for any taxable transaction; Form 60 substitute exists in narrow cases ([proteantech.in](https://www.proteantech.in/articles/NRI-PAN-06-06-2025/)). NRI account triad: **NRE** (INR, fully repatriable, tax-free interest), **NRO** (INR, USD 1M/FY repatriation cap, taxable), **FCNR(B)** (foreign currency, fully repatriable, term deposit) — codified in RBI Master Direction No. 14 on Deposits and Accounts (updated 16 Apr 2025) ([rbi.org.in MD-14](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=10198)). Major banks: SBI / HDFC / ICICI / Axis. FATCA: Standard for OCI / PIO / NRI categorisation; US-resident NRIs supported. LRS retains USD 250,000 / financial-year cap with TCS 20% on >INR 10 lakh remittance (Finance Act 2023, effective 1 Oct 2023; revised exemption thresholds 2024) ([rbi.org.in LRS FAQ](https://www.rbi.org.in/commonperson/English/Scripts/FAQs.aspx?Id=1834)). Hidden trap: cross-categorisation errors (NRE used for India-source income, or NRO used for foreign earnings) trigger FEMA contraventions; Form 15CA + 15CB chartered-accountant cert required for repatriation. Confidence: HIGH — primary RBI documentation is comprehensive and English-native.

**JP (Japan)** — `在留カード` (Zairyu / Residence Card) + `マイナンバー` (My Number) standard; the residency law classifies anyone <6 months as `非居住者` (non-resident). Major banks SMBC / MUFG / Mizuho generally require 6 months address registration; Japan Post Bank (`ゆうちょ銀行`) is the most non-resident-tolerant retail option, and SBI Sumishin Net Bank operates a digital channel via bank-agent partners (registered under the Banking Act). FATCA: Standard but slow KYC; Japan Post Bank straightforward. Hidden trap: the "non-resident yen account" is offered but excludes overseas remittance, cash card, and bill payments — practically useless for property closing; closing buyers route via SG / HK and wire on completion ([japanlivingguide.com](https://www.japanlivingguide.com/expatinfo/money/bank/)). Confidence: HIGH on framework, MEDIUM on bank-by-bank acceptance.

**KH (Cambodia)** — National ID for citizens, passport + valid visa + proof-of-address for foreigners at ABA Bank / Acleda Bank / Canadia. Foreigner-friendly: accounts open same-day at ABA in many branches with E-visa + lease. FATCA: Standard, foreigner-friendly. Hidden trap: NBC mandated dual-currency (KHR + USD) accounts from 19 Mar 2024 with monthly compliance reporting ([nbc.gov.kh](https://www.nbc.gov.kh/english/)); the Bakong system (NBC's CBDC-rail) is ubiquitous but the **Bakong Tourist App** allows QR payments without a Cambodian bank account, narrowing the property-buyer use case for Bakong proper. Loan availability: foreign-borrower LTV typically 50-70% vs 80% local — there is no statutory "Foreign Borrower Certificate" product nationally; the term varies bank to bank. Confidence: MEDIUM — NBC publishes English Prakas, but bank-level execution is oral.

**KR (South Korea)** — `외국인등록번호` / ARC (Alien Registration Card) is the gating credential; one bank account per ID is standard. Major banks KB / Shinhan / Woori / Hana with foreign desks at KB Kookmin (post-Citibank Korea retail exit in 2023) and Shinhan. FATCA: Standard at KB / Hana / Shinhan / Woori; some restrictions on US-IRA / 401k inbound transfers. Hidden trap: USD 50,000/year individual outflow ceiling above which BoK pre-notification is required; banks must report any FX purchase >USD 20,000 to the National Tax Service per case ([bok.or.kr Internationalization of KRW](https://www.bok.or.kr/eng/main/contents.do?menuNo=400195)). 2024 FX Improvement Measures (effective July 2024) extended trading to 02:00 next-day London close and admitted 40 Registered Foreign Institutions; retail outflow ceilings unchanged ([english.moef.go.kr](https://english.moef.go.kr/)). Confidence: HIGH.

**KZ (Kazakhstan)** — `ИИН` (IIN — Individual Identification Number); since 1 Jan 2025 IIN is no longer issued via embassies abroad — must be obtained at a Population Service Centre (PSC) inside Kazakhstan with passport + notarised KZ/RU translation + address proof, 2-3 business days ([astanatimes.com](https://astanatimes.com/2025/04/how-foreign-citizens-can-get-bank-cards-in-kazakhstan/)). Foreigner-friendly: Kaspi / Halyk / Forte. Kaspi distinguishes residence-permit holders (online flow) from non-residents (in-branch consent form, 1-year card validity). Halyk requires a residence permit for full account; opens narrow-scope NR accounts with restrictions. FATCA: Standard, increasingly so for US tech-nomads. Hidden trap: 2022-2023 mass-card-issuance to Russians triggered tightening; three banks paused NR card issuance and resumed under tightened KYC. Confidence: MEDIUM — process changed materially Jan 2025; bank policies still settling.

**LK (Sri Lanka)** — NIC (National Identity Card) for citizens; foreigners use passport + tax ID. Major banks Bank of Ceylon / Hatton National Bank / Sampath Bank. FATCA: Standard, but post-2022 economic-crisis tightening means longer KYC. Hidden trap: Sri Lankan Rupee NR account convertibility was constrained 2022-2023; under the IMF Extended Fund Facility, FX market reflexes have recovered (gross official reserves rebuilt to USD 6.1B by end-2024 = 50.3% of ARA metric; CBSL moved to a single policy rate Nov 2024; vehicle / personal-import restrictions lifted Jan 2025) ([imf.org SL CR 2024-161](https://www.imf.org/-/media/files/publications/cr/2024/english/1lkaea2024001.pdf)). Confidence: MEDIUM — IMF Article IV documents are authoritative, but bank-by-bank execution lags the central-bank pace.

**MO (Macau)** — `BIR` (Bilhete de Identidade de Residente) for residents or Non-Resident Worker blue card for permitted workers; foreign passport alone is generally insufficient at most counters ([gov.mo PS-1065](https://www.gov.mo/en/services/ps-1065/)). Major banks BCM / ICBC Macau / BNU. FATCA: Standard. Hidden trap: tighter than HK on documentary requirements — without BIR or blue card, account opening is bank-discretionary and often refused; ICBC Macau English channel exists but still demands address proof ([icbc.com.mo](https://www.icbc.com.mo/)). Confidence: MEDIUM.

**MV (Maldives)** — Maldivian National ID / Work Permit for residents; foreigners use passport. Major banks Bank of Maldives / Maldives Islamic Bank. FATCA: Standard, but tourism-focused — foreigner property buyers limited by the constitutional ban on foreign freehold (only 99-year leasehold under the SEZ Act, projects >USD 1B). Hidden trap: MMA Foreign Currency Act 32/2024 + Regulation 2024/R-91 (Oct 2024) mandate that all tourism foreign-currency earnings be deposited in commercial banks and converted, with USD ~500/tourist exchange floor ([mfr.mv](https://mfr.mv/financial-sector/mma-publishes-new-foreign-exchange-rule-aimed-to-tackle-u-s-dollar-flow-issues)); BML temporarily suspended foreign card transactions on 25 Aug 2024 and reversed within hours, signalling FX stress. Confidence: LOW — MMA publishes in Dhivehi-first; English secondary sources lag.

**MY (Malaysia)** — MyKad for citizens; passport + MM2H visa or Employment Pass for foreigners at Maybank / CIMB / Public Bank. FATCA: Standard. Hidden trap: MM2H visa simplifies account opening and FX status; June 2024 reform reset the program into Silver (USD 150k FD / 5y / RM 600k property minimum), Gold (USD 500k FD / 15y / RM 1M property), Platinum (USD 1M FD / 20y / RM 2M property), with up to 50% of FD withdrawable after approval for residence purchase / education / medical / tourism ([MOTAC](https://www.mm2h.gov.my/category/overview), 2026 data). Bank Negara FX rules separate resident vs non-resident treatment for ringgit accounts. Confidence: HIGH.

**PH (Philippines)** — TIN (Tax Identification Number) for tax purposes; passport + ACR I-Card for residents at BPI / BDO / Metrobank / UnionBank. FATCA: Standard. Hidden trap: BSP Foreign Currency Deposit Account (FCDA) under RA 6426 carries statutory secrecy provisions (only allows depositor / written-permission / impeachment-court access) — useful for FX shielding but exempt from default reporting; BSP Circular No. 1192 (21 Mar 2024) consolidated the FX rules and reaffirmed non-residents (incl. foreign banks) may open peso accounts with Authorised Agent Banks subject to eligible-source-of-funds tests ([bsp.gov.ph Cir 1192](https://www.bsp.gov.ph/Regulations/Issuances/2024/1192.pdf)). Confidence: HIGH.

**SG (Singapore)** — NRIC for citizens / PR; FIN for Employment Pass / Student Pass / Dependant's Pass holders; passport for non-residents. Major banks DBS / OCBC / UOB; HSBC Singapore / Citi IPB / SCB Singapore for international tier. UOB requires all non-resident foreign directors to attend in person with no exception; DBS / OCBC offer remote video KYC case-by-case for valid-visa holders. Non-EP non-residents at DBS gated to **DBS Treasures** at S$350,000 minimum deposit ([statrys.com](https://statrys.com/sg/guides/banking/can-foreigners-open-a-bank-account)). FATCA: Standard at major banks (DBS US-person-OK; OCBC more restrictive); Citi IPB Singapore needs USD 200k AUM. Hidden trap: enhanced due diligence on foreign directors and source-of-wealth verification per MAS Notice 626 (28 Mar 2024, effective 1 Apr 2024; amended 30 Jun 2025) extends KYC to 4-8 weeks for foreign-owned entities ([mas.gov.sg](https://www.mas.gov.sg/regulation/notices/notice-626)). Confidence: HIGH.

**TH (Thailand)** — Thai Tax ID at the Revenue Department for >180-day residents (short-term ≤14 consecutive / 90 days/year exempt) ([expattaxthailand.com](https://www.expattaxthailand.com/tax-identification-number-thailand/)). Major banks Bangkok Bank / Kasikornbank / SCB; non-resident retail account typically requires work permit OR longer-stay visa (LTR / O-A retirement / Elite); some branches accept tourist visa + 90-day evidence + R.O. 22 (tax residence cert). FATCA: Standard but documentation-heavy. Hidden trap: 90-day reporting (TM47) for any visa-status holder; Bangkok Bank Foreign Currency Deposits in USD/EUR/GBP available but require source-of-funds documentation; cross-border QR via PromptPay added Lao PDR (3 Apr 2024) and Cambodia (8 Apr 2025), bringing the ASEAN linkage to 9 countries — useful for inbound retail spend, not for property closing ([bot.or.th cross-border](https://www.bot.or.th/en/financial-innovation/digital-finance/digital-payment/cross-border-payment.html)). Confidence: HIGH.

**TW (Taiwan)** — ARC (Alien Resident Certificate) + work permit / Gold Card / spouse visa. Major banks Mega International / CTBC / Cathay / E.Sun. FATCA: Standard. Hidden trap: minimum-balance NT$20k at some banks; CBC's 7th amendment to selective credit controls (Sep 2024) cut LTV for second-home loans to 50% (was 60%) nationwide and to 30% for third / high-value housing ([cbc.gov.tw](https://www.cbc.gov.tw/en/cp-448-189518-553d0-2.html)); banks operate under enhanced internal property-lending controls Q4-2024 to Q4-2025. Mortgage availability for foreigners has tightened — total new housing loans Jan-Sep 2025 fell 32.8% YoY. Confidence: HIGH.

**UZ (Uzbekistan)** — `STIR` (Soliq Toʻlovchining Identifikatsiya Raqami / Tax ID); foreigner accounts at NBU / Uzpromstroybank / Kapitalbank. FATCA: Standard, increasingly so for tech-nomads. Hidden trap: AML/CFT framework strengthening per IMF FSAP (2024-25) targets risk-based supervision in insurance / securities / leasing and beneficial-ownership accuracy ([imf.org Uzbekistan FSAP](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099081925064533229/pdf/P506984-16bbc722-0060-46ac-a68c-75e2fe5ab247.pdf)); CBU reduced reserve-requirement ratio on FX deposits to 14% on 13 Jun 2024. New currency-control regime under draft 2024-2025 — published expectations narrow individual outflows. Confidence: LOW — much policy is ministerial decree, oral guidance to banks; English documentation lags Russian / Uzbek originals.

**VN (Vietnam)** — `MST` (Mã số thuế / Tax Code) issued by the General Department of Taxation; passport + visa + tax code at Vietcombank / BIDV / Techcombank. FATCA: Standard. Hidden trap: under Decree 70/2014/ND-CP non-residents and resident foreigners may transfer foreign currency abroad from legal-source FX or buy FX from VND legal-source income — but documentation burden lands at the bank counter ([dazpro.com](https://dazpro.com/decree-70-2014-vietnam-on-foreign-exchange/)). Decree 26/2025/ND-CP (24 Feb 2025) restated SBV functions; Decree 329/2025/ND-CP shifts International Financial Center members to ex-post FX supervision but does not yet apply to general retail buyers ([vietnamnet.vn IFC FX](https://vietnamnet.vn/en/vietnam-issues-new-forex-regulations-for-international-financial-centers-2474785.html)). Confidence: MEDIUM — primary decrees are public; bank-by-bank document-intake practice varies.

**PK (Pakistan)** — the practical foreign-buyer market is **overwhelmingly the diaspora** (non-resident Pakistanis + Pakistani Origin Card holders), channelled through the **State Bank of Pakistan Roshan Digital Account (RDA)** — opened **remotely** with a participating bank, KYC/AML-screened by the bank + SBP; RDA-participating banks include **HBL, UBL, MCB, Meezan, Bank Alfalah** and others (verify the current list at [sbp.org.pk/RDAC](https://www.sbp.org.pk/RDAC/index.html)); the **Roshan Apna Ghar** mortgage runs through RDA banks (conventional + Shariah, 3–25-yr tenor, LTV/markup per-bank — [SBP, sbp.org.pk/RDAC/Ghar.html](https://www.sbp.org.pk/RDAC/Ghar.html)). **Non-origin foreigners** need **prior Federal-Government / Ministry-of-Interior approval to acquire immovable property** (1980 Order under the Foreigners Act 1946) and have **no clear retail mortgage product** — expect a **cash purchase via SBP-approved inward remittance**. **FATCA posture: data not primary-verified this pass — verify per-bank**. Hidden trap (the dominant financial risk): the **PKR is a managed float (NOT a peg) under an active IMF EFF**, and Pakistan operates **exchange controls** via the SBP **Foreign Exchange Manual** — the rupee is **not freely convertible** for residents; **clean repatriability exists IF you buy via RDA/Roshan Apna Ghar** ("investments made through RDAs are fully repatriable and subject to full and final taxation" — [SBP primary, verbatim](https://www.sbp.org.pk/RDAC/Ghar.html)), but **outside that channel repatriation depends on documented inward-remittance provenance** and the operative FE-Manual clause for immovable-property sale-proceeds is not pinned to a single URL — **get bank/SBP confirmation in writing before buying**. **No CBI** (a residency-only Foreigner (Long Term Residency) Order, 2025 exists — "residency rights only … not nationality or citizenship", [BoI FAQ](https://invest.gov.pk/long-term-residency/FAQs)). Confidence: MEDIUM-HIGH on the structural spine (RDA channel, repatriation language, PKR managed-float + capital controls, no-CBI all CONFIRMED verbatim at SBP/BoI primary) — LOW on per-bank LTV/markup + the non-origin-foreigner mortgage gap (none surfaced; do not state an LTV).

**VU (Vanuatu)** — no income-tax-driven personal tax-ID (**zero direct tax** — no income/corporate/CGT/inheritance/wealth/withholding tax; the absence is the evidence, there is no Income Tax Act — [VFIPA](https://investvanuatu.vu/untapped-potential/low-tax-jurisdiction/)); non-resident account-opening requires **passport + proof of address + source-of-funds** + (for the CBI) **enhanced due diligence** under the AML/CFT regime administered by the **Financial Intelligence Unit** (State Law Office) — CBI-driven AML scrutiny is **high** after the EU revocation (verify SOF/KYC at the FIU); banks: **ANZ (Vanuatu)**, **Bank South Pacific (BSP)**, **National Bank of Vanuatu (NBV)**, **BRED Bank (Vanuatu)** (BPCE group), supervised by the [Reserve Bank of Vanuatu](https://www.rbv.gov.vu/); **FATCA posture: data not primary-verified this pass — verify per-bank**. Hidden trap: there is **NO freehold — only registered leases (75-yr ceiling) with N years remaining** under the Land Leases Act [Cap 163], so mortgage lending is **cash-dominant / offshore-financed** (declining lease tenure constrains collateral; do NOT state an LTV); the **VUV is a basket peg (USD/AUD/NZD/EUR), realigned under an IMF-advised framework in 2025** — moderate FX risk for a single-currency buyer, always use a dated rate; and **capital-control / repatriation rules were NOT located at a primary source — do NOT assert "free repatriation"; verify at the [RBV](https://www.rbv.gov.vu/) + IMF AREAER**; **CBI (the DSP donation) is NOT property-linked** and is reputationally/regulatorily contested (EU permanently revoked visa-free Schengen access 12 Dec 2024). Confidence: MEDIUM (four banks + zero-tax structure + basket-peg primary/IMF-sourced; FX-repatriation rules + per-bank US-person policy genuinely not available — hedged, not guessed).

## LATAM + Caribbean

### Regional pattern

Banking access for non-resident foreign property buyers across these 25 jurisdictions splits along four structural axes that drive the actual mechanics far more than country marketing suggests. First, **currency regime**: Ecuador (USD-dollarised since 2000, Banco Pichincha holds USD 19.5 bn assets per Banco Pichincha financials Dec 2024) and Panama (USD-dollarised, no central bank issuing currency) eliminate the FX-account-overlay problem entirely; Argentina sits at the opposite extreme — although the cepo cambiario was lifted 14 Apr 2025 via BCRA Comunicaciones "A" 8226/8227 (source: trade.gov market intelligence Apr 2025), USD-account access for non-residents remains structurally constrained and the floating band 1,000-1,400 ARS/USD is itself a managed regime; Uruguay, Paraguay, Peru, Costa Rica, Bahamas all permit USD accounts at major banks but with documented source-of-funds.

Second, **tax-ID-before-bank dependency**: Brazil is the regional outlier — CPF can be obtained at a Brazilian consulate ABROAD before arrival, which collapses the chicken-and-egg loop that traps buyers in MX/CO/CR/PY (where the residency-card-before-tax-ID-before-bank-account chain typically requires in-country presence). A Receita Federal rule effective 13 Jan 2025 now requires non-resident CPF holders to complete an annual re-registration via the RFB mobile app (selfie + passport) by 31 December each year, or the CPF moves to "Suspended" status that blocks property and banking transactions (sources: U.S. consulate Miami Brazil notice, brasiltax.com 2025). Mexico's RFC requires prior CURP and a residency card per RMF 2025 — tourist-visa applicants are refused (source: lorad.mx RFC checklist 2025; SAT RMF 2025 Anexo 1-A).

Third, **fideicomiso obligation** is unique to Mexico: foreigners cannot hold fee-simple title within the constitutional restricted zone (50 km from coast / 100 km from border) and must use a bank trust at BBVA, HSBC, Banamex/Citi, or Santander — setup ~USD 2,000-3,000, annual fees ~USD 550-1,000 (source: brevitas.com restricted-zone guide 2025; mexlaw.com fideicomiso brief). This requires a separate trust account distinct from the personal banking account.

Fourth, **CFATF/FATF posture re-shapes Caribbean access**: Jamaica was removed from the FATF grey list 28 Jun 2024 (source: Jamaica MoF press release; cfatf-gafic.org news), Bahamas exited FATF increased-monitoring earlier (source: centralbankbahamas.com FATF MER page), and Belize received a near-pristine 2025 4th-round Mutual Evaluation Report — Fully Compliant on 38/40 FATF Recommendations, Largely Compliant on 2 (source: pressoffice.gov.bz Jan 2025) — recovering from its 2011 grey-listing that had cost Belize 87% of correspondent-banking relationships. Net effect: Caribbean foreigner-banking now demands more documentation but is no longer pariah-tier. Private-banking traditions persist in Uruguay (Itaú Private Banking, Santander UY Private Banking) and Panama (post-Panama-Papers tightening, but still foreigner-onboarding-capable). Digital disruptors Nubank and Inter (BR), Yape (PE), CuentaRUT (CL BancoEstado) accelerate retail onboarding for residents but not for non-resident property buyers, who still face full-KYC private-banking flows.

Fifth, **offshore-finance-centre + ECCU de-risking adds a documentation tier on top of currency.** The four UK Overseas Territories now in scope — **Cayman (KY), Bermuda (BM), British Virgin Islands (VG), Turks & Caicos (TC)** — are major or mid-tier offshore-finance centres where non-resident account-opening is *possible without a residency-style tax-ID* (KYD/BMD pegs and USD legal tender remove the FX layer) but onboarding is **heavy KYC + certified source-of-funds + multiple bank references + CRS/FATCA self-certification**, and many property buyers simply **purchase cash + finance offshore against home-country security** to bypass local lender involvement. Critically, **FATCA execution differs by IGA model**: KY (in force 29 Nov 2013), VG (signed 30 Jun 2014) and TC (signed 1 Dec 2014) operate **Model 1** IGAs (FFIs report to the local authority, which exchanges with the IRS), whereas **Bermuda is the regional outlier on a Model 2 IGA** (signed 19 Dec 2013) under which Bermudian FFIs report **directly to the IRS** ([US Treasury FATCA agreements](https://home.treasury.gov/policy-issues/tax-policy/foreign-account-tax-compliance-act); [IRS FATCA governments](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/corporations/fatca-governments)). The four **Eastern Caribbean CBI states** — **St Kitts & Nevis (KN), Antigua & Barbuda (AG), Saint Lucia (LC), Grenada (GD)** — share the **XCD (EC$2.70 = US$1.00 peg since 1976, ECCB)** and run **non-reciprocal Model 1B** IGAs (KN signed 31 Aug 2015; LC signed 19 Nov 2015; GD signed Oct 2016; AG signed 8 Nov 2016 — verify current "in force" status at the IRS list, as AG's has been reported "signed, not in force"); banking is regional (Republic Bank / CIBC Caribbean / RBC / Scotiabank / national + co-operative banks) with the **same heavy-KYC source-of-funds posture**, and **CBI-naturalised citizens access banking on citizen terms** (a structural reason the CBI route dominates the foreign-buyer market). The 2018-onward EU/CFATF de-risking wave (already noted for BS/BB/BZ) applies across all eight: KYC documentation is materially heavier than the "tax-haven, easy banking" reputation suggests.

**PE (Peru)** — RUC at SUNAT for residents/businesses; non-domiciled persons register via SUNAT Virtual at nodomiciliados.sunat.gob.pe; **since March 2025 banks may open a basic account with passport alone, with simpler onboarding and transaction limits** (source: wise.com Peru banking 2025); banks: BCP / Interbank / Scotiabank Perú / BBVA Perú; Yape app dominates retail digital; FATCA: Standard; SBS (Superintendencia de Banca, Seguros y AFP) is the AML supervisor; Confidence: HIGH.

**PY (Paraguay)** — Cédula civil issued post-residency (residency obtainable via SENAD/Migraciones); non-residents/tourists generally cannot open accounts (sources: getresidencyparaguay.com 2026 guide; goldenharbors.com Paraguay banking 2025); banks: Banco Itaú Paraguay (foreigner-friendly per multiple expat reports), Banco Continental, Sudameris Bank — multi-currency USD accounts available with documented source-of-funds; FATCA: Standard; hidden trap: bank-by-bank policy is opaque, "foreigner-friendly" is reputation not guarantee — verify before booking flights; Confidence: LOW-MEDIUM.

**TC (Turks and Caicos Islands)** — no personal tax-ID prerequisite (no central bank, USD is sole legal tender — zero FX/currency-control layer); non-residents open with **passport + proof of address + reference letters + source-of-funds** + CRS/FATCA self-certification; banks: **Scotiabank (Turks & Caicos)** (largest by assets), **RBC Royal Bank**, **CIBC Caribbean (FirstCaribbean)** ([visittci.com — banks & financial institutions](https://www.visittci.com/life-and-business/financial-services/banks-financial-institutions); [TCI FSC licensed banks](https://www.tcifsc.tc/banks-licensed-banks)); FATCA: **Standard for declared US persons** under a **Model 1 IGA signed 1 Dec 2014, in force 25 Jul 2016** ([US Treasury — signed US/TCI Model 1 IGA](https://home.treasury.gov/system/files/131/FATCA-Agreement-TurksandCaicosIslands-12-1-2014.pdf) · [Treasury IGA status table](https://home.treasury.gov/policy-issues/tax-policy/foreign-account-tax-compliance-act); [IRS FATCA governments](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/corporations/fatca-governments)) (2026-09-18 verified) but heavy source-of-funds KYC (offshore-centre onboarding); expat-arm fallback: HSBC Expat Jersey; hidden trap: **no currency-control repatriation gate** (USD jurisdiction, no national currency) — the only "currency" workstream is AML source-of-funds on the way in; many buyers purchase cash; **per-bank minimum-balance figures: data not publicly available — verify**; Confidence: MEDIUM.

**TT (Trinidad and Tobago)** — no income-tax-driven personal tax-ID detailed for the buyer (BIR file number at the [Inland Revenue Division](https://ird.gov.tt/) for taxable matters); non-residents open with **passport + proof of address + reference letters + source-of-funds + KYC** (CRS/FATCA self-certification — verify the current documentation list with the bank); banks: **Republic Bank** (largest, est. 1837), **First Citizens Bank** (state-linked), **Scotiabank Trinidad & Tobago**, **CIBC Caribbean**, **RBC** — all regulated by the [Central Bank of Trinidad and Tobago (CBTT)](https://www.central-bank.org.tt/), plus mortgage-focused **TT Mortgage Bank** + **Home Mortgage Bank**; conveyancing is **attorney-handled** (common-law, no separate notary profession). **FATCA posture: data not primary-verified this pass — verify per-bank; expat-arm fallback HSBC Expat Jersey**. Hidden trap (the single biggest practical exit risk): there is **no legal capital-repatriation bar** — the Exchange Control Act (Chap. 79:50) was substantively liberalised from April 1993 — **BUT a genuine, multi-year USD shortage means authorised dealers RATION forex**, so a foreign seller paid in TTD must source USD through a rationing authorised dealer to repatriate — slow/uncertain "even from USD accounts held in T&T" ([EUROCHAMTT FX challenges](https://eurochamtt.org/); [IMF 2026 Article IV](https://www.imf.org/en/Countries/TTO)); inbound is cleaner because **FIA s.10(1) REQUIRES the purchase price be settled in an internationally traded currency through an authorised dealer**, creating the paper trail; the **TTD is a managed float with a de facto soft USD anchor (~TTD 6.74–6.78/USD, 2025–2026)** — quote the publish-day [CBTT rate](https://www.central-bank.org.tt/exchange-rates-daily/); **no non-resident mortgage product surfaced at a primary source — frame foreign-buyer finance as predominantly cash/equity (inference, not sourced) and verify per-lender**; **NO Citizenship-by-Investment — do NOT list T&T among Caribbean CBI countries** (naturalisation/descent only). Confidence: MEDIUM (named banks + CBTT + FX-rationing exit risk + FIA s.10 hard-currency settlement + no-CBI primary/regulated-sourced; per-bank non-resident terms + US-person policy not published).

**UY (Uruguay)** — Cédula de Identidad uruguaya for residents; non-residents can open accounts at BROU, Santander UY, Itaú UY, BBVA UY, Scotiabank with proof of source of funds and international bank references (source: goldenharbors.com Uruguay banking 2025; livinginuruguay.com expat guide); private banking traditions persist — Itaú Private Banking and Santander UY Private Banking serve high-net-worth clients (minimums data not publicly available — verify directly with each bank's private-banking desk); FATCA: Standard; Uruguay is a CRS-participating jurisdiction — bank secrecy traditions reformed but legal-privacy protections retained per BCU framework (bcu.gub.uy); Confidence: MEDIUM-HIGH.

**VG (British Virgin Islands)** — no personal tax-ID prerequisite (USD is sole legal tender since 1959; no income/CGT — no residency-style TIN for individuals); non-residents open with **rigorous KYC + multiple reference letters + certified source-of-funds** + CRS/FATCA self-certification (a major offshore-finance centre — onboarding is correspondingly strict); banks: **FirstBank Virgin Islands**, **Banco Popular**, **CIBC Caribbean (FirstCaribbean)**, **Scotiabank** (verify current branch footprint) ([thebanks.eu — BVI major banks](https://thebanks.eu/countries/British-Virgin-Islands/major_banks); BVI FSC — [bvifsc.vg](https://www.bvifsc.vg/)); FATCA: **Standard for declared US persons** under a **Model 1 IGA signed 30 Jun 2014** ([MNE Tax — US/BVI Model 1 FATCA IGA](https://mnetax.com/u-s-british-virgin-islands-sign-model-1-fatca-iga-2553); [IRS FATCA governments](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/corporations/fatca-governments)) but heavy source-of-funds KYC; expat-arm fallback: HSBC Expat Jersey; hidden trap: the gating foreign-buyer step is the **Non-Belonger Land Holding Licence (NBLHL)**, NOT a currency/FX-registration filing — but AML source-of-funds documentation is rigorous; thin USD-millions market means many buyers pay cash via offshore structures; Confidence: MEDIUM.

## Africa + Anglo-non-EU

### Regional pattern

The 14-country group splits into three banking archetypes. **Mauritius and Seychelles** are the offshore-friendly historical anchors of the Indian Ocean — both exited the FATF grey list (Mauritius Oct 2021, EU blacklist Jan 2022 per [Bank of Mauritius](https://www.bom.mu/media/media-releases/mauritius-exits-faft-list-jurisdictions-under-increased-monitoring-list); Seychelles VASP Act in force 1 Sept 2024) and now actively court HNW non-resident wealth via private-banking arms (MCB, AfrAsia, SBM, ABSA Seychelles). Account-opening for non-residents is genuinely viable remote+POA at HNW tiers, in-person at retail tiers.

**South Africa** is unique — it operates a full SARB exchange-control regime where the residency tag determines what you can do. Residents get a R1M Single Discretionary Allowance (SDA) plus R10M Foreign Investment Allowance (FIA) per calendar year, both administered by SARS via Tax Compliance Status PINs ([SARB FinSurv FAQ](https://www.resbank.co.za/en/home/what-we-do/financial-surveillance/FinSurvFAQ)). Non-residents open "Non-Resident Rand Accounts" with documentation but are blocked from many investment products. Section 9H "deemed disposal" exit-tax on financial emigration is the principal trap.

**Nigeria and Ghana** are currency-crisis recoveries. Nigeria's CBN unified its FX windows in mid-2023 ("willing buyer, willing seller"), launched EFEMS on 1 Dec 2024 and the FX Code on 28 Jan 2025 ([CBN](https://www.cbn.gov.ng/Out/2025/CCD/CBN_UPDATE_JANUARY_2025_EDITION.pdf)) — naira closed 2025 around ₦1,429/USD, 7.4% YoY appreciation. Ghana's IMF ECF (US$3B, May 2023) concluded at its sixth and final review on 27 Jul 2026, succeeded by a non-financing 36-month Policy Coordination Instrument (5th review Dec 2025, [IMF PR 25/429](https://www.imf.org/en/news/articles/2025/12/17/pr-25429-ghana-imf-completes-the-fifth-review-under-the-ecf-arrangement)) with state-owned bank recapitalisation continuing through end-2025.

**Rwanda, Cabo Verde, Kenya** are smaller-scale but functional. Cabo Verde escudo is pegged 1 EUR = 110.265 CVE (1999, [BCL working paper](https://www.bcl.lu/en/publications/Working-papers/16/BCLWP016.pdf)). Kenya requires a KRA PIN before account opening at all major banks. Rwanda has the most digital-forward foreigner-onboarding (I&M Bank Rwanda online account-opening, BK Diaspora Banking).

**Anglo-non-EU (AU/CA/NZ/US)** are FATCA-native and English-language regulator-transparent — but three of them (AU, CA, NZ) currently have foreign-buyer restrictions on residential property. AU banned foreign persons from established dwellings 1 Apr 2025 to 31 Mar 2027 ([Treasury](https://ministers.treasury.gov.au/ministers/clare-oneil-2024/media-releases/albanese-government-clamping-down-foreign-purchase)); CA extended its non-Canadian purchase ban to 1 Jan 2027 ([Canada.ca](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-finance/news/2024/02/government-announces-two-year-extension-to-ban-on-foreign-ownership-of-canadian-housing.html)); NZ partially reopened in Dec 2025 for AIP/Investor visa holders at NZD 5M+ ([Bell Gully](https://www.bellgully.com/insights/overseas-investment-act-update-door-opens-for-investor-visa-holders-buying-residential-land/)). The US has no federal foreign-buyer ban; ITIN-via-W-7 unlocks bank accounts at most major banks. FATCA is intra-country for the US (target jurisdiction) and varies for the others — Mauritius, Australia and Canada bank-arms publicly serve US persons; Ghana / Cabo Verde / Rwanda US-person desks are less mature.

### Country one-liners

**AU (Australia)** — TFN at ATO via Individual Auto Registration online once visa active (≤28 days for letter, [ATO TFN application](https://www.ato.gov.au/individuals-and-families/tax-file-number/apply-for-a-tfn/foreign-passport-holders-permanent-migrants-and-temporary-visitors-tfn-application)); CommBank Migrant Banking pre-arrival, NAB / Westpac / ANZ post-arrival in-person with passport+visa; FATCA universally accepted at the Big Four, ANZ Premier and Macquarie Private OK with US persons; **trap**: foreign individuals BANNED from buying established dwellings 1 Apr 2025 – 30 Jun 2029 ([foreigninvestment.gov.au](https://foreigninvestment.gov.au/guidance/types-investments/residential-land)), FRCGW raised to 15% on all property regardless of value 1 Jan 2025 ([ATO FRCGW](https://www.ato.gov.au/individuals-and-families/investments-and-assets/capital-gains-tax/foreign-residents-and-capital-gains-tax/foreign-resident-capital-gains-withholding/foreign-resident-capital-gains-withholding-overview)), state foreign-buyer surcharges (NSW/VIC/QLD/WA) and AUSTRAC Tranche 2 enrolment for real-estate professionals from 31 Mar 2026, obligations from 1 Jul 2026 ([AUSTRAC](https://www.austrac.gov.au/industry-and-business/about-amlctf-reforms/about-reforms)); **confidence HIGH**.

**CA (Canada)** — SIN required for full bank account access — only obtainable with work permit / PR / citizenship, ITN as fallback for non-resident tax filing; RBC Newcomer / TD New to Canada / Scotia StartRight pre-arrival packages allow account-opening up to 12 months before landing; FATCA universally accepted, RBC Premier and TD Cross-Border Banking serve US tax-residents (TD CBB threshold USD 500k AUM); **trap**: Prohibition on the Purchase of Residential Property by Non-Canadians Act EXTENDED to 1 Jan 2027 ([Canada.ca 4 Feb 2024](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-finance/news/2024/02/government-announces-two-year-extension-to-ban-on-foreign-ownership-of-canadian-housing.html)) for CMA/CA areas, BC/ON/NS additional Foreign Buyer Tax 20-25%, FINTRAC AML reform 1 Oct 2025 added title insurers as reporting entities and forces real-estate brokers to verify unrepresented parties' identity ([DLA Piper](https://www.dlapiper.com/en/insights/publications/2025/10/regulatory-updates-impacting-reporting-entities-under-canadas-aml-regime)); **confidence HIGH**.

**NZ (New Zealand)** — IRD Number applicable from abroad via IR742 form; ANZ NZ / ASB / BNZ / Westpac NZ / Kiwibank — most accept overseas-passport+visa with in-person verification, ANZ Migrant Banking has pre-arrival pack; FATCA universally accepted; **trap**: foreign-buyer restriction on residential property under Overseas Investment Act has been PARTIALLY LIFTED Dec 2025 — Active Investor Plus, Investor 1 and Investor 2 visa holders can buy NZD 5M+ residential property with OIO consent (~5 working days, NZD 2,040 fee existing / NZD 3,500 new build, [Bell Gully](https://www.bellgully.com/insights/overseas-investment-act-update-door-opens-for-investor-visa-holders-buying-residential-land/) — Royal Assent 19 Dec 2025, in force by May 2026); other foreigners remain restricted; **confidence HIGH**.

**US (United States)** — ITIN via Form W-7 for non-residents (7 weeks normal / 9-11 weeks tax season, foreign passport stands alone as ID, [IRS W-7 instructions Dec 2024](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/iw7)); SSN only for residents/work-eligible; BoA / Chase / Wells Fargo / Citi accept ITIN+passport in person at most branches (state variance), HSBC US Premier (USD 75k AUM) and Charles Schwab International serve cross-border; non-residents file Form W-8BEN not W-9 to bank ([IRS NRA interest](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/nontaxable-types-of-interest-income-for-nonresident-aliens)); **trap**: FBAR/FinCEN 114 + Form 8938 reporting on foreign accounts for US persons; FinCEN BOI reporting applies ONLY to entities formed under foreign law and registered to do business in a US state — narrowed by the 26 Mar 2025 interim final rule and **made permanent by the final rule effective 14 Aug 2026 (91 FR 52508)**, which also exempts BOI of US-person beneficial owners and US-person company applicants and drops the FinCEN-ID update duty for US persons (2026 data, source Federal Register) ([Federal Register 2026-16576](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/08/14/2026-16576/beneficial-ownership-information-reporting-requirement-revision); superseded interim rule: [2025-05199](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/03/26/2025-05199/beneficial-ownership-information-reporting-requirement-revision-and-deadline-extension)); OBBBA signed 4 Jul 2025 made the USD 15M federal estate exemption permanent from 1 Jan 2026 (USD 30M MFJ, indexed from 2027 with 2025 base, [Tax Foundation](https://taxfoundation.org/research/all/federal/one-big-beautiful-bill-act-tax-changes/)) — non-resident aliens still capped at USD 60k US-situs estate exemption unless a treaty applies; **confidence HIGH**.

**ZA (South Africa)** — SARS Tax Reference Number required for residents and full non-resident registration via RAV01 (single point of truth from filing season 2025 per [SARS Non-Residents](https://www.sars.gov.za/individuals/tax-during-all-life-stages-and-events/tax-and-non-residents/)); Standard Bank / FNB / Absa / Nedbank all offer Non-Resident Rand Accounts (in-person preferred, POA possible), Standard Bank Offshore (Isle of Man) for non-resident wealth; HSBC SA retail closed 2025; FATCA broadly accepted, FNB historically more restrictive on US persons; **trap**: Exchange Control under SARB FinSurv — SDA R1M + FIA R10M per calendar year, FIA needs SARS TCS PIN ([SARB FinSurv FAQ](https://www.resbank.co.za/en/home/what-we-do/financial-surveillance/FinSurvFAQ)); Section 9H exit-tax on financial emigration including foreign property; *Edward Nathan Sonnenbergs Inc v Hawarden* [2024] ZASCA 90 (10 Jun 2024) — SCA REVERSED the High Court — buyer (not law firm) bears Business Email Compromise risk on conveyancing payments ([SAFLII](https://www.saflii.org/za/cases/ZASCA/2024/90.html)); eDRS launched 1 Apr 2025 (Proclamation 250 of 2025, [gov.za](https://www.gov.za/news/media-statements/land-reform-and-rural-development-implements-electronic-deeds-registration)) but a 5-year dual manual/electronic period applies; load-shedding suspended since Mar 2025 with brief Feb 2025 Stage 6 episode ([Eskom](https://www.eskom.co.za/loadshedding-suspended-for-261-consecutive-days-resulting-in-year-on-year-diesel-savings-of-r16-46-billion/)); **confidence HIGH**.

**KE (Kenya)** — KRA PIN mandatory before opening any bank account or buying property (passport scan, foreign address, photo via iTax); KCB / Equity Bank / NCBA / Stanbic Kenya foreigner-friendly with passport + work/dependant permit + KRA PIN + Kenyan SIM, "introducer" reference often required for non-residents; FATCA accepted at all major banks, tax-residency self-certification on opening; **trap**: foreigners cannot own freehold agricultural land (max 99-yr leasehold per Constitution Art. 65), 30% Capital Gains Tax for non-residents on land sales; **confidence MEDIUM** (bank-by-bank policy variance, KRA PIN process documented but procedural).

**NG (Nigeria)** — TIN at the NRS (Nigeria Revenue Service, formerly FIRS) via TaxProMax for non-residents with property/business interests; GTBank NRN ([nrn.gtbank.com](https://nrn.gtbank.com/)) and Zenith Bank Diaspora ([zenithbank.com diaspora-banking](https://www.zenithbank.com/personal-banking/bank-accounts/diaspora-banking/)) offer remote opening with notarised foreign-passport bio-data + utility bill + 2 Nigerian-bank reference letters, Access Bank and First Bank similar; FATCA standard at all four; **trap**: CBN unified FX windows mid-2023, EFEMS launched 1 Dec 2024, FX Code 28 Jan 2025 ([CBN](https://www.cbn.gov.ng/Out/2025/CCD/CBN_UPDATE_JANUARY_2025_EDITION.pdf)) — naira ₦1,429/USD end-2025 (7.4% YoY appreciation per [CBN BoP release](https://www.cbn.gov.ng/Out/2025/CCD/CBN%20Release%20on%20BoP.pdf)) — but FX repatriation of property sale proceeds requires Certificate of Capital Importation (CCI) at time of inflow, no CCI = no exit; foreigner land ownership restricted to 99-yr leasehold under Land Use Act 1978; **confidence MEDIUM**.

**GH (Ghana)** — TIN at GRA (now subsumed into Ghana Card system since 2021); GCB Bank / Stanbic Bank Ghana / Ecobank Ghana / Fidelity Bank — Ghana Card (national ID, also issued to foreign residents) or passport + residence permit + utility bill; FATCA standard; **trap**: 2022 sovereign default + IMF Extended Credit Facility USD 3B (May 2023; concluded at the sixth and final review 27 Jul 2026, now a non-financing Policy Coordination Instrument; 5th review completed Dec 2025, [IMF PR 25/429](https://www.imf.org/en/news/articles/2025/12/17/pr-25429-ghana-imf-completes-the-fifth-review-under-the-ecf-arrangement)) — bank recapitalisation continuing through end-2025 with state-owned banks lagging private; cedi (GHS) volatile; foreigners cannot own freehold land (50-yr lease maximum under 1992 Constitution Art. 266); **confidence MEDIUM**.

**MU (Mauritius)** — Tax Identification Number (TAN) via MRA online for non-residents holding property; MCB / SBM Bank / AfrAsia Bank / HSBC Mauritius — most foreigner-friendly in the region, multi-currency (MUR / USD / EUR / GBP) accounts standard, in-person preferred but POA via local agent/lawyer accepted, USD 100k+ minimum balance for non-resident at HNW tiers ([Bank of Mauritius supervised banks list](https://www.bom.mu/financial-stability/regulated-financial-entities)); FATCA notably US-person-friendly via AfrAsia and SBM, HSBC Mauritius private-banking USD 500k minimum; **trap**: Mauritius EXITED FATF grey list Oct 2021 and EU high-risk list 7 Jan 2022 ([Bank of Mauritius release](https://www.bom.mu/media/media-releases/mauritius-exits-faft-list-jurisdictions-under-increased-monitoring-list)) — KYC documentation now substantially heavier than pre-2020 reputation suggests; FSC AML reform ongoing; **confidence HIGH** (regulator publishes in English, FSC + BoM transparent).

## LGBTQ+ & unmarried-partner joint title + co-borrower eligibility (at purchase)

Marital status and the law of *who can co-own and co-borrow* are separable. Across the corpus, **joint title is a property-law act, not a family-law one** — two unmarried or same-sex persons can almost always take title together. The friction sits elsewhere: (1) **lender** willingness to accept co-borrowers who aren't married to each other, and (2) the **protection** of the surviving / non-married partner, which is a succession question handled in `shared/notary-forced-heirship.md`, not here. This sub-section is *purchase-time eligibility only*.

⚠️ **Trap**: "we can both be on the deed" is not "the survivor is protected." Civil-law co-ownership has no default survivorship and no community-property regime for unmarried / same-sex cohabitants — each share passes by succession (forced-heirship + higher non-relative succession tax). Purchase-time fixes: elect the survivorship form where it exists (common-law joint tenancy), hold by fractional shares + a written cohabitation / declaration-of-trust, use a notary-structured usufruct / *tontine* clause in civil-law states, or register a civil partnership (FR PACS / UK civil partnership) where that unlocks spousal-equivalent treatment.

**TITLE side — generally YES everywhere:**

- **Common-law (US, CA, UK, IE, AU, NZ)** — joint tenancy (with survivorship) or tenancy-in-common, any two persons, no marriage required. Strongest path *if* the survivorship form is elected on the deed / TR1.
- **Civil-law (FR *indivision*, DE *Miteigentum*, ES/PT *copropiedad/compropriedade*, IT *comproprietà*, NL *gemeenschappelijk eigendom*, and the rest of the EU/EFTA corpus)** — co-ownership by fractional share is open to any two persons; no marital-status bar.
- **JP** — registered co-ownership (共有 *kyōyū*) open to unmarried / same-sex co-owners.
- **Restrictive markets (AE/UAE, SA, GCC)** — foreign freehold is zone-limited *regardless of couple type*, and title-registration in a designated freehold zone does not bar two co-purchasers — BUT same-sex relations are criminalised, so openly co-purchasing as a same-sex couple carries personal-legal exposure beyond the title mechanics. Proceed only with named local counsel; verify per-emirate at `--permits`.

**MORTGAGE side — unmarried / same-sex co-borrowers:**

- **JP — the documented positive case**: JHF **Flat 35** admits same-sex partners as joint-and-several co-borrowers (連帯債務者), income-combining party (収入合算), and co-owner of the financed property — for applications received on/after **4 Jan 2023** (announced 20 Dec 2022; requires a municipal partnership certificate or notarised pledge), couple group-life cover (デュエット) included ([flat35.com](https://www.flat35.com/topics/20221220.html) + [jhf.go.jp](https://www.jhf.go.jp/topics/topics_20221220.html), PRIMARY). Private JP banks' partner pair-loans exist lender-by-lender — verify the specific bank.
- **US** — ECOA + Fair Housing Act bar marital-status / sex discrimination in credit; unmarried and same-sex co-borrowers are standard.
- **UK, IE, FR, DE, ES, IT, PT, NL, AU, NZ** — joint mortgages for unmarried / same-sex co-borrowers are available in practice; lenders assess combined income + joint-and-several liability, not marital status (practice-level — confirm the individual lender's product terms).
- **AE/UAE, GCC** — no documented openly-same-sex co-borrower product; the penal / personal-status context makes openly-couple co-borrowing inadvisable. Treat as UNVERIFIED.

**EU cross-border note (recognition, not title):** CJEU **C-713/23** *Cupriak-Trojan and Trojan v Wojewoda Mazowiecki* (Grand Chamber, **25 Nov 2025**, [EUR-Lex CELEX 62023CJ0713](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=celex%3A62023CJ0713)) held that a member state must recognise a same-sex marriage lawfully contracted by its nationals in another member state during free-movement, without forcing states to introduce domestic same-sex marriage (builds on *Coman* C-673/16, 5 Jun 2018). This bears on a buyer's *recognition / residence* status, not the title or mortgage directly — but a recognised marriage can unlock spousal lender / notary treatment in some states. The UAE non-Muslim civil-status law (Federal Decree-Law No. 41 of 2022, in force 1 Feb 2023) is opposite-sex-only and does NOT enable same-sex marriage or joint title ([loc.gov GLM](https://www.loc.gov/item/global-legal-monitor/2023-03-13/united-arab-emirates-personal-status-law-for-non-muslims-enters-into-force/)).

**Scale**: marriage equality is in force in ~38 jurisdictions as of 2026 (Pew via [Our World in Data](https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/same-sex-marriage-country-count) counts "nearly 40"), most recently Thailand (in force 23 Jan 2025) and Liechtenstein (1 Jan 2025); a majority of UN states still do not recognise same-sex marriage. In non-recognising states the title-side co-ownership usually still works, but spousal-equivalent lender / notary / succession treatment does not — fall back to fractional shares + a written cohabitation agreement.

**Confidence**: MEDIUM-HIGH. JP Flat 35 (4 Jan 2023), CJEU C-713/23 (25 Nov 2025), UAE Decree-Law 41/2022 scope, and the 38-country count are PRIMARY / authoritative-sourced; common-law / civil-law title structure is well-established. Per-country *lender* practice (beyond US ECOA) is practice-level — confirm the specific bank's terms at purchase. **Last verified**: 2026-06-03.

## Visa-linked blocked-deposit / proof-of-funds (residence-permit stage)

Many residence/long-stay/student permits require the applicant to demonstrate maintenance funds — but there is a decision-critical split between a **blocked account** (the money is *frozen* in a designated account and released only in monthly tranches — a real liquidity lock you cannot spend on the purchase) and a one-time **proof-of-funds** (you only *show* a bank balance, funds stay accessible). The folklore "I just need to show €X" routinely collapses the two. This is the residence-permit financial gate that runs *alongside* the property purchase — distinct from the programme thresholds in `--visa` (`min_threshold`) and the mythologised-floor busting in `shared/visa-programs.md` § Reality-check.

⚠️ **Trap**: the figure is a **per-applicant floor reset yearly** (DE/AT/AU drift annually; re-verify each intake) — dependants add a per-head multiplier on top. A *blocked* account means that year's funds are gone from your liquidity until released monthly — budget it as committed, not available, when you also need closing cash. **EU/EEA/Swiss free-movers are exempt** from these means tests EU-wide.

**🟢 Blocked account — funds FROZEN, released in monthly tranches (a real liquidity lock):**

- **🇩🇪 DE — *Sperrkonto* (the canonical)** — students deposit **€11,904** for year 1 (= €992/mo, the BAföG *Förderungshöchstbetrag*); **Opportunity Card / *Chancenkarte*** job-seekers **€1,091 net/mo (~€13,092/yr)** — both *frozen before the visa issues*, then ~1/12 released monthly after arrival. Also applies to language-course / recognition / au-pair / training permits; self-employed prove funds via a business plan instead. A scholarship or *Verpflichtungserklärung* can substitute. Amount is BAföG-linked + reset yearly (re-verify after WiSe 2026/27). Providers: Fintiba / Expatrio / Coracle / Deutsche Bank — the [Auswärtiges Amt](https://www.auswaertiges-amt.de/en/sperrkonto-388600) deleted its provider list in 2022 and makes no recommendation (page = the *mechanic*; the €992 amount sources to BAföG/BMBF). 2026.
- **🇳🇿 NZ — Funds Transfer Scheme (FTS), *conditional*** — NZ is proof-of-funds by default (show **NZD 20,000/yr** tertiary / NZD 17,000/yr school), **but** Immigration NZ imposes a genuine blocked account — the **ANZ-run FTS** — as an Approval-in-Principle condition on student applicants from **China, India, Nepal, the Philippines, Sri Lanka and Vietnam**: the maintenance sum is frozen in a designated NZ account and auto-released ~NZD 1,666/mo. ANZ is the sole recognised provider (no multi-provider ecosystem). [Immigration NZ — FTS](https://www.immigration.govt.nz/study/study-visas/funds-transfer-scheme/). 2026.

**🟡 Proof of funds — SHOWN, not frozen (funds stay accessible):**

- **🇦🇹 AT** — NAG settlement / student permits: monthly **§293 ASVG *Richtsatz*** — single **€1,308.39/mo**, couple **€2,064.12/mo**, +€201.88/child (2026, BGBl. II 263/2025); the financially-independent / pensioner permit (*Niederlassungsbewilligung – ausgenommen Erwerbstätigkeit*) requires **double** (€2,616.78/mo single). Shown via income / pension / savings / *Verpflichtungserklärung*; **foreign accounts accepted** (VwGH case law — no domestic account required). [BMI *Unterhaltsberechnung* brochure 2026](https://www.bmi.gv.at/312/files/broschuere_unterhaltsberechnung_publikation_a4_2026_converted_webbf.pdf).
- **🇨🇭 CH** — student residence permit (B/L) **CHF 21,000/yr**, shown via a confirmation from a FINMA-licensed bank; federal short-stay "sufficient means" CHF 100/day (CHF 30/day students, SEM). Cantonal — a minority of cantons *may* ask students to block a year of funds, but that is discretion, not uniform (Zurich + Geneva do not). [UZH guidelines](https://www.uzh.ch/en/studies/application/entry/guidelines.html) + SEM.
- **🇫🇷 FR** — long-stay **visitor** (VLS-TS *visiteur*, incl. retirees) **€1,477.93 net/mo (~€17,735/yr)** = net SMIC (2026); **student €615/mo**. Shown via bank statements / guarantor / pension / scholarship; a self-funded student *may* optionally use a bank-blocked-amount letter but it is one accepted option, not a requirement. [service-public.gouv.fr F302](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F302).
- **🇨🇿 CZ** — long-stay/long-term visa (non-business): **15× the subsistence minimum** (3,130 CZK) for month 1 **+ 2×** per further whole month → **46,950 CZK** base (~78,250 CZK for 6 months); business visa **50× = 156,500 CZK** (Act 326/1999 Sb.; Govt Reg. 436/2022, confirmed unchanged for 2026). Shown via bank statement / payment card. [MoI — ipc.gov.cz](https://ipc.gov.cz/en/forms-and-documents/documents/proof-of-funds-for-the-residence-finances/).
- **🇮🇪 IE** — student (Stamp 2) **€10,000** for a one-year course (€833/mo; €6,665 for ≤8-month courses), effective 30 Jun 2025. Shown via bank statement; an **optional** education-bond pilot (≥€10,000 via TransferMate) is a genuine escrow alternative. Stamp 0 / retiree financial-independence tests are separate + case-by-case. [ISD — Student Finances](https://www.irishimmigration.ie/coming-to-study-in-ireland/what-are-my-study-options/a-fee-paying-private-primary-or-secondary-school/information-on-student-finances/).
- **🇦🇺 AU** — student (subclass 500) **AUD 29,710** living-costs floor (12 mo, from 10 May 2024, pegged to 75% of the minimum wage) + AUD 10,394 partner / AUD 4,449 child; parental-income alternative **AUD 87,856 (single) / 102,500 (with family)**. "Genuine access to funds" — must be accessible and explicitly **not** locked in investments (the opposite of a Sperrkonto). [legislation.gov.au — LIN 19/198 as amended by LIN 24/042, compilation 10 May 2024](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F2019L01366/latest/text).

**Honest tail**: most of the corpus has **no fixed published visa-funds floor** — the gate is a consular "sufficient means" judgement (case-by-case) or there is no proof-of-funds requirement at all. Where no figure is published, verify the specific consulate's current means requirement rather than quoting a number. These figures are per-applicant floors that drift yearly — re-confirm at the named authority before relying. Feeds the `--tco` move-budget one-time cash event (blocked deposits are committed, not spendable).

**Confidence**: HIGH for the 8 documented regimes (each amount primary-sourced to the immigration authority / foreign-ministry / statute + date-stamped 2026; built grounded-research → independent audit, which corrected the CH Geneva figure for the LASLP supersession of RIASI, AU's stale 60k/70k income alternative → 87,856/102,500, and the NZ mechanism → ANZ-run FTS conditional on six source countries). MEDIUM on the breadth of the honest tail + cantonal CH variation. **Last verified**: 2026-06-14.

## 7 universal mitigations

1. **Open the bank account BEFORE the property offer goes in, not after.** The "I'll get the account when I sign the deed" plan fails routinely — closing-day wire deadlines collide with 4-12 week non-resident onboarding windows. In FR / DE / ES / IT / SG / HK / SA / SE start the account-opening process before the offer is in (BR CPF, MX RFC, ES NIE can be obtained months ahead via consulate). Use HSBC Expat Jersey as a 2-week pre-arrival universal fallback for any country where the local bank rejects.
2. **Treat residency as a 4-axis credential, not a single status.** (1) Tax-residency for FATCA / CRS reporting; (2) immigration residency for visa-status; (3) tax-domicile for inheritance-tax exposure (UK FA 2025 / FR IFI / DE ErbStG / IT 24-bis); (4) banking-residency for KYC self-cert. Each axis has independent rules. A "permanent visa holder + non-tax-resident" combination unlocks accounts unavailable to tourists but blocks investment products available only to tax residents.
3. **For US persons: HSBC Expat Jersey is the floor; Charles Schwab International is the parallel rail.** Both accept US-tax-persons routinely; Schwab covers the US-side custody / brokerage / US-IRA gap; HSBC Expat covers the multi-currency current-account + international-mortgage gap. At HNW (USD 1M+ AUM), add a private-bank desk (Lombard Odier / LGT / Pictet / UBS WM). For US-tax-person buyers in EU mainstream retail (Sparkasse / Crédit Agricole / Banco Santander mass-market), expect refusal at the branch level — escalate to Premier-tier or international-clients desk before booking the trip.
4. **Verify "non-resident" account capability against the actual transactions you need.** A "non-resident yen account" in Japan looks like a solution but excludes overseas remittance + cash card + bill payments — useless for property closing. JP buyers wire from SG / HK on completion-day. Similar pattern: CH non-resident accounts often access-limited (no e-banking); CN UnionPay-only; SE samordningsnummer accounts no BankID/Swish. Test the bank's published non-resident-account capability matrix against your timeline before relying.
