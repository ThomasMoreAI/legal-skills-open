**Scope**: this doc supplies the *construction-defect* layer — what statutory + private warranties protect a foreign buyer against new-build defects, what off-plan deposit-protection mechanisms exist before the property is delivered, what recurring defect categories appear in each jurisdiction, and what 2024-2026 reforms shape the risk landscape. ## Universal contract

For the property's country, return:

1. **Statutory new-build warranty regime** (cite legal citation): structural warranty period (typical 5-10 years), MEP/non-structural warranty period (typical 1-3 years), governing statute (FR Code civil Art. 1792 + Loi Spinetta of 4 Jan 1978; ES Ley 38/1999 LOE Art. 17; IT Codice Civile Art. 1669 + DL 122/2005; DE BGB §§ 631-651 + § 634a; UK NHBC Buildmark + Building Safety Act 2022; IL Sale (Apartments) Law 5733-1973; IN RERA Act 2016; AU state DBI; CA provincial Tarion / WarrantyBC / GCR; ZA Housing Consumer Protection Measures Act 95/1998; TR TBK Art. 478 + Yapı Denetim 4708; CN Construction Law 1997 Art. 62).
2. **Off-plan deposit-protection mechanism**: bank/insurer guarantee, escrow account, or none. Examples: FR garantie financière d'achèvement (GFA); ES aval bancario or insurance per Ley 57/68 + Ley 20/2015; IT polizza fideiussoria per DL 122/2005; DE notarial release schedule per MaBV (max % per Bautenstand milestone); PL Deweloperski Fundusz Gwarancyjny since 1 Jul 2022; AE RERA escrow account (separate per project); SA WAFI escrow via REGA; IN 70% of buyer payments must be escrowed for project completion per RERA Act; VN bank guarantee per Real Estate Trading Law Art. 56; BR Patrimônio de Afetação (segregated developer estate); UK NHBC progress-payment + Consumer Code for Home Builders; NL Wkb (Wet kwaliteitsborging voor het bouwen) in force 1 Jan 2024; AU state-by-state trust-account.
3. **Common defect categories observed** in the jurisdiction: damp/water ingress (CY/UK monolithic-cladding/NZ leaky-homes); structural settlement (TR pre-amnesty/IL/CN Evergrande tier-3-4); thermal-bridge condensation (UK BSA cladding); inadequate waterproofing (Mediterranean coast + Caribbean); seismic-code violations (TR 6 Feb 2023 / MA Sep 2023); MEP snags universally; balcony failures (US CA Berkeley collapse 2015 → SB-326/SB-721).
4. **Regulatory inspection regime**: independent inspection during construction (DE Bauamt Bauüberwachung; UK Building Control + Approved Inspectors; IE BCAR Assigned Certifier; NL Wkb kwaliteitsborger; FR controleur technique; AU state Building Surveyor; CA provincial inspector); developer self-certification (US various); off-plan progress-payment release tied to milestone inspection (DE MaBV; AT BTVG; BE Loi Breyne).
5. **Recent high-impact incident or systemic risk** (date-stamped) shaping the post-event regulatory regime: TR 6 Feb 2023 earthquake → Yapı Denetim reform; CN Evergrande HK High Court liquidation 29 Jan 2024 + Country Garden / Vanke / Sunac; AU Opal Tower 24 Dec 2018 → NSW DBP Act 2020 + iCIRT Jan 2023; US Surfside 24 Jun 2021 → FL SB-4D 2022; UK Grenfell 14 Jun 2017 → Building Safety Act 2022 + Higher-Risk Buildings Reg; NZ leaky homes 1990s-2000s; ES bubble-era defect rate post-2008.
6. **2024-2026 reforms** (date-stamped) — see Reform calendar below.
7. **Confidence**: HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW per the skill's contract.

## ⚠️ 7 cross-cutting traps

1. **Statutory warranty + off-plan deposit protection are independent layers — both must be verified.** Statutory warranty (e.g., FR garantie décennale, ES Ley 38/1999, IT Art. 1669) protects against defects POST-delivery. Off-plan deposit protection (e.g., FR GFA, ES aval bancario, AE RERA escrow, PL DFG, IN 70% RERA escrow) protects deposits PRE-delivery. A jurisdiction can have one without the other (TR has TBK Art. 478 statutory warranty but minimal off-plan deposit protection — buyers face TAPU-at-delivery risk; LB has FR-derived garantie décennale on paper but no enforceable off-plan protection in the current crisis). Verify both BEFORE signing the off-plan contract.
2. **Statutory warranty period varies 5x across jurisdictions.** 5-year structural is standard in CN / DE / EE / SK / VN / KZ / UZ / SA / TH / PE / EC / CR; 10-year structural is standard in FR / ES / IT / BE / NL / GR / CH / IE / PT / IL / RO / HR / BG / SI / CY / LT / MA / TN / EG / DO / AR / CL / CO / UY / PY / MU / SC / ZA / KR / JP. Common-law jurisdictions (UK / AU / CA / NZ / US / SG / HK) typically rely on private warranty (NHBC / Tarion / WarrantyBC / DBI / NHBRC) which is contractually shorter for snags but longer (10-yr) for structural. The 5-vs-10 difference is critical for foreign buyers' planning horizon.
3. **Off-plan delivery risk is the single largest financial exposure for foreign buyers.** Developer failure mid-project = potentially 100% loss of deposits in jurisdictions without enforceable escrow. **CN Evergrande / Country Garden / Vanke crisis since 2021** has produced 100M+ unfinished homes per various reports (verify at PRC NBS); **TR pre-2023 amnesty buildings** carry HIGHER seismic risk; **VN Tan Hoang Minh + FLC + Vạn Thịnh Phát fraud trials 2024-2025**; **AE 2008-2014 GCC crisis** cancelled an estimated 40-60% of off-plan projects; **BR Encol scandal 1990s** drove Patrimônio de Afetação reform. The CN/VN escrow innovations (post-2022 city-by-city tightening; VN Real Estate Trading Law bank-guarantee Art. 56) and IN RERA 70% escrow + AE RERA single-project-escrow are the strongest protections; PL Deweloperski Fundusz Gwarancyjny since 1 Jul 2022 added systemic backstop in EU CEE.
4. **Post-disaster building-code enforcement gaps create lasting hidden risk.** **TR 6 Feb 2023 ~50,000 deaths** were largely attributed to inadequate seismic-code enforcement + Erdoğan government's 2018-2022 *yapı kayıt belgesi* (construction registration certificate) amnesty programs that legitimised ~9.7 million unlicensed buildings; pre-amnesty buildings carry HIGHER seismic risk than newer ruhsat-permitted construction. **NZ leaky homes 1990s-2000s** monolithic-cladding crisis = $11bn estimated cost from defective-construction litigation. **US Surfside 24 Jun 2021** triggered FL SB-4D milestone inspections + structural integrity reserve studies for buildings ≥3 stories. **UK Grenfell 14 Jun 2017** triggered Building Safety Act 2022 + Higher-Risk Buildings Regulation 2023 + Defective Premises Act 1972 30-year retroactive limitation. **MA 8 Sep 2023 Al Haouz earthquake** triggered Decree 2.24.766 (Sep 2024) modifying the 2002 seismic-construction regulation + reconstruction with updated seismic standards (verify decree number against Bulletin Officiel). Post-disaster reforms ALWAYS lag the disaster — buyers must verify the building's pre-vs-post-reform construction date.
5. **Spain post-2008 bubble defect rate is a frequently-cited datapoint that warrants source-grade verification.** Various sources have cited a 95% post-bubble defect rate for new-builds in Spain — verify at original AEN (Asociación Española de la Normalización) reports + Tribunal Supremo case law. **Cyprus damp construction** in coastal new-builds has been documented in consumer complaints and is referenced in Building Safety Law 158(Ι)/2024 reforms. Both are known hidden traps but specific defect-rate percentages should be cited from primary regulator sources, not developer marketing or aggregator claims.
6. **Common-law warranty schemes vary state-by-state / province-by-province.** **AU**: NSW HBCF Home Building Compensation Fund vs VIC DBI vs QLD QBCC Home Warranty Insurance vs WA Home Indemnity Insurance; NSW DBP Act 2020 + iCIRT Jan 2023 added independent rating system. **CA**: ON Tarion vs BC New Home Warranty (2-5-10) vs QC GCR vs AB New Home Warranty. **US**: Surfside response varies — FL SB-4D vs CA SB-326/SB-721 balcony inspections vs NY Local Law 152. **NZ**: Master Build 10-Year Guarantee is private + Building Act 2004 council-liability for inspections. Foreign buyers cannot assume that "Australian warranty" or "Canadian warranty" is uniform — verify the specific state/province regime.
7. **Energy-performance + sustainability mandates are layering atop construction-quality regulations.** **EU EPBD IV (Reg. 2024/1275)** in force 28 May 2024 + transposition deadline 29 May 2026 — every new building zero-emission from 2030 (residential) / 2028 (public); **EU Construction Products Regulation 2024/3110** Dec 2024 — new framework for CE-marking; **FR RE2020** effective 1 Jan 2022 — carbon-neutral build standard; **DE GEG (Gebäudeenergiegesetz) 2024 amendment** — heat-pump mandate; **NL Wkb 1 Jan 2024** — graded risk classes for kwaliteitsborger; **UK Future Homes Standard 2025** — gas-boiler ban for new-builds. Foreign buyers acquiring older new-builds (2-5 years old) face stranded-asset risk if they later need to retrofit to current energy standards.

## Western Europe + EFTA + microstates + UK

### Regional pattern

Three statutory families dominate the region. The **civil-law décennale family** (FR, BE, LU, MC, IT, ES, PT, GR) imposes 10-year strict liability on builders for defects compromising structural solidity or fitness for purpose, anchored in Code civil Art. 1792 (FR/BE/LU), Art. 1630 (MC), Codice Civile Art. 1669 (IT), LOE Art. 17 (ES), Código Civil Art. 1225 (PT, but capped at 5 years), and AK Art. 692/698 (GR). The **Germanic 5-year-Bauwerk family** (DE, CH, AT, LI) caps Mängelhaftung / Gewährleistung at 5 years for structures under BGB § 634a Abs. 1 Nr. 2, OR Art. 371, and adapts to 3 years under ABGB § 933 in AT/LI — significantly shorter than the décennale regimes. The **common-law warranty-scheme family** (UK, IE) relies on private insurance products (NHBC, Premier Guarantee, HomeBond) plus statutory backstops — the Defective Premises Act 1972 in the UK (extended to 30-year retroactive limitation by the Building Safety Act 2022 for pre-existing defects + 15-year prospective) and BCAR 2014 inspection in IE. **Nordic regimes** layer 5-10 year contractor liability under Bustadoppføringslova (NO, 5y), Konsumenttjänstlagen (SE, 10y reklamation), and Asuntokauppalaki (FI, 10y vastuu + RS-järjestelmä bank security).

**Off-plan deposit protection** varies sharply. Strong: FR (GFA mandatory for VEFA), ES (Ley 38/1999 + Ley 20/2015 aval bancario), IT (DL 122/2005 polizza fideiussoria + decennale postuma), DE (MaBV — max 30% advance, milestone release), AT (BTVG Ratenplan A/B), BE (Loi Breyne 1971 — advance ≤ 5%), NL (Wkb 2024 + traditional bouwgarantieregeling), FI (RS-järjestelmä bank-deposited securities), NO (Bustadoppføringslova mandatory guarantee 3-10% of contract sum), PT (DL 268/94 bank guarantee). Weak / discretionary: GR (no mandatory deposit-protection regime — buyer bears full insolvency risk on off-plan), microstates (AD, MC, SM, LI, MT, IS — no comprehensive statutory deposit-protection equivalent, contract-level negotiation only).

**2024-2026 reform wave**: the EPBD recast (Directive (EU) 2024/1275, in force 28 May 2024, transposition deadline 29 May 2026) and the Construction Products Regulation (EU) 2024/3110 (in force 7 Jan 2025, most provisions apply 8 Jan 2026) reset the EU baseline — zero-emission new builds by 2030, fossil-heating phase-out, digital building logbooks. National implementations: NL Wkb (1 Jan 2024 — private kwaliteitsborger replaces municipal Bouwtoezicht in graded risk classes), DE GEG amendment (1 Jan 2024 — 65% renewable-heat mandate, repealed 29 Jul 2026 by the GModG, BGBl. 2026 I Nr. 226), FR RE2020 (residential 1 Jan 2022, offices/schools 1 Jul 2022, extensions 1 Jan 2023), CH OR Mängelrüge revision (1 Jan 2026 — 60-day notification window for hidden defects), UK Renters' Rights Act 2025 (Royal Assent 27 Oct 2025, Section 21 abolished 1 May 2026 — affects landlord-side rather than build-quality but reshapes new-build BTR economics).

**Defect-rate data — region-wide caveat**: aggregate defect-rate statistics for new builds are *not* published as primary government data in most countries. Frequently-cited figures (e.g. "Spain 95% post-bubble defect rate", "UK Grenfell-era cladding %") circulate as press claims without cross-validatable methodology. Below, where defect-rate data is referenced it is labelled `claim — verify` rather than presented as fact.

**UK Grenfell aftermath** (14 Jun 2017 fire) reshaped the regime: Building Safety Act 2022, Higher-Risk Buildings (Descriptions and Supplementary Provisions) Regulations 2023 (SI 2023/275), Building Liability Orders, leaseholder protections for cladding remediation in buildings ≥11m or 5 storeys, plus the 30-year retroactive Defective Premises Act lookback. Foreign buyers of UK new-builds benefit from the broadest statutory remedy regime in the region.

### Country one-liners

**AD (Andorra)** — Codi civil derives from Catalan-Roman tradition; vicis ocults regime covers hidden defects but *no statutorily mandated décennale-equivalent insurance* analogous to FR Loi Spinetta is published in primary law (verify with Servei de Tràmits, govern.ad). Off-plan deposit protection: contract-level only — no statutory bank-guarantee mandate. Hidden trap: small jurisdiction with limited construction-litigation case-law in public registries; foreign buyers should require contract-level décennale insurance from EU-licensed insurer. **Confidence: LOW**.

**AT (Austria)** — **ABGB § 933 Abs. 1** Gewährleistung 3 years for buildings (shorter than décennale regimes — verify at [ris.bka.gv.at](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/)); **BTVG (Bauträgervertragsgesetz)** governs off-plan with Ratenplan A or B + bank guarantee or insurance; A allows faster developer payout if buyer demonstrates urgent housing need + bank guarantee ≥10%. Hidden trap: 3-year warranty = significantly shorter than FR/IT/ES/UK 10-year regimes; foreign buyers should negotiate contractual extension. 2024-2026: EPBD IV transposition pending. **Confidence: HIGH**.

**BE (Belgium)** — **Code civil Art. 1792 + 2270** garantie décennale 10y (FR-derived); **Loi Breyne du 9 juillet 1971** governs vente sur plan / VEFA — caps advance at 5% of contract, mandates phased payment by construction stage, and requires double reception (provisional + final, ≥1 year apart for "four-seasons" testing); compulsory décennale insurance for entrepreneurs since Loi du 31 mai 2017. Hidden trap: provisional vs. final reception window — defects discovered between the two are still developer-liability, but the décennale clock starts at final reception. **Confidence: HIGH**.

**CH (Switzerland)** — **OR Art. 367-371** + **SIA Norm 118** industry standard: 5-year warranty for hidden defects on immovable works (Art. 371 paras. 2 & 3 OR); **revised warranty law in force 1 Jan 2026** removes the immediate-notification requirement for hidden defects and substitutes a 60-day notification window from discovery (verify at [homburger.ch](https://www.homburger.ch/) commentary + Swiss Federal Council message). Hidden trap: cantonal Bauamt rules vary — building-permit and Bauabnahme processes are cantonal, not federal; foreign buyers in GE / VD / TI face different inspection regimes than ZH / BE. **Confidence: HIGH**.

**DE (Germany)** — **BGB § 634a Abs. 1 Nr. 2** — 5-year Mängelhaftung from Abnahme for buildings; **MaBV (Makler- und Bauträgerverordnung)** — Bauträger may demand max 30% advance, with milestone-tied progress payments. **GEG amendment in force 1 Jan 2024** — 65% renewable-energy mandate for newly installed heating systems, with grace until municipal Wärmeplan dates (30 Jun 2026 for >100k pop., 30 Jun 2028 for ≤100k). **Superseded 29 Jul 2026** — §§ 71–73 GEG deleted by the Gebäudemodernisierungsgesetz (BGBl. 2026 I Nr. 226); the 65 % mandate and both Wärmeplan deadlines never took effect; new gas / oil heating now carries the § 43 biogenic quota from 1 Jan 2029. Hidden trap: 5-year warranty = half of décennale regimes; off-plan buyers should verify Notar-supervised MaBV milestone schedule before signing; defect-rate data ZBauR-cited but not aggregated as primary statistic — `data not publicly available — verify at` [BMWK](https://www.bmwk.de/). **Confidence: HIGH**.

**DK (Denmark)** — **Byggeskadefonden** statutory warranty fund covers defects in state-aided non-profit housing (almene boliger) for 20 years post-completion (5-year and 1-year reporting milestones); private new-builds rely on contractor warranty + Tilbudsloven framework and **Bygningsreglement BR23** (in force 1 Jan 2024 — base technical building code). Hidden trap: Byggeskadefonden cover is limited to subsidised social housing; foreign buyers of private new-builds have no equivalent state-backed defect fund. **Confidence: MEDIUM** (verify scope at [bsf.dk](https://www.bsf.dk/)).

**ES (Spain)** — **Ley 38/1999 de Ordenación de la Edificación (LOE) Art. 17** — 10-year structural / 3-year habitability / 1-year finish; **Ley 57/68 + Ley 20/2015** aval bancario or insurance for off-plan deposit return on developer failure; **Ley 9/2022 de Calidad de la Arquitectura** (in force 16 Jun 2022) layered on LOE establishing Consejo de Calidad de la Arquitectura. Hidden trap: post-2008 bubble new-builds are widely cited as defect-prone in Spanish press, but a single primary statistic (e.g. AEN 95%) is not publicly released as a cross-validatable government dataset — `claim — verify against AEN / Tribunal Supremo case-law`. Autonomous community variance: Cataluña and Madrid enforce LOE more strictly than southern coastal CCAA. **Confidence: HIGH** on statute; **LOW** on aggregate defect-rate.

**FI (Finland)** — **Asuntokauppalaki Chapter 4** — 10-year contractor responsibility from building-authority approval; **RS-järjestelmä** (1972, codified 1 Sep 1995) requires founding shareholder to deposit securities at a bank to protect buyers against bankruptcy and construction defects; mandatory **vuositarkastus** (annual inspection) at 12-15 months. Hidden trap: 10-year period is responsibility (consumer must prove fault), not warranty (presumption); only ~2-year warranty under § 59 Konsumenttjänstlag-equivalent provisions. **Confidence: HIGH**.

**FR (France)** — **Code civil Art. 1792 + Art. 2270** — garantie décennale 10y (structural / unfit for purpose) + garantie biennale 2y (équipements dissociables) + garantie de parfait achèvement 1y; **Loi Spinetta du 4 janvier 1978** mandates dual insurance — assurance dommages-ouvrage (buyer-side, premium roughly 3-5% of construction cost — verify against current FFB/AGEA quotes) + responsabilité civile décennale (builder-side); off-plan VEFA requires garantie financière d'achèvement (GFA). **RE2020 in force 1 Jan 2022** for residential, 1 Jul 2022 for offices/schools, 1 Jan 2023 for extensions — Décret n° 2021-1004 of 29 Jul 2021. Hidden trap: dommages-ouvrage premium often "forgotten" by self-build / CCMI clients — but absence of cover does not eliminate décennale liability; foreign buyers reselling within 10y carry forward the décennale to next purchaser. **Confidence: HIGH**.

**GR (Greece)** — Greek Civil Code (Astikos Kodikas) Art. 692 (acceptance), 698 (risk during works), and Art. 693 etc. — 10-year statutory limitation for defects on immovable works under general AK provisions (verify at [kodikas-astikos primary source](https://www.ministryofjustice.gr/)); no compulsory dommages-ouvrage equivalent. Off-plan deposit-protection regime is **weak** — no mandatory bank-guarantee / escrow scheme analogous to ES Ley 57/68 or DE MaBV; buyer-developer escrow is contract-level only. Hidden trap: avtidsasmenia (αυθαίρετα) — unauthorised construction tolerance regimes (most recently Law 4495/2017 + extensions) mean even "regularised" buildings may carry pre-amnesty structural compromises not surfacing in title search. **Confidence: MEDIUM**.

**IE (Ireland)** — **HomeBond** (since 1978, ~700,000 dwellings registered, currently underwritten by HSB Engineering Insurance Ltd) and **Premier Guarantee** are private 10-year structural-defects insurance schemes; max recovery typically €200,000 (HomeBond); pyrite-related claims excluded. **Building Control (Amendment) Regulations 2014** (BCAR) require Assigned Certifier and Design Certifier roles + commencement notice + statutory inspection regime. Hidden trap: HomeBond is private insurance — not all developers register; verify cover *before* signing contracts; mica/pyrite remediation has been a major defect-class in Donegal/Mayo (Defective Concrete Blocks Grant Scheme established under Pyrite & Mica Redress legislation). **Confidence: HIGH**.

**IS (Iceland)** — **Lög um mannvirki nr. 160/2010** (Building Act) is the master statute for construction safety; **byggingarreglugerð** (Building Regulation) updated multiple times; significant **amendment Law 113/2023** in force 11 Jan 2024 with classification/inspection provisions phased to 1 Feb 2025. No standalone décennale insurance regime in primary law — defect liability falls under general contract-law and Verktakalög principles (verify at [hms.is](https://www.hms.is/)). Hidden trap: post-2024 storm + seismic risk profile means foreign buyers should require independent structural surveys; small market = limited insurer competition. **Confidence: MEDIUM**.

**IT (Italy)** — **Codice Civile Art. 1669** — 10-year strict liability for total/partial ruin or serious defects of buildings intended for long duration; **DL 122/2005 Art. 4** (codified 16 Mar 2019 onward) requires constructor to deliver **polizza decennale postuma** at deed of transfer for new-builds — failure renders the deed void; off-plan also requires **polizza fideiussoria** for deposit protection. Hidden trap: Superbonus 110% (expired 31 Dec 2023, with limited transitional regimes thereafter) drove a renovation-defect litigation surge — quality-controlled SAL (stati avanzamento lavori) often rushed; foreign buyers should differentiate Superbonus-renovated vs. non-Superbonus stock. **Confidence: HIGH**.

**LI (Liechtenstein)** — Sachenrecht + ABGB-derived Werkvertragsrecht (§§ 1151-1159a ABGB) apply general Gewährleistung framework (§§ 922-933b — 3-year warranty for buildings, mirroring AT). No standalone décennale insurance regime; small jurisdiction with limited construction-defect case-law in public registries (verify at [gesetze.li ABGB Werkvertrag](https://www.gesetze.li/chrono/pdf/1974018000)). Hidden trap: foreign buyers should negotiate explicit contractual décennale-equivalent + bank guarantee; planning approvals are Gemeinde-level. **Confidence: LOW**.

**LU (Luxembourg)** — **Code civil Art. 1792 + 2270** — garantie décennale 10y + biennale 2y (FR-derived, of public order — cannot be contractually reduced); VEFA contracts require completion or reimbursement guarantee; mandatory RC décennale for builders. Hidden trap: small market — limited insurer pool may price décennale higher than FR/BE; foreign buyers should verify lalux.lu / equivalent standard policy terms. **Confidence: HIGH**.

**MC (Monaco)** — **Code civil monégasque Art. 1630** — décennale liability for architects and entrepreneurs for ruin from construction or soil defects; **no garantie de parfait achèvement and no garantie biennale** under Monégasque civil law (only the décennale applies — narrower than FR regime). Proposition de loi n° 248 (2019) sought to create compulsory décennale insurance — verify enactment status at [conseil-national.mc](https://www.conseil-national.mc/). Hidden trap: absence of mandatory dommages-ouvrage-equivalent insurance means even where decennial liability exists, recourse against insolvent builder may fail. **Confidence: MEDIUM**.

**MT (Malta)** — **Civil Code Chapter 16, Article 1638** — 15-year liability (longer than most regional regimes) for architect and contractor where building perishes wholly/partially or shows manifest danger of falling owing to construction or soil defect; developers must hold insurance ≥€750,000 per L.N. 136 of 2019; Building & Construction Authority (BCA) supersedes earlier BRO/MEPA roles. Hidden trap: 15-year statute is exceptional; however, post-collapse-incident reforms (2019 onward) primarily address third-party damage rather than buyer-defect remedies; foreign buyers should verify whether the developer's insurance also names them as beneficiary. **Confidence: HIGH**.

**NL (Netherlands)** — **BW 7:758** general defect-liability + **Wet kwaliteitsborging voor het bouwen (Wkb)** in force 1 Jan 2024 — private kwaliteitsborger independently supervises construction in graded risk classes (Klasse 1 simple buildings + smaller commercial through 2028, then phased expansion); contractor must maintain project file 10 years; **BW 7:758 verborgen-gebrekenregeling** complements with extended liability for hidden defects (consult primary text — circulating "20-year" figure is for serious gross-negligence cases, not standard regime). Hidden trap: Wkb shifted public liability from municipality to private quality-assurer — foreign buyers should verify which kwaliteitsborger was assigned and request the project file at completion. **Confidence: HIGH**.

**NO (Norway)** — **Bustadoppføringslova (Lov 1997-06-13 nr. 43)** — 5-year complaint period from handover; mandatory contractor guarantee 3% (developer's plot) or 10% (buyer's plot) of contract sum during construction + reduced post-handover; non-derogable consumer-protective statute (cannot be contracted-out). Hidden trap: 5y is shorter than IT/FR/UK 10y; foreign buyers should require independent FDV documentation (forvaltning, drift, vedlikehold) at handover. **Confidence: HIGH**.

**PT (Portugal)** — **Código Civil Art. 1225** — 5-year warranty (defects from soil, construction, modification, repair, or execution errors) on immovable property destined for long duration; defect must be reported within 1 year of discovery, action filed within 1 year of notification; **Decreto-Lei 268/94** mandates bank guarantee for off-plan. Hidden trap: 5-year cap is half of décennale — foreign buyers should layer contractual extension + insurance; NRAU 2012 reforms governed rental but not new-build warranty; AL (alojamento local) registration friction does not affect build-warranty regime. **Confidence: HIGH**.

**SE (Sweden)** — **Konsumenttjänstlag (1985:716)** — 10-year reklamation period for fixed-structure works on small-scale residential (småhusentreprenad); statutory presumed-warranty of ~2 years under § 59-equivalent (consumer protected during this window without proving fault); after 2 years, burden shifts to consumer. ABS 18 industry standard contract widely used. Hidden trap: 10y is responsibility not guarantee — proof burden on consumer post-year-2; **Boverkets byggregler (BBR)** updated periodically — verify current version at [boverket.se](https://www.boverket.se/). **Confidence: HIGH**.

**SM (San Marino)** — Codice civile sammarinese inherits Italian civil-law tradition; specific provisions for construction defects parallel Italian Codice Civile Art. 1669 (10-year décennale-equivalent for ruin/serious defects on long-duration buildings) but `data not publicly available in EN — verify at` [consigliograndegenerale.sm](https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/) primary source. Off-plan deposit-protection: no statutory equivalent to IT DL 122/2005; contract-level only. Hidden trap: small jurisdiction, limited court precedent in public databases; foreign buyers should require IT-licensed insurer for décennale cover. **Confidence: LOW**.

## CEE + Western Balkans + Baltics + Caucasus

### Regional pattern

The 21-country region splits into two structural-warranty families. **The 10-year decennial bloc** — RO, HR, SI, BG (per type), LT, AL, BA, ME, MK, RS, MD — descends from the SFRJ-era *Zakon o obligacionim odnosima* (1978) Art. 644 (BA federal text and equivalents in HR/RS/ME/MK) and the Latin civil-law decennial tradition (RO Codul civil + Legea 10/1995 Art. 29-30; SI Obligacijski zakonik). **The 5-year (or shorter) bloc** — PL (Kodeks cywilny rękojmia), CZ (OZ §§ 2629-2630 with 2-year pre-existing-defect presumption), HU (the 1985 *kötelező alkalmassági idő* split 5/10 yr by structural element), SK (Občiansky zákonník § 622 — 36 months for new builds), EE, LV (5 % bond for the *defects-liability period*) — derives from German/Austrian *Werkvertrag* with consumer-friendly burden-of-proof rules. The Caucasus three (AM/AZ/GE) followed the post-Soviet RF Civil Code template (5-year statutory limitation for hidden construction defects, Art. 756-757-style); GE has not centrally codified a decennial regime — Georgia regulates via the 2024-amended construction-permit decree and the building-code overhaul effective 1 Jan 2026 that lifts IBC 2024 with state amendments.

**Off-plan deposit-protection is sharply uneven.** Poland is the regional outlier with the *Deweloperski Fundusz Gwarancyjny* (DFG), live since 1 Jul 2022 and extended to all developer agreements from 2 Jul 2024 — closed escrow at 0.1 % / open escrow at 0.45 % of each tranche, mandatory by law, no opt-out. Cyprus uses a different mechanism: deposit the contract at the Land Registry within 6 months under Sale of Property (Specific Performance) Law 81(I)/2011 (amended by 132(I)/2023) to obtain *priority encumbrance* against the parcel. Everywhere else — CZ, HU, RO, BG, HR, SI, SK, BA, RS, ME, MK, AL, MD, EE, LV, LT, GE, AM, AZ — escrow is contractual / market-driven, with bank guarantees common in EU-CEE (HR, EE) and absent / weakly enforced in WB6 + Caucasus.

**Cyprus deserves a separate flag.** Damp / moisture / waterproofing failures in new-build apartments are documented across both republics (south + TRNC); peer-reviewed Cyprus case study (MDPI Buildings 2018) confirms that damp-proof courses are routinely omitted in slab-on-grade construction, and the Cyprus Mail (Nov 2024) noted *no consolidated fire-safety law for buildings existed* before the 2024 Building Safety reforms. The HIPPODAMOS digital-permit platform went live Jul 2024 shifting accountability onto licensed architects/engineers and freeing the authority to inspect on-site.

**The 2024-2026 reform calendar is dominated by EU-driven changes** affecting the 11 EU members in this set (BG, CY, CZ, EE, HR, HU, LT, LV, PL, RO, SI, SK): EPBD IV (Directive (EU) 2024/1275) entered force 28 May 2024, transposition by 29 May 2026 (Art. 17(15) by 1 Jan 2025); revised Construction Products Regulation (EU) 2024/3110 published 18 Dec 2024, in force 7 Jan 2025, main provisions applying 8 Jan 2026, repealing CPR 305/2011 fully by 8 Jan 2040. **Caveat on Western-Balkans + Caucasus statistics.** Centrally published defect-rate / snagging statistics are not maintained by the construction regulators of AL, BA, ME, MK, MD, AM, AZ, GE — quality assessment is done via private inspection, court litigation count, or sectoral surveys. Confidence drops accordingly.

## MENA + GCC + Israel + Türkiye

### Regional pattern

The 13-country MENA / GCC / Israel / Türkiye cluster shows three structurally different build-quality regimes layered on a common off-plan-delivery risk. The **GCC bloc (AE, SA, QA, BH, OM, KW)** has the region's strongest statutory protection for buyer deposits — every member except Kuwait now operates a regulator-supervised escrow regime modelled on Dubai Law No. 8/2007 (RERA escrow + per-project ringfencing + milestone-gated drawdowns + a retention slice for warranty). Saudi Arabia's **WAFI** platform (Off-Plan Sale Law in force April 2024) and Qatar's Aqarat-supervised escrow (Cabinet Resolution 28/2023, with the Qatar Central Bank instructions cascading in 2024-2025) are the two newest entrants; Oman's regime was overhauled by **Royal Decree 79/2025** (in force 10 March 2026), repealing the 2018 escrow decree. Kuwait remains an outlier — off-plan sales to non-Kuwaitis are tightly restricted and the new framework is still a 2025-vintage Decree-Law (Decree 7/2025 amending the 1979 ownership law, plus Decree 195/2025 on company/fund ownership).

The **Maghreb-Mashreq civil-law bloc (MA, TN, EG, LB, JO)** inherits the French *garantie décennale* logic — a 10-year strict-liability structural warranty by operation of code (Egypt CC Art. 651, Jordan CC Art. 788, Morocco/Tunisia COC Title VII, Lebanon COC Art. 668-69). The legal coverage is uniformly strong on paper but enforcement and developer-solvency dramatically diverge: Morocco's **Loi 44-00 + 107-12 VEFA** layer adds a notary-supervised escrow plus a bank/insurer completion guarantee that makes off-plan workable; Egypt has no analogous escrow (deposits flow to developers, with delivery-delay claims piling up under Civil Code Art. 157 / 215 contractual remedies); Lebanon's décennale is theoretically alive but unenforceable in practice given the 98% LBP devaluation (Jan 2023 – Mar 2024) and the 16,260+ buildings flagged at collapse risk by Order of Engineers data. Tunisia mandates per-project décennale insurance under Law 94-9 of 31 Jan 1994, but foreign-buyer authorisation friction (governor-level approval) is the binding constraint, not warranty quality.

**Türkiye** is the watershed: the 6 Feb 2023 Kahramanmaraş double-quake (AFAD official Mw 7.7 + Mw 7.6; USGS lists Mw 7.8 + Mw 7.5; ~50,000 deaths) was traced to two compounding failures — the 2018 *yapı kayıt belgesi* construction-amnesty programme (7.4 million applications legalising non-compliant structures, ≥75,000 of which were inside the eventual quake zone) and weak enforcement of **Yapı Denetim Kanunu 4708/2001**. The post-quake response is now law: **Law No. 7534 of 5 Dec 2024** amended 4708 to add 5-year periodic re-inspection of buildings holding a building-identity certificate; the **Kentsel Dönüşüm (Urban Transformation) Law 6306/2012** Implementation Regulation was rewritten on 4 Feb 2026 (lowered owner-consent threshold from 2/3 to simple majority +1, building-permit guarantee fixed at 6%). Off-plan deposit protection in TR remains structurally weaker than the GCC because **TBK Art. 478** is a *limitation period* (5 years for immovable structures, 20 years for gross negligence) — there is no escrow-style ringfence; deposits are protected primarily through the TKHK consumer-protection regime (Law 6502) and the title-deed (TAPU) transfer at delivery.

**Israel** sits in its own category — the 1973 Sale of Apartments Law (חוק המכר (דירות), as amended in 2011 and 2022) imposes a 4-year inspection / 3-year reverse-burden warranty (תקופת בדק / תקופת אחריות) plus 7-year non-structural and effectively 10-year liability for "non-conforming" defects, all backstopped by mandatory bank guarantee or insurance for **every shekel of buyer deposit** under the 1974 Sale (Apartments) (Assurance of Buyers' Investments) Law and the Sale (Apartments) (Restriction on Receipt of Money) Order ("Sahed" Order). Post-7 Oct 2023 the binding constraint is **labour, not law** — Palestinian-worker exclusion drove a manpower shortage costing the sector ~$830M/month, unsold inventory hit ~80,000 units by Q1 2025.

**Mega-project quality scrutiny** — Saudi NEOM is the regional flag-bearer: a Wall Street Journal-leaked internal audit in early 2025 found "evidence of deliberate manipulation" by project managers; PIF suspended The Line construction on 16 Sep 2025 with ~$50B already spent. Buyers exposed to MBS-Vision-2030 mega-developments (NEOM, Diriyah, Red Sea, ROSHN) should treat the WAFI escrow as protection against **deposit loss**, not against schedule slip or project-cancellation discount.

## APAC + Central Asia

### Regional pattern

APAC + Central Asia is the **highest-stakes region globally for off-plan delivery risk** — driven by the ongoing China developer-failure crisis (Evergrande [HK High Court winding-up order 29 Jan 2024, *Re China Evergrande Group* [2024] HKCFI 363](https://www.dvc.hk/insights/re-china-evergrande-group-2024-hkcfi-363); Country Garden delivering ~74,000 units H1-2025 vs ~150,000 H1-2024; Vanke seeking bond restructuring 2025) and Vietnam's $12.5bn Vạn Thịnh Phát collapse (Truong My Lan death sentence 11 Apr 2024) plus the Tan Hoang Minh $355m bond fraud (Do Anh Dung 8-year sentence 27 Mar 2024). Buyer-protection enforcement varies wildly across the region.

Two jurisdictions have **strong statutory regimes** that approach EU/AU standards: Japan's [Housing Quality Assurance Act 2000](https://www.japaneselawtranslation.go.jp/en/laws/view/4729/en) imposes a 10-year structural defect warranty backed by mandatory MLIT-licensed insurance/deposit under the Act for Secure Execution of Defect Warranty Liability (the *kashi tanpo* regime); South Korea's Multi-Family Housing Management Act provides up to 10-year structural defect liability. India's [RERA Act 2016 Section 14(3)](https://ibclaw.in/section-14-of-real-estate-regulation-and-development-act-2016-rera-adherence-to-sanctioned-plans-and-project-specifications-by-the-promoter/) layers a 5-year structural warranty plus 30-day rectify obligation onto a Section 4(2)(l)(D) **70% escrow** mandate — but enforcement is state-level (MahaRERA suspended 4,812 projects in 2024-2025; other state RERAs visibly weaker).

**Off-plan deposit-protection mechanisms** show three distinct models: (1) **bank-guarantee/escrow mandates** — Vietnam's Law on Real Estate Business 2023 (in force 1 Aug 2024) requires developer bank guarantees with buyer opt-out, plus deposit caps of 5% pre-eligibility / 50-70%/95% staged; India's RERA 70% escrow; China's Urban Real Estate Management Law pre-sale supervised escrow (post-Evergrande tightened 2022 then partially relaxed Nov 2022 with CBIRC bank-guarantee letters); (2) **regulator-licensed pre-sale schemes** — Hong Kong's [Lands Department Consent Scheme via LACO](https://www.landsd.gov.hk/en/dev-control-compliance/landsd-consent-scheme.html) blocks deposit collection until consent granted; Philippines DHSUD License-to-Sell under PD 957; Indonesia's PPJB; (3) **market-driven/weak** — Thailand, Cambodia, Maldives, Uzbekistan, Macau (post-COVID dormant), Sri Lanka (no unified building code until 2026-2028).

**Seismic-quality exposure** spiked 2024: Japan's Noto Peninsula earthquake (1 Jan 2024, M7.6, ≥240 deaths) exposed the gap between pre-1981 and post-2000 wooden-house standards — Wajima/Suzu had Z-factor 0.9 vs national 1.0; Taiwan's Hualien earthquake (3 Apr 2024, ML 7.2) marked 42 buildings "code red" but post-2022 retrofits performed visibly better.

**Korea's jeonse fraud crisis** is structurally distinct — it is not off-plan delivery risk but lump-sum lease-deposit fraud. The Special Act on Support for Victims of Jeonse Fraud (enacted Jun 2023, amended Nov 2024 + Apr 2026) recognised ~38,000+ victims by May 2026 with ₩2.28 trillion ($1.7bn) losses 2022-2024.

## LATAM + Caribbean

### Regional pattern

The civil-law countries of LATAM split into a **5-year vs 10-year structural-warranty divide** rooted in which European code their Civil Code descended from. **Napoleonic / French-derived codes** (Dominican Republic Art 1792; Uruguay Art 1844; Colombia Art 2060 §3; Ecuador Art 1937; Argentina CCyC Art 1273; Panama CC Art 1343) impose a **10-year decennial liability** ("responsabilidad decenal") on builder, architect, and — increasingly — promoter, for total or partial ruin of the work. **Spanish/Italian-derived codes that absorbed the empresario→dueño contractual-warranty model** (Mexico CCF Art 2634; Peru CC Art 1784; Costa Rica CC Art 1185) typically run **5 years** for hidden vices, with no automatic third-party (subsequent-purchaser) standing absent a successor-buyer clause. Brazil sits in the middle: **Código Civil Art 618** is a 5-year *guarantee* of solidity-and-safety, but the action to claim damages prescribes under Art 205 at 10 years, so the practical exposure lengthens once a defect manifests (STJ Súmula 194 line of cases).

The **single most important LATAM innovation for off-plan buyers is Brazil's Patrimônio de Afetação** (Lei 10.931/2004), instituted after the 1999 Encol bankruptcy left 42,000 buyers without delivery. The mechanism segregates each project's land and receivables from the developer's general patrimony — bankruptcy of the incorporator does not pull the affected estate into the bankruptcy mass. **Mexico has no national equivalent**: off-plan protection is fragmentary, leaning on PROFECO consumer-contract registration plus, for foreign buyers in the restricted zone (50 km coast / 100 km border, Art 27 Constitución), the mandatory **fideicomiso de zona restringida** with a CNBV-licensed bank trustee under SRE permit. State-level Civil Codes diverge — CDMX, Quintana Roo, Yucatán, and Jalisco each have their own `Código Civil` numbering, and the federal CCF applies only supplementally.

**Argentina's structural-warranty regime (CCyC Art 1273, 10 years) is among the strongest on paper, but the macro overlay has been the dominant risk for foreign buyers**: cepo cambiario (FX controls) compressed developer cash-flow 2019–April 2025, distorted USD-denominated off-plan contracts, and made escrow effectively impossible. **The cepo was lifted 14 April 2025** under Milei's IMF EFF programme; ISAC construction activity rebounded 15.8% YoY in March 2025. Patrimônio-de-afetação-style segregation **does not exist** in AR — escrow under Ley 25.997 is permissive, not mandatory.

**Chile's Art 18 LGUC** is the region's most granular: **10 years for structural elements, 5 years for installations (electrical/plumbing/sealing), 3 years for finishes** — counted from final reception by the **DOM (Dirección de Obras Municipales)** for structure/installations, and from registration in the Conservador de Bienes Raíces for finishes. **SERNAC has class-action standing** under Ley 19.496 when ≥6 owners in the same complex are affected. Post-2010 Maule and 2024 regional quakes, DOM enforcement variance (Santiago vs regions) is the buyer's real risk, not the statute.

**Caribbean common-law jurisdictions (BB, JM, BS, BZ) have no statutory new-build warranty equivalent to the civil-law decennial**. Protection is contract-driven (express warranty in the Sale & Purchase agreement), with **building-code enforcement via local building authorities**: Bahamas Building Code 3rd Ed. 2003 (180 mph wind / hurricane shutters mandatory, post-Dorian 2019 review ongoing); Belize Building Act Cap. 131 (rev. 2020) + Belize Building Regulations SI No. 157 of 2022 administered by the Central Building Authority; Barbados Planning and Development Act 2019 (no warranty clause). Hurricane-zone insurance underwriters effectively impose the *de facto* defect-detection regime through pre-bind survey requirements rather than statute.

**PY (Paraguay)** — **Código Civil Art 1670** 10-year liability (architect/constructor) for ruin (Ley 1.183/85). Off-plan protection contract-based; no Patrimônio de Afetação equivalent. Hidden trap: secondary market — limited public price data and no MLS — comparable transactions opaque outside Asunción. Defect data not publicly aggregated — verify with Cámara Paraguaya de la Construcción (CAPACO). **Confidence: MEDIUM**.

**TC (Turks & Caicos)** — British Overseas Territory, English common law; **no statutory décennale/LOE-style new-build warranty** — recourse is contractual + tort (BSA 2022/NHBC do NOT apply). TCI enforces a **hurricane building code** (Department of Planning) — the influential model code that fed the OECS Codes; **documented build-quality split**: newer **post-2002 code-compliant** buildings fared far better than older stock in Irma (a code-built utility structure on Grand Turk took only minor damage at Cat-5 winds — visittci / bdcnetwork, secondary). **Baseline event: Hurricane Irma (Sept 2017) passed OVER TCI as a Category 5** (north eyewall over the islands 2+ hours, sustained winds >~156 mph), demolishing roofs/structures + ~1,300 utility poles; **Hurricane Maria followed ~2 weeks later**; worst-hit South Caicos / Grand Turk / Salt Cay (NOAA NHC TCR AL112017). Off-plan deposit protection: **no statutory scheme** — attorney-escrow only. Hidden trap: **post-Irma reporting noted many owners were UNINSURED**, which severely complicated rebuild — pre-2002 stock often has weak roof connections / possible asbestos-lead in oldest builds; verify post-2002-code build + impact glazing + a funded, binding hurricane-insurance position (a major recurring carrying cost). **Confidence: MEDIUM** (no statutory warranty; Irma Cat-5 + post-2002 code split HIGH-sourced).

**TT (Trinidad & Tobago)** — English-derived common-law twin-island republic where the **apex court is the UK Judicial Committee of the Privy Council (JCPC), NOT the CCJ** (T&T acceded only to the CCJ's Original Jurisdiction); foreign acquisition runs via the **Foreign Investment Act Chap. 70:07 licence/threshold regime** (Tobago land always needs the President's licence; Trinidad residential ≤1 acre is licence-free but notice-bound) with hard-currency settlement, over a dual title system (**~75–80% Old-Law deeds, NO state guarantee** — chain-of-title trace required; RPA/Torrens the minority — *est. per a law-firm source*). **No statutory new-build warranty** (no décennale/LOE/RERA equivalent), and — distinctively — **NO mandatory national building code is confirmed in force**: only the **voluntary TTBS small-building guide (TTS 599)** exists, with a mandatory code still being advocated as of 2018 reporting (`verify current code status at the T&T Bureau of Standards — TTBS`). The load-bearing build-quality hazard is **seismic**: T&T is "one of the more seismically active zones in the Eastern Caribbean," averaging **~260 earthquakes >M2.0/yr** with a historical **M7.9 (1766)** on record (UWI Seismic Research Centre, verbatim) — so seismic-aware, engineered construction is the #1 due-diligence item; do **NOT** attribute any "large event expected sooner rather than later" prediction or a "UWI joined calls for a building code" claim to UWI SRC (those circulate but are not on the cited page — the high-hazard conclusion stands on the ~260 quakes/yr + M7.9 alone). Hurricane risk is **comparatively LOW but not zero** (~10–11°N, generally south of the main Atlantic belt; Tobago more exposed; `dated frequency data not publicly available — quantify at the T&T Meteorological Service / ODPM`); wet-season riverine/urban flooding (East-West Corridor / Caroni basin) is material — pull ODPM parcel-level flood maps, never elevate the national rate to a parcel claim. Off-plan deposit protection: **no statutory scheme** — attorney/authorised-dealer settlement, contract-level only. Hidden trap: do NOT assume a French/Spanish-style decennial OR a code-compliance certificate exists; insist on engineered seismic-aware structure + an attorney chain-of-title search on Old-Law parcels; and note **USD rationing can delay repatriating sale proceeds** (the #1 practical foreign-owner exit risk). **Confidence: MEDIUM** (seismic ~260/yr + M7.9 + dual-title split HIGH-sourced; no mandatory code confirmed + hurricane-frequency + parcel-flood data are open gaps to verify).

**UY (Uruguay)** — **Código Civil Art 1844 as reformed by Ley 19.726 (2018)**: **10 yr stability/solidity / 5 yr other defects / 2 yr finishes**; once defect manifests, action prescribes in **4 years** (down from prior 20). Off-plan: BCU-supervised escrow available but not mandatory. Hidden trap: pre-2018 reform applies to projects with delivery before the law's effective date — verify which regime governs based on the *acta de recepción* date. **Confidence: HIGH**.

**VG (British Virgin Islands)** — British Overseas Territory, English common law; **no statutory décennale/LOE-style new-build warranty** — recourse is contractual + tort (BSA 2022/NHBC do NOT apply). **Baseline catastrophe: Hurricane Irma made a DIRECT Category-5 hit on 6 Sep 2017** — the then-Premier's 27 Sep 2017 statement that "about 70 percent of our buildings" were lost or damaged was carried on the BVI Government media centre, **which did not survive the `bvi.gov.vg` → `gov.vg` migration — `the government release is no longer publicly available; verify the damage figures with the BVI Department of Disaster Management (ddm.vg) or the Government of the Virgin Islands press archive at https://gov.vg/press-releases`**; wider reporting put 85% of housing affected and 4,000+ homes destroyed *(secondary)*. The official post-Irma posture is that Cat-5 is "the new norm"; Government **announced a building-code review + upgrade (27 Sep 2017) targeting Cat-5+ resilience + climate/green-building** — ⚠️ **confirm the CURRENT adopted Building Code status with the Town & Country Planning Department** (formal adoption of a Cat-5 standard not independently verified — do not assume it is in force). Off-plan deposit protection: **no statutory scheme** — attorney-escrow only. Hidden trap: **insurance is expensive and hard to obtain post-Irma** (premiums "as much as doubled" for claimants per UNDRR/PreventionWeb; the 2010 census found ~40% of dwellings uninsured) and may be unobtainable on older stock — **pre-Irma timber/older stock carries materially higher loss risk**; confirm post-2017-code build (concrete, hurricane straps, impact glazing, reinforced roof) + structural survey + a binding windstorm quote BEFORE committing. **Confidence: LOW** (no statutory warranty; Irma direct-Cat-5 + insurance scarcity HIGH-sourced; current code-adoption status unverified; the 70% government quotation is now unsourced).

## Africa + Anglo-non-EU

### Regional pattern

**Anglo-non-EU (AU/CA/NZ/US)** runs **state/province-level mandatory new-home warranty schemes** layered on top of provincial builder licensing. The dominant model is a 1-2-10 or 2-5-10 split (workmanship / systems / structural). Implementation differs sharply: NSW HBCF (icare statutory monopoly), VIC DBI, QLD QBCC; Ontario Tarion + HCRA (split regulator/insurer Jan 2021); BC Licensing & Consumer Services under HPA; Quebec GCR; US has no federal warranty regime — instead a market of private 1-2-10 warranties (2-10 HBW, RWC) layered on state Statutes of Repose. Three legacy crises still drive enforcement: **AU Lacrosse 2014 / Opal 24 Dec 2018 / Mascot Jun 2019** triggered the Shergold-Weir Building Confidence Report (2018), the NSW Design and Building Practitioners Act 2020, and iCIRT (Equifax-administered builder rating, 2021+). **NZ leaky homes crisis (1990s-2000s monolithic-cladding defects)** still drives Building Act 10-year longstop litigation through the Weathertight Homes Resolution Service. **US Surfside Champlain Towers South 24 Jun 2021** (98 dead) triggered FL SB-4D 2022 milestone inspections (25/30-year for ≥3-storey buildings), CA SB-326 (HOA condos) + SB-721 (rentals) balcony EEE inspections (deadlines extended to 1 Jan 2026 by AB 2579), and NY Local Law 152 gas-piping cycle.

**Africa-anglophone (KE/NG/ZA/GH)** is regulator-led but enforcement-uneven. **South Africa NHBRC** is the tightest scheme: mandatory builder enrolment under Housing Consumers Protection Measures Act 95/1998 (replaced Jan 2025 by the **Housing Consumer Protection Act**, signed by President Ramaphosa 27 Jan 2025, expanding scope to owner-builders + renovations); 5-year structural + 1-year roof-leak + 3-month finish; ~1.3% enrolment fee. **Kenya NCA** rolled out the **National Building Code 2024** (Legal Notice 47, gazetted 1 Mar 2024, launched 17 Jul 2024) — first revision in 56 years, replacing the 1968 Local Government Adoptive By-laws Order; multi-hazard resilience, LPG, digital connectivity. **Nigeria Lagos LASBCA + CORBON**: enforcement is the failure point — between Jan-Jun 2025 Lagos recorded ≥8 collapses (18 deaths); LASBCA has sealed 3,500+ non-compliant structures since 2022 and now mandates stage-by-stage inspections (post-Ikoyi 21-storey collapse, 46 dead). **Ghana** has the GS 1207:2018 Building Code + ARC (Architects Registration Council, statutory under Architects Act 1969 NLCD 357) but **no mandatory new-home warranty and no statutory escrow** — off-plan deposit fraud is a known unresolved gap (38+ buyers sued one developer in 2024).

**Africa-francophone-derived (MU/CV)** inherits **garantie décennale (10-year structural liability)**. Mauritius mandates it under Building Control Act 2012 (Act 9/2012) — every developer must hold a property-damage policy guaranteeing 10-year structural cover for foundations, supports, beams, framework, load-bearing walls; technical control during construction is a precondition (no retroactive cover for finished buildings). Cape Verde uses Portuguese-derived Civil Code (Decree-Law 31-A/99) with a 5-year contractor warranty + 10-year developer-purchaser warranty cognate to Portugal's Article 1225 — but enforcement is small-island / small-bar.

**Africa-island (RW/SC)** are small-stock markets with modern regulators. **Rwanda Housing Authority** launched **KUBAKA** (replacing BPMIS) 2025, integrated digital permits with stage inspections — Kigali-centric, high standards relative to regional peers. **Seychelles Planning Authority** under the **Physical Planning Act 2021** (replacing Town & Country Planning Act Cap. 237); 2024 Physical Planning (Building) Regulations gazetted SI 43/2024; market-driven warranty.

## Accessibility & adaptable-dwelling build-code

**The trap**: buyers conflate three separate things — (a) a *mandate* that a NEW dwelling be built wheelchair-adaptable, (b) *anti-discrimination* law governing public buildings or multifamily housing, and (c) *retrofit grants* for an existing home. Most jurisdictions mandate far LESS dwelling-level accessibility than buyers assume, and headline EU/UK reforms are routinely overstated. For a buyer who needs (or may later need) step-free living, verify whether the specific dwelling was built under an *in-force* adaptability standard — an announced-but-not-commenced reform protects nobody.

- **AU** — NCC 2022 **Part H8 "Livable Housing Design"** (national commencement **1 Oct 2023**) mandates step-free entry, an entry-level accessible bathroom + shower, and grabrail-ready reinforced walls (≈ Livable Housing Australia *Silver* baseline — the ABCB primary text itself does not use the word "silver"). **Adoption is fragmented**: ACT (15 Jan 2024), NT (1 Oct 2023), VIC (1 May 2024), SA + TAS (1 Oct 2024), QLD (via its *Queensland Modern Homes* initiative) — but **NSW and WA are both listed "Not adopting"** on the ABCB adoption table. A new house in Sydney or Perth carries NO livable-housing mandate; the same house in Melbourne or Canberra does. *(PRIMARY: [abcb.gov.au adoption dates](https://www.abcb.gov.au/ncc-2022-state-and-territory-adoption-dates))*
- **UK (England)** — Approved Document M Vol 1 sets three tiers: **M4(1) "visitable" is the mandatory baseline**; **M4(2) accessible-and-adaptable** and **M4(3) wheelchair-user** apply *only where the local planning authority imposes them as a condition*. The government's **29 Jul 2022** decision to make M4(2) the universal minimum was **announced but, as of 2026, NOT in force** — it was deferred to a further technical consultation and no Statutory Instrument has since commenced it (verify before relying). Do not assume a post-2022 English new-build is M4(2). *(PRIMARY: [gov.uk consultation outcome — raising accessibility standards for new homes](https://www.gov.uk/government/consultations/raising-accessibility-standards-for-new-homes/outcome/raising-accessibility-standards-for-new-homes-summary-of-consultation-responses-and-government-response))*
- **US** — **Fair Housing Act** design+construction requirements (7 items, incl. accessible route, grab-bar-ready walls, usable kitchens/baths) apply to **covered multifamily dwellings (4+ units) first occupied after 13 Mar 1991** — **NOT detached single-family homes**. The **ADA covers public accommodations**, not private residences. There is **no federal wheelchair-adaptability mandate for a single house**; state/local codes (and any HOA rules) govern. *(PRIMARY: [HUD FHA multifamily design+construction](https://www.huduser.gov/portal/publications/fairhsg/multifamily.html) · [ADA Title III](https://archive.ada.gov/ada_title_III.htm))*
- **EU** — The **European Accessibility Act (Directive (EU) 2019/882)**, applicable from **28 Jun 2025**, covers **products and services** (banking, e-commerce, transport, terminals); the **built environment is OPTIONAL** (Art. 4(4): a Member State *may* require it for premises used by clients of covered services). It is **NOT a residential-dwelling accessibility mandate** — dwelling accessibility stays in national building codes. Treat any "the EAA makes homes accessible" claim as overstated. *(PRIMARY: [EUR-Lex Dir (EU) 2019/882](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2019/882/oj/eng))*
- **IE** — **Part M / TGD M** requires new dwellings + extensions to be **"visitable"** (mandatory) — comparable to UK M4(1), not a full wheelchair-adaptable mandate. *(PRIMARY: gov.ie TGD M — S.I. 608/2022)*
- **NZ** — Building Code **D1 + NZS 4121:2001** mandate accessibility for **public-access buildings and communal residential accommodation only**, not individual private houses. *(PRIMARY: [building.govt.nz D1 — buildings that must be accessible](https://www.building.govt.nz/building-code-compliance/d-access/d1-access-routes/buildings-that-must-be-accessible-for-people-with-disabilities))*
- **CA** — residential barrier-free provisions are set **provincially** in each building code; the federal Accessible Canada Act (2019) targets federally regulated entities, not private homes — verify the specific province.

**Retrofit grant worth knowing**: **UK Disabled Facilities Grant (DFG)** — means-tested, up to **£30,000 (England)** / **£36,000 (Wales)** / **£25,000 (Northern Ireland)** toward stairlifts, ramps, level-access showers, widened doors and heating/access adaptations. *(PRIMARY: [gov.uk DFG — what you'll get](https://www.gov.uk/disabled-facilities-grants/what-youll-get))*

**Confidence: HIGH** for AU / UK / US / EU / IE / UK-DFG (all primary-sourced); **MEDIUM** for NZ (private-dwelling carve-out inferred from D1 + NZS 4121 scope) and CA (provincial — not individually re-verified per province). **Last verified**: 2026-06-03.

## EV-charger build-readiness mandate calendar (2024-2027)

A small but fast-moving slice of the energy-transition wave (sibling to the EPBD IV calendar above) now mandates **EV-charging build readiness** — pre-cabling / ducting / conduit, and in some cases an installed charge point — for **new-build and major-renovation residential** property. The buyer question is concrete: *is this dwelling already EV-ready, or will it need a retrofit?* Retrofitting a charge point into an existing parcel (trenching, panel upgrade, sub-board, possible grid-connection upgrade) is materially more expensive than pre-cabling laid at construction. Three jurisdictions in the corpus carry a hard residential mandate; most do not.

- **15 Jun 2022** — **UK (England only)** — **Building Regs Part S** / Approved Document S in force: every **new dwelling** with associated parking → **1 EV charge point**; new residential **>10 parking spaces** → cable routes in the remaining spaces; same on major renovations ending with >10 spaces. **Wales / Scotland / NI have separate building regs — Part S does NOT apply there; verify the devolved code.** *(PRIMARY: [gov.uk AD S](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/infrastructure-for-charging-electric-vehicles-approved-document-s))*
- **1 Jan 2023** — **California (US)** — **CALGreen 2022 edition** (Title 24 Part 11) already required **EV-capable** infrastructure for new residential. The 2025 edition (below) tightens it.
- **28 May 2024** — **EU (27 MS)** — **EPBD recast Directive (EU) 2024/1275, Article 14** "Infrastructure for sustainable mobility" entered force. *(The ROADMAP's "Art. 12" was wrong — the correct citation is **Art. 14**, confirmed against the OJ text.)* *(PRIMARY: [EUR-Lex 2024/1275](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2024/1275/oj/eng))*
- **1 Jan 2026** — **California (US)** — **CALGreen 2025 edition** (Title 24 Part 11, adopted by the Building Standards Commission) effective. New **single-family** → **EV Capable** per dwelling; new **multifamily** → expanded EV-ready requirements (Level 2 readiness for assigned spaces + a share of common/unassigned spaces provided as EV chargers — confirm the exact ratios in the current Part 11 code tables). *(PRIMARY for the Title 24 Pt 11 designation + 1 Jan 2026 effective date: [dgs.ca.gov BSC](https://www.dgs.ca.gov/bsc/calgreen); the per-unit / common-space ratios live in the Part 11 code text, not the BSC landing page.)*
- **29 May 2026** — **EU (27 MS)** — **EPBD Art. 14 transposition deadline.** From here national codes must require, for **new + major-renovation residential with >3 car parking spaces** (car park inside or physically adjacent): **pre-cabling for ≥50% of spaces + ducting for the rest**, plus **≥1 recharging point** on new builds. The rule that binds a parcel is the *national transposition* (lands per-MS through 2025-2026) — check the destination country's building code for the exact in-force date.
- **1 Jan 2027** — **EU (27 MS)** — EPBD Art. 14(2) deadline for **existing non-residential** buildings with **>20 parking spaces** (≥1 recharging point per 10 spaces, or ducting ≥50%). The residential duty is the new-build / major-renovation one, not a retrofit-the-existing-stock duty.
- **Rolling 2024-2026** — **US (non-CA)** — **patchwork**, no federal residential mandate. ~12 states (incl. CA, OR, WA, CO, MD, NJ, RI, MA, NY, VT, IL, NM) plus many cities have added EV-readiness provisions to their building/energy codes (state-by-state opt-in; the relevant IECC EV-charging appendix is adopted per jurisdiction, not federally). Verify the specific state/county code. *(SECONDARY: [ACEEE residential codes DB](https://database.aceee.org/state/residential-codes) — verify per state)*

**Honest tail**: the **majority of the 126-country corpus has no residential EV-ready build mandate** as of mid-2026. Outside the EU-27 (post-transposition), England, and the EV-readiness US states/cities, EV pre-cabling is a developer/buyer specification choice — not a code requirement — and an existing-stock dwelling almost certainly needs a retrofit assessment. For any non-EU / non-UK / non-California property, treat EV-readiness as `data not publicly available — verify the local building code` rather than assuming a mandate.

**Confidence**: **HIGH** on the EU Art. 14 thresholds + transposition date, UK Part S, and California Title 24 Pt 11 effective date (all primary-source verified). **MEDIUM** on the per-state US patchwork count (aggregator snapshot) and on EU national-transposition dates (set per Member State, not centrally). **Last verified**: 2026-06-03.

## 7 universal mitigations

1. **Verify BOTH statutory warranty AND off-plan deposit protection BEFORE signing the off-plan contract.** They are independent layers. A jurisdiction with strong statutory warranty (TR TBK Art. 478, LB FR-derived garantie décennale) can have minimal off-plan deposit protection. Demand to see (a) the statutory warranty citation in the construction contract, (b) the bank guarantee / escrow account number / RERA registration / NHBC certificate / Tarion enrolment number / NHBRC enrolment number / Patrimônio de Afetação registration / Loi Spinetta dommages-ouvrage policy.
2. **For off-plan in CN, TR, VN, AE, EG, BR — verify the developer's specific project escrow + delivery track record.** Don't rely on the country regime — verify the SPECIFIC project. CN Evergrande / Country Garden / Vanke / Sunac all had escrow systems on paper that failed enforcement. AE RERA-escrow protected most deposits during 2008-2014 cancellations but didn't restore quality. Use developer-rating tools where they exist (AU NSW iCIRT since Jan 2023; IN MahaRERA developer scorecard; AE Dubai Land Department developer history). For BR, verify Patrimônio de Afetação is registered for the specific project at the cartório.
3. **For TR — verify yapı kayıt belgesi vs full ruhsat for any pre-2018 building.** Pre-2018 amnesty programs legitimised ~9.7 million unlicensed buildings carrying HIGHER seismic risk. Post-6 Feb 2023 earthquake reform tightened Yapı Denetim 4708 enforcement, but pre-amnesty stock remains in market. For any TR property built pre-2018, demand the original building permit (ruhsat) and the iskan (occupancy permit) — NOT just the yapı kayıt belgesi (amnesty registration certificate).
4. **For US — verify post-Surfside structural-integrity-reserve compliance** for any condo ≥3 stories in FL (SB-4D milestone inspections at 25/30 years), CA (SB-326 + SB-721 balcony inspections every 9 years), NY (Local Law 152 gas piping inspections). Special assessments triggered by milestone inspection findings can run hundreds of thousands of dollars per unit. Demand current Reserve Study + most recent milestone inspection report + Owner Disclosure documents pre-purchase.
5. **For UK + IE — verify post-Grenfell cladding compliance** under Building Safety Act 2022 + Higher-Risk Buildings Regulation 2023; for any building >18m or 7 storeys, demand the Building Safety Case + EWS1 cladding form (rating A1/A2/B1/B2). Leaseholder Protections under BSA 2022 cap leaseholder liability for cladding remediation. IE has parallel Building Control Amendment Regulations 2014 (BCAR) Assigned Certifier requirement post-2014 + retrospective 2024 Apartments Defects (Costs of Remediation) Bill scope.
6. **For NZ — leaky homes legacy + Master Build 10-Year Guarantee verification.** Any building constructed 1990s-2000s with monolithic cladding (no cavity, plaster-on-polystyrene) is in the leaky-homes risk pool. Demand Council LIM (Land Information Memorandum) report + cladding-system documentation + Weathertight Homes Resolution Service (WHRS) status check. Newer builds with Master Build Association membership get 10-year structural warranty.
7. **Re-verify regulator URLs + warranty schemes + recent disaster-driven reforms within 30 days of decision-day.** This document's `Last verified: 2026-05-10`; warranty + off-plan + disaster-driven reforms have a half-life of 12-24 months. Most volatile categories: TR Yapı Denetim post-2023 reform implementation; CN city-by-city escrow tightening; AU NSW DBP Act + iCIRT enforcement; UK BSA 2022 + Higher-Risk Buildings Reg implementation; US FL SB-4D milestone inspection + structural integrity reserve studies; NL Wkb 2024 graded risk-class adjustments; SA Saudi Building Code 2023 enforcement; CY Building Safety Law 158(Ι)/2024 implementation; IN RERA state-by-state enforcement; ES Ley 9/2022 sobre la Calidad de la Arquitectura. Decision-makers should pull current regulator notice + warranty scheme rulebook + most recent post-disaster amendment before relying on any specific number here.
