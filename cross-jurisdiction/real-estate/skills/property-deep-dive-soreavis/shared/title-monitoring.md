# Universal `--title-monitoring` Section — Post-Ownership Title-Fraud Defensive-Instrument & Registry-Alert Registry

Post-ownership title-fraud / deed-theft *defensive instrument* and registry-alert enrolment for absentee foreign owners — which land registry operates a free owner-alert, which supports an anti-fraud restriction on title, whether a non-resident can enrol, and what the loss backstop is. Loaded when `--title-monitoring` is invoked.

**Snapshot**: May 2026.

**Scope**: this is **not** a fraud taxonomy and **not** a transaction-time check. `--scams` (and its `scams-postcompletion.md` extension) catalogue *how* title/deed forgery happens and the post-deed scam vectors; `--remote` covers deed *execution* mechanics; `--integrity` audits the skill's own output. This section is the orthogonal **ongoing post-ownership defensive-instrument & registry-architecture** layer: once you own and live abroad, *what monitoring and what registry-side lock can you put in place, can you as a non-resident actually enrol, and if you are defrauded does a state assurance fund make you whole*. It pairs with `--scams` + `--remote` but is a defensive-instrument registry, not a scam list.

## Universal contract

For the property's country, return:

1. **Registry system type** — Torrens/state-guaranteed-title · deeds-recording (negative) · Grundbuch-judicial / notarial public-faith · mid-transition / no-operational-register (a citable structural fact even where no service exists)
2. **Owner-alert service** — named free service + enrolment URL + **can a non-resident enrol** (eID / national-contact-register / in-country attorney requirement), OR `no standing owner-alert — protection is structural via <notarial/judicial gatekeeping>; verify at <registry>`
3. **Anti-fraud restriction / lock** — the lodgeable instrument that *blocks* (not merely notifies): UK Form LL/RQ restriction · ES/PE inmovilización · JP 不正登記防止申出 · caveat against dealings · disposition block — name it
4. **Loss backstop** — Torrens assurance/compensation fund vs title-insurance norm vs state-liability action vs none
5. **Most actionable step for an absentee foreign owner** (one)
6. **Confidence** — HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW per the skill's contract

## The registry-architecture spine (classify first)

The defence available is set by the register type, not by vigilance:

- **Torrens / state-guaranteed title + statutory Assurance Fund** (AU states, NZ LTA 2017, SG LTA, PH PD 1529, MY post-*Tan Ying Hong*, CA ON/BC, UK registered title). Indefeasibility + a compensation fund: a defrauded owner is *made whole*; the instrument is a registry caveat/restriction, often plus a free owner-alert.
- **Deeds-recording (negative) system** (US counties, ZA Deeds Office, much of the Caribbean, BR cartório). No state guarantee of title; the backstop is **owner's title insurance bought at purchase** plus, where offered, a county/registry fraud-alert subscription (often paid or county-only).
- **Grundbuch-judicial / notarial public-faith** (DE §891/§892 BGB, AT, CH ZGB 973, LI; civil-law notarial-deeds FR/IT/NL/ES/PT/BE/LU/Nordics). A court or notary gatekeeps every entry with public faith → a forged transfer is structurally near-impossible. Typically **no consumer self-alert because none is needed** — though some layer one on top (ES CORPME Alertas, NO Kartverket SMS, NL Kadaster kennisgeving, IE Tailte).
- **Mid-transition / no operational register** (AD no register until ~2027, GR Hellenic Cadastre transition, MT dual to 2035, EG/TN unregistered urfi/older-regime). The defensive instrument is *registration itself* + notarial protocol; non-/mis-registration is the dominant risk, not forgery.

## Verdict bands

| Band | Trigger | Read |
|---|---|---|
| 🟢 | Torrens/state-guaranteed + assurance fund AND a free, non-resident-enrollable owner-alert + a lodgeable restriction | "Enrol in the alert and lodge the restriction; you are both warned and indemnified." |
| 🟡 | Strong structural protection (Grundbuch / notarial public-faith) — forgery near-impossible, so the absence of a consumer alert is not a gap | "Structurally protected; no enrolment needed; verify the notarial/registry chain at purchase." |
| 🟠 | Deeds-recording / no assurance fund — protection depends on the owner buying title insurance + self-checking; alert paid or county-only | "Buy an owner's title-insurance policy at purchase; set up the county/paid alert; self-search periodically." |
| 🔴 | No operational/forgery-resistant register, documented registry-integrity failure, or no alert + no fund + high absentee fraud exposure | "Structural exposure is high for an absentee owner — monitored local counsel + title insurance where available; treat as a holding-structure question." |

## ⚠️ 7 cross-cutting traps

1. **The absentee, mortgage-free owner is the prime target** — no lender and no occupant to notice a forged dealing. Paying cash and living abroad is the exact fraud profile, and the free owner-alert is opt-in and almost never set up by default.
2. **System type, not vigilance, sets the ceiling.** Torrens-indefeasibility + assurance fund = made whole even if defrauded; deeds-recording = you bear the loss unless you bought title insurance; Grundbuch = it almost cannot happen and there is no alert *because none is needed*. Establish the regime before relying on "monitoring".
3. **An alert NOTIFIES; a restriction BLOCKS.** UK Property Alert vs Form LL/RQ restriction; PE Alerta Registral vs Inmovilización de Partida; TR Web-Tapu *Beyan* lock. Notification without a lodged restriction still lets a fraudulent dealing register — you learn of it *after* it completed.
4. **Non-resident enrolment is the catch.** Many free services need a national eID / a reachable national-contact-register address / an in-country attorney (EE/LT eID; NO depends on the contact register; some require domestic ID). The instrument existing ≠ a foreign owner being able to use it — verify the non-resident path explicitly.
5. **Title insurance and a state guarantee are not interchangeable.** In deeds-recording systems an *owner's* title-insurance policy (not just the lender's) bought at purchase is the only real backstop; in Torrens systems the assurance fund is statutory but claims can be hard to prove (e.g. JM ROTA). Neither is automatic.
6. **"There is a digital registry" ≠ "there is a fraud alert."** Highly digitised registries (GCC DLD/Najiz, many CEE e-registers) frequently have no consumer push-alert; the protection is the per-transaction official-channel identity check. An app does not imply monitoring.
7. **Vendor "home title lock" subscriptions overstate protection** and are mostly a US deeds-recording phenomenon; the real instruments are the (free) statutory registry alert + a lodged restriction + owner's title insurance + the assurance fund. Don't let a paid product substitute for the statutory tool (cross-reference the `scams-postcompletion` trap).

## Universal mitigations

Applicable across all 126 countries:

1. **Classify the registry system first** (Torrens+fund / deeds-recording / Grundbuch-judicial / transitional) — it determines whether you need an alert, a restriction, title insurance, or all three.
2. **Enrol in the free owner-alert the day the deed registers**, having verified the non-resident enrolment path (eID / contact-register / attorney).
3. **Lodge the anti-fraud RESTRICTION, not just the alert** (UK Form LL/RQ, PE Inmovilización, JP 不正登記防止申出, caveat against dealings) — a block beats a notification.
4. **In any deeds-recording system, buy an owner's title-insurance policy at purchase** — it is the only backstop and cannot be added cheaply later.
5. **Keep the national-contact-register address and registered email current** so a system that pushes to the *last registered owner* can actually reach you abroad.
6. **Schedule a recurring independent title search** via in-country counsel where no push-alert exists (Grundbuch reliance is fine; deeds-recording reliance is not).
7. **Do not substitute a paid "title-lock" product for the statutory alert + restriction + title insurance + assurance fund**; treat any such marketing as non-substitutive.

## Regional patterns

### Western Europe + EFTA + micro-states + UK (R1)

- **ad** Andorra — Title-fraud defensive instrument: system=notarial deeds (NO operational public land register as of 2026 — confirmed by Andorran primary press: a *Registre de la Propietat* is being legislated and is expected to become operational only ~2027, with a ~10-year transition; today ownership is proven via *escriptura pública* held in the notary's permanent protocol plus the *Cadastre* held by each of the 7 *comuns*/parishes, which only partially functions as a register). owner-alert=none — structural via mandatory Andorran *notari* gatekeeping: before any transaction a notary verifies through the *Cambra de Notaris* that it can proceed (an 800-year notarial title-security model); no online register exists to enroll in; verify at the relevant *comú* cadastre / Cambra de Notaris d'Andorra (cambranotaris.ad). anti-fraud restriction=no formal title-register restriction exists (no register yet); practical equivalent is a standing instruction lodged with the notary holding the protocol. loss backstop=no state assurance fund and no developed title-insurance market — protection rests entirely on notarial diligence and the foreign-investment authorisation file. Most actionable for an absentee foreign owner: keep a certified copy of the *escriptura*, appoint a resident *apoderat* under a narrow power of attorney, and lodge a standing direct-contact instruction with the protocol notary; track the forthcoming Registre de la Propietat and register the property as soon as the law takes effect (data not publicly available for any alert service — none can exist until the register launches; verify at Cambra de Notaris d'Andorra, cambranotaris.ad).

- **at** Austria — Title-fraud defensive instrument: system=Grundbuch-judicial (Grundbuch maintained by the district courts / *Bezirksgericht*; intabulation principle — ownership passes only on court entry; constitutive registration with *Eintragungsgrundsatz* + public faith *Vertrauensgrundsatz* §§1500–1501 ABGB). owner-alert=none standing consumer self-alert — structural protection is strong: transfers require a court-filed deed prepared/authenticated by a notary or lawyer (*Beglaubigung*) and judicial review before *Einverleibung*; verify at Grundbuch via justizonline.gv.at / grundbuch.gv.at. anti-fraud restriction=a *Streitanmerkung* / *Anmerkung der Rangordnung* and notably a registered prohibition of sale/encumbrance (*Belastungs- und Veräußerungsverbot*, §364c ABGB) can be entered to block dealings; a *Plombe* flags a pending application. loss backstop=no title-insurance norm; the court-controlled register with public faith plus state liability for grossly faulty registration (*Amtshaftung*) is the backstop. Most actionable for an absentee foreign owner: register a §364c *Belastungs- und Veräußerungsverbot* in favour of a trusted person/relative — it judicially blocks any sale or mortgage entry.

- **be** Belgium — Title-fraud defensive instrument: system=deeds-recording / notarial civil-law (mortgage registry *Bureau Sécurité juridique* of the FPS Finance / *Algemene Administratie van de Patrimoniumdocumentatie*; transcription of notarial deeds; opposability via *transcription hypothécaire*). owner-alert=none standing consumer self-alert — structural protection via the mandatory *notaire/notaris* who alone may draw and present a transfer deed and who runs identity/anti-money-laundering checks through the *Fednot* network and *e-Registration*; verify at the *Bureau Sécurité juridique* (finances.belgium.be) / notaris.be. anti-fraud restriction=no consumer self-service title freeze; a *transcription* of a judicial claim or seizure (*saisie-exécution / commandement*) can block dealings, but it is litigation-driven, not a preventive owner flag. loss backstop=no state assurance fund and no title-insurance norm; protection rests on notarial liability + the *Fonds de garantie du notariat*. Most actionable for an absentee foreign owner: instruct the notary who holds the *minute* (and any future acquiring notary via the *Fednot* network) that no act proceeds without verified direct contact; consider a usufruct/bare-ownership split with a trusted resident.

- **ch** Switzerland — Title-fraud defensive instrument: system=Grundbuch-judicial/cantonal (cantonal *Grundbuch / registre foncier* offices; constitutive registration with public faith — *öffentlicher Glaube*, art. 973 ZGB; entry of the buyer makes them owner). owner-alert=none standing consumer self-alert — structural protection is strong: every transfer requires a notarised *öffentliche Beurkundung* by a cantonal notary plus registrar review before entry; verify at the cantonal *Grundbuchamt* (swisstopo.admin.ch/en/land-register; cantonal e-portals e.g. terravis). anti-fraud restriction=a *Vormerkung / Anmerkung* (e.g. of a disputed claim or *Verfügungsbeschränkung*) or a judicial blocking can be entered; no consumer-facing self-service freeze. loss backstop=no title-insurance norm; cantonal *Staatshaftung* / register-keeper liability (art. 955 ZGB) plus public-faith protection is the backstop. Most actionable for an absentee foreign owner: rely on notarial gatekeeping; where the canton offers a paid monitoring extract subscription (e.g. via *Terravis* or cantonal e-Grundbuch), subscribe — and lodge a power-of-attorney restriction with the notary (data not publicly available for a free national alert — verify at the cantonal Grundbuchamt).

- **cy** Cyprus — Title-fraud defensive instrument: system=Torrens-style (Department of Lands and Surveys / *Τμήμα Κτηματολογίου και Χωρομετρίας*; title-by-registration with a state-guaranteed *Title Deed* / *πιστοποιητικό εγγραφής*; the register is conclusive). owner-alert=none standing free consumer alert — structural via the DLS title-by-registration model and the registrar's transfer process (in-person/authorised filing at the District Lands Office; the *DLS Portal* lets owners view their own title online); verify at the Department of Lands and Surveys (portal.dls.moi.gov.cy). anti-fraud restriction=a *MEMO* (encumbrance/charge) or a court-ordered prohibition (*απαγόρευση*) can be registered against the title to block transfer; a power-of-attorney can also be flagged. loss backstop=Torrens-style state-guaranteed title; no title-insurance norm (the conclusive register is the guarantee), with statutory rectification/indemnity routes. Most actionable for an absentee foreign owner: confirm a separate *Title Deed* has been issued in your name (Cyprus's historic title-deed backlog is the real risk, not register fraud) and register the DLS Portal account to monitor your own folio; do not grant a broad irrevocable power of attorney.

- **de** Germany — Title-fraud defensive instrument: system=Grundbuch-judicial (Grundbuch kept at the local *Amtsgericht / Grundbuchamt*; constitutive registration — title passes only on entry — with public faith *öffentlicher Glaube*, §891/§892 BGB). owner-alert=none consumer self-alert and none is needed structurally: a transfer requires a notarised *Auflassung* (§925 BGB) before a German notary plus court review; the notary files an *Auflassungsvormerkung* (priority notice) that already blocks a parallel sale/mortgage; verify at the Grundbuchamt (justiz.de / state *Grundbuch-Einsicht* portals). anti-fraud restriction=a *Widerspruch* (§899 BGB) or *Verfügungsbeschränkung / Vormerkung* can be entered against the register to block reliance on an incorrect entry; no consumer-facing self-service freeze (the system makes forged transfer structurally near-impossible). loss backstop=no title-insurance norm; court-controlled register + public faith + state liability for faulty registration (*Amtshaftung*, §839 BGB / Art. 34 GG) is the backstop. Most actionable for an absentee foreign owner: this is structurally one of the safest systems — keep the notary's contact current and, if leaving the property vacant long-term, ask the notary about a registered *Widerspruch* or a §364c-style restriction analogue (sale/encumbrance prohibition under §1136 / family-law tools).

- **dk** Denmark — Title-fraud defensive instrument: system=deeds-recording, fully digital (Tinglysningsretten — the Land Registration Court — single national digital register at tinglysning.dk; rights are not protected until registered; registration requires the national eID *MitID* / a QES). owner-alert=structural digital safeguard: registration of any deed requires MitID-level authentication and the register is run by a court; *tinglysning.dk* lets an owner view their own *adkomst*; verify at Tinglysningsretten / domstol.dk (tinglysning.dk). No discrete free email-alert product surfaced — protection is the MitID gateway + court adjudication. anti-fraud restriction=a *servitut* or a registered injunction/seizure can be entered; no consumer self-service freeze product surfaced. loss backstop=the Danish State is liable for faulty registrations and errors in the Land Register (statutory state guarantee) — no title-insurance norm. Most actionable for an absentee foreign owner: protect the MitID credential (it is the single key that authorises any registration against your title) and periodically check your *adkomst* on tinglysning.dk; the state-liability backstop covers registration errors (data not publicly available for a discrete free alert service — verify at Tinglysningsretten).

- **es** Spain — Title-fraud defensive instrument: system=deeds-recording with strong registry effect (*Registro de la Propiedad*; notarial *escritura pública* + registration; the register confers *fe pública registral*, art. 34 LH). owner-alert=YES, free **Servicio de Alertas al Titular Registral** run by CORPME (Colegio de Registradores) — the registered owner receives immediate email notice of any document presented affecting their property; enrol at the Registradores online portal (sede.registradores.org / registradores.org/registroonline). Non-resident-enrollable: yes — enrolment is for the registered titleholder and is done online (digital certificate / Cl@ve typical), available to overseas owners. anti-fraud restriction=an *anotación preventiva* (e.g. of a claim/demanda or seizure) can be entered to block clean transfer; the alert service itself is the primary preventive tool. loss backstop=no general state assurance fund and no title-insurance norm; *fe pública registral* protects a good-faith registered purchaser, and registrar professional liability applies. Most actionable for an absentee foreign owner: enrol every Spanish property in the free **Alertas al Titular Registral** service — it is the single best preventive instrument and is explicitly designed for this risk.

- **fi** Finland — Title-fraud defensive instrument: system=Torrens-style (National Land Survey of Finland / *Maanmittauslaitos*; title registration *lainhuuto* in the Title and Mortgage Register; registered title has strong public reliance and a public-faith effect). owner-alert=structural — title transfer is processed by the NLS with document/identity review; an owner can view their own title via the NLS *Asiointipalvelu* / *Kiinteistövaihdannan palvelu* (electronic conveyance requires strong Finnish e-ID); verify at Maanmittauslaitos (maanmittauslaitos.fi). No discrete free email-alert product surfaced. anti-fraud restriction=a *vallintarajoitus* (disposition restriction) or a registered claim/seizure can be noted in the register; no consumer self-service freeze product surfaced. loss backstop=statutory state compensation/guarantee for good-faith parties relying on the public Title and Mortgage Register (state liability for register errors) — no title-insurance norm. Most actionable for an absentee foreign owner: rely on the strong public-faith register + state compensation; for electronic conveyance protect the Finnish e-ID and check your *lainhuuto* via the NLS e-service (data not publicly available for a discrete free alert service — verify at Maanmittauslaitos).

- **fr** France — Title-fraud defensive instrument: system=deeds-recording / notarial civil-law (*Service de la publicité foncière*, DGFiP — publication of notarial deeds; opposability via *publicité foncière*, not constitutive title). owner-alert=none consumer self-alert — structural protection is strong via the mandatory *notaire*, who alone may draw and publish a transfer deed and who clears identity/AML and runs the act through the secured **Télé@ctes** chain into the *fichier immobilier*; verify at the *Service de la publicité foncière* (impots.gouv.fr) / notaires.fr. anti-fraud restriction=no consumer self-service title freeze; a judicial *prénotation* / publication of a claim or a *commandement de saisie* can block dealings, but it is litigation-driven. loss backstop=no state assurance fund and no title-insurance norm; protection rests on notarial liability + the profession's collective guarantee fund (*Caisse de garantie / CRCC*). Most actionable for an absentee foreign owner: lodge a standing written instruction with the notary holding the *minute* (and require any acquiring notary, reachable via the *notaires.fr* network) that no act on the property proceeds without verified direct owner contact; do not grant a broad *procuration*.

- **gr** Greece — Title-fraud defensive instrument: system=mixed, transitioning to Torrens-style (legacy notarial deeds-recording at the *Υποθηκοφυλακείο* mortgage registries being replaced by the parcel-based **Εθνικό Κτηματολόγιο / Hellenic Cadastre**, instituted 1995 as a unified system recording legal/technical data on real estate "under the responsibility and guarantee of the State"; through *προανάρτηση/ανάρτηση/κτηματογράφηση* the *Κτηματολογικό Γραφείο* register becomes the authoritative title record for an area). owner-alert=none standing free consumer alert — structural via the mandatory Greek *συμβολαιογράφος* (notary, a neutral state official) for any transfer plus registrar review; an owner can search their property folio on the Hellenic Cadastre portal; verify at the Hellenic Cadastre (ktimatologio.gr / gov.gr). anti-fraud restriction=a *προσημείωση* (prenotation) or a registered claim/seizure (*κατάσχεση*) can be entered against the property; no consumer self-service freeze. loss backstop=no title-insurance norm; the State expressly guarantees Cadastre register data once an area is finalised (statutory cadastral guarantee/indemnity; ownership is a constitutionally protected right, Art. 17), with registrar liability in the interim. Most actionable for an absentee foreign owner: confirm your property is correctly recorded in the Hellenic Cadastre during the *ανάρτηση* window (mis-/non-registration during the cadastral transition is the dominant real risk, not register forgery) and verify the folio periodically via ktimatologio.gr; avoid broad powers of attorney.

- **ie** Ireland — Title-fraud defensive instrument: system=Torrens-style for registered land (Tailte Éireann — *Land Registry* folios = title-by-registration with a state-guaranteed folio; plus a residual *Registry of Deeds* for unregistered land). owner-alert=YES, free **Tailte Éireann Property Alert Service** — email/SMS notice of certain activity on a monitored property; enrol via landdirect.ie; verify at tailte.ie/register-for-property-alerts.
