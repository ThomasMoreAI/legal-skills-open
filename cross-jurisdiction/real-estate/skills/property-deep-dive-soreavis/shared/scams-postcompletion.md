# Universal `--scams` Section — Post-Completion / Foreign-Owner-Targeted Fraud Extension

Post-deed and absentee-foreign-owner-targeted fraud register for the property's country. Loaded when `--scams` is invoked alongside the base PRE-deed fraud layer in `shared/scams.md` and each country playbook.

**Snapshot**: May 2026.

**Scope**: the base `--scams` section covers PRE-deed transaction fraud (closing BEC, title forgery at purchase, off-plan disappearance, foreign-restricted-zone nominee, golden-visa price-inflation, customary-tenure / restitution-lien, concession-vs-freehold). This doc supplies the orthogonal **POST-deed / post-handover** layer: fraud that lands on the foreign owner *after* the deed is signed, when they are typically absentee and least able to react in-country. The base PRE-deed traps are **not** repeated here.

## Universal contract

For the property's country, return:

1. **Post-deed vector(s)** present, drawn from the 7-vector taxonomy below
2. **Regulator advisory / prosecution** where it exists (named authority + reference + date + URL), else `data not publicly available — verify at <authority>`
3. **Strongest documented absentee-foreign-owner post-deed vector** for that country (one)
4. **Structural countermeasure**: the registry-alert / escrow / no-arrears-certificate mechanism available to the absentee owner (named, with enrolment path)
5. **Verdict band**: 🟢 / 🟡 / 🟠 / 🔴 per the table below
6. **Confidence**: HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW per the skill's contract

## The 7 post-deed fraud vectors (what this section screens for)

- **(a)** contractor-deposit-flight *after* completion (renovation deposit taken, work abandoned)
- **(b)** fake utility-disconnect threat (pay-now-or-cut-off, exploiting an owner who cannot verify locally)
- **(c)** fake property-tax / authority-impersonation mailer exploiting the absentee foreign owner
- **(d)** agent-commission dispute — third parties contacting the owner directly to cut the registered agent
- **(e)** strata / HOA / condominio / VvE / copropriété reserve-fund misappropriation
- **(f)** rental-yield fabrication by the management company (withheld income, fabricated occupancy)
- **(g)** owner / power-of-attorney impersonation — fraudulent re-conveyance or re-mortgage of an absentee owner's title (**the highest-loss vector**)

## Verdict bands

| Band | Trigger | Read |
|---|---|---|
| 🟢 | Structural countermeasure in force (registry-alert + escrow gate + state title indemnity); no regulator-confirmed active foreign-owner fraud wave | "Standard absentee-owner hygiene sufficient; enrol in the free registry alert anyway." |
| 🟡 | Recurring authority-impersonation phishing advisories; reserve-fund opacity possible; title system sound | "Enrol in registry alert; pay service charges only into the regulated escrow; verify any IBAN change out-of-band." |
| 🟠 | Confirmed active fraud wave, OR no registry-alert mechanism, OR deeds-recording (non-Torrens) system raising forged-conveyance exposure | "Title insurance + an in-country monitored representative are effectively mandatory before leaving the country." |
| 🔴 | Documented organised title-hijack / forged-deed rings explicitly targeting absentee foreign owners AND weak/absent state guarantee | "Treat absentee freehold as high-risk: holding structure + active monitoring, or reconsider the purchase." |

The verdict turns on **structural exposure**, not phishing volume. Authority-impersonation phishing is near-universal and alone is 🟡; what moves a country to 🟠/🔴 is the (g) title-hijack vector landing on a weak state guarantee or a deeds-recording (non-Torrens) chain-of-title system with no free owner-alert.

## ⚠️ 7 cross-cutting traps

1. **Owning outright is the risk multiplier, not the safety.** UK HM Land Registry data is explicit: absentee/overseas-resident, mortgage-free, empty or tenanted titles are the *highest-risk profile* for owner-impersonation re-conveyance/re-mortgage — precisely because no lender is monitoring the title and no one local notices a fraudulent dealing. The foreign buyer who "paid cash so there's nothing to worry about" has the exact target profile.
2. **The fraud signal is the channel, not the content.** Genuine tax authorities have fixed, knowable channels — Italy's Agenzia delle Entrate communicates only by ordinary mail and never sends unsolicited SMS/email; France's DGFiP never emails a payment link; Germany's Grundsteuer-reform spawned official-impersonation building-access fraud. A foreign owner who doesn't know the genuine channel cannot spot the fake one — establish it *before* leaving.
3. **Service-charge / reserve-fund opacity is the recurring absentee loss, and the countermeasure is jurisdiction-specific.** Dubai RERA Mollak mandates service charges into a RERA-approved escrow (not the manager's account); Bahrain's SLRB will not register a transfer without an owners'-association no-arrears certificate; EU co-ownership statutes mandate reserve-fund audits. Pay only into the building's regulated account — never a manager's personal or company IBAN.
4. **Statutory debt-transfer is not fraud but is exploited like it.** Unpaid IBI / community-fee / property-tax silently rides with the title (Spain is the textbook case) and "ambushes" the new foreign owner; bad actors then leverage the surprise. A pre-completion debt-clearance / no-arrears certificate is the only defence and is often skipped on a foreign purchase.
5. **Not enrolling in the free registry alert is the single most common avoidable failure.** Where it exists it closes most of the (g) gap and costs nothing — UK Property Alert, Morocco ANCFCC "Mouahafadati", Peru SUNARP Alerta Registral, Costa Rica Registro Nacional document-alert, several Indian states' mutation-SMS — but it is opt-in and is essentially never set up for a foreign owner by default.
6. **"Title-lock" subscription marketing overstates protection.** Vendor "home-title-protection" products marketed at absentee owners do not prevent a fraudulent dealing; the real mitigants are the (free) statutory registry alert + title insurance + the state land-title assurance fund. Don't let a paid subscription substitute for the statutory tool.
7. **An unsolicited direct approach to "save the agent commission" is a regulated-profession red flag, not a deal.** In jurisdictions like Cyprus (Estate Agents Law L.71(I)/2010) only a registered agent may lawfully claim commission and unregistered demands against a foreign owner are themselves prosecutable. Route any such approach to the agents' regulator rather than negotiating.

## Universal mitigations

Applicable across all 126 countries, in priority order:

1. **Enrol in the jurisdiction's free registry-alert service the day the deed registers.** Confirmed free services: UK HM Land Registry **Property Alert**; Morocco ANCFCC **"Mouahafadati"** (SMS/email on any title operation; 2017/2019 cooperation specifically protects Moroccans-Residing-Abroad); Peru SUNARP **Alerta Registral** (Resolución 00080-2025-SUNARP/SN, 30 May 2025); Costa Rica **Registro Nacional** document-alert; several Indian states' property-mutation SMS. Where none exists, instruct the land registry / notary to flag the file and schedule a recurring manual title search.
2. **Pay recurring charges only into the building's regulated account.** Verify the service-charge IBAN is the RERA-approved Mollak escrow (Dubai) / the owners'-association statutory account, never a management company's or individual's personal IBAN.
3. **Obtain a pre-completion debt / arrears clearance certificate** (community fees, property tax, utilities) so statutory debt-transfer cannot ambush the new foreign owner; in Bahrain the SLRB no-arrears certificate is a statutory transfer gate — use the equivalent everywhere.
4. **Establish the genuine authority's communication channel before leaving the country**, so a fake tax/utility mailer is identifiable by channel alone.
5. **Buy title insurance in deeds-recording (non-Torrens) jurisdictions** (Barbados, Bahamas, Belize, parts of US, DR legacy parcels) where there is no state title guarantee.
6. **Appoint a vetted in-country representative with a narrowly-scoped, monitored power of attorney** — never an open-ended POA — and re-verify any out-of-band IBAN/instruction change with that representative directly.
7. **Do not substitute a paid "title-lock" product for the free statutory alert + title insurance + assurance fund**; treat any unsolicited commission-cutting approach as a regulator matter.

## Corrections folded (research-surfaced)

These widely-cited claims were corrected and the fix folded into the relevant bullet above:

- **es** — the Endesa fake-utility-bill phishing wave is a current 2026 expat-/absentee-targeted variant (Euro Weekly News, 16 Feb 2026), not a generic pre-2024 email scam; the IBI/community-fee "debt-transfer ambush" is a statutory liability, not a scam per se.
- **uk** — title fraud is rare in count (86 of 4,430,092 FY2024-25 applications, ~0.0019%) but high in value (>£59M prevented FY2024-25; >£194M / 300+ properties 2020-2025), concentrated on absentee/overseas-owned mortgage-free titles (HM Land Registry, 17 Sep 2025).
- **cy** — under L.71(I)/2010 only a registered estate agent may lawfully claim commission (3% default unless agreed in writing); unregistered-broker commission demands against foreign owners are themselves prosecutable.
- **th** — "8-12% guaranteed yield" off-plan framing is a sales device; documented net yields run ~5-7% — the guarantee gap is itself the post-handover fraud signal.
- **mx** — "fideicomiso = the bank can take your property" is wrong; the bank is bare trustee. The real post-deed risk is fee/administration disputes and forged-poder transfers.
- **do vs dm** — several scam compilations attribute Dominica (DM, out of scope) CBI-linked scams to the Dominican Republic (DO); DR's post-deed risk is overlapping-title/forged-deed under its dual Torrens/legacy system.
- **id** — the ~10,500-plot / USD 10.4bn / 7,500-villa nominee figures are an Indonesian Nominee Crisis Working Group estimate relayed via aggregators, not a single primary government release; verify at BKPM / Ministry of ATR/BPN.
- **ca / us** — vendor "title-lock" subscription marketing overstates protection; the primary mitigants are title insurance + free county/registry fraud-alert enrolment.
