# Universal `--relocation` Section

Foreign-buyer relocation logistics for the **90-day post-completion phase**: pets, driving licence, vehicle import, utility setup. Captures the "surprise cost / surprise timeline" patterns that turn a clean purchase into a scrambled move-in. Cross-cuts five subdomains across all 126 countries.

**Snapshot**: 2026-05-09. The relocation landscape moved decisively 2024-2026 — **EU Reg 2016/429** transition ends **22 Apr 2026** (replaces Reg 576/2013 framework — UK pet passports invalidated for EU travel; non-EU residents must use AHC per trip); **EU 4th Driving Licence Directive (Dir 2025/2205)** in force **25 Nov 2025** with transposition deadline **26 Nov 2028**; **CDC Dog Import Form** mandatory for all US dog imports from **1 Aug 2024**; **Warsaw Strefa Czystego Transportu (SCT)** live **1 Jul 2024**, Stage 2 from **1 Jan 2026**; **Kraków LEZ** live **1 Jan 2026**; **Stockholm Miljözon Klass 3** EV-only inner-city live **31 Dec 2024**; **HK Mainland-CN pet quarantine** cut 120→30 days from **3 Jun 2025**; **SG AVS Schedule I/II/III** replaced Cat A/B/C/D **1 Apr 2026**; **AU Experienced Driver Recognition (EDR)** category removed Apr 2025-Feb 2026 (binary recognised-or-not); **AR ARCA** replaces AFIP from **25 Oct 2024** (Decree 953/2024 BO); **NL zero-emission zones** live **1 Jan 2025** (Amsterdam S100, Rotterdam, Utrecht, Eindhoven, Maastricht); **DE Umweltzone green-only universal 2025**; **DE 24h electricity-switching rule** from **6 Jun 2025**; **MX 2025 LESE/LCFE/LPTE/LCNE energy reform** (CFE re-strengthened); **HU NREA** in force **1 Oct 2024**; e-property live **16 Jan 2025**.

**Anti-hallucination**: every cluster ends with `Confidence: HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW` and a `Last verified` date. Where reform timing is fluid (FR ZFE post-28-May-2025 abolition vote; UK-EU pet-travel mutual-recognition negotiation status), the doc flags MEDIUM and points to the relevant parliamentary tracker. Where 2026-04-22 EU 2016/429 transition introduces discontinuity, sections are flagged for re-stamp post-22 Apr 2026. Forbidden phrasings per `shared/anti-hallucination.md` § Forbidden phrasings are avoided throughout.

**Disambiguation — `--relocation` vs `--mains` vs `--connectivity` vs `--remote`**:
- `--relocation` = **buyer's account-holder logistics**. Pet entry + DL exchange + vehicle import + utility account opening. The "surprise costs / surprise timeline" of the 90-day post-completion phase.
- `--mains` = **parcel infrastructure**. Sewer / water / electricity / gas presence at the property; verification path. Pre-purchase concern.
- `--connectivity` = **broadband layer**. Address-level fibre availability, ISPs, tariff bands, Starlink registry, strata trap. Distinct from utility-account-opening.
- `--remote` = **deed-execution mechanisms**. POA / RON / e-conveyancing / eIDAS QES / mortgage residual. Closing process, not move-in process.

When all four run, `--mains` is the parcel layer, `--connectivity` is the broadband layer, `--relocation` is the **account-holder** layer (which IDs / tax codes / residency proofs unlock service signup), and `--remote` is the **closing** layer.

## Settling-in sequence — what unlocks what (the ordered-dependency spine)

The single most common post-arrival mistake is attempting these steps in the wrong order. They form a **dependency chain**, not a checklist: most of the friction is a step blocked because an earlier step isn't done. This is an **ordering** (what unlocks / blocks what), **never a day-numbered calendar** — lead times below are **ranges as the source states them**, and where only a statutory maximum exists it is labelled as such (actual medians are not published).

**The chain (each step typically gated by the one before):**

1. **Residency / address registration** — the keystone. DE *Anmeldung*, NL BRP→BSN, AT *Meldezettel* (within days), CH *Einwohnerkontrolle* (~8 days), ES *empadronamiento*, IT *iscrizione anagrafica*. Unlocks almost everything downstream. → the residence permit that often precedes it is `--visa`.
2. **Tax identification number** — usually gated by step 1. DE *Steuer-ID* (mailed **2–6 weeks** after *Anmeldung* [BZSt]), ES NIE, IT *codice fiscale*, PT NIF, FR *numéro fiscal*, BR CPF, MX RFC. Both the bank and any employer need it.
3. **Local bank account** — usually needs steps 1–2 (proof of address + tax ID); the hard blocker in DE / NL / CH and the most common Catch-22 (below). → `--finance` § Banking access for the per-country onboarding reality + the HSBC-Expat-Jersey pre-arrival fallback.
4. **Public-healthcare enrolment** — needs residence + often the bank + tax ID. DE *Krankenversicherung* mandatory day 1 by law; FR *Carte Vitale* via CPAM after 3-month residence; NL *zorgverzekering* 4-month grace; CH KVG within 3 months. → trap #7 (the 90-day insurance-gap cliff).
5. **Utility connection** — needs proof of address + a local IBAN + tax ID; the last domino. → trap #6 (the 30-day delay trap).

**The Catch-22 edges (a step that blocks itself):** the classic is **bank ⇄ address ⇄ utility** — the utility wants a local IBAN, the bank wants proof of address, registration wants a utility bill or local account. Named workarounds: open a destination account **remotely before arrival** (cross-border IBAN providers — trap #6); obtain the **tax ID via the consulate** pre-arrival where allowed (ES NIE, IT *codice fiscale*); use a registration confirmation / landlord *attestation d'hébergement* as the address proof that breaks the loop (for a new owner, the notary's *attestation d'achat* serves as the FR *justificatif de domicile*). DE's **24-hour electricity-switching rule (6 Jun 2025)** removes one delay vector once the address is registered.

**Honest tail (the rule)**: bucket only by dependency tier ("cannot start until X is done"); bound durations as the **sourced range** (e.g. "DE *Steuer-ID*: 2–6 weeks post-*Anmeldung* [BZSt]"; "EU single-permit decision: statutory ≤ 2 months [Single Permit Directive 2024/1233] — actual varies"). **Do not emit a day-numbered milestone** ("Day 14 → bank") for any country lacking a published per-step actual — which is all of them. Full per-step detail + named per-country authorities live in the clusters below + `--finance` § Banking access.

## Universal contract

For the property's country, return:

1. **Pets** — entry rules, microchip ISO 11784/11785, rabies vaccinations, FAVN/RFFIT titer requirement (where applicable), waiting period, quarantine, banned breeds
2. **Driving licence** — recognition / exchange / re-test + grace period + IDP (1949 Geneva or 1968 Vienna) + senior re-test thresholds + bilateral exchange list
3. **Vehicle import** — temporary admission (ATA Carnet) vs permanent import + EU/EEA single-market vs third-country tariffs + emissions standards (Euro 5/6 / EPA Tier 3 / ADR / Bharat Stage / China 6) + age limits + RHD/LHD restriction + purchase tax / VAT / registration tax + LEZ
4. **Household-goods import** — transfer-of-residence (ToR) customs relief on used personal effects: prior-residence-abroad + owned-&-used windows + import deadline + non-disposal lock + named form (UK ToR1 / US CBP 3299 / EU Reg 1186/2009 / AU B534 / etc.); vehicles + alcohol/tobacco/new goods excluded. See `shared/relocation-household-customs.md`
5. **Utility setup** — electricity / gas / water / waste connection process + ID requirements (NIE / codice fiscale / NIF / etc.) + deposits + foreign-buyer onboarding friction + local IBAN requirement
6. **Healthcare registration** — public-system enrolment timeline + private travel-insurance gap exposure (the parallel 90-day cliff)
7. **2024-2026 reforms** — date-stamped reform calendar
8. **Confidence**: HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW

## Cross-cutting traps to surface every time

These appear across multiple jurisdictions and warrant a flag whenever a foreign buyer is shopping abroad. Detailed mechanism + countries-where-it-bites + mitigation appear in the **Cross-cutting structural detail** section near the end.

1. **Pet rabies titer waiting period — the 6-7 month flow** — AU / NZ / JP / SG (Schedule III) / MY (FAVN-required origins) require **FAVN/RFFIT ≥ 0.5 IU/ml** + **180-day waiting period** (AU clock = lab-receipt date per ABA 2020-A01; JP clock = blood-draw date). Realistic minimum: 7-9 months from first vet visit to pet on destination soil. Budget €2,500-6,000 per pet for AU/NZ relocation. **NOT a trap in**: EU intra-bloc (Reg 576/2013 — pet passport, 21-day post-vaccination wait sufficient); US import (CDC 1 Aug 2024 reform — low/no-risk countries Dog Import Form only); most LATAM/Asia (vaccination + recent health certificate sufficient).

2. **EU pet passport vs Animal Health Certificate (AHC) — post-Brexit chain + EU Reg 2016/429 transition 22 Apr 2026** — Post-Brexit (1 Jan 2021) GB became "Part 2 listed" third country: GB-resident pets need AHC for each EU trip (single-trip, OV-issued ≤ 10 days before EU entry, valid 4 months for onward EU travel + return to GB). **22 Apr 2026 EU Reg 2016/429 enforcement tightens** — EU pet passports issued by UK vets to UK residents during transition no longer valid for EU travel; UK residents must use AHC. Cost £100-300 per AHC. **No EU-UK mutual-recognition treaty published as of May 2026** (correction: brief mentioned this as fluid; primary-source review shows enforcement tightening, not mutual recognition). Re-stamp this trap post-22-Apr-2026.

3. **Driving licence exchange — bilateral patchwork, no global standard** — EU/EEA mutual recognition under **Directive 2006/126/EC** (3rd DLD); successor **Directive (EU) 2025/2205** (4DLD, in force 25 Nov 2025, transposition deadline 26 Nov 2028) extends mutual recognition with new digital-licence + disqualification-portability rules. Beyond EU/EEA, recognition is **bilateral patchwork**. Senior re-test thresholds: NL 75+ medical; IT 70-79 5-yearly + 80+ 2-yearly; UK 70+ self-declaration 3-yearly; JP 70+ cognitive screening + 75+ on-road test (since 13 May 2022). US no national mandate. AU **Experienced Driver Recognition (EDR) category removed Apr 2025 - Feb 2026** — now binary: 28 recognised countries (no test) vs full re-test. Re-test costs €100-2,000 + 3-6 months timeline.

4. **Vehicle import — emissions + age + LHD/RHD trifecta** — Three independent compliance gates must clear. **Emissions**: EU Reg 2018/858 + WLTP (1 Sep 2020); US EPA Tier 3; AU ADR; IN Bharat Stage VI (since 1 Apr 2020); CN China 6. **Age limits**: GCC harmonisation (KSA / BH / QA / KW 5-year cap; UAE no cap but >10y permit; OM 10-year; Bahrain raised to 10y); Kenya 8-year + **2026-01-01 KEBS rule** only RHD vehicles first-registered ≥2019 allowed; NG 12-year (reaffirmed Nov 2025); MU petrol <3y / diesel <4y (post-2025 reform); EG <3y + Euro 4 + **one-vehicle-per-5-years rule** from 26 Dec 2024. **RHD/LHD**: AE prohibits RHD individual import; UK/IE/MT/CY/AU/NZ/JP/SG/IN/PK/TH/MY/ID/ZA RHD; mainland EU + US + CN + KSA + AE LHD-only. **Collector exemptions**: US 25-year NHTSA rule; AU SEVS / Concessional RAV ≥25 years; EU classic >30 years per MS.

5. **LEZ (Low Emission Zone) restrictions — urban driving cost** — three architectures: charge-based (London ULEZ £12.50/day), prohibitory (Madrid 360, Berlin/Munich Umweltzone green-only since 2025), zero-emission (Amsterdam S100 from 1 Jan 2025; Rotterdam, Utrecht, Eindhoven, Maastricht same date — currently freight/commercial; Amsterdam Ring-A10 expansion 2028 planned). Live LEZ landscape May 2026: UK ULEZ Greater London (29 Aug 2023); FR ZFE-m Crit'Air 3 ban 1 Jan 2025 (pedagogical period extended to end 2026); DE 50+ Umweltzonen green-only 2025 universal; ES Madrid 360 citywide ZBE from 1 Jan 2025; PL Warsaw SCT Stage 2 from 1 Jan 2026 + Kraków from 1 Jan 2026; SE Stockholm Miljözon Klass 3 EV-only inner-city 31 Dec 2024; DK Copenhagen Miljøzone expanded 1 Mar 2025; BE Brussels stricter from Jan 2026 (court overturned 2027 postponement). 2024-2026 trend: tightening not relaxing; expect annual ratchets.

6. **Utility setup — foreign-buyer onboarding friction (the 30-day delay trap)** — Catch-22 chain: utility wants local IBAN + tax ID + ID + tenancy proof. Foreign buyer arrives without local bank account → can't pay deposit → can't connect electricity / water / gas → can't move in. Hardest in DE (SCHUFA + Anmeldung + Steueridentifikationsnummer mailed 2-6 wks post-Anmeldung), NL (BSN + RNI/BRP), CH (cantonal Einwohnerkontrolle within 8 days), HU (MVM Next sole universal supplier; foreign-owner cap eligibility restricted), CR (Firma Digital BCCR only for citizens / DIMEX-holding residents — foreign buyers cannot obtain). **Mitigation**: open destination bank account remotely BEFORE moving (Wise / Revolut / N26 / bunq cross-border IBAN); get destination tax ID via consulate where possible (NIE / codice fiscale / NIF). DE 24h electricity-switching rule from 6 Jun 2025 mitigates one delay vector.

7. **Healthcare registration — the parallel 90-day cliff** — Most countries require residency permit + tax ID + bank account + utility bill for public healthcare registration; private travel insurance lapses 90 days post-arrival; gap exposure. DE Krankenversicherung mandatory by law from day 1 (failure = €1,000+ backdated premiums + fines); FR Carte Vitale via CPAM after 3-mo continuous residence; ES Cartilla Sanitaria; IT Tessera Sanitaria via ASL; NL zorgverzekering compulsory + 4-month grace with backdating; CH KVG mandatory within 3 months. **Mitigation**: maintain gap travel-insurance overlap (Cigna Global / IMG / Allianz Care, €500-2,000 per person) until public-healthcare-registration confirmed in writing — not "applied for".

8. **Household-goods customs — the transfer-of-residence (ToR) relief you must claim BEFORE shipping** — almost every destination waives import duty + VAT/GST on *used* personal effects under a ToR / returning-resident relief, but only if you file the named form at the right moment and clear the windows (prior residence abroad + owned-&-used period + import deadline + non-disposal lock). **UK ToR1** needs an approved unique reference number quoted at the border (no URN → billed import duty + 20% VAT on your own furniture); **EU** Reg 1186/2009 = ≥12 mo abroad / ≥6 mo owned & used / import within 12 mo / 12 mo non-disposal (duty EU-wide, import VAT per-MS); **US CBP 3299** (no VAT, effects used ≥1 yr); **SG / JP** import within 6 months of arrival; **AU B534** / **NZ NZCS 218** (>21 mo abroad) / **CA BSF186** / **CH Form 18.44**. Vehicles + alcohol + tobacco + new/commercial goods are always excluded. Full per-regime table: `shared/relocation-household-customs.md`.

## Western Europe (FR, IT, ES, PT, DE, AT, NL, BE, LU, GR, CY, MT, CH, IE, UK)

Pet movement under **Reg 576/2013** (intra-EU) + **Reg 2016/429** transition ending **22 Apr 2026**. Driving-licence mutual recognition under Dir 2006/126/EC + 4DLD 2025/2205 from 26 Nov 2028. Vehicle import: EU intra-market free movement (re-register only); transfer-of-residence VAT exemption requires 6+ months ownership + 12 months residency outside EU; vehicle cannot be sold/lent/hired in first 12 months post-import.

- **FR** (France): **Pets** EU pet passport (Reg 576/2013); microchip ISO 11784/11785; rabies. **Cat 1 dogs** (non-LOF Pit Bull / Am Staff / Tosa / Rottweiler-type) — **import / sale / gift / ownership banned**, even temporarily, penalties up to 6mo prison + €15k. Cat 2 (LOF-pedigreed) allowed with permit + insurance + behavioural eval + muzzle/leash in public ([service-public.gouv.fr](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F1839?lang=en)). **DL**: EU/EEA mutual recognition; UK→FR bilateral exchange post-Brexit operational; UK licence valid for visits. **Senior**: no mandatory medical re-test (self-declared fitness). **Vehicle import**: EU intra → re-register only; 3rd country → 10% duty + 20% VAT typical, CoC required. **LEZ Crit'Air**: 25 ZFE-m zones in 2025; Paris ZFE Crit'Air 3 ban extended to **end 2026** (pedagogical period; sanctions resume after); MPs voted 28 May 2025 to abolish ZFEs nationally — pending Senate + Conseil constitutionnel review (status fluid). Marseille / Rouen / Strasbourg relaxed back to Crit'Air 4/5 ban only. **Utilities**: Enedis (DSO, public monopoly) + supplier (EDF / TotalEnergies / Engie / Octopus / Ilek / 20+ licensed). MES (Mise En Service) typically 5 days; mandatory PFM (Premier Fournisseur Multi-énergie); RIB (Relevé d'Identité Bancaire — French IBAN typical) for direct debit; smart meter Linky deployed nationally. Gas: GRDF DSO. Water: communal régie or delegated (Veolia / Suez / SEDIF Île-de-France / Eau de Paris). **Confidence**: HIGH (multi-source primary cross-check).

- **IT** (Italy): **Pets** EU pet passport; **no national breed ban** (Italy repealed national dangerous-dog list in 2009 — replaced by individual-dog risk assessment under Min. Salute ordinances). Local muzzle/leash rules apply to dogs assessed as risk. **DL**: EU mutual recognition. Non-EU exchange via bilateral. **Senior**: medical re-test every **2 years from age 80**; commissione medica locale. Standard renewal 70-79 every 5 years. **Vehicle import**: EU intra → free; 3rd country → duty + VAT 22% + IPT registration tax (regional). **LEZ**: ZTL (Limited Traffic Zones) per city — Milan **Area C** (charge zone) + **Area B** (24h LEZ banning Euro 5 diesel and below across most of city). Po Valley (Lombardia / Piemonte / Veneto / Emilia) bans Euro 5 diesel from cities >30k pop. Rome / Florence / Pisa / Bologna / Naples each have own ZTL schedules. **Utilities**: e-distribuzione (Enel Group) DSO majority; supplier (Enel Energia / Eni Plenitude / Edison / A2A / etc.). **Codice fiscale mandatory** — cannot transfer/activate without it (Agenzia delle Entrate, 24h in person; remote via consulate possible). Voltura (transfer) faster than new contract; new contracts days–weeks. Standard supply 3kW; 6kW upgrade possible (higher fixed fee). Gas: 2i Rete Gas + Italgas DSOs. Water: regional gestori (Acea Roma / MM Milano / Iren / etc.). **Confidence**: HIGH.

- **ES** (Spain): **Pets** EU pet passport; **PPP regime under Ley 50/1999 + RD 287/2002** still active — Ley 7/2023 (Bienestar Animal) initially proposed individual-sociability test but final text retained PPP framework. National PPP list: Pit Bull / Staffy / Am Staff / Rottweiler / Dogo Argentino / Fila Brasileiro / Tosa / Akita Inu. **Civil liability insurance mandatory for ALL dogs** (PPP min €120k). Catalonia adds Am Bully / Doberman / Dogo Burdeos / Mastín Napolitano / Presa Canario. **DL**: EU mutual recognition. UK→ES bilateral exchange operational from 16 Mar 2023 — 6mo grace from residency; **psicotécnico** (medical/psych) test required. Non-EU: bilateral list via DGT. **Vehicle import**: EU intra → re-register at DGT + IEDMT (registration tax, CO2-banded 0–14.75%); 3rd country → duty + 21% VAT + IEDMT. **DGT Etiqueta Ambiental** (0/ECO/C/B/no-sticker) required; foreign-plated vehicles **not eligible** for DGT etiqueta — must register foreign plates with city before entering ZBE. **Madrid 360 / ZBE**: entire 604 km² municipality declared ZBE 2024; from **1 Jan 2025** only Cat-A vehicles (with environmental label) can circulate within ZBE; non-Madrid-registered no-label vehicles fully prohibited from M-30 ring. Barcelona ZBE (Rondas) live since 2020. >150 Spanish cities required to designate ZBE under Ley 7/2021. **Utilities**: distribuidor (Endesa Distribución / i-DE Iberdrola / UFD Naturgy / E-distribución) + comercializador. PVPC regulated tariff. **Required**: NIE, Spanish IBAN, **CUPS** (20–22 char ID per supply point on every bill), escritura/rental, ID. New build: **boletín eléctrico** (electrician's cert) before connection. Reconnection 1–5 days. Gas: Nedgia DSO. Water: municipal (Canal de Isabel II Madrid / AGBAR/Aigues de Barcelona / Emasagra Granada / Emasesa Sevilla). **Confidence**: HIGH.

- **PT** (Portugal): **Pets** EU pet passport. PT dangerous breeds list (DL 315/2009): Pit Bull / Staffy / Am Staff / Rottweiler / Brazilian Mastiff / Tosa / Argentinian Mastiff — registration + insurance + muzzle in public. **DL**: EU mutual recognition. UK exchange post-Brexit via IMT — bilateral active. **Vehicle import**: EU intra → re-register + ISV (registration tax, complex CO2 + cylinder formula); 3rd country → duty + 23% VAT + ISV. LEZ: Lisbon ZER (Avenida da Liberdade / Baixa) — pre-Euro 3 banned. **Utilities**: **E-REDES** DSO (formerly EDP Distribuição); supplier (EDP Comercial / Iberdrola / Endesa Energia / Galp / Goldenergy). **NIF mandatory** for all utility setup. **CPE** (Código Ponto Entrega) on every bill. Documents: NIF, ID, IBAN, address, CPE. Electricity 1–5 days; water often 1–2 days. Water: **Águas de Portugal** subsidiaries; Lisbon **EPAL**. **Confidence**: HIGH.

- **DE** (Germany): **Pets** EU pet passport. **Listenhunde** federal (under HundVerbrEinfG): nationwide import ban for **Pit Bull / Am Staff / Staffy / Bull Terrier** + crosses (cannot be brought into DE permanently from abroad without local Landesbehörde permit). UK→DE bilateral exchange operational; no re-test if licence valid. **Senior**: no mandatory medical/test (recommended only). **Vehicle import**: EU intra → re-register (KFZ-Zulassungsstelle); 3rd country → 10% duty + 19% VAT. **CoC (EG-Übereinstimmungsbescheinigung)** required. TÜV inspection (HU + AU) required. Without CoC: TÜV Vollabnahme. **Umweltzone (Plakette)**: **green Plakette only** (universal 2025 — red and yellow no longer issued or accepted) typical city zone access (Berlin / Hamburg / München / Frankfurt / Stuttgart / 50+ cities). **Utilities**: Netzbetreiber (DSO) locked by address + Stromanbieter (Vattenfall / E.ON / EnBW / RWE / Octopus / Ostrom / etc.). Grundversorgung (default basic supply) auto-applies — 30–40% above market; 2-week cancellation. **24h-switching rule from 6 Jun 2025** — providers must complete transfer/account opening within 24h (excl. Sundays). Required: name + address + IBAN (German IBAN typically required for SEPA-Lastschrift; foreign IBAN very difficult), Zählernummer, kWh estimate. **SCHUFA** check by most providers (new arrivals lack record — Grundversorgung accepts no SCHUFA). Smart meter rollout partial. **Confidence**: HIGH.

- **AT** (Austria): **Pets** EU pet passport. UK exchange post-Brexit via Führerscheinbehörde (bilateral). **Vehicle import**: EU intra → NoVA (Normverbrauchsabgabe, CO2-banded registration tax — material cost) + 20% VAT (if not transfer-of-residence exempt) + re-registration. 3rd country → duty + VAT + NoVA. **Pickerl** (Umwelt-Plakette): **only N-class commercial / trucks** require sticker (passenger cars M1 EXEMPT). Water: Wiener Wasser (Vienna), municipal elsewhere. **Confidence**: MEDIUM (utility setup specifics for foreigners less documented than DE/FR).

## Nordics + Iceland (SE, FI, NO, DK, IS)

EU/EEA driving-licence mutual recognition. Pet entry under Reg 576/2013 (intra-EU) + Reg 2016/429 (post-22-Apr-2026). **Echinococcus tapeworm 24-120h pre-entry MANDATORY** for **FI, NO, IE, MT** (rabies/echinococcus-protective regime). **Iceland is the strictest pet-import regime in Europe** — mandatory 14-day quarantine + ~ISK 50,000 fees + extensive vaccination panel.

Medical certificate required for non-EU/EEA exchange. Processing 3-4 months. **Vehicle import**: EU intra-market re-register; third-country: 10% duty + 24% VAT + **autovero** (registration tax based on CO₂). ### Vanuatu — **Confidence: MEDIUM** (structural/FX/hazard spine HIGH; pet/DL/vehicle logistics not researched this pass and honestly hedged)

- **Tenure precondition (read before any of the below)**: **there is NO freehold** — the 1980 Constitution vests all land in indigenous custom owners; a foreigner "buys" an **assignment of a registered lease (max 75-year ceiling)** with N years remaining + an annual ground rent. **Years-remaining is the #1 diligence item** — this frames how long any relocation investment is even tenable.
- **Pets**: pet-entry protocol (Biosecurity Vanuatu, Dept of Biosecurity) **not researched this pass — verify with Biosecurity Vanuatu before any move**.
- **Driving licence**: foreign-DL recognition + IDP + resident-conversion path **not researched this pass — verify with the relevant Vanuatu transport authority**.
- **Vehicle import**: duty/age stack **not researched this pass — verify with Vanuatu Customs & Inland Revenue**. (Note: VAT 15% applies on most goods/services; residential rent is VAT-exempt.)
- **Utility setup**: electricity = **UNELCO (ENGIE group)**, main concessionaire for **Port Vila / Efate** (Vanuatu Utilities & Infrastructure / VUI also operates); the grid is limited to urban centres and **outer islands rely on diesel + solar**; tariffs are reported among the higher in the Pacific (secondary). Regulator: **Utilities Regulatory Authority (URA)** — **do NOT quote a tariff number; current electricity/water tariff is not publicly captured here — verify at URA**.
- **FX onboarding**: the **vatu is a basket peg** (USD/AUD/NZD/EUR, actively managed by the RBV, **realigned under a new IMF-advised framework in 2025**) — **moderate FX risk** for a single-currency buyer (the vatu can be realigned against any one basket member, as in 2025); always use a **dated** RBV rate (indicative USD 1 = 117.05 VUV, 12 Jun 2026) and re-pull it. Vanuatu is **zero-direct-tax** (no income/CGT/inheritance/wealth tax), so there is no resident-tax-ID gate of the European kind; on a lease purchase you pay a **stamp duty + government registration fee** (agency sources put these at ~2% / ~5% of purchase price respectively — `est.`/secondary, NOT primary; verify at the Stamp Duties Act + Land Leases Act [Cap 163] fee schedule / Lands Dept).

Outer-island serious-medical access is limited.
- **Hazard overlay (must headline any Vanuatu purchase)**: routinely ranked **#1 in the World Risk Index** — co-located cyclone + earthquake + tsunami + volcanic + SLR exposure (Cyclone Pam Cat 5, 2015; twin Cat-4 cyclones, 2023; **M7.3 quake 17 Dec 2024 ~30 km W of Port Vila**). ## Cross-cutting structural detail

The 7 traps surfaced at the top of this doc each map to a deeper analysis:

### Pet rabies titer waiting period — the 6-7 month flow

**Mechanism**. Four major rabies-island jurisdictions — Australia, New Zealand, Japan, Singapore (Schedule III), Malaysia — require FAVN/RFFIT **rabies neutralising antibody titer** with result **≥ 0.5 IU/ml**, followed by mandatory **180-day waiting period**. AU clock = lab-receipt date (per Animal Biosecurity Advice 2020-A01); JP clock = blood-draw date.

**Realistic minimum end-to-end timeline**:
- Microchip → rabies vaccine → 21-30 day seroconversion → titre blood draw → lab result (2-3 weeks) → 180-day wait → import permit → quarantine slot booking (AU Mickleham: limited slots, often 2-3 month wait) → 10-day quarantine on arrival.
- **Realistic**: 7-9 months from first vet visit to pet on destination soil.
- **Cost**: AU/NZ relocation ~€2,500-6,000 per pet (titre test + permit fees + IATA crate + airline freight + quarantine board).

### EU pet passport vs Animal Health Certificate (AHC) — post-Brexit chain + EU Reg 2016/429 transition 22 Apr 2026

**Post-Brexit reality** (1 Jan 2021): UK pet passports invalid for EU travel; replaced by **EU Animal Health Certificate (AHC)** — single-trip 4-month-valid (10 days for entry, 4 months onward EU travel + return to GB), must be issued by Official Veterinarian ≤ 10 days before EU entry. Cost £100-300 per AHC.

**22 Apr 2026 EU Reg 2016/429 enforcement tightening**: EU pet passports issued by UK vets to UK residents during transition no longer valid for EU travel; UK residents must use AHC. **No EU-UK mutual-recognition treaty published as of May 2026** — primary-source review shows enforcement tightening, not mutual recognition.

### Driving licence exchange — bilateral patchwork, no global standard

**EU/EEA mutual recognition** under Directive 2006/126/EC (3rd DLD); successor Directive (EU) 2025/2205 (4DLD, in force 25 Nov 2025, transposition deadline 26 Nov 2028) extends mutual recognition + new digital-licence + disqualification-portability rules.

**Beyond EU/EEA — bilateral patchwork**:
- **AU**: 2026 reform removed Experienced Driver Recognition (EDR) category. Now binary: 28 recognised countries (no test) vs full re-test. Conversion grace: NSW 3 months, VIC/TAS 6 months.
- **US**: state-by-state — DE bilateral with 27 states + PR; FR bilateral with 18 states.
- **CA**: province-by-province — ON 60 days + reciprocal exchange with US states + AU/AT/BE/FR/DE/GB/HU/DK/IM/JP/KR/NZ/IE/NI/CH/TW/UA.
- **JP**: gaimen kirikae for ~28 listed jurisdictions (DE/FR/UK/AU/NZ/KR/TW/CH/IT/ES/BE/NL etc. — USA NOT on list); others must take theory + practical.

**Senior re-test thresholds**:
- NL 75+ medical mandatory + 5-yearly thereafter
- IT 70-79 every 5 years; 80+ every 2 years
- UK 70+ self-declaration every 3 years
- JP 70+ cognitive screening + 75+ on-road test (since 13 May 2022)
- US no national mandate

### Vehicle import — emissions + age + LHD/RHD trifecta

Three independent compliance gates must clear:

**Emissions**: EU Reg 2018/858 + WLTP testing standard (in force 1 Sep 2020); US EPA Tier 3; AU ADR; IN Bharat Stage VI (since 1 Apr 2020); CN China 6.

**Age limits** (verify pre-shipment):
- KSA / BH / QA / KW: 5-year cap
- BH: relaxed to 10-year (post-2024 reform)
- OM: 10-year
- AE: no statutory cap, but vehicles >10 years require pre-approval permit
- KE: 8-year + 2026-01-01 KEBS rule (only RHD vehicles first-registered ≥2019 allowed)
- NG: 12-year (reaffirmed Nov 2025)
- MU: petrol <3 years / diesel <4 years (post-2025 reform)
- EG: <3 years + Euro 4 + one-vehicle-per-5-years rule from 26 Dec 2024
- DO: 5-year (cars), 15-year (trucks)

**RHD/LHD**:
- LHD-only: most of mainland EU, US, CN, KSA, AE, KW (RHD prohibited)
- RHD-only / dominant: UK, IE, MT, CY, ZA, AU, NZ, JP, SG, IN, PK, TH, MY, ID, ZW, KE
- UK→EU mainland car: headlight beam adjustment + speedometer kmh dial + paperwork ~£500-1,500

**Collector exemptions**:
- US 25-year NHTSA rule
- AU SEVS / Concessional RAV ≥25 years
- EU classic >30 years per MS

### LEZ — urban driving cost

**Three architectures**:
- **Charge-based** (London ULEZ £12.50/day; Crit'Air pass-day in some FR cities)
- **Prohibitory** (Madrid 360, Berlin/Munich Umweltzone green-only since 2025)
- **Zero-emission** (Amsterdam centre from 1 Jan 2025; Rotterdam, Utrecht, Eindhoven, Maastricht same date — currently freight/commercial)

**2024-2026 reform pulse**: most jurisdictions are tightening not relaxing. Buyers should expect annual ratchets. Verify property's exact LEZ status at viewing (FR — certificat-air.gouv.fr; ES — DGT label; DE — Umweltbundesamt LEZ map). Test current vehicle's compliance — green Plakette ≠ Crit'Air-compliant (Diesel Euro 4 = Crit'Air 3, restricted in Paris from 2025).

### Utility setup — foreign-buyer onboarding friction (the 30-day delay trap)

**Catch-22 chain**: utility provider rejects connection without local IBAN + tax ID + ID + tenancy proof. Foreign buyer arrives without local bank account → no local IBAN → no utility connection → property uninhabitable for 2-6 weeks.

**Country-specific patterns**:
- **DE** — utility wants SCHUFA score → SCHUFA needs Anmeldung → Anmeldung needs Steueridentifikationsnummer mailed 2-6 wks post-Anmeldung
- **NL** — utility wants BSN → BSN needs RNI/BRP registration → bank account wants BSN → loop
- **FR** — utility (EDF) wants RIB → RIB needs justificatif de domicile → justificatif from EDF bill → loop. Fix: notary attestation d'achat works as proof.
- **ES** — utility wants NIE + bank account → NIE from extranjería (1-3 wks)
- **IT** — utility wants codice fiscale (5 days from Agenzia delle Entrate) + bank account
- **HU** — MVM Next sole universal supplier post-2022 nationalisation; foreign-owner cap eligibility restricted
- **CR** — Firma Digital BCCR only for citizens / DIMEX-holding residents

### Healthcare registration — the parallel 90-day cliff

Most countries require residency permit + tax ID + bank account + utility bill for public healthcare registration; private travel insurance lapses 90 days post-arrival.

- **DE** — Krankenversicherung mandatory by law from day 1; failure = €1,000+ backdated premiums + fines
- **FR** — Carte Vitale via CPAM after 3-month continuous residence (PUMA/Sécu pathway)
- **ES** — Cartilla Sanitaria via Seguridad Social
- **IT** — Tessera Sanitaria via ASL after residency + codice fiscale + iscrizione anagrafica
- **NL** — Zorgverzekering compulsory + 4-month grace with backdating
- **PT** — SNS via Centro de Saúde, requires NIF + residence + utility
- **CH** — KVG mandatory within 3 months; backdated

## Universal mitigations (apply across all home jurisdictions)

These work regardless of the destination country and prevent the most common 90-day post-completion onboarding pitfalls:

1. **Start pet rabies-titer 7 months pre-move** — for AU/NZ/JP/SG (Schedule III)/MY origin from rabies-uncontrolled. Realistic timeline: titre lab turnaround 2-3 weeks + 180-day wait + permit + slot booking + 10-day quarantine. Skipping this loses 4-6 months of pet-with-family time. Authority: DAFF Animal Biosecurity Advice 2020-A01.

2. **Open destination bank account remotely BEFORE moving** — Wise / Revolut / N26 / bunq cross-border IBAN issuance breaks the utility-deposit catch-22. Lead time: 1-2 weeks. Applies: all of EU + UK + most of OECD. Caveat: some local utilities require truly-domestic IBANs not pan-EU SEPA fintechs — verify with destination DSO.

3. **Get destination tax ID via consulate BEFORE arrival** — NIE (ES, via Spanish consulate, 1-3 wks), codice fiscale (IT, via Italian consulate or remote, 1-2 wks), NIF (PT, via Portuguese consulate or remote), BSN (NL — sometimes via consulate, often needs in-person at RNI/BRP). Lead time: 2-6 wks. Halves day-1 paperwork load.

4. **Verify vehicle compliance BEFORE shipping** — emissions / age / RHD-LHD / LEZ rules. Authority anchor: registered destination agent (RDW NL, RIV CA, RAV AU). Lead time: 1-2 weeks for written compliance opinion. Cost: €100-400 for the opinion vs €5-15k loss on shipping a non-compliant car. Sell at origin if in any doubt.

5. **Check destination DL exchange list BEFORE moving** — bilateral exchange-treaty lists are published by DVLA (UK), DGT (ES), MIT (IT), Kraftfahrt-Bundesamt (DE), JAF (JP). Re-test costs €100-2,000 + 3-6 months timeline. If not on list, factor a pre-move IDP for grace-period driving.

6. **Maintain travel-insurance overlap until public-healthcare-registration confirmed in writing** — typical 90-day gap. Budget €500-2,000 per person for premium gap insurance (Cigna Global / IMG / Allianz Care). Critical: cancellation only after **written confirmation** of public-system enrolment, not "application submitted".

7. **Budget the full "foreign-buyer onboarding friction" — €1k-3k baseline for the 90-day post-completion phase** — utility deposits (€300-800), healthcare gap insurance (€500-1,500), vehicle compliance / sale-and-rebuy delta (€2k-25k), pet shipping (€500-6,000), DL re-test if needed (€100-2,000), tax-ID consulate fees (€10-100). Total varies €1k-30k depending on family size and how much is sold-vs-shipped. Build into TCO at offer stage.
