# Universal `--scams` Section

Country-specific transaction-fraud register: documented buyer-side property scams with regulator advisories, prosecutions, or investigative-journalism evidence. Covers all 126 supported countries.

**Snapshot**: May 2026. Scam patterns evolve with reform cycles — this section is point-in-time. The headline 2024-2026 enforcement waves shaping the landscape: **US wire-fraud at closing** ($2.77B BEC losses 2024 per FBI IC3), **NY/FL/TX deed-theft prosecution wave** (NY AG Marcus Wilcher 3-9y May 2024; FL HB 621 + TX SB 1333), **UK HMLR Property Alert** (£59m blocked FY24-25), **GR Golden Visa price-inflation Circular 1/2026**, **TR CBI "Bona Fide" prosecution** (106 arrests + 451 investors flagged Sept 2025), **ZA Hawarden v ENS SCA 10 Jun 2024** (BEC liability test), **NO Eml. amendment 1 Jul 2025** (hidden bids banned), **MV/SI/RW** Land Authority POA-suspension (Oct 2025) for forged-POA forgery wave, **AE Dubai RERA** (30 developers fined 2024 + Knightsbridge license revoked Nov 2025), **CN Evergrande/Country Garden off-plan delivery default cascade**, **VN Tan Hoang Minh** prosecution (Hanoi People's Court 27 Mar 2024), **KR jeonse fraud** (38,000+ recognized victims by Apr 2026).

**Anti-hallucination**: every scheme should be traceable to a primary source (regulator advisory, prosecution case, embassy travel-advisory page, investigative-journalism series). Where the evidence is forum / expat-press only, label `(forum-reported)` or `(industry-reported)` and surface confidence as MEDIUM/LOW.

**Disambiguation — `--scams` vs `--integrity` vs `--risks`**: `--scams` = country-specific **transaction-fraud** patterns targeting buyers (deed forgery, wire fraud, off-plan disappearance, nominee structures, golden-visa inflation). `--integrity` = data-honesty checks on the listing/cadastre output itself (4 cross-cutting checks in `shared/integrity-checks.md`). `--risks` = natural + technological hazards (flood, earthquake, build-era diagnostics) — physical risk, not fraud.

## Universal contract

For the property's country, surface the highest-evidence buyer-side scam patterns. Each pattern needs:

1. **Scheme name** (with native term where applicable, e.g., MX *ejido*, CR *Zona Marítimo Terrestre*, JP *jimenshi 地面師*, KR *jeonse 전세 villa-king*)
2. **Mechanism** (one sentence — how the fraud works)
3. **Victim profile** (foreign cash buyer / retiree / NRI / off-plan / online deposit / probate)
4. **Documented evidence** — regulator advisory, prosecution, embassy advisory, investigative-journalism URL
5. **Mitigation** (one sentence — which authority/registry/process to verify with)
6. **Confidence**: HIGH (regulator advisory + named prosecution) / MEDIUM (multi-source forum + practitioner) / LOW (anecdotal)

## Cross-cutting traps to surface every time

These appear across multiple regions and warrant a buyer-facing flag whenever the user is shopping abroad:

1. **Wire-fraud at closing (BEC)** — the single biggest-by-$ pattern in US/CA/AU/UK/IE/DE/FR/ES/BE/NL/CH/IT/NZ/ZA. Mechanism: hacker compromises notary/solicitor/conveyancer/title-company/agent email and sends spoofed wire instructions the day before closing. **FBI IC3 2024**: $2.77B BEC losses; ALTA 86% of title firms reported threat increase. **AU ACSC 2024**: A$152.6M (+66% YoY). **ZA SCA Hawarden v ENS (10 Jun 2024)**: conveyancer NOT liable for failure to warn — burden is on buyer to verify. **Mitigation universal**: voice-verify IBAN/account on a number sourced from a public directory (Law Society, state registry, Real Estate Council), NOT from the email itself.

2. **Title fraud / deed forgery / impersonated owner** — most active in **UK** (HMLR Property Alert blocks £59m FY24-25), **US NY/FL/TX** (post-2024 deed-theft criminal statutes — NY AG Wilcher 3-9y May 2024; FL HB 621 1st-degree felony; TX SB 1333), **CA Ontario** (LTAF backstop), **JP** (*jimenshi* — Sekisui House ¥5.5B 2017, ¥1B Tokyo District Court ruling Nov 2024), **PE** (SUNARP Alerta Registral), **CL** (Conservador de Bienes Raíces CLP 223M La Tercera 2024), **PL** (forged POA), **RO** (procură falsificată), **HU** (forged tulajdoni lap), **NG** (fake C of O — EFCC Kaduna 2024), **ZA** (M&G Apr 2026 wave). **Mitigation universal**: title insurance where available; registry-fraud-alert subscription (HMLR Property Alert, ON ONLAND, PE SUNARP Alerta, propertyalert.landregistry.gov.uk); same-day registry pull at signing; restriction-on-register for absent owners.

3. **Off-plan developer disappearance / handover indefinite** — **CY** trapped buyers (~9,500; 2025 Trapped Buyers Law), **TR** (Yetki Belgesi-less + İskan absence), **AE** Dubai (RERA 30 fines 2024 + Knightsbridge license Nov 2025), **EG** (PM Decree 2184/2022 + 2024 timeline-extension regime), **CN** (Evergrande 2021, Country Garden 2023, mortgage-strike 2022), **VN** (Tan Hoang Minh VND 8.6T 27 Mar 2024), **KH** Sihanoukville (~360-1000+ ghost buildings), **MY** (KPKT sick-project registry), **NG** (RevolutionPlus / Lekki diaspora ₦45M+), **MT** FIAU 2024 (+80% real-estate STRs), **AL** Saranda/Vlora/Durrës, **ME** Budva/Tivat/Kotor, **HR** kapara double-sale Adriatic. **Mitigation universal**: bank-guaranteed escrow only (no developer-direct payments); tranche payments tied to architect-certified milestones; existing-stock preference for non-residents.

4. **Foreign-restricted-zone confusion / nominee structure** — illegal under each country's land law and consistently unwound by courts: **TH** Thai-LLC 51% nominee (Land Code §86, FBA §36; Sept 2024 Commerce Ministry taskforce — 820 prosecutions, 12.5B baht damages, 23 defendants 5y prison + 200k baht fines), **ID** Hak Milik via Indonesian nominee (Agrarian Law 5/1960 §26(2) — null and void), **MX** zona restringida fideicomiso hijack + ejido fraud (Bloomberg Tulum Mar 2025; US Embassy advisory), **VN** 30%/250-unit foreign cap (no land), **PH** 40% condo cap, **KH** 70% above ground floor, **MY** state-level minimums, **MA** agricultural-land nominee, **KW** effective foreign-ban via informal proxies. **Mitigation universal**: never use nominee structures; use country's foreign-eligible vehicle (lease, Hak Pakai, BOI/PMA, fideicomiso with bank you choose, etc.).

5. **Golden-visa / residency-by-investment price-inflation** — **TR** $400k CBI: "Bona Fide" network 106 arrests + 1,240 apartments seized + 451 investors flagged for citizenship revocation Sept 2025 (largest documented MENA case). **CY** legacy CBI inflated valuation: 304 citizenships revoked, 77 names on Politis list, 20 facing criminal charges (Cyprus Property News Nov 2024). **GR** Golden Visa price-inflation probe: AML Authority + tax authority active 2024-2025; Greek Circular 1/2026 (31-page directive); buyers paying 2-3× market value with inflated figure on deed to clear threshold. **MT** CBI under EU Court scrutiny 2024-2025. **Mitigation universal**: independent statutory-licensed appraisal; refuse dual-contract / "we'll list higher" pitches; the program officially closed = any "still-available" pitch is fraudulent.

6. **Customary-tenure / restitution-lien trap** — **PL** Warsaw reprywatyzacja (post-WWII restitution claims surfacing 2010-2024; dedicated Reprivatisation Commission since 2017 reversing dozens), **RO** retrocedare (Balkan Insight: €356M overpaid; OCCRP Prince Paul royal-restitution €136M state loss), **GH** stool-land double-sale (Supreme Court flagged "hopelessly confusing records"; Lands Commission documented parcels sold to up to 13 different buyers; Land Act 2020 reform), **NG** family-land double sale (Land Use Act 1978; Governor's consent required), **KE** Coast customary-tenure overlap, **BA** dual cadastre vs Land Book divergence (zemljišna knjiga vs katastar), **JM** family-land "scratched" titles (NLA Systematic Land Registration), **BB** unregistered-title deed-based system, **MK** reciprocity-rule trap. **Mitigation universal**: triangulate registry + customary/community + physical site + adjoining-owner inquiry; chain-of-title back to pre-disruption baseline (1939 PL; 1947/1989 RO; pre-conflict BA; post-emancipation JM/BB).

7. **Concession-vs-freehold tourism-zone** — **CR** Zona Marítimo Terrestre (Tico Times: Tamarindo NJ-couple US$685k case Mar 2024 — concession expired Jan 2023, 14 months pre-purchase; only 50-200m concession, max 51% foreign-held, time-limited revocable), **BR** terrenos de marinha (federal Navy ground-lease only), **MX** zona federal, **BS** Crown foreshore (US Embassy explicit warning), **CV** ZDTI under State concession to developer, **OM** ITC-only freehold, **BH** 6-zone freehold, **QA** freehold vs 99-year usufruct zones, **TN** Wali authorization required, **SC** sanction-required IPTRA, **MV** strata-leasehold not freehold (Constitution bars non-citizen freehold). **Mitigation universal**: zone-status verification at the relevant ministry/authority BEFORE deposit; treat concession/lease as time-limited; do not pay freehold price for use-right.

8. **Unlicensed immigration adviser / "notario" fraud** *(added 2026-06-14)* — paying for visa/immigration advice from someone not legally entitled to give it. In the **US** a *notary public* only witnesses signatures; "notarios" exploit the Spanish-language meaning (where *notario* = a senior lawyer) to sell unauthorised advice — only a state-bar-licensed **attorney** or a **DOJ/EOIR-accredited representative** may advise (USCIS [Find Legal Services](https://www.uscis.gov/scams-fraud-and-misconduct/avoid-scams/find-legal-services); FTC + AILA/ABA [StopNotarioFraud.org](https://www.stopnotariofraud.org/)). Each regulated destination runs a **public register** to check first: **UK** Immigration Advice Authority (IAA — the OISC rebranded to the IAA with effect from [16 Jan 2025](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/enhanced-agenda-and-identity-for-uk-immigration-advice-regulator); advising while unregistered is a criminal offence under the Immigration & Asylum Act 1999 ss.84/91) via [gov.uk/find-an-immigration-adviser](https://www.gov.uk/find-an-immigration-adviser); **CA** College of Immigration & Citizenship Consultants (CICC — RCIC licence "R"+6 digits; the "ghost consultant" is the documented vector); **AU** OMARA register (Migration Act 1958); **NZ** Immigration Advisers Authority (Licensing Act 2007). Most of the **EU** has no separate adviser licence — immigration advice is reserved legal work, so verify the *avocat / Rechtsanwalt / abogado* on the national-bar directory (FR CNB, DE BRAK *BRAV*, ES CGAE *Censo*). **Mitigation universal**: confirm the adviser on the regulator's public register — by licence number, not name — BEFORE paying; a recorded 1-hour consult with a verified professional beats a cheap "consultant".

## Universal mitigations (apply regardless of country)

These five mitigations work across all 126 countries and prevent ~80% of documented losses:

1. **Voice-verify wire instructions on an independently-sourced number** — never trust the email signature, never call back the number "from the email". Use the law-society / state-registrar / public-directory number for the firm. This single step would have prevented the bulk of $2.77B BEC losses 2024 (FBI IC3) + A$152.6M AU + ZA Hawarden v ENS R5.5M + LB fresh-USD vs Lollar deposits.

2. **Pull the cadastre/registry document yourself the day of signing** — not the seller's PDF, not "we already verified". Country sources: registradores.org (ES), nahlizenidokn.cuzk.cz (CZ), epay.ancpi.ro (RO), foldhivatal.hu (HU), zk.pravosudje.hr (HR), katastar.rgz.gov.rs (RS), nekretnine.co.me (ME), e-hiteles tulajdoni lap (HU), Kadaster.nl (NL), Registry of Deeds (PH), SUNARP (PE), Ardhisasa (KE), katastar.gov.mk (MK), Public Service Hall (GE), Tabu (IL/TR), DLD (AE), Public Registry (PA), SNR (CO), nahlizenidokn (CZ), kinnistuportaal.rik.ee (EE), zemesgramata.lv (LV), registrucentras.lt (LT), evidenca.sodisce.si (SI).

3. **Engage independent buyer's lawyer** — NOT the seller's, NOT the agent's recommendation, NOT a "friend of a friend". Especially critical in: ES, IT, CY, GR, MT, MX, BR, CR, PA, DO, ID, TH, PH, NG, KE, AE, TR, IL.

4. **Verify any agent's license at the country's registry before deposit** — UK: redress-scheme + AML registration; US: state real estate commission; CA: provincial Real Estate Council (RECO/BCFSA/REA); AU: NSW Fair Trading PSAA; NZ: REA; FR: Carte T; IT: REA Camera di Commercio; ES: regional API/RAICV; PT: AMI/IMPIC; DE: § 34c GewO; AT: MaklerG; NL: voluntary NVM/VBO; BE: BIV/IPI; LU: autorisation d'établissement; IE: PSRA; GR: GEMI; MT: MCCAA Real Estate Agents Act 2020 (Cap. 615); CY: CREAA; CZ: Zákon 39/2020 register; SK: NARKS; PL: PFRN voluntary post-Lex Gowin; HU: real-estate-supervisory authority; RO: APAIR/RENII; BG: Commercial Register; SI: SPOT; EE: Kutseregister voluntary; LV: LANIDA voluntary; LT: no formal but LRTA; HR: HGK broker registry + insurance ≥200k HRK; RS: Ministry of Trade registry + €15k indemnity; ZA: PPRA FFC; NG: LASRERA; KE: EARB; MU: voluntary; CV: voluntary; SC: voluntary; GH: voluntary; RW: voluntary; TR: Yetki Belgesi at Ministry of Trade; AE: DLD broker card; SA: REGA Wafi; QA: MoJ broker register; JP: MLIT licensed builder; KR: licensed broker via MOLIT; CN: licensed; HK: EAA; SG: CEA; TW: Real Estate Brokerage Management Act register; ID: AREBI voluntary; TH: licensed but loose; MY: Board of Valuers, Appraisers, Estate Agents and Property Managers (BoVEAP); PH: PRC RESA; VN: Real Estate Business Law 29/2023; KH: Ministry of Land; LK: licensed; MV: licensed via Ministry of Tourism; IN: state RERA; US: state RES + post-NAR-2024 buyer agreement; CA: provincial Real Estate Council; AU: state PSAA; NZ: REA.

5. **For foreign-restricted zones: pre-clear eligibility with the relevant authority before signing** — TR military-zone Tapu blacklist; AE freehold-vs-leasehold zones; MX zona restringida fideicomiso; ID Hak Pakai vs Hak Milik; VN/PH/KH/TH foreign-quota; OM ITC-only; BH 6-zone; KW Cabinet-only; QA Cabinet-decreed freehold; SA REGA Non-Saudi platform + Mecca/Medina; TN Wali; MV strata-lease; SC IPTRA; CV ZDTI; LK 99-year lease only; KZ residence-permit; MY state minimums; LI GVG; AD AGIA + FDI authorization; SM reciprocity (Italian code applies practically).

6. **Verify the immigration adviser AND the cross-border tax adviser on the regulator's own public register — before you pay.** Distinct from the property lawyer (#3) and the estate agent (#4): the person handling your *visa* or your *cross-border tax* must be legally entitled to. **Immigration**: a US state-bar attorney or EOIR-accredited rep (never a "notario"), UK IAA, CA CICC, AU OMARA, NZ IAA, or an EU national-bar *avocat / Rechtsanwalt / abogado* (see cross-cutting trap 8). **Cross-border tax**: a member of the profession's statutory chamber/institute — DE Bundessteuerberaterkammer, FR Ordre des Experts-Comptables, UK CIOT / ICAEW, NL RB or NOB, IT CNDCEC *Albo*, US IRS credentialed-preparer directory, ES CGE/REAF — and for estates/trusts a STEP **TEP** qualified in BOTH origin and destination. Full per-register detail + verify-URLs in `shared/cross-border.md` § Verifying the adviser is regulated.
