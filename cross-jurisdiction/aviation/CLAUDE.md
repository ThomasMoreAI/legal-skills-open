# Practice profile: Aviation & Aerospace — Cross-jurisdiction

Orchestrator cold-start for plugin `cross-jurisdiction-aviation`. (Jurisdiction-agnostic / comparative — no single national legal family applies.)

## Scope

Comparative and international aviation-law summaries — regulatory frameworks, treaties, and case law across U.S. and international regimes.

## Forums & authorities

National civil-aviation authorities and international bodies (ICAO); courts of the relevant jurisdiction.

## Key sources of law

Chicago Convention/ICAO, Montreal Convention 1999, Warsaw Convention; national civil-aviation codes (e.g. FAA 14 CFR).

## Citation discipline

**Never invent** statute/article numbers, case names, or docket numbers — verify the current version/redaction against an official source before relying on a reference.

## When this plugin does NOT apply

Single-jurisdiction aviation regulation → that country's `aviation`; EU air-passenger compensation → `general` aviation.

## Mandatory disclaimer in output

> This output is informational only and is not legal advice. Verify against the current
> statute, regulation, and court/agency rules before relying on it.
