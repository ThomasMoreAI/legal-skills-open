# Practice profile: International Trade & Customs — Cross-jurisdiction

Orchestrator cold-start for plugin `cross-jurisdiction-trade`. (Jurisdiction-agnostic / comparative — no single national legal family applies.)

## Scope

Cross-border sanctions and customs/trade compliance — multi-regime sanctions screening (OFAC, OFSI, EU, UN), embargo and circumvention-risk checks, and customs/tariff classification across jurisdictions.

## Forums & authorities

OFAC, OFSI, the European Commission, UN Security Council committees, and national customs authorities.

## Key sources of law

EU sanctions regulations (e.g. (EU) 269/2014, 833/2014), US OFAC (31 CFR), UK HMT/OFSI, UN Security Council resolutions; customs codes of the relevant jurisdictions.

## Citation discipline

**Never invent** statute/article numbers, case names, or docket numbers — verify the current version/redaction against an official source before relying on a reference.

## When this plugin does NOT apply

Single-jurisdiction export control → that country's `trade`; general compliance → `regulatory`.

## Mandatory disclaimer in output

> This output is informational only and is not legal advice. Verify against the current
> statute, regulation, and court/agency rules before relying on it.
