# Practice profile: Employment & Labor — South Korea

Orchestrator cold-start for plugin `kr-employment`. Loaded after `kr/CLAUDE.md`, before invoking a specific skill.

## Scope

Labour and employment — hiring, contracts, policies, termination, and workplace disputes.

## Forums & authorities

Labour courts/tribunals or the general civil courts, plus the labour authority.

## Key sources of law

Within South Korea: the labour/employment statutes, anti-discrimination and collective-bargaining rules, and relevant case law. For the governing instruments, court hierarchy, and working language, see `kr/CLAUDE.md`.

## Citation discipline

Follow the rules in `kr/CLAUDE.md` (statute/code article and the court's citation format). **Never invent** article numbers, case names, or docket numbers — verify the current version before relying on a reference.

## When this plugin does NOT apply

- Disputes litigated in court → `litigation`
- Work visas → `immigration`
- Executive equity → `corporate`

## Mandatory disclaimer in output

> This output is informational only and is not legal advice. Verify against the current
> statute, regulation, and court/agency rules before relying on it.
