# Price disclosure

The Preisbekanntgabeverordnung (PBV, SR 942.211) implements Art. 16–20 UWG. It is enforced by cantonal bodies under federal oversight by SECO, and breach is a criminal offence. Wording from PBV Stand am 1. Januar 2025 and UWG Stand am 1. Januar 2025. Status as at 2026-08-05.

## Statutory frame

Art. 16 Abs. 1 UWG: for goods offered to consumers for sale, the **actual price payable** must be disclosed unless the Federal Council provides an exception; the same duty applies to services designated by the Federal Council. Art. 16a UWG adds unit-price disclosure for measurable goods and services. Art. 17 UWG refers advertising prices to the Federal Council's rules. Art. 18 UWG prohibits misleading price disclosure, misleading references to price reductions, and misleadingly listing further prices alongside the actual price payable.

**Sanction.** Art. 24 Abs. 1 UWG: intentional breach of the disclosure duty, of the advertising rules, misleading price disclosure, breach of the duty to give information, or breach of the Federal Council's implementing rules is punishable with a **Busse bis zu 20 000 Franken**. Abs. 2: negligence is punishable with a fine as well. Unlike Art. 23 UWG this is not limited to complaints.

## Scope — Art. 2 PBV

The ordinance applies to offers of goods for sale to consumers, to transactions economically equivalent to a sale (instalment, hire-purchase, leasing, trade-in), to the services listed in Art. 10 PBV, and to **all advertising directed at consumers for any goods and services** (Art. 2 Abs. 1 lit. d PBV). Consumers are persons buying for purposes unconnected with their commercial or professional activity (Abs. 2).

Note the asymmetry: the price-disclosure duty for services covers only the enumerated sectors of Art. 10 PBV, but the *advertising* rules cover all goods and services.

## The price itself — Art. 3, 4 PBV

> Art. 3 Abs. 1 PBV: Werden den Konsumentinnen und Konsumenten Waren zum Kauf angeboten, so muss mit dem Angebot stets der tatsächlich zu bezahlende Preis in Schweizerfranken (Detailpreis) bekanntgegeben werden.

> Art. 4 Abs. 1 PBV: Überwälzte öffentliche Abgaben, Urheberrechtsvergütungen, vorgezogene Entsorgungsbeiträge sowie weitere nicht frei wählbare Zuschläge jeglicher Art, namentlich für Reservation, Service oder Bearbeitung, müssen im Detailpreis inbegriffen sein. Versandkosten dürfen separat bekanntgegeben werden.

So: value added tax and every non-optional surcharge are inside the displayed price; **shipping may be shown separately**. That is the one carve-out and it is narrow — a "service fee", "booking fee" or "processing fee" is not shipping and must be inside the price.

Art. 4 Abs. 1bis PBV: after a change in the VAT rate, price markings must be adjusted within three months, and during that period consumers must be told by a clearly visible notice that the marking does not yet reflect the change. Art. 4 Abs. 2 PBV: discounts, discount stamps or refunds realisable only after purchase must be disclosed separately and quantified.

Current VAT rates under Art. 25 MWSTG (Stand am 31. März 2025): 8.1 % standard, 2.6 % reduced, and the special rate for accommodation.

## Services — Art. 10 PBV

Art. 10 Abs. 1 PBV lists the sectors where the actual price payable must always be disclosed with the offer: hairdressing; garage servicing; hospitality and hotels; cosmetic institutes and body care; fitness institutes, pools, ice rinks and other sports facilities; taxis; entertainment, museums, exhibitions, fairs and sporting events; hire of vehicles, apparatus and equipment; laundries and textile cleaning; parking and garaging; photo services; courses; flights and package travel; separately invoiced booking, reservation and agency services connected with travel; telecommunications services; value-added telecommunications services; account opening, maintenance and closure, domestic and cross-border payments, payment instruments and currency exchange; timeshare rights; services connected with dispensing medicinal products and medical devices and services of vets and dentists; funeral parlours; notarial services.

Art. 10 Abs. 2 PBV mirrors Art. 4 Abs. 1: passed-on levies, copyright levies and further non-optional surcharges must be included; **Kurtaxen** may be shown separately.

An ordinary online shop selling goods is caught by Art. 3 PBV, not by Art. 10 PBV. A SaaS subscription is not in the Art. 10 list, but its advertising is still caught by Art. 2 Abs. 1 lit. d and Art. 13 PBV.

## Presentation — Art. 7–9, 11 PBV

Prices must be marked on the item or immediately next to it, or in another easily accessible and legible form where per-item marking is impractical (Art. 7 PBV). They must be easily visible, legible and given in figures (Art. 8 PBV). The disclosure must make clear which product and which sales unit the price relates to (Art. 9 Abs. 1 PBV); for services, which type and unit of service or which charging rates (Art. 11 Abs. 2 PBV).

## Advertising and comparison prices — Art. 13, 15–17 PBV

Art. 13 Abs. 1 PBV: where advertising states prices or numerical references to price ranges or limits, the **actual prices payable** must be disclosed. Art. 15 PBV extends the misleading-price rules of Art. 16–18 PBV to advertising.

Art. 16 Abs. 1 PBV — the rule behind every crossed-out price. A comparison price may be shown alongside the actual price payable only where:

> a. er die Ware oder die Dienstleistung zu folgenden Zeitspannen tatsächlich zum Vergleichspreis angeboten hat (Selbstvergleich):
> 1. bis unmittelbar vor der Bekanntgabe des tatsächlich zu bezahlenden Preises, oder
> 2. während mindestens 30 aufeinanderfolgender Tage;
> b. er die Ware oder die Dienstleistung unmittelbar danach tatsächlich zu diesem Preis anbieten wird (Einführungspreis); oder
> c. andere Anbieter im zu berücksichtigenden Marktgebiet die überwiegende Menge gleicher Waren oder Dienstleistungen tatsächlich zu diesem Preis anbieten (Konkurrenzvergleich).

Lit. a was amended by the ordinance of 30 October 2024, **in force since 1 January 2025** (AS 2024 621), which introduced the 30-consecutive-day alternative. Do not describe the Swiss rule using the EU Omnibus formula ("lowest price of the last 30 days") — the Swiss test is different: the comparison price must have been *actually charged*, either immediately before or for at least 30 consecutive days.

Art. 16 Abs. 2 PBV: for introductory and competitor comparisons the type of comparison must be apparent, and the provider must substantiate the conditions on request. Abs. 3: a comparison price under lit. a Ziff. 1 or lit. b may be shown only for half as long as it was or will be applied, and at most two months; a comparison price under lit. a Ziff. 2 may be shown without time limit and reused for successive further reductions. Abs. 3bis preserves the last such comparison price where an item is temporarily withdrawn and reoffered. Abs. 5: catalogue and recommended prices qualify as comparison prices only under the lit. c competitor test.

Art. 17 PBV: numerical references to price reductions, bonuses, trade-in and buy-back offers and gifts are assessed like the disclosure of further prices, and are subject to the disclosure and specification duties.

## Enforcement

Art. 22 PBV: the competent cantonal bodies supervise compliance and report breaches to the competent instances; procedure follows cantonal law. Art. 23 PBV: the Confederation exercises oversight through SECO within the EAER; SECO may issue directives and circulars to the cantons, request information, and report breaches to the cantonal instances. SECO publishes explanatory guidance on the PBV — check it before drafting an unusual price display.

## Checkpoints

- [ ] Every consumer-facing price is the actual price payable, in Swiss francs, including VAT
- [ ] Non-optional surcharges are inside the price; only shipping is shown separately (Art. 4 Abs. 1 PBV)
- [ ] The sales unit and the product are unambiguous (Art. 9 PBV)
- [ ] Unit prices shown where Art. 16a UWG and Art. 5, 6 PBV apply
- [ ] Advertising prices are actual prices payable (Art. 13 Abs. 1 PBV)
- [ ] Every crossed-out or "statt" price satisfies Art. 16 Abs. 1 PBV, with evidence retained
- [ ] Display duration of comparison prices respects Art. 16 Abs. 3 PBV
- [ ] Post-purchase rebates disclosed separately and quantified (Art. 4 Abs. 2 PBV)
- [ ] Service offerings checked against the Art. 10 PBV list
- [ ] After a VAT rate change, markings adjusted within three months with a visible notice in the meantime
- [ ] No EU "lowest price of the last 30 days" wording used for a Swiss shop
