# Intake questionnaire

Answer before drafting anything. Missing answers become `[[MISSING: …]]` in the draft and in the report — never guessed.

## Legal entity

1. Legal form — Einzelunternehmen, Kollektivgesellschaft, Kommanditgesellschaft, GmbH, AG, Verein, Stiftung, or a natural person acting privately?
2. Exact name or Firma as entered in the Handelsregister, in that exact spelling.
3. Registered in the Handelsregister? Sole proprietors must register once annual turnover reaches CHF 100,000 (Art. 931 Abs. 1 OR); members of the liberal professions and farmers are exempt unless they run a commercially organised business.
4. Cantonal Handelsregisteramt and the UID in the format CHE-###.###.### (Art. 930 OR, UIDG SR 431.03).
5. Registered office (Sitz) and the geographic address of the establishment — street, number, postal code, place, country.
6. MWST-registered? MWST number and the "MWST" suffix. Liability starts at CHF 100,000 worldwide turnover from non-exempt supplies (Art. 10 Abs. 2 lit. a MWSTG).
7. Persons with signing authority / managing bodies, as entered in the register.
8. Email address plus at least one further contact channel.

## Languages and reach

9. In which languages is the site published, and in which languages are customers addressed? Each mandatory text must exist in every language of address.
10. Which countries are targeted? Language versions, currencies, delivery zones and payment methods all count as evidence of targeting.
11. Are EU or EEA consumers addressed? If yes, `eu-crossborder.md` applies in parallel.
12. Is the site accessible from Switzerland but operated from abroad? Then check Art. 14 DSG representation.

## Regulated activity

13. Is the activity subject to any authorisation or supervision — FINMA, Swissmedic, cantonal health authority, gambling, security services, employment agency, travel organiser?
14. Is a protected professional title used (Rechtsanwalt, Arzt, Architekt, Treuhänder)? Which cantonal register?

## Data processing

15. What data is collected — contact form, account, order, newsletter, support, server logs, payment data?
16. Purpose per processing, and whether it rests on consent, contract, law, or overriding private interest (Art. 31 DSG).
17. Retention period per category, or the criteria for determining it.
18. Recipients: hosting, payment service provider, shipping, mail sending, analytics, CRM, cloud, AI services.
19. Disclosure abroad? To which state, and on which basis — Anhang 1 DSV adequacy, standard clauses, Art. 17 DSG exception?
20. Are processor contracts in place (Art. 9 DSG), and where are they filed?
21. Profiling? Profiling mit hohem Risiko (Art. 5 lit. g DSG)? Automated individual decisions (Art. 21 DSG)?
22. Besonders schützenswerte Personendaten under Art. 5 lit. c DSG — health, religious, ideological, political or trade-union views or activities, genetic and biometric data identifying a person, ethnicity, administrative and criminal proceedings and sanctions, social assistance measures?
23. Headcount as at 1 January — decides the Art. 24 DSV register exemption.
24. Datenschutzberater appointed under Art. 10 DSG? Optional for private controllers, but unlocks the Art. 23 Abs. 4 DSG exemption from consulting the EDÖB.
25. Is the GDPR triggered under Art. 3(2) GDPR? If yes, an Art. 27 GDPR representative is likely required.

## Selling

26. Sales to consumers, to businesses, or to both?
27. Goods, digital content, digital services, subscriptions, or services performed on site?
28. New or used goods? Determines the Art. 210 Abs. 4 OR floor of two years versus one year.
29. Is warranty to be excluded or limited under Art. 199 OR? Note that this is exposed to Art. 8 UWG in consumer AGB.
30. Is a Garantie offered in addition? Scope, duration, who bears it, what it adds to the statutory warranty.
31. Is a voluntary return policy offered? Period, condition of goods, who pays return shipping. It must never be called Widerrufsrecht.
32. Prices: gross including VAT? Shipping shown separately? Any comparison, crossed-out or introductory prices?
33. Subscriptions with automatic renewal? Notice periods and how they are communicated.
34. Payment methods and payment service providers.

## Marketing

35. Newsletter or other electronic direct advertising planned? How is consent captured and logged?
36. Is the existing-customer exception in Art. 3 Abs. 1 lit. o UWG relied on? Then the contact data must come from a sale, the advertising must be for the provider's own similar goods, and the refusal option must have been pointed out at collection.
37. Telephone marketing planned? The Sterneintrag rule in Art. 3 Abs. 1 lit. u UWG and the caller-ID rule in lit. v apply.
38. Any environmental or climate claims in the marketing? Art. 3 Abs. 1 lit. x UWG requires objective and verifiable substantiation.
39. Influencer cooperations, affiliate links, customer reviews displayed?

## Platform and content

40. User-generated content, comments, forum, marketplace, hosting for third parties?
41. Who moderates, on what basis, and is that written into the AGB?
42. Third-party images, fonts, music, texts — licence evidence on file?

## Accessibility

43. Is the operator a federal or cantonal authority, a concession holder, or otherwise performing a public task? Then Art. 14 Abs. 2 BehiG and Art. 10 BehiV apply.
44. Are EU customers served? Then the EAA applies to the EU-facing offering from 28 June 2025, subject to the microenterprise exemption for services.

## Checkpoints

- [ ] Every language of address listed, with a plan for each mandatory text
- [ ] Targeting of EU customers decided explicitly, not left open
- [ ] Every processing has purpose, basis, recipients and retention
- [ ] Transfers abroad listed by state and by basis
- [ ] Headcount recorded for the Art. 24 DSV test
- [ ] Warranty and return positions decided, with new/used distinction
- [ ] Consent capture mechanism for marketing described
- [ ] All open points listed for the report back to the user
