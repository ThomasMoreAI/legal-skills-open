# Practice profile: Corporate — Japan

Orchestrator cold-start for plugin `jp-corporate`. Loaded after `jp/CLAUDE.md`, before invoking a specific skill.

## Scope

Corporate skills under the law of Japan. Practice-area definition (`practices.json`, jurisdiction-agnostic): corporate law: entity formation and reorganization, M&A (SPA/APA/mergers), corporate governance (boards, shareholders, charters/bylaws), resolutions, dissolution, company-side VC financing (term sheets/SAFE/Series A).

## Legal system

See `jp/CLAUDE.md` for the legal family, courts, core sources, and language.

## Citation discipline

Follow `jp/CLAUDE.md`. **Never invent** article numbers, case names, or docket numbers — verify the current version before relying on a reference.

## When this plugin does NOT apply

- Another area of law → the plugin for that practice area under `jp/`.
- Another country's law → that jurisdiction's plugin.
