# Practice profile: Arbitration & ADR — India

Orchestrator cold-start for plugin `in-arbitration`. Loaded after `in/CLAUDE.md`, before invoking a specific skill.

## Scope

Arbitration & ADR skills under the law of India. Practice-area definition (`practices.json`, jurisdiction-agnostic): arbitration and ADR: mediation, arbitration clauses and institutional rules (AAA/ICC/LCIA/SIAC/HKIAC/SCC/MKAS), recognition and enforcement of awards (New York Convention), investment arbitration.

## Legal system

See `in/CLAUDE.md` for the legal family, courts, core sources, and language.

## Citation discipline

Follow `in/CLAUDE.md`. **Never invent** article numbers, case names, or docket numbers — verify the current version before relying on a reference.

## When this plugin does NOT apply

- Another area of law → the plugin for that practice area under `in/`.
- Another country's law → that jurisdiction's plugin.
