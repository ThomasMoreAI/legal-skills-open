# Pre-Flight Checklist — IRC § 1259 Constructive Sale

- [ ] Is the position confirmed NOT marked to market (§ 1256 / § 475(f))? § 1259(b)(2)(C) excludes those.
- [ ] Is unrealized capital gain verified ($\text{FMV} > \text{Cost Basis}$) before auditing constructive sale rules? (§ 1259(b)(1))
- [ ] Is the FMV used the value **on the offsetting transaction's entry date**, not the reporting date? (§ 1259(a)(1))
- [ ] Does the offsetting transaction map to a per se trigger in § 1259(c)(1)(A)–(D)? Collars and ITM puts must be escalated, not auto-classified — no regulations exist under § 1259(c)(1)(E).
- [ ] Is the offsetting short/swap entry tracked alongside underlying long position lot IDs, with the hedged quantity recorded for partial hedges?
- [ ] Is the 30-day close deadline computed from **the taxpayer's own** tax year end (Jan 30 only for Dec 31 filers)? (§ 1259(c)(3)(A)(i))
- [ ] Is the long position confirmed held throughout the full 60-day period beginning on the close date? (§ 1259(c)(3)(A)(ii))
- [ ] Is the 60-day window free of any § 246(c)(4) risk reduction — protective put, obligation to sell, open short, written call? (§ 1259(c)(3)(A)(iii))
- [ ] If risk was reduced inside the window, has the § 1259(c)(3)(B) carve-out been evaluated before concluding a constructive sale?
- [ ] Have related accounts and entities been aggregated, since § 1259(c)(1) reaches "the taxpayer (or a related person)"?
- [ ] On a trigger, are the § 1259(a)(2) consequences recorded — adjusted basis and restarted holding period?
- [ ] Has a qualified US tax adviser reviewed any `MANUAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED` determination?
