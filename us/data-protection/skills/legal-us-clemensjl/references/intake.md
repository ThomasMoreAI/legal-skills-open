# Intake

Answer before the first legal text is written. Missing answers become `[[MISSING: …]]` in the draft and in the report back to the user. Never guessed.

US obligations are triggered by **where the customers are** and by **volume and revenue thresholds**, not by where the company sits. Questions 12 to 20 decide which state laws apply at all, so they are not optional detail — they are the scope of the whole engagement.

## Entity

1. Legal name exactly as registered, and any DBA / trade name used on the site
2. Entity type (LLC, corporation, sole proprietorship, non-profit) and state of formation
3. Physical mailing address that can appear in a CAN-SPAM footer (street address, registered PO box, or CMRA private mailbox — all three are permitted by 15 U.S.C. § 7704(a)(5)(A)(iii))
4. Contact email and, if any, phone number and toll-free number
5. Is the entity a non-profit? Most state comprehensive privacy laws exempt non-profits; Colorado, Oregon and Delaware do not fully. Flag if yes.

## Site and product

6. What the site does: brochure, blog, SaaS, e-commerce, marketplace, app, community/UGC
7. Does the site or app display, stream or embed **video content** of any kind? (VPPA trigger — 18 U.S.C. § 2710)
8. Does the site host content submitted by third parties (comments, listings, uploads, reviews)? (Section 230 and DMCA § 512 triggers)
9. Is there a subscription, free trial, auto-renewal, or any recurring charge?
10. Is there a physical place of business open to the public? (Changes the ADA Title III analysis in nexus circuits.)
11. Are goods shipped, or are digital goods/services delivered?

## Scope — the threshold questions

12. **Which US states do customers live in?** Not where the company is. If unknown, assume all 50.
13. Number of **distinct US consumers** whose personal data is processed per calendar year, and the number per state where known. The common state thresholds sit at 100,000 and 35,000; some at 25,000; Texas and Nebraska have none.
14. **Annual gross revenue**, worldwide and US. Relevant for CCPA (a fixed dollar threshold) and for several state laws that have none.
15. Does the business **sell** personal data, or **share** it for cross-context behavioural advertising? Any third-party advertising, retargeting or analytics pixel usually counts as a "sale" or "share" under state law definitions even without money changing hands.
16. What percentage of annual revenue comes from selling personal data? (Alternative CCPA trigger and a trigger in several states.)
17. Does the business qualify as a **small business under the SBA size standards**? (Determines whether the Texas law applies at all.)
18. Is any **sensitive data** processed: precise geolocation, race, ethnicity, religion, mental or physical health, sex life or sexual orientation, immigration status, biometric or genetic data, citizenship, union membership, government identifiers, financial account details, children's data?
19. Is any data processed that could be characterised as **consumer health data** in the broad statutory sense (bodily functions, symptoms, gender-affirming care, reproductive care, health-related purchases, inferences from any of these)?
20. Are there users **under 18**? Under 13? Is the service "directed to children" within the meaning of 16 CFR § 312.2, or is there actual knowledge of under-13 users?

## Data practices

21. Categories of personal information collected, per source
22. Purpose for each category, and retention period or the criteria used to set it
23. Categories of third parties each category is disclosed to, and whether the disclosure is a sale, a share for cross-context behavioural advertising, or a service-provider/processor disclosure under contract
24. Do written contracts with the required statutory terms exist with every service provider, processor and contractor?
25. Is profiling used for decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects (credit, housing, employment, education, insurance, healthcare, essential goods)?
26. Is any automated decisionmaking technology used to make or substantially replace a significant decision about a Californian?
27. Are targeted advertising, cross-device tracking, session replay, chat widgets, or fingerprinting scripts in use? List every third-party domain contacted by the site.
28. Is a Global Privacy Control / opt-out preference signal detected and acted on today? How is that verified?

## Marketing

29. Email marketing: list source, opt-in mechanism, suppression list handling, sending platform
30. SMS or MMS marketing: consent capture wording, disclosure of message frequency and rates, opt-out keyword handling, send-time windows
31. Telephone calls to consumers, including calls following an online form fill
32. Influencer, affiliate, or incentivised-review programmes
33. Are customer reviews or testimonials displayed, moderated, filtered, or incentivised?
34. Any "Made in USA", "free", "guaranteed", or comparative-price claim on the site

## Contract and platform

35. Are the terms of service presented as a checkbox at signup or checkout, or only linked in the footer?
36. Is there an arbitration clause and class-action waiver? Which arbitration provider and ruleset?
37. Chosen governing law and forum
38. If UGC is hosted: is a DMCA designated agent currently registered with the U.S. Copyright Office, and on what date was the registration last renewed?
39. Is there a written repeat-infringer policy, and is it actually enforced?

## Regulated verticals

40. Health data received from or on behalf of a covered entity (HIPAA), student records (FERPA), consumer reports or background checks (FCRA), or any financial product, lending, tax preparation, or payment facilitation (GLBA / FTC Safeguards Rule)?
41. Is the business subject to any state licensing regime that carries its own disclosure duty?

## Extraterritorial reach

42. Are goods or services **offered to people in the EU or UK** — EU-language versions, EUR/GBP pricing, EU shipping, EU-targeted ads? If yes, GDPR Art 3(2) applies in addition to US law and an EU/UK representative under Art 27 may be required. Flag; do not silently ignore.
43. Are customers in Canada (CASL, PIPEDA), Brazil (LGPD), or China? Same logic.
