# Sources — where to look, and what each can't do

Load when: choosing where to search, or when a source's coverage or reliability matters.
All free unless noted. Checked 2026-09-28.

| Need | Source | Notes and gaps |
|---|---|---|
| NY statutes (current) | nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/<LAW>/<section> | Official consolidated-law text, current version. Law IDs: CPLR `CVP`, EPTL `EPT`, SCPA `SCP`, DRL `DOM`, FCA `FCT`, GOL `GOB`, BCL `BSC`, LLCL `LLC`, Partnership `PTR`, Penal `PEN`, CPL `CPL`, RPL `RPP`, RPAPL `RPA`, Judiciary `JUD`, SAPA `SAP`, Public Officers `PBO`, GML `GMU`, Labor `LAB`, Insurance `ISC`, VTL `VAT`, Civil Rights `CVR`. Historical versions: use the API (key required) or a paid service |
| NY regulations (NYCRR, incl. 22 NYCRR court rules and Part 1200 RPC) | NY's official NYCRR site (hosted by Westlaw), nycourts.gov/rules | Part 1200 (Rules of Professional Conduct) is also on nycourts.gov |
| NY decisions (official) | nycourts.gov/reporter (Official Reports, slip opinions since 2003) | Authoritative text and official cite |
| NY Court of Appeals | nycourts.gov/ctapps | Decisions, certified-question docket |
| Case law, any US court | CourtListener (courtlistener.com) | Free; API needs a free token. citation-lookup endpoint verifies cites (≤250 per request, 60 valid cites/min). Coverage of older NY trial decisions is incomplete: a 404 there isn't proof a case doesn't exist, so check the Official Reports |
| Historical case law | Caselaw Access Project (static.case.law) | Bulk scans through ~2018; no newer cases |
| Federal statutes, rules | law.cornell.edu (U.S.C., FRCP, FRE, UCC model text), uscode.house.gov | The UCC on LII is the **model** code; NY's enacted UCC can differ, so check nysenate `UCC` |
| Federal regulations | ecfr.gov | Current, point-in-time available |
| U.S. Supreme Court | supremecourt.gov (slip opinions), CourtListener | |
| Dockets / filings | PACER (paid, per page), CourtListener RECAP (free subset), NYSCEF (NY e-filing, public) | |
| Ethics opinions | NYSBA ethics opinions, NYC Bar formal opinions, ABA formal opinions | Persuasive only |
| Citator (good-law check) | Shepard's (Lexis), KeyCite (Westlaw) — paid | Without one: search later opinions citing the case on CourtListener and say no citator was used |

**Web search results are leads, not sources.** Law-firm blogs and summaries are often right and
sometimes out of date; follow them to the primary text.
