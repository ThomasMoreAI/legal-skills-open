# ai-job-analysis-and-relevancy

> Use when checking whether an AI/ML selection tool is grounded in an adequate analysis of work and is demonstrably job-related — Concerns 2-3 of Tippins, Oswald & McPhail (2021). Covers whether a job analysis is necessary (even with a strong criterion-related relationship), acceptable forms and rigor, O*NET and competency-model limits, collecting task importance ratings, SME-judgment agreement, and the legal meaning of job relatedness.

**Triggers:** "does the AI tool need a job analysis", "is this algorithm job-related", "competency model vs job analysis for AI", "O*NET as job analysis", "job relevancy of scraped predictors", "Guardians job analysis".

Part of the **ai-selection-legal-ethical** plugin in [agent-io-skills](https://github.com/OpenMatter-Network/agent-io-skills). Derived from Tippins, Oswald & McPhail (2021), *Scientific, Legal, and Ethical Concerns About AI-Based Personnel Selection Tools* (*Personnel Assessment and Decisions*).

See [`SKILL.md`](SKILL.md) for the full guidance — key concepts, step-by-step process, decision tables, pitfalls, and a checklist.
