# ai-claims-and-stakeholder-audit

> Use when auditing how an AI/ML personnel assessment is described and how it affects people — Components 7-9 (information & perceptions) of the Landers & Behrend (2023) framework. Covers first-party developer claims (do they honestly and transparently follow from the audit evidence?), second-party effects on those assessed (candidate reactions, justice, false positives vs. false negatives, what is communicated), and third-party understanding (employment-law experts, regulators, community, public).

**Triggers:** "developer marketing claims vs evidence", "candidate reactions to AI hiring", "applicant fairness perceptions", "false positive vs false negative impact", "what do regulators/public think", "transparency of AI hiring claims".

Part of the **ai-personnel-assessment** plugin in [agent-io-skills](https://github.com/OpenMatter-Network/agent-io-skills). Derived from Landers & Behrend (2023), *Auditing the AI Auditors* (*American Psychologist*).

See [`SKILL.md`](SKILL.md) for the full guidance — key concepts, step-by-step process, decision tables, pitfalls, and a checklist.
