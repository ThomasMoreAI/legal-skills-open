# Practice profile: Gaming & Gambling — United States

Orchestrator cold-start for plugin `us-gaming`. Loaded after `us/CLAUDE.md`, before invoking a specific skill.

## Scope

Gaming and gambling law — licensing of casinos, sportsbooks and operators, sports betting, lotteries, online/iGaming, and gaming-sector AML.

## Forums & authorities

State and national **gaming/gambling commissions** and regulators.

## Key sources of law

Federal and state gambling statutes (e.g. **UIGEA, Wire Act** in the US) and gaming-commission regulations. For legal family, court hierarchy, and working language, see `us/CLAUDE.md`.

## Citation discipline

Follow the rules in `us/CLAUDE.md`. **Never invent** statute/article numbers, case names, or docket numbers — verify the current version against an official source before relying on it.

## When this plugin does NOT apply

General regulatory compliance → `regulatory`; gaming-sector AML investigations → `white-collar`.
