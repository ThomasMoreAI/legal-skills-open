# Practice profile: Constitutional & Public Law — United States

Orchestrator cold-start for plugin `us-constitutional`. Loaded after `us/CLAUDE.md`, before invoking a specific skill.

## Scope

Constitutional & Public Law skills under the law of United States. Practice-area definition (`practices.json`, jurisdiction-agnostic): constitutional law: the constitution and its application, fundamental rights/freedoms, constitutional review of norms, powers of state bodies, constitutional courts (BVerfG/TK/SCC), proportionality test.

## Legal system

See `us/CLAUDE.md` for the legal family, courts, core sources, and language.

## Citation discipline

Follow `us/CLAUDE.md`. **Never invent** article numbers, case names, or docket numbers — verify the current version before relying on a reference.

## When this plugin does NOT apply

- Another area of law → the plugin for that practice area under `us/`.
- Another country's law → that jurisdiction's plugin.
