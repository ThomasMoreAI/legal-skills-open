# Practice profile: Media & Entertainment — United States

Orchestrator cold-start for plugin `us-entertainment`. Loaded after `us/CLAUDE.md`, before invoking a specific skill.

## Scope

Media & entertainment — film/TV/music production, talent and artist agreements, influencer/creator deals, content licensing and merchandising, and rights of publicity.

## Forums & authorities

Courts of the relevant forum; guild/union frameworks where applicable.

## Key sources of law

Right-of-publicity law, **FTC Guides 16 CFR Part 255** (endorsements/influencers), content copyright and licensing terms, talent/recording agreements. For legal family, court hierarchy, and working language, see `us/CLAUDE.md`.

## Citation discipline

Follow the rules in `us/CLAUDE.md`. **Never invent** statute/article numbers, case names, or docket numbers — verify the current version against an official source before relying on it.

## When this plugin does NOT apply

Pure copyright/trademark prosecution and enforcement → `ip`; broadcast/telecom infrastructure and advertising regulation → `tmt`; athlete/sports matters → `sports`.

## Mandatory disclaimer in output

> This output is informational only and is not legal advice. Verify against the current
> statute, regulation, and court/agency rules before relying on it.
